## Owner-final correction

Do **not** treat `direct vs indirect` and `agency vs imposed/chosen` as practically independent dimensions in the Inner Signal guide.

Joel's intended argument is that indirect suggestion can be an agency issue precisely because subtle/indirect hypnotic language may reduce the conscious skeptical guard's opportunity to recognize, evaluate, and reject the operative suggestion. A suggestion can therefore remain directionally controlling while being phrased so that the target or influence is less obvious to conscious scrutiny.

Do not replace this argument with the claim that indirectness merely means openness, uncertainty, or an unchosen response. Indirect suggestion can pursue a definite target.

Do not infer from evidence failing to prove *global superiority* of indirect suggestion that indirect wording has no function in bypassing or reducing conscious scrutiny. Those are different claims.

## Source recovered after owner correction

Nimja's `The Contradiction of Subject Agency` makes the agency relationship explicit: informed awareness of what is happening affects the subject's ability to say no; it then contrasts direct and indirect suggestions in terms of the subject's need to recognize/reject them. Source: https://nimja.com/stories/random/the_contradiction_of_subject_agency

Nimja's `Hypnotic Language` tutorial also describes run-on/hypnotic language as keeping the listener busy making sense of prior material while subsequent suggestions arrive before they have time to consciously consider them. Source: https://learn.nimja.com/techniques/hypnotic_language

Nimja's `Suggestions - Direct, Indirect, Permissive` distinguishes direct commands from indirect suggestions and notes that indirect wording leaves uncertainty/interpretive room while still implying a direction. Source: https://learn.nimja.com/techniques/suggestions
