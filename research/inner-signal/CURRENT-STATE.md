## Design judgments and limits

The overall synthesis and app workflow remain proposals, not validated clinical rules. Comfort and consent alone do not create missing clinical capabilities. Current orientation, agency, actual response, later functioning, available support and the procedure also matter. A service limitation describes the service rather than proving that the user is incapable. A clinician's presence does not justify coercion or make recovered images historically true.

The re-entry cue is for deliberate future practice after learning entry and return, distinct from everyday alert calming, synchronicity reality-testing and stopping. Do not train or use hypnotic re-entry while driving. A cancellation statement is not proof that an association has instantly disappeared.

Reference reading in this conversation does not train or update a deployed app. Integration and source retrieval must be explicit. Knowledge access can be broad without assuming the product can conduct every procedure it explains.
