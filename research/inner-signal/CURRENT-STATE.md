## Assistant design interpretation, not an additional owner-final instruction

The proposed app scope distinguishes topic knowledge, educational discussion, live procedures, and clinical capability. Comfort or a statement of consent alone is not clinical clearance. Current orientation, agency, adverse responses, functioning, support, and the procedure matter too. A service limitation should be described as a limitation of the service, not proof that the user is personally incapable. A clinician’s presence does not legitimize coercion or make recovered imagery factual. These distinctions are explicit in the proposed design and remain open to editorial discussion; they are not falsely attributed to owner acceptance or to a validated risk algorithm.

The proposed re-entry cue is user-chosen and deliberately used for future sessions after learning entry and return. It is not an indiscriminate external command, memory-recovery trigger, or guarantee of automatic safety. Keep it distinct from everyday alert-calming and the stop/return procedure. Never teach or test trance re-entry while driving. A deactivation statement is not proof that a learned association has been instantly erased.

## Audit outcome and unresolved decisions

Retain r01’s architecture. Proposed bounded improvements:

- Distinguish a person explicitly rejecting wording from unexplained bracing; bodily reaction alone does not identify the cause.
- Teach a short example of producing responsive indirect language at the handoff, rather than merely announcing that the next words are the learner’s.
- Make chosen sound/contact references consistent.
- Keep critical judgment available during hypnosis; defer detailed verification and consequential commitments, not all evaluation.
- Teach entry/return as a skill before optimizing speed; add the optional deliberate re-entry cue after the full practice has been learned.
- Keep affirmative resource development, play, communion and rehearsal alongside inquiry; do not turn the guide into a problem-finding exercise.

Two substantive source concerns are proposals for owner consultation, not accepted corrections: Appendix H’s `A two-breath diagnostic` cannot determine an experience’s origin from whether vulnerability appears quickly; and the `During the next 4–12 weeks` paragraph should not imply postponing help for urgent symptoms. Preserve the owner’s material while raising these specific points directly.
