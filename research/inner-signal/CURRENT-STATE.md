## Assistant design interpretation, not an additional owner-final instruction

The proposed app scope distinguishes topic knowledge, educational discussion, live procedures, and clinical capability. Comfort or a statement of consent alone is not clinical clearance. Current orientation, agency, adverse responses, functioning, support, and the procedure matter too. A service limitation should be described as a limitation of the service, not proof that the user is personally incapable. A clinician’s presence does not legitimize coercion or make recovered imagery factual. These distinctions are explicit in the proposed design and remain open to editorial discussion; they are not falsely attributed to owner acceptance or to a validated risk algorithm.
