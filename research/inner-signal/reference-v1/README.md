## What was completed

The additional reading went beyond the first prototype's narrow needs. It covered substantial further material on sensory learning, direct/indirect suggestion, ego-state assumptions, resources, future orientation, memory procedures, integration, relational mismatch, overprotection, touch, forgiveness, embodiment and spirituality. General hypnosis gaps were supplemented with seven authored ISH encyclopedia entries, selected first-party Nimja technique pages and relevant professional guidance. The SCEH introductory syllabus was used as a coverage baseline, not represented as attended training.

Reading was targeted and source-aware, not cover-to-cover completion of every book and reference. The source ledger distinguishes complete chapters, page ranges, excerpts, previously read material and inaccessible primary research. No missing companion audio was reconstructed.

The editorial decision is that the foundational reading is sufficient to write the guide. More general reading is now less useful than applying the accepted refinements and preserving Joel's actual material. No new book upload is needed for that step.

## What this does not complete

The app has not been updated by the assistant reading these materials. It needs deliberate integration of the reference, appropriate source retrieval for unfamiliar or specialist questions, and tests of actual answers and voice behavior. The authored cases are test specifications, not reported test results. No independent clinical review or human usability/efficacy study was conducted.

This library does not contain all future knowledge about hypnosis. It provides a bounded foundation plus a procedure for retrieving what a particular question requires. It keeps knowledge access broader than live enactment while keeping essential self-practice instructions in the guide itself.

## Editorial constraints carried forward

Keep the induction and rich indirect language. Preserve human/personal and uncertain/mixed sections in place unless consulted. Shorten duplicate instructional functions, not Joel's position or experiences. Self-hypnosis can be a complete endpoint. Keep the approved sleep-playlist replacement. The previously proposed body-response, critical-judgment, cue-learning, metaphysical-diagnostic and support-timing refinements are now owner-accepted in direction.

No citations belong in spoken hypnotic passages. Use a section-matched source appendix, sparse natural links where needed in explanatory prose, and detailed source custody in the working files. A more scholarly internal reference must not make the guide or voice experience sound like an academic paper.

## Reuse boundary

These Markdown files can be read or indexed by a later worker without the book files being public. They contain original paraphrases, not redistributed books or full extracts. A later app integration should retain source/date/claim-role distinctions and must not claim source access that it does not have. Private user episodes remain separate from this public-eligible editorial reference.
