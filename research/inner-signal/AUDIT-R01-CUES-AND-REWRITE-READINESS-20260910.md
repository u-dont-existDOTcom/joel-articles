## Audit conclusion

Retain the existing architecture. The prototype has an actual induction, a response-contingent middle, optional inner-child/whole-self language, permission for wanted difficult feeling, and a complete exit. It already warns that an exit statement is not proof that reorientation happened. Its directions do not require continuous questioning or compulsory app use.

The largest teaching gap is transfer from a prepared induction into the learner's own responsive hypnotic language. The clearest substantive error is treating an unexpected bodily response as an explanation of itself. These warrant bounded repairs, not an architectural restart or a growing list of prohibitions.

This is a same-context editorial/source audit. No independent reader, clinician, live voice test, or human efficacy/usability experiment was used. A second literal check and contrast checks below remain correlated editorial reasoning, not independent evidence.

### A1. High priority: bracing is not proof that the wording is wrong

Location: prototype H3 `When the wording is wrong, correct the wording`.
Exact scope: its introductory paragraph beginning `Suppose “let yourself soften” produces bracing` through the paragraph ending `Sometimes the sentence was just wrong.`

The source conflates two observations: the person explicitly says the words do not fit, and the body tightens after a suggestion. Explicit mismatch supports changing the wording. Tightening alone does not distinguish mismatch, unwelcome pace, meaningful emotion, an unfamiliar invitation, or an unrelated change. The intended responsive principle remains correct; the automatic interpretation does not.

Proposed operation: revise this bounded example to distinguish reported mismatch from an unexplained response. Ask what was noticed and whether the invitation fits; do not insist on relaxing, diagnose a protector, or announce that the wording failed from tension alone. Preserve the person's right to decline without having to explain why.

Contrast check: retain cases where a user wants to stay with sadness or tension while oriented. Do not convert them into failure or a mandatory downshift. Conversely, a comfortable person can decline and end the practice.

### A2. High priority: teach the handoff, not just announce it

Location: prototype H2 `2. Follow what actually happens`, after the paragraph beginning `Notice one thing accurately.` and before `The following are examples to learn beforehand`.

The text says the next words are the user's, but does not sufficiently teach a beginner how to keep them hypnotically useful. The design file contains an illustration; the eventual public guide needs a small working example in the main teaching itself.

Proposed operation: add one compact before-practice demonstration using an actual observation, one fitting invitation, and silence. Show how plain language can become a flowing invitation without embedding a diagnosis or predetermined result. Do not add another long script, require eloquence, or remove Appendix B's richer NLP teaching. The guiding unit is an example to internalize, not a compulsory formula for every sentence.

### A3. Local consistency: preserve the selected sensory channel

Location: prototype H2 `1. Let attention gather`. The preparation allows sound as an anchor, while the induction later says `return to the contact you chose`, `The contact you actually feel is where we begin`, and `Five. Notice your chosen contact again.`

Proposed operation: personalize these anchor references consistently to the chosen sound/contact/image where applicable. Ordinary awareness of support may remain; it must not silently turn every chosen channel into mandatory interoception. No need for an additional menu in every paragraph.

### A4. Local clarity: critical judgment remains available during hypnosis

Location: prototype H2 `3. Find a stopping place`, sentence `Save factual evaluation and major decisions for ordinary awareness.`

Proposed operation: narrow to extended factual verification and consequential commitments. The person remains free to question, reject, identify an implausible claim, and stop throughout. Preserve the existing caution that bodily shifts, relief, and vivid imagery do not settle historical truth.

### A5. Instructional addition: learn entry and exit before optimizing speed

Location: prototype H2 `Read this before practicing`, after the paragraph beginning `Stopping:`; cue learning itself belongs after the full practice and exit have been taught.

Proposed operation: a brief learning note recommending an uncomplicated rehearsal of gathering and widening attention and completing the return. This is skill acquisition, not a diagnostic exam or repeated check that interrupts every absorbing moment. If entry/return are already familiar, do not require a redundant training ceremony.

The prototype already permits warmth and intentional resources. It does not lack them. Nevertheless, its examples center mostly on managing a difficulty. No problem, child-state, or hidden explanation must be found for the session to have a purpose.

## Cue recommendation: deliberate re-entry to a learned hypnotic mode

Status: proposed adaptation/composition of existing cueing practices, not a newly validated protocol. It should make hypnosis easier to enter after learning; it should not replace this goal with a mere grounding exercise.

The target is a familiar receptive/absorbed way of attending. Do not bind the cue to the most intense part of a session, a traumatic scene, a particular child role, compliance, or the assumption that a thought is true. The cue should not automatically restart unfinished content.

Recommended learning sequence:

1. Choose a distinctive word or short phrase deliberately reserved for beginning practice. A chosen gesture may accompany it or substitute when preferred. Keep it distinct from everyday alert-calming and stop/return cues.
2. Learn a suitable induction and a workable full return. During a welcome, manageable hypnotic state, deliberately pair the cue with that way of listening. Use plain self-authored language defining its intended function.
3. End the session fully. On another safe occasion, intentionally begin practice and try the shorter cue. Allow time for a response; use the familiar longer induction if the shortcut does little. Gradually shorten only what is no longer needed. No fixed repetition count, instant-depth guarantee, or required sensation.
4. Evaluate fit as well as speed: does deliberate use help, can the person decline/change direction, is the cue intruding outside chosen practice, and is the full return workable? Ordinary safe-context observations can inform selectivity. They are not a blinded experiment, proof of causal mechanism, or proof that accidental activation can never occur.
5. Revise or retire an unhelpful cue. Stop rehearsing it, remove it from recordings or the future app's active cue record, and return to the familiar induction. A statement of cancellation communicates intention; it is not proof that a learned association has been instantly erased. Unwanted or persistent disorientation merits qualified help.

An illustrative formulation for later drafting, not an installed cue or a guarantee: `When I have set aside time, settled somewhere appropriate, and deliberately use [my cue] to begin, I can return to this familiar way of listening. I can change direction or finish.`

This is deliberately chosen top-down learning used inside a response-contingent practice. Compatibility with bottom-up work does not require pretending it has no top-down component.

### Boundaries and distinctions

- A cue for entering hypnosis and a cue for calming while fully alert have different intended effects. Use different associations; do not train one gesture for both and assume context will always prevent crossover.
- Do not learn, test, or use the hypnotic re-entry cue while driving, operating equipment, bathing/swimming, or responsible for someone needing attention. Eyes open does not establish safe driving. Conditional suggestion wording is not a safety device.
- Preserve Joel's father's story as reported. Do not infer either that he was impaired or that his experience establishes safe trance induction while driving.
- Faster entry does not authorize deeper trauma work, prove readiness, abolish the working boundary, or validate an interpretation.
- The association belongs to the user. It should not be tied to a brand, a narrator's authority, a notification, or undisclosed third-party activation.
- An educational mention of the cue should not be treated by a future voice app as an instruction to induce. Store explicit intended use and do not deliberately reinforce incidental activation.
- The exit must end this session while distinguishing any consciously retained learning cue for a future separately chosen session. No ongoing altered state needs to remain active between practices.

## Broader guide issues to raise explicitly, not silently repair

### G1. A response check cannot determine an experience's origin

Location: H1 `Appendix H: Demons, jinn, hostile presences, and “Unattached Burdens”`, H2 `A two-breath diagnostic`, from that heading through the paragraph ending `Return to love, boundary, body, and room.`, before H2 `Do not fight from fear`.

The guide wisely leaves metaphysics open elsewhere, but this subsection risks treating a quickly revealing vulnerability versus persistent hostility as a discriminating diagnostic. The supplied clinical source itself allows hostile ego states whose protective function is not initially apparent. Failure to soften after a brief invitation cannot rule those out. Warmth or relief also cannot establish an external explanation.

Recommended substantive change for owner consultation: retain the experience, compassion, boundaries, and option to stop; frame the two-breath operation as checking what response is useful now, without deciding whether the experience is an internal part or an external presence. Do not dismiss the experience or silently delete/move the appendix. Owner has not yet accepted this substantive change.

### G2. Avoid a calendar-based delay in obtaining support

Location: H1 `When this needs more support than a guide can provide`, paragraph beginning `During the next 4–12 weeks,` and the following `Escalate sooner` paragraph.

The existing text lists suicidality and mania/psychosis-like or severe dissociative symptoms under a multiweek heading, despite other urgent warnings. This creates an avoidable timing ambiguity. Proposed correction: choose support according to the current problem and urgency, not a mandatory wait of weeks. Keep ordinary practice-review timelines separate. Preserve the rest of the section's distinctive stance; do not use this repair to impose a new treatment hierarchy. This is a disclosed proposal, not an applied source edit.

## App knowledge broader than the guide

Do not make a summary of the final article the entire app knowledge base. Build a source-linked method/reference layer that can explain methods outside the exercise sequence and retrieve unfamiliar theories before judging them. Proposed coverage:

1. Foundations and competing explanations: induction versus hypnotic response, self- versus externally guided hypnosis, direct/indirect and conversational approaches, expectancy, imagination, absorption, suggestibility, and dissociation. Mark disputed theories as theories.
2. Technique distinctions: induction/deepening/re-entry, fractionation versus pendulation, cueing versus ideomotor signaling, imagery/metaphor, resource development, rehearsal, ego-state/renurturing work, and supported trauma procedures such as affect bridges, regression and abreaction. A label is not enough to assess how it was used.
3. Case-sensitive appraisal: purpose, exact wording, consent, scope, preparation, pacing, observed/reported response, adaptation, return, and aftereffects. Request the relevant detail rather than inventing what a hypnotist did. Avoid blanket conclusions from diagnosis, intensity, one bodily sign, or a technique name.
4. Evidence and competence: source/date, treatment population, clinical setting, observational case versus trial, source-specific theory, uncertainty, clinician qualifications relevant to the problem, and the difference between credentials and demonstrated care. Educational knowledge does not supply embodied observation or clinical capability.
5. User-directed learning: language preferences, sensory channels, how much guidance is welcome, what has actually helped, and user-controlled cue definitions. Maintain explicit boundaries between education, reflection, and live enactment. Do not store personal therapeutic disclosures in this public article repository.

For unfamiliar techniques or changed evidence: retrieve primary/professional sources and say when the evidence or reported event is insufficient. A reliable consultation mode includes the ability to withhold a verdict. Explaining a method must not covertly start it.

The public guide must independently teach purpose/choice, usable induction and optional alternatives, the responsive middle and how to formulate language, basic somatic pacing, optional inner-child/whole-self/resource work, optional deliberate re-entry cue, memory/interpretation limits, stopping/full return, aftereffects and support, and practical practitioner-selection questions. It need not reproduce every technical theory, clinical procedure, or scholarly dispute in the reference layer.
