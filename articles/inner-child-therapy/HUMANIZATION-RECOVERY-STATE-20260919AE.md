## What changed

### 1. Generation scope grew
Earlier transfer attempts were paragraph-sized and carried a small number of live source functions.

Recent Episode 008 Pass A/B attempts asked for a complete subsection carrying many preservation/safety/decision functions at once.

This turns continuation into constrained coverage optimization.

### 2. Critic knowledge moved into the writer environment
The current project now activates a much larger humanization stack before prose:
- preservation/source-integrity gates;
- positive and negative calibration;
- marching-efficiency diagnostics;
- generator-learning compiler;
- invention restrictions;
- stopping boundaries;
- detector/history awareness at the supervisor layer.

Even when the immediate writer packet hides the ledger, a writer launched inside the Article Skill project is not equivalent to an ordinary fresh writing context.

The project itself records the corresponding failure mode:
**critic/diagnostic learning is strong; reliable generator transfer is not.**

### 3. Recent packets encoded the answer's architecture
Pass A exposed semantic obligations directly.
Pass B2 converted them into a prose scene, and the model copied the scene paragraph verbatim before expanding the remaining instructions in order.

Those runs were not clean tests of `continue from Human prose`.

### 4. The acceptance standard also became more discriminating
Some historical Pangram-Human model passages, especially EFT, were later judged by Joel to still look AI-shaped.

That explains part of the apparent regression, but not all of it:
Episode 002/005 remain direct evidence that paragraph continuation could preserve a Human boundary under the earlier workflow.

## Supported causal diagnosis

Best-supported current hypothesis:
**the writer has become overconditioned by the humanization/critic environment and by larger multi-function generation units. The system is better at policing prose than at simply continuing a thought.**

This is a supported inference from the workflow change and failure topology, not proof of a unique hidden model mechanism.

There is currently no evidence establishing a provider/model regression. A model update remains possible but unverified.
