# Grounding reviewer v3: two new checks, validated (2026-09-30, turn 5)

**Why.** Joel rethought P1's "leave it" from his own experience (03:29 UTC): someone can feel less and less safe with a partner for reasons inside them, so "leave it" is too simple. Every earlier review had passed that line, including V7, which caught the "only reason" in the next sentence. Joel asked whether the reviewer could have seen it, and how.

It could, from the article alone. Catch the Hook already teaches that a reaction can be old material ("Somebody may really have crossed a boundary and still have hit something old in you"). "Leave it" acted on the feeling of being less safe as if that were settled. The reviewer missed it because its rules checked an instruction only against the guide ("Check that it's the guide's instruction, with the guide's conditions"), and this one matched the guide word for word ("Leave a relationship that keeps eroding safety").

**The two checks added** (`grounding.txt`; the blind runs had no worked examples of either):
1. **MISFIRES.** Test every instruction, including a faithful carry of the guide or of the author's ruling, against three or four different readers and against the article's own model of people. Look hardest at costly or hard-to-undo instructions.
2. **Referents.** Check each "it"/"this"/"that" in a paragraph's first two sentences against the end of the paragraph before. This was aimed at the "If you did it" Joel caught in z1.

Outputs are in `v3-outputs-20260930.md`, and targets in `v3-targets/` and `../targets/`.

**What stays:**
- MISFIRES stays. It caught Joel's point blind, and a real gap in the new wording. It raised no MISFIRES flag on two accepted controls, and one on Joel's P4 ending for his call.
- The referent check is gone from both prompts. It missed Joel's "it" 4 times out of 4, because every model reader took "After the day" as pinning "it" to the act. It also made one debatable flag.
- In its place, `tells_lint.py` gives a REVIEW warning (R1) when an action "it" ("did it", "do it", "tried it") falls in a paragraph's first two sentences. It only catches that one kind, and the fix it points to is the one Joel made: name the thing ("the Protector action(s)").
