A Smaller Doorway: Goodwill

Sometimes "I love you" is more than you can honestly say, especially to somebody who's treated you badly, and even the usual loving-kindness line, "May you be happy," can feel like rewarding them. "May you be loving, peaceful, and free" might be easier to mean. If they were any of those things, they probably wouldn't have done it in the first place. And it doesn't mean they get to come back over for coffee.

It might open up a little warmth, or it might just stay a quiet intention. And if "I love you" goes flat when you aim it at yourself or your little one, the same wish works there, too.
