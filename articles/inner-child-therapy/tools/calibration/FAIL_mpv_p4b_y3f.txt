If the day goes by without you doing it, look at what actually happened before you decide why. Maybe it was fear, but maybe you weren't sure what you were going for, or didn't know how, or were exhausted, or something practical came up. And if you did it but didn't feel better right away, that doesn't mean you failed. Checking it off doesn't prove it helped either, so keep or adjust the acts depending on what they add, like being safer, closer to your little one, more capable, or enjoying things more. Sometimes what an act adds isn't what you picked it for.
