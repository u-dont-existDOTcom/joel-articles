If you did it, not feeling better right away doesn't mean it failed, and you can ask yourself again, "What is this practice supposed to help, and how would I know if it's helping?" Not doing it is harder to figure out. Fear is the first guess, and it might be right. But if you weren't sure what you were going for, you probably didn't know how to do it either, and trying without knowing how wears you out fast. Even a practical snag, like whoever said they'd help not showing up, looks bigger when the goal's fuzzy.
