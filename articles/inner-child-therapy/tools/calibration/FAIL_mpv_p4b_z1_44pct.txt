After the day, look at what actually happened. If you did it, not feeling better right away doesn't mean it failed, and you can ask yourself again, "What is this practice supposed to help, and how would I know if it's helping?" Not doing it is harder to figure out. Fear is the first guess, and it might be right, but maybe you didn't know how, or weren't sure what you were going for. Being worn out can stop you too, and sometimes whoever said they'd help couldn't make it.
