Plenty of people are still at the putting-up-with part. If that's you, and there's little or no warmth for your little one yet, protecting them still begins the relationship. Your first care might be a real meal instead of something eaten standing at the counter, or cancelling one obligation that was going to wear them out this week. Warmth can come later (which feels backwards). Take one act from all this and make it specific: when, what's the smallest first step, and do you need help, or money, or a form? If you can see a hard moment coming, like the person you're cancelling on pushing back, practice what you'll say out loud a few times beforehand.
