# Repeats check: "Borrow the Adult Before You Can Be the Adult"

Source: repeats-borrow-h1-prompt.txt (article blocks B0 to B228). The h1 runs from B89 to B124.

## How to read this

**Scope.** One sentence in the prompt's "Where to look hardest" paragraph is garbled ("Report only repeats and contradictions where every instance is inside that h1 was put together from several sources at different times..."). Read one way, it limits the report to repeats wholly inside the h1. The same paragraph also asks for checks against the rest of the article, and of the rest against itself. I reported everything I found and tagged each item:

- [inside h1]: every instance is in B89 to B124
- [h1 + elsewhere]: at least one instance inside the h1 and one outside
- [outside h1]: no instance in the h1

Under the strict reading, only these count: repeat 4 (its B95, B97 and B99 instances), repeat 19, repeat 21 (its B100 and B119 instances), and contradiction C3.

**Each h2 of the h1 on its own.** The only repeat inside a single h2 is repeat 4 (all three of its h1 instances are in "Borrow One Competency at a Time"). "Noticing Counts", "Borrow Love—or Borrow the Perspective of Care" and "Become the Adult Apprentice" have none inside themselves.

**Images.** B0 and B28 are summary charts of the method, so most of their points come back in the text by design. I listed only the two overlaps a reader meets almost word for word (repeats 1 and 8).

Quotes are in italics and copied exactly from the article.

## 2. Contradictions and opposite pulls

**C1. B47 vs B92 (and B0)** [h1 + elsewhere]
- B47: *the usual inner-child prompt to “ask your little one what they need” can get confusing*
- B92: *What does Little Joel need right now?*
- B0: *ask what this younger state needs*
- Pull: B47 warns that "ask what they need" can confuse people who don't hear a second voice. B92 then recommends that very question, and the top chart's daily practice includes it, with no nod to the warning.
- Does the article say why? Not directly. One reconciling reading: B92 is self-talk in the third person, which needs no second voice to answer.
- Better placed: B47, the section built for people without an inner monologue.

**C2. B97 vs B129** [h1 + elsewhere]

**C3. B96 and B105 vs B124** [inside h1: "Borrow One Competency at a Time", "Borrow Love—or Borrow the Perspective of Care", "Become the Adult Apprentice"]
