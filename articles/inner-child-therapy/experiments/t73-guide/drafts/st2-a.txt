The biggest difference is spirituality. Schema therapy grew out of cognitive therapy ([Jeffrey Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Young_(psychologist)), who developed it, did his postdoctoral work with Aaron Beck), and it leaves spirituality out, so borrowing love from God, or from the universe, the way this guide does, isn't part of it. IFS goes the other way, and overboard IMO, which is the next section. Most of the other differences depend on which therapist you get.
