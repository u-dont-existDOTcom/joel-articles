The big difference for me is spirituality, which schema therapy leaves out. It grew out of cognitive therapy, after all: [Jeffrey Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Young_(psychologist)), who developed it, did his postdoctoral work with Aaron Beck. IFS goes the other way, and goes overboard on it IMO, with that perfect Self waiting underneath everything (next section). In this guide, the spiritual side is something you can borrow from, like a loan, while you keep your own judgment. Beyond that, a lot depends on the therapist.
