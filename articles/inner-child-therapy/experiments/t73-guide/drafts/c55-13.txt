My father often suggested folks remember a similar-level (or even harder) challenge that they were able to handle. Maybe you survived a car crash and surgery a few years ago, but now you're having trouble with emotional eating. You can draw strength from the past. As my sister, a historian, likes to say, ["The past didn't go anywhere."](https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/april-rosenblum-the-past-didn-t-go-anywhere) Your own challenge doesn't need to be as dramatic as a car crash, as long as it really happened. So who or what helped you get through it? Is one piece of that still within reach? If you catch yourself thinking "I survived a car crash, so why can't I stop eating when I'm upset?", that's the past turned into a stick to hit yourself with. Maybe you're more worn out now, or missing whatever helped back then. (No example? Try another doorway.)
