Not just any schema or IFS therapist will do, though. When you call one, I'd ask if they actually do the reparenting part, and then help you take it over yourself, or if it's mostly questionnaires. Will they take a no, if you'd rather skip the imagery or the empty chair? Or do they call it "avoidance"? Spiritual experiences are another good test. You want someone who neither tells you what yours really are nor treats them as symptoms. (Bonus points if they can admit a mistake and not get weird about it 🤭) A listing in a directory, or a certificate, doesn't tell you any of this, so the first call matters.
