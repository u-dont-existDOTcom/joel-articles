After the day, look at what actually happened. If you did the Protector action(s), not feeling better right away is information to learn from. It doesn't mean failure, since healing is nonlinear, and sometimes standing up for yourself might make you feel worse at first. And you can ask yourself again, "What is this practice supposed to help, and how would I know if it's helping?" Not doing it is harder to figure out. Fear is the first guess, but maybe you didn't know how or why. Being worn out can stop you too, and sometimes whoever said they'd help couldn't make it. Hey, we've all been there. 🤗
