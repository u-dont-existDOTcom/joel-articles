And not just any schema or IFS therapist will be a good fit for this kind of work. In a first call, I'd ask whether they really do the reparenting part and then help you take it over, or mostly hand out questionnaires and talk about your thinking. What if you'd rather skip the imagery, or talking to an empty chair? A good one takes that no and doesn't call it "avoidance." Ask how they'd handle your spiritual experiences, too, since you want someone who won't tell you what they really are, or treat them as a symptom. And can they admit it when they've gotten something wrong? A listing in a directory doesn't tell you any of that.
