## Exact internal V25

> ## When the Present-Day Adult Is Dangerous to the Child
>
> Suppose the safety question is skipped and the child is brought forward anyway. The child recoils. If the present-day adult already enjoys fear, humiliation, or domination, the exercise has just handed that adult more of the thing they enjoy. The reaction tells you the child is frightened; it does not tell you the adult has become safe.
>
> That adult may still need help, but the work has to happen without using the child as either evidence or leverage. Disturbing thoughts, unusual beliefs, even past harm can all be part of the picture without meaning the person currently wants to hurt anyone, so the question stays with what the adult now endorses and can choose. A person who calls themselves sovereign while insisting they cannot refuse an urge or authority has plenty to examine there without settling the metaphysics. If the harmful intent later changes, the evidence can come from the adult's life—repeated choices when exploitation would still be rewarding. The child can stay outside that process; sometimes a sustained relationship with another person is the more appropriate place for the work.
