## What drifted afterward

### 1. We changed the research question

After the live Episode 007 success, work increasingly tested whether the skill could be **distilled into a fresh zero-shot writer packet**.

R16/R17 fresh-writer experiments therefore tested portability of the live apprenticeship, not the already-demonstrated supervised humanization loop.

R17's failure is evidence against that portable whole-subsection interface. It is not evidence that the original supervised model-humanization loop stopped existing.

### 2. We changed the generation input

Successful work transformed literal prose under target-local feedback.

Later work increasingly generated from preservation/function inventories. The semantic ledger became a latent outline and repeatedly produced:
- comprehensive mini-lessons;
- synthetic reader examples designed to prove the thesis;
- one-function-per-sentence marching advancement.

That is a different generation problem from humanizing literal failed prose.

### 3. We over-weighted same-model pre-admission

The successful campaign learned from external localization. Later passes increasingly rejected or redesigned prose through correlated same-context criticism before acquiring comparable target-local external evidence.

Negative self-audit is useful, but it cannot replace the external feedback loop that actually trained the live Episode 007 context.

### 4. We shrank the target without earned green islands

The serial Unit A/A1 experiments narrowed scope before any new owner/Pangram green region had been demonstrated.

Episode 007 narrowed scope **after** evidence established stable Human regions. The later unit decomposition tried to manufacture that localization structurally.

### 5. The owner-cognition proposal changed the task entirely

The proposed `owner cognition -> minimal assembly` route may be useful for ordinary authorship, but it does not test humanization ability. It is therefore rejected for the present capability experiment.
