Block numbers are this article's (ARTICLE-READER.md, the article without its lane notes).
- The inner child: "your little one", "the child", "the inner child" throughout; B8 "that rightly vulnerable child".
- The adult the reader builds, one grown-up with three jobs. B8: "Instead, we need to separately develop the qualities of a good inner parent for that rightly vulnerable child: the loving Nurturer, safe Protector, and wise Guide." B9: "That's one grown-up doing three jobs, not three more people moving in to help with the rent, and each job keeps the others in balance. ... The Protector gets you both out of there, and on the way home the Nurturer tells them they did great ... And the Guide helps them think twice about the next weird party". B11: "If the phrase "inner parent" calls up the wrong archetype for you, pick another one. Your future self might work, or the captain of a ship, so long as they'd do those three jobs." B32's heading calls it the Inner Adult ("Don't Give the Inner Adult Away"); B408: "the inner adult you build and practice occupying: Nurturer, Protector, and Leader, Guide, or Guru." B321: "As the Guide, you choose what your little one learns ... and you hold standards, the way a good parent would." So in this article "inner parent" names the adult the reader builds, not the internalized voice.
- The internalized caregiver voice. B205: "I'd look at the parent "script" you inherited: how you learned to treat yourself from how people treated you growing up, long before you started building your Nurturer, Protector and Guide. It can be warm, but it's often more like a critic, or a warden with rules." B206: "If it's a critic, the parent you inherited also measures whatever you show the world against an impossible ideal". B12 and B62 call it "the inherited critic voice" and "the inherited critic".
- Borrowed adulthood: someone else, or something you do, lending one of the built adult's jobs for a while, until it's yours (B66 "Borrow the Adult Before You Can Be the Adult"; B207: "the new grown-up can start outside your head, as someone who likes you the way you are, or as something you do to protect your little one"; B409). God or a spiritual relationship can lend love the same way (Ask God for a Loan, from B99).
- The present-day adult: the adult identity the reader has now, which for some people is dangerous to the child (B58 "When the Present-Day Adult Is Dangerous to the Child"; B59: "They need to first deconstruct the adult identity they've got.").
- Protective parts, the guards, which are not the Protector role. B50: "your protective parts are busy with the flood"; B75: "after the part guarding you has been heard ... your ego can act like a rigid security guard"; B219 to B226, "Two Common Protective Patterns" and "Hear the Protective Part First" (B223: "Whichever protective part showed up, try talking to it in the same Nurturer voice you meant for your little one."); B234: "I'd thank whatever part of you came up with the escape plan"; Altered States calls them "those guards from The Urge to Escape".
- The appendix's IFS "Self" and "parts" (B406 to B413) are another method's figures, compared with this one; they aren't the article's own.
