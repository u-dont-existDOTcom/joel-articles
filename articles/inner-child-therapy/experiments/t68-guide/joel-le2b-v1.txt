Something small and practical can mark the ending, like boxing up the old keys. Nobody needs to know the next beginning to close this one. When the not-knowing gets unbearable, a new identity can sure look tempting. Like a borrowed coat that doesn't fit but at least it's warm. But you can't go far with a borrowed coat anyway, so don't force the future on. Keep taking care of yourself, and grieve what ended with your little one beside you while the next life takes shape.
