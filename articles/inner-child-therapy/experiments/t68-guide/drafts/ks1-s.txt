When somebody tells me they want to kill themselves, I get stricter about spirituality, not looser. Maybe you're reading this with a plan, or the means, close by. Then before anything else, get another human being involved, and put some distance between you and the means. A crisis line or an emergency number counts, and the person on the other end can be a total stranger. Do the same if you can't stop yourself, or you're badly disoriented or intoxicated. Can't tell if that's you? Treat it like it is. This isn't me answering whether life is worth living. It just keeps the question open long enough to look at.
