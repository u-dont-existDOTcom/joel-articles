My father often suggested remembering a time when I met a difficulty in a way I respected. Somewhere in your history there's probably one of those, a time you handled something hard and came out of it respecting yourself a little. Go find it. What did you do, and what was helping you then? If one piece of that is still within reach, use it. The example can be small (it doesn't have to top this one). It's also no proof you should manage the same way today, when you might be more worn out or missing whatever helped you then. (No example? Try another way in.)
