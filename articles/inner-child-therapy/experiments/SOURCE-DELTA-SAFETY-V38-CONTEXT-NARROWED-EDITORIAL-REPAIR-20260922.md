## Exact internal V38

> ## When the Present-Day Adult Is Dangerous to the Child
>
> You can pass the readiness check above and still not be safe for the child. Someone may be calm enough to pause and choose while still enjoying frightening or humiliating vulnerable people. In that case, the child's recoil doesn't tell you the adult is safe; it may be exactly the reaction the adult likes.
>
> Alarming thoughts or beliefs are a different issue. Someone can be horrified by an intrusive thought they have no wish to act on, and something harmful they did in the past does not by itself tell you what they intend now. If they later say that has changed, it has to show up in the adult's choices over time, including when exploiting vulnerability would still feel rewarding. Some of that work may need another steady person involved for a while, with the child left out of the process until the adult side is safer.
