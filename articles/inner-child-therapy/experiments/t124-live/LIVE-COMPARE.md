Let the adult learn that feeling first; then bring it to the child. Do not demand trust as payment for a temporary ceasefire.

During the altered state, don’t keep interrupting a useful private process with courtroom-level skepticism. If the child suddenly feels real, or a memory, body sequence, image, or realization carries enormous meaning, stay with the therapeutic task while you remain oriented and able to stop. Historical certainty is a separate question when you are going to accuse, confront, report, publish, or otherwise act externally on what emerged. Felt certainty can matter therapeutically without by itself settling external fact.

Afterward, notice where the access actually is. Maybe it vanished with the state. Maybe it carried over and is fading. It is to carry some of what became real into sober love, protection, grief, practice, or action.
