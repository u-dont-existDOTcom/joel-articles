## Exact internal V37

> ## When the Present-Day Adult Is Dangerous to the Child
>
> What would actually make it safe to bring the child forward? Not simply that the adult can sit calmly and make choices, because someone who enjoys frightening or humiliating vulnerable people can use that capacity too; and not the child's recoil, which tells you the child is frightened and may be exactly the reaction the adult likes. At the same time, an adult can sound alarming for a completely different reason—a violent intrusive thought they hate, a strange belief, something ugly in their past—and none of that settles what they want now. So this is something the adult has to show in their own choices over time, including when exploitation would still feel rewarding, sometimes with another person staying with that work long enough to see whether the change is real. While that is being worked out, the child stays out of it completely.
