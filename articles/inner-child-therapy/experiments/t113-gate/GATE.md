# Turn 113: the review sheet's new words through the owner-delivery gate

Every heading on the turn-113 review sheet whose words are new, and the one sentence with new words (his 109 A), went through [owner_delivery_gate.py](https://github.com/u-dont-existDOTcom/joel-articles/blob/handoff/claude-dangerous-adult-20260924-1631/tools/humanization/owner_delivery_gate.py) (init, check, deliver) before the sheet shows it. That takes a fresh linter run (a FAIL is never waivable), the writer's verdict on all 33 rows of the tell inventory with a reason for each linter REVIEW finding ([fill_ledgers.py](https://github.com/u-dont-existDOTcom/joel-articles/blob/handoff/claude-dangerous-adult-20260924-1631/articles/inner-child-therapy/experiments/t113-gate/fill_ledgers.py)), and three separately saved reviews (preservation and two cold reads) by fresh Sonnet subagents, each with its own context id. The sheet's builder ([build_review.py](https://github.com/u-dont-existDOTcom/joel-articles/blob/handoff/claude-dangerous-adult-20260924-1631/articles/inner-child-therapy/experiments/t113-review/build_review.py)) takes each one's text from its FOR-JOEL.txt and stops if one isn't there or doesn't match.

## What it covers, and what it doesn't

- **Covered:** each heading whose words aren't the paragraph's own (a "new" heading in [proposals.py](https://github.com/u-dont-existDOTcom/joel-articles/blob/handoff/claude-dangerous-adult-20260924-1631/articles/inner-child-therapy/experiments/t113-shape/proposals.py)), as its exact words, with its section as it would read (CONTEXT.txt) and the paragraphs it stands over (SOURCE.txt); and the Solomon sentence.
- **Not covered:** the headings made of a paragraph's own first words, the first words a heading now says (dropped), the splits and the one moved sentence. None adds a word: proposals.py checks that a lifted heading is the paragraph's own words, that a drop or a cut only takes words away, that a split only adds a break at a sentence boundary, and that the move adds or loses no word. The gate is for generated prose, "not owner-authored prose".

## Three rounds

- **Round 1** ([make_gate_files.py](https://github.com/u-dont-existDOTcom/joel-articles/blob/handoff/claude-dangerous-adult-20260924-1631/articles/inner-child-therapy/experiments/t113-gate/make_gate_files.py), 17 items, two halves per role). Four FAILs, each for a heading that didn't say what a skimming reader needs: "If your case is different", "Coming back confused or spaced out" (its change also didn't say it replaced his sentence), "If you might be vulnerable" and "If a part says no". "Feeling less safe" passed, but the cold read gave it his own words, so it left the gate. The cold read 2 reviewers of both halves ran out of output room, so cold read 2 for the round-1 items that stayed was done in round 2.
- **Round 2** ([make_gate_files_r2.py](https://github.com/u-dont-existDOTcom/joel-articles/blob/handoff/claude-dangerous-adult-20260924-1631/articles/inner-child-therapy/experiments/t113-gate/make_gate_files_r2.py)): the headings the round-1 reviewers asked for ("If you're doing it right and it still isn't working", "When to stop", "Coming back from trips confused or spaced out", "If you might be prone to mania or psychosis"), each heading whose section had changed around it, in a new folder, and cold read 2 for the rest. All PASS.
- **Round 3** ([make_gate_files_r3.py](https://github.com/u-dont-existDOTcom/joel-articles/blob/handoff/claude-dangerous-adult-20260924-1631/articles/inner-child-therapy/experiments/t113-gate/make_gate_files_r3.py), 19 items, the last): after the four section cold reads ([COLD-READ.md](https://github.com/u-dont-existDOTcom/joel-articles/blob/handoff/claude-dangerous-adult-20260924-1631/articles/inner-child-therapy/experiments/t113-shape/COLD-READ.md)), every case heading became a #### and some headings were reworded or added. Each heading with new words in a section that changed got a new folder with its section as it reads now, the unchanged headings too.

A round-3 FAIL was not redrafted: its item fell back to the paragraph's own words. start-stop-r3 ("When to stop") failed (preservation: Names the action, not the situation: 'When to stop' has none of the words a reader in that situation would look for (part, hesitates, says no, touch, sex, going deeper, altered state), so a skimmer cannot tell whether the paragraph applies to them, which is what the headings are for. Reads as the general stopping rule although the paragraph gives one trigger (a part hesitating about touch or sex, or saying no to going deeper or an altered state), so it can be taken for the complete list; it also collides with the later 'If you'd rather stop' (quitting the practice), a different meaning of stop. Better heading: 'If a part hesitates or says no' (or, longer and more exact, 'If a part hesitates about touch or sex, or says no to going deeper').; cold read 2: 'When to stop' names no trigger and no object (stop what?), so a reader skimming for their own situation cannot recognize 'my part hesitated or said no'; it reads as the guide's general stopping rule, but the paragraph covers one case (a part's hesitation or no about touch, sex, going deeper or an altered state). The heading hides the paragraph's actual instruction ('not test it') and its tie to the anxiety paragraph just above ('I'd go see'), so 'even when it sounds like a prediction' has nothing to hang on for a reader who skipped 'If it's anxiety'. Better heading: 'If a part hesitates or says no' (it names the situation, like the section's other case headings 'If it's anxiety' and 'If it tells you to do something').).

Its fallback is his whole condition as the stop rule's heading, "If a part hesitates about being touched or about sex, or says no to going deeper in this work or to an altered state", and the paragraph starts "I'd stop there and not test it, even when it sounds like a prediction." It has no new words, so it left the gate. It came after the round-3 reviews of the section's other new headings, so their CONTEXT.txt still shows "When to stop" in that place; nothing else in the section changed.
