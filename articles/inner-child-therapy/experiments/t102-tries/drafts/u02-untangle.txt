Differentiation is slow, and it goes on your whole life. If a family, group or relationship won't let you do this while you're still part of it, temporary distance may be necessary. And sometimes you need the break anyway, because it's hard to hear what you actually want while the old programming is still playing in your ear. Some people come back later on different terms. Sometimes the leaving turns out to be permanent, because going back would mean disappearing again. You don't have to know yet which one yours is. As you differentiate, you learn to tell the difference.
