Differentiation is slow, and it goes on your whole life. If a family, group or relationship won't let you do this while you're still part of it, temporary distance may be necessary. It can help to get away for a while anyway, until the old programming quiets down and you can tell what you actually want. Some people come back later on different terms. Sometimes the leaving turns out to be permanent, because going back would mean disappearing again. You don't have to know yet which one yours is. As you differentiate, you learn to tell the difference.
