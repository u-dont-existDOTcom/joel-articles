## Candidate audit

### Paragraph 1

`Write the answer you want to send.` immediately puts the passage into manual mode.

The next sentence uses a deliberate three-part example set:
`The rude version, the pleading version, the five-paragraph closing argument...`

That is vivid, but visibly composed. It is followed by one sentence for the apology obligation and one sentence explaining the permission created by privacy. The paragraph therefore maps cleanly onto the first preservation functions instead of behaving as one lived event.

`Nobody is receiving this draft` is also too absolute. The exercise concerns not sending it **yet**; some version may later be sent. The sentence compresses a temporary condition into a polished certainty.

### Paragraph 2

This paragraph is the strongest part, but it still advances through a designed sequence:

1. return later;
2. abandon perfect neutrality;
3. wait until urgency decreases;
4. identify unfairness;
5. identify retaliatory language;
6. identify confusion;
7. fix those parts;
8. preserve the sentence being erased in anticipation of the other person's reaction.

The exact sequence mirrors the source ledger even though the packet said the ledger was not a sentence order.

Several lines become clipped verdict tiles:

- `Fix those parts.`
- `Their reaction matters.`
- `It does not get to write your side for you.`

The last two form a polished antithesis that summarizes the lesson after the event has already carried it.

`you want them to hurt as much as you do` is an invented psychological realization about the reader. It is framed hypothetically rather than as a factual claim, but it still supplies a dramatic motive that the source did not require.

`when every sentence no longer feels like an emergency` is fluent therapeutic compression. It sounds crafted rather than discovered inside this particular rereading.

### Paragraph 3

This is the clearest ledger-to-prose conversion.

- `Keep the apology that is yours.` carries responsibility.
- `Don't let it eat the complaint.` carries preservation of protest.
- `I should not have said that` beside `I still mean no` balances apology and boundary.
- `The clearer version may not sound nicer.` carries the explicit better-is-not-softer rule.
- `shorter, or firmer, or less interested...` carries the outcome range.
- the final sentence carries saying less or sending only the useful core.

The ideas are good. The realization is too perfectly arranged. The apology/complaint pair, the two quoted clauses, and clearer/not-nicer all create successive balanced contrasts. The paragraph reads as a compact teaching artifact rather than thought still moving.

### Paragraph 4

The manual cadence becomes unmistakable:

- pause;
- announce the pause;
- make only a keepable promise;
- reject an arbitrary deadline;
- protect immediately if needed;
- reread;
- decide whether to send anything.

`Don't promise tomorrow because you need tonight to get quieter` adds an unsupported timetable and a writerly emotional phrase in order to concretize the keepable-promise rule. It is not a source error severe enough to break preservation, but it is fabricated specificity produced by an abstract requirement.

`Then decide whether any of it still belongs in their hands` is a polished closing image. It completes the mini-lesson cleanly instead of simply stopping where the person's attention naturally stops.

## What was wrong with the writer instructions

### 1. The preservation ledger was used as the composition environment

The packet gave the writer eight explicit source obligations, then six explicit anti-pattern prohibitions, then told the writer not to convert them into steps. The disclaimer could not remove the topology the prompt had already created.

The same representation was being asked to serve as:

- semantic custody;
- composition input;
- architecture warning;
- implicit self-certification checklist.

That virtually guarantees traceability prose. A model can demonstrate obedience most safely by realizing each obligation in turn.

### 2. The positive instruction was weaker than the negative stack

The useful positive operation was:
`let the private draft and rereading behave like one real event.`

But it was surrounded by a much larger field of required meanings, prohibited forms, experimental rationale, and output rules. The writer therefore optimized for coverage and avoidance rather than following the event.

### 3. It asked for situated writing without supplying a situation rich enough to inhabit

`Write from inside that situation` sounds concrete, but the packet supplied only an abstract relational problem. It supplied no actual draft, moment of rereading, phrase the person notices, change of mind, or source-grounded reader situation.

At the same time it correctly prohibited invented Joel autobiography, facts, chronology, named people, and claims about motives. With real particulars unavailable and invention constrained, generic second-person instruction was the safest remaining genre.

### 4. It exposed critic and detector language to the writer

The packet told the writer it was producing a `Human-writing substrate`, avoiding a `known model-shaped realization`, and participating in a GPT-5.6 Pro comparison. That activates meta-writing and anti-detector problem solving rather than the article's local human concern.

Model identity, detector rationale, prior failures, and experimental purpose belong with the supervisor, not in the writer's prose environment.

### 5. The prompt demanded near-total explicit coverage

Eight source bullets plus the safety boundary created pressure to fit nearly every function into a short subsection. Calling them `preservation constraints, not a sentence order` did not change the practical incentive: omitted or deeply implicit functions risked failure, so the model made each one legible.

That legibility became sustained maximum semantic efficiency.

### 6. It mixed two incompatible goals

The packet simultaneously tried to run:

1. a raw model-substrate comparison, and
2. a production-quality Joel-byline continuation.

For a clean substrate comparison, dense task-specific anti-pattern instructions confound the model comparison. For production prose, withholding owner-aligned thought routes and current generation architecture removes the best available positive guidance.

The result therefore tests `Pro under a dense preservation-and-prohibition packet`, not Pro's general ability to write naturally and not a clean Joel-voice workflow.

### 7. The claimed isolation was only tool-read isolation

Telling a writer not to inspect other Episode 008 files prevents additional GitHub reads. It does not erase project or conversation context already present to that model surface. Therefore the run may be fresh relative to those files, but it should not be described as proven informational isolation unless it occurred in a genuinely fresh context without the hidden target history.

### 8. The launch selected a superseded architecture

The recovery branch already contained a lighter Pass B architecture that removes the source ledger and detector rationale from the writer-facing payload, then applies them in a separate post-generation gate.

That separation is the correct direction. The Pass A packet put the very material that should have remained supervisor-side back in front of the generator.

## Corrected architecture

Do not repair Pass A by adding more prohibitions or paraphrasing the same ledger.

Use this separation:

### Writer-facing payload

Give the writer only:

- the accepted local article context;
- the live relational pressure;
- one concrete private-draft/reread event;
- a small positive composition operation based on human attention order;
- hard invention and immediate-protection boundaries;
- the exact output boundary.

Do not give the writer the full preservation ledger, detector rationale, prior failure taxonomy, or a long anti-pattern list.

### Supervisor-side gate

After prose exists:

1. test all preservation units;
2. reverse-audit additions and omissions;
3. audit complete-boundary thought shape;
4. reject global marching before detector spend;
5. use Pangram only when the candidate is already editorially plausible and the result could change the next action.

The existing Pass B light payload and post-generation gate implement this separation. The next structurally valid model comparison is a genuinely fresh Pro run on that light payload, not another Pass A variant.
