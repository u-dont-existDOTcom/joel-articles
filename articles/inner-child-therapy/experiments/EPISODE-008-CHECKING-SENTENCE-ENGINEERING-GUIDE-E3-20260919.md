## Diagnosis

E2 escaped some polished abstraction but overcorrected into clipped marching:
one fact -> one fact -> one fact -> question -> repeated question -> useful exception -> dinner -> stop -> fork.

It also manufactured a cute dinner/fork micro-scene and still assigned almost every sentence one obvious job.

## Engineering tickets

### Ticket A — sentences 1–3

**MERGE.**

Keep:
- the event is being replayed;
- real hurt;
- awareness that the person's own response may also have been poor.

Change:
make these one developing thought rather than three verdicts. The sentence should feel like the reason the person is still mentally there, not an executive summary of three facts.

### Ticket B — sentences 4–6

**REWRITE AS ONE RECURRING QUESTION + POSSIBLE REAL DISCOVERY.**

Remove:
- `A little later:`;
- the staged repetition;
- generic `you notice something you missed before`.

Keep:
sometimes returning really does change the person's understanding.

Required movement:
the person asks one question because they are genuinely unsure; sometimes the return changes something, and sometimes the same question simply comes back.

Do not turn this into a criterion or taxonomy.

### Ticket C — sentences 7–9

**DELETE THE DINNER/FORK SCENE AND REBUILD THE EXIT.**

The dinner + fork image is visibly engineered.

Keep:
- ordinary life/activity/relationship is waiting;
- the thought need not be fully resolved;
- the person can stop chasing certainty and return to life.

Add one missing source function naturally:
the same question returning is not by itself proof that the inner child has brought a new request.

Do not end with a maxim or balanced synthesis.
