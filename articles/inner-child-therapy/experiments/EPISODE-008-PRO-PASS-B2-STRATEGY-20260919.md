## Why B1 changed

The first Pass B payload improved on Pass A by removing the explicit preservation checklist from the writer environment, but it still exposed:
- a long preceding article block containing some polished/balanced prose that could prime the continuation;
- editor-facing framing such as `The reader has reached a difficult place`;
- composition instructions phrased like paragraph-function guidance.

That left two avoidable generator priors active at once: the article's more polished advice register and the supervisor's editorial register.

## B2 writer-facing architecture

The fresh writer now receives only:
1. the strongest locally necessary tail of the accepted article, beginning `If you really did hurt them, repair it.`;
2. the target heading;
3. one compact, scene-like description of writing privately and rereading later;
4. the two hard behavioral boundaries that must be present at generation time:
   - a later fuller-answer promise must be keepable;
   - immediate protection is never delayed for the exercise;
5. invention boundaries;
6. the exact output boundary.

The writer does **not** receive:
- experiment purpose;
- Pangram/detector language;
- model-comparison rationale;
- prior failed candidates;
- the full preservation ledger;
- an anti-pattern taxonomy;
- the supervisor's humanization diagnosis.

## Why the shorter article context is deliberate

The target continuation depends directly on the apology / complaint / guilt pressure in the final two accepted paragraphs. Earlier paragraphs in `Also Look Outward` are valid article content but are not necessary for this local continuation and include a more polished explanatory register. Removing them from the writer packet does not alter article authority; it changes only the generation context.

This is a priming hypothesis, not a proven detector rule: a fresh writer may continue the cadence/register it has just read. B2 therefore supplies the minimum locally necessary accepted prose and avoids using merely detector-Human prose as a style exemplar.

## Supervisor-side gate

After generation, the writer output is audited against:
`experiments/EPISODE-008-PRO-PASS-B-POSTGEN-GATE-20260919.md`

That gate remains supervisor-only.

Order:
1. preservation/function mapping;
2. reverse delta audit;
3. whole-boundary Human-shape cold audit;
4. owner editorial judgment;
5. Pangram only if the candidate is already plausible and the result can change the next action.

Do not repair a globally model-shaped candidate by feeding the full ledger back into the same whole-subsection writer.

If the candidate has a genuinely usable substrate with a localized residual, residual repair may be considered. If the whole passage remains model-shaped, mark B2 failed and switch architecture/model substrate rather than adding another prohibition stack.

## Experiment interpretation

B2 tests a different causal premise from Pass A:
**can a fresh Pro writer produce a better continuation when the generative environment itself is natural and sparse, while semantic completeness is enforced only after prose exists?**

A good result would advance the reusable generation goal. A bad result would falsify this prompt/substrate route more cleanly than another Pass A rewrite.

No Pangram call is authorized merely because B2 exists.
