## Exact internal V28

> ## When the Present-Day Adult Is Dangerous to the Child
>
> Suppose the safety question is skipped and the child is brought forward anyway. The child recoils. If the present-day adult already enjoys fear, humiliation, or domination, the exercise has just handed that adult more of the thing they enjoy. The reaction tells you the child is frightened; it does not tell you the adult has become safe.
>
> Now imagine a different person: an intrusive thought frightens them precisely because they do not want it and have no wish to act on it. They may sound more alarming than the calm adult in the first paragraph, but the safety question is different. One person's mind or history contains something frightening; the other person is actually endorsing harm. That difference matters more than how strange a belief sounds or what label the person uses. Even a claim like “I'm sovereign” is only useful insofar as there is real room to refuse the harmful impulse or authority behind it. If there isn't, that is adult work. The child does not clarify it.
>
> Later, if the adult says the harmful intent is gone, the place to look is still adult life. There will be chances to exploit vulnerability without involving the child; what happens in those moments is the relevant evidence. If the person keeps choosing differently even when exploitation would still be rewarding, the change is being demonstrated where it belongs. For some people, that takes a steady relationship with another person who can stay engaged without helping them manipulate better. The child never had to become the test.
