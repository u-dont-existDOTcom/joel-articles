## P1 thought shape

- this happens often while writing replies;
- if doing well, he may catch himself and think: `wait, that's not the best way to put it`;
- the aim is not suppressing upset;
- the aim is expressing the same feeling/position in a way that can be heard;
- text makes this easier than speaking because there is time for another pass before sending;
- texting can be useful practice for speaking;
- owning wrongdoing is tricky when the "wrong" was mainly low-IQ/low-EQ communication rather than substantive wrongdoing;
- when he knows he actually did something wrong, apologizing can be easier;
- apologizing for excessive directness is harder because directness is associated with honesty.

## P3 thought shape

- returning to the writer is part of the process;
- when emotions pop up, that writer is often the inner child;
- the adult is not stealing the child's pencil because the first version was embarrassing;
- the adult is holding the child's hand and showing him a better way.
