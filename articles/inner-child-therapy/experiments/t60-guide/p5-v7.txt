I don't think the Guide needs certainty. It just chooses the next sane step that fits your values, even though nobody knows how things will turn out, and that's how your little one gets to keep growing toward a whole life. Some problems are blocked for real for a while, like when someone else has to sign off first. Waiting on that isn't laziness or avoidance, so I wouldn't force a workaround that pretends the block isn't there. I'd keep in mind what would get it moving again, and meanwhile put my energy into something else that matters and can move. One stuck thing doesn't have to hold your whole life hostage.
