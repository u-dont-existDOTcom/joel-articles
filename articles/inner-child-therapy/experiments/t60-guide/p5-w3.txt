The Guide doesn't need certainty. If it waited to know how everything turns out, life would stay on hold, and your little one's world would stay small. So it chooses the next sane step that fits your values while the future remains unknown, and that's how it helps your little one grow toward a whole life. Sometimes something really is stuck on someone else's end (you've asked and followed up, and nothing's left on your side for now), and waiting on that isn't avoidance. The Guide can let it wait, keeping an eye out for when it frees up, and keep the rest of your life moving.
