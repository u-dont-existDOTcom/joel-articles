The Guide doesn't need certainty, which is lucky, because some things just can't move yet. It picks the next sane step that fits your values while the future's still unknown, and that's how it helps your little one grow toward a whole life. When a problem really is blocked for now (you're waiting on someone else's decision, say), that's not a lack of motivation or avoidance, and there's no use forcing a workaround that pretends the block isn't there. Keep in mind what would open it back up, and meanwhile put your energy into something else that matters and can move. One stuck problem doesn't have to put your whole life on hold.
