The Guide doesn't need certainty. It chooses the next sane step that fits your values while the future remains unknown, and that's how it helps your little one grow toward a whole life. Waiting to be sure first can keep life on hold, and then your little one's world stays small. Sometimes something really is stuck on someone else's end (you've asked, and followed up, and there's nothing left for you to do yet). That isn't avoidance. The Guide can let it wait, keeping an eye out for when it frees up, and keep the rest of your life moving, so your little one keeps growing anyway.
