The Guide doesn't need certainty. It chooses the next sane step that fits your values, even though the future remains unknown, and that's how it helps your little one grow toward a whole life. Some problems are actually blocked for now (like if you need someone else's signature first), and waiting on that isn't a lack of motivation or avoidance. So it's not about forcing some workaround that just pretends the block isn't there. Keep in mind what it would take to get unblocked, and in the meantime put your energy into something else that matters and can move forward. One stuck problem doesn't need to hold your whole life hostage.
