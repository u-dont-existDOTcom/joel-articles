The Guide doesn't need certainty. It chooses the next sane step that fits your values, even while the future is unknown, so your little one can keep growing toward a whole life. Sometimes a problem is truly blocked for now, like when you're waiting on someone else's decision, and that's not a failure of motivation, or avoidance. There's no point inventing a workaround that pretends the block isn't there. Just remember what would open things back up, and meanwhile put your energy into something else that matters and can move. One stuck problem doesn't have to put your whole life on hold.
