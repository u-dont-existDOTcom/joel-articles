The Guide doesn't need certainty, which is good news, because the future usually stays unknown. It just chooses the next sane step that fits your values, and that's how it helps your little one grow toward a whole life. Some problems really are blocked for a while (you're waiting on somebody else's decision, say). That's not a lack of motivation, and it isn't avoidance either, so don't bother forcing a workaround that pretends the block isn't there. Keep an eye on what would open it back up, and meanwhile put your energy into something else that matters and can move. One stuck thing doesn't have to put your whole life on hold.
