The Guide doesn't need certainty. It chooses the next sane step that fits your values while the future is still unknown (you don't have to see the whole road before you take a step). That's how it helps your little one keep growing toward a whole life. Sometimes a problem really is blocked for now, like when you're waiting on someone else's decision, and that's not a failure of motivation or avoidance. There's no use pretending the block isn't there and forcing a workaround. Keep in mind what would open it back up, and meanwhile, put your energy into something else that matters and can move. One stuck problem doesn't have to put your whole life on hold.
