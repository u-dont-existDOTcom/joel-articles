The Guide doesn't need certainty. If it waited to know how everything turns out, life would stay on hold, and your little one's world would stay small. So it chooses the next sane step that fits your values while the future remains unknown, and that's how it helps your little one grow toward a whole life. Sometimes something really is stuck on someone else's end (you've asked, followed up, and there's nothing left on your side for now). That isn't avoidance. You can let it wait, keep an eye out for when it frees up, and keep the rest of your life moving.
