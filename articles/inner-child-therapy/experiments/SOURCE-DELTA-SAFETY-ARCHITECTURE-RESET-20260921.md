## Retired architecture for this pass

The repeated candidate family effectively moved as:

`unsafe intent -> adult self-inquiry -> identity/sovereignty branch -> religion caveat -> helper/no-contact constraints -> human-care stop`

The identity branch repeatedly behaved like a detachable requirement. Local antecedent and audience fixes did not change that.

## Structurally different architecture selected

The next attempt will organize the section around the **safety gate itself**, not around the preservation-unit order or reader roles:

1. **Gate failure in concrete terms** — enough adult capacity/regulation is not enough; current exploitative intent means the child is not used as a test or gratification source.
2. **What does and does not establish the gate** — current endorsement and repeated choices are distinguished from religious identity, unusual belief, intrusive/charged mental content, past wrongdoing, or a verbal promise. The sovereignty material appears here only as one behavioral-agency check: can the person pause, question, refuse, or revise rather than claiming they must obey.
3. **What work remains when the gate is closed** — stay adult-focused: wanted help/change, suffering if any, identity benefits/costs/beneficiaries, expected life/happiness.
4. **Care without child access** — listening/care can continue without flattering cruelty, theology combat, or exploitation coaching; all direct/indirect child-contact routes remain closed.
5. **Uneven stop** — sustained real-human care may be more useful than another internal/bot exercise.

This is not a sentence plan. The protected functions remain outside generation as constraints; the listed movements are mesoscale live-question topology only.

## Why this is materially different

The failed family asked, in effect, `what should this reader/person do next?`.

The replacement asks:
- `what is the safety gate actually detecting?`
- `what evidence is and is not relevant to that gate?`
- `once child work is excluded, what remains available?`

That gives the identity/religion/sovereignty material a causal home inside the evidence distinction rather than requiring a special ideological detour.
