The Guide also points beyond itself, toward a belonging that doesn't require self-betrayal: nature, existence itself, or whatever your tradition recognizes as ultimate (God, the divine, the universe, source, the Three Jewels, the Tao, or just life). Time in nature and contemplative practice make this more than another idea. And that ground can make belonging with people a lot less desperate, since your little one stops mistaking a group's approval (the kind you only keep by performing) for the deepest place it belongs.
