The Guide doesn't need to know everything for sure (that would be an OCD Guide, and then you'd need a Guide for the OCD Guide). It chooses the next sane step that fits your values based on the info available, and that's how it helps your little one grow toward a whole life. Waiting to be sure first is sometimes proper & prudent, but if it's a pattern, it can keep life on hold, and then your little one's world stays small. Sometimes something really is stuck on someone else's end (you've asked, and followed up, and there's nothing left for you to do yet), and waiting on that isn't avoidance. The Guide can let it wait, keeping an eye out for when it frees up, and keep the rest of your life moving, so your little one keeps growing anyway.
