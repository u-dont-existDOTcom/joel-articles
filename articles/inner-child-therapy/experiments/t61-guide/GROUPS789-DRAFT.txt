The Guide also points beyond itself, toward a belonging that doesn't require betraying yourself. That might be nature, or existence itself, or whatever your tradition recognizes as ultimate (God, the divine, the universe, source, the Three Jewels, the Tao, life). Time outside and contemplative practice make it more than another nice idea. With that ground under you, belonging with people gets a lot less desperate, because your little one isn't mistaking a group's approval (the kind you only keep by performing) for the deepest place it belongs.

You can call this part of you whatever fits: Leader, Guide, Wise One, Sage, Inner Teacher or Director are all fine. "Guru" carries real spiritual weight for some readers, and for cult survivors it can be a trigger word, which is understandable. What matters more than the name is that it keeps pointing you past itself.
