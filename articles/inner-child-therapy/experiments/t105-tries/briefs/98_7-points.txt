- A session can go really well: you feel real care for your little one and say what it needed to hear.
- A few days later you're skipping lunch and saying yes to everything again.
- A lot of people's first guess is that they didn't go deep enough. Joel doesn't think that's it, and he doesn't think the session failed either.
- Try a tiny bit of what you did for your little one out in your day, like texting a friend that you're having a rough day instead of saying you're fine.
- That can be harder than the meditation was.
- One small thing like that can teach your little one more than ten more good meditations.
