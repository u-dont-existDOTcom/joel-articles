Say you have a really good session. You're kind to your little one and say what it needed to hear. Then a few days later you're skipping lunch and saying yes to everything again. Most people's first guess is that they didn't go deep enough. I doubt that, and I don't think the session failed. Your little one learns more from one small thing you do during the week than from ten more good meditations, and that part can be harder than the meditation was. So take a tiny bit of what you did in there into your day. Text a friend that you're having a rough day instead of saying you're fine.
