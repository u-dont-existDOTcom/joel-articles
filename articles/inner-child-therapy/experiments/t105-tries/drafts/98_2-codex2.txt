After the day, look at what actually happened. Say you told someone what you'd planned to say and felt worse afterward. Standing up for yourself might do that at first. See what you can learn from how it went, even if you don't feel better right away. 🤗 Not doing it is harder to figure out. Fear is the first guess, though maybe you didn't know how or why. You could have been worn out, or been counting on someone who said they'd help and then couldn't make it.
