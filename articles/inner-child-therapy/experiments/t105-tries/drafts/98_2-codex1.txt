After the day, look at what actually happened. If you did the Protector actions and don't feel better right away, you can learn from that. You don't heal at a steady pace, and standing up for yourself might make you feel worse at first. Not doing it is harder to figure out. We've all had days like that. 🤗 Fear is the first guess, but maybe you didn't know how or why. You might have been too worn out to do it, or whoever said they'd help couldn't make it.
