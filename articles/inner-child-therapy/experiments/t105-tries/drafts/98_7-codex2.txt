Say you've skipped lunch again and you're saying yes to everything, a few days after a session that went really well. Try doing a tiny bit of what you did for your little one as you go about your day. One small thing can teach them more than ten more good meditations, so going deeper next time isn't the fix. If you're having a rough day, text a friend and tell them instead of saying you're fine. Sending that text can be harder than the meditation was.
