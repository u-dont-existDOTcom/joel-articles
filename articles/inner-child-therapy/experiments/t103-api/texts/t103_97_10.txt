But if you’re still thinking of staking your life on the opposite claim, that death is just nothing, look into that claim first. Researchers at the University of Virginia have been collecting cases of small children who talk about a past life for about sixty years, and there’s a big pile of near-death accounts too. I don’t think any of it can tell you what dying will be like for you, but I’d read a few of those cases before betting my life on “nothing.”
