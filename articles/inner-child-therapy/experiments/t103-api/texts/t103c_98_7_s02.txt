Sometimes the session itself goes really well. You feel real care for your little one and say exactly what they needed to hear, and a few days later you’re skipping lunch and saying yes to everything again. A lot of people figure it didn’t work, or they didn’t go deep enough. I think it just hasn’t carried over into the rest of your week yet. So try a tiny bit of the same care in your day, like texting a friend that you’re having a rough day instead of saying you’re fine. One small thing like that can teach your little one more than ten more good meditations.
