Sometimes a session goes great, and a few days later you're skipping lunch and saying yes to everything again. Your first guess might be that you didn't go deep enough. I'd guess the session was fine, and you just haven't brought any of it out with you yet. One small real thing can teach your little one more than ten more good meditations. So whatever you did for it in there, I'd try a tiny bit of it out here, like texting a friend that you're having a rough day instead of saying you're fine.
