Some people have the opposite problem: it feels like they're connecting, and it's all happening near the surface. A long meditation followed by sleep (a second sleep works best, after you've already slept once) can open up much more direct stuff. So if that night's dream hits you with way more feeling and need than your daytime session got anywhere near, take the gap as a sign the session stayed shallow. A sign, not a verdict. Then ask what's available now that wasn't, and how you see it. If nightmares start wrecking your sleep, change the dose before you give up on the whole thing (earlier in the day, or shorter).
