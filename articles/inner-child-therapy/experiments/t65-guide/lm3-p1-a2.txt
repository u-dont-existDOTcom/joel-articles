Rumi says you have to keep breaking your heart until it stays open. Some people find that deep grieving turns into warmth, or connection, or a feeling in the body of being held by the whole universe. The feeling is real, though it doesn't prove what the body is doing to make it. It can go the other way, too: being cared for can bring up sadness about the care you didn't get, and that doesn't mean the love failed. Stay with it while you can still be present and choose, and slow down when it starts to swamp you.
