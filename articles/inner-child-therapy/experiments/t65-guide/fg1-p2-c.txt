What can help is seeing how their history and pain, and what they were capable of at the time, shaped the harm they did. Given everything they'd become by then, it was the only move they had. Hurt people often pass on pain they never processed. When you see that for yourself (not because someone told you that you should), forgiveness can show up on its own, and the rage that made them the one uniquely guilty person can soften into something more accurate.
