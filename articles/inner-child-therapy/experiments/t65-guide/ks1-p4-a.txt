Buddhism doesn't teach that death is an off switch, or a free get-out-of-jail card back to the source. It teaches that repeated thought and intention shape the mind, that this keeps going through death and rebirth, that hell realms are possible, and that a human birth is a rare chance to understand suffering and end its causes. You don't have to believe that. But if you're about to bet your life on the opposite, look into it first. Near-death experiences and seriously studied rebirth reports can't give you reliable odds about death, but they can shake the certainty that destroying the body guarantees nothing comes after.
