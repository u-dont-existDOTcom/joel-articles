That future you also knows why you can't pull this off perfectly today. What would they do for you, if they were here? Maybe they'd be kind about it, or maybe they'd just make you some food. Some days you won't be able to get into their head at all. Then do whatever small honest thing you can (a "no," a pause), no faking the feeling required. You can picture a wiser future version of the other person too, but don't count on meeting them. They may never agree with you or be sorry, and what they do now is what you go by.
