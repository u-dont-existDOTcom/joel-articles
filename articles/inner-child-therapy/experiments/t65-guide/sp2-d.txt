When you're stuck, remember a time you met a hard thing in a way you respect. What did you do, and what helped, whether it was a person or just enough sleep? Is one part of that around now? A small, real example is enough, and it doesn't have to beat this one. Just don't let it turn into "I did it then, so I should be fine now," because your limits today are real. (If nothing comes, there are other doorways.)
