Rumi says you have to keep breaking your heart until it stays open. Sounds intense, and sometimes it is. For some people, deep grieving turns into warmth, or into this full-body sense of being held by the universe. That's real as an experience, though it doesn't explain what the body is doing. And it can go the other way: somebody's kindness can make you cry about the kindness you never got, which doesn't mean the love failed. Go as far as you can while you're still present and choosing, and slow down when it starts to swamp you.
