Telling it like a story doesn't make it made up, and it doesn't prove you remember it right. It doesn't let anybody off, either: the trial stops, and whatever repair is owed is still owed, whoever did the harm. Listen to the narrator, though. If it starts sounding like "And so that's the way it happened, you idiot," stop. Same if the shame goes up or you start to feel unreal, and telling it colder or from farther away won't fix that. Then ask what the younger you in that story needs from you now.
