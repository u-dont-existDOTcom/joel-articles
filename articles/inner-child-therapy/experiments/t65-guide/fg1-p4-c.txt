Forgiving yourself works the same way, and if that one's stuck, forgiving other people tends to stick too. Own what you did and its impact (only yours, not what was someone else's). Let the remorse land, repair what you can, and then you're allowed to stop punishing yourself, with the responsibility still yours. Some harms take years. Living in resentment for good is its own trap, so come back to the question of how they got capable of it now and then, and let the understanding change how you feel when it's ready to.
