When picturing your little one feels fake, try a real photo instead. There's no face or posture to invent; you're just looking. Go gently, and don't decide ahead of time what the picture is supposed to prove. Notice what your body does and what feelings or urges show up, and what that younger you seems to want you to get. Then sort it. Some of it you'll remember yourself, and some of it someone told you later ("You hated that coat"). Some you're guessing from the picture, and some might be an image or a dream, or you honestly don't know. Then ask what your little one needs from you now, and do a bit of it.
