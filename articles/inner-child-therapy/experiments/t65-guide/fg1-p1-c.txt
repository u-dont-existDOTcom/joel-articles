Most people get to some kind of forgiveness eventually, or at least out from under the resentment. But it doesn't come on a schedule ("Okay, it's been a year, time to forgive Dad"), and you don't need it first to be safe, set boundaries, hold someone accountable or get better. Resentment does narrow what you can see, and it keeps your little one organized around the injury. Forcing it early just gets you an enlightened-looking performance while the wound goes underground.
