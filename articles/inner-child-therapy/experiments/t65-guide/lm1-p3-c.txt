Getting the meanness out of the way leaves more room for warmth. And the thing people mention most is putting their guard down, by choice, once the guarding part has had its say. If love has never felt safe, the ego can act like a bouncer who won't let the feeling through the door, and vulnerability is what lets it in. So the warmth might not be missing. It might be stuck behind a cold part that's protecting something, and that part tends to step aside once it's been heard (see Hear the Protective Part First).
