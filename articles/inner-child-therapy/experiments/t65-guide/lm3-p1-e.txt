There's a Rumi line about breaking your heart until it stays open. For some people that's close to literal: they grieve hard, and what's underneath turns out to be warmth, or a bodily sense of being held by the universe. The feeling is real, even though it can't tell you what's happening physically. It can go the other way too, when someone's kindness brings up grief for the kindness you never got. That's not the love failing. Go only as far as you can while you're still present and choosing, and slow down when it starts to drown you.
