First things first. If you're close to acting, get another human being involved now (a crisis line or emergency number counts), and get some distance from the means. Do this if you can't stop yourself, if you're badly disoriented or intoxicated, or if the means are in front of you, and do it if you can't tell whether you're about to act. It isn't my answer to whether life is worth living. It just buys the question enough time to be looked at.
