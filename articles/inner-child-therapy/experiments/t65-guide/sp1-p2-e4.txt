That future you also knows why you can't pull this off perfectly today. What would they do for you, if they were here? Maybe they'd be kind about it, or maybe they'd just make you some food. On the days you can't reach them, a small honest move still counts, like saying no or taking a pause, and you don't have to pretend you feel fine. You can imagine the other person's wiser future self, too. Just don't assume that person agrees with you or is sorry, and don't let them stand in for how this person acts now.
