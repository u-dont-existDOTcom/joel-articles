This one's for a day when you feel steady enough, because it widens the circle, and you want to stay in your own shoes while it does. Name what you're feeling, in plain words. Maybe it's loneliness. Maybe it's craving something you know is bad for you. Then remind yourself: "Other people feel this too. Just like me, they want relief. Just like me, they sometimes make it worse trying to get away from it. May we all get enough support to stay with it, so the pain stops with us."
