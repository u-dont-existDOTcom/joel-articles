You can see that momentum within this life, too. Rumination feeds rumination, and isolation can deepen despair. The more you hate yourself, the better your mind gets at it. Physical life keeps handing you redirections, though, like other people and things you have to do, and help when you reach for it. If the many traditions and the accumulating scientific evidence are right that the mind's causal process goes on after the body dies, killing the body may take away some of those redirections without taking away the suffering.
