Rumi says you have to keep breaking your heart until it stays open. For some people, deep grieving does turn into warmth, or into this full-body sense of being held by the universe. That's a real experience, though it doesn't tell you what the body is doing to produce it. And it can run the other way: being cared for can make you sad about the care you never got, and that's not the love failing. Stay with it as long as you're still present and can choose, and slow down when it starts swamping you.
