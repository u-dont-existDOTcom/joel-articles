This one's for when talking or picturing gets you only silence. Write with your wrong hand, the clumsy one, whose letters already look like a first grader's (or a ransom note). Put down whatever scared or hurt feelings are really there, without inventing any. Then answer with your good hand, as the grown-up who's got their back (kind, protective, maybe with a plan). And if the protective part grabs the pen first, fine. It gets the wrong hand until it's had its say.
