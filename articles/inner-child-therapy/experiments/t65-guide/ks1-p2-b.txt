Before getting philosophical, make sure there's an adult in the room. If the part that wants to die feels like all of you, use the Witness or the borrowed-adult move from earlier, and invite in any part that can watch what's happening. A part that can protect your body, or just put off a decision that can't be undone, counts too. If you can already watch the suicidal state without being swallowed by it, you can skip this step.
