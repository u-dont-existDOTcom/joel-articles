If talking to your little one or picturing them just gets you silence, try paper. Let your wrong hand write down whatever scared or hurt stuff is really in there (its handwriting already looks like a six-year-old's, or a ransom note, which is about right). Then your good hand writes back as the grown-up who's on their side. Sometimes the protective part grabs the pen first. Let it. It gets the wrong hand until it's had its say.
