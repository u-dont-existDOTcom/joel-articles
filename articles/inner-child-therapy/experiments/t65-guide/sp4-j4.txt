This one's for when talking or picturing gets you only silence. Write with your wrong hand, the clumsy one, whose letters already look like a first grader's (or a ransom note). Put down whatever scared or hurt feelings are really there, without inventing any. Then answer with your good hand, the grown-up one. If a protective part shows up and grabs the pen first, let it keep the wrong hand until it's said what it came to say.
