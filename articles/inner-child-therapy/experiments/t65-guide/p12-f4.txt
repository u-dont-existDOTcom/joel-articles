Love bombing doesn't show in how much affection there is. It shows in what that affection is doing to your head (are the red flags from last week starting to look kind of cute?). Praise and special attention (from one person, or from a whole group) can make the evidence vanish, and they can turn someone into the exception to rules you'd use with anybody else. Arguing with the feeling won't get you anywhere, so slow down instead, and check what's true before you decide anything.
