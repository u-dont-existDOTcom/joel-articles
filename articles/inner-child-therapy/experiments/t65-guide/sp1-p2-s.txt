That future you also knows why you can't pull this off perfectly today. What would they do for you, if they were here? Maybe they'd be kind about it, or maybe they'd just make you some food. If you can't get into their head at all, do the smallest honest thing you can anyway (a no, a pause) and don't fake the feeling. And you can picture the other person's wiser future self, as long as you remember it's a picture: it won't change what they do now, or promise they'll agree with you or be sorry.
