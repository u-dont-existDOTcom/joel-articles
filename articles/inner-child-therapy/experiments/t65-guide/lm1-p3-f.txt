With the meanness out of the way, warmth has room to come in. And what people mention most is letting their guard down, on purpose, once the guarding part has had its say. If love has never felt safe, the ego can stand at the door like a bouncer who's been told "no feelings tonight," and vulnerability is the gap that lets one through. So the warmth may not be gone. It may be parked behind a cold part that's protecting something, and that part tends to step aside once it's heard.
