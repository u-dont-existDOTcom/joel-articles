Some people have the opposite problem. It feels like they're connecting with their little one, and the contact is staying near the surface. Meditating for a few hours and then sleeping, especially a second sleep after you've already slept once, can sometimes open up much more direct stuff. If that night's dream has way more feeling and need in it than the daytime session ever reached, take the gap as a sign the session stayed shallow. A sign, not a verdict.
