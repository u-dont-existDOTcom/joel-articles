Sessions need a clear ending, or they kind of leak into the rest of the day. So look up and name where you are. Notice your feet, or the chair holding you, and let your jaw and hands go soft. Maybe the next thing is a walk, or maybe it's bed. Either way, say "That's enough for today. We're going back to the life we're building." and go do it, with whatever you're still feeling tagging along. That part counts as healing, too.
