Give every session an ending you can feel. Say out loud where you are, if nobody's around to hear it. Notice your feet, or the chair holding you up, and let your jaw and hands unclench. Then decide what's next (a walk, or just sleep) and tell your little one, "That's enough for today. We're going back to the life we're building." Whatever's still stirred up can come with you. Going back to your life is part of this work.
