And if losing everything is already on the table, what supposedly impossible change is still too frightening to try? Maybe it's a relationship, a job or a home. Maybe it's an identity, an obligation, a belief or a role you've been carrying. Those changes aren't free, and some can't be taken back. They're still smaller than dying, and you can make them once you're calmer, with help. Look into those questions, and into the life you've been afraid to change, while you still have a human body and people around to help redirect the path.
