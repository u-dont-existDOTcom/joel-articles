Every session needs an ending, a short one is fine, or the processing just keeps going in the background while you're trying to live your life. So when you're done, look around the room and say where you are, out loud if you want. Feel your feet, or your butt in the chair (hehe). Let your jaw and hands relax. Then tell your little one something like "Okay, that's enough for today. Back to the life we're building," and go do the next thing, whether that's dinner or a nap. You don't have to finish feeling everything first. Getting back to your life is part of the healing.
