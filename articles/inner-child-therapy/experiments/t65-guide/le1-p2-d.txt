So what has really ended? And what are you still trying to keep alive? What don't you know yet, and what is your little one afraid is going to happen in the gap? A small ritual can help mark the ending without pretending you know what comes next, like boxing up their stuff or one last walk around the old neighborhood. Don't grab a new identity just to make the not-knowing stop. You can keep your routines, take care of your body, grieve, and stay with your little one while the next life takes shape.
