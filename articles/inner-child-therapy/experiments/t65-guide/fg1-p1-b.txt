For a lot of people, healing includes some kind of forgiveness eventually, or at least getting out from under the resentment. It doesn't run on a schedule, though, and you don't need it before you can be safe, set boundaries, hold someone accountable or recover. Resentment can shrink what you're able to see and keep your little one organized around the injury. Then again, pushing yourself to forgive early tends to become an enlightened performance, with the wound gone underground.
