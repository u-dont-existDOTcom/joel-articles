# Simple Practices

## Borrow Hindsight

A question to borrow from your future self: "How would I respond to this once I understood it more clearly, in balance and with compassion?" It can change how the whole thing looks. It can also just make you feel a bit more like that person, or less alone (sometimes all at once). Keep in mind it's only their best guess. No perfect Higher Self is handing down answers.

That future you also knows why you can't pull this off perfectly today. What would they do for you, if they were here? Maybe they'd be kind about it, or maybe they'd just make you some food. You can borrow a wiser future version of the other person, too, as long as you don't assume they'll end up on your side, or sorry.
