Forgiving yourself works the same way. You own what you did. Only the part that was yours, though. You let yourself feel the remorse, and you repair what you can, by apologizing or making amends. Changing how you act from now on counts too. Then you're allowed to stop holding yourself hostage. The responsibility stays. When forgiving yourself is hard, forgiving other people tends to be harder too. Some harms take years. You might feel like you should have forgiven them by now. Don't push it. Forgiving too soon is one trap, and living in resentment permanently is the other. Over time, keep coming back to how they became capable of what they did, until the understanding is real enough to change how you feel.
