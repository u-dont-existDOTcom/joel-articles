Actively resenting whoever hurt you can narrow what you see and keep your little one's world built around the injury. For many people, healing eventually includes some forgiveness, or at least getting out from under that resentment. That might make you want to hurry it along. You don't need forgiveness first to be safe or set boundaries, though, and the same goes for holding them accountable and getting justice. Recovering can come first, too. Forgiveness can't be put on a schedule, and if you're pressured into it early, you get an enlightened-looking performance while the wound gets pushed underground.
