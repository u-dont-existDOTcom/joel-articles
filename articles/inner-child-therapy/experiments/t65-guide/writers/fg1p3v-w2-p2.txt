"The only move they had" can sound like letting them off, if you're the one they hurt. Seeing how they got there doesn't make what they did harmless, though, or mean no other option existed in theory. What it means is that no punishment can go back and give them the awareness they didn't have enough of. Or the support, or the ability. What's worth doing is repairing what can be repaired, and making some other response more available now. The harm is real, and accountability stays. A boundary can still be right, and so can legal consequences. So can judging what they did as wrong, and distance, up to no contact for good. You can understand them and hold them responsible at the same time. You can forgive them and never speak to them again. "I understand how you became this way" can sit beside "I now understand why I shouldn't rely on you for this."
