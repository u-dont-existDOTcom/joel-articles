Maybe part of you keeps asking, "Shouldn't I have forgiven them by now?" For many people, healing does get around to some forgiveness, or at least to getting out from under the resentment. It just doesn't keep a schedule. You don't need it first to be safe or to set boundaries, and justice doesn't have to wait for it, or making them answer for it, or getting better. Resentment narrows what you see and keeps your little one organized around the injury. But if you're pushed into forgiving early, you get an enlightened-looking performance while the wound goes underground.
