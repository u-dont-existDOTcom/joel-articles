Noticing counts as progress, even if it's only now, looking back, that you think "oh, that was a hook." If you interrupted the pattern somewhere, notice that too. What's the next small repair, or something protective you can do? And what moment of warmth or pleasure did you actually get? Or it might have been a moment of courage, or of connection. Repair what can be repaired and renew the intention, and then go to sleep.
