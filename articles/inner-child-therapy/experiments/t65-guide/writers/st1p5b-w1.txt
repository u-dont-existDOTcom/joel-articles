This can be the healthiest kind of borrowed adulthood. The peer doesn't become your parent for good. They might feel a bit like one while it's your turn to talk. They show you something a grown-up can do and help you join in. After a while they hand it back to you. Therapy is still useful for what therapy does, and a serious healing path can include both kinds of help. Community and disciplined peer support like this do something different.
