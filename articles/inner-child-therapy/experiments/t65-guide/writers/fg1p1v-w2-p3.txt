Pressure to forgive early leaves you with a performance that looks enlightened, while the wound goes underground. Still, for many people, healing eventually includes some forgiveness, or at least getting out from under active resentment. Forgiveness can't be scheduled, and it doesn't have to come before you recover. You can be safe and set boundaries without it, and the same goes for holding whoever hurt you accountable or getting justice. But active resentment can narrow what you see and keep your little one organized around the injury. When you're in it, it can feel like finally seeing that person clearly.
