Love moves in a loop. It goes from spiritual experience to your little one, on into the grown-up relationship, from you into other people, and back again. Maybe you're not sure which door you came in through. The loop matters more than the door. Community can work in both directions. Sometimes it's the people who come first: they love you, you feel safe and like you belong, and something deeper gets room to open. Other times the opening comes first, and afterward different people and relationships start turning up (you "manifest" them, if you like that word). Then community helps make the opening practical and real, something your body knows and not just your head.
