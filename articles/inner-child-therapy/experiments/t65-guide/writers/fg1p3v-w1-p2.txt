The harm is real, though, and they're still accountable for it. Boundaries and distance can still be right, up to no contact for good. So can legal consequences, and judging them for it. You can understand them and hold them responsible, and you can forgive someone you'll never speak to again. "I understand how you became this way" can sit beside "I now understand why I shouldn't rely on you for this." Both of those are a kind of understanding. Seeing how they got there doesn't make what they did harmless, or mean nothing else was possible in theory. But punishment can't go back and create the awareness or support they didn't have enough of, and it can't hand them the ability. What's worth doing is repairing what can be repaired, and making a different response easier to reach for now.
