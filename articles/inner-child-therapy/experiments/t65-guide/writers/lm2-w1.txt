Some people hear all this about love and answer, "No. I hate myself." If that's what you find, don't argue with it. It deserves an honest hearing. You might look inside and think the hate is all there is. But if you opened this article, something in you wanted things to be different. The same goes if you came to therapy or asked someone for help. Or if you'd decided you were hopeless and then tried again anyway.

What was that something in you? Maybe hope, or desperation. It could have been curiosity or fear. Maybe it was duty. Or it was more like, "I'm not going to keep suffering like this." It might even have been love. You don't need to decide which. Ask that part what it hoped might happen when it brought you here, and follow that thread.
