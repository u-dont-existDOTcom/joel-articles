Missing someone doesn't have to become one more problem to solve. Sometimes the ache itself can be a cue for love, so don't push the missing away. If there's love in it, let "I miss you" become "I love you." Or offer a simple wish, "May you be safe. May you be held in love," or however you'd put it. Maybe you picture them, or maybe you just feel who the care is for. This is about what happens in you, so you don't have to believe they receive anything. It doesn't replace grieving or contact. You still keep whatever boundaries you need, and the practical stuff still has to get done. If sending love makes you more compulsive or unreal, make it simpler: "I miss you, and I care." Same if you can't get back to your life. You can also offer some of that same care to the part of you that's still here.
