The words you use in this work direct your attention. When a painful or negative way of putting things isn't itself what needs naming, pick words that point toward what you want to strengthen, like an ability or a relationship. It can be something you do, too. "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" usually gives the mind a clearer direction than bringing in a label like "dependency" just to deny it or argue with it. Telling yourself you're not dependent still has you thinking about dependency. Especially with your little one and in hypnosis, make the direction easy to imagine. What's actually needed? Warmth or protection, maybe. Or steadiness, or rest.
