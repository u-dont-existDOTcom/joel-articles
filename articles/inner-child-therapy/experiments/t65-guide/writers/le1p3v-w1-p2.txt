Maybe you keep telling someone "I miss you" in your head. If love is there, let it become "I love you," or offer a simple wish: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." Picture them, or just feel yourself sending the care their way. Don't push the missing away, though. It doesn't have to turn into another problem to solve. Sometimes the ache itself can become a cue for love. This is about what happens in you, so you don't have to believe they receive anything. It doesn't replace grieving or being in touch with them. You'd still keep any boundaries you need and do the practical things. If sending love makes you more compulsive or unreal, make it simpler: "I miss you, and I care." Do that, too, if you can't get back to your life. You can also offer the part of you that's still here some of the same care.
