In this work, you're steering attention with your words. Unless a painful or negative way of putting it is what needs naming, steer toward what you want to strengthen: an ability or a relationship. Or something you do. "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" usually gives the mind a clearer direction than bringing in a label like "dependency" just to deny it or argue with it. Then you're still talking about dependency. In inner-child work and hypnosis especially, make the direction easy to imagine: warmth, protection, steadiness, rest, or whatever is actually needed.
