Reparenting usually gets talked about as something you do alone, or with a therapist. But your little one got hurt in relationships, and that kind of hurt only heals so far on your own. Sometimes one person ends up carrying a lot of the borrowed grown-up jobs for you: the food, the planning, health decisions, rides, your social life, reassurance, protection, company. Or just the feeling that somebody competent is next to you. If you've got someone like that while you're struggling, that's a huge blessing.

Over time, reparenting can help you take in some of what they've been showing you, and practice it yourself. Some of the jobs can go to friends or community, or to professionals and services, and some to routines and plain old tools (a calendar never gets tired of you). So which ones could become more yours? Which are better shared around? And which really belong in that relationship? The idea is for your little one to get reliable care through more than one door, while the relationship stays as precious and important as it is.
