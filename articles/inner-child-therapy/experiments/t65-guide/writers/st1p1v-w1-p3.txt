In this work, you end up paying attention to whatever your words point at. So unless the painful or negative way of putting things is itself what needs naming, you're better off aiming them at what you want to strengthen. What would that be for you? Maybe an ability or a relationship. Or something you'd like to do more. With your little one and in hypnosis especially, make it easy to imagine: warmth, protection, steadiness, rest, or whatever is actually needed. Saying "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" usually gives your mind a clearer direction than bringing in a label like "dependency" just to deny it or argue with it.
