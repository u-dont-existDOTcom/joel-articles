Community can work both ways. Sometimes loving people help you feel safe enough, and like you belong, so something deeper can open up. Other times the opening comes first and changes what kinds of people and relationships you can "manifest." Then community helps you make it practical and real, and live it in your body. You might already know which was yours. Either way, love moves in a loop, and the loop matters more than which door you came in through. It goes from spiritual experience to your little one, then into the grown-up relationship. From you it goes into other people, and back again.
