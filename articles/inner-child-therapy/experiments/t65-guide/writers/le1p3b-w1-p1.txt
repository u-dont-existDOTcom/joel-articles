Missing someone doesn't have to be one more problem to solve. Don't push the missing away. Sometimes the ache itself can become a cue for love. If love is there, let "I miss you" turn into "I love you," or offer a simple wish: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." Picture them, or just feel your care toward them. Do they get any of it? This is about what happens in you, so you don't have to believe they do. It doesn't replace grieving or contact, and it doesn't replace boundaries or practical steps either. If sending love makes you more compulsive or unreal, make it simpler: "I miss you, and I care." Same if you can't get back to your life. You can also offer some of that care to the part of you that's still here.
