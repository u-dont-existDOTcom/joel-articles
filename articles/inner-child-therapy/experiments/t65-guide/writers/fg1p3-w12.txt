They still have to answer for what they did, and it still hurt. Seeing how they got there doesn't make it okay, and in theory they could have done something else. It's just that punishing them now can't reach back and give them what they were missing then, the sense to see it or the help. Or the skills. What's left is fixing what can be fixed, and making it easier for something different to happen now. None of that means you have to let them back in. Boundaries can still be right, and the law, and a plain "that was wrong." So can distance, all the way to never speaking to them again, and you could forgive them anyway. "I understand how you became this way" can sit right next to "I now understand why I shouldn't rely on you for this."
