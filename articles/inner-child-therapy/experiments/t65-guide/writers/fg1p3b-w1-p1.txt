None of that makes what they did harmless, and they're still accountable. You might be thinking they could have done something else, and in theory, they could have. But punishing them can't go back and give them the awareness, support or ability they didn't have enough of, so the useful work is repairing what can be repaired and making it easier to respond differently now. Boundaries and legal consequences can still be right. So can judging what they did as wrong, and keeping your distance, all the way to no contact for good. You can understand them and hold them responsible, and forgiving them doesn't mean ever speaking to them again. In your head, that might sound like "I understand how you became this way," right next to "I now understand why I shouldn't rely on you for this."
