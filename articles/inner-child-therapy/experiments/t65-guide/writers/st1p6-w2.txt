Community can work in both directions. Sometimes loving people help you feel safe and like you belong, enough that a deeper opening becomes possible. Your little one might be checking whether they mean it. Other times the opening comes first and changes what kinds of people and relationships you can "manifest." Then community helps make the opening practical and real, something your body knows. The door you came in through matters less than the loop. Love moves from spiritual experience to your little one, then into the grown-up relationship. From you it goes into other people, and back again.
