You might tell yourself, "But I'm not dependent on anyone." The words you use in this work direct your attention, though. Unless the painful or negative thing itself needs naming, prefer words that point toward the ability or relationship you want to strengthen. Or toward something you want to do more. Saying "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" usually gives your mind a clearer direction than bringing in a label like "dependency" just to deny it or argue with it. In inner-child work and hypnosis especially, make that direction easy to imagine: warmth, protection, steadiness, rest, or whatever's really needed.
