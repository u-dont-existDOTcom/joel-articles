Over time, reparenting can help you take in and practice some of what that person has shown you. Maybe you've already caught yourself saying "it'll be okay" the way they do. Other jobs can be shared with friends and community. Professionals or services can help, and so can routines and simple tools. The aim is for your little one to feel reliably cared for from more than one place, while the relationship itself stays precious and important. Which of those jobs can become more yours, and which are healthiest shared? And which really belong inside that relationship?
