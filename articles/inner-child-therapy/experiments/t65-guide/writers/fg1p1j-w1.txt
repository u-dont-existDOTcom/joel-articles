For many people, healing eventually includes some forgiveness. Or at least getting out from under active resentment toward whoever hurt you. But you can't put it on a schedule, and you don't need it first to recover. You can be safe and set boundaries before you've forgiven them, and the same goes for holding someone accountable or getting justice. Active resentment can narrow what you see, though, and keep the injury at the center of everything for your little one. Maybe it feels like you're supposed to be over this by now. If you forgive them early under that kind of pressure, it's an enlightened-looking performance, while the wound goes underground.
