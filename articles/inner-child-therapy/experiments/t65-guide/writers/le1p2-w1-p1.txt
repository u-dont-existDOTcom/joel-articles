Ask yourself what has really ended, and what you're still trying to keep going. What isn't known yet? And what is your little one afraid will happen in the gap? It might not have the words yet. What practical act or small ritual would mark the ending without pretending you know the next beginning? Don't force a new you just because not knowing feels unbearable. The grown-up you can keep your routines and protect your body. Grieving what ended is part of it, and so is staying with your little one while the next life takes shape.
