There's no schedule for forgiveness, and you can recover without waiting for it. Pressured to forgive early, you get an enlightened-looking performance while the wound goes underground. You don't need forgiveness first to be safe or set boundaries, and you can hold whoever hurt you accountable and get justice without it. Still, for many people, healing eventually includes some forgiveness, or at least getting out from under active resentment. I mean the kind where you keep rehearsing what you'd tell them. Resenting them that actively can narrow what you see and keep your little one's world arranged around the injury.
