Seeing all that doesn't make what they did harmless, or mean that in theory nothing else was possible. Since it was the only move they had available, though, punishment can't go back and give them the awareness or support they were short on. Or the ability. The useful work is repairing what can be repaired, and making a different move more available now. They still have to answer for it, morally and sometimes legally, and the harm is real. Boundaries and distance can still be right, for good if it comes to that. You can understand them and still hold them responsible. You can also forgive someone you'll never speak to again. In your head, "I understand how you became this way" can sit right next to "I now understand why I shouldn't rely on you for this."
