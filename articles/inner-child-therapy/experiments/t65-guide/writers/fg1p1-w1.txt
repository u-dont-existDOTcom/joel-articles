For many people, healing eventually includes some forgiveness, or at least getting out from under active resentment. You can't schedule it. And you don't need it first to be safe (please don't wait) or set boundaries. Holding someone accountable and getting justice can come first, and recovering can too. While you're actively resenting them, your view can narrow. Your little one can stay built around the injury, too. Maybe you feel you should be over it by now. But forgiving early under pressure gets you an enlightened-looking performance while the wound goes underground.
