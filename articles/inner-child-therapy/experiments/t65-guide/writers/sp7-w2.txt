Breathe however you normally do (nothing special). After yourself, include anyone else who comes to mind. If that's someone who hurt you, you might wonder whether you have to forgive them, or imagine their childhood. You don't, and the wish asks for no contact with anyone. Whether you ever contact them is a separate decision, and never one to make while you're flooded. The wish is there so you stop carrying a feeling this many people share as proof that you alone are defective.
