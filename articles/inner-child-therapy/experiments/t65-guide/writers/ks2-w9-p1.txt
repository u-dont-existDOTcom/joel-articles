Is there a grown-up in the room? That's worth checking before anything philosophical. When the part that wants to die feels like all of you, it's hard to tell, so notice who's doing the noticing (Noticing Counts), or borrow an adult. A part that only wants to keep your body safe until morning counts. So does one that would rather put off a decision you can't undo. If no part of you can watch yet, skip the questions for now, and let the person you called be the adult until one can. And if you can already watch the suicidal state without being swallowed by it, go on.
