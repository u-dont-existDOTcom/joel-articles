Maybe you've been told you can't heal until you forgive. You can't schedule forgiveness, though, and pushing for it early gets you a show of peace while the wound goes underground. Being safe doesn't wait on it (please don't wait). Neither do boundaries, or making them answer for it. Justice can come first, and so can getting better. For many people, healing does include some forgiveness eventually, or at least getting out from under the resentment. Resentment narrows what you see, and your little one stays organized around the injury.
