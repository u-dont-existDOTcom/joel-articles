Missing someone doesn't have to turn into another problem to solve. Sometimes the ache itself can become a cue for love. Don't push the missing away. Maybe you're already telling them "I miss you" in your head. If there's love there, let that become "I love you," or offer a simple wish: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." Or something like that. Picture them, or just feel which way you're sending the care. This is about what happens in you, so you don't have to believe they receive anything. It doesn't replace grieving, or getting in touch with them. If you need a boundary, you still need it, and the practical stuff still needs doing. And if sending love makes you more compulsive, or more unreal, make it simpler. Same if you can't get back to your life. Just "I miss you, and I care." You can also offer some of the same care to the part of you that's still here.
