Some people have the opposite problem. It feels like they're connecting with their little one, and it's all staying near the surface. A long meditation and then sleep, especially a second sleep after you've already slept once, can open up much more direct stuff. So say that night's dream hits you with far more feeling and need than the session came anywhere near. You might lie there afterward wondering where all that was hiding. That gap is the sign (a good session that just didn't stick around is something else). Ask what came up that wasn't there before, and how you see it now. And if nightmares start wrecking your sleep, change the dose first, earlier in the day or shorter, before you give up on the whole thing.
