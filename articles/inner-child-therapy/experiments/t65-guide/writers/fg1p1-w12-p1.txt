Forgiveness, for a lot of people, does come eventually, or at least they stop living under the resentment. Not on a schedule, though. And you don't need it first. Not to be safe, not to set boundaries. Holding someone accountable doesn't wait for it, and neither does justice, or getting better. Resentment is a strange kind of company: it narrows what you can see and keeps your little one organized around the injury. Pressure to forgive early just gives you a calm, enlightened-looking surface while the wound goes underground.
