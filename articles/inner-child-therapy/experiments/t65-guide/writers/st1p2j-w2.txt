This doesn't mean forcing everything to sound positive, though. Grief is grief, and if something's dangerous, say so. Harm and anger still need accurate words, and so do limits and consent. Your little one might be wondering whose fault it was, and that needs an accurate answer too. Same with what's practically possible and what isn't. Once all that's been named, there's no need to add a picture of what not to become. Put most of your energy into what can be protected, practiced, repaired, chosen or grown.
