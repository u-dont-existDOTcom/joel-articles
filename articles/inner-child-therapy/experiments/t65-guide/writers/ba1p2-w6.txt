Maybe they're the one who always knows what to cook when you can't think straight. Over time, reparenting can help you pick up some of that and practice it yourself. Not all of it has to land on you or on them, either. Friends and community can carry some, and so can professionals, services, routines and simple tools. What you want is for your little one to feel reliably cared for in more than one way, while the relationship stays as precious and important as it is. So which jobs could become more yours, and which are healthiest shared? A few really do belong inside that relationship.
