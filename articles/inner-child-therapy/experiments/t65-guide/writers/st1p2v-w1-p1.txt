None of this means forcing things to sound positive. Grief is grief, and danger should be called danger. Harm and anger still need accurate words, and so do limits and consent. Same with accountability, and what's practically possible. If your little one only hears the bright side, they might decide, "Oh, so we don't talk about that." Once what's real has been named, skip any unneeded picture of what they shouldn't become. Put most of your energy into what can be protected or practiced, or repaired. Or chosen, or grown.
