Before getting philosophical, check whether there's a grown-up in the room. Maybe it feels like there isn't, like the part that wants to die is all of you. Then notice who's doing the noticing (Noticing Counts), or borrow an adult like in Borrow the Adult Before You Can Be the Adult, and see if any part of you can watch this, or keep your body safe, or wait on a decision you can't take back. If nothing answers yet, the questions can wait, and the person you reached out to is the adult for now. And if you're already able to watch the state without getting swallowed by it, go ahead.
