If you're curious about that bigger happiness, follow the curiosity instead of pretending to believe something. Read people from different traditions, and judge a path by its fruits (what it does for the people on it). Meet people whose lives make you wonder, the kind you walk away from thinking, "Where does that come from?"

Sometimes care isn't what's missing. A meal or a boundary can still help, and so can a kind sentence. But when the whole horizon of life feels too small, none of that touches the question underneath: "Why should I want this life at all?" You might not have put it in those words. Take that question seriously, though. If being alive feels like something you just endure, what would have to be possible for it to feel worth discovering?
