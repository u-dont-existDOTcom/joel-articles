Therapy is still useful for what therapy does, though. Taking turns like this can be borrowed adulthood at its healthiest. Your peer doesn't become your parent for good. They show you something a grown-up can do and help you do it with them. For a while it might still feel like theirs. Then they hand it back to you. A serious healing path can include both. Community does something different from therapy, and so does peer support that keeps to its rules.
