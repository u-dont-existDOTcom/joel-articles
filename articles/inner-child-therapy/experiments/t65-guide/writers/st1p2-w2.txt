I don't mean forcing everything to sound positive, though. Grief is grief, and danger should be called danger. Harm and anger still need accurate words, and so do limits and accountability. Consent too, and whatever practical constraints there are. Some of those are hard to say to someone you're comforting. Once what's real has been named, spend most of your energy on what can be protected, practiced, repaired, chosen or grown. If nobody needs a picture of what the person shouldn't become, leave it out.
