In this work, your words point your attention somewhere. When the painful or negative way of putting it isn't what needs naming, go with words for what you want to strengthen, like a relationship, or something you can do. Part of you might say, "I can't get dependent on them." Something like "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" usually gives your mind a clearer direction than bringing in a label like "dependency" just to deny it or argue with it. With your little one and in hypnosis especially, make the direction easy to imagine: warmth, protection, steadiness, rest, or whatever's really needed.
