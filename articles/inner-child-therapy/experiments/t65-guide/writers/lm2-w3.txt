Some people hear all this about love and answer, "No. I hate myself." You may have thought it a moment ago. If that's what you find, it deserves an honest hearing instead of an argument. But if you opened this article or came to therapy, something in you wanted things to be different. The same goes if you asked someone for help, or tried again after deciding you were hopeless.

What was that something? It could have been hope or desperation. Curiosity, maybe. Fear, or duty. Or it was more like, "I'm not going to keep suffering like this." Maybe it was love. You don't need to decide which. Ask that part what it hoped might happen when it brought you here, and follow that thread.
