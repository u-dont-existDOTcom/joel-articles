What can help is understanding what led them to hurt you. Their conditioning and history shaped what they did. So did their pain, and what they could and couldn't do. Given everything they'd become by that moment, it was the only move they had. Hurt people often pass on pain they never worked through. You may have heard that from somebody telling you to forgive. When you actually see it, though, you might find yourself forgiving them, and if you've held them uniquely to blame, the rage can soften into something more accurate.
