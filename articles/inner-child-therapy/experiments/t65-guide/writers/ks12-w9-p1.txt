If you're about to act, or can't stop yourself, get another person involved before anything else, and put some distance between you and the means. A crisis line or an emergency number counts as a person here. The same goes if you're badly disoriented, or drunk or high, or the means are right in front of you. Not sure whether that's you right now? Then treat it like it is. None of this answers whether your life is worth living. It keeps the question open long enough to look at it.
