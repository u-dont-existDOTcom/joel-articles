Some people have the opposite problem. It feels like you're connecting with your little one, but the contact is staying near the surface. Meditating for a long time and then sleeping can open up much more emotionally direct stuff, especially in a second sleep, after you've already slept once. If that night's dream carries far more feeling and need than the daytime session came anywhere near, you might wake up wondering where all that was. That gap is the sign, and the difference is information about the session (a good session that just didn't carry into your day is something else). Ask what became available, and how you see it now. If repeated nightmares start wrecking your sleep, change the dose before giving up on the whole thing. Try it earlier in the day, or shorter.
