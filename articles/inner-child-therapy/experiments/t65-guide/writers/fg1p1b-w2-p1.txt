Maybe you feel you should have forgiven whoever hurt you by now. Forgiveness can't be put on a schedule, though. Pressure to forgive early gets you an enlightened-looking performance, with the wound still there underneath. And you don't have to forgive first to be safe or set boundaries. Same with holding them accountable. Getting justice and recovering can come first, too. But for many people, healing does eventually include some forgiveness, or at least getting out from under active resentment. Active resentment can narrow what you see and keep your little one's world revolving around the injury.
