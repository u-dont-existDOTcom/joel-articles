Maybe what you're facing is awful, though. Steering like this doesn't mean you have to be positive about it. Grief is grief, and danger should be called danger. Harm and anger still need accurate words, and so do limits and consent. Accountability too, and whatever practical constraints you're under. Once you've named what's real, spend most of your energy on what can be protected or practiced. Or repaired. Skip any picture of what not to become, unless it's needed. What can you choose, or grow?
