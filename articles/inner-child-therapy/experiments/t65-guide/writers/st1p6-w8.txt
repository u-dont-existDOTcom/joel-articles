Love moves in a loop. It goes from spiritual experience to your little one, on into the grown-up relationship, from you into other people, and back again. Maybe you're not sure which door you came in through. The loop matters more than the door. Community can work in both directions. Loving people can make you safe enough, and make you belong enough, for something deeper to open. Or the opening comes first. Then it changes who you can "manifest," and the people help you make it practical and real, and live it in your body.
