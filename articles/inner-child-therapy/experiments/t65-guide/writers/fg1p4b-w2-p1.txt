Forgiving yourself works the same way. Own what you did (only what was yours), and let yourself feel the remorse. Repair what you can, by apologizing or making amends. Changing the behavior counts too. Then you're allowed to stop holding yourself hostage, and the responsibility stays. If you're thinking "not me, not after what I did," forgiving other people tends to be harder too. Forgiving too soon is one trap, and staying resentful forever is the other. Over time, keep coming back to how they became capable of what they did, until the understanding is real enough to change how you feel. Don't push it, though. Some harms take years.
