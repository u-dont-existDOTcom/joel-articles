Forgiving yourself works the same way. Own what you did, and only what was yours. Let the remorse in, that "I wish I hadn't" feeling. Repair what you can by apologizing and making amends. Changing how you act counts too. Then you're allowed to stop holding yourself hostage. That can feel like getting away with something. The responsibility stays, though. When forgiving yourself is hard, forgiving other people tends to be harder too. Some harms take years. Forgiving too soon is one trap, and the other is staying resentful for good. Don't push it. Come back over time to the question of how they became capable of what they did, until the understanding is real enough that you feel differently.
