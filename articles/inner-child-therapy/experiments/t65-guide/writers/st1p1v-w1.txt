The words you use in this work point your attention somewhere. So when a painful or negative way of putting things isn't itself what needs naming, pick words that point toward what you want to strengthen, like an ability or a relationship. Or something you do. "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" usually gives your mind a clearer direction than bringing in a label like "dependency" just to deny it or argue with it. With your little one and in hypnosis especially, make the direction easy to imagine: warmth, protection, steadiness, rest, or whatever is actually needed.
