For many people, healing eventually includes some forgiveness, or at least getting out from under active resentment. You can't put that on a schedule, and you don't need it before you can recover. You can be safe and set boundaries while you're nowhere near forgiving whoever hurt you. Same with holding them accountable and getting justice. Actively resenting them, though, can give you tunnel vision and keep your little one's world revolving around the injury. Maybe you feel like you should have forgiven them by now. But forgiving early under pressure like that turns into a performance that looks enlightened, while the wound goes underground.
