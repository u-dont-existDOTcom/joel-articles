The words you use in this work direct your attention. Unless it's the painful or negative side that needs naming, point your words toward what you want to strengthen, whether that's something you can do or a relationship. "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" usually gives your mind a clearer direction than bringing in a label like "dependency" just to deny it or argue with it. How would "You're not dependent" sound to your little one? With them and in hypnosis especially, make the direction easy to imagine: warmth, protection, steadiness, rest, or whatever is really needed.
