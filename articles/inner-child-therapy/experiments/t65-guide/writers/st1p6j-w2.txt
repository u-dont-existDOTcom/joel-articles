Community can work in both directions. Sometimes people who love you help you feel safe and like you belong, enough that something deeper can open up. Maybe you notice you've stopped rehearsing what to say around them. Other times the opening comes first and changes what kinds of people and relationships you can "manifest." Then community helps make the opening practical and real, something you live in your body. The loop matters more than which door you came in through anyway. Love moves from spiritual experience to your little one, and from there into the grown-up relationship. From you into other people, and back again.
