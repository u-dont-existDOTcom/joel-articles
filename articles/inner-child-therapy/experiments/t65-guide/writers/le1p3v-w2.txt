Missing someone doesn't have to turn into another problem to solve. Don't push the missing away. Sometimes the ache itself can become a cue for love. If there's love there, let "I miss you" become "I love you." You've probably been telling them the first one in your head already. Or offer a simple wish: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." Picture them, or just feel the direction of the care. This is about what happens in you, so you don't need to believe they receive anything. It doesn't replace grieving, or getting in touch. You'd keep your boundaries and do the practical stuff anyway. If sending love makes you more compulsive or unreal, make it simpler: "I miss you, and I care." Do the same if you can't get back to your life. You can also offer some of the same care to the part of you that's still here.
