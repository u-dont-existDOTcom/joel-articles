Grief is grief, though. Partway through all that steering, you might think, "But it really was dangerous," and if it was, it should be called dangerous. This isn't about being positive whether it's true or not. You still need the plain words for harm and for anger. Limits need them, and so does consent. Same for who's responsible, and for what's really possible right now. After that, spend most of your energy where something can be protected or practiced or repaired. Or chosen, or grown. (The picture of what your little one shouldn't become can wait until it's needed.)
