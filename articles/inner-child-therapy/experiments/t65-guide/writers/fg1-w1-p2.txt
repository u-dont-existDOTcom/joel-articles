What can help is seeing how somebody's conditioning and history shaped the harmful thing they did. Their pain shaped it too, and so did what they could and couldn't handle. Given everything they'd become by that moment, it was the only move they had available. Which might get you wondering who hurt them first. Hurt people often pass on pain they never worked through. When that becomes something you see, and not a moral instruction, you may find yourself forgiving them. The rage, the kind where it's all on them, can soften into something more accurate.
