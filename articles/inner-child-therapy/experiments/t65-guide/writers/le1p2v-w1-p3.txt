In the meantime, the grown-up you can stick to your routines and protect your body. Grieve what ended, too. But what has actually ended? And what are you still trying to keep going? Don't force yourself to become somebody new because the not-knowing feels unbearable. What isn't known yet? What practical act or small ritual would mark the ending without pretending you know the next beginning? Ask your little one what they're afraid will happen in the gap. They might take a while to tell you. Stay with them while the next life takes shape.
