Telling a memory as a story doesn't turn it into fiction or prove every detail is right. And it only ends the trial. If you did the harm, you're still responsible, and any repair that's owed is still owed. If someone hurt you, the line doesn't say what they did was okay. Stop if the narrator's voice turns sneering or the shame gets bigger. Same if you start to feel unreal. "I'll tell it from further away, then," you might think. More distance won't help with those. If the line does help, keep it to the line. Then you can ask what the younger you in that story needs from you now.
