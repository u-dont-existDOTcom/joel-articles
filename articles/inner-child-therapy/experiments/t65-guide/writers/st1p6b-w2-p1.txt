Community can work in both directions. Sometimes loving people give you enough safety and belonging for a deeper opening to become possible. Other times the opening comes first and changes what kinds of people and relationships you can "manifest." You might only notice that later, in who's around you. Then community helps make the opening practical and real, something you live in your body. Either way, love goes around in a loop, and the loop matters more than which door you came in through. It moves from spiritual experience to your little one, then into the grown-up relationship. From you it goes into other people, and back again.
