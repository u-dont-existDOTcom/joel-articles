Words aim attention. If you tell yourself, "I'm not dependent on anyone," the word that sticks is "dependent." So unless the painful thing is what needs naming, aim the words at what you're building: the ability, the relationship, the thing you want to do more of. "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" usually gives the mind a clearer direction than bringing in a label like "dependency" just to argue with it. In inner-child work and hypnosis especially, make it something you can picture (warmth, protection, steadiness, rest, whatever's really needed).
