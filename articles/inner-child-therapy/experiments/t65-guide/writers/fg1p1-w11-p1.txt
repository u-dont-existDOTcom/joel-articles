Maybe you've heard "You have to forgive to heal." For many people healing does come to include some forgiveness, or at least getting out from under active resentment. It just can't be put on a schedule, and it isn't the price of anything else: you can get safe without it, set boundaries, hold someone accountable, get justice and recover. Resentment narrows what you see and keeps your little one organized around the injury. But forgive early because you're told to, and you get the enlightened version ("I've made my peace") while the wound goes underground.
