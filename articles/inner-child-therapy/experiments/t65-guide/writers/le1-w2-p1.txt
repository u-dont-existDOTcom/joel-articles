In that stretch, ask what has actually ended, and what you're still trying to keep going. What isn't known yet? What's your little one afraid will happen in the gap? Maybe it thinks it'll always be like this. What practical act or small ritual would mark the ending, without pretending you know the next beginning? Don't force a new you to show up just because not knowing feels unbearable. Meanwhile, the grown-up you keeps your routines and protects your body. Grieve what ended, and stay with your little one while the next life takes shape.
