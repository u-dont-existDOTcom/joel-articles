So what has actually ended, and what are you still trying to keep going? Sometimes both have the same answer. Then there's what isn't known yet. What is your little one afraid will happen in the gap? What practical act or small ritual would mark the ending without pretending you know the next beginning? When not knowing feels unbearable, you might want to be someone new already, but don't force it. The grown-up you can keep your routines and protect your body. You can stay with your little one while the next life takes shape, and you can grieve what ended.
