None of that makes what they did harmless. Could they have done something else? In theory they could have. Punishment can't go back, though, and give them the awareness or support they were missing, or the ability, so the useful work is repairing what can be repaired and making a different response easier to reach now. They're still accountable, and the harm is real. Boundaries can still be right, and so can legal consequences and a moral judgment. So can keeping your distance, all the way to no contact ever again. None of it rules out understanding them, or forgiving them. "I understand how you became this way" can sit right beside "I now understand why I shouldn't rely on you for this."
