What can help is seeing what shaped the harm they did. Their conditioning and history went into it. So did their pain, and what they could and couldn't do. Hurt people often pass on pain they never worked through. With everything they'd become by then, it was their only move. You might hear that as a moral instruction, like now you're supposed to understand them. When you see it for yourself, though, forgiveness may come on its own. And your rage at them as the only one to blame can soften into something more accurate.
