Community can work in two directions. Sometimes loving people make you feel safe enough, and like you belong, that a deeper opening can happen. Other times the opening comes first and changes the kinds of people and relationships you can "manifest." You might wonder where all these loving people came from. Then community helps you make the opening practical and real, and live it in your body. The loop matters more than which door you came in through: love moves from spiritual experience to your little one, from your little one into the grown-up relationship, from you into other people, and back again.
