Before you advise your little one, it can help to find out what kind of company they want: "Do you want me to mostly listen, help you untangle this, give you ideas, or some mix?" Same goes for anybody else. You don't have to ask every time, like a ritual. When it's obvious which they want, follow that. If you'd only be guessing, though, one sentence can keep you from giving advice to somebody who needed to be heard. Or from just listening when they wanted help thinking it through.
