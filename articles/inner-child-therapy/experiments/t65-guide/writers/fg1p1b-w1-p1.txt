Maybe you're nowhere near forgiving whoever hurt you. For many people, healing does eventually include some forgiveness, or at least getting out from under active resentment, but you don't need it first to recover. You can be safe and set boundaries without it, and hold them accountable or get justice. You can't forgive on a schedule anyway. If you forgive early because you're supposed to, you get an enlightened-looking act, with the wound still there underneath. Then again, actively resenting them can narrow what you see and keep your little one organized around the injury.
