Missing someone doesn't have to become another problem to solve. Sometimes the ache itself can turn into a cue for love, so don't push the missing away. If love is there, let "I miss you" become "I love you," or offer a simple wish, something like "May you be safe. May you be held in love." You can picture them, or just feel the direction of the care. Maybe you don't believe they receive any of it. You don't have to, since this is about what happens in you. It doesn't replace grieving or contact, or boundaries, or whatever practical thing needs doing. If sending love makes you more compulsive or unreal, make it simpler: "I miss you, and I care." The same goes if you can't get back to your life. You can also offer some of that care to the part of you that's still here.
