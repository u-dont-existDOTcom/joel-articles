Without forgiving whoever hurt you, you can still be safe and set boundaries. Holding them accountable and getting justice don't need it either. For many people, healing does eventually include some forgiveness, or at least getting out from under active resentment. Maybe you hope saying "I forgive them" will make it true. Nobody can put forgiveness on a schedule, and you don't need it first to recover. Active resentment can narrow what you see, though, and keep your little one centered on the injury. Pressure to forgive early gets you a performance that looks enlightened, while the wound gets pushed underground.
