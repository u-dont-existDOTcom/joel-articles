Grief is grief, though. Partway through all that steering, you might think, "But it really was dangerous," and if it was, it should be called dangerous. This isn't about being positive whether it's true or not. Harm still gets called harm, and anger gets called anger. Your limits need their real names. So does consent, and so does who's accountable. Whatever's actually possible, too (sometimes that's not much). Once all that has been said, most of the energy can go to what can be protected or practiced, repaired, chosen or grown. A picture of what your little one shouldn't become can stay out of it unless you need one.
