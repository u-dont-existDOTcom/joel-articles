None of this is forced positivity, though. Grief is grief, and if something is dangerous, call it dangerous. You still need accurate words for harm, anger, limits, accountability, consent and practical constraints. Some of those can be hard to say to your little one. Once you've named what's real, put most of your energy into what can be protected or practiced. For that, you don't need a picture of what not to become. What can be repaired? What can you choose now, or grow?
