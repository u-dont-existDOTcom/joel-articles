For many people, healing eventually includes some forgiveness, or at least getting out from under active resentment. You can't put it on a schedule. And you don't need it first to be safe or to set boundaries. It isn't a condition for holding someone accountable or getting justice, and recovery doesn't wait on it either. While you're actively resenting someone, what you see can narrow, and your little one can stay organized around the injury. So you might want to hurry up and forgive already. But pressure to forgive early gets you an enlightened-looking performance, while the wound goes underground.
