In that stretch, ask what has really ended, and what you're still trying to keep going. Sometimes both answers are the same thing. What isn't known yet, and what is your little one afraid will happen in the gap? Look for a practical act or small ritual that would mark the ending without pretending you know the next beginning. Don't force a new identity because not knowing feels unbearable. Meanwhile, the grown-up you can keep up your routines and protect your body. You can grieve what ended, and stay with your little one while the next life takes shape.
