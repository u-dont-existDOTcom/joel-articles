Maybe someone's told you that you'll never heal until you forgive. For a lot of people, healing does eventually include some forgiveness, or at least getting out from under active resentment. But it can't be put on a schedule, and you don't need it first to be safe, set a boundary or hold anyone accountable. Justice and recovering don't wait on it either. Resentment does narrow what you can see, though, and it keeps your little one organized around the injury. Forcing forgiveness early just gets you an enlightened-looking act while the wound goes underground.
