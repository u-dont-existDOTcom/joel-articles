For many people, healing eventually includes some forgiveness, or at least getting out from under active resentment. If you're still angry at whoever hurt you, that might sound far off. Forgiveness can't be put on a schedule, and you don't need it first to recover. Being safe and setting boundaries don't depend on it, and you can hold them accountable and get justice without it. But active resentment can narrow what you see and keep your little one's world revolving around the injury. Pressure to forgive early gets you a performance that looks enlightened, while the wound goes underground.
