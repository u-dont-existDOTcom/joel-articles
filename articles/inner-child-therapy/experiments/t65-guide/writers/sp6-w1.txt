In the morning, pick one small intention: "Today I will notice one hook before I act from it." You don't have to know yet which hook it'll be. Whichever one shows up will do. At night, look back over the day without putting yourself on trial. Where did you notice the hook? And where did it run its whole course anyway?

Maybe you only catch it now, looking back: "Oh, I got hooked there." That's still noticing, and it counts as progress. If you interrupted the pattern somewhere, notice that too. What comes next, a small repair or some way to protect yourself? And what moment did you actually get that felt warm, or that you enjoyed? It might have been a time you were brave, or felt close to someone. Repair whatever can be repaired. Then renew the intention and go to sleep.
