Sometimes loving people give you enough safety and belonging that something deeper can open up. Other times it goes the other way around: the opening comes first and changes what kinds of people and relationships you can "manifest." You might wonder where all these loving people were hiding. Then community helps make the opening practical and real, right down into your body. It's all one loop, and the loop matters more than which door you came in through. Love goes from spiritual experience to your little one, from your little one into the grown-up relationship, from you into other people, and back again.
