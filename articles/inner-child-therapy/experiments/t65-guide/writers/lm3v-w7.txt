Rumi said to keep breaking your heart until it stays open. Some people find that deep grieving gives way to warmth or connection. For some it's a feeling in the body, like being held. (That's them describing how it feels. It doesn't prove how the body does that.) Love can open grief from the other side, too. Someone's kind to you and you think, "Why am I crying?" Being cared for can bring up sadness for the care that was missing. That doesn't mean the love failed. Stay with it while you're present and still have a choice, and if it's swamping you more than it's opening you, slow down, the way you'd turn the song off for the night.
