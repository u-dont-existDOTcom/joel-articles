The three grown-up jobs work between people, too. A good peer, someone you take turns listening to as equals, can lend you the Nurturer by staying with you and being warm. And the Protector, by keeping what you say private and sticking to the agreed time. If you say no, they don't push. Your little one might be watching for that. As for your Guide, they don't supply it or decide what your experience means. But a carefully timed question can help you hear your own Guide. They ask without interpreting or giving advice, and leave the answer to you. Then it's their turn.
