Reparenting is usually talked about as something you do alone, or with a therapist. But your little one got hurt in relationships, and that kind of hurt can only heal so far on your own. Having someone who can care for you when you're struggling can be an enormous blessing. Sometimes one person ends up carrying a lot of the borrowed grown-up jobs at once: food, planning, health decisions, rides, your social life, reassurance, protection, company. Or maybe it's just that feeling of "okay, I'm with somebody who knows what they're doing."

Over time, reparenting can help you take in some of what that person has modeled for you, and practice it. In a way, they've been showing you how all along. Other jobs can be shared with friends and community, or with professionals or services. Some can go to routines and ordinary tools. Which jobs can become more yours, and which are healthiest shared? The aim is for your little one to feel reliable care through more than one way, while the relationship itself stays precious and important. And which jobs really belong inside that relationship?
