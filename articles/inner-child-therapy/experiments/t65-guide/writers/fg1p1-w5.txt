For a lot of people, healing gets around to some forgiveness eventually, or at least to getting out from under the resentment. It won't go on a schedule, though. Maybe somebody's told you you'll never heal until you forgive. You don't need it first to be safe, or to set boundaries. Holding someone accountable doesn't wait on it either, and neither does justice, or recovering. Resentment does narrow what you can see, and it keeps your little one organized around the injury. But pressure to forgive early gets you a show ("I'm at peace with it") while the wound goes underground.
