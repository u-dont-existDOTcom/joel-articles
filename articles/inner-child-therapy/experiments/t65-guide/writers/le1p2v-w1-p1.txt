In that stretch, ask what has actually ended, and what you're still trying to keep going. What isn't known yet? What is your little one afraid will happen in the gap? Maybe they're scared whoever you turn into next won't have room for them. And what practical act or small ritual would mark the ending without pretending you know the next beginning? Don't force a new identity just because not knowing feels unbearable. While the next life takes shape, the grown-up you can stay with your little one and keep your routines. You can protect your body, and grieve what ended.
