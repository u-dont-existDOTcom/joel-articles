Missing someone doesn't have to turn into another problem to solve. Don't push the missing away. Sometimes the ache itself can become a cue for love. If love is there, let "I miss you" become "I love you," or offer a simple wish: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." Picture them, or just feel which direction you're sending the care. You might wonder whether any of it reaches them. You don't have to believe it does, since this is about what happens in you. Sending love doesn't replace grieving, or being in touch with them. You'd still keep any boundaries you need and do the practical things. If sending love makes you more compulsive or unreal, make it simpler: "I miss you, and I care." Same if you can't get back to your life. You can also offer some of the same care to the part of you that's still here.
