If you're still furious with whoever hurt you, you don't have to fix that first. Getting safe, setting boundaries, holding them accountable, getting justice and recovering can all happen without your having forgiven anybody. For many people healing does end up including some forgiveness, or at least getting out from under the resentment, but it can't be scheduled. And forcing it early gets you a performance of peace while the wound goes underground. What resentment does cost is room: it narrows what you see and keeps your little one organized around the injury.
