None of that makes what they did harmless, and it doesn't mean nothing else was possible in theory. It means punishing them can't reach back and hand them the awareness or support they didn't have. You might still wish it could. The useful part is repairing what can be repaired, and making a different response easier from here on. The harm is real, and accountability stays. Boundaries can still be right, and so can legal consequences, moral judgment or staying away for good. Understanding them doesn't cancel any of that, and neither does forgiving them. "I understand how you became this way" can sit right next to "I now understand why I shouldn't rely on you for this."
