Telling it as a story doesn't make it fiction, or prove every detail is right. It ends the trial. Whoever did the harm is still accountable: if it was you, any repair that's owed is still owed, and if someone hurt you, the line doesn't say what they did was okay. Stop if the grandpa voice starts sneering or the shame gets bigger. Same if you start to feel unreal. You might think, "I'll just tell it from further away, then." More distance won't help with those. If the line helps, keep it to just that. Then you can ask what the younger you in that story needs from you now.
