Understanding them doesn't make what they did harmless, and in theory there were other options. It means punishment can't go back and create the awareness, support or ability they were short on. What's worth doing is repairing what can be repaired, and making a different response more available now. The harm is real, and they're still accountable. Boundaries and legal consequences can still be right, and so can moral judgment and distance, even permanent no-contact. You can understand them and hold them responsible. And yes, you can forgive someone you'll never speak to again. "I understand how you became this way" can sit right beside "I now understand why I shouldn't rely on you for this."
