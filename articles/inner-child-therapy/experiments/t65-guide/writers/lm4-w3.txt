If that bigger happiness makes you curious, follow the curiosity instead of pretending to believe something. Read what people from different traditions have written, and meet people whose lives make you wonder. Some of what you read may sound strange. Judge a path by its fruits, meaning what it has made of the people who follow it.

Sometimes care isn't what's missing. The whole horizon of life feels too small, and a meal, a boundary or a kind sentence can help without touching the question underneath: "Why should I want this life at all?" That's hard to say out loud. Take that question seriously anyway: what would have to be possible for being alive to feel worth discovering, not just enduring?
