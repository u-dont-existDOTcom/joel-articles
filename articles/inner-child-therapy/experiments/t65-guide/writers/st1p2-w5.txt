This isn't about putting a happy face on things. If someone's ever told you to look on the bright side while you were grieving, you know why. Grief is grief, and if something's dangerous, say so. Harm and anger still need their real names, and so do your limits, who's responsible, what someone agreed to, and what's actually possible. Once you've said what's true, though, put most of your effort into what's yours to protect and practice, and what can still be repaired. Choosing and growing count too. A picture of what you shouldn't turn into can wait until it's useful.
