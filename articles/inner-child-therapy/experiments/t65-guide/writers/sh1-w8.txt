Some people have the opposite problem: it feels like they're connecting, and the contact stays near the surface. A long meditation and then sleep, especially a second sleep after you've already slept once, can open up much more direct stuff. Then the dream that night hits harder than the whole session did, with way more feeling and need in it, and you wake up wondering where all that was during the day. That's the sign this is you (a session that was good and just didn't stick around is something else). Whatever came up, what's available to you now that wasn't before, and how do you see it? And if nightmares start wrecking your sleep, try a smaller dose, earlier in the day or shorter, before you write off the whole thing.
