Forgiving yourself works the same way. Own what you did (only what was yours), and let yourself feel the remorse. Repair what you can, by apologizing or making amends. Changing the behavior counts too. Then you're allowed to stop holding yourself hostage, and the responsibility stays. If you're thinking "not me, not after what I did," forgiving other people tends to be harder too. Don't push it. Some harms take years, and forgiving too soon is as much a trap as never getting out of the resentment. What you can do is keep coming back to one question, how did they become capable of it, until the answer is real enough to change how you feel.
