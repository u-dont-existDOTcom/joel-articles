Lots of people do end up forgiving, at least a little, or they stop living under the resentment. It doesn't happen on schedule. And you don't have to get there first to be safe, set limits, make them answer for it, see justice done or get better. Maybe you've been told otherwise. Holding a grudge can shrink what you're able to see, though, and keep your little one circling the injury. Then again, if you're pushed to forgive too early, you get a show of being at peace ("I've let it go") while the hurt goes underground.
