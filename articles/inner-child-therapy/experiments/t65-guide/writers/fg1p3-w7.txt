That doesn't make what they did okay, and in theory there were other things they could have done. But punishing them now can't reach back and give them what they were missing then, the sense to see it or the help they needed. Or the skills. What's left is fixing what can be fixed, and making it easier for something different to happen now. They still have to answer for it, and it still hurt. Keeping your limits can still be right. So can the law, if it comes to that, and saying plainly that it was wrong, and keeping away from them, for good if you need to. None of it stops you from understanding them, or forgiving them. "I understand how you became this way" can sit right next to "I now understand why I shouldn't rely on you for this."
