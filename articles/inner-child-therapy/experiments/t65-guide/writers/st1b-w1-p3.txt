Community can work in both directions. Sometimes people who love you give you enough safety and belonging that something deeper can open up. Other times the opening comes first, and it changes what kinds of people and relationships you can "manifest." You might wonder what to do with it. Then community helps the opening become something practical and real that your body knows too. There's a loop in all this, and the loop matters more than which door you came in through. Love moves from spiritual experience to your little one, from your little one into the grown-up relationship, from you into other people, and back again.
