You can forgive them and never speak to them again. You can understand them and hold them responsible, too. Maybe you're thinking "I understand how you became this way," and right beside it, "I now understand why I shouldn't rely on you for this." Seeing how they got there means punishing them can't go back and create the awareness or support they didn't have enough of. Or the ability they were short on. The useful part is repairing whatever can be repaired, and making it easier to respond differently now. That understanding doesn't make what they did harmless, though, or mean no other option existed in theory. The harm is real, and accountability stays. Boundaries and legal consequences can still be right. So can judging what they did as wrong. The right call can be distance, too, or no contact ever again.
