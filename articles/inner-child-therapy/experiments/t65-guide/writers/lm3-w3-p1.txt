Rumi says you have to keep breaking your heart until it stays open. Some people find that deep grieving gives way to warmth or connection, and some feel held in the body. That's an experience people describe, and it isn't proof of how the body produces it. Love can open grief from the other side, too. Being cared for can bring up sadness for the care that was missing. You might think, "This is nice, so why am I crying?" The sadness doesn't mean the love failed. Stay with the feeling while you're present and can still choose, and slow down when it's swamping you instead of opening you.
