Your words direct your attention in this work. Unless something needs saying in painful or negative terms, point them toward what you want to strengthen, like an ability or a relationship. Or something you do. What would you want more of? "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" usually gives your mind a clearer direction than bringing in a label like "dependency" just to deny it or argue with it. In inner-child work and hypnosis especially, make the direction easy to imagine: warmth or protection, steadiness or rest, whatever's really needed.
