Love moves from spiritual experience to your little one, and from there into the grown-up relationship. From you it goes into other people, and comes back. That loop matters more than which door you came in through. Community can work in both directions. Sometimes loving people help you feel safe enough, and like you belong with them, for a deeper opening to become possible. Your little one might be watching for that. Other times the opening comes first, and it changes what kinds of people and relationships you can "manifest." Then community helps you make it practical and real, and live it in your body.
