Forgiving yourself works the same way, and when it's hard, forgiving other people tends to be harder too. Own what you did ("that one's on me"), and only what was yours. Let yourself feel the remorse. Repair what you can by apologizing and making amends, and by changing the behavior. Then you're allowed to stop holding yourself hostage. That can feel like getting away with something. The responsibility stays, though. Some harms take years. Forgiving too soon is one trap, and the other is living permanently inside resentment. Don't push it. Just keep coming back to how the person who did it became capable of it, until the understanding is real enough to change how you feel.
