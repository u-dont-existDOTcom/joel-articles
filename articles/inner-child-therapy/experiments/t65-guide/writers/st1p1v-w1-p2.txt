When someone's doing that much for you, you might bring up a label like "dependency" just to deny it or argue with it. "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" usually gives your mind a clearer direction. In this work, the words you use point your attention somewhere. So unless a painful or negative angle is itself what needs naming, go with words that point at what you want to strengthen: an ability, a relationship or something you do. With your little one and in hypnosis especially, make where you're headed easy to imagine. Would warmth help, or protection? Maybe it's steadiness, or rest. Or whatever is actually needed.
