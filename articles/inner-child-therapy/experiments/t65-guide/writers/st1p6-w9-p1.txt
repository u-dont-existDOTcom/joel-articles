Love moves in a loop. It goes from spiritual experience to your little one, on into the grown-up relationship, from you into other people, and back again. Maybe you're not sure which door you came in through. The loop matters more than the door. Community can work in both directions. Sometimes it's the people. They love you until you feel safe and like you belong, and then something deeper opens. Sometimes it's the opening, and afterward you "manifest" different people and relationships than before. Then community helps make it practical and real, lived in your body and not only felt once.
