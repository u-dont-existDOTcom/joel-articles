If not knowing feels unbearable, don't force a new identity to make it stop. Ask yourself what's really over, and what you're still trying to keep going. You could mark the ending with some practical act or a small ritual, without pretending you know the next beginning. The grown-up you can keep your routines and protect your body. Grieve what ended, and stay with your little one while the next life takes shape. What isn't known yet? What is your little one afraid will happen in the gap? That it'll be like this forever, maybe.
