Over time, you can use reparenting to take in and practice some of what that person has shown you by example. Other jobs can be shared with friends and community, or with professionals or services. Routines and ordinary tools, too. Which of these jobs can become more yours? Which are healthiest shared? And which really belong inside that relationship? The aim is for your little one to feel reliable care in more than one way, while the relationship itself stays precious and important.
