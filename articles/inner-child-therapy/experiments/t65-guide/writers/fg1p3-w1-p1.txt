None of that makes what they did harmless, or means they had no other option in theory. Part of you might want them punished. But punishment can't go back and give them the awareness or support they didn't have enough of. Or the ability. The useful work is repairing what can be repaired, and making a different move more available now. They're still accountable, though, and the harm is real. Boundaries, legal consequences, moral judgment, distance and cutting off contact for good can all still be right. You can understand them and hold them responsible. And forgiving them doesn't mean you ever have to speak to them again. "I understand how you became this way" can sit beside "I now understand why I shouldn't rely on you for this."
