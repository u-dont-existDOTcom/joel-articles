The words you use in this work point your attention somewhere. Unless the painful thing itself is what needs naming, pick words that point at what you're trying to strengthen, like an ability or a relationship, or something you want to do more of. Try telling yourself, "I'm not dependent." Where did your mind go just then? (Probably to "dependent.") "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" usually gives it a clearer direction. With your little one, and in hypnosis especially, make that direction easy to picture: warmth, protection, steadiness, rest, or whatever's really needed.
