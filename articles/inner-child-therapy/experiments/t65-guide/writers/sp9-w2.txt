Every session with your little one needs a definite ending, photo or not. Look around and name where you are. Feel where you're touching something, like the floor under your feet or the chair, and relax your jaw and hands. Name one normal thing you'll do next, whatever that is, and say something like "That's enough for today. We're returning to the life we're building." Maybe it feels like you two were just getting somewhere. Going back to your life is part of the healing, too. The session doesn't have to resolve every feeling before you make food or go to sleep.
