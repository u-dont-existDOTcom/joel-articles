When your little one gets that loud, ask what they're afraid will happen in the gap. What isn't known yet? Not knowing can feel so unbearable you want to hurry up and be somebody new. Don't force a new identity, though. The grown-up you can keep routines and protect your body. You can grieve what ended, and stay with your little one while the next life takes shape. What has really ended, and what are you still trying to keep going? Think of a practical act or small ritual to mark the ending without pretending you know what begins next.
