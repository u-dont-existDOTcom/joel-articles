So ask what's actually over, and what you're still trying to keep alive. What don't you know yet? What is your little one scared will happen in between? It might not have words for that yet. Something small can mark the ending, a thing you do or a little ritual, without pretending you know what's next. Don't grab a new identity just because not knowing is unbearable. In the meantime the grown-up you keeps the routines going and looks after your body. Grieve what's gone. Stay with your little one while the next life takes shape.
