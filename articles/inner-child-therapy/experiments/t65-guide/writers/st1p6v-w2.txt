Community can work in both directions. Sometimes loving people help you feel safe and like you belong, enough for a deeper opening to become possible. Somewhere in there you start thinking maybe you could let this in. Other times the opening comes first, and it changes what kinds of people and relationships you can "manifest." Then community helps make that opening practical and real, something you live in your body. Which door you came in through matters less than the loop. Love moves from spiritual experience to your little one, and on into the grown-up relationship. Then from you into other people, and back again.
