This one's for when talking or picturing gets you only silence. Write with your wrong hand, the clumsy one, whose letters already look like a first grader's (or a ransom note). Whatever comes out, hurt or scared, let it be what's really there. If it's nothing, write that, and don't make up lines for them. Then your good hand answers, the way a grown-up who's got their back would. Sometimes the protective part grabs the pen first. Then it gets the wrong hand until it's said its piece.
