Seeing how they got there doesn't make what they did harmless, or mean nothing else was possible in theory. But punishment can't go back and create the awareness or support they didn't have enough of. Or give them the ability. What's worth doing is repairing what can be repaired, and making a different response easier to reach for now. If you're thinking "so they just get away with it?", they're still accountable, and the harm is real. Legal consequences and judging them for it can still be right. So can boundaries and distance, up to no contact for good. You can understand them and hold them responsible. You can forgive someone you'll never speak to again. "I understand how you became this way" can sit beside "I now understand why I shouldn't rely on you for this."
