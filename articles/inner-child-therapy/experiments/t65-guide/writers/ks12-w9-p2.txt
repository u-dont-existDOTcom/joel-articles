Before getting philosophical, check that there's a grown-up in the room. If the part that wants to die feels like all of you, notice who's doing the noticing (Noticing Counts), or borrow an adult, and invite in any part of you that can watch, keep your body safe, or put off a decision you can't undo. Maybe nothing answers yet. Then the questions can wait, and the person from the first step is the adult for now. And if you can already watch the suicidal state without being swallowed by it, skip ahead.
