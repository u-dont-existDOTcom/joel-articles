For many people, healing does get around to some forgiveness eventually, or at least to not being run by the resentment anymore. It can't be put on a schedule (nobody gets a gold star for forgiving fastest). And you don't have to forgive first to get safe, set boundaries, hold someone accountable, get justice or get better. Resentment isn't free, though. It narrows what you see, and it keeps your little one arranged around the injury. Forgiving early under pressure isn't free either (mostly it buys you a nice performance of peace while the wound goes underground).
