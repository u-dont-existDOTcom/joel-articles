"Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" usually gives your mind a clearer direction than bringing in a label like "dependency" just to deny it or argue with it. Maybe you've had that argument with yourself already. In this work your words direct your attention, so unless something has to be put in painful or negative terms, aim them at what you want to strengthen: an ability or a relationship. Or something you do. With your little one and in hypnosis especially, make the direction easy to imagine: warmth, protection, steadiness, rest or whatever's really needed.
