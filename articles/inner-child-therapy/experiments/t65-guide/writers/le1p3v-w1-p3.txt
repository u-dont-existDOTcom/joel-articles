If you miss someone, don't push the missing away. It doesn't have to become another problem to solve. Sometimes the ache itself can become a cue for love. Any love you send is about what happens in you, so you don't have to believe they receive anything. If love is there, let "I miss you" become "I love you," or offer a simple wish: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." Picture them, or just feel which direction you're sending the care. This doesn't replace grieving or being in touch with them. You'd still keep any boundaries you need and do the practical things. If sending love makes you more compulsive or unreal, or you can't get back to your life, make it simpler: "I miss you, and I care." That one still says you miss them. You can also offer some of the same care to the part of you that's still here.
