What can help is understanding how they got to be someone who'd do that. Their history went into it. So did what was done to them, and their own pain. By then there wasn't much they could handle. Given all of it, that move was the only one they had. Hurt people often pass on pain they never worked through. You may have heard that from somebody telling you to forgive. When you see it for yourself, though, you might find yourself forgiving them, and if you've held them uniquely to blame, the rage can soften into something more accurate.
