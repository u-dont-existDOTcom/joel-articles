Missing someone doesn't have to be another problem to solve. Don't push the missing away. Sometimes the ache itself can become a cue for love. Maybe you're already saying "I miss you" in your head. If love is there, let that turn into "I love you," or offer a simple wish: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." Picture them, or just feel the care going out to them. You don't have to believe they receive anything, since this is about what happens in you. It doesn't replace grieving or being in touch with them. You'd still keep whatever boundaries you need, and do the practical things. If sending love makes you more compulsive or unreal, make it simpler: "I miss you, and I care." Do the same if you can't get back to your life. You can also offer some of that care to the part of you that's still here.
