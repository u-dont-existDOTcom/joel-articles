In this work, you steer your attention with the words you use. Unless something painful or negative is itself what needs naming, pick words that point toward the ability or the relationship you want to strengthen. Or something you want to do more. Maybe while you were picturing that person, part of you asked, "Isn't that dependency?" Saying "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" usually gives your mind a clearer direction than bringing in a label like "dependency" just to deny it or argue with it. Especially with your little one, and in hypnosis, make where you're headed easy to imagine. It might be warmth or protection. Or steadiness, or rest, or whatever is actually needed.
