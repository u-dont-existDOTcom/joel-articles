That doesn't make what they did harmless. In theory there were other options; they just weren't ones that person could reach by then. Punishing them can't go back and give them the awareness or support they were short on, or the ability. What's left is repairing what can be repaired, and making a different response easier to reach now. The harm is still real, and they're still accountable. Boundaries can still be right, and so can legal consequences, a moral judgment, distance, or cutting contact for good. You can understand someone, hold them responsible, never speak to them again and forgive them, all at once. "I understand how you became this way" can sit right next to "I now understand why I shouldn't rely on you for this."
