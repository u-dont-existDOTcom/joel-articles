For many people, healing does get around to some forgiveness eventually, or at least to not being run by the resentment anymore. It can't be put on a schedule (nobody gets a gold star for forgiving fastest). And you don't have to forgive first to get safe, set boundaries, hold someone accountable, get justice or get better. Staying in active resentment has a cost, though: it narrows what you can see, and your little one ends up organized around the injury. Forgiving early because you're pushed to has a cost too. You get a very enlightened-looking performance, and the wound just moves underground.
