Breathe however you normally breathe, nothing special. After yourself, wish the same for anyone else who comes to mind. If it's someone who hurt you, maybe part of you goes, wait, them too? It isn't asking you to forgive them, or to imagine their childhood. None of this involves contacting anyone. Whether you ever do is a separate decision, and never one to make while you're flooded. All this is so you stop carrying a feeling this many people share, as proof that you alone are defective.
