Over time, reparenting can help you take in and practice some of what that person has shown you. You might hear yourself talking to your little one the way they talk to you. Other jobs can be shared with friends, community, professionals or services, routines and ordinary tools. The aim is for your little one to feel reliable care in more than one way, while the relationship itself stays precious and important. Which of the jobs they carry can become more yours, and which are healthiest shared? And which really belong inside that relationship?
