Sometimes a deeper opening comes first and changes what kinds of people and relationships you can "manifest." You might look around and wonder when you started knowing people like this. Then community helps make that opening practical and real, and you live it in your body. Love moves from spiritual experience to your little one, into the grown-up relationship, from you into other people, and back again. The loop matters more than which door you came in through, and community can work in both directions. Sometimes loving people help you feel safe and like you belong, enough for a deeper opening to become possible.
