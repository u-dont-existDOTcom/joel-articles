If you're curious about that bigger happiness, follow the curiosity instead of pretending to believe something. Read people from different traditions, and meet people whose lives make you wonder. You might already know someone like that, who's happy in a way you can't quite figure out. And judge a path by its fruits: what are the people who follow it like?

Sometimes the whole horizon of life feels too small. Care isn't what's missing then. A meal can help, or a boundary, or a kind sentence, without touching the question underneath: "Why should I want this life at all?" Take that question seriously. Maybe right now, life is something you're just enduring. What would have to be possible for being alive to feel worth discovering?
