And if it was the only move they had, punishing them can't go back and give them the awareness or support they didn't have enough of. Or the ability, if that's what ran short. Seeing how they got there doesn't make what they did harmless, or mean no other option existed in theory. The useful work is repairing what can be repaired and making a different response more possible now. Accountability stays, and the harm is real. Boundaries and legal consequences can still be right. So can judging what they did as wrong, or keeping your distance, all the way to no contact ever again. In your own head, "I understand how you became this way" can sit right beside "I now understand why I shouldn't rely on you for this." You can understand them and hold them responsible. You can also never speak to them again, and forgive them.
