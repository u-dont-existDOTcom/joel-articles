Love moves in a loop. It goes from spiritual experience to your little one, on into the grown-up relationship, from you into other people, and back again. Maybe you're not sure which door you came in through. The loop matters more than the door. Community can work in both directions. Sometimes loving people help you feel safe and like you belong, and that's enough for a deeper opening to become possible. Other times the opening comes first and changes what kinds of people and relationships you can "manifest." Then community helps make that opening practical and real, and helps you live it in your body.
