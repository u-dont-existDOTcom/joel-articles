If you miss someone, don't push the missing away. It doesn't have to turn into another problem to solve, either. Sometimes the ache itself can become a cue for love. If love is there, let "I miss you" become "I love you," or offer a simple wish: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." Picture them, or just feel the direction of the care. This is about what happens in you. Do they receive any of it? You don't have to believe they do. None of this replaces grieving, or contact with them. You'd still keep whatever boundaries you need and handle the practical things. If you can't get back to your life after sending love, make it simpler: "I miss you, and I care." The same goes if sending love is making you more compulsive or unreal. And you can offer some of that same care to the part of you that's still here.
