None of this means forcing everything to sound positive. Grief is grief, and danger should be called danger. Imagine being little and scared of something real, and someone telling you everything's fine. You still need accurate words for harm and anger. It's the same with limits and consent. And be accurate about who's responsible for what, and about whatever can't be done in practice. Once you've named what's real, skip painting a picture of what not to become, unless it's needed. Put most of your energy into what can be protected or repaired. Or practiced. Maybe there's something you can choose, or grow.
