They still have to answer for what they did, and it still hurt. Seeing how they got there doesn't make it okay, and in theory they could have done something else. It's just that punishing them now can't reach back and give them what they were missing then, the sense to see it or the help. Or the skills. What's left is fixing what can be fixed, and making it easier for something different to happen now. Maybe you've decided you're never speaking to them again. That can still be the right call, and so can a boundary, or the law, or saying out loud that it was wrong. None of it rules out forgiving them someday. "I understand how you became this way" can sit right next to "I now understand why I shouldn't rely on you for this."
