If journaling to make sense of things leaves you more confused, a simpler memory aid is fine. That could be a short note with the date and what happened, or a note of what helped. A reminder or a next step works too. So does a voice note. Maybe you still catch yourself wondering what it all means. A memory aid keeps track of things without you having to work that out at the same time, and it doesn't have to explain your life. Looking for patterns can come later, if it's useful at all.
