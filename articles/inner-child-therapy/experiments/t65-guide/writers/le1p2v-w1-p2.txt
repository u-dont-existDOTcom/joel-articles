Don't force a new identity because not knowing feels unbearable. It can get so bad you'd take almost any answer to "who am I now?" just to have one. The grown-up you can protect your body and keep up your routines. You can grieve what ended, and stay with your little one while the next life takes shape. What has actually ended, and what are you still trying to keep going? Ask what isn't known yet, and what your little one is afraid will happen in the gap. What practical act or small ritual would mark the ending without pretending you know what begins next?
