The words you use in this work direct attention. So when a painful or negative way of putting something isn't what needs naming, prefer words that point toward the ability, relationship or action you want to strengthen. Maybe a word like "dependency" came up while you were picturing that person. "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" usually gives the mind a clearer direction than bringing that label in just to deny it or argue with it. Especially in inner-child work and hypnosis, make the direction easy to imagine: whatever's actually needed, like warmth or protection. Sometimes it's steadiness, or just rest.
