Give every session with your little one a definite ending. Look around and name where you are, and feel your feet or the chair, wherever you're touching something. Let your jaw and hands relax. They might have been tight this whole time. Then name one normal thing you'll do next and say something like, "That's enough for today. We're returning to the life we're building." Maybe it doesn't feel like enough. Going back to your life is part of the healing, too, and the session doesn't have to resolve every feeling before you make food or go to sleep.
