Give every session with your little one a definite ending. You might want to stay until everything settles. But going back to your life is part of the healing, too. The session doesn't have to resolve every feeling before you make food or answer a message. Or before you take a walk or go to sleep. Name one ordinary thing you'll do next and say something like, "That's enough for today. We're returning to the life we're building." Then look around and name where you are. Feel where you're touching something, like your feet or the chair, and relax your jaw and hands (they may be tight by now).
