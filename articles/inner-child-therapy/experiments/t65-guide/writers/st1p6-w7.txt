Love moves in a loop. It goes from spiritual experience to your little one, on into the grown-up relationship, from you into other people, and back again. Maybe you're not sure which door you came in through. The loop matters more than the door. Community can work in both directions. Sometimes the people come first. They love you, you feel safe with them and like you belong, and something deeper has room to open. Other times the opening comes first and changes who turns up in your life (some people call that "manifesting"). Then community is how it gets practical and real, down into your body.
