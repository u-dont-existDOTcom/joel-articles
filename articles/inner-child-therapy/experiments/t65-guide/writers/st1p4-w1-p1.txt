The three grown-up jobs work between people too. A good peer (someone you take turns listening to, as equals) can lend you the Nurturer by being warm company. And the Protector, by keeping what you say private and sticking to the time you agreed on. If you say no, they don't push. But they don't act as your Guide, or decide what your experience means. Maybe they're thinking, "Oh, I know what this is about," or "Here's what you should do." They keep it to themselves. A question from them at the right moment can help you hear your own Guide, and what you answer is up to you. Then you trade places.
