For many people, healing does get around to some forgiveness eventually, or at least to not being run by the resentment anymore. It can't be put on a schedule (nobody gets a gold star for forgiving fastest). And you don't have to forgive first to get safe, set boundaries, hold someone accountable, get justice or get better. Resentment does narrow what you see, though, and it keeps your little one organized around the injury. But pushed early, forgiveness turns into a performance, all peace on the surface, while the wound goes underground.
