Before any philosophy, you need a grown-up in the room, and when the part that wants to die feels like all of you, that grown-up can be hard to find. So look small. Is there a part that can just watch what's happening? (That's what Noticing Counts was about.) Or a part that wants to keep your body safe, or to hold off on anything you can't take back? You could also borrow one, as in Borrow the Adult Before You Can Be the Adult. If none shows up yet, the questions wait, and the person you reached is the adult for now. If you're already able to watch the suicidal state without being swallowed by it, go on to the questions.
