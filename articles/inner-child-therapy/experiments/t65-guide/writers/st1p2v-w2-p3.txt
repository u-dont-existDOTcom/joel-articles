If something is dangerous, it should be called danger. Your little one might be wondering whether you noticed. Steering isn't about making things sound nicer than they are. Grief is grief, and harm and anger still need accurate words, the same as limits and consent. Accountability too, and what's possible in practice. Once all that's named, skip any picture of what someone shouldn't become, if nobody needs it. Put most of your energy into what can be protected or practiced. There might be something to repair, a choice to make, or something you can grow.
