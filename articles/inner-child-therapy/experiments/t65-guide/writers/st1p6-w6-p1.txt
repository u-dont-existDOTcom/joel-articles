Love moves in a loop. It goes from spiritual experience to your little one, on into the grown-up relationship, from you into other people, and back again. Maybe you're not sure which door you came in through. The loop matters more than the door. Community can work in both directions. Loving people can make you feel safe enough, and like you belong enough, for a deeper opening to happen. Or the opening happens first and changes who and what you can "manifest." Then the people around you help make it practical and real, and help you live it in your body instead of only thinking about it.
