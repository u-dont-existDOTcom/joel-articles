When something doesn't need saying in painful or negative terms, pick words that point toward what you want to strengthen: an ability or a relationship, or something you do. Your words steer your attention in this work. "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" usually gives your mind a clearer direction than bringing in a label like "dependency" just to deny it or argue with it. In inner-child work and hypnosis especially, make whatever's really needed easy to imagine. Warmth or protection, maybe. What would steadiness feel like, or rest?
