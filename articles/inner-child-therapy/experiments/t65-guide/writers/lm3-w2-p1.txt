Rumi says you have to keep breaking your heart until it stays open. Some people find that deep grieving gives way to warmth, or to feeling connected. Some feel held, in the body. That describes what they feel, and it doesn't prove how the body produces it. Being loved can open grief from the other side, too. Someone takes care of you, and you think, "Why does this make me sad?" It can be sadness for the care that was missing, and that doesn't mean the love failed. Stay with the grief while you're present and can still choose, and when it's swamping you instead of opening you, slow down.
