So what has actually ended? It can feel like everything did. And what are you still trying to keep going? What isn't known yet, and what's your little one afraid will happen in the gap? Maybe you could mark the ending with something practical, or a small ritual, without pretending you know what comes next. When the not-knowing feels unbearable, any new identity can seem better than none, but don't force one. The grown-up you can keep your routines, though they might feel pointless, and protect your body. You can grieve what ended, and stay with your little one while the next life takes shape.
