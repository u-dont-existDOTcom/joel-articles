Sometimes the ache of missing someone can itself become a cue for love. Don't push the missing away. It doesn't have to be one more problem to solve. If there's love in it, let "I miss you" become "I love you," or offer a simple wish: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." Picture them, or just feel the direction of your care. You don't have to believe they receive anything, since this is about what happens in you. It doesn't replace grieving, or reaching out. If there's a boundary to keep, you keep it, and the same goes for anything practical. If sending love makes you more compulsive or unreal, make it simpler: "I miss you, and I care." That one still has the missing in it. Do the same if you can't get back to your life. You can also offer some of that care to the part of you that's still here.
