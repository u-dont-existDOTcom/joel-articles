If you're about to act, or can't stop yourself, first get another human being involved, and put distance between you and whatever you'd use. Do that too if you're badly disoriented or intoxicated, or if it's right in front of you. A crisis line or an emergency number counts as another human being. It can be a stranger. If you're reading this and can't tell whether you're about to act, treat it as if you are. This won't answer whether life is worth living. It keeps the question open long enough to look into it.

Before getting philosophical, make sure there's an adult in the room. If the part that wants to die feels like all of you, notice who's doing the noticing (Noticing Counts) or borrow an adult, whichever's easier, and invite any part that can watch what's happening or protect your body. Maybe one can put off a decision you can't undo. Skip all that if you're already able to watch the suicidal state without being completely caught up in it. If no part of you can watch yet, the questions wait, and the person you got involved is the adult for now.
