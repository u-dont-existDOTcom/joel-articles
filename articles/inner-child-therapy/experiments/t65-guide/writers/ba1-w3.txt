Reparenting is usually talked about as something you do alone or with a therapist. But your little one was hurt in relationships, and that kind of hurt can only heal so far in isolation. Sometimes one person ends up carrying a lot of the borrowed grown-up jobs at once: food, planning, health decisions, rides, social life, reassurance, protection, company, or just the sense that somebody competent is beside you. Maybe you have someone like that. Having someone who can care for you when you're struggling can be an enormous blessing.

Over time, you can use reparenting to take in and practice some of what that person has shown you by example. Other jobs can be shared with friends and community, or with professionals or services. Routines and ordinary tools, too. Which of these jobs can become more yours? Which are healthiest shared? And which really belong inside that relationship? The aim is for your little one to feel reliable care in more than one way, while the relationship itself stays precious and important.
