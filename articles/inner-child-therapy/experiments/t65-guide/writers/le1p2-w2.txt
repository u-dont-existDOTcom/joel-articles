What has really ended, and what are you still trying to keep going? Sometimes it's the same thing. What practical act or small ritual would mark the ending without pretending you know what's next? And what isn't known yet? Ask your little one what it's afraid will happen in the gap. If not knowing feels unbearable, it's tempting to grab a new you. Don't force it, though. The grown-up you can keep your routines and protect your body. Grieving what ended is part of it, and so is staying with your little one while the next life takes shape.
