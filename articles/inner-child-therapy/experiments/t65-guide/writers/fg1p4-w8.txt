Forgiving yourself works the same way, and if that's hard, forgiving other people tends to be harder too. Own what you did, and only what was yours. Let the remorse land ("oh no, I really did that"). Repair what you can, with an apology or amends, or by changing what you do. After that you're allowed to stop holding yourself hostage, and that might feel like getting away with something. The responsibility stays, though. Some harms take years. Forgiving too soon is one trap, and living inside resentment for good is the other. So don't push it. Keep coming back to how they became capable of it, until the understanding is real enough to change how you feel.
