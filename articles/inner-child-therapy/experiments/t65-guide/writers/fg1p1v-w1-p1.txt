For a lot of people, healing eventually includes some forgiveness, or at least getting out from under active resentment. Maybe you can't imagine forgiving whoever hurt you. Forgiveness can't be put on a schedule anyway, and you don't need it first to be safe or set boundaries. You can hold them accountable and get justice without it. You can recover first, too. Actively resenting them can narrow what you see, though, and keep your little one's world revolving around the injury. If you're pressured to forgive early, you get an enlightened-looking performance, and the wound is still there underneath.
