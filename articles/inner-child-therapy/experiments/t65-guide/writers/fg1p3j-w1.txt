That doesn't make what they did harmless, and in theory there were other options. Maybe you've gone over those many times. But punishing them can't go back and give them the awareness or ability they didn't have enough of. Or the support. The useful thing is to repair what can be repaired and make it easier to respond differently now. They're still accountable, though, and the harm is real. Boundaries and legal consequences can still be right. So can judging what they did as wrong, and keeping your distance, up to cutting them off for good. You can understand them, hold them responsible, never speak to them again, and forgive them. In your head, "I understand how you became this way" can sit beside "I now understand why I shouldn't rely on you for this."
