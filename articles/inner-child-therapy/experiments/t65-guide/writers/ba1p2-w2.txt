Over time, reparenting can help you take in and practice some of what that person has shown you. Maybe you've already caught yourself saying "it'll be okay" the way they do. Other jobs can be shared with friends and community, or with professionals or services. Routines and simple tools can help too. The aim is for your little one to feel reliably cared for in more than one way, while the relationship itself stays precious and important. So which of their jobs can become more yours, and which are healthiest shared? Which really belong inside that relationship?
