Community can work in both directions. Sometimes loving people give you enough safety and belonging for a deeper opening to become possible. Maybe part of you is waiting for that. Other times the opening comes first and changes what kinds of people and relationships you can "manifest." Then community helps make the opening practical and real, and helps you live it in your body. Which door you came in through matters less than the loop: love moves from spiritual experience to your little one, from your little one into the grown-up relationship. From you into other people, and back again.
