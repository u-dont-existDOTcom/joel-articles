For lots of people, healing gets around to some forgiveness eventually, or at least to not living under the resentment anymore. You can't put it on a schedule. And you don't need it first to be safe (please don't wait for it), or to set boundaries. Making them answer for it can come first, and so can justice, and so can getting better. Resentment does shrink what you can see, though. It keeps your little one circling the injury. But if you push yourself to forgive before you're there, you get a nice show of being at peace, and the hurt just goes underground.
