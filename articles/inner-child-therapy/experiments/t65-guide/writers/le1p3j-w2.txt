Missing someone doesn't have to turn into another problem to solve. Sometimes the ache itself can become a cue for love, so don't push the missing away. If love is there, let "I miss you" become "I love you," or offer a simple wish: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." Picture them, or just feel the direction of the care. Maybe you don't believe they'd get any of it. You don't need to. This is about what happens in you, and it doesn't replace grieving, or contact with them. It won't set a boundary for you or handle the practical stuff, either. If sending love makes you more compulsive or unreal, make it simpler: "I miss you, and I care." Same goes if you can't get back to your life afterward. You can also offer some of the same care to the part of you that's still here.
