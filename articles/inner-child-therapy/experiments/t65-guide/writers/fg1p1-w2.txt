For many people, healing eventually includes some forgiveness, or at least getting out from under active resentment. You can't schedule forgiveness, though. If you're not there, you're not there. And you don't need it first to be safe or set boundaries. Holding someone accountable and getting justice can come first too, and so can recovering. Still, active resentment can narrow what you see and keep the injury at the center of your little one's world. Pressure to forgive early gets you an act that looks enlightened, while the wound goes underground.
