Give every session with your little one a definite ending. Look around and name where you are. Feel where your body touches something, like your feet on the floor or the chair, and let your jaw and hands relax. Name one normal thing you'll do next, and say something like "That's enough for today. We're returning to the life we're building." You might think, "But I'm not done." Going back to your life is part of the healing, too. The session doesn't have to resolve every feeling before you make food or go to sleep.
