This one's for when talking or picturing gets you only silence. Write with your wrong hand, the clumsy one, whose letters already look like a first grader's (or a ransom note). You're not making up lines for your little one here. Whatever scared or hurt feelings are actually in you go on the page, and one word is plenty. Your good hand writes back, as the grown-up who's got their back. And if the protective part shows up first, hand it the clumsy pen and let it finish.
