Forgiving yourself works the same way. When that's hard, forgiving other people tends to be harder too. Own what you did (only what was yours) and let the remorse register. Repair what you can with an apology or amends. Changing the behavior is also part of the repair. Then you're allowed to stop holding yourself hostage, which can feel like getting away with it. The responsibility stays, though. With other people, some harms take years to forgive. Forgiving too soon is one trap, and living permanently inside resentment is the other. Don't push it. Over time, keep coming back to how they became capable of what they did, until the understanding is real enough to change how you feel.
