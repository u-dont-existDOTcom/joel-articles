None of this means making everything sound positive. Grief is grief, and danger should be called danger. Harm and anger still need accurate words, and so do limits and consent. And who's accountable, and what's possible in practice. Imagine being scared of something real and getting a pep talk. Once it's all been named, put most of your energy into what can be protected or practiced. What can be repaired? What can you choose, or grow? If nobody needs a picture of what someone shouldn't become, leave it out.
