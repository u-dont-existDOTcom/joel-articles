Some people have the opposite problem: it feels like you're connecting with your little one, but the contact stays near the surface. Meditating for a long time and then sleeping, especially a second sleep, after you've already slept once, can open up much more emotionally direct material. The sign is the gap. If that night's dream has far more feeling and need than you got anywhere near in your daytime session, you might wake up thinking, "Where was all this?" That difference is information about the session (a good session that just didn't carry into the day is a different case). Ask what became available, and how you see it now. If repeated nightmares start wrecking your sleep, change the dose (earlier in the day, or shorter) before giving up on the whole thing.
