The three grown-up jobs work between people too. A good peer, someone you take turns listening to as equals, can lend you the Nurturer by being there and being warm. They can lend you the Protector by keeping what you say private and sticking to the agreed time. If you say no, they respect it. Maybe your little one is watching for that. They don't supply your Guide, though, or decide what your experience means. A carefully timed question can help your own Guide get heard. They don't interpret or give advice, and the answer is yours. Then you switch roles.
