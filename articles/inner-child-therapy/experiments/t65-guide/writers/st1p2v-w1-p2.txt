Grief is grief, though. Partway through all that steering, you might think, "But it really was dangerous," and if it was, it should be called dangerous. This isn't about being positive whether it's true or not. You still need accurate words for harm and anger, and for limits and consent. For accountability too, and practical constraints. After what's real has been named, leave out any unneeded picture of what your little one shouldn't become. Put most of your energy into what can be protected or repaired, practiced, chosen or grown.
