Or maybe you're just getting through the days right now. Sometimes care isn't what's missing: everything life seems to offer feels too small. A meal or a boundary can help, and so can a kind sentence, without touching the question underneath, "Why should I want this life at all?" It can feel strange to ask that when you're fed and safe. Take that question seriously anyway. What would have to be possible for being alive to feel worth discovering, and not just something to get through?
