Missing someone doesn't have to turn into one more problem to solve. Sometimes the ache itself can be a cue for love, so don't push it away. If love is there, let "I miss you" become "I love you," or a simple wish: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." Picture them, or just feel yourself sending it their way. Does any of it reach them? You don't have to believe it does, since this is about what happens in you. It doesn't replace grieving or getting in touch. You'd still keep your boundaries and do whatever practical things need doing. If sending love makes you more compulsive or unreal, make it simpler: "I miss you, and I care." Same if you can't get back to your life afterward. You can also offer some of the same care to the part of you that's still here.
