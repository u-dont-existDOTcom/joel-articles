A few questions for the gap. What has really ended here? What are you still trying to keep going? What don't you know yet? What's your little one scared will happen while you're in between? Then find some small way to mark the ending, a practical act or a little ritual, without pretending to know what's next. And try not to grab a new identity just because the not-knowing is unbearable. Your routines and your body still need you, your grief does too, and so does your little one.
