When you're done, give the session a real ending, or it can follow you around all day like a song stuck in your head. Look around and say where you are. Maybe feel your feet on the floor, or the chair under you, and let your jaw and hands unclench (they probably did some clenching). Then pick the next normal thing, like brushing your teeth for bed, and tell your little one, "That's enough for today. We're going back to the life we're building." You don't have to have every feeling sorted out first. Going back to your life is part of the healing, too.
