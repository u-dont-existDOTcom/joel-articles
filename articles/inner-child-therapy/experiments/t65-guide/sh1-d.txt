Some people have the opposite problem: it feels like they're connecting, and it's all happening near the surface. Meditating for hours and then sleeping (especially the second sleep, after you've already slept once) can bring up much more direct stuff. If the dream that night has way more feeling and need in it than the daytime session ever got to, take that gap as a sign the session stayed shallow, a sign and not a verdict. Ask what's available now, and what you make of it. And if nightmares start wrecking your sleep, change the dose, earlier in the day or shorter, before you give up on the whole thing.
