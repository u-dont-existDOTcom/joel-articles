You can see that momentum within this life, too. Rumination feeds rumination. Isolation can deepen despair, and the more you hate yourself, the better your mind gets at it. Meanwhile physical life keeps handing you obstacles and redirections: other people, the world you can touch, responsibilities, practice, help. If the many traditions, and the evidence some researchers point to, are right that the mind's causal process goes on after the body dies, killing the body may take away some of those redirections without taking away the suffering.
