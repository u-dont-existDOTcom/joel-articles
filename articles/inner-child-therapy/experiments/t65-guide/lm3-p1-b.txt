Rumi says to keep breaking your heart until it stays open, which sounds intense, and it can be. For some people, deep grieving turns into warmth or connection, or a sense in the body of being held by the whole universe. That's real as an experience, though it doesn't tell you what's going on physically. And being cared for can bring grief up, too, for the care you never got, which doesn't mean the love failed. Go only as deep as you can while staying present and able to choose. If it starts swamping you, slow down.
