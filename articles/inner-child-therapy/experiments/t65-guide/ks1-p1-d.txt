If you might act on it soon, stop reading for a second and get another person involved, right now. A crisis line or emergency number counts. Then put some distance between you and the means. That goes for anyone who's about to act or can't stop themselves, anyone badly disoriented or high or drunk, and anyone with the means right there. Not sure which you are? Treat it as if you're about to act. None of this answers whether life is worth living. It keeps the question open long enough to look at it.
