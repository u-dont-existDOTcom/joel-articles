A story ending doesn't turn the memory into fiction, and it doesn't prove it either. Nobody gets let off, and nobody's repair gets canceled. The case just stops being argued. Listen to how the narrator sounds, though, and stop if they start sneering ("...and so that's the way it happened, dummy"), or if the shame grows, or you start feeling unreal. More distance won't fix those. And if the phrase helps, keep it short and ask what the person in that story needs from you now.
