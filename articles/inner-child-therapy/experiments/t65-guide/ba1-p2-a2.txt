Over time, reparenting can help you take in some of what that person has been modeling for you, and practice it yourself. Other jobs can be shared around, with friends, community, professionals or services, routines and plain old tools. Which of those jobs can become more yours? Which are healthiest shared? Which belong inside the relationship? The point is for your little one to feel reliable care through more than one door, while the relationship itself stays as precious as it is.
