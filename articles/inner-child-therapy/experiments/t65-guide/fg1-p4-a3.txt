Forgiving yourself works the same way, and when it's hard, forgiving other people tends to be harder too. Own what you did and its impact, without taking on what wasn't yours. Let the remorse register and repair what you can. After that you're allowed to stop punishing yourself, and the responsibility stays yours. Some harms take years. Living permanently inside resentment is its own trap, so come back to the question of how they became capable of it now and then, until the understanding is real enough to change how you feel.
