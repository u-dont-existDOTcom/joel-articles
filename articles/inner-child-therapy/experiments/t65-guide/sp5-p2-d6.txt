This doesn't make the memory fiction or prove it right, and it doesn't let anyone off the hook. The trial ends; any repair that's owed is still owed, whoever owes it. Keep an ear on the narrator. If you hear "...and that's the way it happened, dummy," stop, and stop too if the shame grows or you start to feel unreal (telling it colder won't help). If the line works, keep it to one line, and ask what the person in that story needs from you now.
