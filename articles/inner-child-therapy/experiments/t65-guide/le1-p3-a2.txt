Missing someone doesn't have to become another problem to solve, either. Sometimes the ache itself can turn into a cue for love. If love is there, let "I miss you" become "I love you," or try a simple wish: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." You can picture them, or just feel the direction of the care. This is about what happens in you. It doesn't depend on them receiving anything, and it doesn't replace grieving, contact, boundaries or doing something practical. If sending love makes you more compulsive or unreal, or you can't get back to your life, make it simpler: "I miss you, and I care." And you can turn some of that same care toward the part of you that's still here.
