When a hard memory comes up and you can handle it, tell it to yourself like a story and finish with "And so that's the way it happened!" (If the start is the hard part, "Once upon a time…" gets you going.) It's a little corny, but the memory starts sounding like a chapter of your life, where before it sounded like a trial still in session, with you in the defendant's chair.

The story version is still true, and it's no proof you got every detail right. Listen to how the narrator sounds. A sneer means stop. So does more shame, or a feeling that you're not quite real, and stepping further back won't fix either one. If the line helps, leave it at one line (no epilogue needed).
