End each session on purpose, or it just keeps running in the background all day. Look up and say where you are ("okay, kitchen"). Feel your feet on the floor or the chair under you, and unclench your jaw and your hands (they're probably clenched). Pick the next normal thing, and bed counts, then say: "That's enough for today. We're going back to the life we're building." Going back to your day is part of the healing too, and whatever you're still feeling can come along.
