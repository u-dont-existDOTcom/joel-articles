And sometimes care isn't what's missing at all. The whole horizon of life just feels too small. A meal or a boundary or one kind sentence can help, but it doesn't touch the question underneath: "Why should I want this life at all?" That question deserves a real answer. What would have to be possible for being alive to feel worth exploring, not just enduring? Curiosity can be enough to start: other people say they've felt a kind of love or peace I never have, and I don't know if their explanation is right, but what if it's possible for me? What you've felt so far isn't the limit of what's possible.
