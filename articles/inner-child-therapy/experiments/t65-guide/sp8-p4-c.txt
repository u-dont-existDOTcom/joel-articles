Remember that somebody chose that moment and framed it. Whatever was happening outside the frame isn't in it, and neither is the reason you looked the way you did. A photo or a jolt in your body can say something real about how you feel today, the way a dream can, and still prove nothing about the story the photo seems to tell. So if it starts to look like something serious happened, don't confront or accuse anybody based on this alone. Write down exactly what came up and how, and talk it over with someone steady who won't lead you toward an answer.
