This one's for when you're steady, so that widening the circle won't pull you out of your own shoes. Pick the feeling and call it what it is. Lonely. Ashamed. Craving something. Then say: "Other people feel this too. Just like me, they want it to stop. Just like me, they sometimes make it worse trying to get out of it. May we all get enough support to stay with it, so the pain stops with us."

Breathe however you normally breathe, and put yourself first in that wish, then anyone else who comes to mind. This isn't about forgiving someone who hurt you, or picturing their childhood. And it's no reason to get in touch with them while you're flooded. The point is to stop carrying a feeling everybody has as proof that you're the broken one.
