Reparenting usually gets framed as something you do alone or with a therapist. But your little one got hurt in relationships, and a relational injury can only heal so far in isolation. Sometimes one person ends up carrying a lot of the borrowed-adult jobs at once: food, planning, health decisions, rides, social life, reassurance, protection, company, or just the sense that somebody competent is next to you. Having someone like that when you're struggling can be a huge blessing.

Over time, reparenting can help you take in some of what that person has been modeling for you, and practice it yourself. Other jobs can be shared around, with friends, community, professionals or services, routines and plain old tools. Which of these can become more yours? Which are healthiest shared? Which belong inside the relationship? The point is for your little one to feel reliable care through more than one door, while the relationship itself stays as precious as it is.
