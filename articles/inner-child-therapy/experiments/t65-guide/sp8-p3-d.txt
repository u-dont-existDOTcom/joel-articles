Some questions are better left alone: "What abuse must have happened?" "Who did this to me?" "What memory is my body hiding?" Those lead. So does staring at the same photo until a story finally turns up. Memory is suggestible like that. Guided imagery and suggestive questions can plant memories, and [in one experiment](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/BF03196318), half the people shown a doctored childhood photo of a balloon ride came to remember the ride, at least partly. They'd never been in a balloon.
