But if picturing someone kind only makes you feel more threatened or lonelier, go smaller: what's the least cruel thing a decent grown-up would do for you right now? Maybe it's not calling yourself an idiot tonight. Do that. Nobody's grading whether you were loved enough. It's scaffolding, and the warmth can grow in on its own time, if it does.
