One thing that tends to help is seeing the whole chain: what happened to them, what they learned, what they could and couldn't do by the time they got to you. From inside all that, the harmful move was the only one they had. "Hurt people hurt people" is a cliché because it's often true. And once you see it for yourself, forgiveness sometimes just arrives, uninvited, and the rage that made them the villain of the whole story softens into something more accurate.
