Sessions need an ending, or they turn into the whole day. When you're done, look around and name the room you're in. Notice your feet, or the chair. Unclench your jaw and your hands (they're usually clenched). Pick the next everyday thing, like a shower, and say: "That's enough for today. We're going back to the life we're building." Then go do it, with whatever's still unsettled coming along.
