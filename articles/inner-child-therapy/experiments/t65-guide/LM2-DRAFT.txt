Some people hear all this about love and say, "No. I hate myself." If that's what you find in there, I don't want to argue you out of it. It deserves an honest hearing. But I don't want to skip over something else, either. If you opened this article, or went to therapy, or asked someone for help, or tried again after you'd decided you were hopeless, something in you wanted things to be different.

Maybe it's hope. Maybe it's desperation, or curiosity, or fear, or just refusing to keep suffering like this. Maybe it's love. We don't have to decide which one it is right now. Ask that part, "What were you hoping might happen when you brought me here?" and follow that thread wherever it goes.
