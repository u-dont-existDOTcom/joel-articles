Sometimes care isn't what's missing. The whole horizon of life feels too small, and a meal or a boundary or one kind sentence can help without touching the question underneath: "Why should I want this life at all?" Take that question seriously. What would have to be possible for being alive to feel worth discovering, not just enduring? Curiosity can be enough here, something like "People say they've felt a love I never have. I don't know if I buy their explanation, but what if it's possible for me?" What you've felt so far isn't the limit of what's possible.
