Sessions need an ending, or they turn into the whole day. So when you're done, look around and name where you are, feel your feet or the chair, and let your jaw and hands go (they're usually clenched). Then pick what's next, like a walk or going to sleep, and say, "That's enough for today. We're going back to the life we're building." Your feelings don't have to be sorted out first. Getting back to your life is part of the healing.
