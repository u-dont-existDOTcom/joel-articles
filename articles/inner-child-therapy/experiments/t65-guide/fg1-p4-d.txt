Forgiving yourself runs the same way (and if you can't forgive yourself, forgiving them tends to get harder too). Own what you did and how it landed, and only that, not what was someone else's. Let the remorse register and fix what you can, and then you're allowed to stop punishing yourself, with the responsibility still yours. Some harms take years. Living in resentment for good is a trap too, so keep coming back to how they got capable of it, until that understanding changes how you feel.
