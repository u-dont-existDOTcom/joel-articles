Some people have the opposite problem: it feels like they're connecting, and the contact is staying near the surface. A long meditation and then sleep (especially a second sleep, after you've already slept once) can open up much more direct stuff. If that night's dream carries way more feeling and need than your daytime session got anywhere near, that gap is a sign the session stayed near the surface, a sign and not a verdict. Ask what became available, and how you see it now. And if nightmares start wrecking your sleep, change the dose before you give up on the whole thing. Earlier in the day, or a shorter session, might be enough.
