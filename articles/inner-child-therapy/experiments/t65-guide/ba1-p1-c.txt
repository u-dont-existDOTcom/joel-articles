Reparenting usually gets pictured as something you do alone, or with a therapist. But your little one got hurt in relationships, and a relational injury can only heal so far on your own. Sometimes one person ends up doing a lot of the grown-up jobs for you at once: groceries, planning, doctor stuff, rides, your social life, reassurance, protection, company, or just being the competent person next to you. If you have someone like that when you're struggling, that's a huge blessing.
