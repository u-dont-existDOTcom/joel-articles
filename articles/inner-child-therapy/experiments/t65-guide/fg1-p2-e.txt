Want to know what usually helps? Seeing how their history and their pain, and what they could and couldn't do by then, shaped the thing they did. Given everything they'd become by that moment, it was the only move they had. Hurt people often pass on pain they never processed. When that's something you see yourself, nobody needs to tell you to forgive. It can happen on its own, and the rage that made them the one guilty person softens into something truer.
