This one's for when talking or picturing gets you only silence. Write with your wrong hand, the clumsy one, whose letters already look like a first grader's (or a ransom note). Whatever scared or hurt feelings are really there go down on the page, nothing invented. Your good hand writes back as the grown-up who's got their back. Sometimes the protective part grabs the pen first, and that's allowed. It keeps the wrong hand until it's had its say.
