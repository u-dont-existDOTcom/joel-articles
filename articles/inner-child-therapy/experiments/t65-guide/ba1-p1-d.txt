Reparenting usually sounds like something you do alone, or in a therapist's office. But your little one got hurt in relationships, so a relational injury can only heal so far in isolation. Sometimes one person ends up doing a lot of the borrowed-grown-up jobs for you: cooking, planning, doctor appointments, rides, social plans, reassurance, protection, company, or just being the competent one in the room. If you've got someone like that while you're struggling, that's a real blessing.
