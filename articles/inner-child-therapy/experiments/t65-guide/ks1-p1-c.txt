First, before anything else: if you might act on it soon, get another human being involved right now, and put distance between yourself and the means. A crisis line or an emergency number counts as another human being. This is for you if you're about to act, if you can't stop yourself, if you're badly disoriented or intoxicated, or if the means are right in front of you. If you're not sure whether you're about to act, treat it as if you are. None of this is my answer to whether life is worth living. It keeps the question open long enough to look into it.
