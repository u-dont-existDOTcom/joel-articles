If losing everything is already on the table, what change is still too scary to try? Maybe a relationship. Maybe a job, or where you live, or who you've always been told you are. Those changes cost something, and some can't be undone, but they're smaller than dying, and you can make them later, calmer, with help. While you still have a body and people around you, look into those questions, and into the life you've been afraid to change.
