For a lot of people, healing eventually includes some kind of forgiveness, or at least getting out from under active resentment. It can't be put on a schedule, though, and you don't need it first to be safe, set boundaries, hold someone accountable or get better. Resentment can narrow what you see and keep your little one organized around the injury. But forcing yourself to forgive early tends to turn into an enlightened performance while the wound goes underground.
