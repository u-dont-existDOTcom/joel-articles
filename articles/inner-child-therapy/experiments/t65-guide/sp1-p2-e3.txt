That future you also knows why you can't pull this off perfectly today. What would they do for you, if they were here? Maybe they'd be kind about it, or maybe they'd just make you some food. If you can't get into their head at all, do the smallest honest thing you can anyway (a no, a pause) and don't fake the feeling. You can picture a wiser future version of the other person too, but it's only a picture. It doesn't take back what they keep doing, and it doesn't mean they'll agree with you or be sorry someday.
