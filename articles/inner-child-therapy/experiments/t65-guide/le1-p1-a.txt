Your little one often gets loudest in the gap after one life has ended and before the next one has formed. Think of a breakup, a move to another country, an illness, losing your faith or a role, a child leaving home, or leaving a family or community on different terms. Tibetan Buddhism calls an in-between state a bardo. You don't have to buy its teachings about death and rebirth to use the everyday part: endings leave a stretch with no familiar identity and nothing clear to replace it yet.
