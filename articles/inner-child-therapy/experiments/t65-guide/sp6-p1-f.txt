When you wake up, pick one hook to watch for. Something like "Today I'll notice one hook before I act on it" is plenty (it's kind of a big ask anyway). At night, look back at the day the way you'd look over a friend's shoulder at theirs, kindly. Where did you notice the hook, and where did it run its whole routine anyway? What's the next small repair, or the next thing that would protect your little one?
