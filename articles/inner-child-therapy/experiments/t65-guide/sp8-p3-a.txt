Some questions to skip: "What abuse must have happened?" "Who did this to me?" "What memory is my body hiding?" And don't keep questioning one photo until a story finally shows up. That's a real risk. Guided imagery and suggestive statements can create false memories, and [in one experiment](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/BF03196318), half the people shown a doctored childhood photo came to remember, at least partly, a hot-air balloon ride they never took.
