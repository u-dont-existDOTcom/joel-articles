And try not to ask it leading questions, like "What abuse must have happened?", "Who did this to me?" or "What memory is my body hiding?" Grilling one photo until a story shows up is a good way to get a story, but not necessarily a true one. Guided imagery and suggestive questions can create false memories. [In one study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/BF03196318), people shown a doctored photo of a childhood hot-air balloon ride they never took started remembering it, about half of them, at least partly.
