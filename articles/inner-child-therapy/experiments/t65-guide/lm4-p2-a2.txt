But a big spiritual experience can show you a kind of peace, or a love, you didn't know existed, and then "I want you to be happy" suddenly means something much bigger. That's partly why loving-kindness meditation can sound almost comically simple to someone who's never been near what it's pointing at, and so powerful to someone who has. Saying "may you be happy" over and over isn't automatically some profound love. It's one way of finding that deeper love and growing it.
