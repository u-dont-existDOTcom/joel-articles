That future you also knows why you can't pull this off perfectly today. What would they do for you, if they were here? Maybe they'd be kind about it, or maybe they'd just make you some food. If you can't get into their head at all, do the smallest honest thing you can anyway (a no, a pause), and skip faking the feeling. Picturing a wiser version of the other person is allowed too (it's only a picture, though). They may never agree with you or be sorry, and what they keep doing now is still what you go by.
