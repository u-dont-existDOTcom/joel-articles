Skip questions like "What abuse must have happened?", "Who did this to me?" or "What memory is my body hiding?" And if you catch yourself grilling the same photo until a story turns up, stop there. Suggestive questions and guided imagery can plant memories of things that never happened. [In one study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/BF03196318), researchers put people's childhood photos into a hot-air balloon ride that never happened, and half of them came to remember it, at least partly.
