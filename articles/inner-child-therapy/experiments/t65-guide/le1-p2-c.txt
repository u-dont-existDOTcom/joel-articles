So ask what has really ended, and what you're still trying to keep going. What isn't known yet? What is your little one scared will happen in the gap? ("Nobody's ever going to want me again" is a popular one.) And what small ritual, or practical thing, could mark the ending without pretending you know what's next? Don't grab a new identity just because not knowing feels unbearable. You can keep your routines, take care of your body, grieve what ended and stay with your little one while the next life takes shape.
