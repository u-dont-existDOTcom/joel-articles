When you're grounded enough to think about other people without losing track of yourself, name the feeling you've got, plainly, like "scared" or "grieving." Then try saying this, out loud if you can: "Other people feel this too. Just like me, they want it to stop. Just like me, they sometimes make it worse trying to get out of it. May we all get enough support to stay with it, and not pass the pain on."
