Rumi says you have to keep breaking your heart until it stays open. Some people find that deep grieving turns into warmth, or connection, or a feeling in the body of being held by the whole universe. That's a description of what they felt, though, not proof of how it works. And it can go the other way, too: being cared for can bring up sadness about the care you didn't get. That doesn't mean the love failed.
