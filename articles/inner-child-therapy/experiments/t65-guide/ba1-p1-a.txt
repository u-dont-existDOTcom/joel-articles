Reparenting usually gets framed as something you do alone or with a therapist. But your little one got hurt in relationships, and a relational injury can only heal so far in isolation. Sometimes one person ends up carrying a lot of the borrowed-adult jobs at once: food, planning, health decisions, rides, social life, reassurance, protection, company, or just the sense that somebody competent is next to you. Having someone like that when you're struggling can be a huge blessing.
