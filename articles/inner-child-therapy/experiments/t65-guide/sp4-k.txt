If talking or picturing just gets you crickets, try writing. Your little one gets your wrong hand, since its handwriting already looks like a six-year-old's, and the grown-up gets your good one. Let the wrong hand put down whatever scared or hurt stuff is really there (no making up lines for them), and then answer it as the grown-up, with love and a little protection. If a protective part grabs the pen first, that's fine. It gets the wrong hand until it's had its say.
