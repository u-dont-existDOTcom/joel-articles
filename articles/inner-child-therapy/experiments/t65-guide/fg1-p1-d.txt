Will you ever forgive them? Maybe. A lot of people get there, or at least stop living inside the resentment. But nobody gets to put it on a schedule, and you don't need it first to be safe, or set boundaries, or get better. Resentment narrows what you see and keeps your little one built around the injury. And forgiving too early tends to turn into a spiritual act you put on for an audience (often yourself) while the wound goes underground.
