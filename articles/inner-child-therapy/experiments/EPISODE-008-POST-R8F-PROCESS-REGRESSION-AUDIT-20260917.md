## What changed afterward

### Change 1 — local residual repair became broad subsection reconstruction

The successful loop repaired literal currently failing spans. Later Episode 008 work increasingly regenerated larger natural sections or subsections from abstract architecture/function inventories.

R12 `Don't Assume It's All You` was generated as a new four-paragraph subsection after calibration on Predictions A/C. There is no durable literal sentence/span repair table tied to R12.

This is not the validated Episode 007 method.

### Change 2 — the preservation ledger moved upstream into generation

In the successful loop, protected units constrained fidelity while the prose was generated from one local live job. Later generation visibly discharged many preservation functions in prose: one-event uncertainty, repeated behavior, motive uncertainty, revisability, promises versus behavior, guilt ambiguity, protection, action without understanding, and the observing/between-us/next-move functions.

That converted a fidelity ledger into an implicit prose outline. R12 preserved meaning cleanly but recreated comprehensive counselor architecture.

### Change 3 — exact local operations were replaced by higher-level prohibitions and architecture language

Episode 007 guides said things such as:
- delete this sentence as a separate takeaway;
- merge this fairness caveat into the live sentence;
- keep this concrete interaction;
- demote this secondary obstacle;
- stop before the known-Human consequence sentence.

R12 planning instead relied more on abstractions such as event-led opening, boundary continuity, no checklist/listicle, omit premature Witness labels, and combine Predictions A/C. Those are useful diagnoses but are not equivalent to a literal executable repair table.

### Change 4 — three predictions became part of the writing environment

Three actual-prose predictions are useful for owner calibration when they expose materially different assumptions. But after Joel noted A and C were similar, combining A and C did not constitute a materially different architecture. The prediction stage became additional generated prose/rationale without preserving the successful method fingerprint.

The successful Episode 007 endpoint did not depend on choosing among three whole-section realizations. It depended on progressively shrinking the red surface and repairing exact residuals.

### Change 5 — rule accumulation increased after each failure

After R10/R11, the workflow accumulated owner-supersession rules, anti-listicle rules, anti-repetition rules, prediction-format rules, planning/generation separation, preservation rebinding rules, Witness-routing rules, and increasingly elaborate cold-audit criteria.

Most individual rules are valid. The process problem is that they became the writer's environment rather than lightweight supervisor checks. This matches the standing project warning that repeated self-critique is correlated evidence and that accumulating anti-pattern rules can over-constrain generation.

### Change 6 — the R12 audit then misclassified method failure as strategy failure

The first R12 audit passed despite a visible paragraph-2 concern; the re-audit correctly rejected R12's architecture. The subsequent method audit then concluded that the strategy itself had failed and recommended a new architecture comparison.

That conclusion skipped a required discriminating question: **Was R12 a faithful reproduction of the last validated successful method?**

Answer: **No.** It lacked a durable literal repair table and used full-subsection reconstruction rather than local residual execution.

Therefore R12 is evidence against the R12 method variant, not evidence that the validated Episode 007 method is exhausted.

## Correct causal diagnosis

The recent regression is primarily **method drift**, not proof that the validated process stopped working.

- Source/authority: no failure.
- Preservation: semantically successful, but allowed to shape generation too much.
- Planning: drifted from literal local operations toward subsection architecture/prohibitions.
- Generation: drifted from local residual execution toward fresh comprehensive realization.
- Audit: failed to compare method identity against the known successful fingerprint before escalating strategy.
- Strategy conclusion: previous `replacement required` conclusion was premature.
