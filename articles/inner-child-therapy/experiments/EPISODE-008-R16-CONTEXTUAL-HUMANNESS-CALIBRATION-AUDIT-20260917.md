Therefore the proposition `the repository contains many historical Human/AI examples` is not equivalent to `the fresh writer has enough activated positive calibration to generate Human prose`.

## Causal interpretation

The strongest supported explanation is **contextual competence without sufficient durable distillation**.

The earlier writer functionally learned inside the active correction trajectory. That learning was available through its current context, not through a persistent model-weight update caused by the conversation. A later fresh chat starts from the base model plus whatever explicit artifacts are loaded.

The repository preserved many useful abstract lessons, detector findings, and historical pointers, but the positive generative teaching set is still sparse. In several places exact historical before/after bytes are `LEGACY_INDEX_ONLY` and therefore intentionally excluded from fresh few-shot retrieval until recovered.

This also explains the critic/generator asymmetry: abstract anti-pattern rules can make the model increasingly articulate about why prose is AI-shaped without supplying enough literal positive transformations for the generator to inhabit a different prose distribution.
