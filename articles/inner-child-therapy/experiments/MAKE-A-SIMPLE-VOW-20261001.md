## The plan: one article paragraph per guide paragraph

**The h2** is the guide's own heading, "Make a Simple Vow". It says what the section does.

| # | guide paragraph | plan |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | "When promises still carry some credibility, a quiet vow can help." Skip it if the present adult still intends to frighten, exploit, or harm; otherwise make it, with or without picturing the two of you on a swing | P1. The skip condition is a callback to When the Present-Day Adult Is Dangerous to the Child, which already says it all; a few words, worded so an ordinary reader doesn't start wondering whether they're the dangerous one (E93). The guide's "child-contact exercise" isn't a thing in the article, so it goes. Ends leading into the vow. |
| G2 | The vow: "I know you are in there. I love you.* You do not have to love me back or trust me yet. I cannot promise … I can promise … You do not have to believe these words. Watch what I do." | P2, a quote in the reader's voice to their little one. Every promise and non-promise stays. "You don't have to love me back" repeats Love Doesn't Wait P6 on purpose: the vow makes that answer a promise. No asterisk; P3 refers to the "I love you" in words. |
| G3 | Say "I love you" only when it's honest; otherwise a sincere wish for their wellbeing or a commitment not to be cruel, without pretending they feel the same | P3. A callback to A Smaller Doorway: Goodwill, which already offers "May you be loving, peaceful, and free" for when "I love you" goes flat. "Without pretending they feel the same" reads as: the stand-in isn't the same as love, and you don't pretend it is. The other reading (your little one doesn't feel the same) is the vow's own "you don't have to love me back". |
| G4 | Trust may still answer "Big whoop", and that's allowed. Choose one response small enough to repeat, practical or relational. Not Every Hero P4 already says to pick one act and its smallest first step; what's new here is "small enough to repeat". |
| G5 | You take the adult position again whenever the child appears. Trust grows from consistency, compassion, and action rather than one beautiful session | P5. **Cut** "rather than one beautiful session", which repeats G4's last sentence. |
