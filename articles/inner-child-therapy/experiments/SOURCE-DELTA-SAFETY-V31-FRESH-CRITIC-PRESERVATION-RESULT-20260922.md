> ## When the Present-Day Adult Is Dangerous to the Child
>
> Suppose the safety question is skipped and the child is brought forward anyway. The child recoils. The reaction tells you the child is frightened; it does not tell you the adult has become safe.

A frightening mind or history does not settle what a person intends now. Some of that work may need another person who can stay with the adult without colluding with the harm; the child remains completely outside it.
