## Exact candidate

### Write It. Don't Send It Yet.

Write the response you actually want to give—or just say it in your head. Don't send it yet.

Come back to it later as if a friend had handed it to you. There may be a line that's only trying to win the fight; cut that. But underneath the rant you may find a pretty ordinary apology, request, or no. Don't clean those away just because the first draft was ugly. The message that actually goes out can be shorter. It can wait. It can also be nothing. If you need time, say so. Only promise tomorrow if you really plan to come back tomorrow. And if somebody needs protecting now, forget the draft and protect them.

Then go back to the first draft and stop editing. What was the person who wrote it trying so hard to get somebody to understand? Did they need protection, reassurance, or actual help? The first version isn't automatically childish just because it was messy or angry. It may contain the most mature protest in the whole exchange. Care for the writer even if you decide not to send what they wrote.
