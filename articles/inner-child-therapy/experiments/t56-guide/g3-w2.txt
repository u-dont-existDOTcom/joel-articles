The opposite trap is forced adulthood: perfect discipline, no visible needs, and very little joy or closeness. If you're stuck there, part of you is probably already itching to tighten control again. But the next adult act may be saying what you'd prefer or asking for help, which might feel like cheating. Or it may be letting a safe person see something imperfect.

Judge the strategy as well as the need: wanting closeness is legitimate, but demanding instant replies or punishing someone for needing space isn't the same thing. The Guide helps you find a workable way to meet that need without erasing either of you: "I want them close, so what can I ask for that leaves them some room?"
