# Whole-article duplicate check: t56 guide material

Checked on 2026-10-15. Every point below was searched against the whole article as numbered in the brief (B0 to B232), not only the spot where its group would go.

**Totals:** 16 groups, 94 points: 24 SAID, 52 PARTLY, 18 NEW. No group has draft paragraphs yet, so there were no draft sentences to check. No group said the article already carries part of its passage.

What the marks mean:

- SAID: the article already makes the point, closely enough that a reader would feel told twice.
- PARTLY: some of it is there. The entry says what's there and what's missing.
- NEW: the article doesn't make the point.

Quotes are copied exactly from the brief: from the article when a block number comes first, otherwise from the guide passage.
