The opposite trap is forced adulthood: demanding perfect discipline of yourself and no visible needs, with very little joy or closeness. It's that inherited "don't be needy" rule, mistaken for the Guide, so tightening control feels grown-up. If you're stuck there, the next adult act may be asking for help or saying what you'd prefer instead. If that feels needy, it's the old rule talking. Your little one learned to hide their needs so they could keep belonging, and may not have stopped.

Once a need does show, judge the strategy as well as the need. Wanting closeness is legitimate. Demanding instant replies or punishing someone for needing space isn't the same thing. From the inside, that can feel like "I just want to be close." The Guide helps you find a workable way to meet the need without erasing you or the other person.
