The opposite trap is forced adulthood: demanding perfect discipline of yourself and no visible needs, with very little joy or closeness. Forced adulthood is the parent you inherited saying "don't be needy" while passing for the Guide, which is why tightening control again feels grown-up. If you're stuck there, the next adult act may instead be asking for help, or saying what you'd prefer. You might think "I'm being so needy" before you've said a word.

That's the old rule talking. Needs were among the things your little one learned to hide to keep belonging. Once a need does show, judge the strategy as well as the need. Wanting closeness is legitimate. Demanding instant replies isn't the same thing, and neither is punishing someone for needing space. When you're the one waiting, it can all blur together. The Guide helps you find a workable way to meet the need without erasing you or the other person.
