The opposite trap is forced adulthood: demanding perfect discipline of yourself and no visible needs, with very little joy or closeness. It's the old "don't be needy" rule passing for the Guide. Your little one learned to hide their needs so they could keep belonging. No wonder tightening control again feels like the grown-up thing, and asking for anything feels needy. If you're stuck there, the next adult act may be asking for help anyway, or saying what you'd prefer.

Once a need does show, judge the strategy as well as the need. Wanting closeness is legitimate. You might want a lot of it, after all that hiding. Demanding instant replies or punishing someone for needing space isn't the same thing. The Guide helps you find some workable way to meet the need without erasing you or the other person.
