## 2. Conflicts to watch

1. **Two job descriptions.** h1: "The Guide’s job is not to police every mental event." Installed (The Chicken-and-Egg Problem): "giving ourselves a mission as well. That's the Guide's job." Fit the thought-sorting into that job.
2. **Timing.** h1: "Build warmth and safety first. Let direction emerge once there is a coherent child to orient." Installed: "The Guide can be you too, just a calmer you." (Borrow One Competency at a Time); "You can usually reparent and build who you are at the same time." (From Survival to Experimental Play); "the warmth can come after" (Not Every Hero Wears A Cape). A strict order contradicts all three; the h1's risk is a child "spiritually overruled".
3. **Standards and difficulty.** h1: "holds standards, requires difficult things for growth" Installed: "The old parent in your head may demand immediate talent" (Let the Child Be Bad at Things); "I'd go shallower." (Before You Try to Go Deep). The Guide's standards can't sound like that parent.
4. **Escape.** h1: "escape from anything difficult can masquerade as self-love." Installed (When the Urge to Escape Arrives): "I'd thank whatever part of you came up with the escape plan, and slow down." Don't shame escape urges.
5. **Help and imperfection.** h1: "In that case the next adult act may be asking for help" Installed: "so try it before you call next time." (Become the Adult Apprentice); "Privacy isn't fakery." (Also Look Outward). Without "In that case", help-seeking and disclosure become general rules.
6. **Letting thoughts pass.** h1: "let the rest pass without turning it into a permanent verdict." Installed (Catch the Hook Before the Story Takes Over): "Transient feelings and thoughts can be meaningful signals." Don't slide into "throwing everything transient away".
7. **Leaving.** h1: "leave when staying requires disappearance." Installed (Four Pairs That Commonly Hook the Child): "temporary distance may be necessary. Some people come back later on different terms." Keep return possible; the disappearing image is already used there.
8. **Manipulators.** h1: "Some manipulative people are too smart to present themselves as perfect." Installed (Also Look Outward): "nor does it prove they're manipulating you." Keep the h1's "may itself be honest".
9. **The ultimate.** h1: "The Guide points toward a belonging that doesn’t require self-betrayal" Installed (My Journey): "Maybe ordinary life already gives you enough love and meaning. Great." The ultimate stays optional.
10. **Repeats to avoid presenting as new.** "Key adds the third adult function" (installed: "until Key pointed it out"); "A thought can appear without your endorsing it." (installed: "Everybody gets scary thoughts they'd rather not have sometimes"); "learn, repair, choose more skillfully" (installed: "What can you learn or repair now"); "Praise, mirroring, pity, attraction" (installed: "because of praise or pity, fear or attraction"). Installed calls the function only Guide; "Leader, Guide, or Guru" shouldn't rename it.

## 3. Easy to flip

- "Without direction, reparenting can become permissive." Keep "can".
- "can masquerade as self-love." Keep "can"; candy and screens aren't condemned outright.
- "In that case the next adult act may be asking for help" Keep the scope, "may", the "or" list, and "safe person".
- "Judge the strategy as well as the need." Keep "as well as".
- "without erasing either person" Keep both people.
- "not to police every mental event." Keep "every".
- "Shame about a chosen action can sometimes signal" Keep "can sometimes" and "merely for appearing".
- "You can love people without becoming the group" Keep "You can", both staying and leaving, and "requires disappearance".
- "or Director are all fine." Keep the permission; Guru isn't banned.
- "An external guru who demands surrender" Keep that scope; leaning on someone "can actually help for a while".
- "Some manipulative people" Keep "Some".
- "may itself be honest; it is also evidence" Keep both halves.
- "The Protector must notice" Keep the Protector here, and "must".
- "Slow the decision down and verify." Don't let it become cutting contact or trusting the feeling.
- "Don’t ban the joke" Keep it, with "consequential".
- "Most people need" Keep "Most".
- "Let direction emerge" Keep it a permission, with no schedule.
- "The realization may be completely genuine." Keep "may".
- "Don’t let mystery outrun love indefinitely." Keep "indefinitely".
