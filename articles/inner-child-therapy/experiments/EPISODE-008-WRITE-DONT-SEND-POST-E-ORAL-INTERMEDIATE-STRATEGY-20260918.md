# Episode 008 — Write It. Don't Send It Yet — strategy escalation after five AI-1.0 variants

Date: 2026-09-18
Status: **DIRECT ARTICLE-GENERATION FAMILY FLAT / SWITCH TO ORAL-FRIEND INTERMEDIATE**

## Measured evidence

Five materially different article-realization attempts have all returned Pangram AI 1.0:

- A: procedural caring-editor realization;
- B: broader socially located rewrite;
- C: explicit two-job architecture;
- D: source-grounded angry-reply situation;
- E: single concrete editing scene.

No Human island has appeared.

## Failed assumption

The active loop itself is working operationally, but the current generator surface is not.

All five candidates were generated directly as polished article prose. Even when the local architecture changed, the model remained in **guide-writing mode** and Pangram treated the complete boundary as AI.

Continuing to vary the article prose directly would now be materially similar optimization.

## Structurally different approach

Use an **oral-friend intermediate**.

The model first realizes the source as if answering one real person who says:

`I'm furious and I've written a message I want to send. What should I do with it?`

The intermediate is allowed to be spoken, uneven, reactive, and socially situated. It is not publication prose and is not Pangram-tested.

Then perform **minimum publication normalization**:
- remove only obvious conversation scaffolding that cannot live in the article;
- keep the thought route, unevenness, and social movement;
- preserve all source functions;
- do not reorganize the result back into a complete mini-manual.

## Why this is different

This changes the generative act before wording:
- direct article writer -> polished guide;
- oral friend response -> socially reactive advice;
- minimal normalization -> article.

The preservation ledger remains a supervisor check after the realization, not the oral answer's outline.

## Test threshold

One candidate under this architecture.

If it remains AI 1.0, do not keep polishing the same intermediate method. Compare another structurally different route.

Pangram GUI remains inside the loop.
