# Inner Child Therapy — source-delta safety humanization strategy switch

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: **ACTIVE METHOD SWITCH / SAME-STRUCTURE LOCAL REPAIR STOPPED**

## Trigger

Three fresh isolated prose audits of successive rewrites identified the same underlying structural failure despite local repair:

1. audience position shifted among self-reader, assessed person, narrator/evaluator, and helper;
2. identity / sovereignty / religion material arrived as an attached requirement cluster rather than a consequence of the live safety question;
3. the adult-focused inquiry accumulated as a questionnaire/checklist rather than changing the reader's understanding of what work remains possible.

This satisfies the method-escalation threshold. Another local wording pass on the same paragraph architecture is not authorized as evidence of progress.

## Failed causal premise

The prior approach assumed that the raw-source functions could be kept in essentially raw-source paragraph order and made natural by:
- clarifying antecedents;
- changing pronouns;
- smoothing the helper handoff;
- making individual questions less slogan-like.

Fresh audits showed that the problem was not primarily local wording. The **section-level audience and function topology** remained assembled from obligations.

## Structurally different approaches considered

### A. Explicit helper-centered section

Make the whole section primarily instructions to a helper/practitioner, with a short note that a self-reader can apply the same gate to themselves.

Advantages:
- eliminates audience oscillation;
- naturally houses the care-without-enabling and sustained-real-human-care functions.

Cost:
- the article is primarily a self-directed guide; making the entire section helper-first would over-route the section toward a secondary audience.

Disposition: **not selected**.

### B. Neutral adult-safety decision boundary

Keep the prose reader-neutral around one governing distinction:
- regulation/presence does not establish safety;
- current endorsed harmful/exploitative intent closes child work;
- while closed, work stays with present-day adult problems/agency;
- identity/sovereignty material is used only as one adult-agency example, not a new topic;
- beliefs/thoughts/past acts and verbal promises are evidence cautions around the same decision boundary;
- helper guidance appears only once, after the decision rule is clear;
- direct/indirect child contact remains closed until harmful intent changes;
- sustained real-human care remains available.

Advantages:
- keeps both self-readers and helpers inside the same decision logic without switching addressees every paragraph;
- makes the identity/religion material subordinate to current intent/behavior rather than a separate conceptual detour;
- preserves all B1-B10 units without using preservation-unit order as prose order.

Disposition: **SELECTED**.

## New mesoscale movement

Not a sentence plan:

`capacity != safety -> harmful intent closes child contact -> adult-level work remains -> evidence is current endorsement/behavior rather than labels or promises -> care may continue without opening indirect child routes`

The identity / sovereignty material is subordinate evidence inside `adult-level work remains`, not a new branch of the section.
