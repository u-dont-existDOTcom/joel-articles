## A pointer that needs no sort: what the guide doesn't define

Made with [undefined_parts.py](https://github.com/u-dont-existDOTcom/joel-articles/blob/main/tools/humanization/undefined_parts.py) on the 4 November text. 12 of its headings have sections under them and no text of their own: 7 of its 9 parts, one of its three roles (the Nurturer), and four groups inside the parts: "Rediscover Play and Your Own Preferences", "Differentiation: Keeping Your Own Ground", "Finding People and Building Trust", "Appendix".

A model asked what such a part is for can only read it off what sits under it now. The frame for runs 2 to 4 (FRAME-2) was written with the instruction not to do that, and still said:

- the Nurturer: "The Nurturer's work: giving the child love, warmth, comfort and goodwill, and growing the capacity for them when they do not yet come naturally. That includes borrowing love from other people or from a spiritual source, drawing the child in, and widening the sense of what happiness is possible. Any passage about generating, receiving or sustaining affection and joy toward oneself, or about what to do when love will not come, is this role's work."
- Discovering and Healing Old Patterns: "Goes below the surface once contact exists: seeing where inherited habits of distrust, shame, harshness or blame came from, and reworking painful memories and the inner critic with more understanding, forgiveness and healthier responses. Anything about the origin and repair of past hurts and learned patterns belongs here."

Those two lines give the old place to 8 of the 20 sections he moved: the five spiritual sections he put under the Guide ("Ask God for a Loan 🫶🏿", "Invite Your Little One In", "Betrayed by a Teacher", "Toward Divine Love", "The Joy In Sorrow") and the three on distrust, shame and forgiveness ("How To Let People In", "Shame Says “Unlovable”", "Forgiving, Not Forgetting"). His answer to question 114 overrode the second for shame and forgiveness (run 4); no run put a spiritual section under the Guide. In turn 132, with no frame and a rule that only said a spiritual practice goes under "the role that holds the spiritual side", GPT put three of the five there (its first outline with the rules).

**As a pointer to sections it is no better than chance:** 10 of his 20 moved sections sat directly under a heading with no text of its own, and so did 66 of all 148 (8.9 expected). Its use is the questions: one for each part or role the guide doesn't define ("what is it for, and what looks as if it belongs but doesn't?"), 12 here against 79 from the readers' doubt, and one answer can move a group at once, as his answer to question 114 moved shame and forgiveness together.
