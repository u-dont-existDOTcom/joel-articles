## Repeated concessive-balancing tic

The model repeatedly reconciles adjacent preservation obligations with compact dialectical packaging.

B11 examples:

- `One ugly conversation can be awful and still not establish emotional immaturity.`
- `Some of that guilt may belong to the harm you caused, but it still doesn't tell you how much of the rest of the conflict is yours.`
- `Maybe after all that they can repeat your point back accurately and still think your no is unfair.`

This is not merely a lexical `still` problem.

The structural habit is:
`A is true + B is also true -> compress both into one polished concession`.

That repeated balancing contributes directly to the lockstep feel because each sentence efficiently resolves one preservation tension before moving to the next.
