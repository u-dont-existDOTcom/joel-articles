Going back can still matter, since realizations are a real part of life. But returning to the same wound can also become a search for certainty in an inherently uncertain existence. Spending all day asking, "Did I process it properly? Did I grieve it enough?" is clearly not what we're after here. And the checking doesn't always stay in your head. A body scan can turn into one, or a confession, a date, a hookup, one more late-night search, if what you're really after is proof. That you're lovable, say, or that you're not a bad person. It might calm things down for a while, and then the next little wobble matters even more.
