Sometimes you go in to say hi and can't find your way back out. 😵‍💫 Or going in is the very thing making it worse. That's when I'd come all the way out first, like climbing out of the deep end of the pool. Look at the actual room you're in. Text a friend, or lock the door if that's what keeps you safe. You can try again another day with just that eyes-open hello. Some days one breath of it is plenty. This isn't an extreme sport!
