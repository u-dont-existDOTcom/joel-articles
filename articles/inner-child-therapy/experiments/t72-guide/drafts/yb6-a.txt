So what can you change? Shorter is the obvious one, or not so deep, with less imagery. Timing counts too: right after a fight with your boss isn't great. 🙃 Some people do better when a stuffed animal stands in for their little one, or a drawing, instead of picturing them straight on. Others keep their eyes open and hold onto something real, like a cup of tea. And it's always allowed to be less alone with it, whether that means calling someone after, or doing it while somebody's home.
