Say you close your eyes to check in, and next thing you know you're back in the bad years and can't find the way out. Or every time you go in, it gets worse instead of better. That's your cue to come all the way back. Open your eyes, find the light switch, count the chairs if you have to. 😅 Call a friend, or lock the door if what scared you is real. You can go back in another day, and maybe just to wave from the doorway. Some days a single breath is plenty.
