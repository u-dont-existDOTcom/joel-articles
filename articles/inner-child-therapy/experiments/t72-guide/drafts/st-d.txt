So what's different? Mostly emphasis, and it varies a lot from one therapist to the next. I'd rather keep it to one grown-up with three jobs than a whole cast of modes, and I'd start by borrowing just one of those jobs if that's all you can manage. Locking the door or leaving a bad situation counts as reparenting from day one, too. And I wouldn't treat a picture that pops up, or a feeling in your belly, as evidence about your childhood. A good schema therapist may well agree. It's worth asking them.
