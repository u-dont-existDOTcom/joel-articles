Of the clinical approaches I know, schema therapy is the closest to this guide. Its therapists do what they call [limited reparenting](https://schematherapysociety.org/Limited-Reparenting): within professional limits, they give you some of what a good parent would, with warmth and firmness and some playfulness too. Over time you take that in, and it becomes your own "Healthy Adult," who looks after your "Vulnerable Child" and stands up to the punishing parent in your head. That sounds a lot like the Nurturer and Protector to me, and like borrowed adulthood, since the therapist's version becomes yours bit by bit.
