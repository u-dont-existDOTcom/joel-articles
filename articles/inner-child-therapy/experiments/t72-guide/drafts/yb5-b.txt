So how were the next few days? How did you sleep, and could you get through your day okay? Do you want to go back toward what came up, or is some part of you saying no? (And was it hard but doable, or just too much? A lot of the time you just know.) If you honestly can't tell yet, I wouldn't rush to call it healing or harm. I'd just make the next one a bit easier, one change at a time, so you learn what made the difference. Like what? Shorter, maybe, or not as deep, with less imagery. Or at a better time, since right before bed might not be it. 😅 A teddy bear or a doodle can stand in for your little one if picturing them is a lot. Eyes open helps some people, or holding onto something real, like a warm mug. And doing it alone isn't required either. More support around you is a change too.
