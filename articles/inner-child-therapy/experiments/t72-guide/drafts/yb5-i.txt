So how were the next few days? How did you sleep, and could you get through your day okay? Do you want to go back toward what came up, or is some part of you saying no? (And was it hard but doable, or just too much? A lot of the time you just know.) If you honestly can't tell yet, I wouldn't rush to call it healing or harm. I'd just make the next one a bit easier, one change at a time, so you learn what made the difference. Shorter, shallower, a better time of day, eyes open, a teddy bear standing in for your little one, a friend in the next room, whatever helps.
