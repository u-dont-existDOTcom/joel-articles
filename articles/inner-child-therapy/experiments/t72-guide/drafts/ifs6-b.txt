So which kind of therapist? I'd learn a bit about the good and bad of both IFS and reparenting first. A grounded, trauma-informed IFS therapist who doesn't put ideas in your head can really help, especially one who gets dissociation. If it's your little one's pain you're dealing with, though, or shame, a good reparenting therapist may be the better fit. Same for attachment wounds. And if you keep abandoning yourself, or there's no inner adult you can count on yet, that's a reparenting therapist's thing too.
