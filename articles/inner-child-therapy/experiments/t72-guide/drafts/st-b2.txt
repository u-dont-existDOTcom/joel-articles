The differences are mostly a matter of emphasis, and a lot depends on the therapist. Here it's one grown-up with three jobs, and borrowing just one of them is a fine place to start. Keeping yourself safe right now counts as reparenting from day one, and an image or a body sensation isn't proof of what happened back then. So with a schema therapist, it's worth asking how they'd handle those, and what happens if imagery isn't for you.
