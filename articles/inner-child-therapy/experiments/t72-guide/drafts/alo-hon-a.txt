And reaching out doesn't mean telling strangers everything. Privacy isn't fakery. People can earn more of you by how they handle the smaller stuff, like whether that embarrassing story from middle school ends up in the group chat 🙈, and how they take it when you say no. But if a relationship is getting deep and you're still keeping up a fake normal, happy version of yourself to hold onto it, I'd either bring more of the truth in, carefully, or let it be less deep for now. (If telling could put you in real danger, like violence, or being outed where that isn't safe, protect yourself first.) And when someone finally accepts you, it can feel amazing. It still doesn't prove you're meant for each other, or that they've earned your trust yet.
