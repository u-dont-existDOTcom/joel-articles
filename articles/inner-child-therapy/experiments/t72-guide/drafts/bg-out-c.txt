And what if you can't come back out once you're in there? Or going inward is the very thing making it worse? Then come out first, all the way, the way you'd turn the lights on when a movie gets too scary. Look at the actual room you're in. Text a friend, or lock the door if that's what keeps you safe. The deeper stuff can wait for a day when you can do that eyes-open hello first. Some days one breath of it is plenty. 😅
