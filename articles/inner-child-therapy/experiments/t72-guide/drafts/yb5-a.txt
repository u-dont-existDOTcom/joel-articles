So how were the next few days? How did you sleep, and could you get through your day okay? Do you want to go back toward what came up, or is some part of you saying no? (And was it hard but doable, or just too much? A lot of the time you just know.) If you honestly can't tell yet, I wouldn't rush to call it healing or harm. I'd just make the next one a bit easier, one change at a time, so you learn what made the difference. Like what? It could be shorter, or not as deep, with less imagery. Or at a better time, since right before bed might not be it. 😅 A teddy bear or a doodle can stand in for your little one if picturing them is a lot. You can keep your eyes open, or hold onto something real, like a warm mug. And you don't have to do it alone. More support around you is a change too.
