Think of it more like a volume knob than an on/off switch. You can turn it down plenty of ways (shorter, shallower, fewer pictures in your head, eyes open with your feet on the floor, a better time of day, a friend nearby, or whatever else helps). And if looking straight at your little one is a lot, let a doodle or a stuffed animal stand in for them. Your little one is probably a pro at pretend anyway!
