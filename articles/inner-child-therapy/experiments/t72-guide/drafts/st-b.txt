The differences are mostly a matter of emphasis, and a lot depends on the therapist. Here it's one grown-up with three jobs, and you can start by borrowing just one of them. Keeping yourself safe right now counts as reparenting from day one, and an image or a body sensation doesn't get treated as proof of what happened back then. If you see a schema therapist, I'd ask how they handle those, and what happens if you'd rather skip the imagery.
