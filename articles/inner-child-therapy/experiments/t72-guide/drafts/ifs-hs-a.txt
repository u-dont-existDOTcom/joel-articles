Is a perfect Higher Self already waiting in there to be your guide or parent? I'd call that claim [spiritual bypassing](https://goenka.u-dont-exist.com/). I've had the Higher Self perspective myself, though. It's a somewhat less subjective way of seeing everything as functioning perfectly, ego illusions and all, which matches what people say about it. Where I part ways with IFS is the wisdom part. I don't think the Higher Self has any specific wisdom. It's a vantage point, where more wisdom and compassion can come up, but problems can also look like they were never problems at all from up there, and that can leave you with less of both. You can see both in the guru types who supposedly embody their Higher Self, often in the same person. [Anthony de Mello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_de_Mello_(Jesuit_priest)), the Jesuit priest who mixed Christian, Hindu, Buddhist and Sufi teachings, taught people to see through the ego's traps and come back to the present, and he was a psychotherapist too. He seemed to drift between those modes in a way that contradicted itself.
