Before choosing a therapist, get to know the strengths and pitfalls of IFS and reparenting. An IFS therapist can help if they're grounded and trauma-informed and don't put ideas in your head. Even better if they understand dissociation. For your little one's pain, or for shame, a good reparenting therapist may fit better. The same goes for attachment wounds or abandoning yourself, or not having an inner adult you can count on.
