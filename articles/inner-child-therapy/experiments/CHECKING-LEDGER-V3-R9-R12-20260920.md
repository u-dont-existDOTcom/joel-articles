## Ledger v3

The unit of judgment is not just the sentence. Score both **local realization** and **dependency topology**.

### Strong AI tells

**A1 — semantic ledger visibility**
A reader can map most sentences directly to one protected source function in the same order as the preservation ledger.

**A2 — local closure density**
A sentence explains, labels or concludes the implication of the immediately preceding sentence rather than advancing/complicating the live thought.

**A3 — symmetrical branching**
Useful/unhelpful, true/false, maybe-A/maybe-B or concession/caveat branches receive matched packaging mainly because both must be preserved.

**A4 — instructional staircase**
Condition -> instruction -> reason -> next condition -> next instruction, even when sentence lengths vary.

**A5 — synthetic voice**
First-person stance, hesitation, self-correction, colloquial reaction, repetition, fragment or aside was added mainly to create a Human-looking surface and is not demanded by the source reasoning.

### Strong Human-facing tells

Only count H when it is source-earned, not decorative.

**H1 — unequal dependency**
One source concern genuinely consumes multiple clauses/sentences while another remains subordinate or compressed.

**H2 — unresolved dependency**
A real source ambiguity survives into a later sentence/paragraph without an explanatory wrap.

**H3 — consequential interruption**
A source-priority change genuinely interrupts the current line of thought (for example, a real-world safety issue outranking further introspection).

**H4 — authorial judgment with consequence**
The prose owns a judgment that changes what happens next; it is not a neutral summary with `I think` attached.

**H5 — earned recurrence/metaphor**
A source-grounded repeated question/image does several jobs across the passage without being translated into conceptual aftercare.

### E

Ordinary syntax, questions, fragments, imperatives, colloquial words, sentence length, conjunctions etc. that could plausibly occur in either Human or model prose.

### Gate

A realization is detector-eligible only when:
- fidelity PASS;
- A1 = 0 at the natural-boundary level;
- A4 = 0;
- no A5 dependency;
- no repairable A2/A3 dominating a paragraph;
- >=2 H signals that are source-earned;
- paragraph roles are genuinely unequal rather than equal mini-lessons of different word counts.
