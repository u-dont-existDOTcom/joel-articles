Date: 2026-09-20
Status: **DIRECT OWNER CORRECTION / ACTIVE HUMANIZATION RULE**

Joel correction:

> i'm not sure what the context is, you always need to give me the surrounding context for me to write well

## Rule

Whenever asking Joel to rewrite, repair, judge, or reauthor a paragraph for humanization learning, provide the **actual surrounding paragraph chain first**.

Minimum owner packet:
- nearest heading;
- preceding paragraph(s) needed to understand what functions are already covered;
- exact target paragraph;
- following paragraph(s) needed to understand what functions are about to be covered;
- provenance/lock status where it matters;
- explicit note about whether the target appears to duplicate neighboring functions.

Do not ask Joel to write from an isolated semantic summary when paragraph placement determines:
- what can be left implicit;
- what would be repetitive;
- what bridge is actually needed;
- where the thought should stop;
- whether the target paragraph should exist at all.

## Why this matters

The 2026-09-20 conflict rewrite request omitted surrounding context. Once the real chain was inspected, the target was visibly adjacent to:
- a direct instruction to repair genuine harm;
- a following owner-grounded section about owning poor communication without hiding the underlying upset;
- additional surrounding material on boundaries, realistic expectations, and response management.

Therefore the owner was being asked to regenerate functions already partly handled by neighboring paragraphs.

This contaminates teaching evidence:
a rewrite produced without necessary context may reflect duplicated prompt obligations rather than Joel's natural article-level thought selection.

## Training/evaluation consequence

If owner context was insufficient:
- preserve the rewrite as owner prose;
- classify it as **context-limited owner reauthoring**;
- do not promote its structure into a general generation lesson yet;
- re-present the target with full surrounding context before deriving paragraph-level omission/retention lessons.

## Standing delivery rule

For any future owner comparison or rewrite request, exact before/current + proposed material + relevant surrounding context must all be visible in the same response.
