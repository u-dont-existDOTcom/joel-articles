Retained-route alternative: follow the author's associative progression and keep each complication beside its first mention. Benefit: conversational exploration and fewer pointers. Cost: several long detours before repeatable practice.

This is a reading route, not a requirement to finish self-work before reaching toward people or to perform every exercise.
