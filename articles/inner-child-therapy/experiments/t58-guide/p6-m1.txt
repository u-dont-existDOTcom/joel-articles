Some problems have a next step you can take today, and some just can't move yet. Say the car needs a part that's on back order. Waiting for it isn't you being lazy or avoiding anything, so it's fine to let that one sit. Just don't tape the old part back on and pretend it's fixed. Remember what you're waiting on and what would get things moving again (the shop calling to say it's in), and spend your energy on something else that can move in the meantime.
