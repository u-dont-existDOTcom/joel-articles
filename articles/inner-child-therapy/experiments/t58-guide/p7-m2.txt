And when the car's stuck and the rent's coming up in the same week, the stuck one doesn't get to stop the other. I'd pick whichever helps most with what you're going for and can move today, unless something else really can't wait. If it all feels like one big tangle, try saying it in one sentence ("Car's stuck, but the rent can't wait") and pick one thing to do next. You don't have to work anything else out first, unless the answer would change what that thing is.
