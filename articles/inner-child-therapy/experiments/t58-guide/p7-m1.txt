When a few things go wrong at once, one can be stuck while another still has a next step. So which one gets you today? I'd go with whatever helps most with what you're going for and can move now, unless something else is due tomorrow. If it's all one big scramble, say what's going on in one sentence and pick one thing to do next. That's plenty. Only stop to work something out if the answer would change what that one thing is.
