If you've got a few things going wrong at once, you don't have to solve them in order. The stuck one can sit while you deal with the one that can move. I'd go with whatever matters most for what you're trying to do and can happen today, unless something's due. And if it's all a jumble, one sentence about it and one next step is enough to get going. Anything else can wait, unless knowing it would change that step.
