Some weeks it's the car and a sick cat at the same time, and your head's going round and round. The car can wait for its part, but the cat can't, so the cat gets today. That's usually how I'd pick: whatever matters most to you and can move now. And if you're too scrambled to pick at all, say it in one sentence ("Car's stuck, cat first") and do just the next thing it tells you. Leave every other question for later, unless the answer would change that.
