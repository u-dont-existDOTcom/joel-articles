Then there are the problems you can't move yet, however much you want to. Maybe the new job can't start until the background check clears. Your little one might hear the old parent in your head calling that lazy. How would you even be lazy at a background check? Waiting on something that's out of your hands isn't laziness, and it isn't avoiding anything. The Guide keeps track of what you're waiting for and what would open things up again, without pretending the check doesn't matter. Meanwhile, there's usually something else you can do.
