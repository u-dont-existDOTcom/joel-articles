One awful conversation can take up a ridiculous amount of space in your head. You remember every stupid sentence, replay the worst part, and pretty soon it can feel as if you've finally figured out the whole relationship. I wouldn't trust that feeling too quickly. [Lindsay Gibson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VlNpgFWOLPw) keeps the focus on repeated relational behavior. Before turning one awful exchange into a verdict on who somebody is, I’d want to see what I keep running into with them the next few times we talk.
