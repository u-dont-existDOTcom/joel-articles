My father often suggested folks remember a similar-level (or even harder) challenge that they were able to handle. Maybe you survived a car crash and surgery a few years ago, but now you're having trouble with emotional eating. You can draw strength from the past. As my sister, a historian, likes to say, ["The past didn't go anywhere."](https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/april-rosenblum-the-past-didn-t-go-anywhere) So who or what got you through it, and is one piece of that still within reach? The memory doesn't need to be a dramatic one, as long as it really happened. Just don't turn it into "I survived a car crash, so why can't I stop eating when I'm upset?" Maybe you're more worn out now, or missing whatever helped back then. No example? No problem, there are other doorways.
