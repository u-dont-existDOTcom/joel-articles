Ever tried telling yourself "I'm not dependent"? Notice where your mind went. Words point your attention, and "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" points it somewhere you'd want to go. So unless the painful thing itself needs naming, talk toward what you'd like to grow, like being able to do something, or a relationship. With your little one, and in hypnosis especially, make that easy to picture: warmth, protection, steadiness, rest, whatever's really needed.
