If somebody tells you "you're not dependent," guess where your mind goes? Right to "dependent." Words point your attention, whether you mean them to or not, so unless the painful thing is what needs naming, it's usually better to talk about what you want more of, like being able to do something, or a relationship. "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" gives your mind a much clearer place to go. With your little one, and in hypnosis especially, make that easy to imagine, like warmth, or rest, whatever is really needed.
