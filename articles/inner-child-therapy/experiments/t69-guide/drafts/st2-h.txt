I'm not talking about forced positivity here, like slapping a smiley face on a funeral. Grief is grief, and danger should be called danger. Harm and anger, someone's no, consent, who's accountable, practical limits and all that still need their real names too. But once reality's been named, most of the energy can go into what you can protect or practice, repair, choose, or just let grow, whatever fits, instead of painting some big unnecessary picture of what you shouldn't become.
