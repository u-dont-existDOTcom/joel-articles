Words point your attention. Tell yourself "I'm not dependent" and you're still thinking about being dependent (funny how that works), so unless the painful thing really needs naming, talk toward what you want to grow instead, like a skill or a relationship. "Let's find you more ways to feel looked after" is a direction your mind can take. With your little one, and in hypnosis especially, make that direction easy to picture: a warm kitchen, a door that locks, a nap, whatever's needed.
