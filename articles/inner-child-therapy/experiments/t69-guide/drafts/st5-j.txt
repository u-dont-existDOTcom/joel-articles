Say your partner in this is really good at staying calm while you fall apart. They're not going to be your parent forever, and they shouldn't be. They show you what that calm looks like and you practice it with them there. After a while it's more yours than theirs, which is borrowed adulthood at its healthiest. It's also worth naming afterward what you got from them, and doing one piece of it yourself. Therapy still has its place for what therapy does. A peer and a community do something different, and a serious healing path can include both of them.
