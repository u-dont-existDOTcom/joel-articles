So is the person you trade listening with supposed to turn into your new parent? They shouldn't, and that's borrowed adulthood at its healthiest: they show you how something's done, you get to try it with them there, and then they hand it back. Afterward it helps to name what you got and do one piece of it yourself. That doesn't make therapy useless, by the way, it's still good for what therapy does. Peers and community just do something different, and a serious healing path can have both.
