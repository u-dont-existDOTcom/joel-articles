If you keep telling yourself "I'm not dependent," your mind still lands on "dependent," since whatever you put into words is where your attention goes. So unless the painful thing itself needs naming, talk about what you'd like more of, like being able to ask for help, or a relationship you're growing. Something like "Let's find you more ways to feel looked after" gives your mind somewhere to go. With your little one, and in hypnosis especially, make it something they can picture (a warm kitchen, a door that locks, whatever they really need).
