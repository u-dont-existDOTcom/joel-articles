The words you use in this kind of work direct attention. So when the painful or negative thing isn't itself what needs naming, it's better to use words that point toward what you want to strengthen, whether that's a relationship or something you want to be able to do. "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" usually gives the mind a clearer direction than bringing up a label like "dependency" just to deny it or argue with it (your mind hears "dependency" either way). With your little one, and in hypnosis especially, make the direction easy to imagine: warmth, protection, choice, steadiness, connection, honesty, courage, rest, or whatever is really needed.
