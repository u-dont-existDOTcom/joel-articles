This can be borrowed adulthood in its healthiest form. The peer doesn't become your permanent parent (that's kind of the point). They show you something they can do and help you join in, and then they hand it back. Afterward, name what you got from them, and do one piece of it for yourself. Therapy is still useful for what therapy does. Community and disciplined peer support just do something different, and a serious healing path can include both.
