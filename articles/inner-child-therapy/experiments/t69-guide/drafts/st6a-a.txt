Community can go either way. Sometimes it's the people first: they love you until you feel safe, and like you belong, and then something deeper can open. Other times the opening comes first. You find a love or a meaning you couldn't have imagined before, and it changes what kind of people and relationships you "manifest" in your life. Then community is what brings it down to earth, so you live it in your body and it isn't just a nice memory.
