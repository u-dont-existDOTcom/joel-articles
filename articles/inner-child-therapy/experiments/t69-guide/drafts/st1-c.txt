The words you use point your attention somewhere, so it's usually better to talk about what you want more of, like being able to do something, or a relationship. Unless the painful thing is what needs naming, of course, then name it. If you tell yourself "I'm not dependent," guess where your mind goes? Right to "dependent." Something like "Let's help you build more ways to feel cared for and supported" gives it a much clearer direction. With your little one, and in hypnosis especially, make that direction easy to imagine (warmth, protection, rest, or whatever is really needed).
