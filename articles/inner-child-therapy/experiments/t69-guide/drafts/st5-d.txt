This is borrowed adulthood in its healthiest form, I'd say. The peer isn't trying to become your permanent parent. They show you how it's done, you do it together for a while, and then it's yours, kind of like a driving instructor who has zero interest in chauffeuring you around for the rest of your life. Therapy's still great for the things therapy is good at, too. Peers and community just do something different, and there's no reason a serious healing path can't have both.
