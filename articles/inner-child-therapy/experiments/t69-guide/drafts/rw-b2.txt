My father often suggested folks remember a similar-level (or even harder) challenge that they were able to handle. Maybe you survived a car crash and surgery a few years ago, but now you're having trouble with emotional eating. You can draw strength from the past. As my sister, a historian, likes to say, ["The past didn't go anywhere."](https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/april-rosenblum-the-past-didn-t-go-anywhere) What was helping you then? If one piece of that is still within reach, use it. It doesn't have to be a dramatic example, as long as it's real. It's also no proof you should manage the same way today, when you might be more worn out or missing whatever helped you then. (No example? Try another way in.)
