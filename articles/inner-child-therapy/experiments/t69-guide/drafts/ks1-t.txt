Maybe you're reading this about to act, or with the means close by. Then before anything else, get another human being involved, and put some distance between you and the means. A crisis line or an emergency number counts, and the person on the other end can be a total stranger. Do the same if you can't stop yourself, or you're badly disoriented or intoxicated. Can't tell if that's you? Treat it like it is. Getting help and some distance isn't me answering whether life is worth living. It just keeps the question open long enough to look at.
