Ever notice how "I'm not dependent" keeps you thinking about being dependent? Words point your attention, whether you want them to or not. So unless the hard thing itself needs naming, talk about what you want more of, like being able to ask for help, or a friendship you're growing. "Let's find you more ways to feel looked after" goes somewhere. With your little one, and in hypnosis especially, make it something you can picture, like a warm kitchen or a door that locks, whatever they really need.
