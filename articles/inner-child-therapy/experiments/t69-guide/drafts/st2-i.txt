Now, this isn't forced positivity, slapping a smiley face on a funeral or whatever. Grief is grief. Danger should be called danger, and so should harm and anger, or someone's no (or the lack of a yes). Accountability and practical limits still need accurate words too. But once reality's been named, most of the energy goes into what can be protected or practiced, repaired, chosen or grown, whatever fits, rather than painting some big unnecessary picture of what someone shouldn't turn into.
