[Hearthwork](https://hearthwork.u-dont-exist.com), my peer-counseling framework, begins: Equal time. Attention without interpretation, advice, or fixing. You speak. I listen. Then we switch. Presence, Warmth, Discipline, Confidentiality. So it's a third kind of relationship, next to ordinary friendship and therapy. Your little one may need one like that, and modern life rarely hands it to you.
