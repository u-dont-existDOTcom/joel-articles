The basic premise of [Hearthwork](https://hearthwork.u-dont-exist.com), my peer-counseling framework, is: Equal time. Attention without interpretation, advice, or fixing. You speak. I listen. Then we switch. Presence, Warmth, Discipline, Confidentiality. It's kind of a third type of relationship, alongside ordinary friendship and therapy, and one your little one might really need, because modern life rarely gives you anything like it.
