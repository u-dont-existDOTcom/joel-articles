[Hearthwork](https://hearthwork.u-dont-exist.com), my peer-counseling framework, begins: Equal time. Attention without interpretation, advice, or fixing. You speak. I listen. Then we switch. Presence, Warmth, Discipline, Confidentiality. That makes it a third kind of relationship, next to friendship and therapy, and maybe one your little one needs. Modern life doesn't offer many of those.
