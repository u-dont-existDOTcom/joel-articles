I try not to prosletyze about spirituality, because I've made that mistake before, and I now see that it's better to let folks find it when they are ready. But when someone tells me they want to kill themself, I consider the topic to be brutally opened by default, regardless of whether they meant to do that, because if they actually do kill their body (which they might mistake to be their self), that's a very powerful spiritual challenge right there, even if they didn't realize it would be.
