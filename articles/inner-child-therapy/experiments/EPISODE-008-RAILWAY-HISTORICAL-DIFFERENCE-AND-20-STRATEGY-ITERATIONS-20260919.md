## Concise diagnosis

The September 19 failure was not simply “Railway got worse.”

The known historical difference is that September 15 gave Railway a **thought-shaped source inside a full natural boundary**: one fixed Human paragraph posed the chicken-and-egg problem, and one coherent source paragraph moved causally from receiving an adult function to gradually doing part of it oneself. Railway had room to realize that as a whole second paragraph.

September 19 gave Railway a **coverage-shaped source**: do not hide the upset; avoid regrettable delivery; ask for time if needed; preserve an immediate-response/protection exception. Those remain four separate jobs whether represented as bullets or as one prose paragraph. Railway allocated approximately one sentence to each job and produced the same compact requirement march across projects.

The interaction comparison also contained an unrecognized confound. A Railway **fresh thread is not necessarily a clean writer context**. Fresh threads loaded and automatically updated project-level working memory. In Mission Control, a fresh thread reproduced a pre-existing “Version 8” prose architecture that was absent from the immediate prompt. In a newly created empty Railway project, the same minimal continuation prompt first reverted to an aphorism, a menu, invented timing, and a final instruction. After corrections were stored in that project, the identical minimal prompt improved in a later fresh thread.

That project-memory finding is strong evidence that the September 19 “fresh thread” controls were not context-free. It does **not** prove what happened on September 15, because the exact September 15 Railway prompt, project, thread ID, fresh/resumed status, and project working memory were not preserved.

## Twenty-call result

Exactly 20 Railway **writing** calls were run. Two later working-memory cleanup calls are excluded from that count.

The first six calls reproduced the historical controls in the existing Human Design and Mission Control projects. Call 6 exposed the project-memory confound. Calls 7–20 ran in a new no-service Railway project, `inner-child-humanization-exp`, created only for this experiment.

The useful progression was:

1. Stop feeding the next paragraph as a list of meanings to cover.
2. Let the preceding Human prose supply the live question.
3. Ask for spoken thought while the speaker is still finding the words, rather than polished article logic.
4. In a resumed thread, correct only the defect that actually appeared.
5. Verify transfer in a fresh thread on a different source.
6. Treat project-level Railway memory as part of the generation condition, not invisible plumbing.

This is an empirical condition set, not yet a validated prompt architecture.
