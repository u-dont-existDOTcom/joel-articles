## Specific AI-shaped lines

### `The original fight doesn't magically become okay. It just looks different.`

This is more conversational than R17 but may be **simulated looseness**. The middle fragment has almost no independent content; its main function is to break the otherwise efficient cadence. It therefore risks looking like a model deliberately adding a human-sounding hesitation.

Semantically faithful, but its placement is suspiciously convenient: the boundary function arrives immediately after disagreement because the source ledger requires both. It reads more like preserved-function coverage than like the social situation naturally producing that thought.
