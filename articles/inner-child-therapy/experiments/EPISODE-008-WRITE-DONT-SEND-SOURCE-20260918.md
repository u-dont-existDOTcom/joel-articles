## Active source functions

From the frozen Episode 008 source ledger:

### Caring-editor/private-draft practice
- draft the response privately, or say it internally;
- do not send immediately;
- edit while staying on the writer's side and still being fair;
- preserve valid protest;
- preserve responsibility that is actually the reader's;
- preserve a real request or boundary;
- "better" is not automatically softer/nicer;
- possible outcomes include send / say less / pause / no response;
- if promising a later fuller response, the promise should be real/keepable;
- do not delay necessary protection merely to finish the exercise.

### Return care to the writer
- return attention to the person/state that produced the first draft;
- ask what they were feeling / trying to get understood;
- identify needs such as protection, reassurance, or concrete help where supported;
- first draft is not automatically childish because it is angry/messy;
- it may contain mature protest;
- edit the communication without rejecting its author;
- a good outward response and inward care are separate jobs.

## Literal model realization available for transformation

From preservation-clean Episode 008 R5:

```markdown
### Write It. Don't Send It Yet.

Write the response you actually want to give—or just say it in your head. Don't send it yet.

Now read it as somebody who is on the writer's side and still wants to be fair. Fix what is unfair or confusing, but don't erase a real protest, request, or boundary just because it came out angry. If some responsibility really is yours, keep that too. Better doesn't necessarily mean nicer. Maybe the final answer is shorter. Maybe you wait, say less, or don't respond. “I need some time. I'll give you a fuller response tomorrow” is fine if you can actually keep that promise. If protection is needed now, don't postpone it to finish the exercise.

Then go back to the first draft and stop editing. What was the person who wrote it trying so hard to get somebody to understand? Did they need protection, reassurance, or actual help? The first version isn't automatically childish just because it was messy or angry. It may contain the most mature protest in the whole exchange. Care for the writer even if you decide not to send what they wrote.
```
