## Exact internal V22

> ## When the Present-Day Adult Is Dangerous to the Child
>
> One difficult case is a person who can look calm, oriented, and perfectly capable of making choices while still wanting to exploit vulnerability. In that situation, regulation is not the problem. The adult position itself is unsafe. Bringing the child forward “just to see” would make the child's fear part of the experiment, and in the worst case part of the reward.
>
> That does not mean danger can be read off from whatever sounds disturbing. An intrusive thought, an unusual religious belief, or something harmful in a person's past may matter, but none tells you by itself what the person intends now. The harder distinction is between having disturbing material and actually endorsing harm. A similar problem appears with claims of sovereignty. If someone says they are sovereign and also says they literally cannot refuse an urge, doctrine, group, spirit, or master, the contradiction is practical before it is philosophical: there may not yet be enough freedom to choose against the harmful position. That gives the adult work somewhere to go without involving the child. Help can continue around the person's choices and the life those choices are producing, without requiring agreement with their beliefs and without helping them become better at manipulation.
>
> If the person later says the harmful intent is gone, there is still no reason to use the child as proof. Whether the change is real will show up in what happens over time, especially when exploiting someone vulnerable would still be rewarding. Until there is something real to trust, the child stays out of the work altogether; for some people, sustained human care will matter more than another internal exercise.
