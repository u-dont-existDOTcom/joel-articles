Date: 2026-09-17

## Why this is a different architecture

R16 and R17 asked an autonomous fresh writer to realize the complete subsection from an enumerated preservation set. Even with richer examples and the explicit cadence correction, R17 still produced one-function-per-sentence marching prose.

The replacement architecture does **not** ask for another whole-subsection realization. It starts from social situations that can carry multiple protected functions at once, keeps Joel's exact owner paragraph fixed, and composes locally under supervision.

## Fixed article authority

Keep the exact owner paragraph byte-for-byte:

> You may not know for certain whether the other person is or could be capable of a healthier response, and neither do they. If they promise to change, that's probably as useful as their prior promises were. Think about how long it has taken you to become more healthy. Could you have done that from one moment to the next based on a promise? Sometimes we can be a better support for someone when we maintain a realistic boundary, so that we can hold high expectations, and still feel prepared for their failure to meet them.

Do not rewrite it for detector reasons.

## Social-situation seeds

These are **thought routes**, not frozen prose. They are grounded only in already-authorized subsection meaning.

### Seed A — one disastrous conversation versus a pattern

Reader-recognition route:
- Have you ever had a conversation go badly, then had the other person come back later when they were calmer and actually deal with what you said?
- That is different from a recurring situation where the thing you brought up keeps disappearing, regardless of how the individual argument started.
- Lindsay Gibson belongs here as support for looking at repeated relational behavior rather than using one ugly exchange as a diagnosis.

Protected functions potentially carried together:
- one event is weak evidence;
- time/disagreement/their own boundary may matter;
- repeated behavior matters;
- Gibson attribution/link.

### Seed B — repair one real wrong without surrendering the entire disagreement

Reader-recognition route:
- Have you ever realized you really did owe an apology for one thing you said or did, then noticed yourself quietly giving up the whole original issue because the other person was still upset?
- In this branch the harm is real and repair is actually owed.
- The reader's guilt is still not a measuring instrument that tells them how much of the conflict is theirs, and it does not prove manipulation either.

Protected functions potentially carried together:
- real harm and real repair obligation;
- self-abandonment under another person's disappointment;
- guilt proves neither total fault nor manipulation.

### Seed C — the conversation never reaches mutual understanding

Reader-recognition route:
- Have you ever explained the same point in several ways and realized that the conversation had quietly changed from `say what matters` into `get them to agree that I am right before I can stop`?
- The other person may still think the no is unfair or the boundary is wrong.
- The unresolved disagreement can remain unresolved while the reader decides what to do.

Protected functions potentially carried together:
- mutual understanding may fail;
- conveying the point can count;
- saying no can count;
- ending repetitive self-defense can count;
- action need not wait for agreement.

## Composition order

1. Draft one social-situation unit at a time from Seeds A, B, and C.
2. Do not require every unit to end in a lesson sentence.
3. Keep the exact owner paragraph as its own anchor and decide placement by reader flow after local units exist.
4. Ban fresh clipped standalone `Great.`, `Fine.`, and `Good.`
5. After each unit, audit for predictable marching advancement. If every sentence has one crisp advancing job, re-realize the unit before assembly.
6. Assemble the natural subsection only after the local units are useful on their own and connect without a function-ledger staircase.
7. Run full preservation proof. The ledger is fidelity evidence, not the prose outline.
8. Run whole-boundary cadence audit.
9. Joel gives the first decisive style judgment on the assembled model prose.
10. Pangram only when the prose is not obviously model-shaped and preservation has zero unexplained substantive deltas.
