## What changed from the successful Episode 007 campaign

### 1. The campaign narrowed without reliable model-green evidence

Episode 007 stayed broad through R7. It narrowed only after external localization showed stable Human/high islands.

Episode 008 narrowed much earlier:
- B11 owner feedback correctly froze Joel's exact paragraph;
- then Red1 was split from Red2/Red3;
- then F2 paragraph 1 was provisionally held;
- then F3/F4 repaired paragraph 2 around that hold.

The F2 hold had no owner endorsement, detector localization, or independent validation. It became a pseudo-green and caused premature residual-mode optimization.

### 2. The critic became more capable while the generator accumulated constraints

Current generation is now surrounded by:
- marching warnings;
- concessive-balance warnings;
- mimetic-repetition warnings;
- vague-`thing` warnings;
- synthetic-specificity warnings;
- preference-wrapper warnings;
- preservation-ledger constraints;
- local stop conditions;
- increasingly specific residual prompts.

Many of these are valid diagnostics.

Together, however, they became the writing environment. Fresh writers often produced prose that complied locally while retaining the same engineered global cadence.

This matches the project-level warning that repeated self-critique becomes correlated evidence and that adding anti-pattern rules can over-constrain generation.

### 3. Preservation machinery leaked into generation architecture

F1 is the clearest test:
- prior failed prose was hidden;
- the writer was genuinely fresh;
- the packet exposed six preservation functions;
- the output mapped those functions almost one sentence each.

Hiding the ledger in F2 improved the first paragraph, which supports the diagnosis that explicit preservation obligations were becoming the outline.

The supervisor then over-narrowed around that partial improvement instead of restoring broad generation.

### 4. External evidence stopped driving scope

Episode 007:
`broad candidate -> owner/detector localization -> freeze proven good islands -> narrow`.

Current Episode 008:
`candidate -> same-context audit -> local rewrite -> stricter same-context audit -> smaller rewrite -> fresh packet -> local hold -> smaller rewrite`.

The second sequence lets the critic determine scope mostly from its own correlated judgments.

### 5. The current subsection is a harder generative shape

Episode 007 `My Journey` contained rich owner-grounded material:
- autobiography;
- concrete lived scenes;
- strong personal judgments;
- asymmetrical paragraph purposes;
- natural time movement.

The current outward-looking material is mostly general relational guidance with several fairness/qualification obligations.

That source shape gives the model fewer naturally uneven thought routes and makes `balanced counselor mini-essay` prose more likely.

This is a contributing factor, not an excuse for the process drift.

## What did NOT change

The current failure comes from phase/routing drift:
- broad mode ended too early;
- provisional improvement was mistaken for reliable green evidence;
- verification constraints became generation constraints;
- same-context criticism drove too many successive local edits.
