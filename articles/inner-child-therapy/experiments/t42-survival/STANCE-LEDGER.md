## Easy to flip

- "Recovery includes more than uncovering a fully formed 'true self'": not "isn't about uncovering" (the little one is "still inside us, even if hiding").
- "the performed self gets better at surviving while the less-practiced self remains blurry" and "not a new division": don't rename them fake and true and shed the fake one.
- "Possible signs": not a diagnosis. A guessed answer to "What does the child feel?" isn't proof there's no child ("it felt like I was making it up at first", My Journey).
- "change dramatically with the people around you": keep "with the people around you", or any change of mind becomes a symptom.
- Reflection and nature alone: not a condition for reaching out to people (Also Look Outward).
- "tentative words … revise": keep both, or what surfaces becomes a verdict.
- "temporary, intentional distance": an option for some people, not advice to step back from family or church.
- "can usually proceed together" and "the first phase": identity isn't a prerequisite for reparenting; the conditions include safety, which is Protector work.
- The harmful-identity rule: keep "firmly held" and "current"; don't soften "do not bring the child forward" into "I'd hold off".
