When the Adult Voice Feels Fake
—
Minimal state-transfer revision · 2026-10-03 candidate · r0200

For some people, the adult voice can stay fake in ordinary consciousness and become real only in an altered state. The child may suddenly feel unmistakably present; love, grief, innocence, or protection that sounded like therapy language can become emotionally obvious. If that happens, don’t throw it away afterward just because your sober mind cannot reproduce the feeling. Also don’t turn the intensity into proof that every image or memory is literal. The next task is transfer: can any of what became real remain available in ordinary life?

You do not have to make inner-child therapy fully work sober before altered-state work can help. Learn the map sober instead: know the child, guards, Nurturer, Protector, Guide, safety gates, and how you will close the session. That way, if the child or previously inaccessible material suddenly becomes real, you already have some idea how to meet it. The gate matters. Altered states can get past the guards before the adult functions are ready to hold what appears. Build whatever sober Nurturer and Protector capacity you can, or borrow those capacities from grounded support that does not seize authority over the experience. Preparation does not require forcing the child to feel real in advance; it gives whatever opens somewhere to land.

Minimal state-transfer revision · 2026-10-03 candidate · r0411

During the altered state, don’t keep interrupting a useful private process with courtroom-level skepticism. If the child suddenly feels real, or a memory, body sequence, image, or realization carries enormous meaning, stay with the therapeutic task while you remain oriented and able to stop. Historical certainty is a separate question when you are going to accuse, confront, report, publish, or otherwise act externally on what emerged. Felt certainty can matter therapeutically without by itself settling external fact.

Minimal state-transfer revision · 2026-10-03 candidate · r0412

Afterward, notice where the access actually is. Maybe it vanished with the state. Maybe it carried over and is fading. Maybe some remains sober, or the relationship is becoming genuinely available in ordinary life. The goal is not to reproduce the altered state. It is to carry some of what became real into sober love, protection, grief, practice, or action.

Minimal state-transfer revision · 2026-10-03 candidate · r0413

Before another altered-state session, ask what you are going back for. Is there genuinely new material to explore, or are you mainly trying to remember, re-believe, re-feel, or recover access to something you already found? Repetition by itself does not mean avoidance, dependence, or failed integration. But when the main purpose is restoring access, the transfer problem deserves attention before another session becomes the automatic answer.

Minimal state-transfer revision · 2026-10-03 candidate · r0415

If the child cannot receive the love—or can receive it only in the altered state—don’t automatically answer by escalating the intensity or trying to force another breakthrough. Meet whatever is preventing the transfer. And if another altered-state session really is opening new material, start from what you already learned rather than resetting the therapy to zero.
