## Preservation units (gate step 2)

h2 body:
- **BL-U1** (S1): the capacity for love can be present (others, animals) but inaccessible or unsafe when the recipient is you. Must remain here; may reword.
- **BL-U2** (S2): think of someone you naturally love (pet, baby, friend, your child, partner); feel the actual love first without turning it into self-love; let the body remember it; then see whether a little moves toward the younger self.
- **BL-U3** (S3): don't force the transfer; knowing the capacity exists somewhere in you is already useful; you're borrowing a familiar pathway, not making a feeling on command.
- **BL-U4** (S4): how the care is offered matters (words, imagery, presence, action). "I care about you" can carry patience or a demand to hurry up. If closeness feels intrusive, change it (more distance, no touch, another voice, another source). Trouble with this offer doesn't show an inability to receive love. Let the response teach you how to care, instead of making the child accept your version.
- **BL-U5** (S5): it needn't depend on a lucky biography. If nobody works as a proxy, imagine someone whose intention is simply to take good care of you (ideal parent, wise aunt, decent therapist, fictional character, future self, an adult you invent). They needn't advise you or make you feel loving back. Feeling accompanied can be enough.
