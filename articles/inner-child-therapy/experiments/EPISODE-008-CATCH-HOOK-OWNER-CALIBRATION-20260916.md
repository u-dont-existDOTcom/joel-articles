## Purpose

## Starting Prediction C — owner reported AI/high

```text
You might notice the hook before you have any clue what part of you is doing the hooking. Or you might notice it embarrassingly late. The jaw is already tight, the stomach is already involved, half the text is written—and then you remember you're supposed to be observing yourself. Maybe forget the detective work for a minute. Child, protector, old wound... you can figure that out later. Sometimes the hand is already going toward a drink, or you've vanished into scrolling, or you're halfway through deciding what the entire relationship means. Pema Chödrön calls this tightening and urge before the story has finished forming *shenpa*.

When you catch it, look around and say you're hooked. If nobody is in danger, take a breath or three before you finish the thing you were doing. You do not need those breaths to settle the case. Maybe there's enough adult around to be nice to the kid for a second. Or enough Protector to stop something stupid. Or maybe all you've got is “not yet,” which is still more useful than sending the message from inside the reaction.

Leave it there for ten minutes. The text, the drink, the promise, whatever giant conclusion suddenly seems obvious. Later, ask the boring question: what actually happened? Somebody may really have crossed a boundary and still have hit something old in you. “My stomach tightened” is what happened inside. “They're evil” is already what you think it means. You can work out the rest when you're less busy being the reaction.
```

## What the ladder teaches

- Detector behavior is interactional. A Human/medium first two paragraphs became AI/high when the old third paragraph was restored.
- Removing the explicit `Later, ask the ... question` bridge helped. The bridge was not just wording; it restored the predictable `pause -> later analysis -> conclusion` route.
- A locally Human first paragraph did not protect the larger boundary. When combined with the still-model-shaped second paragraph, the boundary regressed.
- The owner’s second-paragraph change changes the **social act** of the paragraph: noticing the hook becomes something momentarily appreciated (`free honesty`, `awesome I could notice it`) rather than a neutral procedural instruction.
- The final owner paragraph changes the mode again: it becomes an explicit authorial disagreement with a New Age frame, rather than neutral counselor exposition. This is stronger than a pure same-thought style tweak and should not be misclassified as clean detector-only realization evidence.
- Emoji, `and/or`, `being/not doing`, and other individual tokens are not isolated causes. Do not promote them to phrase rules.

## Alignment classification

- Probes 1–5 are useful controlled owner-edit evidence with largely stable thought/function.
- The **final Human/high owner version is local owner authority and should be kept exactly**, but its explicit `depart from the new age wisdom...` framing adds an authorial stance not present in the model Prediction C. For retrieval/training purposes classify the final endpoint as **OWNER_REAUTHORING / owner-final realization**, not a clean `ALIGNED_SAME_THOUGHT` pair.
- The cleanest lesson for transfer is therefore the sequence of interactional minimal repairs, not copying the final owner wording into unrelated prose.
