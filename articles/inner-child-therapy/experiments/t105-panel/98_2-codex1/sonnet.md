AI:
1. "🤗" - A hugging-face emoji dropped between sentences is the chatbot warmth tic, and it sits oddly in otherwise plain instructional prose.
2. "We've all had days like that." - A detail-free solidarity line that exists only to normalize the reader's feelings, the reflexive "you're not alone" reassurance typical of supportive-assistant text.
3. "You don't heal at a steady pace, and standing up for yourself might make you feel worse at first." - A "healing isn't linear" platitude in a tidy two-part sentence with a safety hedge ("might", "at first"), the cautious can't-be-wrong therapeutic register models default to.
4. "maybe you didn't know how or why" and "You might have been too worn out to do it" - A softly hedged menu of blame-free explanations that covers every base so no reader feels judged, the exhaustive, gentle enumeration typical of AI empathy.
5. "look at what actually happened" and "you can learn from that" - Stock coaching reframes that turn a setback into neutral "learning" without a single concrete example.

WHOLE:
The rhythm is a run of short, even, plain declaratives, each cushioned by a hedge or a comfort ("might", "maybe", "right away", "at first"), which gives a flat, staccato, reassurance-by-formula cadence. It is built like a template (instruction, then consolation, then a menu of kind possible reasons) in a generic, non-blaming therapist-chatbot voice with no concrete moment, example or personal stake, and the casual touches (contractions, the emoji) feel applied on top of a smooth surface rather than growing out of it.

HUMAN:
- "Not doing it is harder to figure out." - Blunt, plain-spoken and a little unpolished, with a vague "it" that a model would normally spell out.
- "or whoever said they'd help couldn't make it" - A loose, conversational, concrete real-life detail rather than a tidy abstraction.
- "Fear is the first guess" - Slightly off-idiom, unpolished phrasing; a model would more likely say something like "the obvious explanation".
