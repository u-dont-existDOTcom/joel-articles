## Exact Unit A candidate

One bad fight isn't enough to decide somebody is emotionally immature. Sometimes they come back later and actually talk about what you were trying to say, even if they still disagree with you or keep the boundary you hated. What gets my attention is when the original thing keeps disappearing—the kind of repeated relational pattern [Lindsay Gibson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VlNpgFWOLPw) writes about in her work on emotional immaturity. You bring something up, the conversation wanders off somewhere else, and afterward you're left thinking, wait, did we ever even talk about that?
