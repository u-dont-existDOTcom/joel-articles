## Direct owner correction after reviewing calibration examples

Joel identified a basic AI-shaped gestalt that the prior system had repeatedly decomposed into narrower labels and then lost during generation:

**predictable marching advancement**.

The problem is not simply brevity, polish, explanation, advice, or a familiar outline. The problem is sustained **maximum semantic efficiency** across consecutive sentences:

- every sentence has an obvious job;
- every job moves the argument one clean notch forward;
- almost nothing lingers, doubles back, reacts, wanders socially, or occupies space for a reason other than conceptual advancement;
- the prose feels like an instruction manual or compressed editorial plan;
- sentence after sentence is optimized to get the idea across with minimum prose.

Joel's exact clarification: occasional maximum efficiency is good. **Do not repair this by merely expanding the prose.** The issue is the repeated distribution across a passage, which becomes harsh and robotic.

## Positive generation target

Do not deliberately make prose inefficient. Do not pad, ramble, add fake asides, invent anecdotes, insert unnecessary repetition, or manufacture errors.

Instead, avoid compressing away the natural cognitive and social route by which the thought is arriving.

Depending on the actual source, a human paragraph may spend an extra beat with a reaction, allow an example to carry meaning before naming it, shift socially toward the reader, qualify or reconsider something without immediately packaging the lesson, or let two sentences share a thought that a model would compress into one elegant conclusion.

The target is **variable thought duration and natural cognitive/social movement**, not randomness or verbosity.

## Whole-boundary admission check

After drafting, privately reduce every model-written sentence to a short description of its job.

If the resulting sequence looks like a perfect staircase of functions — each sentence does one crisp thing and the next advances immediately — reject or revise the passage even if every sentence looks individually reasonable.

Then inspect the literal prose. Do not fix the problem by adding filler. Ask whether the source supports a less optimized route, a different stopping point, more time inside the actual social/lived situation, or a sentence whose job is not merely to advance the argument.
