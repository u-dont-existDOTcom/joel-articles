## Exact internal V32

> ## When the Present-Day Adult Is Dangerous to the Child
>
> Suppose the safety question gets skipped and the child comes forward anyway. The child recoils. If the adult gets something out of frightening or humiliating someone vulnerable, the exercise has just given them more of it. The child's fear tells you the child is scared. It doesn't tell you the adult is safe.
>
> Now take somebody who is horrified by an intrusive thought they don't want and have no wish to act on. They may sound more alarming than the first person, but the thought itself is not the same thing as wanting harm. A strange belief or something ugly in their past can matter too; neither tells you by itself what they want now. With the intrusive thought, the person is already in conflict with the harm. With the dangerous adult, there may be no conflict yet: the harm is wanted. If that changes, it should show up in the adult's choices over time, especially when hurting or exploiting somebody would still be tempting. There is no reason to bring the child back to prove it.
>
> That kind of change can take time and another person staying with the adult side of it without helping the harmful part along. The child stays out of all of it.
