And I’m certainly not preaching “prosperity philosophy,” even though it can seem to start at that. We still need to remember that if we can only be thankful for the good things we have, we can fall into the trap of thinking that what we have is happiness itself. I’m ultimately reaching for something much deeper. Because getting what you want is an endless loop, and it doesn’t lead to being content with less. When you can see that less is more, at a deeper level, that’s where the real gratitude flourishes.
