Missing someone can feel like one more problem to solve. Does it have to be? Sometimes the ache works more like an alarm clock for love, if you don't shove it away. When the love's there, let "I miss you" turn into "I love you." Or send them a simple wish: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." You can picture them while you do it. Or just feel the care heading their way, whatever's easier.
