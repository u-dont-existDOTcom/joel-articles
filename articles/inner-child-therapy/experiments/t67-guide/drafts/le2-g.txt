So go ahead and sort it out a bit. What really ended, and what are you still trying to keep going? Some of it just isn't known yet, and that's a different pile. Your little one probably has a worst-case story about the gap, so ask them what it is. A small ritual or something practical can mark the ending, like boxing up the old keys, and nobody has to know the next beginning for that. If the not-knowing gets unbearable, grabbing a new identity just to have one starts to look like a plan. Don't force it, though. The grown-up you can keep your body and your routines going, and grieve what ended with your little one beside you while the next life takes shape.
