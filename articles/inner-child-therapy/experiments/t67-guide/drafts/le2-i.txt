In the gap, your little one may feel like everything's over. So sort it out together. What really ended? What are you still trying to keep going, and what just isn't known yet? Ask them what they're afraid will happen in the meantime. Marking the ending with something small helps some people (boxing up the old keys, say), as long as it doesn't pretend to know the next beginning. And don't force a new identity just because the not-knowing is unbearable. Keep your body and your routines going, and grieve what ended with your little one beside you while the next life takes shape. (If the waiting turns into a way of life, The Inner Guide Comes Later has more on that.)
