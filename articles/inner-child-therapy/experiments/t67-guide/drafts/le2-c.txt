Go ahead and ask what actually ended, and what you're still trying to keep alive anyway. What isn't known yet? Then check in with your little one about what they're afraid will happen in the gap. You might mark the ending with something practical, or a small ritual, whatever feels right, as long as it doesn't pretend you know what's next. When the uncertainty feels unbearable, a new identity can look like a life raft. Don't force one just to get out of the water. The grown-up you can keep the routines going in the meantime, and look after your body. Grieving what ended is part of the job, and so is staying with your little one while the next life takes shape.
