Buddhism, for one, won't let you off that easy. There, death is no off switch, and no new-age free pass back to the source, either. Whatever you keep thinking and intending, again and again, inclines the mind, and that carries on through death and rebirth (hell realms included, sorry). A human birth is the rare one, where you get a real shot at understanding suffering and ending what causes it. You don't have to believe any of that.
