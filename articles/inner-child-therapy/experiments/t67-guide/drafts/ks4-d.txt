From a Buddhist point of view, death isn't really an exit. It's not an off switch, and it's not some new-age reset where you get a free get-out-of-jail card back to the source. What you keep thinking and intending inclines the mind (that's the whole idea behind practice), and that causal process carries right on through death and rebirth. Hell realms are possible. A human birth, meanwhile, is a rare opportunity to understand suffering and bring its causes to an end. You don't have to believe any of that, though.
