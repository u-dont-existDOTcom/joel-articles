The ache of missing someone doesn't have to become another thing to fix, and pushing it away won't help either. Sometimes it turns into a cue for love. If the love's there, go ahead and let "I miss you" become "I love you," or offer a little loving-kindness wish: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." Picture them while you do it, if that comes easily. Or skip the picture and just feel the care heading their way.
