This is about what happens in you. Do they receive any of it? Maybe, maybe not, and you don't need to believe they do. Either way it's not a replacement for grieving, or for contact, and it doesn't cancel your boundaries or the practical things that still need doing. If sending love starts to make you more compulsive or unreal, like you can't come back to your own life, make it simpler: "I miss you, and I care." And maybe offer some of that care inward, to the part of you that's still here.
