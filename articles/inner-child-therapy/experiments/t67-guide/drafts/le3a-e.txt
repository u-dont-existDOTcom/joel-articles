And missing someone? That doesn't have to become another problem to solve, and there's no need to push it away. The ache itself can be a cue for love. If the love's there, go ahead and let "I miss you" turn into "I love you," or offer a simple loving-kindness wish ("May you be safe. May you be held in love."). Picture them if you want. Or don't, and just feel which way the care is going.
