Buddhism, for one, won't let you off that easy. Death isn't the off switch some people are hoping for, and it isn't a new-age free pass back to the source. The mind leans toward whatever you keep thinking and intending, and in that view dying doesn't straighten it out: the lean carries right on through death and rebirth. Yes, hell realms included. A human birth is the rare one, where you get a real shot at understanding suffering and ending what causes it. You don't have to believe any of that.
