Go ahead and mark the ending if that helps, with something practical or a small ritual, like boxing up the old keys. No need to know what's next for that. And if the not-knowing gets unbearable, don't force a new identity just to make it stop. The grown-up you has enough to do in the meantime, between the routines and looking after your body. There's grieving to do, too. Mostly, your little one needs you there while the next life takes shape.
