A lot of people hope death works like an off switch, or like the new-age version, where you get a free get-out-of-jail card back to the source. Buddhism, for one, won't let you off that easy. The way it sees things, the mind leans toward whatever you keep thinking and intending, and dying doesn't straighten that out. The lean carries right on through death and rebirth. Hell realms are possible too (not exactly the brochure). A human birth is the rare one, where you get a real shot at understanding suffering and ending what causes it. You don't have to believe any of that.
