A small ritual can mark the ending, or something practical, like boxing up the old keys. Think of it as closing a door before you know which room is next. When the not-knowing gets unbearable, grabbing a new identity can feel like a relief, the way any port does in a storm. Don't force it. The grown-up you has work in the meantime, keeping your body and your routines going, and grieving what ended with your little one beside you while the next life takes shape.
