Buddhism, for one, won't let you off that easy. Death isn't an off switch there, and it isn't the new-age version either, where you get a free get-out-of-jail card back to the source. What you think and intend over and over trains the mind, and the training doesn't stop when you die. It goes along into the next birth. Hell realms are possible too (not exactly the brochure). A human birth is the rare one, where you get a real shot at understanding suffering and ending what causes it. You don't have to believe any of that.
