Missing someone hurts, and it's tempting to treat that as a problem to solve, or to push it away. Try letting the ache be a cue for love instead. If love's there, "I miss you" can turn into "I love you." Or you could send a simple wish their way: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." Some people picture the person. Others just feel the care heading off in their direction, wherever that is.
