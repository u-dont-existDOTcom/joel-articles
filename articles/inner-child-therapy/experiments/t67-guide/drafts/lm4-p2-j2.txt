But a big spiritual experience can show you a kind of peace, or love, that you didn't know existed, and then "I want you to be happy" suddenly means something much bigger. That's partly why loving-kindness meditation sounds almost comically simple until you've been near what it's pointing at, and then it can hit hard. Saying "may you be happy" over and over won't make it profound love by itself (you can mean it about as much as "have a nice day"). It's a way of finding that deeper love and growing it.
