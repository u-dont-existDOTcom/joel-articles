Your little one might be sure everything's over. So what actually ended? Maybe mostly the marriage, while you're still trying to keep some of the old life going out of habit. Some of it just isn't known yet, which isn't the same as gone. Ask your little one what they're scared will happen in the meantime. If it helps, mark the ending with something small and practical, like boxing up the old keys. You don't need to know the next beginning to close this one. And when the not-knowing gets unbearable, a new identity can look awfully tempting, even one that doesn't fit. Don't force it. Keep your body and your routines going, and grieve what ended with your little one beside you while the next life takes shape.
