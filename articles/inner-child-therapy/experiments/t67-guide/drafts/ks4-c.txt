Buddhism doesn't do the off switch. It doesn't do the new-age version either, where death hands you a free get-out-of-jail card and you float back to the source. If anything, the jail comes with you. Whatever you keep thinking and intending inclines the mind, and that keeps going right through death into rebirth, with hell realms possible on the way. A human birth is a rare opportunity in all that, a chance to understand suffering and bring its causes to an end. Don't have to believe it, of course.
