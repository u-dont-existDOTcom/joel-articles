Some people mark the ending with something practical, or a small ritual, like boxing up the old keys. That's fine as long as nobody's pretending to know the next beginning. And the urge to grab a new identity, just so the not-knowing stops? Totally understandable, but don't force it. Meanwhile your body and your routines still need looking after, and what ended still needs grieving. Your little one mostly needs you to stick around while the next life takes shape.
