Buddhism, for one, won't let you off that easy. Death isn't an off switch there, and it isn't the new-age version either, where you get a free get-out-of-jail card back to the source. Whatever you keep thinking and intending, over and over, bends the mind that way, like a path through the grass that gets deeper every time somebody walks it. And that path keeps right on going through death and into the next birth. Hell realms are possible too (not exactly the brochure). A human birth is the rare one, where you get a real shot at understanding suffering and ending what causes it. You don't have to believe any of that.
