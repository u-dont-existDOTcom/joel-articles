Missing someone hurts, and it doesn't have to become one more problem to solve. Don't push it away, either. The ache itself can be a cue for love, if you let it. When the love's there, "I miss you" can turn into "I love you." Or you can send a simple wish their way: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." Picture them, or don't, and just feel the care going in their direction.
