In the gap it's easy to treat everything as over. So sort it into piles: what really ended, and what you're still trying to keep going. Then there's the pile of stuff that just isn't known yet. Your little one probably has a fear about the gap too, so ask them what it is. Marking the ending can be practical, or a small ritual, as long as it doesn't claim to know the next beginning (you don't know it yet, and that's kind of the point). Try not to force a new identity just because the not-knowing is unbearable. Meanwhile, keep the routines going and take care of your body. Grieve what ended, as much as it needs. Your little one needs you there with them while the next life takes shape.
