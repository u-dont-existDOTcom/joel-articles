First sort out what actually ended from what you're still trying to keep going anyway, out of habit or hope. Some of it just isn't known yet. Then ask your little one what they're afraid will happen in the meantime (little ones are great at worst-case scenarios). Something practical can mark the ending, or a small ritual, like boxing up the old keys, as long as you're not pretending you know what comes next. And when the not-knowing gets unbearable, a new identity off the rack can look awfully good, like a coat that doesn't fit but at least it's a coat. Don't force one. Keep your routines and look after your body. Grieving what ended is part of it. Mostly, stay with your little one while the next life takes shape.
