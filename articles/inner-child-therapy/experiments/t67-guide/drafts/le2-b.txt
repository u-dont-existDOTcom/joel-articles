So what really ended? Not everything, probably, though it can feel that way. Some of what you're still trying to keep going belongs to the old life, and some of it just isn't known yet, which is a different thing. Your little one may already have a worst-case story about the gap, so ask them what they're scared of. A small ritual can mark the ending (nothing grand, maybe a last walk past the old place). Just don't pretend you know the next beginning. And go easy on grabbing a new identity because the uncertainty is unbearable. Keep your routines going in the meantime, and look after your body. Grieve what ended. Your little one needs company while the next life takes shape, and that's your job.
