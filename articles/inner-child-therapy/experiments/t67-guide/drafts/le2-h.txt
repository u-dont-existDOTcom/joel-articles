Your little one might be asking, "Is it all over now?" Probably not all of it. Something did end, and some of what you're still trying to keep going may belong to the old life. A lot just isn't known yet. So ask them what they're scared will happen while you wait. Something practical can mark the ending, or a small ritual, like boxing up the old keys, and nobody needs to know what's next for that. When the not-knowing gets unbearable, a new identity can look like a quick fix. Don't force one. Keep your body and your routines going, and grieve what ended with your little one beside you while the next life takes shape.
