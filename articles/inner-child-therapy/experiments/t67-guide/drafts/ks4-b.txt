So what does Buddhism say happens? Not the off switch a lot of people are counting on, and not the new-age free pass back to the source either. The idea is that what you keep thinking and intending tilts the mind a little more every time, kind of like water wearing a channel into rock. That tilt doesn't stop at death. It carries on through death and rebirth, and hell realms are in the picture too. A human birth is the rare chance to understand suffering (dukkha, in Pali) and bring its causes to an end. You don't have to buy any of it, of course.
