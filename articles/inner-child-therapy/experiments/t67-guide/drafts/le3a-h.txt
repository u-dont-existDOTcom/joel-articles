When you miss someone, it's tempting to turn the ache into one more problem to solve, or to shove it away. Here's another option: let it be a cue for love. If the love's there, "I miss you" becomes "I love you." Or try a simple loving-kindness wish ("May you be safe. May you be held in love."), picturing them if that helps, or just feeling which way the care is going, like pointing a compass.
