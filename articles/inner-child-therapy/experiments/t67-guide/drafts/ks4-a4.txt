Buddhism, for one, won't let you off that easy. Death isn't an off switch there, and it isn't the new-age version either, where you get a free get-out-of-jail card back to the source. Whatever you keep thinking and intending inclines the mind that way. Then you die, and it keeps going anyway, through rebirth, like a habit that hasn't heard the body's gone. Hell realms are possible too (not exactly the brochure). A human birth is the rare one, where you get a real shot at understanding suffering and ending what causes it. You don't have to believe any of that.
