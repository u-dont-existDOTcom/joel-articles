Missing someone doesn't have to turn into one more problem to solve. Don't shove the ache away, either. It can be a cue for love, kind of like a doorbell. If the love is there, let "I miss you" turn into "I love you," or send them a simple wish: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." You can picture them, or just feel which way the care is pointing. This happens in you, so you don't need to believe they get any of it. It's not instead of grieving, though, or of getting in touch if that's right for you. Boundaries still count. So do the practical things. If it makes you more compulsive, or kind of unreal, so it's hard to get back to your life, make it simpler: "I miss you, and I care." And the part of you that's still here could use some of that care too.
