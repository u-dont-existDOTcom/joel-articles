When you miss someone, the ache can turn into one more thing to fix. It doesn't have to. Sometimes it works better as a cue for love, the way a song on the radio can bring a person right back. So don't push it away. If love's there, let "I miss you" become "I love you," or send a simple wish: "May you be safe. May you be held in love." Picture them, if that's easy, or just feel the direction the care is going.
