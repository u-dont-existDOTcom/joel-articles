Buddhism isn't much comfort here, if you were hoping death works like an off switch, or a new-age free pass back to the source. The way it sees things, whatever you think and intend over and over gets worn into the mind, sort of like a groove in a record, and the record doesn't stop playing when the body dies. It keeps going through rebirth. Hell realms are possible. And a human birth is the rare chance to understand suffering and end its causes. You don't have to believe a word of it.
