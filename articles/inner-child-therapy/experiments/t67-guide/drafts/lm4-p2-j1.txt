Then a big spiritual experience comes along and shows you a peace or a love you didn't know existed, and "I want you to be happy" suddenly means a lot more. It's like finding out the house has a whole upstairs. That's partly why loving-kindness meditation can sound almost silly to someone who's never been up there, and so powerful to someone who has. You can say "may you be happy" a thousand times and mean about as much as "have a nice day." The practice is one way of finding that bigger love, and growing it.
