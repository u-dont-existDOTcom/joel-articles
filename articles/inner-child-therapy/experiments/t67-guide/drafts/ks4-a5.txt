Buddhism, for one, won't let you off that easy. Death isn't an off switch there, and it isn't the new-age version either, where you get a free get-out-of-jail card back to the source. The mind keeps going in whatever direction you keep pushing it, with every thought and intention you repeat. Death doesn't interrupt that, so it carries on through rebirth. Hell realms are possible too (not exactly the brochure). A human birth is the rare one, where you get a real shot at understanding suffering and ending what causes it. You don't have to believe any of that.
