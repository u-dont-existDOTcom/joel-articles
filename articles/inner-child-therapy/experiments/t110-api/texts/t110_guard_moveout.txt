That part doesn’t have to move out. It’s been living there way longer than the grown-up you. It can keep watch while you take one small step toward your little one. I think watching is what it wants anyway, to find out if there’s a grown-up here now who could call it off if things got to be too much. It doesn’t have to be all or nothing, either. Maybe it would settle for a lighter cover for now, like blinds tilted open a crack, and let you peek out for a minute (ST-CA, section 9.3.4). If it says no, I’d listen. When it lets you, keep an eye on that part too, for any change at all.
