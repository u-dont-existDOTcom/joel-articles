## 1. A line or two under each part and role with no text of its own

A reader who opens the contents sees only a part's name. Where the guide says nothing under it, they guess what it holds, as the models did. Each line below is drafted from his own words, given beside it.

- **Building Your Inner Adult** (h1, before its first section): "Your Inner Adult has three jobs: protection blocks, love accepts, and the guide directs. Until each one grows in you, you can borrow it from someone who has it."
  - From: his answer to question 114 ("love accepts. protection blocks. guide directs."); the opening ("a loving Nurturer, a safe Adult Protector [...] and a wise Guide"); Five Stages ("Begin to borrow adulthood").
- **The Nurturer** (h2, before its first section): "Love accepts. The Nurturer is who you'd go to with shame, or when you need forgiveness: someone who takes care of you, holds you, stays with you and listens. Divine love goes beyond that, and the Guide can take you there."
  - From: his answers to questions 114 ("when you have shame or you need forgiveness, it is someone who feeling like a nurturer that you go to") and 115 ("divine love is beyond taking care of someone, or hugging them, or being with them or listening to them"); his line "This is what the Inner Guide can bring you to".
- **Safety and Readiness** (h1, before its first section): "Before you go deep, make sure there's enough safety around you to continue: in your body, with the people in your life, and with yourself."
  - From: Five Stages ("make sure there's enough safety around you to continue with reparenting"); what the part holds.
- **Practice With Your Little One** (h1, before its first section): "These are ways to sit down with your little one, and any of the three roles can use them: how to start, how to listen, and how to close the session."
  - From: Five Stages ("saying the right thing when you sit down with your little one helps"); his answer to question 114 (a practice "could be done by any of the roles").
- **When Practice Gets Hard** (h1, before its first section): "At first it's deliberate, and it might feel difficult: fake, empty, shallow, or like something to escape. Here's what helps, and how to tell whether it's working."
  - From: the opening ("At first, it's deliberate and might feel difficult"); what the part holds.
- **Altered States** (h1, before its first section): "Psychedelics and intensive meditation can deepen this work or turn it into escapism. Here's how to prepare, what to do with a peak, and what to do if a trip leaves reality behind."
  - From: Therapy or Escapism? ("intensive meditation or psychedelics [...] can get you past the guards before there's a grown-up big enough to hold whatever comes through"); what the part holds.
- **Play and Your Preferences** (h2, before its first section): "Your little one wants to play too, and to find out what they really like."
  - From: Play Is Part of the Work ("Your little one wants to play, too").
- **Keeping Your Own Ground** (h2, before its first section): "You can belong without trading away your own inner knowing."
  - From: Belonging Bargains ("pull you away from your own inner-knowing in return for belonging").

Conclusion and Appendix and Appendix need none. Instead of separate lines, one "how this guide is laid out" paragraph near the top could do the same job for a first-time reader.

## 2. A pointer where readers would look somewhere else

Five blind card sorts (runs 3 to 6 in the contents test, each with a different frame) filed every section where a reader thought it belongs. Where at least 3 of the 5 would look under another part, or another role, than the one it sits in now, a real reader may look there too. A pointer with a link goes in that place:

- **Ask God for a Loan 🫶🏿**: 3 of 5 readings would look in Building Your Inner Adult (under The Nurturer); it is in Building Your Inner Adult (under The Inner Guide). Already pointed to from the Nurturer: Divine Love: Bigger Door links to Toward Divine Love.
- **A Heart to Child Loop**: 5 of 5 readings would look in Building Your Inner Adult (under The Nurturer); it is in Building Your Inner Adult (under The Inner Guide).
  - At the end of If Love Isn't Coming Yet, under the Nurturer (after "That takes warmth, and if there isn't any yet, borrow some."): "Once you have a little warmth, [A Heart to Child Loop](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/189761959/a-heart-to-child-loop) sends it down to your little one."
- **Make a Simple Vow**: 3 of 5 readings would look in Building Your Inner Adult (under The Protector); it is in Practice With Your Little One.
  - At Earning trust, day to day, under the Protector, after "like keeping a promise to yourself nobody else knew about": "A vow to your little one is one of those promises ([Make a Simple Vow](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/189761959/make-a-simple-vow))."
- **“And So That’s the Way It Happened!”**: 3 of 5 readings would look in Building Your Inner Adult (under The Inner Guide); it is in Building Your Inner Adult (under The Nurturer).
  - At the end of Re-do Trauma Memories: "Another way to settle a hard memory is to finish it like a story: [“And So That’s the Way It Happened!”](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/189761959/and-so-thats-the-way-it-happened), under the Nurturer."
- **Answer the Disciplinarian**: 3 of 5 readings would look in Building Your Inner Adult (under The Inner Guide); it is in Building Your Inner Adult (under The Protector).

## 3. Then test it with readers

Once he has picked what goes in, the contents test from 16 and 17 November (fresh readers, the sidebar's headings, real situations, "where would you look first?") runs again on the new contents. It shows whether the lines and pointers changed where readers look.
