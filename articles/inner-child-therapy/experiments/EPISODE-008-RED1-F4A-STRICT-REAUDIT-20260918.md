1. awful conversation occupies excessive mental space;

4. time changes the next interaction;
5. the other person may hear the reader;
6. disagreement/boundary remain;

`salience -> overgeneralization -> caution -> counterexample over time -> fairness caveat -> source synthesis`.

### Residual concessive balancing

The sentence itself is semantically appropriate and source-faithful.
