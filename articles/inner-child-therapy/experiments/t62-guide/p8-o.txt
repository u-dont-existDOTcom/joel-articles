The Guide also points past itself, to somewhere you belong without having to give up being yourself. For some of us that's the woods or the ocean, or just being alive at all, and for a lot of people it's God, or whatever their tradition puts at the bottom of everything. You'll feel it more after a long walk in the woods, or a good sit in prayer or meditation, than you will from reading about it here. And once it's there, losing a group's approval still stings, sure, but your little one won't feel like it's lost its place in the world, because the Guide keeps reminding it where it really belongs.
