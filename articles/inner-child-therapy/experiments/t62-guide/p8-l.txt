The Guide also points beyond itself, toward a belonging that doesn't require betraying yourself: nature, existence itself, or whatever your tradition recognizes as ultimate. Time outside and contemplative practice make that more than another nice idea. Over time, it can make belonging with people a lot less desperate, because your little one isn't stuck thinking, "if this group drops me, I'm nobody." The Guide keeps reminding it that a group's approval, especially the kind you only keep by performing, isn't the deepest place it belongs.
