The Guide also points beyond itself, toward a belonging that doesn't require betraying yourself. That might be nature, or existence itself, or whatever your tradition recognizes as ultimate (God, the divine, the universe, source, the Three Jewels, the Tao, life). Time outside and contemplative practice make it more than another nice idea, and contemplative practice can be as simple as sitting on a rock for a while. That ground can make belonging with people a lot less desperate over time, as the Guide keeps reminding your little one that a group's approval isn't the deepest place it belongs.
