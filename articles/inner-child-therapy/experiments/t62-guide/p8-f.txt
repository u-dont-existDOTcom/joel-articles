The Guide also points beyond itself, toward a belonging that doesn't ask you to betray yourself (no membership dues, no loyalty oath). That could be nature, or just existing at all. It could be whatever your tradition sees as ultimate, God, say, or the Tao. Spending real time in nature, or in contemplative practice, is what makes it more than another idea. Over time, that ground can make belonging with people a lot less desperate. The Guide keeps reminding your little one that a group's approval isn't the deepest place it belongs, especially the kind you only keep by performing.
