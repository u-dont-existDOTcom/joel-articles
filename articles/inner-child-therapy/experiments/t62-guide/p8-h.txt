The Guide also points beyond itself, toward a belonging that doesn't require betraying yourself (no membership dues, no loyalty oath). That might be nature, or existence itself, or whatever your tradition recognizes as ultimate (I'll let you fill in the name). Time outside and contemplative practice make it more than another nice idea. And that ground can make belonging with people a lot less desperate. Not overnight, but the Guide keeps reminding your little one that a group's approval, the kind you only keep by performing, isn't the deepest place it belongs.
