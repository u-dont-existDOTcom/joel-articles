The Guide also points beyond itself, toward a belonging deeper than any group, one that doesn't cost you yourself. That might be nature, or existence itself. It might be whatever your tradition sees as ultimate (that part is optional). Love can come from any of these places, and you also belong there, which is a little different. Maybe you've felt that outside somewhere, like you didn't need anybody's okay to be there. The way in is time in nature and contemplative practice, like meditation.
