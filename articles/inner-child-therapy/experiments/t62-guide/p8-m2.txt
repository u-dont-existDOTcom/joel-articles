The Guide also points past itself, to somewhere you belong without having to give up being yourself. That might be the woods or the ocean, or just being alive at all. It might be God, if that's your word for it, or whatever your tradition puts at the bottom of everything. You don't get there by thinking about it so much as by spending real time outside, or sitting with it in prayer or meditation. Once that's under you, a group cooling on you can still sting, but it isn't the end of the world anymore, as the Guide keeps showing your little one that it doesn't need the group's thumbs-up to belong.
