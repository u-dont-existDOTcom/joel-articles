The Guide also points past itself, to somewhere you belong without having to give up being yourself. For some of us that's the woods or the ocean, or just being alive at all, and for a lot of people it's God, or whatever their tradition puts at the bottom of everything. Reading about it won't get you far (sorry); a long walk outside or a quiet sit in prayer or meditation does a lot more. And with that under you, a group cooling on you can still hurt, but your little one has somewhere else to stand, and the Guide keeps reminding it of that.
