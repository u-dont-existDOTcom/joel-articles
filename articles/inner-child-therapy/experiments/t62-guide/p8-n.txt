The Guide also points past itself, to somewhere you belong without having to give up being yourself. For some of us that's the woods or the ocean, or just being alive at all, and for a lot of people it's God, or whatever their tradition puts at the bottom of everything. It sinks in when you actually spend time outside, or sit with it in prayer or meditation (reading about it is a start, but only a start). Then a group cooling on you can still sting, but it isn't the end of the world anymore. Your little one doesn't need the group's thumbs-up to know it belongs.
