Picture your crew from work, or your church, deciding tomorrow that you're out. I'd want your little one to have somewhere it belongs that can't vote it out, and that's where the Guide points: a belonging that doesn't require betraying yourself. That might be nature, or existence itself, or whatever your tradition recognizes as ultimate. Time outside and contemplative practice make it more than another nice idea. And that ground can make belonging with people a lot less desperate (not overnight), as the Guide keeps reminding your little one that a group's approval isn't the deepest place it belongs.
