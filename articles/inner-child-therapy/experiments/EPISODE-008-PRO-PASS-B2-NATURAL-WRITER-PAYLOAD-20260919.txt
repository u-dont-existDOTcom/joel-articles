The next heading is:

### Write It. Don't Send It Yet.

They don't answer right away. They write the reply they actually want to send and leave it unsent. Later they read it again. A sentence that felt necessary at first may now look unfair or muddled; something they were tempted to soften may still be exactly what they mean. If an apology is genuinely theirs, it can stay without swallowing the complaint.

Continue from there. Stay with the rereading until they know what, if anything, they want to say to the other person. If they promise a fuller answer later, it has to be a promise they can actually keep. If they need to protect themselves now, the exercise waits.

Do not invent Joel's autobiography, named people, new facts, therapeutic claims, new safety rules, or motives for the other person.
