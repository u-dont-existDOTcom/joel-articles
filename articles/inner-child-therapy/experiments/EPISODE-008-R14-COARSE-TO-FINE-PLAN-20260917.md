## Meaning that must survive

The preservation ledger is a fidelity check, **not the order of prose generation**. The whole realization must preserve these functions without visibly marching through them as criteria:

1. Not every hook is mainly old material; what the other person actually does matters.
2. Repeated behavior matters more than endless motive-solving. Keep the Lindsay Gibson attribution/link.
3. One disagreement, needing time, or having a boundary does not by itself establish emotional immaturity. Assessment stays specific and revisable; future behavior can change the judgment.
4. Protection/action does not have to wait for perfect certainty or another injury; promises alone are not behavior change.
5. Guilt can coexist with a real need to repair something **and** with self-abandonment to stop another person's disappointment. Guilt alone does not settle which is happening and does not prove manipulation.
6. Mutual understanding may never arrive. Saying what matters, saying no, or ending the interaction without prolonged self-defense can still be a sane outcome.
7. Preserve the functional ability to notice what is happening inside, what is happening between people, and what to do next, but do **not** prematurely name Witness or rebuild `Witness / Protector / Guide` as a hidden three-part taxonomy.
