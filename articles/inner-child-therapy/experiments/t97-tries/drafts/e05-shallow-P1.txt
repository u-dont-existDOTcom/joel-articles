Some people have the opposite problem. It feels like you're connecting with your little one, but the contact is staying near the surface. Meditating for a long time and then sleeping can open up much more emotionally direct stuff, especially in a second sleep, after you've already slept once. What showed me that the daytime pl/ork had stayed shallow was the difference at night. Say you wake up from a dream soaked in way more feeling and need than anything the session got near. Then this is probably you. A nice session that just wears off is a different thing. I'd ask what the dream let you feel that the session didn't, and look at it again after your morning coffee. And if nightmares start wrecking your sleep, dial the practice down by doing it earlier in the day, or for less time.
