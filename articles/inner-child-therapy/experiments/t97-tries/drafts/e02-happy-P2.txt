But maybe you've had a big spiritual experience, and for a while you felt a peace or a love you didn't know existed. After that, "I want you to be happy" can mean something much bigger, almost overnight. That's why loving-kindness meditation can sound comically simple to someone who's never been near that, and so powerful to someone who has. Saying "may you be happy" over and over won't zap you into profound love, but it's one way people find that love and grow it.
