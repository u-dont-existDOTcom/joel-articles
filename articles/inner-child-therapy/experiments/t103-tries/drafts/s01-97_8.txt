Milder reactions afterward count as caution signs too, like getting confused or spaced out (dissociating) again and again. If any of this is severe or risky, I'd get professional help right away, especially if it keeps going or comes back while you're sober. And I wouldn't suddenly quit a prescribed medicine, or anything your body is dependent on, just because it might be part of the problem. I'd ask a doctor how to come off it safely.
