The opposite trap, forced adulthood, looks more like hauling all the grocery bags up the stairs in one trip while a friend holds the door with nothing to carry. You hold yourself to perfect discipline and never let a need show. There isn't much joy in that, or closeness either. Halfway up, with your fingers going purple, all you're thinking about is getting a better grip. The grown-up you might say what you'd like for once, or ask someone safe, "Could you take the eggs?"
