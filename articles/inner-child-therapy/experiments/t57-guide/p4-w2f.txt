Once a need does show, after all that hiding, it's okay to want a lot of closeness. Then someone you love goes for a walk alone, and you've sent "hello??" before they've reached the corner. And when they get back, you go quiet on them all night. Look at how you're going about getting the need met, too. The Guide can help you find a way that leaves room for both of you, like a hug at the door and a "see you in an hour." But give the need a fair hearing first: I think your little one wants to know they're coming back.
