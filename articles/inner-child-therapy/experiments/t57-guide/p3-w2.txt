Then there's the opposite trap, forced adulthood, where you hold yourself to perfect discipline and never let a need show. There's very little joy or closeness in it. If you're stuck there, the next grown-up step (and I don't mean tightening your grip again) might be asking someone safe for help, or saying what you'd like. That's hard if you're the one who ends up in the kitchen doing dishes at every party. It's quieter in there, and you know what to do with your hands.
