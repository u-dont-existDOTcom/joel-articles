Or you're the one doing the dishes at your own birthday party while everyone keeps saying, "Leave those, come sit down!" Your little one would love to, but you never let a need show. That's the opposite trap, forced adulthood, where you hold yourself to perfect discipline and there's very little joy or closeness. If you're stuck there, you might think the grown-up thing is to tighten your grip again, but the next grown-up step may be asking someone safe for help, or saying what you'd like.
