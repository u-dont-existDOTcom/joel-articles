You can slide the other way too, into forced adulthood, and end up saying "Keep driving, I can hold it." You expect perfect discipline from yourself and never let a need show. Meanwhile your little one has been yelling about it since the last rest stop. There isn't much fun in living like that, or much closeness. You'll want to hold on harder, but the grown-up thing might be to ask someone safe for help, or just say what you'd like: "Can we stop somewhere soon?"
