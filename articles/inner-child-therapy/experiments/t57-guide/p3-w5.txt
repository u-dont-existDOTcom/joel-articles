For some of you, though, the next grown-up step might be asking someone safe to take the other end of the couch. Or telling your friends you'd like to come to the lake, now that they've stopped inviting you. Either of those can feel like a lot if you're the one who makes the bed in hotels and puts the heating pad away before anyone comes over. That's called forced adulthood, the opposite trap. You'll probably want to tighten your grip again instead.
