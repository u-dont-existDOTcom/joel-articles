I think the opposite trap is sneakier, since perfect discipline looks so grown-up. You're on your towel twisting one arm up behind you to get sunscreen on the middle of your back, because asking someone would mean letting a need show. Meanwhile everybody you came with is in the water having fun. In forced adulthood, the urge is to try harder alone, maybe with one of those sponges on a long handle. You'll still miss a spot. I'd call someone safe over and say, "Could you do my back?" or "I want to swim too!"
