# Stance check 2: opening of "The Inner Guide Comes Later"

Checked every sentence of the rewrite that states a position, rule, assessment or claim (P1 as installed, unchanged from the published text; new P2 Go Fish, P3 fever and kitchen, P4 the need) against the whole published essay.

## Conflicts in the rewrite

### 1. P3 ranks forced adulthood as "the sneakier" trap (a first-person view the essay doesn't support)

- **Rewrite:** "I think the opposite trap is the sneakier one, since from the outside it just looks like having your act together."
- **Essay:** "The Guide is the one people overlook the most." (The Chicken-and-Egg Problem). Also: "It can be the old 'don't be needy' rule passing for the Guide. No wonder tightening control again feels like the grown-up thing." (The Inner Guide Comes Later, published P3)
- The essay never ranks the two traps. It gives each its own disguise (permissiveness "can pass for self-love"; forced adulthood can pass for the Guide), and what it says people overlook most is the Guide, whose absence can turn reparenting permissive.
- **A reader would wrongly believe:** Joel holds that over-control is the harder trap to catch, and that it hides by fooling onlookers, when the essay says it can fool you, by passing for your own Guide.

### 2. P4 swaps "without erasing you or the other person" for "works for you both" (a specific limit blurred into a general one)

- **Rewrite:** "The Guide helps you find a way that works for you both."
- **Essay:** "The Guide helps you find some workable way to meet the need without erasing you or the other person." (The Inner Guide Comes Later, published P4). Where the essay aims at both sides elsewhere, it keeps the same guard: "until both sides feel good about it, or good enough, as long as what you needed to say is still in there" (Building Trust With Your Little One / Not Every Hero Wears A Cape).
- **A reader would wrongly believe:** the test is a win-win both people are content with, instead of the essay's specific limit that neither self gets erased while the need is met.

## Contradictions in the published text

None found. Read the way people talk, the published P1 to P4 agree with each other and with the rest of the essay, so there is nothing here for the rewrite to have kept or fixed.
