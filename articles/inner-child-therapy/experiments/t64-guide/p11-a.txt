Some manipulative people are too smart to act perfect. They'll tell you right away how broken they are, or how honest they're being about their flaws, so every warning about them starts to sound like proof of how deep they are. That's when the Protector needs to notice your compassion explaining away what you've already seen. Someone who says, "I have honesty issues and I'm working on them," may well be telling the truth. They're also telling you they have an honesty problem right now. Knowing about a flaw isn't the same as fixing it.
