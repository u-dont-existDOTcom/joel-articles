Some manipulative people are too smart to act perfect. They show you their wounds and their flaws right away, so when a friend warns you about them, your little one thinks, "Yeah, but they already told me that." The Protector has to notice when compassion starts covering for what you've seen with your own eyes. If someone says, "I have honesty issues and I'm working on them," that may be honest. It's also evidence of an honesty problem now, because seeing a flaw in yourself isn't the same as correcting it.
