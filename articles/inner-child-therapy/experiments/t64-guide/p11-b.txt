Some manipulative people are too smart to act perfect. They lead with their wounds, or with how brutally honest they are about their flaws (very disarming, which is sort of the point), and every warning you hear about them just becomes more proof of how deep they are. Your Protector has to catch the moment compassion starts explaining away what you've already seen. "I have honesty issues and I'm working on them" may be a perfectly honest thing to say. It's also telling you there's an honesty problem right now, and knowing about a problem isn't fixing it.
