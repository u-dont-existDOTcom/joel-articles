Some manipulative people are too smart to act perfect. They'll own up to everything first ("I know, I'm a lot, I've got my demons"), and after that every warning about them sounds like it's about how deep and honest they are. The Protector watches for the moment your compassion starts excusing what you've already seen. Someone who says, "I have honesty issues and I'm working on them," might be honest about that. They've also just told you there's an honesty problem right now (seeing a flaw isn't the same as fixing it).
