### C. Insert the new H2 immediately after readiness

## When the Present-Day Adult Is Dangerous to the Child

There is another reason to leave the child alone: sometimes the adult is there, but the adult is not trying to protect the child. If the present-day person actually wants to frighten or exploit the vulnerable part, bringing the child into the work to “see what happens” is already putting the child in front of the problem; a frightened reaction only proves that the child is frightened. This isn't about every disturbing thought or unusual belief, or even about the fact that somebody has done harm before. The question is what the adult is choosing now, and until that changes, the work stays with the adult, not the child.
