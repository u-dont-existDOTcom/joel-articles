You might go looking for your little one and get mad instead, or realize you’ve been staring at the rug. I count that as pl/ork too, even if it feels like it’s in the way. Before you decide where it’s coming from, ask it what it’s trying to stop, or what it wants. You can tell that part something like, “I see you. I’m not going to force my way past you. What’s going on?” Or however you’d actually say that. Then wait and listen patiently.
