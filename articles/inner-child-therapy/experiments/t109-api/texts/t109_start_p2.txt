Maybe it just has something to say. It could be your little one, mad that you took so long to come back, or a part of you that’s trying to protect you. Maybe it’s that parent you inherited, or something from earlier today, or a bit of each. I wouldn’t spend long deciding what to call each part, though. Every part of you learns from how the others treat it, your little one included. A part of you that’s trying to protect you might warn you about something, and when you hear it out, it doesn’t have to get any louder.
