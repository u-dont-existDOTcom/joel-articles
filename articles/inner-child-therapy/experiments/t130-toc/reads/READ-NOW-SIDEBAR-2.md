## 2. What gets in the way

**Cut off before the point**
- **Allow Céline to explain the …** and **The Chicken-and-Egg Pro…**: a name and a half-phrase, so a newcomer can't tell who Céline is or what the problem is. These are the first two headings anyone sees.
- **Your Body Might Need So…**, **When the Present-Day Ad…**, **Shame can make you fee…**, **How to Forgive Without F…**, **Show Up When Nothing’…**: the cut lands exactly on the word that says the topic.
- **If You Want to Kill Yourself** is the one crisis heading, and it is the fourth item under Safety and Readiness. Its children, **The Spiritual Side of the …** and **This human life is the mo…**, are cut off too.
- **Altered States and Intensive …**, **Bringing Reparenting Into Re…**, **Discovering and Healing Old…**: the H1 titles are truncated, and the contents don't say what sits under them.
- **When the Practice Gets Diffi…** has five children that all start with "When…" or "Sometimes…" (**When the Adult Voice Feel…**, **Sometimes There Isn’t a Cl…**, **When the Practice Feels R…**, **When Healing Turns Into C…**, **How Would You Know It’s …**). The sidebar cuts each one before its distinguishing word.
- **Reframing attacks as u…**, **Earning their trust, day to…**, **If it tells you to do somet…**, **Before You Tell the Har…**, **If you keep coming back …**, **Before you quit a medica…**, **Non-altered states can f…**: each is cut off before its point.

**"Borrow" hides the difference**
- Seven headings start with "Borrow": **Borrow the Adult To Beco…**, **Borrow One Competency…**, **Borrow Someone Else’s L…**, **Borrow Your Love For So…**, **Borrow the Guide to Buil…**, **Borrow Hindsight**, and **Finding a Therapist to Borr…**. The sidebar shows the same word seven times, so the reader can't tell them apart.

**Sections in the wrong place or at the wrong level**
- **Notice the Part That Cam…** sits under **Your Body Might Need So…**, but the topic reads as being about a part, not the body.
- **Ill Will and Loving-Kindne…** sits under **When the Present-Day Ad…**. The link is not visible.
- **Protecting and Building Tru…** has three near-identical children: **Protecting them from yo…**, **Protecting from the Pare…** and **Reframing attacks as u…**. **Answering the Strict Discip…** (in a different H1) covers a similar subject, the harsh parental voice. Situation 4 could land in either place.
- **Talk to the hand!✋🏿** is under Protecting, while **Journal With Each Hand** and **Which thoughts to talk to** sit in Practicing. The reader may expect these to be one topic.
- Divine love shows up three times: **A Bigger Doorway: Divin…**, **Guide Yourself To Divine …** and **Ask God for a Loan 🫶🏿**, which at 901 words is the longest section. **When the Spiritual Relati…** may overlap with the Signs of Manipulation material.
- Hindsight shows up three times: **Borrow Hindsight**, **Remember When You A…** and **Use hindsight to “re-do” tr…** (in another H1). The last is a candidate for a duplicate.
- **Discovering and Healing Old…** has only two H2s, both about discipline. Its title promises more than it holds.
- **Physical Pain Makes It H…** sits under **When the Urge to Escape …** and is about pain, not escape. Another body heading sits in Safety.
- **When Escape Leaves Reali…** and **When the Urge to Escape …** both use "escape" in different H1s. The first holds eight unrelated children: bad trips, paranoia, medication, mixing substances, and **Who counts as trusted**, which belongs near the "finding people" material.

- **Rediscover Play and Your …** and **Differentiation: Keeping Yo…** both have 0 words and sit under "Bringing Reparenting Into Re…" with the therapist, speaking-up and grief sections. That H1 has 146 words of its own.

- **“And So That’s the Way…** is a quotation used as a heading, so it says nothing about its content.
- **Conclusion and Appendix** is one H1 holding two unrelated things.
- Casing is inconsistent. Some headings use Title Case and some use sentence case ("If it’s anxiety", "If no answer comes").
