## 2. What gets in the way

**Headings that don't say what's under them**

- **"Pl/ork With Your Little One" and "When Pl/ork Gets Difficult."** I can't tell what "Pl/ork" is, and I couldn't type it into a search box. These are two of the biggest parts of the guide, so my first reaction on seeing them was confusion. I'd read it as play plus work, but that is a guess from the slash.
- **"Hearthwork: Take Turns."** It uses a proper name with no intro. It sits under "Find People, Build Trust," so I'd guess it's a peer-support practice, but nothing says so.
- **"Céline and the Inner Child" and "Lucia’s Reparenting."** These are first names with no role attached. I'd guess they're example people or stories, but a reader skimming wouldn't know. The Lucia one is also buried in the Appendix, far from the main text.
- **"The Chicken-and-Egg."** This is 615 words and it doesn't name which two things are the chicken and the egg. "My Journey" (974 words, the biggest block in the opening part) doesn't say what the journey is.
- **"Dip Your Toes In," "Whatever Showed Up," "Close the Session," "Borrow One Capacity," "Noticing Counts."** All of these are cute or abstract. "Whatever Showed Up" is the first thing under the practice part and tells me nothing. "Close the Session" assumes I know there is a session.
- **"Healing or Checking?"** Checking what? I'd guess checking whether you've healed, but it reads as a pair with no clear second half. "Real, but Still Shallow?" is similar.
- **"Hurt or Threatened?"** (23 words) is cryptic standing alone under "Speaking Up in Conflict."
- **"Journal With Each Hand" and "Talk to the hand!✋🏿."** The first doesn't say what it is (writing with both hands? two voices?). The second is a joke, and I can't tell what it's about until I open it.
- **"What Self Would Die?" and "Precious Human Life."** For someone in crisis these subheadings are philosophical and cryptic. The section is only 122 words of its own, and nothing in the headings says "do this now" or "get help." That matters more here than anywhere else in the guide.
- **"Listen To Your Body, Or…"** The trailing "Or…" looks like the sidebar cut it off. It is the only heading that reads as truncated, and I can't tell what the "or" is.
- **"Four Pairs That Hook," "Other Folks’ Hooks," "Catch the Hook."** The "hook" vocabulary is used three times in different places. Nothing in the contents introduces it, and "Four Pairs" doesn't say pairs of what.
- **"Altered States & Practice."** Nothing in this title says psychedelics, retreats, or trips. A reader who wants that has to guess from "Peak," "Trips," and "Paranoia." "Practice" is also misleading, since the content is closer to risks.
- **"Between Old & New Lives."** It could be about moving, a breakup, or leaving a community. It hides "Missing Someone" underneath.
- **"Safety and Readiness," "The Nurturer," "Play and Your Preferences," "Keep Your Own Ground"** and the other 0-word parents. They are fine as containers, but with no words of their own they don't tell me what the group is for.

**Sections that look misplaced or at the wrong level**

- **"Therapy Stirs Things Up"** sits under "Body Needs Love First." It's about the aftermath of therapy or somatic sessions, so I'd expect it under "Find a Therapist," under "When Pl/ork Gets Difficult," or at top level in readiness. Nesting it under the body section hides it from the person who needs it (situation 8).
- **"A Teacher Betrayed You"** is the last child of "The Guide." That's an odd home. It looks like it belongs with "Signs of Manipulation" under "Borrowed Adulthood," or in the part about finding people and therapists.
- **"Signs of Manipulation"** (572 words) is under "Borrowed Adulthood" with no hint of whose manipulation. I only guessed it covers spiritual teachers.
- **"My Trips Came Too Soon"** is a personal story sitting as a sibling of the clinical-looking "Losing Touch With Reality." It reads like a readiness warning, or it belongs with "My Journey."
- **"Social Media Hooks Too," "Quitting Meds & Alcohol," "What you mix"** sit under "Losing Touch With Reality." Social media isn't a loss of touch with reality, and quitting meds or alcohol is a safety topic that gets 35 words in a long list of fragments.
- **"Invite Your Little One In" and "A Heart to Child Loop"** are under "The Guide," but they look like practice with the little one and would sit better in "Pl/ork With Your Little One."
- **"Belong Beyond People"** is under "Toward Divine Love" in "Building Your Inner Adult," while the belonging material ("Belonging Bargains," "Find People, Build Trust") is in "Reparenting in Real Life." Belonging is in two places.
- **"Which thoughts to talk to," "If no answer comes," "If you do get an answer," "If It Gives Instructions"** hang under "Journal With Each Hand," so the case where I get no image is split between "No Inner Monologue?" and "No Clear Little One Yet?" and "If no answer comes."
- **"Spiritual Attack: Love"** (519 words) is under "Involuntary Altered States." The title doesn't say what attacks what, and it seems to overlap with the Divine Love material in "The Guide."
- **"Reparenting in Real Life" has 146 words** of its own while every other top-level part has 0. That is a small inconsistency, and the intro isn't signalled.

**Things that look like they're in two places**

- **Divine love:** "Divine Love: Bigger Door" (22 words, under The Nurturer) and "Toward Divine Love" with "Ask God for a Loan 🫶🏿" (901 words, under The Guide). The 22-word stub looks like a pointer to the other.
- **Borrowing:** "Borrowed Adulthood," "Borrow, Don’t Sell Out," "Borrow One Capacity," "Borrow Their Love," "Borrow Your Love," "Borrow the Guide," "Borrow Hindsight." "Borrow Their Love" and "Borrow Your Love" are almost the same heading, so I can't tell which to open.
- **Escape:** "The Urge to Escape" (when practice gets difficult) and "Therapy or Escapism?" (altered states). Both could catch situations 14, 23 and 28.
- **Is it working:** "Real, but Still Shallow?," "Healing or Checking?," "Is It Helping Your Life?," and "Change the Strategy?" with its three children. These four are neighbours in one part, and the first heading I open might not be the right one.
- **Discipline and critic:** "Discipline Done Right?" (under Re-do Trauma Memories), "Answer the Disciplinarian," "The Parent You Inherited," "Reframe the Critic," and "Repair Your Own Attacks." Strict or punishing parents show up in three parts.
- **Body:** "Body Needs Love First," "Physical Pain," and "Listen To Your Body, Or…" all concern the body but sit in three different parts.
- **Therapy:** "Therapy Stirs Things Up" (Safety), "Find a Therapist" (Real Life), and "Therapy or Escapism?" (Altered States) all start with the word therapy and mean three different things. Also "AI therapy" is a child of "Find a Therapist."
- **Keep reading for secrets and shame:** "Shame Says “Unlovable”" and "Before You Tell Secrets" are in different parts, and the shame one is under The Nurturer, which I wouldn't guess.

**Small style inconsistencies that make scanning harder:** "AI therapy," "Who counts as trusted," "If no answer comes," "If you do get an answer," "What you mix," "If a past trip went badly," "If you’d rather stop" are in sentence case, while the rest are in title case. Several headings are questions ("Healing or Checking?," "Happy While They Hurt?") and several are commands, and the mix doesn't tell me which are for diagnosing and which are for doing. Very short stubs (22, 23, 29, 31, 35, 36, 37 words) look like headings with almost nothing under them.

**One note on cutting-off:** the contents shown to me have no "…" cut-offs on any heading, so the only possible truncation is the literal "Or…" in "Listen To Your Body, Or…".
