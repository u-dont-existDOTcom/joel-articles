## 2. What gets in the way

**Headings that don't say what's under them**

- **"Pl/ork With Your Little One" and "When Pl/ork Gets Difficult."** The coined word is used before anything introduces it, and the sidebar never explains it. I couldn't tell these were the practice chapters until I read the sections underneath. That left me unsure where to go for situations 9 and 35.
- **"The Protector," "The Nurturer," "The Guide."** They are roles, and the contents doesn't say what each does. "Five Stages of Reparenting" lists no stages, so I can't tell whether these three are stages. Someone with situation 3 or 4 has to guess which role holds their problem.
- **The "Borrow" headings.** There are "Borrow One Capacity," "Borrow Their Love," "Borrow Your Love," "Borrow the Guide," "Borrow Hindsight" and "Borrow, Don’t Sell Out." They sit close together and look alike, so situation 3 could send me to any of them.
- **"Therapy or Escapism?"** The title is too general. It sits under Altered States, but nothing in the sidebar says it is about psychedelics. I only guessed it for situation 14 because of the parent heading and the sections around it.
- **"Spiritual Attack: Love."** I can't tell what it covers. It is under "Involuntary Altered States," so I took it to be about frightening experiences. That is why I put sleep paralysis (situation 15) there as a guess. Sleep paralysis is never named.
- **"Reparenting in Real Life."** It is a vague umbrella for relationships, therapists, conflict and breakups. Its subsections need to be read to be told apart. "Between Old & New Lives" doesn't say what changes.
- **"Healing Old Patterns."** It has two children and a vague name. Situation 7, which is about resentment, doesn't point here at all.
- **"Listen To Your Body, Or…"** The heading looks cut off. I can't tell what the "Or" leads to.
- **"Hearthwork: Take Turns."** The name is unexplained, and I couldn't tell it is a peer-support method.
- **Other names with no hint of content.** "Céline and the Inner Child" and "Lucia’s Reparenting" are people's names with no explanation. "Talk to the hand!✋🏿" is a joke title. "Four Pairs That Hook" doesn't say pairs of what. "Safety and Readiness," "Building Your Inner Adult" and "Altered States & Practice" read as chapter names, and "Practice" doesn't seem to fit the other half.
- **Zero-word parents.** Many parents show 0 words, for example "Play and Your Preferences" and "Keep Your Own Ground." That is fine for a pure container, but "The Nurturer" at 0 words has no intro and its children are all unlabelled by type.

**Sections that look misplaced, at the wrong level, or under the wrong parent**

- **"A Teacher Betrayed You"** is under "The Guide," and "Signs of Manipulation" is under "Borrowed Adulthood." Both look like the same topic (a trusted teacher who takes over your decisions), and they are far apart. Situation 6 sends me to one or the other.
- **"Therapy Stirs Things Up"** is a level-3 child of "Body Needs Love First." Situation 8 (nightmares and rawness after a somatic session) is really about therapy side effects, but the parent heading is about the body. I'd have missed it without scanning level 3.
- **"Divine Love: Bigger Door"** is under "The Nurturer" (22 words). "Toward Divine Love" and "Ask God for a Loan" are under "The Guide." That looks like two homes for the divine-love topic. "Goodwill: A Smaller Door" is its pair, so the pairing splits across two parents.
- **Inner cruelty is in three places.** There is "“No. I Hate Myself.”" and "When Cruelty Feels Good" in Safety, then "The Parent You Inherited" and "Repair Your Own Attacks" under The Protector. Situation 4 (self-insult in your father's voice) could go to either. "Reframe the Critic" at level 4 adds a third candidate.
- **"Discipline Done Right?"** is a child of "Re-do Trauma Memories." The sibling "Answer the Disciplinarian" is a separate level-2 section. They look like they belong together, and "Discipline Done Right?" sits oddly under a memory-work parent.
- **"Social Media Hooks Too"** is under "Losing Touch With Reality." It reads as a topic of its own, and it sits near "Catch the Hook" in meaning. "Quitting Meds & Alcohol" and "What you mix" are listed there as if they were symptoms of losing touch with reality.
- **"My Trips Came Too Soon"** is a 399-word personal story at level 2. It is the only story in the chapter, and it sits after the risk sections like a stray.
- **"AI therapy"** is a child of "Find a Therapist," but situation 23 (2 a.m., about to open an app) is closer to "The Urge to Escape" under "When Pl/ork Gets Difficult." The two sit in separate chapters.
- **Situation 17 (partner just left)** has no obvious home. "Missing Someone" is a child of "Between Old & New Lives," and I guessed there. A "My Journey" chapter near the start is 974 words and is the largest single section, which looks like a story sitting where a reader might expect a concept.
- **"Reparenting in Real Life"** has 146 words of its own and also contains "Find a Therapist," "Speaking Up in Conflict" and "Find People, Build Trust," which have little to do with "Play." The order seems arbitrary.

**Things that look like they're in two places**

- Teacher manipulation: "Signs of Manipulation" and "A Teacher Betrayed You" (see above).
- Divine love: "Divine Love: Bigger Door" and "Toward Divine Love" (see above).
- Secrets and shame: "Shame Says “Unlovable”" and "Before You Tell Secrets." Situation 31 could go to either, and so could 22.
- Disciplinarian: "Discipline Done Right?" and "Answer the Disciplinarian."
- Therapy: "Therapy Stirs Things Up," "Find a Therapist," "Therapy or Escapism?" and the appendix's "Schema Therapy." The word "therapy" appears in four unrelated places, so it is a poor scan word.

**Small inconsistencies**

- Capitalization of sentence-style headings varies: "If you do get an answer," "If no answer comes," "If a past trip went badly," "Who counts as trusted," "What you mix," "AI therapy" and "Which thoughts to talk to" are lowercase, while neighboring headings are title case.
- "Journal With Each Hand" doesn't say what the hands stand for.
- "Ask God for a Loan 🫶🏿" is the longest section (901 words) and sits at level 4, deep under "Toward Divine Love," so it is easy to miss.
