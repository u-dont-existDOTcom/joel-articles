## 2. What gets in the way

**Headings that don't say what's under them**
- **The Protector, The Nurturer, The Inner Guide.** These are role names with no verb or problem attached. A reader in trouble can't tell which role handles their situation. The Protector holds the inner-critic material (situation 4), which "Protector" doesn't suggest.
- **Talk to the hand!✋🏿.** It is playful but opaque. I can't tell if it's about boundaries, the inner critic, or something else.
- **“And So That’s the Way…** It is cut off mid-quotation, so it says nothing. It is also an H4 with 77 words under "When Shame Gets Stuck".
- **Smaller Door: Goodwill / Bigger Door: Divine Love.** The door metaphor hides the topic. "Bigger Door" has only 22 words, so a reader who opens it for a love bigger than any person (situation 30) gets almost nothing.
- **Belonging Bargains and Four Pairs That Hook.** The metaphor hides that this is about family roles and conditional acceptance. I opened it for situation 11 only as a guess.
- **Hooked on Healing.** It could mean addiction to therapy, or being stuck in a loop of grieving. I read it as the second for situation 13, but that is a guess.
- **Real but Shallow?, Right Steps, No Results?, Healthy, Not Perfect.** None names what is shallow, what the steps are, or what is healthy.
- **Attacked by a Demon?** It doesn't say sleep paralysis or night terrors. It sits under "Altered States & Retreats", so someone with that experience (situation 15) wouldn't think to look there unless they've done drugs or been on a retreat.
- **My Trips Came Too Soon** reads as the author's story, or as a warning. It's unclear which.
- **Chicken-and-Egg Problem, Céline on the Inner Child, My Journey.** The first doesn't name the two things. The second assumes you know who Céline is. "My Journey" is 974 words of memoir at the start, before any guidance.
- **Several "Borrow" headings:** Borrow an Adult to Be One, Borrow Someone’s Love, Borrow the Love You Give, Borrow a Guide to Build It, Borrow Hindsight. They look alike in a narrow sidebar and don't separate by situation.
- **Show Up for No Reason, The Love Has to Move, The Gap Between Lives, Hearthwork: Take Turns.** All are vague or use an unintroduced term. "Hearthwork" appears in the sidebar with no explanation, and "Take Turns" doesn't say turns at what.
- **Sidebar truncation.** I count only one cut-off heading, “And So That’s the Way…. Other long headings fit.
- **Inconsistent capitalization.** Some headings are lowercase: "If a past trip went badly", "What you mix", "Who counts as trusted", "AI therapy", "If it’s anxiety", "If no answer comes", "If you do get an answer", "If you’d rather stop". They look like sub-bullets, not like headings I could scan for.

**Sections that seem to be in the wrong place, level, or parent**
- **Betrayed by a Teacher** sits at the bottom of "The Inner Guide". It belongs beside "Don’t Buy an Outer Adult" and "Signs of Manipulation" under "Borrow an Adult to Be One", where a reader in situation 6 would look.
- **Invite Your Little One In and A Heart to Child Loop** are under "The Inner Guide", but sound like practice. They probably belong under "Practice With Your Little One".
- **Who counts as trusted** is under "When Drugs Warp Reality". It is about people to confide in, so it fits "Finding Your People", next to "Before Telling Secrets".
- **It Happens Sober Too, Is Paranoia a Warning?, Spaced Out After Trips?** The first two aren't drug-specific, and the third sounds like a general dissociation topic. They sit under a drugs heading, so someone who isn't using won't open them.
- **Ask God for a Loan 🫶🏿** is an H4 with 901 words, the biggest block under "Toward Divine Love", buried at the lowest level. It deserves a higher level, or at least a clearer name.
- **Schema Therapy and Internal Family Systems** are siblings of "Similar Therapies" (109 words). They look like children of it, but sit at the same level. "My Framework’s Roots" and "Teachers and Resources" are fine as siblings.
- **Healing Old Patterns** is a top-level section with only two H2s and about 1,000 words in all. "Answer the Disciplinarian" looks like the inner-critic material under "The Parent You Inherited" and could belong in "The Protector". "Re-do Painful Memories" may fit "Practice With Your Little One".
- **Therapy Stirred You Up? and The Part That Came Here** are children of "Your Body May Need Love". The first is about being raw after therapy, not about the body (situation 8). The second is unclear.
- **If You Want to Kill Yourself** is the fourth H2 under "Safety and Readiness", after body and "When You’re the Danger". A reader in crisis has to scan past three other sections. It also has two H3 children ("The Spiritual Side", "This Precious Human Life").
- **My Trips Came Too Soon** is the last H2 under "Altered States & Retreats", after the section on drugs. It is separate from "What to Do With a Peak".

**Things that look like they're in two places**
- **Divine love:** "Bigger Door: Divine Love" (Nurturer, 22 words) and "Toward Divine Love" (Inner Guide, 347 words, plus 1,237 words in its H4 children).
- **The inner critic and harsh discipline:** "The Parent You Inherited" with "Mr. Rogers’ Reframe", "Answer the Disciplinarian" and "Discipline Done Right?".
- **Trust and love:** "Earning Trust Day to Day" (Protector) and "Love Before Trust ❤️" (Nurturer).
- **No image or no feeling:** "No Inner Monologue?" (Practice) and "No Clear Child Yet?" (When Practice Gets Hard), plus "When It Feels Fake" and "Real but Shallow?" next to each other.
- **The body:** "Your Body May Need Love" (Safety) and "Pain Makes It Harder" with "Listen to Your Body!" (When Practice Gets Hard).
- **Escaping:** "The Urge to Escape" (When Practice Gets Hard), "Therapy or Escapism?" (Altered States), and "Spoil or Clamp Down?" (Inner Guide).
- **Therapists:** "Finding a Therapist" with "AI therapy" under Real Life, and "Similar Therapies" in the Appendix.
- **Danger to others:** "When You’re the Danger" (Safety) and "Protect Them From You" (Protector).
- **Secrets and telling others:** "When Shame Gets Stuck" (Nurturer), "Before Telling Secrets" (Finding Your People), and "Who counts as trusted" (drugs).
- **Where to start:** "Dip Your Toes In First" and "Ready for the Guide?" read as the same question about being steady enough.

**Smaller notes**
- Top-level sections with 0 words ("Safety and Readiness", "Building Your Inner Adult", "Practice With Your Little One", etc.) are fine as containers. But "Understanding Reparenting" has its own 227 words, while "Reparenting in Real Life" has 146 and its H2s "Rediscover Play" and "Keeping Your Own Ground" have 0. That pattern is inconsistent.
- The sidebar goes to four levels in "Building Your Inner Adult". Narrow indentation there makes it hard to tell which H3 or H4 belongs to which parent.
- "Missing Someone" is the only child of "The Gap Between Lives". It is the closest match I could find for a partner leaving (situation 17), but it reads as being about distance, not a break-up.
