## 2. What gets in the way

**Headings that don't say what's under them**

- **Chicken-and-Egg Problem** (under Understanding Reparenting): it doesn't say which two things come first and second. A reader can't tell whether it is about love and trust, inner adult and child, or something else.
- **Céline on the Inner Child**: an unfamiliar first name with no hint of who she is. A skimmer won't open it.
- **Talk to the hand!✋🏿**: a joke heading. It sits under The Protector, so I'd guess setting boundaries, but it never says so.
- **Mr. Rogers’ Reframe**: it doesn't say what is being reframed. It is also nested under "The Parent You Inherited", and the link isn't obvious.
- **“And So That’s the Way…**: it's cut off, and it's a quotation, so it tells me nothing. It sits under "When Shame Gets Stuck".
- **The Part That Came Here**: unclear. It is also an odd child of "Your Body May Need Love".
- **Belonging Bargains** and **Four Pairs That Hook**: cryptic. I only half-guess they cover fitting in by playing a part. People with that problem (my situations 11 and 38) won't see themselves in them.
- **Hooked on Healing**, **Real but Shallow?** and **A Passing Feeling**: all vague, and they sound alike. I can't tell which one is for "I feel nothing", which for "I'm obsessing over this", and which for "this mood will pass".
- **Catch the Hook Early**: "hook" is never defined. It could mean a craving, an anger trigger or an old pattern. Situations 36 and 37 both land on "hook" or "escape" headings and are hard to tell apart.
- **Attacked by a Demon?**: it doesn't mention sleep paralysis or the frozen, heavy-chest feeling. Someone in that spot has to guess from the word "demon".
- **What to Do With a Peak**: the part is called "Retreats", but no heading under it says retreat. I got to this one from "retreat", not from the heading.
- **Who counts as trusted**: trusted for what? It is buried among drug headings, and reads like a different topic.
- **When One Life Ends**: this could be a death, a breakup or a move. "Missing Someone" under it doesn't settle it.
- **Hearthwork: Take Turns**: "Hearthwork" is an unexplained coined term, and the heading doesn't say it is a peer-support method. It is the only place a partner who gets upset when you share (situation 34) could land, and it is a guess.
- **Smaller Door: Goodwill** and **Bigger Door: Divine Love**: the metaphor hides the content. The second is only 22 words and sits last in its group.
- **Borrow …** headings: "Borrow an Adult to Be One", "Borrow Someone’s Love", "Borrow the Love You Give", "Borrow a Guide to Build It", "Borrow Hindsight". The repeated verb makes them blur together in a narrow sidebar.
- **Appendix** and **Similar Therapies**: "Similar Therapies" is a heading with its own text, and its siblings "Schema Therapy" and "Internal Family Systems" are the actual similar therapies. They look like they should sit under it.

**Cut off or hard to read in a narrow sidebar**

- The "…" cut on **“And So That’s the Way…** is the only truncation, but it hurts.
- The lowercase subheads under "Journal With Each Hand" ("Which thoughts to talk to", "If no answer comes", "If you do get an answer") don't match the title-case style elsewhere. Read alone, they have no subject.
- "If It Tells You to Act" is only 38 words. It looks like a safety item but sits at the bottom of the journaling section.

**Sections that seem to be in the wrong place or at the wrong level**

- **"Betrayed by a Teacher"** is the last item under "The Inner Guide". Its natural home is next to "Don’t Buy an Outer Adult" and "Signs of Manipulation" under "Borrow an Adult to Be One". My situation 6 pointed to the Adult section and I only noticed this second spot afterward.
- **"If You Want to Kill Yourself"** (with "The Spiritual Side" and "This Precious Human Life") is a ## in the middle of Safety and Readiness. It comes after "When You’re the Danger" and is easy to miss. Someone in crisis needs it to be the most findable heading in the guide, and nothing marks it as urgent. Its 122 words are also small next to the spiritual material under it.
- **"Pain Makes It Harder"** and **"Listen to Your Body!"** sit under "The Urge to Escape". Body pain isn't escape. It overlaps "Your Body May Need Love" in Safety and Readiness, so a reader with unexplained flare-ups (situation 24) has two possible places.
- **"Forgiving Yourself"** is a #### under "Forgiving, Not Forgetting", but it follows "When Shame Gets Stuck" in topic. It reads as a late add-on, and at 66 words it is probably thin.
- **"Rediscover Play"** and **"Keeping Your Own Ground"** are both 0 words, and they sit directly under a # that has its own 146 words ("Reparenting in Real Life"). Other parts that have 0 words show the same blank-parent pattern, so this one is just inconsistent. The shape of Real Life is wide and deep: "Finding Your People" alone has seven ###s.
- **"Healing Old Patterns"** has just two ##s ("Re-do Painful Memories" with "Discipline Done Right?", and "Answer the Disciplinarian"). It looks like a stub of a part, and its content could sit under Practice or The Protector. "Answer the Disciplinarian" echoes "The Parent You Inherited" and "Protect Them From You".
- **"Speaking Up in Conflict"** holds "Write It. Don’t Send It Yet." (576 words). That is also what situation 19 (hurtful message online) needs, but the section reads as family or relationship conflict. "Catch the Hook Early" in the Practice part is a second plausible place.
- **"AI therapy"** is under "Finding a Therapist", which makes sense for professionals, but it is also what a person alone at 2 a.m. (situation 23) would look for. I didn't find it first.
- **"My Trips Came Too Soon"** (399 words) is a personal story at the very end of Altered States, away from "My Journey" at the start of the guide. It reads as a memoir piece rather than guidance.
- **"Conclusion and Appendix"** is one # that joins two different things. "Always Worthy of Love" is a closing note, and "Appendix" (0 words) holds reference material. They could be two parts.

**Anything that looks like it's in two places**

- **Divine love** appears at least four times: "Bigger Door: Divine Love" under The Nurturer, "Toward Divine Love" under The Inner Guide, "Ask God for a Loan 🫶🏿" (901 words, the longest ####), and "You Belong Everywhere". I can't tell which is the main one. My situation 30 was a guess between the first two.
- **Readiness** appears twice: "Dip Your Toes In First" and "Your Body May Need Love" in Safety and Readiness, and "Ready for the Guide?" under The Inner Guide. A cautious reader can't tell which applies.
- **Urges to escape** appear in several places: "The Urge to Escape", "Hooked on Healing", "Therapy or Escapism?", and "Spaced Out After Trips?". Situations 23, 28 and 36 all landed on "The Urge to Escape", and 28 and 36 are quite different (a numbing habit versus a craving in the moment).
- **Trust** appears in "Love Before Trust ❤️", "Earning Trust Day to Day", and "Who counts as trusted". The first two belong to different parents (Nurturer and Protector), which is confusing.
- **Inner critic and harshness** appear in "The Parent You Inherited", "Protect Them From You", "Answer the Disciplinarian", and "Spoil or Clamp Down?". Situation 4 (calling yourself an idiot like your father) has no single obvious heading.
- **"Invite Your Little One In"** and **"A Heart to Child Loop"** sit under The Inner Guide, but the next part is "Practice With Your Little One". The reader meets the little one twice, in two parts.
- **Therapists and AI** show up in "Finding a Therapist" and in the "Reparenting Therapists" and "Similar Therapies" areas of the appendix. It's unclear whether the appendix repeats that content or covers something different.
- **Somatic and body** content shows in "Your Body May Need Love", "Therapy Stirred You Up?", "Pain Makes It Harder", and "Listen to Your Body!".
