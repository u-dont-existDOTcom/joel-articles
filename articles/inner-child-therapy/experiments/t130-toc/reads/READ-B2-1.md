## 2. What gets in the way

**Headings that don't say what's under them**

- **"Chicken-and-Egg Problem"**: chicken and egg of what? Nothing in the sidebar says.
- **"Céline on the Inner Child"**: a reader who doesn't know who Céline is can't tell whether this is a source, a story, or a person in the guide.
- **"The Part That Came Here"**: the "part" and "here" could mean almost anything. It also sits under "Your Body May Need Love" with nothing visible linking it to the body.
- **"“And So That’s the Way…"**: cut off mid-quote, so the sidebar gives no topic at all. It sits under "When Shame Gets Stuck", so it's presumably a quote, but I can't tell.
- **"Smaller Door: Goodwill" / "Bigger Door: Divine Love"**: the door metaphor hides the topic. "Door" to what? The 22-word "Bigger Door" is nearly empty.
- **"Four Pairs That Hook"**: four pairs of what? Not guessable.
- **"Hearthwork: Take Turns"**: a coined term that is never introduced in the contents, and "take turns" doesn't say whether it's about friends, partners or a practice.
- **"Talk to the hand!✋🏿"**: a joke title. I wouldn't guess it's about the inner protector setting boundaries.
- **"Who counts as trusted"** (trusted for what?), **"What you mix"** (mix what?), **"Which thoughts to talk to"**: all too short to tell what's under them. The first two are inside "When Drugs Warp Reality".
- **"When One Life Ends" / "Missing Someone"**: from the title alone I couldn't tell whether this is about death, a breakup or a move. I only guessed it for the breakup situation.
- **"Real but Shallow?"** and **"A Passing Feeling"**: they don't name the thing that's real, shallow or passing.
- **"The Nurturer", "The Protector", "The Inner Guide"**: these are role names, not topics. A reader who hasn't read the guide can't tell which one handles self-criticism, love or limits.
- **"Healing Old Patterns"**: very broad, and what's under it is only memories and a disciplinarian.
- **"Conclusion and Appendix"**: two different things under one heading.

**Cut off or inconsistent in form**

- Heading casing and phrasing change from section to section. Some are Title Case, and others are sentence case ("If a past trip went badly", "If no answer comes", "If you do get an answer", "If it’s anxiety", "If you’d rather stop", "AI therapy"). This makes the sidebar read as if different people wrote it.
- Emoji appear on only a few headings ("Love Before Trust ❤️", "Ask God for a Loan 🫶🏿", "Talk to the hand!✋🏿"), so they look random rather than like a system.

**Sections that seem to be in the wrong place, level or parent**

- **Divine love is in two places.** "Bigger Door: Divine Love" (22 words) is under The Nurturer, and "Toward Divine Love" (347 words, with "Ask God for a Loan" at 901) is under The Inner Guide. The biggest block on the topic sits under the Guide, which isn't where I'd look. I'd expect the Nurturer.
- **The teacher and the outer adult are in two places.** "Don’t Buy an Outer Adult" and "Signs of Manipulation" are under "Borrow an Adult to Be One". "Betrayed by a Teacher" (102 words) is last under The Inner Guide. A reader who has a teacher they lean on won't know to look in both.
- **"Invite Your Little One In" and "A Heart to Child Loop" are under The Inner Guide**, but "Practice With Your Little One" is its own part. They look like practice material in the wrong part.
- **The body is in three places.** "Your Body May Need Love" is in Safety and Readiness, "Pain Makes It Harder" (with "Listen to Your Body!" at level 4) is in When Practice Gets Hard, and "Therapy Stirred You Up?" is under the body heading. "Therapy Stirred You Up?" isn't really about the body, so it looks like the wrong parent.
- **Escape is in three places.** "Therapy or Escapism?" (Altered States), "The Urge to Escape" (When Practice Gets Hard) and "Hooked on Healing" overlap. A reader in situation 23, 28 or 36 can't tell which is meant for them.
- **"Protect Them From You" (The Protector) and "When You’re the Danger" (Safety and Readiness)** look like the same topic in two parts.
- **Self-criticism is split.** "The Parent You Inherited" (with "Mr. Rogers’ Reframe") is under The Protector, and "Answer the Disciplinarian" is in Healing Old Patterns. "Discipline Done Right?" sits under "Re-do Painful Memories", so discipline appears in three places.
- **Healing Old Patterns has only two sections** and both are about childhood punishment and memory. It looks like it belongs in Practice With Your Little One or Building Your Inner Adult, not as its own part.
- **Reparenting in Real Life is a grab bag** of play, family, friends, therapist, conflict and grief. It is also where the biggest self-contained topics sit, such as "Finding a Therapist", yet the therapist topic isn't mentioned in the Safety part, where a reader in crisis might look.
- **Heading levels look uneven.**
  - "Rediscover Play" and "Keeping Your Own Ground" are level 2 with 0 words of their own, and the content sits in level 3 children.
  - "The Spiritual Side" and "This Precious Human Life" are under "If You Want to Kill Yourself", which is only 122 words. The crisis heading carries little text while its two children carry 618, so someone in crisis may stop at the short parent.
  - "Forgiving Yourself" (66 words) is a level 4 under "Forgiving, Not Forgetting", so it is easy to miss. It could be what situation 7 needs.
- **"Real in an Altered State"** (under "Therapy or Escapism?") and **"Real but Shallow?"** (When Practice Gets Hard) both ask about what's real, which confuses the two.
- **"My Trips Came Too Soon"** is the last item in Altered States, after the sections that warn about drugs. A reader planning a trip (situation 14) would probably find the warning sections first. The personal story may be what persuades them, and it is buried.
- **Safety ordering.** "If You Want to Kill Yourself" is the fifth heading in Safety and Readiness, after four other sections. In an emergency, the sidebar gives no signal that it is the urgent one. It isn't first, flagged, or in a separate place.
- **Empty parents (0 words)** are everywhere: Safety and Readiness, Building Your Inner Adult, The Nurturer, Practice With Your Little One, Healing Old Patterns, When Practice Gets Hard, Altered States & Retreats, Rediscover Play, Keeping Your Own Ground, Conclusion and Appendix, Appendix. They look like folders, which is fine, but a reader can't tell where an introduction ends and a list begins.
- **Tiny sections** such as "Spaced Out After Trips?" (29 words), "Quitting Meds or Alcohol" (35), "If It Tells You to Act" (38) and "If you’d rather stop" (35) look important but are barely there. They are probably pointers rather than real sections, and I'd wonder whether they belong inside the section above.
