## Process mistake

- broad rewrite;
- literal rewrite;
- fresh writer;
- internal cognition;

- Pro continuation.

That made each run restart the prose instead of accumulating a stable good surface.

## Constructive analogue of the successful repair loop

Use progressive forward construction.

1. Freeze the accepted preceding article text.
2. Under the next heading, generate only the next natural beat of thought.
3. Do not ask for the complete subsection or hand the writer the preservation ledger.
4. Judge the literal beat editorially.
5. If it is bad, replace only that beat; do not rebuild prior accepted text.
6. When a beat is accepted, freeze it byte-for-byte.
7. Generate the next beat from the accepted prefix and local article context.
8. Continue until a natural paragraph/subsection boundary exists.
9. Run the full preservation gate afterward.
10. Only then use Pangram on the complete model-generated boundary.
11. If Pangram localizes red spans, switch immediately to the proven red-region repair controller.

## Writer payload principle

The writer should see:
- accepted preceding prose;
- accepted model-generated prefix, if any;
- the one live social/cognitive pressure for the next beat;
- invention/safety boundaries.

The writer should not see:
- the complete preservation checklist;
- detector history;
- a large anti-pattern archive;
- all intended paragraph jobs;
- the full ending in advance.

This prevents the source ledger from becoming the prose outline.
