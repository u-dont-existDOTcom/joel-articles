(Next section, How Much Happiness Do You Know Is Possible?) When you wish someone well, what do you have in mind? Probably something like, “I want them to have enough to eat, a safe place to sleep, some people who like them, etc.” Wanting this for someone is a form of love (this is the kind babies already understand). It’s a good and important thing, this practical love. But the horizon of your wishes may be partly determined by how much happiness/peace/freedom you know to be possible.
