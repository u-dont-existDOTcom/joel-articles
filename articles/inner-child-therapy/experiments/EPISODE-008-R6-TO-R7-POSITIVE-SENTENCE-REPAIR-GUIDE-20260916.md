## Strong attacks considered but NOT selected as the main repair

1. **Delete most examples/inventories.** Rejected because many are frozen preservation units: alternative communication routes, hook behaviors, harm-reduction categories, adult roles, and safety outcomes carry unique functions. The repair should change how they move through the prose, not silently remove them.
2. **Remove all headings and make one continuous essay.** Rejected because R4 already tested a consolidation/continuous-through-line move and regressed to Pangram AI 1.0. The headings also perform genuine guide navigation.
3. **Shorten the whole section aggressively.** Rejected as a primary rule. Episode 007 and current working lessons show that overcompletion matters, but necessary sequencing still matters too. Compression is used only where a separate sentence is aftercare or a matched checklist beat.
4. **Make the prose quirky/slangy or add personal scenes.** Rejected because no owner provenance exists for new anecdotes/friends/memories and fake texture is forbidden. Humanization must come from thought movement and register, not camouflage.
5. **Freeze every short punchy sentence.** Rejected. Some (`Guilt isn't a verdict`, `Sometimes you've already explained enough`) may be useful; others can still function as model-like mini-conclusions. Each is judged by what it changes for the reader, not by sentence length.
6. **Eliminate every list of three.** Rejected as a mechanical rule. The article has a genuine three-role model (Nurturer/Protector/Leader or Witness/Protector/Guide depending context) and some safety inventories are semantically required. The target is to eliminate decorative/equalized taxonomy, not necessary grouped content.
7. **Replace every qualification with a firm claim.** Rejected because uncertainty around present grievance vs old material, one bad conversation vs a pattern, and can't/won't/can't-now is source-protected meaning. The repair is to make uncertainty arise from the lived situation rather than from generic counselor balancing.
8. **Change the exact Human/high somatic island because its list of modalities still looks list-like.** Rejected under the known-green guard and owner evidence. It is the calibration anchor and remains byte-for-byte frozen unless a real editorial defect independent of detector concerns appears.
