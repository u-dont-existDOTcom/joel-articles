# Episode 008 — Pro Pass B post-generation gate

Date: 2026-09-19
Status: **POST-GENERATION ONLY / DO NOT USE AS WRITER OUTLINE**

## Purpose

This gate holds the source obligations that were removed from the writer-facing prompt. Its job is to test fidelity after prose exists, not to tell the generator what sentence to write next.

A candidate is preservation-clean only if, across the complete outward/private-draft movement, it lets the reader:

- draft or internally say the response they actually want to give without sending it immediately;
- come back to it while remaining on their own side and still being fair;
- remove what is unfair or confusing without automatically erasing a legitimate protest, request, no, or boundary;
- keep responsibility or an apology that is genuinely theirs;
- avoid treating a better response as automatically softer or nicer;
- end up sending it, saying less, pausing, or sending nothing, without forcing those outcomes into a symmetrical list;
- make only a real, keepable promise if they tell the other person they will answer more fully later;
- avoid delaying necessary protection merely to complete the exercise.

## Fidelity and authority

Reject any candidate that invents Joel autobiography, facts, chronology presented as Joel's experience, named people, therapeutic claims, new safety rules, or claims about the other person's motives.

The accepted preceding article text remains context, not permission to alter it.

## Human-shape audit

After preservation passes, cold-read the literal candidate for:
- one-function-per-sentence marching;
- balanced synthesis that exists mainly to reconcile source bullets;
- explanatory aftercare after the thought has landed;
- outcome lists disguised as prose;
- generic counselor language;
- polished mini-lesson closure;
- fake casualness or fabricated specificity.

Do not repair a failed all-red candidate by adding another prohibition to the writer prompt. If the candidate is broadly model-shaped, reject it and change inference substrate or composition architecture.

Pangram is downstream evidence only after the candidate is preservation-clean and editorially plausible.
