Daniel Kahneman and Gary Klein came at intuition from two camps of research that were supposed to be at odds, and the paper they wrote together is called [Conditions for Intuitive Expertise: A Failure to Disagree](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0016755). They start from intuition being "sometimes marvelous and sometimes flawed." Yours too, probably. So which kind is a given hunch? How sure it feels won't tell you. Roughly, they'd ask whether the situation is predictable at all, and whether you've been in it enough times to learn it. A hunch might be catching something real that your head hasn't put into words yet, and your head can check it, unless it's tired of not knowing and glad to hand the whole thing over, to a hunch or to somebody who sounds sure.
