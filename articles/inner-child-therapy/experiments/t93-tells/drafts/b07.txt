Rumi says you have to keep breaking your heart until it stays open. Some people find that deep grieving gives way to warmth, or to feeling connected. Some feel held, in the body. Being loved can open grief, too. Someone takes care of you, and you think, "Why does this make me sad?" It can be sadness for the care that was missing. While you're present and still choosing, stay with it. Crying that deep can overwhelm you, too. It can retraumatize, mostly when nobody's with you or you've disappeared into the sadness. Once it's swamping you instead of opening you, slow down (like turning the song off for the night).
