Say you learned somewhere along the way that if you need someone, they leave. You probably never said it to yourself in words, but it steers you before you know why. That pull is one way of dealing with it. So is going along with things you don't like, or insisting you don't need anybody. Some people go along for months, blow up, then feel so bad about it they go right back to going along. With any of these, I'd ask what it's protecting you from before I'd call it a bad habit. (Sometimes a sweet is just a sweet, though, as The Inner Guide gets into.)
