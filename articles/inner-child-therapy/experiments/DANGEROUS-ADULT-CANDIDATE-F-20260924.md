## When the Present-Day Adult Is Dangerous to the Child

There's another reason to leave the child alone that has nothing to do with being too flooded. The adult can be fully present and still not be safe for vulnerability. This isn't about having a strange or religious belief, an intrusive thought you hate, being angry, using charged language, having an unwanted fantasy, or having done something harmful in the past. None of that by itself tells you what somebody intends now. The line here is present endorsement and intent: whether the adult currently wants or enjoys frightening, humiliating, exploiting, or harming what's vulnerable.

If that's where you are, being calm enough to pause and choose doesn't solve the problem. Don't bring the child forward even for a minute to see what happens. A frightened reaction proves that the child is frightened, not that you're safe. And if producing that fear is rewarding, then using the child as a test has already made the exercise part of the harm.

The child can stay out while the adult gets the attention. Something brought you here, and there is still plenty to look at without using the child to do it. The identity or stance itself becomes part of the material: what you get from it, what it has cost, who it serves, and whether your life and happiness look anything like what you expected. If you call yourself sovereign while also saying an urge or an authority has to be obeyed, that is part of the same adult work. The authority might be a doctrine, a group, a spirit, or a master. Sovereignty here has to include being able to stop and question what you're being told, refuse it, and change your mind. Otherwise something else is making the choice.

Care does not have to stop while that gets worked out. Listening to somebody is different from praising cruelty, fighting them about theology, or helping them become better at exploiting people. But a promise alone does not make child contact safe either. Protective credibility comes from repeated choices not to exploit vulnerability, including when doing it would feel rewarding. Until that exists, changing the route does not change the gate. A childhood photo is still a way of bringing the child in. So is hypnosis, or imagining a borrowed parent or spiritual figure making the contact for you. Inner dialogue is the most obvious route, but not the only one.

For some people, this may be work that needs steady care from actual people more than another exercise done alone or with a bot.
