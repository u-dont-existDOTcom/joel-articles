# Repeats at the end of The Inner Guide Comes Later

Scope: P5 = B238, P6 = B239, P7 = B240, P8 = B241, checked against the whole article. All four are in "The Inner Guide Comes Later" (called Inner Guide below).

## 1. Repeats, in article order

1. B28, B146, B241: own it, repair it, act differently
- B28, My Journey (study chart): "responsibility, remorse, rectify, release"
- B146, Love Doesn't Have to Wait for Trust: "learn or repair now, and how would you act differently"
- B241, Inner Guide: "you see it was wrong, you make amends"
- Later instances: the path is told again. B241 adds the Buddha, the king, and remorse as a signal worth attending to. B70 and B144 say it too.
- Better: B146; there repair is the point, while in B241 it is a contrast with the jingle.

2. B41, B240: ugly thoughts don't make you bad
- B41, When the Present-Day Adult Is Dangerous to the Child: "Everybody gets scary thoughts they'd rather not have sometimes"
- B240, Inner Guide: "Minds come up with ugly and embarrassing stuff all the time"
- Later instance: the reassurance is told again. It adds the funeral example, thoughts opposite to your beliefs, and what the Guide does with the thought.
- Better: B240, because it shows what to do; B41 is a passing aside.

3. B123, B238: act where you can (weaker)
- B123, When the Spiritual Relationship Hurts: "act where it's yours to act"
- B238, Inner Guide: "go do the next thing that matters to you and can move today"
- Later instance: adds practical steps (write down what you're waiting on), but the principle is told again.
- Better: B238, the practical form; B123 is a one-line aside.

4. B173, B240: let a stray thought go
- B173, Start With Whatever Showed Up: "Let a stray thought go as just thinking"
- B240, Inner Guide: "lets the jingle go"
- Later instance: the instruction is told again. It adds the case and "what you'd stand behind".
- Better: B240, because it gives a case; B173 is one sentence.

5. B238, B239 (adjacent): do what matters and can move
- B238, Inner Guide: "go do the next thing that matters to you and can move today"
- B239, Inner Guide: "she matters more to you and you can do something about her"
- Later instance: the cat restates B238's test. Only the one-sentence triage for a mess in your head is new.
- Better: B238, because it states the rule and why (waiting isn't laziness).

6. B240, B241 (adjacent): no spotless mind
- B240, Inner Guide: "The Guide isn't there to police every thought"
- B241, Inner Guide: "expected anybody to be the spotless-minded type"
- Later instance: the opening sentence ("either") is told again. The rest adds the Buddha's methods, "kamma is what you intend", and the king.
- Better: B240, because it comes with an example; B241 echoes it.

## 2. Contradictions

None firm. One borderline: B63 (Catch the Hook Before the Story Takes Over), "Transient feelings and thoughts can be meaningful signals", against B241 (Inner Guide), "just not giving it any attention", and B240, "lets the jingle go". B63 warns against throwing thoughts away; B240 and B241 say to let a thought go. The difference (a random jingle versus a body or danger signal) is implied, never stated.
