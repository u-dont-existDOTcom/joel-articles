Forced adulthood can get into your head too, where it wants everything as spotless as that kitchen. At your grandpa's funeral, some dumb jingle starts up in your head and you nearly laugh during the eulogy. That doesn't mean you didn't love him, or that something's wrong with you. Minds come up with ugly and embarrassing stuff all the time, some of it the opposite of what you believe, and bodies do their own thing too. The Guide isn't there to police every thought. It just notices the jingle. You didn't mean it, and it isn't who you are, so it can go, and you can do what you'd stand behind, which here is probably squeezing your mom's hand and listening.
