Picture your crew from work, or your church, deciding tomorrow that you're out. I'd want your little one to have somewhere it belongs that can't vote it out, and that's where the Guide points. For a lot of people that's the woods behind the house, or the sea. For others it's God, or whatever their tradition puts at the very bottom of things, or just the fact that they're here at all. It sinks in on long walks, and in prayer or meditation, a lot more than it ever does in a book. You'd still be sad about the crew. You just wouldn't feel like you'd stopped existing.
