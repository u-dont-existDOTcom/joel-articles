The Guide can also point you toward a kind of belonging that doesn't cost you yourself. For some people that's nature, or just being alive at all. For others it's whatever their tradition calls ultimate, like God, the universe, the Tao or the Three Jewels. It doesn't stay an idea for long if you spend real time outside, or sit with it in prayer or meditation. And once you've got that underneath you, the group chat going quiet stops feeling like the end of the world.
