Picture your crew from work, or your church, deciding tomorrow that you're out. I'd want your little one to have somewhere it belongs that can't vote it out, and that's where the Guide points. Some people find that in the woods behind the house. Some find it in God, or in whatever their tradition puts at the very bottom of things, and some in the plain fact of being here at all. It sinks in on long walks, and in prayer or meditation, a lot more than it does in a book. You'd still be sad about the crew, but you wouldn't feel like you'd stopped existing.
