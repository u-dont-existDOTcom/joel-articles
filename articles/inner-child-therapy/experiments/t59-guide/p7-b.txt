Your mind won't always behave, either, and demanding that it should is just forced adulthood moving indoors. Say some dumb jingle starts up in your head at your grandpa's funeral and you nearly laugh during the eulogy. It doesn't mean you didn't love him, and nothing's wrong with you. Minds come up with ugly, contradictory and embarrassing stuff all the time, and the body does its own thing too. The Guide doesn't police every thought. It notices the jingle, knows you didn't mean it and it isn't who you are, and goes with what you'd stand behind, like squeezing your mom's hand and listening. The jingle will fade.
