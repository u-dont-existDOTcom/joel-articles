Forced adulthood can move into your head too, and then it wants your thoughts as clean as that kitchen. At your grandpa's funeral, some dumb jingle starts up in your head and you nearly laugh during the eulogy. That doesn't mean you didn't love him, or that something's wrong with you. Minds come up with ugly, contradictory and embarrassing stuff all the time, and bodies do their own thing too. The Guide isn't there to police every thought. It notices the jingle, knows you didn't mean it and it isn't who you are, and goes with what you'd stand behind, like squeezing your mom's hand and listening. The jingle can fade on its own.
