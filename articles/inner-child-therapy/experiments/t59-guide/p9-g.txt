Picture your crew from work, or your church, deciding tomorrow that you're out. I'd want your little one to have somewhere it belongs that can't vote it out. For some people that's the ocean, or just being alive at all. For others it's God or the Tao, whatever their tradition puts at the very bottom of things. The Guide can point you there, and long walks outside will make it more than an idea, the same as prayer or meditation will. Getting voted out would still hurt, but it wouldn't be life or death.
