Not every bargain is with people, though. The Guide can point your little one toward somewhere it belongs no matter what, without signing anything away. That might be a forest you love, or just the fact of being here at all. It might be God or the Tao, whatever your tradition puts at the very bottom of things. You'll know it better from an afternoon by the river than from reading about it, and a long sit in prayer or meditation works too. Once that's under your feet, losing a friend's approval still hurts, but it doesn't feel like life or death anymore.
