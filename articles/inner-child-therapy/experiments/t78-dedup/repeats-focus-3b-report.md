# Repeats and contradictions, focus range B294-B344

Ordered by the block in the focus range (the later one if both are). Quotes are exact; section names are shortened.

## 1. Repeats

1. B205 (Parent You Inherited), B296 (No Clear Child Yet), B298 (Survival to Experimental Play). “hide that part of their authentic self”; “asking might have gotten you in trouble”; “when curiosity or mistakes got you punished”. Point: punished for being yourself, you played a role to belong. B296 and B298 feel like the same thing told twice; B298 adds the attention spent reading danger. B298 carries it better, with the mechanism.

2. B298-B299 (Survival to Experimental Play), B318 and B320 (Let the Child Be Bad at Things). “People grow into who they are by trying things on”; “For identity you also need some experiments out in real life”; “play becomes part of forming your identity”. Point: identity forms by trying things on, as play. B318-B320 tell it again, adding the practice (a new interest, surviving being a beginner). B318-B320 is the natural place, as the practice step.

3. B61 (Dangerous Adult), B328 (Inner Guide). “Everybody gets scary thoughts they'd rather not have sometimes, and they don't make you a bad person”; “Minds come up with ugly and embarrassing stuff all the time”. Point: unwanted thoughts don't make you bad. B328 adds the funeral jingle and the Guide's response, but the reassurance is told again. B328 carries it better; in B61 it is a side remark.

4. B329 (Inner Guide), B387 (Forgiveness). “one first recognizes a fault, makes amends, and then restrains themself”; “Repair what you can, by apologizing or making amends”. Point: the same steps after wrongdoing. B387 adds remorse and ending self-punishment; B329 adds intention and the intrusive-thought exception. Partial; B387 is the natural home.

5. B310 (Four Pairs), B331 (Inner Guide). “Whose approval suddenly feels like I can't live without it?”; “If they stop liking me, I won't belong anywhere”. Point: catch the moment approval feels essential. B331 adds the reminder of a deeper belonging; the watch itself is told again. Partial.

6. B10 (Chicken-and-Egg), B331 (Inner Guide). “pick another one. Your future self might work, or the captain of a ship”; “this job doesn't have to be called the Guide. The Wise One works, or your Inner Teacher”. Point: you may rename the role. B331 adds names and the cult-survivor caution, but the permission is told again. B10 is the natural place.

7. B333 (Timing), B412 (IFS). “Otherwise the Guide just overrules your little one in the name of something higher”; “it has to work with the Nurturer and Protector and come after them”. Point: the Guide must come after the Nurturer and Protector. B412 adds only the contrast with IFS's Higher Self. B333 is the natural place.

8. B334 (Timing), B110 (Ask God for a Loan), B382 (Hearthwork). “Has it reached your little one yet?”; “You can have an enormous spiritual feast upstairs while the child is still starving downstairs”; “From a spiritual experience to your little one”. Point: a spiritual opening counts only when the love reaches the little one, then others. Told again; B382 adds that community can come first. B110 carries it best, with the image.

9. B22 (My Journey), B339 (How Much Happiness). “Conditional love felt radically insufficient, so I kept looking for something deeper”; “Sometimes care isn't the main thing missing”. Point: ordinary love or care may not reach a deeper need. B339 adds the existential framing and “zest”, but a reader feels told again. B22 carries it, with the personal story.

10. B338 and B340 (How Much Happiness). “Then follow the curiosity, and skip pretending to believe”; “Curiosity can be enough here”. Point: curiosity about bigger happiness is enough; no need to believe. B340 adds a sample thought and the “limits of what's possible” line; the permission is told twice, two blocks apart. B340 is the fuller form.

11. B52 (Body Might Need Love First), B344 (Stays Shallow). “shorter, or shallower, at a better time of day”; “do less of the practice, earlier in the day or for a shorter time”. Point: when a session disturbs sleep or the day, shorten it or move it earlier. B344 adds the nightmare case and the dream test, but the adjustment is told again. B52 is the more general place.

## 2. Contradictions

1. B299 (Survival to Experimental Play) and B322 (Inner Guide) vs B333 (Timing). “You can usually reparent and build who you are at the same time”; “You ask hard things of it so it can grow” versus “the direction can grow once there's a clear little one in there to guide”. With no clear child yet, stretching starts now in the first two but waits in B333, and no reason is given. Mild; B322's “at its own pace” softens it.

## 3. Callbacks that rely on distant sections (not counted as repeats)

- B316 (Four Pairs) and B419 (Teachers and Practice Resources): “Key developed this identity and differentiation portion of the method”; “the identity and differentiation work from Bowen I mentioned earlier”. Marked as a callback by the author.
- B241 (Altered States Can Deepen the Therapy) points ahead to B334 (Timing): “it's the same test as in Timing Is Individual, further down”; “whether it made your love deeper or wider”. Signalled by the author.
- B342 (Somatic Heart Opening) calls back B4 (opening): “like turning the song off for the night”; “turn the song off for tonight”. It also restates B4's caution that crying too deep can retraumatize, so some readers will feel it twice.

## 4. The five repeats a reader would most notice

Repeats 10, 1, 2, 8 and 7 (the first three sit closest together; 8 and 7 recur the most).
