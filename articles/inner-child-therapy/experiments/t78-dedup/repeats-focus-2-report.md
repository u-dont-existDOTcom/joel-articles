## Contradictions (all mild)

1. B203 (Adult Voice Feels Fake) vs B235 (Urge to Escape). B203: "For some people it only stops feeling fake in an altered state, like on MDMA or deep in meditation." B235: "I’d hold off until there’s enough Nurturer and Protector around." One reads as an open door without caution, the other says wait.

2. B44 (Before You Go Deep) vs B231 (Change the Strategy). B44: "A full break from your little one is a last resort." B231: "I'd change course sooner (stopping counts)." Perhaps different things (pausing the relationship versus changing practice), but a reader may see two stopping thresholds.

3. B25 (My Journey) vs B200 (Adult Voice Feels Fake). B25: "it felt like I was making it up at first", and he kept going. B200: "unintentional gaslighting to the little one to ask to connect with them when you don't believe they're even real". Neither addresses the other.
