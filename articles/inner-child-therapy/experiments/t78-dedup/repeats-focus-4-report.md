# Repeats and contradictions: focus range B345 to B419

Every item involves a block in B345 to B419. Quotes are exact; each instance names its section.

## 1. Repeats, in article order

1. **Borrowed help is handed back to you.** B12 (The Chicken-and-Egg Problem) "more and more of those little parts you can do yourself"; B347 (Borrowed Adulthood in Relationship) "start doing it yourself, even that calendar bit"; B380 (Hearthwork: Taking Turns) "give it back to you"; B405 (Appendix, How This Relates to Schema Therapy) "the therapist's version becomes yours bit by bit". Each names a new helper, but the principle is told again; B405 adds only a label. Better: B12, the definition.

2. **Judge people by what they keep doing, not by words or promises.** B33 (Don't Give the Inner Adult Away) "look at their track record"; B84 (Borrow Hindsight) "What the current one keeps doing is still what you go by"; B354 (Opening up honestly to the right people at the right pace) "People can earn more of you by how they handle the smaller stuff"; B359 and B360 (Other Folks Also Have Hooks) "count on what they keep showing you they can do", "as useful as their prior promises were". New situation each time, same rule five times. Better: B359 to B360, fullest and in its own section.

3. **Relational hurt needs other people.** B45 (Before You Try to Go Deep) "reaching toward people is part of taking care of your little one too"; B346 (Borrowed Adulthood in Relationship) "that kind of hurt can only heal so far on your own". Told again at B346's opening; the section then adds one person carrying many jobs. Better: B346, the section built on it.

4. **Gratitude or positive wording must not override grief.** B104 (Your Loan is PRE-APPROVED!) "I’m not pushing “toxic positivity.”"; B374 (Speak Toward What You Are Building) "we don't hide from shadow stuff with toxic positivity". Same caution, same phrase; B374 adds that harm gets called harm. Better: B374, wider (harm, anger, responsibility).

5. **Understanding why harm happened doesn't cancel responsibility.** B134 (Love Doesn't Have to Wait for Trust) "if it hurt people or damaged a life, that may need repair"; B175 ("And So That’s the Way It Happened!") "whatever repair is owed is still owed"; B386 and B387 (How to Forgive Without Forgetting) "Seeing how they got there doesn't make it okay.", "the responsibility stays". B386 to B387 add boundaries, distance and self-forgiveness steps, but the core is told a third and fourth time. Better: B386 to B387, the dedicated section.

6. **Dating or new people as proof you are lovable.** B153 (When Healing Turns Into Checking) "proof that you're good and lovable"; B348 (Borrowed Adulthood in Relationship) "hoping someone will make it all stop hurting"; B350 (First Social Connections, Done Right) "if you need them to prove you’re lovable". B350 is mostly told again; B348 adds that romance won't rescue or reward you. Better: B348, fuller.

7. **Sending care to someone is inner work; contact is separate.** B172 (Just Like Me) "Getting in touch is a separate decision"; B393 (When One Life Has Ended and the Next Hasn’t Begun) "or for getting in touch when that's right, and your boundaries still apply". B393 adds that it doesn't replace grieving; the contact point is told again. Better: B172, first and plainer.

8. **The Guide comes after the Nurturer and Protector.** B333 (Timing Is Individual) "the Guide just overrules your little one in the name of something higher"; B412 (Appendix, How This Relates to IFS) "it has to work with the Nurturer and Protector and come after them". B412 adds the Higher Self comparison; the sequencing rule is told again. Better: B333, which gives the reason.

9. **Forgiving too soon is a show.** B384 (How to Forgive Without Forgetting) "an enlightened-looking act, with the wound gone underground"; B387 (same section) "Forgive too soon and it's only a show". Three blocks apart; B387 adds only that some harms take years. Likewise B385 "Picture everything that had made them who they were" and B387 "come back to how whoever did it got to be someone who could do that" give one exercise twice. Better: B384, which says why.

## 2. Contradictions (both mild)

1. B360 (Other Folks Also Have Hooks) "so that we can hold high expectations, and still feel prepared for their failure to meet them" versus B386 (How to Forgive Without Forgetting) "Understanding somebody's limitations should make my expectations more realistic". High versus more realistic, with no word on how they fit.
2. B358 (Other Folks Also Have Hooks) "don't go only by the one fight where they crossed your boundary" versus B362 (same section) "even once, old stuff or not, get safe first". Don't weigh one incident versus one is enough to act; reconcilable as character versus safety, but never separated.

- B397 (Finding a Therapist to Borrow Adulthood) "I'll talk more about those in the appendix" is paid off by B405 (Appendix, Schema Therapy) "schema therapy is the closest to this guide".
- B401 (You Are Worthy of Love. Always.) "back in Your Loan is PRE-APPROVED!" and B406 (Appendix, Schema Therapy) "like back in Ask God for a Loan" point to B99 to B110.
- B408, B409, B410 (Appendix, How This Relates to IFS) point back to B222 to B226, B8 ("like back in The Chicken-and-Egg Problem") and B207 ("(The Parent You Inherited again)"); B410 restates borrowing one job at a time.
- B419 (Appendix, Teachers and Practice Resources) "I mentioned earlier" relies on B316, which credits the same work.
