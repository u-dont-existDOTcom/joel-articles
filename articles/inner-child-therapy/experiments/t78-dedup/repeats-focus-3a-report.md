Entries follow the instance inside the range; section names are shortened to their first words.

## 2. Contradictions

1. B272 (My Psychedelic Experiences) and B284 (If You Want to Kill Yourself). "don’t call the cops and expect it to go well" / "A crisis line or an emergency number counts". B272 is about an illegal-drug episode, but "In general" widens it; nothing says how they fit.

2. B292 (This human life), first sentence against last. "when I had the least interest in continued existence" / "don’t throw away the ticket". The first reads as praise for not wanting to exist, the second as keep living; nothing reconciles them.
