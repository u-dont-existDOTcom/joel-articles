# Repeats and contradictions: opening through "Before You Try to Go Deep" (B1 to B65)

## 1. Repeats
1. B9 (Chicken-and-Egg), B22 (My Journey). "small and like a never-ending therapy session" / "processing your wounded inner child isn't freedom either". Point: inner-child work alone is too small a life. B22 adds spirituality as the bigger horizon. B22 sits better.
2. B9, B325 (The Inner Guide). "over-rely on their discipline or their mission" / "finally having your act together". Point: discipline used to avoid being cared for. B325 adds the kitchen example and a way out. B325 carries it.
3. B12 (Chicken-and-Egg), B72 (Borrow One Competency at a Time). "somebody else may have to do one part of the adult role" / "Borrow one function at a time". Point: borrow one adult function. B72 adds the lenders. B72 carries it; B12 defines it.
4. B15, B16 (Chicken-and-Egg, adjacent), B232 (When to Change the Strategy). "start actually doing some of it in the rest of your life" / "The sessions are practice for that." / "try a tiny bit of it out here". Point: carry care into daily life. B16 restates B15; B232 adds the good-session-then-relapse case. B16 carries it.
5. B18, B25 (My Journey), B271 (My Psychedelic Experiences Came A Bit Too Soon). "spiritual part of me felt completely free" / "it didn't reach everything" / "bring that love down from the heavens". Point: spiritual freedom left the child stuff untouched. B25 adds specifics. B18 carries it.
6. B23, B24 (My Journey, adjacent). "I was well-loved as a child" / "I wasn't lacking love". Point: a loving childhood. B24 adds nothing; B28 says it again. B23 carries it.
7. B43 (Before You Try to Go Deep), B53 (Your Body Might Need Some Love First). "to prove to themselves they can take it" / "toughen themselves up in a cauldron". Point: don't push to prove you can take it. B53 adds the learning edge. B53 carries it.
8. B43, B231 (When to Change the Strategy). "going in at all keeps making it worse" / "change course sooner (stopping counts)". Point: if it makes things worse, stop. B231 adds the "nope" part. B43 carries it.
9. B45, B346 (Borrowed Adulthood in Relationship). "reaching toward people is part of taking care of your little one" / "can only heal so far on your own". Point: relational hurt needs people. B346 adds one person doing many jobs. B346 carries it.
10. B47, B139 (Love Doesn't Have to Wait for Trust). "slower exhales and a little gentle self-massage" / "slower exhales may help you stay with them". Point: slower exhales for stomach tension. B139 adds that calm doesn't make the complaint wrong. B47 carries it.
11. B53, B203 (When the Adult Voice Feels Fake), B189 (Use Childhood Photographs Gently). "They aren't evidence that what you saw in them happened" / "that alone doesn't prove they happened" / "stories aren't necessarily the truth". Point: dreams, images and memories from sessions are not proof. B189 adds the balloon study; B203 adds nothing. B189 carries it.
12. B55 (You Don't Need an Inner Monologue), B215 (Start With Whatever Showed Up). "don't invent one" / "different forms, like as a voice or just a hunch". Point: the reply needn't be a voice. B215 adds a safety screen on acting. B55 to B57 carries it.
13. B59, B64 (When the Present-Day Adult Is Dangerous to the Child). "not the place to start" / "their little one stays out of it". Point: keep the child out for people who enjoy scaring. B64 adds loving-kindness as the remedy. B64 carries it.
14. B61, B62 (same section). "why would you bring your inner child into the middle of them" / "before they bring their inner child anywhere near it". Point: hold the child back while sorting out scary thoughts. B62 adds the inherited-critic check. B62 carries it.

## 2. Contradictions
1. Feeling worse: fine or a reason to back out. B27 (My Journey) "therapy can sometimes make you feel worse than before" and B122 (Not Every Hero Wears A Cape) "might make you feel worse at first", against B43 "going in at all keeps making it worse" and B231. Probably reconcilable through the next-few-days test in B51 to B52, which B27 and B43 never point to.
2. Stopping: last resort or fine. B44 (Before You Try to Go Deep) "A full break from your little one is a last resort." against B231 (When to Change the Strategy) "change course sooner (stopping counts)". A full break may differ from stopping an exercise, but the text does not say so.
