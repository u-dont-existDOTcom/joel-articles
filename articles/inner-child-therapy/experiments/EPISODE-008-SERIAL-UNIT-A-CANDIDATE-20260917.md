One bad fight can make somebody look terrible. You replay it afterward and it starts to feel like you've finally found out who they really are. Then they come back later and actually talk about what you said. They still disagree, but now you can at least talk about it. Or it turns out you ran into a boundary of theirs and you still hate the boundary. The original fight doesn't magically become okay. It just looks different. One conversation? I don't know. I wouldn't make it carry that much weight.

I'd start paying attention when the ending itself starts to feel familiar. [Lindsay Gibson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VlNpgFWOLPw) keeps coming back to repeated behavior in her work on emotional immaturity, and that seems like the saner thing to watch. You bring up a problem, and somehow you're talking about something else while the original thing is still sitting there. Next time it's a different subject and somehow you're back in the same place.
