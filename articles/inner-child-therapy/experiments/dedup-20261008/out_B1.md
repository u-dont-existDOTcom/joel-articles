How to read this:

- SAID: the article already makes the point, for the same situation or close enough that a reader would feel told twice.

- NEW: nothing in the article makes this point.
- Draft sentences are numbered by draft and order: D1.1 is the first sentence of [D1]. Each is copied exactly, followed by the guide point it carries and that point's status in the article.
