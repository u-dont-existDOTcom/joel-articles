## Themes

- **Belonging bought with a performance.** Your little one learned "to hide that part of their authentic self which wasn't allowed, so they could keep belonging" (The Parent You Inherited), firm. A relationship held by a "fake normal, happy version of yourself" gets "more of the truth in, carefully" or less depth (Also Look Outward), advice ("I'd"). Later: "mistaking conditional approval for the deepest belonging" (The Inner Guide Comes Later).
- **Earning worth.** "There was never anything wrong with the child's worth to begin with." (The Chicken-and-Egg Problem), firm. Asking someone out "to prove you're lovable" makes them "part of the test" (Also Look Outward), firm. Later: "not proving our worth" (Let the Child Be Bad at Things).
- **Highs and enjoyment.** After an altered state, "I'd still hang on to what you felt, even once it's gone" (When the Adult Voice Feels Fake). The goal is "from weird to silly and playful" (The Chicken-and-Egg Problem). Both firm. Later: "The beautiful state can become a hook too." (Altered States Can Deepen the Therapy)
- **Caretaking and dependence.** Ending up "reassuring them they're not a bad person" is posed as a question (Also Look Outward). A teacher should "wean us from dependency altogether" (Don't Give the Inner Adult Away), firm. Later: being cared for "can be an enormous blessing" (Borrowed Adulthood in Relationship).
- **Hooks (Pema's shenpa).** Catching one, even late, earns "That's awesome I could notice it!" (Catch the Hook), firm. A feeling is "real as a feeling, but it doesn't settle who you are" (Catch the Hook), firm. Later: "Praise, mirroring, pity, attraction … can make the child hand over judgment voluntarily." (The Inner Guide Comes Later)
- **What's yours without an audience.** "what you like when nobody's grading you" (From Survival to Experimental Play), firm. Telling what's "authentically yours vs something planted or manipulated" takes time (Catch the Hook), firm. Later: "follow the curiosity rather than pretending to believe" (How Much Happiness Do You Know Is Possible?)
- **Caring without losing ground.** Rehearse a hard talk till it's good enough, "as long as what you needed to say is still in there" (Not Every Hero Wears A Cape), firm. Later: "without erasing either person" (The Inner Guide Comes Later).
- **Staying, distance, leaving.** "Keep the good people you've already got." (Also Look Outward), firm. A tradition can guide "until you develop your own internal compass" (Borrow One Competency at a Time), an option. If hurt or threatened "even once … get safe first" (Also Look Outward), a rule. Later: "stay when staying serves life, and leave when staying requires disappearance" (The Inner Guide Comes Later).
- **Slow and lifelong.** "The quest for self-knowledge is a life's journey" (Borrow Love), firm, yet "real growing up is not something you do forever" (When Healing Turns Into Checking), firm. Later: "Endless processing can become another way to avoid living." (Close the Session and Return to Life)

## Easy to flip

- "untangling from family emotional systems, religious orthodoxies … that pull you away from your own knowing": not family or faith as such. Untangling isn't leaving, and "requires" stays. "some conformity" is ordinary; the bargain is "self-betrayal", not "compromise".
- "can become a 'human doer'", "In its controlling form, caretaking can drift", "can all be real gifts … when": keep every "can", "controlling form" and "when". Not "caretakers keep people dependent" or "drop the gifts".
- "The unparented child takes those bargains": survival, not a flaw. Not only children of bad parents (Joel was "well-loved", My Journey).
- "noticing when praise, pity, fear, attraction, or resonance suddenly changes what you think you know": keep "suddenly"; changing your mind isn't the alarm. Feelings "can be meaningful signals" (Catch the Hook).
- "Commonly", "a quick map": not the full list. Keep "not only when something hurts" and "might disappear".
- "Am I protecting a real value, or trying to preserve a high?": open; either answer can be right.
- "The aim is not to stop enjoying pleasure, praise, gain, or belonging": no renunciation. The trouble is "conditions for having a self".
- "can happen inside", "Selective belonging still leaves room to belong" and "leave when staying would require disappearing": not "differentiation means leaving" or belonging less, nor "leave when you disagree".
- "autonomous faith is healthier than religious compliance" (and the other two pairs): firm comparisons within each kind. Not "religion is unhealthy".
- The ordinary-life list is "the movement", not a test. Others' views stop "automatically" replacing yours; you "notice"; caring about their feelings stays; "recognizably yourself", not identical everywhere; "question", not reject, "while holding the discomfort".
- "slow and lifelong" and "learns to tell the difference": no finish line, and no endless processing. It's the reader's judgment, not Joel's ruling.
- "Temporary distance may be necessary when a system won't allow it from inside": conditional, not advice. It's distance from that system, not from people, and danger doesn't wait ("get safe first", Also Look Outward).
- "return later on different terms" and "Sometimes the leaving is permanent": both possible, neither the goal. The leaving is permanent to avoid "disappearing again", not as punishment.
