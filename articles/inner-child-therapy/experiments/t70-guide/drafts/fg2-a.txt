The understanding that helps me is seeing how a person's conditioning, history, pain, and capacity shaped the harmful move they made. Given everything that had made them who they were by that moment, it was the only move they had. Hurt people often pass on pain they never worked through. You may have heard that from somebody telling you to forgive. When you see it for yourself, though, you might find yourself forgiving them, and if you've held them uniquely to blame, the rage can soften into something more accurate.
