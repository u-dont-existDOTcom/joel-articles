The understanding that helps me is seeing how a person's conditioning, history, pain, and capacity shaped the harmful move they made. From in there, given who they'd become by then, it was the only move they had. You may have heard that hurt people pass on pain they never worked through, usually from somebody telling you to forgive. When you see it for yourself, though, you might find yourself forgiving them, and if you've held them uniquely to blame, the rage can soften into something more accurate.
