It's not so hard to understand that words have power. They point your attention somewhere, so aim them at what you want more of, whether that's something you'd like to be able to do or a relationship. Say you tell yourself "I'm not dependent." Well, now you're thinking about being dependent. Being totally independent also isn't realistic nor ideal, and can even seem like just a reaction to the original question. We can often strike a nice nuanced poetic balance, like: "I'm provided for by the universe, like the birds & lilies Jesus reminded us of." With your little one, and in hypnosis especially, make it easy to picture: warmth, protection, steadiness, rest, whatever's really needed to melt into your happy place. You'll save a ton of money on vacations!
