Of the clinical approaches I know, schema therapy is the closest to this guide. Its therapists do what they call [limited reparenting](https://schematherapysociety.org/Limited-Reparenting): within professional limits, they give you some of what a good parent would, with warmth and firmness and some playfulness too. Over time you take that in, and it becomes your own "Healthy Adult," who looks after your "Vulnerable Child" and stands up to the punishing parent in your head. It sets limits, too. And it gets on with [adult life](https://schematherapy.com/id72.htm), like work and the things you've committed to. Sound familiar? That's a Nurturer, a Protector and a Guide by other names, and since the therapist's version becomes yours bit by bit, it's borrowed adulthood too.
