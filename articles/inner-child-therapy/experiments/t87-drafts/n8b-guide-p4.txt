I think the opposite trap can feel a lot like finally having your act together. It's forced adulthood, and it sounds like "Nope, all good!" from somebody with a fever who's cleaning the whole kitchen anyway. Not a lot of fun in that kitchen, and nobody gets very close to the person in it. If that's you, clamping down harder is going to feel like the fix, but it's just more of the same. I'd try croaking to someone you trust, "Could you pick up some soup?" Or maybe, "I'd really like to just watch a movie with you." If you keep swinging between spoiling and clamping down, try a middle way. Stay warm and say clearly what happens next, and if you need a minute, say when you'll be back.
