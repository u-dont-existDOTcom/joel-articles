Daniel Kahneman and Gary Klein came at intuition from two camps of research that were supposed to be at odds, and the paper they wrote together is called [Conditions for Intuitive Expertise: A Failure to Disagree](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0016755). They start from intuition being "sometimes marvelous and sometimes flawed." Yours too, probably. So which kind is a given hunch? How sure it feels won't tell you. Roughly, they'd ask two things. Is this the kind of situation that's predictable at all? And have you been in it enough times to learn it? A hunch might be catching something real that your head hasn't put into words yet, so listen to it, and then let your head check it. I wouldn't let either one decide by itself, though. Your head can get tired of not knowing and be glad to hand the whole thing over, to a hunch or to somebody who sounds sure.
