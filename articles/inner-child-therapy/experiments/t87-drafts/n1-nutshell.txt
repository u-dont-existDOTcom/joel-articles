Growing up, we all learned how to read what happens to us and how to react to it. We also learned how to treat ourselves, mostly from how people treated us. Nobody asked us first. Some of that still works fine, and some was made for a crisis that ended years ago. Reparenting means building a grown-up in you who's in a real relationship with the little one you still are, as a Nurturer, an Adult Protector (hereafter just the Protector) and a Guide. That grown-up catches an old pattern as it kicks in and takes care of what needs care. Over time it figures out better ways to respond, too. That's a lot of effort at first. With practice, though, the care starts coming on its own. You notice you're wiped out and you just rest, and nobody has to call a meeting in your head about it. Then less of your life goes into managing yourself, and more of it goes into living (and playing!). We can still look closer when something's off. It just doesn't have to be a full-time job.
