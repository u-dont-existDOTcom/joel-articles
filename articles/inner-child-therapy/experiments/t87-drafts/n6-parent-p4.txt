That voice can get into your reparenting, too. A good routine kept in the warden's tone still feels like punishment, so your little one goes along with it for a while and then starts avoiding it. If answering the voice feels disloyal, it helps to give it a name of its own, like the Critic (it doesn't have to be a flattering name), so you're arguing with what it says and leaving your parents out of it. You could also write its lines down, with who said them and when. Comfort your little one first, then write a reply to each line, firm or funny but never mean (ST-CA has this as an exercise, section 18.12.2).
