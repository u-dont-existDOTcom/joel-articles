The other one shows up when you get close to something painful, as a sudden pull to do anything but feel it (even a yawn can come with an "I'm so done with this" feeling). It might believe there's no grown-up around who could hold what's coming up. I'd want out too. That yawn might actually be the opposite of checking out, though. Re-evaluation Counseling, a kind of peer counseling, counts yawning as emotional discharge, a feeling on its way out. The skeptic and the pull both started out guarding you against something that really hurt, and they might still be at it when it's mostly hurting you. If you treat either one like an enemy, or like proof that something's wrong with you, it'll probably just think "See? I knew it," and stay in charge. I wonder what it would do if it didn't have to.
