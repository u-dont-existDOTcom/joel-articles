Reparenting is usually talked about as something you do alone, or with a therapist. But your little one got hurt by people, so some of the healing has to happen with people too. Often it's mostly one person who ends up covering the grown-up jobs for you. They cook, they keep track of the appointments, they drive, they're the one who says it'll be okay, or they're just the competent person in the room. Maybe you're picturing them right now. Having someone like that while you're struggling can be an enormous blessing.
