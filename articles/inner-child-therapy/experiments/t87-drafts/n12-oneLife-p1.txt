Your little one can get really loud in the gap after one life ends and before the next one has formed. A breakup will do it, or moving to another country, an illness, losing your faith or a role, your youngest moving out. So can leaving a family or community, or staying in one on new terms. A therapist or helper stepping back can do it too, after you've grown a lot. Tibetan Buddhism calls an in-between state a bardo. You don't have to buy what it teaches about death and rebirth to use the everyday part: endings leave a stretch where the old you is gone and the new one hasn't shown up.
