That pull is one way of handling something you learned to expect, like "if I need someone, they'll leave." Usually nothing in your head says it out loud. There's just a sense of how it'll go, and it shapes what you do before you can say why. Going along with things you don't like is another way of handling it, and so is insisting you don't need anybody. Some people swing between going along for months and blowing up, then feel so bad about it they go right back to going along. Those can all be doing the same job, so I'd look at what a response is doing for you more than at what it looks like. (Sometimes a sweet is just a sweet, though, as The Inner Guide gets into.)
