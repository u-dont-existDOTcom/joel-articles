Borrowed Adulthood in Relationship

Reparenting is usually talked about as something you do alone, or with a therapist. But your little one got hurt by people, so some of the healing has to happen with people too. Often it's mostly one person carrying a lot of it, maybe the one who drives you to your appointments and says it'll be okay. Maybe you're picturing them right now. Having someone like that while you're struggling can be an enormous blessing. If it's more distributed, with various people you can count on for small parts, that can be more sustainable, even if it doesn't feel as monumental.

Over time, reparenting can help you take in some of what they've been doing for you, and start doing it yourself, even that calendar bit. What you're after is care your little one can count on from more than one place, while the inner relationship stays precious and important. Other jobs can go to friends or community, outsource to the tools or pros, whatever works. You'll find the balance is dynamic between inner & outer reliance, and going too far in either extreme isn't generally the healthiest.
