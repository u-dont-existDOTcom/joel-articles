Let the Child Be Bad at Things

Reflection can show you what got sMothered (typo?) or suppressed. For identity you also need some experiments out in real life, so go try something new just for you. Maybe a foraging group, or a painting class. Stargazing can be awe-inspiring, and you might even see a UFO! Notice what holds your attention when nobody is impressed.

The old parent in your head may demand immediate talent, though, and then the first time you're awkward at it, that's "evidence" that you're stupid or clumsy. Or you hear, "Come on, you're not really the type." I think your little one is listening to see if anybody's going to stick up for them. The grown-up you're building has to be the one who does: "It's okay if we're bad at this, nobody's on trial. We're just seeing if we like it."

Give a new interest enough tries to get past the humiliation of being a beginner, when you keep telling people, "Sorry, I'm new." Maybe after that you still genuinely dislike it, and then you may quit. You may also discover a part of yourself that never had permission to exist.
