The old parent in your head may demand immediate talent, though, and then your first awkward attempt becomes "evidence" that you're stupid or clumsy. Or that you aren't "really" that kind of person (which is still the old parent talking). The grown-up you has to protect your little one from that verdict right as it lands, since patching things up afterward won't be enough on its own. Something like "You're allowed to be bad at this, and you're not proving your worth here. We're finding out whether you enjoy it."
