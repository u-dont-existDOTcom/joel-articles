The old parent in your head may demand immediate talent, though. Then your first awkward attempt becomes "evidence" that you're stupid or clumsy. Or that you're not "really" that kind of person, which is a lot to decide from one try. Patching things up with your little one afterward is good, but the grown-up you has to protect them from that verdict as it lands: "You're allowed to be bad at this. You're finding out whether you enjoy it, not proving your worth."
