This is pl/ork in its most literal form, where play becomes part of forming who you are. Give a new interest enough repetitions to get past the humiliation of being a beginner, and one of those times you might notice you forgot to be embarrassed. Maybe after that you still think "I just don't like this," and it doesn't sound like that old internal parent. Then you may quit. You may also discover a part of yourself that never had permission to exist.
