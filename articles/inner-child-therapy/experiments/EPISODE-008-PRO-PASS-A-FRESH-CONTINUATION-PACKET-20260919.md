## Source meaning for this outward/private-draft movement

The practice should let the reader:
- draft or internally say the response they actually want to give without sending it immediately;
- come back to it while remaining on their own side and still being fair;
- remove what is unfair/confusing without automatically erasing a legitimate protest, request, no, or boundary;
- keep responsibility or an apology that is genuinely theirs;
- understand that a better response is not automatically a softer/nicer response;
- end up sending it, saying less, pausing, or sending nothing;
- make only a real/keepable promise if they tell the other person they will answer more fully later;
- not delay necessary protection merely to complete the exercise.

These are preservation constraints, **not a sentence order**.
