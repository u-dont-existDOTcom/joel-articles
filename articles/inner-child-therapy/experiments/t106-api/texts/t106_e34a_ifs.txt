Is a perfect Higher Self already waiting in there to be your guide or parent? I’d call that claim spiritual bypassing. I’ve had the Higher Self perspective myself, though. It’s a somewhat less subjective way of seeing things as working perfectly in reality, despite the ego’s illusions, which matches what people say about it. Where I part ways with IFS is the wisdom part. I don’t think the Higher Self has any specific wisdom. It’s a vantage point, where more wisdom and compassion can come up, but problems can also look like they were never problems at all from up there, and that can ironically lead instead to more foolish indifference.
