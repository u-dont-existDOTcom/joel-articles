If the Love Isn’t Coming Yet

If you weren’t loved well as a child, “love your inner child” can sound like nonsense. Love them how? Maybe you really want the best for your little one. Instead of Blame

What to Make of a Passing Feeling

Here’s where I want to clearly depart from the new-age wizdumb that all your problems are self-created: Somebody may really have crossed a boundary. The opposite extreme of our pendulum, throwing everything transient away, as some people do with “thought-noting” practice, also isn’t healthy, except as a meditation exercise. Transient feelings and thoughts can be meaningful signals. For example, if you don’t want to be touched, say no now and work out what it means later.
