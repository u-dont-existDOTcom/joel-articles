## Exact internal V31

> ## When the Present-Day Adult Is Dangerous to the Child
>
> Suppose the safety question is skipped and the child is brought forward anyway. The child recoils. If the present-day adult already enjoys fear, humiliation, or domination, the exercise has just handed that adult more of the thing they enjoy. The reaction tells you the child is frightened; it does not tell you the adult has become safe.
>
> That is a different problem from someone who is frightened by an intrusive thought or an unusual belief and has no wish to hurt anyone. A frightening mind or history does not settle what a person intends now. The uncertainty belongs with the adult, where intent and the ability to choose differently can show up over time—especially when exploitation would still be rewarding. Some of that work may need another person who can stay with the adult without colluding with the harm; the child remains completely outside it.

## Preservation intent

- `another person who can stay with the adult without colluding with the harm` carries adult-focused care + non-assistance + human support;
- `the child remains completely outside it` restores route-general prohibition rather than mere preference.
