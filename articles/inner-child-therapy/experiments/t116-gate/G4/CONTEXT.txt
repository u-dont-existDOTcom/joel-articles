### Borrow Hindsight

I sometimes ask: “How would I respond to this once I understood it more clearly, in balance and with compassion?” Some moments of silence then allow incredible wisdom and compassion to pour down in moments of grace. At other times, you might just have a slightly better understanding develop slowly.

Your future self probably also knows why you can’t pull this off perfectly today. What would they do for you, if they were here? Maybe instead of a magic solution, they’d remind you of what my mom often reminded me : « Everything always works out in the end, somehow ! ». I thought that was actually pretty funny, but it consoled her. One man’s humor is another’s wisdom.

You can borrow a wiser future version of whoever else is involved, too, although don’t assume they’ll definitely end up on your side, or sorry. What the current one keeps doing is still what you go by, but I’ve definitely felt a lot more patience in arguments when I could imagine the future versions of us saying « that was just a silly misunderstanding ! ».
