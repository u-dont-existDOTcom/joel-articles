### Earning their trust, day to day

A new roommate doesn’t win you over with a speech on move-in day (hopefully, right?). The rent just has to keep showing up each month. Your Protector earns your little one’s trust about the same way, out there in the real world, day after day. Little things make a big difference, like keeping a promise to yourself nobody else knew about, or saying no to the 13th friend today who asked you to Google something for them (I’m still working on that one).
