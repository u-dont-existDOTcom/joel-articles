## If You Want to Kill Yourself

Maybe you’re reading this about to act, or with the means close by. Then before anything else, get another human being involved, and put some distance between you and the means. A crisis line counts, and the person on the other end can be a total stranger.

As for the internal psychology of it, when the part that wants to die feels like all of you, go ahead and notice who’s doing the noticing (more on that in Noticing Counts, further on), or borrow an adult from the adult lending library in your head :).
