## Exact internal V27

> ## When the Present-Day Adult Is Dangerous to the Child
>
> Suppose the safety question is skipped and the child is brought forward anyway. The child recoils. If the present-day adult already enjoys fear, humiliation, or domination, the exercise has just handed that adult more of the thing they enjoy. The reaction tells you the child is frightened; it does not tell you the adult has become safe.
>
> Now compare that with someone who has an intrusive thought they hate or a religious belief that sounds alarming to you, but no wish to hurt anyone. Their inner material may look worse, yet it is not the same problem. The difference can be worked out with the adult—what they actually endorse, whether they can choose differently—without bringing the child back in or helping the harmful part get better at what it does. If that begins to change, it should show up in adult life over time, especially when exploitation would still be rewarding; the child has no role in proving it. Sometimes another person needs to stay with that work for a while.
