So ask a therapist or a helpline what they'd have to report, and who can see their notes, before you get into names and details. With an AI, start with the kind of help you're looking for and leave the names and details out. For legal trouble, talk to a lawyer, since what you tell them is protected differently. But when you talk to the people who can help, don't leave out a danger that's real right now. Sometimes reporting what you did, or what you're afraid you might do, is what protects someone, even with the legal consequences. And if anyone's in danger right now, get them safe first and sort out the rest later.
