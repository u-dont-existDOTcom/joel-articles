So before you get into names and details, ask the therapist or the helpline what they'd have to report, and who gets to see their notes. With an AI, start with what kind of help you want, and leave out the names and the play-by-play. If it's legal trouble, a lawyer is who you want, since what you tell them is protected differently. Just don't leave out a danger that's happening now when you talk to the people who can help with it. And sometimes telling on yourself, about what you did or what you're scared you might do, is what keeps someone safe, even when there are legal consequences. If anyone's in danger right now, though, get them safe first and figure out the rest later.
