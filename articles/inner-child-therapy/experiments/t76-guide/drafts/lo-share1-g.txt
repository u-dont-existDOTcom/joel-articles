Then there's the stuff that's hardest to tell anyone, though it's often exactly what you need help with. Maybe it's an urge that scares you, or something you did that could get you in legal trouble. (An urge you've never acted on isn't the same as something you did, by the way.) You know that DM you deleted? It can still be sitting on their phone as a screenshot 🙈, and whatever's on a company's server can get handed over if a court asks. A fake name doesn't guarantee you're anonymous, either. Therapists and doctors (teachers too, and a few others) may have to report some things, depending on where you live, and a few rules apply to everyone. It's not like every crime you mention gets reported, though. And the cops? Definitely not your therapist.
