Doctors and therapists too. If you've been hurt by care before, or held somewhere against your will, of course that changes who you'd go to and what you'd say. Help you pick yourself can be safer than the same ward again, or your family taking over by default, especially with someone you trust along to speak up for you. Just don't present a dark thought that flashed through during a pain attack as a plan, or use it to bargain for pain meds. And if you really are in danger right now, get safe first.
