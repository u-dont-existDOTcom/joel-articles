Whichever stage you're at, saying the right thing when you sit down with your little one helps, but you also have to start actually doing some of it in the rest of your life. Your little one starts believing the grown-up you when they catch you lying down because you're wiped out, instead of pushing through one more chore. Reparenting isn't homework you hand in and forget, after all. It's the whole relationship. Feeling safe inside while you say what you really need, or what you won't put up with anymore, is the actual work, whether or not you cried in meditation that day.
