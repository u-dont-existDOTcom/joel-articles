Then there's the stuff that's hardest to tell anyone, though it's often exactly what you need help with. Maybe it's an urge that scares you, or something you did that could get you in legal trouble. (An urge you've never acted on isn't the same as something you did, by the way.) You know that DM you deleted? It can still be sitting on their phone as a screenshot 🙈, and whatever's on a company's server can get handed over if a court asks. That fake name you used? Not as anonymous as it feels. Therapists and doctors can be required to report some things, depending on where you live. So can teachers and a few others. Some rules even cover everyone. That's still a long way from reporting every crime you mention, though. And the cops are a whole other story. They're definitely not your therapist.
