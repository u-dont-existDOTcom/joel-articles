Physical pain can bring on the urge to escape too, and then whatever knocked it out last time starts looking real good, even if you know it'll leave you worse. I'd get to the ER or urgent care during the attack instead, so somebody can examine it and maybe treat it, even if every doctor so far has shrugged. Wishing the pain would just stop isn't a suicide plan, so don't dress it up as one to get relief. But if you're scared you'll actually hurt yourself, get someone with you right away (If You Want to Kill Yourself has the first steps).
