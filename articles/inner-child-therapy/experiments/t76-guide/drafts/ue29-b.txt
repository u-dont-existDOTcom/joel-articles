But what if the attacks come almost every day? You can't move into the ER. 😅 And normal test results don't make the pain imaginary! I'd ask for a chronic pain specialist and a written plan for the flare-ups you can see coming, including the new or severe signs that mean you go in right away. In France, for example, your doctor can request an assessment at a chronic pain center (a structure douleur chronique), and ask for priority when it's warranted, though there's no guaranteed appointment or wait time. And if a treatment the specialist tries helps, that still doesn't prove what's causing the pain.
