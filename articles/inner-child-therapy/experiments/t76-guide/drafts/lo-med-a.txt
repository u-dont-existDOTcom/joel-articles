The same goes for doctors and therapists. If care has hurt you before, or you were held somewhere against your will, that history gets a say in who you go to and how much you tell them. Help you choose yourself, maybe with someone you trust along to speak up for you, can be safer than the same ward again, or your family taking over by default. A passing dark thought during a pain attack isn't a plan, though, so don't present it as one, or use it to bargain for pain relief. And if you really are in danger right now, getting safe comes first.
