Keeping yourself safe counts as one of those acts, too. If you leave somewhere you're being hurt, or ask a friend to come with you, that's still the grown-up you looking out for your little one (if that way of putting it fits you), so it isn't some separate job you have to finish before the real work starts. Help from outside is its own thing, though. A doctor or a shelter can serve the same care, but they're not an exercise, and reparenting doesn't replace them.
