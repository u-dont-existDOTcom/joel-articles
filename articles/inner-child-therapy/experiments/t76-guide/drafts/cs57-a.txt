Whichever stage you're at, saying the right thing when you sit down with your little one helps, but you also have to start actually doing some of it in the rest of your life. Your little one starts believing the grown-up you when they catch you lying down because you're wiped out, instead of pushing through one more chore. Reparenting isn't an exercise you finish, after all. It's the relationship itself, so feeling safe inside while you say what you really feel and need, and what you won't put up with, is the work, even on a day when a meditation gets you no tears and no relief.
