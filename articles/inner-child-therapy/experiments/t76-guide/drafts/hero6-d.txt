Keeping yourself safe counts as one of those acts too. Say you finally walk out of a place where you keep getting hurt, or you ask a friend to come with you to the scary appointment. (Leaning on a friend doesn't make it any less your step!) That's the grown-up you looking out for your little one, if you like thinking of it that way, so it's not some separate chore to finish before the real work starts. A doctor or a shelter is a different thing, though. They can be part of the same care, but they aren't exercises, and reparenting doesn't replace them.
