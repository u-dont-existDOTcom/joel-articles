Then there's the stuff that's hardest to tell anyone, though it's often exactly what you need help with. Maybe it's an urge that scares you, or something you did that could get you in legal trouble. (An urge you've never acted on isn't the same as something you did, by the way.) Before I told anyone, I'd want to know where it could end up. That DM you deleted can still be on someone's phone as a screenshot, or saved somewhere a court can ask for it, and a fake name doesn't guarantee you're anonymous. Therapists, doctors, teachers and some others may have to report certain things, and some rules apply to everybody, depending on where you live. That doesn't mean every crime you mention gets reported. And the cops are definitely not your therapist.
