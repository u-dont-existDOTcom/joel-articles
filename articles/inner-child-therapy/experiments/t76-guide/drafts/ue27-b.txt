The urge to escape can come from physical pain, too. Picture being curled up on the bathroom floor, ready to take whatever knocked it out last time, even though you know it'll leave you worse tomorrow. That pain deserves a doctor, even if a protective part is in there too. If an attack's unbearable, getting to the ER or urgent care while it's happening can mean somebody finally examines it and treats it, even if every doctor before shrugged. Nobody can promise you a particular medicine, or a cure, though. And if you're scared you might actually hurt yourself, or can't tell whether you're safe, get someone with you right away, like in If You Want to Kill Yourself.
