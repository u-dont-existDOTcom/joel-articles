So before you spill the names and the gory details, ask first. "What would you have to report, and who gets to read your notes?" is a fair question, and a good therapist or helpline won't mind it. With an AI, I'd say what kind of help I'm after and keep the names out of it. Legal trouble is a lawyer's job, since what you tell a lawyer is protected in a different way. None of that means hiding a danger that's happening now, though! If someone could get hurt, tell the people who can help, and get them safe before you worry about any of this. And sometimes telling on yourself, about what you did or what you're scared you might do, is what keeps someone else safe, even with the legal fallout.
