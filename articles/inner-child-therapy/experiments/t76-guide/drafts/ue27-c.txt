Physical pain can bring on the urge to escape too. When you're curled up on the bathroom floor, whatever knocked it out last time starts looking real good, even though you know it'll leave you worse. I'd go to the ER or urgent care while the attack is happening instead, so somebody can actually examine it and maybe treat it, even if every doctor before just shrugged. Nobody can promise you a particular medicine or a cure. If the pain has you wishing it would all just stop, that's not a suicide plan, so don't dress it up as one to get relief. But if you're scared you'll actually hurt yourself, or can't tell whether you're safe, get someone with you right away, even if care has hurt you before (see If You Want to Kill Yourself).
