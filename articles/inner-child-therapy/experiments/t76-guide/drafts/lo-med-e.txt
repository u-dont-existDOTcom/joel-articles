The same goes for doctors and therapists. If a hospital ever held you against your will, or a treatment hurt you, no wonder asking for help feels scary. You get to pick who comes with you, like a friend who'll speak up for you, instead of automatically calling the relative who never respected your wishes. And you can ask what any treatment is supposed to do before you say yes. If pain ever has you wishing it would all just stop, that's not a suicide plan, so don't dress it up as one to get pain relief. But if you really are thinking about acting on it, or can't keep yourself safe, tell someone who can protect you. What happened back then doesn't make the danger now go away.
