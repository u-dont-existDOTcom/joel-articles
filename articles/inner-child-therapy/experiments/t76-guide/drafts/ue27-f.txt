Physical pain can bring on the urge to escape too. You're curled up on the bathroom floor, and whatever knocked it out last time starts looking real good, though you know it'll leave you worse. I'd get to the ER or urgent care while the attack is still going, so somebody can examine it, and maybe treat it. Never mind that every doctor so far shrugged. Wishing the pain would just stop isn't a suicide plan, so don't dress it up as one to get relief. But if you're scared you'll actually hurt yourself, get someone with you right away (If You Want to Kill Yourself has the first steps).
