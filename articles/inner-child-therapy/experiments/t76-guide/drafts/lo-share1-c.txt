Then there are the things that are hardest to tell anyone, even though they're often what you most need help with, like an urge that scares you or something you did that could get you in legal trouble. Before you tell, know where it can end up. Anything you write online or in an AI app can be screenshotted, or kept and handed over when a court asks for it, and a fake name or a deleted message won't make you anonymous. Therapists, doctors, teachers and some others may have to report certain things, and a few rules apply to everyone, depending on where you live. That doesn't mean every crime you mention gets reported, though. The police aren't there to keep your secrets the way a therapist might, either.
