And yes, keeping yourself safe counts as one of those acts. Say you get out of a place where you're being hurt, or ask a friend to come with you so you're not alone there. That's you looking after your little one (if that's how you like to see it), and you don't have to finish it before the real work can start. It is the work. Outside help is different. A doctor or a shelter can be part of the same care, but they're not exercises, and reparenting doesn't replace them.
