Then there's the stuff that's hardest to tell anyone, even though it's often exactly what you need help with, like a scary urge or something you did that could get you in legal trouble. Before you spill it, know where it can go. Whatever you write online or in an AI app can be screenshotted, or saved and handed over if a court asks, and a fake name or hitting delete won't make you anonymous. Therapists, doctors, teachers and some others may have to report certain things, and some rules apply to everybody, depending on where you live. That doesn't mean every crime you mention gets reported. And the cops are definitely not your therapist. 😬
