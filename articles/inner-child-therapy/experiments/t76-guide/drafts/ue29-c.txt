But what if the attacks come almost every day? You can't move into the ER. And normal test results don't make the pain imaginary, just as a treatment that helps doesn't prove what's causing it! I'd ask for a chronic pain specialist and a written plan for the flare-ups you can see coming, including the new or severe signs that mean you go to the ER right away. In France, for example, your doctor can request an assessment at a chronic pain center (a structure douleur chronique), and ask for priority when it's warranted, though there's no guaranteed appointment or wait time.
