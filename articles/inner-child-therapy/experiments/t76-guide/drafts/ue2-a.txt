Physical pain can set off that urge too, and then the escape plan might be whatever numbed it last time, even if you know it makes things worse. When the pain gets unbearable, taking care of yourself can mean calling urgent medical services to get it looked at, and maybe treated, even if earlier tests never found the cause. Normal results don't make your pain imaginary. If severe pain keeps coming back, though, I wouldn't make the emergency room the plan for every attack. Ask your doctor about a pain specialist and a written plan for the flare-ups you can see coming, with urgent care as the backup. In France, for example, a doctor can request an assessment at a chronic pain center, and ask for priority when it's warranted, but nothing guarantees you an appointment or a particular medicine. And if the pain has you thinking about dying, the first steps in If You Want to Kill Yourself come before all of this.
