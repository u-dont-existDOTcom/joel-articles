The same goes for doctors and therapists. If care has hurt you before, or you were held somewhere against your will, you can weigh that when you choose who to go to and how much to tell them. Help you choose for yourself can be safer than the same ward again, or your family taking over by default, especially with someone you trust along to speak up for you. A passing dark thought during a pain attack isn't a plan, though, so don't present it as one, or use it to bargain for pain relief. And if you really are in danger right now, getting safe comes first.
