Pain can set off that urge too, the physical kind. When your body's screaming, the escape plan might be whatever knocked it out last time, even if you know it'll make you worse. If it's unbearable, go get it looked at, at urgent care or the ER, and maybe get something for it, even if every test so far came back normal. (Normal tests don't mean you're making it up!) But if it's pain that keeps coming back, you can't live in the ER either. Ask your doctor for a pain specialist and a written plan for the flare-ups you can see coming, and keep urgent care as the backup. In France, for instance, a doctor can ask a chronic pain center to see you, with priority if it's warranted, though nobody can promise you an appointment or a certain medicine. And if the pain has you thinking about dying, If You Want to Kill Yourself comes first.
