Whichever stage you're at, saying the right thing when you sit down with your little one helps, but you also have to start actually doing some of it in the rest of your life. Your little one starts believing the grown-up you when they catch you lying down because you're wiped out, instead of pushing through one more chore. Or when you finally tell your boss no, and feel safe inside while you say it. That's reparenting, whether or not a meditation ever made you cry. You don't finish it in a session, either. The sessions are practice for moments like that.
