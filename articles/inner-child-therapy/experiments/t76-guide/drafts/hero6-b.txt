Keeping yourself safe is one of those acts too, by the way. Walking out of a place where someone's hurting you, or getting a friend to come along to the scary appointment, that's the grown-up you looking out for your little one (if you like putting it that way). So it's not some separate thing to get through before the "real" work starts. Help from outside, like a doctor or a shelter, is its own thing, though. It can be part of the same care, but it isn't an exercise, and reparenting doesn't replace it.
