Same with doctors and therapists. If care has hurt you before, or you got locked up against your will, you're allowed to factor that in when you pick who to see and what to tell them. A doctor you chose yourself, maybe with a friend along to speak up for you, can be a lot safer than going back to the same ward, or having your family run the show by default. Just don't turn a dark thought that flashes by during a pain attack into a plan you announce, or a bargaining chip for pain meds. And if you really are in danger right now, safety first.
