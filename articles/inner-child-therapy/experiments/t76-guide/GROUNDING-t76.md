## 6. lo-share-c (PGQ-039, two paragraphs): MUST-FIX (one sentence)

P1 sets "an urge that scares you" beside "something you did that could get you in legal trouble", then speaks only of crimes ("That doesn't mean every crime you mention gets reported, though."). - SHOULD: "regulated professional services" as the contrast to online places. Professionals appear only as "may have to report certain things"; no hint that they are generally bound to keep things private, with limits, so nowhere looks safe.
- SHOULD (low): "what gets documented"; "illegal imagery/links" ("leave the names and details out" covers identities and narrative, not links).
- Carried: online records, screenshots, legal demands; pseudonyms and deleting; duties vary by place, some rules apply to everyone; not every crime reported; police not confidential; ask first about reporting and notes; AI: general kind of help; lawyer; current risk told; reporting can protect; immediate danger first.

- "won't make you anonymous": the entry says pseudonyms and deleting "do not ensure anonymity". "won't" is absolute.
- "even though they're often what you most need help with": not in the entry; leans the way the Reader need does (don't discourage early help). Acceptable.
- "since what you tell them is protected differently": matches "legal privilege differs from professional confidentiality", but it is a legal claim with no "where you live", and can be heard as "secret".

DISTORTED / VOICE
- Not a prohibition catalog or a set of evasion tips: one "don't", and P1 says fake names and deletion don't work. But P2 is five imperatives in a row with no "I'd" ("So ask…", "start with…", "talk to a lawyer", "don't leave out…", "get them safe first…"), where the paragraphs around it say "I'd call somebody", "I'd either bring more of the truth in, carefully", "I'd bet on more than one place". The entry: "Merge with those passages rather than duplicating them."
- Gate: "Before actual public placement, obtain appropriate legal review of explicit jurisdictional statements." No jurisdiction is named, but "a few rules apply to everyone", "handed over when a court asks for it" and "protected differently" are still legal claims for that review.

VERDICT MUST-FIX (one sentence), plus SHOULDs. Fixes:
1. P1 after "That doesn't mean every crime you mention gets reported, though.": "And an urge you've never acted on isn't the same as something you did."
2. P1: "won't make you anonymous" -> "doesn't keep you anonymous for sure".
