## Exact internal V34

> ## When the Present-Day Adult Is Dangerous to the Child
>
> Being able to pause and make choices isn't enough by itself before you bring the inner child forward; the present-day adult also has to be on the child's side. Suppose they aren't—suppose they still enjoy fear, humiliation, or domination. The inner child recoils. The exercise has just handed the adult more of the thing they enjoy. The reaction tells you the child is frightened; it does not tell you the adult has become safe.
>
> That is a different problem from someone who is frightened by an intrusive thought or an unusual belief and has no wish to hurt anyone. A frightening mind or history does not settle what a person intends now. The question stays with the present-day adult: what do they actually want to do, and can they choose differently? If they later say the harmful intent is gone, the statement itself doesn't settle it; the change has to keep showing up in their choices over time, including when exploiting vulnerability would still feel rewarding. Some of that adult work may need another person who can stay involved without colluding with the harm; the inner child stays completely outside that adult work.
