## The actual five recovered families

A: live claim -> source/observation/event -> consequence -> stop if the live question is answered.
B: practical uncertainty -> discriminating reader question/action -> decision-relevant distinction -> stop.
C: real situation -> current mechanism -> unresolved limitation -> needed alternative, detailed only after it has a job.
D: odd observation -> curiosity -> relevant extension/source -> present interpretation.
E: continuing thought, unevenly developed -> genuine complication -> earned change of direction/scale.

These are relations between thoughts, not a required sentence count. A node may continue across sentences, a sentence may carry several nodes, and a branch may stay unresolved. Replacing every paragraph with the same chain would repeat the original failure.

## What the previous evidence DOES NOT establish

- No recovered artifact here demonstrates a representative 10–20-map library extracted from the full set of definitely owner-written articles.
- The named library has FIVE broad families, not twenty independently validated paragraph forms.
- The SE/Yoga trials tested a small subset on narrow targets. A tiny owner-reported Human result is not proof that the larger map-based method worked.
- A map can faithfully describe good prose but fail as a writing instruction. Description, executable instantiation and accepted output remain different facts.
- The absence of a broader library in these recovered files is not proof that no further work exists elsewhere.
