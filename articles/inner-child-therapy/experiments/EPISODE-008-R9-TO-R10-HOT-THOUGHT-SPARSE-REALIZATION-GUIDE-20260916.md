## Why R9 failed

### Instruction-design failure — primary

The R8->R9 guide said `unequal thought duration`, but operationalized that instruction with a row for nearly every conceptual station and an optional playful/meme lens for many of them. That created three hidden pressures:

1. **Every station looked rewrite-worthy.** A guide intended to make only the hot thoughts linger instead invited local elaboration almost everywhere.
2. **Play became a layer.** Supplying a playful option beside many rows made cultural/metaphorical realization salient throughout the section. The writer could obey locally while producing a regular `explain -> crafted image -> continue` rhythm globally.
3. **No global edit-dose budget enforced asymmetry.** Nothing prevented every locally reasonable expansion from accumulating. R9 grew from R8's 1549 UI words to 2379 UI words, about 54% longer. `Unequal expansion` became broad expansion.

The guide also did not distinguish strongly enough between protected *meaning* and protected *surface explanation*. Preservation units therefore kept receiving explicit reader-visible sentences instead of being allowed to hitchhike inside another sentence or remain implicit when an already-preserved example/action carried the same function.

### Implementation failure — secondary but real

The writer then amplified those design flaws:

- it used many of the optional images rather than treating them as rare possibilities (steering wheel, Pixar committee, movie/theater, Supreme Court, original-footage/subtitles, customer support, note under door, etc.);
- it expanded housekeeping as well as hot thoughts;
- it kept the inner-monologue capability ladder and the relationship/checking mini-essays underneath the extra words;
- several playful lines became polished local closures rather than replacing explanation.

So the failure was **both design and execution**, with design upstream. The writer was over-permitted to elaborate, and then over-applied that permission.

## R10 enforcement change

Do not interpret Strategy A as `decompress each span`. R10 uses four explicit span roles:

- **HOT — expand selectively.** This is where the reader is psychologically stuck or the thought is actually surprising. It may receive extra sentences, repetition, circling, or one playful social act.
- **CARRY — preserve function without giving it its own rhetorical stop.** Fold it into a neighboring sentence, dependent clause, example, or consequence. It may not open a new mini-essay.
- **LOCK — preserve exact current wording.** No detector-motivated edits.
- **SCAFFOLD — remove/replace the realization-only packaging while preserving its protected function elsewhere.** No standalone replacement is required if the function is already carried.

### Global constraints

- Preserve current idea order.
- Target roughly **1600–1850 UI words**. This is not a compression goal; it is an anti-global-expansion enforcement check. Any HOT expansion must be offset by harder compression of CARRY/SCAFFOLD material.
- Use **at most two newly crafted playful/cultural/metaphorical images in the entire section**. Existing owner wording does not count. A new image is admitted only if it *replaces* explanation; if the prose explains the same point again afterward, remove the image or the explanation.
- No `Fine.`, `Good.`, `Great.` micro-verdicts.
- Do not give every preservation unit a sentence. Several protected functions may map to one natural sentence or paragraph.
- No paragraph is required to conclude itself. If the next paragraph supplies the consequence, stop without a takeaway.
- Preserve both native embeds in position.

## What R10 must *not* do

- Do not rewrite every row just because it appears in the table. HOT spans change visibly; many CARRY spans should become shorter or disappear as standalone sentences.
- Do not preserve R9's crafted images as protected content. They are assistant realization, not owner authority.
- Do not add new jokes/references simply because playfulness was owner-authorized.
- Do not turn `hot thought` into another repeated paragraph template (`scene -> joke -> lesson`).
- Do not exceed the word budget by explaining why a compressed qualification is still true.
