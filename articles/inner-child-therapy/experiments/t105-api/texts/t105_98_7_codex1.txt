Sometimes you have a really good session where you feel how much you care about your little one and say what they needed to hear. A few days later you’re skipping lunch and saying yes to everything again. For a lot of people, the first thought is that they didn’t go deep enough. I doubt that’s the problem, and the session doesn’t sound like a failure to me. Try a tiny bit of what you did for your little one as you go about your day, because one small thing like that can teach them more than ten more good meditations. Text a friend that you’re having a rough day instead of saying you’re fine, for instance. Sending that text can be harder than the meditation was.
