The other one shows up right as you get close to something painful. Suddenly you'd rather check your phone, or do anything but feel it (even a yawn can come with an "I'm so done with this" feeling). Maybe it doesn't believe there's a grown-up around who could handle what's coming. I'd want out too. That yawn might actually be the opposite of checking out, though. Re-evaluation Counseling, a kind of peer counseling, counts yawning as emotional discharge, a feeling on its way out. The skeptic and the pull both started out guarding you against something that really hurt. If you treat either one like an enemy, or like proof that something's wrong with you, it'll probably just think "See? I knew it," and stay in charge.
