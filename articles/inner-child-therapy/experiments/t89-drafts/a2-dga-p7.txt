You can't spot love bombing by how much affection there is, so watch what it's doing to your head (are the red flags from last week starting to look kind of cute?). A group can do it too, with all its praise and special attention, and suddenly someone's an exception to rules you'd use with anybody else. I wouldn't argue with the feeling. I'd give it a few days before deciding anything big, and check the facts in the meantime.
