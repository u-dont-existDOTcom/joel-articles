# The Three Adult Functions: what to merge, what to keep

Brainstorm for Joel, 2026-09-27. Joel (04:19 UTC): "the three adult functions section is largely duplicating prior stuff, but not completely so we should brainstorm about what needs to be merged vs stay there".

## An organizing idea for the section (proposal)

The article already has a section for **borrowing** each job from someone else (`Borrow the Adult Before You Can Be the Adult`, ending with the Apprentice: steal what the friend did). `The Three Adult Functions` can be what each job looks like **once it's yours**. On that reading, its intro carries only what's new at that stage. Anything the article already says, or says better in a later section, goes there instead.

The new material, by this test:
- **One grown-up, three jobs.** They're not three people, and one act can be two jobs.
- **The Nurturer also enjoys your little one,** not only comforts or manages them.
- **The Protector is what makes the warmth believable,** inside too.
- **The Guide doesn't need to know the future,** and Joel thinks it's the most important one.
- **Worth isn't what's missing; capacity is.**
