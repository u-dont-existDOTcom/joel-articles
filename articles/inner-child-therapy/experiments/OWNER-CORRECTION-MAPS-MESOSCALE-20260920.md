# Owner correction — maps operate above sentence granularity

Date: 2026-09-20
Status: **DIRECT OWNER CORRECTION / ACTIVE MAP-TOOLBOX RULE**

Joel's correction:

> the point of the map is to give you more than just a sentence or two. if you break everythign down that small it's going to sound uniform and AI

## Correct interpretation

A paragraph/section map exists to provide **mesoscale thought topology** that the model does not otherwise vary reliably.

The map should control relations across several sentences and, where appropriate, several paragraphs:
- where a thought is allowed to stay;
- where it turns;
- which branch gets more space;
- whether a complication interrupts rather than neatly follows;
- where the author returns to an earlier concern;
- where a short paragraph is allowed to sit next to a long one;
- where the section stops without completing every implication.

Sentence-level engineering is subordinate to that larger movement. It repairs local realization **inside a selected map node or transition**. It must not convert every sentence into an isolated ticket and mechanically concatenate one-job replacements, because that creates another uniform model rhythm.

## Scale hierarchy

1. **Section/natural boundary map** — the main route through the thought.
2. **Paragraph roles inside that route** — deliberately unequal in length, density and function.
3. **Sentence/clause realization** — locally repaired only where needed.

Do not invert that hierarchy by creating sentence operations first and inferring a paragraph afterward.

## Short-paragraph consequence

A short complete paragraph can remain short, but its role is interpreted **inside the larger map**. The point is not that short paragraphs need no map. The map may explain why that paragraph is short: opening warning, pivot, interruption, bridge, aside, or stop.

Therefore:
- do not expand a short paragraph merely to instantiate a map;
- do not treat that short paragraph as the entire map either;
- place it inside a larger natural-boundary topology before deciding what neighboring prose should do.

## Current correction to the checking work

The 29-word checking hook should not have been treated as the whole map target.

The relevant target is the larger `When Healing Turns Into Checking` movement:
- brief warning that healing can become checking while the wound is real;
- longer discrimination between genuinely new processing and reassurance-checking;

The owner-final evaluation paragraph remains exact and is not rewritten.

A natural source map may guide this multi-paragraph movement; individual paragraphs keep their source-required scale. Paragraph-level detector evidence remains separate and must not be manufactured by padding short paragraphs.

## Relationship to prior success

Episode 007 sentence-by-sentence repair remains useful, but as **local execution within a larger passage**, not as the generator of the passage topology itself.

The failed E2/E4 style experiments that made isolated sentence tickets and mechanically concatenated them are examples of the danger this rule addresses: they replaced polished AI with clipped/marching AI.

## Durable learning object

For future map experiments preserve:
`literal donor natural boundary -> mesoscale symbolic map -> target natural boundary before -> paragraph-role assignment -> local sentence operations only where necessary -> target after -> preservation/editorial/detector outcome`.

Do not reduce the record to a phrase rule or a list of sentence prohibitions.
