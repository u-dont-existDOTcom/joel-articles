## Target operations

### Sentences 1–3
**MERGE / DEMOTE INSTRUCTION SCAFFOLD.**

Protected function:
write the real response privately and do not send immediately.

Causal defect:
three short imperative beats create a manufactured opening and put one function in each sentence.

Required structural change:
make the unsent draft the ordinary starting condition, not a three-step instruction sequence.

### Sentences 4–7
**REWRITE AS ONE REREADING EVENT.**

Protected functions:
- unfair/mean wording can be removed;
- a valid no/request/protest can remain;
- actual responsibility/apology can remain.

Causal defect:
the current prose performs casualness sentence by sentence (`ugh`, `Keep that`, `put that in too`) while still marching through the preservation ledger.

Required structural change:
let rereading the message reveal more than one thing at once. Do not assign a separate sentence to protest and apology.

### Sentences 8–9
**MERGE / REMOVE MATCHED `Sometimes` CLOSURE.**

Protected functions:
the final response may be much shorter or there may be nothing useful to send.

Causal defect:
two matched outcome sentences create a tidy binary close.

Required structural change:
let the outcome fall out of the rereading process without presenting two symmetrical options.
