## Exact internal V24

> ## When the Present-Day Adult Is Dangerous to the Child
>
> Regulation is morally neutral. Somebody can be calm, articulate, and perfectly able to pause before acting, and still use that capacity to hurt people more deliberately. That is why the readiness test above is not enough by itself. If the present-day adult still enjoys vulnerability as something to dominate, the problem is not whether the child can be reached. Reaching the child would simply make more vulnerability available. Even the child's fear would be useless as evidence: a frightened reaction tells you the child is frightened, not that the adult has become safer.
>
> Disturbing material in the adult is not the same thing as dangerous intent. Intrusive thoughts, unusual beliefs, or a harmful past may deserve attention without meaning the person wants to hurt someone now. What matters is whether harm is currently endorsed and whether the person can choose against it. Claims of sovereignty matter only at that point. Someone who says they are sovereign but also says an urge, doctrine, group, spirit, or master cannot be refused is describing a problem of agency, whether or not anyone agrees about the metaphysics. There is still work to do with that adult agency; therapy can address it without becoming useful to the manipulation itself.
>
> If the adult later says the harmful intent has changed, there is still no reason to ask the child to demonstrate it. The change has to show up repeatedly in the person's life, including when exploitation would still be rewarding; for some people, reaching that point may take sustained work with another human being. The child can remain outside that process.
