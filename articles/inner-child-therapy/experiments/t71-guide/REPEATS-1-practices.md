- B262 ("And So That's the Way It Happened!"): "whatever repair is owed is still owed"
- B144 ("Love Doesn't Have to Wait for Trust"): "if it hurt people or damaged a life, that may need repair"
- B325 ("How to Forgive Without Forgetting"): "and the responsibility stays"
- B70 ("Also Look Outward"): "If you really did hurt them, repair it."

Adds? B325 adds the full self-forgiveness sequence (own it, remorse, repair, change, stop holding yourself hostage). B262 adds nothing over B144 and B70 except tying the point to the courtroom image, so a reader feels told again, mildly.
Better: B325, the natural home, with the steps.

**2. Close a practice by asking the younger you what they need now**
- B263 ("And So That's the Way It Happened!"): "What do they need from you now?"
- B266 ("Use Childhood Photographs Gently"): "Ask what your little one needs from you now, and do a bit of it."

Adds? B266 adds "do a bit of it". Otherwise it is the same closing question in two adjacent practices.
Better: B266, since it carries the action; B263 stops at the question.

**3. Processing the child can become a way to avoid living**
- B272 ("Close the Session and Return to Life"): "the processing can go on and on, until it's just one more way to avoid living your life"
- B88 ("When Healing Turns Into Checking"): "a ritual you have to complete before you're allowed to live, stop there"
- B85 (same section): "Did I process it properly? Did I grieve it enough?"
- B20 ("My Journey"): "Spending your whole life processing your wounded inner child isn't freedom either."

Adds? B272 adds a remedy (the Present Time ending and body cues), but the warning itself is a fourth telling, so a reader feels told again.
Better: B88 carries the warning (sharper, as a stop rule, in its own section). The closing steps belong in B272; its "Otherwise..." sentence is the redundant part.

**4. You don't have to finish healing before going back to life**
- B273 ("Close the Session and Return to Life"): "Nothing has to be resolved first, and whatever you're still feeling can come along."
- B88 ("When Healing Turns Into Checking"): "a ritual you have to complete before you're allowed to live"
- B73 ("Also Look Outward"): "Do you have to get yourself sorted out first, so you'll be better company? I really don't think so."

Adds? B273 adds a script for the little one and the idea that returning is itself part of healing. The permission is the one already given in B88 and B73 (B73 in a relationship setting). B273's last line is also the positive form of B272's caution, so those two blocks lean on each other.
Better: B273, the more natural place (the moment of ending); B88 states it more sharply.
