# Repeats and contradictions: "Speak Toward What You Are Building" (B348 to B357)

Every block from B349 to B357 was checked against the others there and against the whole article. Only pairs touching those blocks are listed. Quotes are exact.

Counts: 8 repeats, 1 contradiction (mild), 3 callbacks, 5 near misses.

## 1. Repeats (article order)

**1. B61 (Catch the Hook Before the Story Takes Over) and B350 (Speak Toward What You Are Building)**
- B61: "Somebody may really have crossed a boundary. That's not just because they are your karmic partner"
- B350: "that's harm, and it gets called harm, even if it was also a learning experience"
- Point: real harm stays harm even when a karmic or learning reading also applies.
- Later instance: adds only the little one's voice and the Nurturer-first step. A light echo.
- Better: B61. It names the opposing view and argues against it, where harm between people is the topic.

**2. B120 (Your Loan is PRE-APPROVED!), B249 (Timing Is Individual), B357 (Speak Toward What You Are Building)**
- B120: "You can have an enormous spiritual feast upstairs while the child is still starving downstairs."
- B249: "Has it reached your little one yet? And does it show with people, in being more honest and dependable"
- B357: "From a spiritual experience to your little one, then into the grown-up relationship, then out to other people, and back again."
- Point: a spiritual experience only counts once it reaches the little one and shows up with people.
- Later instance: B357 adds the step out to other people and back again. The first half of its chain retells B249, so a reader feels told again.
- Better: B249. It poses the test as questions, in the section about spiritual timing.

**3. B248 and B249 (Timing Is Individual), B356 and B357 (both Speak Toward What You Are Building)**
- B249: "Yours may have come long before you had much of a Nurturer or Protector"
- B356: "Community can work in both directions, too." and "Either way, the community is what helps make it practical, real, down to earth, embodied and so on."
- B357: "it doesn't always go the same way round (the door you came in through matters less than the loop)" and "Having a beautiful experience on the inside isn't really the point."
- Point: (a) safety and opening can come in either order (B248 gives safety first, B249 opening first); (b) the inner experience must be made real through other people.
- Later instance: B357 adds nothing to (a) or (b) beyond B356, one paragraph earlier, so a reader feels told again. B356 adds the community angle.
- Better: B356. It gives both concrete cases and the reason; B357 compresses it.

**4. B347 (Borrowed Adulthood in Relationship) and B349 (Speak Toward What You Are Building)**
- B347: "the balance is dynamic between inner & outer reliance, and going too far in either extreme isn't generally the healthiest"
- B349: "Being totally independent also isn't realistic nor ideal, and can even seem like just a reaction to the original question."
- Point: relying wholly on yourself is not healthy; a balance is.
- Later instance: adds that total independence can be a mere reaction, but as an aside. The claim is told again a heading later.
- Better: B347. It is the point of that passage; in B349 it is a side remark.

**5. B347 (Borrowed Adulthood in Relationship) and B355 (Speak Toward What You Are Building)**
- B347: "Other jobs can go to friends or community, outsource to the tools or pros, whatever works."
- B355: "Professional therapy's still useful for what it does, and peers and community do something else, so a serious healing path can include both."
- Point: healing can draw on professionals and on friends, peers or community together.
- Later instance: adds little. That peers do something else was already in B353, same section ("alongside ordinary friendship and therapy"), and using both repeats B347. A light repeat.
- Better: B355. It says what each side does; B347 is a passing list.

**6. B351 and B352 (both Speak Toward What You Are Building)**
- B351: "Before giving anyone advice, your little one included, it can help to find out what kind of company they want"
- B352: "Your little one deserves that courtesy too."
- Point: the ask-first courtesy applies to the little one as well.
- Later instance: adds nothing; it restates "your little one included" from the paragraph before. A reader feels told again.
- Better: B351. The rule and the little-one case sit together where the rule is introduced.

**7. B351 and B352 (both Speak Toward What You Are Building)**
- B351: "Not that you have to make a ritual of asking every time."
- B352: "Once anyone tells you what they want from you, remember it, so they don't get quizzed every time"
- Point: don't ask what someone wants every single time.
- Later instance: adds how (remember what they told you) and "don't guess it from their gender or another stereotype". The not-every-time part is told again.
- Better: B352. It gives the method; B351's sentence only says not to.

**8. B353 and B354 (both Speak Toward What You Are Building)**
- B353: "Equal time. Attention without interpretation, advice, or fixing. You speak. I listen. Then we switch."
- B354: "When you take turns listening with a good peer, as equals" and "They keep it to themselves."
- Point: peer sessions are equal turns, with no interpretation or advice from the listener.
- Later instance: adds the mapping onto the three jobs (warmth lends the Nurturer, privacy and the time limit lend the Protector, no advice means the peer is not your Guide). Turn-taking and no-advice are told again.
- Better: B353 carries the rule; B354 carries the mapping.

## 2. Contradictions

**1. (mild) B353 and B355 (both Speak Toward What You Are Building)**
- B353: "It's kind of a third type of relationship, alongside ordinary friendship and therapy"
- B355: "Is that therapy? Well, not the mainstream kind, but yes, for sure."
- Pull: peer work is first set apart from therapy, then called therapy. "not the mainstream kind" half-explains it, and B355's own last sentence ("peers and community do something else") sides with B353, so "yes, for sure" is the odd one out.

No other contradiction involving the section was found.

## 3. The five repeats a reader would most notice

6, 8, 3, 7, 2 (most noticeable first). All but 2 sit in adjacent paragraphs.

## Callbacks that rely on earlier sections (not counted as repeats)

- B355 "borrowed adulthood in its healthiest form" relies on B11 (The Chicken-and-Egg Problem), which defines the term, and on Don't Give the Inner Adult Away (B30 to B33). Its "give it back to you" retells the lend-then-own idea of B11 and B347; the term flags the callback. The study chart (B28, My Journey) has the same line: "The peer supports the adult; the peer does not replace the adult".
- B350 and B354 rely on B8 (The Chicken-and-Egg Problem) for the three jobs: "That's one grown-up doing three jobs, not three more people moving in". B354's opening sentence also restates B346 (Borrowed Adulthood in Relationship), that others can do some of the grown-up jobs, but the rest of B354 is new.
- B357 "inner-child pl/ork" relies on B171 (Start With Whatever Showed Up), which defines it.

## Near misses (not counted)

- B350 and B115 (Your Loan is PRE-APPROVED!): both warn against toxic positivity, one in a gratitude passage, one about positive self-talk.
- B350 and B2 (the opening, before the first heading): tears help but have limits. B2: "I could have kept crying forever." B350: "letting tears flow can be immensely therapeutic, as long as they don't hijack your perspective". The limits differ.
- B349 against B154 (Make a Simple Vow) and B158 (Remind Yourself): "aim them at what you want more of" versus "you won't be cruel to them on purpose" and "stop attacking myself". Nothing says why both hold.
- B350 and B279 (Use Childhood Photographs Gently): "that's harm, and it gets called harm" versus "please don't confront or accuse anyone based only on what came up here". Compatible, but B350 does not say which hurt it means.
- B351 and B352 against B47 and B48 (You Don't Need an Inner Monologue): asking the little one and remembering its answer assumes it answers; B48 covers the case where nothing does.
