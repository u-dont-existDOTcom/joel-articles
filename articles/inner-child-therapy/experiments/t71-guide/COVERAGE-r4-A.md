# Coverage check A: guide lines 1-520 against the rewritten article

Each entry is a point in the guide that the article does not carry, or carries only in part. Entries run in guide order. Points the article carries in other words, or in another section, are not listed. "Minor" marks a small loss; the rest are points a reader would notice. The closest article passage is a nearest match, not always a real equivalent. No dosing or recipe lines in 1-520 are missing.
