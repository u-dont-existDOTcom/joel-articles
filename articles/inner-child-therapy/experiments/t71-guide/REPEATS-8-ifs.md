# Repeats and contradictions: "How This Relates to IFS" (B360-B368)

Scope: every block in "## How This Relates to IFS" and "### Related Clinical Work" (B360-B368) was checked against the others there and against the rest of the article, including "Teachers and Practice Resources" (B369-B372). Only items involving at least one of B360-B368 are listed. "IFS section" below means Appendix > How This Relates to IFS.

## 1. Repeats (article order)

**1. Borrow one adult job at a time when you can't reach the adult.**
- B11, The Chicken-and-Egg Problem: "Sometimes somebody else may have to do one part of the adult role with you or for you first."
- B95, Borrow One Competency at a Time: "Borrow one function at a time from a figure who embodies it."
- B363, IFS section: "Then borrow. One grown-up job at a time, from a friend who'll play Protector for a night"
- Later adds: only the IFS framing (offered instead of a Self that leads) and the friend-plays-Protector wording. A reader would feel told the same thing again. The phone-ringing example in the same sentence is a callback (see 1b).
- Better: B11. It explains how the borrowing works and hands pieces back; B363 is a recap.

**2. Memory and imagery work is open to suggestion.**
- B57, Your Body Might Need Some Love First: "to confirm a memory or rebuild one. Or to suggest one."
- B277, Use Childhood Photographs Gently: "Memory is an amorphous thing, easily influenced by guided imagery and suggestions"
- B364, IFS section: "all memories and plenty of room for suggestion, which can knock you off balance"
- Later adds: a new target (IFS unburdening) and extra risk groups (dissociation, spiritual bypassing). The caution itself is the third or fourth time (B165, When the Adult Voice Feels Fake, says it too). Mostly told again.
- Better: B277. It gives the reason and the balloon study; B364 leans on it.

**3. The third job can go by other names, Guru included (minor).**
- B242, The Inner Guide Comes Later: The Wise One works, or your Inner Teacher, and "Guru" does for some people
- B362, IFS section: "Nurturer, Protector, and Leader, Guide, or Guru."
- Later adds: only "Leader". A faint echo.
- Better: B242. It gives the reason and the cult-survivor caveat; B362 just lists labels.

**4. There is no perfect Higher Self already there.**
- B252, Borrow Hindsight: "No perfect Higher Self is handing down answers."
- B362, IFS section: "I emphasize the inner adult you build and practice occupying"
- B363, IFS section: "a perfect Higher Self is already waiting underneath everything"
- Later adds: B362 adds the IFS contrast. B363's last sentence restates B362's divergence as a rejection and adds only the labels "neo-Advaita" and "spiritual-bypassing". B252 to B363 is the same phrase and claim. A reader would feel told again, and B362 to B363 sit back to back.
- Better: B362. It sets the claim against IFS's own wording right where the contrast is drawn.

**5. Seek helpers who won't steer you toward an answer.**
- B279, Use Childhood Photographs Gently: "get support from someone level-headed who won't lead you toward an answer"
- B366, IFS section: "You'd want one who's trauma-informed and doesn't put ideas in your head"
- Later adds: trauma-informed, understands dissociation, and the therapist-choosing context. The no-steering requirement is the same. Mild echo. (B364 to B366 is risk then remedy, not a repeat.)
- Better: B366. Fuller criteria, in the place where a therapist is being chosen.

**6. A gentle, skilled practitioner makes IFS fine.**
- B365, IFS section: "A gentle practitioner can walk you through it fine."
- B366, IFS section: "They may, if they're skilled and grounded."
- Later adds: what to look for and the reparenting alternative. The baseline "it depends on the practitioner" is told twice in consecutive blocks.
- Better: B366. It is the natural place for practitioner advice; B365's sentence is the redundant one.

**7. The method is my own blend; the sources aren't presented as backing it whole (mild).**
- B368, Related Clinical Work: "I borrow particular distinctions and practices rather than presenting the whole synthesis as their consensus"
- B370, Teachers and Practice Resources: "none follows my whole approach or has expressed interest in doing so"
- Later adds: the mirror image. B368 says he doesn't claim the approaches' backing; B370 says he doesn't fully endorse the teachers, with a reason. Same disclaimer for two groups, two blocks apart; a reader may feel it twice.
- Better: B368. It is broader and comes first; B370 is natural for the teacher list but repeats the stance.

## 1b. Callbacks (not counted as repeats)

## 2. Contradictions

**1. Warnings about IFS versus "filter for IFS".**
- B365, IFS section: "an institute with certifications behind the model doesn't by itself make it any truer, or safer"
- B366, IFS section: "a good reparenting therapist may fit better"
- B372, Teachers and Practice Resources: "IFS-trained therapists often have the most relevant background for inner-child pl/ork"
- The IFS section says people can end up more fragmented (B365), that certification doesn't make it safer, and that reparenting may fit better for the little one's pain (B366). B372 then says to filter for IFS therapists as the most relevant for inner-child work. No reason is given for the switch (for example, a directory with no reparenting filter), and B372 drops B366's conditions (skilled, trauma-informed, no suggesting). Mild, since B366's hedges leave room for both.
