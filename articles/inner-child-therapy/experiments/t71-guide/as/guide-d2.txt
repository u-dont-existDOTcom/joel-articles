The gate matters. Altered states can get past the guards before the adult functions are ready to hold what appears. Build sober Nurturer and Protector capacity first, or borrow those capacities from grounded support that does not seize authority over the experience. A personal history of voices, paranoia, major loss of reality testing, or persistent perceptual disturbance after a psychoactive substance belongs inside this gate before planning another altered-state session, including with a different substance.
