## 1. Repeats (in article order of the earliest block cited)

**1. Divine love passed down to the little one (weak).**
- B120 (Your Loan is PRE-APPROVED!): "You can have an enormous spiritual feast upstairs while the child is still starving downstairs."
- B196 (Urge): "bring that love down from the heavens to my inner child"
- B314 (Require): "Send some of it down to your frightened little one"

Point: borrowed or divine love is meant to be handed down to the little one. B314 adds the spiritual-attack use, passing it on toward what you fear, and the support becoming your own; the handing-down move is told a third time. Better: B120, which has the reason and the payoff; B314 applies it.

**2. Immediate safety step when something pushes you toward harm (moderate).**
- B291 (If You Want to Kill Yourself): "get another human being involved, and put some distance between you and the means" (it also covers anyone "badly disoriented or intoxicated")
- B315 (Require): "move away from that open window and get some outside support"
- Partial echoes: B176 (Start With Whatever Showed Up) "if you're in danger, get safe first"; B203 (Urge) "Get professional help right away if any of this is severe or risky"; B313 (Require) "Same goes if it demands you hurt or use anybody, yourself included." (B313 only classifies the presence, so it is not the same step.)

Point: get distance from the means and a person involved. B315 adds voices and urges from spiritual attack, and the line "Know the limit of what you can handle, but don't turn it into a prison for life."; the safety step itself a reader has already had. Better: B291, which is fuller and sits in the section about acting on self-harm.

**3. The gate before deep altered-state work (moderate).**
- B28 (My Journey, image note): "Build sober Nurturer and Protector capacity first"
- B195 (Urge): "I'd hold off until there's enough Nurturer and Protector around"
- B301 (Altered): "I'd learn to do inner-child therapy sober first, in your ordinary mindstate"
- B302 (Altered): "get enough Nurturer and Protector in the room before you go deep"

Point: do not go deep with altered states until the inner adult is strong enough. B302 is marked as a callback ("Remember those guards from the urge to escape? Same deal here"), but the instruction after it is the same one. It adds that borrowed help must come from "someone steady who won't try to run your trip for you" (B194 in Urge already asks for "someone steady"). B301's "Low doses" also restates B195's "lower doses" and B197's "slow and gentle protocols". A reader is told the gate a third time, softened by the pointer. Better: B195 carries the reason (the guards, no grown-up big enough to hold what comes through); the Altered section is the natural place for the gate itself.

**4. Trance means you can't freak out (strong).**
- B199 (Urge): "When you're in a trance, you can't freak out"
- B309 (Require): "In trance you can basically work with the fear." and "You can't ruin your life at all in trance"

Point: in a trance, scary experiences do not turn into panic or ruin. B309 adds sleep paralysis, the consequences of freaking out (hospital, housing, jail, suicide) and the bad-hypnotic-suggestion exception; B199 adds the THH/DMT dosing use that B309 lacks. The central claim is simply told again. Better: B309 (mechanism, exception, consequences); B199 is a bare aside inside the psychosis story.

**5. Love sent to the attacking demon on psychedelics (moderate).**
- B302 (Altered): "It can be supremely empowering when you can send love and see how it fixes the situation."
- B310 (Require): "I sent unconditional love to this demon on me."
- B311 (Require): "I’ve also trained other people to do this during spiritual attack while on psychedelics."

Point: on a psychedelic, meet the attacker with love and it resolves. B302 previews what B310 shows and B311 restates for the psychedelic case. B310-B311 add the evidence, the loving-kindness prerequisite and the comparison with exorcism. B302 also raises the demon topic before B304 opens it again. Better: B310-B311, which have the evidence; B302's last sentence is spent before the story arrives.

**6. Without a grown-up inside, you are an easy target (strong).**
- B304 (Require): "since most people never actually grew up, they are easy targets for every kind of influence"
- B317 (Non-altered): "we’re always being manipulated and attacked by whatever evil force sees us as an easy target"
- B318 (Non-altered): "Wandering the world aimlessly and getting manipulated by all kinds of evil forces."
- Earlier, narrower form, not counted: B9 (The Chicken-and-Egg Problem) "without an inner Guide, it's easy to go looking for one in a cult"

Point: no developed inner parent means open to outside manipulation. B304 has already widened it to "every kind of influence", so B317 reads as the same widening; it adds social media and the benefit of having the inner parent. B318 restates the evil-forces claim in the very next block, to answer the idea that reparenting is silly. Better: B317, because its heading is the non-altered case.

**7. Love does not mean dropping your defenses (weak).**
- B312 (Require): "Loving someone does not mean you leave the front door unlocked"

Point: loving or forgiving someone does not cancel protection. Each applies it to a different case, and B312 adds the astral-versus-physical caveat. A reader may feel the principle again. Better: B324, which gives the reasoning.

## 2. Contradictions

**C1. Is the attacker real, or just fear?** B309 (Require): "You recognize it has no real world significance and it's purely just fear" versus B302 (Altered) "It can be real", B304 (Require) "attacked by a literal demon", and B310 (Require) "once that love was seen as enduring, the demon left". One passage treats the frightening thing as fear to work with; the others treat it as a real entity that love drives off. B312's "The astral plane is a bit different from the 3D." could reconcile them but is not tied to B309, and "it" in B309 could mean only the fear. Medium-low.

**C2. Paranoia as a warning sign versus "not necessarily paranoia".** B198 (Urge): "Paranoia during a rough trip is a warning sign for psychosis." versus B302 (Altered): "feeling attacked by a demon on a psychedelic is not necessarily paranoia". B302 flags the clash itself and gives a reason ("It can be real"), so it is not unexplained. But nothing tells the reader how to tell a real attack from paranoia, and B203 (Urge) says "If you can't tell whether you're safe, I'd treat that as risky." Medium.

Checked, not counted:
- B305 (Require) is wary of psychiatrists and pills while B203 says get professional help; B305 itself adds "That said, professional assessment may be warranted in some situations."
- B301 (Altered) lists iboga among low-dose helpers while B200 (Urge) says "just a small amount of male iboga root bark got me a bit hypomanic". B301 does not say male, and B302 says the caution signs count.
- B318 "reparenting sounds silly" against B18 (My Journey) "sounded silly to me": different jobs.
