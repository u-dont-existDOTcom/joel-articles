And if a trip or some other altered state once showed you a depth of love or wellbeing you'd never imagined, that matters. It can stay a reference point, and you don't have to recreate it. You might wonder why you can't just feel like that all the time. Well, feeling it once and reaching it only in a certain state are two different things. Reaching it reliably in daily life is a third.

A beautiful state can become a hook too, whether it's a psychedelic opening or a dream. Hypnosis or a meditation high can do the same, and so can a sudden flood of love. Once it's the new standard, daily life feels like failure. Maybe you catch yourself thinking you should be back there by now. But however intense or beautiful it was, and however much relief or revelation it brought, that doesn't prove what you saw historically happened, or that your healing is complete. Watch the fruits instead. They show up after the state ends, in what's gotten more honest and protective, and in what you can rely on now.

If your little one can't receive the love, it's easy to think, okay, I just have to push harder. But don't answer by turning up the intensity or forcing another breakthrough. Something's keeping the love from getting through, so meet that, whatever it is.
