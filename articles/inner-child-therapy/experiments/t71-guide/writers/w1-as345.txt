And as for the beautiful side of going deep, an extraordinary opening you've had can stay a reference point. It doesn't have to turn into "Now I have to get back there." If an altered state once showed you a depth of love or wellbeing you'd never imagined, that matters. But having it once is different from being able to reach it only in a particular state, and both are different from reaching it reliably day to day.

The beautiful state can become a hook too. It could be a psychedelic opening or a dream. Maybe it was a hypnosis session, or a meditation high. Sometimes it's just a sudden flood of love. Any of those can become the new standard, and then ordinary life feels like failure. You had it, so why can't you feel it now?

However intense or beautiful it was, and however much of a relief or a revelation, it doesn't prove that what you saw happened historically, or that your healing is complete. Look for what becomes more honest after the state ends, and more protective and reliable. It's the same "watch the fruits" as with a spiritual path. And if your little one can't receive the love, don't answer that by turning up the intensity or forcing another breakthrough. Something is keeping the love from getting through, so meet that, whatever it is.
