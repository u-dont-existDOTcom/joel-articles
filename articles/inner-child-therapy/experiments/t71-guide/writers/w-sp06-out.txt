VERSION 1
## One Intention in the Morning, One Review at Night

In the morning, give yourself one modest intention: "Today I will notice one hook before I act from it." Then at night, how'd it go? Look back over the day, and leave out the cross-examining. Maybe there was one you caught right as you started tightening up. Or it's only now, thinking it over, that you realize, oh, that was a hook, and you went through the whole sequence anyway. That counts as progress too, seeing it afterward. For that one, what's the next small repair, or something the Protector could do? And what good came your way today? You might not think of any right away, and then there's a warm moment you'd forgotten about, or something you enjoyed. Perhaps you got a little braver somewhere, or felt close to somebody. Whatever can be repaired, go ahead and repair it. Then renew the intention for tomorrow and go to sleep.

VERSION 2
## One Intention in the Morning, One Review at Night

At night, before you go to sleep, think back to the one modest intention you set that morning: "Today I will notice one hook before I act from it." Then go over the day and see how it went, and keep the courtroom out of it. You might find a moment you did catch one, right as you started to tighten up. If you only spotted one afterward, once you'd gone through the whole sequence anyway, does that count? Well, it's after the fact, but yes, count it as progress. And as for that one, what would the next small repair be, or is there something protective you could do?

Then go looking for whatever good came to you today. Something warm, maybe, or just something fun. If you're used to only checking what went wrong, this part might feel a little strange. Maybe you found a bit of courage somewhere, or had a moment of feeling close to someone. Repair what can be repaired. Then renew the intention for tomorrow and get some sleep.

VERSION 3
## One Intention in the Morning, One Review at Night

When you get up, give yourself one modest intention: "Today I will notice one hook before I act from it." Then at some point in the day you might feel yourself tighten up and think, wait, is this one?

At night, look back over the day, and don't go building a case against yourself. The first thing that comes up might be a hook that got you anyway, where you went through the whole thing and only saw it afterward. Spotting it counts as progress, even if you're already flopping around in the boat by then 😂. What's the next small thing you could do about that one, some repair, or something protective? And there might be one you did catch in time, too. Then look for the good stuff you got today, some warmth, or something you enjoyed. Maybe you got brave about something, or there was somebody you felt close to. Once whatever can be repaired is repaired, say the intention again for tomorrow and go to sleep.
