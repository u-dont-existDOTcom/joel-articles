VERSION 1
## Just Like Me

This one's a way to stop carrying around a feeling that everybody has, like it's proof that you alone are defective. And it sure can seem like proof, when you're in the middle of it. So name the feeling plainly, just the one (fear, shame, loneliness, craving, grief, anger or confusion, whichever it is), and say, "Other people know this feeling too. Just like me, they want relief. Just like me, they sometimes make it worse trying to escape it. May all of us find enough support to stay present and not pass the pain forward." That second "just like me" might sting a little.

Only do any of this when you've got your bearings, enough that thinking about other people won't pull you away from yourself. If someone who abused you is one of those other people, nobody's asking you to forgive them, or imagine what their childhood was like. Getting in touch with them while you're flooded isn't part of it either. Breathe normally, and let the wish include you first, then whoever comes to mind.

VERSION 2
## Just Like Me

"Other people know this feeling too. Just like me, they want relief. Just like me, they sometimes make it worse trying to escape it. May all of us find enough support to stay present and not pass the pain forward." That "all of us" is a big circle, and it can include people who hurt you. You don't have to forgive an abuser to say it, though, or picture their childhood. Same with getting in touch with them while you're flooded. And "this feeling" is just one, named plainly before you start, whether it's fear or shame, loneliness, craving, grief, anger or confusion.

Whichever it is, it's one everybody has, and saying the wish is a way to stop carrying it as proof that something's wrong with you, and only you. If letting everyone in would pull you away from yourself right now, wait until you've got your bearings. Breathe normally and let the wish include you first, and after that anyone who comes to mind. That part might feel backwards, if you're used to going last.

VERSION 3
## Just Like Me

Some people hear "just like me" and brace themselves, like they're about to be told to forgive someone. This isn't a demand to forgive an abuser, though, or to imagine their childhood. It doesn't mean getting in touch with them while you're flooded, either. Only try it when you've got your bearings, enough that bringing other people into it won't pull you away from yourself. You're the first one it's for, after all.

Pick one feeling and name it plainly, like fear, shame, loneliness, craving, grief, anger or confusion, whatever it is. Everybody has it, and this is a way to stop carrying it around as proof that you're the only one who's defective. Breathe normally and say this, letting the wish include you and then anyone who comes to mind: "Other people know this feeling too. Just like me, they want relief. Just like me, they sometimes make it worse trying to escape it. May all of us find enough support to stay present and not pass the pain forward."
