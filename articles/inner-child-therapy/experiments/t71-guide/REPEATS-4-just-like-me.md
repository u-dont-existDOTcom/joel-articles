## 1. Repeats (article order)

**1. B117 and B261**
- B117, section "Your Loan is PRE-APPROVED!" (under "Ask God for a Loan"): "the plight of the countless other beings suffering at that very moment, authentically bringing up a wellspring of compassion"
- B261, section "Just Like Me": "think of how many people are feeling that exact thing right now, and wanting it to stop, just like you"
- Shared point: others are hurting right now as you are, and holding that opens compassion.
- Later one adds a practice around it (name the feeling, the "you first" wish, "just like you" even for bad coping). The core move is the same, so a reader feels an echo of B117.
- Better: B261. It is the instruction itself, in the practices section; B117 mentions it in passing inside a gratitude story.

**2. B262 and B327**
- B262, section "Just Like Me": "Forgiving them isn't part of it, and neither is picturing their childhood."
- B327, section "How to Forgive Without Forgetting": "And you don't have to forgive first to get safe, set boundaries, hold someone accountable, get justice or get better."
- Shared point: you are not required to forgive the person who hurt you (yet).
- Later one adds a great deal (forgiveness may come eventually, no schedule, forced early forgiveness is an act). B262's clause reads as a scope note, so the echo is faint.
- Better: B327, the dedicated place, with reasons.

**3. B262 and B336**
- B262, section "Just Like Me": "Getting in touch is a separate decision, for a much calmer day, if it ever comes."
- B336, section "When One Life Has Ended and the Next Hasn’t Begun": "It doesn't stand in for grieving, though, or for getting in touch when that's right, and your boundaries still apply."
- Shared point: a private wishing practice does not decide whether to contact the person.
- Later one adds grieving, boundaries and practical matters, and the person is someone you miss, not someone who hurt you. The caution itself is the same, even down to "getting in touch", so a reader likely feels told again.
- Better: B336. It carries the fuller caution at the point where contact is most tempting; B262's is the thinner echo.

**4. B262 and B350**
- B262, section "Just Like Me": "With that many people feeling the same thing, the theory that you're the defective one gets kind of hard to keep up."
- B350, section "You Are Worthy of Love. Always.": "And you aren't alone. There's help, and lots of other people are out there learning to look after their own little ones."
- Shared point: many others share this, so you are not alone or uniquely broken.
- Later one adds "there's help" and speaks of the reparenting work rather than the feeling. It reads as a closing reassurance, so the echo is faint.
- Better: B262, because it gives the reason; B350 only states the conclusion.

## 2. Contradictions

None that a reasonable reader would count. The two closest, both set aside:
- B262 puts "picturing their childhood" outside this practice, while B328 ("Picture everything that had made them who they were by that moment") and B330 ("come back to how whoever did it got to be someone who could do that") ask for it. B262 limits itself to this practice and B327 says forgiveness comes later, so there is no conflict.
- B261 says this kind of wishing works best on a steadier day, while B335 says "When you miss someone, let the ache be a cue for love." B335 is about one person, and B336 adds its own stop sign ("if it starts making you more compulsive, or a bit unreal"), so there is no conflict.

Callbacks: none into or out of the section. Within the section, B262's last line pays off B261's "how many people" step; that is not a repeat.
