That said, I'm not telling you to avoid IFS therapists. A good one can help a lot, especially a grounded, trauma-informed one who really gets dissociation and won't steer you toward answers. But for inner-child pain, like shame or a habit of abandoning yourself, I'd lean toward a good reparenting therapist, and the same goes if there's no reliable grown-up in there yet. Whoever you pick, know the strengths and the pitfalls going in.
