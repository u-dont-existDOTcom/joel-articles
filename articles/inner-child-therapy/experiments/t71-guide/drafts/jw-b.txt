Sometimes you ask and get silence, and then you're just sitting there wondering if you're doing it wrong. Try writing instead, with your wrong hand, the clumsy one whose letters already look like a little kid's. Whatever scared or hurt feelings are really there go on the page, nothing invented. Your good hand writes back as the grown-up, doing whichever of its three jobs is needed. And if the protective part shows up first, it gets the clumsy hand and its say before anyone asks your little one for theirs.
