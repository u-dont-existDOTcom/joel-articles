Sometimes your little one just won't talk, and picturing them gets you nowhere either. That's when it's worth grabbing a pen and switching hands. Your clumsy hand already writes like a first grader, so make it your little one's, and have it put down whatever scared or hurt stuff is really there (no making it up to fill the silence). Your usual hand answers as the grown-up who's got their back. And if the protective part grabs the pen first? Then let it use the clumsy hand first. The guard gets to talk before your little one does.
