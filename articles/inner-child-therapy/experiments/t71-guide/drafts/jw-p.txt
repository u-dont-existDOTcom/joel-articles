Lucia Capacchione, an art therapist, has been teaching this one for decades (it's in her book [Recovery of Your Inner Child](https://www.luciac.com/)). Sounds almost too simple, I know. Your non-dominant hand writes down whatever scared or hurt feelings are really there (nothing made up). It writes like a first grader anyway, so it's a natural fit. Then your usual hand writes back as the grown-up. If talking or picturing has only gotten you silence, give the clumsy hand a try. A protective part might grab the pen first, and then it gets that hand until it's had its say.
