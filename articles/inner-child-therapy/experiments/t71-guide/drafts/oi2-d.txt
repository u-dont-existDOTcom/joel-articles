At night he went over the day in a little book, with a black spot for every fault, and he admits he was surprised to find himself "so much fuller of faults" than he'd imagined. He scraped out old spots to make room for new ones until the paper was full of holes. You can leave the spots out. Just look at where you caught the hook, and where it ran its routine on you anyway. Only seeing it now, looking back? That counts too. Whoever noticed wasn't all the way inside it.
