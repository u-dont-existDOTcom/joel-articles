Benjamin Franklin ran a version of this. His [morning question](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/20203) was "What good shall I do this day?" and his evening one "What good have I done to-day?" Here the morning gets one small intention instead, like "Today I'll notice one hook before I act on it." Then the evening asks where you caught the hook, and where it ran its routine on you anyway. And what's the next small repair, or something that'd protect your little one?
