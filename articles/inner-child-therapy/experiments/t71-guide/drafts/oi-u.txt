The night half of this is a lot like the [Examen](https://www.ignatianspirituality.com/ignatian-prayer/the-examen/), the evening review Jesuits have done for centuries, just aimed at hooks. Not bad company, honestly. In the morning you pick one small intention, like "Today I'll notice one hook before I act on it." At night you look back over the day. Where did you catch the hook, and where did it run its routine anyway? What's the next small repair, or something that'd protect your little one?
