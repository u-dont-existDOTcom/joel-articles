Lucia Capacchione, an art therapist, has been teaching this one for decades (it's in her book [Recovery of Your Inner Child](https://www.luciac.com/)). Her trick is simple: the hand that writes like a first grader gets to be the first grader. So the scared or hurt feelings go down in your non-dominant hand (nothing made up), and your usual hand answers as the grown-up. If talking or picturing gets you only silence, the clumsy hand is often chattier. And if the protective part grabs the pen first, it gets that hand until it's had its say.
