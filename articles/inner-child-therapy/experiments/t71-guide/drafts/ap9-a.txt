This framework grew out of my own practice, plus the teachers and collaborators I credit below. Later revisions also compare notes with other approaches, like schema therapy, compassion-focused therapy, attachment repair, motivational interviewing, mentalizing, metacognitive therapy, behavioral activation, radically open DBT, emotion-focused therapy, spiritually integrated psychotherapy and so on. They don't all claim the same things. I borrow particular distinctions and practices from them, and I'm not saying they'd agree with the whole synthesis, or that their research backs it up as a package.
