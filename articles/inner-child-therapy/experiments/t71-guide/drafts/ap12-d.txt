IFS (Internal Family Systems) overlaps a lot with what I'm describing here. It has protective parts too, and it teaches people to approach them without shame or a fight, so "meet the protector first" fits right in. My reparenting method diverges from IFS at an important point, though, and it's about who's doing the healing. Standard IFS says it's the Self, a calm, compassionate source that's supposed to show up once your parts "unblend." For me it's the inner adult you build and practice being, that one grown-up doing all three jobs.
