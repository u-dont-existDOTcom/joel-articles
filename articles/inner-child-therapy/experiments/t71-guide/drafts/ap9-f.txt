This framework developed through the teachers and collaborators credited below and my own practice. Later revisions also compare notes with other approaches, like schema therapy, compassion-focused therapy, attachment repair, motivational interviewing, mentalizing, metacognitive therapy, behavioral activation, radically open DBT, emotion-focused therapy, spiritually integrated psychotherapy and so on. I borrow particular distinctions and practices rather than presenting the whole synthesis as their consensus or claiming that their research validates it as a package. Those don't even agree with each other on everything!
