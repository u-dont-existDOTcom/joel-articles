Jack Kornfield has a whole book on what comes next, [After the Ecstasy, the Laundry](https://jackkornfield.com/after-the-ecstasy-the-laundry/). Love that title. Because if that night turns into the bar, every morning with the laundry starts to feel like failing, and the night itself, gorgeous as it was, can't prove that what you saw really happened, or that you're done healing. The laundry can, a bit. Are you more honest these days? More protective of your little one, easier to count on?
