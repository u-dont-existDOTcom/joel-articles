The beautiful state can become a hook, too. Say you had one amazing night on a psychedelic, or a dream like that, and now it's the bar, so regular life starts to feel like failing. How intense or beautiful it was doesn't prove that what you saw really happened, or that you're done healing. I'd look at what's changed once it wears off. Are you more honest, more protective of your little one, more reliable, all that?
