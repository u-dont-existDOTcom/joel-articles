Put the vulnerable thoughts and feelings down with the hand you don't write with, then answer them with the one you do, as the grown-up you. If talking to your little one or picturing them has only gotten you silence, this can get you past it. A protective part might show up ahead of your little one, and if it does, it gets the clumsy hand first. When you switch, that's your grown-up holding the pen. The other hand already writes like a child, so let it take as long as it takes.

Sometimes you write and write to make sense of things, and come out more mixed up than when you started. Then try a memory aid instead: the date and what happened, kept short, or a note about what helped. A reminder or a next step works too. Or a voice note, if you'd rather skip the paper. You're keeping track of what's been going on, and you don't have to make sense of it while you're doing that. A memory aid doesn't need to explain your life. If looking for patterns seems useful later, the notes will be there.
