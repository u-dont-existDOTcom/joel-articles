The night half of this is a lot like the [Examen](https://www.ignatianspirituality.com/ignatian-prayer/the-examen/), the evening review Jesuits have done for centuries, just aimed at hooks. Mornings, pick one small intention, like "Today I'll notice one hook before I act on it." At night, look back over the day. Where did you catch the hook, and where did it run its routine anyway? Then what's the next small repair, or something that'd protect your little one?
