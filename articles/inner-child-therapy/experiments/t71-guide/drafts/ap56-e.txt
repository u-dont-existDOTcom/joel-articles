IFS calls these wounded young parts “exiles.” I find that pathologizing. I prefer to speak of a child who needs parenting and integration, rather than naming them by their wounded or excluded condition. And "unburdening," often done after witnessing a part's origin story? It can be powerful. It can also get dramatic, lots of memories and lots of room for suggestion, enough to knock someone off balance if they tend to dissociate (DID-like fragmentation included) or to spiritually bypass.
