Lucia Capacchione, an art therapist, wrote a whole book on this one, [The Power of Your Other Hand](https://www.luciac.com/). Love that title. Your other hand, the clumsy one, writes like a first grader anyway, so it gets the scared or hurt feelings (nothing made up). Then your usual hand writes back as the grown-up. Plenty of people who get only silence when they try talking to their little one find that other hand has a lot to say. And if a protective part grabs the pen first, it gets that hand until it's had its say.
