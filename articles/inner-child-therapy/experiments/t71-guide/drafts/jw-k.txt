Talking gets you nowhere, and the pictures won't come either? Then grab a pen. Write with your non-dominant hand, the clumsy one, whose letters already look like a first grader's (kinda the point, actually). Let it put down whatever scared or hurt feelings are really there, nothing made up. Your usual hand writes back as the grown-up. And if the protective part grabs the pen first, that's fine too, it gets the clumsy hand until it's had its say.
