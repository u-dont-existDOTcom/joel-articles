Benjamin Franklin ran a version of this. His [morning question](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/20203) was "What good shall I do this day?" and his evening one "What good have I done to-day?" Big questions. Yours can be one hook, like "Today I'll notice one hook before I act on it." And at night his "What good have I done?" turns into where you caught the hook, and where it got you anyway, before you noticed. For that one, what's the next small repair, or something that'd protect your little one?
