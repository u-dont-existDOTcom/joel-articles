None of this means you should stay away from IFS therapists. A grounded, trauma-informed one who doesn't steer you toward answers can help a lot, especially if they really get dissociation. But for inner-child pain, like shame or a habit of abandoning yourself, I'd lean toward a good reparenting therapist, and the same goes if there's no reliable grown-up in there yet. Whoever you pick, know the strengths and the pitfalls going in.
