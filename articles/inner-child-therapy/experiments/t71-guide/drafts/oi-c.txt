This one's about as low-tech as it gets. You wake up and pick one small thing to watch for, like "Today I'll notice one hook before I act on it." Honestly, one is plenty, since hooks are sneaky and don't announce themselves. Then at night, before bed, you look back over the day the way a friend would, no cross-examination. Where did you catch it? Where did it run the whole routine anyway? What's one small repair, or some little thing that would protect your little one tomorrow?
