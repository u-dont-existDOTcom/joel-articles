Benjamin Franklin ran a version of this. His [morning question](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/20203) was "What good shall I do this day?" and his evening one "What good have I done to-day?" Big questions. Yours can be one hook, like "Today I'll notice one hook before I act on it." By evening you'll probably have one you caught, and one that ran its whole routine on you anyway. For that one, what's the next small repair, or something that'd protect your little one?
