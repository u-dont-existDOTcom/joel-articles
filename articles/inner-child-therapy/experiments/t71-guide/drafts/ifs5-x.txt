Schwartz says "all of us are born with many sub-minds," and the IFS map of them keeps expanding: managers, firefighters, exiles, polarized parts, legacy burdens, Self-energy, guides, new subparts... That's a lot of rooms to get lost in. Some practitioners use the model gently. Others seem to leave people more fragmented while trying to fix the fragmenting. And institutes and certifications don't make a framework any truer, or safer for that matter.
