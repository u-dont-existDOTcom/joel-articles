Sometimes community comes first, and sometimes the opening does. Loving people can give somebody enough safety and belonging that a deeper opening becomes possible. Or it's the other way around: somebody has a big opening first, a love or a meaning they couldn't have imagined before, and then they start to "manifest" different kinds of people and relationships in their life. Either way, the community is what helps make it practical, real, down to earth, embodied and so on.
