Daniel Kahneman and Gary Klein came at intuition from two camps of research that were supposed to be at odds, and the paper they wrote together is called [Conditions for Intuitive Expertise: A Failure to Disagree](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0016755). They start from intuition being "sometimes marvelous and sometimes flawed." Yours too, probably. So which kind is a given hunch? How sure it feels won't tell you. Their answer, roughly, is to ask whether the situation is predictable at all, and whether you've had a real chance to learn it. A hunch can pick up on a pattern before your head can explain it, so hear it out, and then let your head have a look. Neither one should decide alone, though. Your head can get tired of not knowing and be glad to hand the whole thing over, to a hunch or to somebody who sounds sure.
