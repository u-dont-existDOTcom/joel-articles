This framework grew out of my own practice, plus the teachers and collaborators I credit below. Later revisions also compare notes with other approaches, like schema therapy, compassion-focused therapy, attachment repair, motivational interviewing, mentalizing, metacognitive therapy, behavioral activation, radically open DBT, emotion-focused therapy, spiritually integrated psychotherapy and so on. Those don't even agree with each other on everything! So I borrow particular ideas and practices from them, and that's it. I'm not saying they'd sign off on the whole synthesis, or that their research proves it as a package.
