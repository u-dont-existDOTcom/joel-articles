Does saying it make the memory fiction? Well, no. It's still what happened, as far as you remember it, even if some details are fuzzy, and nobody's off the hook either (any repair that's owed is still owed). Keep an ear on the narrator, though. If they start to sneer, stop. Stop too if you feel more ashamed, or kind of unreal, since telling it from farther away won't fix that. When it does help, let it stay one line, and maybe ask the younger you in that story what they need from you now.
