This one's a way to stop carrying around a feeling that everybody has, like it's proof that you alone are defective. And it sure can seem like proof, when you're in the middle of it. So name the feeling plainly. Just the one, like loneliness. Then say, "Other people know this feeling too. Just like me, they want relief. Just like me, they sometimes make it worse trying to escape it. May all of us find enough support to stay present and not pass the pain forward." That second "just like me" might sting a little.

Only do any of this when you've got your bearings, enough that thinking about other people won't pull you away from yourself. If someone who abused you is one of those other people, nobody's asking you to forgive them, or imagine what their childhood was like. Getting in touch with them while you're flooded isn't part of it either. Breathe normally, and let the wish include you first, then whoever comes to mind.
