Benjamin Franklin ran a version of this. His [morning question](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/20203) was "What good shall I do this day?" and his evening one "What good have I done to-day?" Not exactly small questions. Even so, he worked on his thirteen virtues one at a time, a week apiece. So you can stick to one hook, whichever one shows up. "Today I'll notice one hook before I act on it," say.
