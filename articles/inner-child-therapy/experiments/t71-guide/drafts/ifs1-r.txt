Richard Schwartz started [Internal Family Systems](https://ifs-institute.com/) (IFS) back in the 1980s. This method overlaps with it a lot. IFS recognizes protective parts too, and teaches people to approach them without shame or a fight, so "meet the protector first" fits right in. My reparenting method diverges from IFS at an important point. In standard IFS, the healer is the Self, a calm, compassionate source that's supposed to show up once your parts "unblend." I emphasize the inner adult you build and practice occupying: Nurturer, Protector, and Leader, Guide, or Guru.
