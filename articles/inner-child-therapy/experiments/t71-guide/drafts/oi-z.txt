Benjamin Franklin ran a version of this. His [morning question](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/20203) was "What good shall I do this day?" and his evening one "What good have I done to-day?" Franklin aimed high, as usual. Here the aim is one small intention, like "Today I'll notice one hook before I act on it." And the evening question is where you caught the hook, and where it ran its routine on you anyway, plus what the next small repair is, or something that'd protect your little one.
