However intense or beautiful it was, and however much of a relief or a revelation, it doesn't prove that what you saw happened historically, or that your healing is complete. Look for what becomes more honest after the state ends, and more protective and reliable. It's the same "watch the fruits" as with a spiritual path. And if your little one can't receive the love, don't answer that by turning up the intensity or forcing another breakthrough. Something is keeping the love from getting through, so meet that, whatever it is.
