If you know Internal Family Systems (IFS), some of this will sound familiar. IFS talks about protective parts too, and about approaching them without shame or a fight, so meeting the protector first fits right in. Where my reparenting method really parts ways with IFS is the question of who does the healing. Standard IFS says it's the Self, a calm, compassionate source that's supposed to be there once your parts "unblend." For me it's the inner adult you build and practice being, that one grown-up doing all three jobs.
