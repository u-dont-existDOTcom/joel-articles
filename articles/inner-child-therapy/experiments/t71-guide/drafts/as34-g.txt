Say you had one of those nights where it felt like the whole universe loved you, and now you're back at work feeling pretty much nothing. That night still matters, as a reference point. But if you keep chasing it, it turns into a hook, and every regular day starts to feel like failing. And no matter how beautiful it was, it doesn't prove that what you saw really happened, or that you're done healing. Feeling it once, even feeling it again in that state, is a long way from living in it day to day. So I'd watch what changes afterward. Are you more honest now, more protective of your little one, more reliable, all that?
