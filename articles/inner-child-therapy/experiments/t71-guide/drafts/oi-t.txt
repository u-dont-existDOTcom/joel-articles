The night half of this is a lot like the [Examen](https://www.ignatianspirituality.com/ignatian-prayer/the-examen/), the evening review Jesuits have done for centuries. They look back over the day for where God showed up. Here you're looking for hooks: where did you catch one, and where did it run its whole routine anyway? Then what's the next small repair, or something that'd protect your little one tomorrow? (The morning half is one small intention, like "Today I'll notice one hook before I act on it.")
