The night half of this is a lot like the [Examen](https://www.ignatianspirituality.com/ignatian-prayer/the-examen/), the evening review Jesuits have done for centuries, just aimed at hooks. You start the day with one small intention, like "Today I'll notice one hook before I act on it." Then at night you go back over it, the way you'd look through the day's photos. Oh, there's one I caught. And there's one that got me anyway, so what's the next small repair, or something that'd protect my little one?
