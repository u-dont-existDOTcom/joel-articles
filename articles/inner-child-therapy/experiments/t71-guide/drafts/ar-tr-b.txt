Sleep paralysis is a trance-like state, and trance can be "more scary" because you're trapped in it, but it's also less actually dangerous. You can't ruin your life at all in trance (unless a bad hypnotic suggestion gets deeply implanted, perhaps), while without trance you can "freak out" in a way that might end up getting you committed to a mental hospital, thrown out of your housing, going to jail, possibly even killing yourself. In trance you can basically work with the fear. You recognize it has no real world significance and it's purely just fear, so it's super useful that way.
