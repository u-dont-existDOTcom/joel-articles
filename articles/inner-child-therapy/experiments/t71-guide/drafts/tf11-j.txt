Daniel Kahneman and Gary Klein came at intuition from two camps of research that were supposed to be at odds, and the paper they wrote together is called [Conditions for Intuitive Expertise: A Failure to Disagree](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0016755). They start from intuition being "sometimes marvelous and sometimes flawed." Yours too, probably. A hunch can pick up on a pattern before your head can explain it, like knowing a friend's upset from one word in a text. How sure it feels won't tell you which kind you've got, though. So hear it out, then let your head have a look, and don't let either one decide alone. Your head can get tired of not knowing and be glad to hand the whole thing over, to a hunch or to somebody who sounds sure.
