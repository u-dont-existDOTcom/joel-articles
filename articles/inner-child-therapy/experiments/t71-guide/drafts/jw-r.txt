Lucia Capacchione, an art therapist, wrote a whole book on this one, [The Power of Your Other Hand](https://www.luciac.com/). As titles go, it's pretty literal. Your other hand, the clumsy one, writes like a first grader anyway, so it gets the scared or hurt feelings (nothing made up). Your usual hand gets the grown-up's part and writes back. If talking or picturing has only gotten you silence, see what the other hand does with a pen. And if a protective part grabs it first? Then it gets that hand until it's had its say.
