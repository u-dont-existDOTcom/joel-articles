If talking or picturing gets you nothing but silence, try paper. Write with your wrong hand, the clumsy one whose letters already look like a first grader's, and put down whatever scared or hurt feelings are really there (no making things up for your little one). Then answer with your good hand, as the grown-up, doing whichever of its three jobs is needed. Does the protective part grab the pen first? Then it gets the wrong hand first, until it's had its say, and your little one can wait their turn.
