Lucia Capacchione, an art therapist, has been teaching this one for decades (it's in her book [Recovery of Your Inner Child](https://www.luciac.com/)). Write with your non-dominant hand, the clumsy one, whose letters already look like a first grader's, and let it put down whatever scared or hurt feelings are really there, nothing made up. Then your usual hand writes back as the grown-up. If talking to your little one or picturing them gets you only silence, this can get past it. And if the protective part grabs the pen first? It gets the clumsy hand until it's had its say.
