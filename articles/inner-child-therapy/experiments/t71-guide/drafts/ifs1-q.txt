So how's this different from Internal Family Systems (IFS)? Honestly, in a lot of ways it isn't. IFS recognizes protective parts too, and teaches people to approach them without shame or a fight, so "meet the protector first" fits right in. But my reparenting method diverges from IFS at an important point. In standard IFS, the healer is the Self, a calm, compassionate source that's supposed to show up once your parts "unblend." For me it's the inner adult you build and practice being, that one grown-up doing all three jobs.
