Whoever hurt you might come to mind, too. Forgiving them isn't part of it, and neither is picturing their childhood. Getting in touch is a separate decision, for a much calmer day, if it ever comes. With that many people feeling the same thing, the theory that you're the defective one gets kind of hard to keep up. (It's a gentler cousin of Pema Chödrön's [tonglen](https://www.lionsroar.com/how-to-practice-tonglen-meditation/). Normal breathing is fine.)
