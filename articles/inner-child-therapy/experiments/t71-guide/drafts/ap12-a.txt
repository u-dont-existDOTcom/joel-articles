If you know Internal Family Systems (IFS), a lot of this will look familiar. IFS recognizes protective parts too, and teaches people to approach them without shame or a fight, so meeting the protector first fits right in. My reparenting method diverges from IFS at an important point. In standard IFS, the healer is the Self, a calm, compassionate source inside you that's supposed to show up once your parts "unblend." I emphasize the inner adult you build and practice occupying: Nurturer, Protector, and Leader, Guide, or Guru.
