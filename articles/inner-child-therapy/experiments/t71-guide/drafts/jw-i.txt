What if your little one just won't talk, and picturing them gets you nowhere? Then try a pen in your other hand, the clumsy one, whose letters already look like a first grader's. Let it put down whatever scared or hurt feelings are really there, nothing made up. Then your usual hand answers as the grown-up who's got their back. And if the protective part grabs the pen first, it gets the clumsy hand until it's said its piece.
