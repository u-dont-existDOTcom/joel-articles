Then comes the part Jack Kornfield named a whole book after, [After the Ecstasy, the Laundry](https://jackkornfield.com/after-the-ecstasy-the-laundry/). A night like that can turn into a hook. It becomes the bar, and every morning since feels like failing. Gorgeous as it was, it doesn't prove that what you saw really happened, or that you're done healing. The weeks after tell you more. Are you a bit more honest? More protective of your little one, easier to count on?
