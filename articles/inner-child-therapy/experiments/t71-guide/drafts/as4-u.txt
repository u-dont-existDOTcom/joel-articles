Jack Kornfield has a whole book on what comes next, [After the Ecstasy, the Laundry](https://jackkornfield.com/after-the-ecstasy-the-laundry/). The title kind of says it all. That night can't be the bar for every morning after, or regular life starts to feel like failing. And gorgeous as it was, it doesn't prove that what you saw really happened, or that you're done healing. The laundry will tell you more. Are you a bit more honest these days, more protective of your little one, easier to count on?
