Benjamin Franklin ran a version of this. His [morning question](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/20203) was "What good shall I do this day?" and his evening one "What good have I done to-day?" Big questions. Yours can be one hook, like "Today I'll notice one hook before I act on it." At night, swap his question for two smaller ones. Where'd you catch the hook, and where'd it get you anyway? Then what's the next small repair, or something that'd protect your little one?
