Now, let's not throw out the beautiful stuff either. If a trip once showed you a love you didn't know existed, that's precious, and it can stay a reference point. Does that mean you have to keep chasing it? Well, no. Feeling it once on a psychedelic is still a long way from living in it day to day, and day to day is where we're headed anyway. The beautiful state can become a hook too, where every normal day feels like failing next to that one night. And however intense it was, it doesn't prove that what you saw really happened, or that you're done healing. I'd watch the fruits instead: since then, are you more honest, more protective of your little one, more reliable, all that? Turns out there's no express lane to enlightenment, sadly.
