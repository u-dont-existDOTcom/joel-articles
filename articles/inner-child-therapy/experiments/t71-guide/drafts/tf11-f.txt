Say you meet somebody and something in you goes, "I know this person." That might be onto something, since a hunch can catch a pattern before you can explain it. It might also be the old hook with a new face on it, so check it the slow way too: how have hunches like this worked out before, and does the conclusion follow from anything? Watch for the part of you that's tired of not knowing and would happily hand the whole question to the hunch, or to somebody who sounds sure. Daniel Kahneman and Gary Klein came from two camps of research on intuition that were supposed to disagree, and in [their paper](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0016755) they still agree that "Subjective experience is not a reliable indicator of judgment accuracy."
