The night half of this is a lot like the [Examen](https://www.ignatianspirituality.com/ignatian-prayer/the-examen/), the evening review Jesuits have done for centuries, just aimed at hooks. The morning half is one small intention, say "Today I'll notice one hook before I act on it." By bedtime there's usually some of each to look back on, a hook you caught and one that ran its whole routine on you anyway. For that second one, what's the next small repair, or something that'd protect your little one?
