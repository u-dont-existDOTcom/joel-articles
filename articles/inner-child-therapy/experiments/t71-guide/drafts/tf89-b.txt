Once you're the grown-up, your little one stays in the picture, and they bring the stuff adults tend to lose. Silliness and irreverence, rawness, creativity, that kind of thing, and maybe a connection to the Great Mystery from before any path gave it a name. You draw on that, and give back, more communion than command. My post [Treasuring the Inner Child's Gift](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/p/treasuring-the-inner-childs-gift) is one example (its subtitle: "Reparenting is often more Play than Work!").
