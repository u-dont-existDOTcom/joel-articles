Mornings, pick one small intention, like "Today I'll notice one hook before I act on it." At night, look back over the day the way a friend would, someone on your side. Where'd you catch the hook, and where'd it get you anyway? Either way, noticing counts, even if you're only spotting it now, in bed. Same if you broke the pattern somewhere. Then what's one small repair, or something that'd protect your little one tomorrow? And what good did you actually get today? A warm moment, some nerve you didn't know you had, whatever it was. Then it's the same intention tomorrow, and bed.
