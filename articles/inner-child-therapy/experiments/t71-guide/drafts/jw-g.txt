Maybe you've asked your little one how they're feeling and gotten nothing, and you're thinking, "Great, even they won't talk to me." Try a pen. Write with your clumsy hand, whose letters already look like a first grader's, and let it put down whatever scared or hurt feelings are really there, nothing made up. Then answer with your usual hand, as the grown-up who's got their back. If the protective part grabs the pen first, it gets the clumsy hand until it's said its piece.
