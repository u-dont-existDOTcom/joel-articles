IFS calls these wounded young parts "exiles." I find that pathologizing. I prefer to speak of a child who needs parenting and integration, rather than naming them by their wounded or excluded condition. IFS "unburdening," often done after witnessing a part's origin story, can be powerful. But it can also get dramatic, with a lot of focus on memories and plenty of room for suggestion, and that can knock people off balance, especially if they tend to dissociate (DID-like fragmentation included) or to spiritually bypass.
