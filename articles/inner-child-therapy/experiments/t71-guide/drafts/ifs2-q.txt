And what if that calm inner source just won't show up for you? That's where borrowed adulthood comes in. Maybe you borrow one grown-up job from a friend, or from a plan you've made, and take it in bit by bit. I have no use for another neo-Advaita, [spiritual-bypassing](http://goenka.u-dont-exist.com/) claim that a perfect Higher Self is already waiting underneath everything. A grown-up you can trust gets built through care and boundaries, and through action and direction.
