At night, before you go to sleep, think back to the one modest intention you set that morning: "Today I will notice one hook before I act from it." Then go over the day and see how it went, and keep the courtroom out of it. You might find a moment you did catch one, right as you started to tighten up. If you only spotted one afterward, once you'd gone through the whole sequence anyway, does that count? Well, it's after the fact, but yes, count it as progress. And as for that one, what would the next small repair be, or is there something protective you could do?

Then go looking for whatever good came to you today. Something warm, maybe. Or just something fun. If you're used to only checking what went wrong, this part might feel a little strange. Maybe you found a bit of courage somewhere, or had a moment of feeling close to someone. Repair what can be repaired. Then renew the intention for tomorrow and get some sleep.
