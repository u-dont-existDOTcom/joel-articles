Some little ones just won't talk, no matter how nicely you ask. Is that a dead end? Well, not if you have a pen. Write with your clumsy hand, whose letters already look like a first grader's, and let it put down whatever scared or hurt feelings are really there, nothing made up. Your usual hand answers as the grown-up who's got their back. And if the protective part grabs the pen first, it gets the clumsy hand first.
