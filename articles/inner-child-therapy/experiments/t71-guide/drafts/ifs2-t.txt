My reparenting method diverges from IFS at an important point. In standard IFS, the healer is the Self, a calm, compassionate inner source that's supposed to be available once your parts "unblend." I emphasize the inner adult you build and practice occupying: Nurturer, Protector, and Leader, Guide, or Guru. That's still one grown-up with three jobs, like back in The Chicken-and-Egg Problem.
