Daniel Kahneman and Gary Klein came at intuition from two camps of research that were supposed to be at odds, and the paper they wrote together is called [Conditions for Intuitive Expertise: A Failure to Disagree](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0016755). They start from intuition being "sometimes marvelous and sometimes flawed." Yours too, probably. A hunch can pick up on a pattern before your head can explain it, and how sure it feels won't tell you which kind you've got, so let your head have a look. Have hunches like this one been right before? Does the conclusion follow? Neither one should decide alone, though. Your head can get tired of not knowing and be glad to hand the whole thing over, to a hunch or to somebody who sounds sure.
