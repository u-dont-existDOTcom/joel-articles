Intuition is great at catching a pattern before you can explain it. Checking is a different job: does it contradict itself, how has it done before, does the conclusion actually follow? And the part of you that does the checking can get tired of not knowing, and be pretty relieved to hand the job off, to a hunch or to somebody else. I wouldn't let either one take over. A strong intuitive click is real information, and it still has to check out.
