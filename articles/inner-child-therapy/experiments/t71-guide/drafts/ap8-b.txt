A good IFS therapist may still help. Look for a grounded, trauma-informed one who doesn't steer you toward answers, especially someone who really understands dissociation. But if it's inner-child pain you're dealing with (shame, say, or a habit of abandoning yourself), a good reparenting therapist may fit better, and the same goes if there's no reliable grown-up in there yet. Either way, it's worth knowing the strengths and pitfalls of both before you pick.
