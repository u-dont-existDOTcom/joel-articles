My reparenting method diverges from IFS at an important point. The IFS Institute's [outline of the model](https://ifs-institute.com/resources/articles/internal-family-systems-model-outline) says "everyone has a Self," and that the Self "can and should lead," once your parts stop "blending" with it. I emphasize the inner adult you build and practice occupying: Nurturer, Protector, and Leader, Guide, or Guru. That's still one grown-up with three jobs, like back in The Chicken-and-Egg Problem.
