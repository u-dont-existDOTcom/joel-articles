Being the grown-up doesn't mean your little one has to leave the room. They carry a lot of what adults tend to lose, like the silliness and the irreverence, the rawness, the creativity, all that, and maybe a connection to the Great Mystery from before any path gave it a name. That's the spark of life they bring, and you get to draw on it and give back, so it's more communion than command. For one example of the spark, there's my post [Treasuring the Inner Child's Gift](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/p/treasuring-the-inner-childs-gift).
