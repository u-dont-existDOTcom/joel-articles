Lucia Capacchione, an art therapist, has been teaching this one for decades (it's in her book [Recovery of Your Inner Child](https://www.luciac.com/)). Her idea is simple. The hand you don't usually write with already writes like a first grader, so it gets the scared or hurt feelings, and the hand you do write with answers as the grown-up. Sometimes talking to your little one or picturing them gets you only silence, and then the clumsy hand turns out to be quite chatty. If the protective part grabs the pen first, though, it gets that hand until it's had its say.
