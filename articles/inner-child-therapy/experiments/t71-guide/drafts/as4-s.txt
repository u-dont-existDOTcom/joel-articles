Jack Kornfield has a whole book on what comes next, [After the Ecstasy, the Laundry](https://jackkornfield.com/after-the-ecstasy-the-laundry/). That one amazing night can turn into a hook, if it becomes the bar and every morning since feels like failing. And however gorgeous it was, it doesn't prove that what you saw really happened, or that you're done healing. The weeks after tell you more. Are you a bit more honest? More protective of your little one, easier to count on?
