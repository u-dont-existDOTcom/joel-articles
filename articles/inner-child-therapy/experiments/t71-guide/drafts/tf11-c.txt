A hunch can be onto something before you can say why, since it can pick up on a pattern before your head has the words for it. Then let your head check it, for holes in the story and for how hunches like it have done before. Does the conclusion follow from it? Watch for getting tired of not knowing, too, because it can be a relief to hand the whole question over to a hunch, or to somebody else. I wouldn't let either one run the show alone. A really strong click is still just something to weigh.
