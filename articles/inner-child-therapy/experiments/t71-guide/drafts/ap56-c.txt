IFS calls these wounded young parts “exiles.” I find that pathologizing. I prefer to speak of a child who needs parenting and integration, rather than naming them by their wounded or excluded condition. Then there's "unburdening," where the outline says parts can be helped to drop their burdens and "return to their natural balance," often after you've witnessed a part's origin story. That can be powerful. It can also get dramatic, with a lot of focus on memories and plenty of room for suggestion, and that can knock people off balance, especially if they tend to dissociate (DID-like fragmentation included) or to spiritually bypass.
