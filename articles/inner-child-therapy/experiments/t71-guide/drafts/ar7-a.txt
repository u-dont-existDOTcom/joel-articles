If you’re not able yet (or in the moment) to summon real love for an enemy, you can first send love to your frightened little one from your adult Nurturer. Many people also find they can borrow support from God, Jesus, angels, devas, or whatever loving spiritual presence fits their tradition. If I were to do that, I would still be framing it as “Please help this situation with love” rather than “Strike down the enemy!” But if a presence seems to enjoy your fear, it's not one of your grown-ups, borrowed or not, whatever it says it is. Same goes if it dares you to hurt or take advantage of anybody, yourself included.
