The night half of this is a lot like the [Examen](https://www.ignatianspirituality.com/ignatian-prayer/the-examen/), the evening review Jesuits have done for centuries, just aimed at hooks. It sounds like an exam, but nobody's grading you, here either. In the morning you pick one small intention, like "Today I'll notice one hook before I act on it." At bedtime you look back. Where did you catch the hook, and where did it run its routine anyway? Then what's the next small repair, or something that'd protect your little one?
