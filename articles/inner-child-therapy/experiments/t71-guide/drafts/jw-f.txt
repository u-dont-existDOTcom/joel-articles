This one's for when your little one won't talk and picturing them gets you nowhere. Grab a pen with your wrong hand. Your writing comes out all wobbly, like a first grader's, which is perfect, because that's whose hand it is now. Let it put down whatever scared or hurt stuff is really there, no making things up. Then your usual hand writes back, as the grown-up who's got their back. And if the protective part grabs the pen first? Let it go first, the way a bouncer checks you out before anyone else gets in.
