Borrowed adulthood is where that difference gets sharper. When you can't reach that calm inner source, you can borrow one adult job and take it in gradually. It might come from a relationship or an image. Or from a plan, or something you do. I have no use for another neo-Advaita, [spiritual-bypassing](http://goenka.u-dont-exist.com/) claim that a perfect Higher Self is already waiting underneath everything. Borrow Hindsight touched on this too: no perfect Higher Self is handing down answers. A grown-up your little one can trust gets built through care and boundaries, and through action and direction.
