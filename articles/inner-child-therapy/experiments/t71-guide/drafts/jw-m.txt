Lucia Capacchione, an art therapist, has been teaching this one for decades (it's in her book [Recovery of Your Inner Child](https://www.luciac.com/)). In her version, your non-dominant hand gets the scared or hurt stuff, and your usual hand writes back as the grown-up. The clumsy hand's letters already look like a first grader's, too. People who get only silence from talking to their little one or picturing them sometimes find that hand has plenty to say. And if the protective part grabs the pen first, it gets that hand until it's had its say.
