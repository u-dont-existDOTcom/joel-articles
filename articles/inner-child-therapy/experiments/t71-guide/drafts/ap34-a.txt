Borrowed adulthood makes the difference even clearer. If that calm inner source just isn't there for you, borrow one grown-up job at a time and take it in gradually. It might come from a relationship, or an image, or a plan, whatever works. I have no use for another neo-Advaita, [spiritual-bypassing](http://goenka.u-dont-exist.com/) claim that a perfect Higher Self is already waiting underneath everything. The grown-up you can trust is something you build, by caring for yourself and holding boundaries, and by actually doing things and choosing where you're headed.
