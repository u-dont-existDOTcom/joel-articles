This framework grew out of my own practice, plus the teachers and collaborators I credit below. Later revisions also compare notes with other approaches, like schema therapy, compassion-focused therapy, attachment repair, motivational interviewing, mentalizing, metacognitive therapy, behavioral activation, radically open DBT, emotion-focused therapy, spiritually integrated psychotherapy and so on. They don't all agree with each other, either. I've borrowed specific ideas and practices from them, but they're not signing off on the whole thing, and I'm not claiming their research proves it works as a package.
