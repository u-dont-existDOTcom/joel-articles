Benjamin Franklin ran a version of this. His [morning question](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/20203) was "What good shall I do this day?" and his evening one "What good have I done to-day?" He had thirteen virtues to work on, and he took them one at a time, a week each. So one hook a day is plenty, whichever one shows up. Something like "Today I'll notice one hook before I act on it."
