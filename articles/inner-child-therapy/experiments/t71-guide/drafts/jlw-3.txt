Some people hear "just like me" and brace themselves, like they're about to be told to forgive someone. This isn't a demand to forgive an abuser, though, or to imagine their childhood. It doesn't mean getting in touch with them while you're flooded, either. Only try it when you've got your bearings, enough that bringing other people into it won't pull you away from yourself. You're the first one it's for, after all.

Pick one feeling and name it plainly, like fear or loneliness, whatever it is. Everybody has it, and this is a way to stop carrying it around as proof that you're the only one who's defective. Breathe normally and say this, letting the wish include you and then anyone who comes to mind: "Other people know this feeling too. Just like me, they want relief. Just like me, they sometimes make it worse trying to escape it. May all of us find enough support to stay present and not pass the pain forward."
