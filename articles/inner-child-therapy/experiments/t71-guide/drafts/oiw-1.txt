In the morning, give yourself one modest intention: "Today I will notice one hook before I act from it." Then at night, how'd it go? Look back over the day, and leave out the cross-examining. Maybe there was one you caught right as you started tightening up. Or it's only now, thinking it over, that you realize, oh, that was a hook, and you went through the whole sequence anyway. That counts as progress too, seeing it afterward. For that one, what's the next small repair, or something the Protector could do? And what good came your way today? You might not think of any right away, and then there's a warm moment you'd forgotten about, or something you enjoyed. Perhaps you got a little braver somewhere, or felt close to somebody. Repair whatever can be repaired. Then renew the intention for tomorrow and go to sleep.
