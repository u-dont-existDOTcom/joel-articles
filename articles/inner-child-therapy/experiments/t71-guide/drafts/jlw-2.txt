"Other people know this feeling too. Just like me, they want relief. Just like me, they sometimes make it worse trying to escape it. May all of us find enough support to stay present and not pass the pain forward." That "all of us" is a big circle, and it can include people who hurt you. You don't have to forgive an abuser to say it, though, or picture their childhood. Same with getting in touch with them while you're flooded. And "this feeling" is just one, named plainly before you start, whether it's fear or loneliness.

Whichever it is, everybody has it. Saying the wish is a way to stop carrying it as proof that something's wrong with you, and only you. If letting everyone in would pull you away from yourself right now, wait until you've got your bearings. Breathe normally and let the wish include you first, and after that anyone who comes to mind. That part might feel backwards, if you're used to going last.
