Picture the love traveling from your heart down to your little one. If some comes back up, let it, and send it on down again, so it goes around like a loop. That part's a gift from them, and they don't owe it to you, so if nothing comes back, just keep sending yours. No warmth today? Then rest your hands there and notice what you find, no faking required. Maybe a wish for their good is still there, like back in A Smaller Doorway. If not, borrow one sentence from somebody safe: "I won't attack you while we sit here." Non-judgement is a place to start. It can blossom into love over time.
