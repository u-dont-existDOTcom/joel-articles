IFS calls these wounded young parts “exiles.” I find that pathologizing. I prefer to speak of a child who needs parenting and integration, rather than naming them by their wounded or excluded condition. Then there's "unburdening," often done after you've witnessed a part's origin story. It can be powerful, sure. It can also get dramatic, all memories and plenty of room for suggestion, which can knock you off balance, especially if you tend to dissociate (DID-like fragmentation included) or to spiritually bypass.
