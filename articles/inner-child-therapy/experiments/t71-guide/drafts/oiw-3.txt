When you get up, give yourself one modest intention: "Today I will notice one hook before I act from it." Then at some point in the day you might feel yourself tighten up and think, wait, is this one?

At night, look back over the day, and don't go building a case against yourself. The first thing that comes up might be a hook that got you anyway, where you went through the whole thing and only saw it afterward. Spotting it counts as progress, even if you're already flopping around in the boat by then 😂. What's one small repair you could make for that one, or something protective you could do? And there might be one you did catch in time, too. Then look for the good stuff you got today, some warmth, or something you enjoyed. Maybe you got brave about something, or there was somebody you felt close to. Once whatever can be repaired is repaired, say the intention again for tomorrow and go to sleep.
