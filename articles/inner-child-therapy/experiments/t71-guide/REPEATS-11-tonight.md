## 1. Repeats (article order)

**1. B2 and B4 [opening, before the first heading]; B297 [Somatic Heart Opening].**
"I could have kept crying forever." / "got so deep into crying that it became traumatizing" / "it can retraumatize when nobody's with you"

Its ending is a signposted callback (below), but the caution is stated again. Better place: B4, right after the song advice.

**2. B3 [opening]; B297 [Somatic Heart Opening].**
"although sadness is often the first step to open up" / "Some people find that deep grieving gives way to warmth, or to feeling connected."
Point: grief as the route to an open heart. B297 adds Rumi, the caveat that a felt experience does not prove its mechanism, and the reverse direction (being loved opens grief). Core claim told again, lightly. Better: B297, fuller.

**3. B10 [The Chicken-and-Egg Problem]; B96 and B100 [Borrow One Competency at a Time].**

**4. B10 [Chicken-and-Egg Problem]; B177 [Start With Whatever Showed Up]; B247 [Inner Guide Comes Later].**

**5. B21 [My Journey]; B246-B247 [The Inner Guide Comes Later]; B364 [You Are Worthy of Love. Always.].**

**6. B31 (last sentence) and B32 [Don't Give the Inner Adult Away]; B248-B250 [The Inner Guide Comes Later].**
"The test is whether the relationship makes me more grounded, truthful, and free." / "wean us from dependency altogether" / "watch what it's doing to your head"

**7. B97 [Borrow One Competency at a Time]; B155 [Make a Simple Vow]; B264 [A Heart-to-Child Loop]; B291 [When Love Still Feels Missing].**
"you can at least stop judging your little one" / "just promise your little one you won't be cruel to them on purpose" / "I won't attack you while we sit here" / "go smaller still, to the least cruel response a decent adult could offer your little one"

B291 adds only the scaffolding and not-a-test lines. B131 [Not Every Hero Wears A Cape], B292 [When Love Still Feels Missing] and the study chart in B29 [My Journey] say it too. Better: B291's section is the dedicated home. B155 is the first complete version.

**8. B97 [Borrow One Competency]; B153 [Make a Simple Vow]; B264 [Heart-to-Child Loop].**
