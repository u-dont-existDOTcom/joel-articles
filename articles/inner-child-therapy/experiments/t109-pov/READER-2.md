## 4
SECTION: If the Love Isn't Coming Yet
QUOTE: What people most often say helped is letting their defenses down.
LABEL: POV
MISSING: The reader is meant to take lowering defenses as the thing that helps, and "I mean by choice, after the part guarding you has been heard" depends on it, but "what people most often say" is nobody in particular, so it reads as a summary of experience with no case behind it.
POVS:
- ARTICLE-HINTED: "A lot of meditators are great at watching. I was, and baby me was still starving." suggests his own watcher stance was a guard that kept the feeling out, so "people" could be him, and lowering it is what changed.
- NEW OWNER CHOICE: Is this one particular person's account (a friend, someone you've talked with) whose guard came down, and what did that look like for them?
PICK: ARTICLE-HINTED if the meditator line is the real case, since it is already in the text and turns "people" into one person; otherwise the owner choice, because either way a named case replaces "what people most often say".
QUESTION: Whose experience is behind "what people most often say helped": yours as the watching meditator, a friend's, or people you've talked with, and what did lowering the guard look like for them?

## 8
SECTION: Your Loan is PRE-APPROVED!
QUOTE: I hope you are able to experience this when you try it out for a while, and I’d really love to know how it works for you!
LABEL: POV
MISSING: The reader is meant to try gratitude for a while and report back, but "I hope" and "I'd really love to know" are wants with no reason and no place to send the answer, so the closer could end any author's section.
POVS:
- SOURCE-SUPPORTED: "I think it’s essential if we want to live in that love instead of occasionally visiting it." is his own reason for hoping they get there.
- ARTICLE-HINTED: "That may sound like a silly, quirky waste of time to some folks." suggests the invitation is aimed at skeptics, the ones who expect it to feel silly.
- NEW OWNER CHOICE: Do readers have a place to tell you (comments, email), and has anyone told you how this worked for them, and what did they say?
PICK: SOURCE-SUPPORTED, since his "essential... instead of occasionally visiting it" is the only reason already in the text; if he has no wish to hear back, end the sentence at "belabor the point further here".
QUESTION: Do you want replies to this, and if so where should readers send them?
