# Turn 111: how two lines in Just Like Me got in

Your notes on the review sheet (2026-10-25 20:08 UTC): "cut this, idk how this got put in, but it sticks out strangely: "Some of them make it worse trying to get away from it. Just like you, sometimes. 😅"" and "also not sure why it was decided to leave out forgiving". Both lines go back to the Just Like Me practice in your published guide ([the Substack text of 2026-10-03](https://github.com/u-dont-existDOTcom/joel-articles/blob/handoff/claude-dangerous-adult-20260924-1631/articles/inner-child-therapy/source/inner-child-guide-substack-20261003.txt), its Just Like Me section), and each changed in my rewrites.

## "Some of them make it worse trying to get away from it. Just like you, sometimes. 😅"

- **The guide** had it as a line of the words you say to yourself: "Just like me, they sometimes make it worse while trying to escape it."
- **Turn 65** (2026-10-17, my draft, [sp7-p1-c.txt](https://github.com/u-dont-existDOTcom/joel-articles/blob/handoff/claude-dangerous-adult-20260924-1631/articles/inner-child-therapy/experiments/t65-guide/sp7-p1-c.txt)) turned the said-aloud wish into narration, and I made that line two pieces, "Some of them make it worse trying to get away from it. Just like you, sometimes. 😅", with the fragment and the emoji added to sound casual. It read Human in Pangram alone.
- **Turn 93's tell round** made the paragraph's steps one sentence and didn't touch this line. The turn-109 point-of-view read, GPT's review and the turn-110 sheet didn't flag it either.

It stuck out because once the wish was narration, the line had lost the script it was part of: a reader gets a passing observation about other people's coping, then a wink.

## "I'd leave out forgiving them and picturing their childhood."

- **The guide**: "This is not a demand to forgive an abuser, imagine their childhood, or open contact while you are flooded." So the practice didn't require any of that. It never said to leave forgiveness out.
- **Turn 71** (2026-10-18, my draft, [jl2-l.txt](https://github.com/u-dont-existDOTcom/joel-articles/blob/handoff/claude-dangerous-adult-20260924-1631/articles/inner-child-therapy/experiments/t71-guide/drafts/jl2-l.txt)): "Forgiving them isn't part of it, and neither is picturing their childhood." That's still "not required", if barely.
- **Turn 93's tell round** (item a11 of [TELLS-ROUND2.md](https://github.com/u-dont-existDOTcom/joel-articles/blob/handoff/claude-dangerous-adult-20260924-1631/articles/inner-child-therapy/experiments/t93-tells/TELLS-ROUND2.md)) made the parallel negation "one plain sentence": "I'd leave out forgiving them and picturing their childhood." That turned "isn't required" into "leave it out", which reads as advice against forgiving.

So nobody decided to leave forgiveness out. A tell fix changed what the sentence claimed, and no read after it caught the shift: the turn-109 point-of-view read and GPT's review both took "leave out" at face value, and my P6 card offered to add "here".

Your version of the paragraph doesn't have the line at all, and I'd keep it that way: How to Forgive Without Forgetting covers forgiveness, including that it can't be put on a schedule.
