Once you're in that happy place, look at the other person too. The psychologist Lindsay Gibson writes about people she calls emotionally immature, and one of her books is Disentangling from Emotionally Immature People; one fight, or one “no” or one boundary of theirs that made you mad, doesn't make someone that. Can they hear you disagree, look at their own part, and make room for how it was for you, or does it keep ending the same way, where you bring up something that hurt and end up looking after their feelings, reassuring them they're not a bad person?
