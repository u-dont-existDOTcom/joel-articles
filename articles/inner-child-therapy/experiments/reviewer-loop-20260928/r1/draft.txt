Once you're in that happy place, look at the other person too. Can they hear you disagree, look at their own part, and make room for how it was for you, or does it keep ending the same way, where you bring up something that hurt and end up looking after their feelings, reassuring them they're not a bad person, while yours drop out of the conversation? The psychologist Lindsay Gibson calls people like that emotionally immature, and one of her books is Disentangling from Emotionally Immature People. But if all you have is one fight, or one “no” or one boundary of theirs that made you mad, that doesn't settle whether this person is like that.
