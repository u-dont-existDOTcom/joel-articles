Once you're in that happy place, look at the other person too, and look at more than the one fight where they crossed your boundary, and, the other way around, more than the one “no” or boundary of theirs that made you mad — granted, they're allowed to say no to you. Does it keep ending the same way, with you promising to be more careful how you bring things up? (The psychologist Lindsay Gibson calls people like that emotionally immature, and one of her books is Disentangling from Emotionally Immature People.) Or can they hear you disagree and look at their own part?
