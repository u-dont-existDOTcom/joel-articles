If you are having trouble thinking of something, it is OK for the Protector to go first. If you are not experiencing lots of warm feelings for your little one, start by doing something that shows you care. For example: lock the door when you go to sleep each night; commit to eating a real meal every day; say no to one thing you are expected to do; put your phone down at midnight. You can work on the warm feelings later!

Choose one thing from this list to work on. When are you going to do it? How will you start? What help do you need to do it? Rehearse what you are going to say if needed. Afterwards, review how it went. If you did not do it, work out why not. Were you scared? Did you not know what you wanted? Did you not know how to get it? Were you too tired? Was something getting in the way? If you did do it, but did not feel better, it may be that what you did was not helpful. However, just because you did it, does not mean it was helpful! Only keep doing things that result in your little one being safer, closer to you, more able, happier.
