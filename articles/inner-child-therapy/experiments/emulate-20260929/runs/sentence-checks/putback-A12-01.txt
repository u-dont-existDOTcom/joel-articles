You might notice the hook before you have any clue what part of you is doing the hooking. Sometimes you won't notice it until you've already tensed up your jaw, or whatever, and until you've already done some part of reacting—until you've already written half the text, for example. It's okay to forget, for the time being, to try to figure out which part it is. Just be aware, as Pema Chödron has explained , that there's a kind of tightening, a kind of "I need to do something" sensation that comes before you finish making up the story. She calls this shenpa .
