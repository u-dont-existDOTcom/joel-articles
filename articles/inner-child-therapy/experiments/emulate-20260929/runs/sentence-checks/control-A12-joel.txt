You might notice the hook before you have any clue what part of you is doing the hooking. Or you might notice it embarrassingly late. The jaw and stomach are already tight, half the text is written, and then you remember you're supposed to be observing yourself. Maybe forget the parts detective work for a minute. You can figure that out later.  Pema Chödrön calls this tightening and urge before the story has finished forming, "shenpa."
