Even if you do n't have any words coming to mind to express what you feel like saying to him, you can still try to think of a sentence to say to him. If you do, use those words to say or write something to him, instead of just talking to him in your imagination. But if this feels like homework, skip it-he may be responding in other ways that you can identify (butterflies, urge to run, recurring images, song lyrics, etc.) Sometimes nothing “answers” and you just know what would be kind.

It may or may not be possible to tell which came first, the feeling or the words you use to understand the feeling. But that does n't matter-you can still use words to help you understand your feelings.
