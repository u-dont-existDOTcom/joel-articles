Do whatever it is that needs to be done (usually this will be safety related). At the start of the next round of processing, ask yourself why you are here again. Why has something changed since you were last here? Did you finally feel something you had only been thinking about? Did you notice something new enough to change a decision or how you respond? Or are you asking the same question again because the answer didn't feel certain enough? Grief does often repeat itself, and that doesn't mean that the child is asking for something new.
