You might barely have spontaneous words in your mind and still be perfectly capable of thinking a sentence on purpose. Try it and see. If you do, use those words to say or write something to him, instead of just talking to him in your imagination. But if this feels like homework, skip it-he may be responding in other ways that you can identify (butterflies, urge to run, recurring images, song lyrics, etc.) Or you may not get any response at all, and still have a good idea of what would be a loving thing to say to him.

It may or may not be possible to tell which came first, the feeling or the words you use to understand the feeling. But that does n't matter-you can still use words to help you understand your feelings.
