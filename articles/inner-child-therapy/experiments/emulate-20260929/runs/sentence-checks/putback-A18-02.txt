Even if you do n't have any words coming to mind to express what you feel like saying to him, you can still try to think of a sentence to say to him. If words are there, use them; say the thing aloud, write it, whatever gets it out of the strange imaginary-conversation zone. But if this feels like homework, skip it-he may be responding in other ways that you can identify (butterflies, urge to run, recurring images, song lyrics, etc.) Or you may not get any response at all, and still have a good idea of what would be a loving thing to say to him.

It may or may not be possible to tell which came first, the feeling or the words you use to understand the feeling. But that does n't matter-you can still use words to help you understand your feelings.
