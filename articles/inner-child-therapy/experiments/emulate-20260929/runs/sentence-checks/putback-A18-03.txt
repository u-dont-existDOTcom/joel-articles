Even if you do n't have any words coming to mind to express what you feel like saying to him, you can still try to think of a sentence to say to him. If you do, use those words to say or write something to him, instead of just talking to him in your imagination. And if that starts feeling like homework you're doing for a child who isn't answering, drop it. Maybe what's actually happening is a knot in your stomach, an urge to leave, some image that keeps coming up, a song that suddenly feels relevant. Or you may not get any response at all, and still have a good idea of what would be a loving thing to say to him.

It may or may not be possible to tell which came first, the feeling or the words you use to understand the feeling. But that does n't matter-you can still use words to help you understand your feelings.
