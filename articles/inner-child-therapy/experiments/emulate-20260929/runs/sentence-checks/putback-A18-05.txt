Even if you do n't have any words coming to mind to express what you feel like saying to him, you can still try to think of a sentence to say to him. If you do, use those words to say or write something to him, instead of just talking to him in your imagination. But if this feels like homework, skip it-he may be responding in other ways that you can identify (butterflies, urge to run, recurring images, song lyrics, etc.) Or you may not get any response at all, and still have a good idea of what would be a loving thing to say to him.

The order gets murky too. Feeling can precede language, language can expose a feeling, and afterward you may not know which happened first. But that does n't matter-you can still use words to help you understand your feelings.
