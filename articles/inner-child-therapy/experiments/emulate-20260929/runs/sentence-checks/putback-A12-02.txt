Sometimes you'll notice you're hooked before you know which part of you is doing the hooking. Or you might notice it embarrassingly late. The jaw is already tight, the stomach is already involved, half the text is written, and then you remember you're supposed to be observing yourself. It's okay to forget, for the time being, to try to figure out which part it is. Just be aware, as Pema Chödron has explained , that there's a kind of tightening, a kind of "I need to do something" sensation that comes before you finish making up the story. She calls this shenpa .
