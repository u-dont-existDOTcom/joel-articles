Monogamy, particularly the sexual aspect of it, has become much more important since agriculture, and the advent of property and inheritance. By the Industrial Revolution it was being universally enforced in some strict form, often through both law and social pressure. Prior to that, there were many tribal cultures that were far more relaxed about allowing sexual and even emotional contact with others outside of the primary partnership. I believe the term "social monogamy" has been coined to describe this.
