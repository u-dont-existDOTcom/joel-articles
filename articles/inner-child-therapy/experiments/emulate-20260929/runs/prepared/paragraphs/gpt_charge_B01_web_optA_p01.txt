If you are having trouble thinking of something, it is OK for the Protector to go first. If you are not experiencing lots of warm feelings for your little one, start by doing something that shows you care. For example: lock the door when you go to sleep each night; commit to eating a real meal every day; say no to one thing you are expected to do; put your phone down at 12 pm each night. You can work on the warm feelings later!
