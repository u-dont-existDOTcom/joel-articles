Afterward, look at what actually happened, because feeling no relief doesn't mean it failed. Remember that if you didn't feel better after doing something it doesn't mean that doing it was a waste of time, and likewise if you did feel better it doesn't mean that you should always do that thing. Some things are useful to do to keep you safe, or to be close to someone, or to help you do things you enjoy, or to get better at things, but most of the time you can just tick them off. If you didn't do it, don't decide why before you've looked, since it could be fear, second thoughts about the goal, not knowing how, being worn out, or the friend you'd lined up not answering.
