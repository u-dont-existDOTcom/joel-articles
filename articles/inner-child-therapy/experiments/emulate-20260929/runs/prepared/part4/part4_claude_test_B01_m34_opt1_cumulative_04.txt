Don’t worry if you can’t find anything that makes you feel warm and fuzzy for your little one. In that case, do this exercise for your Protector. They probably can think of a few things they do that demonstrate their care, like locking the door at night or making sure they eat regularly or saying no to social plans in order to get rest or putting their phone down at midnight. The warm fuzzy feelings can come later.

Pick one thing from this list. Figure out specifically what you will do, when you will do it, what the first step is and who can help you. If there's a hard moment you can see coming, it can help to practice what you'll say ahead of time. Afterward, look at what really happened. If you didn't do it, don't assume you know why. It could have been fear, not knowing what you were aiming for, not knowing how, being exhausted, or something practical getting in the way. And if you did it and didn't feel better right away, that doesn't mean it failed, but doing it doesn't prove it helped either. Keep what actually makes your little one safer, closer to you, more capable, or happier, not just what gets a check mark.
