Once you've done it (or not!) you can then see what happened, and how it made you feel. Doing it doesn't prove it helped either; the acts worth keeping leave things safer, closer, more capable or more enjoyable, and others only earn a tick. If you didn't do it, don't decide why before you've looked, since it could be fear, second thoughts about the goal, not knowing how, being worn out, or the friend you'd lined up not answering.
