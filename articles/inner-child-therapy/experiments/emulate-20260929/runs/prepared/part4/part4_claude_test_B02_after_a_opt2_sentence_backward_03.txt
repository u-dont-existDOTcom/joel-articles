Then reflect on whether you did it or not. If you did it but didn’t feel any better, that’s ok, the action might not have been beneficial to you, but if it was something important that made you safer, closer to someone or something, gave you more ability or made your life more enjoyable, then tick it off anyway. Afterward, look at what actually happened, because feeling no relief doesn't mean it failed. Had you had second thoughts about whether you actually wanted to do it? Were you unclear how to do it? Were you too tired? Had your friend not got back to you?
