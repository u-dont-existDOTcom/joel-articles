It’s okay for the Protector to go first. It’s okay for you to start by doing something that you know will care for your little one even if you don’t yet have warm feelings toward them. Locking the door, eating a meal, cancelling something on your calendar, putting your phone down when you wake up in the middle of the night — the warm feelings can come later.

Pick one thing from the above that you can do. When will you do it? What’s the first step you can take? What do you need to help you do it? If there’s a hard part, say it out loud now to practice. Afterward, look at what really happened. If you didn't do it, don't assume you know why. It could have been fear, not knowing what you were aiming for, not knowing how, being exhausted, or something practical getting in the way. And if you did it and didn't feel better right away, that doesn't mean it failed, but doing it doesn't prove it helped either. Keep what actually makes your little one safer, closer to you, more capable, or happier, not just what gets a check mark.
