Afterwards, debrief. If you weren’t able to do it, what got in the way? Were you scared? Did you not know what you were going for? Did you not know how to do it? Were you too tired? Was something else in the way? If you did it, did it help? Just because you did it doesn’t mean it will help. Keep only those things that help your little one to feel safe, to feel close to you, to be more functional, or to be happy.
