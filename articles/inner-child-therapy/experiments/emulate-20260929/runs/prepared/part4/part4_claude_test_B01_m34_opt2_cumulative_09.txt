It’s okay for the Protector to go first. It’s okay for you to start by doing something that you know will care for your little one even if you don’t yet have warm feelings toward them. Locking the door, eating a meal, cancelling something on your calendar, putting your phone down when you wake up in the middle of the night — the warm feelings can come later.

Pick one thing from the above that you can do. When will you do it? What’s the first step you can take? What do you need to help you do it? If there’s a hard part, say it out loud now to practice. Afterwards, debrief. If you weren’t able to do it, what got in the way? Were you scared? Did you not know what you were going for? Did you not know how to do it? Were you too tired? Was something else in the way? If you did it, did it help? Keep what actually makes your little one safer, closer to you, more capable, or happier, not just what gets a check mark.
