Once you've done it (or not!) you can then see what happened, and how it made you feel. Doing it doesn't prove it helped either; the acts worth keeping leave things safer, closer, more capable or more enjoyable, and others only earn a tick. On the other hand, if you didn't do the thing you had planned to, don't jump to conclusions. Maybe you didn't feel able to do it, or maybe you didn't want to do it in the end, or maybe you didn't know how, or maybe you were too tired, or maybe the friend you were going to ask to help you didn't reply.
