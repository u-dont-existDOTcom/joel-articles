Then reflect on whether you did it or not. Afterward, look at what actually happened, because feeling no relief doesn't mean it failed. Doing it doesn't prove it helped either; the acts worth keeping leave things safer, closer, more capable or more enjoyable, and others only earn a tick. If you didn't do it, don't decide why before you've looked, since it could be fear, second thoughts about the goal, not knowing how, being worn out, or the friend you'd lined up not answering.
