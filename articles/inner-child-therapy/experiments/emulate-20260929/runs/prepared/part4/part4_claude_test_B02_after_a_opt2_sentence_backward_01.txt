If you did it but didn’t feel any better, that’s ok, the action might not have been beneficial to you, but if it was something important that made you safer, closer to someone or something, gave you more ability or made your life more enjoyable, then tick it off anyway. If you didn’t do what you said you would, don’t judge yourself, just consider why not? Were you afraid? Had you had second thoughts about whether you actually wanted to do it? Were you unclear how to do it? Were you too tired? Had your friend not got back to you?
