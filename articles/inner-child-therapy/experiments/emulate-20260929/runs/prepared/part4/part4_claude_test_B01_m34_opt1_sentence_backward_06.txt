Don’t worry if you can’t find anything that makes you feel warm and fuzzy for your little one. In that case, do this exercise for your Protector. They probably can think of a few things they do that demonstrate their care, like locking the door at night or making sure they eat regularly or saying no to social plans in order to get rest or putting their phone down at midnight. The warm fuzzy feelings can come later.

Pick one thing from this list. Figure out specifically what you will do, when you will do it, what the first step is and who can help you. Consider rehearsing what you will say in difficult moments. Later, debrief. Afterward, look at what really happened. Did you not know what you wanted? Did you not know how to do what you wanted? Were you too tired? Was something getting in the way? If you did do what you planned, notice if you feel better. Keep in mind that doing something that should make your little one safe or close or competent or happy doesn’t necessarily mean it did. Only do things that actually help.
