Once you've done it (or not!) you can then see what happened, and how it made you feel. Remember that if you didn't feel better after doing something it doesn't mean that doing it was a waste of time, and likewise if you did feel better it doesn't mean that you should always do that thing. Some things are useful to do to keep you safe, or to be close to someone, or to help you do things you enjoy, or to get better at things, but most of the time you can just tick them off. If you didn't do what you planned, wait before deciding why. Maybe you didn't feel able to do it, or maybe you didn't want to do it in the end, or maybe you didn't know how, or maybe you were too tired, or maybe the friend you were going to ask to help you didn't reply.
