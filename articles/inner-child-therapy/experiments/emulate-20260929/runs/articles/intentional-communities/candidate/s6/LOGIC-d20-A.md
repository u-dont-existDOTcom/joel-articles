## Findings: CHANGED and AMBIGUOUS

1. P3 CHANGED. "gets through her commune applications" → "the applications to her commune": Seba now runs a commune and screens applicants; the published phrase most naturally makes her the applicant.
2. P4 AMBIGUOUS. "I’d rather build…" → "on a personal level, it makes more sense to me…": can narrow a general project to his private life.
3. P4 AMBIGUOUS. "I’d rather have community…" → "It's better to have community…, right?": can turn his preference into a general claim the reader is asked to accept.
4. P5 CHANGED. Bare keep/limit question → "Let the community decide if I can use my social media, or ChatGPT? … these technologies don't just affect you…": the author now defends community say over members' personal app use against adult autonomy, and predicts many will balk.
5. P8 CHANGED. "should decide in advance" → "should think through… consider in advance": thinking the questions through now seems enough; the published text requires decisions.
6. P8 AMBIGUOUS. "may prefer natural care and still need…" → "but what happens when someone in the community needs…?": can read as one member's need against the community's preference.
7. P8 AMBIGUOUS. "a different level of medical involvement" → "accept (or not accept) different levels": the community's level can drop out as the reference point.
8. P10 AMBIGUOUS. "links isolation with higher risks" → "notes an increased risk… among people who are socially isolated": can be read as isolation causing the risk.
