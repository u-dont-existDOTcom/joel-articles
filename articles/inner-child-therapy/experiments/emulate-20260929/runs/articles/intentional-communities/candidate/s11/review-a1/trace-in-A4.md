## P15 → P15g

CONTEXT BEFORE: “Nothing suppressed, everything processed” can be taken to mean that someone in the group must tell all, even if they don’t want to. I have seen that risk in groups that use the ZEGG Forum practice. A group can start out inviting honesty and end up treating privacy as resistance.

PUBLISHED P15:
Peer counseling can become surveillance delivered in a warm tone. Medicine prerequisites can create gatekeepers with power over people at their most open. “Willing to be seen” can become a demand that everybody reveal whatever the group wants to know.

REWRITE P15g:
Peer counseling can become peer surveillance, even if the tone is warm. And when things like that are required as pre-reqs for people to get medicine, it puts a lot of power in the hands of the gatekeepers, right when people are at their most open. Even something couched in words like “willing to be seen” could become a demand that people disclose whatever the group wants them to.

CONTEXT AFTER: The boundary is simple. These aren't things that give the community power over me, they are simply a set of principles I am attempting to live up to.
