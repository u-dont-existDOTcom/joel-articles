## Contradictions in the published text

**1. §11 calls the practices "never powers the community gains", but the essay gates on them elsewhere**

- §11: "the principles are commitments I choose for my own practice, never powers the community gains over me"; "Members can decline to share without producing a therapeutic explanation for the refusal."
- Elsewhere the community does gate on the practices:
  - "The medicine stays behind the other pl/ork. Reparenting, somatic capacity, and peer counseling come first" (My Ceremony Principles).
  - "Have they begun the relevant practices?" (From Visitor to Member—Slowly).
  - "You are unwilling to examine your behavior when jealousy or conflict appears" is one reason the community may not be right for you right now (The Math of Absorption).
  - §11 itself warns that "Medicine prerequisites can create gatekeepers", and those prerequisites are a power the community holds.
- Your 2026-10-11 answers also conflict with it:
  - People who don't follow the community's processes won't fit in, and if living with them becomes impossible, "they will be kicked out".
  - On privacy: "it depends on the situation".
  - Satan-worshippers "get booted."
- Rewrite: the passage is gone ("Well, it is gatekeeping"; "privacy also can be resistance").

**2. The §11 floor conflicts with your 2026-10-11 answers**

- §11: "a floor the majority cannot vote away: … private thought, contact with people outside the community…"
- Your answer on outside relationships: "the commune needs to intervene and help people decide on how to communicate with outsiders" in tricky cases. Your rights paragraph adds a condition: "as long as those relationships don't interfere." Booting someone for worshipping Satan, even privately, also cuts against "private thought" as a right no vote can touch.
- Rewrite: the passage is gone. Your rights paragraph replaces the list.

**3. §11 says to take disagreement at face value, but the essay's diagnosis says disputes carry hurt**

- §11: "Disagreement has to be answered as disagreement"; "When a community begins diagnosing dissent instead of responding to it, leave…"
- Elsewhere:
  - Human pain "simply got translated into politics" (The New Age May Dawn Suddenly).
  - "Eventually politics begins carrying emotional material that nobody knows how to name" (The secular failure).
  - A forming group should "Find the jealousy, humiliation, fear, or old material involved" (Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community).
- The essay argues that disputes often carry old hurt, which has to be processed. §11 rules out reading dissent that way.
- Rewrite: fixed ("Sometimes it really is their wounded ego projection… But again, balance").

## Questions on the author's own paragraphs

**1.** "But if we are all starting together from scratch, then we would all be at an equal probationary level with each other."
Resources, Land, and Exit Terms says: "If the founder's name is on the deed, you have a landlord with a philosophy." In §11's override test, owning the land is one of the things that stop a community from overriding its founder. Does "starting from scratch" include people gathering on land you've bought? If so, is the probation really equal while the deed is in your name?

**2.** "But privacy also can be resistance to the communal spirit, so there has to be a dynamic, living, always re-assessed balance…"
The Four Parts That Have to Pl/ork Together says: "authority stays with the practitioner; the community provides the container." Who sets that balance between privacy and what a member owes the community: the member, or the group?

**3.** "…maintain relationships with others outside the community, as long as those relationships don't interfere with the community as a whole"
Children Need Freedom Inside Real Protection says "Grandparents, cousins, local friends, teachers, and other trusted adults give children perspective and somewhere to speak." It also says "At least one trusted adult, advocate, or reporting route should be reachable without going through community leadership." Does the "don't interfere" limit, or the commune stepping in on contact with outsiders, also cover a child's outside adult? Take a grandmother who is demonizing the commune.
