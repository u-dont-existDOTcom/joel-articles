Make sure kids have relationships and a reporting route outside the community. Grandparents, cousins, friends from down the street, teachers and other trusted adults give them perspective on their life, and someone to talk to. Make sure they have at least one trusted adult, advocate or reporting route that doesn't go through the leadership of the community. In 1984, Vermont authorities [raided Island Pond and seized 112 children](https://vtdigger.org/2024/06/21/40-years-later-island-pond-has-little-interest-in-revisiting-its-historic-raid/), and a court returned them after rejecting the state's blanket request. State intervention can fail spectacularly. Trying to enclose children completely within a community can also fail. Children need more than one world.
