# Stance check: section 6 rewrite, "Which Connections to the Technosphere Do You Keep?" (review d2)

I checked every sentence in the rewrite that states a position, rule, assessment or claim about how things work against the whole published essay. Sentences not listed below agree with the essay.

**1. Full dependence becomes any participation**
- Rewrite: "If you opt into all of them, the outside system can reach directly into every essential function."
- Essay: "Full dependence means the outside system can reach directly into every essential function." (Which Connections to the Technosphere Do You Keep?); "The practical endpoint may therefore be a nearly moneyless community with one small collective cash boundary." (The Four Parts That Have to Pl/ork Together, 4. The container)
- What a reader would wrongly believe: using all of these systems at all, even in a limited way, exposes every essential function, so each connection is either kept or dropped. The essay says full dependence is the danger and treats limiting a connection as an option.

**2. Scare quotes cast doubt on natural medicine**
- Rewrite: “A community may be interested in having as "natural" medicine as possible, but what happens when someone in the community needs trauma surgery”
- Essay: "A community may prefer natural care and still need trauma surgery" (Which Connections to the Technosphere Do You Keep?); "grateful my parents didn't vaccinate me" (The Grown Children Get the Final Review); "Careful, peer-guided use of serious plant medicines" (The Four Parts That Have to Pl/ork Together, 3. Medicine pl/ork)
- What a reader would wrongly believe: Joel treats "natural" medicine as a doubtful or naive label. The essay states the preference neutrally and is openly sympathetic to it.

**3. "Decide in advance" softened to "think through"**
- Rewrite: "It is really important to think through in advance how people in the community are going to have access to these services"
- Essay: "It should decide in advance how members access care" (Which Connections to the Technosphere Do You Keep?); "Decide what requires professional or emergency care before anybody takes anything." (The Medicine Part, Without Pretending It Isn't There, My Ceremony Principles)
- What a reader would wrongly believe: the essay asks only for thinking ahead about medical access and guarantees. It actually asks for agreements settled before anyone is sick.

**4. Common resources narrowed to common money**
- Rewrite: "what will be guaranteed with common money"; the same narrowing comes back in "making common and/or outside money available when it's needed"
- Essay: "what the community guarantees from common resources" (Which Connections to the Technosphere Do You Keep?); "Define exit rights in whatever unit makes sense—cash if the community uses it" and "so exit is not a paper right that works only when the community has spare money" (Resources, Land, and Exit Terms)
- What a reader would wrongly believe: the community's medical guarantee is counted in cash. The essay frames it in common resources such as care, transport and housing, and moves the community toward using less and less money.

**5. Protecting choice becomes backing any choice**
- Rewrite: "all community members should already have agreed to support that person in whatever they decide to do"
- Essay: "A catastrophic-illness agreement should protect choice" and "what may require outside money or insurance" (Which Connections to the Technosphere Do You Keep?); "A group can absorb only as much need as its members have free resource to meet." (The Math of Absorption, and Who This Isn't For)
- What a reader would wrongly believe: every member has agreed in advance to back, pay for (with outside money too) and provide transport for any treatment a gravely ill member chooses, with no limit. The essay describes an agreement that protects that member's right to choose, within what the community actually guarantees.

**6. A link becomes documented impact**
- Rewrite: "the Surgeon General's advisory documents the impact social connection has on several health outcomes."
- Essay: "The Surgeon General's advisory links isolation with higher risks across several outcomes." (Which Connections to the Technosphere Do You Keep?)
- What a reader would wrongly believe: the advisory showed that social connection causes changes in health. The essay cites it only for linking isolation with higher risk.

Conflicts found: 6 (conflict 4 appears in two sentences).
