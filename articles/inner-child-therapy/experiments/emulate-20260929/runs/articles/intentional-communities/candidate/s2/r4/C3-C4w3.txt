If you look at Google Trends for “ecovillage” and “intentional community”, you can see that interest drifted down for years, then bent upward sharply in the mid-2020s.

That's around when AI stopped being something you read about in the tech news and started changing people's jobs and relationships and how they see the future. I can't prove that's why the searches went up. The timing is hard to ignore, though. Forming a village started to look like a backup plan. It used to sound like something from the seventies.
