# Section 12 pairs (T2): published paragraph and rewrite

Section 12 is "The Math of Absorption, and Who This Isn't For". The rewrite keeps the published order. In the published section the list after "This community may not be right for you right now if:" is four separate items; the rewrite folds the lead-in and the first item into one paragraph and starts the others with "Or if", "The same goes if" and "And if". The published P8 ("Everyone carries both, and the ratio changes.") and P9 (the author's own story) are unchanged and not traced here.

## P5pub+P6pub → P5a

CONTEXT BEFORE: Some people have years of contemplative practice or therapy behind them. Some got really lucky in childhood. Some are in the middle of an emergency, just surviving, and need care more than they need responsibility. Most of us move around between those over a lifetime.

PUBLISHED:

In this model, the community shares some functions that modern society assigns to therapists. Peer counseling distributes the capacity to listen, accompany, and help people process ordinary emotional material.

That does not make the community infinitely absorbent.

REWRITE:

In the model I’m imagining, some of the jobs modern society hands to therapists get shared out across the community. Peer counseling spreads the capacity to listen, to be with someone through something, and to help people work through ordinary emotional stuff. That doesn’t mean the community can absorb everything, though.

CONTEXT AFTER: The phrase free resource comes from Re-evaluation Counseling. It means how much attention, steadiness, skill and practical capacity you’ve got left once you’ve dealt with enough of your own immediate stuff. Need is whatever needs support right now.
