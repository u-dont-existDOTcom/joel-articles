Most of the writing about communities hasn’t caught up on psychedelics, which have gone from taboo toward regulated use really fast. Licensed psilocybin service centers started opening in Oregon in 2023. Colorado created a regulated system for natural medicines and began licensing in 2025, and New Mexico enacted a Medical Psilocybin Act also in 2025. In Australia, since 2023, authorized psychiatrists have had limited access to psilocybin and MDMA for a specific list of conditions.
