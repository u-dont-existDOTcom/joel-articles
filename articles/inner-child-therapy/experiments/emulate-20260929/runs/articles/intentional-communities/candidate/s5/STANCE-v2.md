# Stance check: section 5 rewrite ("The Medicine Part, Without Pretending It Isn't There")

I checked every sentence in the rewrite that states Joel's position, a wish, a rule, an assessment of a community or person, or a claim about how things work. I compared each one with the whole published essay, not only with the published version of section 5.

## 1. Friends become judges of which insights are real

- **Rewrite** (subsection "Community Is What Makes Psychedelics Pl/ork Best!"): "friends who know you and your history well enough to see which parts of what you're bringing back are insight"
- **Essay, section 5** (same subsection, published wording): "Friends who know your history may notice which parts resemble a new understanding and which parts resemble your mother."
- **Essay, section 4** ("One principle runs through all of them", also the roof of the four-parts diagram): "authority stays with the practitioner"
- **Type:** strengthening. The "may" is dropped, and "resemble a new understanding" becomes "are insight". A hedged guess about resemblance turns into a verdict about which visions are real.
- **What a reader would wrongly believe:** that Joel thinks friends who know you can reliably sort your genuine psychedelic insights from nonsense, giving peers authority over your experience that the essay leaves with you.
