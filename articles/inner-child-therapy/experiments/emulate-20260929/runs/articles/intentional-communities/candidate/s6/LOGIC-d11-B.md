## P3
| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Degree of doubt | "I don't know exactly how much of the future fear is accurate" | "Do I think the fears about the future are legitimate? I don't know. Maybe." | CHANGED. "Exactly how much of" grants that some fear is accurate and doubts only the amount; a yes/no answered "Maybe" leaves open whether any of it holds. |
| Predicate | "accurate" | "legitimate" | AMBIGUOUS. (a) well-founded, likely to come true (= accurate); (b) reasonable to feel, a verdict on the fearful, not the forecast. |
| Alternatives | "…, or do all three" | "Maybe… Maybe… Maybe all of these things will happen" | SAME. Separate "maybe"s stay alternatives; "all" is still one option. |
| Reach of the third outcome | "the administrative layer between us and nearly everything" | "a layer of bureaucracy between humanity and whatever it is we're trying to do" | CHANGED (mild). The one intermediary becomes one layer among others; hedged "nearly everything" becomes unhedged; a neutral role becomes an obstacle. |
| Who applies to whom | "gets through her commune applications" | "finishes going through all the applications to her commune" | CHANGED within this section. The rewrite makes her a commune's gatekeeper; the original also lets her be the applicant (as in "her college applications"). SAME only if earlier text says she runs a commune. |

## P4
| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| First comparison | "I'd rather build … than wait" | "On a personal level, it makes more sense to me to focus on cultivating … than to wait" | SAME. Same ranking, still personal. |
| Second comparison | "I'd rather have community and discover … than need an exit …" | "It's better to have community and find out … than to need an exit …, right?" | AMBIGUOUS. (a) rhetorical tag: a general claim offered as obvious, firmer and wider than a personal preference; (b) a real request for the reader's confirmation, weaker than the original. |

## P5
| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scope of "useful" | "The useful design question is: which … do you keep" | "What connections … would be useful to limit or keep" | CHANGED. The original asserts keep-versus-limit is the right question to ask; the rewrite asks which limits or keeps would pay off, with no claim about the framing. |
| and → or | "which … do you keep, and which do you limit?" | "limit or keep" | AMBIGUOUS. (a) sort every connection into keep or limit (= original); (b) list only connections worth acting on, the rest unsorted. |
| Modality, condition | "do you keep" | "would be useful … if you were to design a community" | CHANGED (mild). Reader takes it as a hypothetical exercise for community designers, not a choice they face. |

## P6
| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rejection, dependence | "Full rejection can create…"; "Full dependence means…" | "If you opt out of them completely, it can cause…"; "If you depend on them completely…" | SAME. "Can" kept; "means" → "if" keeps the direction; "completely" carries the same all/each ambiguity as "full". |

## P7
| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| and → but | "accepted at work and restricted at home" | "use it at work but restrict it at home" | SAME (both at once). |
| What varies; may → fact | "A tool may be accepted…, shared…, or delayed…" | "Some use it… Some share it… Others hold off…" | CHANGED. The original lists treatments any tool may get, varying tool by tool even inside one community; the rewrite sorts communities into three camps toward one technology, as reported fact. |
| Negation scope | "shared rather than individually owned" | "share it instead of everyone owning one" | AMBIGUOUS (minor). (a) held in common, not one each (= original); (b) a few own one and lend it, which "rather than individually owned" excludes. |
| rather than → and; is → shouldn't | "a communal decision rather than an automatic software update" | "a decision for the group to make, and that it shouldn't get downloaded onto your device while you sleep" | CHANGED. The original's one understanding uses the update as the author's comparison; the rewrite attributes to the Amish a second, normative belief, literally about downloads onto a person's device. |

## P8
| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concession | "may prefer natural care and still need …, or a specialist" | "may prefer natural care, but what happens if someone … needs …, or any number of specialist services?" | SAME. Possibility, concession, "or" kept. |
| Modality | "It should decide in advance" | "The community really needs to hash out in advance" | CHANGED (mild). Advice becomes emphatic necessity. |
| Scope of "in advance" | all four items in one "decide in advance" sentence | only access under "hash out in advance"; three free-standing questions | AMBIGUOUS. (a) the agenda to settle beforehand (= original); (b) open questions with no advance requirement. |
| Sets | "how members access care"; "what the community guarantees" | "access to these services"; "What kind of care is going to be guaranteed" | CHANGED (mild). Access narrows from care in general to the listed outside services; guarantees narrow from "what" to kinds of care. |
| Which vs whether; may → will | "what may require outside money or insurance" | "Will there be some things that will require outside dollars…?" | AMBIGUOUS. (a) rhetorical: some things certainly will (stronger than "may"); (b) literal: whether any will (weaker than deciding which). |
| Who acts on whom | "how to treat somebody who chooses a different level of medical involvement" | "what if someone wants more (or less) medical care than what the community wants to provide?" | CHANGED. A member's choice becomes a want set against the community's supply, "less" demoted to a parenthesis. Reader takes it as a question of how much care the community provides, not of how it deals with someone who chose differently. |

## P9
| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prohibition; or | "No member should have to prove ideological loyalty while frightened or sick"; "common resources or outside funds when needed" | "No member should have to jump through ideological hoops when they're scared or sick"; "common resources or external funds if needed" | SAME. |
| Who arranges | "A catastrophic-illness agreement should protect…" | "Members need to have pre-existing agreements in place…" | AMBIGUOUS. (a) members must be covered (= original); (b) each member must arrange their own, moving the duty from the community (P8 "It should decide") to individuals. |
| Modality | "should protect" | "need to have … so that … will be protected" | CHANGED (mild). Reader takes agreements as required; the original recommends what one covers. |
| Possibility → condition | "the possibility that the community's preferred approach won't be enough" | "they can get access to what they need if the community's preferred methods are not sufficient" | AMBIGUOUS. (a) a fallback for when the approach fails (= original); (b) access only once the methods are found insufficient, a gate the original avoids by protecting the possibility itself. |

## P10
| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| What has measured value | "social connection itself has measurable health value" | "being part of a community, having good social connections, is measurably good for a person's health" | CHANGED. Reader takes the measured benefit to belong to community membership (equated with good connections) and to each person; the original measures social connection and says only that community "can change" daily conditions. |
| Association vs cause | "links isolation with higher risks across several outcomes" | "notes an increased risk … if one is socially isolated" | AMBIGUOUS. (a) isolated people show higher risk (association, = original); (b) isolation raises a person's risk (cause). |
| Negation; modality | "won't cure every illness"; "It can change the daily conditions" | "not a panacea"; "but it can make a difference, day to day, in the conditions" | SAME (not-all; "can" kept). |
