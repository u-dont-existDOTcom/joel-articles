Later, another influencer with a big following floated the commune idea, and their comment section started to become a recruitment board. It was chaotic. People were sincere, though, and it was revealing that they were finding potential members there the way they’d normally find a used sofa.

Some commenters weren’t there to join, and the oldest objection showed up almost right away. The argument was that maybe most people don’t want the responsibility anarchism calls for, and communal property becomes “owned by everyone, cared for by nobody.”
