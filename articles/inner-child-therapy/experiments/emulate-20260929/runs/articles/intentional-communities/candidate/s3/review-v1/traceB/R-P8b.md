Even more fundamentally, they fail to decouple these roles. If a person has earned power in one role (a community leader, a therapist, a farmer, a business manager, etc), then they can translate that power to the other roles. In each case, it is concerning if a person has too much control over (a) identifying the problem, (b) controlling evidence related to the problem, (c) identifying a solution, and (d) making it costly to resist the solution or exit the situation.
