This is the escuelita referenced in my title. The term “escuelita,” meaning “little school,” comes from the Zapatistas in Mexico. Their escuelitas are places where people come and live for a while to learn how the Zapatistas practice autonomy in their communities. In the same way, people would live in these healing communities to heal themselves and to learn how to practice the methods they’re using, so that some of them could leave and set up another healing community somewhere else. Otherwise, we’ve built a lovely refuge for whoever got there first.
