Original: `.../intentional-communities/paste/intentional-communities-baseline-01.txt` (line numbers below are that file's lines).

Method: read only the original and the twelve candidate files. Afterwards ran a word-level diff of each candidate against its matching original paragraph(s) to catch small wording differences. No rewrite notes were consulted.

Marks
- SAME = same words.
- SAME-MEANING = different words, same content.

Typography (all files, not counted as meaning): the original uses curly apostrophes and curly double quotes. P1, P10, P11, P12, P13, P14-P15 use straight apostrophes. 10. "that never really left him" -> "that for him was primary": SHIFTED. A lasting hold on him, hedged by "never really" -> a rank of importance ("primary"). The persistence and the hedge are gone.
11. "how could human beings live together" -> "How do we live together": SHIFTED. Modal "could" -> "do"; "human beings" -> "we"; reported question -> direct question.
12. "without reproducing the same loneliness, domination, and stupidity we were supposedly escaping" -> "without recreating the same loneliness, domination, and stupidity we were supposedly escaping": SAME-MEANING ("reproducing" -> "recreating"). The three nouns and "supposedly" are SAME.
