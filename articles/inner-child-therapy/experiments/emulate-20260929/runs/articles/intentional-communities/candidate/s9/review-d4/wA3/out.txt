In 2023 the Zapatistas announced, after a long internal review, that they were dissolving the Rebel Zapatista Autonomous Municipalities and Good Government Juntas. In the new arrangement they moved authority toward Local Autonomous Governments in each community. Those local bodies can coordinate through regional collectives, but the description is explicit that the coordinating levels aren't superior to the communities, and that they depend on them.

Most institutions keep adding layers and offices as they age. The Zapatistas reviewed theirs and pushed authority down. I mean, they changed the system they were actually living in. They didn't go and put out a leadership book and open up a certification program 😂

This is also where I need to restrain my enthusiasm. A new ecovillage can't copy their visible diagram and instantly get the trust underneath it. Zapatista autonomy grew out of Indigenous communities with long histories and shared land struggles. People in those communities had language and kinship in common. And they had decades of collective defense behind them.

Their diagram isn't the useful thing. The Zapatistas themselves have warned people not to import or export Zapatismo as a ready-made model. What's useful is how they learn. They build, and they ask. They find out where practice fails, and they change the structure.

They're also not proof of my complete economic path, which ends with eliminating outside money. It starts with unpriced internal necessities and goes through a common purse on the way. Zapatista communities use collective work to support their schools and clinics, and their autonomous government and resistance. They also sell products and trade. They receive outside support, too. What they do show is that major necessities and public functions can be taken out of ordinary market purchase and governed collectively. Outside help can build capability, but it can also create gatekeepers and uneven dependence when the specialist or the donor remains indispensable. Or when the organization does.
