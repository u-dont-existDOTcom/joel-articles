The Amish, over generations, have made community decisions about where and if they want to use a piece of technology based on how it affects their community. Different Amish communities make different decisions. Some use it at work but restrict it at home. Some share it instead of everyone owning one. Others hold off on using it until they know more about its social effects. Whatever else you think of the Amish, they at least understand that adopting a technology is a decision for the group to make, and that it shouldn't get downloaded onto your device while you sleep.
