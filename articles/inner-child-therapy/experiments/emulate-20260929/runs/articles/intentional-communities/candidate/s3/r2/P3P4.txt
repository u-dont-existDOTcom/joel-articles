The Federation of Egalitarian Communities and other income-sharing groups have developed some sophisticated systems for dealing with issues of labor, money, and decision-making. But the best meeting procedure in the world won’t make two people who hate each other stop hating. At best, it will determine who gets to talk about their hatred, and whether the motion needs a second.

Eventually, when people are together long enough, politics will become a way of carrying emotional material that nobody can name. It will be an argument about a schedule or committee or noise or food or some policy or other, but it won’t be about that.
