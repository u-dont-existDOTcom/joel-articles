In 2023, after a long internal review, the Zapatistas announced that they were dissolving the Rebel Zapatista Autonomous Municipalities and the Good Government Juntas. In their new arrangement, authority moved toward Local Autonomous Governments in each community. Those local bodies can coordinate through regional collectives. But the coordinating levels are explicitly described as dependent on the communities rather than superior to them.

The Zapatistas reviewed their institutions and pushed authority downward! They didn't issue a leadership book and open a certification program 😂 They changed the system they were actually living in. Most institutions, as they age, accumulate layers and offices instead.

This is also where I need to restrain my enthusiasm. Zapatista autonomy grew from Indigenous communities with long histories and shared land struggles. They had language and kinship in common. A new ecovillage can't copy the visible diagram and instantly acquire the trust beneath it. Those communities also had decades of collective defense.

The useful thing isn't the Zapatistas' diagram. What's useful is how they learn. They build, and they ask. And they discover where practice fails, and they change the structure. The Zapatistas themselves have warned people not to import or export Zapatismo as a ready-made model.

They're also not proof of my complete economic path. That path goes from unpriced internal necessities to a common purse. From there it goes on to eliminating outside money. Zapatista communities use collective work to support schools and clinics. They use it to support autonomous government and resistance too. They also sell products and trade. And they receive outside support. What they do show is that major necessities and public functions can be taken out of ordinary market purchase and governed collectively. Outside help can build capability, but it can also create gatekeepers and uneven dependence when the specialist or the donor remains indispensable. The same can happen when it's an organization that remains indispensable.
