If you look up “ecovillage” and “intentional community” on Google Trends, you’ll see interest going down slowly for years until the mid-2020s, when it turns around and climbs steeply.

That’s around when AI went from being a curiosity in tech news to reorganizing people’s jobs, feeds, relationships, and expectations of the future. I can’t prove AI is the reason the curve turned. It’s hard to look at the timing and shrug it off, though. Suddenly “maybe we should form a village” sounded less like a 1972 leftover and more like a backup plan.
