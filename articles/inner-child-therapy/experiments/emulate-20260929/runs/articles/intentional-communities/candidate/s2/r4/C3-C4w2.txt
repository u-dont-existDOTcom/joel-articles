If you look at Google Trends for “ecovillage” and “intentional community”, you can see that interest drifted down for years, then bent upward sharply in the mid-2020s.

That's right around when AI stopped being just something you read about in tech news. It started changing people's jobs. It started changing their relationships and how they see the future. I can't prove that's why those lines went up, but the timing is hard to ignore. People began thinking about forming a village as a backup plan.
