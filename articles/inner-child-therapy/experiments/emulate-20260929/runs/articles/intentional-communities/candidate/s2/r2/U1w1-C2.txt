In 2023, the U.S. Surgeon General’s advisory reported that roughly half of American adults experience loneliness, and it treated social connection as a public health issue rather than a sentimental extra. The longing that shaped my father’s movie is suddenly everywhere again, and loneliness came first in the question he was trying to answer.

Whether you want to call that loneliness an official “epidemic” or not, it’s obvious that a lot of people can’t tolerate modern life in isolation anymore. They may disagree about politics, food, God, sex, or whether shoes are oppressive, but they agree that something about the current arrangement is starvin’ them.
