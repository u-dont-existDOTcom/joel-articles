Later, when another large influencer floated the commune idea, people under the post were finding prospective members the way they might find a used sofa on any other day. It was chaotic and sincere, and revealing too. The comment section was starting to turn into a recruitment board.

Some of the commenters weren’t trying to join, and there was an objection: maybe most people don’t want to take on the responsibility anarchism requires, and communal property ends up “owned by everyone, cared for by nobody.” It’s the oldest one, and it showed up almost immediately.
