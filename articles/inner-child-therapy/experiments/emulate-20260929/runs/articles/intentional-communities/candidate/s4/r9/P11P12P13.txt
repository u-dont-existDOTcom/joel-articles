Inner-child reparenting means learning to identify as the best inner adult, which is comprised of the three inner adult roles: Nurturer, Protector, and Guide. The newly developed adult reparents their newly recognized inner child. The child receives all that love, protection, and guidance that you give it, and in this process actually gains more freedom to remain alive inside you.

And it's built on somatic regulation first. Listening to the body's feelings before making a decision with the mind. And that actually symbolizes the adult/child relationship to me in some way as well.

My contemplative practice is grounded in the early Buddhist suttas, with a critical reading. Most are treasure, while some are obviously late additions (sometimes to the point of real absurdity). A living community should relate to its own teachings in that same critical manner while staying devoted to following what they've decided on so far.
