If you look at Google Trends for terms like “ecovillage” or “intentional community”, you can see that after a long decline in interest, the curve has bent upward dramatically in the mid-2020s.

I have no proof that the rise of AI is connected to this uptick, but it’s hard to ignore the coincidence in timing. AI became serious around this time, and now it’s radically changing the way we live. Suddenly “maybe we should form a village” sounded less like a 1972 leftover and more like a backup plan.
