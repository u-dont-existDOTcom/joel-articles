Their new arrangement moved authority toward Local Autonomous Governments in each community. Those local bodies can coordinate through regional collectives, but the coordinating levels are explicitly described as dependent on the communities rather than superior to them.

REWRITE (v2):

Their new arrangement moved authority toward a Local Autonomous Government in each community. Those local governments can coordinate through regional collectives, but those collectives aren't above the local governments. The Zapatistas describe the coordinating levels explicitly as depending on the communities.

An escuelita can fail by becoming a visitor business. Residents spend their lives performing community for paying outsiders, and guest numbers replace daughter communities as the success metric. REWRITE (v2):

It shouldn't be about making people feel transformed for a week. It should teach enough of the social and material system that some of them can build it somewhere else.

REWRITE (v2):

I'm borrowing their way of teaching and their ambition to replicate, without claiming the Zapatistas already proved my whole model.
