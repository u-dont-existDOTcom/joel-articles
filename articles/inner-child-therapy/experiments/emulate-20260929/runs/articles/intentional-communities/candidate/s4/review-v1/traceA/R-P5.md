These are just rough categories that bleed into one another. In practice, one move in a jealousy situation might also affect how the community governs itself, and how it shares resources can turn into a psychological issue. Or, the way a community uses medicine may create status, or the way it raises children may expose values the founders never talked about. I mostly break them out like this to help see what a community is missing, not because living in a community fits neatly into categories.
