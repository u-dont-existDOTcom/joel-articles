# Stance check: section 10 rewrite ("From One Community to a Movement")

## Conflicts in the rewrite

**1. Subsection heading** (replaces the published "Federation Without Building Another State")

- Rewrite: "Federated Communities Make a Movement, Improve Resilience"
- Essay, section 10, "Federation Without Building Another State": "The Federation of Egalitarian Communities says it is currently rebuilding, which is a useful warning."
- Same subsection: "Federation work is work: … all consume labor and material capacity before local crises have eaten both."
- A reader would wrongly believe that Joel claims federating makes communities more resilient. The essay never says this anywhere, and this passage presents federation as costly, fallible work that uses up the capacity local crises need.

## Contradictions in the published text

I found none. Published section 10 doesn't take back any of its own claims, and none of its claims reverses a position stated elsewhere in the essay. These are the closest tensions I checked and rejected:

- "Try the different rule inside the parent group" vs. "Some experiments cannot be tested cheaply" (both in section 10): the second sentence qualifies the first rather than retracting it, and the gradient already steps up "if it needs more independence."
- "eventually somebody has to solve … how they remain connected" (section 10) vs. "If you teach other people to build, hearing about it matters less" ("Practice Comes Before Property," closing lines): the closing line is about Joel hearing about projects, not about communities' ties to each other.
- "If someone can theoretically move but housing, … travel costs … fall into cracks, the freedom is decorative" (section 10) vs. "If it cannot, it can still avoid punishing departure or withholding the knowledge and records a young adult needs to function elsewhere" ("The Door Must Open Outward at Adulthood"): these are different situations (moving between federated communities vs. a grown child leaving). Also, "fall into cracks" asks that someone be responsible for those costs, not that the community pay them.

Rewrite conflicts: 1. Published-text contradictions: 0.
