Medical care is one of the toughest connections to work out. A community may prefer natural care, but what happens if someone in the community needs trauma surgery, antibiotics, dentistry, emergency obstetric care, or any number of specialist services? The community really needs to hash out in advance how people in it are going to have access to these services. What kind of care is going to be guaranteed to community members by the community using common resources? Will there be some things that will require outside dollars or some form of insurance? And what if someone wants more (or less) medical care than what the community wants to provide?
