Put “ecovillage” and “intentional community” into Google Trends. For years, interest sank a little at a time, and then in the mid-2020s it turned and started climbing steeply.

Around then, AI went from a novelty you read about in the tech news to something that was rearranging people’s jobs, feeds, relationships, and expectations of the future. I can’t prove that’s the reason for the turn. It’s hard to look past the timing, though. Suddenly “maybe we should form a village” sounded less like a 1972 leftover and more like a backup plan.
