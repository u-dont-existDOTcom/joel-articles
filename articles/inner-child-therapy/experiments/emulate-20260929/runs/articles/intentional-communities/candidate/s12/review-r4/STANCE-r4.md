# Stance check: section 12 rewrite, round 4

Section 12 is "The Math of Absorption, and Who This Isn't For".

## Conflicts in the rewrite

**1. The small-group point becomes size-neutral**

- Rewrite: "Whether there are eight people in the community or eighty, someone still has to grow the food, fix the cars…"
- Essay, section 12: "small communities need members most and can absorb disruption least. Eight people still need food grown, equipment repaired…" Same section, two paragraphs earlier: "Eighty people can often distribute the same need across many relationships and practical roles."
- A reader would wrongly believe the essential chores weigh as heavily on eighty people as on eight, so the sentence no longer explains why small groups absorb disruption least.

**2. "Practical skills" softened to "some skill"**

- Rewrite: "So early on, members usually need to have more resource than acute need, and some skill to contribute, and a willingness to learn."
- Essay, section 12: "plus practical skills and a willingness to learn." Section "Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community": "A group can have extraordinary emotional skill and still die because the well fails"
- A reader would wrongly believe any skill qualifies an early member, emotional or counseling skill included, rather than the practical skills that keep food, equipment and water going.

**3. Outside review required at the founding stage (conflicts with a stated position; carried over from the published text)**

- Rewrite: "that's going to require actually making concrete criteria, allowing for outside review, allowing for re-visiting a 'not now' decision"
- Joel's stated position, 2026-10-11 18:35 UTC: "before critical mass, my rules apply, because it's my land […] i decide who stays, i decide who leaves, i decide everything."
- A reader would wrongly believe that before critical mass, a "not now" on Joel's land must meet set criteria and face outside review, though he says he decides alone until he accepts partners.

## Contradictions in the published text

**1. Outside review of "not now" decisions, against Joel's rule for the period before critical mass**

- Published, section 12: "Any real process would need concrete criteria, outside review, and a way to revisit a 'not now' decision."
- It conflicts with Joel's 2026-10-11 position quoted in item 3. The section is written for founding groups and early members ("A founding group should not pretend it can replace those"; "So early members usually need more available resource than acute need"). A reasonable reader therefore takes the requirement to cover the period when, by his newer position, he alone decides who stays and who leaves. After critical mass, the sentence fits his section 11 rewrite: "No single person or permanent group of people should … decide who gets into or stays".
- The rewrite still has it (item 3 above).

No sentence in the published section takes back another, and I found no other place where it conflicts with the rest of the essay.

Conflicts in the rewrite: 3. Contradictions in the published text: 1.
