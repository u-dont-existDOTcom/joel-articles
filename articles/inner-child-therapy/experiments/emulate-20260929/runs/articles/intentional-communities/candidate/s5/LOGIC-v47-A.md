# Logic audit v47-A: P4, P12, P13

Scope: P4, P12 and P13 only; the other 30 paragraphs were audited separately. Numbering counts every non-heading, non-image text block in `original-section.md` and `R-section.md` (33 in each): P4 "Most intentional communities…", P12 "In community, a sober sitter…", P13 "Peers can also reality-check…". Only relations whose wording differs are listed.

## P4

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Negation scope | "still avoid saying so publicly" | "are still not open about it" | **AMBIGUOUS.** (a) They don't say it publicly (= published). (b) With "publicly" gone, "not open" can mean secretive in general, including with members, visitors or applicants: more concealment than the published sentence claims. |
| Modality of the first two reactions | "parents may worry about children, neighbors may imagine chaos" | "possible concerns … from parents worried about children, from neighbors imagining chaos" | **AMBIGUOUS.** (a) "possible" governs the whole list, so every reaction stays hypothetical (= published). (b) The participles carry no modal, so the parents already are worried and the neighbors already imagine chaos; only their concerns are "possible". The insurers ("might") and officials ("may") keep their own modals. |
| Cause | Two sentences side by side; the reason is implied | "…not open about it, due to possible concerns…" | SAME. The implied explanation becomes explicit and runs the same way (feared reactions → silence). |
| Concession | "Even where the law permits some use," heads all four reactions | "(even where the law allows some use)" inside the "concerns" phrase | SAME. Still covers all four; the other attachment ("not open even where legal") is what the published pair implies anyway. |
| Whose concern | Each party reacts; the community's anticipation is implied | "concerns from" each party | SAME. Both readings (the parties' concerns, the community's concerns about them) fit the published picture. |
| Insurers | "insurers may disappear" | "insurance providers who might drop them" | SAME. Insurers still withdraw from the communities ("them"); "might" is still possibility. |
| Officials | "a local official may understand none of the distinctions" | "local officials who may not understand any of the distinctions" | SAME. "may not … any" = "may … none"; one official → officials generally doesn't change the risk. |

## P12

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Watched/held: link and modality | "a sober sitter may be somebody who has known you for years. Being watched and being held feel different." | "your sober sitter may be someone who's known you for years, and you feel the difference between being watched and being held" | **AMBIGUOUS.** (a) A general truth that the two feel different (= published). (b) The "and" clause sits under "In a community" but outside "may", so it can say that in community you do feel held rather than watched: the published possibility (the sitter *may* be a long-time friend) becomes a definite outcome. |
| Who screens and observes | "screening and continuous observation are essential" (no agent) | "you need someone to properly screen you beforehand and watch you continuously" | **AMBIGUOUS.** (a) Both tasks are needed, by whoever does them (= published). (b) One "someone" does both, and right after "someone who's known you for years" that reads as the friend-sitter, so medical screening becomes the sitter's job. The published sentence gives screening to no one (P22 lists screening and keeping a sitter as separate duties). |
| Necessity | "are essential" | "you need … properly … beforehand" | SAME. Both state necessity; "properly" and "beforehand" spell out what screening already means. |
| Example and class | "long, medically risky experiences such as iboga" | "iboga or other long and medically risky experiences" | SAME. Iboga is still one member of the class; "and" still requires both properties. |
| Scope and reason | "…such as iboga, where screening … are essential" | separate sentence: "For those you need…" | SAME. The requirement covers the whole class and still explains why the sitter matters more there. |
| Degree | "This matters especially" | "This is especially important" | SAME. |
| Frame, determiner | "In community, a sober sitter" | "In a community, your sober sitter" | SAME. |

## P13

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Modality | "Psychedelics produce genuine insight and convincing nonsense" | "Psychedelics can give you insight and they can give you convincing nonsense" | **CHANGED (mild).** What psychedelics do becomes what they can do: a reader would take convincing nonsense as something that may happen, where the published sentence states that psychedelics produce it, just as they produce insight. |
| Both at once, within one download | "produce genuine insight and convincing nonsense" + "which parts resemble a new understanding and which parts resemble your mother" | two separate "can" clauses + "the difference between new understanding and… mom" | **AMBIGUOUS.** (a) One download mixes both and friends sort it part by part (= published). (b) Each download is either insight or nonsense, and friends judge it whole. "All in the same bright shining light" leans toward (a) but doesn't restore "which parts". |
| Quantifier | "Friends who know your history" | "A few friends who know your history" | **AMBIGUOUS.** (a) A small circle of such friends can do it (compatible). (b) Only a few of the friends who know your history may notice; the published sentence gives that ability to such friends in general. |
| Hedge | "may notice which parts resemble a new understanding and which parts resemble your mother" | "may notice the difference between new understanding and… mom" | **CHANGED (mild).** Friends now tell real new understanding from mother-material; the published friends only notice which parts look like which. |
| Presupposition | "the downloads" | "any downloads you may have had" | SAME. The hedge on whether there were downloads leaves the claim (peers can check them) intact. |
| Likeness | "with identical lighting" | "all in the same bright shining light" | SAME. |
| Referent | "your mother" | "… mom" | SAME. |

## CHANGED and AMBIGUOUS

1. P4: "avoid saying so publicly" → "not open about it". AMBIGUOUS: may be read as general secrecy, including toward members and visitors, not only public silence.
2. P4: "parents may worry about children, neighbors may imagine chaos" → "possible concerns … from parents worried about children, from neighbors imagining chaos". AMBIGUOUS: may be read as parents already worried and neighbors already imagining chaos.
3. P12: "may be somebody who has known you for years. Being watched and being held feel different." → "may be someone who's known you for years, and you feel the difference…". AMBIGUOUS: may be read as a promise that in community you will feel held, rather than a general contrast attached to a possibility.
4. P12: "screening and continuous observation are essential" → "you need someone to properly screen you beforehand and watch you continuously". AMBIGUOUS: may be read as the long-known sitter doing the medical screening.
5. P13: "produce genuine insight and convincing nonsense" → "can give you insight and they can give you convincing nonsense". CHANGED (mild): nonsense becomes a possibility rather than something psychedelics produce.
6. P13: "which parts resemble … and which parts resemble …" → "the difference between new understanding and… mom". AMBIGUOUS: may be read as each download being one or the other, judged whole, rather than a mixture sorted part by part.
7. P13: "Friends who know your history" → "A few friends who know your history". AMBIGUOUS: may be read as only a few such friends being able to notice.
8. P13: "resemble" → "the difference between". CHANGED (mild): friends discern what is genuine instead of noticing likenesses.
