# Stance check: section 9 rewrite ("The Best Model I’ve Seen: Zapatistas"), review d5

## Conflicts in the rewrite

None found. The paraphrased paragraphs (P1–P8, P13–P17) keep the positions the essay states elsewhere.

## Contradictions in the published text

**1. The Zapatista example teaches opposite lessons in two places.**

- Section 9: "The Zapatistas reviewed theirs and pushed authority downward." Also: "the coordinating levels are explicitly described as dependent on the communities rather than superior to them."
- "When Somebody Has to Go" (under "Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community"): "The Zapatistas are an important correction here: autonomous institutions can review local power without becoming the state." The test that section sets: "a competent layer that is not reviewing its own conduct."

Section 9 praises the Zapatistas for dissolving their regional bodies and making the levels above the community depend on the communities. The later section offers Zapatista institutions as the model of an outside layer that checks local power. The essay never says which Zapatista body does that checking after 2023, or how a level that depends on the communities can be the independent check the later section asks for. A reader gets two lessons from one example that pull opposite ways: move power down into each community, or keep a layer above the community to review it.

Rewrite: still has it, more sharply. P9 keeps "moved authority downward", and P9–P11 now call power held at higher levels a "degeneration". See question 1.

## Questions on the author's own paragraphs

**1. P9 and P11 vs "When Somebody Has to Go."**

P9: "it had crept up and consolidated itself at higher levels over time for practical reasons." P11: "the Zapatistas were successfully able to reverse this degeneration."

"When Somebody Has to Go" (not rewritten): "The Zapatistas are an important correction here: autonomous institutions can review local power without becoming the state."

Your paragraphs make power above the community the decay the Zapatistas undid; the later section makes Zapatista institutions above the community the model for outside review. Which lesson do you want readers to take from the Zapatistas? If both, which Zapatista body reviews local power now that the Juntas are dissolved?

**2. P9 and P11 vs P8 (kept from the published text).**

P8: "When different municipalities encountered different problems and uneven progress, that experience generated another structure rather than being forced to fit the original plan."

P9: "The original idea was to have power vested in the local communities, but it had crept up … for practical reasons."

P8 uses the step from municipalities to the Good Government Boards (P9's "Good Government Juntas") to illustrate "one of the most useful Zapatista principles for community design": leaving the original plan when practice demands it. P9 and P11 call power moving away from the original idea, for practical reasons, into those same bodies a "degeneration". Were the Boards practice-first design that later accumulated power, or was the move upward itself the degeneration? As the section stands, a reader meets the same development praised in one paragraph and called decay in the next.
