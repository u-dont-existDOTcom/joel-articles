# Section 11 pairs (B4): published paragraph and rewrite

Section 11 is "Founderism: The Four Questions". The rewrite keeps the published order.

## P18 → P18p

CONTEXT BEFORE: “Nothing suppressed” applies to myself and my relationship with my own material. It doesn't give anyone the right to pull anything out of me. Any member can choose not to share material, and they don't need to provide a therapeutic rationale for not sharing.

PUBLISHED P18:
Medicine is optional. Abstaining cannot reduce membership rights. No individual or permanent clique controls admission. Disagreement has to be answered as disagreement.

REWRITE P18p:
If someone offers you medicine, you can always say no. Your decision not to take medicine will not affect your rights as a member. No person or permanent group of people will have the power to let people in. And when you disagree with someone, they have to answer your disagreement as a disagreement.

CONTEXT AFTER: “Your resistance is your wound talking” is pretty much a conversation ender. Once you notice a community diagnosing dissent like this instead of answering it, leave. Don't wait until they schedule a group process about your leaving.
