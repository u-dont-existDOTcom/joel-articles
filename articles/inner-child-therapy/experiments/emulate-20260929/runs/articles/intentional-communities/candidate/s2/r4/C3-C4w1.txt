If you look at Google Trends for “ecovillage” and “intentional community”, you can see that interest drifted down for years, then bent upward sharply in the mid-2020s.

That's right around when AI stopped being something you read about in tech news and started changing people's jobs and relationships and how they think about the future. I can't prove that's why interest went up. But the timing is hard to ignore, and people started talking about forming villages again, as a backup plan.
