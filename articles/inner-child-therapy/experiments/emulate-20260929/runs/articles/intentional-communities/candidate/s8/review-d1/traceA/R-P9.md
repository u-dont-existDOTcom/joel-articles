Experiments with kids sleeping and living apart from their parents should be approached with special caution. Research on Israeli kibbutzim found that [the practice of children sleeping in a communal setting eventually came to an end, due to a combination of developmental findings, parental preference and social change](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12395568/). Kids can benefit from high-quality group care and from contact with several different loving adults, but replacing a secure attachment to their parents is a different matter.
