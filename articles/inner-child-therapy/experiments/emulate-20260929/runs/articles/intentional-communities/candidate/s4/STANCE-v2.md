# Stance check: section 4 rewrite ("The Four Parts That Have to Pl/ork Together")

I checked every sentence in the rewrite that states a position, wish, rule, assessment or claim about how things work against the whole published essay. Only the conflicts are listed below.

## 1. The outward *escuelita* purpose becomes optional

- **Rewrite:** "And when we take on the purpose of an outward *escuelita* and federation, then we have even more reason to do so" (this comes right after the rewrite says that healing alone makes it "make sense to bind our lives together").
- **Essay** (opening section, "The New Age May Dawn Suddenly"): "The healing communities I'm proposing should do the same [as the Zapatista *Escuelita*] … Otherwise, we've built a lovely refuge for whoever got there first." See also published section 4: "the community exists not only to protect the people already inside it, but to help other people build."
- **What a reader would wrongly believe:** that a community built only around healing already justifies binding lives together, and that teaching outsiders and seeding new communities is an extra a group may choose to add. The essay says these communities should do it.

## 2. A hedge about the evidence is dropped on the moneyless endpoint (minor)

- **Rewrite:** "I don't know of any modern community that has gone this far"
- **Essay** (section 4, "4. The container"): "I did not find a well-documented modern community that has fully reached that point."
- **What a reader would wrongly believe:** that Joel knows of no such community at all. The essay says only that he found no well-documented community that has fully reached that stage.
