Most of the writing about communities is outdated when it comes to psychedelics, because they’ve gone from taboo toward regulated use so fast. Licensed psilocybin service centers started opening in Oregon in 2023. Colorado created a regulated system for natural medicines and began licensing in 2025, and New Mexico enacted a Medical Psilocybin Act also in 2025. In Australia, since 2023, authorized psychiatrists have had limited access to psilocybin and MDMA for a specific list of conditions.
