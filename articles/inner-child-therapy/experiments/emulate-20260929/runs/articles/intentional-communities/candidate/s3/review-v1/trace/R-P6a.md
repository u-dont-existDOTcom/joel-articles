[The Farm](https://thefarmcommunity.com/), in Tennessee, at its largest about 1,500 people, had many beautiful values. But in the early days, the community’s spirituality and social life revolved around Stephen Gaskin. From what former members have shared, the doctrine ran deep into members’ intimate decisions. It’s one thing to talk about a universal consciousness. It’s another to barely be able to escape one man’s opinions.
