4. The container

Membership, land, shared resources, governance, children, and exit.

In the long run, I'd like to see communities like these use less and less money. I see three stages:

First, no one buys from anyone else in the community to meet their basic needs (food, housing, care, tools, education, transport), but each person may work outside the community and keep what they earn. However, if they do work outside, they contribute an agreed upon share of their income to the community. This is probably the most realistic way to get started.
