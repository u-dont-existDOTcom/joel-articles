On Google Trends, interest in “ecovillage” and “intentional community” turned sharply upward in the mid-2020s. Before that, both had been drifting down for years.

That turn happened around the same time AI stopped being a curiosity you read about in tech news and started reorganizing people’s jobs, their feeds, their relationships, and what they expected from the future. I can’t prove AI caused it, but the timing is hard to ignore. Suddenly “maybe we should form a village” sounded less like a 1972 leftover and more like a backup plan. My father never thought of it as a leftover.
