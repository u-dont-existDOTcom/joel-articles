Medical care is one of the toughest connections to work out. A community may prefer natural care, but what happens if someone in the community needs trauma surgery, antibiotics, dentistry, emergency obstetric care, or any number of specialist services? It is really important for the community to decide in advance how members are going to get care; what it will guarantee from common resources; what may require money from outside sources or insurance; and how it will treat people who choose a different level of medical involvement.
