# Logic audit d11-A: "Which Connections to the Technosphere Do You Keep?"

Logical relations only; tables list only relations whose wording differs.

AMBIGUOUS* = the published wording allows two readings and the rewrite picks one: CHANGED unless the author meant that one (the deciding context is outside this folder).

## P1

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| What feels urgent | "only one reason this feels urgent" | "only one reason building community feels urgent" | AMBIGUOUS*. "this" can be building community or the heading's question (which technosphere ties to keep). If the latter, the polycrisis now reads as a reason to build community, not to loosen dependence. |

## P2

Not rewritten. No changed relations.

## P3

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Degree of the doubt | "I don't know exactly how much of the future fear is accurate" | "Do I think the fears about the future are legitimate? I don't know. Maybe." | CHANGED. Published implies some of the fear is accurate and doubts only how much; a yes/no question answered "Maybe" makes the author unsure the fears are warranted at all. "Accurate" (will it happen) also becomes "legitimate" (is it reasonable). |
| Alternatives | "may improve…, destroy…, become…, or do all three" | "Maybe… Maybe… Maybe… Maybe all of these things will happen" | SAME. Both leave open which outcome comes and add the all-three case. |
| Set counted | "destroy millions of jobs" | "millions of people will lose their jobs to AI" | CHANGED (minor). Jobs eliminated become people laid off; positions can vanish without that many people losing work. |
| Degree and scope of mediation | "the administrative layer between us and nearly everything" | "a layer of bureaucracy between humanity and whatever it is we're trying to do" | CHANGED. The one layer that administers our access to nearly everything (dependence, control) becomes one more layer of red tape in front of our goals (friction). |
| Who does what | "gets through her commune applications" | "finishes going through all the applications to her commune" | AMBIGUOUS*. Published can mean applications to her commune, which she reviews, or her own applications to communes. If the latter, a reader now thinks she runs one. |

## P4

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preference and its scope | "I'd rather build … than wait for certainty" | "On a personal level, it makes more sense to me to focus on cultivating … than to wait" | AMBIGUOUS. (a) It only marks the view as his own, like "I'd rather". (b) "On a personal level" implies that collectively, waiting might make more sense, a concession the published text doesn't make. |
| Preference made a claim | "I'd rather have community and discover … than need an exit…" | "It's better to have community and find out … than to need an exit…, right?" | AMBIGUOUS. (a) Rhetorical tag: a flat claim that this is better, stronger than a preference. (b) Real question: he is unsure and seeks the reader's assent, weaker. |

## P5

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scope of "useful" | "The useful design question is: which … do you keep, and which do you limit?" | "What connections … would be useful to limit or keep…?" | CHANGED. Published says keep-or-limit is the right question. In the rewrite "useful" attaches to each connection, so usefulness can read as the test for keeping one; the section's example test (P7) is effect on community life. |
| Condition and mood | "which … do you keep" | "if you were to design a community" | CHANGED. A present question to the reader becomes a hypothetical for community founders, not a choice about the reader's own ties. |
| Connective | "which … keep, and which … limit" | "limit or keep" | SAME. Each connection is still either kept or limited. |

## P6

No changed relations ("Full rejection can create" / "Full dependence means" → "If you opt out of them completely, it can cause" / "If you depend on them completely" keep possibility and condition).

## P7

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| What varies | "A tool may be accepted at work and restricted at home, shared…, or delayed…" | "Some use it at work but restrict it at home. Some share it… Others hold off…" | AMBIGUOUS. (a) For a given technology, communities split among these treatments (consistent). (b) Communities are types with one standing strategy each, "Others" a separate set; published says any tool may get any treatment and assigns none to particular groups. |
| Contrast | "a communal decision rather than an automatic software update"; "shared rather than individually owned" | "a decision for the group…, and that it shouldn't get downloaded…"; "share it instead of everyone owning one" | SAME. Both contrasts survive; "shouldn't" states a norm the published sentence implies. |

## P8

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who needs; concession | "may prefer natural care and still need…" | "but what happens if someone in the community needs…?" | CHANGED (minor). Published: the community itself, whatever its preference, can still need these. Rewrite: one member's contingency against the group's preference, blurring it with the later case of a member who chooses differently. |
| Modality | "It should decide in advance" | "really needs to hash out in advance" | CHANGED (minor). Advice becomes necessity. |
| Scope of "in advance" and the duty | one sentence: decide in advance how…, what…, what…, and how… | only access stays under "needs to hash out in advance"; the other three become separate questions | AMBIGUOUS. (a) The questions are the agenda of that advance hash-out. (b) They are open questions the author raises, with no stated duty or deadline. |
| Sets | "how members access care"; "what the community guarantees" | "access to these services"; "What kind of care is going to be guaranteed" | CHANGED (minor). Access to care in general narrows to the listed outside services, and the guarantee narrows to care (published leaves room for money or transport). |
| Which → whether; may → will | "what may require outside money or insurance" | "Will there be some things that will require outside dollars…?" | CHANGED. Published presupposes some care may need outside money and asks which; the rewrite asks whether anything will, as if the answer could be nothing. |
| Who does what to whom | "how to treat somebody who chooses a different level of medical involvement" | "what if someone wants more (or less) medical care than what the community wants to provide?" | CHANGED. How the community treats a member who chooses differently, in either direction, becomes one person's wants against the community's provision, "more" foregrounded and "less" bracketed; "chooses" weakens to "wants". |

## P9

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who arranges; modality | "A catastrophic-illness agreement should protect…" | "Members need to have pre-existing agreements in place so that…" | AMBIGUOUS. (a) Members jointly hold one agreement covering whoever falls ill: same, though "should" is now "need to". (b) Each member must arrange their own, moving the burden from the community (P8: "It should decide") to the person it protects. |
| Object of protection | "the possibility that the community's preferred approach won't be enough" | "they can get access to what they need if the community's preferred methods are not sufficient" | CHANGED. Published asks the agreement to keep open the possibility that the community's approach fails. The rewrite promises access to whatever a member needs when it does, a guarantee the published text doesn't make. |

## P10

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subject of the health claim | "social connection itself has measurable health value" | "being part of a community, having good social connections, is measurably good for a person's health" | CHANGED. The measured benefit now attaches to community membership, equated with or listed beside "good" connections, and to each person. Published credits social connection itself and leaves community as one source of it. |
| Association vs cause | "links isolation with higher risks across several outcomes" | "notes an increased risk for a range of health conditions if one is socially isolated" | AMBIGUOUS. (a) Isolated people show higher risk, as "links" says. (b) Being isolated raises a person's risk: a causal, individual claim where the published text reports only a link. Also "several outcomes" → "a range of health conditions". |
| Negation and contrast | "Community won't cure every illness. It can change…" | "Community's not a panacea, but it can make a difference…" | SAME. "Not every" holds; the implied contrast is made explicit. |
