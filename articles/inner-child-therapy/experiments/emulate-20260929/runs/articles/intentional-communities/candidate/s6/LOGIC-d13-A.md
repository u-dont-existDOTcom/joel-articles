## P3

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Partitive vs degree | "how much of the future fear is accurate" | "How accurate are the fears about the future?" | SAME: both allow partial accuracy. |
| Disjunction | "may improve…, destroy…, become…, or do all three" | four "Maybe…" sentences, ending "Maybe all of these things will happen" | SAME: each stays a separate possibility; no "also"; co-occurrence was already offered by "or do all three". |
| Agent | "AI may … destroy millions of jobs" | "millions of people will lose their jobs to AI" | SAME: AI remains the cause. |
| Quantifier and domain | "between us and nearly everything" | "between humanity and whatever it is we're trying to do" | CHANGED: "nearly" is gone and the range shifts from everything to our aims (a second reading: one vague goal). Reader: AI may stand between us and all our purposes, with no exceptions. |
| Who processes whose applications | "before Chantress Seba gets through her commune applications" | "before Chantress Seba finishes going through all the applications to her commune" | CHANGED unless earlier sections establish that Seba runs a commune. The published phrase also reads as Seba applying to communes (compare "before she gets through her college applications"); the rewrite makes her the reviewer of others' applications to her commune. |

## P4

|---|---|---|---|
| Scope of the preference | "I'd rather build more human independence than wait for certainty" | "On a personal level, it makes more sense to me to focus on cultivating more human independence than to wait for certainty" | AMBIGUOUS (minor): (a) "personally, I think" = the original; (b) "in my own life", implying the answer could differ at a collective level, a contrast the original doesn't draw. ## P5

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mood, condition, assertion | "The useful design question is: which connections … do you keep, and which do you limit?" | "What connections … would you keep, and which would you limit, if you were to design a community?" | CHANGED: a present-tense question becomes a counterfactual ("would … if you were to") limited to designing a community, and the claim that this is *the useful* question is gone. ## P7

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alternatives | "A tool may be accepted at work and restricted at home, shared rather than individually owned, or delayed until…" | "Sometimes they use it at work but restrict it at home. Sometimes they share it… Sometimes they hold off…" | SAME: "sometimes" spreads the options over cases and never says one tool gets all three; this is not "also". "may" (variety) → "sometimes" (frequency) is equivalent here. |
| Conjunction | "accepted at work and restricted at home" | "use it at work but restrict it at home" | SAME: both still hold together; "but" adds only contrast. |
| Negation over a quantifier | "shared rather than individually owned" | "share it instead of everyone owning one" | SAME on the natural reading, one shared item rather than one each (strictly, it rules out only everyone owning one). |
| Timing | "delayed until its social effect becomes clearer" | "hold off … until they know more about its social effects" | SAME. |
| Scope of the contrast | "they at least understand that adoption is a communal decision rather than an automatic software update" | "…a decision for the group to make. It doesn't just get downloaded onto your device while you sleep." | AMBIGUOUS (minor): the contrast now stands outside "understand". (a) Still what the Amish grasp = the original. (b) The narrator's flat claim that technology doesn't arrive unbidden on the reader's device; the original doesn't say this, and its contrast implies that outside the Amish, adoption does arrive that way. |

## P8

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concession; modality of the need | "may prefer natural care and still need trauma surgery…" | "may prefer natural care, but what happens if someone in the community needs trauma surgery…?" | AMBIGUOUS: (a) rhetorical: someone will need it, so then what? = the original (the preference doesn't remove the need); (b) a contingency: an edge case that may never come. |
| Set | "how members access care" | "how people in it are going to have access to care" (next sentence: "guaranteed to community members") | AMBIGUOUS (minor): (a) people in it = members; (b) access planning covers everyone present (guests, children, non-members) while guarantees go only to members, a split the original doesn't make. |
| Sequence: "in advance" | "It should decide in advance how…, what…, what…, and how…" (governs all four) | appears only at the end: "These are all questions that a community should consider and decide on in advance" | AMBIGUOUS (minor): (a) "these" includes access = the original; (b) "these" is only the three direct questions, so access is "hashed out" with no timing. |
| Quantity | "or a specialist" | "or any number of specialist services" | SAME. |
| Normative → future | "how to treat somebody who chooses a different level…" | "how will the community deal with people who choose to accept (or not accept) different levels…" | SAME: still governed by "should … decide"; both directions kept. |
