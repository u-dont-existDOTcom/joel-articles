The longing that shaped my father’s film is suddenly everywhere again, and in 2023 the U.S. Surgeon General’s advisory treated social connection as a public health issue instead of a sentimental extra. Loneliness was already in my father’s question, and the advisory reported that roughly half of American adults experience it.

Whether you want to call that loneliness an official “epidemic” or not, it’s obvious that a lot of people can’t tolerate modern life in isolation anymore. They may disagree about politics, food, God, sex, or whether shoes are oppressive, but they agree that something about the current arrangement is starvin’ them.
