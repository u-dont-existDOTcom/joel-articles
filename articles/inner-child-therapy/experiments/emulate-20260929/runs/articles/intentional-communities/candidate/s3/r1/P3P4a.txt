The Federation of Egalitarian Communities and other income-sharing communities have developed some sophisticated systems for dealing with issues of labor, money, and decision-making. But the best meeting procedure in the world won’t make two people who hate each other stop. At best, it will determine who gets to talk about their hatred, and whether the motion needs a second.

Sooner or later, politics will carry emotional material that nobody names. People will argue about schedules, or committees, or noise, or food, or whatever – but not about the real issue at hand.
