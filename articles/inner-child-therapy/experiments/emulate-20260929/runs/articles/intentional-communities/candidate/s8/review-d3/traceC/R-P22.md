The Amish let their young people make the decision to join the church on their own when they're adults, and [roughly 85 percent or more of them end up joining](https://groups.etown.edu/amishstudies/social-organization/population-growth/). Pop culture makes *Rumspringa* seem far wilder and more universal than it is, but it's still a big deal that they're baptized as adults, because then belonging is a commitment instead of a fact imposed at birth.
