Later, all wages and other income earned by the members from work outside the community, or from business inside the community, will be paid into the common purse of the community. No member accumulates private wealth while a member of the community. Some version of this is how things have been done in the Bruderhof community for generations, and my father lived there for 17 years. It is also done, in a somewhat different way, at Twin Oaks, and a number of other income-sharing communities. So a common purse isn’t just a theory.
