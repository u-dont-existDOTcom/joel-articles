## P1pub → P1t6

CONTEXT BEFORE: If your human rights are unfairly stripped from you, even by a majority vote, then you can vote with your feet.

PUBLISHED:

This article asks people to discuss jealousy, God, death, childhood, money, sex, psychedelics, and what happens when somebody wants to leave. Many perfectly intelligent adults would rather discuss solar panels for six hours.

REWRITE:

Many perfectly intelligent grownups would rather sit around for six hours talking about solar panels than talk about jealousy, God, death, childhood, money, sex, psychedelics, or somebody wanting to leave, which is what I ask people to do in this article.

CONTEXT AFTER: This does not surprise me, since we are taught to put on a show of competence in modern society. We have a lot of incentives to hide ourselves in our lives and disappear behind our screens the second things get uncomfortable. We have been encouraged by our institutions to be narrow in our skills, and then they act surprised when nobody can sit through a conflict without appointing a subcommittee or blocking somebody.

## P3pub → P3n3

CONTEXT BEFORE: This does not surprise me, since we are taught to put on a show of competence in modern society. We have a lot of incentives to hide ourselves in our lives and disappear behind our screens the second things get uncomfortable. We have been encouraged by our institutions to be narrow in our skills, and then they act surprised when nobody can sit through a conflict without appointing a subcommittee or blocking somebody.

PUBLISHED:

So the question isn’t whether a person is “good enough” for community. The practical question is how much attention they currently have available for other people, how willing they are to examine their own reactions, and whether the group has enough capacity to support them without being consumed.

REWRITE:

Instead of asking "Is this person good enough?", the practical questions to ask are, "How much attention can this person bring to other people right now? How available is he or she to engage in self-examination? And how available are we to this person, without being consumed by him or her?"

CONTEXT AFTER: Some people have engaged in long practices of contemplation or therapy, or have had very good luck in childhood; other people are just trying to survive some emergency in their life and need care more than responsibility. Most of us are all of these at different times in our lives.

## P5pub → P5n3

CONTEXT BEFORE: [the chart, image 17: "The Math of Absorption", "A community can absorb only as much need as its members have free resource to meet."; 8 people with 2 in crisis, "1 in 4 — everything goes to triage"; 80 people with 5 in crisis, "1 in 16 — carried, and barely felt"; "The cruel paradox: small communities need new members most and can absorb them least."]

PUBLISHED:

In this model, the community shares some functions that modern society assigns to therapists. [Peer counseling](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/p/hearthwork-an-integrated-peer-counseling) distributes the capacity to listen, accompany, and help people process ordinary emotional material.

REWRITE:

In the model I’m imagining, some of the functions that are outsourced to therapists in modern society are distributed throughout the community. In other words, there’s a lot of [peer counseling](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/p/hearthwork-an-integrated-peer-counseling) going on, people listening to each other, being there for each other and helping each other process ordinary emotional stuff.

CONTEXT AFTER: But it’s not infinite.

## P7pub → P7n1

CONTEXT BEFORE: But it’s not infinite.

PUBLISHED:

I borrow the phrase [*free resource*](https://www.rc.org/) from Re-evaluation Counseling. It means the attention, steadiness, skill, and practical capacity a person has available after dealing with enough of their own immediate material. *Need* is what currently requires support.

REWRITE:

I’m going to use the phrase “[free resource](https://www.rc.org/),” which comes from Re-evaluation Counseling, to refer to the attention, steadiness, skill and practical capacity people have left over for others once they’ve dealt with enough of their own immediate stuff, and “need” to refer to the amount of help people need at a given time.

CONTEXT AFTER: Everyone has a certain amount of both, and the ratio varies.
