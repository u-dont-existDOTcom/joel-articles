A New We — a tour of European ecovillages showing how many different arrangements hide under one label.

Within Reach — a couple bicycles 6,500 miles through roughly one hundred communities.

Seeking the Good Life in America — Twin Oaks, Acorn, and Light Morning compared.

Voices of Cohousing — the partial-community option: high neighborliness with more private autonomy.

Inside the Bruderhof — high-commitment religious community, including the autonomy it requires members to surrender. My father lived there for 17 years. The Bruderhof also has an extensive YouTube channel.

Living in the Future: Lammas — community-building meets land use and planning law.

Books and Papers, in Reading Order

For Child-Rearing

Jean Liedloff, The Continuum Concept (1975) — a foundational natural-child-rearing book based on Liedloff’s time with the Ye’kuana. Read it as provocative observation rather than settled anthropology.

Michaeleen Doucleff, Hunt, Gather, Parent (2021) — a practical, accessible book about children participating in family life, autonomy, and distributed care.

Practical Community-Building Guides

Diana Leafe Christian, Creating a Life Together — start here for sequencing, membership, money, land, and agreements.

Yana Ludwig, Building Belonging — a whole-systems guide to intentional community.

Governance Manuals

Rau and Koch-Gonzalez, Many Voices One Song — a practical sociocracy manual.

Schaub and colleagues, Building United Judgment — consensus decision-making.

Ludwig and Gimnig, The Cooperative Culture Handbook — exercises for cooperation, conflict, and group culture.
