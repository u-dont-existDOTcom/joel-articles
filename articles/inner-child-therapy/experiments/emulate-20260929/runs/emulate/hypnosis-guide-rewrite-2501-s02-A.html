The difference between a good and a bad guide is a good guide will enhance your adult self, a bad guide seeks to replace your adult self. A good guide will allow you to say no. A bad guide will convince you that your no is resistance, ego, fear, low vibration, the result of past trauma, or sin or whatever, and that you just need more guidance.

Hypnosis Specific Items to Check for in an Audit

Are you told in advance how the induction mechanics will work?

Is it possible to ask one or more of these questions and have the process stopped or modified to allow you to return to full alertness?

Does the guide claim that not noticing is an indication of it working, secretly?

Does the guide use role play, ownership, obedience, age, identity, belonging, or other methods to weaken your adult self?

Are the effects of fractionation, cues, future actions, or memory manipulated described in advance?

Are physical effects used to demonstrate something that is not obvious?

Does the exit return you to your normal identity and allow normal function, and make it clear that it is over?

If you cannot get clear answers to these questions the process should be stopped immediately to allow you to return to full alertness, the script should be reviewed, or the process changed, or you should leave.
