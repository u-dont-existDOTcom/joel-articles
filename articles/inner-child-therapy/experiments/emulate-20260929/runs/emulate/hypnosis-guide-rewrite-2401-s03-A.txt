Pattern 3: Presuppositions

Note on the use of presuppositions: Do not use them to presume things that are not true. Only use them to presume things that are definitely true. The only things that you can presume when you are working with a client are: 1) The client is choosing to do this work 2) That it will take some amount of time 3) That the client is capable of doing the work 4) That the work will be done in a safe manner 5) That the parts will love you when you have the courage to love them 6) That you are the adult and that you know what you are doing.

It will be important to understand this before changing it.

Pattern #4: Cause-effect words (as, while, because, when, after, before, which means, etc.):

Be careful to not make the cause-effect too strong.
