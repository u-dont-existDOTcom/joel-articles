Using synchronicity as your own wake-up bell

Some people, as soon as they notice a ‘synchronicity’ begin wondering what the synchronicity means. Sometimes this can lead to useful insights, and sometimes this can lead to a compulsive search for patterns, where none exist. For me, I find it’s most useful to use the noticing of synchronicities as a ‘wake-up’ cue.

Here’s the cue I’ve installed:

Whenever I notice a ‘synchronicity’, I use that noticing to wake me up into a more expanded state of awareness in that moment. In other words, I use the noticing of a ‘synchronicity’ as a cue to feel my body and the space around me and realise that, in fact, the ‘synchronicity’ is in fact meaningless, and then I expand my state out of the current state, and from the highest possible state I can access (which is still in my body, in this moment, in my normal human reality, based on the facts of my life) I look in and I see more objectively and clearly and from a place of peace and ease and freedom and joy and gratitude, what is actually going on.

In shorthand: notice ‘synchronicity’ - pause - orient yourself - expand your state - look at the situation from this more expanded state to see what’s really going on (reality test the current state) - see what needs to be heard - take one appropriate action from love.

The advantage of installing a ‘synchronicity’ cue is that it takes you out of identification with the current state, whilst allowing you to hear what the state may be trying to tell you (if it’s a ‘negative’ state, there may be a message from an Inner Child part, or a Protector, or a need to grieve, or a message from your body about a physical problem, or a need to take some action in the world, and so on).
