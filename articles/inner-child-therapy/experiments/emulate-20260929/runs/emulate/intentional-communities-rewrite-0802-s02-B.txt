Children Need Freedom Inside Real Protection

It is important for the kids to have their own areas, where they can develop their child-society and their own games.

I remember the Unitarian youth camp as one of my freest experiences. At ZEGG, the kids in the Kinderhaus also have such a world. There, the adults watch from the outside that it is safe.

This can also be misunderstood, so we have to watch out for:

I am NOT one of those people living in the suburbs, who believe that every good relationship between an adult and a kid that does not belong to him/her is suspected of being a sexual one. Kids should have many adults around who like them. These adults should also be able to react, if something is going wrong. But I think that fear here destroys more than it safeguards.

The kids should have the power to decide for themselves, and say “no” to an adult. Adults may not expect affection from kids, physical contact, or obedience (except for the legitimate needs of the kids). Adults may not have private access to the kids.
It is a no-go area to want to have secrets with kids. That is, one may not do anything with a kid that one does not want the kid to tell his/her mother or another adult. Gifts, games, physical contact, conversations, relationships, etc. must not be hidden from the mother (or other important person). Exceptions are surprises, but here one should say when it will be revealed. It is not permitted to build secrets, with which one wants to isolate the kid.
