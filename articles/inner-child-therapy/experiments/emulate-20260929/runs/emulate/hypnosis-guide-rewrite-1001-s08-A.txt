More often than not, that part of you called the protector needs evidence. It’s not so much that they need another insight – they need to know that you are doing something to protect yourself – holding your boundary, completing a task, eating something nourishing, not sending a text, keeping a promise, getting some sleep, contacting your therapist, acknowledging a debt you owe someone, writing down what’s true for you.

Protecting yourself at the right time, and then stopping

Protector as bodily sequence of:

orienting, then assessing, then recognising the difference between real/uncertain/imagined danger, then setting a boundary (only one at a time, and a proportionate boundary), then verifying that you are safe enough, based on reality, and then standing down from the task of protecting yourself, and resting.

Allowing your ‘mature protector’ to say it doesn’t know what is going on, and asking another human being for help, or deciding to postpone coming to any conclusions, and/or committing yourself to a practice that doesn’t allow trance states. Your goal is to develop discernment, and also stop scanning for danger when the danger has gone!

Reparenting of your ‘inner child’

If you are reading this, you probably recognise that reparenting therapy and self-hypnosis are a perfect match. You may or may not see a child. You may see yourself at a younger age, or you may take on a posture, or hear a voice, or recall a memory, or feel an emotion, or just experience yourself as vulnerable in some way. If you can’t find a child, that’s fine – just work with a protector you can recognise, or just notice a sensation or feeling or preference in the moment.
