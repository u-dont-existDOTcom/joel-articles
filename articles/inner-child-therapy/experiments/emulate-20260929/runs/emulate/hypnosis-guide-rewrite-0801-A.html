Why doing it myself worked better for me

It's interesting... I had a session with a professional hypnotist, but it didn't do much. It may be that I was sober at the time and therefore not particularly suggestible. But I think it's more than that.

When I talk to my subconscious, if I use a phrase that isn't quite right, I can feel it. I can ask for clarification about what it does mean. When I see an image, I can sort of intuitively discern whether it's meant as guidance, or is it fear, or a desire to avoid something, or wishful thinking, or something else entirely.

A hypnotist wouldn't be able to do that because they're not inside my mind. But when I do it myself, I can talk back and forth very quickly to discern exactly what's going on. Of course, there are advantages to having someone else guide the process, but this is one advantage to doing it yourself.
