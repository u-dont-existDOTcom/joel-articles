Appendix D: Anxiety — body first

Remember that your anxiety is an expression of a Protector and it’s trying to protect you from something it thinks would be intolerable, like danger, shame, abandonment, failure, exposure, illness, rejection, loss of control etc. So don’t start by cross examining it. Start by:

Body > Inner Child > Inquiry > Action

Love to your inner child.

Try the hand on heart, hand on solar plexus practice.

Sincerely say “little one you don’t have to do this alone and you don’t have to figure it out, I’m here now and I’m an adult so let me take care of it. You can just let me take care of it.” Don’t just say the words expecting your subconscious to do something. You need to sincerely say it as if you really do have a little child there that you love and want to help.

Try reduced breathing (Buteyko style)

If you have problems with overbreathing and panic this technique can be powerful, but be careful with it at first as if you hold you breathe to create air hunger the anxiety can initially increase before it decreases so do it gently.

Try breathing through your nose and making your inhales small, your exhales quiet and then pause a little. The idea is to make your breathing quiet and less dramatic. Stop if it doesn’t feel right.
