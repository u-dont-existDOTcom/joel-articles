Appendix B: A Little Bit About Neurolinguistic Programming

Although you don’t need to know anything about Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP) to do this work, it’s useful to know a little bit more about NLP in order to make the process more precise. Also keep in mind that NLP can become twisted into a bag of tricks to influence people in an unskillful way. The method in this book is based upon the capacities for love, truth, contact, and consent.

There are four layers of NLP that are useful here.

Sensory NLP – how to change sensory data
State NLP – how to more fully access useful states
Strategy NLP – how to rehearse new strategies
Language NLP – how to pace reality, open up awareness, presume capacity in a safe way, link cause and effect, embed commands, and ask de-hypnotic questions to clear up vague, overly inclusive, manipulative, and grandiose statements.

The Milton Model of Language (Without the Secret Sales Seminar)

Often commands, like “Relax,” “Stop being so anxious,” or “Trust me!” simply don’t work.

One of the benefits of using indirect language is to bypass people’s knee-jerk arguments to protect themselves. It may sound a little sneaky, but often you DO want to get to a particular place with a person even if their present state doesn’t recognize that yet.
