i) Return fully. Then at a later suitable time deliberately start the practice again, trying out your shortcut to re-entry. If it works, then gradually you can make your longer induction shorter. If it doesn’t work, then you can just continue with your old way of getting into hypnotic experience. There is no need to worry about how many times you repeat the re-entry cue, about how deep you are, or about whether or not you reach a particular state.

ii) See if your re-entry cue is working – and that it doesn’t work when you don’t want it to – and that if you change your mind about using it, you can go back to the way things were. It is important that you are in control and not following the narrator, notification, or other trigger such as over-hearing the re-entry cue. The point is to get in faster, not that you can then deal with new material faster.

Never try this out when driving a car or undertaking any activity that needs you to be actively engaged, even if you do it with your eyes open and even if you use conditionality.

If you don’t want to use your re-entry cue any more, then simply don’t rehearse it and remove it from any recording or make it inactive in any app. You can return to the longer induction if you want to. Tell yourself when you are not in an altered state of mind that you are doing this. It doesn’t matter if the ‘cancellation’ sentence doesn’t break the association immediately, because if it persists and is unwanted or disorientating, then get help from a qualified person. When you are in an altered state of mind at a future date, that will be a separate decision for that time, because when the altered state of mind ends, so does the day.
