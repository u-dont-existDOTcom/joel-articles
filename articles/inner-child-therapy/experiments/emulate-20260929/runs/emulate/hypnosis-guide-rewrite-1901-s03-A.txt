You dissociate or feel unreal

If you find yourself drifting during your practice, open your eyes, move, touch something textured, name five objects you can see, or simply end your practice for the day.

If you become grandiose in your practice:

Remind yourself that you are going to have to go back to eating, sleeping, doing chores, being humble, and getting feedback. Tell yourself, “I have to be able to take what I am having here with me into sleeping, eating, letting time pass, being humble and getting feedback.

If you become too analytical in your practice:

Remind yourself that it is time to stop trying to make the practice better. Put your hands on your heart and solar plexus and say, “I am not here to do this. I am here to be in relationship with something much deeper than my surface mind. What can I do to make this practice more like a communion and less like a performance?”

Sometimes the answer is to be quiet, to rest, or to do something. Take the action that comes to you.

If you become too blissful or diffuse in your practice:

See if you can become more specific in the bliss. Ask yourself, “What is love asking me to do now?”

If you feel you should know the truth that is hidden:

Remind yourself not to pry. Tell yourself, “Let me know the capacity first, the content later.”

If you keep daydreaming:

Sit up and open your eyes.

“What real action is this fantasy replacing?”
