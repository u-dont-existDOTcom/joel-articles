CAUTION/PSYCHEDELICS: Neither Uridine nor 5-HTP are to be used as trip ‘enhancers.’ They are employed as part of a repair process, but due to their ability to rapidly alter the brain’s chemistry, can have an impact on your sensitivity to psychedelic medicines. 5-HTP, as a precursor to serotonin, is serotonergic in nature and should not be taken with SSRI’s, SNRI’s, MAOI’s, Linezolid, Lithium, Tramadol, Methadone, Fentanyl, Dextromethorphan (DXM), MDMA, St. John’s wort, SAM-e, L-tryptophan, etc. Be careful with 5-HTP and ayahuasca/harmalas due to harmalas MAO-A inhibitory properties and be very careful with iboga/ibogaine due to ibogaine/noribogaine’s interaction with serotonin as well.

Do not take 5-HTP the same day as psychedelics or near ayahuasca/harmalas or iboga unless specifically under medical review. Uridine on the other hand, while not serotonergic in nature, may impact dream states, visuals, cravings, tolerance, etc. It is not recommended to take Uridine Monophosphate (UMP) the same day as psychedelics and be aware that your previous psychedelic dosage may be too high if you notice a change in sensitivity.
