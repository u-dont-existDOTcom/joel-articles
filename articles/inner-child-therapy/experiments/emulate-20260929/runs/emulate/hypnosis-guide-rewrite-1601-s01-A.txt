Let the next response guide you

Having gone through induction and inner meeting, you’re now in a place where you can learn to respond effectively to what is happening, rather than relying on a speech to tell the other part what to do. Read some of the examples below, then try doing it without looking at the screen.

Identify something accurately, if you can, and make an invitation, maybe to do something small and harmless, or an acknowledgement, or to get to know more. Then acknowledge what happens, and wait for something else to come through - it could be sensation, image, gesture, feeling or words.

How would you say it in your own words?

I notice my chest tightened when I imagined asking for help...

As I do that, maybe I could give myself some time to find out if there’s anything you’d like to say, or anything you’d like to change, or anything you’d like to stay with?

That’s enough language for one invitation - now just listen. If it feels like it, you can use simple language like ‘What happens next?’ to find out what happens next, but if something like a feeling is developing, maybe stop talking and let it develop. Or maybe not - see what works for you.
