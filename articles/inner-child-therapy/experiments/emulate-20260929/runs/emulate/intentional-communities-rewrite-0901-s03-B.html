In fact, the Zapatistas caution against importing or exporting Zapatismo, that what is valuable is not their diagram but that they build, learn, and change their structure accordingly.

Also, they are not an example of the full path of removing the pricing of internal necessities, and the development of a common purse, and the eventual removal of money with the outside world. They use collective work to support their schools, clinics, government, and resistance, among other things. But they also sell products, trade, and have outside support. They are an example of how to remove major necessities and public functions from the market, and to govern those. They also are an example of the problems of depending on outside help, which can create gatekeepers and unequal relationships.

To celebrate the 10th anniversary of their autonomous governments, the Zapatistas held an Escuelita, inviting people to live with families and learn how they built their autonomy. The first Escuelitas had 1500 "students", subsequent ones had several thousand.

A possible failure mode of an escuelita is if the community ends up serving outsiders who pay to come visit them, instead of focusing on their daughter communities, and measuring their success by how many guests they get instead of by how well they are teaching others how to build the social and material system they embody.
