Stay awake when your mind likes to escape

Sit up, open your eyes, feel your feet.

Affirm: I am not going away, I am coming.

Look at something, feel your spine.

Am I escaping into daydreams for guidance, to work through emotions or out of avoidance?

“If it’s the first two, show me a course of action to take, if the third, help me enter the present and see what I am avoiding”.

If you find it difficult to feel still, if you want to go to sleep or find yourself escaping into a haze, you could use an induction that turns up the alertness. Try it with your eyes open. Create a movement with your hand, repeat it and say you are feeling more and more clear. Then stop the movement and see if you get an echo effect.

Try saying: “With your eyes open press your thumb and forefinger together, release them, do it again and feel more clear, once more and even clearer, now stop doing it deliberately and notice if you feel an echo effect in your hands or anywhere else or if it stops dead. Work at a level of alertness that feels right to you, sitting upright.”

You could use an induction with two hands:

Put your hands on your heart and on your solar plexus

Acknowledge the parts, e.g. nurturer in your heart, the part of you that feels vulnerable like a child in your solar plexus.

Breathe gently.

“Love above. Child below. Adult present.”
