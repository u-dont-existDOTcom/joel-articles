If it feels too hard to think of something you can do that will come from a warm place in your heart for your little one, start with something the Protector can do, and see if you can find the warm feelings for them later on.

Pick one thing. Make a plan about how you will do it. When will be a good time? What's the first step towards doing it? Who can help you do it? If you think there will be a tricky bit, practice what you will say. When you have had a chance to try it out, reflect on how it went. If you didn't do it, ask yourself if you were scared, or if you didn't know what you were aiming for, or didn't know how to do it, or if you were too tired, or if something else got in the way. If you did do it, notice if you felt better afterwards. Just doing it doesn't mean it was helpful, but keep trying the things that you notice keep your little one safe, or bring you closer, or help them to learn, or make them happy.
