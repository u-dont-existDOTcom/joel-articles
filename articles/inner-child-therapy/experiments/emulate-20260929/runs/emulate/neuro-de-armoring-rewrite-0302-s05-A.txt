As the armor decays, you will also encounter something many find frightening, yet is a beautiful byproduct of decay: Interoception, or the ability to “feel” your body again. Van Der Kolk talks about this a lot in his book, but in short you have conditioned yourself to be numb to your body so that you can remain in your head and avoid the pain you feel in your chest.

When the dopamine based armor decays, you will begin to feel your body again; your heart beating, your gut churning, the wind against your skin. Do not mistake this as anxiety. This is your body coming back to life. Enjoy it.
