Do you usually think in words?

If not, when doing this script, are you able to access words or phrases in your head easily, sometimes, rarely, or not at all (this does not need to be as if you can hear a voice talking)? Do you ever access words in your head, for instance when planning, remembering or reflecting on things or when upset? If you try, can you form words in your head, as if saying a sentence to yourself in your head?

Note: this is not about whether you feel things first at a particular time, but whether you always feel things first. It’s ok to not know! This isn’t a diagnosis, it’s just to help you pick the right script.

If you don’t think in words, wherever it says ‘say’ or ‘ask’ or ‘listen’ in the guide, please think of an alternative (you could say things out loud, write it down, use a recording, or even just do it without saying anything at all). Also, you do not have to talk in full sentences to your child or protector – just do whatever you can. There doesn’t have to be an image present instead! Remember, you can change things up as you go along if something isn’t working for you.
