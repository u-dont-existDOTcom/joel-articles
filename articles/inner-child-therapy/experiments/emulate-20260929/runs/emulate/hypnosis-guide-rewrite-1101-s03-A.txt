Some questions to ask yourself to get started:

What is it that you like? What do you like? What do you believe? What do you want? (without it being rebellious or complying to a certain group). Are you betraying yourself to be part of a certain group? What is the group that you are trying to be a part of? What arena is it that you are trying to prove yourself to be part of? What is it that you would like to try out, if no one would be able to be impressed by it? What relationships are good for you? What do you feel in your body?

What would be a good thing to try out for your inner child? (It has to have nothing to do with what would look good on your resume). Try out painting, dancing, gardening, learning a language, a sport, helping out for a good cause, or anything that you want – your inherited parent might tell you that you are not good enough at it immediately, but you have to tell your inherited parent that you are an adult now and that you are just trying to find out if you like it or not – it takes a few tries for your inner child to get over the humiliation of not being good at it immediately.

For your inner child it is crucial to be part of a group, as it would be like survival for a child. This is why people join families, religious groups, get into romantic relationships, join political tribes, spiritual groups, institutions, online groups, etc. When you are adulting you need to make sure that you are not betraying yourself to be part of a group, you need to find your own ground. This is one of the main issues to work on when you are becoming an adult. Listen to the voices in your subconscious mind – that is what this self hypnosis guide is all about.
