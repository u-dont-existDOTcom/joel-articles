WARNING: please read carefully Phosphatidyl Serine is good for people that have high cortisol, so if you find yourself with the symptoms below, then you probably don't have high cortisol and you should not take Phosphatidyl Serine as it reduces cortisol and its effects, and low cortisol symptoms are very similar to high cortisol!

You feel numb, flat, you have no drive, etc

You find yourself MORE wired when you take phosphatidyl serine (you have more racing thoughts, you wake up at 3am, you become more irritable, you get more stressed dreams, etc). This may seem odd but when your cortisol is too low your body has to rely more on adrenaline to keep blood pressure up and that will make you feel more wired and stressed.

If any of these apply to you, phosphatidyl serine is probably not a good supplement for you, so DO NOT increase the dosage if you are taking it, just stop taking it. If you want to be sure, then get a 4 point salivary cortisol test done to make sure that your cortisol is high before you take it. Some people also report low blood pressure, gain of water weight, and restless legs syndrome, all signs of low cortisol.

If you feel very wired and tired, you can't 'turn off', you wake up at 3am, etc then your cortisol is probably high and phosphatidyl serine is probably a good idea. If you feel numb, you don't have a lot of drive, etc, then your cortisol is probably not high and phosphatidyl serine will likely make things worse, so avoid it or get the cortisol test done before you take it.
