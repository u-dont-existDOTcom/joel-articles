“Free resource” is a term I borrow from Re-evaluation Counseling. It means basically how much resource you have that is free to use to deal with other people’s need, ie need you are carrying that needs to be dealt with.

Everybody has both. The question is the ratio between them.

There have been times when my need has been way, way, way up and my free resource very low. Good Lord, what have I been through? My first wife tried to kill me when she went psychotic, thinking I was her (sociopathic) father. She took my son from me. She tried to kill my mother. Her pimp (yes, she had one) followed my sister’s family to the point they had to move to Canada to get away from him.

And that’s just a start. So when I say people need to get a bit more stable, to do a bit more inner pl/ork before they come in to help build a community, it’s from experience. There was very little of me to give to others at those times.

A community can only take in as much need as they have free resource to deal with. If you’ve got a group of 8 people, say, and 2 of them are in need a lot of the time, that’s a big proportion of 8. But in a group of 80 it might be very small indeed.

It’s a question of having the resource to take people in, rather than just feeling for them. Taking people in when you don’t have the resource can often be damaging to both the existing group and those you are trying to take in.
