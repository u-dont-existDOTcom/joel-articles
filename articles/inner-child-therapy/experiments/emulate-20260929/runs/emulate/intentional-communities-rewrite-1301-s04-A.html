From Visitor to Member

The Farm grew very quickly, and lots of people were desperate, so they didn't have a lot of filters. It may be spiritually best to be open, but it's still a practical burden.

So, a membership process is good for everyone.

Lots of correspondence - make sure they've read the right stuff, know why you're doing what you're doing, doing the practices you're doing, etc.

Short visit - a week or two, they help out, but let's face it, most people can be charming for that long. Well done! 😅

Long visit - longer, when things get difficult, do they fold or do they respond well? Some people can even fake it for a few months, but at least you're gathering data.

Provisional membership - make sure it's clear what this means, how long it is, etc. Make sure the person recruiting the new member isn't in charge of the process.
