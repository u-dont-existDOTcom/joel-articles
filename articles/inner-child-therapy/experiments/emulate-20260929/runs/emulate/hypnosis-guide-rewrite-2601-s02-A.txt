EFT tapping

Do EFT. Basically you say out loud what you're afraid of, while tapping on your body. The evidence is iffy, but there are now 7 RCTs for anxiety disorders (found in a systematic review in 2025). EFT was better than no intervention in some of them, so be aware of it, but maybe don't rely on it over first-line treatments.

Say something like: “Even though this anxiety is here, I am willing to be kind to the part of me that is afraid.”

Do Somatic Experiencing . Get more info and a free intro session here: Somatic Guide . Try to be gentle with this and not re-traumatize yourself.

Ask yourself: “Where is the activation? Where is neutral? Can I move between the two slowly?”

Do Gendlin Focusing.

Ask yourself: “What is the felt sense of this whole anxiety?”

Don't answer from your head. Wait for a vague bodily sense of the whole thing.

Ask yourself: “What would the felt sense like me to know?”

Try out a word or phrase. Does it fit with the felt sense? Then examine the situation logically. Both can correct the other.
