In classic NLP this is the “Swish.” It’s the act of deliberately swiping one representation of self in your imagination for another. Instead of one heavy handedly replacing another, allow yourself to notice what is there, and with curiosity & playfulness notice what else could be there. See if you can hold both in view. See what your body is telling you. Then, if you’re choosing a new image or behavior, see if you can notice what will be involved in choosing that image, in the near future and in the far future.
