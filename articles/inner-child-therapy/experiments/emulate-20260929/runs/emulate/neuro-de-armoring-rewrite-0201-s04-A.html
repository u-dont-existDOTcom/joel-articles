Tool: Uridine Monophosphate What evidence is there that it is effective? Well, there isn't really any good evidence of the use of Uridine Monophosphate (UMP) for the treatment of DAWS, but there is some pretty good evidence that uridine, when combined with choline and DHA, crosses the blood brain barrier and is used as a precursor for building brain phosphatide, which is used for building synaptic membranes (Wurtman et al). In fact, DHA's ability to produce dendritic spines was increased by UMP in animals, and DHA+UMP+choline increased synaptic membrane components and increased synaptic protein in that study. Also, when aged rats were given UMP in their diet, they had increased potassium-evoked dopamine release (a measure of dopamine neuron function) and an increase in neurite outgrowth. So it's a weak extrapolation to humans, and largely anecdotal, but certainly plausible.

Scott has been taking choline (in eggs) and DHA+EPA (fish oil), and upon taking a normal dose of UMP, he found that after a day or so, he was again dreaming vividly, and his dreams included real memories from his childhood. His psychedelic tolerance seemed to go down, and his desire to take ketamine or LSD went down dramatically. So it's possible that the UMP helped regrow D3 receptors, though it's certainly not proven. If you're wondering whether you might be deficient in your ability to regrow synaptic membranes and dopamine receptors, it might be worth trying UMP to see if it's a strong signal for you.
