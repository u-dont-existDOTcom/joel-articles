As your armor decays, you will begin to feel something you may find frightening. Van der Kolk calls it Interoception. Put simply, you will begin to “feel” your body again. For so long, you’ve been numb, existing in your head to escape the hurt in your chest.

As your dopamine armor decays, you will feel your heart beating. You will feel your gut. You will feel the wind against your skin. While it may feel like anxiety, it is not. This is not a symptom of sickness. This is a symptom of returning to life. Welcome it.
