Make sure you choose a mediator or ombud to help you if you need it. It won’t do any good to call in a mediator at the height of the crisis, when no one knows the neutral stranger. No one will trust the mediator. Remember that mediation is a tool to help fix things, not to replace the need for proper evidence, proper safeguarding, proper adjudication or even proper separation at the end of the road if you’ve run out of consent.
