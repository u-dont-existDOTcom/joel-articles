And when a child comes forward, it’s important to not start healing them right away.

See if you can say, “Little one, I know this is hard, so I want you to know that I’m here. You don’t have to explain why you’re here, you don’t have to be healed, you don’t have to show me something impressive. I just want to know, what do you need to feel better connected with me?”

They might need warmth, play, protection, to grieve, to be angry, food, rest, a boundary, a safer relationship, less fantasy, less work, or an adult to follow through on a promise for a day.

Don’t rush, when you notice a younger part, protector, feeling or body signal that needs something, try:

preparing, inspecting, asking if they’d like you to come closer or if they’d rather have warmth, space, words, silence or no contact at all, helping with one thing they need, waiting, seeing if they now want to connect more (they don’t have to merge with you, go away, forgive someone or even be happy), resting and seeing if anything needs to be done differently next time.
