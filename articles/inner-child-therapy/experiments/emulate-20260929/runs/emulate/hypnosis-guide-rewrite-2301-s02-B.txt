Core prompts

Here are some general mantras/prayers you can use:

Clarity: “My wise subconscious, reveal to me what is most true, kind and timely. If what is most true is too intense to integrate, please convey the information as a symbol, body signal or simple next step. Show me how much I can be at peace prior to revealing the content.”

Resistance: “What are you protecting me from and what can help you trust me 1% more?”

Inner child: “What do you need and how can I support you?”

Protector: “What can I do in the real world to make this system feel safe?”

Leader/Guru: “What is most true, loving, in alignment with life and for the most good of all beings involved?”

Working with intuition: “Allow intuitive input, analytical input, body feedback and reality testing.”
