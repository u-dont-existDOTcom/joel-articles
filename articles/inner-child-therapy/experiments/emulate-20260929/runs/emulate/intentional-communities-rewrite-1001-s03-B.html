How are exchange & obligations between communities to be handled? Even if there is no money inside a community, there are still lots of types of transactions. These include gifts, common pool resources, loans, in kind exchange, mutual labor, emergency housing, shared building, & visits for training or building relationships. Not all of these result in obligations between communities. The federation should keep accounting for each of these purposes, but need not enforce parity or even put a price on each transaction. If cash is used for interfacing with outside parties, then the federation should also handle grants, loans, guarantees, & shared enterprises. It is important that solidarity not mean unlimited liability or unlimited access to the labor, stores, land, or tools of other communities.

When two rules come into conflict, a record of the conflict should be kept. The record should include the case, the principles in conflict, original judgments, final reasoning, dissent, consequences, & the version of the rules under which the case was heard. When the rules change, the case should be run again under the new rules. This helps different generations of people re-live constitutional crises.
