Creating an anchor (a doorway back)

An anchor is anything you can use to trigger a state. The most common anchors are physical: putting a hand on your heart, touching two fingers together. However, they can also be breath, a word, a posturing, a scent, a song… To anchor a state, find something in the real world that represents the state you wish to access. Feel that state, and while you are feeling it (make sure you know that you’re feeling it), use the anchor and say something like ‘this is the state that I want to be able to access easily’. To use this as a way of going back into hypnosis, follow the instructions for ‘shortcut back into hypnosis’ in the main induction section. Make sure you anchor a state that is both calm and alert, and that you use that as a way of going into a good, receptive state (see shortcut back into hypnosis) but that you also have something to trigger synchronicity that you know will wake you up.
