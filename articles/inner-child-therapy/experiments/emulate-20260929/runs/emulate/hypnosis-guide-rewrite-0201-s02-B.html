Do you think in words?

Before choosing a script, it may be helpful to know if and how you use words in your head. When you think, do you often hear words or phrases in your head (not out loud, but in your mind)? Do you ever, sometimes or rarely hear words in your head when you think? Do you ever hear words in your head when you are planning, remembering, reflecting, upset, etc? If you don’t naturally hear words when you think, can you form a sentence in your head if you try?

This is different from feeling first in a particular moment. You may not be sure, and that is ok. We are just trying to determine the best way for you to do this work.

Where it says “say” or “ask” or “listen” in this guide, you can choose to say things out loud, write them down, record them, etc. You do not have to “say” or “listen” to a sentence in your head, including when you are “talking” to the child or the protector. You do not have to see an image instead. Try the different options to see what works for you.
