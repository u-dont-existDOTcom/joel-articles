The Four Parts That Have to Pl/ork Together

The problem I see with most communities is that they end up focusing on only the part of the model that appeals to the founders. The political types write endless bylaws, the spiritual ones meditate, the therapists process and the psychedelics decide that everyone is God and to fuck the deed, man.

This model has four parts. Yes, I know it’s a gross oversimplification of a complex set of issues surrounding jealousy, children, land, money, god, iboga etc, but bear with me here. I’ve published very detailed how to guides on the first three, which you can read free on this site, no bullshit sales funnel, no woo woo retreat to sell. This article will mostly talk about the 4th.

Doing all of these for just food, housing and logistical support is not enough, it needs to be in service to something you find meaningful enough to make it worth how difficult it will inevitably be. A shared inner practice allows the community to be of service to each other’s healing from fear. An outward facing escuelita and federation purpose gives you something to do together other than just make sure that the people inside the community are happy.
