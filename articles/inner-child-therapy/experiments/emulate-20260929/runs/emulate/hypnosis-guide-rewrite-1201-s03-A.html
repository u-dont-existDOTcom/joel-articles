They are trying to make you do something after the session is over, by using words, gestures, blinks, sounds, listening etc.

They are using amnesia or inflating truth (dreaming, not remembering, imagery, body etc).

They are ending the recording, leaving you in a role, or preventing you from judging for yourself, or not allowing you to move normally, leaving you blank, or in some other way attempting to keep you in a changed state of consciousness indefinitely.

If you think that they are trying to hypnotize you, then look for ways in which you can make a real choice. Is there a way for the recording NOT to induce you, to be lighthearted, to use a different way of doing things, to allow you to be fully alert, or to stop, or to take a break? If you say 'no' then it MUST stop. Remember, just because the narrator is using a nice voice, it doesn't mean you have to agree to something if you don't want to. Saying nothing, or saying you're not sure, does NOT give your permission.
