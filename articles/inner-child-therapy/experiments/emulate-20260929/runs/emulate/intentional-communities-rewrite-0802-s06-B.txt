Make sure you discuss your values about raising children BEFORE you have them.

Just because you share a home, a culture, and even a bed, it doesn’t mean you will agree on how to raise children. Some of the most common conflict areas between parents: television and screen time, vaccination schedules, school selection, religious practice, food selection, discipline, medical practice, etc. Each of the 20 adults in this picture were raised differently, so it is not surprising that they have different ideas of how children ought to be raised.

Good will alone will not answer these questions. In order to provide a child with a coherent experience of the world, a community needs to agree on the basic principles of raising children, while still allowing flexibility for parents to be parents.
