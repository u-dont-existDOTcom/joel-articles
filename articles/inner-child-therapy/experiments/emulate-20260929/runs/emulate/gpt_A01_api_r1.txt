This song by Celine is named “On ne change pas”. Which means “we don’t change” And I believe it’s true. When we grow up, we do not stop being the child we were at the beginning. So many things that happen to us when we’re grown-up touch us as we were still children, and it makes us feel as vulnerable, as unimportant, as lonely, as helpless, as impotent, as impulsive, as scared, as incapable, as in despair, as in the need to find someone to pick us above all the others and to love us…
