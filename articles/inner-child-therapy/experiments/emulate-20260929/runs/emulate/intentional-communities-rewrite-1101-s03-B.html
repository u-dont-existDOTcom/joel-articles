Some of the standard signs for cult behaviour to watch for daily: Making the founder more and more important with time Seeing any disagreement as failure Implementation error as explanation for all bad results Accumulating insider knowledge Lack of public measures for results Moral contamination of leaving members

None of these on their own is a clear signal, but if many apply, you should be careful and listen to their explanations of why they are in a unique situation.

How can the model described here degenerate into a cult?

All of the things mentioned in this article can be abused.

“Nothing suppressed, all processed” can turn into forced disclosure. A danger, that I see in some communities, when they work with the ZEGG Forum.

Peer counseling can turn into peer surveillance, making medicine a prerequisite can make for nasty gatekeeper behaviour, “being willing to be seen” can lead to forced disclosure.

All the points mentioned above are guidelines for myself, not something, I can claim from others.

“Nothing suppressed” applies to myself, it is not an invitation for people to dig into my material. Members can decline to share without being forced to explain why in therapyse.

Working on ones medicine is not a prerequisite for membership. Not doing so will not limit your rights inside the community. Also there is not one single person, nor a close group of people, who can decide, who may enter and who may not. Objections have to be dealt with.
