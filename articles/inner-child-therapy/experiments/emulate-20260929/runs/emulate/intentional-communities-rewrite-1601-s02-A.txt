It’s good to point out that many groups have been meeting weekly for two years or more and are real communities even if they may never buy land. There is much healing of post divorce, illness, grief, bad parenting and loneliness that can happen before land is even considered.

If you’re reading this and you’re remote or constrained in some way, there is still a lot you can build just in the portion you have access to. Many simple ideas are available to help you build more wealth – weekly meals between two houses, exchanging counseling with a friend (even over video chat), sharing tools and services like child care, transportation, food prep, saunas, acro-yoga, dance improv, mother’s circles, cuddle puddles, etc. if you’re hippie enough.

There is a lot to be learned from committing to showing up for a few households reliably over six months.

What Can I Do This Month?

Here’s a good place to start for your first month:

Begin your inner pl/ork. You can start with our free Reparenting Guide and Hearthwork Manual , or whatever is serious for you.

Find 2 or 3 people to form a practice group with, to meet regularly with.

Some places you might find good people: Quaker meetings (unprogrammed), restorative practice groups, open-hearted religious congregions, mature recovery groups, meditation groups, people returning from long retreats who seem to be kind as fuck, etc.
