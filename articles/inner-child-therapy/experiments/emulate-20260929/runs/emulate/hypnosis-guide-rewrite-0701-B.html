Forget the left-brain/right-brain cartoon

The “syncing up the hemispheres of the brain” part is more poetic than I would want to claim as actual neuroscience. This large study of 1011 people using resting state functional MRI does not support the kind of pop neuro idea that you can have people who are globally more left or right brained. (Although of course particular functions and networks are lateralized.)

More about bringing your verbal, analytic, imaginal, emotional, somatic, intuitive, relational and spiritual processing modes into cooperative congruence.

When the loving mind and the analytical mind are joined in perfect harmony, the heart is warm and the mind is bright. When the intuitive mind and the rational mind are equally balanced, there is no dogma and no doubt. When the body is listened to, there is the wisdom of yes, no, and not yet. When the imagination is unlocked, the field of possibility expands. And when these are brought into the service of the mature, adult self, there is the peace of knowing exactly what to do next.

Without the analytical mind, the loving mind generates fantasy. Without the loving mind, the analytical mind is sterile. Without the analytical mind to test it, the intuitive mind is delusion. Without the intuitive mind to inform it, the analytical mind is dead.

If it doesn’t change you for the better, it’s meaningless.
