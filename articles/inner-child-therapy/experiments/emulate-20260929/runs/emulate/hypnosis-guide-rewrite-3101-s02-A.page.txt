Use rhythm: breath or a pulse. It can be any natural rhythm. Humming, tapping, sounds outside, or even counting in your head. If you don’t want to do a rhythm that’s fine too. You might also just repeat a short phrase like “here, and here again” to help you. If you use a rhythm you can let it go at the end and see what comes up first when it’s quiet again. Don’t try to be blank, just see if anything comes up, a word, a feeling, something you need to do. When you’re ready just go back to the normal rhythm again.
Use an object: Imagine all the bits of what’s in your head as if it were a ball of yarn or some bubbles and unwind it until it’s all there. You could also do this by knotting a piece up and putting it somewhere you can see. You don’t have to do anything to the object, just seeing it can be enough. You can describe it in words if that’s easier. Don’t use counting unless you’re putting the strands down. You’re not getting rid of anything, just seeing what you need to look after and what you need to let go, keeping what you need to keep. When you’re ready just go back.
Use a journey: Listen to where you actually are and feel your body. Then imagine walking to a place you choose. You could follow the sounds of your footsteps or make up scenery as you go or even talk to people on the way or at the end. Just make sure you know there’s no hidden meaning to anything you see. The idea is that you don’t know what will happen when you get there. When you’re ready just go back the same way and close the space when you’re finished.
