6. Using a gradual wave. Imagine a small neutral area of your body and notice what is already there. Imagine the wave moving to the next area, or keep the same area. Pause to notice the movement of the wave. Keep imagining that the wave is moving to the next comfortable area. Notice that it does not fix any medical condition, but can ask what that part of the body needs and how it could be helped. Then imagine the wave moving away and notice your whole body, and where you are making contact. Bring it back as needed.

7. Imagining a workshop for working on patient care. Prepare the time, the place, the tools, and boundaries that you will need. Invite a signal, feeling or younger state to come as close as they want to. Consider the language that you will use that will not fragment you. Ask what they need, wait, reconnect if welcomed, rest and reassess as needed. You may want to invite an adult state or borrow a function to help you. Choose your words, move at your own pace, take action as needed, with the goal of making the part good as you, not erasing its history. Close off the space and remind yourself that you are a whole person and that you can do this as an adult. Return.

8. Imagining yourself embodying a resource. Imagine what the resource is and make sure that you understand it. Imagine that you have one percent more access to that resource. You may want to just notice it instead of embodying it. Repeat as needed. Notice what you want to do, say, imagine or if you want to do nothing. Return keeping the resource and one thing that you want to do with it. Remind yourself that you are you.
