Why it’s critical to discuss child-rearing values before having kids

It is more or less inevitable that even couples who live together, who come from the same culture, who share the same bed, will have disagreements about how to raise their child: how much screen time is OK? Vaccinate or not? How to handle school, religion, food, discipline, medical care, etc. etc. Multiply that scenario by twenty adults who each bring their own experiences of childhood to the table.

Even if everyone comes to the table with good will, which is critical, the questions remain how much commonality is required in principles of child rearing for the community to have coherence, while leaving enough space for parents to parent.
