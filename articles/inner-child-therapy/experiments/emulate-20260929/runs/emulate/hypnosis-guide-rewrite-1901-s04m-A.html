Is there a way in which you feel divine love has not yet descended in the wound?

Sometimes when we work with a child it opens to love or freedom in a way you have never experienced with a child before. Sometimes when we pray we feel abandonment or condemnation come up, or when we open to a teacher we feel betrayed or damaged in a sacred way. What is needed to be questioned here? Why do you feel that more of the same is not the answer?

Sometimes practical things need to be done until your view of the horizon in life is greater. Sometimes you’re just curious how to be more happy without really believing it. Sometimes you realise others seem to have enough love and meaning in their life to do what they want to do. It’s ok to not make this a thing you have to do.

You feel stuck in endless processing.

It can be that you have a real wound but you also have a checking loop. Ask yourself if processing it in this way is going to allow you to feel, understand, decide or do something differently or are you just doing it to check to see if anything has changed to reassure yourself. It’s fine to process or do practices in a similar way when grief arises but you don’t always need to do it in a novel way every time.

If it is a checking loop, allow the thought to be there, don’t argue with it or try to make it go away, choose something to do or someone to relate to. You don’t have to have a meeting inside every time a thought comes up or do a certain thing to make it ok to get on with living your life.

Ask “What would the Leader do here?”

You keep doing hypnosis but don’t change anything in your life.

Ask “What would make your inner child realise you are real?”

Then do it.
