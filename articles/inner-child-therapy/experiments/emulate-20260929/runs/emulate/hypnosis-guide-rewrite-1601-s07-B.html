Finish without forcing a revelation

End: When you feel that you have received enough, end the process by not allowing yourself to raise any more questions. Then ask yourself what you can do to help you get back out and go about your day. Thank all the capacities that have participated in your work. Realise that something you do in your life may be important, it does not have to be an action.

Note: Your analytical mind is with you all the time and you can use it at any time. But put off using it fully, to examine what you have experienced in the light of facts and to make any commitments until you are fully back. Not all pictures you see are from your memory and not all changes/relaxations in your body are due to what you may have imagined.

After you have finished (even if you ended early) use the Come all the way back process to return fully. It may be useful to write a few lines about what has happened. In no case do you have to write anything other than what really came up. You do not have to include: a particular phrase, a body message, a little one, a protector, a boundary, a repair or a don't force it.
