To become a full member of the community, you get all the rights to land and other resources, but you are also subject to all of the responsibilities over the long term.

Regardless of what stage you are at, focus on actual behaviors, rather than vibes (“they use spiritual woo words” or “they talk like they went through therapy”). It’s hard to gather a lot of data in a short time; 6 months will help, but be especially careful and rely on actual behaviors (not vibes!) if letting someone have access to higher-risk areas like kids, large shared resources, medicine, cars, personal information, etc.
Before letting someone become a provisional member, make sure you have simulated as much as possible what it will be like when they move in. Have you made a real decision together? Have you done a work task or shared resource task together? Have you processed a conflict afterward?

All people who participate in the community (visitors, volunteers, workers, renters, interns, etc) should have political membership and all of the above, especially if the decision affects them (money, access to shared resources, etc). It is not okay to have a servant class and give them fewer rights than you have at your other commune.
