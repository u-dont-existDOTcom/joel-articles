What Connections To The Technosphere Do You Want To Maintain?

This isn’t even getting into the loneliness aspect, which I’ve written about here . And there are so many more elements of the polycrisis, which I’ve written about here .

I don’t know how much of the above is unfounded fear. Will AI make everything way better for me? Maybe! Will it take millions of peoples’ jobs? Also maybe! Will it end up being the administrative layer between me and literally everything in my life before I finish applying to all the communes Chantress Seba has told me to apply to? …Well, yes.

But it can’t hurt to be as human as humanly possible, right? If we don’t need to escape, having these skills and being part of a community will be great. And if we do need to escape, it’s a lot better to have prepared for it than to spend the next ten years optimizing your feed .

So, what connections do you want to maintain? And what do you want to limit?

Medical care, banking, phones, vehicle, school, supply chain, government ID, internet, energy, etc. etc. etc. It’s up to you to decide where the balance is between rejecting all technology (which can cause a lot of unneeded suffering) and being totally reliant on the outside.

The Amish are really good at figuring out which technologies to allow in their communities. Different groups make different decisions, but in general they’re really good at having a community-wide conversation about what technology will help or harm their community. Check out how they live, they might give you some inspiration!
