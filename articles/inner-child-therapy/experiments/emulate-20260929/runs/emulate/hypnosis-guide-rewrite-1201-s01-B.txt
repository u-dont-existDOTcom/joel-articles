De-hypnosis: wake up inside the influence

There are many benefits for learning self-hypnosis. One benefit is that it teaches you how you can be influenced by other people. You are being influenced all the time: by your computer, phone and TV screens, by advertisements, by politicians, by religion, by erotic imprinting, by charismatic people, by being excluded or included, by shame, by sense of urgency, etc. Sometimes that influence is good, like a lullaby, or a good prayer, or a good teacher, or a beautiful song, or love.

But if you feel pressured to do something urgently, to believe that you are special, that you might miss something, that you might be excluded, sexually pressured, shamed, confused, that you need to be rescued, that this is destiny, that you must obey, that this is a secret that you need to know, that someone is very sure of something very quickly, etc., then it’s possible that you are being manipulated.

Remember to stop, not to obey immediately, not to be rebellious by automatism, but wake up from other people’s influence.

Beware of the following sentences, when using hypnotic language patterns to manipulate you:

« When you admit… », « When you realize that I am right… », « The only reason why… », « A part of you knows that… », « Your higher self wants you to… », « Everybody knows that… », « If you don’t believe that, that’s because your ego is afraid of…»

How powerful it is, grammatically!
