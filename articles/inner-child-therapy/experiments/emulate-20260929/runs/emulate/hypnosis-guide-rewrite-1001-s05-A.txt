The Nurturer Part of the warmth comes from really enjoying your child, and not pushing away. Communicating the sense of “It is nice to spend time with you”.

“It’s ok to feel this” “I am here for you” “You don’t have to do anything” “You are not too much” “You can be small with me” “You can be angry with me” “You can be sad with me” “I am here and I will be here”

The Protector

The Protector steps in to make the warmth real.

“I am going to make this boundary” “I am going to walk away from this conversation” “I am going to stop texting the person that destabilises you” “I am going to eat” “I am going to get some sleep” “I am going to do x small thing I said I would do” “I am not going to let you deal with the consequences of being an adult”

A lot of the time inner child work fails because it is all decoration. The voice of the adult is not doing the thing that is destabilising the child, not eating, avoiding doing things, breaking promises not dealing with the consequences. And until you do, the child wont trust you.

If you can’t generate the warmth, start with the Protector. Do something to protect your child. Make it small. Reflect on if you did it. If you didn’t do it, reflect on why not. Was is fear, exhaustion, lack of skills, something getting in the way, was the plan just shit? If you did do it, reflect on if it was done just to get it off the to do list, or if it was actually necessary.
