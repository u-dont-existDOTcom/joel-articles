Forget the left-brain/right-brain cartoon

In general, the phrasing of “synching the two hemispheres of the brain” is something I use to describe the state, but I wouldn’t read too much into it in terms of literal neuroscience. In fact, a recent large-scale resting state fMRI study (n=1011) found that this kind of language doesn’t really reflect the science when applied globally to whole people even though certain functions and neural networks are lateralized.

In other words, we’re simply trying to bring all of the modes of human functioning (verbal, analytical, imaginal, emotional, somatic, intuitive, relational, spiritual, etc.) into a cooperative relationship with each other.

When this happens, the warmth of the loving mind is balanced by the brightness of the analytical mind; the clarity of the intuitive mind is held in balance by the reason of the rational mind; the wisdom of the body is activated; the creative power of the imagination is unlocked; and the adult takes charge of action in the world. An experience of peace comes together with a sense of precision.

This is necessary because the loving mind without the analytical mind can lead to fantasy, and the analytical mind without the loving mind leads to sterility. Similarly, the intuitive mind without testing leads to delusion, and testing without allowing for intuition leads to deadness. You get the idea.

If the state doesn’t transform you in some way, then it’s not that powerful.
