Appendix G: Altered states

This article is not encouraging the use of substances, but a lot of people do discover self-hypnosis, contact their inner child or have a dialogue with their Higher Self while in an altered state of consciousness.

The key is to not use it to force a revelation, but to help you love more and improve your life.

A lot of people have opened to a certain state of consciousness in the past and experienced it. The problem is that they can only access it in that state. The goal is to make it available to you in your daily life. Be aware that just because you've experienced it at one time doesn't mean your child can experience it now.

While or after you are in an altered state of consciousness, ask for help to:

Ground yourself Receive that which will help you integrate Remember the love Ask you to come back when it's time Help you apply what you've learned to your daily life in a significant way

Do not use self-hypnosis to keep you in an altered state of consciousness indefinitely, help you remember things, increase your fear, replace sleep, prevent you from getting needed medical care, or prevent you from communicating with others.

If you find that being in an altered state of consciousness prevents you from loving, grounding yourself, being truthful, sleeping, communicating with others or performing normal daily activities, it is time to stop and integrate.
