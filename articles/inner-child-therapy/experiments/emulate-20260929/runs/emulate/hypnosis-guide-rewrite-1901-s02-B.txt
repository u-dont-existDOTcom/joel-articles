You may find that your answer to the question ‘what do you need to know to trust me?’ is extremely un-mystical. It might be ‘you need to know that you will set a boundary,’ or ‘…that you will keep a promise,’ or ‘…that you won’t abandon us again,’ or ‘… that you will not just give us insight without doing something about it.’

The inner child won’t appear

If you can recognise a guard, talk to it. If you can’t, simply talk to the feeling or the preference you are experiencing.

‘I know that you don’t want to let me in, but I wonder what you do want to know to make it safe?’

If no child states appear, that’s fine. Work with what you do have. You can build identity and do reparenting work, even if you only identify one preference, curiosity or beginner.

You get anxious

The first thing to decide is whether the anxiety feels like you can cope with it and want to; or whether you need to do something to reduce it. If you feel it’s getting too much, building rapidly or is overwhelming you, stop what you are doing and find some ways to help your body feel better.

Put your feet on the floor. Sit in a chair. Breathe. Look around the room.

Talk to your little one and your protector. ‘I’m here now. Thank you for trying to tell me something. You don’t need to scream at me to get my attention. If there is something you need me to do in the real world, can you tell me what it is? If there isn’t, can you calm down and let your body relax?’

If your anxiety gives you chest pain, makes you faint, gives you suicidal thoughts, psychosis-like or mania-like symptoms, makes you very dissociated, stops you sleeping, or gives you urges to harm yourself or someone else, please get professional help.
