A useful map of the mind

In my guides here, I use the word "subconscious" to refer to all those things that are subconscious: perceptions, emotions, desires, avoidances, images, memories, body-signals, habits, intuitions and automatic behaviors, etc. Sometimes I refer to it as a single mind and sometimes I speak to the ecology of different parts. Both are just maps for the territory and neither map requires a priesthood to interpret it for you.

Sometimes we need to speak to the parts for precision and sometimes we need to speak to the Higher Self to establish coherence. Communion with both is important.

Here’s an example of speaking to the deeper mind as a whole:

"I invoke my wise subconscious to help me understand…"

Here's an example of speaking to the various parts of the subconscious mind:

“My child part that wants love, my protector part that wants safety, my critic part that wants to achieve standards, my fantasy making part that wants to live in a fantasy world, my body memory part, my social part that wants to fit in, my spiritual part that wants to connect to God or source or to benefit all beings and my wiser part that wants to know what is true…."

Sometimes we need to start from the perspective of unity and sometimes from differentiation:

"I invoke my Higher Self, my universal heart-mind, my wise subconscious to help me…."

"Is there a part that is afraid? Is there a part that is protecting you? Is there a part that is avoiding something? Is there a part that knows what you need to do? Is there a part that is too young to deal with this?"

Then, we always want to move back to the perspective of “all of this is part of one life” and to empower the adult self to lead that life from a place of love.
