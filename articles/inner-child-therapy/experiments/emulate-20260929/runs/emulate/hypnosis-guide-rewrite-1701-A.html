The 15-minute daily practice

It’s best if you just do the same thing, rather than try to learn a second way of getting into your subconscious. Sit back for fifteen minutes. It’s not a therapeutic situation where time factors are important, but fifteen minutes is a good time to set aside for yourself. A few minutes are needed to “get under” and a few more to come back up. If you need to at any time, you can interrupt the recording and spend five minutes just listening in silence.

An alternative is to become aware of three things you can see, three things you can hear, and three sensations in your body, then two of each, then one of each, and then to count from five to one and start listening.

Once you’ve done this a few times you can abbreviate the time it takes to get to where you want to be by abbreviating the induction – even to the point of not playing it back. But that doesn’t mean you have to throw the recordings away. You can also include the re-entry cue if you want to speed up the process of getting there.

When you come out of this experience ask yourself if there is anything you need to warm or protect, anything you need to direct towards your Truth, your Love and your Life. Is there anything you need to explore or build within yourself? Is there anything you can take with you? If there is something you need to do, make a very concrete plan to do it: “If _________ happens, then I will do ________.” Check back later to see how it’s working out for you in terms of sleep, relationships and general function.
