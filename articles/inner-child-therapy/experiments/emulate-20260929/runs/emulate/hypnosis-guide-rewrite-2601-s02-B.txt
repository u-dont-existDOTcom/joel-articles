Try EFT . It's weirdly simple, just a bunch of bodily motions you can do while thinking about and acknowledging the fear. The science for it isn't super rigorous yet, but this systematic review in 2025 found it to be used in 7 RCTs for anxiety disorders and to be better than no intervention in several. Not that it should be used instead of whatever first-line therapy would be recommended, but it's at least an indication that it might be worth trying.

Gendlin Focusing . I would start with something like "what is the felt sense of this whole anxiety?"

Don't answer from your head, but try to get a vague feel in your body for the whole thing. Then, "what would this felt sense like me to know?"

Try a word or phrase and see if it fits the felt sense. Then be careful and examine whether that word or phrase is actually true about the situation. The idea is that both can help correct the other.
