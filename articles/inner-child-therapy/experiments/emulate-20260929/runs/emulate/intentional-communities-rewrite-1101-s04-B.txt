“Your resistance is just your wound talking” is one of the best ways to stifle an argument in a community, and if anyone says this to you, you should immediately be thinking about how to get away from that community before they decide to hold a group process about how you’re leaving.

There should be a level of basic bodily safety, access to food and sleep, ability to get medical care on your own, ability to have your own thoughts, ability to talk to people outside the community, ability to report when you or someone else is being hurt without involving the friends of the person who is hurting you, and ability to leave that cannot be voted away by a majority.
