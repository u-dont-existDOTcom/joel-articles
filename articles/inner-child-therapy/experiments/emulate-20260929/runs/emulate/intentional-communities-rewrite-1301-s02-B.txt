Please make sure to choose your values first!

So often we end up choosing communities like we were choosing a vacation rental. Because the land is beautiful, or there’s a pond, or everyone seems so nice when you visit.

But the pond’s gonna be there when you start trying to date someone in the community and find out that you have to bring documentation that you are fully vaccinated.

So before you choose a community to visit, write down your values. What do you believe about children? Money? Property? Romance? Medicine? Food? Spirituality? Technology? Privacy? Dealing with conflict? Leaving the community?

Also take into account sexuality and gender: is it ok for people to be LGBT? Would it be ok for LGBT people to be in relationships and be part of the community? Do the community’s members feel that it is important to question their sexual identities when they are young? What would happen if one of the children of the community grew up to be gay or transgender?

Also, it’s ok to have “we should try out new things all the time” as one of your values. Make sure you include that if that’s you.

Then go meet a community and see how their actions and words match up with your own values. Don’t forget to ask about conflict, decision making, what happens when people want to leave, and who owns the land.
