What not to do

The subjects: voices, pictures, spontaneous body movements, body signals and such do not represent reality. They do not need to be obeyed. Reality test.

Do not embark on a quest for large downloads or to figure out which demons, jinn, or other Unattached Burdens are present in your system until you have stabilized and gotten assistance if you are having trouble reality testing or functioning.

Do not use self hypnosis while driving a car, bathing, swimming, operating machinery, watching small children or engaged in any activity where you need to be actively involved in the activity.

Do not use self hypnosis to force memories, go into a trance to see if something did or did not happen, or to suppress medical symptoms.

Do not use self hypnosis to override normal body signals such as hunger, fatigue, pain, and normal emotional states such as grief, anger, and fear.

Do not stay in an altered state of consciousness for extended periods of time, nor assume your intuitive thoughts while in an altered state of consciousness are reality. Reality test.

Do not let one part of you take over: the child part of you is not in charge, the Protector of you cannot keep love out, the Nurturer in you cannot negate the law of cause and effect, the Leader of you cannot function without the child part, etc. etc. You have all of these abilities and more, so use them all.

Do not use NLP techniques to override Protectors, convince yourself of something you are not sure of, manipulate others, or to heal your body when your body is telling you it cannot be healed at this time.

Do not assume you are being induced into a trance if you experience doubt, distraction, resistance, no response to the induction, or if you find yourself not wanting to do what you are being told to do.

Do not assume that your unconscious will always keep you safe. Take safety precautions.

Do not assume that if you feel something in your body that you have suffered a trauma, are ill, toxic, possessed, attached or that you are changing, or that what you feel is coming from your subconscious mind.

Do not assume that if you imagine your body healing that it is healing on a physical, neurological or cellular level, or that any illness has been cured.

Do not allow yourself to be given covert commands, activated by a third party without your knowledge, compelled to do something you do not wish to do, forced to switch to another identity or have amnesia without your conscious approval. If you choose to give yourself a key word, phrase or other activation, make sure it is something you can use at will and that it serves a good purpose for you. You may change your mind at a later date about the activation, so make sure that you can review it and negate it at will, though simply saying "cancel" may not be enough to remove it.

If you are awake at the end of a session, make sure that you return to your normal sense of self and tell yourself that the session is over.
