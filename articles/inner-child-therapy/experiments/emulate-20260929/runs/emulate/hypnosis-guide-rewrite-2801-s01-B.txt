Appendix F: Self-trust, outside guidance, and oracle-like states

See Synchronicity: Using It As A Cue Above

How susceptible you are to different forms of suggestibility varies enormously. You may be more susceptible to other-led hypnosis, self hypnosis, in certain states, or you may not be very susceptible to commands but very susceptible to imagery, music, metaphors or spiritual connections. Having someone else there can be very useful for many reasons, but having yourself there is useful for other reasons.

So, while I believe self hypnosis is great for interacting with your subconscious on a daily basis, having someone else there who is very skilled at what they do is the best for dealing with complicated trauma issues, blindspots, dissociation, destabilisation and relational issues.

How To Find A Hypnotist Who Will Be Good For You

Ask, ask, ask! Just because someone has qualifications and training doesn’t mean they are good at what they do, particularly if you need someone who can deal with trauma. Make sure they have the training you need, not just training in hypnosis but training in trauma if that is what you need.

Talk to them about what you need, what has worked for you in the past, what hasn’t, how direct you like them to be, what types of things you don’t like (touch, imagery etc) and how you would prefer them to stop you if you needed it. Ask how they deal with situations that might come up for you. Even if they use very firm encouragement to carry on with what they need to do, they should always stop if you say you want them to.
