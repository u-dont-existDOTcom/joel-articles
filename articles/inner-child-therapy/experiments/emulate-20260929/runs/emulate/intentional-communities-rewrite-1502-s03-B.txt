It's a Western thing to need to move from isolation towards tribe. But in a lot of African cultures, the movement is more the opposite! So when you do find Africans who are into this scene, it's likely they've been alienated by their culture/traditional village life and moved to the city but are then looking for a way back "in."

More generally though, I think that this is definitely a global thing and not a "Western lifestyle" thing. Go to any village that is still living in this way and soak it all up! Just be respectful. They are not there for you, go to help them, to learn their language, to be there with them. Don't look for them to give you a spiritual service.

As for process, it's quite cheap and easy. It did take a little work for me to get in to Sénégal but in the end it was pretty straightforward. You need to know French, ideally Wolof too, not so much English. And be ready for immense amounts of Teranga (that's Sénégal for "hospitality") - I live in a house with my good friend's whole family, they won't even let me pay rent. Like anywhere, there can be lots of watchfulness, social backwardness, bureaucracies, etc..
