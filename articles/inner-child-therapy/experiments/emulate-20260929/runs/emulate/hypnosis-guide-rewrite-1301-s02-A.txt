Hypnosis Scripts -- Writing Your Own 1. Have a purpose in mind, and pick one starting anchor or image that supports that purpose, and one way to get back. Keep it simple! You do not need all the trappings of floating down stairs thru gardens into inner rooms, talking to an inner child in colored light, etc. If you decide to change images, do it consciously and choose a new image to support your purpose. Ask yourself: "Do I need to be warm? Firm? Directing? Neutral?"

2. Imagery is not required - it is a signal mechanism, but the signal can be carried by many senses, rhythms, movement, words, spatial sense, simple knowing, no response, etc. Be sure to use the same "language" to come back, and make sure your counting is meaningful in the context of your chosen world. Don't let yourself be influenced to the point of losing your own-ness by the "qualities" you invite in.

3. Choose the following carefully, and read your script out loud to yourself to see how it sounds. Long, arty sentences are great in writing, but turn to soup under induction. a) one central verb b) one sensory quality c) one short phrase to use as a refrain d) phrases to use for transition

4. Before you start

a) read the induction and "Come all the way back" parts while awake b) decide what you want to explore c) decide what you want to leave alone d) decide how long you want to spend with this e) remember - you can ALWAYS bring in love, play, confidence, creativity, communion, etc. f) make sure you give yourself enough time to come back and reflect on the experience
