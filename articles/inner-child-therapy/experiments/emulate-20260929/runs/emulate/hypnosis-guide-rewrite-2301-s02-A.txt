Core prompts

Here are some ideas to try…

For clarity: “Hey wise subconscious of mine, tell me what you will. If it’s too intense for me to integrate, symbolize it or give me a signal in my body or tell me what to do next to get more clarity. Just show me how much I can integrate and still be at peace with myself, before you show me what needs integrating.”

When you come up against resistance: “What are you protecting me from? What can I do to gain your trust by 1%?”

When speaking to your inner child: “What do you need and what can we do about it?”

When speaking to a Protector: “What can we do in the real world to make this protection system feel safe?”

When speaking to a Leader/Guru: “What is the best way to move forward in truth, love, life and in the benefit of all?”

When exploring intuitions: “Let me get the intuition, then analyse it, get some body feedback about my analysis and then test it out in reality to see if I’m on the right track.”
