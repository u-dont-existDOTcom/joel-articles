Practice Comes Before Property

Too often we start with the land and put all our eggs in that basket, because that’s the thing that requires money, and we don’t have the emotional readiness. This often means that the people with the money get to own the land and set the vision of the project in writing (or at least legally binding documents) before the group has had a chance to fully evolve.

First we need to do the work – the practice, the processing, the getting clear on values, figuring out how to handle membership and ownership and exiting the project, etc. etc. Then we can dream about the land.

Similarly, we need to think in stages for our economic liberation. First we might form a cluster and take some of the basic stuff out of the marketplace, with people who earn outside putting in a certain amount. Then we can move to a common purse, where we are able to provide for all the members from that purse and fairly distribute the work of provisioning the common. Then we can move to less outside money, or no outside money, if that’s what we want. But it doesn’t help to just say “money is impure” and we shouldn’t touch it.

If You Can’t Move

A lot of us are stuck – jobs, kids, pets, debt, mortgages, sick parents, etc. But there’s still a lot of pl/ork we can do.

Sometimes it seems like people are trying to move their unprocessed selves to 40 acres.

Even if you can’t move, there’s a ton of inner pl/ork you can do – peer counseling, reparenting, somatic work, processing jealousy and conflict, etc.
