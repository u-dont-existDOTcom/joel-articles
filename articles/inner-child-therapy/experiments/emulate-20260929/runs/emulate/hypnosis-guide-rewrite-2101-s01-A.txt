If you think you need more support than that

The key to a successful practice of self hypnosis is to consider it a relationship, and approach it the way you would any relationship where you are in need of care.

Get help now if you are in danger or have significant disorientation, physical symptoms, new or escalating suicidal ideation, severe insomnia, mania or psychosis symptoms, or debilitating dissociative symptoms. There are lots of other things to try, and you don't have to wait 4 to 12 weeks to see if this works.

If things are OK otherwise, start out with shorter sessions, be warm with yourself, pick something you can easily warm up to, and make sure you come all the way back out at the end of each session. Then, over a week or two, keep track of a particular thing you want to influence, such as anxiety, daydreaming, relationship repair, dealing with something you've been avoiding, sleeping, emotional regulation, intuitive accuracy, etc. If you find your ability to function is deteriorating or you are becoming more certain of memories you can't check out, re-evaluate.

Get some other appropriate help if you are dealing with recurring panic attacks, compulsive behaviors, substance-induced instability, or major relationship instability.
