Releasing from resentment without spiritual bypassing or living in offense forever.

Where do we start when it comes to forgiveness?

What happened? What was the impact? What boundaries do you need? What got this person to this place? What is theirs? What is yours? How do we bring them to accountability? What does release look like without denial?

What about forgiving yourself? Remorse. What did you do? What wasn’t yours to do? Remorse is good; it shows you did something you shouldn’t have done or violated your own code of ethics. You have to be able to acknowledge what you did and not make yourself a criminal for doing something wrong. Take responsibility for your part, feel the remorse, do what you can to rectify the situation or make sure it won’t happen again, and release yourself. Responsible. Remorse. Rectify. Release.
