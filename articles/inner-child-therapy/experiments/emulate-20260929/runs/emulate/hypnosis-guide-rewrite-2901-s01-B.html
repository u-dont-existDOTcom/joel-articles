Appendix G: Altered states

Please note this does not encourage the use of substances, but many of us have found self-hypnosis and contact with our inner children and Higher Selves when in altered states of consciousness.

Please don’t use altered states of consciousness to force revelation, but to enhance your ability to tap into love and to enhance your life.

Please be aware that while you may have experienced an opening to these realms in altered states of consciousness in the past, this does not mean that you need to get into that state in order to tap into it. While there is a difference between opening to this love, being able to access it only in a certain state of consciousness, and being able to tap into it in day to day life, please be aware that the experience does help to open you to what is possible. If you are not able to tap into it, please be aware that going to a deeper place may not help if your inner child is not able to accept it.

When you are in an altered state of consciousness or afterwards, ask for:

Help to be grounded. Help to only take on that which you can integrate. Help to tap into this feeling of love at all times. Help to get back into an altered state of consciousness when it is time. Help to translate what you have learned into one actionable change in your day to day life.

Please don’t use self hypnosis to: Keep you in an altered state of consciousness indefinitely. Help you to remember things. Make you more afraid. Help you to not need to sleep. Help you to not need to take medical treatments. Help you to not communicate.

Please stop using self hypnosis to: Make you less loving. Make you less grounded. Make you less honest. Make you need less sleep. Make you less able to communicate. Make you less able to fulfill your normal responsibilities.
