Serotonin Bottleneck Pattern: 5-HTP + Low-Dose P5P

Warning: only attempt this if you suspect low serotonin. Signs of low serotonin include insomnia (specifically lack of sleep initiation), depression (specifically low mood and low warmth), nighttime anxiety, rumination, etc. It's also helpful if you have genetic or clinical data suggesting low serotonin (Scott specifically has genetic data suggesting a bottleneck at the TPH2 enzyme, which produces 5-HTP). Don't try this if you are prone to agitation or mania, or if you are already on serotonergic medications or psychedelics/entactogens.

5-HTP is a more direct precursor to serotonin. It avoids a conversion step (tryptophan -> 5-HTP). There is a good general 5-HTP review here , which explains serotonin biology as well as the uses of 5-HTP for mood, anxiety, sleep, obesity, myoclonus, serotonin toxicity, etc. The data for its efficacy for depression or sleep is mixed and weaker than many internet sources would suggest.

Scott takes 100mg of 5-HTP sublingually at night, and 10-20mg of P5P sublingually in the morning. P5P is the active form of Vitamin B6, and is a necessary cofactor for the enzyme aromatic amino acid decarboxylase. (Note that Scott is genetically slow-COMT, so is very sensitive to P5P, so he requires low doses.)
