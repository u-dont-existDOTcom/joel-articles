From Visitor to Member should be Slow

At The Farm, we grew up really quickly. Lots of people came to us because they were in need, and we didn’t have a lot of filters. We felt it was more open spiritually, but there was a practical burden.

A membership process is nice for everyone. It makes sure the people joining are going to be able to receive what they need from the group.

Correspond - are they reading? Do they get the vision? Are they doing the practice?

Short Visit - Have them come out for a week or two. They can usually be charming for this long. Congrats!

Long Visit - Have them come out for a longer visit, where they will be a bit annoying. You will be able to see how they deal with conflict, attraction, disappointment, boredom, etc. Some can still be pro-fakers for this length of time. 😅

Provisional Member - What does it mean? How long is it? How do you become a member? Make sure the person who invited them isn’t the only one who can make that decision.
