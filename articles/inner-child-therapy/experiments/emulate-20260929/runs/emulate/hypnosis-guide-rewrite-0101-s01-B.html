How to use this guide (this is a big one)

Learn one induction and the entire return in the Quick start section. Use “Let the next response guide you” for the part that can’t be recorded. Then go to the sections about doing it daily or weekly for some ideas about how to make it your own. There’s a lot of extra stuff in the appendices which you may want to check out to answer questions you may have, or for extra tools to add (NLP language patterns, how to use it for anxiety issues, using daydreams and other altered states, what to do if you encounter a hostile presence, learning to sleep better, influence, and more inductions). All sources are listed at the end in Appendix K.

Introduction

Hello Lovey Bunny!

You were born with a brilliant, fully functioning mind, capable of dreaming, protecting, analyzing, imitating, loving, remembering, hiding, revealing, healing. All its layers just weren’t communicating with each other.

Instead of letting it develop naturally, we tried to push through, not feel our emotions, pretend we were confident, follow or not follow authority figures, pretend we were grown up. In the meantime our deeper mind was running its own programs and all these different parts of us weren’t communicating - our childlike self, our guard dog, our intellect, our body, our intuition, our fantasy life, etc.
