One of the things that distinguish a good guide from a bad one is that a good one will add to your adult self; a bad one will replace your adult self with something else. Another is that a good guide will work with your ‘no’, whereas a bad guide will see your ‘no’ as resistance, ‘ego’, ‘fear’, ‘low vibration’, ‘trauma’, ‘sin’, or as evidence for why you need more guidance.

Hypnosis – running an audit

Are you being told how the induction mechanics will work?

Are there ways that you can ‘step out’ (either to a point where the induction is paused and/or modified, or in a way that brings you to full alertness and allows you to cease the induction)?

Is it suggested that ‘not noticing’ is evidence that something is working (and, as such, secret)?

Are tactics being used to lower the capacity of your adult self to make decisions (role-play, downloading new ‘ownership’, obedience exercises, introducing a ‘child’s perspective’, suggesting a new ‘identity’ or ‘belonging’)?

Are the effects of fractionation, cues, or other impacts on your subsequent behaviour/memory etc been described to you beforehand?

Are ‘body sensations’ being used to download ‘meanings’?

Does the exit return you to your normal identity, and also allow you to move, orient yourself, return to critical etc, and make it clear that the induction has ceased?

If in any of these cases you’re not sure of the answer, you can always ‘step out’ to full alertness and either ask for a script, modify the induction, or cease it completely.
