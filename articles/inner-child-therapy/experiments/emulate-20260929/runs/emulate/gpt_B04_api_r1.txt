What if you find yourself still at work at 7pm on a Thursday? Well, think about why - did you get distracted by "one more quick thing", were you afraid of what people would think, or did you simply feel too tired to leave? If you did leave on time but didn't feel better, that doesn't mean that leaving on time didn't work - and vice versa. If it works for you and you get to spend time with your little one, keep doing it!
