Troubleshooting

Nothing happens

Or start smaller:

Show me one body sensation? Show me one word? Is today listening or resting?

It feels fake.

First see what’s actually fake, the words, image, expectation, or your stepping into a role. If it’s the first 3, and you’re not seeing/hearing/etc an inner voice or image that’s not you resisting or not having adult capacities yet. Just correct what’s there that you explicitly don’t want. If you suspect you it is resistance or a part protecting you, talk to them.

What are you protecting me from? Are you thinking I’m going to lie to myself? Do you think this is fake, that I’m playing a role?

Often you’ll find the voice is a parent you’re inheriting and it’s trying to enforce rules about how you fit in. Don’t argue with them just find out what rules they are enforcing and then work on making your adult voice more believable to them with normal life evidence gathering.

If it is that you don’t have the adult capacities you need yet, stop demanding to have an inner voice that believes it. If you have observing capacities borrow the ones you need from someone, real or made up, that has them and try acting on even one line/ action and see how it’s received.

Your subconscious isn’t going to answer you?
