Many people think that they can simply start working with their inner child.

Only, when they try, they are numb or cynical or they get angry or hungry or they feel the need to check their phone or they get sexual urges or they start planning or they fall asleep or they dissociate or they think that it is stupid.

One of the most important protectors is our inherited “parent”. This is not the inner adult which we will create (the nurturing inner adult, the protective inner adult, the leader/guru inner adult), this is the voice we internalized as a child from our parents. Most of the time this voice is like a harsh critic or a warden in a prison, telling us that we are not allowed to cry, not allowed to take care of our needs, not allowed to be “a pain in the ass”, not allowed to be independent, etc. Even when we start taking care of our inner child, this voice can tell us that what we are doing is stupid and not real. How to work with this warden/critic?

“You, I see you and I want to know you. What are you afraid will happen if you move aside?”

Then wait for the answer. “You will fool yourself”, “it will be fake”, “the pain will destroy you”, “you will use spirit to escape”, “you will forgive, you fool!”, “you will think that your fantasy is a guidance”, “you will abandon us” etc.

“Ok, I understand. You don’t have to move, but what could make you trust me a bit more? Just 1% more.”
