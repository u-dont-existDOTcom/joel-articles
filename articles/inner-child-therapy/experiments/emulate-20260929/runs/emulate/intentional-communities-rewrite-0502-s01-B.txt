The trust is different than it is when you pay for it. When you pay for support it’s a sexual experience with strangers (usually). Sometimes that’s okay, but often the person with the feather has only known you for 4 hours and has already put together an entire cosmology to explain you.

When you do it within a community, you can have a sober sitter who has known you for years and who can watch and hold you in a way that is different. This is especially important in longer, medically risky experiences like iboga, where screening and sitting is a must.

There are peers there to reality check your downloads. Psychedelics can give you deep insight, or they can give you nonsense. It can be hard to tell the difference in the moment. When you have friends who know you well, it can be easier to tell what is insight, and what is a new way to relate to your mom.

There is a community to create healthy timing around the ceremonies. Many traditional forms of these medicines involve the community, and the timing of ceremonies. This can make it easier to not do them too often, and to integrate.

It is easier to make arrangements, like trusted child care, transportation (no driving bitches), support, water, etc.

There are people there to play with! How often do you go to a rave and end up being extremely intimate with someone you just met? Do it in a community where you already know people, listening to ecstatic music and dancing your freaky dance. 💃🕺
