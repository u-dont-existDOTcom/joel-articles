Signal-Dial (GluD Receptors): Recently, a group at Hopkins discovered GluD receptors in the brain that serve as a signal dial, regulating background levels of “hum.” While NMDA receptors are turned on by voltage spikes, GluD receptors are critical in setting the level of tone. When GluD receptors are armored, the signal dial may be stuck at “High” (in conditions like Cerebellar Ataxia) or “Low” (in conditions like Apathy or Schizotypy).

Do you think D-serine could help in memory updating in a therapeutic setting? I thought it acted on NMDA receptors to help with learning, but I see here that it also acts on GluD receptors to regulate tone/activity at the synapse. The form of D-serine that has been studied is D-cycloserine. It appears to have some merit as an augmenter of exposure therapy due to its ability to facilitate NMDA-receptor dependent memory consolidation. But it appears to facilitate whatever learning you’re doing, so if a session goes badly it could make things worse. If the session ends with fear reduction, though, it may help consolidate the extinction learning. D-serine may have the same effect.
