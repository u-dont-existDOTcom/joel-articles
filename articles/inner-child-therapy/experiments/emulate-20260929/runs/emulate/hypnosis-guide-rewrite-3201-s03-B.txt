Inner speech and putting experience into words

A recent study did not focus on adapting to self-hypnosis, but simply found that there are people who report inner speech and those who don’t, and that people differ in how much they report it. See Not Everybody Has an Inner Voice by Kaspar Nedergaard and Gary Lupyan (2024)

Memory, support, and choosing a practitioner

See chapter 7 in Trauma and Memory by Elizabeth Halligey Levine (2015) for a discussion of the difference between “compelling experience and historical verification” of memories. The US Department of Veteran Affairs has an overview of hypnosis that includes cautions about strong reactions and false memories. The American School of Clinical Hypnosis has an explanation of how they certify practitioners that illustrates the difference between certification and competence. The links we have included in the sections on evidence and finding a specialist are specific to the claims made on those pages and are not intended to be a general endorsement of hypnosis.
