I would say it’s not good as new, but good as you. Maybe you want to feel more supported and connected than you do now, but still be you. Also, if you’re uncomfortable with the parts language, you can reframe it however you like. Just think of it as parts of you that act a certain way, or signals, or feelings, or think of them how you want. Just whatever makes it feel less split-y.

How do you play or rest while still having an ‘adult’ there?

There’s a lot of different ways to do this, but I would say, for reparenting, it’s more like playing how a younger state would want to play. If they’re interested in a certain texture or drawing or moving or whatever, you can provide that for them. You can talk to them in a way that feels interesting to them, maybe use a plushie or something and change your voice a bit, etc. The important part is that you keep your reality testing. Remember that you’re not actually a child, or a pet, or a doll, or anything like that. You’re not being owned or anything like that. For resting, it’s more like giving yourself permission to not have to make decisions for a certain amount of time. Put off any unfinished tasks that you can see and have the ‘adult’ part of you watch over you.

How do you have a dialogue with your non-dominant hand?

If it’s hard to visualize or say out loud and it feels fake, you can always write it out. Have your non-dominant hand talk for the child part or protector part, even if the handwriting comes out bad. Then answer with your dominant hand as the adult part. For example:

Non-dominant hand: I don’t trust you.

Dominant hand: I’m sorry you feel that way. I know you don’t trust me, but I still love you. Tell me how I’ve let you down. I won’t ask you to trust me, but I will listen to you. I will also make a promise to you and fulfill it today.

Non-dominant hand: What will you promise?

Dominant hand: I will make sure we eat, clean off our desk, and not text that person that makes us feel unsafe.
