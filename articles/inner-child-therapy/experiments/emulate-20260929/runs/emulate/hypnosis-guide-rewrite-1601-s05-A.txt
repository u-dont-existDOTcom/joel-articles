Work with the impulse. If there is an impulse to turn, reach, push, or change posture, explore acting on it, by making a small voluntary movement that feels physically comfortable, or just imagine acting on the impulse. You don’t need to make yourself shake or “release” anything. If a movement creates pain or symptoms that worry you, stop making that movement.

Offer what you are offered.

If you feel younger, offer yourself company, but don’t try to make yourself literally younger, and don’t try to figure out why you are young. If you recognize a protector, listen to what it has to say, but don’t try to make it go away. Nothing is going to be exiled from the meeting.

See if you can say, “I am here for you. Would it be ok if I were here with you? Or would you rather I gave you some space?”

If you are told not to come closer, don’t come closer. Notice what it is like to receive the answer you asked for. If your Nurturer speaks too softly, raise his voice. If your Protector doesn’t know how to set a boundary, set it for her. If your Leader doesn’t know what you want in life yet, tell him. Just do the one function that isn’t available to you yet. Except for the Witness, don’t start over; just do the step if it isn’t already done.
