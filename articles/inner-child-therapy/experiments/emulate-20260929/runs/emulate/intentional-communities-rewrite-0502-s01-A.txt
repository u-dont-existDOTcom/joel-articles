Some of these advantages are not present if you just buy your support. Paying someone is not exactly the same as trusting someone. Unless you trust the person you're paying, you're essentially paying a stranger to have sex with you for one night. Sometimes that's OK, but sometimes you get a guy with a feather who thinks he has a comprehensive cosmology despite having known you for only four hours.

If you're going to use a sober "sitter", they'll probably know you if you're doing it in community. It's different to be watched (and held) by someone who knows you, especially for longer experiences which may be medically risky (iboga etc). This is partially because they can screen you, but also because they're watching you the whole time.

People you know can help you reality check your downloads. Psychedelics give you both insight and nonsense. It helps to have peers who know you around to help differentiate.

Community helps you time your ceremonies. Many "traditional" forms of using medicine involve using in a community setting at specific times. This prevents you from using too much, since most of us humans can be compulsive.

Community makes it easier to arrange for things like childcare, not having to drive, having water, someone to sit with you afterwards, etc.

Finally, you get to play with other people. A lot of raves etc. suffer from the fact that people try to be intimate with other people when they don't actually know them. In a community, you can have ecstatic experiences with music and dancing with people you actually know. Plus it's just more fun to dance with other people. 💃🕺
