Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community

Don’t buy the land until you have:

At least gone through one “real” conflict as a group, together.

Not a “we are all one and there is no conflict, we are just a group of people in a vision!” or “we’re all cool, we’ll work it out”. But a real “Wow, when you did this and said that, I felt really angry because….” “I see, and when you said that back it made me feel defensive because…” “Ok, so it’s coming up for you because of this other material you’re working on….” (jealousy, feeling humiliated, whatever) and then being able to come to some agreement once the pressure has changed.

Until then, you’re a cool circle of people who probably shouldn’t be getting a mortgage together just yet.

Rent a space, borrow a space, meet at people’s houses, etc, but find out how much conflict you can handle as a group before you hire a surveyor.

Do the work of budgeting all the boring capacities you’ll need (water, housing, food storage, health care, tools, seasonal work, transportation, schooling, market, outside debts (if you want them), etc, as well as the skills required. Lots of people can do conflict well, but if the well goes dry or only one person knows where the tools are, you’re in trouble.
