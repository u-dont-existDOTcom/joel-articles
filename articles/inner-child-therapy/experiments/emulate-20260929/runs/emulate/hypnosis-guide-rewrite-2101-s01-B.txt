If you need more help than what this guide provides

It’s important to approach self hypnosis as a relationship with yourself. If you need some help in a situation this guide isn’t a substitute for

Get some help now if you’re in danger, very confused, unable to look after yourself, have physical symptoms like chest pains or fainting, new or worsening suicidal thoughts, can’t sleep at all, have symptoms that resemble mania or psychosis, or have dissociative symptoms that affect your ability to function. Self hypnosis is something you should spend at least 4-12 weeks practicing to get the hang of. It’s not a quick fix for problems that need immediate attention. If these problems are new or they’ve recently become much worse it’s best to get some help to make sure everything’s okay.

Start with shorter sessions, make sure you feel warm, pick a subject that doesn’t take too much work, and make sure you come all the way out at the end of the session. Work on one target at a time for a week or two, like anxiety, daydreaming, repairing a relationship, tackling a task you’ve been putting off, improving your sleep, regulating your emotions, or becoming more accurate with your intuitions. If you notice that you’re becoming less able to function, or you’re developing very strong memories of things that you’re not sure actually happened, you may need to reconsider what you’re doing.

You may also want to get some help that’s more suited to your needs if you struggle with recurring panic attacks, compulsive behaviors, substances, or major relationship instability.
