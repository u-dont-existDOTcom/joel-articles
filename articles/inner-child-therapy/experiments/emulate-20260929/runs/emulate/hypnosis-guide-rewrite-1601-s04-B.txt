Turn your attention to yourself and notice if you are feeling guilty. Remind yourself that guilt does not necessarily mean you have done something wrong, or that you are being manipulated. Ask yourself if you have done something wrong, that you need to make up for. If not, are you giving up your position, in order to stop someone else from being disappointed in you? Decide what you want to accomplish, remembering that it needs to be realistic. Sometimes we can accomplish mutual understanding, other times we can only convey information, say no or terminate the interaction.

Change your level of contact

Begin to contact this, when you are ready. As you do, broaden your attention to include aspects of your environment, or your body that feel more okay. Move back and forth between the difficult aspects and the more okay aspects. (This is called pendulation and titration). Use your experience to guide you in how much is enough, remembering that you do not have to do a certain number.

Return to this when you are ready. If you find that you are losing track of what is going on around you, feeling that things are not real, or you are not able to change or stop what you are doing, then stop exploring internally and use the process for a full return. If you continue to feel lost, seek out human assistance.
