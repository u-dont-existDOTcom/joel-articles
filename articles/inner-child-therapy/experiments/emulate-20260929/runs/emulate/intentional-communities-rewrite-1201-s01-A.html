Do you want to talk about why you’re jealous of your brother, what it means that God doesn’t exist, why you’re terrified of dying, what your parents did to you as a child, how you spend your money, how you have sex, what it felt like when you took psilocybin, or why you want to leave? If not, please leave now. Most fully formed adults who can read this page could sit and talk about solar panel technology for six hours straight.

Instead, perhaps ask yourself whether or not you have the bandwidth to take a look at yourself, and whether or not we have the bandwidth to assist you.

Some people come to communities like this one after many years of contemplative practice or therapy. Others have had extremely lucky childhoods. Still others are struggling to survive and need assistance. Most of us are somewhere in between, and all of these places may be relevant at different times in our lives.
