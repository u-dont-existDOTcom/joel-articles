Many communities, including Twin Oaks, East Wind, Acorn, and numerous other intentional communities and ecovillages welcome visitors. If you are interested in visiting a community, be sure to read their requirements, as they may have changed since this page was written.

Visiting communities is a great way to discover what you like and don't like about togetherness, privacy, labor systems, communal showers, etc.

Do these things before you start looking for land.

There is no application, and I am not selling a retreat. The manuals are free. I'm in Sénégal and will probably buy some land here, but am not declaring a commune. Where there is care, play, mutual usefulness and growth in honesty, community will arise.

I am writing this and publishing the film because of the influence of the Bruderhof community on my father, who made the film, set up the organization to do the research for it, and carried me along with him through a series of experiments in communal living.

Maybe someone will do something with what they learn from these manuals, and maybe they will teach others, who will do something.

If you do something, maybe we will hear about it. If you teach others to do something, it doesn't matter if we hear about it or not.
