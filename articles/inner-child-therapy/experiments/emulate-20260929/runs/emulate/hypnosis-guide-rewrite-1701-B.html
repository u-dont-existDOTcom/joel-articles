The 15-minute daily practice

Transcription: Use the same practice. Don't try to learn two different scripts at once. For 15 minutes we don't need that much time in order to be very effective therapeutically in the use of hypnosis. Just use 15 minutes. Use a few minutes to go in, leave a few minutes in the middle to let the practice just unfold however it wants to unfold and then use a few minutes to come out at the end. If you want to use that five minutes in the middle to just listen in silence that's fine. You can break it at any time to do something different.

An alternative induction: Once you're ready to go in notice 3 things you can see, 3 things you can hear and 3 things that you can feel in your body; then do 2 of each and then 1 of each and then count 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 and then start listening.

Once you've done it a couple of times you can shorten up the recording and not even play it back. It's there if you want to use it. If you want to skip to coming back in just say to yourself when you're ready to come back in, if you've already done this a couple of times.

When you're done just ask yourself what does this need? Does it need warmth or protection? What's the next direction that would best serve your highest truth, your highest love and your highest sense of life? Is there something that you want to explore or build within yourself that would be helpful? What can you take with you? If you're going to take action what is the specific action that you want to take? If possible make it an if/then plan where you say, if a certain cue happens then you're going to take a specific small action that will help you to achieve your goal. Then just notice later how you're sleeping, how you're relating, how you're functioning.
