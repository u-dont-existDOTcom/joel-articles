Serotonin Bottleneck Pattern: 5-HTP + Low-Dose P5P

Note that this is for a very specific phenotype, not for everyone who is depressed. It is for people who look like they have low serotonin: eg, trouble initiating sleep, depressed mood, low feelings of warmth, nighttime anxiety, repetitive thoughts, etc. Also relevant is genetic data. In my case, I had evidence of bottlenecking at the TPH2 step of serotonin synthesis, and had a positive response to 5-HTP, improving my sleep and mood. It is contraindicated in people who are agitated, manic, or already taking other serotonergic medications (including serotonergic psychedelics and entactogens).

This works by directly providing a precursor of serotonin, bypassing the rate-limiting step from tryptophan to 5-HTP. Here is a good review of 5-HTP and its relation to serotonin biology, mood, anxiety, sleep, obesity, myoclonus, serotonin toxicity, etc. It is somewhat effective for depression and sleep issues, although the evidence is mixed and not as strong as some of the glowing Internet testimonials. However, it does have a real effect on serotonin level, and if it works for you, you can feel it quickly.

In my case, I take 100mg 5-HTP sublingually at night, and 10-20mg P5P sublingually in the morning. P5P is the active form of vitamin B6, and is a cofactor in the aromatic amino acid decarboxylase reaction. However, I am slow-COMT and very sensitive to P5P, so most people can take more than I can.
