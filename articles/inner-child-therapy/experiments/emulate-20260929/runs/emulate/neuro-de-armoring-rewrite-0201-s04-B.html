Tool: Uridine Monophosphate. Note that the best evidence for Uridine’s efficacy in treating DAWS isn’t direct (because no such study has been done), but comes from the basic science showing that uridine, along with choline and DHA, are rate limiting circulating precursors for synthesizing brain phosphatides (membranes, like those of synapses). Wurtman and colleagues have shown that supplementing DHA with uridine and choline has the effect of increasing synaptic membrane phosphatide components, as well as increasing the number of synaptic membrane proteins. Wurtman also has demonstrated that DHA’s ability to increase dendritic spine formation is enhanced by UMP. A separate study has shown that feeding UMP in the diet increases potassium-evoked dopamine release, and another has shown UMP increases neurite outgrowth in aged rats. So, while still largely an extrapolation to human DAWS, it’s an educated one with a lot of convincing supporting data (in addition to my anecdotal experience).

Since I was already taking choline (from eggs) and DHA/EPA (from fish oil), adding UMP (standard dose) led to a relatively rapid (within a day) reinstatement of vivid dreaming, including dreams containing actual childhood memories. Also, my psychedelic tolerance appeared to have dropped significantly, leading to a massive decrease in my desire to use ketamine and LSD to “feel good” again. While it’s hard to say that my D3 receptors regenerated, it’s possible. In any case, UMP sent a strong signal that my dendritic spine membranes (and/or dopamine receptors) had been starved.
