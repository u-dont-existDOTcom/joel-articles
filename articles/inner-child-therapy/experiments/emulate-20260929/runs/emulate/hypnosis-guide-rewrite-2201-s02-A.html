If you are in Chat GPT, you can also upload an image of a drawing or picture, or read a poem or sing a song to explain, if those are available in the current chat. If not, the current chat will inform you of that. You might want to comment on what is in the thing you are sharing, and on why it means something to you, since those are different from what the app may find in it, if it can find anything. It may not interpret it as you do, but you can correct it, without justification.

Remember, even an app that can hear you is still an app, and will need to ask what is going on. It doesn't have the intuitive, embodied knowledge a skilled therapist would have. It can, however, help you to learn more, and to make more informed choices about what to do.
