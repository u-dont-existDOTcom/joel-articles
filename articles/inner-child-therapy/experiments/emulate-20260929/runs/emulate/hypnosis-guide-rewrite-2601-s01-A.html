Appendix D: Anxiety — body first

Remember anxiety is a Protector. It is trying to protect you from something it believes you cannot handle, whether it’s danger or shame, abandonment or failure, being found out, getting sick, being rejected, losing control etc. So don’t cross examine it. Instead start by:

  1. Working with your body, 2) Working with your Inner Child and 3) Work on the Inquiry and 4) take action.

Working with your Inner Child (show it love)

  1. Practice putting your hand on your heart and another on your solar plexus and saying:

“Even though you are little, you are not alone. You don’t have to figure anything out. I’m here now and I’m a big girl/boy now.

Make sure you say this with the love you have for your Inner Child and not just as a way of thinking it will seep into your subconscious mind.

  1. Work on reducing your breathing (see Buteyko method etc)

To do this you need to learn to breathe through your nose, take in very small breaths and exhale gently. There will be a pause at the end of each breath. Be careful to go slow as not breathing enough initially increases your anxiety for a while.
