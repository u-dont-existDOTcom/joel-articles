No Trance Inner Contact

Pacing: you’re eyes are open, you can say what room you’re in, what day it is, feel your feet, read, write etc. Suggest you ask a question but don’t pace any further. Client can respond, say he doesn’t know, lie back and rest, change the question, ask someone else, stop the exercise etc. There is no need to pace any further, this is a record of autonomous behaviour. No deepener required. Integration is a process of reality testing and taking action based on what you have found out. Exit from the exercise is simply to stop doing it.
