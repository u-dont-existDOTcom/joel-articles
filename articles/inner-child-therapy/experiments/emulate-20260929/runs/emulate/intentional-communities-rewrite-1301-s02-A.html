Please make sure to choose your values first!

Too often, I see people choose a community the same way they pick an AirBNB -- the land is gorgeous, the pond is stunning, and everyone seems really nice!

Please take the time to choose your values BEFORE you visit a community. What do you believe about kids? Money? Land ownership? Relationships? Vaccinations? Food? Spirituality? Technology? Privacy? Conflict resolution? Leaving the community?

Of course, sexuality and gender fit in here as well -- are you open to having LGBT people in the community? Are you open to them having relationships? How will you protect them? Will you indoctrinate children into questioning their sexual identities as soon as possible? What if one of your kids turns out to be gay/trans/etc? Please make sure your community is discussing these things!

Also, it is okay to have "experimentation" as a value. Just make sure you say that you want to experiment, because the person next to you may not!

Once you choose your values, you can look for a group that matches those values, or at least complements them. Also, please pay attention to how well a group LIVES their values, not just how they talk about their values. And don't be afraid to ask the "scary" questions -- how do you handle conflict? Can someone veto things? What happened when someone tried to leave? Who owns the land? Remember, at the end of the day, the view will still be there...
