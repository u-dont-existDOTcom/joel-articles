Because often, the result is a lot of flooding, a lot of panic, and often, a lot of false information or confabulation, lots of dissociation, and generally speaking, a worse Tuesday. And if someone is flooded, it’s hard for the adult part to do the good regulation and supporting that they can usually do. It’s hard for them to take a step back, consider options, check what’s real and what’s not, make a plan, and make an empowered decision. Sometimes, the answer is that there needs to be silence, because the adult isn’t capable of holding the information in a regulated, supported, humble way.

So, ask yourself:

How can I increase capacity by another 5% so that my conscious mind can be given the information?

Can you give the information to my conscious mind in a symbolic way?

Can you tell me what I need to do to be protected without telling me everything?

Do I need the information, or do I need to work on increasing my capacity?

Who can help me with this?

And here’s a great thing to say to your own mind: “Hey, parts of my subconscious mind that are wise, if there’s something my conscious mind can’t handle, I don’t want you to just violently break it open. I want you to find a way to build my capacity so that I can handle it. I want you to prepare me so that I can hear it in a safe way.”

One other thing: things that come up in hypnotic states and imagery are rarely “real” in a literal sense. Especially when it comes to trauma, work with someone to help you with this. Your mind is symbolic and it compresses information and distorts it, and it gets a lot of its imagery from pop culture and fear.
