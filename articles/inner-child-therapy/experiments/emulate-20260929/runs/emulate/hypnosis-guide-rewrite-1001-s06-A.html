The Leader, Guide, or Guru

What does your Leader do to provide direction? You might also call this part the Leader, Guide, Guru, Wise One, Sage, Inner Teacher, Director, Higher Adult, etc.

Does it push you to be perfectly disciplined, ignoring your needs? Does it know that sometimes the most adult thing to do is to ask for help, express your preferences, or let someone who is safe see your imperfections? Does it know that while it is legitimate to want closeness, it is not good strategy to punish someone for needing space, and can it think of other ways of meeting your need?

Some mantras this part might use: We are not only here to feel good. We are here to be whole. We are here to belong to life itself, not only to people who give us approval. We are here to be of service to truth, beauty, love, God, source, nature, the universe, dharma, Tao, the benefit of all beings, etc.

What does your Adult do? Start with whichever aspect feels most accessible to you. Then see if you can develop the protective function, and the direction function so that it supports the child part’s growth but also its need for care, play, support, etc.
