A New We, tour of european ecovillages

Within Reach, couple bikes 6500 miles through ~100 comms

Seeking the Good Life in America, look at Twin Oaks, Acorn, Light Morning

Voices of Cohousing, partial community option, aim to maximize neighborliness while still having a lot of autonomy

Inside the Bruderhof, high commitment religious community, look into how much autonomy is given up. my dad spent 17 years of his life there. they also have a whole bunch on youtube.

Living in the Future: Lammas, look at the community building process as well as the ins and outs of dealing with land use / planning law.

Books and papers, in order of reading

For Child Rearing (for now):

Jean Liedloff - Continuum Concept, 1975. This is the most important book on natural child rearing. It is based on her experience living with the Yekuna. Don't take it too seriously as anthropology, but read it anyway.

Michaeleen Doucleff - Hunt, Gather, Parent, 2021. Very useful and practical, and much more accessible. Focuses on kids being involved in the family, giving them autonomy, and the idea that they should be cared for by everyone.

Practical Community Building Guides:

Diana Leafe Christian - Creating a Life Together. Good place to start, it walks you through the order of things and gives useful practical advice on things like membership, money, land, agreements, etc.

Yana Ludwig - Building Belonging. A whole systems guide to building an intentional community.

Rau, Koch-Gonzalez - Many Voices One Song. A practical manual on sociocracy.

Schaub et al - Building United Judgement. On using consensus.

Ludwig, Gimnig - The Cooperative Culture Handbook. Has a bunch of useful exercises on building a cooperative culture, dealing with conflict, etc.
