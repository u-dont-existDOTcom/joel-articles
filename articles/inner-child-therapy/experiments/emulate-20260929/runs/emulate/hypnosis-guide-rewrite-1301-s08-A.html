Sleep induction

Sleep induction is a bit different, because (unlike in inner work) you are not going to return back, write things down and act. Analysis is off duty till the next day, so there is no need to feel that you will see everything in the dream and you must know this night, now.

For a nice sleep hypnosis induction, check out Nimja's sleep playlist .

Fractionation (optional)

It might be good to do some fractionation (returning back to ordinary state and returning to absorption again), but inform the subject that you are doing that and that he can stop. Also, he should know that you are not going deeper necessarily, he can choose . E.g.

"You can now open your eyes and return to ordinary awareness when you want. You can also choose to continue. If you do that, just soften your eyes or close them and notice what is going on inside..."
