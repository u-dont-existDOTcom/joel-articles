Sometimes start with the whole

If it’s useful for you to start by differentiating 12 parts and giving them all office space, by all means! But for some people, that approach may feel fragmenting. So in that case, it can be better to start with the sacred whole.

So you might start by saying: “I’m coming from my universal heart. Or my Higher Self. Or the part of me that was here before all these wounds and roles and families and groups and identities and ideologies and stories came along. Help me understand what would be true/kind/timely/stabilizing/useful.”

And if even Higher Self and universal heart don’t feel right or they feel like they’re other people’s language and not yours, then start with whatever is helping you to witness. Just noticing what you notice, and your body, and that you were younger once. You don’t have to have a fully developed inner adult right away to start this work; the very act of noticing is enough to get started.

In fact, if you can notice and differentiate already, you probably don’t need to do much Witness work. (And coming from Higher Self doesn’t mean you already have adult functions well-developed; it’s a way of helping you develop them.)

Differentiate only as needed – “Am I speaking from my inner child here, or a protector? Is this coming from my body? Is this fear? Is this intuition? Is this social conditioning? Is this divine guidance, or my interpretation of divine guidance?”

But start from your heart. Differentiate as needed. And then come back to your heart again.
