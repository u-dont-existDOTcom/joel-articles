A shortcut back into hypnosis.

It's better to learn how to do a good induction and how to bring the person out of hypnosis properly first. Once you know how to do a good induction then it's possible to add a shortcut to get back into a useful hypnotic state later on. My dad used a mudra (joining 2 fingers together like an 'OK' sign) to put himself into a relaxed state, even when he was driving. However, I would recommend that you use different key's for when you want to stay alert (like when driving) and when you want to go into hypnosis.

Pick a word, a phrase or even combine this with a gesture if you want to, and whilst in a good hypnotic state (not at the height of an emotional experience, child state or traumatic scene) link this to going into a good hypnotic state.

When you want to go back into hypnosis, make sure that you have some time to do this in and that it's appropriate to the situation, then use your key to go back into hypnosis deliberately. When you're ready you can change direction or finish up.
