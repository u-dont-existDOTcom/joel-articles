What would a Minimally Competent Adult do to take care of that? It’s usually something really simple like eat or go to bed. Sometimes it’s something like, “Don’t send that text.” If the place you’re in is not safe, sometimes you need to leave the place you’re in, or lock the door and get help. Even if you feel like a child as you are doing this, that’s ok. You can explain to the little one afterwords that you had to do this for them, and that this is what the Protector does.
