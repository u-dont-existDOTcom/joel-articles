Pattern #1: Pacing & Leading (Stating what is true)

Pacing and leading simply means stating what is already true:

“It’s completely true that the anxiety and body activation is here and that a protector wants to take care of things. It’s also true that one breath at a time, you can begin to bring your adult self into listening.”

The simplest pacing and leading statement follows this loop:

“It’s completely true that _________ (insert current sensation, emotion, etc.) is here and it’s also true that your body/mind is doing its best to take care of things by ________________ (insert current behavior, thought process, etc.). It’s also true that your adult self is here, one breath at a time, and is ready to begin to listen to ________________ (insert what you want them to become aware of next).”

Pattern #2: Verbs that open to awareness

These include: notice, realize, recognize, sense, feel, wonder, imagine, picture, listen, allow, discover, remember, consider, etc.

“______, I wonder if you will notice whether the answer comes as an image, a word, a sense of pressure or warmth, or perhaps as a knowing of what to do next?”
