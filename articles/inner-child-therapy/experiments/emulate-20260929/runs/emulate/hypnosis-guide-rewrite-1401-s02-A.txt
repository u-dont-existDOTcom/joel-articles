What to do if your recording stops: The recording has stopped. The session is now over. Open your eyes. You are in (place) on (day). Push your feet or hands down, move your body, look around and use the 5 step count to bring yourself fully awake.

Separate recordings for sleep: It’s fine for a sleep recording to end in sleep or just lying there resting in a quiet space, but for a recording where you’re meant to be awake, ending it in a way that keeps you in role, or lacking normal judgement, or having reduced ability to move, or blank, or with an undefined reset word, or even simply suggesting that you might want to stay like this indefinitely is not a good idea.
