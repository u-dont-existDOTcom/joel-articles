Closing

It’s not about “conquering” your subconscious, or your body, or your “rational thinking”, or some other part you imagine is holding you back. It’s about making friends with that part, because it’s probably a protector.

It’s not about how deep you can go into hypnosis. It’s about becoming a trustworthy adult.

It’s about earning the right to come closer to the kid by listening to her for a while. It’s about making some small promises and keeping them so the guards can sit down for a moment. It’s about showing your loving side so your thinking mind doesn’t have to brace against it. It’s about using your thinking mind so your loving side doesn’t turn into fantasy. And it’s about integrating it in your life so your adult has something to promise in the first place.

Try it in the app!

With the Inner Signal app you get both the hypnosis sessions with a voice and the method to develop your own language, together with reflections on your own reports and the possibility to ask questions about what we’re doing. But of course you can also learn to do it yourself without an app, and/or find someone to do it with.
