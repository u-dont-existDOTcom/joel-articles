Sometimes start with the whole

For some people, it might be more fragmenting to begin by counting to twelve and handing out business cards to all the parts in their office building. If that’s you, then begin with the sacred.

“I’m coming from the perspective of the universal heart. I’m coming from the perspective of my Higher Self. I’m coming from the part of me that existed before any wounding, before any particular role or job, before any family or group affiliation, before any identities, before any ideology or belief, before any story at all. Please help me understand what’s true and kind and timely and stabilizing and useful.”

If even that is too much for you, or if you feel like those terms are coming from outside yourself, then begin by cultivating the practice of witnessing. If you can notice what’s going on in the room, or in your body, then you’re at a more developed state than the younger parts of you. That’s enough of a differentiation to begin with.

And if you can already do that, then there’s no need to work on cultivating the Witness state. Instead, work on cultivating whatever adult functions you’re missing, and if the language of the Higher Self helps you to do that, then by all means use it.

Once you’ve done that, begin to differentiate only when it’s useful and relevant. “Is this coming from my inner child?” “Is this coming from a protector?” “Is this coming from my body?” “Is this fear?” “Is this intuition?” “Is this social conditioning?” “Is this divine guidance, or my interpretation of divine guidance?”

Always begin from the heart, differentiate as needed, and then return to the heart again.
