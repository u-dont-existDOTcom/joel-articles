We actually ran into the only white man in all of San Cristobal and he seemed like he was CIA because he sat us down and asked us why we were there, who we were gonna work with, what we wanted to do, when we were leaving, etc.

So it was nice to feel important for a moment even if we are just college kids in a dying car. 😂

Everything that we experienced in Oventik was like a dream, from playing basketball outside with the Zapatista youth, to listening to people talk about their history and philosophy (I had to translate for my friends because my Spanish is better haha). People come from all over to learn and to make themselves harder to attack.

The Zapatistas seem very spiritual, I believe they have some sort of secret relationship with ayahuasca but they wont tell me hahaha, I could be wrong about that.

One thing I love about them is their system of ‘mandar obedeciendo,’ or leading by obeying. The people who are sent out to represent the community are beholden to the decisions of the assembly.

The Zapatistas are the closest whole example I can think of. It’s not so much how they elect their leaders but how they operate autonomously in all spheres of society: education, health, justice, production (in a collective way), political formation, teaching others, and in coordination with one another.
