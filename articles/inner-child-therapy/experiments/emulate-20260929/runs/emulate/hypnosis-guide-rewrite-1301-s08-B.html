Induction to sleep :

It’s a little different. If you are working with yourself, at the end you get back to your adult part, you write and you act. But if you are working on going to sleep, the analysis part can go to sleep too. The subject will be notified that all revelatory part of the work can wait.

See Nimja’s sleep playlist to know how to do a sleep hypnosis.

Fractionation (facultative) :

Fractionation is the possibility of coming back and to come in and out of the absorption. It is good to say it when you do it, in order that the answers of the subject are not automatically « deeper ».

« If you want, you can open your eyes and come back. You can also decide to stop the session here. Otherwise, you can close your eyes (or soften your gaze) and you will see what will happen. You can also decide to stop at this moment. »
