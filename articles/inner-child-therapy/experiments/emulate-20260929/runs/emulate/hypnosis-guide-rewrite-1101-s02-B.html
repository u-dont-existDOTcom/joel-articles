You can rebel all you want, but you cant choose…yet!

Why? In my view it comes down to simple development. When a child is focused on mitigating pain and increasing attachment, they can’t focus on finding out what they like, don’t like, what they want to try and don’t want to try, and all those other things that help make up a sense of self. That’s a simple way of saying it, there are certainly more involved ways of discussing the neuroscience behind it, but I like to keep it simple.

Sometimes it helps to remove yourself from your social group temporarily. Just long enough to differentiate yourself from them. But not long enough to fall into the trap of isolation.

Another way to explain it is the difference between a human doer and a human being. When someone doesn’t feel they have worth inside themselves, they must feel it outside. Whether it’s through beauty, performance, intellect, status, spirituality, being needed etc. These are all ways a human doer attempts to buy their sense of belonging. If they fail in their chosen arena, they fail as a person.
