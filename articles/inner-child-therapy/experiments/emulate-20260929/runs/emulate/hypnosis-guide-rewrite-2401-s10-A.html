1. Represent the pattern (how you experience it. What modality or representation; location, distance, size, colour, movement, etc)
  1. What is this pattern trying to do for you.
  1. Adjust (as necessary) the representation (move it further away, turn down the volume, change posture, etc)
  1. Introduce the 3 adults (Nurturer, Protector, Leader) and have them interact with it.
  1. Anchor the state (heart, fingers, breath, posture, etc)
  1. Future pace (in a similar situation, what are you going to do).
  1. Test and collect evidence (what’s different in your body, does the behaviour feel ok, is it do-able, what other evidence have you that this is the right thing to do).
  1. Exit and act.
