Body supports

Sometimes it's hard to do because your mind is too activated. So you also need to work with your body. Massage your foot. Massage your scalp, your temple, your neck, your shoulder. It might be good to apply heat, or pressure, like with a weighted blanket. Maybe turn off the lights, play some calming music you are familiar with. Maybe use a massage gun, if you have one (but not on the front of your neck, and not on injured, numb, bruised, inflamed, recently surgically-incised body parts, or varicose veins, or anywhere it hurts or makes you feel dizzy or tingly or otherwise bad).

Others find it helpful to use heat, darkness, music, massage, weighted blankets, breathwork, familiar objects, or some other technology they are familiar with.

(Some people use personal relaxation technology. On my ZHoly3O page I talk about using ozonated charcoal "holy water", and Blushield.) Then work with stabilizing your body, and then listen to what's going on inside you, and reality test it, and then do what you need to do to ground yourself.

Anxiety Script

Working with the body. Feet, chair, breath, room.

“Ok little one, I hear you. Ok Protector, I hear you.

If you think there’s something I need to do, tell me what I need to do. If there’s not something I need to do, help me calm down.

I won’t abandon you, I won’t worship you. I’ll listen to you, I’ll reality test what you’re telling me, and I’ll do what needs to be done.”
