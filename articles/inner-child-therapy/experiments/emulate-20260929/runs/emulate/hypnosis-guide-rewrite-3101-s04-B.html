1. No-trance inner contact method :

Observable Pacing : Eyes open. Acknowledge the room and what day it is. Feel your feet. You can even read or write while doing the exercise. In other words, you don’t have to focus or narrow your awareness any further in order to do this exercise. You are given the invitation to ask a question, but you can choose whether or not to do so. You can choose how to answer, whether by answering the question, writing down “I’m not sure”, resting, asking a different question, getting someone else’s point of view, or stopping the exercise altogether. The signal recording is similarly autonomous. No deepener is used. You can remain completely alert throughout the exercise. The “handoff” is done by allowing your internal adult to answer the question in writing or by helping you to make a choice regarding what to do to solve your problem. Integration/exit : You can reality test the signal you receive and/or take a small action to begin implementing your answer. You can exit the exercise at any time, since you don’t have to put yourself into an altered state in order to do the exercise.
