Appendix C: The extended de-hypnosis checklist

The Influence Defense Checklist

So, you think you might be influenced? Remember these words:

If it can't stand up to questioning, it's not true.

If it can't stand up to boundaries, it's not love.

If it can't stand up till the morning after, it's not from the Spirit.

If it sounds good to your 15 year old self but not your adult self, it's not for you.

Then, ask yourself these questions:

Is there a sense of urgency?

Am I being encouraged to keep this a secret from others?

Is there a sense of shame associated with this?

Am I being threatened, directly or indirectly, with exclusion?

Do I feel specially chosen, gifted, blessed?

Is there a sexual charge to this?

Is there a sense of spiritual superiority involved?

Is there pressure to give money, sex, time, loyalty?

Am I being asked to not speak, not question, to follow?

Am I being helped to see my choices narrowing?

Am I being discouraged from asking questions?

Is my questioning or resistance being pathologized?

Am I being helped to see "normal" life as dull, disappointing, etc?

Does it feel like my child parts are being seduced, scared or rescued?

Is my adult self still allowed to say "No"?

If you answered "Yes" to more than a few of these, you need to stop and not go deeper into the trance.
