Just because a ceremony is peer led doesn’t mean you go easy on potential contraindications. Medical and psychiatric conditions should be screened for, medication combinations should be considered and checked, a sober sitter should be present who is well enough qualified to move toward professional and/or emergency medical care if necessary. The fact that authority is more distributed requires everyone to be MORE qualified to handle this stuff, not less.

Also, I am very careful about some medicines/protocols that I feel are less stabilizing and more medical in nature (some ketamine, some mdma, bufo, etc). Reasons are detailed in those articles.

  1. The medicine is secondary to the pl/ork. Reparenting, somatic work, peer counseling etc are required. The medicine doesn’t create these.

This will help keep out the experience collectors. If you’re not willing to put in the work to be a better listener for 6 months, you’re not going to be willing to be more relationed because some visions told you to be.

  1. Integration is key. This is what happens when the ceremony is over in your regular life.

There are other things, of course, but these are the big ones. Preperation, nutrition, interaction screening, dose considerations, emergency planning etc are all important. I’ve got most of the current safety info on those in Altered States Triage.
