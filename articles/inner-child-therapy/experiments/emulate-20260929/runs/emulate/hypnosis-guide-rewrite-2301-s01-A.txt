Appendix A: Prompt Engineering

Your subconscious is not an AI model. But there are some useful patterns in how AI models are prompted that can help you be a bit more precise in how you talk to yourself, helping you be more present, honest, loving, and able to pick up the signal you’re looking for.

There are 7 prompt elements that can be useful to include for more precision:

Role: Who/what are you talking to?

Context: What’s the context?

Intention: What do you want to change?

Constraint: What constraints can keep this safe, truthful, kind, non-forcing, non-delusional, focused on capacity before content, etc?

Output: What kind of output would be useful? Image, phrase, body sensation, memory, next action, question, symbol, silence, rest, etc?

Evaluation: What does it mean for the answer to be good? True? Kind? Timely? Stabilizing? Specific? Testable?

Iteration: How might you want to iterate on the prompt based on what you experience in life?

It’s also good to have a prompt that helps your analytical brain relax into not needing to force an answer. Something like:

“If it’s most beneficial for the system to take more time to process this, and the best answer right now is silence, then please guide my body into deeper and deeper comfortable rest.”

Sometimes the answer is silence – nothing’s happening. Sometimes the answer is “not yet” or “rest” or “build your capacity” or “do the basic thing you already know to do”.
