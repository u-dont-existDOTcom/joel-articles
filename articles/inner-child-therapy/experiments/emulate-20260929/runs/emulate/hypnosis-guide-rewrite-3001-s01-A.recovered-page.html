Appendix H: Working with Demons, Jinn, Hostile Presences, “Unattached Burdens”

I’m including this appendix based on real-life experience.

People practicing self-hypnosis, meditation, lucid dreaming, loveyhuasca, going through times of great grief, earnest prayer, the forefront of plant medicine, or other altered states of consciousness sometimes come into contact with something that feels very unlike a simple emotion or young part of self, something that seems like a demon, a jinn, a parasitic energy, a dark intelligence, a terrifying archetype, etc. Sometimes these voices and images feel intrusive, hateful, seductive, mocking, or otherwise alien.

Whether or not these are “real” on a metaphysical level, or are merely symbolic forms taken on by the mind for complex reasons to do with trauma, dissociated energy, “burdens” passed on by others, etc., the most important thing to focus on is still: “What can I do in response to this that will bring me into safety, clarity, lovingness, groundedness, lack of hooking?”

The very first thing to do is the same as always: check in on medical issues, environmental issues, bodily issues, stimulation issues, mind-opening issues. Often the best thing to do in the face of what seems like a demon, a jinn, a hostile presence, an intrusive presence, is to open the eyes, ground in the body, notice the room around you, and/or reduce stimulation in some way (like by leaving a situation where you’re surrounded by people who don’t seem friendly), and just generally to NOT DO ANYTHING to increase stimulation or delve deeper into the situation or try to ascertain the metaphysical truth of the situation. You might say to the presence:

“Right now, I can only bring in things that I can integrate. If this is too much for me to integrate right now, we can come back to this later on when I am more able to integrate it. I can bring my adult self into this situation, and I can bring my body into safety right now.”
