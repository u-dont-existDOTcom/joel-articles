Experiment Before You Divorce

That said, i want to lower the barrier to trying out new ideas. So if you want to try something different, we can experiment with different rules in various configurations, first maybe within the parent group, then as a semi-autonomous part of the group, then maybe as a seed group nearby with some shared resources (like selected tools, child care, business stuff, friendship, etc). Moving to a totally fissioned group is just one point on a gradient of experimentation distance.

A) Some experiments can't be done without trying them on a bigger scale, with different rules, and different land, etc. Trying them out with some of the group's resources might not give them a fair shake.

Federation, without turning into a state.

I think that once we get to this size, we need to think about how to spread out into a federation. What do we want the federation to do? Help spread the practice. Help train and seed new groups. Make sure that we don't become just another bunch of happy enclaves. Stay different enough to learn from eachother.
