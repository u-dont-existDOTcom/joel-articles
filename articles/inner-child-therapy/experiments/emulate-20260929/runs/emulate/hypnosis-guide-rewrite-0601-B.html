Hypnosis Evidence

There isn’t magical evidence, but there is some real evidence. In 2024 an overview of 49 meta-analysis of hypnosis on 261 distinct primary studies showed that “the strongest evidence was observed in the domains of pain, medical procedures, and children/adolescents….”Varying quality across different outcomes and study types.

A systematic review of RCTs of self-hypnosis showed good results for “pain, childbirth, pediatric applications, and stress/anxiety.” Again, it depends on how it is used. If you are learning a real skill to use on yourself - good. If you are just listening to a file - not as good, but if you listen several times it may help.

I am interested in helping you learn a skill to use inside yourself - not becoming a consumer of fancy trance audio files.

The Mayo Clinic warns “if you’ve experienced stressful events in the past, hypnosis may trigger strong emotional reactions. Other forms of treatment may be more appropriate for dealing with historical trauma.”

DO use self-hypnosis to regulate, communicate, reparent, clarify, change habits, get present to safety, integrate emotions, and do things in your life.

DO NOT use self-hypnosis to force yourself to remember trauma, prove trauma happened, suppress medical symptoms, destabilize yourself, or not get appropriate treatment for your conditions.
