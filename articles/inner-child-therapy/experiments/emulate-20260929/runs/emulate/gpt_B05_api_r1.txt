Building Trust With Your Little One

Keep Your Word

It’s okay if you don’t feel love or any other warm fuzzy feeling for your little one yet. The Protector can do these things first and the feelings may come later.

Choose one. Be specific about what you’re going to do, when you’re going to do it, what the first step is, and what you need to help you to do it. If there’s anything you need to practice in order to say or do, practice it. After you’ve had a chance to try it out, debrief with yourself about what happened. If you didn’t do what you said you were going to do, ask yourself if you were afraid, if you didn’t know what you were aiming for, if you didn’t know how to do it, if you were too tired or if there was some other practical problem. If you did what you said you were going to do but didn’t feel any better, recognize that just because you did it doesn’t mean that it was helpful. On the other hand, if it helped your little one to be safe, close, competent, or happy, keep doing it.
