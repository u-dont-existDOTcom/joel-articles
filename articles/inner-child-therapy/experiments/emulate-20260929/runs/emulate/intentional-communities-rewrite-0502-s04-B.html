After the Psychedelic Calling

It is very common for someone who has had a deep psychedelic or mystical experience to feel a strong urge to find or form their tribe or village, and help other people have that experience.

I have felt that urge, and I know that it can sometimes lead to an inflated sense of one’s abilities, especially if the person has not yet tried to help other people. People are harder to help than they appear. Some don’t want to be helped, and resist all attempts to help. Some want to be helped, but continue to behave in ways that undermine their stated goals. And some have problems that are too big for any but the most experienced and skilled.

While this “Calling” is real, it is not a good idea to act on it from a sense of omnipotence. I recommend the sections below on capacity and membership to avoid the pitfalls of forming a group in a moment of collective spiritual euphoria.

Furthermore, once this Calling has had a chance to sober up a bit, it can be channeled into productive activity. One useful role of a community like this one is as a training ground for its members, who may one day want to leave and form a new community of their own. This, I think, is a good answer to those who say that a place like this is just an escape for people who can afford to drop out.
