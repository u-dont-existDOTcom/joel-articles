So what if you don't have land? You can still have a group that meets weekly for two years. You can still create a community that fundamentally changes the way you deal with divorce, illness, grief, parenting, and loneliness.

If you're too far away or otherwise constrained from building out the whole thing, build the part you can. Commit to having dinner together once a week with two other households. Exchange counseling hours with a friend, either in person or by video chat. Share other resources like tools, child care, transportation, food prep, or a sauna. If you're sufficiently hippie, take acro-yoga, do dance improv, hold a mothers' circle, or make a cuddle puddle.

Even if you only have three households reliably showing up for each other for half a year, you'll learn a lot more than if you keep fantasizing about getting land.

What can you do this month?

Start some inner pl/ork. Read the reparenting guide (free!), the Hearthwork manual, or start some other serious practice.

Start a practice group with 2-3 people. Don't call it a commune, just commit to meeting regularly.

Go where decent humans gather: unprogrammed Quakers, restorative practice groups, open-hearted religious congregations, mature recovery groups, meditation groups, or other people who have been away on retreat for awhile and come back nicer.
