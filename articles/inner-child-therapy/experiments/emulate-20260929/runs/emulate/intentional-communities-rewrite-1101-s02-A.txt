Edit: Parallel implementations should be allowed, in other words the method should be forkable. If the community can change what I'm doing in a way that seems to work better for them, and I'm not part of the decision process, that's a good thing.

Also, four questions:

Can the community override the founder? Can they replace the founder in their role? Can they change the founder's vision? Can they survive without the founder?

Seems boring but who has the power? Who owns the deed? Who owns the housing, food, cars, tools, etc? Who has the off-site bank account? Who has the power to decide who comes in? Who reviews complaints? Who has the records? Who has the title? Who is going to be able to decide who has access to kids, medicine, etc, and who can make complaints go away?

If you can't do the first one without the person you're trying to override doing it, you can't do it.

These are good questions to ask about any community, teacher, or method you might get involved in, so feel free to ask them of me if I ever actually get to the point of buying land and having people come live with me. :)
