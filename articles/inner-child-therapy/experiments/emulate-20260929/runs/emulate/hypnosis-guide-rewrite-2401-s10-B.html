1. Represent it in some way (Ask: Where do you experience it? As a picture, sound, body sensation? Where in front of you is it? How far away? How big is it? What colour? Is it moving or still?)
  1. Ask the pattern, what is it trying to do for you?
  1. Have it change its form, volume, distance, posture etc
  1. Call in the three adults and have them interact with it (Nurturer, Protector, Leader)
  1. Anchor the state (Where do you feel it in your body? In your heart, at the tip of your fingers, your breathing, in your posture etc
  1. Future pace it. (The next time the original cue comes up what do you need to do to access the new behaviour?)
  1. Test it out (How does your body feel now? Are you behaving in a more reasonable way now? How do you know you are right in what you are saying?)
  1. Exit and take action
