Do not fight from fear

The usual response to this might be to fight or banish it, to hate it, to try to prove we’re stronger, to argue with it, to freak out, or even to identify with it (i.e., “That’s me!”). Some people might pray, use mantras, protective means, banishing rituals, or other forms of exorcism. At times, these might be helpful. But if done in fear, they can only feed the energy of what you’re trying to banish.

Fears gives it center stage, hate gives it a hook, pride makes it an opponent, panic gives it a nervous system.

I don’t need to hate this. I don’t need to merge with it. I don’t need to obey it. I don’t need to defeat it from fear. I can simply be lovingly aware.

Establish your body boundary with compassion and close the door

If you can’t even imagine being loving toward it because it seems so alien, so hostile, so parasitic, so cold, so violating of boundaries, then don’t love it in a soft, sentimental way, but be loving with a boundary.

Sit up, press your feet to the ground, lift up your spine, pull your shoulders gently back, and feel your body’s skin boundary all around your torso.

While you’re feeling that, you might say, “While I don’t need to hate this and while I wish all suffering well by returning it to where it belongs, the boundary of my skin is an adult thing, and I’m not giving you permission to come into my house.”
