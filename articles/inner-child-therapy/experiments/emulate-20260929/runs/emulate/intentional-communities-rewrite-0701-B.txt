Jealousy Can Capture Monogamy or Free Love

Eventualy, romantic attraction and pair-bonding will re-organize a community. And in a community, unlike in a city, all of the people involved in a couple (exes, etc.) will be closely interlinked in many aspects of daily life. What might seem like a couple’s private issue, becomes a community issue.

I hear people arguing on a ‘should we allow monogamy or free love’ level, which IMO, is one level too late. I discuss this at length in my Romance Guide , but in short: Both monogamy and ‘free love’ are subject to the same issues of jealousy. Simply being in close proximity to a lot of people exacerbates this.

Monogamy is often a way of codifying fear/possesiveness into a relationship structure. ‘Free love’ can be a way of spreading that fear out over a larger spreadsheet. Neither of them necessarily does the interal pl/ork to release the fear/jealousy/possesiveness (which comes from childhood).

I think that within a community, couples need to be allowed to form and choose whatever relationship form they choose.

BUT, basic things like housing, kids, community membership, access to basic stuff, etc. cannot be tied to a specific relationship form.

Communities like ZEGG and Tamera have some interesting things to offer here, as they’ve been experimenting in this area for decades. They at least have the decency to admit that the erotic is a thing that exists, and not pretend that all 20 people in a community are monks/nuns.

BUT the absolutely crucial issue is that jealousy cannot be allowed to run the show. It must be dealt with – allowed to be expressed, felt, explored, and released.

This will be a group issue, to some extent, but I don’t think the group should/ can decide what relationship forms are or aren’t allowed. It will be messy.
