go with a rhythm (breathing, humming, tapping, sound from outside, silence, counting, etc); repeating “here, and here again” works well; stop the rhythm at one point and see what comes up with the first quiet beat; don’t need to blank out, just see if it clarifies a word, feeling, need, action, etc; return to rhythm
create an object to represent the gnarly thing you’re focused on (could be made of multiple pieces/strands); use an image that unravels (unwinding yarn, bubbles, etc) or one that places the knots somewhere you can see it but leaves the object essentially the same; you can also simply name the parts as they come to you; don’t count the pieces unless you’re getting rid of them; nothing needs to go away, just see if any pieces need to be taken care of; keep what’s useful and return knowing that you have a place to put the rest where you can still see it
use your real senses to notice some sounds and body sensations, then imagine a place you want to go; use footsteps, scenery, etc, to get there, or just skip to talking with someone/something; not necessary to figure out the meaning of things you see/hear/feel, just notice that you don’t know what’s going to happen next; see who or what you want to talk to; return the same way and close up the space you created
