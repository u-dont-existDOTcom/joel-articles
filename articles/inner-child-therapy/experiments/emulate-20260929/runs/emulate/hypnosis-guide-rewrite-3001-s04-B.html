If it might be psychosis or destabilization

There is a big difference between having a symbolic experience of “hostile presences” that can be dealt with by grounding oneself and bringing in love, etc. and having a persistant experience of such that leads to paranoia, inability to sleep, potential for one to do something dangerous, inability to reality test, etc.

If someone feels like they are being attacked for days on end, are unable to sleep or function, are hearing voices, are being compelled to hurt themself or others, are becoming manic, are unable to communicate normally, are unable to reality test what is symbolic and what is not, etc., then it is time to seek help.

If doing this is leading you to become more loving, more grounded, more able to relate to others, more able to be honest and function in life, etc. then carry on. If doing this is leading you to become more paranoid, unable to sleep, grandiose, unable to relate to others, afraid and unable to function in a “real” way, then it is time to stop going deeper into this and seek help.

The “here’s a great cosmic download for you” test

At times, hostile presences/shadow/grandiose parts of oneself/etc. may attempt to seduce one via making them feel overly-important, as if they are receiving a great cosmic download, etc.

“If I am supposed to do something with this information that is as simple as washing dishes or cleaning my room, etc., then just tell me that. I don’t need all this imagery. If the information you are giving me does not lead to simple acts like washing dishes, cleaning my room, etc., then it is of no use to me on my path.”
