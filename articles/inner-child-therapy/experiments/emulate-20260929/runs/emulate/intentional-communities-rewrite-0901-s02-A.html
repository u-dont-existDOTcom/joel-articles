We talk a lot in The Fire and the Word about movement between levels of structure, how a family is part of a village, which is part of a region and on and on. One of the key organizing principles of the Zapatistas has been to let practice lead and theory follow. They created their Municipalities, and then created an association of Municipalities which was a kind of rehearsal for the Juntas de Buen Gobierno. As they struggled with the different problems and level of development in the various municipalities, a new structure was born.

In 2023 the Zapatistas announced that they were dissolving the MRAs and the Juntas, after a long process of review. Now they are working with Local Autonomous Governments in each community, which can associate in regional collectives, but which are not, as they emphasize, superior to the Local Governments.

In most institutional evolution things get more and more layered and complex with time. The Zapatistas have done a review, and moved things in the opposite direction. They didn’t write a book on leadership and start a certification process, they went ahead and changed the way things work.

Now, it is important here to put a curb on my enthusiasm and remind people that Zapatista autonomy is based on the rich history and context of these Indigenous people. It’s not something you can just copy.
