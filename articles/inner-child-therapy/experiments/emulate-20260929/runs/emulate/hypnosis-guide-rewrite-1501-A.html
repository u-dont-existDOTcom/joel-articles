The actual inner meeting

First, make sure you’re sitting and not laying down, unless you want to fall asleep or slip into a daydream or dissociate. Sit up, but not stiff like you’re in the military, soften your jaw and make sure you’re aware of your surroundings a little bit. If you want to lay down, save it for when you want to go to sleep, take a good long rest or have dreams about a certain topic.

Second, say to yourself, “I’m not going to leave life, I’m going to listen more deeply to life.”

Third, say, “Higher Self, universal heart-mind connection, wise subconscious mind, body intelligence, inner child(ren), inner adult(s), protectors and anything else that can be helpful, show or tell me what I need to know right now, in a way that is helpful and loving and stabilizing and real.”

Fourth, tell your conscious mind to be quiet for awhile.

This doesn’t mean you have to (or even want to) stop all thought processes, but just let them flow through you as you hold the above intention in the back of your mind and see what pops up as salient. Thoughts, sensations, images, doubts, emotions, etc.

Fifth, say, “Please show or tell me what you need me to know or tell you what you need to know, and I’ll sit silently and listen for five minutes.”

Sixth, listen. It may be words, it may be a sensation or memory or color. It may be an inner child or protector, it may be pain or it may be warm and fuzzy. It may be silence. It may be something you need to DO or feel grateful about. It may be that you need to stop. It may be that you’re done for the day. Listen to it all and believe none of it.
