Rebel yes, but you can’t choose yet.

It’s not so much about a specific brain system, but it is useful to know that when you grow up avoiding pain and staying attached, then you don’t have the time or bandwidth to play and explore enough to find out what you like or don’t like, who you are, what you want to try out in the world, etc.

Sometimes it takes stepping away for a temporary but intentional time from the group or people for long enough so you can find out what you like, apart from the social feedback. But not too long, because that will not help you.

Now this is where the human doer and human being come in. If you don’t feel worthy inside of you, then you have to earn your worth outside of you, so you do that by being beautiful, or a great performer, smart, or achieve status, or be spiritual or needed by everyone. This is the bargain you make with yourself to feel that you belong. And if you don’t succeed in your chosen arena, then you feel you don’t exist.
