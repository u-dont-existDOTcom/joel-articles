Using synchronicity as your own wake-up bell

Some people are really interested in what synchronicity might mean. For some it leads to insights, for others it can be a kind of compulsive behaviour, just finding patterns. For me personally it is very helpful to use it as a kind of wake up bell.

Install yourself a wake up cue for whenever you see synchronicity like this:

Whenever I see synchronicity I use it as a wake up call. I immediately wake up in whatever state I am in, feeling my body and the room I am in. Seeing synchronicity doesn’t mean anything by itself. I immediately look at the situation with a wider view to see if I am seeing the situation objectively, or if I am looking at it through my current state. I look for the highest perspective I can find on the situation – without leaving my body, without leaving the facts and without leaving the human reality.

Then I let myself move into the highest state I can that is still objective – be it clarity, ease, peace, freedom, joy, gratitude or whatever.

You could say: Seeing synchronicity -> pause -> orient yourself -> widen -> see if you are in reality -> listen -> do one thing that you can do about the situation in a loving way.

This way you will not get stuck in the state, but you will still listen to the message that the state might have, especially if it is a negative state. It might be a message from the Inner Child, a Protector, a grief or a message from the body about a body problem or it might be a message that you really need to do something about the situation.
