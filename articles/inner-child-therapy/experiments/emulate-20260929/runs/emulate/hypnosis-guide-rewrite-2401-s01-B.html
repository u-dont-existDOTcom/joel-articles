The 4 kinds of NLP Sensory NLP -- changing the sensory formats (e.g., picture, sound, distance, size, brightness, volume, posture, body pressure, etc.)

State NLP -- developing easier access to desired states (e.g., warmth, adulthood, courage, steadiness, love, clarity, etc.)

Strategy NLP -- rehearsing a more effective strategy (When ____ comes up, I'll respond more like an adult by doing _____.)

Language NLP -- designing a sentence to pace reality, open awareness, presume capacities (that don't violate safety), establish a cause-effect link, embed a command, and/or ask a de-hypnotic question (when someone's being vague, absolute, manipulative, or grandiose).
