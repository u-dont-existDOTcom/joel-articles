The problem is that sometimes the pressure creates flooding, panic, false certainty, confabulation, dissociation, or just a general worse tuesday. When flooding happens it is harder for the adult to access their adult abilities (like pausing, accessing options, reality testing, planning, making choices etc.) so it is often better to help them regulate and feel supported before attempting to gain more insight through dialoging. At those times keeping the person in the dark can be a protective measure because the adult is unable to regulate, support, be humble and have the capacity to take in what the part has to share.

Instead try asking something like:

Is there a way for you to show me the content without breaking it open violently and without preparing me for it?

Is there a way to increase my conscious mind’s ability to take in the information just 5%?

Is there a way for you to show me the truth using symbols?

Is there something you can show me that will help me know how to protect you that doesn’t include opening up the information to my conscious mind yet?

Is there someone who can help you show me?

Or if you are talking to the subconscious mind in general you might say something like:

I know that sometimes you know things that my conscious mind can’t handle yet. I want you to know that I don’t want you to break open that information violently to my conscious mind, I want you to help prepare my conscious mind to take it in, help ground me so I can take it in, help me get the support I need to take it in, tell me what I need to do next to help you.

Please note that even when using hypnotic techniques to access imagery, it may not be literally what happened, the mind uses symbols, compression, distortion, cultural influences, fears etc. Get support from others if you are using this to work through trauma or memories.
