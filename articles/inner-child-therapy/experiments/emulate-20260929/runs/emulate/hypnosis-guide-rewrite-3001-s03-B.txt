Do not fight from fear

Our first response may be to fight, banish, hate, prove our power, argue, panic, and/or identify with the self that is being threatened. Often, prayers, mantras, protection, banishing and exorcism forms are useful. But often, if the fight comes from a place of fear, it may end up holding on tighter to what you want to banish.

In coming from fear, you give it centre stage. In coming from hate, you give it something to hook onto. In coming from pride, you give it an opponent. In coming from panic, you give it a nervous system.

I do not need to hate you. I do not need to merge with you. I do not need to obey you. I do not need to fight you from fear. I can come to a place of loving awareness.

Establish bodily boundaries. While coming from a place of compassion, still close the door.

If it feels hostile, cold, alien, parasitic, violating of your boundaries, don’t sit there and feel soft, sentimental love for it. Come from a place of love that has boundaries.

Sit up. Push your feet down. Lift your spine. Pull your shoulder blades back gently. Feel the boundary of your skin. Feel the boundary of your body.

While coming from compassion, you might say, “While I recognise that you are suffering, and while I want you to go back to the space that you belong in, in order to alleviate your suffering, I want to make it very clear that the boundary of my skin is the boundary of my adult life and you are not allowed into my house.”
