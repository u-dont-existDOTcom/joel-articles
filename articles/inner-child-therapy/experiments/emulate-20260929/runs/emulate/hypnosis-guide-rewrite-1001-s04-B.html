This is not the way to end a complaint. If there is anything true in the complaint hear it and respond to it. Your ability to hear the angry complaint, not to strike back, to see where you did not do what you were supposed to do, and to make up for it, are as much part of the evidence of your love as feeding and caring for your children, setting good limits, taking them to their appointments, and keeping your promises. Not striking back is a part of the expression of your love, not a replacement for it.

What you need to see is that no matter what the content of the vow and the blaming message, it is teaching the same lesson: that you cannot love until you are loved in a certain way. Sometimes these two messages come from different positions, sometimes they are blended and come from the same position. It does not matter how old you were when you received the message.

If the defendant is younger than you are now, remember to ask what the child knew at the time, what options he had, and what the consequences were of his choices. Even if he did wrong and has to take responsibility for it and make it up, he still does not need to be punished for it.

Two Hand Practice:

Put one of your hands on the center of your chest to connect with your ability to love. Put the other hand on your solar plexus just under your chest to connect with the child.

Send love from your heart down to the child, and see what comes back to you. Engage in a conversation with him.

Love is above. Child is below. Adult is there, too.
