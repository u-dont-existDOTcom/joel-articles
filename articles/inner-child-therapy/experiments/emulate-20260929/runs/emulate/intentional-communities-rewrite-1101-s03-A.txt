Some Cult Signs to watch out for on a daily basis: A person (usually the founder) becomes more and more central in the group’s consciousness. Disagreeing with him/her is increasingly regarded as a failure. Bad outcomes of group actions are not regarded as such, but as bad implementations. The more you know as an insider, the less you have to prove to the public. The group does not care about measuring it’s outcomes. People who used to belong to the group are seen as being contaminated by their time in the group.

The occurence of these things, even several at the same time, does not mean that there is something fishy going on. Still, it is worth observing them, before the founder of the group starts explaining why his/her particular situation is special and different.

How the model of this page could turn into a cult

All the practices presented in this article could be abused, for example

The principle of “nothing suppressed, everything processed” could be abused to force members to reveal stuff, as it happened in some ZEGG Forumin my opinion.

The practice of peer counseling could be abused to get to know stuff about members. Making doing some medicine a pre-requisite for joining could be a way for people to take control over the group. The principle of being willing to be seen could be abused to force people to reveal stuff.

This is why I regard the principles above as binding for myself, but not for the community.

I will work on not suppressing anything related to the material, but I will not try to get people to reveal anything. Members may abstain from revealing stuff without having to bring in therapeutic arguments why they do so.

Doing medicine will never be a requirement for being a member, and not doing it will not limit your rights in any way. Giving people the right to join the group will not be reserved to any person or closed group of people, and disagreeing with someone joining has to be stated openly.
