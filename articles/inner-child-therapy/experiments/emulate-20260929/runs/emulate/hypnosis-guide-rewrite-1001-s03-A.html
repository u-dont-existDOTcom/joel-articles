Going Younger is not Giving up your Adult Judgment

It’s possible for the lesser developed feelings state within you to communicate without you having to give your judgment to someone else to hold, someone outside yourself that you feel is more knowledgable, a caretaker. It’s not even necessary for you to go younger in real life to feel the wonder, the playfulness, the sensation, the vulnerablity, the need, the want to not have to make decisions.

I can allow a lesser developed part of myself to talk without giving my adult judgment to someone else to hold. I can allow a part of myself that is younger to talk without giving my judgment to a guide, a recording, a person. My adult self will always be there to listen, to evaluate, to protect, to stop what is going on if need be.

I can get back the feeling of wonder, the spontaneity, the playfulness, the sensation, the ability to state what I need, the ability to move emotion quickly without the obedience, the helplessness, the scolding, the lack of judgment.

I can love a feelings state in me that doesn’t trust that I love it.

Ok, you don’t believe me. That’s fine. I believe that you don’t believe me. But I still love you. And I don’t need you to love me. Tell me what you need to know to believe that I love you.
