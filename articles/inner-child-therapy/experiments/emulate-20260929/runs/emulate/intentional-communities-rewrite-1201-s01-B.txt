Before we get into the math of absorption, it may be useful to describe for whom this is not intended.

The honest truth is, that many intelligent adults would rather spend six hours discussing how to put solar panels on the roof, than talking about their feelings of jealousy, or God, or death, or childhood, or money, or sex, or psychedelics, or how to deal with someone wanting to leave.

This isn’t so much a matter of being “good enough” for a community, but more of a matter of having enough attention to devote to the work of community, and the work on self, and also, not taking up too much of that capacity.

Some people have spent many years in contemplative or therapeutic practices, and some have been lucky in their childhood, but many are in the midst of difficult times that need a lot of care. Most of us have been there at one time or another in our life.

One of the things that we try to do, is divide up some of the functions that many people have dealt with through therapists, by developing peer counseling capacities, so that the basic emotional work that everyone has to deal with, can be dealt with in a community of peers.
