When “your voice” is everybody else’s voice

It's possible that you may not be able to connect with your inner child at all. This tends to be more common when you grew up in a very controlling environment - whether a family, religion, community, achievement-oriented, etc. When you were pressured to perform at a young age, it's possible you were never in touch with how you felt or what you wanted, or believed, or liked, etc. because you were told all those things. In this case, the command to "love yourself" may seem moot because you don't have a self to love! In this case, do the reparenting things above, but also work on building your sense of self. For some people, connecting with the child self is easy. For others, it requires creating the safety to be able to try things out.

You may need to do this if:

You don't know what you want unless someone tells you what to want

You find your opinions differ drastically based on who you are with

You find yourself agreeing to things in the moment, and then you later realize you didn't agree

You feel responsible for other people's emotions to the detriment of yourself

Trying to love yourself feels like you're putting on a show

When you ask your inner child how they feel, you can only guess
