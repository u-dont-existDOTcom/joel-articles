Appendix C: The extended de-hypnosis checklist

Checklist to hold when someone, something, some ad, ideology, spiritual thing, sexual thing or voice inside you is pressuring you to do something.

First state “I can be aware of this influence and not go along with it or resist it. I can slow down and ask questions of it and I can use my senses and ask questions from love, truth and humility in ordinary life.
Second state “If it’s true it will be ok to ask questions. If it’s love it will be ok to set boundaries. If it’s true guidance it will be there after some sleep. If it’s really for me, I won’t have to give up being a functioning adult to do it.
Then ask yourself these questions.

Are there any elements of a) urgency b) secrecy c)shame d) threats e) specialness f)sexual charge g) spiritual superiority h) asking for money, sex, loyalty, silence or following commands i) making you feel like you have no other choices j) making you feel bad for questioning it k) making you feel like there’s something wrong with you for not going along with it l) making ordinary life seem bad or boring m) appealing to some child part inside you to either seduce, scare or ‘rescue’ that part n) making you feel like you can’t say no.

If several of these are true, don’t go further into trance and make decisions. Come back into your body, into the room, get some food, sleep and time before making any decisions.
