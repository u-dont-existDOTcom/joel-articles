When Somebody Has to Go

A safe community might need to eject people who turn out to be dangerously (in a serious and generalized way) unfit for it, perhaps even despite efforts at restorative justice (within a reasonable timeframe).

But this is a power subject to terrible abuses, so having a pre-established process for how this will be done, and what will constitute cause for protective action, is extremely important. Among other things, this needs to include provisions for evidence, recusal, temporary alternatives, reply, appeal, separation (housing, resources, money, records, family), and outside assistance.

Note that "outside" was originally written with nation-state institutions in mind, but that was probably a bad idea. What's actually needed here is just some competent outside layer, which could conceivably include other communities, other layers of a federation, "professional" services, or even autonomist regions' own courts/regulators/emergency services (as in the Zapatistas). (Although autonomism doesn't let you waive your non-waivable individual rights!)
