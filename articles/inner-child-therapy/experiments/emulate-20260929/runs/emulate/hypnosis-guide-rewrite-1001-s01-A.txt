Who are your inner child, guards, and 3 inner adults?

Your inner child doesn’t trust you yet just because you said a few kind sentences to him or her during meditation. Your inner child needs to build trust in you, your present day you.

For convenience I divide up your present day you in 3 functions. These are not real ‘beings’, but just 3 functions you as a present day person can use to reparent your inner child. These 3 functions are the Nurturer, Protector and Leader/Guru.

Your inner child is worth so much more, but that’s not the point here. S/he is playful, tender, irreverent, creative, full of wonder etc. etc.

What if you can’t take the ‘present day you’ role yet?

Some people can call up one of the above mentioned functions quite easily, others can’t. Others try, but then their mind goes blank, or they only seem to have the inherited critic, or they realize they are still a child pretending to be an adult.

To deal with this I use a ‘bridge’ to a complete present day you. This ‘bridge’ is what I for the time being call the borrowed adulthood. It’s something I developed myself, based on many ‘proven’ parts, although the whole is not proven yet.

If needed, learn to witness. If you can already differentiate the different inner ‘positions’ within you, and know who needs to be brought in, go straight to the next step.
