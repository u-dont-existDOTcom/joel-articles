That’s all, and now return fully… When it suits you, at a later time, deliberately begin your practice and try out the shortcut. If it helps, you can gradually reduce the length of the longer induction. If not, continue with what you know works. Don’t worry about trying it a certain number of times, or achieving a certain depth instantly, or whether or not it feels the same.

What is important is that, as ever, it works when you want it to, and that it doesn’t when you don’t. It also needs to be the case that, if you change your mind, you can get back to where you were. It should not work as if you were being obeying a narrator, or a notification, or somebody you overheard. The fact that you may be able to ‘get under’ faster does not affect what you can then work with.

Please don’t drive, or do anything which requires you to be actively paying attention, while practising, testing, or using the hypnotic re-entry, even if your eyes are open, and even if you include the conditional part.

If, for whatever reason, you no longer want to use the re-entry cue, simply stop rehearsing it, remove it from any recording (or, if you are using an app, stop following the active instructions) and return to using the induction that you are familiar with. Although you could say something to yourself to ‘cancel’ it while you are awake, even that is not necessary in order for the association to be removed. If you do find that the problem persists, or that you are finding it disorientating, please seek qualified assistance. If, however, you do decide to keep the re-entry cue, remember that it is only intended to take you into a session, at a future time, which you choose separately. When you come back from being under, that altered state of mind has finished for the day.
