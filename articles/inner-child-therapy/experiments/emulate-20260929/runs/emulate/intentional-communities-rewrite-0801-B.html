The Grown Children Get the Final Review

My own childhood violated a lot of “mainstream” rules, so it’s a little hard to talk about what I don’t want to repeat in a children’s community I’m helping to create, but I will try. First, I need to say that I’m really glad my mother nursed me till I was five, I’m really glad my parents didn’t vaccinate me, and I’m really glad for a lot of stuff that my parents did for me that is not exactly mainstream.

The vaccination thing is important to me, so if we’re going to have children in a community, we need to be in agreement on some basic values. I don’t want to create a community that everyone will like, but we do need some agreement on health issues, etc. when it comes to children.

What a grown child of this community says about it is far more important than what the founders of the community say about it.
