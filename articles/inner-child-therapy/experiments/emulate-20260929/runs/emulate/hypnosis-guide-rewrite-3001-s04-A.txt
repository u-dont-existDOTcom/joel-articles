If it might be psychosis or destabilization

What I’m talking about is something that’s clearly symbolic and that is resolved easily through grounding and generating feelings of love. This is not what I am talking about:

If you feel like you are under attack for days on end, unable to sleep or function, feel compelled to hurt yourself or others, are hearing voices (especially command hallucinations), experiencing mania, or simply are unable to communicate or tell whether what you are experiencing is real or “just” symbolic, then you need to seek help. This is not an issue to be treated with hypnosis.

If these techniques are helping you be more loving, grounded, relational, honest, and functional, then keep on keeping on. If they are making you more paranoid, sleepless, grandiose, isolated, scared, or unreal, then stop and get help.

The “Cosmic Download” Test for Maintaining Humility

Sometimes the hostile presences, the shadow, the grandiose parts of you, and the imagery you see in altered states want to mess with you by making you feel special, important, and chosen. Sometimes they will give you “downloads” from “cosmic” sources to make you feel like you are so important that you shouldn’t be wasting your time washing dishes or doing whatever mundane task you need to be doing.

If this happens, thank them for the imagery, but tell them that unless it helps you wash the dishes, clean up your room, or do whatever mundane tasks you need to do, it’s not helpful on your path.
