Things you need to know:

This may be extremely un-mystical. That’s OK. Try: Will you set the boundary? Will you keep the promise? Will you stop abandoning me? Will you stop substituting insight for action?

The inner child will not appear

If you can recognize a guard, talk to it. If not, just talk to the feeling or preference you are experiencing. Try: Would you let me know what I need to do to make it safe for you to let me in?

If no child-state appears, that’s OK too. Just focus on building your identity or reparenting yourself in this session. Try focusing on: preferences, curiosities, or something you can do as a beginner.

You get anxious

First, is it manageable and do you want to work with it? If not, you’ll need to dial down the contact you are having. If it starts to ramp up and you feel overwhelmed, you need to focus on the body, not the content. Try:

Ground yourself. Feel your feet on the ground or your butt in the chair. Slow your breathing. Look around you and name things you see.

Talk to the child. Tell the child you are there for them. Talk to the protector and thank it for trying to protect you. Let it know you don’t need it to scream at you for you to pay attention. If there is something that needs to be done, ask it to tell you what it is. If there is nothing to do, tell the protector it can stand down.

If your anxiety is accompanied by chest pain, fainting, suicidal thoughts, psychosis-like symptoms, mania-like symptoms, severe dissociative symptoms, inability to sleep, or urges to hurt yourself or someone else, you need to get professional help. For more, see the anxiety toolbox in Appendix D .
