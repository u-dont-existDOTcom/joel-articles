Many approaches to hypnosis have seemed too mechanical to me, a matter of giving commands to the subconscious. I have preferred to use hypnosis to communicate with and listen to the different parts of my mind -- the frightened inner child, protective parts, the body, intuitive parts, and the part of me that needs to function in day-to-day life.

In Using Hypnosis to Communicate with Your Subconscious Mind , you'll learn how to use self-hypnosis to bring your conscious mind, subconscious mind, body, inner child, protectors, adult part, and Higher Self into a meeting, by gathering their attention.

Then you can proceed with the simple approach of listening to them, warming up to them, asking for protection, getting clarity about what you need from them and what they need from you, and then doing some reality testing, moving into action.

There's no need for dramatic trance behavior, though you may have some. You'll find this approach effective if it leads to a better relationship between the parts of your mind, and if it proves effective in your life.

I find it helps to think of this as communion -- no need for the conscious mind to shout out its demands from the roof of the house while the subconscious mind is being neglected, forcing it to communicate in the only ways it can -- through problems, dreams, cravings, daydreams, and other messages from the body.
