Dopamine Receptor Burnout (DAWS Pattern) – Uridine Monophosphate

This is also not for everyone with depression, it’s for a certain population that seems to have burned out dopamine receptors. The symptom of low dopamine receptor expression seems to be a gray, anhedonic feeling, lack of vivid dreaming, low visuals on psychedelics, needing more of whatever you’re using to feel good, an ever-escalating desire to use things like ketamine, lsd, or other novel interventions to get a dopamine “hit”. This seems to be similar to DAWS (dopamine agonist withdrawal syndrome) that occurs when patients taper off dopamine agonists like pramipexole and ropinirole – “DAWS is characterized by anxiety, panic, dysphoria, depression, agitation, fatigue, autonomic symptoms, pain, and drug cravings after discontinuation or dose reduction of dopamine agonists.” In my case, I believe I experienced DAWS from downregulation of dopamine D2/D3 receptors in my reward system from taking dopamine agonists, which was exacerbated by attempting to “self-medicate” by hitting myself with frequent lsd microdoses and other things like ketamine to stimulate dopamine. Long story short, it’s a very specific phenotype and likely not useful for everyone.
