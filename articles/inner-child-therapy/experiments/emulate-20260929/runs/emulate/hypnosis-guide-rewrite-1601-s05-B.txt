Step 1: If you have any impulses, try them out. Moving, or even visualizing moving, in a way that is small, voluntary, and physically comfortable. If the movements start to feel uncomfortable, painful, or create other symptoms you don’t like, stop the movements. You do not have to act out any impulses, shake, or release in this exercise.

Provide what you are being asked for.

If you feel like you are younger, provide yourself with the company you are asking for. You do not have to literally become younger, or even know the history of why you are younger. If you recognize that you are a protector, hear what you have to say, but you don’t have to step aside. No one is being exiled in this meeting.

See if providing yourself with company, or space, is what you need.

If the answer is “no, don’t come any closer,” then don’t come any closer, and notice what it is like to have your answer heard. Remember, each Nurturer may have a different tone they use when they provide what is needed. Each Protector may have a different way of protecting. Each Leader will lead you in a different way that you want to live your life. You don’t have to say anything to them, you just need to take on their function until you can do that function yourself. Only use the Witness to get you started in a step, don’t start any steps over with the Witness.
