Lots of communities, including Twin Oaks, East Wind, Acorn and many other intentional communities and ecovillages welcome visitors. If you’re interested in visiting any community, check their web site for up-to-date information on their visitor requirements.

Visiting will help you explore what you like and don’t like about togetherness, privacy, labor systems, communal showers, and all the other details that will make or break a community for you.

Don’t start looking for land until you’ve done these three things.

I’m not going to sell you a application form or a retreat. These manuals are free. I’m in Sénégal now, where I expect to buy land. I won’t advertise a commune when I do: wherever I create the conditions for love, play, work and truthfulness, people will want to come and be a part of it.

My father was shaped by the Bruderhof commune, which led him to make a movie, start a research organization, and carry me along on many communal experiments. It’s been decades since then, but I’m publishing the movie now and writing these manuals.

Maybe that will be enough for someone to build something based on them, and for that something to teach enough other people to build something, and so on.

If you do build something, maybe you’ll want to let me know. But if you teach enough other people to build something, it won’t matter.
