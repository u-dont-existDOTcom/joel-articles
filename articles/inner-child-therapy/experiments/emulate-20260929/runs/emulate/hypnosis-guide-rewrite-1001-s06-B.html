The Leader, Guide, or Guru

This is a description of the function of Leader. You might alternatively call this function, Guide, Guru, Wise One, Sage, Inner Teacher, Director, Higher Adult, or whatever name feels accurate to you.

Leader helps us to know where we are going. Sometimes being true to Leader means doing something difficult. But it is not about perfectly disciplined, disciplined all the time, ignoring all our needs. Sometimes, Leader knows the most adult thing to do is ask for help. Sometimes it is expressing a preference. Sometimes it is allowing someone who is safe to see our imperfections. Sometimes it is knowing that it’s ok to want closeness with others without punishing them for needing space. Sometimes it’s finding a way to meet our need in a different way.

Here are some quotes that might be helpful for you to share with your Leader:

We are not only here to feel good. We are here to be whole. We are here to be part of life. We are here to serve truth, beauty, love, God, source, nature, universe, dharma, Tao, the benefit of all beings.

Start with the adult function that is most accessible to you. If you need to start with protection, that’s fine. Then make sure that you give direction to the child in a way that doesn’t invalidate the child’s need for support and play.

Working with protectors

Note: If you are not working with protectors, or this language doesn’t feel useful to you, skip over this section. Be aware that just because you have a sensation, or get distracted, or think of something that doesn’t feel right, that doesn’t mean that a protector is present. It could be that you are hungry, or that you have a legitimate reason for not feeling that something is right, or that a sentence just doesn’t feel right to you for some reason.
