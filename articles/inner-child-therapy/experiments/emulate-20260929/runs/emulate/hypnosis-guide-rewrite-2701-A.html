App D) How to know if daydreaming is being useful or unhelpful

Sometimes daydreaming is useful - it’s a form of intuition, simulation, helping us digest emotional pain, creative, helping us rehearse, teaching us in metaphor, or it’s the subconscious showing us something about the future or something we’re afraid of or want. Sometimes it’s a form of avoiding what’s real - addiction to fantasy, a dissociation, obsession with a romantic idea, a way to not grow up.

Then ask yourself what you’re avoiding doing in real life - sending a message, not sending a message, cleaning up, eating, sleeping, grieving, being creative, apologising, leaving, telling the truth, planning, asking for help, getting on with what you need to get on with, or pursuing unavailable love.
