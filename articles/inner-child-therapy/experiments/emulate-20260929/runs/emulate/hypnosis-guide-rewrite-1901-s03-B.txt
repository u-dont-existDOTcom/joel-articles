You dissociate or feel unreal

Sit up, open your eyes, move your body, touch something textured, name five objects out loud, etc., until the practice dies down and you can go back to your day.

You’re getting grandiose

Go eat, sleep, do chores, let someone humble you, etc., until you realize: “If this is true, it has to be able to survive sleep, food, time, humbling, feedback, etc.”

You’re getting too analytical

Stop what you’re doing (it’s become too technical/performance-y), put your hands on your heart and solar plexus, and say: “I’m not here to do this. I’m here to be in relationship with something far deeper than my surface mind.” Then ask what you need to do to transform the experience into something more communion-oriented.

Often the answer is to be silent, to rest, or to go do something. Do it.

You’re getting too blissful/diffuse

Find something specific to do that comes from love. Ask love what it is.

You feel like you have to find out what you’re supposed to be hiding from yourself right now

Don’t do that. Say: “Show me the capacity, not the content.”

You keep daydreaming

Seriously, just sit up and open your eyes.

“What real action is this fantasy replacing?”

You get stuck in spiritual bypass
