7) Any exchange or obligation between us should be honest. Even if we don't use money amongst ourselves, we'll need to distinguish between giving food, medicine, loans of vehicles or tools, reciprocal labour exchange, housing someone temporarily, helping build a building, and travelling to another community for training or relationship building. Some of these create obligations, others don't. We can keep records for each purpose without needing to try to make them equal or to put a price on everything. When we use cash in interfacing with the outside world, we will similarly need to distinguish between grants, loans, guarantees and shared enterprise commitments. And while we stand in solidarity with each other, we shouldn't agree to unlimited liability for each other, nor have unlimited access to each others labour, stores, land or tools.

When two principles are found to be in conflict in practice, record the case (what the conflicting principles were, what the initial thoughts and final reasoning were, what dissent there was, what the consequences were and what the exact text of the rules were at the time). When the rule is changed, run the case again.
