Communes Are Hot Again 🔥

Do you remember the movie my dad made a while ago about how people were longing for village again? The US Surgeon General just released a 2023 Advisory on loneliness, declaring that about 50% of Americans are experiencing it, and that we need to work on cultivating social connection as a matter of public health.

Whether or not we want to call it an “epidemic”, it’s clear that a very large number of people have reached the end of their ability to tolerate the increasingly isolated modern lifestyle. And these are people all over the political spectrum, all over the food spectrum, all over the God spectrum, all over the sex spectrum, all over the shoes are social oppression spectrum, etc.

Check out the google trends for ecovillage and intentional community. After years of decline, interest is skyrocketing in the mid-2020s.

I have no idea if this is related to AI or not, but it does roughly coincide with the time when AI started to significantly impact people’s lives.
