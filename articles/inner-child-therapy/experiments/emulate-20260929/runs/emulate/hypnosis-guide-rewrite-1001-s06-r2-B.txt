The Leader, Guide, or Guru

I’m talking about the Leader aspect of the self. You may prefer another name like Leader, Guide, Guru, Wise One, Sage, Inner Teacher, Director, Higher Adult, etc. The Leader gives direction.

Sometimes this direction involves asking for something difficult. The Leader doesn’t need to resort to perfect discipline and denying our needs. Often, the most mature, “adult” thing to do is ask for help, ask for what we want, or acknowledge our imperfections to someone we feel safe enough to do that with. It’s ok to want to be close with others, but punishing them when we need space is not the only strategy to getting our need for space met.

“We are not here just to feel good. We are here to be whole. We are here to connect to Life. We are here to serve truth, beauty, love, God, source, nature, the universe, dharma, Tao, and the betterment of all beings.”

Work on bringing the different aspects of the Adult online. Sometimes we have to get the Protective function online first. And remember that the Direction aspect of Adult is not just to help the Child function in the world, but also to take care of and support the Child’s need for play and fun.

Working With the Protectors

Again, only use this language if you are aware of these types of patterns and it makes sense to explore them. Just because something is happening in you doesn’t mean you know why.
