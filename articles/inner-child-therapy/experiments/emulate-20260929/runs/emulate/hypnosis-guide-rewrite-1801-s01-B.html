The weekly deeper practice

It may be useful to spend more time, but not necessarily more intensity, on the same practice a few times a week. Find your Higher Self or go into a neutral witnessing mode; follow where you are led to use adult function and examine whether you are using bypassing; take whatever exit you need to take, completely; and take whatever action seems needed in ordinary reality.

If possible, take the action you need to take immediately, so that your subconscious sees that you did it and does not need to set up another internal meeting to convince you to do something as simple as washing the dishes.

Capacity Before Content Rule

Do not request that the subconscious bring you information or truth in an altered state if doing so would be medically unsafe or if doing so could lead to panic attacks, sleep deprivation, nausea, destabilization, mania, psychotic-like symptoms, or too much chemical alteration: stabilize first. Truth that will destabilize you is not ready to come out yet; request capacity, not content.

Your subconscious may have information or truth you are not ready for in conscious state, about your sexuality, grief, trauma, family, betrayal, shame, vocation, relationship, illness, spirituality, addiction, identity, morality, or

You may feel the need to interrogate the subconscious:

“I demand you tell me right now!”

“Tell me everything right this second!”

“Stop hiding it from me!”
