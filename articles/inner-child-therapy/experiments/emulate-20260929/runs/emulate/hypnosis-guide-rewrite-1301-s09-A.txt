How to Create a Shortcut to Getting Back into Hypnosis

Before you create a shortcut to getting back into hypnosis, make sure you have at least one good way of getting into hypnosis that you find useful, and a good way of coming out of hypnosis completely. My dad used to put two fingers together in a mudra (like the “ok” sign) to put himself into a relaxed state, but I don’t recommend putting yourself into hypnosis while driving! It’s better to have one form of alert, calming hypnosis for that, and use a different way of cueing yourself for hypnosis when you want to go inside.

Pick a word or phrase, and if you like, a gesture. Don’t use a peak emotional time of the hypnosis, or a child state, or a trauma dealing scene to pair up with the shortcut, but a time when you’re in a good, receptive state that you’re familiar with from other hypnosis. Program yourself that when you use that word or phrase (and gesture if you have one), you will go into that state, but only when you’ve deliberately set aside time and are in an appropriate place to do so. Also program yourself that when you use that word or phrase, you can change what you’re doing in hypnosis or come out of hypnosis.
