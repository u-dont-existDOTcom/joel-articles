Quick start: do this first

The Pattern Gather Attention. Really notice things. Offer a response. Wait. Return all the way back. (Optional: pick something difficult, or something you want to love or be curious or playful or confident about.) Note: nothing needs to be dramatic!

Preparation Read both the induction below and the full return in waking life before your first session. Make sure you have time and that you’re physically secure wherever you’ll be. Pick one thing you want to do. Pick what you’re leaving alone today. Put screens away, this isn’t a checklist, you’re conducting.

The Induction Respond to whatever you notice – sensation, image, phrase, feeling, objection, silence… Let your response guide you to the next response. Bring in more warmth, or set boundaries, or get some direction, or take your time however you need to. When you’re ready, do the full return. Take note of what happened and what you need to do in the real world.

Exception If you feel panicky, disoriented, very unreal, or like you can’t function, you should probably return (and get whatever support is appropriate). This is different from choosing to stay with a difficult feeling.
