If your state is already opened, go straight to ‘contain the process before you deepen it’:

Your state may already be opened – by loveyhuasca, meditation, entering a lucid dream, letting yourself feel grief, listening to music, prayer, erotic energy, exhaustion, experiencing something beautiful etc. In this case it is better to stabilise the process before deepening it.

  1. Acknowledge the fact that your state is already opened, and that you don’t need to deepen it, you just need to aim and stabilise it.

Look around you, remind yourself where you are, what date it is (or at least what day of the week it is), feel your body – your feet, hands, spine etc. Use your thumbs to push firmly against your first two fingers, and focus on the physical friction you are creating.

Remind yourself that only that which you can integrate will be allowed to appear. Your body will be kept safe, and you are an adult, so you don’t need to let the ‘inner child’ take over.

Once you feel oriented enough, make your intention, for example: “Show me what I need to know – whether it be a truth, a symbol, a feeling, a question, an action etc. But only show me as much as I can integrate.”
