Stabilize before you interpret

There are many times when we may engage in powerful states of self hypnosis - Loveyhuasca, meditation, Lucid Dreaming, grieving, sexual energy, prayer, lack of sleep, and many other times during our plant medicine journey.

What I want to point out is that not every powerful state is meaningful. Sometimes it is, sometimes it is our inner child trying to communicate. Sometimes it is a protector showing us it's fear. Sometimes it is a time to process shame, grief, anger, longing, etc. However, sometimes our body is just experiencing too much, there is too much chaos in our environment, lack of sleep, and we are simply in a confused state. Not every image you see is meaningful, not every bad feeling is here to teach you something, and not every download you get at 2am when you've set up all of the proper lighting is something you should believe.

So, what to do before you begin to interpret the meaning of any state:

“What kind of problem is happening?”
