In any given moment, you may ask yourself:

“Where am I not being myself in order to belong?”

And then you can ask yourself: “Is this guidance or is this belonging?”, “Is this love or is this fear?”, “Is this conscience or is this conditioning?”, “Is this devotion or is this compliance”, “Is this rebellion or is this freedom?”

And then, wait for your body to tell you. It’s not always right, but it’s worth asking.
