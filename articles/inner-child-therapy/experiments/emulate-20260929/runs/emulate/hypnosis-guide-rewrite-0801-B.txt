Why doing it myself worked better for me

It’s rather interesting you should ask, I find that self-hypnosis is much more useful than seeing a hypnotist. They did very little for me. Perhaps it was because I was sober at the time and didn’t feel suggestible.

When I do self-hypnosis, I can stop when something doesn’t feel quite right and ask my subconscious what it means by something or if a certain image is intended to be guidance, fear, avoidance, wishful thinking, etc. I can hear a “no” from my body when my conscious mind is trying to convince me something that isn’t true.

It’s hard for another person to do that when they aren’t in your mind when you’re receiving suggestions. Of course there can be benefits to having someone guide you through the process so that you don’t miss something that you aren’t aware of or so that you don’t do something incorrectly, but there are definite benefits to doing it yourself.
