A useful map of the mind

In this guide, for simplicity we often talk to the entire subconscious mind as if it were one entity. The subconscious encompasses all of your perceptions, emotional reactions, desires, aversions, inner images, memories, body signals, habits, intuitive guidance, and automatic behavior. Sometimes it is better to think of the subconscious as one mind. Other times, it is better to be more precise and think of it as an ecology of parts. It is all just maps. You don’t have to form a religious cult around any of them.

Sometimes we work with parts for greater precision. Sometimes we work with the Higher Self for greater coherence. Often we move back and forth in communion with both.

When we want coherence, we talk to the entire subconscious as one entity:

Dear Wise Subconscious Mind…

When we want greater precision, we acknowledge that the subconscious encompasses different parts with different needs and agendas: a child part who wants love, a protector part who wants to be safe, a critical part who wants to maintain certain standards, a fantasy-making part, body memory parts, social parts who want to be popular, a spiritual part who wants to be close to God or source or to benefit all, a wise part who wants to know what is true, etc.

Sometimes we start by working with all of the parts as one, to avoid scattering the system:

Dear Higher Self, Universal Heart-Mind, or Wise Subconscious…

But then, when necessary, we differentiate:

Which part is afraid? Which part is protecting? Which part is avoiding? Which part knows what to do? Which part is too young for this?

And then we always return to a sense of oneness – all of these parts are here to contribute to one life, ideally led by the loving adult self.
