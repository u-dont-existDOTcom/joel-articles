Then, once you’ve done it (or not!) you can then see what happened, and how it made you feel. It’s important to remember that if you didn’t feel any better after doing it, that doesn’t mean it was a waste of time. And if you did feel better, that doesn’t mean you should always do that thing. Maybe it’s something you need to do to keep you safe, or be close to someone, or help you do something you like, or get better at something – most of the time you can just tick it off. And if you didn’t do the thing, that doesn’t mean anything either. Maybe you didn’t feel you could do it, or you didn’t want to do it in the end, or you didn’t know how to do it, or you were too tired, or the friend you thought you could ask to help you didn’t reply.
