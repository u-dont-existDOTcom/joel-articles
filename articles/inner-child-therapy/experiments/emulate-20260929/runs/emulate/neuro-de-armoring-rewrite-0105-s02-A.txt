The Fix (what I call the “Jammer”): It has been discovered recently that in order to restore memories, a certain amount of visual processing power is needed. If you use up that processing power while the memory is being restored by doing a visual-spatial working memory task (like playing Tetris) the memory will still be restored but with a permanent decrease in the “sting”

My favorite way to do this (it doesn’t require an app or even a phone and I find it works better than simply counting) is what I call “Color-Path Connect”. This works because it forces your brain to map 3-D space and uses up the processing power your brain would normally use to “save” the memory. It works like this:

1) Pick a color (let’s say Blue) 2) Find all the blue things in the room and connect them in your mind from left to right 3) To make it harder (you’ll need to do this for 10-20 mins right after being triggered) rotate the objects in your mind, or imagine them changing into a different shape (so the blue chair might now become a blue cube)
