How do we take synchronicity out of retirement? Stop rehearsing it. Go into full wakefulness and say something like “from this moment forward I am dissolving the association between synchronicity and cue. Synchronicity is no longer going to be a cue for me.” Then take it off your list of cues and stop rehearsing.

Reframing – changing the meaning of something so that it is not a lie.

A common form of reframing is to find out how the “problem” is actually a good thing. This is a bit of a lie in my view. I prefer more honest forms of reframing, such as asking “what else is true?” Or “what is the positive intent of this part?” Or asking what various parts of you would say: “What would my Nurturer say?” “What would my Protector say?” “What would my Leader say?”

Perceptual Positions – seeing from a different point of view.

The common perceptual positions are from your own point of view; from inside the other person; from a witness position; from the point of view of the universal heart. A key thing to remember is that perceptual positions should not be used to let people off the hook, but rather to get yourself out of the prison you are creating for yourself by only seeing from one angle.

Future Pacing – going into the future to test things out.

A common failure of future pacing is not going far enough into the future. Don’t just say “I now understand what I need to do.” Go into a place in the future where you will be tested, see the cue, and rehearse doing what you need to do.
