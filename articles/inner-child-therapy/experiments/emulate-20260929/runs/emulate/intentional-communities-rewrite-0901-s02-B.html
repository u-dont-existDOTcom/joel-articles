As I’ve said, the Fire and the Word, its movement goes from family to village, village to region, region to larger structures. Here’s a great thing we can learn from the Zapatistas for community design: always do the practice first, then the theory. First they did the autonomous municipalities. Then they rehearsed with the association of autonomous municipalities, which gave them the experience to do the Good Government Boards. But, each municipality was facing different problems and advancing at different rates, so what did they do? Practice first, then theory! They created yet another structure.

In 2023, the Zapatistas announced the dissolution of the Rebel Zapatista Autonomous Municipalities and the Good Government Juntas, after an enormous amount of internal review, in favor of moving forward with Local Autonomous Governments, one in each community. They clarified that these local governments could, of course, create coordinating collectives at the regional level, but these would be dependent on the Local Autonomous Governments and not superior to them.

Most institutions, as they age, accumulate more and more layers and offices. The Zapatistas did a review and pushed authority back down. Another thing they didn’t do: write a book on leadership and start a certification program. They changed the system they are living in.
