Quick start: do this first

Gathering attention & noticing one thing → responding to just that thing in a way that fits → waiting for what happens next → coming all the way back out. It can be used to address difficulties or just to cultivate something pleasant, like love, curiosity, playfulness, confidence, etc. Nothing dramatic needs to happen.

Read through the induction and full return while you’re awake so you know what to expect when you close your eyes. Find a time and place where you’ll be physically secure for the entire time. Set an intention for what you want to get out of this. What will you focus on? What will you leave alone today? Just pick one thing. Once you’re ready, put this screen out of your awareness and conduct the experience yourself according to the instructions.

You’ll have a response in the form of a sensation, an image, a phrase, a feeling, an objection, or just silence. Follow the instructions to respond to your response, and then to come completely back out. Afterwards, make notes of what happened and whether it calls for any actions in the “real world”.

If you become panicky, disoriented, overly “unreal”, or otherwise impaired in your ability to function, this is not doing what you chose to do. Bring yourself back out to the room and whatever else is appropriate. This is different from feeling bad if you chose to experience something that you know will feel bad.
