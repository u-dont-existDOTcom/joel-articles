The Leader, Guide, or Guru

Leader: one function of this part might be to provide direction for the system. You might want to call this part Guide, Guru, Wise One, Sage, Inner Teacher, Director, Higher Adult, or whatever feels right for you.

You might say something like, “Part of my job is to provide direction for the system, even if the direction is difficult. I don’t need to be perfectly disciplined and have no needs myself in order to do that, though. Sometimes the most adult thing for me to do is to ask for help, or to express a preference or an imperfection to someone I trust is safe. And while the desire for closeness is legitimate, punishing someone for needing space is not a good strategy for getting it. Let’s think of other ways to get this need met.”

You might also say something like, “We are not only here to feel good. We are here to be whole. We are here to be part of life. We are here to serve truth, beauty, love, God, source, nature, the universe, dharma, Tao, the benefit of all beings, etc.”

Begin with whatever Adult function feels most accessible to you. Work on Protection. Work on Direction in a way that is nurturing, playful, supportive, etc.

Note: if you don’t recognize parts or if the language of parts doesn’t make sense to you, you might skip this section. Just because you have a sensation, or get distracted, or think of something to say, doesn’t mean there’s a part behind it. It might just be a need for food, a legitimate skepticism, a sentence that doesn’t ring true, etc.
