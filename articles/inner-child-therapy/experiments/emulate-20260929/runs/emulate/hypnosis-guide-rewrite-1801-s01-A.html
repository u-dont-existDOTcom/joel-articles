The weekly deeper practice

This will be once or even twice a week for a while, and if you wish, you can lengthen this time. It doesn't need to be more intense. You can start the process with your Higher Self or a neutral witnessing part, ask what comes up to be followed, get an adult to help if needed, find out if you're bypassing, and then allow the process to exit fully so that you can take whatever action is needed.

If it's appropriate, take the action immediately (such as washing the dishes) so that your subconscious realizes that you don't need it to act out to get you to do something.

Capacity before content

There is one more rule that must be applied when using altered states: Do not ask for something that would be medically unsafe for you to know at the present time, could cause you to panic, could deprive you of sleep, might make you feel nauseated, could destabilize you, may cause mania, might create psychosis-like symptoms, or could result in your being out of control chemically. If you need to be stabilized first, then do that. It's not the time to go directly for the truth if you'll be destabilized by it. You are working for capacity, not content.

What kind of content might your subconscious have that your conscious self needs to avoid? It could be related to sexuality, grief, trauma, a family truth, betrayal, shame, vocation, a relationship, fears about illness, a spiritual calling, an addiction, a question of identity, or a moral issue.
