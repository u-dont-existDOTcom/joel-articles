The Grown Children Get the Final Review

I could write a bit about my own childhood, which flew in the face of most mainstream rules, but I think I’ll save that for another time. In short, however, I was nursed until age five, not vaccinated, and generally enjoyed all that my parents gave me (which I am very grateful for, by the way – almost all of it).

I think the vaccination point is a good one to throw in there, though, because that’s something that will decide if we’re able to raise our children in community or not. You have to have some common values if you’re going to put your heads together and form a community around raising kids, I think.

An actual review from a grown child of the community would be much more valuable than anything the founders could say.
