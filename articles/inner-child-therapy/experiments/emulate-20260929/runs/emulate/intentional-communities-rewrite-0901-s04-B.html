I looked pretty hard for such a chain of evidence during my research. I found some evidence of alumni networks and attempts to organize those that had completed the Escuelita back in their home countries, but I wasn’t able to find a clear chain of evidence linking an Escuelita to the creation of an organization to data on how well members of that organization (and their kids, if the organization had a pedagogical component) did after the fact. Another note is that the Zapatistas were already influential on an international stage before the Escuelita, so the mere fact that something is similar to Zapatista practices doesn’t necessarily mean it was influenced by the Escuelita. Rather, it seems more accurate to say that people are being influenced by the Zapatistas to try to replicate their pedagogical model.
