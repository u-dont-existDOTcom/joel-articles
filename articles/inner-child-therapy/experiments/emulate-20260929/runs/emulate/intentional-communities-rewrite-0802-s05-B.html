It is true that a kids upbringing is going to limit his or her ability to do what he or she wants with his or her life. Also it would be rare to find a normal person giving their kid a bunch of money to go out in to the world at age 18. If the whole community has little or no money, then I am sure they are worried about more immediate things such as food, medical, etc.

However, I would say that it should be possible for them to make it so it is not artificially impossible for a kid to leave the community. If the kids have grown up with no money, then they at least should be educated on what they need to know to survive in the money world outside of their community. Also, they should make sure that the kids have something (education, a job skill, friends, family, another community to go to, etc) that will give them at least one way forward in life. If the community has money, then they can help the kids by giving them some money, a place to live, a car, tools, etc to help them get by until they get on their feet. If the community doesn't have any money, then they can at least make sure they don't do anything that would hinder the kids from leaving. It is impossible to give someone complete freedom in life, but they can at least try to make it so that the kids are not held back due to path dependence.

Of course, some of them may choose to leave and even start their own communities with better values, but even if this is an evolving movement, it could still feel like losing kids to the parents.
