Come all the way back

Bring back in reverse order of how you got out (if you floated up, come back down to regain weight in your chosen body, if you got small come back up to regular size, if you went back to an earlier state or met parts, be sure that you are whole in the present, in your body and with adult judgment, if you traveled somewhere, go back the same way you went), only bringing back with you those things that you have consciously chosen.

You may want to say something like: “I am bringing back with me only that which is useful to me at the present time. All that I need at the present time, I will bring back with me now; all that I do not need to bring back at the present time, I will bring back at another time when I am more able to handle it. I give myself permission to end this session. The only thing that I am bringing back with me is [state any conscious intention that you want to carry back into your regular life or any cue that you will use to do this work at a later time, but be sure that it is something that will not continue the altered state of consciousness that you are in now]. I am coming back to being [your name] in your body, with your adult judgment.”

Slowly count back down from five to one: “One, feeling the weight of your body; two, breathing normally; three, becoming aware of the sounds around you and where you are; four, moving your fingers, toes, shoulders, and eyes; five, opening your eyes, back in the here and now, feeling normal and alert, and ending this session.”

Ground yourself by stating what day or date it is and looking around at things to your left and right, both near and far. Move around a bit before getting up, and then get up when you feel ready. Be sure that you are oriented and alert (this is not necessarily determined by the fact that you were able to count back to one). Take note of anything that came up that you need to do something about, and then proceed to do something normal.
