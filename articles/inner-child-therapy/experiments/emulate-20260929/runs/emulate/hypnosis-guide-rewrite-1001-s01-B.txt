Inner child, guards and the three inner adults.

Your inner child is unlikely to be trusting towards you simply because you say a few nice sentences to it in meditation. s/he will become trusting towards you if your present day adult is trustworthy.

I talk about the three inner adults because I like to divide our adult’s reparenting role into three parts – the Nurturer, the Protector and the Leader/Guru. However, this is only for convenience: these are not three different beings, of course, but the one complete present day adult.

What the inner child needs from the adult.

NB! It’s important to remember that the inner child doesn’t need to develop the adult capacities – the inner child is worthy as s/he is! S/he only needs a relationship with a present day adult. The inner child, in turn, can enrich the adult with playfulness, tenderness, irreverence, creativity etc.

What if I can’t ‘be’ the adult?

Some people find it difficult to ‘be’ the adult: when asked to be e.g. the Protector they either go blank or find that they become an ‘inherited’ critic or some other child pretending to be an adult.

If that’s the case for you, you can use an Author-developed bridging technique to ‘be a complete adult’ – ‘borrow’ it from someone/somewhere else for a while. The validity of the whole technique hasn’t been tested, but its components (1-5) have.

If necessary, witness, but if you can distinguish the positions, go straight to ‘being’ the adult you need.
