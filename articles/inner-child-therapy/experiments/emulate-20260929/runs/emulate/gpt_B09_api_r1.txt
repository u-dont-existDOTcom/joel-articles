Remember the Protector? He's not just working outside, he's working inside too. In fact it's very likely that the Protector is acting even when your emotional state is flooding with 100% fear, rage, craving, shame, etc. It's the Protector making decisions as to what you are going to do about these feelings: What to say, what to text, what to buy, what to eat, what to take, who to sleep with, who to commit to, etc. Even when you've put the phone in the other room and it's 2am and your little one is feeling very alone.
