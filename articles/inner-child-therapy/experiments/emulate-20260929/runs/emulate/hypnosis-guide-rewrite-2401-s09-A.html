The classic NLP approach to using the Swish is to simply replace the old image with the new one. Here we will keep the old image/pattern available, and simply notice and compare our embodiment and response as we imagine enacting the old pattern vs. the new one, and think about what each would entail in the future.

This is a form of parts integration, without resolving the conflict.

Some things I will not use NLP for:

Overriding a real signal

Overriding fear (before exploring what it is afraid of)

Memory retrieval

Manipulating others (including to believe their intuitions)

Erase painful or uncomfortable feelings (feeling hungry, sad, angry, or physical symptoms from a medical problem)

While panicking, feeling destabilized, manic, "psychotic," overly chemed, or otherwise disconnected from reality

Manipulating others by:

Making them feel spiritually inferior

Using embedded commands

Creating a false sense of urgency

Bypassing consent by assuming "your subconscious wants this"

Taking advantage of altered states, trances, attraction, weakness, shame, or grief

What I will do in an NLP sequence:

Induce or contain

Ask for permission
