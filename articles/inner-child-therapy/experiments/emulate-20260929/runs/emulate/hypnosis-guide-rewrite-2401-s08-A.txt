How to retire a synchronicity: Just take it off the list of synchronicities you are using as cues and stop rehearsing them. You might add a little conscious thought such as “From this moment forth I no longer need to use synchronicity as a cue in my life” or “I’m letting go of the need for coincidences to remind me of xxxx”. Say this in full consciousness. But the act of removing it from your list and not rehearsing it is what will make it go.

How to reframe: To reframe is to change the meaning of something. Most reframing I’ve seen requires one to lie to oneself in order to find a new meaning (i.e. reframing a fear by telling oneself that it’s actually a gift). I don’t like lying to myself so I reframe by asking questions such as “What else can be true?” or by asking “What is the positive intent here?” or by running the issue through a number of sub personalities asking them each what their view is on the issue (i.e. what would my Nurturer say? or Protector? or Leader?).

Perceptual positions are great for reframing: Try looking at an issue from 1st person (yourself) 2nd person (inside the other person involved feeling as if you are them) 3rd person (looking at both of you as a witness) and from a 4th position of the universal heart.

But don’t use it as a way of letting bad behavior off the hook. Use it to get out of the prison of only being able to see an issue from one angle.

Future pacing is a way to test whether or not you truly understand something. In order to future pace yourself ask yourself where you might run into a situation that would test your understanding, see it in your mind’s eye and rehearse how you would respond to the cue.
