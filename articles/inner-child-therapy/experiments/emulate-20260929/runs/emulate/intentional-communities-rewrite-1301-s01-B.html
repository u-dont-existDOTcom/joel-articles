Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community

Don’t get the land until you have a community.

Don’t have a community until you’ve gone through a conflict (when money and/or land ownership are on the line) without papering it over, or coming to some temporary agreement, but until you’ve really resolved it. That is, people need to really talk about what happened, then go deeper into the personal issues that arose for them (jealousy, feelings of humiliation, etc.) and then make a new agreement that comes from a different place.

Until that happens, you’re just a group of people with a dream sitting in a circle, and I wouldn’t take out a mortgage as yet.

Have your meetings somewhere you’re renting, borrowing, at someone’s house… whatever. But test out the conflict handling skills of the group before you bring in a surveyor.

…and make sure you have the resources to do all the other stuff you need to do (water, housing, food, health care, tools, labor, transportation, schooling, access to markets, debts, cash if you’re using it, etc., etc., etc.) Don’t make the mistake of thinking that because you’re all brilliant, super-effective emotionally intelligent people, that you don’t also need to be practical.
