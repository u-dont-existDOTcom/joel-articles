One of the greatest things about Psychedelics Pl/ork is the community!

A crucial component of the integration process is having a community that is well suited to support you. Most often when people have a clear sighted moment in their life, they go right back into the environment they were in when they needed to have that insight to begin with. Within a community that supports you during your experience, someone will be able to pick up on what is going on for you in the coming months.
