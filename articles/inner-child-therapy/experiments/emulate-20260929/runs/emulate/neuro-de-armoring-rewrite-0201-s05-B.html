How to Trial UMP: Start with a low dose of 150-300mg in the morning with food for 3-7 days. Continue your Omega 3 supplementation, and make sure you are getting enough choline (eat eggs, supplement phosphatidyl choline, CDP-choline, or whatever works for you). Do not start UMP on the same day that you start anything else like 5-HTP, agmatine, d-serine, keto diet, iboga, shrooms, LSD, ketamine, or anything else.

If you experience any side effects, discontinue use. The most common side effect is a “choline headache”—feeling slightly spaced out and brain foggy. This happens because UMP increases the rate of membrane synthesis and if you don’t have enough choline in your system to keep up with the demand, you’ll feel like your brain is starved for choline. Other side effects include insomnia, jitteriness, racing thoughts, irritability, emotional flatness, nausea, stomach cramps, loose stool. If you feel wired, emotionally flat, irritable, or “weird” in any way, stop taking UMP.

UMP can increase vividness of dreams and decrease psychedelic tolerance, both of which can be dangerous. Don’t take UMP on the days that you take psychedelics, and be aware that your normal dose of psychedelics may be too strong when you do decide to use them again. Trying to get the visuals that you used to get at a certain dose could be overshooting it big time.
