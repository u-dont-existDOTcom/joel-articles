Troubleshooting

Nothing happens

You might want to start with something small. “Can you show me one sensation in my body?” Or “Can you show me one word.” Or “Can you tell me if you think I need to listen or rest today.”

It feels fake

First figure out why it feels fake. Is it the words? The image? The expectation? The role? Not having an inner voice or image is not the same as resistance or lack of adult capacity. If the invitation is explicitly something you don’t want, correct it. If you can recognize a part that’s protecting you, ask them: “What are you protecting me from? Are you afraid I’m going to be lying again? Are you afraid this is just me playing a part?” If the voice sounds like your inherited parent, and it’s enforcing an old belonging rule, don’t fight it, just ask yourself what rule it is. Then ask yourself what would make the new adult voice more real to you. If you realize you don’t yet have the adult capacity you need for this part, stop asking for a convincing voice. If you have the observing capacity, you can borrow other adult capacities, whether from a real or imaginary person. Try out a sentence or an action and see how it’s received.
