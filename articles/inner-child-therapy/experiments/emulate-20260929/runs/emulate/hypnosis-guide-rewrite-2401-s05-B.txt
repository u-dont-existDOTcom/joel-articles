Creating an anchor / a doorway back

An anchor is any cue that you associate with a state. It can be physical (putting your hand over your heart, touching two fingers together), respiratory (a certain way of breathing), auditory (a word, a song), physical state (crossing your legs, standing in a certain way), olfactory (a certain smell), etc. First find a time when you actually are in the state that you want to access more easily, then while you’re experiencing that state start doing the cue and say something to yourself like “This is the state of XXXXX and it is available to me.” If you are anchoring a way back into a hypnotic state, don’t go for the peak of the experience, just find a nice receptive state that you can work with. (See “How to create a shortcut back into hypnosis” above, in the main induction section). Make sure that you have a different way of waking yourself up than the one that is used to calm you down and keep you alert.
