And if a child does turn up? Don’t rush to healing etc etc, just be with it for a while. You can say stuff like:

Hiya! What a lovely surprise. Just so you know I’m not here to explain stuff or fix stuff or try and turn you into something impressive. I just wanted to find out if there was anything I could do that would make you feel a bit less lonely.

Maybe it needs some warmth, playing, protection, grieving, angry letting out, food, rest, boundaries, less fantasy, less overworking, someone to keep to their agreements…..

And if you do want to do something? Don’t rush to repair it.

Let your younger selves, protectors, feelings or body signals guide you. Prepare, inspect, ask for permission, only respond to one need at a time, wait, reconnect, rest and then reassess.
