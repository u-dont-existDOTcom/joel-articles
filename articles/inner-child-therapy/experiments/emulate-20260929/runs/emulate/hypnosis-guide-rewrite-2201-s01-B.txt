Closing

The first rule of ego-state integration is that no part is the enemy. Not the subconscious, not your body, not the part that’s sharp and insightful, or even the part that’s been tense and protective for years.

The goal isn’t to get as deep under hypnosis as possible. It’s to become an adult you can trust.

It starts with listening to the kid well enough that they can come closer. It includes finding ways to keep the promises so the guards can sit down and relax. It takes loving yourself enough that thought doesn’t need to brace for impact, and thinking clearly enough that love isn’t just fantasy. It’s practice, applied to real life, by the part of you that can make and keep promises.
