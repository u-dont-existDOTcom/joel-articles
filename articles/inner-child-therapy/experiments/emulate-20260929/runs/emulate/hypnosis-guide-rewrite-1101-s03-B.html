Some Questions to Ask Yourself to Discover Who You Really Are:

What do you like to do, what do you believe in, what do you want to do, not because you’re rebelling against your parents, not to be different, not to impress others, not because you want to belong, not because you’re afraid of the consequences, but simply because it comes from within you. What things do you like to do that your parent’s inner child would not let you do because it’s “not good enough, you’re not talented, what will others think, you’ll never be as good as”. What things would you like to try if no one would ever know and you would not have to impress anyone? With who and where do you feel like you belong and can be your true self? What are your body sensations telling you?

Give your inner child a chance to try things that have no social value. Try something new: painting, dancing, gardening, learn a language, try a sport, do some volunteer work, play music or whatever. Don’t let your parent’s inner child stop you. Your adult self needs to overrule your parent’s inner child and let your inner child have some fun and give it enough practice to learn the new skill without being humiliated.

For your inner child belonging is extremely important in order to survive. This is why people will do crazy things to belong to a family, religion, romantic relationship, political tribe, spiritual group, institution or even an online group. Part of adulting is discovering who you are without belonging to a group and without betraying yourself to belong to a group.
