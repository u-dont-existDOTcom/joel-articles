The actual inner meeting

Make sure you are sitting, not laying down. Laying down can make you more likely to dream, dissociate, or go to sleep. Sit up tall, but not military tall. Loosen your jaw, and stay conscious of your surroundings. Laying down is for sleep, deep rest, and incubating dreams.

“I am not leaving life, I am listening deeply to life.”

Dear Higher Self, universal heart-mind, wise subconscious mind, intelligence of the body, inner child, inner healthy adults, and protectors, please tell me what I need to know in a way that is useful, kind, stabilizing, and real.

I am asking this from my conscious mind to listen.

This doesn’t mean to stop your thoughts, it just means to allow the thoughts, sensations, images, doubts, and emotions to come and go while keeping this intention in the background of your mind, allowing that which is salient to come forward.

“I will now wait in silence for 5 minutes to be told what I need to know or to be asked what needs to be known.”

You may receive a phrase, sensation, memory, color, inner child, protector, pain, warmth, silence, task, gratitude, urge to stop, or that the practice for the day is over.

Listen to it all, and believe none of it.
