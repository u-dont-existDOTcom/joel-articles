Script for a High Ensure that you are in a place where you can be safe without requiring driving, water, machinery, or supervision of others before you proceed. If you are already intoxicated or acutely confused, the later part of the script, already open? might be useful, but otherwise do not intensify unsafe behavior or conditions with hypnosis.

Ensure that you know what kind of encouragement you want, that you know if your eyes will be closed, if you will come out and go back in and come out again. If using a recording and it's possible, read through the script. Know that you can always say 'stop' to end the exercise, 'less' to have less done, 'quiet' to have the talking stop. You can always open your eyes and end the exercise. You don't even need approval from any part of you to end the exercise.

Until you are familiar with the way of going in and coming out used in this exercise, practice it with no subject, so that your response will require less and less effort, and not require your supervision. Know that no means no. No silence will be taken as authorization for the narrator to continue.
