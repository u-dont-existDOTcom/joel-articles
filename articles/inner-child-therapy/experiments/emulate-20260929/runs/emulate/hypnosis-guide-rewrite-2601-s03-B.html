Body supports

Sometimes the body is too activated and you can’t do the mental aspects and you have to go physical. This is a harder one and often involves help from others: Foot massage, scalp massage, temple massage, neck massage (not the front part), shoulder massage, heat, pressure, weighted blankets, going into the dark, playing familiar calming music, massage gun (be careful with this one; not on injured parts, not on areas without feeling, not on bruises, not on inflamed areas, not on areas that have had recent surgery, not on varicose veins, and stop if it creates pain, dizziness, tingling, or other bad symptoms).

Others may find that they need warmth, darkness, music, massage, weighted blankets, breathwork, holding an object, or some other personal piece of technology.

(Personal piece of technology: I write on my ZHoly3O page about a piece of technology that makes ozonated charcoal “holy water” that I use when I need to relax. Some people also find Blushield helpful.) Once you have stabilized the body, move on to listening to your inner guidance, reality testing, and doing your one thing.

Anxiety Script:

#1: Stabilize the Body (Feet, Chair, Breath, Room)

You are ok, little one. You are protected, protector.

If there is something you need me to do, tell me what the first step is. If there isn’t, help me stand down.

I will not ignore you. I will listen, reality test, and do what needs to be done.
