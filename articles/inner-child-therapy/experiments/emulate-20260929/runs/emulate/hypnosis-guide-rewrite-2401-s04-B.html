System, can you show me the next step without showing me the entire wound?

Pattern 8: Tag Questions – Isn’t it… Can’t it… Doesn’t it…. These can be a little oily, so use sparingly.

Your body already knows how to rest, doesn’t it?

Pattern 9: Nominalizations – Healing, safety, trust, integration, communion, clarity, rest, etc. These are vague and that can be a good thing at times, as the subconscious will interpret them with meaningful concreteness.

And it’s possible to feel more safe, in whatever way your body knows how.

Pattern 10: The most important de-hypnosis tool in the Meta Model:

How, specifically? According to whom? Compared to what? Always? Never? What, exactly, would happen? How do you know? How do you know that’s true? What would you have to see/hear/experience to know that isn’t true?

While the Milton Model is great for opening up possibility space, always use the Meta Model to make sure you’re coming back to the truth.

Other useful tools (sensory/state/strategy):

Submodalities – Choose a relevant state (fear, craving, fantasy, belief, memory, etc.) and work with whatever you can sense about it (near, far, bright, dim, loud, quiet, moving, still, heavy, light, in your body, outside your body, etc.). If you can’t sense anything about the state, don’t use this tool.
