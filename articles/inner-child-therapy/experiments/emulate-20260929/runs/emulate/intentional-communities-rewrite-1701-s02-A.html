What Helps Communities Last—and Whether Longevity Is Enough

Communities That Abide by Dmitry Orlov. Uncomfortable argument that long-lasting communities all have a) communal property, b) separation, c) boundaries, and d) ideology that they can use to hold themselves together. Also a useful discussion of how cohesion and capture are similar.

Measuring Success in Intentional Communities by Rubin, Willis, and Ludwig.

Commitment and Community by Rosabeth Moss Kanter. Interesting comparison of 19th century communes.

Alienation and Charisma: A Study of Communes by Benjamin Zablocki. Useful field research.

The Tyranny of Structurelessness by Jo Freeman (1970). A classic.

On the Zapatistas

The Fire and the Word by Gloria Muñoz Ramírez and Subcomandante Marcos. Details how the movement came to be, why they practice before they theorize, how they established autonomous institutions, and more about the original Good Government Boards.

Enlace Zapatista has a good archive of Zapatista publications, including explanations of their new autonomy in 2023.
