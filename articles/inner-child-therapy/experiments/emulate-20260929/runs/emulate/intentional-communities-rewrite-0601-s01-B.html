What Connections Will You Leave to the Technosphere?

(Loneliness is part of it, but I’ve written about the polycrisis here )

We don’t really know what the future holds and a lot of the scarily prophecies about AI could be totally wrong, but it is entirely possible that millions of people will lose their jobs and find that AI has inserted itself into the administrative layer of everyone’s life before your Chantress Seba can fill out applications to move into a commune.

It would be better to work on being human again and then not need an exit strategy, than need an exit strategy.

So what connections to the technosphere do you want to keep?

What about medical care, banking, phones, vehicles, schooling, supply chain, government ID, internet, energy? It’s tempting to reject all of them, but that’s not necessarily wise or will lead to needless suffering. At the same time, complete connection will make you vulnerable to all that the outside may wish to bring upon you.

The Amish have been thoughtful about this and weighed different technologies against the health of their communities. Different communities have come to different decisions and I think they are wise to treat it as a community affair.
