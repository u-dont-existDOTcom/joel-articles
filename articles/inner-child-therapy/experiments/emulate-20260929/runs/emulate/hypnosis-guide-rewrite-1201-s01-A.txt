De-hypnosis: wake up inside the influence

Practising self-hypnosis will help you become aware of when others are trying to influence you. This is a constant in life: screens, ads, politics, religion, erotic imprinting, authority, need for belonging, shame, urgency, repetition, etc. Influence can be benign: lullaby, prayer, good teaching, song, love, etc. But it’s always good to ask the question ‘how is this influencing my ability to see and choose for myself?’

When you feel urgency, that you’re special, fear of missing out, excluded unless you do something, sexual energy, shame, confusion, fantasy of someone rescuing you, that you have a destiny, you should obey someone or something, that you have secret knowledge, or that you know something very quickly and with certainty, you are probably being manipulated.

Don’t obey. Don’t rebel. Wake up.

Watch out for language patterns that are hypnotic and manipulative, especially those that presume your agreement or attempt to force a particular choice on you, or attempt to smuggle in ideas without you noticing:

‘When you admit that…’ ‘As you realise that I’m right…’ ‘The only reason you’re doing this is…’ ‘Part of you knows that…’ ‘Your higher self knows that…’ ‘Everyone knows that…’ ‘The only reason you’re not agreeing is your ego is afraid of…’

Learn the grammar of manipulation and it will become obvious.
