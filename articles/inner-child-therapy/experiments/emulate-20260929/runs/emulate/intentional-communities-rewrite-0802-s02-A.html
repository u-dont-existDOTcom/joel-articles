Children Need Freedom Inside Real Protection

Children need their own territories, their own space in which to develop a child-society, create their own games etc.

I still remember from my own childhood how free I felt when I was at a Unitarian youth camp. It is the same with the children in ZEGG's Kinderhaus, the adults take care of the safety outside.

This can easily be misunderstood, so there are certain things we have to take care of:

First: I am totally against the attitude, especially in the USA in big suburbs, that every relationship between a child and an adult who is not related is seen as suspicious. It is much better if a child has many adults around them, who like them and whom they like, and who would be concerned if anything happened to them. It is fear that leads to abuses.

So the points we take to avoid child abuses are:

  • Children have the right to say "No" and adults have no right to any physical or emotional contact with them, nor do they have the right to expect them to obey (unless it is to prevent danger), or to contact them in private.
  • There should not be any secrets between children and adults. No adult has the right to ask a child not to tell his mother (or any other person the child trusts) about anything (about gifts, games, physical contacts, conversations or relationships). This does not apply to surprises, since these have a date when they will be revealed. Secrets serve to separate a child from his mother or other adults.
