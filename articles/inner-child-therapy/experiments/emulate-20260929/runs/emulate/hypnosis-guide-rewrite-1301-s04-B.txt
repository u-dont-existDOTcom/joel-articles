Read this onto a tape recorder, or read it out loud to yourself, or learn to move your mouth as if reading and use your silent voice. Choose one of the anchoring methods - a sound, a contact on your body, or the two hand practice - and choose one of the opening phrases. Use the same anchoring method each time it occurs in the induction. Pause briefly between paragraphs. Do not read out loud the directions for reading.

Fix your gaze on something in the room. Just notice the difference between looking for something to look at and letting something that is already there come into your view.

As you settle back onto the surface beneath you, just notice the feeling of contact with that surface. Perhaps you can become aware of a sound or of your breathing.
