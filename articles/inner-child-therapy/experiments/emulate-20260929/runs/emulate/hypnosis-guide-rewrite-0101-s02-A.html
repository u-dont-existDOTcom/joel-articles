Too much hypnosis teaching is about sending commands down to a subconscious ‘machine’. For me it never quite worked. It always seemed to me that there were a lot of parts to me and it was much more effective to sit down with them all and listen to what they all had to say.

In this guide I take you through a self-hypnosis process that brings all parts of you: conscious mind, subconscious, body, inner child, protectors, adult self and Higher Self to an inner meeting by using self hypnosis to gather all their attention.

I teach you my simple to learn listen, warm, protect, clarify, reality test and act process.

There is no need for dramatic trance as we will be testing what we agree in the real world.

It is a powerful way to come into communion with yourself – as your subconscious can’t speak it lets you know what it thinks through symptoms, dreams, cravings, daydreams and other signs.
