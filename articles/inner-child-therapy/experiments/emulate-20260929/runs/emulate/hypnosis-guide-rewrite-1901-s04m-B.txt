What if this is an area where the love of the divine has not descended yet?

Sometimes when we are really open to something, we can see a glimpse of how much more love or freedom there could be. Yet we cannot just will our kid to get it. Sometimes when we pray, it brings up a feeling of abandonment or condemnation. Or we feel betrayed by a teacher. What is asking to come up and be questioned here? Is it that we need to do more of the same thing?

Sometimes it is good to just do something that helps. Right now the horizon of your life may be too small to encompass what you need. Yet you are curious. There is more happiness possible. It doesn’t have to be real, you just need to be curious. Sometimes we just need to realize that we have enough love and meaning in our life to live the life we want. Maybe we don’t need to do any more spirituality.

You feel stuck and like you are processing forever

Sometimes there is a real wound to deal with and sometimes there is a checking loop. Ask yourself if you will feel or do things differently if you process this thing out right now. Are you hoping to feel reassured by figuring it out? It is okay to deal with grief and do practice, it doesn’t all have to be new.

If it is a checking loop, allow the thought to be there and go do something you have already chosen to do. Engage in life and relationships without needing to meet inside or do a ritual every time you have a thought.

What would your Leader do in this situation?

Why do you keep doing hypnosis when you don’t change your life?

What would reassure the kid that you are really there?

Then go do it.
