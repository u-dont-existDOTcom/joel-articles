In the Fix (called ”The Jammer”) we use the fact that memories need visual processing power in order to be restored. If you busy that part of the brain (Visual-Spatial Working Memory) with something else (like playing Tetris) the memory gets restored, but the ”sting” out of it is gone forever.

Instead of playing Tetris (it requires you to download an app and grab your phone at a vulnerable moment) we teach a method that works better than ”counting”. It’s called ”Color-Path Connect”. It forces the brain to map 3D space and ”steals” the processing power that is needed to ”save” the memory.

Pick a Color (Example: Blue) Find all Blue things in the room and connect them in your mind (from left to right) Add complexity: Rotate the objects in your mind or change them into something else Do this for 10-20 minutes after a trigger and you ”jam” the memory.
