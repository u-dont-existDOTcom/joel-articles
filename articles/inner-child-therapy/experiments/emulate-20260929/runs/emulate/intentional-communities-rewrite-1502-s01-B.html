What are the Lessons to be learnt from the Black Tax?

One of the strange quirkiness that still persists in the communal tribal system, is that whenever one person is able to secure a means of income, suddenly the entire family becomes dependent on this one individual for his or her upkeep. This is popularly referred to as the Black Tax. It is basically the ancestral belief that when one rises, then all must rise with him/her and so all are entitled to a share of your income. It makes perfect sense if you approach it from this perspective: your parents, your siblings, your extended family, they were all part of the making of you, they fed you, clothed you, protected you, brought you up, shared their houses with you, and now that you have a means to earn, it is only fair that you share with them. Remember back then, it was all shared, so the organism survives as one.

The problem with this system nowadays, is that it is soul destroying. See, everything isn’t shared anymore, but you are still expected to share your income. You never get a chance to build for yourself, you never become secure, and the people you share with, they never get a chance to become stable either because they are living off of you. This is because although we now live in an individualistic cash-based society, we are still governed by the ancestral laws.
