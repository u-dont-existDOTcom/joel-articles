Pattern #1: Pacing and Leading (Stating Truth)

What is Pacing and Leading?

It’s ok to feel anxious and have your body so activated right now. You’ve got a great Protector in you! One breath at a time, we can begin to have your Adult part listen in.

Truth + Invite: “It’s completely true that you’re feeling/experiencing ________ right now (sensation or emotion). It makes complete sense that your body/mind is responding by doing/thinking ________ (behavior or thoughts that are happening now). One thing that is also completely true is that your Adult part is here with us right now, breathing in and out, in this room. Could your Adult part begin to listen in to what’s coming up?”

Pattern #2: Using Verbs that Help Open a Person’s Awareness

Verbs to use: notice, realize, recognize, sense, feel, wonder, imagine, picture, listen, allow, discover, remember, consider, etc.

Begin to notice what form the answer takes for you. Is it a picture? A word? A body sensation? Or perhaps a knowing of the next action to take?
