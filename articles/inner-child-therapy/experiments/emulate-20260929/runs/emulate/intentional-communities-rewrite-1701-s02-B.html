What Helps Communities Last—and Whether Longevity Is Enough

Dmitry Orlov, Communities That Abide , makes the uncomfortable argument that most long-lived communities have tended to rely on at least some form of communal property, deliberate separation from broader society, strongly enforced boundaries between members and non-members, and an ideology to which members are expected to adhere. His argument is that high cohesion and capture can look the same from a distance.
