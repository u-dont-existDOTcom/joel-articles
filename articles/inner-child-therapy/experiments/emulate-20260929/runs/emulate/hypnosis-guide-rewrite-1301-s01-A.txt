Induction: gathering attention without disappearing

The purpose of the induction is to gather up all your scattered bits of attention so that you can start to hear the quieter stuff. It is not intended to make you surrender, go to sleep or obey. If you are sober and distracted, analytical or guarded, it may be worth doing an actual induction. If you are already trancey (from meditation, loveyhuasca, lucid dreaming, dealing with strong grief, music, prayer, fatigue, etc), you can go straight to the orientation and containment.

Sometimes it’s useful to do just enough of a trance to feel focused and warm and receptive and embodied and able to stop. You may not need to go deeper than that.

What’s actually going on in an induction?

There are many ways to do an induction: preparing, gathering, deepening and knowing what to say when. Counting is just one way to do an induction. There are lots of others (some of which you probably already do). You can use conversation, story, movement, sound, curiosity…whatever, to organize your attention in a way that’s useful to you. Just name what you’re doing. There’s no need to be sneaky or overly elegant.
