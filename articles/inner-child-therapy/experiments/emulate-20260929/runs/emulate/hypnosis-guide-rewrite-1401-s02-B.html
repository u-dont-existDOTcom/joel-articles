This is a safety procedure for when you are using a recording and for some reason, it has ended and the recording is over. This is to be used if you are listening to a recording and it suddenly stops and you are not allowed to come out of the experience. Say the following: " The recording has ended and the session is over". Open your eyes, state where you are, state what day it is. This does not apply to recordings that are meant to help you sleep or put you to sleep. In that case it is acceptable to either go to sleep or simply lie quietly until you awaken. It does not apply to recordings that will not allow you to come out of them while still in a role, with diminished judgement, diminished ability to move, blankness, a word that you do not know the meaning of, or any other way that keeps you in an altered state indefinitely.
