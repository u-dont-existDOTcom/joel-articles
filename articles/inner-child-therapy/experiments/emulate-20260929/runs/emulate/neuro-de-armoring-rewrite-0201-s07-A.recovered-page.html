Begin with a lower amount if you think you’ll be sensitive to it. Maybe start with just 50mg at night for 2 or 3 nights. Then, if the lower amount seems to be helping, move up to 100mg. It may be helpful to add some P5P to the stack, taken in the morning. It is a vitamin that can help convert the 5-HTP. Start with 10mg and see if moving up to 20mg helps. Be sure to take it alone for a few days, do not combine with UMP, agmatine, D-serine, and hold off on trying any psychedelics or changing other medications until you have a good idea how you are responding to it. It should be obvious from the start by how your sleeping and dreaming is, as well as your mood.

If you experience any negative side affects, stop taking it immediately. Side affects can include nausea, GI issues, headache, intense dreaming, insomnia, sweating, tremor, anxiety, irritability, agitation, and even possibly hypomania. If you become agitated, start sweating, get diarrhea, develop tremor or clonus, get a fever, feel confused, or develop a rapid heart rate or unstable blood pressure, stop taking it immediately. You could be developing Serotonin Toxicity, which can be dangerous.

You should not be putting 5-HTP in your body when taking other drugs that increase serotonin levels. See these two links for more information on that: Case report on dietary supplements and This one on Serotonin Syndrome in general. It is almost always a result of too many things in the body that increase serotonin, so be mindful not to take it on days you are using psychedelics, especially anything with MAOI’s like ayahuasca (harmala) or iboga (ibogaine).
