APPENDIX J: Library of inductions to try out

First preview the sequence, then commit to trying it out for one purpose. Just decide whether to try starting it or not. Use any of the families as light, eyes open, or substitute no-trance contact for the trance. Notice what happens, including noticing that nothing happened, then decide what to do next based on that. You can move, imagine, switch sensory channels, do nothing, or stop the induction and come out fully back to waking.

If you don’t get vivid imagery, translate that into words, or rhythm, or space, or touch, or movement, or just knowing.

Start with some stretching and releasing of the body.

Notice where you are being supported, and the position of one hand and/or foot. Either push and stretch a bit and release, or imagine that. See if you can notice whether anything is different, or if not, notice that nothing is different. (Do for no more than 3 areas.) See if you can arrange for that sequence to be led by noticing where you want “release”, whether that be in your body or an internal voice, and whether you need to provide support for it. Come back to normal by moving back to normal.

Notice where you are being supported, and imagine that you are letting that support take over just 1% of the effort it is taking to support yourself. You can either imagine that you are floating, or that your weight and pressure are the same. See if you can notice whether anything is different, or if not, notice that nothing is different. You can deliberately fractionate by announcing that you will breathe in normally, and on the way out pay attention to the support. Come back to normal by deliberately deciding to take on your normal weight and have firm contact with the support.
