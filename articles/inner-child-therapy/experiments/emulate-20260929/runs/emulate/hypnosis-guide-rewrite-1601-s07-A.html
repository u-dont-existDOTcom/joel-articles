Finish without forcing a revelation

Once enough has been received and you wish to end the work, do not open up any new questions. Instead, ask what it will take for you to be able to end the work at this time. Thank everyone you have worked with and consider what is important for you to do with what you have received. It may be something as simple as getting something you need, setting a boundary, resting, or taking action to repair a relationship. On the other hand, it may be that no action is required at this time.

Allow your analytical mind to stay with you throughout the process. It is fine to question, disagree, or end the work at any time. However, if possible, leave more extensive fact-checking and making any commitments to yourself to take or not take a certain action until after you have returned. Meaningful images you receive are not necessarily memories of things that actually happened and shifts or releases in your body do not necessarily mean that what you are hearing is correct.

Use the Come all the way back script to return even if you have ended the work early. After you have returned it can be helpful to jot down a few notes about what you experienced. Try to remember to include a brief description of what came up, what you did with it, what happened, and if there is anything you are uncertain about. If it is helpful to you, you may also want to write down any important phrases that came up, messages your body may be communicating, needs of a child part, messages from protectors, boundaries you need to set, ways you need to repair relationships with others or yourself, or things you need to not force. However, only do this if it is relevant to your experience.
