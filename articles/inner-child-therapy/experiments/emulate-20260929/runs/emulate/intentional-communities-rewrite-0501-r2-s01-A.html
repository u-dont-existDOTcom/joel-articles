The Medicine Part, Without Pretending It Isn’t There

TL;DR A lot has changed legally in the last couple of years and is not well reflected in community-related literature. Among other changes, Oregon will have licensed psilocybin service centers starting in 2023, Colorado will have a regulated natural medicine system with licensing starting in 2025, New Mexico passed their Medical Psilocbin Act in 2025, and Australia has allowed limited use of psilocybin and MDMA by psychiatrists for certain conditions since 2023.

There have been some setbacks too, like the US FDA's 2024 Complete Response Letter asking for more evidence for MDMA, or the fact that Portugal's drug policy only decriminalizes personal use of small quantities while still criminalizing trafficking (but "Portugal legalized everything!" is a much catchier headline).

There are now real legal differences between countries, states, substances, religious vs non religious status, and activities, and getting real legal advice where you are and for what you're doing is important if you're trying to do something medically.

Even where legal, most communities are still quiet about their psychedelic use out of fear of the negative reactions of parents, neighbors, insurance companies, and local officials who may not know or care about the legal distinctions.
