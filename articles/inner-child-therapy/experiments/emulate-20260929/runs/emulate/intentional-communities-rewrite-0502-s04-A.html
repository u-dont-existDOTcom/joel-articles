The Psychedelic Calling

Many of the people interested in gathering and forming tribes and villages have had deep psychedelic and mystical experiences and feel compelled to help other people reach them.

I’ve had the same experience. It doesn’t take long to realize that not everybody wants to change, or that many people say they do but then do the opposite, or that people have enormous problems that you may not be able to deal with effectively despite your positive intent and feeling that love can cure anything.

That said, I also think that the “calling” is real and that trying to act on the feeling that you can do anything is a mistake.

One of the biggest problems of groups that form in the euphoria of a collective spiritual experience is that they grow too fast. That’s why I have the sections on capacity and on membership later in this article.

When the calling becomes more sober, it can be very useful. One of the good things about forming a community is that it can be a training ground for people who go on to form other communities, which helps to answer the criticism that forming communes is a way for people who have the resources and skills to run away from the world.
