There is a common misconception that when we go to communicate with our inner child, we start by talking to the child.

Instead we often experience numbness, cynicism, anger, hunger, urges to grab our phone, sexual urges, planning, sleepiness, dissociation or feeling like what we’re doing is stupid.

One of these voices is your inherited parent. This is the internalized voice of your parents and how you experienced them as a child. This is separate from your inner adult, which you’re building with the Nurturer, Protector and Leader/Guru. Often your inherited parent is a harsh critic or a warden in jail who says things like “don’t cry”, “don’t be so needy”, “don’t embarrass us”, “don’t be so independent”, “this is fake” etc.

Try saying: “I notice you. I see that you’re trying to protect something. I want to learn from you. What are you protecting me from?”

Then wait and see what comes up. It might be something like “you’re going to lie to yourself again”, “this is another fake thing you’re doing”, “if you do this you’ll be destroyed by the pain”, “this is another way you’re using spirituality to get away from dealing with your life”, “you’re going to forgive people too early” “you’re going to think this fantasy is real and that it’s coming from a higher guidance”, “you’re going to abandon us after this session” etc.

Then say: “That makes sense. You don’t have to move yet. What would make it 1% easier for you to trust me?”
