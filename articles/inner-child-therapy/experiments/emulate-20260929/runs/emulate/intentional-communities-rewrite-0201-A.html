Communes Are Hot Again 🔥

The same intense longing that motivated my dad to make this film a few years ago is in the air again. In 2023, the US surgeon general declared that 50% of Americans are lonely, and made social connection a health imperative.

Whether you want to call it an epidemic or not, it’s obvious that a lot of people simply cannot tolerate modern life in isolation any more. This seems to be true regardless of politics, opinions on food, beliefs about God, proclivities for sex, or views on whether shoes are a tool of oppression. People agree that the way things are now is starvin’ them.

If you look at Google trends for terms like “ecovillage” or “intentional community” you can see that after a long decline in interest, the curve has bent upward dramatically in the mid-2020s.

I have no proof that the rise of AI is connected to this uptick, but it’s hard to ignore the coincidence in timing. AI became serious around this time, and now it’s radically changing the way we live.
