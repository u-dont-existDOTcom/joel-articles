Let the next response guide you

After completing the induction and you are within your meeting:

Learn to improvise, don’t use speeches. (Read these first and then don’t look at the screen when practicing)

Make an accurate observation, make a small offer, (treat them well, or give them more time), only acknowledge that which occurs, then wait. Whether it be sensation, image, gesture, feeling or words.

Learning to find your own words:

“I notice that my chest got tight when I first imagined asking for help,

As I notice this, I can take some time to see if I can find out what it is,

Then just listen…..you don’t need any more language to make the offer, and in fact, if a feeling is developing and you need to stop using language to understand it, then just say “what happens next?” or something even simpler.
