How to use: Trial period should be 3-7 days at 150mg-300mg taken in the AM WITH FOOD. Ensure adequate omega 3s are being taken, especially DHA/EPA. Also, ensure adequate choline is being consumed in the diet (eggs) or via supplementation (phosphatidyl choline, CDP choline, etc) Do NOT introduce UMP on the same day as other variable supplements or diet changes (5-HTP, Agmatine, D-serine, keto diet, iboga, shrooms, LSD, ketamine, etc)

Common side-effects or reasons to discontinue supplementation: A common side effect of UMP (and PC) is the "choline headache". This is a spaced out brain fog feeling that comes from rapid upregulation of membrane syntheis with low choline levels. Other side effects that are reasons to quit UMP include insomnia, jitteriness, racing thoughts, irritability, emotional flatness, nausea, stomach cramps, loose stools. Basically, if UMP makes you feel wired, numb, or irritable in a weird way, it is not for you and you should quit taking it.

Finally, UMP can cause vivid dreams and lower your psychedelic tolerance. Do NOT take UMP on days that you plan to use psychedelics. If you do use psychedelics after taking UMP, keep in mind your tolerance may be lower, so previously well-tolerated doses may now be too strong for you. Do not try to chase the visual threshold you were previously accustomed to, as you may go too far.
