Appendix A. How to ask the question better

Your subconscious is not an AI, but some experience from AI prompt engineering may be useful here. Good prompt may help you to be more present, more honest, more loving, and more accurate in detecting the signal.

There are 7 elements of good prompt that may be useful to make the question more precise:

Role — who and what are you talking to.

Context — what’s the situation.

Intention — what kind of change you want.

Constraint — what are the conditions you want to make sure like safe, truthful, kind, not forcing, not creating fake certainty, not giving you more than you can digest, etc.

Output — in what form do you expect the answer — image, phrase, body signal, memory, next action, question, symbol, silence, rest, etc.

Evaluation — how will you know that the answer is correct — true, kind, in the right time, helping to stabilize you, specific enough, possible to test, etc.

Iteration — if you need to change the prompt based on the life feedback.

It’s good to have some safe fallback like:

“If the system needs more time to process the request and the most suitable answer in this moment is the silence, then initiate the deeper comfortable rest in the body. Consider this a success too.”

Because sometimes there is no answer because nothing is happening, or the answer is that it’s not yet, you need more rest, more building, or just doing the normal thing you already know how to do.
