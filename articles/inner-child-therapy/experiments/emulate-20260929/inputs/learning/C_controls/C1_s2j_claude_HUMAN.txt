You can protect your little one from yourself too, like by repairing after an internal attack. "What's an 'internal attack'?" Calling yourself an idiot for losing your keys again, for one. Your little one was listening, so you go back and apologize to them. If they're sarcastic about it, you let it go by instead of snapping back. They don't trust you yet, and you don't ask them to. They complain about something, they're right, and you say so. All of that shows as clearly to them as the clean room did. It's proof you love them, but they can still tell the difference between being put up with and being loved.
