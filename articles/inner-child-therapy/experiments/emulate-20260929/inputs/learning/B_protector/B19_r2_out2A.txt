You promised your little one a cinnamon roll if you finally took the car in about the squeaky brakes, so you do, and then you actually go get one. Most of the Protector's job is about that size, though at its biggest it's getting out of a relationship you're not safe in. Your little one starts to trust the cinnamon rolls, after plenty of "Oh, sweetie" from grown-ups who went right back to what they were doing.
