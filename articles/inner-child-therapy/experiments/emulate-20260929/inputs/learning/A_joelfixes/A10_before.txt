I developed this practical map from the rest of the method. Treat the complete sequence as a provisional synthesis: some components have direct research behind them, while the full arrangement is my own:

1. The child alone: orient to the present and establish enough outside safety to begin.
2. The child with a borrowed adult: experience one reliable adult function through a real, remembered, imagined, or symbolic figure.
3. The adult apprentice: care for the child together with support and do at least one part yourself.
4. The inner adult: repeat warmth, protection, and direction until those capacities become familiar enough to summon.
5. The adult and child in relationship: the adult leads without exiling the child, and the child no longer has to run the whole life alone.

You don’t have to stop being the child. You become larger than the child-state. A useful sentence is: “I am still this child, and I am also becoming the adult who stays.”
