But one of her ideas fits what I’m trying to say: when two people come together, something appears between them that is larger than either person alone. She calls it the spirit of the relationship. I don’t think that spirit belongs to us. Nobody can command it to remain. At our best, we serve it. We tell the truth, keep doing our own inner play & work (pl/ork), ask the community for help, and listen for what the relationship is becoming. When we start thinking we own it or deserve it, that presumptuousness tends to push it away.
