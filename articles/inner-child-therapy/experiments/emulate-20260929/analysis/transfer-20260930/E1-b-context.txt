Intentional, meaning you pick one of those acts and decide when you'll do it, and what the smallest first step is (like with the envelope). Some of them are only one step anyway. If it needs money or somebody's help, sort that out before the day, and if you'll have to tell someone something they won't like, say it out loud to yourself ahead of time. Then put yourself in their shoes to see how it sounds, and go back & forth like that a couple times until both sides feel good about it, or good enough.

After the day, look at what actually happened. If you did the Protector action(s), not feeling better right away is information to learn from. It doesn't mean failure, because the process is dynamic. And you can ask yourself again, "What is this practice supposed to help, and how would I know if it's helping?" Not doing it is harder to figure out. Fear is the first guess, but maybe you didn't know how or why. Being worn out can stop you too, and sometimes whoever said they'd help couldn't make it. Hey, we've all been there. 🤗

Love Doesn't Have to Wait for Trust

Sometimes the love is there and giving it still feels dangerous. Why would that be? I think it's because we've tied love and trust together, as if one has to prove the other first, and they really don't have to. It's like holding back a hug from a friend till you're sure they didn't talk behind your back.
