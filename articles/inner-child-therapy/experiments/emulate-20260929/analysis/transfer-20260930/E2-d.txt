Your little one might look at your grown-up life and say, "I don't trust you." You can answer, "I believe you. And I love you." I think the "I believe you" part matters more than it looks, since they're braced for you to argue. Trust can go back over the record, the old promises first, and it'll find the times you didn't protect them and the internal attacks that haven't stopped. Love doesn't run on that record. You can give it today and let the verdict take as long as it takes.
