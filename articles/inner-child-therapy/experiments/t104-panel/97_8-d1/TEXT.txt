I'd count the milder stuff too, like coming back from trip after trip confused or spaced out. If it's bad, or it's putting you in danger, I'd see a doctor or a therapist right away. That goes double if it keeps happening when you're sober. And if you think a prescribed medicine is part of the problem, I wouldn't just stop taking it.
