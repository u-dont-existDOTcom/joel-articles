Your little one can get really loud in the gap after one life ends and before the next one has formed. A breakup will do it, or moving to another country, an illness, losing your faith or a role, your youngest moving out. So can leaving a family or a community, or staying but on new terms. Even a therapist or helper stepping back, once you’ve grown a lot, can set it off. In Tibetan Buddhism there’s a word for an in-between state like this: a bardo. I’m not asking you to believe what they teach about death and rebirth. The word is just handy for that weird stretch after an ending, when the old you is gone and the new you hasn’t shown up yet.
