Once a need does show, after all that hiding, it’s okay to want a lot of closeness. So then someone you love goes for a walk alone, and you’ve texted “hello??” before they’re at the corner. When they get back, you sulk all night. Wanting them close is fine! Blowing up their phone and sulking all night is a different story. Your Guide could help you ask for what you need instead, like a hug before they go and a rough idea of when they’ll be back. Before any of that, though, I’d let your little one say what’s going on. I think it just wants to know they’re coming back.
