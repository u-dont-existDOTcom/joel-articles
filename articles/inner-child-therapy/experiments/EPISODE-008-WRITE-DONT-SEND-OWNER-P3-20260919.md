## Exact owner prose

The first draft is often the part of me that got hurt grabbing the pencil first. I don't want the adult to snatch it away because what came out was angry or messy or not especially skillful. Usually there was something real underneath it that I was trying to get across: this hurt, I need you to understand what happened, I need you to stop doing that, I need some help here. The adult can sit beside the child, keep his hand on the pencil, and help him find a way to say it that the other person might actually hear. But that's a different job from taking care of the person who wrote the first version. Maybe he needs protection, maybe reassurance, maybe actual help. Making the message better doesn't mean deciding the protest itself was childish just because it came out badly.
