## New blocking order before owner delivery

For any generated humanization candidate:

1. **Literal comprehension read first**
   - read the prose in publication order with no ledger labels;
   - summarize each sentence in ordinary language;
   - verify referents, causal links, chronology, and what the sentence is asking the reader to hold in mind;
   - if a sentence requires backtracking or unpacking multiple hidden relations, repair or reject it;
   - if the paragraph's distinction is harder to understand than the source distinction, reject it.

2. **Ordinary-reader continuity**
   - what would the reader think the point is after each sentence?
   - does the next sentence follow naturally?
   - is any pronoun, qualification, or contrast ambiguous?
   - can the section be understood without knowing the preservation ledger?

3. **Only after readability passes**
   - preservation proof;
   - mesoscale/architecture audit;
   - Human/AI-shape diagnostics;
   - detector eligibility.

A candidate may not be delivered merely because preservation and internal humanization ledgers pass.

## Anti-rationalization rule

If a cold audit notices:
- density;
- difficult syntax;
- too many dependencies;
- unclear referent;
- reader reconstruction burden;
- ambiguity not intentionally required by the source;

do not defend it because:
- functions are connected;
- the map is correct;
- the sentence carries multiple obligations;
- the prose avoids one-function-per-sentence cadence;
- the detector has not yet rejected it.

Readability outranks anti-pattern optimization.
