Before the wisdom window closes, the main thing is to find out whether you are actually compatible. Do you have the same goals and values, especially around children, religion, politics, health, love, and romance? Where do you want to live? Do you both want to find a community? What kind of relationship do you want with technology and money?

Ask deep questions. Conversation-card games may seem silly, but they can help people get past superficial talk and see if they’re compatible.

But asking, “How do you handle jealousy?” can get me a beautiful answer that tells me almost nothing. I’d rather ask what she thinks she would do in an actual situation. If all she can give me is a general answer, I assume she probably doesn’t know yet.

A card game can create its own little high too. If we spend two hours telling each other things nobody else knows, we may feel incredibly understood and close, but that may mean the game worked without telling us whether the relationship will.

You can also bring the person to a party and see how they act, bring them around your family or friends, or take them to activities you enjoy and see if they really enjoy them too. Try doing what you would normally do in your day alone, but with them, and see how it feels. Is it weird? Are they wondering why you do this stuff? Do they look bored, like they’d rather just hit you up later than be with you while you’re doing ordinary stuff? Can you guys be normal together and not just romantic or dating?

See their negative side first too. If there is no negative side, that’s a red flag for fakery. Ask whether the spiritual story they tell about themselves survives an inconvenient PMS afternoon. How recent is the healing story—two months or two years? Jealousy, abandonment, possessiveness, and the “I’m so much better than I used to be” story may not be understood until there is stress and entanglement. By then, of course, you’re stressed and entangled. 😆

Sometimes the only thing I have is a little “wait” feeling. It doesn’t prove anything. But if I’m about to ignore a question because I really want this to work, that’s enough reason for me to ask the question before I keep going.

If they’re nasty to their friends, family, or other people, assume they will eventually be nasty to you. We mostly seem to get attracted to the inner-child part because we’re coming from our inner child and we want to have fun. But you also need to find out whether there is a healthy adult there before you’re too bonded to want the answer.
