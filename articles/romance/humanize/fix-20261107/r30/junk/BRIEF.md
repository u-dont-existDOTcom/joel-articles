WHAT COUNTS AS JUNK, in particular:
- a sentence or paragraph that says again what the paragraph before or the section before already said;
- a listicle: three or more items, clauses or short sentences in a row built the same way, or a run of questions where
  one is part of another or two overlap;
- a paragraph that doesn't follow from the one before it, or whose sentences don't follow each other, or whose point
  comes last;
- writing too vague to say what it means, a label for a thing instead of the thing, a pronoun ("she", "it", "this",
  "they") with two possible referents;
- a sentence about the essay itself that isn't true where it stands;
- a short line standing alone that sums up or turns ("That was my mistake.", "Everything.");
- anything else in the ways below.
