REWRITE:
But once a partner has become the default caregiver and that’s taken for granted, it’s time to bring in other support, or the romance could collapse under the burden. Then the question is whether the romance actually does need less intensity, becomes friendship, or ends. Asking that question out loud is usually where to start, though I can’t answer it for others (and couldn’t always answer it for myself).

REWRITE:
Many people ask this question of honesty, but it’s unfair, because honesty is its own reward and won’t produce a certain outcome. It might repair the relationship, open it, turn the romance into friendship, preserve the family through separation, or show you that ending it is better. But if the other person won’t look at the relationship honestly, you can’t make the honesty mutual, and then you have to decide what you can live with. Temporary separation helped Bee and I, and it also became more permanent, although I’ve always thought maybe we would get back together in a few decades.
