## Item 1
MARKED:
- "Before me, Bee had also stayed..." — way 12: jumps from the knee story to an ex-boyfriend with nothing saying how it follows; way 8: "who was totally broke and crazy and supported him" strings two clauses with "and"
- "Later she blamed herself for staying through that..." — way 10: "that" could be the staying, the boyfriend, or the supporting
- "Helping carry somebody through a temporary incapacity..." — way 3: "X is different from Y" contrast; way 4: "temporary incapacity" and "somebody" are vague; way 21: "permanent support system" is a label
- "Some women are fixer types, which can create the opposite pattern." — way 21: "the opposite pattern" is a label; way 10: opposite of what; way 6: ends on a wise-sounding line

REWRITE:
Before me, Bee had also stayed with a boyfriend who was totally broke and crazy, and she supported him for a while. Later she blamed herself for staying with him through all that, and I agree. Helping carry somebody through a temporary incapacity, like my knee, is a different thing from becoming the permanent support system for a man who won’t take responsibility. Some women are fixer types, and they can end up in that second spot again and again.

REWRITE:

Then ask whether your judgment has gotten better or you keep falling for the same thing again, and what your friends or exes would say.

And if you shut down, did that mean you stopped talking to your partner for days, so they felt abandoned?
