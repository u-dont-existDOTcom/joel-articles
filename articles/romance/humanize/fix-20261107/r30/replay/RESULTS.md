**Result.** Fixed 5 of 14, partly 5, missed 4. With the lessons, a fresh writer reliably takes out a short line standing
alone and a sentence about the text that isn't true, and says the thing for a label in the easy cases. It doesn't yet:
cut a list of three to the two items that carry the point (3 of 4 kept all three), see that a question is too vague to
answer as asked (346, 409), move the point first in a paragraph whose order is wrong (73), or keep from adding details
and lines of its own (73, 4, 437), which is the worst of these for him ("don't confabulate things about my life"). So
the brief gets three changes (lesson romance-2026-11-22f): a list of three goes to the two items that carry the point; a
question is asked the way the reader could answer it; and nothing new goes in, not a detail, a reason or a line, that
the paragraph or its section doesn't already hold.
