Living alone is easy in so many ways, like I can make my bed whenever I want. When I have a girlfriend, making the bed in the morning can become one of the ways I show her I love her. If I don’t do it well, somehow it may become a bad omen that I don’t care about her, which is a lot of emotional weight for a bed.

Dealing with somebody else’s emotional insecurities can also be exhausting. It’s easy to feel free when I don’t have to do that, especially since I’m a bit of an introvert and 10% autistic. That freedom works wonderfully until I’m in a romantic relationship, and suddenly I’m expected to read her mind, silences included, and check in constantly, all without using that expert “management” of mine to manipulate her.
