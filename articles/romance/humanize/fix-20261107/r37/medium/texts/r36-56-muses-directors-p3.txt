In my experience, women often prefer me to say directly:

“This is where I want to go. This is what I think we should do. Are you game?”

She might have a better idea, or just not want to do it, but she still wants me to take initiative.

I don’t think that goes against equality, since equality doesn’t have to mean dividing every role 50/50. If I like driving and for her it’s cooking, great, and whatever she’s way better at, she’ll probably do more of. Even when I’m the one leading, I still want to know what she sees. Mandar obedeciendo—Lead by following—as the Zapatistas say.
