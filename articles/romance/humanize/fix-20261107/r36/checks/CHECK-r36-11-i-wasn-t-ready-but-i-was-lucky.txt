At twenty, I thought I was totally fine until I became enamored with Alba. She was Dominican, and warm in a way I didn’t understand. Kissing your BFF on the lips was normal to her. I thought she was in love with me. She did love me and wanted me living with her, but not in the way I wanted. The confusion threw me into a horrible spell for years. I was even a bit suicidal while living with her.

Then Bee cracked me open. My second wife and I were about as in love as two people could be, considering that every other day she would demonize me because of BPD. Before Bee, I didn’t know what In Love meant. Nobody knows until it happens. It’s a completely different kind of reality. It’s spiritual.

Did it heal her? We still can’t understand. During our two years together, it seemed to stabilize and heal some things while provoking her demons even more. Did it heal me? The relationship almost killed me, but I would never undo it. I still love her so much. I haven’t spoken with her in a year, but I love sending her love.

Relationships ALWAYS reveal what I can’t see. Suddenly my latent selfishness sprouts up. The depth that is actually available versus the depth I idealize. The other person’s selfishness and dishonesty and superficiality. How deeply I really love. How deep my illusions go. Whether my spirituality survives contact with another human being.
