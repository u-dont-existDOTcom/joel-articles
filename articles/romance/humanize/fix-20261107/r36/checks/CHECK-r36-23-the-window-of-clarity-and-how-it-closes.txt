The attachment theory part comes from my own experience and from reading I’ve done. Sex turns on bonding chemistry in our brain (like oxytocin, dopamine, vasopressin) that usually helps us form attachments, so that we don't just abandon our children.

People call oxytocin the “love hormone,” as if getting more attached should make you feel closer and safer. And if closeness was safe when you were little, maybe it does feel a bit like coming home, but if you were neglected, abused, or if your mom or dad or closest people disappeared on you, then you might deep down disbelieve that you're worthy of anyone staying with you. And then you can get more attached *and* more freaked out or suspicious about them leaving, and you might even go into fight or flight mode.
