# Fix these passages of Joel's romance essay so they make sense and follow

Joel is the author; the essay is in his first-person voice: plain, warm, direct, a little playful. A reader went through
the essay for passages that don't make sense or don't follow ("junk"), and a second reader confirmed eleven of them.
Each item in PARAS.md shows its section with the passage to fix marked ">>> FIX" and the problem the readers found.

For each item:
1. Read the whole section first. Work out what the passage is trying to say in its place: what the reader should
   understand or do after it, and how it follows from the paragraph before.
2. Rewrite only the marked paragraph(s) so they say that plainly and follow from what comes before. Fix the problem the
   readers found, and anything else in the marked text that reads one of the ways in HIS-FIXES.md (the way Joel fixes
   drafts: the point first; short lines that sum up joined or cut; a list cut only where items overlap; a label replaced
   by the thing; a vague question replaced by the real one; no "X does not Y" or "not X but Y" frames; no "honestly",
   "matters", "quietly", "one ordinary").
3. Keep every point the passage makes that the section doesn't already make elsewhere, every joke, name, fact and story
   of his. Add nothing: no detail, example, feeling, reason or line the section doesn't already hold. One exception: the
   reason a sentence is there may be said in plain words when the section already implies it.
4. A sentence that only says again what the section already says may go. When you cut one, name the sentence of the
   section that already makes its point.
5. Don't touch the context paragraphs. Use curly quote marks and apostrophes (’ “ ”) as the essay does.

Answer for every item, in order, in exactly this shape:

## Item N (id)
WHAT IT SAYS: one sentence, what the passage is for in its section
REWRITE:
the rewritten paragraph(s), separated by a blank line, alone on these lines
CHANGE: meaning kept / a point cut (name it and where the section already makes it) / a point changed (say how)
WHY: one or two short lines on what was wrong and what the rewrite does
