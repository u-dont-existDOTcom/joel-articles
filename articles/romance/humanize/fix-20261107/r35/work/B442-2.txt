Many people ask this question of honesty, but it’s unfair: honesty can lead anywhere, and it’s worth having wherever it leads. It might repair the relationship, open it, turn the romance into friendship, preserve the family through separation, or show you that ending it is better. Temporary separation helped Bee and I. It also became more permanent, although I’ve always thought maybe we would get back together in a few decades. Any of that takes both people, though: if the other person won’t look at what’s true, you can’t make it mutual. Then you have to decide what you can live with.
