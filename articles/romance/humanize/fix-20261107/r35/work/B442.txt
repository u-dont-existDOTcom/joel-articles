Many people ask this question of honesty, but it’s unfair, because honesty is worth having whatever it leads to, and it can lead to very different places. It might repair the relationship, make it an open one, turn the romance into friendship, preserve the family through separation, or show you that ending it is better. With Bee and me, it led to a temporary separation, which helped us, and that separation then became more permanent, although I’ve always thought maybe we would get back together in a few decades. Any of those outcomes takes both of you, though, so if the other person won’t look at the truth with you, you can’t make it mutual, and then you have to decide what you can live with.
