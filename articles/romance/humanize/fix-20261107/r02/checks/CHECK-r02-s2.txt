Forced celibacy doesn’t abolish anyone’s sexuality. For most people it eventually sends it somewhere weird. Why wouldn’t it?

The robes come with perks too. A monk in a poor country gets free food, a place to live, status, and a way out of ordinary responsibilities. In the West it’s mostly about status. A guy nobody respected much as a layman shaves his head, becomes Venerable Somebody, and suddenly people are bowing to him.

He can still sincerely want liberation. He can also enjoy the bowing.
