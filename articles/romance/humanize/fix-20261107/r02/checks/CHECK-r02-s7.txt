The most recent conflicts are the best place to look. Were you able to regulate at all during the conflict? What did you do to the other person? How did you repair afterward? What did you learn? Has your judgment gotten better, or do you keep falling for the same stuff?

I’d also want to know what their friends or exes would say about them, since people are pretty bad at judging their own progress.

And if somebody tells me, “I shut down,” okay, but what does that look like? Did you go silent on your partner for days? Did they feel abandoned?
