Lots of couples promise forever and nobody else. Look around at how many actually live that way. In a polyamorous relationship the boundaries might be more negotiable, but then somebody has to sit down and negotiate them, down to who gets to stay the night.

Polyamory can be dishonest too, like when someone who can’t get close to any one person dates four at once and calls it polyamory. Or when someone keeps cheating until they finally announce they’re poly, without necessarily doing the work on themselves that would make it ethical. If you can’t do monogamy, say so, since pretending can do more damage than an affair would have.
