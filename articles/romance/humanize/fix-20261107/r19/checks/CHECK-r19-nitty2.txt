Living alone is easy in so many ways. I can make my bed whenever I want. When I have a girlfriend, making the bed in the morning can become one of the ways I show her I love her. If I don’t do it well, somehow it may become a bad omen that I don’t care about her, which is a lot of emotional weight for a bed.

Dealing with somebody else’s emotional insecurities can also be exhausting. It’s easy to feel free when I don’t have to do that, especially since I’m a bit of an introvert and 10% autistic. All that works wonderfully until I’m in a romantic relationship and suddenly I’m expected to mind-read, silences included, and check in constantly (no manipulating with all that expert “management,” though).
