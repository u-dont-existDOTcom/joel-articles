Forced celibacy doesn’t abolish anyone’s sexuality. For most people it eventually sends it somewhere weird. Why wouldn’t it?

Some people become monks for practical reasons too. In a poor country, the robes come with free food, a place to live, status, and a way out of ordinary responsibilities. Even in the West, where the free food matters less, a guy who never got much respect as a layman becomes Venerable Somebody, and suddenly people bow to him.

Of course, he can sincerely want liberation and still enjoy the bowing.
