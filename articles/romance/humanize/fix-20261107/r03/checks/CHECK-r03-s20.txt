Then the questions change for me. If the romance changes, which parts are still supposed to remain? Are we promising actions, or low-key pretending to promise permanent feelings? Which needs are we still going to meet outside the relationship?

And the biggie: what happens if one of us falls in love with someone else? What counts as betrayal, and what’s just a sign we need a serious talk? If our lives change, can we honestly sit down and rethink the agreement? If we split up, what do we still owe each other, and what do we owe our kids, if we have any?
