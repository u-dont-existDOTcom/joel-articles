Forced celibacy doesn’t abolish anyone’s sexuality. For most people it eventually sends it somewhere weird. Why wouldn’t it?

Of course, plenty of monks sincerely want liberation. But people can want several things at once, and some become monks for practical reasons too. In a poor country, the robes get you free room and board and status, and nobody expects you to earn a living or raise a family anymore. In the West the free food probably isn’t the draw, but a guy nobody respected much as a layman can shave his head, become Venerable Somebody, and suddenly people are bowing to him.
