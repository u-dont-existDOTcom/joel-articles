[1] ## 31. Can making love be a spiritual practice? (304 to 309) (live 304, 305, 306)

[2] I’m Buddhist, and the early Buddhist texts have nothing on tantric sex as a path to awakening, but plenty of the people they show getting seriously awakened were lay people.

[3] In DN 16, the Buddha talks about people who had died in the town of Nādikā and mentions 510 lay stream-enterers, 96 once-returners, and more then fifty lay non-returners, the first three of the four stages of awakening.

[4] SN 55.24 has an almost comic complaint: when the Buddha declared Sarakāni a stream-enterer, other Sakyans grumbled, in affect, “Who isn’t a stream-enterer these days?”
