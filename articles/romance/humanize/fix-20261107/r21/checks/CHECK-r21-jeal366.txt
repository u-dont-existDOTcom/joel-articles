I do believe two people can choose sexual exclusivity honestly. They can admit that attraction to other people exists and decide not to act on it each day.

I’d rather be able to mention any attraction casually, including the little ones that don’t mean anything. If you’re only supposed to bring up the serious ones, every time you do it turns into a whole sit-down talk, so people mostly just keep quiet.

Lots of couples promise to be each other’s one and only forever, and way fewer of them pull it off. In a polyamorous relationship the boundaries might be more negotiable, but then you actually have to do the negotiating.

Polyamory can be just as dishonest, though, and these days I think a lot of people use it because going deep with one person is more than they can handle. Or it’s their way of admitting they can’t keep it in their pants, without necessarily doing the work on themselves that would make the whole arrangement ethical. If you can’t do monogamy, it’s still more honest to just say so than to fake it. Sometimes all that pretending does more damage than the other relationship ever would have.

I learned most of this by screwing it up. Some of it I just thought through, and I’m still not sure about all of it.
