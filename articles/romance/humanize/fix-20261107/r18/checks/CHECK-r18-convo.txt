Card games and social gatherings don’t tell you everything, but I wouldn’t ask someone out of the blue, “What did you do wrong in your past relationships?” That would be a pretty weird date.

At the right time, though, in the right way (especially if they bring it up tangentially), I would ask about their past relationships, especially the recent ones. If every story ends with everything being the other person’s fault, I’d ask, “Okay, so what lessons did you find for yourself?”

“I have baggage” tells me almost nothing. What are you having trouble with now? What might make it hard for you to function well in a relationship?

I’d want both of us to be honest about how effective we actually are at handling our own issues. What addictions or impulsive behaviors can’t we reliably control? How dependable are we? What are we probably going to ask forgiveness for more than once? That tells me more than either of us announcing that we’re healed.

The most recent conflicts are the best place to look. Were you able to regulate at all during the conflict? What did you do to the other person? How did you repair afterward? What did you learn? Has your judgment gotten better, or do you keep falling for the same stuff? What would your friends or exes say?

And if you tell me you shut down, okay, what does that mean: did you stop talking to your partner for days, and did they feel abandoned?
