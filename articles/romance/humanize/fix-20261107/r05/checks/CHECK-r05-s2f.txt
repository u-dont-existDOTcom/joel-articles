Forced celibacy doesn’t abolish anyone’s sexuality. For most people it eventually sends it somewhere weird. Why wouldn’t it?

Some people become monks for practical reasons too. In a poor country, it’s free food, a roof and respect, and nobody expects you to hold down a job or raise a family. And even where the food doesn’t matter, like in the West, a guy who was a nobody as a layman gets to be Venerable Somebody, and people bow to him.

Of course, he may sincerely want liberation, too. People can want several things at once.
