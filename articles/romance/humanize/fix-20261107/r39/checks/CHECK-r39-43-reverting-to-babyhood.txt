Relationship may activate wounds that were already there. Sometimes it may open a level of the heart that is simply very young and vulnerable, whether you knew there was a wound there or not. You become like a baby again. There’s a reason lovers call each other “baby.” And after all, Christ said "unless you change and become like little children, you will never enter the kingdom of heaven" (Matthew 18:3). Well, the Kingdom of Heaven may require extensive therapy to arrive at if being a baby was difficult the first time for either of us.

As mentioned with Bee, but also with Key, and H., there was always some sense that they were remembering how their dad treated them and judging me according to how close to him—or different from him—I was. That put me in a tough position. I want to play the role they need in some way, but I want to hold onto the actual reality of me being me, with no need for putting on a show.
