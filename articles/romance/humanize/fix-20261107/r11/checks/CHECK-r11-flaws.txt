My own list isn’t especially flattering: I’m impulsive, and I idealize too much, although I don’t demonize. I’m very open and not very private. I care more about honesty than the standard romantic ideal. I still love my exes. After being deeply in love, I have overly high expectations, so I’m hard to please with a basic relationship.

I’m also extremely unique and have deeply held convictions many people won’t agree with. I’m anti-vax, anti-money, anti-civilization, etc. And I’m not yet living the way I want to live (astrology predicts I will by 2030 😃).

A woman should know that before she starts fitting me into the life or fantasy she already planned.

Key was an inner-child therapist and Reiki healer. Both Key and Bee were extremely curious and open-minded, which I loved about them. They were open to spiritual experiences, very connected to childlike wonder, and were always seeing what they needed to work on. They both meditated a lot.

I saw all that and thought anyone with that much spiritual depth and curiosity must already be far along on the path.

That was my mistake. Their meditations may have helped them a lot. I still had no idea how either woman would act when a relationship got ugly. If somebody starts with enough trauma, huge progress can still leave a lot unresolved.

My own blind spot is that I have trouble understanding where people are at mentally and emotionally. A partner may agree with everything I say and then tell me later, “Didn’t you understand I was FAWNING?”

No, I really didn’t. If you’re fawning, tell me when you can, because I can’t know what’s happening in your head just because it feels obvious to you.
