# Notes on the romance article's headings (Fable)

These are things a new name can't fix. Nothing here is done: each is for Joel to decide. The headings in
`PROPOSAL-FABLE.json` keep every section's level and place.

1. **S001 and S002 are calls to action, not sections.** S001 ("Interactive guide-based convo here.") has no text, and
   S002 ("Try my Couples card game app...") sits over the article's opening (the story of his dad's answer, who the
   guide is for). I'd make both plain linked lines or buttons, so the sidebar starts at the first real section, and
   leave the opening without a heading. If the opening should be listed, "Why I Wrote This" fits an h1. If they stay
   headings, the JSON has short names for them ("Ask an AI About This Guide", "Try My Couples Card Game"), but then the
   opening is still filed under the card game.

2. **Three h1s go straight to h3.** S009 is followed by S010, S011, S012 (h3) with no h2 above them, though S015 under
   the same h1 is an h2. S034 is followed by S035 to S041, all h3 (with S038 an h4). S067 is followed by S068 (h3),
   then S069 and S070 (h2). I'd raise S010 to S012, S035 to S037, S039 to S041 and S068 to h2, and S013, S014 and S038
   to h3. The sidebar indents by level, so these now sit deeper than their neighbours for no reason.

3. **S008 holds two things.** Its first three paragraphs are about labels and commitment. Its last two ("I can't decide
   what is okay for anybody else..." and "Outside a loving poly community...") are the answer to S006's question about
   casual sex, and a reader looking for that answer won't look under a heading about labels. I'd give those two
   paragraphs their own h3, "Where It Can Be Honest", or move them to the end of S006.

4. **S056 holds two things.** It starts with jealousy (the switch, B.'s jealousy, how to say it). From "I do believe two
   people can choose sexual exclusivity honestly" on, it is about choosing exclusivity or polyamory. I'd put an h3
   there, "Choosing Exclusivity Honestly" being too long, something like "Exclusive or Poly?".

5. **S019 may be under the wrong parent.** The stage between interest and sex is about how a relationship starts, not
   how to find a partner (S015). It would sit better as the first section under S020 "Starting on the Right Foot",
   which has no text of its own and goes straight to the window of clarity.

6. **S068's last paragraph is a different subject.** "If you've been there for someone, don't abandon them in a
   crisis..." is about how to leave someone who depends on you, not about clashing memories. It could go up into S067's
   own text, or down into S069.
