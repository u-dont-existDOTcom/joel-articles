- Line 58 "Conscious Tools 🧭" (25 words): says nothing about what is under it. It holds imagining hard parts, psychedelics and "why this sounds artificial", which don't obviously belong together. I would never look here for situations 31, 32 or 43.
- Line 53 "Who Holds a Couple Up?" (666 words): could be about friends, community, children, or who carries the relationship. I'd try it as a catch-all for "why couples struggle" and for friends-take-sides (27, 40), and I'm not sure it fits either.
- Line 72 "Tough Love" (620 words): last heading, so it reads like a closing or an ending-a-relationship section. Unclear what it is about.
- Line 26 "Keep an Outside View": outside view of what? Could be friends, counseling, or perspective on idealization.
- Line 28 "Anti-PTSD Is Risky Too": jargon, and "too" points back to something I can't see. I only found it because situation 17 felt like a pattern problem.
