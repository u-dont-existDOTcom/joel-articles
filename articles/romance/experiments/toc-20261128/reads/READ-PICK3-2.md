## 2. What gets in the way

**Unclear or two-way headings**
- Line 20 "The Missing Middle": says nothing about what the middle is between. It sits last under "How to Find a Partner", so I'd read it as a missing group of people (neither app nor community), not as a slow stage between flirting and sex.
- Line 09 "Labels Only Describe": could be about situationship labels or about labels in general (boyfriend/girlfriend). It's under a casual-sex parent, so it's hard to find when you're a committed couple asking what a label means.
- Line 72 "Tough Love": last line, no hint of what it covers. It could be advice on being harsh with a partner, or a closing thought. 620 words is big for such a vague label.
- Line 47 "Fantasy": one word. Sexual fantasy, or fantasy about the partner?
- Line 52 "De-ontologizing FTW": jargon. A reader can't tell it's about soulmate/one-and-only beliefs. It is playful in a useful way only after you've seen "Twin Flames?" above.
- Line 36 "From Flow to Nitty Gritty": says nothing of the topic (the honeymoon ending?).
- Line 37 "Reverting to Babyhood" and line 40 "Growing Up Again": both read as childhood or inner-child topics. Neither says it's about how partners regress and grow inside a relationship.
- Line 42 "Ahead in Different Ways": ahead of what? Could be about partners at different life stages, or about the partner who is more developed.
- Line 11 "Not Ready, but Lucky": unclear who is lucky or why.
- Line 61 "Getting Unstuck" and line 62 "Did the Therapy Work?": under "Psychedelic Timing" they read as general therapy. A reader with a stuck couple would look under 64 or 65 first.
- Line 63 "Why This Sounds Artificial": a reader can't tell what "this" is. It could be the whole guide's advice (checkins, imagined disasters) or only the psychedelic tools, which is where it sits.
- Line 68 "Should You Leave?" has only 103 words and the real content is below it in 69. 70 and 71 sit under it as ## and are really about after.
