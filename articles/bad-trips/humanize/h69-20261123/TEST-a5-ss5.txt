Since they're already overstimulated, it helps to clear out the crowd and stop any arguing. While it's natural for people who are overstimulated to start pacing or exercising to "burn it off," the problem is that creates more body heat, and it raises the risk of their muscles breaking down into the blood, which hurts the kidneys (rhabdomyolysis). But if they feel a strong urge to keep moving (akathisia), I'd have them tap their fingertips on their head instead (the Emotional Freedom Technique), which can soothe that urge without the over-activity risk.
