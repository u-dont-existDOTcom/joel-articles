The ER doctors will want to know exactly what they took, so before the ambulance comes, **I'd gather their pill bottles and packages**. I'd also jot down how much of each they took and when, street drugs and supplements included. If they have a seizure while you wait for an ambulance, do the first aid in [Active Seizure](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/active-seizure) above. Then I'd tell the medics whether their breathing got better or worse afterward, since [the CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/epilepsy/first-aid-for-seizures/index.html) counts trouble breathing after a seizure as a danger sign.
