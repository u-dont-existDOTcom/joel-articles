## Call Medics For:

Slow, shallow breathing can mean an opioid overdose, which naloxone reverses, so if you have it (the Narcan nasal spray), spray it into one nostril right away. It doesn't matter if they think they only took MDMA, since fentanyl, an opioid, has turned up in pills sold as MDMA, and naloxone does no harm without one. But naloxone can wear off before the opioid does, so call medics even if they wake up, and if their breathing slows again before help comes, give the second spray in the pack.
