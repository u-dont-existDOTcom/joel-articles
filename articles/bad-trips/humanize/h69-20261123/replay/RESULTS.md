paragraph he fixed. His fix this round: my naloxone paragraph, from AI High 90 to
[Human High 99](https://www.pangram.com/history/73e392f4-d32e-46d3-82c5-0cc0894c0a06) in his thirteen checks.

**Result.** The writer made the three small fixes and missed the two that his fix turned on. Said as rules, "the doubt
first" and "the state before the act" didn't carry: the writer read its paragraph as already answering the doubt
(the "It doesn't matter if" sentence) and saw no missing state. So the next writer's brief asks them as questions of
each sentence, the way the readers' brief does (ways 13 and 25 of [tools/humanization/his_fixes_brief.md](https://github.com/u-dont-existDOTcom/joel-articles/blob/main/tools/humanization/his_fixes_brief.md)): what would
the reader find strange here, and is it answered before the first thing they're told to do; and for each act that
changes the person (wake up, calm down), where the text says the state it changes from. And it repeats the rule the
writer broke: nothing new goes in (lesson bad-trips-2026-11-23g).
