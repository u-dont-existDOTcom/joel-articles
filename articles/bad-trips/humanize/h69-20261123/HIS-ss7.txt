The ER doctors will want to know exactly what they took, so before the ambulance comes, **I'd gather their pill bottles and packages**. I'd also jot down how much of each they took and when, street drugs and supplements included. If they have a seizure while you wait for an ambulance, do the first aid in [Active Seizure](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/active-seizure) above.
