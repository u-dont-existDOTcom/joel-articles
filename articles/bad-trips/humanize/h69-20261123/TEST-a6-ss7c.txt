The ER doctors will want to know exactly what they took, so before the ambulance comes, **I'd gather their pill bottles and packages**. I'd also jot down how much of each they took and when, street drugs and supplements included. If a seizure comes before the ambulance does, I'd do the first aid in [Active Seizure](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/active-seizure) above. And I'd keep track of how their breathing is doing afterward, as that's one of the main indicators of how dangerous the seizure is, according to [the CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/epilepsy/first-aid-for-seizures/index.html).
