Women are most at risk of this: at [one rave](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23476039/), about one in four women who took MDMA had low sodium, against 3% of men ([a recent review](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/add.70255)). To tell whether too much water is the problem, ask whether they've drunk a lot, and look for bloating, puffy hands, or rings that feel tight ([wilderness medicine guidelines](https://www.aafp.org/pubs/afp/issues/2021/0215/p252.html)).
