Overstimulating also dehydrates them, but we can't give fluids unless they're fully alert, able to swallow and not vomiting, and then just small sips of an electrolyte drink. If they still show the signs of low electrolytes from [MDMA: Magnesium Alarm](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/mdma-magnesium-alarm) above, they may need **a hypertonic electrolyte drink with more magnesium and potassium than a normal one**. That can be as simple as a salty potato broth with some magnesium chloride flakes stirred in, since potatoes are full of potassium. If you don't have the flakes, Epsom salt works too, although it causes diarrhea more easily. Whatever you give, give it slowly to see how it affects them, since their potassium could be running low or high: MDMA can drain it, but muscles breaking down into the blood can push it up. So I'd stick to food like the potato broth for potassium, since too much pure potassium is dangerous for anyone.
