**If the person is fully alert and able to swallow, I would give cyproheptadine early**, since it blocks serotonin receptors. So far, the evidence for this med has only been [case reports](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9696181/), and [a recent review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39791184/) found them insufficient to show that it works. I wouldn't wait to see whether it does before doing the other steps. And most people won't have it at home anyway, since it needs a prescription in the US, though a telehealth doctor might write one quickly, and only some countries sell it without one. If you can get it, [the guidelines](https://www.mdpoison.com/media/SOP/mdpoisoncom/healthcareprofessionals/antidote-facts/Cyproheptadine%20Antidote%20Facts.pdf) say to give an adult 12mg to start, then 2mg every 2 hours if symptoms persist, up to 32mg per 24 hours. And tell medics how much you gave.
