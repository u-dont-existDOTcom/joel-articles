While you wait, you might think of [niacin](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/niacin-flushes-trips-out), which this guide uses for ending a trip, but it's actually unsafe with serotonin syndrome, since at the doses used to end a trip it can cause flushing, dizziness and low blood pressure ([NIH](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Niacin-Consumer/)). [DMSO](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/dmso), though, seems to help prevent seizures, so if there's any sign of an overdose, I'd have them smell it, or rub a little on a clean patch of skin (add 30% water if it makes the skin itch), then slowly use more as long as it seems to help. I'd also leave out this guide's [Syrian rue oil](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/syrian-rue-oil-gentler) for now: made at home it has almost none of the harmala alkaloids, but I couldn't find any research on it with MDMA, so it could help or harm here. Magnesium chloride is the other one I'd use, since many people with serotonin syndrome run low on magnesium ([a nursing review](https://doi.org/10.1097/01.NME.0000432871.59839.f3)). If they're fully alert and able to swallow, I'd give it by mouth, especially with MDMA-related jaw tension or twitching, since those can be signs they're running low (the amounts are in [MDMA: Magnesium Alarm](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/mdma-magnesium-alarm), above). Topical magnesium chloride if they're not able to swallow, or Epsom salt if that's all you have, though Epsom salt gets through the skin less well.
