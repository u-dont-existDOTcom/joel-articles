A whole leaf can easily end the trip and, in my experience, may keep you from tripping for days afterward. A tiny nibble, though, can sometimes smooth psychedelic nausea and even improve the trip without ending it. For nausea I'd try 200mg of leaf at a time, or 10mg of the extract paste. I'd give more than that only if the person doesn't mind their trip getting less intense. But it takes only a little more than a nibble to end the trip, so I use it for nausea only when the person is okay with their trip possibly ending.
