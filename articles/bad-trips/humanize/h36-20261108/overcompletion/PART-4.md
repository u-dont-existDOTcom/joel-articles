## YOUR PART

[B315] ## No Rash Actions: Visions Are Not Commands

[B316] An intense altered state can make everything feel final, cosmic, and urgent, and it might feel like you have to act right now. But more likely the urgency is just part of the state, and acting on it isn't safe.

[B317] **Put yourself on a 24–72 hour action lock.**

[B318] Until you have slept and eaten, maybe don't drive, travel, break up, make up, quit your job, confess everything, confront or accuse that list of people you've been meaning to... Think twice before sending whatever ridiculous message you're planning on to Judge Amos, posting that revelation you'll forget tomorrow, giving away your BMW, buying a new BMW. After you decide that you are permanently enlightened or permanently damaged, and you explained the whole universe to your parents in 42 seconds, you can still change your mind the next day.

[B319] **Write it down, get some sleep, and go back over it with one grounded person once your head is clear.**

[B320] Treat trip content first as **experience-content**, rather than fact-content. Because you saw it, it might feel like you have to act on it, and because it felt cosmic, obeying it might seem wise. But more likely you had an experience, and it can wait until the state has passed.

[B321] **No revelation makes murder, assault, coercion, sexual boundary-crossing, reckless driving, or self-harm safe tonight.** Anything requiring violated consent or harm belongs in the notebook until you can review it with a clear head.

[B323] ## When the Trip Feels Amazing: Euphoric Certainty

[B324] The action lock matters especially when the state feels blissful: *I am God. I am chosen. Everybody must hear this tonight. I finally understand everything.* Bliss is not proof of safety, and it often makes grounding less welcome.

[B326] **Two questions while glowing:**

[B327] 1. **Have I slept?** Sleep comes before mission.
2. **Has this happened before, and how did it end?** A recurring pattern with lost sleep, racing thoughts, pressured speech, or everyone saying you are not yourself needs grounded support before action.

[B328] The revelation will still exist after breakfast. If it is true, it survives sleep. If it cannot survive one night of rest, it was an emergency impulse wearing angel wings.

[B329] ## (Re)defining "Bad Trip"

[B330a] A bad trip is one of those terms thrown around very loosely in the psychedelic space. It can refer to various things which may be intertwined.

[B330b] When too much hits you at once, your nervous system can become flooded, and nothing makes sense, even as the brain still tries to derive meanings that might create unhelpful feedback loops. Underlying those interpretations are factors like the dose of what drug(s) you took and how your body handles them, or how hard you pushed a practice. Old painful memories could have been triggered, or you may have gone in with a fearful or unloving mindset, or the setting or people around you could have fed into it. Be kind to yourself or to whoever's going through it, because blaming anyone only makes it heavier.

[B330c] Some say there's no such thing as a bad trip, because it's all a doorway to shadow work. But if you had no intention of doing shadow work, that explanation might have come too late. Other bad trips seem unrelated to shadow work, although any negative experience can lead into it. Samsara offers no shortage of difficult experiences to process. It still makes sense to reduce present-moment trauma so we can address what was already there.

[B332] ## Spiritual ≠ "Getting Your Ass Kicked"

[B333] The triage above is meant to make trips useful. Some people believe they deserve suffering because of karma or self-hatred. Suffering by itself fixes nobody. Suffering that yields insight requires enough clarity of mind and heart to learn from it.

[B334] This is one side of the difference between [spiritual bypassing](http://goenka.u-dont-exist.com) and [spiritual practice](http://nibbana.u-dont-exist.com):

[B335] Bypassing reaches for an ultimate view--"we are all One," "I don't exist," "this is all karma," "everything is Love"--while ignoring the person shaking in the corner.

[B336] Practice says love is here, so we make the room safer, call for help when needed, stop pretending, and hold the experience without becoming it.

[B337] That is tripsitting, shadow work, and the path becoming practical.

[B338] ## The Other Bypass: Feeling Good Forever

[B339] A well-rounded trip should offer practical lessons and contact with suffering as well as freedom from suffering, lest we become "so spiritually high that we are no earthly good." "Life is full of suffering" is Buddha's First Noble Truth. Ignoring that side of life becomes dissociation or spiritual bypassing in the opposite direction, followed eventually by statements like "Everything is Love, including violence."

[B340] Many people meditate or take psychedelics to feel good. I am definitely one of them, and Buddha also said meditation should generally feel good. My friend Eshwar, however, found that shadow work made up about 99% of his path to heart-chakra awakening.

[B341] Modern people carry so much trauma--and some people did even in Buddha's time--that shadow work and inner-child reparenting may need to come first, even before authentic liberation from the identity illusion.

[B342] When shadow work is bypassed in meditation and therapy, it may appear in ordinary life without explaining itself. I encountered a lot of it in my first two marriages, to my bewilderment. I wish everybody could keep shadow work inside the safer rooms of meditation, therapy, journaling, prayer, and honest conversation.

[B344] Shadow work to me is neither BDSM nor "feeding your demons." It means bringing loving awareness to the parts of the mind built in fear, pain, humiliation, rejection, and survival. [Inner-child reparenting ](http://innerchild.u-dont-exist.com)is central. Most negative mind-states aren't some error in your philosophy, they're just young parts of you that are still waiting for love and protection and permission, and for the explanation nobody ever gave them.

[B347] ## Hang up the phone, get off the Healing Treadmill

[B348] (a picture)

[B349] If you use psychedelics (or MDMA) to achieve a goal like healing or spiritual liberation, you need to already be on the same path so that they aren't radically changing your ordinary understanding of things. If the trip is so crazy that you can't believe it a few days later, chances are, like me, you'll end up taking that drug again and again to understand it.

[B350] With healthy drugs like [Loveyhuasca](http://loveyhuasca.info), which has literally saved my life on numerous occasions from unknown infections, there's less of an argument for "Hang up the phone" as Alan Watts famously said you should do "once you get the message." But what we shouldn't be doing is feeling like we need to use these substances *because we can't integrate them.*

[B351] (a video link)

[B352] That happened to me at first when I started with ayahuasca. The ego deaths were so far beyond my ordinary understanding of reality that I dismissed them each time within a week. Then I felt a compulsion to have another ego death soon after. Once I learned to meditate into the experience ([and beyond](http://nibbana.u-dont-exist.com)) without ayahuasca, that compulsion was gone. If I had learned about Buddhism and if I could have accepted that my view of reality was limited prior to ayahuasca, then probably just one or two ego deaths would have been enough inspiration for me.

[B353] With [toxic drugs like MDMA & ketamine](http://mdmaket.u-dont-exist.com), that can be a recipe for long-term harm to your physical, and/or mental health. Ketamine is super easy to abuse because it's a dissociative psychedelic. All your problems are magically **gone, **and if you take enough, you get some crazy trips that you can barely remember later. [5-MeO-DMT](http://bufo.u-dont-exist.com) is the more psychedelic version of ketamine. See my guide on that, because it shares a lot of the same warnings, altho I do think it has more potential to be helpful when used correctly.

[B354] Here's a [reddit poster ](https://www.reddit.com/r/mdmatherapy/comments/17nne25/reaching_resolution_32_sessions_over_5_years_for/)who talks about going through tremendous pain with MDMA as she uncovered childhood sex abuse memories. It took her dozens of sessions that felt harmful before she finally had one that fully got her to accept what happened to her (and a huge somatic release which looked like a re-enactment of the abuse). So there are some people who argue that if MDMA opened up your wounds, just keep taking more MDMA until it's fixed. Although [on this thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/mdmatherapy/comments/1woxh97/deep_lingering_grief_two_years_post_mdmaassisted/), there are some who disagree with that simplistic advice. I know a few people who quit because they simply couldn't keep going through the pain that MDMA brought up for them. And as the commenters on the first thread note, very few people would go thru that dozens of times hoping for something different to happen.

[B355] My perspective is that these people are not correctly preparing for the [inner child reparenting](http://innerchild.u-dont-exist.com) that MDMA suddenly provides experiential access to. Because they are unprepared to make the best of the experience, and unprepared to integrate it after, it's sort of like being thrown into the deep end of a swimming pool before you learn to swim. You flail around for a bit then terrified, you go back to holding on to the wall and begging to get out. It really will take a long time to learn to swim that way, for most folks. On the other hand, without MDMA or loveyhuasca, many people are unable to really do inner child therapy, because it sounds fake to them, just as ego death sounded fake to me before I experienced it. So I think these approaches complement each other greatly.

[B364] ## Give the Session a Beginning and an End

[B365] (a picture)

[B366a] If you're planning a session, think through beforehand how it will start and end, and what happens in between. Rest and timing come first: don't begin already depleted, and ideally start earlier in the day, so there's time afterward for food, some normal company and most importantly, sleep.

[B366b] Set up the space you'll be in, and decide ahead of time what would make you call for outside help. Go in curious about what might come up, along with gentle intentions, not harboring demands for revelations, buried memories, good cries or spiritual breakthroughs. Sometimes people find that when they prepare to purify themselves and open up to love, they don't even end up needing the experience itself. The work is already done.

[B366c] One woman I met in Kenya was going to take shrooms to fix her tobacco and marijuana addictions. Before she took the shrooms, she simply realized she was addicted to those substances because she was trying to escape anxiety. So instead, she let the anxiety come, and accepted the feeling until it left. Her addictions vanished, no shroom trip required.

[B366d] If you're taking a psychoactive drug, though, know what you're taking and roughly how much, and look up what it mixes badly with and any medical concerns. If you've taken this amount before, that's a good starting place to understand how it could affect you this time, although people's sensitivities can vary from one session to the next, sometimes quite dramatically. If you've never taken this dose or done this type of meditation this intensely before, make sure you're not jumping into the deep end all at once.

[B366e] During the experience, the sitter (the sober person with you, not literally someone sitting on your face!) keeps you safe and offers you choices if relevant.

[B366f] As the experience winds down, give it some closure. Stop adding stimulation (I really don't like the MDMA protocols that include redosing, as it amplifies the neurotoxicity concerns). Check where you are and what day it is. Eat and drink a bit to replenish your electrolytes.

[B366g] Jot down anything you want to remember before you forget, and let your body know the session is over with some gentle massage. Keep the rest of the day free, and the next morning too if possible, since your brain is still in a vulnerable, flexible, "plastic" state, and the goal is not to cement in worse new patterns than what you had before!

[B370] Use this sequence for planned sessions. Once somebody is already in trouble, return to the [triage app](https://ibogaqueen.com/1625-2/) instead of trying to improve the ceremony plan.

[B371] ## The Fear/Panic Loop

[B372] A common loop is fear trying to solve fear. Terror appears, and the mind answers it with metaphysics, diagnosis, conspiracy, spiritual certainty, or desperate action. That usually feeds the terror.

[B373] (a picture)

[B374] **Loop breaker:**

[B375] 1. **Name it:** "This is fear trying to solve fear."
2. **Stop debating:** no metaphysics, diagnosis, or life decisions at the peak.
3. **Move down the ladder:** room → body → human → time.
4. **Repeat:** "This is a wave. I don't have to solve it at the peak."
