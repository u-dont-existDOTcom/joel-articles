[B376] ## Dealing With Sorrow: Missing Versus [Sending Love](http://love.u-dont-exist.com/)

[B377] I once found a pamphlet helpful on sorrow, although it was too "ultimate" and Advaita for my taste. I now think focusing on love is more helpful than focusing on missing somebody or something.

[B378] > *Missing hurts. Sending love feels good.*

[B379] This sounds almost embarrassingly obvious, but it took me months of daily loveyhuasca after my ex-wife Bee left to understand it.

[B380] Missing someone is a contraction. There's hunger and ache in it, it keeps circling back to you, and there's the painful fantasy that loving them means needing them to come back. Sending love is different. Sending love is pleasure in itself, even when the person is gone. Love can bless without grabbing.

[B381] The difference is enormous.

[B382] The mistake people often make in love states is simply enjoying the love--or sending heart emojis to everybody. That can feel like the whole point, and it's nice.

[B383] But the deepest utility is sending love to the places where love is missing.

[B384] - Send love to the person you miss without demanding their return.
- Send love to the part of you still waiting for them.
- Send love to the inner child who believed somebody else had to rescue him.
- Send love to the ashamed, angry, lonely, terrified, and exiled parts.

[B385] ## Hand on Heart and Gratitude 🫶🏿

[B386] A hand on the heart can change the direction of a trip, especially when the problem is emotional contraction, grief, fear, or loneliness. There's no need to manufacture loving thoughts, you're just giving the body one simple signal: love is here.

[B387] Put one hand on the heart and the other on the solar plexus just below it, sending love to the inner child. Sometimes that shifts the whole field. Once love reaches the inner child, send it toward all beings suffering now. Recognizing that you are not the only one suffering can open awareness in a liberating way, with real heart magic.

[B388] Gratitude comes like icing on the cake. Being thankful for every lesson, difficult experience, reprieve, heart opening, and moment of compassion can create a deep connection with the sacred. Never force gratitude. Let it bubble up gently.

[B390] ### Touch, Consent, and Compassion

[B391] Self-touch is different from somebody else touching you. Compassion does not mean grabbing, hugging, restraining, stroking, or crowding. Ask first: **"Would touch help, or would you rather have space?"** Good support may be silent presence from six feet away.

[B392] When the person says stop, freezes, pulls away, or seems unsure, stop. Touch must remain nonsexual, consensual, and unrelated to the helper's needs.

[B394] ## Demons, Jinn, Entities, and Shadow Forces

[B395] I have faced demons and jinn during psychedelic and exorcism-type situations. The most important thing I discovered was that fighting them did not help. Sending love did.

[B396] In one especially dramatic situation involving another person, the entity seemed to attack both of us psychically. I sent it love, and within a few seconds it left. The other person could not do that immediately. I had to show her, and it took hours of practice before it worked because she had never gone deeply into "Love Thy Enemy."

[B397] I don't have to settle whether these things are objectively real or internal formations because the response is mostly the same.

[B398] Hatred, fear, shame, pride, and rage make hooks. Love removes hooks.

[B399] The common mistake is fighting the terrifying thing with the energy it already consumes. Panic fights panic, hatred fights hatred, pride fights pride, and suddenly a small war is running inside consciousness.

[B400] Love is a better move, although it is difficult advice. Almost nobody can authentically love what terrifies them while they are terrified. So train before you need it, sending love to a frightening image, to someone you hate (without approving of what they did), to your shame and your body, to sickness and aging and fear and death, and to the inner child. Practice being the space rather than the storm.

[B401] It might look passive, but this is how you become unhookable. A demon can fight your hatred, feed on your fear, argue with your pride or seduce your grandiosity, but there's nothing in love for it to grab.

[B404] When somebody is medically, physically, or psychologically too overwhelmed to reach love, stabilize first. Don't teach nondual demonology to somebody who needs the lights down and a doctor.

[B405] ## Abuse Memories, Recovered Memories, and Certainty

[B406] Psychedelics and intense practices can bring up true trauma, symbolic material, distorted fragments, fears, fantasies, archetypes, past-life narratives, and mixtures of all of them. Don't dismiss what arises. Don't treat the certainty you feel in that state as evidence either.

[B407] **Don't confront tonight**

[B408] When you see or remember abuse, betrayal, murder, past lives, hidden crimes, or cosmic plots, write it down and get safe. Don't confront anybody while intoxicated or destabilized. Return to it after sleep with a trauma-informed therapist, integration specialist, or grounded person you trust.

[B409] The experience may matter deeply. It could be true psychologically, or symbolically, or literally, or only partly true, or false in the form it first took. You don't have to decide tonight.

[B410] ## Dealing With Shadow Thoughts

[B411] When we try to purify the mind, something natural and frightening may happen: the opposite becomes more visible, almost like Newton's Third Law--every action arrives with an equal and opposite reaction.

[B412] Shadow thoughts and feelings are not inherently a problem. They co-arise naturally and do not need to be attached to, like shadows cast by objects in sunlight. I also notice them after excessive focus. The mind becomes stressed and seeks an exit from the pressure, often in the opposite direction from the focus.

[B413] Relax, unwind, unbind, and unweave the identity illusion, mind, and unified experience. My pamphlet on already-perfect goes further into this.

[B414] I am not repeating the simplistic Hermetic claim that opposites are required to understand anything. A hated baby does **not** feel love, and a loved baby does **not** feel hate. In the subconscious world of concepts, however, two sides are often considered together.

[B415] During a trip, don't identify with every shadow thought, suppress it, confess it to everybody, or convert it into a permanent diagnosis. Notice: **"A thought is here. A feeling is here. It is intense, but it is not the whole of me."** Then return to room, body, human, time, and love.

[B419] ## After the Storm

[B420] Don't make huge decisions or diagnose yourself from an intense night. You don't need to assume every vision was literal, nor that anything you realized is actually true (or false). Ask your body what it needs from you now. I'm betting it will tell you: Give me sleep, natural food, pure water, a nice nature walk. It helps to integrate the experience via journaling your feelings, and talking with someone grounded (not the 20 chaotic people who are still coming down). To prevent your mind from giving spirituality a negative connotation, engage in some calm prayer or gentle meditation.

[B424] ### What Kind of Aftermath Is This?

[B425] When symptoms linger, name the pattern rather than collapsing everything into "I'm broken." Different patterns need different support.

[B426] - **Anxiety and fear:** grounding, routine, reduced stimulation, CBT-style reality testing, and therapy when it persists.
- **Sleep disturbance and nightmares:** sleep hygiene, no more substances or intense practice, and professional help when severe or prolonged.
- **Derealization and depersonalization:** ordinary-world grounding, exercise, nature, outside attention, less rumination, peer or family support, and therapy when needed.
- **Visual distortions or HPPD-like symptoms:** reduce stress and substance use; seek professional support when symptoms interfere with life.
- **Social disconnection and shame:** one or two safe humans, peer support, and no social-media crowdsourcing while raw.
- **Existential shock:** write it down, slow down, and return to practical love before building a metaphysical system.
- **Paranoia or psychosis-like beliefs:** professional support quickly, especially when beliefs drive unsafe action.
- **Somatic hyperarousal:** gentle movement, breathing, grounding, and medical care for severe, unusual, or worrying symptoms.

[B427] **No "another ceremony will fix this" reflex.**

[B428] Don't take more psychedelics, cannabis, ketamine, intense breathwork, fast, push kundalini, deprive yourself of sleep, meditate or stretch to extremes, or schedule "another ceremony to fix it" while destabilized. Deep practice can wait until the floor is back.

[B429] Then ask what actually happened. What was medical or environmental? What was emotional? What was old trauma? What was fantasy? What was useful? What changed behavior? What needs therapy, an apology, rest, or plain practical action?

[B430] It can feel like the more cosmic the story sounds, the bigger the insight. But the fruit of an insight is whether suffering goes down and love gets more available.
