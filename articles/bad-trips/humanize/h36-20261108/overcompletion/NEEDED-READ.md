# The needed read on No Rash Actions' "experience-content" line, as it would read cut (2026-11-08)

The overcompletion reader for part 4 called B320's last two sentences ("Because you saw it, it might feel like you have
to act on it, ... But more likely you had an experience, and it can wait until the state has passed.") a second saying
of B316. A fresh Haiku agent, given `tools/humanization/needed_read_brief.md` with the line that would be left ("Treat
trip content first as experience-content, rather than fact-content."), the section's paragraphs before it, and what it
is for, found it is the paragraph's whole instruction and that it doesn't make sense alone: "experience-content" and
"fact-content" are "invented compound terms a high-school reader would not know", and "a high-school reader would
probably get the contrast and not the action, because the sentence never says what to do or think differently". So the
two sentences are the term's explanation where it is first used (`SKILL.md`, Writing for Pangram, fix 2), not
overcompletion, and they stay.
