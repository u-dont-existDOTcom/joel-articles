2. **If the person is fully alert and able to swallow, I would give cyproheptadine right away**, since it blocks serotonin receptors. So far, the evidence for it has only been [case reports](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39791184/), where nobody could compare it against people who didn't get it. When doctors [gave it to five patients](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9696181/), three had no signs left within two hours, and the other two took longer, so keep going with the other steps while you see whether it works.
