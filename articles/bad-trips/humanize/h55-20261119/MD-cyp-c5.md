2. **If the person is fully alert and able to swallow, I'd give them cyproheptadine right away**, an old allergy medicine that also blocks serotonin receptors. The evidence for it is only [case reports](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39791184/) so far (nobody has compared it against people who didn't get it), but in [a small series of five ER patients](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9696181/), three of them had no symptoms left within two hours. Keep cooling them down either way, and get help now if their signs are moderate or severe, before you know whether it works.
