When their jaw tenses, that's also the time to stop taking MDMA and get them to slow down and cool off. Keep watching them: a headache that keeps getting worse can mean their sodium is getting too low, and so can confusion or vomiting. Weakness or palpitations can mean their potassium is dropping. A seizure or a collapse raises the stakes, so call medics, since overheating and MDMA toxicity can cause those too. Even a single normal dose can drop sodium. [When healthy volunteers took it once in clinical trials](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39546312/), about a third ended up with low sodium (hyponatremia). None of the volunteers whose drinking was limited did, so keep water to small sips.
