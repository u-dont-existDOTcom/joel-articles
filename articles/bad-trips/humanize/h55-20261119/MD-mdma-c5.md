Once their jaw tenses, I'd make that their last dose of MDMA, since more only adds to the toxicity, and get them to slow down and cool off. I'd also watch how much plain water they drink. If they're downing bottle after bottle, [even a single normal dose](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39546312/) raises the risk of their sodium dropping too low, so give them an electrolyte drink to sip when they're thirsty instead. Low sodium tends to show up as a headache that keeps getting worse, often with throwing up or confusion, and low potassium can leave their muscles so weak they can barely stand, or their heartbeat feeling irregular (palpitations). Call medics for any of these, and get them there right away for a seizure or collapse.
