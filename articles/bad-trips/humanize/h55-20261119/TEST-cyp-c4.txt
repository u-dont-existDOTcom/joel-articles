If the person is fully alert and able to swallow, I would give cyproheptadine right away, since it blocks serotonin receptors. So far, the evidence for it has only been case reports, where nobody could compare it against people who didn't get it. When doctors gave it to five patients, three had no signs left within two hours, and the other two took longer, so keep going with the other steps while you see whether it works.
