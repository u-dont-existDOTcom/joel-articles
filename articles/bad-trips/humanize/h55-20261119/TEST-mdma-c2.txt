When their jaw tenses, that's also the time to stop taking MDMA and get them to slow down and cool off. Watch for a headache that keeps getting worse. If they also get confused or start vomiting, their sodium may be getting too low, and even a single normal dose can do that: when healthy volunteers took it once in clinical trials, about a third ended up with low sodium (hyponatremia). None of the ones whose drinking was limited did, so keep water to small sips. Weakness or palpitations can mean low potassium instead. A seizure or a collapse raises the stakes, so call medics, since overheating and MDMA toxicity can cause those too.
