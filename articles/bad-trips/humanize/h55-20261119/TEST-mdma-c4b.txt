Once their jaw tenses, that's their last dose of MDMA, so get them to slow down and cool off. If they're drinking a lot of plain water, even a single normal dose raises the risk of their sodium dropping too low, so I'd have them sip an electrolyte drink when they're thirsty. A headache that gets worse and worse is how low sodium tends to show up, often with throwing up or confusion, and it's time to call medics. The same goes for muscles getting weaker and weaker, or palpitations where the heart skips beats, which can mean their potassium is low. And a seizure or collapse means the MDMA toxicity has become an emergency, so get medics there right away.
