If the person is fully alert and able to swallow, I would give cyproheptadine early, since it blocks serotonin receptors. So far, the evidence for it has only been case reports, and a recent review found them too weak to show that it works. When doctors gave it by mouth to five patients, three had no signs left within two hours, and the other two after a second dose. I wouldn't wait for it to work before doing the other steps.
