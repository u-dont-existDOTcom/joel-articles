Once their jaw tenses, that's their last dose of MDMA, so get them to slow down and cool off. If they drink a lot of plain water, [even a single normal dose](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39546312/) raises the risk of their sodium dropping too low, so have them sip an electrolyte drink when they're thirsty instead. If a headache keeps getting worse, the vomiting keeps coming back, or they're getting more and more confused, their sodium may already be low, so call medics. Do the same if their muscles keep getting weaker or their palpitations keep getting stronger, which can mean low potassium, like my second wife had. A seizure or collapse can also come from overheating or MDMA toxicity, so if either happens, call medics right away.
