Once their jaw tenses, that's their last dose of MDMA, so get them to slow down and cool off. Is a headache getting worse and worse, does the vomiting keep coming back, or are they more and more confused? Their sodium may be getting too low, so call medics. If they've been drinking a lot of plain water, [even a single normal dose](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39546312/) raises that risk, so have them sip an electrolyte drink when they're thirsty instead. Are their muscles getting weaker, or their palpitations getting stronger? That can mean low potassium, like my second wife had, and they need medics for that too. A seizure or collapse can also come from overheating or MDMA toxicity, so if either happens, call medics right away.
