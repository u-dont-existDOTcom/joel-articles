Once their jaw tenses, I'd make that their last dose of MDMA and get them to slow down and cool off. I'd also watch how much plain water they drink, because if it's a lot, even a single normal dose raises the risk of their sodium dropping too low (an electrolyte drink, sipped when they're thirsty, is better). A headache that keeps getting worse, especially with throwing up or confusion, is how low sodium often shows up, and that's when I'd call medics. Muscles that keep getting weaker, or a heart that starts skipping beats (palpitations), can mean their potassium is low, which is what they found in my second wife, so that's a call to medics too. And a seizure or collapse means MDMA toxicity has turned into an emergency, so get medics there right away.
