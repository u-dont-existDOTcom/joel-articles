Once their jaw tenses, that's their last dose of MDMA, so get them to slow down and cool off. If they're drinking a lot of plain water, even a single normal dose can drop their sodium too low, so I'd have them sip an electrolyte drink when they're thirsty. A headache that gets worse and worse is how that tends to show up, often with throwing up or confusion, and it's time to call medics. Muscle weakness or palpitations, where the heart pounds or skips, can mean their potassium is low instead, like my second wife's, and that needs help too. And if they have a seizure or collapse, which overheating or MDMA toxicity can also cause, that raises it to an emergency: call medics right away.
