If the person is fully alert and able to swallow, I'd give them cyproheptadine right away. It's an old allergy medicine that also blocks serotonin receptors, which is why it can help here. The evidence for it is only case reports so far (nobody has compared it against people who didn't get it), but in a small series of five ER patients, three had no symptoms left within two hours, and the other two took a bit longer. Even so, I wouldn't wait to see if it works before cooling them down and getting help for moderate or severe signs.
