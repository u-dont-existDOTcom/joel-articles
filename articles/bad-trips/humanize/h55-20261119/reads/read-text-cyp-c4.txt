If the person is fully alert and able to swallow, I would give cyproheptadine early, since it blocks serotonin receptors. So far, the evidence for it has only been case reports, where nobody could compare it against people who didn't get it. When doctors gave it to five patients, three had no signs left within two hours, and the other two after a second dose. So it can take a while even when it works, and I wouldn't wait on it before doing the other steps.
