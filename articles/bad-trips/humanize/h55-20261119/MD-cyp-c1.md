2. **If the person is fully alert and able to swallow, I would give cyproheptadine early.** It blocks serotonin receptors, but so far the evidence for it has only been case reports, and [a recent review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39791184/) found them too weak to say whether it changes the outcome. When doctors [gave it by mouth to five patients](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9696181/), three had no signs left within two hours, and the other two after a second dose. I wouldn't wait to see whether it works before doing the other steps.
