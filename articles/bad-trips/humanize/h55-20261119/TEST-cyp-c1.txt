If the person is fully alert and able to swallow, I would give cyproheptadine early. It blocks serotonin receptors, but so far the evidence for it has only been case reports, and a recent review found them too weak to say whether it changes the outcome. When doctors gave it by mouth to five patients, three had no signs left within two hours, and the other two after a second dose. I wouldn't wait to see whether it works before doing the other steps.
