The Fear/Panic Loop

A common loop is fear trying to solve fear. Terror appears, and the mind answers it with metaphysics, diagnosis, conspiracy, spiritual certainty, or desperate action. That usually feeds the terror.

Loop breaker:

Name it: "This is fear trying to solve fear."
Stop debating: no metaphysics, diagnosis, or life decisions while peaking.
Move down the ladder: room → body → human → time.
Repeat: "This is a wave. I don't have to solve it while peaking."
