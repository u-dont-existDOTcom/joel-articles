Hand on Heart and Gratitude 🫶🏿

A hand on the heart can change the direction of a trip, especially when the problem is emotional contraction, grief, fear, or loneliness. The point is not to manufacture loving thoughts. Give the body one simple signal: love is here.

Put one hand on the heart and the other on the solar plexus just below it, sending love to the inner child. Sometimes that shifts the whole field. Once love reaches the inner child, send it toward all beings suffering now. Recognizing that you are not the only one suffering can open awareness in a liberating way, with real heart magic.

Gratitude comes like icing on the cake. Being thankful for every lesson, difficult experience, reprieve, heart opening, and moment of compassion can create a deep connection with the sacred. Never force gratitude. Let it bubble up gently.

Touch, Consent, and Compassion

Self-touch is different from somebody else touching you. Compassion does not mean grabbing, hugging, restraining, stroking, or crowding. Ask first: "Would touch help, or would you rather have space?" Good support may be silent presence from six feet away.

When the person says stop, freezes, pulls away, or seems unsure, stop. Touch must remain nonsexual, consensual, and unrelated to the helper's needs.
