Psychological First Aid: The First Five Minutes

Anything that could let someone hurt themselves in the next five minutes takes priority, so get the car keys out of reach and turn the stove off, and move them away from roads and balconies, or pools of water for that matter. Letting them move around is fine, and pacing can help, but keep them from taking more of any substance. If they want to go confront somebody, that can wait. And if they want to leave, have someone go with them. Put any weapons away and keep the person away from crowds and strangers. If they want to post their big revelation about the universe, or message an ex, take the phone.

They're feeling overwhelmed, so dim the lights, and clear out the extra people around them.

Then remind them where they are and what's happening, since a trip can make them lose track of it:

You're in [place]. It's [date/time]. You took [substance, if known], and its effects will peak and then change.

Demons, Jinn, Entities, and Shadow Forces

I have faced demons and jinn during psychedelic and exorcism-type situations. The most important thing I discovered was that fighting them did not help. Sending love did.

In one especially dramatic situation involving another person, the entity seemed to attack both of us psychically. I sent it love, and within a few seconds it left. The other person could not do that immediately. I had to show her, and it took hours of practice before it worked because she had never gone deeply into "Love Thy Enemy."

I don't have to settle whether these things are objectively real or internal formations because the response is mostly the same.

Hatred, fear, shame, pride, and rage make hooks. Love removes hooks.

The common mistake is fighting the terrifying thing with the energy it already consumes. Panic fights panic, hatred fights hatred, pride fights pride, and suddenly a small war is running inside consciousness.

Love is a better move, although it is difficult advice. Almost nobody can authentically love what terrifies them while they are terrified. Train before the emergency: send love to a frightening image, a hated person without approving what they did, your shame, your body, sickness, aging, fear, death, and the inner child. Practice being the space rather than the storm.

This is not passivity. It is becoming unhookable. A demon can fight hatred, feed on fear, argue with pride, and seduce grandiosity. Love gives it nothing to grab.

Safety rule

Even a completely real-feeling entity does not make decisions for your body. No entity may tell you to hurt yourself or another person, drive, jump, flee, accuse somebody, cross a sexual boundary, or make a life decision tonight.

When somebody is medically, physically, or psychologically too overwhelmed to reach love, stabilize first. Don't teach nondual demonology to somebody who needs the lights down and a doctor.
