If a trip comes with diarrhea or nausea, a toxic heaviness or a dirty body-feeling, jaw tension, loss of bodily control or neurological disorganization, the mind may feel like it's going crazy because the gut or the brain is going crazy. By loss of bodily control I mean someone wetting or soiling themselves, or not being able to sit up, stand, walk or talk. Neurological disorganization is my catch-all for that plus a whole lot more, so I'd go through [the brain checks](#§my-brain-feels-damaged) further up, which say when to call medics.
