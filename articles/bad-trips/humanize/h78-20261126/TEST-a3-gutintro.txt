The gut tools are the first things I would use, even preventively on the come-up (while the drug is still kicking in), because I want to address possible toxins before I put the distress down to something more mysterious, like a spiritual sign.

After all, nausea and diarrhea are common on psychedelics, and people generally don't feel good mentally during the nausea and gut pain parts of the come-up. Diarrhea isn't necessarily bad, since it can be a flushing out of toxins, and usually there's a lot of relief once everything starts coming out in the toilet. But severe diarrhea can flush out too much fluid, and being badly dried out is on [the list to call medics for](#§call-medics-for). Still, you shouldn't have a lot of gut discomfort if the toxins in the gut get bound, which is what the tools below do, and dosing lower on the psychedelic helps too.
