After all, nausea and diarrhea are common on psychedelics, and people generally don't feel good mentally during the nausea and gut pain parts of the come-up. Diarrhea isn't necessarily bad, since it can be a flushing out of toxins, and usually there's a lot of relief once everything starts coming out in the toilet. But severe diarrhea can flush out too much fluid, and being badly dried out (not peeing for hours, or dizzy when they stand up) is on [the list to call medics for](#§call-medics-for).
