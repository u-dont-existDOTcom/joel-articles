These are the first things I would use, even preventively on the come-up, because I want to address possible toxins before assigning the distress to something more mysterious.

Nausea and diarrhea are common on psychedelics anyway, and people generally don't feel good mentally during the nausea and gut pain parts of the come-up. But you shouldn't have a lot of gut discomfort if you do things right: ideally we bind the toxins in the gut to avoid too much nausea or diarrhea, and dosing lower on the psychedelic can help too.

Diarrhea is actually not as bad necessarily, since it can be a flushing out of toxins, and usually there's a lot of relief once everything starts coming out in the toilet. It depends how severe the diarrhea is though, because then it can flush out too much fluid and cause dehydration.
