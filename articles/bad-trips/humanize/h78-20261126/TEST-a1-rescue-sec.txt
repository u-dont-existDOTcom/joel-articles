This hierarchy is for acute situations, including emergencies--not merely uncomfortable trips.

In general it's good to try all these things in very low, increasing doses before they are needed in crisis.

Sometimes the mind isn't in a metaphysical crisis. The body feels awful and the altered mind writes cosmic horror to explain it. Nausea, heat, exhaustion, dehydration, overstimulation, and sleep loss all get read as spiritual signs very quickly.

A horrible thought isn't automatically a revelation. The body may need quiet, water, cooling, warmth, rest, or a calmer room. The body is part of the spiritual field.

The hierarchy appears before the mystical sections because love doesn't require us to ignore the body.

If a trip comes with diarrhea or nausea, a toxic heaviness or a dirty body-feeling, jaw tension, loss of bodily control or neurological disorganization, the mind may feel like it's going crazy because the gut or the brain is going crazy. Loss of bodily control includes wetting or soiling themselves and not being able to sit up, stand, walk or talk, basically everything related to controlling the body. That's also something to [call medics for](#§call-medics-for), and the remedies can begin while help is being arranged. Neurological disorganization is basically that plus a whole lot more, a catch-all term for things like confusion, clumsiness, shaking, or light and sound being too much.

Those trips are what the remedies below are for, and the shadow work further down is for a mainly emotional crisis. If you're in mental hell and gut hell at the same time, you don't need to try to tell which is from what, you address the gut first. If the gut is fine, obviously the mental hell is not related to the gut. A gut-driven mental hell is often mistaken as shadow work.
