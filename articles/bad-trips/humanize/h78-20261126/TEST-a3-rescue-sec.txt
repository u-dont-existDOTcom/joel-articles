This hierarchy is for acute situations, including emergencies--not merely uncomfortable trips.

In general it's good to try all these things in very low, increasing doses before they are needed in crisis.

Sometimes the mind isn't in a metaphysical crisis. The body feels awful and the altered mind writes cosmic horror to explain it. Nausea, heat, exhaustion, dehydration, overstimulation, and sleep loss all get read as spiritual signs very quickly.

A horrible thought isn't automatically a revelation. The body may need quiet, water, cooling, warmth, rest, or a calmer room. The body is part of the spiritual field.

The hierarchy appears before the mystical sections because love doesn't require us to ignore the body.

The mind may feel like it's going crazy because the gut or the brain is going crazy. I'd suspect that when a trip comes with diarrhea or nausea, a toxic heaviness or a dirty body-feeling, jaw tension, loss of bodily control (someone wetting or soiling themselves, or not being able to sit up, stand, walk or talk) or neurological disorganization, which is my catch-all for that plus a whole lot more. For those last two I'd go through [the brain checks](#§my-brain-feels-damaged) further up, which say when to call medics.

Those trips are what the remedies below are for, and the shadow work further down is for a mainly emotional crisis. If you're in mental hell and gut hell at the same time, you don't need to try to tell which is from what, so just address the gut first with [the gut tools](#§gut-toxin-and-die-off-tools). A gut-driven mental hell is often mistaken as shadow work.
