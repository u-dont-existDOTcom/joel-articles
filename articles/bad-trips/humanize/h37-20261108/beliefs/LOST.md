## Where the loss sits

The loss is concentrated, not spread evenly, and it comes in two places. First, whole master sections that were mostly Joel's framing were cut down to their rule: the Quick Checklist's shadow-work and love branches (findings 2-3), the Longer Triage (1, 4, 5), and the hierarchy's opening and closing paragraphs (6-7). The guide kept the remedies and the instructions in these but dropped the sentences that said why. Second, the shadow-work end of the guide lost its argument: "The Other Bypass" lost its "skip this inner work and it leaks into everything" paragraph (15-16), and "Demons, Jinn..." lost why love works and the entity safety rule (17). The rescue chemistry (DMSO, MDMA, magnesium, zeolite, pes-caprae, niacin) is almost untouched, and so are his stories (Bee, the second wife, the baby, the Kenya trips, the demon with another person, the Reddit poster).

The three sections that lost the most of him are: (1) Quick Checklist plus Longer Triage, which left no trace of love-first, shadow work as a branch, or "can love be reached yet" (findings 1-5); (2) My Acute Rescue Hierarchy, whose opening and closing framing is the body-first-then-love argument (6-7); (3) The Other Bypass / Demons, Jinn, Entities, and Shadow Forces together (15-17). Psychological First Aid and Grounding Menu each lost one or two lines (9-11), and the remaining findings (8, 12-14, 18) are single sentences.
