## Psychological First Aid: The First Five Minutes  [B233]

[B235] (a picture)

[B236] [HIS OWN] Anything that could let someone hurt themselves in the next five minutes takes priority, so get the car keys out of reach and turn the stove off, and move them away from roads and balconies, or pools of water for that matter. Letting them move around is fine, and pacing can help, but keep them from taking more of any substance. If they want to go confront somebody, that can wait. And if they want to leave, have someone go with them. Put any weapons away and keep the person away from crowds and strangers. If they want to post their big revelation about the universe, or message an ex, take the phone.

[B240] [HIS OWN] They're feeling overwhelmed, so dim the lights, and clear out the extra people around them.

[B242] [HIS OWN] Sometimes it can be helpful to remind them where they are and what's happening, since a trip can make them lose track of the fact that they took a substance which has temporary effects.

[B244] If a practice like meditation or fasting brought this on, have them stop it now, since doing more of it only makes this stronger. There's no drug wearing off here, so it could be over soon or take a while.

[R27-PFA1] [HIS OWN] As the person helping, you want to play the role of the calm, caring anchor to the safe present moment, not the witch doctor guru who knows magic spells. So don't force eye contact, touch, music, prayer, chanting on them, just offer gently if it might help. After they've slept, they may wish to talk more about their experience.

[R27-PFA2] [HIS OWN] But if they want to talk about it right now, don't try to win an argument with their visions, the entities they spoke to, the memories that surfaced, or whatever cosmic conclusions they reached ten minutes ago... But don't let silence validate anything harmful either, like a plan that ends with somebody getting hurt.

[R27-PFA3] [HIS OWN] Sometimes people get stuck in a panic loop, where they make interpretations to try to solve a feeling of panic, but then those create another kind of panic. It's helpful to remind them that sometimes feelings just need to be felt, so they can move and release, and then thinking can be more clear afterwards.

[B373] (a picture)

[R27-PFA4] [HIS OWN] If they're being attacked by a demon, let your care help them connect to love, which cancels fear. Hopefully they've been practicing that prior. Don't encourage them to believe they are crazy, impure, weak or broken. While many interpretations are possible, a therapeutic one would not be that they're being punished or failing the medicine.

[R27-PFA5] [HIS OWN] I actually get something like this regularly when I take a high dose of something (or meditate too much), where I feel like I'm at risk of ruining my life if I don't stay away from sex and money. And there's a voice that really commands me to leave both of those completely (which led to 3 weeks of wandering in Malaysia & Cambodia without money once). What actually helped me in one case when receiving this stern order from a higher power, was to ask it to clarify. I said, "I agree, that makes sense as a Buddhist, and I'd like to leave those behind at some point, especially money. But what's the best timeline to benefit all involved? Some people are dependent on me, I have a son, and so forth." The voice didn't respond, it just went away. So I took it more like a long-term suggestion than the urgency I assumed it needed based on how loud it was at first.

### Stillness or Movement?  [B248]

[B249] Test stillness and movement rather than turning them into rival religions. Try one for two minutes and ask: *Does this make me safer and clearer, or more activated and impulsive?*

[B250] When lying down feels like imprisonment, pace slowly in a contained space. When pacing begins to resemble escape, sit against a wall or lie under a blanket. The goal is to let the nervous system discharge without creating a new danger.

[B251] **Parallel processing** can help when lying still turns into immobile introspection. Let one part of attention stay with the body, fear, grief, or memory while another part completes a simple, safe task with a visible endpoint: sort ten objects, draw, fold five towels, or wash one cup. Finishing something small helps confirm that time is moving and ordinary reality is still here. Stop if the task increases agitation, speed, or risk.

[B313] **Movement evidence note:** the best-supported general advice is to create a safe, low-stimulation environment and choose responses that reduce danger. A [2024 study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-79931-w) of strategies for resolving challenging psychedelic experiences identified physical movement, intentional breathing, sensory engagement, sleep-seeking, and social support among the ways people navigate difficulty.

## Grounding Menu: Choose One or Two Things  [B294]

[B295] Grounding is love becoming practical. Choose one or two options rather than all of them. Twelve simultaneous techniques can become another form of panic.

[B252] A useful order is **room → body → human → time → meaning**. Most people begin with meaning because the experience feels cosmic.

[B296] (a picture)

[B297] **Body**

[B298] - Feel both feet against the floor.
- Put one hand on the heart and one on the solar plexus.
- Hold something with weight or texture.
- Slowly unclench the jaw, hands, belly, and shoulders.

[B301] **Breath**

[B302] - Exhale longer than you inhale for ten breaths.
- Try inhaling for four and exhaling for six, without strain.
- Or breathe softly and slowly through the nose, taking in slightly less air than desired until there is faint air hunger.
- Never force breath-holds while intoxicated or panicked. Stop for increased fear, dizziness, chest pain, or medical concern.
- Whisper: "This is a wave. I can love the part of me that feels it."

[B303] **Music**

[B304] - Silence is a real option.
- Use familiar music when possible. I wouldn't force a predesigned emotional arc or an unfamiliar therapeutic playlist on somebody who dislikes it.
- Try a steady drum rhythm for stabilization or grounding; try airy harp-like or meditative music when the person wants something gentler and spiritually uplifting.
- Soothing meditative music, like that of Chantress Seba and others on my [music page](http://music.u-dont-exist.com/), can be very helpful.
- Keep the stop or change control immediate.

[B307] **Human**

[B308] - Ask one calm person to stay as the lead anchor.
- Let them remind you where you are.
- Don't process with the most reactive person in your life.

[B309] **Time**

[B310] - Remember that these states usually rise, peak, and change.
- Put every big decision into tomorrow's notebook.

[B311] **Ordinary world**

[B312] - Eat simple healthy food, such as fruit, when you can.
- Walk slowly with a clear-headed person in a safe environment.
- Go outside only away from roads, heights, crowds, and water.
- Avoid strenuous exercise with overheating, chest pain, faintness, severe confusion, stimulant use, an unknown mixture, or any medical concern.

[B314] **Buteyko evidence note:** published evidence for Buteyko-style breathing mainly concerns asthma and breathing retraining, rather than psychedelic or spiritual-practice crisis. I include it because panic-related over-breathing can lower CO<sub>2</sub> and directly cause tingling, lightheadedness, confusion, and unreality feelings ([Hopkins Medicine](https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/health/conditions-and-diseases/hyperventilation)). Use the simpler longer-exhale option first. Never strain or turn breathing into another achievement.

## Get One Human Anchor  [B253]

[B254] Many bad trips get worse because somebody is alone with a frightened or over-certain mind. Other times it's the opposite: too many people crowd in and start advising, arguing, spiritualizing, or panicking.

[B255] So **one calm person should be the anchor** and take the lead. One person can only handle so much, though. When fatigue or logistics get to be too much, or keeping them safe does, or there's a risk of violence or self-harm, bring in **one more calm adult or a crisis professional** to back them up.

[B256] (a picture)

[B257] **Script for the person in the altered state:**

[B258] > I'm in an intense psychedelic or altered state and I need grounding. I don't need you to solve my life. Please stay with me, speak slowly, remind me where I am, and help me not make any big decisions until tomorrow.

[B259] **Script for the sitter or friend:**

[B260] > I'm here. You're not alone. This is a wave. We don't have to solve your whole life right now. Let's make your body safe, keep things simple, and stay together.

### Alone at 3 a.m.  [B271]

[B272] The [triage app](https://ibogaqueen.com/1625-2/) has the stripped-down 3 a.m. plan. Once the lamp is on, the keys are away, and **"No big actions until morning"** is visible, use the phone only to reach a calm person or support service.

[B273] Send the human-anchor script.

[R31-ALONE] [HIS OWN] While you wait, put one hand on your heart and the other on your solar plexus just under your chest, and say to yourself: "This feeling is allowed to move through. I can love the part of me that feels it."

[B274] When the body still needs movement, keep it contained and boring: brush your teeth, stretch gently, take a calm shower, or do a few slow wall push-ups. Then protect sleep.

## For the Person Helping: Calm Anchor, Not Guru  [B279]

[B280] The Zendo Project, a harm-reduction group that offers peer support at festivals and other events to people having a hard time in an altered state, sums up its approach in four principles: creating a safe space, sitting with the person rather than guiding them, talking them through it rather than down from it, and remembering that difficult isn't necessarily bad.

[B82] Crying, grieving, remembering, or touching a genuine wound calls for loving awareness. Interrogation, analysis every thirty seconds, and "bro, you're purging ancestral karma" while somebody white-knuckles the carpet are generally less helpful.

[B83] Stay present. If it helps, you can say to yourself: *This feeling is allowed to move through. I don't have to become it. I can love the part of me that feels it.* Or simply: *Can I send love to the one who's suffering?*

### Do  [B281]

[B282] - Ask simple questions: "Would less light help?" "Music or quiet?" "Closer or farther away?" "Water?" (in small sips) "Need the bathroom?"
- Reflect without arguing: "That sounds terrifying." "I'm with you." "Let's breathe together."
- Keep the room quiet, private, warm, and physically safe, and move anyone they're wary of somewhere else.
- Call for help early when safety is slipping.
- Translate metaphysics into shared safety values: "This feels sacred and huge. Sacred power protects life tonight."
- Set behavioral boundaries without humiliation: "I won't help anybody hurt themselves or another person. We are keeping every body safe."

### When What They Say Turns Toward Harm  [B285]

[B286] When somebody says, "I am God, so I can kill," leading with "No you're not" or "That's delusional" can turn the encounter into a status fight. Nodding along as though harm is spiritually neutral is also unacceptable. Treat the statement as a safety signal and build a bridge back toward non-harm.

[B287] **Non-combative philosophical grounding:**

[B288] > I hear how vast and certain this feels. Many traditions say God is in everyone. That makes every body sacred, not disposable. Peace is the godly move tonight. No proof is needed. We protect life and sleep first.

[B289] Other useful lines: "Maybe you are touching something divine. Divine doesn't need a violent test." "Tonight the commandment is no harm, no proof, no mission, sleep first."

[B290] Redirect within the person's value-world toward peace and protection, increase containment, and bring in crisis support as early and as non-coercively as the situation allows.

[B291] When talk of harm continues, shift from philosophy to safety. Create distance from potential targets and keep your own exit. Don't block, crowd, threaten, mock, or physically wrestle away a weapon. Quietly remove means only when that can be done without confrontation or turning yourself into the target.

[B292] Call crisis or emergency services for a specific target, available means, movement toward action, escalating agitation, a weapon, violence underway, or a situation you can't contain.

[B293] Be brief and specific with emergency services: "Someone is in an altered-state or mental-health crisis, has threatened to harm others, may have access to [means if known], and needs a medical or mental-health crisis response with de-escalation support. Please send crisis-trained responders or EMS if available."

## No Rash Actions: Visions Are Not Commands  [B315]

[B316] An intense altered state can make everything feel final, cosmic, and urgent, and it might feel like you have to act right now. But more likely the urgency is just part of the state, and acting on it isn't safe.

[B317] **Put yourself on a 24–72 hour action lock.**

[B318] [HIS OWN] Until you have slept and eaten, maybe don't drive, travel, break up, make up, quit your job, confess everything, confront or accuse that list of people you've been meaning to... Think twice before sending whatever ridiculous message you're planning on to Judge Amos, posting that revelation you'll forget tomorrow, giving away your BMW, buying a new BMW. After you decide that you are permanently enlightened or permanently damaged, and you explained the whole universe to your parents in 42 seconds, you can still change your mind the next day.

[B319] **Write it down, get some sleep, and go back over it with one grounded person once your head is clear.**

[B320] Treat trip content first as **experience-content**, rather than fact-content. Because you saw it, it might feel like you have to act on it, and because it felt cosmic, obeying it might seem wise. But more likely you had an experience, and it can wait until the state has passed.

[B321] **No revelation makes murder, assault, coercion, sexual boundary-crossing, reckless driving, or self-harm safe tonight.** Anything requiring violated consent or harm belongs in the notebook until you can review it with a clear head.

## When the Trip Feels Amazing: Euphoric Certainty  [B323]

[B324] The action lock matters especially when the state feels blissful: *I am God. I am chosen. Everybody must hear this tonight. I finally understand everything.* Bliss is not proof of safety, and it often makes grounding less welcome.

[B326] **Two questions while glowing:**

[B327] 1. **Have I slept?** Sleep comes before mission.
2. **Has this happened before, and how did it end?** A recurring pattern with lost sleep, racing thoughts, pressured speech, or everyone saying you are not yourself needs grounded support before action.

[B328] The revelation will still exist after breakfast. If it is true, it survives sleep. If it cannot survive one night of rest, it was an emergency impulse wearing angel wings.
