# My Acute Rescue Hierarchy of Remedies  [B138]

[B139] This hierarchy is for acute situations, including emergencies--not merely uncomfortable trips.

[N-AN-Q1] [HIS OWN] In general it's good to try all these things in very low, increasing doses before they are needed in crisis.

[B86] Sometimes the mind isn't in a metaphysical crisis. The body feels awful and the altered mind writes cosmic horror to explain it. Nausea, heat, exhaustion, dehydration, overstimulation, and sleep loss all become spiritual content very quickly.

[B87] A horrible thought isn't automatically a revelation. The body may need quiet, water, cooling, warmth, rest, or a calmer room. The body is part of the spiritual field.

[R27-HIER] [HIS OWN] The hierarchy appears before the mystical sections because love doesn't require us to ignore the body.

[B141] A trip with diarrhea, nausea, toxic heaviness, a dirty body-feeling, loss of bodily control, jaw tension, neurological disorganization, or the sense that the mind is going crazy because the gut or brain is going crazy belongs in a different branch from a mainly emotional crisis. A gut-driven mental hell is often mistaken as shadow work.

## How I label the evidence  [B147]

[B148] - **Established acute treatment:** standard seizure first aid, rapid rescue benzodiazepines, cooling dangerous hyperthermia, and treating documented electrolyte abnormalities.
- **Limited human evidence:** interventions with clinical reports or small trials but no strong modern outcome evidence.
- **Mechanism or preclinical evidence:** biologically plausible effects shown mainly in cells or animals.
- **Personal field signal:** what I have repeatedly observed in myself or other people. Repetition and a clear immediate endpoint make an anecdote more useful, but they do not turn it into a population rate.

## Some Remedies Require More Caution  [B217]

[B219] [Humic acid](http://humicacid.u-dont-exist.com/) *when eaten*, can produce a hyperintelligent state for me. From the outside that may look manic, and it could destabilize somebody prone to mania or without meditation training. I wouldn't use it casually during a bad trip. Smelling it does not produce that hyperintelligent state but does feel relaxing and very beneficial for brain health, so I'd start with that.

[B220] [Cinnamon](http://cinnamon.u-dont-exist.com/) can produce an intense "brain surgery" or "brain scraping" effect, especially the first time it combines with an ayahuasca-like state. That may be therapeutic in the right context, but it is not a casual rescue tool.

[B221] I would also be extremely careful with *Acacia confusa* eaten with *Mimosa hostilis*. For me, that combination produced hellishly intense psychosis-like and autism-like states before transforming into a week of extraordinary creativity and joy. The later benefit did not make the crisis recommendable.

[B222] [Iboga](http://iboga.u-dont-exist.com/)--especially male iboga--potentiates many drugs and can be dangerous. A low amount combined well for me; doubling it became dangerously intense. I include that only as a warning.

## Gut-Toxin and Parasite-Die-Off Tools  [B150]

[B151] These are the first things I would use, even preventively on the come-up, because I want to address possible toxins before assigning the distress to something more mysterious.

### Zeolite and Ferment in [**ZHoly3O**](http://zholy3o.u-dont-exist.com/)  [B152]

[B153] When the trip feels like sick stomach, nausea, diarrhea, toxic heaviness, a dirty body-feeling, parasite die-off, or mental hell driven by gut hell, zeolite and the ZHoly3O ferment are often my first thoughts.

[B154] Many ferments help to different degrees, including kombucha, kimchi, and apple vinegar. I prefer fermented beet juice made with apple vinegar and salt. Smelling the ferment helps a lot. Drinking a spoonful can also help, although it may cause itching in somebody who breaks down histamine poorly, which then requires activated charcoal to reduce gut histamine.

[B158] Most sober people don't feel much from zeolite. Its effect becomes more obvious when a trip has a toxic or gut-driven component. I put a spoonful into water--some people need a teaspoon, others a tablespoon--and sip slowly. Rubbing it on the body or simply holding the jar also helps me.

[B155] In my experiments, zeolite has to be prepared correctly. I use powdered zeolite rather than nano-liquid zeolite. Many products did nothing for me, and quality mattered enormously. Most zeolites have clogged pores because they haven't been cleaned.

[B156] For my ozonated zeolite, I mix the powder into water and use an ozone-water purifier in a glass vessel because ozone destroys plastic. I mean ozonating water, never inhaling ozone gas. With a machine producing about two grams of ozone per hour, ten minutes produced a working zeolite for me. Less time did not work as well, and more time did not noticeably improve it.

[B157] I also keep zeolite away from metal during preparation. Zeolite adsorbs metals, which is part of why it is used for heavy-metal removal, and I don't want it sitting against a metal vessel or utensil before ingestion. I use glass for ozonation and storage whenever possible.

### Actual Activated Charcoal  [B159]

[B160] Activated charcoal can help gut toxicity, but it adsorbs drugs. Sometimes that is exactly the point. It can also flatten or reduce a trip, so I don't treat charcoal and zeolite as interchangeable.

[B161] When the goal is to reduce drug absorption or stop a toxic gut situation, charcoal may be relevant. When I want to preserve the trip while improving the gut feeling, I prefer zeolite.

[B162] Charcoal can also adsorb medications somebody depends on. I would use it cautiously around necessary medicines and tell the medics if charcoal was taken.

### [**DMSO**](http://dmso.u-dont-exist.com/)  [B199]

[B200] DMSO is another stabilizer I use when a trip becomes too intense and I don't want to abort it. It is also my primary emergency neuroprotectant when the problem feels like acute neurological damage, altered consciousness, loss of bodily control, or severe drug-related brain dysfunction.

[B201] It overlaps with aged ozonated-charcoal water, but the two don't always work equally. Sometimes DMSO works better; sometimes the water does. I don't yet know why. In my experience there is generally no harm in taking both or mixing them. Ozonated-charcoal water has what I consider a perfect safety profile; DMSO is a close second in my experience.

[B202] **Side effects:**

[B203] - **Astringency:** 100% DMSO usually irritates skin on the upper half of the body. When it itches, dilute it with water or real aloe juice without added chemicals. Leaving 100% DMSO on the skin can crenate it, and the skin may take a week to look plump again. DMSO can also burn dry mouth tissue, so the mouth should be moist first.
- **Nausea:** when drinking DMSO, dilute it in water, juice, or milk and sip gradually. Sensitivity varies.
- **Reduced sleep:** DMSO reduces how long I sleep when I take too much, take it too late, or use it over too many consecutive days. It builds up past a threshold that seems to differ between people. [In rats, higher doses made sleep lighter](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378427405000810). Some people sleep better with DMSO because it reduces whatever symptom had been keeping them awake.
- **Body odor:** above a certain dose you may smell like sulfur. I don't smell when I put it into milk.

[B204] **Interactions:**

[B205] - Don't put it on dirty skin; it can carry chemicals inward.
- Don't take it with drugs that should not absorb rapidly: DMSO can strengthen other medicines taken with it. With harmalas, don't put the harmala in the DMSO or take them at exactly the same time; taking them a bit apart is totally fine, and the point is to take DMSO if you've already absorbed the harmalas and the aya is too crazy.
- Don't combine it with cholinergic drugs because DMSO is also cholinergic, although I haven't found anecdotes of this causing a real problem.

[B206] **Contraindications:**

[B207] - DMSO has produced both anticonvulsant and proconvulsant effects depending on dose and seizure model. I consider it a plausible adjunct for excitotoxic or temporal-lobe-type seizure activity, not a proven universal seizure rescue.
- It may worsen a cluster headache, even though rubbing DMSO--especially with magnesium chloride--on the head can alleviate tension headaches.
- It may release histamine in some people. Use caution with MCAS.

### Syrian Rue Oil Makes the Trip Gentle & Sweet  [R30-RUE1]

[R30-RUE2] [HIS OWN] Syrian rue (*Peganum harmala*) can be mixed into warm/hot oil (like coconut oil), just enough to cover it, then squeezed out thru cheese cloth. Within some minutes of the seeds soaking in oil (if it's hot, or maybe an hour if it's warm), the oil contains the oil-soluble harmalas, not harmine, harmaline, or THH in quantities that could make it a potent MAOI. In my experience there's no MAOI effect in Syrian rue extracted with oil (it won't activate DMT, rather it reduces DMT's effects). These oil-soluble harmalas have no effect when eaten, but when absorbed thru mucous membranes or vaped, they chill out psychedelic trip intensity, making them more gentle and sweet. On their own, they have some mild psychedelic properties though (like I felt like half of my head was sort of transparent), and significant mood boosting properties.

[R30-RUE3] [HIS OWN] I have a friend with schizophrenia who would use about 1-2mL of this oil rectally when he was in psychosis, which helped at least as much as niacin and DMSO, equally effective as Geodon, an antipsychotic his doctors prescribed which makes him feel like a zombie.

[R30-RUE4] [HIS OWN] Another option is vaping the whole Syrian rue seeds, as it seems that these oil soluble harmalas are concentrated on the outside of the seeds. Once ground up, vaping the powder is more stimulating, less relaxing (but variable since all is mixed together). However, if vaping the whole seeds, I would not assume there's no MAOI effect (even though it still seems to reduce DMT effects) since depending maybe on temperature and time, you might also vaporize the harmine and harmaline.

## When the Goal Is to Stop a Psychedelic Trip  [B169]

### Beach Morning Glory--*Ipomoea pes-caprae*  [B170]

[B171] By beach morning glory I mean *Ipomoea pes-caprae*, rather than morning-glory plants in general. The distinction matters. I have tried many related plants and cousins, and they did not work the same way. As far as I know, nobody else sells it.

[B172] Its primary role in this guide is not ordinary nausea control. In my experience, *Ipomoea pes-caprae* is the ultimate sledgehammer for stopping a psychedelic trip cold and fast.

[B173] A tiny nibble can sometimes smooth psychedelic nausea and improve the trip without killing it, but the difference between that amount and a trip-aborting amount is narrow. I use it for nausea only when the person is comfortable with the possibility that the trip may end.

[B174] Even picking a leaf can affect me when the broken juicy part touches sensitive skin, especially under the fingernails. Avoiding contact with the broken portion prevents that effect.

[B175] A whole leaf can easily end the trip and, in my experience, may prevent tripping for days afterward.

[B176] This was not only personal. I once gave beach morning glory to a woman experiencing nausea and dysphoria during a psychedelic state. I was rushing to help and may have given more than I would now. Within a couple of minutes she shifted from miserable to very happy. That helped convince me the effect wasn't limited to my body.

[B178] I do not yet have data showing that pes-caprae aborts MDMA. MDMA remains a separate branch centered on magnesium, temperature, sodium, potassium, hydration, serotonin toxicity, seizure, and cardiovascular symptoms.

### A Final Trip Killer: Niacin (a Form of Vitamin B3)  [B210]

[B211] Flushing niacin has effectively aborted trips for me and friends, although I dislike it compared with beach morning glory.

[B212] I include it because most people don't have access to *Ipomoea pes-caprae*, although I supply it. A vitamin is not automatically harmless. Niacin has been used for decades by orthomolecular doctors for psychotic conditions and seems to act both like a natural benzodiazepine and an antipsychotic. The clinical literature is discussed in *Niacin: The Real Story* by Abram Hoffer, M.D.

[B213] **Side effects:**

[B214] - **Niacin flush:** itchy sunburn-like heat. This is normal, although some people find it intolerable and others enjoy it.
- Heartburn in sensitive stomachs or after too much at once.
- Drowsiness, usually with terrible sleep quality.
- Higher or repeated doses can create real risk, especially with liver problems, alcohol, medications, or sensitive physiology.

[B215] Other forms of vitamin B3 seem less reliable for relaxation. Niacinamide sometimes relaxes people and sometimes creates hyperfocus, depending on their state. Avoid sustained-release niacin and the useless non-flush hexanicotinate form.
