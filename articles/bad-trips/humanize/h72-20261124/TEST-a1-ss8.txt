Since a seizure is one of the main dangers while you wait, I'd use [DMSO](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/dmso), which seems to help prevent them, going by people's reports rather than studies: at any sign of an overdose, have them smell it or rub a little on a clean patch of skin (with 30% water if the skin itches), then use a little more at a time, since some people react more strongly than others. The one I'd leave out is [niacin](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/niacin-flushes-trips-out), the remedy this guide uses for ending a trip: at those doses it can cause flushing and low blood pressure ([NIH](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Niacin-Consumer/)), which is unsafe with serotonin syndrome. The other remedy I'd keep for a last resort, if nothing else is helping and no medics are coming, is [Syrian rue oil](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/syrian-rue-oil-gentler), since it makes trips, ayahuasca included, much less intense. What makes the brew in step 1 dangerous with MDMA is that it blocks MAO, and the oil doesn't, but nobody has studied the oil with MDMA at all.
