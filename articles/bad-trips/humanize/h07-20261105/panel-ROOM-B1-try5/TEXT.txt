Then look at the room. If five concerned people are talking at once, keep one or two with them and send the rest to another room. Turn the big overhead light off and use a lamp, because a huge amount of what looks like profound psychedelic crisis is a frightened nervous system stuck in an annoying room. Move them if they're trapped beside somebody they don't trust, and ask before you touch them. Don't let anyone else touch them when they don't want it, either. Find out if they're too hot or too cold. Ask about the bathroom while you're at it; they might be desperate to go.
