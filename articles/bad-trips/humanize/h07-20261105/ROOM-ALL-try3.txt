Then look at the room. A huge amount of what looks like profound psychedelic crisis is a frightened nervous system stuck in an annoying room, so turn off the big light and put a lamp on, and if five concerned people are talking at once, keep one or two and send the rest to another room. Ask before you touch them, and make sure everyone else does too. If they're trapped beside somebody they don't trust, move them. Find out if they're too hot or too cold, or desperate for the bathroom.

Ask "music or quiet?" and keep the music low if they want it. If a song seems to be making things worse, change it. This is a bad time to defend the playlist. Speak slowly and use fewer words, and please don't explain the universe at somebody who is already frightened by it. Offer water, and leave it if they'd rather not. If they do drink, have them sip rather than chug, since a lot of water fast is dangerous on MDMA.
