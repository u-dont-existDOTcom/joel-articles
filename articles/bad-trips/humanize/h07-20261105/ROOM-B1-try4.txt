If the big light is on and five concerned people are talking at once, no wonder they're scared, because a huge amount of what looks like profound psychedelic crisis is a frightened nervous system stuck in an annoying room. Put a lamp on instead, keep one or two people with them and send the rest out. Don't let anyone touch them unless they want it, and that goes for you too.
