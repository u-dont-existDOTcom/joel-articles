S1 Then look at the room, because a huge amount of what gets called profound psychedelic crisis is a frightened nervous system stuck in an annoying room.
S2 Dim the lights if they're too bright, and if five concerned people are talking at once, clear most of them out.
S3 If somebody's touching them and they don't want it, ask them to stop, and ask before you touch them yourself.
S4 Check that they're not too hot or too cold, take them to the bathroom if they need it, and if they're trapped beside somebody they don't trust, move them.
S5 Ask "music or quiet?"
S6 If they want music, keep it low.
S7 If a track starts pulling the experience somewhere unwanted, change it.
S8 This is a bad time to defend the playlist.
S9 Offer water without forcing it.
S10 Sip rather than chug.
S11 Speak slowly and use fewer words.
S12 Please don't explain the universe at somebody who is already frightened by it.
