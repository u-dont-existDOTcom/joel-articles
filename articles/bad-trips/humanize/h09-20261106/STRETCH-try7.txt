A lot of what looks like a deep psychedelic crisis is a scared person in a room that's getting on their nerves, so look around the room next. Switch the overhead light off and leave a lamp on. If five worried friends are all asking whether they're okay, that's five people they feel they have to answer, so send most of them to the kitchen and let one calm person they trust stay. That person should talk slowly and say very little. Maybe somebody keeps touching them and they hate it, or they're stuck next to someone they're wary of. Whoever it is needs to back off. Before touching them, ask if they'd like a hand on the shoulder. And check whether they're too hot or too cold, or desperate for the bathroom.

Ask "Would you like music or is quiet best?" Songs can turn scary halfway through, so change any that do. Gulping water on a queasy stomach can make them throw up, so if they're thirsty, give them small sips.
