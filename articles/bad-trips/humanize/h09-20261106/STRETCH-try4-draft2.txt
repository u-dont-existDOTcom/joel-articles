If five worried friends are all asking whether they're okay, that's five people they feel they have to answer, so send most of them to the kitchen. One calm person they trust is plenty, and if that's you, talk slowly and don't say much. Somebody might be touching them when they don't want it, or they're stuck next to someone they don't trust, and whoever it is needs to back off. Before you touch them yourself, ask if they'd like a hand on the shoulder. And check whether they need a blanket or an open window, or are desperate for the bathroom.
