A lot of what looks like a deep psychedelic crisis is a scared person in a room that's getting on their nerves, so look around the room next. Switch off the overhead light if it's too bright. If five worried friends are all asking whether they're okay, send most of them to the kitchen. One calm person they trust is plenty, and if that's you, talk slowly and don't say much. Somebody might be touching them when they don't want it, or they're stuck next to someone they don't trust, and whoever it is needs to back off. Before you touch them yourself, ask if they'd like a hand on the shoulder. And check whether they're too hot or too cold, or desperate for the bathroom.

If they're thirsty, give them water to sip slowly, since lots of people feel sick when they're tripping and gulping it can make them throw up. If the music's horrible to them, ask "Would you like music or is quiet best?" A song can also turn scary halfway through, and then you change it.
