A lot of what looks like a deep psychedelic crisis is a scared person in a room that's getting on their nerves, so look around the room next. Maybe the light's too bright or the music's horrible to them. Are five concerned people talking at once? Somebody might be touching them when they don't want it, or they're stuck beside someone they don't trust. And check whether they're too hot or too cold, or desperate for the bathroom.

Fixing those things is often enough to settle them, so dim the lights, and clear out the extra people around them until it's you, or one calm person they trust, doing the talking. Your voice and your hands count as part of the room, so talk slowly and keep it short, and before you touch them at all, ask if they'd like a hand on the shoulder. Tripping often upsets the stomach, so if they're thirsty, offer water and suggest small sips.

Ask "Would you like music or is quiet best?" If a song starts scaring them more, change it.
