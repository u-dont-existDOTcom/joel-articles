A lot of what looks like a deep psychedelic crisis is a scared person in a room that's getting on their nerves, so look around the room next. Maybe the light's too bright or the music's horrible to them. Are five concerned people talking at once? Somebody might be touching them when they don't want it, or they're stuck beside someone they don't trust. And check whether they're too hot or too cold, or desperate for the bathroom.

All of that hits harder on a psychedelic, so dim the lights, and clear out the extra people around them, leaving one calm person they trust to do the talking. If that's you, your voice and your hands count as part of the room too, so talk slowly and keep it short, and before you touch them at all, ask if they'd like a hand on the shoulder. Lots of people feel sick when they're tripping, and gulping water can make them throw up, so if they're thirsty, give them water to sip slowly.

Ask "Would you like music or is quiet best?" If a song starts scaring them more, change it or turn it off.
