Once their jaw tenses, that should be their last dose of MDMA, so get them to slow down and cool off. While it's natural to want to dance on MDMA, I'd tell everyone not to dance at all, since dancing makes the heat and the sweat that drain their salt. If they drink a lot of plain water, [even a single normal MDMA dose](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39546312/) raises the risk of their sodium dropping too low, so have them sip an electrolyte drink when they're thirsty instead. Plain water is the risk because MDMA makes the body hold on to water, which thins out the salt left in their blood, all the more after they've sweated a lot. The following indicators would justify calling medics: If their confusion or headache worsens, or vomiting won't stop (sodium might be too low by then for an electrolyte drink to fix; while you wait for medics, if they're alert and not vomiting, give them something very salty instead, like strong broth). That has to be a lot of salt, more than a snack gives: [guidelines for hikers who drank too much water](https://www.aafp.org/pubs/afp/issues/2021/0215/p252.html) use three or four bouillon cubes in half a cup of water. If their muscles keep getting weaker or their heart palpitations keep getting stronger, that could be from dangerously low potassium, like my second wife had. A [seizure](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/active-seizure) or collapse can result from low sodium, magnesium or potassium, and can also come from overheating or MDMA toxicity.
