- Trouble breathing, breathing that gets slow and shallow, or lips that turn blue or gray
- A [seizure](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/active-seizure) after they took too much, or an amount nobody knows, or one that goes on over 5 minutes on a normal dose
- They collapse, you can't wake them the normal way, or they're getting harder to reach by the minute
- They're dangerously hot (not just sweaty), their muscles go rigid, or they have a fever with muscles jerking that they can't stop (clonus)
- No sleep for days
- One side of their body suddenly goes weak, or they can't speak, can't walk normally, or wet or soil themselves
- They say their brain feels damaged and they can't do [the brain checks](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/my-brain-feels-damaged), or they're getting worse fast
- [Chest pain](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/chest-pain-and-injury) that's severe, getting worse, spreading to an arm or the jaw, or came on with hard movement, or that comes with shortness of breath, sweating, faintness, stroke signs or collapse
- A serious injury, bleeding that won't stop, or a hit to the head, neck or back
- Vomiting that keeps coming back, bad dehydration, blood in their vomit or stool, or bad stomach pain
- [Signs of serotonin syndrome](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/how-to-check-for-ss) after mixing an MAOI, which keeps serotonin from breaking down (some older antidepressants, or the harmalas in ayahuasca and Syrian rue seeds), with something that raises serotonin, like MDMA or most antidepressants
