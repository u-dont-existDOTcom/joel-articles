Since a seizure is one of the main dangers while you wait, I'd use [DMSO](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/dmso), which seems to help prevent them, going by people's reports rather than studies: at any sign of an overdose, have them smell it or rub a little on a clean patch of skin (with 30% water if the skin itches), then use a little more at a time, since some people react more strongly than others. You might also think of [niacin](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/niacin-flushes-trips-out), which this guide uses for ending a trip, but it's unsafe with serotonin syndrome, since at the doses used to end a trip it can cause flushing and low blood pressure ([NIH](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Niacin-Consumer/)). The other remedy I'd keep for a last resort, if nothing else is helping and no medics are coming, is [Syrian rue oil](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/syrian-rue-oil-gentler), since it makes trips, ayahuasca included, much less intense. The oil isn't the Syrian rue brew from step 1, which blocks MAO and is dangerous with MDMA for that reason, but nobody has studied the oil with MDMA either.
