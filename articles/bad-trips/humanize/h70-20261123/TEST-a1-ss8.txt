While you wait, I'd use [DMSO](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/dmso), since it seems to help prevent seizures: at any sign of an overdose, have them smell it or rub a little on a clean patch of skin (add 30% water if it makes the skin itch), then use more, a little at a time, as long as it helps, as some people react more strongly than others. You might also think of [niacin](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/niacin-flushes-trips-out), which this guide uses for ending a trip, but it's unsafe with serotonin syndrome: at the doses used to end a trip it can cause flushing, dizziness and low blood pressure ([NIH](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Niacin-Consumer/)). [Syrian rue oil](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/syrian-rue-oil-gentler) isn't the Syrian rue brew from step 1, though: it doesn't block MAO, which is what makes the brew dangerous with MDMA, and it makes trips, ayahuasca included, much less intense. Nobody has studied it with MDMA, so I'd keep it as a last resort, for when nothing else is helping and no medics are coming.
