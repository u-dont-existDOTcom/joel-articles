Cannabis is something people try at the very start of an aura, while they're still awake enough to vape or smoke on purpose. What it does seems to depend on the person and on how much THC is in it. Some say it stops the seizure from going any further, and one woman's EEG showed fewer seizure discharges on the days she smoked. The people who say it brought seizures on mostly name THC vapes and concentrates, though. And in rats that get absence seizures, the brief staring kind, high-THC smoke made them worse while injected CBD cut them by about half.
