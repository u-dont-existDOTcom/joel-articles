Anything that could let someone hurt themselves in the next five minutes takes priority, so get the car keys out of reach and turn the stove off, and move them away from roads and balconies, or pools of water for that matter. Put any weapons away and keep the person away from crowds and strangers. If they want to post their big revelation about the universe, or message an ex, take the phone.

Pacing is fine, so let them walk laps of the room or the yard if they need to. Put the rest of whatever they took out of reach too, since more only makes the trip stronger and longer. Having it out with somebody can wait until it's worn off, and if they insist on leaving the house, walk with them.
