Cannabis at the start of an aura can go either way, so it's for someone who already knows how it affects their seizures. Some people say smoking or vaping it the moment an aura starts keeps the seizure from going further, and one woman's EEG showed fewer seizure discharges on the days she smoked. Others say THC vapes and concentrates brought their seizures on. And in rats, high-THC smoke made absence seizures worse, which are the brief staring kind. Smoking or vaping also takes someone fully awake, so never try it on someone who's already convulsing.
