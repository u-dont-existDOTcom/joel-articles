Some people with epilepsy smoke or vape cannabis the moment an aura starts, and they say it keeps the seizure from going further. One woman's EEG showed fewer seizure discharges on the days she smoked. Other people say THC vapes and concentrates brought their seizures on, though. And in rats, high-THC smoke made absence seizures worse, which are the brief staring kind. It takes someone fully awake to smoke or vape safely, so this is just for the warning stage, never for someone who's already convulsing.
