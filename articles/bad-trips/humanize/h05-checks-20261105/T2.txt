And topical CBD hasn't been well-studied. There was a short 12-week trial done, where they used a CBD skin gel, and it didn't do any better than placebo. The gel got CBD into the blood at about the levels of the starting oral dose, 5 to 10mg/kg. The approved oral doses go up to 20 or 25mg/kg a day, so the dosing may have been too low. And CBD through the skin goes straight into the blood, while swallowed CBD passes through the liver first. The liver is where some of it gets converted to 7-OH-CBD, which stopped seizures in mice about as well as CBD itself, so the gel may have missed out on that too.
