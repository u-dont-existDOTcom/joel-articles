Some people feel a seizure coming before it takes hold, and in that warning a few things people report can still stop it for some of them, depending on what sets it off. If it's flashing or flickering light, cover one eye with a hand and turn away from the light, because closing the eyes doesn't block enough of it. Some people stop a seizure at the warning stage with a strong smell like jasmine, and some train themselves to link one smell with feeling calm and then sniff it when a seizure threatens. When overstimulation is what sets it off, a cool, quiet, dark and comfortable space can help.
