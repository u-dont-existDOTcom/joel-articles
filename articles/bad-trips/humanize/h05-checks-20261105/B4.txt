As for cannabis, people do smoke or vape it at the start of an aura, while they're still awake enough to do it on purpose. Some swear it stops the seizure from going any further, and there was one woman whose EEG showed fewer seizure discharges on the days she smoked. But other people say THC vapes and concentrates brought their seizures on, and in rats that get the brief staring seizures called absence seizures, high-THC smoke made them worse while injected CBD cut them about in half. So the THC may be what makes it go either way.
