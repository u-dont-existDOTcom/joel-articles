Bypassing is answering someone's suffering with a big spiritual truth, like "this is all karma" or "everything is Love," and ignoring the person shaking in the corner.

Practice says love is here too, so we take care of that person: we dim the lights and clear away anything they could get hurt on, and if it's more than we can handle, we call for help. And we stop pretending nothing's wrong, since the feeling is allowed to move through, and nobody has to become it.
