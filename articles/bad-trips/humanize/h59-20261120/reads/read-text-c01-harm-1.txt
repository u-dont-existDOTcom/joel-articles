When somebody says, "I am God, so I can kill," take it as a sign they could really hurt someone. Start from what they believe and lead them back to not hurting anyone, like the lines below do when they say God is in everyone. Arguing ("No you're not," "That's delusional") can make them dig in to prove they're God, and if you nod along as if hurting someone could be spiritually fine, they might take it as your blessing.
