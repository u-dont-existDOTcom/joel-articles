Ask before you touch someone who's tripping, even to put a hand on their heart: "Would touch help, or would you rather have space?" A hug can feel like too much in that state, however much you mean it as comfort, and holding them down can feel like an attack. Sometimes the best support is to sit six feet away and stay silent.

If they say stop, stop, and the same goes if they pull away or freeze, or you're not sure they want it. Touch should never be sexual, and if part of why you want to touch them is that it would make you feel better, don't.
