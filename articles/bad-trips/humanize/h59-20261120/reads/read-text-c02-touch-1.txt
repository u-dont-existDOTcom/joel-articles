Ask before you touch someone who's tripping, even to put a hand on their heart: "Would touch help, or would you rather have space?" In that state a hug can be too much, and if you hold them down they might feel attacked and fight you. If they'd rather have space, sit about six feet away and only talk if they want to.

Stop as soon as they say so, and if they pull away or freeze, stop then too, since someone that overwhelmed may be past saying it. If what they want is unclear, ask again. Be honest with yourself about why you want to touch them. If it's mostly to calm yourself down, hold off, and if there's anything sexual in it, don't touch them at all.
