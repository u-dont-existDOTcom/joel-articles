When somebody says, "I am God, so I can kill," take it as a sign that someone could get hurt. Start from what they believe and lead them back to not hurting anyone, like the lines below do when they say God is in everyone. Arguing ("No you're not," "That's delusional") can turn things into a fight over who's right, and if you nod along as if hurting someone could be spiritually fine, they might hear it as permission.
