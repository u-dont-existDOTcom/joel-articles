Bypassing is answering someone's suffering with a big spiritual truth, like "this is all karma" or "everything is Love," and ignoring the person shaking in the corner.

Practice says love is here too, so we take care of that person. We dim the lights and clear away anything they could get hurt on, and if it gets to be more than we can handle, we call for help. We also stop pretending nothing's wrong, and remind them that what they're feeling is allowed to move through, so they don't have to become it.
