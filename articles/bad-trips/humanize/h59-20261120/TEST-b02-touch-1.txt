Ask before you touch someone who's tripping, even to put a hand on their heart: "Would touch help, or would you rather have space?" A hug can feel like too much in that state, and holding them down can feel like an attack. Sometimes the best support is to sit six feet away and stay silent.

If they say stop, stop, and the same goes if they pull away or freeze, or you're not sure they want it. Check your own reasons, too: if you mostly want to touch them to calm yourself down, or there's anything sexual in it, don't.
