Ask before you touch someone who's tripping, even to put your hand on their heart: "Would touch help, or would you rather have space?" In that state a hug can be too much. And if you hold them down, they might feel attacked and fight you. If they'd rather have space, sit about six feet away and only talk if they want to.

Stop as soon as they say stop. If they pull away or freeze, stop then too, since they might be too overwhelmed to say it. If what they want is unclear, ask again. Be honest with yourself about why you want to touch them. If it's mostly to calm yourself down, hold off. Never touch them in a sexual way, whatever they say.
