Then remind them where they are and what's happening, since a trip can make them lose track of both:

You're in [place], and it's [date/time]. You took [substance, if known], and the effects will peak and then ease off. It's intense right now, but it'll pass, and we can sort out the rest of your life tomorrow.

If a practice like meditation or fasting brought this on, have them stop now, since doing more of it only makes this stronger. Nobody can say how long it'll last, with no drug wearing off on a schedule. So look after their body in the meantime: once they're alert and not throwing up, food and warmth help it calm down, especially after fasting.
