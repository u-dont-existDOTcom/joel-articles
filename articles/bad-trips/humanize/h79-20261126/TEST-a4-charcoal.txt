I don't treat charcoal and zeolite as interchangeable: activated charcoal helps with gut toxicity, but drugs bind to it too (it adsorbs them), so it can flatten or reduce a trip.

So if someone took too much, or the gut itself is toxic, charcoal is relevant, since it can stop absorption. If the trip is going fine and only the gut feels bad, I give [zeolite](#§zeocharge-and-ferment), which leaves the trip alone.

Medicines bind to charcoal too, so I'd think twice before giving it to someone who depends on one, and I'd tell any medics who come that charcoal was taken.
