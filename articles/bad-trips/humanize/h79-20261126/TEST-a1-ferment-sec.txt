Zeolite and the [ZHoly3O](http://zholy3o.u-dont-exist.com/) ferment are often my first thoughts when the trip feels like a sick stomach, with nausea or diarrhea and that toxic heaviness or dirty body-feeling, or like mental hell driven by gut hell. I'd think of them for parasite die-off too.

The ZHoly3O ferment contains more than just fermented beet juice, but you can ferment your own beet juice or even use various other ferments. I found fermented beet juice made with apple vinegar and salt was best so far, but kimchi and kombucha also helped. Most yogurts weren't fermented enough to work, and apple vinegar by itself helped a little. Smelling the ferment helps a lot. Drinking a spoonful can also help, although it may cause itching in somebody who breaks down histamine poorly, which then requires activated charcoal to reduce gut histamine.
