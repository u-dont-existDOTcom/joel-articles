For my ozonated zeolite, I mix the powder into water and use an ozone-water purifier in a glass vessel because ozone destroys plastic, and I mean ozonating water, never inhaling ozone gas. With a machine producing about two grams of ozone per hour, ten minutes produced a working zeolite for me, however much zeolite was in the water. Less time didn't work as well, and going past ten minutes didn't noticeably improve the zeolite.
