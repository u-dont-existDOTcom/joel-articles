Activated charcoal helps with toxins in the gut, but drugs bind to it too, so it can flatten or reduce a trip, and that's why I don't treat charcoal and zeolite as interchangeable.

That makes charcoal the one to give when the goal is to stop more of the drug being absorbed, or when the gut itself is toxic. I use [zeolite](#§zeocharge-and-ferment) instead when I want to keep the trip and only make the gut feel better.

Medicines bind to charcoal too, so I'd think twice before giving it to someone who depends on a medicine, and if medics come, I'd tell them charcoal was taken.
