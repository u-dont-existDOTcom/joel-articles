Gather what the ER will need:
their pill bottles and packages
exactly what they took, how much of each, and when
which dose was the last, and any dose they recently increased
any supplements or recreational drugs
their temperature, pulse and blood pressure, if you can take them
While you wait, don't reach for niacin to end the trip: it's unsafe with serotonin syndrome. Leave out DMSO too, which this guide uses for other emergencies, since it isn't part of treating serotonin syndrome. What I would consider instead, once they're fully alert, is magnesium chloride by mouth, especially for MDMA-related jaw tension or muscle twitching (the amounts are in the MDMA section above).
