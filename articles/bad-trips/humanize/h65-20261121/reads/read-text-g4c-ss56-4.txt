Keep the room calm and keep them still. They're already overstimulated, so I'd keep crowds and arguments away. I also wouldn't let them pace or exercise to "burn it off," since muscle work makes more heat and raises the risk of rhabdomyolysis, where damaged muscle leaks into the blood and can hurt the kidneys. If they feel a strong urge to keep moving (akathisia), I'd stay with them and help them keep still anyway.
Give fluids only if they're fully alert and not vomiting, and then only small sips of an electrolyte drink. I wouldn't force fluids, food, pills or charcoal on them if they're confused, vomiting or sleepy, or at risk of a seizure, since they could choke.
