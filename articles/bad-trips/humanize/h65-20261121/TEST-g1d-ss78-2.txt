Gather what the ER will need: bring their pill bottles and packages, and write down exactly what they took. For each substance, note the dose and when they took it, which dose was the last, and any dose they recently raised, then list any supplements or recreational drugs. If you can take their temperature, pulse and blood pressure, write those down as well.
While you wait, leave out niacin and DMSO. Niacin may be this guide's trip killer, but it is unsafe with serotonin syndrome, and DMSO isn't one of these steps either. Once they're fully alert, I'd consider magnesium chloride by mouth instead, as in the MDMA section above, especially when MDMA has their jaw clenched or their muscles twitching.
