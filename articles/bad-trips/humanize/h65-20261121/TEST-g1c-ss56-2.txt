Keep the room calm and keep them still, since they're already overstimulated. That means no crowds or arguments around them, and no pacing or exercise to "burn it off." If they feel a strong urge to keep moving, which is called akathisia, help them stay put anyway, because every bit of muscle work adds heat. It also raises their risk of rhabdomyolysis, where damaged muscle leaks into the blood and can hurt the kidneys.
Give fluids only if they're fully alert and not vomiting, and then just small sips of an electrolyte drink, like oral rehydration salts mixed in water. Someone who is confused, sleepy or vomiting, or at risk of a seizure, could breathe in whatever you give them, so don't force fluids, food, pills or charcoal on them.
