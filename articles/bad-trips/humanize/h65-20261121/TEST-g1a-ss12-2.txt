Stop every suspected serotonergic substance, meaning anything that raises serotonin. If they're on an antidepressant (an SSRI, SNRI or MAOI), give them no more of it, and the same goes for tramadol, a painkiller, and for the antibiotic linezolid. No more MDMA or stimulant redoses either, and none of the supplements 5-HTP, St John's wort or SAM-e. Any harmala or ayahuasca-type MAOI counts too, and so does DXM cough syrup.
If the person is fully alert and able to swallow, I would give cyproheptadine early, since it blocks serotonin receptors. So far, the evidence for this med has only been case reports, and a recent review found them insufficient to show that it works. I wouldn't wait to see whether it does before doing the other steps.
