Cool them down without holding up transport:
Get them into a cool room and take off any extra clothing.
Put a fan on them, with cool damp cloths on their skin.
If they're hot, hold ice packs wrapped in cloth at the neck, armpits or groin.
Don't put them in a bath if they're confused, seizing or very weak.
If you think of giving a fever reducer like acetaminophen or ibuprofen, it won't work: fever reducers do not help with heat generated by muscles working this hard.
