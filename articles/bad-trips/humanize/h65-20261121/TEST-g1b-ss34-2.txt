Call emergency help and arrange transport. Don't drive yourself. If you can't tell what your friend mixed, a poison control line can help you work out the combination, so call one too once emergency help is on its way.
Cool them down without holding up transport: if they're hot, get them into a cool room and take off their extra clothing. Then cool their skin with a fan and damp cloths, and put ice packs wrapped in cloth at their neck, armpits or groin. Don't put them in a cool bath, though, if they're confused, seizing or very weak, since they could slip under the water. If you're thinking of a fever reducer, it won't help here: fever reducers do not bring down heat generated by muscles working this hard, so keep cooling them from the outside.
