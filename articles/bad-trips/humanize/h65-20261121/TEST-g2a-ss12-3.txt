Stop all suspected serotonergic substances, meaning anything that raises serotonin, like:
their antidepressant, if it's an SSRI, SNRI or MAOI, or if you're not sure what kind it is
tramadol for pain, or the antibiotic linezolid
another dose of MDMA, or of any stimulant
5-HTP, St John's wort or SAM-e
harmala, or an ayahuasca-type MAOI
even DXM cough syrup
If the person is fully alert and able to swallow, I would give cyproheptadine early, since it blocks serotonin receptors. So far, the evidence for this med has only been case reports, and a recent review found them insufficient to show that it works. I wouldn't wait to see whether it does before doing the other steps.
