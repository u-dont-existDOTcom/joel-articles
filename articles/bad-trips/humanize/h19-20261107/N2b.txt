Use a 24–72 hour action lock.

Until you've slept and eaten, don't drive or travel, and don't break up with anyone or quit your job. Don't confess everything, or confront or accuse anybody. Don't send the message or post the revelation, don't give away possessions, and don't call your ex, or your parents to explain the universe to them. And don't decide you're permanently enlightened, or permanently damaged, either.
