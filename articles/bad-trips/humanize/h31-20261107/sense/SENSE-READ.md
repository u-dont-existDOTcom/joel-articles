# Sense read

## 1. Helper's Don't list
Unclear words: "entities" (beings seen?); "Reflect the feeling" (no example); "the medicine" (the drug?).
Claims: "Don't try to win an argument" plus "Don't validate harmful conclusions" leaves the helper with nothing to say; "harmful conclusions" is undefined. "redirect toward ... sleep": a person mid-trip usually can't sleep.
Repeats: none.
Missing: what to do if the person is in danger.

## 2. After the Storm
Unclear words: "kundalini", "destabilized", "until the floor is back", "ordinary body", "Deep practice", "another ceremony", "the fruit of an insight".
Claims:
- "Don't take more psychedelics, cannabis, ketamine, intense breathwork, fast, push kundalini, deprive yourself of sleep, meditate or stretch to extremes" is ungrammatical. "Take ... fast, push kundalini" can't be parsed, and "stretch to extremes" may mean physical stretching.
- "Deep practice can wait until the floor is back": no way to tell when, no time frame.
- "ask what actually happened": ask whom? "What was fantasy? What was old trauma?" gives no way to tell them apart.
- The last paragraph says "suffering goes down and love gets more available" without saying over what time span.
Repeats: "ordinary" in both the don't and stabilize sentences (minor).
Missing: when to get professional help if still unsettled.

## 3. Distortions list
Unclear words: "Cognitive distortions" never defined; "Spiritual bypassing" explained only by its example.
Claims: "can create or prolong the crisis": these are thinking habits, not causes. The section is "Why Bad Trips Happen", yet no actual cause is named. Nothing says what to do about any of them.
Repeats: none.
Missing: the step from spotting the thought to acting on it.

## 4. Session beginning and end
Unclear words: "altered-state work", "sitter", "Low-directivity support", "Integration time", "let the body understand that the session has ended", "return to the room and date".
Claims:
- "think through the beginning, middle, and end": the six list items don't map to those three.
- "When you have a choice:" has no clear referent.
- "decide in advance what requires outside help": no example given.
- "eat and hydrate when safe": safe how?
- "the sitter protects safety and offers choices" doesn't say when to step in.
Repeats: sleep and food appear in both "Rest and timing" and "Integration time", and the time afterward is covered twice.
Missing: the middle of the session; the heading promises a beginning and an end only.
