As for topical CBD, it hasn't been well-studied. There was a short 12-week trial done, where they used a CBD skin gel, and although absorption was good, it didn't work any better than placebo to prevent seizures. The study authors suggested the dosing may have been too low, or that CBD just doesn't work as well for focal seizures as it does in Dravet syndrome, where many of the seizures are generalized. Generalized are the type people usually have with MDMA & psychedelic overdoses.
