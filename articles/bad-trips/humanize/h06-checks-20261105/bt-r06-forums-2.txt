I also read on forums that some people swear smoking at the aura stops the seizure from going any further, and in one study a woman's EEG showed fewer seizure discharges on the days she smoked. But other people say THC vapes and concentrates brought their seizures on. So the THC may be what makes it go either way. That's what happened in rats that get the brief staring seizures called absence seizures: high-THC smoke made them worse, while CBD injections cut them about in half.
