Serotonin syndrome can look like some of the emergencies above, with a seizure, dangerous overheating or tremor, but the rescue measures in those other branches do not necessarily treat it, so check for it too whenever they've had something that raises serotonin. That includes ayahuasca and other MAOI brews, MDMA, DXM cough syrup, many antidepressants, the painkiller tramadol, the antibiotic linezolid, other MAOIs and some opioids. It can come on after too much of one of them or after a change in a medication, but I'd suspect it most when two of them were combined.
