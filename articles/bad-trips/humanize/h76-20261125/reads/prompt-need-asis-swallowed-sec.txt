S3: Or is it rapid breathing, heart rate, and agitation, signs of stimulant toxicity?
S4: Do you see symptoms of [serotonin toxicity](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/how-to-check-for-ss), like seizure, dangerous overheating, tremor?
S5: Do they have vomiting or diarrhea beyond what they can handle?
S6: Some of these symptoms are also normal, like vomiting and diarrhea, which are helpful in undoing an overdose, up to a point.
S7: Slow breathing is normal with iboga, but if cardiac irregularities come along with it, that's an extra danger sign.
S8: [Activated charcoal](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/activated-charcoal) may help after some recent oral ingestions when the substance binds to it and the person is fully alert enough to swallow safely.
S9: It does not bind every toxin, can adsorb necessary medication, and creates aspiration risk in somebody who is vomiting, sedated, seizing, or unable to protect the airway.
S10: [Clinical review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6620762/).
S11: Poison control may be useful when the substance, combination, delayed risk, or value of charcoal is unclear.

A sentence the reader doesn't need is one they could lose and still do what the paragraph is for, with nothing
missing. These are not reasons to keep a sentence: it is true; it has a source; it is interesting; it is a careful
hypothesis or a testable guess; it explains a disease or a case the reader doesn't have; it qualifies another claim the
reader didn't need either; it was already in the text. Read also for sense: a sentence whose words don't make sense
together, or that mixes pictures (a dial, a door, a floor and a leak for one thing), goes on the list too, with why.

Before you list a sentence for giving the reader nothing to do, ask what it means they should do. A caution, a risk
or a difference between people often implies an act the sentence doesn't say ("Sensitivity varies" means: start
with less): that sentence is needed, and its NEEDS line says the act it implies, so the writer can add it.
