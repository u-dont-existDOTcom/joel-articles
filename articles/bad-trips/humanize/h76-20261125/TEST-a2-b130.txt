Serotonin syndrome can look like some of the emergencies above, since it can also bring on a seizure or dangerous overheating. But the rescue measures in those other branches do not necessarily treat it, so check for it too whenever they've had something that raises serotonin. That includes ayahuasca and other MAOI brews, MDMA, DXM cough syrup and many antidepressants, as well as other MAOIs, the painkiller tramadol, the antibiotic linezolid and some opioids. Too much of any one of them can bring on serotonin syndrome, and so can a medication that was just started or had its dose raised, but I'd suspect it most when two of them were combined.
