**People with impaired kidneys do need to be more cautious with large, rapid magnesium doses because they may not clear magnesium normally. But they should be far more worried about the MDMA, because of what it can do to their kidneys.** It can cause [acute kidney injury](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27917269/), which is worse for someone whose kidneys are already impaired, along with overheating, muscle breakdown (rhabdomyolysis) and dangerous electrolyte shifts. So kidney impairment is a strong reason not to take MDMA. However, if somebody with serious kidney impairment has already taken it and their jaw tension is getting worse, I wouldn't tell them to sit there with it, or to copy my one-gram-per-minute approach blindly, but to treat the situation as higher-risk and call medics sooner, since the MDMA is the bigger danger to them and they may not clear the magnesium normally.
