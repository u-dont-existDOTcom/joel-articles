MARKED:
- S7: 10 — "Pulling away or freezing" has nobody's name on it (the tripper, or you pulling away?); his way names who, e.g. "If they pull away or freeze, that means stop too".
- S9: 4, 10 — "who it's for" leaves the two possible answers unnamed and "it" could be the touch or the asking; his way would say "ask yourself whether the touch is for them or for you".
