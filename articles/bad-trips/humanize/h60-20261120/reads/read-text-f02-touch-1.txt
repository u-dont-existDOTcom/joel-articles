Ask before you touch someone who's tripping, even to put your hand on their heart: "Would touch help, or would you rather have space?" Even a hug can be too much when they're already overwhelmed. And if you hold them down, they might feel attacked and fight you. If they'd rather have space, sit about six feet away and only talk if they want to.

Stop as soon as they say stop. Pulling away or freezing means stop too, since they might be too overwhelmed to tell you to stop. Ask again whenever you can't tell what they want. Before you touch them, ask yourself who it's for. If it's mostly to calm yourself down, hold off. Never touch them in a sexual way, even if they ask you to.
