MARKED:
- S2: 4 — "what they believe" and "the lines below" don't say what the belief is or which lines they mean. His way would name the belief and say what the script says, for example that God is in everyone.
- S2: 10 — "like the lines below do" could point at the script that follows or at some earlier lines, and "them" could be the friend or the victim. His way would say "the script below" and give the person a name.
