When somebody says, "I am God, so I can kill," take it as a sign they could really hurt someone. To lead them back to not hurting anyone, start from what they believe, like the script below does: God is in everyone, so every body is sacred. Arguing can make them dig in to prove they're God, so don't tell them "No you're not" or "That's delusional." Nodding along is no good either, since they might think you're okay with them hurting someone.
