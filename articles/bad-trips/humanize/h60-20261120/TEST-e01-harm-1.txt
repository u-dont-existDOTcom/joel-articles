When somebody says, "I am God, so I can kill," take it as a sign they could really hurt someone. To lead them back to not hurting anyone, start from what they believe, like the lines below do by saying God is in everyone. Arguing ("No you're not," "That's delusional") can make them dig in to prove they're God. And if you nod along as if hurting someone could be spiritually fine, they might think you're okay with them doing it.
