A token dose may not be enough to make the jaw release. More than once, I've dissolved **Dead Sea magnesium chloride hexahydrate flakes** in water and taken about **one gram per minute by mouth for around twenty minutes** before mine fully released. I can take around twenty grams without diarrhea, and I only get it above about forty grams. Magnesium chloride is also [less likely to cause diarrhea than several other kinds of magnesium](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10509448/), including the ones people take on purpose as laxatives.
