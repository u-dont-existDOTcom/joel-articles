**People with impaired kidneys do need to be more cautious with large, rapid magnesium doses because they may not clear magnesium normally. But they should be far more worried about the MDMA**, which can cause overheating, rhabdomyolysis, dangerous electrolyte shifts and [acute kidney injury](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27917269/). So kidney impairment is a strong reason not to take MDMA, although once someone has taken it, it's no reason to sit there with worsening jaw tension. I wouldn't tell somebody with serious kidney impairment to copy my one-gram-per-minute approach blindly, though: I'd tell them to treat it as higher-risk and call medics sooner.
