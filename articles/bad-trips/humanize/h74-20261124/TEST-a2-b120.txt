**If someone has impaired kidneys, they do need to be more cautious with large, rapid magnesium doses like my one-gram-per-minute approach, because they may not clear magnesium normally. But the MDMA is a far bigger danger to their kidneys**, since besides overheating them and causing dangerous electrolyte shifts, it can injure the kidneys directly ([acute kidney injury](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27917269/)) and through rhabdomyolysis, which is muscle breaking down into the blood. So kidney trouble is a strong reason not to take MDMA, and if they've taken it anyway, their worsening jaw tension still needs treating. With serious kidney impairment, though, I'd treat it as a higher-risk case and get help sooner, and I wouldn't have them copy my approach blindly.
