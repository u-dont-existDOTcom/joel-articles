relation; the reason where it's needed), each illustrated from other paragraphs only, and the whole section as it stood
before his fix with the two paragraphs marked. It never saw his versions.

**Result: one of two at Human High.** The ways carried where they are about form (the aside, the contrast word, the
vague word), and the B118 rewrite read Human High in one window, where his version with one space read a Medium window
on its last sentence. What didn't carry is what only he knows: that a word of the post was a model's, and the reason
potassium follows magnesium. So the next writer's brief says which words of the paragraph are his (from his versions and
answers) and which are only the post's, since a writer told to keep every word of the post keeps a model's word too.
