Around twenty grams is fine for my gut, and I've only had diarrhea above about forty grams. I use magnesium chloride because it absorbs [far better than magnesium oxide](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11794633/), so you can see how it's helping, usually within a minute. Magnesium chloride is also [less likely to cause diarrhea than several other kinds of magnesium](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10509448/), including the ones people take on purpose as laxatives.
