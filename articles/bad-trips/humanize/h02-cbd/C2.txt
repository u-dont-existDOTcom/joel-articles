I would also use topical magnesium chloride and consider topical DMSO with a known CBD preparation as adjuncts. CBD on the skin hasn't been shown to help with seizures, though. In a 12-week trial, a CBD gel got into the blood about as well as oral CBD does and still did no better than placebo. Swallowed CBD looks better: it's approved for the seizures that come with Lennox-Gastaut syndrome, Dravet syndrome and tuberous sclerosis complex, and in a 2025 study, people taking a lot of it, around 23 mg/kg a day (about 1,400 mg if you weigh 60 kg), had about half to three quarters fewer focal seizures over almost three years. That study had no placebo group, and neither study tested CBD as a quick rescue during a seizure. DMSO cut seizure activity in a rat temporal-lobe epilepsy model and protects nerve cells in several ways, but at some doses it made things worse in an absence-epilepsy model. So I see it as an experimental extra that might help, and I wouldn't wait on it while a seizure keeps going.
