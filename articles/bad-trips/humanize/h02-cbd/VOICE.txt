If someone is worried about a bleed, I'd probably skip DMSO, but it depends on how severe their issue is. It's not a powerful blood thinner like heparin, unless maybe injected; it's basically a dietary blood thinner. I've never had any issues with bleeding too much from consuming DMSO, and never heard of it either, but if bleeding is already a medical issue I'd want to think about that more.

With harmalas, don't put the harmala in the DMSO or take them at exactly the same time; taking them a bit apart is totally fine, and the point is to take DMSO if you've already absorbed the harmalas and the aya is too crazy.

Ocean water is one of the simplest and strangest stabilizers I have found. I noticed it in Kenya and Florida. Rain water also works for me, but I've only tested it near the ocean.

When a trip is too strong, touching ocean water can be extremely soothing. Touching the outside of a bottle doesn't do it. Direct contact with the water seems to matter, even when the water has been stored in a bottle.

In general it's good to try all these things in very low, increasing doses before they are needed in crisis.
