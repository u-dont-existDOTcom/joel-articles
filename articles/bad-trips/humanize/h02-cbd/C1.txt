I would also use topical magnesium chloride and consider topical DMSO with a known CBD preparation as adjuncts. Purified oral CBD is approved for the seizures that come with Lennox-Gastaut syndrome, Dravet syndrome and tuberous sclerosis complex, but for other focal seizures the results go both ways. In a 12-week trial, a CBD skin gel got into the blood about as well as oral CBD does, and it still didn't beat placebo. In a 2025 study, people on high-dose oral CBD, around 23 mg/kg a day (about 1,400 mg if you weigh 60 kg), had roughly half to three quarters fewer focal seizures over almost three years, but nobody in that study got a placebo. Neither study tested CBD as a quick rescue during a seizure. DMSO cut seizure activity in a rat temporal-lobe epilepsy model and protects nerve cells in several ways, but at some doses it made things worse in an absence-epilepsy model. So I see it as an experimental extra that might help, and I wouldn't wait on it while a seizure keeps going.
