I would also use topical magnesium chloride and consider topical DMSO with a known CBD preparation as adjuncts. CBD on the skin does get into the blood about as well as swallowed CBD, at least in a 12-week trial of a CBD gel, but the gel did no better than placebo for focal seizures. Swallowed, it's an approved seizure medicine for Lennox-Gastaut syndrome, Dravet syndrome and tuberous sclerosis complex, and in a 2025 study people with other focal epilepsies who took a lot of it, around 23 mg/kg a day, which comes to about 1,400 mg if you weigh 60 kg, had their seizures drop by half to three quarters over almost three years, though nobody in that study got a placebo to compare. None of these studies tried CBD as a rescue in the middle of a seizure. DMSO calmed seizure activity in a rat temporal-lobe epilepsy model and protects nerve cells in several ways, while at some doses it did the opposite in an absence-epilepsy model, so I treat it as a plausible experiment to add while you deal with the seizure, and I wouldn't wait on it.
