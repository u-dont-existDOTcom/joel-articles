If you're wondering whether that was just me, the research on DMSO and the heart has only been in animals and lab dishes so far, but it points that way. Rats given it for three days before a heart attack lost about three quarters less heart muscle (nobody gave it to them after the attack started, though). And since most heart attacks come from a clot blocking an artery, it's good that DMSO also slowed clotting, in mice and in human platelets (the blood cells that start clots) in a dish.
