Serotonin syndrome comes from too much serotonin, so I'd stop anything suspected of adding more (serotonergic substances). No more MDMA or other stimulants, of course, but also no DXM cough syrup, the painkiller tramadol or the antibiotic linezolid. Even if they take an antidepressant every day (an SSRI like Prozac, or an SNRI or MAOI), I'd skip it until a doctor says to restart it, since missing a dose is much less risky than more serotonin right now. If they drank ayahuasca or another harmala brew, like Syrian rue, no more of that either, since those work the way MAOIs do. And check whether they take supplements people don't think of as drugs, like 5-HTP, St John's wort or SAM-e.
