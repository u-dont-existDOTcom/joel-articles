S14: While you wait for the ambulance, I'd cool them down.
S15: Their twitching, tense muscles make a lot of heat, and overheating is the most dangerous part of serotonin syndrome.
S16: So move them to a cool room, take off extra clothes, and use a fan and cool damp cloths.
S17: If they're hot to the touch, I'd also put ice packs wrapped in cloth at the neck, armpits or groin.
S18: A cold bath would cool them faster, but if they're confused, seizing or very weak, they could slip under or breathe in water, so don't put them in one.
S19: And fever reducers like Tylenol or ibuprofen do not help here, because this heat is generated by their muscles, not by a fever.

S21: Since they're already overstimulated, it helps to clear out the crowd and stop any arguing.
