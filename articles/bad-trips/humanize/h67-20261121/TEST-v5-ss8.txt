This guide suggests niacin for ending a trip, and DMSO for brain injuries, seizures and chest pain, but I wouldn't use either one here. Niacin is actually unsafe with serotonin syndrome. For MDMA-related jaw tension or muscle activity, though, I would consider oral magnesium chloride if they're fully alert and able to swallow (the amounts are in MDMA: Magnesium Alarm, above).
