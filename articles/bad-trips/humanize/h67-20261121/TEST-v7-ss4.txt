Their twitching and tense muscles are making a lot of heat, and overheating is the most dangerous part of serotonin syndrome, so while you wait for the ambulance, I'd get them somewhere cool, take off their extra clothes, and set a fan blowing over cool damp cloths on their skin. If they still feel hot, ice packs wrapped in cloth help most at the neck, armpits and groin. A cold bath would cool them faster, but if they're confused, seizing or very weak, they could slip under or breathe in water, so don't put them in one. And don't count on fever reducers like Tylenol or ibuprofen, which do not help with heat generated by muscles.
