Their twitching and tense muscles are making a lot of heat, and overheating is the most dangerous part of serotonin syndrome. So while you wait for the ambulance, move them somewhere cool, take off extra clothes, and use a fan and cool damp cloths. If they're hot to the touch, I'd also put ice packs wrapped in cloth at the neck, armpits or groin, where big blood vessels run close to the skin. A cold bath would cool them faster, but if they're confused, seizing or very weak, they could slip under or breathe in water, so don't put them in one. And though fever reducers like Tylenol or ibuprofen seem like the obvious thing for someone this hot, they do not help here, because this heat is generated by their muscles, not by a fever.
