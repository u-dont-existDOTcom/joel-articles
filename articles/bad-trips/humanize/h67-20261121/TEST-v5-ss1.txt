Serotonin syndrome comes from too much serotonin, so I'd stop anything suspected of adding more (serotonergic substances). No more MDMA or other stimulants, of course, but also no DXM cough syrup, the painkiller tramadol or the antibiotic linezolid. I'd hold off on even their prescribed antidepressant until a doctor says to restart it, whether it's an SSRI like Prozac, an SNRI like Effexor or an MAOI. Ayahuasca and other harmala brews, like Syrian rue, act as MAOIs too, and don't forget the supplements people don't think of as drugs, like 5-HTP, St John's wort and SAM-e.
