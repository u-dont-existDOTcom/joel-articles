When you call medics, ask for an ambulance, since the person can get worse fast, with seizures or dangerous overheating. It may seem faster to drive them to the ER yourself, but don't: paramedics can start treating them as soon as they arrive, and if they have a seizure in your car, you can't help them and drive at the same time. If you don't know what was mixed, poison control can help work that out once the ambulance is on its way, though they can't treat it.
