The ER doctors will want to know exactly what they took, so before the ambulance comes, I'd gather their pill bottles and packages and jot down how much of each they took and when, street drugs and supplements included. If a dose went up lately, note that too, since a bigger dose alone can set off serotonin syndrome. And if you have a thermometer or a blood pressure cuff, write down the numbers with the time, which shows the doctors whether things are getting better or worse.
