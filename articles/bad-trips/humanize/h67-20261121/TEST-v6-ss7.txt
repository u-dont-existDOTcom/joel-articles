Before the ambulance comes, I'd gather their pill bottles and packages, so the ER doctors can see exactly what they took. Then I'd write down how much of each they took and when, including any recreational drugs and supplements. Mark any dose they raised lately, since that can be enough to set off serotonin syndrome. If you can take their temperature, pulse or blood pressure, write the numbers down with the time, so the doctors can see whether the person is getting better or worse.
