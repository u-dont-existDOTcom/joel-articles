When you call medics, ask for an ambulance, since the person can get worse fast, with seizures or dangerous overheating. It may seem faster to drive them to the ER yourself, but don't: paramedics can start treating them right there, and if they have a seizure in your car, you can't help them and drive at the same time. Poison control can't treat serotonin syndrome, but if you don't know what was mixed, they can help work that out once the ambulance is on its way.
