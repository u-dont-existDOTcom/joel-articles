The ER doctors will want to know exactly what they took, so before the ambulance comes, I'd gather their pill bottles and packages. I'd also jot down how much of each and when, street drugs and supplements included. If they raised the dose of anything lately, note that too, since a bigger dose alone can set off serotonin syndrome. And if you have a thermometer or a blood pressure cuff, take readings every so often and write them down with the time, so the doctors can see whether things are getting better or worse.
