Chest Pain and Injury

DMSO is also my first remedy for many injuries and some chest pain. After I overused erythritol, I developed angina that lasted for days. I took DMSO, the pain disappeared within about a minute, and it never returned.

The heart research on DMSO is only in animals and lab dishes so far, but it suggests DMSO protects the heart. In rats given DMSO before a heart attack, about three quarters less heart muscle died, and their blood showed far less sign of heart damage. Other studies found it slowed the clotting that blocks arteries and brings on a heart attack, in human platelets too.
