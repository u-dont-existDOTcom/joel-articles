During a trip, try not to take every dark thought that comes up as the truth about you, since it's often just the flip side of whatever you've been focusing on. Pushing the thought away tends to make it come back stronger, so let it be there. You can tell the person sitting with you, but maybe hold off on confessing it to everybody else, or deciding it means something is permanently wrong with you. Just notice what's there: "A thought is here. A feeling is here. It is intense, but it is not the whole of me." Then return to room, body, human, time, and love.
