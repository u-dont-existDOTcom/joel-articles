During a trip, a dark thought can show up that feels like the plain truth about you, like suddenly hating someone you love, or being sure you're a fraud. It's usually not the truth, just the opposite of whatever you were working so hard on, coming into focus. Shoving it down only makes it come back louder. Tell the person sitting with you if you want to, but don't go texting your whole family about it at 3 a.m., and don't decide you've got some lifelong disorder either. Just notice the thought and the feeling that came with it. They're intense, sure, but they aren't all of you. Then come back to the room you're in, your body, the person with you, what time it is, and when you can, love.
