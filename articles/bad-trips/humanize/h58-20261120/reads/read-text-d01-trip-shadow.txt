During a trip, try not to take every shadow thought as the truth about you, since it's often just the other half of a pair showing up, and don't push it away either, because that tends to make it come back stronger. Maybe hold off on confessing it to everybody, too, or turning it into some permanent diagnosis of what's wrong with you. Just notice it: "A thought is here. A feeling is here. It is intense, but it is not the whole of me." Then return to room, body, human, time, and love.
