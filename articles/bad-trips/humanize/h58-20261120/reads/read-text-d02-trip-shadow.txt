During a trip, try not to take every shadow thought as the truth about you. It's often just the other half of a pair showing up, and pushing it away tends to make it come back stronger. Maybe hold off on confessing it to everybody too, since a trip is about the worst time to decide something is permanently wrong with you. Just notice it: "A thought is here. A feeling is here. It is intense, but it is not the whole of me." Then return to room, body, human, time, and love.
