Side effects:

Astringency: 100% DMSO usually irritates skin on the upper half of the body. When the skin itches, dilute the DMSO with water or real aloe juice without added chemicals. Leaving 100% DMSO on the skin can crenate (shrivel) it, and the skin may take a week to look plump again. DMSO can also burn dry mouth tissue, so wet your mouth first.
Nausea: when drinking DMSO, dilute it in water, juice, or milk and sip gradually. Sensitivity varies, so try less at first.
Reduced sleep: DMSO reduces how long I sleep when I take too much, take it too late, or use it over too many consecutive days. It builds up past a threshold that seems to differ between people, so it's best taken in the morning, and reduce the dose if you notice you're sleeping less. In rats, higher doses made sleep less deep. Some people sleep better with DMSO because it reduces whatever symptom had been keeping them awake.
Body odor: above a certain dose you may smell like sulfur. I don't smell when I put it into milk.
