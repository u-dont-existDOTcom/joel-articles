Once their jaw tenses, that should be their last dose of MDMA, so get them to slow down and cool off. If they drink a lot of plain water, even a single normal MDMA dose raises the risk of their sodium dropping too low, so have them sip an electrolyte drink when they're thirsty instead. The following indicators would justify calling medics: If their confusion or headache worsens, or vomiting won't stop (by then their sodium may already be too low, so stop all drinks, electrolyte ones too, since they'd only thin it further). If their muscles keep getting weaker or their heart palpitations keep getting stronger, that could be from dangerously low potassium, like my second wife had. A seizure or collapse can result from low sodium or potassium, and can also come from overheating or MDMA toxicity.
