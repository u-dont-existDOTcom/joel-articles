Recognizing serotonin toxicity

Any one of the following patterns makes serotonin toxicity more likely. Are their muscles jerking in a rhythm by themselves, with nothing setting it off (that's called clonus)? Does the jerking start when you stretch a muscle, like flexing their foot up quickly, and are they also agitated or sweating? Do you see their eyes darting back and forth in a rhythm, again with agitation or sweating? Are they shaking, with reflexes that are way too brisk? Or are their muscles rigid, with a temperature above 38°C (100.4°F) and that eye or stretch-triggered clonus too?

Don't wait to see every one of these before you act, though. Dangerous heat, rigidity, clonus, confusion, or rapid worsening deserves medical help.
