Acute Medical and Neurological Rescue

An emergency means act now, and sometimes the fastest help is right where they are. Someone who's severely altered but physically stable may come down sooner with your help than after an ambulance ride and hours in an emergency room.

So check the body first. Are they breathing normally, and is their temperature safe? Are they free of seizures, with nothing suggesting a brain injury? Are they awake enough to work with you, and can you keep them physically safe? If all of that's true, calming and stabilizing the trip may be enough.
