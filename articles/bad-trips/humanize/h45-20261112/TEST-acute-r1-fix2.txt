Acute Medical and Neurological Rescue

An emergency means act now, and sometimes the fastest help is right where they are. If they're severely altered but physically stable, you may bring them down faster yourself than an ambulance ride and hours in an emergency room would.

So check the body first. Are they breathing normally, and is their temperature safe? No seizures, and nothing that looks like a brain injury? Are they awake enough to work with you, and can you keep them from getting hurt? If all that checks out, calming and stabilizing the trip may be enough.
