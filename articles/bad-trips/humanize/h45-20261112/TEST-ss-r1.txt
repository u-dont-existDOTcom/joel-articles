Recognizing serotonin toxicity

Any one of the following patterns makes serotonin toxicity more likely. Are their muscles contracting and relaxing in a rhythm on their own, with nothing setting it off (spontaneous clonus)? Or does that rhythm start when a muscle is stretched, like when you flex their ankle up quickly (inducible clonus), and come with agitation or sweating? Do you see their eyes darting back and forth in a fast rhythm by themselves (ocular clonus), again with agitation or sweating? Are they shaking, and are their reflexes abnormally brisk (tremor and hyperreflexia)? Or are their muscles rigid, with a temperature above 38°C (100.4°F) and eye or inducible clonus too (hypertonia and fever)?

Don't wait to see every one of these before you act, though. Dangerous heat, rigidity, clonus, confusion, or rapid worsening deserves medical help.
