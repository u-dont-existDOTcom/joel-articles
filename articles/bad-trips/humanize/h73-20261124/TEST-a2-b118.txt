What happened to my second wife is why I look past magnesium: she had severe convulsions after an MDMA overdose, and at the hospital they found her potassium critically low, described to me as essentially zero. IV potassium was the only treatment they gave her. So I treat serious MDMA toxicity as a broader electrolyte emergency, potassium included, even though magnesium is still the first and most useful intervention once the jaw tenses.
