**People with impaired kidneys do need to be more cautious with large, rapid magnesium doses because they may not clear magnesium normally. But they should be far more worried about the MDMA.** Besides overheating them and causing dangerous electrolyte shifts, it can hurt the kidneys directly: it can cause [acute kidney injury](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27917269/), and so can the muscle breakdown it brings on (rhabdomyolysis). So kidney trouble is a strong reason not to take MDMA at all, and once someone has taken it, their jaw tension still needs treating. With serious kidney impairment, I wouldn't copy my one-gram-per-minute approach blindly: I'd treat the situation as higher-risk and get help sooner.
