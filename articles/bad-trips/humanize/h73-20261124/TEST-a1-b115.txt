A token dose may not be enough: more than once, I've dissolved **Dead Sea magnesium chloride hexahydrate flakes** in water and taken about **one gram per minute by mouth for around twenty minutes** before my jaw fully released. Around twenty grams hasn't given me diarrhea, and I've only had it above about forty grams. Magnesium chloride is also [less likely to cause diarrhea than several other kinds of magnesium](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10509448/), including the ones people take on purpose as laxatives.
