Magnesium taken before the MDMA peak also makes the peak less euphoric for me, but I don't mind a little less euphoria, since in my experience a relaxed jaw comes with a much lower risk of the night turning into an emergency.

My second wife had severe convulsions after an MDMA overdose, and at the hospital they found her potassium critically low, described to me as essentially zero, and IV potassium was the only treatment they gave her. So I treat serious MDMA toxicity as a broader electrolyte emergency, potassium included, even though magnesium is still the first and most useful intervention once the jaw tenses.
