**If someone has impaired kidneys, they do need to be more cautious with large and rapid magnesium doses, because they may not clear magnesium normally. The MDMA is a far bigger danger to their kidneys, though.** Besides overheating them and causing dangerous electrolyte shifts, it can hurt the kidneys directly. [Acute kidney injury](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27917269/) can come from the MDMA itself or from the muscle breakdown it brings on (rhabdomyolysis). So kidney trouble is a strong reason to skip the drug altogether, and if they've already taken it, their jaw tension still needs treating. With serious kidney impairment, I wouldn't have them copy my one-gram-per-minute approach as is. I'd treat their case as higher-risk and call medics sooner than I would for someone with healthy kidneys.
