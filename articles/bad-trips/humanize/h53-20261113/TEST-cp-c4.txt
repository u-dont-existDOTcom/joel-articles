The heart research on DMSO has only been in animals and lab dishes so far. Rats given DMSO for three days before a heart attack lost about three quarters less heart muscle than rats without it, though that study didn't try giving it after the attack had started. Most heart attacks come from a clot blocking an artery, so it's a good sign that DMSO also slowed clotting, in mice and in human platelets (the blood cells that start clots) in a dish.
