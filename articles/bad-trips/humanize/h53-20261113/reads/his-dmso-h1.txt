S2: It's routinely used in humans who receive stem cell transplants, as it is the best cell preservative that we've found so far.
S3: There is evidence (Kortleve, et al, 2023) that using too much DMSO (10% vs 5%) in stem cell transfusions increases mild side effects in adult humans.

S5: Translating mouse research to humans isn't straightforward.
