S1 Psychological First Aid: The First Five Minutes The triage app gives the order.
S2 This section is about carrying it out without becoming one more source of pressure.
S3 Anything that could let someone hurt themselves in the next five minutes takes priority, so get the car keys out of reach and turn the stove off, and move them away from roads and balconies, or pools of water for that matter.
S4 Letting them move around is fine, and pacing can help, but keep them from taking more of any substance.
S5 If they want to go confront somebody, that can wait.
S6 And if they want to leave, have someone go with them.
S7 Put any weapons away and keep the person away from crowds and strangers.
S8 If they want to post their big revelation about the universe, or message an ex, take the phone.
S9 A lot of what looks like a deep psychedelic crisis is a scared person in a room that's getting on their nerves, so look around the room next.
S10 Maybe the light's too bright or the music's horrible.
S11 Are five concerned people talking at once?
S12 Somebody might be touching them when they don't want it, or they're stuck beside someone they don't trust.
S13 And check whether they're too hot or too cold, or desperate for the bathroom.
S14 They're feeling overwhelmed, so dim the lights, and clear out the extra people around them.
S15 Give them ordinary reality: You're in [place].
S16 It's [date/time].
S17 You took [substance, if known].
S18 These effects rise, peak, and change.
S19 This is intense, but it will change.
S20 We don't need to solve your life tonight.
S21 When the state came from meditation, breathwork, fasting, kundalini-style practice, extreme stretching, ritual, or a retreat, say: I am in an intense state.
S22 I am stopping the practice now.
S23 My job is ordinary safety.
S24 From there, choose one safe body experiment and one calm human.
S25 The next two sections explain those choices.
S26 Protect sleep, especially when the person is euphoric or grandiose, and let meaning wait until the nervous system has somewhere to stand.
S27 A non-drug altered state may have a less predictable clock than a substance.
S28 The practical move remains simple: stop the practice, eat something ordinary when safe, let the body settle, keep warm, and sleep.
S29 An open portal does not obligate you to keep pushing through it.
