S1 Psychological First Aid: The First Five Minutes The triage app gives you the order to do things in, and this section is about doing them in a way that takes pressure off the person.
S2 Anything that could let someone hurt themselves in the next five minutes takes priority, so get the car keys out of reach and turn the stove off, and move them away from roads and balconies, or pools of water for that matter.
S3 Letting them move around is fine, and pacing can help, but keep them from taking more of any substance.
S4 If they want to go confront somebody, that can wait.
S5 And if they want to leave, have someone go with them.
S6 Put any weapons away and keep the person away from crowds and strangers.
S7 If they want to post their big revelation about the universe, or message an ex, take the phone.
S8 A lot of what looks like a deep psychedelic crisis is a scared person in a room that's getting on their nerves, so look around the room next.
S9 Maybe the light's too bright or the music's horrible.
S10 Are five concerned people talking at once?
S11 Somebody might be touching them and they hate it, or they're stuck beside someone they're wary of.
S12 And check whether they're too hot or too cold, or desperate for the bathroom.
S13 They're feeling overwhelmed, so dim the lights, and clear out the extra people around them.
S14 Then remind them where they are and what's happening, since a trip can make them lose track of it: You're in [place].
S15 It's [date/time].
S16 You took [substance, if known], and its effects will peak and then change.
S17 It's intense right now, and it'll pass.
S18 We can sort out the rest of your life tomorrow.
S19 If the state came from a practice, like meditation or fasting, the person can say: I'm in an intense state, so I'm stopping the practice now, and my only job is to stay safe.
S20 A state from a practice has no drug wearing off on a schedule, so there's no telling how long it will last.
S21 Once they can eat safely, food and warmth help their body settle.
S22 Then help them pick one safe thing to try with their body and one calm person to stay with them, which the next two sections are about.
S23 Protect their sleep too, especially if they're euphoric or grandiose, and leave what it all meant until they've slept.
