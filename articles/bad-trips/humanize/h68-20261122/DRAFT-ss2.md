**If the person is fully alert and able to swallow, I would give cyproheptadine early**, since it blocks serotonin receptors. So far, the evidence for this med has only been [case reports](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9696181/), and [a recent review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39791184/) found them insufficient to show that it works. I wouldn't wait to see whether it does before doing the other steps. And most people won't have it at home anyway, since it needs a prescription in the US, though a telehealth doctor might write one quickly, and only some countries sell it without one.
