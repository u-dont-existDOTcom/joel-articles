Overstimulating also dehydrates them, but we can't give fluids unless they're fully alert, able to swallow and not vomiting, and then just small sips of an electrolyte drink. If they still show the signs of low electrolytes from [MDMA: Magnesium Alarm](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/mdma-magnesium-alarm) above, they may need **a hypertonic electrolyte drink with more salt and magnesium than a normal one**. That can be as simple as a pinch of salt and some magnesium chloride flakes stirred into an ordinary one, or a salty broth with the flakes in it. And while you might think to add potassium too, since it can run low after MDMA, I wouldn't add any, because muscles breaking down into the blood release a lot of it, and too much can stop the heart.
