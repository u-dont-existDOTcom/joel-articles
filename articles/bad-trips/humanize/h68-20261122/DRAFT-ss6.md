Overstimulating also dehydrates them, but we can't give fluids unless they're fully alert, able to swallow and not vomiting, and then just small sips of an electrolyte drink. If they still show the signs of low electrolytes from [MDMA: Magnesium Alarm](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/mdma-magnesium-alarm) above, they may need **a hypertonic electrolyte drink with more magnesium and potassium than a normal one**, which can be as simple as a normal one with a bit of extra salt, potassium and magnesium thrown in, or a salty potato soup with some Epsom salt.
