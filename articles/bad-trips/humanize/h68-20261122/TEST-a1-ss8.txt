While you wait, you might think of this guide's other remedies, like niacin for ending a trip or DMSO for brain injuries, seizures and chest pain, but I wouldn't use either one here. Niacin is actually unsafe with serotonin syndrome, since at the doses used to end a trip it can cause flushing, dizziness and low blood pressure (NIH), which could complicate their care. Syrian rue oil from the stabilizers is a maybe: made at home it has almost none of the harmala alkaloids, and it might even help with MDMA. But since I couldn't find any research on it with MDMA, it isn't the first thing I'd try, and if I did, I'd raise the dose slowly and watch whether it helps. Magnesium is different, since many people with serotonin syndrome run low on it (a nursing review), so for MDMA-related jaw tension or muscle activity, I would consider oral magnesium chloride if they're fully alert and able to swallow (the amounts are in MDMA: Magnesium Alarm, above). Topical magnesium chloride if they're not able to swallow.
