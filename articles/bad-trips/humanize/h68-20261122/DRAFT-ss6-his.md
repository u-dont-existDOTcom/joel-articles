Overstimulating also dehydrates them, but we can't give fluids unless they're fully alert, able to swallow and not vomiting, and then just small sips of an electrolyte drink. If they still show the signs of low electrolytes from [MDMA: Magnesium Alarm](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/mdma-magnesium-alarm) above, they may need **a hypertonic electrolyte drink with more magnesium and potassium than a normal one**. That can be as simple as an ordinary one with a bit of each thrown in, plus extra salt, or even a salty potato soup with some Epsom salt.
