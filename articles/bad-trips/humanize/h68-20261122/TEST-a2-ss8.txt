While you wait, you might think of this guide's other remedies, like niacin for ending a trip or DMSO for brain injuries, seizures and chest pain, but I wouldn't use either one here. Niacin is actually unsafe with serotonin syndrome, since at the doses used to end a trip it can cause flushing, dizziness and low blood pressure (NIH). I'd also leave out this guide's Syrian rue oil for now: made at home it has almost none of the harmala alkaloids and might even help, but I couldn't find any research on it with MDMA. Magnesium chloride is the one I would consider, since many people with serotonin syndrome run low on magnesium (a nursing review). If they're fully alert and able to swallow, I'd give it by mouth, especially with MDMA-related jaw tension or muscle activity (the amounts are in MDMA: Magnesium Alarm, above). Topical magnesium chloride if they're not able to swallow.
