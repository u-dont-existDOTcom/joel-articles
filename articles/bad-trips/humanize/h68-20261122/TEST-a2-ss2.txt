If the person is fully alert and able to swallow, I would give cyproheptadine early, since it blocks serotonin receptors. So far, the evidence for this med has only been case reports, and a recent review found them insufficient to show that it works. I wouldn't wait to see whether it does before doing the other steps. Most people won't have it at home anyway, since in the US it needs a prescription (a telehealth doctor might write one quickly), and only some countries sell it without one.
