If the person is fully alert and able to swallow, I would give cyproheptadine early, since it blocks serotonin receptors. So far, the evidence for this med has only been case reports, and a recent review found them insufficient to show that it works. I wouldn't wait to see whether it does before doing the other steps. Getting it may take a while anyway: in the US it needs a prescription, though a telehealth doctor might write one quickly for you to pick up, and in some other countries it's sold without one.
