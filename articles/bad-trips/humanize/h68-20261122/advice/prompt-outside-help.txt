S3: ## Call Medics For:
S4: - Trouble breathing, blue or gray lips, or respiratory depression
S5: - A seizure after taking too much (or an unknown amount), or one on a normal dose lasting over 5 minutes
S6: - Collapse, inability to wake normally, or rapidly worsening consciousness
S7: - Dangerous overheating (not just regular sweating), severe rigidity, or clonus with fever
S8: - Severe insomnia
S9: - Sudden one-sided weakness, loss of speech, inability to walk normally, or loss of control of bodily functions
S10: - A report that the brain feels damaged when the person cannot complete a basic neurological check or is rapidly worsening
S11: - Severe, worsening, radiating, or exertional chest pain--or chest pain with breathlessness, sweating, faintness, neurological changes, or collapse
S12: - Serious injury, uncontrolled bleeding, or a possible head or spinal injury
S13: - Repeated vomiting, severe dehydration, blood, or severe abdominal pain
S14: - Concerning symptoms after an MAOI, which slows the breakdown of serotonin (some older antidepressants, and the harmalas in ayahuasca and Syrian rue seeds), plus a serotonergic substance, one that raises serotonin, like MDMA or most antidepressants
S15: The remedies below can begin while help is being arranged.
S16: ## If Someone Might Get Hurt
S17: You can sit through a lot with someone, but the moment you think they might hurt themselves or anybody else, get urgent help.
S18: It doesn't have to be a proven death wish.
S19: They might just start heading out the door and get lost, hit by a car, drown, fall off a cliff, etc.
S20: Another cue to get help is if they start acting delusional.
S21: If for whatever reason you feel like this is more than you can handle, reach out.
S22: Use local emergency services (911 in the United States) for immediate danger, a medical emergency, a weapon, or a crime in progress.
S23: In the United States, 988 can provide mental-health crisis guidance, de-escalation, and connection to local resources when no immediate physical emergency exists.
S24: Outside the United States, identify the local emergency number and crisis line before you ever need them.
