If their breathing gets slow and shallow, give them naloxone (the Narcan nasal spray) if you have it. It doesn't matter if they think they only took MDMA, since fentanyl, an opioid, has turned up in pills sold as MDMA, and naloxone does no harm when there's no opioid. It wears off in 30 to 90 minutes, sooner than some opioids do, so call medics either way.
