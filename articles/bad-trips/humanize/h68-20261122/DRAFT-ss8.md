While you wait, you might think of this guide's other remedies, like niacin for ending a trip or DMSO for brain injuries, seizures and chest pain, but I wouldn't use either one here. Niacin is actually unsafe with serotonin syndrome, since at the doses used to end a trip it can cause flushing, dizziness and low blood pressure ([NIH](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Niacin-Consumer/)). I'd also leave out this guide's Syrian rue oil for now: made at home it has almost none of the harmala alkaloids, but I couldn't find any research on it with MDMA, so it could help or harm here. Magnesium chloride is the one I would consider, since many people with serotonin syndrome run low on magnesium ([a nursing review](https://doi.org/10.1097/01.NME.0000432871.59839.f3)). If they're fully alert and able to swallow, I'd give it by mouth, especially with MDMA-related jaw tension or muscle activity (the amounts are in [MDMA: Magnesium Alarm](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/mdma-magnesium-alarm), above). Topical magnesium chloride if they're not able to swallow.
