I am not repeating the simplistic Hermetic claim that opposites are required to understand anything. A hated baby does not feel love, and a loved baby does not feel hate. In the subconscious world of concepts, however, two sides are often considered together.

While you're still tripping, maybe don't take every shadow thought as the truth about you, or try to suppress it either. Confessing it to everyone in the room can wait, and so can turning it into some permanent diagnosis... You can just notice it instead, something like "this is a thought and a feeling, and it's intense, but it isn't the whole of me." Then return to room, body, human, time, and love.
