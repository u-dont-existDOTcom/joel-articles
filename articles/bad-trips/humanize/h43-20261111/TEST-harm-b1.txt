Risk of Harm: When Peer Support Isn't Enough

Get urgent help the moment somebody might hurt themselves or anyone else, or you can't keep them physically safe, like when they keep heading for roads and high places, or water for that matter. Voices telling them what to do are a reason too, and so is a fixed, terrifying belief they've started acting on. Get help just as fast if they turn violent or they're so severely confused they're unreachable. Dangerous physical symptoms count, and so does going so long without sleep that their judgment is falling apart. And if it's more than you can safely handle, call for help then too.
