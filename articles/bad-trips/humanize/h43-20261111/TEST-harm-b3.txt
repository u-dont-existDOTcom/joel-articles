Risk of Harm: When Peer Support Isn't Enough

You can sit through a lot with someone, but not everything. The moment you think they might hurt themselves or anybody else, get urgent help, even if you're not sure. I'd get help too if I couldn't keep them physically safe, like when they keep heading for the road or the water, or trying to get up somewhere high. It's time as well when voices are telling them what to do, or they've started acting on some terrifying belief they won't let go of, and any violence means calling right away. Someone so severely confused that they're unreachable needs more than a friend can give. So does someone whose body is showing dangerous signs, like trouble breathing or overheating, or who hasn't slept in so long that their judgment is going. If you find yourself wondering whether this is more than you can safely handle, it probably is, so call.
