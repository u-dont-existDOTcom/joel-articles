Risk of Harm: When Peer Support Isn't Enough

Peer support only goes so far. If it looks like someone might hurt themselves or anyone else, or you can't keep them physically safe, say they keep making for the road or the water, or climbing somewhere high, get urgent help right then. Voices telling them what to do are a reason to call, and so is a fixed, terrifying belief they've started acting on. If it turns violent, call right away. If they're so severely confused they're unreachable, or they haven't slept in so long their judgment is falling apart, that's past what a sitter can carry too, and so are the dangerous physical symptoms listed above. And if it's more than you can safely handle, call.
