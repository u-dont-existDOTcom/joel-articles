Acute Medical and Neurological Rescue

I'd calm and stabilize the trip right where they are, as long as none of the signs on the Call medics list above show up and I can keep them from getting hurt. Even from a severe trip, they can come down faster that way than after an ambulance ride and hours in an emergency room.

In an emergency room, a scared, psychotic-feeling person will often get a benzodiazepine. Those only sometimes help with psychosis, and in some people they bring on more agitation or fear instead, a paradoxical reaction. Antipsychotics may work better for psychosis, but they carry their own risks and interactions, and the staff giving them often don't know what was taken, if anything.
