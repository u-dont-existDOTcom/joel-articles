- S12: 2 — same repeated "Their ... turn/get" shape as S11 and S13.
- S13: 2 — same repeated "They/Their ..." opening as S11 and S12.
- S15: 4 — "one that's substantially different from their usual ones" does not say what counts as different; his way would spell it out (for example a longer seizure, a different kind of movement, or slower recovery than usual).
