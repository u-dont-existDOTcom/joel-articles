Acute Medical and Neurological Rescue

I'd calm and stabilize the trip right where they are, as long as none of the signs on the Call medics list above show up and I can keep them from getting hurt. Even from a severe trip, they can come down faster that way than after an ambulance ride and hours in an emergency room.

In an emergency room, a scared and psychotic-feeling person will often get a benzodiazepine. Don't count on it helping with psychosis, since in some people it does the opposite and brings on more agitation or fear, which is called a paradoxical reaction. Antipsychotics may work better for psychosis, but they carry their own risks and interactions. The staff also often have no idea what was taken, if anything, so if you do end up there, tell them.
