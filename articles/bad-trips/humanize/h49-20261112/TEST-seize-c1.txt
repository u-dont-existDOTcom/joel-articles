Active Seizure

Time the seizure, and while it lasts:
- Move anything hard or sharp away from them so they don't get hurt.
- Loosen any tight clothing.
- Turn them gently onto their side, which helps them breathe.
- Don't restrain them, and don't put anything in their mouth while they're convulsing or not fully conscious.

Call medics if any of these happen:
- A convulsive seizure goes on for about five minutes.
- Another seizure starts before they've recovered from the last one.
- They have trouble breathing.
- They get dangerously hot.
- They're seriously hurt.
- It's their first seizure, or it's substantially different from the ones they usually have.
