If they have a rescue medicine for seizures with them, like one they carry for epilepsy, I'd give it when and how its label says. It's a benzodiazepine, the kind of drug doctors reach for first when a seizure won't stop. A pill is no use while someone's seizing, so it'll be a nasal spray or a rectal gel, or sometimes an intramuscular shot. After giving it, I'd keep a close eye on their breathing, since a benzodiazepine adds to the risk of it getting slow and shallow, especially if they've also taken respiratory depressants like opioids or alcohol.
