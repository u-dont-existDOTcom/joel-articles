If Someone Says "My Brain Feels Damaged": DMSO + Humic Acid + Electrolytes First

I believe DMSO works better for stroke and most acute brain damage than anything else readily available to ordinary people. My judgment goes further than the clinical evidence, but that evidence points the same way. People who'd had a stroke from a clot may have recovered better on DMSO in a preliminary trial. Doctors have also used it in a small series of patients to bring down dangerous pressure inside the skull. And animal stroke research found it shrank the area of the brain that died, even when treatment started after the stroke, so it may still help if you start late. It also suppresses NMDA- and AMPA-mediated excitotoxicity, the overexcited nerve signaling that kills brain cells after a stroke.
