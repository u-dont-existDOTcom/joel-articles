If Someone Says "My Brain Feels Damaged": DMSO + Humic Acid + Electrolytes First

I believe DMSO works better for stroke and most acute brain damage than anything else readily available to ordinary people. That's more than the clinical evidence shows so far, but what there is points the same way. In animal stroke research, it shrank the area of the brain that died even when it was given after the stroke, so it may still help after the damage has started. A preliminary trial in people who'd had a stroke from a clot found they may have recovered better on it, and doctors have used it in a small series of patients to bring down dangerous pressure inside the skull. It also calms the overexcited nerve signaling that kills brain cells after a stroke (NMDA- and AMPA-mediated excitotoxicity).
