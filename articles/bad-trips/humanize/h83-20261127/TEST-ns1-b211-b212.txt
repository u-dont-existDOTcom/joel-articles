Flushing niacin has effectively aborted trips for me and friends, although I dislike it compared with [beach morning glory](#§beach-morning-glory).

I include it because most people don't have access to *Ipomoea pes-caprae*. Niacin has been used for decades by orthomolecular doctors for psychotic conditions and seems to act both like a natural benzodiazepine and an antipsychotic. The clinical literature is discussed in *Niacin: The Real Story* by Abram Hoffer, M.D.
