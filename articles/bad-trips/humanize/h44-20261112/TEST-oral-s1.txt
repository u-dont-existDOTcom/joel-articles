Recent Oral Toxic Ingestion

Before you pick a remedy for an "overdose", find out what they actually swallowed, how much and when, because that word alone doesn't tell you much. Then look at what their body is doing. Slow, shallow breathing (respiratory depression) is a different problem from a racing stimulant high, and both are different from serotonin toxicity, a seizure, dangerous overheating, etc., so each gets its own remedy.

Activated charcoal can help if they swallowed something recently that charcoal binds to, as long as they're awake enough to swallow safely. It won't bind every toxin, and it can soak up medicine they need. And if they're vomiting, sedated, seizing or too out of it to protect their airway, there's a real aspiration risk, meaning they could breathe it into their lungs. Clinical review.

Poison control can be useful when you're not sure what the substance or combination does, or whether there's a delayed risk, and they can tell you whether charcoal would be of any value. I don't treat calling them as a mandatory ritual, though, since how useful they are depends on the country and on whoever picks up. They're there to help you decide, and the first aid is still yours to do.
