This hierarchy is for acute situations, including emergencies--not merely uncomfortable trips.

In general it's good to try all these things in very low, increasing doses before they are needed in crisis.

Sometimes the mind isn't in a metaphysical crisis. The body feels awful and the altered mind writes cosmic horror to explain it. Nausea, heat, exhaustion, dehydration, overstimulation, and sleep loss all get read as spiritual content very quickly.
