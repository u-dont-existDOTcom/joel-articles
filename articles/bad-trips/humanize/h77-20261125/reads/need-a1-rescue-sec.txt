S7: Gives the acts: quiet, water, cooling, warmth, rest, a calmer room.
S8: Nothing the reader can act on or decide with; it restates S4-S7 as a slogan.

S11: States what the remedies are for (gut/brain trouble) as against the shadow work below (mainly emotional crisis), so the reader can choose the right section.
S12: Implies an act: before treating a mental hell as shadow work, check whether the gut is driving it.
