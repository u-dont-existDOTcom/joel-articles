This hierarchy is for acute situations, including emergencies--not merely uncomfortable trips.

In general it's good to try all these things in very low, increasing doses before they are needed in crisis.

Sometimes the mind isn't in a metaphysical crisis. The body feels awful and the altered mind writes cosmic horror to explain it. Nausea, heat, exhaustion, dehydration, overstimulation, and sleep loss all get read as spiritual content very quickly.

A horrible thought isn't automatically a revelation. The body may need quiet, water, cooling, warmth, rest, or a calmer room. The body is part of the spiritual field.

The hierarchy appears before the mystical sections because love doesn't require us to ignore the body.

If a trip comes with diarrhea or nausea, a toxic heaviness or a dirty body-feeling, jaw tension, loss of bodily control or neurological disorganization, the mind may feel like it's going crazy only because the gut or the brain is. That's what the remedies below are for, while the shadow work further down is for a mainly emotional crisis. I say this because a gut-driven mental hell is often mistaken as shadow work.
