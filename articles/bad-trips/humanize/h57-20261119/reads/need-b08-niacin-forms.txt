NEEDS:
S1: Knowing the main expected side effect (flush, itchy sunburn-like heat) so they recognize it when it happens.
S2: Knowing the flush is normal and not a danger sign, so they don't panic or stop, and that how bad it feels varies.
S3: Knowing heartburn is a risk, and that a sensitive stomach or too much at once raises it.
S4: Knowing niacin can make them drowsy and that sleep afterward may be poor.
S5: Knowing the real risk (liver harm at higher or repeated doses) and who should be most careful.
S6: Knowing that other B3 forms are less dependable for relaxation, so plain niacin is the one to use.
S7: Knowing what niacinamide does if they consider it, and that the result depends on their state.
S8: Knowing which forms to avoid (sustained-release, and the "no-flush" inositol hexanicotinate).

UNNEEDED:
- none
