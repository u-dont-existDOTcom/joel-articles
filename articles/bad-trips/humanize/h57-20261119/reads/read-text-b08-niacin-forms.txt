Niacin flush: itchy sunburn-like heat. This is normal, although some people find it intolerable and others enjoy it.
Heartburn in sensitive stomachs or after too much at once.
Drowsiness, usually with terrible sleep quality.
Higher or repeated doses can harm the liver, especially with liver problems, alcohol, medications, or sensitive physiology.

Other forms of vitamin B3 seem less reliable for relaxation. Niacinamide sometimes relaxes people and sometimes creates hyperfocus, depending on the state they're in. Avoid sustained-release niacin and the useless "no-flush" kind (inositol hexanicotinate).
