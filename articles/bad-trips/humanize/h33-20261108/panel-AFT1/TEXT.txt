When symptoms linger, try to name the pattern. "I'm broken" lumps everything together, and different patterns need different support. If it's mostly anxiety and fear that stayed with you, grounding helps, and so does a steady routine with less going on around you. You can also check the scary thoughts against what's happening, the way CBT does it. If you can't sleep or keep having nightmares, look after your sleep first, and stay away from more substances or intense practice for now. If either one is severe or drags on, get professional help.
