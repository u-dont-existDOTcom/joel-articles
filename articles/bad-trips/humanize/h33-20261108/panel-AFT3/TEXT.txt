When symptoms are still hanging around after the trip, try to name what kind they are, since "I'm broken" tends to swallow everything at once, and the different kinds get better with different things.

If it's mostly anxiety and fear that stayed, grounding usually helps, along with a routine that's as steady and low-key as you can make it for a while, ideally with fewer people around and less news, that kind of thing. You can also test the scary thoughts against what's happening around you, the way CBT does, although if the fear keeps coming back, that's a good time to find a therapist.

If you can't sleep, or the nightmares keep coming, look after your sleep first, before you try to make sense of anything, and stay away from more substances and from pushing your practice until it settles, which can take a little while. Get professional help if it's severe or just won't let up.
