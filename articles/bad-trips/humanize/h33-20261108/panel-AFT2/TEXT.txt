When symptoms linger, it's worth figuring out what kind they are, since "I'm broken" covers everything at once and each kind is helped by something different. Leftover anxiety and fear tend to settle with grounding and a quiet, steady routine, with fewer people and less noise. It also helps to check the scary thoughts against what's going on around you, the way CBT does it. Sleep problems are their own thing. With nightmares or bad nights, sleep comes first, which means no more substances and no pushing a practice for a while. Bring in a professional if the fear or the bad nights hang on, or get really bad.
