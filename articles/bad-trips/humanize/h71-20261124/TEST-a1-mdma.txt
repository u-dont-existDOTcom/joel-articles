Once their jaw tenses, that should be their last dose of MDMA, so get them to slow down and cool off. If they drink a lot of plain water, [even a single normal MDMA dose](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39546312/) raises the risk of their sodium dropping too low, so have them sip an electrolyte drink when they're thirsty instead. Plain water is the risk because MDMA makes the body hold on to water, so the water thins out whatever salt they haven't sweated out already. Women are most at risk of this, based on the results we have from [one study done at a rave](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23476039/): sodium was mildly low in 1 in 4 of the women on ecstasy there, vs only 3% of the men. Women also had four times the odds of low sodium among the MDMA cases reported to [California's poison control](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17084942/). But a [lab study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4923534/) found no such difference when people took pure MDMA and drank water. I think that's partly because the lab used real MDMA, while the "MDMA" people take at festivals is ecstasy, which may have something else in it. The lab also dosed everyone by their weight, and at a festival the same pill hits a smaller body harder. The following indicators would justify calling medics: If their confusion or headache worsens, or vomiting won't stop (sodium might be too low by then for an electrolyte drink to fix; while you wait for medics, if they're alert and not vomiting, give them something very salty instead, like strong broth). That has to be a lot of salt, more than a snack gives: [guidelines for hikers who drank too much water](https://www.aafp.org/pubs/afp/issues/2021/0215/p252.html) use three or four bouillon cubes in half a cup of water. If their muscles keep getting weaker or their heart palpitations keep getting stronger, that could be from dangerously low potassium, like my second wife had. A [seizure](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/170920118/active-seizure) or collapse can result from low sodium, magnesium or potassium, and can also come from overheating or MDMA toxicity.
