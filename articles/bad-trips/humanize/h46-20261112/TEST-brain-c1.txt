If Someone Says "My Brain Feels Damaged": DMSO + Humic Acid First

While the DMSO is working, check them over. Several of these checks are recognized stroke warning signs:
- First, are they awake and breathing normally, and how hot are they?
- Have them talk and smile: slurred speech, or one side of the face drooping, is a warning sign.
- Ask them to hold both arms out in front of them. If an arm drifts down, that's another.
- Watch them stand and walk, if that's safe. Losing their balance counts, and so does losing control of their bladder or bowels.
- Keep an eye out for a seizure, or for vomiting again and again.
- Ask if they have a really bad headache or chest pain, and check whether their vision has changed.

When they're too out of it to answer you or do the checks, count that as a warning sign too, since nobody can see what's happening in their brain. If everything checks out but the brain-damage feeling stays, keep going with the DMSO and check them again soon.
