If Someone Says "My Brain Feels Damaged": DMSO + Humic Acid First

While the DMSO is working, check them over. Several of these checks are recognized stroke warning signs:
- First, are they awake? Check their breathing, and feel whether they're very hot.
- Have them talk and smile: slurred speech, or one side of the face drooping, is a warning sign.
- Ask them to hold both arms out in front of them. If an arm drifts down, that's another.
- Watch them stand and walk, if that's safe. Losing their balance counts, and so does losing control of their bladder or bowels.
- Look out for a seizure. Vomiting again and again is a sign as well.
- Ask whether they have a really bad headache, and whether their chest hurts.
- Check if their vision has changed.

For objective stroke-like signs, I would use DMSO immediately while someone calls medics. When they're too out of it to answer you or do the checks, count that as a warning sign too, since nobody can see what's happening in their brain. If everything checks out but the brain-damage feeling stays, keep going with the DMSO and check them again soon.
