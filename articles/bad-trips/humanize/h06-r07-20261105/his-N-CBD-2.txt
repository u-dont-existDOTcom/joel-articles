As for topical CBD, it hasn't been well-studied. There was a short 12-week trial done, where they used a CBD skin gel, and although absorption was good, it didn't work any better than placebo to prevent seizures. The study authors suggested the dosing may have been too low, or it works better for generalized rather than focal seizures. Generalized are the type people usually have with MDMA & psychedelic overdoses.
