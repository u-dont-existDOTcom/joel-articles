If Someone Says "My Brain Feels Damaged": DMSO + Humic Acid First

While the DMSO is working, check them over. Several of these checks look for recognized stroke warning signs. If any check turns up a problem (or they're too out of it to answer you), keep the DMSO going while someone calls medics:
- First, are they awake and breathing normally?
- Feel whether they're very hot.
- Have them talk and smile. Is their speech slurred, or does one side of their face droop?
- Ask them to hold both arms out in front of them, and see if one drifts down.
- If it's safe, have them stand and walk a few steps to see if they keep their balance. Wetting or soiling themselves is another sign to watch for.
- Watch for a seizure, and for vomiting that keeps coming back.
- Ask about a really bad headache or chest pain.
- Check if their vision has changed.

If everything checks out but the brain-damage feeling stays, keep going DMSO-first and check them again soon.
