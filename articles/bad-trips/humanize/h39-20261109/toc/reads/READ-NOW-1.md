## 2. What gets in the way

**Remedies sit under the wrong parent.** Every remedy group (gut-toxin tools, nausea smoothers, stop the trip, stabilizers, niacin) is a child of `## How I label the evidence`, an 85-word legend. A reader scanning for "stop the trip" has to look inside a heading about evidence labels. The legend should come first, and the groups should be siblings of it. `## Some Remedies Require More Caution` then ends up as a sibling too, after all the remedies. It is a warning that should come before them.

**Two "Rescue" sections.** `# Acute Medical and Neurological Rescue` and `# My Acute Rescue Hierarchy` read as the same thing. Only the content (emergencies versus remedies) tells them apart, and a skimmer can't see that.

**The same things are in two places.**
- DMSO and humic acid are in the medical section (`## If Someone Says "My Brain Feels Damaged": DMSO + Humic Acid First`, 473 words) and again as `#### Humic Acid Brings Brain to Optimal Function` and `#### **DMSO**` (432 words).
- Charcoal appears three times: `#### Actual Activated Charcoal`, `#### Shungite, Aged Ozonated-Charcoal Water, or Boiled Charcoal Water`, and `## Recent Oral Toxic Ingestion`.
- `### Who to Contact, and When` (under Get One Human Anchor) overlaps `# When to Bring In Outside Help`. A reader looking for who to call has two candidates and can't tell which is for what.

**Empty or one-word parents.** `# When to Bring In Outside Help` has 1 word, so it works as a label only. `### When the Goal Is to Stop a Psychedelic Trip`, `### Mental Stabilizers That Don't Abort the Trip` and `### A Final Trip Killer` have 0 words each. They are fine as group labels, but `### A Final Trip Killer` holds one remedy (niacin) and its name doesn't say what it is for. Niacin ends the trip, so it belongs with the "stop the trip" group, not in a group of its own.

**Headings that don't say what's under them.**
- `# Inner Signal App to the Rescue!` doesn't say it is a quick triage app. A first-time reader doesn't know what "Inner Signal" is.
- `### Do` says nothing. Do what? It sits under the helper section, but "Do" next to "When the Content Turns Toward Harm" doesn't tell the reader what the list is.
- `## Dealing With Sorrow: Missing Versus Sending Love` has two readings: grief for the dead, or missing a person during the trip.
- `## Hand on Heart and Gratitude 🫶🏿` doesn't say it is a practice or a technique.
- `### The Joker Poem` (493 words) is the second-biggest block in its section and the heading doesn't say what it is for.
- `### What Kind of Aftermath Is This?` is 669 words, the biggest block in the section, and the question doesn't say it sorts aftermaths by type.
- `## Spiritual ≠ "Getting Your Ass Kicked"` can be read two ways: either spiritual growth isn't punishment, or a bad trip isn't spiritual.
- `### Who to Contact, and When` doesn't say who the contacts are (friends, professionals, hotlines).
- `## Grounding Menu: Choose One or Two Things` doesn't say these are for the person in the trip.

**Sections in odd places.**
- `## When the Trip Feels Amazing: Euphoric Certainty` sits between "No Rash Actions" and "(Re)defining 'Bad Trip'". It belongs next to `## The Other Bypass: Feeling Good Forever`. "The Other Bypass" has no visible first bypass before it, and this section would be that.
- `## (Re)defining "Bad Trip"` is a framing piece, but it comes after a long run of crisis sections. It reads like a section that should come close to the start of the psychological part. I wouldn't move it before the crisis help, since a person in panic won't read it.
- `## Grounding Menu` (584 words) is the most obvious companion to `## Psychological First Aid: The First Five Minutes`, but it comes after the helper section.
- `### Touch, Consent, and Compassion` is under "Hand on Heart", and also fits with the helper's duties. A helper looking for rules on touching someone wouldn't look there.
- `### When the Content Turns Toward Harm` is under the helper section, but it's also what a reader in situation 14 needs. It could be in `# When to Bring In Outside Help` too.
- `## When Peer Support Isn't Enough` (131 words) sits with the medic and mental-health lists, and this works.

**Levels.** The remedies run to four levels (H1, H2, H3, H4), deeper than anything else in the guide. After promoting the groups the deepest level is H3.

The greeting ("Hello, Lovey Bunny! ...") opens `# Shadow Work and Tripsitting Yourself or Others`, which comes after the medical and remedy parts, as the writer decided. The 76 words under that heading carry it, so the part starts where it should.

### Changes and reasons

1. **`# My Acute Rescue Hierarchy` becomes `# My Acute Rescue Hierarchy of Remedies`.** The reader can then tell it from `# Acute Medical and Neurological Rescue`.
2. **`## How I label the evidence` stops being the parent of the remedies.** It stays first in the section as a legend. The remedy groups move up to H2 and their items to H3 (this accounts for every group and `####` item in the list above), so the remedies are no longer under an evidence legend and nothing goes four levels deep.
3. **`## Some Remedies Require More Caution` moves before the remedy groups.** A warning is read before the remedies it applies to, not after the last one.
4. **`### A Final Trip Killer` is dropped as a heading, and `#### Niacin` goes under `## When the Goal Is to Stop a Psychedelic Trip`.** Niacin is a stop-the-trip remedy, so it goes with the other one (Beach Morning Glory). This also removes a group that held one item and a heading that didn't say what it was for.
5. **The medical section's order is changed:** Active Seizure first, then Serotonin Syndrome, MDMA and the brain-damage heading, and `## Chest Pain and Injury` and `## Recent Oral Toxic Ingestion` last. Seizure and serotonin toxicity are the most urgent and look alike ("shaking all over"), so they sit together at the top (situation 1). The 473-word DMSO + Humic Acid heading stays in this section, because the reader who hears "my brain is broken" looks here.
6. **DMSO, humic acid and the charcoal items are kept in both places.** I don't know what each says, so I would not merge them blind. They need a cross-reference line or a check that they say different things. This is a question for the writer; I'm not cutting anything.
7. **`## Grounding Menu` moves up, right after `## Psychological First Aid: The First Five Minutes`.** It's the thing a panicking person's helper needs next (situation 4), and it is 584 words of tools that don't depend on the helper section.
8. **`### Who to Contact, and When` becomes `### Which Friend to Contact, and When`.** This is a guess about what it holds, based on its parent "Get One Human Anchor". The point is to separate it from the outside-help section (situation 15). If it's about professionals too, the writer should keep the old name and fix the other heading.
9. **`### Do` becomes `### What to Do`.** A bare verb doesn't tell the reader what the list is.
10. **`## When the Trip Feels Amazing: Euphoric Certainty` moves next to `## The Other Bypass: Feeling Good Forever`.** They're the two sides of the same trap, and "the other bypass" now follows its first one. `## (Re)defining "Bad Trip"` and `## Spiritual ≠ "Getting Your Ass Kicked"` come before them as the framing.
11. **`## No Rash Actions: Visions Are Not Commands` stays ahead of the framing sections.** It's a crisis section (situation 9), so it comes with the other crisis help.
12. **No heading is renamed where I could not tell what is under it and could not guess safely.** `## Dealing With Sorrow: Missing Versus Sending Love`, `## Hand on Heart and Gratitude`, `### The Joker Poem` and `### What Kind of Aftermath Is This?` are unclear to me from the contents alone. I've left them in his words and flagged them in part 2; the writer knows what's under them and I don't. The playful wording of "Hang up the phone, get off the Healing Treadmill" and "Metta NMT Sniffer Makes the Trip Chill & Lovey!" is untouched.
13. **Nothing is added and nothing is cut.** Every heading in the guide's contents is still there.
