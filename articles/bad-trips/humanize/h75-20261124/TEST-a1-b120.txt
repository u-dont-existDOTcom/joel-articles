**People with impaired kidneys do need to be more cautious with large, rapid magnesium doses because they may not clear magnesium normally. But they should be far more worried about the MDMA**, since MDMA can cause [acute kidney injury](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27917269/) and rhabdomyolysis, on top of the overheating and dangerous electrolyte shifts. That makes kidney impairment a strong reason not to take MDMA, although once they've taken it, they still shouldn't sit there with worsening jaw tension. I just wouldn't tell somebody with serious kidney impairment to copy my one-gram-per-minute approach blindly, since that's a higher-risk situation where they should get help sooner.
