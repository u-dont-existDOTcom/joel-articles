Someone with impaired kidneys does need to be cautious with a large, rapid magnesium dose, since their kidneys may not clear magnesium normally. But the MDMA should worry them far more. It can cause overheating and rhabdomyolysis (muscle breaking down into the blood), and it can cause dangerous electrolyte shifts and acute kidney injury. Kidney impairment is a strong reason to skip MDMA altogether. Once someone has taken it and their jaw is getting worse, the kidneys are no excuse for sitting there. Someone with serious kidney impairment should not copy my one-gram-per-minute approach blindly. Treat the situation as higher-risk and get help sooner.
