If someone has impaired kidneys, they do need to be cautious with large magnesium doses taken fast, because their kidneys may not clear the magnesium normally. The MDMA should worry them a lot more than the magnesium, though. It can overheat the body, and it can cause rhabdomyolysis (muscle breaking down into the blood), dangerous electrolyte shifts and acute kidney injury. Kidney impairment is a strong reason to skip MDMA altogether, and once they've taken it, it's no reason to sit there with worsening jaw tension. With serious kidney impairment I wouldn't have them copy my one-gram-per-minute approach blindly, since I'd treat it as a higher-risk situation and get help sooner.
