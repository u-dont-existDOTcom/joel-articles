Call medics if any of these happen:
- They have a seizure after taking too much, or you don't know how much they took.
- Any other seizure goes on for over 5 minutes.
- Their breathing gets slow and shallow.
- Their lips turn blue or gray.
- Their forehead feels very hot (not just sweaty).
- They injure their head, break a bone, or bleeding won't stop.

Psychedelics can lower the seizure threshold even at a normal dose, so someone with epilepsy or who's otherwise prone to seizures might have a seizure without taking too much. That's most likely less dangerous than an actual drug overdose seizure, which is why you give it 5 minutes before calling.
