Call medics if any of these happen:
- They have a seizure after taking too much.
- Any other seizure goes on for over 5 minutes.
- Their breathing gets slow and shallow.
- Their lips turn blue or gray.
- Their forehead feels very hot to the touch, not just sweaty.
- They injure their head, break a bone, or bleeding won't stop.

Even a normal dose of a psychedelic can lower the seizure threshold, making seizures easier to set off, so a person whose threshold is already low, like someone with epilepsy, might have a seizure. That's most likely less dangerous than a seizure from an actual overdose.
