## Psychological First Aid: The First Five Minutes

The [triage app](https://ibogaqueen.com/1625-2/) gives the order. This section is about carrying it out without becoming one more source of pressure.

![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/944bef30-ca59-415b-a2e8-809a29ac2ba6_1122x1402.png)

Begin with whatever could hurt somebody in the next five minutes. Put away the car keys. Turn off the stove. Move away from roads, balconies, water, weapons, crowds, and strangers. Take the phone when somebody is preparing to publish the complete revelation of the universe or message an ex.

Movement is allowed. Unsafe movement is not. No driving, redosing, confrontations, or leaving alone.

Then look at the room. Is the light too bright? Is the music horrible? Are five concerned people talking at once? Is somebody touching the person when touch is unwelcome? Are they too hot, too cold, desperate for the bathroom, or trapped beside somebody they don't trust?

A huge amount of what gets called profound psychedelic crisis is a frightened nervous system stuck in an annoying room.

Dim the lights. Lower the sound. Clear out the extra people. Offer water without forcing it. Sip rather than chug. Ask before touching. Speak slowly and use fewer words. Please don't explain the universe at somebody who is already frightened by it.

Ask "music or quiet?" If a track starts pulling the experience somewhere unwanted, change it. This is a bad time to defend the playlist.

Give them ordinary reality:

> You're in *[place]*. It's *[date/time]*. You took *[substance, if known]*. These effects rise, peak, and change. This is intense, but it will change. We don't need to solve your life tonight.

When the state came from meditation, breathwork, fasting, *kundalini*-style practice, extreme stretching, ritual, or a retreat, say:

> I am in an intense state. I am stopping the practice now. My job is ordinary safety.

From there, choose one safe body experiment and one calm human. The next two sections explain those choices. Protect sleep, especially when the person is euphoric or grandiose, and let meaning wait until the nervous system has somewhere to stand.

A non-drug altered state may have a less predictable clock than a substance. The practical move remains simple: stop the practice, eat something ordinary when safe, let the body settle, keep warm, and sleep. An open portal does not obligate you to keep pushing through it.

### Stillness or Movement?

Test them rather than turning them into rival religions. Try one for two minutes and ask: *Does this make me safer and clearer, or more activated and impulsive?*

When lying down feels like imprisonment, pace slowly in a contained space. When pacing begins to resemble escape, sit against a wall or lie under a blanket. The goal is to let the nervous system discharge without creating a new danger.

**Parallel processing** can help when lying still turns into immobile introspection. Let one part of attention stay with the body, fear, grief, or memory while another part completes a simple, safe task with a visible endpoint: sort ten objects, draw, fold five towels, or wash one cup. Finishing something small helps confirm that time is moving and ordinary reality is still here. Stop if the task increases agitation, speed, or risk.

A useful order is **room → body → human → time → meaning**. Most people begin with meaning because the experience feels cosmic. When the nervous system is on fire, love becomes practical first.
