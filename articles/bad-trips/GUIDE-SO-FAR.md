# When to Get Outside Help

![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/fcb02067-8041-4f0d-aaa5-8ccf8cd48531_1122x1402.png)

## Call Medics For:

- Breathing that gets slow and shallow, or any trouble breathing
- Lips that turn blue or gray
- A [seizure](#§active-seizure) after they took too much or nobody knows how much, or one on a normal dose that goes past 5 minutes
- Collapsing, or not waking when you shake them and say their name
- Getting harder to wake by the minute
- A forehead that's dangerously hot to the touch, not just sweaty
- Muscles gone rigid, or a fever along with jerking they can't stop (clonus)
- No sleep for days
- Sudden weakness on one side of the body, or losing the ability to speak
- Not being able to walk normally
- Wetting or soiling themselves
- The feeling that their brain is damaged, when they can't do [the brain checks](#§my-brain-feels-damaged) or are quickly getting worse at them
- [Chest pain](#§chest-pain-and-injury) that's severe or getting worse, or that spreads into an arm or the jaw
- Chest pain that came on with hard movement, or that comes with sweating or shortness of breath, or makes them feel faint
- A serious injury, like uncontrolled bleeding or a possible head or spine injury
- Vomiting that won't stop
- Blood in their vomit or stool
- Being badly dried out: not peeing for hours, or dizzy when they stand up
- Bad stomach pain
- [Concerning symptoms](#§how-to-check-for-ss) after they took an MAOI, which keeps serotonin from breaking down (some older antidepressants, or the harmalas in ayahuasca and Syrian rue seeds), with something that raises serotonin, like MDMA or most antidepressants

Slow, shallow breathing and losing consciousness can indicate an opioid overdose, which wouldn't seem to make sense if they think they took MDMA, but in fact, opioids like fentanyl have turned up in pills sold as MDMA. Luckily, opioid overdoses are the easiest thing to treat. Naloxone reverses them safely, so if you have it, spray it into a nostril right away. But naloxone can wear off before the opioid does, so call medics even if it works to wake them up, and if their breathing slows again before help comes, give the second spray in the pack.

[The remedies below](#§medical-and-brain-rescue) can begin while help is being arranged.

## If Someone Might Get Hurt

You can sit through a lot with someone, but the moment you think they might hurt themselves or anybody else, get urgent help. It doesn't have to be a proven death wish. They might just start heading out the door and get lost, hit by a car, drown, fall off a cliff, etc. Another cue to get help is if they start acting on a delusion.

If for whatever reason you feel like this is more than you can handle, reach out.

Use local emergency services (911 in the United States) for immediate danger, a medical emergency, a weapon, or a crime in progress. In the United States, 988 can provide mental-health crisis guidance, de-escalation, and connection to local resources when no immediate physical emergency exists. Outside the United States, identify the local emergency number and [crisis line](#§who-to-contact) before you ever need them.

# Medical and Brain Rescue

I'd [calm and stabilize the trip](#§too-intense-stabilizers) right where they are, as long as none of the signs on the [Call medics list](#§call-medics-for) above show up and I can keep them from getting hurt. Even from a severe trip, they can come down faster that way than after an ambulance ride and hours in an emergency room.

In an emergency room, a scared and psychotic-feeling person will often get a benzodiazepine. Don't count on it helping with psychosis, since in some people it brings on a paradoxical reaction, with more agitation or fear. Antipsychotics may work better for psychosis, but they carry their own risks and interactions. The staff also often have no idea what was taken, if anything, so if you do end up there, tell them.

## "My Brain Feels Damaged"

Don't dismiss someone who says their brain feels damaged just because they're high or can't explain what they feel in medical language, since something could really be going wrong in their brain. [DMSO](http://dmso.u-dont-exist.com/) is my first immediate remedy while I ask what the symptoms actually are. I usually know within about a minute whether it is helping. Smelling [humic acid ](http://humicacid.u-dont-exist.com)or tasting a tiny bit can also help a lot, in my experience, but I'm still collecting data from others. I give electrolytes to drink also, because magnesium (and then potassium) can be depleted by some drugs, especially MDMA and iboga, although I don't think just electrolytes would be sufficient to reverse damage already underway.

While the DMSO is working, check for [stroke warning signs](https://www.stroke.org/en/about-stroke/stroke-symptoms) that would trigger a call to medics:

- First, are they unconscious or breathing abnormally?
- Feel whether they're very hot.
- Have them talk and smile. Is their speech slurred, or does one side of their face droop?
- Ask them to hold both arms out in front of them, and see if one drifts down.
- If you can safely hold them as they try, have them stand and walk a few steps to see if they keep their balance.
- Wetting or soiling themselves is another sign to watch for.
- Watch for a [seizure](#§active-seizure), and for vomiting that keeps coming back.
- Ask about a really bad headache or [chest pain](#§chest-pain-and-injury).

When they're too out of it to help you do the checks, or if the brain-damage feeling stays despite sufficient treatment, then you've done what you can do, and it's time to call for help.

I believe, based on [my experience and those of others I've heard from, as well as the books I've read on this](http://dmso.u-dont-exist.com/), that DMSO works better for most acute brain damage than anything else readily available. The clinical evidence is incomplete, but it leans this way. In animals, when given after a stroke, [it shrank the area of the brain that died](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15744247/) (which is a good thing). Some patients whose stroke came from a clot seemed to recover better on DMSO in [a human trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11783757/), and doctors have used it in [a handful of patients](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6462399/) to bring down dangerous pressure inside the skull. But more relevant to the drug overdose issue this article deals with, DMSO calms [NMDA- and AMPA-mediated excitotoxicity](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11421595/) that kills brain cells, at least in rat brain cells in a dish.

If someone is worried about a bleed, I'd probably skip DMSO, but it depends on how severe their issue is. It's not a powerful blood thinner like heparin, unless maybe injected; it's basically a dietary blood thinner. I've never had any issues with bleeding too much from consuming DMSO, and never heard of it either, but if bleeding is already a medical issue I'd want to think about that more. They can feel better on DMSO with a clot or a bleed still there. So if any of the checks above fail, call medics right away, before the window for treating a stroke closes.

1. **[DMSO](#§dmso) immediately. Smell humic acid; if that doesn't help, take a 5mg lick of it.** (See [humic acid evidence here](http://humicacid.u-dont-exist.com).) **Smell the [Metta NMT sniffer](#§metta-sniffer-chill-love)** (described in [loveyhuasca guide](http://loveyhuasca.info)).
2. **Ask what the symptoms actually are while the DMSO is being used.** Do not manufacture reassurance from missing information.
3. **If this is a psychedelic, use [*Ipomoea pes-caprae*](#§beach-morning-glory) to stop the trip.** At this point safety matters more than preserving the experience.
4. **If this is MDMA, move to magnesium chloride and [the broader electrolyte branch](#§mdma-magnesium-alarm) instead.**
5. **Bring in medical help in parallel** for objective neurological signs, dangerous heat, prolonged or repeated seizure, breathing trouble, severe chest pain, collapse, or when basic stability cannot be established.

## Active Seizure

Time the seizure, and while it lasts:

- Move any dangerous objects away from them, especially anything hard or sharp.
- Don't restrain them, since that can cause injuries, but do put pillows where appropriate, especially under the head.
- Loosen any tight clothing.
- Turn them gently onto their side, which helps them breathe.
- Keep everything out of their mouth while they're convulsing or not fully conscious.

Call medics if any of these happen:

- They have a seizure after taking too much, or you don't know how much they took
- Note that psychedelics do reduce the seizure threshold, so if someone is prone to seizures, a normal dose could produce a seizure for them which doesn't indicate brain damage, but if it goes on over 5 minutes, that's too much
- Their breathing gets slow and shallow
- Their lips turn blue or gray
- When you touch their forehead, it's very hot (not just sweaty)
- They injure their head, break a bone, or bleeding won't stop

If they have a rescue medicine for epilepsy seizures with them, give it as the label instructs. These medicines are [benzodiazepines](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9477921/) in nasal spray or rectal gel, or sometimes an intramuscular shot (pills can't be swallowed). After giving it, keep a close eye on their breathing, since a benzodiazepine adds to the risk of it getting slow and shallow, especially if they've also taken other respiratory depressants like opioids or alcohol.

### Seizure: What Else Helps

Some people get a warning before a seizure takes hold, which is called an aura. That's when the things people report can still stop it, depending on what sets their seizures off. If it's flashing or flickering light, cover one eye with a hand and turn away from the light, because closing the eyes doesn't block enough of it. Some people stop a seizure at the warning stage with a strong smell like jasmine, and some train themselves to link one smell with feeling calm and then sniff it when a seizure threatens. When overstimulation is what sets it off, a cool, quiet, dark and comfortable space can help. I'd also try sniffing [humic/fulvic acid powder](#§humic-acid-for-the-brain), although that's just a hunch based on the effects I notice so far.

During the seizure, alongside any rescue medicine, I would use topical magnesium chloride and consider topical [DMSO](#§dmso) with a known-genuine CBD preparation (a lot of CBD is fake or very dilute). Seizures come in all different types, and we have no study on whether CBD could be a rescue remedy for a seizure ([just anecdotes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCxGSrUsB0Q)), even though we do have studies showing long-term effectiveness to prevent them: Purified oral CBD is [approved](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/210365s024s029s030lbl.pdf) for the seizures of Lennox-Gastaut syndrome, Dravet syndrome and tuberous sclerosis complex.

As for CBD/cannabis, people do smoke or vape it at the start of an aura, while they're still awake enough to do it on purpose. There are also people who find THC and/or CBD works as a nasal spray or sublingually, and there's even a medical THC nasal spray marketed for seizure interruption in Brazil by a non-profit patients' association called ABRACE. They [testified in Congress](https://escriba.camara.leg.br/escriba-servicosweb/html/58445) that in more than 30 tests they did with children having seizures, the nasal spray ended those seizures within 30 seconds. You can see it being used on a child in this vid: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bgVi5o_2v_w

I also read on forums some swear it stops the seizure from going any further, and there was [one woman whose EEG](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27763968/) showed fewer seizure discharges on the days she smoked. But a few people on the same forums said THC vapes and concentrates brought their seizures on. And [rats bred to have seizures](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33370468/) had more of them after high-THC smoke.

As for topical CBD, it hasn't been well-studied. There was a short [12-week trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35802375/) done, where they used a CBD skin gel, and although absorption was good, it didn't work any better than placebo to prevent seizures. The study authors suggested the dosing may have been too low, or that adults with focal epilepsy just don't respond as well as people with the syndromes oral CBD is approved for. The seizures in Dravet and Lennox-Gastaut syndrome are mostly generalized. Generalized are the type people usually have with MDMA & psychedelic overdoses, so topical CBD might do better for those than it did in that trial.

As for DMSO, it did reduce seizure activity in [rats with temporal-lobe epilepsy](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23643984/), and we know it has several neuroprotective mechanisms, but it produced the opposite effect at some doses in an [absence-epilepsy model](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21320528/). While a seizure continues, the rescue medicine comes first, and I'd still consider DMSO alongside it, although that's experimental.

## MDMA: Magnesium Alarm

With MDMA, I treat jaw tension as the earliest warning that the body needs more magnesium. I don't have studies to back this up, but everyone I know who has taken enough magnesium chloride to make the jaw tension fully release has avoided the MDMA toxicity symptoms they get otherwise.

A token dose may not be enough to make the jaw release. More than once, I've dissolved **Dead Sea magnesium chloride hexahydrate flakes** in water and taken about **one gram per minute by mouth for around twenty minutes** before mine fully released. Around twenty grams is fine for my gut, and I've only had diarrhea above about forty grams. And magnesium chloride is [less likely to cause diarrhea than several other kinds of magnesium](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10509448/), including the ones people take on purpose as laxatives. I use it mostly because it's what I know works best: it absorbs faster and better than any other form, so you can see how it's helping, usually within a minute.

Magnesium taken before the MDMA peak also makes the peak less euphoric for me, which makes me think the magnesium calms some of the MDMA's stimulation, besides meeting a need for magnesium that's rising fast. I don't mind a little less euphoria, since in my experience a relaxed jaw means a much lower risk of the MDMA toxicity symptoms.

My second wife had severe convulsions after an MDMA overdose. At the hospital, they figured out what caused that: her potassium was found to be critically low, and IV potassium was the only treatment they gave her. That experience is what leads me to treat serious MDMA toxicity as a broader electrolyte emergency, although magnesium is still the first and most useful supplement to give once the jaw tenses, since potassium gets depleted once magnesium runs low.

Once their jaw tenses, that should be their last dose of MDMA, so get them to slow down and cool off. If they drink a lot of plain water, [even a single normal MDMA dose](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39546312/) raises the risk of their sodium dropping too low, so have them sip an electrolyte drink when they're thirsty instead. Plain water is the risk because the drug keeps them from peeing out the extra, so it thins out the salt in their blood. Women are most at risk of this, based on the results we have: at [a rave in the Netherlands](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23476039/), sodium was mildly low in 1 in 4 of the women on ecstasy vs only 3% of the men. [California's poison control](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17084942/) found the same pattern in the MDMA cases it got, with women at four times the odds. The following indicators would justify calling medics: If their confusion or headache worsens, or vomiting won't stop (sodium might be too low by then for an electrolyte drink to fix; while you wait for medics, if they're alert and not vomiting, give them something very salty instead, like strong broth). That has to be a lot of salt, more than a snack gives: [guidelines for hikers who drank too much water](https://www.aafp.org/pubs/afp/issues/2021/0215/p252.html) use three or four bouillon cubes in half a cup of water. If their muscles keep getting weaker or their heart palpitations keep getting stronger, that could be from dangerously low potassium, like my second wife had. A [seizure](#§active-seizure) or collapse can result from low sodium, magnesium or potassium, and can also come from overheating or MDMA toxicity.

That's why I'd tell everyone not to dance on MDMA at all, however good the music is, since dancing makes the heat and the sweat that drain their salt. And really, what's the point of MDMA if someone wants to dance? For that they can take some San Pedro cactus or mescaline, which is way way safer, and still very therapeutic. Real MDMA, the kind that's therapeutic and helps people access very early childhood trauma, possibly better than San Pedro can in some cases, isn't the kind people want to dance on, which is why you don't see people in the MAPS trials of MDMA therapy saying "I NEED TO DANCE!" No, they're sitting there doing the therapy, and even if the science isn't clear on this yet, I and everyone I know are totally clear about what the real thing feels like. It's not like "I don't feel like dancing", it's like "I can barely move", couch lock. And in the same exact setting, the pills have gone both ways on me: some had me wanting to dance, and some left me in that couch lock. So if a pill makes someone feel like dancing, I'm highly suspicious that it contains amphetamines, or that it isn't real MDMA at all, maybe 6-APB, a similar drug. That might also be why women get low sodium far more often than men outside the lab, where what people take as "MDMA" is ecstasy, while a [lab study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4923534/) that gave people pure MDMA and water found no difference between them. Then again, the lab dosed everyone by their weight, and at a festival a small woman takes the same pill as a big guy, so she's getting a lot more for her size.

**People with impaired kidneys do need to be more cautious with high, rapid magnesium dosing, because they may not clear excess magnesium out well. But they should be far more worried about taking the MDMA** which depletes it (and shifts other electrolytes dangerously), since MDMA can [injure the kidneys](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27917269/) even more, not to mention potential muscle breakdown and overheating. However, if they have already taken the MDMA, I'm not saying they should sit there and let the jaw tension get worse. I just wouldn't tell them to copy my gram-per-minute magnesium chloride dosing approach blindly, since that's a higher-risk situation where they should be taken to a hospital ASAP.

## Chest Pain and Injury

[DMSO](#§dmso) is also my first remedy for many injuries and some chest pain. After I overused erythritol, I developed angina that lasted for days. I took DMSO, the pain disappeared within about a minute, and it never returned.

So far, the heart research on DMSO has only been in animals and lab dishes, and only as a prophylactic (preventative treatment). When they [gave it to rats for three days before a heart attack](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19904216/), those rats lost about three quarters less heart muscle than the rats who didn't get DMSO. Most heart attacks come from a clot blocking an artery, so it's a good sign that DMSO also [slowed clotting, in mice](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17000906/) and [in human platelets](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20035720/) (the blood cells that start clots) in a dish.

For steady chest pain that's mild, I'd just use DMSO + magnesium chloride on the chest, as long as they're fully alert. For the following more serious signs (or if unsure), I'd also call medics:

- The pain is worsening.
- Hard movement (like dancing or climbing) brought it on.
- It spreads from the chest into an arm or the jaw.
- Shortness of breath or sweating shows up also.
- Any of the stroke signs from the [brain checks](#§my-brain-feels-damaged) above show up, like slurred speech or a drooping face.
- They feel faint, or they collapse.

Keep in mind that DMSO can also block pain signals, so even if the pain fades, it could still be worth having a medical checkup later.

## Swallowed Too Much?

"Overdose" is too vague to choose a remedy. Figure out how much of what was swallowed and how long ago, then see what the body is doing: are they suffering from [slow and shallow breathing](#§call-medics-for) (respiratory depression, from opioids, benzos and alcohol)? Or is it rapid breathing, heart rate, and agitation, signs of stimulant toxicity? Do you see symptoms of [serotonin toxicity](#§how-to-check-for-ss), like seizure, dangerous overheating, tremor? Do they have vomiting or diarrhea beyond what they can handle? Some of these symptoms are also normal, like vomiting and diarrhea, which are helpful in undoing an overdose, up to a point. Slow breathing is normal with iboga, but if cardiac irregularities come along with it, that's an extra danger sign.

[Activated charcoal](#§activated-charcoal) is safe and can help with many drug overdoses (and toxins in the gut). It only helps with what binds to it, which leaves out alcohol and lithium among other things, and it can also adsorb medication they need. [The usual advice](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6620762/) says it's only useful within an hour of the drug overdose, but that's not true for many plant medicines which can take hours to digest. Obviously don't give anything by mouth to someone who can't swallow, as they could aspirate it into their lungs and die. And don't give any fine powders to anyone directly, mix in water first to prevent choking.

If you're unsure about the interactions between the drugs taken, it could be helpful to check with AI & Poison Control. They might have important info, especially if the drugs are well-known. They can also help you with standard first aid instructions. But I wouldn't ask Poison Control about charcoal. Someone I know did, and came away very confused after being told [they aren't allowed to tell anyone to take it](https://www.poison.org/articles/2017-jun/activated-charcoal).

## Serotonin Syndrome (SS)?

Serotonin syndrome can look like some of the emergencies above, since it can also bring on a seizure or dangerous overheating. But the rescue measures in those other branches do not necessarily treat it, so check for it too whenever they've had something that raises serotonin. That includes ayahuasca and other MAOI brews (or any other MAOI), MDMA, DXM cough syrup and many antidepressants, as well as the antibiotic linezolid, the painkiller tramadol and some other opioids. Too much of any one of them can bring on serotonin syndrome, and so can a medication that was just started or had its dose raised, but I'd suspect it most when two of them were combined.

### How to Check for SS

To diagnose serotonin syndrome, we can use the Hunter criteria, the same checklist clinicians use. Using Hunter, one or more of these patterns makes the diagnosis more likely:

- Spontaneous clonus: involuntary rhythmic muscle spasms, with no stimulation needed to set it off, would be one full pattern
- Inducible clonus, spasms set off by stretching/flexing a muscle, plus agitation or sweating would be counted as one pattern altogether.
- Ocular clonus (rapid involuntary eye movements) plus agitation or sweating, another pattern together.
- Another is tremor (shaking) along with hyperreflexia (abnormally brisk reflexes).
- Lastly, Hypertonia (muscular rigidity) with fever above 38°C (100.4°F), as well as ocular or inducible clonus.

So if you see any of these patterns, call medics.

### Act Now on SS

1. Serotonin syndrome comes from too much serotonin, so **I'd stop anything suspected of adding more** (serotonergic substances). So no more MDMA or other stimulants, of course, but also no DXM cough syrup, the painkiller tramadol or the antibiotic linezolid. It doesn't matter if they are prescribed an antidepressant (an SSRI like Prozac, or an SNRI or MAOI), it's too risky right now. If they drank ayahuasca or another harmala brew, like Syrian rue, no more of that either, since those work the way MAOIs do. And check whether they take supplements people don't think of as drugs, like 5-HTP, St John's wort or SAM-e.
2. **If the person is fully alert and able to swallow, I would give cyproheptadine early**, since it blocks serotonin receptors. So far, the evidence for this med has only been [case reports](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9696181/), and [a recent review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39791184/) found them insufficient to show that it works. I wouldn't wait to see whether it does before doing the other steps. And most people won't have it at home anyway, since it needs a prescription in the US, though a telehealth doctor might write one quickly, and only some countries sell it without one. If you can get it, [the guidelines](https://www.mdpoison.com/media/SOP/mdpoisoncom/healthcareprofessionals/antidote-facts/Cyproheptadine%20Antidote%20Facts.pdf) say to give an adult 12mg to start, then 2mg every 2 hours if symptoms persist, up to 32mg per 24 hours. And tell medics how much you gave.
3. **When you call medics, ask for an ambulance**, since the person can get worse fast, with seizures or dangerous overheating. It may seem faster to drive them to the ER yourself, but don't: paramedics can start treating them right there, and if they have a seizure in your car, you can't help them and drive at the same time. And while you wait, call poison control if you don't know what was mixed, as they might be able to help you work that out, even if they can't treat serotonin syndrome.
4. Since bodily overheating is the most dangerous part of serotonin syndrome, you should address their twitching and tense muscles that are making a lot of heat, while you wait for the ambulance. So **I'd get them somewhere cool**, take off their extra clothes, and set a fan blowing over cool damp cloths on their skin. If they still feel hot, put ice packs wrapped in cloth where they can absorb the most heat: at the neck, armpits and groin. Although a cold bath would cool them faster, they could slip under and breathe in water if they're confused, seizing, or very weak, so it's not advisable. And while you might think fever reducers like Tylenol or ibuprofen could work by reducing body heat in general, they don't work on the heat generated by muscles.
5. Since they're already overstimulated, it helps to clear out the crowd and stop any arguing. While it's natural for people who are overstimulated to start pacing or exercising to "burn it off," the problem is that creates more body heat, and it raises the risk of their muscles breaking down into the blood, which hurts the kidneys (rhabdomyolysis). But if they feel a strong urge to keep moving (akathisia), I'd have them do some tapping (the Emotional Freedom Technique, fingers tapping the head), which can soothe that urge without the over-activity risk.
6. Overstimulating also dehydrates them, but we can't give fluids unless they're fully alert, able to swallow and not vomiting, and then just small sips of an electrolyte drink. If they still show the signs of low electrolytes from [MDMA: Magnesium Alarm](#§mdma-magnesium-alarm) above, they may need **more salt, potassium and magnesium than a normal electrolyte drink has**. Salty potato chips give the salt and the potassium with nothing to cook, so I'd let them eat as many as they want. That's the only way I'd give potassium, since too much pure potassium is dangerous for anyone. Their potassium could be running high as well as low, though: MDMA can drain it, but muscles breaking down into the blood push it up. So if they start vomiting or get diarrhea, I'd stop the chips, since too much potassium brings those on and the chips have to stop either way while they're being sick. Magnesium also runs low in many people with serotonin syndrome ([a nursing review](https://doi.org/10.1097/01.NME.0000432871.59839.f3)). Magnesium chloride tastes too bad for most people to drink, but the skin absorbs it, so I'd stir a handful of magnesium chloride flakes into a little water and rub the mix on their skin. If you don't have the flakes, Epsom salt, which is magnesium sulfate rather than a kind of table salt, works the same way, just less well. If they can stand the taste, they can also take magnesium chloride by mouth, especially with MDMA-related jaw tension or twitching (the amounts are in [MDMA: Magnesium Alarm](#§mdma-magnesium-alarm), above).
7. The ER doctors will want to know exactly what they took, so before the ambulance comes, **I'd gather their pill bottles and packages**. I'd also jot down how much of each they took and when, street drugs and supplements included. And if they have a seizure before the ambulance gets there, I'd do the first aid in [Active Seizure](#§active-seizure) above, and keep track of how their breathing is doing afterward, as that's one of the main indicators of how dangerous the seizure is, according to [the CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/epilepsy/first-aid-for-seizures/index.html).
8. Since a seizure is one of the main dangers while you wait, I'd use [DMSO](#§dmso), which seems to help prevent them, going by people's reports rather than studies: at any sign of an overdose, have them smell it or rub a little on a clean patch of skin (with 30% water if the skin itches), then use a little more at a time, since some people react more strongly than others. You might also think of [niacin](#§niacin-flushes-trips-out), which this guide uses for ending a trip, but it's unsafe with serotonin syndrome, since at that dose it can cause flushing and a drop in blood pressure ([NIH](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Niacin-Consumer/)). The other remedy I'd keep for a last resort, if nothing else is helping and no medics are coming, is [Syrian rue oil](#§syrian-rue-oil-gentler), since it makes trips, ayahuasca included, much less intense. The oil isn't the Syrian rue brew from step 1, which blocks MAO and is dangerous with MDMA for that reason, but nobody has studied the oil with MDMA either.

# My Acute Rescue Remedies

This hierarchy is for acute situations, including emergencies--not merely uncomfortable trips.

In general it's good to try all these things in very low, increasing doses before they are needed in crisis.

Sometimes the mind isn't in a metaphysical crisis. The body feels awful and the altered mind writes cosmic horror to explain it. Nausea, heat, exhaustion, dehydration, overstimulation, and sleep loss all get read as spiritual signs very quickly.

A horrible thought isn't automatically a revelation. The body may need quiet, water, cooling, warmth, rest, or a calmer room. The body is part of the spiritual field.

The hierarchy appears before the mystical sections because love doesn't require us to ignore the body.

If a trip comes with diarrhea or nausea, a toxic heaviness or a dirty body-feeling, or jaw tension, the mind may feel like it's going crazy because the gut or the brain is going crazy. The same goes for loss of bodily control (someone wetting or soiling themselves, or not being able to sit up, stand, walk or talk) and for neurological disorganization, my catch-all for that loss of control plus a whole lot more. Losing control of the body like that is on [the list to call medics for](#§call-medics-for), and [the brain checks](#§my-brain-feels-damaged) further up show what else to look for.

Those trips are what the remedies below are for, and the shadow work further down is for a mainly emotional crisis. If you're in mental hell and gut hell at the same time, address the gut first with [the gut tools](#§gut-toxin-and-die-off-tools). You don't need to try to tell which is from what. A gut-driven mental hell is often mistaken as shadow work.

## Gut-Toxin & Die-Off Tools

The gut tools are the first things I would use, even preventively on the come-up (while the drug is still kicking in), because I want to address possible toxins before I put the distress down to something more mysterious, like a spiritual sign.

After all, nausea and diarrhea are common on psychedelics, and people generally don't feel good mentally during the nausea and gut pain parts of the come-up. Diarrhea can be a flushing out of toxins, and usually there's a lot of relief once everything starts coming out in the toilet. But severe diarrhea can flush out too much fluid, and being badly dried out is on [the list to call medics for](#§call-medics-for). Still, you shouldn't have a lot of gut discomfort if the toxins in the gut get bound, which is what the tools below do, and dosing lower on the psychedelic helps too.

### [ZeoCharge](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/p/here-is-why-everyone-should-use-zeocharge) & Ferment

Zeolite and the [ZHoly3O](http://zholy3o.u-dont-exist.com/) ferment are often my first thoughts when the trip feels like a sick stomach, with nausea or diarrhea and that toxic heaviness or dirty body-feeling, or like mental hell driven by gut hell. I'd think of them for parasite die-off too.

The ZHoly3O ferment contains more than just fermented beet juice, but you can ferment your own beet juice or even use other ferments. I found fermented beet made with apple vinegar and salt was best so far, though kimchi and kombucha also helped. Most yogurts weren't fermented enough to work, and apple vinegar by itself only helped a little. Smelling the ferment helps a lot, and drinking a spoonful can also help, although it may cause itching in somebody who breaks down histamine poorly, which then requires [activated charcoal](#§activated-charcoal) to reduce gut histamine.

Most sober people don't feel much from zeolite, but when the trip is toxic or the gut is driving it, the effect is easy to feel. I put a spoonful of zeolite into a cup of water or juice and sip slowly (some people need a teaspoon, others a tablespoon). Rubbing zeolite mixed in water on my body, or just holding its jar in my hand, helps me too.

In my experiments, zeolite must have its pores cleaned correctly, or else it's useless. I use powdered zeolite rather than nano-liquid zeolite, because there's almost nothing in that liquid, and it's generally not cleaned either. Only one brand of zeolite I tried actually worked without needing to be ozonated, although that brand didn't have the best lab results for metals.

For my ozonated zeolite, I mix the powder into water and use an ozone-water purifier in a glass vessel because ozone destroys plastic. I mean ozonating water, never inhaling ozone gas. With a machine producing about two grams of ozone per hour, ten minutes produced a working zeolite for me, however much zeolite was in the water. Less time didn't work as well, and more time didn't noticeably improve the zeolite.

Zeolite binds metals, which is part of why it is used for heavy-metal removal, so I don't put it into a metal thing or use a metal spoon. I use glass for ozonating and storing it whenever I can.

### Activated Charcoal

I don't treat charcoal and zeolite as interchangeable, because drugs bind to activated charcoal as well as gut toxins, so it can flatten or reduce a trip.

So if someone took too much, or the gut itself is toxic, I give charcoal. If the trip is going fine and only the gut feels bad, I give [zeolite](#§zeocharge-and-ferment), which leaves the trip alone.

Medicines bind to charcoal too, so I'd think twice before giving it to someone who depends on one, and I'd tell any medics who come that charcoal was taken.

### [**DMSO**](http://dmso.u-dont-exist.com/)

DMSO is another stabilizer I use when a trip becomes too intense and I don't want to abort it. It is also my primary emergency neuroprotectant when the problem feels like acute neurological damage, altered consciousness, loss of bodily control, or severe drug-related brain dysfunction.

In my experience, DMSO makes me alert, smarter, happier, healthier, and more relaxed, and pretty much everyone I've heard from on forums and in real life says the same thing. It also is one of the only things that can instantly turn a bad psychedelic trip good. When I nebulize it, within a minute, my brain feels crystal clean and super blissed out (much more bliss than other routes).

It overlaps with [aged ozonated-charcoal water](#§shungite-holy-water), but the two don't always work equally. Sometimes DMSO works better, and sometimes the water wins. I don't yet know why, but you can also just use both rather than try to figure out which for when. Ozonated-charcoal water has what I consider a perfect safety profile, and DMSO is a close second.

One famous [1967 study by Ramirez & Luza](https://nyaspubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/epdf/10.1111/j.1749-6632.1967.tb34937.x) found it more effective than the best antipsychotic drugs at the time, slashing mental patients' psych ward stays in half, and that included patients with severe anxiety, though it hasn't been tested (that I know of, yet) for PTSD. It's routinely used in humans who receive stem cell transplants, as it is the best cell preservative that we've found so far. There is evidence ([Kortleve, et al, 2023](https://assets-eu.researchsquare.com/files/rs-2624641/v1/45119f29-3464-4680-8468-490477ae59ae.pdf?c=1688109028)) that using too much DMSO (10% vs 5%) in stem cell transfusions increases mild side effects in adult humans.

Children's brains may be at risk from DMSO, though: it killed brain cells in mice up to a month old, even at a low dose, and in a dish of rat neurons ([Hanslick, et al, 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19100327/)). Translating mouse research to humans isn't straightforward.

**Side effects:**

- **Astringency:** 100% DMSO usually irritates skin on the upper half of the body. When the skin itches, dilute the DMSO with water or real aloe juice without added chemicals. Leaving 100% DMSO on the skin can crenate (shrivel) it, and the skin may take a week to look plump again. DMSO can also burn dry mouth tissue, so wet your mouth first.
- **Nausea:** when drinking DMSO, dilute it in water, juice, or milk and sip gradually. Sensitivity to DMSO varies, so start with less than you planned.
- **Reduced sleep:** DMSO reduces how long I sleep when I take too much, take it too late, or use it over too many consecutive days. It builds up past a threshold that seems to differ between people, so it's best taken in the morning, and reduce the dose if you notice you're sleeping less. [In rats, higher doses of DMSO made sleep less deep](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378427405000810). Some people sleep better with DMSO because it reduces whatever symptom had been keeping them awake.
- **Body odor:** above a certain dose you may smell like sulfur. I don't smell when I put it into milk.

**Interactions:**

- Don't put it on dirty skin; it can carry chemicals inward.
- Don't take it with drugs that should not absorb rapidly: DMSO can strengthen other medicines taken with it. With harmalas, don't put the harmala in the DMSO or take them at exactly the same time; taking them a bit apart is totally fine, and the point is to take DMSO if you've already absorbed the harmalas and the aya is too crazy.
- Don't combine it with cholinergic drugs because DMSO is also cholinergic, although I haven't found anecdotes of this causing a real problem.

**Contraindications:**

- With seizures it can go either way: in animal studies it calmed one kind and, at some doses, made another kind worse (more on that in [Active Seizure](#§active-seizure)).
- It may worsen a cluster headache, even though rubbing DMSO--especially with magnesium chloride--on the head can alleviate tension headaches.
- It may release histamine in some people. Use caution with MCAS.

### Syrian Rue Oil: Gentler

**[Tried by Me and Friends]**

Syrian rue (*Peganum harmala*) can be mixed into warm/hot oil (like coconut oil), just enough to cover it, then squeezed out thru cheese cloth. I have a friend with schizophrenia who would use about 1-2mL of this oil rectally when he was in psychosis, which helped at least as much as niacin and DMSO, equally effective as Geodon, an antipsychotic his doctors prescribed which makes him feel like a zombie.

Another option is vaping the whole Syrian rue seeds, as it seems that these oil soluble harmalas are concentrated on the outside of the seeds. However, if vaping the whole seeds, I would not assume there's no MAOI effect (even though it still seems to reduce DMT effects) since depending maybe on temperature and time, you might also vaporize the harmine and harmaline.

### Niacin "Flushes" Trips Out

Flushing niacin has effectively aborted trips for me and friends, although I dislike it compared with [beach morning glory](#§beach-morning-glory).

I include it because most people don't have access to *Ipomoea pes-caprae*, although I supply it. Niacin has been used for decades by orthomolecular doctors for psychotic conditions and seems to act both like a natural benzodiazepine and an antipsychotic. The clinical literature is discussed in *Niacin: The Real Story* by Abram Hoffer, M.D.

**Side effects:**

- **Niacin flush:** itchy sunburn-like heat. The flush is normal, although some people find it intolerable and others enjoy it.
- Heartburn in sensitive stomachs or after too much at once.
- Drowsiness, usually with terrible sleep quality.
- [Sustained-release niacin](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8309029/), or repeated doses through the day, can create a real risk of liver damage, especially with liver problems, alcohol, or medications.

Other forms of vitamin B3 seem less reliable than niacin for relaxation. Niacinamide relaxes some people, but it can make others hyperfocused, depending on the state the person is in. Avoid sustained-release niacin and the useless "no-flush" kind (inositol hexanicotinate).

## The First Five Minutes

![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/944bef30-ca59-415b-a2e8-809a29ac2ba6_1122x1402.png)

Anything that could let someone hurt themselves in the next five minutes takes priority, so get the car keys out of reach and turn the stove off, and move them away from roads and balconies, or pools of water for that matter. Letting them move around is fine, and pacing can help, but keep them from taking more of any substance or getting into confrontations. And if they want to leave, have someone go with them. Put any weapons away and keep the person away from crowds and strangers. If they want to post their big revelation about the universe, or message an ex, take the phone.

They're feeling overwhelmed, so dim the lights, and clear out the extra people around them.

Sometimes it can be helpful to remind them where they are and what's happening, since a trip can make them lose track of the fact that they took a substance which has temporary effects.

If a practice like meditation or fasting brought this on, have them stop it now, since doing more of it only makes this stronger. There's no drug wearing off here, so it could be over soon or take a while.

As the person helping, you want to play the role of the calm, caring anchor to the safe present moment, not the witch doctor guru who knows magic spells. So don't force eye contact, touch, music, prayer, chanting on them, just offer gently if it might help. After they've slept, they may wish to talk more about their experience.

But if they want to talk about it right now, don't try to win an argument with their visions, the entities they spoke to, the memories that surfaced, or whatever cosmic conclusions they reached ten minutes ago... But don't let silence validate anything harmful either, like a plan that ends with somebody getting hurt.

Sometimes people get stuck in a panic loop, where they make interpretations to try to solve a feeling of panic, but then those create another kind of panic. It's helpful to remind them that sometimes feelings just need to be felt, so they can move and release, and then thinking can be more clear afterwards.

![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/633de218-0966-491c-aac4-6b4a366195ce_1122x1402.png)

If they're being attacked by a demon, let your care help them connect to love, which cancels fear. Hopefully they've been practicing that prior. Don't encourage them to believe they are crazy, impure, weak or broken. While many interpretations are possible, a therapeutic one would not be that they're being punished or failing the medicine.

I actually get something like this regularly when I take a high dose of something (or meditate too much), where I feel like I'm at risk of ruining my life if I don't stay away from sex and money. And there's a voice that really commands me to leave both of those completely (which led to 3 weeks of wandering in Malaysia & Cambodia without money once). What actually helped me in one case when receiving this stern order from a higher power, was to ask it to clarify. I said, "I agree, that makes sense as a Buddhist, and I'd like to leave those behind at some point, especially money. But what's the best timeline to benefit all involved? Some people are dependent on me, I have a son, and so forth." The voice didn't respond, it just went away. So I took it more like a long-term suggestion than the urgency I assumed it needed based on how loud it was at first.

## Grounding Menu

Grounding is love becoming practical. Choose one or two options rather than all of them.

A useful order is **room → body → human → time → meaning**. Most people begin with meaning because the experience feels cosmic.

![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/296952b0-1468-44da-9878-093d82942785_1122x1402.png)

**Body**

- Put one hand on the heart and one on the solar plexus.

**Smell**

- Scratch & sniff a lime peel, or smell an authentic calming essential oil, the Metta NMT sniffer or humic acid powder (see [Too Intense? Stabilizers](#§too-intense-stabilizers)).

**Breath**

- Exhale longer than you inhale for ten breaths.
- Or try Buteyko reduced breathing: breathe softly and slowly through the nose, taking in slightly less air than desired until there is faint air hunger.
- Systema continuous partial breathing in coordination with relaxed movement and muscular detensioning is excellent for entering a flow state. It helps to watch [a video](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Nar2-nvomAk) on how to do it prior though, as it's a bit tricky to understand compared to simple reduced breathing.
- Never force breath-holds while intoxicated or panicked. Stop for increased fear, dizziness, chest pain, or medical concern.
- Whisper: "This is a wave. I can love the part of me that feels it."

**Music**

- Use familiar music when possible. I wouldn't force a predesigned emotional arc or an unfamiliar therapeutic playlist on somebody who dislikes it.
- Try a steady drum rhythm for stabilization or grounding; try airy harp-like or meditative music when the person wants something gentler and spiritually uplifting.
- Soothing meditative music, like that of Chantress Seba and others on my [music page](http://music.u-dont-exist.com/), can be very helpful.

**Human**

- Don't process with the most reactive person in your life.

**Time**

- Remember that these states usually rise, peak, and change.
- Put every big decision into tomorrow's notebook.

**Ordinary world**

- Eat simple healthy food, such as fruit, when you can.
- Walk slowly with a clear-headed person in a safe environment.
- Going outside can help too, if it's somewhere quiet with nothing high to fall from, away from roads and water.
- Avoid strenuous exercise if you're overheating or have chest pain, and hold off too if you feel faint or badly confused. Skip exercise too if you've taken a stimulant or an unknown mixture, or have any other medical concern.

## Get One Human Anchor

Being alone with a frightened or over-certain mind can make a bad trip worse. So can a crowd of people telling them what it all means, or panicking right along with them.

So have **one calm person be the anchor** and take the lead. One person can only handle so much, though. If fatigue or logistics start getting to be too much for the anchor, or keeping everyone from getting hurt does, bring in **one more calm adult or a crisis professional** to back them up, and get that help right away if there's any risk of violence or self-harm.

![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/2b8889eb-85e8-466f-99af-f71e48caeb61_1122x1402.png)

**Script for the person in the altered state:**

> I'm in an intense psychedelic or altered state and I need grounding. I don't need you to solve my life. Please stay with me, speak slowly, remind me where I am, and help me not make any big decisions until tomorrow.

While you wait, put one hand on your heart and the other on your solar plexus just under your chest, and say to yourself: "This feeling is allowed to move through. I can love the part of me that feels it."

## Helping? Anchor, Not Guru

The Zendo Project, a harm-reduction group that offers peer support at festivals and other events to people having a hard time in an altered state, sums up its approach in four principles: creating a safe space, sitting with the person rather than guiding them, talking them through it rather than down from it, and remembering that difficult isn't necessarily bad.

Crying, grieving, remembering, or touching a genuine wound calls for loving awareness. Interrogation, analysis every thirty seconds, and "bro, you're purging ancestral karma" while somebody white-knuckles the carpet are generally less helpful.

Stay present. If it helps, you can say to yourself: *This feeling is allowed to move through. I don't have to become it. I can love the part of me that feels it.* Or simply: *Can I send love to the one who's suffering?*

### What to Do and Say

- Ask simple questions: "Would less light help?" "Music or quiet?" "Closer or farther away?" "Water?" (in small sips) "Need the bathroom?"
- Set behavioral boundaries without humiliation: "I won't help anybody hurt themselves or another person. We are keeping every body safe."

### When Talk Turns to Harm

When somebody says, "I am God, so I can kill," take it as a sign they could really hurt someone. To lead them back to not hurting anyone, start from what they believe, like the script below does: "Yes, I know you are God. God is in everyone, so every body is sacred." If instead you argue, that can make them dig in to prove they're God, so don't tell them "No you're not" or "That's delusional." And the other failing alternative, just nodding along, might let them think you're okay with them hurting someone.

Other useful lines: "Maybe you are touching something divine. Divine doesn't need a violent test." "Tonight the commandment is no harm, no proof, no mission, sleep first."

When talk of harm continues, shift from philosophy to safety. Create distance from potential targets and keep your own exit. Don't block, crowd, threaten, mock, or physically wrestle away a weapon. Quietly remove means only when that can be done without confrontation or turning yourself into the target.

Be brief and specific with emergency services: "Someone is in an altered-state or mental-health crisis, has threatened to harm others, may have access to [means if known], and needs a medical or mental-health crisis response with de-escalation support. Please send crisis-trained responders or EMS if available."

## No Rash Actions

An intense altered state can make everything feel final, cosmic, and urgent, and it might feel like you have to act right now. But more likely the urgency is just part of the state, and acting on it isn't safe.

**Put yourself on an action lock: nothing life-changing for 24–72 hours.**

Until you have slept and eaten, maybe don't drive, travel, break up, make up, quit your job, confess everything, confront or accuse that list of people you've been meaning to... Think twice before sending whatever ridiculous message you're planning on to Judge Amos, posting that revelation you'll forget tomorrow, giving away your BMW, buying a new BMW. After you decide that you are permanently enlightened or permanently damaged, and you explained the whole universe to your parents in 42 seconds, you can still change your mind the next day.

**Write it down, get some sleep, and go back over it with one grounded person once your head is clear.**

Treat trip content first as **experience-content**, rather than fact-content. Because you saw it, it might feel like you have to act on it, and because it felt cosmic, obeying it might seem wise. But more likely you had an experience, and it can wait until the state has passed.

**No revelation makes murder, assault, coercion, sexual boundary-crossing, reckless driving, or self-harm safe tonight.** Anything requiring violated consent or harm belongs in the notebook until you can review it with a clear head.

## Dealing With Sorrow

I once found a pamphlet helpful on sorrow, although it was too "ultimate" and Advaita for my taste. I now think focusing on love is more helpful than focusing on missing somebody or something.

> *Missing hurts. [Sending love](http://love.u-dont-exist.com/) feels good.*

This sounds almost embarrassingly obvious, but it took me months of daily loveyhuasca after my ex-wife Bee left to understand it.

Missing someone is a contraction. There's hunger and ache in it, it keeps circling back to you, and there's the painful fantasy that loving them means needing them to come back. Sending love is different. Sending love is pleasure in itself, even when the person is gone. Love can bless without grabbing.

The difference is enormous.

The mistake people often make in love states is simply enjoying the love--or sending heart emojis to everybody. That can feel like the whole point, and it's nice.

But the deepest utility is sending love to the places where love is missing.

- Send love to the person you miss without demanding their return.
- Send love to the part of you still waiting for them.
- Send love to the inner child who believed somebody else had to rescue him.
- Send love to the ashamed, angry, lonely, terrified, and exiled parts.

## Hand on Heart, Gratitude

A hand on the heart can change the direction of a trip, especially when the problem is emotional contraction, grief, fear, or loneliness. There's no need to manufacture loving thoughts, you're just giving the body one simple signal: love is here.

Put one hand on the heart and the other on the solar plexus just below it, sending love to the inner child. Sometimes that shifts the whole field. Once love reaches the inner child, send it toward all beings suffering now. Recognizing that you are not the only one suffering can open awareness in a liberating way, with real heart magic.

Gratitude comes like icing on the cake. Being thankful for every lesson, difficult experience, reprieve, heart opening, and moment of compassion can create a deep connection with the sacred. Never force gratitude. Let it bubble up gently.

### Ask Before You Touch

Ask before you touch someone who's tripping, even to put your hand on their heart: **"Would respectful touch help, or would you rather have space?"** A hug can be too much when they're already overwhelmed. ## Demons, Jinn, and Entities

I have faced demons and jinn during psychedelic and exorcism-type situations. The most important thing I discovered was that fighting them did not help. Sending love did.

In one especially dramatic situation involving another person, the entity seemed to attack both of us psychically. I sent it love, and within a few seconds it left. The other person could not do that immediately. I had to show her, and it took hours of practice before it worked because she had never gone deeply into "Love Thy Enemy."

I don't have to settle whether these things are objectively real or internal formations because the response is mostly the same.

Hatred, fear, shame, pride, and rage make hooks. Love removes hooks.

The common mistake is fighting the terrifying thing with the energy it already consumes. Panic fights panic, hatred fights hatred, pride fights pride, and suddenly a small war is running inside consciousness.

Love is a better move, although it is difficult advice. Almost nobody can authentically love what terrifies them while they are terrified. So train before you need it, sending love to a frightening image, to someone you hate (without approving of what they did), to your shame and your body, to sickness and aging and fear and death, and to the inner child. Practice being the space rather than the storm.

It might look passive, but this is how you become unhookable. When you meet a demon with love, the demon has nothing to hook into, since its hooks catch on hatred, fear, pride and grandiosity, not on love.

If someone's too overwhelmed to send love, because they're sick or too scared to think straight, help them get steady first. Turn the lights down, and get them whatever help they need. Once they can think straight again, you can teach them to send the demon love.

## Abuse Memories Surface?

Psychedelics and intense practices can bring up true trauma, symbolic material, distorted fragments, fears, fantasies, archetypes, past-life narratives, and mixtures of all of them. Don't dismiss what arises. Don't treat the certainty you feel in that state as evidence either.

**Don't confront tonight**

When you see or remember abuse, betrayal, murder, past lives, hidden crimes, or cosmic plots, write it down and get safe. Don't confront anybody while intoxicated or destabilized. Return to it after sleep with a trauma-informed therapist, integration specialist, or grounded person you trust.

The experience may matter deeply. It could be true psychologically, or symbolically, or literally, or only partly true, or false in the form it first took. You don't have to decide tonight.

## Shadow Thoughts

When we try to purify the mind, something natural and frightening may happen: the opposite becomes more visible, almost like Newton's Third Law--every action arrives with an equal and opposite reaction.

Shadow thoughts and feelings are not inherently a problem. They co-arise naturally and do not need to be attached to, like shadows cast by objects in sunlight.

I am not repeating the simplistic Hermetic claim that opposites are required to understand anything. A hated baby does **not** feel love, and a loved baby does **not** feel hate. In the subconscious mind, however, concepts often do arise as pairs.

Overly focusing on the "light" or positive side of things can cause the subconscious to strategically highlight the darker stuff. That might be because you're trying to ignore it, and the subconscious is wise enough to know that ignoring problems only makes them worse, so it brings them up (often in dreams, or trips).

But over-focusing the mind for any reason can also create pressure. When the mind is stressed, it seeks an exit from the pressure, often in the opposite direction from what's focusing it. An intensive meditation retreat, or a weighty obligation, or a large psychedelic dose could all be putting that focusing pressure on and leading to dark stuff coming into focus as a misunderstood signal to ease up.

Shadow thoughts also serve to show us the danger with *identifying* with *any* contents of the mind. Whatever comes through the mind should be seen like water flowing over rocks. In the first stage of working with the mind, we re-identify as the gold-panners, sifting through the infinite possibilities, and making use of the best, truest, loveliest, most skillful thoughts and feelings, without taking those to be ourself. We don't need to suppress the shadow thoughts, confess them, or use them to diagnose ourselves. Simply notice: "A thought or feeling is passing through. As heavy as it feels to hold or block it, that is as liberating as it feels to let it go."

So relax, unwind, unbind, and unweave the identity illusion, mind, and unified experience. My pamphlet on already-perfect goes further into this.

## (Re)defining "Bad Trip"

A bad trip is one of those terms thrown around very loosely in the psychedelic space. It can refer to various things which may be intertwined.

When too much hits you at once, your nervous system can become flooded, and nothing makes sense, even as the brain still tries to derive meanings that might create unhelpful feedback loops. Underlying those interpretations are factors like the dose of what drug(s) you took and how your body handles them, or how hard you pushed a practice. Old painful memories could have been triggered, or you may have gone in with a fearful or unloving mindset, or the setting or people around you could have fed into it. Be kind to yourself or to whoever's going through it, because blaming anyone only makes it heavier.

Some say there's no such thing as a bad trip, because it's all a doorway to shadow work. But if you had no intention of doing shadow work today, you might feel unprepared and bewildered. And even though every difficult experience can be a learning or healing opportunity, it still makes sense to first reduce the trauma the trip itself is causing, so we can address what was already there underneath. It's like if someone throws a pile of garbage on some spot on the floor we forgot to clean... well, thanks for showing me that spot, but now I have to clean up the new mess first.

And we could practice to see that spot on the floor without such a crude pointer. Samsara, the cycle of existence, already offers no shortage of difficult experiences to process. For example, sometimes focusing on what we want shows us what's blocking it:

## Spiritual ≠ "Ass-Kicking"

[The triage above](#§when-to-get-outside-help) is meant to make trips useful. Some people believe they deserve suffering because of karma or self-hatred. Suffering by itself fixes nobody. Suffering that yields insight requires enough clarity of mind and heart to learn from it.

This is one side of the difference between [spiritual bypassing](http://goenka.u-dont-exist.com) and [spiritual practice](http://nibbana.u-dont-exist.com):

Bypassing reaches for an ultimate view--"we are all One," "I don't exist," "this is all karma," "everything is Love"--while ignoring the person shaking in the corner.

Practice says love is here, so we make the room safer, call for help when needed, stop pretending, and hold the experience without becoming it.

That is tripsitting, shadow work, and the path becoming practical.

## The Feel-Good Bypass

A well-rounded trip should offer practical lessons and contact with suffering as well as freedom from suffering, lest we become "so spiritually high that we are no earthly good." "Life is full of suffering" is Buddha's First Noble Truth. Ignoring that side of life becomes dissociation or spiritual bypassing in the opposite direction, followed eventually by statements like "Everything is Love, including violence."

Many people meditate or take psychedelics to feel good. I am definitely one of them, and Buddha also said meditation should generally feel good. My friend Eshwar, however, found that shadow work made up about 99% of his path to heart-chakra awakening.

Modern people carry so much trauma--and some people did even in Buddha's time--that shadow work and inner-child reparenting may need to come first, even before authentic liberation from the identity illusion.

When shadow work is bypassed in meditation and therapy, it may appear in ordinary life without explaining itself. I encountered a lot of it in my first two marriages, to my bewilderment. I wish everybody could keep shadow work inside the safer rooms of meditation, therapy, journaling, prayer, and honest conversation.

# After the Storm

Don't make huge decisions or diagnose yourself from an intense night. You don't need to assume every vision was literal, nor that anything you realized is actually true (or false). Ask your body what it needs from you now. I'm betting it will tell you: Give me sleep, natural food, pure water, a nice nature walk. It helps to integrate the experience via journaling your feelings, and talking with someone grounded (not the 20 chaotic people who are still coming down). To prevent your mind from giving spirituality a negative connotation, engage in some calm prayer or gentle meditation.

**No "another ceremony will fix this" reflex.**

Don't take more psychedelics, cannabis or ketamine, do intense breathwork, fast, push kundalini, deprive yourself of sleep, meditate or stretch to extremes, or schedule "another ceremony to fix it" while destabilized. Deep practice can wait until the floor is back.

Then ask what actually happened. What was medical or environmental? What was emotional? What was old trauma? What was fantasy? What was useful? What changed behavior? What needs therapy, an apology, rest, or plain practical action?

It can feel like the more cosmic the story sounds, the bigger the insight. But the fruit of an insight is whether suffering goes down and love gets more available.

## Lingering Symptoms?

When symptoms linger after a trip, people too often put everything into the "I'm broken" bucket, the way children sometimes give up at the slightest difficulty. While a professional can be called in to help at any point, self-diagnostics and basic fix strategies usually obviate the need for one. Try to find the underlying psychological or physical patterns driving the symptoms:

- **Anxiety and fear:** with this pattern, it helps to first figure out what is providing the stimulation for the anxiety. CBT-style reality testing, checking the frightening thought against what's actually happening, is one way to do that which could be explored with a therapist if it persists, but can be done without one to start with. In general, it can help to have grounding/relaxing routines (like morning or bedtime yin yoga, meeting a friend for lunch).
- **Sleep disturbance and nightmares:** I'd first ensure no more substances are being used, including "normal" ones like coffee, which some people become extra sensitive to after certain psychedelics (especially iboga). Pay attention to sleep hygiene, especially what you eat at what time, and what you might not be processing during the day which then grabs attention at night.
- **Derealization and depersonalization (when the world, or you yourself, feel unreal):** Is it due to isolation? Rumination (which often goes along with isolation)? Peer and family support are crucial to combat those. Some things that tend to help are related to reconnecting with the feelings in your body: taking walks in nature (with bare feet for grounding to the Earth), doing gentle yin yoga, and [Somatic Experiencing](http://somatic.u-dont-exist.com/), a therapy that works through body sensations.
- **Visual distortions/Hallucinogen Persisting Perception Disorder (HPPD)-like symptoms:** These can be long-lasting, and people often end up just getting used to it, but it helps to reduce stress and avoid any further use of psychedelics. I'd also try [DMSO](#§dmso), [niacin](#§niacin-flushes-trips-out), and other neuroprotective/restorative treatments.
- **Social disconnection and shame:** The biggest thing here is if you can find one or two trusted people who won't judge you. Get whatever it is off your chest. If you can't find someone like that, talk to a therapist. You'd be surprised how many people have gone through something like this and come out the other side realizing it wasn't what they made it out to be, myself included. Check [my guide on shame](http://shame.u-dont-exist.com/) for more info.
- **Existential shock:** This one's pretty normal if someone has a deep journey, especially one they weren't prepared for. It helps to go back one step to focus on loving kindness and sending love to the inner child before getting lost in a philosophical ocean. Finding others who've been through a similar experience could be helpful, or harmful, depending on which rabbit hole it was you fell into. I find reading the Buddhist suttas supremely helpful.
- **Paranoia or psychosis-like beliefs:** Here we gotta be careful because this can spiral out of control and get dangerous quickly. I'd seek professional support while utilizing the relatively safe and natural antipsychotics mentioned in this guide, like DMSO, [syrian rue OIL](#§syrian-rue-oil-gentler), and niacin.
- **Somatic hyperarousal:** This is not rumination, but more like akathisia, bodily restlessness. Try gentle movements (that yin yoga again!), slower breathing, grounding, and medical care if the symptoms are severe, unusual or longer-lasting than expected. I'd still go for my trusty relaxing herbs first, like [beach morning glory](#§beach-morning-glory), Syrian rue oil or the [Metta NMT sniffer](#§metta-sniffer-chill-love), and natural antipsychotics like DMSO and niacin.

## Off the Healing Treadmill

![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/ccdad771-dde3-4618-ba38-830eb35b4e59_1254x1254.png)

If you use psychedelics (or MDMA) to achieve a goal like healing or spiritual liberation, you need to already be on the same path so that they aren't radically changing your ordinary understanding of things. If the trip is so crazy that you can't believe it a few days later, chances are, like me, you'll end up taking that drug again and again to understand it.

With healthy drugs like [Loveyhuasca](http://loveyhuasca.info), which has literally saved my life on numerous occasions from unknown infections, there's less of an argument for "Hang up the phone" as Alan Watts famously said you should do "once you get the message." But what we shouldn't be doing is feeling like we need to use these substances *because we can't integrate them.*

[YouTube video 9yp0lvYKK6Q](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9yp0lvYKK6Q)

That happened to me at first when I started with ayahuasca. The ego deaths were so far beyond my ordinary understanding of reality that I dismissed them each time within a week. Then I felt a compulsion to have another ego death soon after. Once I learned to meditate into the experience ([and beyond](http://nibbana.u-dont-exist.com)) without ayahuasca, that compulsion was gone. If I had learned about Buddhism and if I could have accepted that my view of reality was limited prior to ayahuasca, then probably just one or two ego deaths would have been enough inspiration for me.

With [toxic drugs like MDMA & ketamine](http://mdmaket.u-dont-exist.com), that can be a recipe for long-term harm to your physical, and/or mental health. Ketamine is super easy to abuse because it's a dissociative psychedelic. All your problems are magically **gone, **and if you take enough, you get some crazy trips that you can barely remember later. [5-MeO-DMT](http://bufo.u-dont-exist.com) is the more psychedelic version of ketamine. See my guide on that, because it shares a lot of the same warnings, altho I do think it has more potential to be helpful when used correctly.

Here's a [reddit poster ](https://www.reddit.com/r/mdmatherapy/comments/17nne25/reaching_resolution_32_sessions_over_5_years_for/)who talks about going through tremendous pain with MDMA as she uncovered childhood sex abuse memories. It took her dozens of sessions that felt harmful before she finally had one that fully got her to accept what happened to her (and a huge somatic release which looked like a re-enactment of the abuse). So there are some people who argue that if MDMA opened up your wounds, just keep taking more MDMA until it's fixed. Although [on this thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/mdmatherapy/comments/1woxh97/deep_lingering_grief_two_years_post_mdmaassisted/), there are some who disagree with that simplistic advice. I know a few people who quit because they simply couldn't keep going through the pain that MDMA brought up for them. And as the commenters on the first thread note, very few people would go thru that dozens of times hoping for something different to happen.

My perspective is that these people are not correctly preparing for the [inner child reparenting](http://innerchild.u-dont-exist.com) that MDMA suddenly provides experiential access to. Because they are unprepared to make the best of the experience, and unprepared to integrate it after, it's sort of like being thrown into the deep end of a swimming pool before you learn to swim. You flail around for a bit then terrified, you go back to holding on to the wall and begging to get out. It really will take a long time to learn to swim that way, for most folks. On the other hand, without MDMA or loveyhuasca, many people are unable to really do inner child therapy, because it sounds fake to them, just as ego death sounded fake to me before I experienced it. So I think these approaches complement each other greatly.

# Plan a Trip or Ceremony

![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/5ae3cffc-d155-41ef-b1af-c547efa8f52f_1122x1402.png)

If you're planning a session, think through beforehand how it will start and end, and what happens in between. Rest and timing come first: don't begin already depleted, and ideally start earlier in the day, so there's time afterward for food, some normal company and most importantly, sleep.

Set up the space you'll be in, and decide ahead of time what would make you call for outside help. Go in curious about what might come up, along with gentle intentions, not harboring demands for revelations, buried memories, good cries or spiritual breakthroughs. And train before you need it, sending love to a frightening image, to someone you hate (without approving of what they did), and to the inner child. Sometimes people find that when they prepare to purify themselves and open up to love, they don't even end up needing the experience itself. The work is already done.

One woman I met in Kenya was going to take shrooms to fix her tobacco and marijuana addictions. Before she took the shrooms, she simply realized she was addicted to those substances because she was trying to escape anxiety. So instead, she let the anxiety come, and accepted the feeling until it left. Her addictions vanished, no shroom trip required.

If you're taking a psychoactive drug, though, know what you're taking and roughly how much, and look up what it mixes badly with and any medical concerns. If you've taken this amount before, that's a good starting place to understand how it could affect you this time, although people's sensitivities can vary from one session to the next, sometimes quite dramatically. If you've never taken this dose or done this type of meditation this intensely before, make sure you're not jumping into the deep end all at once.

During the experience, the sitter (the sober person with you, not literally someone sitting on your face!) keeps you safe and offers you choices if relevant.

As the experience winds down, give it some closure. Stop adding stimulation (I really don't like the MDMA protocols that include redosing, as it amplifies the neurotoxicity concerns). Check where you are and what day it is. Eat and drink a bit to replenish your electrolytes.

Jot down anything you want to remember before you forget, and let your body know the session is over with some gentle massage. Keep the rest of the day free, and the next morning too if possible, since your brain is still in a vulnerable, flexible, "plastic" state, and the goal is not to cement in worse new patterns than what you had before!

Use this sequence for planned sessions. Once somebody is already in trouble, return to the [triage app](https://ibogaqueen.com/1625-2/) instead of trying to improve the ceremony plan.
