## 2. Contradictions

**1. Room before body, or body before room.** B4 "fix the room, help the body" · B252 "body → room → human → time → meaning" · B375 "body → room → human → time." The checklist says "Use this in order." and first aid (B236-246) follows it; B252, B375 and B415 swap the first two steps without saying why. Better: B4, the order the reader is told to follow.

**2. DMSO first, but not with harmalas.** B9 "Use DMSO immediately while checking" · B136 "harmala/ayahuasca-type MAOI exposure" · B205 "Don’t take it with drugs that should not absorb rapidly, including corticosteroids, NSAIDs, and harmalas." DMSO is the first move (B9, B144) and a stabilizer (B200) in an article that often assumes ayahuasca, which B136 counts as harmala exposure; B205 rules the pairing out without saying whether that covers ayahuasca. Better: B205, the only place that weighs the interaction.

**3. Eating humic acid in a bad trip.** B11 "tasting a few milligrams of it can produce hyperintelligence" · B144 "take a 5mg lick of it" · B184 "Licking a tiny bit like 5mg does even more" · B219 "I wouldn’t use it casually during a bad trip." B144 makes the lick step one when the brain feels damaged and B184 lists it among stabilizers; B219 warns that eating it can look manic and says to start by smelling. B144 also drops B11's ayahuasca-only limit. Better: B219, which gives the risk.

**4. How long the action lock lasts.** B31 "No big actions until morning." · B317 "Use a 24–72 hour action lock." · B318 "Until you have slept and eaten". Morning, one to three days, or after sleep and food; nothing reconciles them. Better: B317-318, the dedicated rule.

**5. Whether charcoal belongs only after a recent oral ingestion.** B54 "Charcoal belongs only in the separate recent-oral-ingestion branch." · B154 "requires activated charcoal to reduce gut histamine" · B161 "stop a toxic gut situation, charcoal may be relevant". B154 and B160-161 use charcoal outside that branch. Better: B160-162, which say when it fits.

**6. Ocean water: different from charcoal water, or similar.** B188 "a grounding, cooling, organizing effect different from DMSO, aged ozonated-charcoal water" · B190 "These work similarly to ocean water." Two blocks apart, with no word on how both hold. Better: B188, which names the effect.
