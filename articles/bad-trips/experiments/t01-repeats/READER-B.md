## 2. Contradictions

**C1. Room or body first?** B4 "fix the room, help the body"; B252 "body → room → human → time → meaning". B4 says "Use this in order." B63 keeps the room before the body, but B252, B375 and B415 put the body first. No reason is given.

**C2. Humic acid in a bad trip.** B144 "Smell humic acid, take a 5mg lick of it."; B219 "I wouldn’t use it casually during a bad trip." B144 gives the lick for any brain-damage report. B11 has seen it work only with ayahuasca and loveyhuasca. B219 warns that eating it can destabilize someone and says to start by smelling it. B219's "casually" may be what separates them, but neither passage says so.

**C3. Is charcoal only for recent ingestion?** B54 "Charcoal belongs only in the separate recent-oral-ingestion branch."; B154 "which then requires activated charcoal to reduce gut histamine"; B161 "or stop a toxic gut situation, charcoal may be relevant". The later passages use it outside that branch.

**C4. Ocean water and charcoal water: different or similar?** B188 "different from DMSO, aged ozonated-charcoal water"; B190 "These work similarly to ocean water." The two passages are two blocks apart.

**C5. DMSO and harmalas.** B205 "including corticosteroids, NSAIDs, and harmalas"; B136 "harmala/ayahuasca-type MAOI exposure"; B200 "DMSO is another stabilizer I use when a trip becomes too intense". The article links harmalas to ayahuasca, yet it offers DMSO for any trip that gets too intense and puts it first for any psychedelic (B10, B144). It makes no exception for ayahuasca.

**C6. DMSO thins the blood, yet comes first when a bleed is possible.** B205 "DMSO also thins the blood"; B106 "DMSO cannot identify whether the problem is a clot, a hemorrhage". B106 still uses it at once for stroke-like signs and doesn't say why the thinning doesn't matter there.

**C7. What ICEERS and Fireside are.** B62 "integration-oriented support after difficult psychedelic experiences; not emergency care."; B264 "U.S.-only according to its current FAQ"; B438 "Free tripsitting hotline at". B438 calls both a tripsitting hotline and leaves out Fireside's U.S. limit.

**C8. How long the action lock lasts.** B31 "No big actions until morning."; B317 "Use a 24–72 hour action lock."; B318 "Until you have slept and eaten". That's three different lengths, and the article never says which applies when.
