# Repeats and contradictions: bad-trips guide (reader B)

Counts: 56 repeats, 8 contradictions, 10 callbacks (plus 3 forward references). Every quote is copied from the article. A range like B236-237 means one passage.

## 1. Repeats

**1. The order: safety, room, body, human, sleep, then meaning.** B4 "fix the room, help the body, find one calm human, protect sleep"; B57 "Meaning still waits until the person is safe"; B63 "safety, room, body, human, no rash actions, sleep if possible, then meaning"; B145 "we can return to the deeper question"; B246 "let meaning wait until the nervous system has somewhere to stand"; B252 "body → room → human → time → meaning". B252 adds time (and swaps body and room, contradiction 1). The rest are told again. Better: B4, the checklist's opening rule.

**2. B94 is the fullest list. B112 and B124 copy its seizure and chest-pain lines, and B72 is told again after B6. Better: B94, the section built to list them.

**3.
