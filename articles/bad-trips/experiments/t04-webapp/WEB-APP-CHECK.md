**What it is.** A page that runs in the reader's browser, not a chatbot: a 60-second first pass, an interactive triage
(checkboxes and menus, then "Show triage response"), an expanded quick checklist, a responder handoff note, a triage
ladder, a table of his field notes with an "evidence-weighted caution" for each, and live-support links. So each of the
checklist's situations was put to it by setting the menus and reading the answer it writes.

## Across the whole app

- **No amounts anywhere.** Searched over the page and its script: no milligrams, no grams of magnesium, no per-minute
  rate. Each remedy points back to "the guide's field plan" or "the familiar plan".
- **Every remedy of his carries a caution in the table**, written in GPT's register: zeolite and ZHoly3O "No established
  evidence for acute psychedelic distress"; pes-caprae "Field evidence rather than a validated clinical antidote";
  niacin "Not a validated psychedelic antidote"; DMSO "do not establish complete acute stroke treatment"; ocean water "do
  not drink seawater".
- **Not in the app at all:** humic acid, coffee cherry, the Buteyko breathing, the name beach morning glory, Love Thy Enemy.
- **The routing matches the guide:** DMSO first for a brain-damage feeling, pes-caprae to stop a psychedelic trip,
  magnesium for MDMA, the action lock, the room, one human anchor, the 3 a.m. plan, love and inner-child work.

## What it means for moving the checklist

The app routes the way the guide does, but it can't stand in for the checklist as it is: its medical-danger answer tells
the reader not to start a field rescue, and it leaves out every amount and several of his remedies. Either the
checklist stays in the guide (it is now 15 short steps, each linking to its section) and the app stays the interactive
companion, or the app's lines above are rewritten to match the guide before the checklist moves.
