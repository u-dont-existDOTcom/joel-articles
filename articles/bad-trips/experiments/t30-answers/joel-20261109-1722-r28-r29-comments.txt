## RUE-SAFETY (answered)

Syrian rue can be mixed into warm/hot oil (like coconut oil), just enough to cover it, then squeezed out thru cheese cloth. Within some minutes of the seeds soaking in oil (if it's hot, or maybe an hour if it's warm), the oil contains the oil-soluble harmalas, not harmine, harmaline, or THH in quantities that could make it a potent MAOi (unless one extracts with hexane and then eats a 1-2tbsp of that extract). These oil-soluble harmalas have no effect when eaten, but when absorbed thru mucous membranes or vaped, they chill out psychedelic trip intensity, making them more gentle and sweet. On their own, they have some mild psychedelic properties though (like I felt like half of my head was sort of transparent), and significant mood boosting properties. 

I have a friend with schizophrenia would would use about 1-2mL of this oil rectally when he was in psychosis, which helped at least as much as niacin an DMSO, equally effective as Geodon, an antipsychotic his doctors prescribed which makes him feel like a zombie.

Another option is vaping the whole syrian rue seeds, as it seems that these oil soluble harmalas are concentrated on the outside of the seeds. Once ground up, vaping the powder is more stimulating, less relaxing (but variable since all is mixed together). However, if vaping the whole seeds, I would not assume there's no MAOi effect (even though it still seems to reduce DMT effects) since depending maybe on temperature and time, you might also vaporize the harmine and harmaline.
