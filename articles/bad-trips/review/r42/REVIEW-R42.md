**Why:** Your question, whether to keep giving electrolytes while calling medics: no, nothing more to drink once those signs show. Electrolyte and sports drinks carry far less salt than blood, so drinking more of them still thins it; the wilderness-medicine guidelines on low sodium from overdrinking name water and sports drinks as the cause and restrict fluids once it's suspected, and hospitals treat it with fluid restriction or a concentrated salt drip (for MDMA too). Vomiting means a drink won't stay down, and confusion makes choking a risk. "Too late for an easy fix" meant that, but it reads as "nothing can be done". And low sodium, not potassium, is what most often brings the seizures in MDMA cases; very low potassium brings weakness and heart rhythm trouble, which is how it can bring a collapse.
