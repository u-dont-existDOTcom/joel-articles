## RUE-OIL: Syrian rue oil: your notes as a new entry under Mental Stabilizers (Your notes, placed)

*DMSO, P11*

**What changes:** A new entry after DMSO, in your words from your comment: what the oil is and how you make it, what it does, your friend's experience, and vaping the whole seeds. Typos fixed ("would would", "an DMSO", "MAOi"); "(*Peganum harmala*)" added after the first "Syrian rue", so a reader knows the plant; and from your comment on the aftermath card, "In my experience there's no MAOI effect in Syrian rue extracted with oil (it won't activate DMT, rather it reduces DMT's effects)." **New heading** (h4, in your style, from your words "more gentle and sweet"): Syrian Rue Oil Makes the Trip Gentle & Sweet

**New line**: Syrian rue (*Peganum harmala*) can be mixed into warm/hot oil (like coconut oil), just enough to cover it, then squeezed out thru cheese cloth. Within some minutes of the seeds soaking in oil (if it's hot, or maybe an hour if it's warm), the oil contains the oil-soluble harmalas, not harmine, harmaline, or THH in quantities that could make it a potent MAOI. In my experience there's no MAOI effect in Syrian rue extracted with oil (it won't activate DMT, rather it reduces DMT's effects). These oil-soluble harmalas have no effect when eaten, but when absorbed thru mucous membranes or vaped, they chill out psychedelic trip intensity, making them more gentle and sweet. On their own, they have some mild psychedelic properties though (like I felt like half of my head was sort of transparent), and significant mood boosting properties.

**New line**: I have a friend with schizophrenia who would use about 1-2mL of this oil rectally when he was in psychosis, which helped at least as much as niacin and DMSO, equally effective as Geodon, an antipsychotic his doctors prescribed which makes him feel like a zombie.

**New line**: Another option is vaping the whole Syrian rue seeds, as it seems that these oil soluble harmalas are concentrated on the outside of the seeds. Once ground up, vaping the powder is more stimulating, less relaxing (but variable since all is mixed together). However, if vaping the whole seeds, I would not assume there's no MAOI effect (even though it still seems to reduce DMT effects) since depending maybe on temperature and time, you might also vaporize the harmine and harmaline.

**Why:** Your aftermath list names "syrian rue OIL" among the natural antipsychotics "mentioned in this guide", and the guide never said what it is. After DMSO, because DMSO's interactions already talk about harmalas. The hexane aside is a method and an amount for a strong MAOI extract, which a reader looking for a calming oil doesn't need; the next card says what to keep away from instead.

**What stays:** Everything else under Mental Stabilizers, and your paranoia line, which now has the oil it names.

*DMSO, P12*

**What changes:** One paragraph of mine after your entry: "How much harmine and harmaline end up in a home-made oil can vary, so don't use Syrian rue in any form alongside antidepressants, MDMA, other MAOIs, or anything else on the stop list under Suspected Serotonin Syndrome. And if someone's on a prescribed antipsychotic, don't swap it for this oil without checking with whoever prescribed it."

**New line**: How much harmine and harmaline end up in a home-made oil can vary, so don't use Syrian rue in any form alongside antidepressants, MDMA, other MAOIs, or anything else on the stop list under Suspected Serotonin Syndrome. And if someone's on a prescribed antipsychotic, don't swap it for this oil without checking with whoever prescribed it.

**Why:** Your entry says the oil carries little harmine or harmaline, and that vaped whole seeds might carry more. I couldn't confirm or rule out the oil part: the studies of the seeds' oil I found measured its fatty acids, not its alkaloids, and a traditional Syrian rue oil tested in a 2015 trial was "standardized by high-performance liquid chromatography", which for this plant usually means harmine and harmaline, though I couldn't open the full paper to see how much ([what I checked](https://github.com/u-dont-existDOTcom/joel-articles/blob/claude/bad-trips-20261105/articles/bad-trips/humanize/h39-20261109/RUE.md)). Harmine is among the plant's volatile compounds too, which fits your caution about vaping. The guide's serotonin section already lists harmala exposure among the things to stop. And your friend's story compares the oil with Geodon, so someone in psychosis who reads it needs to know not to drop a prescription on its strength.
