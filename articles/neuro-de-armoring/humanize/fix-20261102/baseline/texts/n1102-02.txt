The Modern “Role-Self” vs. The Ancient “True-Self”

As Dr. Bruce Perry explains, our brains evolved in hunter-gatherer bands with a ratio of 4 developmentally mature adults to 1 child. Today, our “enriched” environments offer 1 immature adult to 4 children.
