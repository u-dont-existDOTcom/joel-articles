If you're sensitive, start lower and do the trial with 50 mg 5-HTP at night for 2–3 nights, then go up to 100 mg only if the lower amount is clearly helpful and isn't activating. You can also add 10–20 mg P5P in the morning, especially if the 5-HTP feels like it "almost works" but isn't converting cleanly. Don't do the trial at the same time as other changes (like UMP, agmatine, D-serine, psychedelics, or medication changes), since the point is to see whether the 5-HTP itself is doing anything. If it's the right tool, the first thing you'll usually notice is your sleep quality, the tone of your dreams, a warmer mood, or less rumination at night.
