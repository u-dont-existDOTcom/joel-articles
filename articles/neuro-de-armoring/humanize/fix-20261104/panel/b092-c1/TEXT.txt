If the 5-HTP gives you nausea or stomach discomfort, stop the trial. Loose stools or a headache are reasons to stop too. Stop immediately if you get agitation together with sweating, diarrhea, tremor or clonus, and fever, confusion, a fast heart rate or unstable blood pressure. That pattern can be serotonin toxicity, which can become dangerous. Other milder stop signs include anxiety, irritability, emotional agitation, sweating, tremor, or a too-bright, kind of hypomanic feeling. Insomnia and dreams that get vivid or intense are also reasons to stop.
