Wurtman's research group found that giving the three together can build up the synapses' membranes and proteins, and that in animals, UMP made DHA better at getting neurons to grow new connection points, which they call DHA-induced dendritic spine formation. They also fed UMP to old rats, and the rats' neurons released more dopamine when they were stimulated with potassium, and grew more nerve fibers. So it's plausible it works on the synapses this way, but for DAWS in people, all we have is a cautious extrapolation from those studies, plus anecdotes like Scott's.
