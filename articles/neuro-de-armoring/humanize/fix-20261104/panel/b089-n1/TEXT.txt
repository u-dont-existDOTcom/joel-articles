The tool: 5-HTP skips the step where the body turns tryptophan into 5-HTP, so it's a more direct serotonin precursor. A broad review of 5-HTP discusses its role in serotonin biology and its use for mood, anxiety, sleep, obesity, myoclonus, and in serotonin-toxicity cases. The evidence for depression and sleep is mixed, and not as strong as people online make it sound, but the mechanism is real enough that people who respond to it can feel it quickly.
