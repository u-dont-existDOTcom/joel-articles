Don't mix 5-HTP casually with anything else that raises serotonin, like antidepressants or MAOIs. Serotonin syndrome usually comes from that kind of interaction, when several drugs or supplements get stacked, and [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/007272.htm) has an overview of what it looks like. Case reports and reviews keep warning about the risk with 5-HTP, like [this case report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5580516/), where a young guy on sertraline (an antidepressant) took 5-HTP with a whole pile of weight-loss and energy supplements before a workout, and ended up with serotonin syndrome so bad that surgeons had to cut open both of his legs and his forearm to relieve the pressure. Luckily he recovered. If you're doing psychedelics, avoid 5-HTP on your psychedelic days, and be especially strict around ayahuasca's [harmala MAOI chemistry](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7678905/) and around iboga or ibogaine.
