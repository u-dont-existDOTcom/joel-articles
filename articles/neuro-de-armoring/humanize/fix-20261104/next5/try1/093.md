Hard interaction rule: Don't mix 5-HTP casually with other serotonin-raising drugs or supplements, as serotonin syndrome is most often a multi-drug / stack problem. There's case literature and clinical reviews warning about that risk when 5-HTP is combined with antidepressants or MAOIs (see this [dietary supplement interaction case report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5580516/)). [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/007272.htm) also has a general overview of serotonin syndrome. If you're doing psychedelics, avoid 5-HTP on your psychedelic days, and be especially strict around [ayahuasca / harmala MAOI chemistry](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7678905/) and iboga / ibogaine.
