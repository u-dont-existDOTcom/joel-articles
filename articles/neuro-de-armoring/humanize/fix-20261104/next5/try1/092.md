Side effects / stop signs: The big one to watch out for is agitation + sweating + diarrhea + tremor/clonus + fever, confusion, fast heart rate, or unstable blood pressure. Stop immediately if you get that, since it can be a sign of serotonin toxicity, which can become dangerous. Other stop signs include nausea, stomach discomfort, loose stools, headache, vivid or intense dreams, insomnia, sweating, tremor, anxiety, irritability, emotional agitation, or a too-bright / hypomanic feeling.
