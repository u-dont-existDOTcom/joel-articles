- Scott already had choline from eggs and DHA/EPA from fish oil.
- He added a standard dose of UMP (uridine monophosphate).
- He got a striking response within a day.
- His vivid dreams came back, and real childhood memories came back in his dreams.
- His psychedelic tolerance seemed to drop.
- His ketamine/LSD craving fell hard.
- That does not prove UMP rebuilt his D3 receptors overnight.
- It does suggest that, in the right person, UMP can be a very clean signal that the system the brain uses to rebuild synaptic membranes and recover dopamine receptors (the "synaptic-membrane / dopamine-receptor-recovery axis") was starved. (The label itself can go if the meaning stays.)
