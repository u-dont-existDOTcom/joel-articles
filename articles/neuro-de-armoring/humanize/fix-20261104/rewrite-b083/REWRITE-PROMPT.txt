The tool: Uridine monophosphate (UMP). There's no study showing that "UMP cures DAWS", only more basic research on what uridine, choline, and DHA do in the brain. All three get carried in the blood to the brain, which uses them to make the fatty membranes at its synapses (circulating precursors for brain phosphatide / synaptic membrane synthesis). Wurtman's research group found that giving the three together can increase synaptic membrane components and synaptic proteins, and that in animals, adding UMP to DHA made the neurons grow more new connection points (DHA-induced dendritic spine formation). And old rats that got UMP in their food released more dopamine when their neurons were stimulated with potassium, and grew more nerve fibers. So it's plausible that it works on the synapses this way, but using it for DAWS in people means carefully extrapolating from those studies and from anecdotes like Scott's.
