The tool: Uridine monophosphate (UMP). There's no study showing that "UMP cures DAWS", so the best evidence we have is more basic than that. Uridine, choline, and DHA are circulating precursors (building blocks carried in the blood) for brain phosphatide / synaptic membrane synthesis, that is, for making the fatty membranes at the connections between neurons. Wurtman's group has shown that [DHA + uridine + choline can increase synaptic membrane components and synaptic proteins](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4011061/), that [DHA-induced dendritic spine formation is enhanced by UMP](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2140951/) in animals (the spines are the tiny bumps on neurons where connections form), and that [dietary UMP increased potassium-evoked dopamine release and neurite outgrowth in aged rats](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16055952/) (old rats that got UMP in their food released more dopamine and grew more nerve fibers). So the mechanism is plausible, but the DAWS use is still a cautious human extrapolation (applying the animal findings to people) plus anecdote.
