The tool: Uridine monophosphate (UMP). There's no study showing that "UMP cures DAWS", only more basic research on what uridine does in the brain with choline and DHA. All three get carried in the blood to the brain, which uses them to make the fatty membranes at its synapses (circulating precursors for brain phosphatide / synaptic membrane synthesis). Using UMP for DAWS in people still means carefully extrapolating from that research and from anecdotes like Scott's, though it's plausible that it works on the synapses this way. Wurtman's research group found that [giving the three together can increase synaptic membrane components and synaptic proteins](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4011061/). In animals, adding UMP to DHA made neurons grow more new connection points, what's called [DHA-induced dendritic spine formation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2140951/). [Old rats got UMP in their food, and when their neurons were stimulated with potassium, they released more dopamine](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16055952/). The rats grew more nerve fibers too.
