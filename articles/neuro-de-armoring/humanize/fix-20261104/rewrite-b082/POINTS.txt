- The tool: Uridine monophosphate (UMP). (Keep "The tool:" at the start; every section of the article opens its tool this way.)
- There is no study showing that "UMP cures DAWS"; the evidence there is is more basic.
- Uridine, choline and DHA are carried in the blood to the brain, which uses them to make the fatty membranes at its synapses (keep the term "circulating precursors for brain phosphatide / synaptic membrane synthesis").
- Wurtman's research group found that giving DHA, uridine and choline together can increase synaptic membrane components and synaptic proteins.
- In animals, adding UMP to DHA made neurons grow more new connection points (keep the term "DHA-induced dendritic spine formation").
- Old rats that got UMP in their food released more dopamine when their neurons were stimulated with potassium, and grew more nerve fibers (keep "potassium").
- So it's plausible that it works on the synapses this way, but using it for DAWS in people is still a careful extrapolation from those studies, plus anecdotes like Scott's.
