The tool: Say your body isn't turning tryptophan into 5-HTP very well. Taking 5-HTP actually skips that step, giving your body a more direct serotonin precursor, and people who respond can feel it quickly. The evidence for depression and sleep is mixed, though. People online make it sound stronger than it is. This [broad review of 5-HTP](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7796270/) discusses serotonin biology and uses for mood, anxiety, sleep, obesity and myoclonus. It covers the serotonin-toxicity risk too.
