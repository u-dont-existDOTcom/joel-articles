- The paragraph is the uridine (UMP) trial's side effects and stop signs; it can open with its label, "Side effects / stop signs:".
- In practice (from experience, not from the studies), the most common problem is the "choline headache" or a spaced-out brain fog.
- That isn't in the studies. The likely reason: if UMP speeds up membrane synthesis while choline availability is low, the brain can feel under-supplied.
- Other stop signs include insomnia, jitteriness, racing thoughts, irritability, emotional flatness, nausea, stomach cramps, or loose stools (all eight stay).
- If it makes you wired, numb, or weirdly irritable, stop.
- Don't push through.
