If UMP gives you a "choline headache" or spaced-out brain fog, that's the most common problem in practice, though it isn't in the studies. If it makes you wired or numb, stop; same if you get weirdly irritable, and don't push through it. Other stop signs include insomnia, jitteriness, racing thoughts, irritability, emotional flatness and nausea. Stop for stomach cramps or loose stools too. The likely reason for the headache or fog is that UMP speeds up membrane synthesis, and with low choline availability your brain can feel under-supplied.
