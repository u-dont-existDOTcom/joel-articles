Dopamine Receptor Burnout / DAWS Pattern: Uridine Monophosphate
Who this is for: Uridine isn't for everyone with depression. It could be helpful, though, for folks that show a “burned-out dopamine receptor” pattern, where everything feels gray and there's no desire for anything (anhedonia). These folks also find that dreams and psychedelic visuals are weak, so they need unusually high doses to feel anything, and as a result they tend to compulsively reach for ketamine/LSD/novelty just to get a hit. In the medical literature, this burnout pattern is talked about with regard to dopamine agonist withdrawal syndrome (DAWS). People with DAWS, after tapering off dopamine agonist drugs, present with symptoms of anxiety, panic, dysphoria, depression, agitation, fatigue, autonomic symptoms, pain, and drug cravings. Scott’s case looked like DAWS with dopamine receptor D2/D3 reward-system downregulation, which then got worse from frequent LSD microdosing and from trying to force dopamine hits with ketamine.
