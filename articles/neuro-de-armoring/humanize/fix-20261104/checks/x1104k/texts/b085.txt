Side effects / stop signs: Stop if it makes you wired, numb, or weirdly irritable, and don't try to push through it. The most common problem people run into is the "choline headache", or a spaced-out kind of brain fog, which you won't find in the studies, but it's likely because UMP speeds up membrane synthesis, so if your choline availability is low, your brain can end up under-supplied. Other stop signs include insomnia, jitteriness and racing thoughts, irritability, going emotionally flat, or an upset stomach with nausea, cramps or loose stools.
