Scott took 100 mg 5-HTP sublingually at night, with 10–20 mg P5P sublingually in the morning. The P5P isn't there as a stimulant, but as the active form of B6, which the enzyme that turns 5-HTP into serotonin (aromatic amino-acid decarboxylase) needs. He's slow-COMT and sensitive to too much P5P, so the low dose matters, and more isn't better.
