Hard interaction rule: Don't mix 5-HTP casually with other serotonin-raising drugs or supplements, as serotonin syndrome is most often a multi-drug / stack problem. There's case literature and clinical reviews warning about that risk when 5-HTP is combined with antidepressants or MAOIs (see this dietary supplement interaction case report). MedlinePlus also has a general overview of serotonin syndrome. If you're doing psychedelics, avoid 5-HTP on your psychedelic days, and be especially strict around ayahuasca / harmala MAOI chemistry and iboga / ibogaine.
