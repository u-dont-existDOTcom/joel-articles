Side effects / stop signs: Stop immediately if agitation, sweating, diarrhea and tremor or clonus all hit together, plus fever, confusion, a fast heart rate, or unstable blood pressure, since that pattern points to serotonin toxicity, which can get dangerous. Otherwise the side effects to stop for are nausea, stomach discomfort or loose stools, headaches, vivid or intense dreams, insomnia, sweating or tremor on their own, feeling anxious, irritable or agitated, or a too-bright, kind of hypomanic feeling.
