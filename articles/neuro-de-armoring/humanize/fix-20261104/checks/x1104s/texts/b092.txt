Side effects / stop signs: Stop immediately if agitation, sweating, diarrhea and tremor or clonus all hit together, plus fever, confusion, a fast heart rate, or unstable blood pressure. That pattern points to serotonin toxicity, which can get dangerous. The milder side effects are still a reason to stop the trial: your stomach might get upset, with nausea, discomfort or loose stools, you could get headaches, vivid or intense dreams or insomnia, you might sweat or get a tremor, or you could feel anxious, irritable or agitated, or get a too-bright, almost hypomanic feeling.
