The tool: Uridine monophosphate (UMP). There's no study showing that "UMP cures DAWS", so the best evidence we have is more basic than that. Uridine, choline, and DHA are circulating precursors (building blocks carried in the blood) for brain phosphatide / synaptic membrane synthesis, that is, for making the fatty membranes at the connections between neurons. Wurtman's research group found that giving DHA, uridine and choline together can increase the building blocks and proteins of those synaptic membranes. In animals, UMP made the DHA-induced growth of new dendritic spines stronger (the spines are the tiny bumps on neurons where they connect to each other). And when old rats got UMP in their food, they released more dopamine when their neurons were stimulated, and grew more nerve fibers (neurite outgrowth). So it's plausible that it works on the synapses this way, but using it for DAWS in people is still a cautious extrapolation from those studies, plus anecdotes like Scott's.
