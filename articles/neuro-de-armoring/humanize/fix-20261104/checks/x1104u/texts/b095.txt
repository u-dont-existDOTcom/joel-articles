Recent neuroscience (Tian-Ming Gao, et al, 2025) has shown in male mice that anxiety and depression are a result of reduced hippocampal ATP signaling. Calabrese, et al (2024) also showed that ketones and cutting down on carbs are highly effective for anxiety, depression and other mental illnesses. That's one study, but it's part of much broader research. So I'd trial 20mg of PQQ in the morning to boost ATP, with ketones, which your neurons can still burn for energy when they're struggling to burn sugar.
