If your cortisol is normal or low, low ATP signaling in the brain might be what's behind your anxiety or depression. Recent neuroscience (Tian-Ming Gao, et al, 2025) has shown in male mice that anxiety and depression are a result of reduced hippocampal ATP signaling. So I'd trial 20mg of PQQ in the morning to boost ATP, with ketones, which your neurons can still burn for energy when they're struggling to burn sugar. In Calabrese, et al (2024), three adults on a ketogenic diet all went into full remission of depression and anxiety within a few months. That's only three people, but it's part of much broader research.
