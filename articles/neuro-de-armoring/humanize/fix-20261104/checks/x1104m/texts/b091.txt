If small doses tend to hit you hard, do the 5-HTP trial with 50 mg at night for 2–3 nights. And don't mix the trial with UMP, agmatine, D-serine, psychedelics, or medication changes. Go up to 100 mg only if the lower dose is clearly helpful and isn't activating. P5P is optional here, 10–20 mg in the morning, especially if the 5-HTP feels like it "almost works" but the conversion isn't quite right. If it's the right tool, you usually notice it first in how you sleep or how your dreams feel. Or your mood is warmer, or you get to nighttime and aren't going over things in your head as much.
