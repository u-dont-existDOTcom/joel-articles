Side effects / stop signs: In practice the most common problem is the "choline headache", or a spaced-out kind of brain fog. That isn't in the studies, but the likely reason is that UMP speeds up membrane synthesis while choline availability is low, so the system feels under-supplied. Other stop signs include insomnia, jitteriness, racing thoughts, irritability, emotional flatness, nausea, stomach cramps, or loose stools. If it makes you wired, numb, or weirdly irritable, stop, and don't push through.
