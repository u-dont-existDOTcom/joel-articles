If you're sensitive, start lower, with 50 mg 5-HTP at night for 2–3 nights, and go up to 100 mg only if the lower amount is clearly helpful and isn't activating. You can add 10–20 mg P5P in the morning, especially if the 5-HTP feels like it "almost works" but isn't converting cleanly. Don't combine the trial with other changes (like UMP, agmatine, D-serine, psychedelics, or medication changes). If it's the right tool, the first thing you'll usually notice is better sleep, a different dream tone, warmer mood, or less rumination at night.
