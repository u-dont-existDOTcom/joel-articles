Scott was already getting choline from eggs and DHA/EPA from fish oil when he added a standard dose of UMP, and within a day his vivid dreams were back, with real childhood memories in them. His psychedelic tolerance seemed to drop too, and his ketamine/LSD cravings fell off hard. Since he already had the choline and the DHA, the uridine looks like the piece he was missing. Whether or not his D3 dopamine receptors were rebuilt that fast, in the right person a quick response like that can be a very clear sign that the synaptic-membrane / dopamine-receptor-recovery axis (the system that keeps rebuilding the membranes and dopamine receptors where neurons connect) was starved.
