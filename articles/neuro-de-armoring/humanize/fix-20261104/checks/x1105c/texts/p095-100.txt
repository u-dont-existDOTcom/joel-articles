You must pick one of two ketone strategies, because the salts in one bring their own sodium while the diet in the other drains it. If you're in a crisis and need the fog gone fast, there's Strategy A, the "Crisis Mode". You drink BHB (beta-hydroxybutyrate) salts and keep eating a standard low-sugar diet.

For the long run there's Strategy B, the "Resilience Mode", a keto diet with carbs under 20g/day so your liver makes the ketones itself. That only works if fat becomes your fuel, so eat fat until you're full. Don't fill up on lean protein like chicken breast instead, because your body turns the extra protein into sugar through gluconeogenesis, and that keeps you out of ketosis. Switching over often brings on the keto flu, and to avoid it, refer to my Keto Adaptation Guide.
