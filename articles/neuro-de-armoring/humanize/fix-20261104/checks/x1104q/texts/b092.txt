Side effects / stop signs: Stop immediately if you get agitation + sweating + diarrhea + tremor/clonus (your muscles jerking on their own) + fever, confusion, fast heart rate, or unstable blood pressure, because that pattern can be serotonin toxicity, which can become dangerous. The milder stop signs are stomach stuff like nausea, discomfort or loose stools, plus headache, vivid or intense dreams, insomnia, sweating, tremor, anxiety, irritability, emotional agitation, or a too-bright, kind of hypomanic feeling.
