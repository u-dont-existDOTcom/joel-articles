Who this is for: Uridine isn't for everyone with depression. It could be helpful, though, for folks that show a "burned-out dopamine receptor" pattern, where everything feels gray and there's no desire for anything (anhedonia). These folks also find that dreams and psychedelic visuals are weak, so they need unusually high doses to feel anything, and as a result they tend to compulsively reach for ketamine/LSD/novelty just to get a hit. In the medical literature, this burnout pattern is talked about with regard to dopamine agonist withdrawal syndrome (DAWS). People with DAWS, after tapering off dopamine agonist drugs (like pramipexole or ropinirole), present with symptoms of anxiety, panic, dysphoria, depression, agitation, fatigue, autonomic symptoms, pain, and drug cravings. Scott's case looked like DAWS with dopamine receptor D2/D3 reward-system downregulation, which then got worse from frequent LSD microdosing and from trying to force dopamine hits with ketamine.

Do a trial with 150-300mg Uridine, in the morning with food. It shouldn't take more than 3 to 7 days to get an idea of whether or not it's beneficial. Keep your Omega-3's up, especially DHA and EPA. Make sure you have sufficient Choline - from eggs, Phosphatidylcholine, CDP-choline, etc, whichever works best for you. Don't start the uridine at the same time as starting some other major variable (like 5-HTP, Agmatine, D-serine, Keto diet, Iboga, Shrooms, LSD, Ketamine, etc), since the point is to see whether uridine itself changes the signal.

Neurological De-Armoring is about fixing the underpinnings, not redecorating. It's a protocol that serves as a metabolic solvent, via HDAC inhibition, that softens the "concrete" in the brainstem by using Glutamate modulation and supplementing Ketones (BHB) to allow us to dig under the house, and pour a new foundation, so we can eventually update the psychological software upstairs.

Scott added a standard dose of UMP, and within a day his ketamine/LSD cravings fell hard. He already had choline covered with eggs, and took fish oil for DHA/EPA. His psychedelic tolerance seemed to drop too. You can't prove from this whether UMP rebuilt his D3 receptors overnight, but it does suggest that in the right person, UMP can show you very clearly when the brain's system for rebuilding synaptic membranes and recovering dopamine receptors has been starved of building materials. Scott was having vivid dreams again too, with real childhood memories surfacing.

Precautions/Side Effects: Do not use concurrently with opioids and/or kratom (will reduce tolerance), clonidine and/or guanfacine, blood pressure lowering medications (including nitrates and PDE-5 inhibitors), or other complex "psych" stacks without having a physician competent to consider the likelihoods of problematic interactions. Not recommended for use in pregnancy or breast-feeding. Use with extreme caution in those with a prior psychotic episode, lower end blood pressure, or kidney or liver issues.
