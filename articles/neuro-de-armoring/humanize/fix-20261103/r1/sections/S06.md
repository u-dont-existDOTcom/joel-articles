### Dopamine Receptor Burnout / DAWS Pattern: Uridine Monophosphate

Uridine is for the specific “burned-out dopamine receptor” pattern, so it is not for everyone with depression. That pattern is gray anhedonia, weak dreams, weak psychedelic visuals, needing unusually high doses to feel anything, compulsively reaching for ketamine/LSD/novelty just to get a hit, and especially a history of dopamine agonist withdrawal from drugs like pramipexole or ropinirole. The medical literature calls this [dopamine agonist withdrawal syndrome](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23686524/) (DAWS) and describes anxiety, panic, dysphoria, depression, agitation, fatigue, autonomic symptoms, pain, and drug cravings after dopamine-agonist tapering. With Scott, the pattern looked like DAWS with D2/D3 reward-system downregulation, and then frequent LSD microdosing and ketamine attempts to force dopamine hits made it worse. So I’m talking about a specific phenotype here, and I’m not claiming everyone needs more dopamine.

The tool is Uridine monophosphate (UMP). There’s no study showing “UMP cures DAWS”; the evidence is more basic than that. Uridine, choline, and DHA are circulating precursors for brain phosphatide / synaptic membrane synthesis, and Wurtman’s group has shown that [DHA + uridine + choline can increase synaptic membrane components and synaptic proteins](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4011061/), that [DHA-induced dendritic spine formation is enhanced by UMP](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2140951/) in animals, and that [dietary UMP increased potassium-evoked dopamine release and neurite outgrowth in aged rats](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16055952/). So the mechanism is plausible, but using it for DAWS is still a cautious human extrapolation plus anecdote.

Scott already had choline from eggs and DHA/EPA from fish oil, and after adding standard-dose UMP he got a striking response within a day: vivid dreams returned, real childhood memories came back in dreams, his psychedelic tolerance seemed to drop, and his ketamine/LSD craving fell hard. I wouldn’t conclude that UMP rebuilt D3 receptors overnight, but it does suggest that, in the right person, UMP can be a very clean signal that the synaptic-membrane / dopamine-receptor-recovery axis was starved.

To trial it: UMP 150–300 mg in the morning with food for 3–7 days. Keep omega-3s in place, especially DHA/EPA, and make sure there’s enough choline from eggs, phosphatidylcholine, CDP-choline, or another choline source you tolerate. Do not start UMP on the same day you start 5-HTP, agmatine, D-serine, keto, iboga, shrooms, LSD, ketamine, or any other major variable, since you’re trying to see whether UMP itself changes the signal.

The most common problem is the “choline headache” or a spaced-out brain fog: if UMP accelerates membrane synthesis while choline availability is low, the system can feel under-supplied. Other stop signs include insomnia, jitteriness, racing thoughts, irritability, emotional flatness, nausea, stomach cramps, or loose stools. If it makes you wired, numb, or weirdly irritable, stop. Do not push through.

If UMP makes your dreams vivid or suddenly lowers your psychedelic tolerance, treat that as a safety signal; that’s the psychedelic caution here. Do not take UMP on psychedelic days. And if you use psychedelics later, assume your previous dose may be too strong now, and that chasing the old visual threshold can overshoot.
