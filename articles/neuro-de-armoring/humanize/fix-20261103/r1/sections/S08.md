## Part 3: THE SHIELD (Flu-Proofing & Voltage Control)

The goal here is to stabilize voltage, so you don’t get the “Biological Crash” (Headaches/Panic).

### 1. Electrolytes (Choose Your Path)

If on Quick Fix, Strategy A (BHB Salts / Standard Diet):Sodium: Standard intake. DO NOT load extra salt (Risk of High Blood Pressure).

Potassium is how you install the “Brake Pads” for your nervous system. Your neurons have “brakes” called KCNQ channels (Potassium Channels) that stop them from firing uncontrollably, and these brakes run on Potassium ions. If you’re low on potassium, which is common in high stress, your brakes fail. You can push the pedal (breathe), but the car won’t stop.

So here’s the mandate: You must hit 4,700 mg of Potassium daily, and from food only. (Supplements are dangerous and limited to 99mg).

The Menu (The “Shield” Foods):

Potato (Baked with skin): ~925 mg (The King of Potassium).

Beet Greens (Cooked): ~1,300 mg per cup.

Salmon: ~1,000 mg per filet.

Avocado: ~700-900 mg.

Coconut Water: ~600 mg per cup.

In practice, that’s 2 large potatoes and 2 cups of greens every single day. It saturates your cells and lowers your “resting voltage,” so you become physically harder to trigger.

It protects the proteasome, so the cleanup machinery you’re working so hard to build doesn’t get broken down too early. Sulforaphane is also a potent Nrf2 activator, and if you’re in a “Glutamate Storm,” it helps clear oxidative stress.

NAC: 1.8g – 2.4g (divided doses). *Mops up glutamate.*

Agmatine Sulfate (Optional Trial): Start with 250–500 mg in the morning or early afternoon for 3 days. If it clearly reduces the hot/itchy/reactive state without flattening mood, use 500 mg once or twice daily. For this protocol, cap at 1 g/day unless clinician-supervised.

Best fit: Glutamate Storm + rumination/compulsion + inflammatory or neuropathic “pain-y” body state.

Cycle rule: use it as a 7–14 day anxiety-phase experiment, not a forever baseline. Stop if flatness, anhedonia, apathy, irritability, insomnia, dizziness, nausea, loose stools, diarrhea, or increased craving appears.

Timing rule: avoid agmatine on D-Serine/DCS-style reconsolidation days, or keep it at least 12–24 hours away from the session.
