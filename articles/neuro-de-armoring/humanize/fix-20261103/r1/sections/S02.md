## Psychology is the Software; Biology is the Hardware.

Most therapy works on the “code” (thoughts/behaviors) and leaves the “motherboard” alone, though the brain’s physical structure and chemistry are often overheating or damaged. So this protocol starts with four specific neuro-biological mechanisms, which must be addressed *before* the psychological tools can be effective.

Dr. Bruce Perry compares treating trauma to building a house. CBT & EMDR style therapies try to “decorate” the house by changing thoughts/behaviors in the Cortex, while the foundation (the Brainstem) is crumbling underneath. Good luck hanging curtains in a house that’s falling down.

The Modern “Role-Self” vs. The Ancient “True-Self”

Psychologist Lindsay Gibson has a good explanation for why the brain builds this armor in the first place: [*Adult children of emotionally immature parents*](https://www.amazon.com/Adult-Children-Emotionally-Immature-Parents/dp/B01F2L1HLM/ref=sr_1_1?crid=2JLWO51H6BXO1&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.WNWQbN2kbLNCbYq_W-fodAEeQ_UlnBi9Ju6cPln8M1TPg-RV-3-nRjn0Qj-b2kgxvix3vglikzPoFdr6m1bV4ZrTYX1qWRDNS0Dbk17dV3oK-KP3CXCX7QstfIkQGtiiLMJyKwT9N66J8RW_F6c7YoH8oGEsjPnyij0gNk5i-nnTMPGQa4VFhVtocMt46Fax3GwdCYOwIduJYQPc1LjZSgr9sS7b1bTey37wCvpdEnc.YYpzi2NWlm3O9D3YaGlV-1PGakmscQwxvmqh3imaiA8&dib_tag=se&keywords=children+of+emotionally+immature+parents&qid=1766678988&s=books&sprefix=children+of+emotionally+immature+parents%2Cstripbooks%2C395&sr=1-1) survive by building a “Role-Self”, a defensive personality whose job is to please people and manage the parent’s emotions. But we can’t just blame our immature parents for this Role Self. It really is true what they say: “It takes a village to raise a child,” and a big modern society where one or two people look after the children is nowhere near enough. As Dr. Bruce Perry explains, our brains evolved in hunter-gatherer bands with a ratio of 4 developmentally mature adults to 1 child.

Today, our “enriched” environments offer 1 immature adult to 4 children. That’s less than 1/24th of the relational density our biology is built for, so when people tell me about the “Void” they feel, I take it literally. Once you’ve de-armored, you can’t go back to a lonely life: you must aggressively engineer a “High-Density” social environment (a tribe), or the brain will eventually reach for the armor again just to survive the cold.

As we grow up, our “Role-Self” becomes calcified by Dopamine and DeltaFosB. It becomes a prison. A lot of the time the addiction is just what keeps the Role-Self running, and the protocol is meant to dissolve the chemistry holding that false persona together, so that your “True-Self”, the person you were before the trauma, can come back.

That’s why I call Neurological De-Armoring underpinning. The work happens under the house: modulating Glutamate and introducing Ketones (BHB) act like an HDAC inhibitor, which softens the neural concrete of the brainstem, and then the psychological “software” updates upstairs can install.

Dr. Perry also says we need to temporarily give up on the “hour-long breakthrough” that therapy is supposed to deliver. If your nervous system is sensitized and armored, sixty minutes of trauma processing is more than it can take in, and it floods. According to his data, the “therapeutic window” for a dysregulated brain is often only seconds long.

So we go for tiny doses of safety instead, like 30 seconds of regulated breathing, twenty times a day. That’s closer to physical therapy for a broken nervous system, where frequency counts for more than intensity.
