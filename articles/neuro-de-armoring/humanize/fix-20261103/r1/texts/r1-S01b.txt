Agmatine Warning (The Voltage Governor): unlike magnesium, a basic nutrient, or phosphatidylcholine, a membrane nutrient, agmatine is a multi-target neuromodulator. It can affect NMDA/glutamate signaling, imidazoline/alpha-2 adrenergic tone, nitric-oxide pathways, and opioid reward/tolerance.

So without clinician review, do not combine agmatine casually with opioids, kratom, clonidine/guanfacine, blood-pressure medication, nitrates, PDE-5 inhibitors, MAOIs, or complex psychiatric medication stacks. Avoid it during pregnancy or breastfeeding too, and be especially cautious with it if you have bipolar disorder, a psychosis history, unstable panic, low blood pressure, kidney/liver disease, or an active substance relapse risk.

Stop immediately if it causes flatness, anhedonia, emotional blunting, new depression, agitation, insomnia, dizziness, palpitations, nausea, loose stools, diarrhea, or increased craving, and do not try to “push through” the numbness. If the world gets more gray on agmatine, it’s the wrong tool for that phase.

Uridine & 5-HTP Warning for Psychedelic Users: these aren’t “trip enhancers.” They’re repair-oriented levers, and they can change how sensitive your brain is quickly. 5-HTP is serotonergic, since it’s a direct serotonin precursor, so do not combine it with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, linezolid, lithium, tramadol, methadone, fentanyl, DXM, MDMA, St. John’s wort, SAM-e, L-tryptophan, or other serotonin-raising stacks without clinician review. Be especially conservative around ayahuasca / harmalas, because MAO-A inhibition can amplify serotonin load, and around iboga / ibogaine, because ibogaine and noribogaine interact with serotonin transport biology and already carry serious cardiac and neurological risk.

In practice that means: do not take 5-HTP on the same day as psychedelics, and do not use it anywhere near ayahuasca/harmalas or iboga unless the whole medicine plan has been medically reviewed. Uridine isn’t serotonergic, but it may still change dream vividness, visual sensitivity, craving, and psychedelic tolerance. Do not take UMP on the same day as psychedelics either, and if your sensitivity changes, assume your old psychedelic dose may now be too high.
