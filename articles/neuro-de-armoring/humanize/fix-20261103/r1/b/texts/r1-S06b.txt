To trial it: UMP 150–300 mg in the morning with food for 3–7 days. Keep omega-3s in place, especially DHA/EPA, and make sure there’s enough choline from eggs, phosphatidylcholine, CDP-choline, or another choline source you tolerate. Do not start UMP on the same day you start 5-HTP, agmatine, D-serine, keto, iboga, shrooms, LSD, ketamine, or any other major variable, since you’re trying to see whether UMP itself changes the signal.

The most common problem is the “choline headache” or a spaced-out brain fog: if UMP accelerates membrane synthesis while choline availability is low, the system can feel under-supplied. Other stop signs include insomnia, jitteriness, racing thoughts, irritability, emotional flatness, nausea, stomach cramps, or loose stools. If it makes you wired, numb, or weirdly irritable, stop. Do not push through.

If UMP makes your dreams vivid or suddenly lowers your psychedelic tolerance, treat that as a safety signal; that’s the psychedelic caution here. Do not take UMP on psychedelic days. And if you use psychedelics later, assume your previous dose may be too strong now, and that chasing the old visual threshold can overshoot.
