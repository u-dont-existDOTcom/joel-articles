Tetris needs an app or a phone. The “Color-Path Connect” method doesn’t, and it’s more effective than simple counting, because it forces your brain to map 3D space, which competes directly with the visual “saving” of a traumatic memory.

How to play:Pick a color (e.g., Blue).

Trace a Path: Find every blue object in the room and mentally draw a line connecting them from left to right.

Add Complexity: Now, mentally “rotate” those objects in your mind or imagine them changing into a different shape (e.g., “That blue chair is now a blue cube”).

You must do this for 10–20 minutes immediately after a trigger to “jam” the memory reconsolidation.
