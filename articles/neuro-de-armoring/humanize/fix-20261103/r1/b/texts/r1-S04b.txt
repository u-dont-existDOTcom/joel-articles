D. The “Lagging” System: Toxicity & Hypometabolism (The Fog)

“Brain Fog” is often a fuel crisis. Trauma causes cerebral insulin resistance, so your neurons are swimming in glucose (sugar) and can’t burn it, like a flooded engine.

If the brain can’t burn sugar, we switch fuels: PQQ to boost ATP, and Ketones, which bypass the broken glucose pathways and enter the mitochondria directly. This turns the lights back on immediately. We alternate hot and cold showers to boost p131 and heat shock proteins and reduce inflammation.

E. The “Save Button”: Memory Reconsolidation

Every time you have a flashback, your brain tries to “save” the memory again, and that often reinforces the emotional trauma loop.

“The Jammer” works because restoring a memory takes visual processing power: if you keep your Visual-Spatial Working Memory busy (playing Tetris, for example) during the critical window after a flashback, the brain fails to “save” the visual details. The memory is restored, but the emotional “sting” is permanently reduced.
