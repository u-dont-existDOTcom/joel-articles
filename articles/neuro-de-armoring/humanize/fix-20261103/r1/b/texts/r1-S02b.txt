As we grow up, our “Role-Self” becomes calcified by Dopamine and DeltaFosB. It becomes a prison. A lot of the time the addiction is just what keeps the Role-Self running, and the protocol is meant to dissolve the chemistry holding that false persona together, so that your “True-Self”, the person you were before the trauma, can come back.

That’s why I call Neurological De-Armoring underpinning. The work happens under the house: modulating Glutamate and introducing Ketones (BHB) act like an HDAC inhibitor, which softens the neural concrete of the brainstem, and then the psychological “software” updates upstairs can install.

Dr. Perry also says we need to temporarily give up on the “hour-long breakthrough” that therapy is supposed to deliver. If your nervous system is sensitized and armored, sixty minutes of trauma processing is more than it can take in, and it floods. According to his data, the “therapeutic window” for a dysregulated brain is often only seconds long.

So we go for tiny doses of safety instead, like 30 seconds of regulated breathing, twenty times a day. That’s closer to physical therapy for a broken nervous system, where frequency counts for more than intensity.
