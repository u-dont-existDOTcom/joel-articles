Part 1: THE SCIENCE (Why We Do This)

Before applying the tools, you must understand the specific “Hardware Failures” that occur during trauma, withdrawal, or chronic stress.

A. The Metabolic Red Line

We tend to assume we can “think” our way out of a trigger, and the data says otherwise: anatomy overrides intellect. Dr. Perry describes a “Metabolic Red Line” for the traumatized brain. When the resting heart rate is elevated or heart-rate-variability reduced (a state of “Fear”), the brain physically shunts blood flow away from the Cortex (Logic/Reason) and down to the Brainstem (Survival).

Functional IQ drops substantially in that state. Worse, it floods the Nucleus Accumbens with the exact cocktail of catecholamines that transcribes new DeltaFosB proteins (the “armor”). So trying to process trauma while you’re living above this red line actively re-enforces the armor.

B. The “Overheating” CPU: Glutamate Excitotoxicity

Glutamate is the brain’s primary excitatory neurotransmitter, and under stress the brain floods with it, the “voltage spike” where the engine is redlining.
