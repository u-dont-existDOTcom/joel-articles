Uridine & 5-HTP Warning for Psychedelic Users: Neither one is a “trip enhancer.” They’re repair tools, and either one can shift how sensitive your brain is fast. 5-HTP turns directly into serotonin, so stacking it with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, linezolid, lithium, tramadol, methadone, fentanyl, DXM, MDMA, St. John’s wort, SAM-e, L-tryptophan, or anything else that pushes serotonin up is off the table unless your prescriber has gone over the combination. Ayahuasca and harmalas are a bigger problem still, since their MAO-A inhibition can pile more serotonin on top, and so are iboga and ibogaine, where ibogaine and noribogaine act on serotonin transport and already come with serious heart and neurological risk.
