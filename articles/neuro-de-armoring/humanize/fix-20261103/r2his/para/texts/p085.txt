Side effects / stop signs: Most common is the “choline headache” or spaced-out brain fog (if UMP accelerates membrane synthesis while choline availability is low, the system can feel under-supplied). Other stop signs include insomnia, jitteriness, racing thoughts, irritability, emotional flatness, nausea, stomach cramps, or loose stools. Stop if it makes you wired, numb, or weirdly irritable, and do not push through.
