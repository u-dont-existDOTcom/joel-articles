Uridine & 5-HTP Warning for Psychedelic Users: Not to be used as “trip enhancers” (these are repair-oriented levers that can change brain sensitivity quickly). 5-HTP is a direct serotonin precursor, and therefore serotonergic: do not combine with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, linezolid, lithium, tramadol, methadone, fentanyl, DXM, MDMA, St. John’s wort, SAM-e, L-tryptophan, or other serotonin-raising stacks without clinician review. Use extra conservatively around ayahuasca and/or harmalas (MAO-A inhibition can amplify serotonin load), and iboga and/or ibogaine (ibogaine and noribogaine interact with serotonin transport biology, and already carry serious cardiac and neurological risk).
