Agmatine Warning (The Voltage Governor): Agmatine is a multi-target neuromodulator, not a basic nutrient like magnesium or a membrane nutrient like phosphatidylcholine, and it can affect NMDA/glutamate signaling, imidazoline/alpha-2 adrenergic tone, nitric-oxide pathways, and opioid reward/tolerance.

Stop taking it immediately if it causes flatness, anhedonia, emotional blunting, new depression, agitation, insomnia, dizziness, palpitations, nausea, loose stools, diarrhea, or increased craving. Don’t try to “push through” the numbness, since if agmatine makes the world more gray, it’s the wrong tool for that phase.

Uridine & 5-HTP Warning for Psychedelic Users: These are repair-oriented levers that can change brain sensitivity quickly, so don’t treat them as “trip enhancers.” 5-HTP is serotonergic, since it’s a direct serotonin precursor, so do not combine it with serotonin-raising stacks (like SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, linezolid, lithium, tramadol, methadone, fentanyl, DXM, MDMA, St. John’s wort, SAM-e, or L-tryptophan) without clinician review. Be especially conservative around ayahuasca/harmalas (MAO-A inhibition can amplify serotonin load), and around iboga/ibogaine, since ibogaine and noribogaine interact with serotonin transport biology, on top of already carrying serious cardiac and neurological risk.

Rule: don’t take 5-HTP on the same day as psychedelics, and don’t use it anywhere near ayahuasca/harmalas or iboga unless the whole medicine plan has been medically reviewed. Uridine may still change dream vividness, visual sensitivity, craving, and psychedelic tolerance even though it isn’t serotonergic, so do not take UMP on the same day as psychedelics either, and if sensitivity changes, assume your old psychedelic dose may now be too high.
