Who this is for: This is not for everyone with depression, only for a specific phenotype, the “burned-out dopamine receptor” pattern (gray anhedonia, weak dreams, weak psychedelic visuals, needing unusually high doses to feel anything, compulsive reaching for ketamine/LSD/novelty just to get a hit), and especially for people with a history of dopamine agonist withdrawal from drugs like pramipexole or ropinirole. The medical literature calls this [dopamine agonist withdrawal syndrome](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23686524/) (DAWS), and describes what happens after dopamine-agonist tapering (anxiety, panic, dysphoria, depression, agitation, fatigue, autonomic symptoms, pain, and drug cravings). In Scott’s case it looked like DAWS with D2/D3 reward-system downregulation, made worse by frequent LSD microdosing and ketamine attempts to force dopamine hits.
