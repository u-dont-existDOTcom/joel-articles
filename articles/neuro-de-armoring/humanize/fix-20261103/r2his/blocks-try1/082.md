The tool: Uridine monophosphate (UMP). There’s no study showing that “UMP cures DAWS,” so the best evidence is the more basic fact that uridine, choline, and DHA are circulating precursors for brain phosphatide / synaptic membrane synthesis. Wurtman’s group has shown that [DHA + uridine + choline can increase synaptic membrane components and synaptic proteins](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4011061/), that [DHA-induced dendritic spine formation is enhanced by UMP](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2140951/) in animals, and that [dietary UMP increased potassium-evoked dopamine release and neurite outgrowth in aged rats](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16055952/), so the mechanism is plausible, but the DAWS use is still a cautious human extrapolation plus anecdote.
