Uridine & 5-HTP Warning for Psychedelic Users: These are repair-oriented levers that can change brain sensitivity quickly, so don’t treat them as “trip enhancers.” 5-HTP is serotonergic, since it’s a direct serotonin precursor, so do not combine it with serotonin-raising stacks (like SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, linezolid, lithium, tramadol, methadone, fentanyl, DXM, MDMA, St. John’s wort, SAM-e, or L-tryptophan) without clinician review. Be especially conservative around ayahuasca/harmalas (MAO-A inhibition can amplify serotonin load), and around iboga/ibogaine, since ibogaine and noribogaine interact with serotonin transport biology, on top of already carrying serious cardiac and neurological risk.
