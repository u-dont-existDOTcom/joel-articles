Side effects / stop signs: The most common problem is the “choline headache” or spaced-out brain fog, since if UMP accelerates membrane synthesis while choline availability is low, the system can feel under-supplied. Other stop signs include insomnia, jitteriness, racing thoughts, irritability, emotional flatness, nausea, stomach cramps, or loose stools, and if it makes you wired, numb, or weirdly irritable, stop taking it, and don’t push through.
