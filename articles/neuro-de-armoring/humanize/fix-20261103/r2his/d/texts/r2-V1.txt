Agmatine Warning (The Voltage Governor): Agmatine isn’t a basic nutrient like magnesium, or a membrane nutrient like phosphatidylcholine. It’s a neuromodulator that works on several different systems at once (NMDA/glutamate signaling, imidazoline/alpha-2 adrenergic tone, nitric-oxide pathways, and opioid reward/tolerance).

Stop taking it right away if you notice flatness, anhedonia, emotional blunting, new depression, agitation, insomnia, dizziness, palpitations, nausea, loose stools or diarrhea, or more craving than usual. Don’t try to “push through” the numbness either. If agmatine makes the world more gray, it’s just the wrong tool for that phase of the protocol.

Uridine & 5-HTP Warning for Psychedelic Users: Don’t think of these as “trip enhancers.” They’re repair-oriented, and they can change how sensitive your brain is pretty quickly. 5-HTP is a direct serotonin precursor, which makes it serotonergic, so do not combine it with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, linezolid, lithium, tramadol, methadone, fentanyl, DXM, MDMA, St. John’s wort, SAM-e, L-tryptophan, or any other serotonin-raising stack without clinician review. With ayahuasca/harmalas I’d be extra conservative, because the MAO-A inhibition can amplify the serotonin load, and the same with iboga/ibogaine, since ibogaine and noribogaine interact with serotonin transport biology, and on top of that already carry serious cardiac and neurological risk.

So as a rule, do not take 5-HTP on the same day as psychedelics, and do not use it anywhere near ayahuasca/harmalas or iboga unless someone has medically reviewed the whole medicine plan. Uridine isn’t serotonergic, but it can still change your dream vividness, visual sensitivity, craving, and psychedelic tolerance, so do not take UMP on the same day as psychedelics either. And if your sensitivity changes, assume your old psychedelic dose may now be too high.
