Side effects / stop signs: The most common problem is what’s called the “choline headache,” or a spaced-out brain fog, which can happen if UMP speeds up membrane synthesis while your choline availability is low, so the system can feel under-supplied. Other stop signs include insomnia, jitteriness, racing thoughts, irritability, emotional flatness, nausea, stomach cramps, or loose stools. If it makes you wired, numb, or weirdly irritable, stop taking it, and do not push through it.
