Who this is for: Uridine is not for everyone with depression. This is for a specific “burned-out dopamine receptor” pattern, where everything feels gray (anhedonia), dreams and psychedelic visuals are weak, it takes unusually high doses to feel anything, and you keep compulsively reaching for ketamine/LSD/novelty just to get a hit, especially if you have a history of dopamine agonist withdrawal from drugs like pramipexole or ropinirole. In the medical literature it’s called [dopamine agonist withdrawal syndrome](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23686524/) (DAWS), and they describe anxiety, panic, dysphoria, depression, agitation, fatigue, autonomic symptoms, pain, and drug cravings after people taper off dopamine agonists. Scott’s case looked like DAWS with D2/D3 reward-system downregulation, which then got worse from frequent LSD microdosing and from trying to force dopamine hits with ketamine. That’s a pretty specific phenotype, and I’m not saying everyone needs dopamine.
