The Problem: To protect itself from the Glutamate storm, the brain eventually releases Dynorphin, a chemical blanket that suppresses dopamine. This creates the "Void"--a state of apathy, grayness, and an inability to feel pleasure. The two aren't one mechanism, though: DeltaFosB accumulates inside the dynorphin neurons themselves, and what it does there is turn dynorphin down, not up ([Zachariou, et al, 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16415864/)).
