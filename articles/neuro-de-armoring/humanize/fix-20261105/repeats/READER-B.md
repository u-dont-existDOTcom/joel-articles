## 2. Contradictions

1. **Four mechanisms or five.** B20 says "four specific neuro-biological mechanisms", but Part 1 then runs from A to E, ending at B55: `E. The "Save Button": Memory Reconsolidation`. A reader who counts will notice.

2. **How certain the DeltaFosB link is.** B32: "data proves that anatomy overrides intellect". B33: "this state floods the Nucleus Accumbens with the exact cocktail of catecholamines that *transcribes* new DeltaFosB proteins". Then B36: "DeltaFosB may be the master switch, but the research isn't settled." The topic is the same and the blocks are three apart, but the certainty is opposite and nothing says why. But B140 says "Rhythmic First, Visual Second", B142 has 5 minutes of rhythm first, and B143 says "Once your heart rate is within normal range, you cerebral cortex is receptive." Nothing says how "immediately" fits with the wait.

5. **Which iboga for numbness.** B132: `Female Iboga: For "Numbness/Fog" (Anima).` B182: "*If Numb:* Micro-dose Male Iboga".

6. **Mushroom microdose amount.** For the same strains, B134 gives "(0.1g - 0.2g)" and B163 gives "(100-300mg daily)". The upper limit is 200 mg in one and 300 mg in the other.

7. **Where therapy sessions fit.** B21 says `Standard CBT & EMDR style therapies attempt to "decorate" the house`, and B27 says `we need to temporarily abandon the therapy ideal of the "hour-long breakthrough."` But B146, inside the acute Glutamate Storm scenario, says D-serine before a de-armoring session `can cement the benefits of therapies like EMDR`.
