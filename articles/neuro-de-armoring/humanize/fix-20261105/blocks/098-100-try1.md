Strategy B, the "Resilience Mode", is a keto diet. Carbs stay under 20g/day so the liver makes the ketones itself. Fat becomes your fuel, so eat it until you're full. Don't rely on lean protein like chicken breast alone, because your body turns the extra protein into sugar through gluconeogenesis, and that keeps you out of ketosis.
