Strategy B, the "Resilience Mode", is the slow one. It's for the long run: a keto diet, with carbs under 20g/day so your liver makes the ketones itself. That only works if fat becomes your fuel, so eat fat until you're full. Don't rely on lean protein like chicken breast alone, because your body turns the extra protein into sugar through gluconeogenesis, and that keeps you out of ketosis. Switching over can bring on side effects for a while, and to avoid them, refer to my [Keto Adaptation Guide](http://ketoflu.u-dont-exist.com/).
