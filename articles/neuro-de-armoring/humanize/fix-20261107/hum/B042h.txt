The Repair (L-Serine): Your brain makes D-Serine out of L-Serine (Wolosker, et al, 1999), so taking L-Serine feeds the NMDA spot, and maybe the GluD channels too, that D-serine switches on, which is what helps a session that ends with low fear and some mastery stick. The conversion isn't very efficient though, so some people take D-serine itself instead (more risk, but also more benefit).
