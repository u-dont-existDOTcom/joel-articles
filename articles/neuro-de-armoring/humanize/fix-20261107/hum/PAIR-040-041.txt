The Fix ("Lock, Stock, and Barrel"):The Brake (NAC): NAC hands the brain's support cells cystine, an amino acid they swap out for glutamate, and in rats that glutamate sitting outside the synapse trips a brake, the mGluR2/3 receptors, which cut how much glutamate gets fired in (Moran, et al, 2005).

The Lock (Zinc/Magnesium): Magnesium sits in the NMDA channel and plugs it while the cell is resting (Jalali-Yazdi, et al, 2018), and zinc mostly makes the channel open less often, with a weaker plug of its own (Amico-Ruvio, et al, 2011), so less of the voltage gets through either way.
