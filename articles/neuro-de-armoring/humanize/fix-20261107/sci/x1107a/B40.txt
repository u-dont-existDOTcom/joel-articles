The Fix ("Lock, Stock, and Barrel"):The Vacuum (NAC): It works less like a vacuum than a brake. NAC gives the brain's support cells cystine (an amino acid), which they swap for glutamate, and in rat studies that glutamate outside the synapse trips a brake (the mGluR2/3 receptors) that cuts how much glutamate gets fired into the synapse (Moran, et al, 2005).
