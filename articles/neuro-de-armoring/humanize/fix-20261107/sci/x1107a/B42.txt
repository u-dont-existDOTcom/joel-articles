The Repair (L-Serine): L-Serine is what the brain makes D-Serine from (Wolosker, et al, 1999), so it can feed the spot on the NMDA receptor (and maybe the GluD channels) that D-serine switches on, and help therapy sessions that end with low fear and a sense of mastery stick better. The conversion isn't very efficient though, so some people take D-serine itself instead (more risk, but also more benefit).
