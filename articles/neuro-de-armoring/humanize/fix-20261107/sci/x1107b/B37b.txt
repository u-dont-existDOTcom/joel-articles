The Mechanism: Excess Glutamate over-activates NMDA receptors, the doors on brain cells that glutamate unlocks, acting like a key stuck in the "On" position, so too much calcium pours into the cells. In extreme cases, like a stroke or a brain injury, that calcium overload kills them, which is what "excitotoxicity" means (Sattler & Tymianski, 2001). What you feel from a glutamate storm depends on which circuit is firing too hard, and under stress the rise reaches the amygdala, the part of the brain that flags threats (Wilson, 2015), so the feeling it produces is threat: anxiety, rage, the "itchy brain", not pleasure. Block the NMDA receptors there and anxious behaviour goes down in animals (Boyle, 2013).
