The Mechanism: When Glutamate floods in, it over-activates NMDA receptors, like a key stuck in the "On" position, so too much calcium pours into the cells, and in extreme cases like a stroke or a seizure that kills them (that's "excitotoxicity"). The anxiety, rage or "itchy brain" you feel in a glutamate storm is more likely that over-excitement itself, not a sign your neurons are dying.
