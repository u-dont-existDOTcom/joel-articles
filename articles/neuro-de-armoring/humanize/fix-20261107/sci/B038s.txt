The Signal Dial (GluD Receptors): So if NMDA is the spark, is there anything else turning the signal up or down? Well, Johns Hopkins researchers recently found that GluD receptors, which were mainly known for helping build synapses, are channels in their own right, opened by D-serine (one of the tools below) and GABA rather than by glutamate. A mutation that causes cerebellar ataxia leaves that channel leaking, stuck partly on, and it's only a hypothesis so far, one nobody has tested, that an armored state could jam it too, on "High" as hyper-vigilance or on "Low" as apathy or schizotypy.
