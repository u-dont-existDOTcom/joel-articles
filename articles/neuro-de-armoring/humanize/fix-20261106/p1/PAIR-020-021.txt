If you've done a lot of therapy and still react the same way, the therapy may not be the problem. Standard therapy tries to rewrite the "code" (your thoughts and behaviors), but if the "motherboard" (the brain's physical structure and chemistry) is overheating or damaged, the new code won't run. So the science behind this protocol looks at four specific neuro-biological problems that must be addressed before psychological tools can really work.

The "Underpinning" Principle: Dr. Bruce Perry compares treating trauma to building a house. CBT, EMDR and similar therapies try to "decorate" the house, changing thoughts and behaviors in the Cortex, while the foundation, the Brainstem, is crumbling, and you can't hang curtains in a house that's falling down.
