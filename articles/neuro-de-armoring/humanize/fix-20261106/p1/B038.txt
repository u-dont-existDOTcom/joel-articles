The Signal Dial (GluD Receptors): There's also a second dial. Johns Hopkins researchers have recently discovered that the brain uses GluD receptors like a master volume knob: NMDA is the spark, and GluD sets the baseline "hum" of the synapse. In an armored state, this dial is often jammed, either on "High" (Cerebellar Ataxia/Hyper-vigilance) or on "Low" (Apathy/Schizotypy).
