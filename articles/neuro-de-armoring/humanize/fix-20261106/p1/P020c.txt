Maybe you've done years of CBT or EMDR and you still freeze up or blow up at the same old triggers. Standard therapy is trying to rewrite the "code" (your thoughts and behaviors), while the "motherboard" (the brain's physical structure and chemistry) is often overheating or damaged, and code doesn't run well on a motherboard like that. This protocol goes after four specific neuro-biological problems that must be addressed before psychological tools can do much.
