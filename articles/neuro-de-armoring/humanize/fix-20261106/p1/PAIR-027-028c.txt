The Anti-Breakthrough (Micro-Dosing): Long therapy sessions that leave you wrecked for days aren't doing what they're supposed to. Dr. Perry found the "therapeutic window" for a dysregulated brain is often only seconds long, and a sensitized, armored nervous system just can't take in sixty minutes of trauma processing, so the "hour-long breakthrough" has to wait.

So the goal is Micro-Dosing Safety: 30 seconds of regulated breathing, twenty times a day. It's more like physical therapy for a broken nervous system than running a marathon, and how often you do it matters more than how hard.
