The Anti-Breakthrough (Micro-Dosing): If long therapy sessions leave you more overwhelmed than helped, that's actually normal. Dr. Perry explains that a dysregulated brain's "therapeutic window" is often only seconds long, so a sensitized, armored nervous system just can't take in sixty minutes of trauma processing. So for now we need to give up the therapy ideal of the "hour-long breakthrough."

So the goal is Micro-Dosing Safety: 30 seconds of regulated breathing, twenty times a day. It's more like physical therapy for a broken nervous system than running a marathon, and how often you do it matters more than how hard.
