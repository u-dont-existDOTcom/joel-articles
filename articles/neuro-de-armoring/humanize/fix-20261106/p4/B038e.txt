The Signal Dial (GluD Receptors): Johns Hopkins researchers recently discovered that the brain also uses GluD receptors as a kind of master volume knob, so if NMDA is the spark, GluD sets the baseline "hum" of the synapse. In an armored state that dial often gets jammed, either on "High" (Cerebellar Ataxia/Hyper-vigilance) or on "Low" (Apathy/Schizotypy).
