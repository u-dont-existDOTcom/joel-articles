The Anti-Breakthrough (Micro-Dosing): Dr. Perry found that for a dysregulated brain, the "therapeutic window" is often only seconds long, so a sensitized, armored nervous system just can't take in sixty minutes of trauma processing, which is why long sessions can leave you wrecked for days. So for now, we give up on the "hour-long breakthrough."

Instead, aim for Micro-Dosing Safety: 30 seconds of regulated breathing, twenty times a day. It's more like physical therapy for a broken nervous system than running a marathon, so how often you do it matters more than how hard you push.
