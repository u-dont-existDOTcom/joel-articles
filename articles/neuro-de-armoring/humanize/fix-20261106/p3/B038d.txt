The Signal Dial (GluD Receptors): NMDA isn't the only control, though. Johns Hopkins researchers have recently discovered that the brain also uses GluD receptors as a kind of master volume knob, so if NMDA is the spark, GluD sets the baseline "hum" of the synapse. And in an armored state, that dial often gets jammed, either on "High" (Cerebellar Ataxia/Hyper-vigilance) or on "Low" (Apathy/Schizotypy).
