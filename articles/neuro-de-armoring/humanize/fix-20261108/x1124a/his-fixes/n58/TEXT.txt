For the zinc, I'd use zinc carnosine, up to 75 mg daily (16 mg elemental zinc), which is a standard dose. This form also helps repair leaky gut (Mahmood, et al, 2007), and it's better tolerated than other forms of zinc, but if it causes nausea, take less at a time. Zinc should be taken with copper (1–2 mg elemental; avoid cupric oxide, and use copper gluconate or citrate). For L-serine, I'd do 5–15 g, depending how it feels. Up to 30 g has been used in trials for neurodegenerative conditions (Levine, et al, 2017), but with more GI distress at higher doses.
