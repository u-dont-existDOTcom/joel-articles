DMSO (99.9%): Smell it, as in the Fog table below. In rat brain cells in a dish, it quickly turned down the NMDA and AMPA glutamate signals and the calcium they let in, and kept glutamate from killing the cells (Lu & Mattson, 2001). In another lab, the same strengths killed rat brain cells in a dish on their own, and DMSO injected into young mice set off widespread cell death in their brains (Hanslick, et al, 2009), so keep it away from pregnancy and children. How much reaches your brain when you smell it hasn't been measured, and no trial has tested it for anxiety or PTSD, though many people say it helps.
