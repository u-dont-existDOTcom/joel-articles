## SC-DMSO: DMSO in the Glutamate Shield: what it does to glutamate, and who should keep away

*New item after 2. The Glutamate Shield (Anxiety):, P9*

**As it would read:**

> DMSO (99.9%): Smell it, as in the Fog table below. In rat brain cells in a dish, DMSO at 0.5 to 1.5% quickly turned down the signals glutamate sends through its NMDA and AMPA receptors, and the calcium they let in. It also kept glutamate from killing the cells (Lu & Mattson, 2001). In another lab, DMSO at 0.5% and 1% killed rat brain cells in a dish by itself, and injected into young mice, it set off widespread cell death in their brains (Hanslick, et al, 2009). So keep it away from pregnancy and children. How much reaches your brain when you smell it hasn't been measured. No trial has tested it for anxiety or PTSD, so whether it helps rests on what people report, and many say it does.
