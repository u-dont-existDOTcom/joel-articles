THE TEXT:
S1: DMSO (99.9%): Smell it, as in the Fog table below.
S2: In rat brain cells in a dish, DMSO at 0.5 to 1.5% quickly turned down the signals glutamate sends through its NMDA and AMPA receptors, and the calcium they let in.
S3: It also kept glutamate from killing the cells (Lu & Mattson, 2001).
S4: In people, a 1967 study in Lima gave DMSO, injected into the muscle, to 42 psychiatric inpatients, nine of them with severe anxiety or obsessive-compulsive disorder.
S5: They grew calmer within days, and the acute cases left the hospital sooner than similar patients on the usual treatments of that time (Ramírez & Luza, 1967).
S6: There was no placebo, though, and the patients they were compared with weren't picked at random.
S7: But in a second dish study, DMSO at 0.5% and 1% killed rat brain cells by itself, and injected into young mice, it set off widespread cell death in their brains (Hanslick, et al, 2009).
S8: So keep it away from pregnancy and children.
S9: How much reaches your brain when you smell it hasn't been measured, and no trial has tested it for PTSD, so for that, it rests on what people report, and many say it helps.
