THE TEXT:
S1: DMSO (99.9%): Smell it, as in the Fog table below.
S2: In rat brain cells in a dish, DMSO at 0.5 to 1.5% quickly turned down the signals glutamate sends through its NMDA and AMPA receptors, and the calcium they let in.
S3: It also kept glutamate from killing the cells (Lu & Mattson, 2001).
S4: In another lab, DMSO at 0.5% and 1% killed rat brain cells in a dish by itself, and injected into young mice, it set off widespread cell death in their brains (Hanslick, et al, 2009).
S5: So keep it away from pregnancy and children.
S6: How much reaches your brain when you smell it hasn't been measured.
S7: No trial has tested it for anxiety or PTSD, so whether it helps rests on what people report, and many say it does.
