## His study, checked

- [Lu & Mattson, 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11421595/) (Exp Neurol; doi 10.1006/exnr.2001.7686; abstract):
  in cultured rat hippocampal neurons, DMSO at 0.5 to 1.5% (the strengths "to which neurons are typically exposed in
  experimental studies and in human patients") suppressed, "in a rapidly reversible manner", the currents and calcium
  influx set off by glutamate, NMDA and AMPA, and prevented excitotoxic death. It says what he says it does, in a dish.

## The other side

- [Hanslick, et al, 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19100327/) (Neurobiol Dis; doi 10.1016/j.nbd.2008.11.006):
  DMSO injected into mice from birth to 30 days old set off widespread cell death in the brain, at doses from 0.3 ml/kg;
  in cultured rat hippocampal neurons, DMSO at 0.5% and 1.0% killed neurons on its own.
- Rats exposed as infants (PMID 33838015, 2021): lasting changes in behavior and glia; cultured astrocytes (PMID
  25238609, 2014): mitochondria damaged; tau hyperphosphorylation in cells and, through hypothermia, in mice (PMID
  22768202, 2012).
- In people, given into a vein with frozen stem cells: rare seizures, strokes and encephalopathy (PMID 17353475, 2007;
  PMID 28121745, 2017).

## In people, other uses

- Its approved use is in the bladder, for interstitial cystitis; on the skin it gives quick, short pain relief; side
  effects are common but mostly mild and grow with strength (skin rash and itch, stomach upset by mouth, red cells
  breaking down when given into a vein); it carries other chemicals through the skin; its safety with other drugs isn't
  established ([Swanson, 1985](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3916302/)).
- Into a vein after head injury, small uncontrolled series lowered pressure in the skull; that alone doesn't show lasting
  benefit ([Huang & Wang, 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42939788/), a review); one series of six found it hard
  to give safely (fluid and salt problems) and cautioned against it (PMID 6462399, 1984).
- How much reaches the brain when it is smelled: no study found.
