Lion's Mane & Ashwagandha: I'm not advising the mushroom lion's mane or the herb ashwagandha, even though they're popular for focus and stress, because some people report anhedonia (nothing feels good) and sexual dysfunction that hang around even after they stop taking them. What people call post-ashwa or post-lion's mane syndrome is rare, but it's the same kind of thing SSRI antidepressants and the hair-loss drug finasteride can do, so I'd keep it in mind.
