So if you try to process trauma while you're above this red line, you're adding to the armor instead of taking it off. Above the line your functional IQ drops a lot, and stress chemicals called catecholamines flood the Nucleus Accumbens, a reward center deep in the brain. There they set off the making of DeltaFosB, a protein that may be what holds the armor in place, though the research on that isn't settled. Only work on your trauma while you're under that line, which trauma therapists call staying in your "window of tolerance".
