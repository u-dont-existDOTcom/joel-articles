If you try to process trauma while you're above this red line, you're adding to the armor instead of taking it off. Above the line your functional IQ drops a lot, and stress chemicals called catecholamines flood the Nucleus Accumbens, a reward center deep in the brain, where they build up DeltaFosB, the protein this protocol treats as the armor itself, though research hasn't settled whether it is. Only work on your trauma while you're under that line (what trauma therapists call your "window of tolerance").
