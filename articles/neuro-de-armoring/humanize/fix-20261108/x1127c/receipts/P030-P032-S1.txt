To see what the tools in this protocol do, let's look first at what goes wrong in the brain under trauma, withdrawal or chronic stress, since that's what they're built to fix.

If you've tried to talk yourself out of a trigger and couldn't, it's because blood flow in your brain shifts away from the Cortex, which handles logic and reasoning, toward the Brainstem, the part that keeps you alive. That happens once your resting heart rate is higher than usual, or the time between beats varies less than usual (a smartwatch shows both), and it lasts until you calm down. Perry describes the point where that happens as a "Metabolic Red Line" for the traumatized brain, and calls the state past it "Fear".
