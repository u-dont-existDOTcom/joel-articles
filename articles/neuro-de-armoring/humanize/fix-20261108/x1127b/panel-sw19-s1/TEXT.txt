So the emptiness you may feel (the "Void") is real, since it's what that missing company feels like. Once you've de-armored, going back to a lonely life will feel worse than it did with the armor on. Gather a tribe around you, people you're close to and see most days, or your brain will eventually reach for the armor again just to survive the cold.
