That works out to about 1/16th of the grown-ups per child that our brains evolved with (Perry, 2023). So the emptiness you feel (the "Void") is real, since it's what that missing company feels like. Once you've de-armored, going back to a lonely life will tend to feel worse than it did with the armor on. So gather a tribe of people you're close to and who you see most days, or you'll eventually put the armor back on just to get through the loneliness.
