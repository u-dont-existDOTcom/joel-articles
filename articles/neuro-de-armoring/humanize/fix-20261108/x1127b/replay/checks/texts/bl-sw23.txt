The Anti-Breakthrough (Micro-Dosing): A brain stuck in alarm can often take in healing work for only a few seconds at a time, Perry finds. So for now we have to give up on the hour-long breakthrough that therapy aims for, since an hour spent working on the trauma itself overwhelms an armored nervous system more than it heals it. Instead, this protocol works in doses of a few seconds, which is another kind of micro-dosing, with healing work instead of a drug.

In practice, I'd do something like 30 seconds of slow breathing, about 20 times a day.
