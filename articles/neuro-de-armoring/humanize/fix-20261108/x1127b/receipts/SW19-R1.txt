So a child today gets about one-sixteenth of the grown-ups that a child had when our brains evolved (Perry, 2023). If you often feel empty (the "Void"), that feeling is real, since you're living with a fraction of the people we're built to have around us. That loneliness is what the armor has been numbing, so once you've de-armored, going back to a lonely life will hurt more than it did with the armor on. So I'd find a tribe of people you're close to and see most days, or you'll end up putting the armor back on just to get through the loneliness.
