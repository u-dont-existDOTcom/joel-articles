In Perry's picture, the brainstem is the foundation of the house, and Neurological De-Armoring works on it before any redecorating upstairs. Trauma leaves that foundation set hard, like concrete, so the protocol softens it the way a solvent would, by calming glutamate (the brain's main "go" signal) and adding the ketone BHB, which blocks enzymes called HDACs that can keep new learning from sticking. Then we can dig out the old concrete and pour a new foundation, and only after that work on the psychology upstairs.
