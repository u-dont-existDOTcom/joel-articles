Neurological De-Armoring is about fixing the underpinnings, not redecorating. Trauma leaves the brainstem set hard, like concrete, and the protocol softens it the way a solvent would, by calming glutamate (the brain's main "go" signal) and with ketones (BHB), which block HDACs, the enzymes that can keep new learning from sticking. Then we can dig under the house and pour a new foundation, and only after that work on the psychology upstairs.
