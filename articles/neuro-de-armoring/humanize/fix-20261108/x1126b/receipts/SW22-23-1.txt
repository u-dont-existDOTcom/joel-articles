The Anti-Breakthrough (Micro-Dosing): A brain stuck in alarm can often take in healing work for only a few seconds at a time, Dr. Perry finds. So for now we have to give up on the hour-long breakthrough that therapy aims for, since an hour spent working on the trauma itself overwhelms an armored nervous system more than it heals it. Instead, this protocol works in doses of a few seconds, and those small doses are what I mean by micro-dosing here.

Your goal is small doses of feeling safe in your body: 30 seconds of slow, steady breathing, twenty times a day. It works the way physical therapy rebuilds an injured knee, where many short sessions do more than a long one.
