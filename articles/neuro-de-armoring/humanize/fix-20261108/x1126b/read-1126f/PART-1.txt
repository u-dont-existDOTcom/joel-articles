## Serotonin Bottleneck Pattern: 5-HTP + Low-Dose P5P (two of its paragraphs, as they would read)

Who this is for: 5-HTP is not for every depressed person either. It could help, though, with a low-serotonin-looking pattern, where it's hard to fall asleep and your mood is low, without much warmth to it. At night you get anxious and keep going over the same things. It makes more sense if there's also a genetic or clinical reason to suspect a bottleneck in serotonin synthesis, like with Scott, whose genetic data pointed to a TPH2 bottleneck, and 5-HTP helped his sleep and mood. 5-HTP skips over that bottleneck, because your body uses it to make serotonin directly, so people who respond to it can feel it quickly, although it's not as strong for sleep or mood as some hype it up to be (see this broad review of 5-HTP). I'd avoid casually experimenting with 5-HTP if you're agitated or manic, as it could sometimes push that further. And don't take it with anything else that raises serotonin, like most antidepressants or MDMA, since together they can cause serotonin syndrome, a dangerous overload of serotonin (the warnings at the top list those drugs).

Serotonin syndrome usually comes from mixing 5-HTP casually with anything else that raises serotonin, like antidepressants or MAOIs. Reviews keep warning about the risk with 5-HTP, like this case report, where a young guy on sertraline (an antidepressant) took 5-HTP with a whole pile of weight-loss and energy supplements before a workout, and ended up with serotonin syndrome so bad that surgeons had to cut open both of his legs and his forearm to relieve the pressure! Luckily he recovered. If you're doing psychedelics, be especially strict around ayahuasca's harmala MAOI chemistry and around iboga or ibogaine: never take 5-HTP on the same day as ayahuasca or iboga, though it's fine the day before, or the day after ayahuasca. After a low dose of iboga, wait a day, and after a bigger dose usually a few days. After a flood dose (one big dose), people often wait weeks before taking anything psychoactive, mainly so they don't block the after-effects they took iboga for. While you're microdosing iboga, leave 5-HTP out altogether. With mushrooms, it's up to you whether to take 5-HTP, and I don't think it should be a problem with this protocol's mushroom microdoses.
