NEEDS:
S1: The size of the shortfall, which is half of what the paragraph is for: we get about one-sixteenth of the company our biology needs. As written the reader can't picture it, because "relational density" isn't explained (one-sixteenth of what: people around you, hours with them, people per household?) and "operating on a density" doesn't make sense as a phrase. It needs the plain measure in the same sentence.
S2: Nothing the other sentences don't give. Its one possible use is to tell the reader that the emptiness they feel comes from that shortage of company and not from a fault in them, but it doesn't say that: "Void" arrives capitalised and in quotes as if already defined, and nothing here says what it is. The act it would imply (fix the company, not yourself) is already S4's.
S3: The other half of what the paragraph is for: what the shortfall means once they have de-armored. A lonely life they could bear with the armor on is one they can't go back to without it. "Cannot" is left open (it will hurt more? the armor will come back?) until S4 answers it, so the two could be joined.
S4: The act and the reason for it: build a life with many people in it, close and often, or the armor comes back. It is the only sentence that tells the reader what to do. But "aggressively engineer a High-Density social environment" doesn't say what to do (how many people, how often, living with them or seeing them), and the reader needs that to act.

UNNEEDED:
- S2: The "Void" you feel is real. — The reader can lose it and still learn how far short modern life falls (S1) and what that means after de-armoring (S3, S4). It adds no fact and no act of its own, and "Void" is a name the paragraph never explains. If the writer wants its point, it belongs inside S1 in plain words: the emptiness you feel is that shortage, not something wrong with you.
- S4: You must aggressively engineer a "High-Density" social environment (A Tribe), or the brain will eventually reach for the armor again just to survive the cold. — Listed for sense only; the reader needs it, so fix it, don't cut it. It mixes four pictures for one thing: engineering, density, a tribe, and armor worn against cold (armor is for blows, not for cold; with S2's "Void" the loneliness is an emptiness, a thinness and a cold in three sentences). "High-Density" in quotes and "(A Tribe)" in capitals read as labels from somewhere else, and "the brain will reach for the armor" has the brain reaching for something. Say it once, plainly: live among many people you are close to, or you will close up again because being that alone hurts too much.
