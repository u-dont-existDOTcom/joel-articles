Stop immediately if it causes flatness, anhedonia, emotional blunting, new depression, agitation, insomnia, dizziness, palpitations, nausea, loose stools, diarrhea, or increased craving. Only the stomach problems showed up in the human trials, but the flatness happens in practice, so do not "push through" numbness. In rare reports, the anhedonia lasted for weeks after people stopped taking it. If agmatine makes the world more gray, it is the wrong tool for that phase.
