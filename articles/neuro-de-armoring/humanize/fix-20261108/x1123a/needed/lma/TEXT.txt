Lion's Mane & Ashwagandha: I don't advise either one, and this protocol doesn't use them. People on forums and YouTube report rare cases where they were left with anhedonia (when nothing feels good anymore) and sexual problems for months after stopping. No study has looked into it yet (the trials of both mostly found mild side effects), but it's the same kind of lasting effect regulators already warn about for SSRIs and finasteride.
