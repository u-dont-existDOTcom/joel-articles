Lion's Mane & Ashwagandha: Lots of people take these for focus and stress, but I'd leave both out. People say these herbs left them with anhedonia (when nothing feels good anymore) and sexual dysfunction that persisted even after they stopped, in rare cases reported on forums and YouTube. Nobody has studied these effects in the two herbs yet, but regulators already warn that SSRIs and finasteride can leave the same kind behind.
