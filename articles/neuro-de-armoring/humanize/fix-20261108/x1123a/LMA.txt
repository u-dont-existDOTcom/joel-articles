Lion's Mane & Ashwagandha: Lion's mane for focus and ashwagandha for stress are popular, but I don't advise either one, and this protocol doesn't use them. People on forums and YouTube report rare cases where they were left with anhedonia (when nothing feels good anymore) and sexual problems for months after they stopped taking them. These lasting effects haven't been studied yet, but they're the same kind that regulators already warn can follow SSRIs and finasteride.
