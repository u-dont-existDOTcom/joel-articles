Uridine (the UMP in this protocol) doesn't raise serotonin, so its caution is a different one, and it comes from experience like Scott's below, since I don't have studies on it: within a day of starting it, psychedelics can hit harder, visuals included. So do not take UMP on the same day as a psychedelic trip, and on your first trip after starting it, assume your old psychedelic dose may now be too high and take less. It may also make your dreams more vivid and your cravings weaker, which are the benefits you're hoping for.
