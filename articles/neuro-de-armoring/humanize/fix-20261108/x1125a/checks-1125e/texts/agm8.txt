Agmatine Warning (The Voltage Governor): Agmatine is optional in this protocol, and although it's sold as a supplement, it's a neuromodulator, here as a brake on NMDA/glutamate signaling. It can interact with a lot more medications than a basic nutrient like magnesium or a membrane nutrient like phosphatidylcholine would, since it also acts on imidazoline/alpha-2 adrenergic tone, nitric-oxide pathways, and opioid reward/tolerance.
