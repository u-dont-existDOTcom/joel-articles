## W-HTPTOP: The 5-HTP warning for psychedelic users: three tries, none Human

*In place of A Complete Guide to Bio-De-Armoring, P13*

**As it would read:**

> Uridine & 5-HTP Warning for Psychedelic Users: These two supplements are in this protocol for repair, so don't use them as "trip enhancers." In practice they can quickly change how strongly psychedelics hit you, so don't count on your usual dose acting the way it used to. 5-HTP is also what your body makes serotonin out of, so taking it on top of anything else that raises serotonin is how people end up with serotonin syndrome, which can be deadly (see [the 5-HTP section](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/181671197/serotonin-bottleneck-pattern-5-htp-low-dose-p5p)). So never take it with any of these: SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, linezolid, lithium, tramadol, methadone, fentanyl, DXM, MDMA, St. John's wort, SAM-e or L-tryptophan. Rule: psychedelics act on serotonin too, so do not take 5-HTP on the same day as a psychedelic trip, and do not use it anywhere near ayahuasca/harmalas or iboga, iboga microdoses included. That's because ayahuasca's harmalas inhibit MAO-A, which amplifies the serotonin load, while ibogaine and noribogaine interact with serotonin transport themselves, on top of the serious cardiac and neurological risk iboga already carries.
