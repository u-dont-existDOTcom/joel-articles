Cognitive Warning: As you de-armor, you will feel more naked and exposed than you're used to, and you'll assume everyone can see your anxiety, when really they notice far less of it than you think (Dr. Nicholas Epley calls this the "Illusion of Transparency" in his book Mindwise).

Your armored brain also reads its own threat level into neutral faces, so you will think people are judging you when they're mostly just thinking about themselves. So the rule during this protocol is, do not trust your social intuition: assume you are misreading every room, at least until your heart rate is back down to your usual resting rate, and under 90 BPM, since a racing heart means your reasoning brain is offline (see the "Metabolic Red Line" below).
