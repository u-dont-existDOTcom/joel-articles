The Rule: Seek "Boring Love", meaning parallel play (like reading next to someone) and touch that feels safe. As tempting as "Exciting Love" is (romance, high-stakes drama), avoid it, since it spikes Phasic Dopamine and puts the armor right back up.

Cognitive Warning: As you de-armor, you will feel more naked and exposed than you're used to, and you'll assume everyone can see your anxiety, which they mostly can't (Dr. Nicholas Epley calls that assumption the "Illusion of Transparency" in his book Mindwise).
