Uridine (the UMP in this protocol) doesn't raise serotonin, but it may still change how vivid your dreams are, how much you crave things, and how sensitive you are to psychedelics and their visuals; that's from experience, like Scott's below, not from studies. So do not take UMP on the same day as a psychedelic trip either, and if your sensitivity changes, assume your old psychedelic dose may now be too high.
