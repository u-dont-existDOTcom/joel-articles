Agmatine Warning (The Voltage Governor): Agmatine is sold as a supplement, but it is not a basic nutrient like magnesium or a membrane nutrient like phosphatidylcholine. It's a neuromodulator, which means it changes how several of the brain's signaling systems work at once (NMDA/glutamate signaling, imidazoline/alpha-2 adrenergic tone, nitric-oxide pathways, and opioid reward/tolerance), and that's where the precautions below come from.
