Behavioral Warning (The Isolation Trap): This protocol has you starve your brain of "Phasic" Dopamine, the bursts you get from excitement, novelty and the chase. The easiest way to do that is to hole up alone, but do not isolate completely, because total social isolation locks the armor in place, even if you've never had an addiction. It raises your stress hormone, cortisol, which sensitizes your brain to glutamate (the brain's main excitatory signal, explained below). Humans are social animals, so what you need is the calm kind of company that anchors you with the bonding hormone oxytocin ("Oxytocinergic Anchoring").
