Uridine & 5-HTP Warning for Psychedelic Users: These two supplements are in this protocol for repair, so don't use them as "trip enhancers." In practice they can quickly change how sensitive you are to psychedelics, and one of them brings a second danger. 5-HTP is what your body makes serotonin out of, so taking it on top of anything else that raises serotonin is how people end up with serotonin syndrome, an overload that can be deadly if untreated (see the 5-HTP section). So do not take it if you're on any of these: antidepressants (SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs), the antibiotic linezolid, lithium, the opioids tramadol, methadone and fentanyl, the cough suppressant DXM, MDMA, or the supplements St. John's wort, SAM-e and L-tryptophan. Rule: do not take 5-HTP on the same day as a psychedelic trip, and do not use it anywhere near ayahuasca/harmalas or iboga, iboga microdoses included. Ayahuasca's harmalas inhibit MAO-A, the enzyme that breaks serotonin down, which amplifies the serotonin load, and ibogaine and noribogaine (what your body turns ibogaine into) interact with serotonin transport as well, on top of the serious cardiac and neurological risk iboga already carries.
