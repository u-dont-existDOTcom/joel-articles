Agmatine Warning (The Voltage Governor): Agmatine is optional in this protocol, and it's sold as a supplement, but it isn't a basic nutrient like magnesium or a membrane nutrient like phosphatidylcholine. It's a neuromodulator that's here as a brake on NMDA/glutamate signaling, and since it also acts on imidazoline/alpha-2 adrenergic tone, nitric-oxide pathways, and opioid reward/tolerance, it can interact with the drugs listed below.
