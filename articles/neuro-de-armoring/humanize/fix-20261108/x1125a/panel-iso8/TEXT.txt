Behavioral Warning (The Isolation Trap): This protocol demands starving your brain of "Phasic" Dopamine (excitement, novelty, the chase), and the easiest way to do that is to hole up alone. But do not isolate completely, because total social isolation spikes cortisol, the chronic stress hormone, and that sensitizes your brain to glutamate and locks the armor in place even if you've never had an addiction. You still need people, since we're social animals, just in the calm ways the rule below describes (the "Oxytocinergic Anchoring").
