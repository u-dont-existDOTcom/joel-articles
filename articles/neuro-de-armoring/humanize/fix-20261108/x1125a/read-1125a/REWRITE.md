### A Complete Guide to Bio-De-Armoring

⚠️ CRITICAL MEDICAL DISCLAIMER:

DMSO Safety: This protocol has you smell DMSO for anxiety and brain fog. It's a solvent that goes straight through your skin and lungs into your blood, and it can carry in whatever else is in the bottle or on your skin. So you should use 99.9% Pharmaceutical Grade DMSO, ideally in a glass bottle, since it can leach chemicals out of some plastics. It's sold on Amazon and other sites (I've never had a bad batch). Before using it, wash any lotions or other products off your hands and nose. Your lungs count too, so if you're a smoker, breathe in only a little at a time, since it can pull the tar from your lungs into your blood, and stop if you feel weak right afterwards, which is the first sign of that (there's more on all this in my [full DMSO reference](http://dmso.u-dont-exist.com/)).

Electrolytes: This protocol has you eating [a lot more potassium](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/181671197/1-electrolytes-choose-your-path) than most people get, so consult a doctor first if you have kidney disease or heart conditions, or take a blood pressure drug that holds on to potassium. Those are the ACE inhibitors and ARBs, and with either kind, or with weak kidneys, potassium can build up in your blood.

Ethnobotanicals: The plant medicines in this protocol are iboga (an African root bark) and psilocybin mushrooms. Both are illegal in many jurisdictions and carry risks, and iboga's are the serious ones, since it can be dangerous for your heart and nervous system. So never combine Iboga with most medications (especially heart & psych meds), and if you take any, check it against my full [iboga guide here](http://iboga.u-dont-exist.com/) first (the iboga [microdosing protocol is here](http://ibogamicroprotocol.u-dont-exist.com/)).

Behavioral Warning (The Isolation Trap): While this protocol demands starving the brain of "Phasic" Dopamine (excitement, novelty, the chase), do not isolate completely. Total social isolation spikes Cortisol, the chronic stress hormone, which sensitizes the brain to Glutamate and effectively locks the armor in place even without any addiction. You need the "Oxytocinergic Anchoring" of healthy social relationships, because humans are social animals.

The Rule: Seek "Boring Love" (parallel play, reading next to someone, safe touch). Avoid "Exciting Love" (romance, high-stakes drama), as this spikes Phasic Dopamine and resets the armor.

Cognitive Warning: As you de-armor, you will feel naked and exposed. You will assume everyone can see your anxiety. Dr. Nicholas Epley, [*Mindwise*](https://www.amazon.com/Mindwise-Misunderstand-Others-Think-Believe/dp/030774356X), calls this the "Illusion of Transparency."

Your armored brain also reads the threat it feels into neutral faces, so you will think people are judging you when they're mostly just thinking about themselves. So during this protocol, do not trust your social intuition: assume you are misreading every room, at least until your heart rate is back down to your usual resting rate, and under 90 BPM, since a racing heart means your reasoning brain is offline (see the ["Metabolic Red Line"](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/181671197/a-the-metabolic-red-line) below).

Lion's Mane & Ashwagandha: I'm not advising either of these, even though they're popular herbs for focus and stress, because some people report anhedonia (no desire) and sexual dysfunction long after they stop taking them. Post-ashwa and post-lion's mane syndrome are rare, but it's the same kind of thing SSRIs and finasteride can do, so it's something to keep in mind.

Agmatine Warning (The Voltage Governor): Agmatine is not a basic nutrient like magnesium or a membrane nutrient like phosphatidylcholine. It is a multi-target neuromodulator that can affect NMDA/glutamate signaling, imidazoline/alpha-2 adrenergic tone, nitric-oxide pathways, and opioid reward/tolerance.

Precautions/Side Effects: Do not use concurrently with opioids and/or kratom (will reduce tolerance), clonidine and/or guanfacine, blood pressure lowering medications (including nitrates and PDE-5 inhibitors), or other complex psychoactive stacks (anything that works on the brain, beyond what this protocol pairs it with) without having a physician competent to consider the likelihoods of problematic interactions. Not recommended for use in pregnancy or breast-feeding. Use with extreme caution in those with a prior psychotic episode, lower end blood pressure, or kidney or liver issues.

Agmatine can leave some folks flat and numb, according to reports online (although in the human studies we've only seen stomach problems), so stop it right away if you notice you're flatter or more depressed than before you started. I wouldn't push through that signal, because a few people say the numbness hung on for weeks after they quit, so it's not like one of those side effects that you'd only feel at first. Also stop if agmatine winds you up instead (more irritable, can't sleep), or you get dizziness, palpitations, nausea, loose stools or stronger cravings.

Uridine & 5-HTP Warning for Psychedelic Users: These are not "trip enhancers." They are repair-oriented levers that, in practice, can change brain sensitivity quickly. 5-HTP is serotonergic because it is a direct serotonin precursor. Do not combine it with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, linezolid, lithium, tramadol, methadone, fentanyl, DXM, MDMA, St. John's wort, SAM-e, L-tryptophan, or other serotonin-raising stacks. Be especially conservative around ayahuasca / harmalas because MAO-A inhibition can amplify serotonin load, and around iboga / ibogaine because ibogaine and noribogaine interact with serotonin transport biology and already carry serious cardiac and neurological risk. Rule: do not take 5-HTP on the same day as a psychedelic trip, and do not use it anywhere near ayahuasca/harmalas or iboga.

Uridine (the UMP in this protocol) doesn't raise serotonin, so its caution is a different one, and it comes from experience like [Scott's below](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/181671197/dopamine-receptor-burnout-daws-pattern-uridine-monophosphate), since I don't have studies on it: within a day of starting it, psychedelics can hit harder, visuals included. So do not take UMP on the same day as a psychedelic trip, and on your first trip after starting it, assume your old psychedelic dose may now be too high and take less. It may also make your dreams more vivid and your cravings weaker, which are the benefits you're hoping for.
