Uridine & 5-HTP Warning for Psychedelic Users: These are not "trip enhancers," they're in this protocol for repair, and in practice they can change your brain's sensitivity quickly, so your usual dose might not act the way you expect. 5-HTP is the one to be careful with, because your body makes serotonin out of it, and serotonin syndrome (which can be deadly, see the 5-HTP section) usually comes from mixing it with something else that raises serotonin. So do not combine it with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, linezolid, lithium, tramadol, methadone, fentanyl, DXM, MDMA, St. John's wort, SAM-e, L-tryptophan, or other serotonin-raising stacks. Rule: do not take 5-HTP on the same day as a psychedelic trip, and do not use it anywhere near ayahuasca/harmalas or iboga (microdoses of iboga included), since ayahuasca's MAO-A inhibition can amplify the serotonin load, and ibogaine and noribogaine interact with serotonin transport on top of iboga's already serious cardiac and neurological risk.
