Uridine & 5-HTP Warning for Psychedelic Users: These two supplements are in this protocol for repair, so don't use them as "trip enhancers." In practice they can quickly change how sensitive you are to psychedelics, and with 5-HTP there's a second danger, which is serotonin. Your body makes serotonin out of 5-HTP, and stacking it on anything else that raises serotonin is how people end up with serotonin syndrome, an overload that can be deadly if untreated (see the 5-HTP section). So do not combine it with antidepressants (SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs), the antibiotic linezolid, lithium, the opioids tramadol, methadone and fentanyl, the cough suppressant DXM, MDMA, or the supplements St. John's wort, SAM-e and L-tryptophan, and if you're already on one of these, it's the 5-HTP you leave out. Rule: do not take 5-HTP on the same day as a psychedelic trip, and do not use it anywhere near ayahuasca/harmalas or iboga, iboga microdoses included. Ayahuasca's harmalas inhibit MAO-A, the enzyme that breaks serotonin down, which amplifies the serotonin load, and ibogaine and noribogaine (what your body turns ibogaine into) interact with serotonin transport as well, on top of the serious cardiac and neurological risk iboga already carries.
