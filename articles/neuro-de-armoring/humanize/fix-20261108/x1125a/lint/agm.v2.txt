Agmatine Warning (The Voltage Governor): Agmatine is an optional add-on in this protocol, and although it's sold as a supplement, it is not a basic nutrient like magnesium or a membrane nutrient like phosphatidylcholine. It's a neuromodulator that acts on several of the brain's signaling systems at once (NMDA/glutamate signaling, imidazoline/alpha-2 adrenergic tone, nitric-oxide pathways, and the reward and tolerance you get from opioids), so it can clash with any drug that works on the same systems. If you take it, go by the precautions below, which name those drugs.
