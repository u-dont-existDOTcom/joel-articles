Behavioral Warning (The Isolation Trap): This protocol demands starving the brain of "Phasic" Dopamine, the bursts you get from excitement, novelty and the chase, but do not isolate completely while you're doing that. Total social isolation spikes cortisol, the chronic stress hormone, which makes the brain more sensitive to glutamate (its main excitatory neurotransmitter) and effectively locks the armor in place, even if you've never had an addiction. Humans are social animals, so you still need healthy social relationships to anchor you with oxytocin, the bonding hormone ("Oxytocinergic Anchoring").
