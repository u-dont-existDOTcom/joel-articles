Uridine & 5-HTP Warning for Psychedelic Users: These two supplements are in this protocol for repair, so don't use them as "trip enhancers." But they can quickly change how strongly psychedelics hit you, so don't count on your usual dose acting the way it used to. 5-HTP is also what your body makes serotonin out of, and serotonin syndrome, which can be deadly, usually comes from mixing it casually with anything else that raises serotonin (see the 5-HTP section). So never take it with any of these: SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, linezolid, lithium, tramadol, methadone, fentanyl, DXM, MDMA, St. John's wort, SAM-e or L-tryptophan. Rule: psychedelics act on serotonin too, so do not take 5-HTP on the same day as a psychedelic trip, and do not use it anywhere near ayahuasca/harmalas or iboga, iboga microdoses included. Ayahuasca's harmalas are MAOIs themselves (they inhibit MAO-A, which amplifies the serotonin load), and ibogaine and noribogaine interact with serotonin transport, on top of the serious cardiac and neurological risk iboga already carries.
