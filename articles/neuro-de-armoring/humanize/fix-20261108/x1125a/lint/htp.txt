Uridine & 5-HTP Warning for Psychedelic Users: These two supplements are in this protocol for repair, not as "trip enhancers," and in practice they can change your brain's sensitivity quickly. 5-HTP is what your body makes serotonin from, and stacking it on anything else that raises serotonin is how people end up with serotonin syndrome (see the 5-HTP section). So do not combine it with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, linezolid, lithium, tramadol, methadone, fentanyl, DXM, MDMA, St. John's wort, SAM-e, L-tryptophan, or other serotonin-raising stacks. Be especially conservative around ayahuasca, because its harmalas inhibit MAO-A, the enzyme that breaks serotonin down, which can amplify the serotonin load, and around iboga, because ibogaine and noribogaine (what your body turns ibogaine into) interact with serotonin transport and already carry serious cardiac and neurological risk. Rule: do not take 5-HTP on the same day as a psychedelic trip, and do not use it anywhere near ayahuasca/harmalas or iboga.
