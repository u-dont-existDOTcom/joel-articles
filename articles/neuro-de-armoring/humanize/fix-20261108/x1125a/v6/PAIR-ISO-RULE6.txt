Behavioral Warning (The Isolation Trap): This protocol demands starving your brain of "Phasic" Dopamine (excitement, novelty, the chase), and the easiest way to do that is to hole up alone, but do not isolate completely. Total social isolation spikes cortisol, the chronic stress hormone, which makes your brain more sensitive to glutamate, and that locks the armor in place whether or not you've ever had an addiction. We're social animals, so what you need is the calm kind of company, the kind that gives you oxytocin instead of a dopamine spike (the "Oxytocinergic Anchoring" the rule below is about).

The Rule: Seek "Boring Love", meaning parallel play (like reading next to someone) and touch that feels safe. As tempting as "Exciting Love" is (romance, high-stakes drama), avoid it, since it spikes Phasic Dopamine and puts the armor right back up.
