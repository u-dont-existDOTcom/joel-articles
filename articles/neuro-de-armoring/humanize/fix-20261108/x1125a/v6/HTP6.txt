Uridine & 5-HTP Warning for Psychedelic Users: These are not "trip enhancers," they're in this protocol for repair, and in practice they can quickly change how sensitive you are to psychedelics, so go lower than your usual dose until you see how it hits now. 5-HTP is the one to be careful with, because your body makes serotonin out of it, and serotonin syndrome usually comes from mixing 5-HTP with something else that raises serotonin, which can be deadly (see the 5-HTP section). So do not combine it with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, linezolid, lithium, tramadol, methadone, fentanyl, DXM, MDMA, St. John's wort, SAM-e, L-tryptophan, or other serotonin-raising stacks. Rule: psychedelics act on serotonin too, so do not take 5-HTP on the same day as a psychedelic trip, and do not use it anywhere near ayahuasca/harmalas or iboga (microdoses of iboga included). Ayahuasca's MAO-A inhibition can amplify the serotonin load, and ibogaine and noribogaine interact with serotonin transport on top of iboga's already serious cardiac and neurological risk.
