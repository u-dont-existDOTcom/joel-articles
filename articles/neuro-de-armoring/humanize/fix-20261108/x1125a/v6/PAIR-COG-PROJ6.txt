Cognitive Warning: As you de-armor, you will feel more naked and exposed than you're used to, and you'll assume everyone can see your anxiety, when really they notice far less of it than you think (Dr. Nicholas Epley calls this the "Illusion of Transparency" in his book Mindwise).

You'll also feel like people are judging you, when they're mostly just thinking about themselves, because an armored brain reads its own threat level into neutral faces. So during this protocol, do not trust your social intuition. A racing heart means your reasoning brain is offline (see the "Metabolic Red Line" below), so assume you are misreading every room, at least until your heart rate is back down to your usual resting rate, and under 90 BPM.
