Agmatine Warning (The Voltage Governor): Agmatine is optional in this protocol, and it's sold as a supplement, but don't think of it as a basic nutrient like magnesium or a membrane nutrient like phosphatidylcholine. It's a neuromodulator, and it works on a lot at once: NMDA/glutamate signaling, imidazoline/alpha-2 adrenergic tone, nitric-oxide pathways, and the reward and tolerance you get from opioids. So it can clash with the drugs that work on those same systems, which is what the precautions below are for.
