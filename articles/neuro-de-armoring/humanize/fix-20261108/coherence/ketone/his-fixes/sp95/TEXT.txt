Which ketone strategy you're on decides how you handle salt: the salts in one bring their own sodium, while the diet in the other drains it, so on the diet, a drink of the salts just counts toward the extra sodium you need. If you're in a crisis and need the fog to lift fast, there's Strategy A, the "Crisis Mode". You drink BHB (beta-hydroxybutyrate) salts and keep eating a standard low-sugar diet. In trials, ketone supplements gave thinking a modest lift, from the first hours as well as over weeks (Bonnechère, et al, 2026).
