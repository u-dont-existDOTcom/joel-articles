Your ketone strategy decides how you handle salt, since the salts bring their own sodium while the diet drains it. If you're in a crisis and need the fog to lift fast, there's Strategy A, the "Crisis Mode". You drink BHB (beta-hydroxybutyrate) salts and keep eating a standard low-sugar diet. In trials, ketone esters, salts and MCT oil improved thinking a little against a placebo, from a single dose as well as over two weeks or more ([Bonnechère, et al, 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42063954/)).
