The Fix ("Lock, Stock, and Barrel"): The Brake (NAC): NAC gives the brain's support cells cystine (an amino acid), which they trade for glutamate, and in rats that glutamate sitting outside the synapse turns on a brake that cuts how much glutamate gets released into it (Moran, et al, 2005).

The Lock (Zinc/Magnesium): Magnesium physically plugs the NMDA receptor's channel while the cell is at rest (Jalali-Yazdi, et al, 2018), and zinc mostly makes the channel open less often (Amico-Ruvio, et al, 2011), so less calcium gets in.
