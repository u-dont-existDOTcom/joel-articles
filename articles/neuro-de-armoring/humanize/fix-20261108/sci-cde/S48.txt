Why don't these drugs work for everyone? Nobody knows for sure. This protocol tries something else. The ketone BHB blocks enzymes called HDACs (Shimazu, et al, 2013), and in mice that couldn't hold on to extinction learning (learning that a cue is now safe), an HDAC blocker made the new learning last (Whittle & Singewald, 2014). Whether ketosis does that in people hasn't been tested.
