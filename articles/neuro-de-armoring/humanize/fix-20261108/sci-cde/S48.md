Why don't these drugs work for everyone? Nobody knows for sure. This protocol tries something else. The ketone BHB blocks enzymes called HDACs ([Shimazu, et al, 2013](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/4w889738)), and in mice that couldn't hold on to extinction learning (learning that a cue is now safe), an HDAC blocker made the new learning last ([Whittle & Singewald, 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3961057)). Whether ketosis does that in people hasn't been tested.
