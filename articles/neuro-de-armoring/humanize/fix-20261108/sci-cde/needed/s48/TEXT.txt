Why don't these drugs work for everyone? Nobody knows for sure, and it isn't that they can't touch the brain's structure: in people with PTSD, a year on paroxetine grew the hippocampus, a memory center that is often smaller in PTSD, by about 5% (Vermetten, et al, 2003). This protocol tries something else. The ketone BHB blocks enzymes called HDACs (Shimazu, et al, 2013), and in mice that couldn't hold on to extinction learning (learning that a cue is now safe), an HDAC blocker made the new learning last (Whittle & Singewald, 2014). Whether ketosis does that in people hasn't been tested.
