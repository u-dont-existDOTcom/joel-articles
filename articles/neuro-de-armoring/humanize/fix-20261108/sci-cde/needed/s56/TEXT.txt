The Fix ("The Jammer"): Memories require visual processing power to be restored. If you occupy the Visual-Spatial Working Memory (e.g., playing Tetris) during the critical window after a flashback, the brain fails to "save" the visual details as strongly, and the memory flashes back less often. After car crashes, people who briefly recalled the crash and then played about 20 minutes of Tetris in the emergency room had 62% fewer intrusive memories over the next week (Iyadurai, et al, 2018). In the lab, the reminder and Tetris together cut flashbacks of a day-old memory, and neither did alone (James, et al, 2015).
