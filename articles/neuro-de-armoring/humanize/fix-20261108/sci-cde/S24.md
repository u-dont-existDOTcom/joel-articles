We are operating on about one-sixteenth of the relational density our biology requires ([Perry, 2023](https://issuu.com/familyjusticegroup/docs/20230629_journal_final/s/27001732)). The "Void" you feel is real. Once you have de-armored, you cannot simply return to a lonely life.
