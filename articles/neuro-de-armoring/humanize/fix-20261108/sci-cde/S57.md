Instead of Tetris (which requires app/phone), you can use the "Color-Path Connect" method. It hasn't been tested the way Tetris has, but it is built on the same idea: it makes your brain map 3D space, which competes with the visual "saving" of a traumatic memory. Skip counting and word games, though: in the lab, a verbal quiz game after a trauma film led to more flashbacks, not fewer ([Holmes, et al, 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21085661/)).
