The Fix (The Unlock): DeltaFosB does fade on its own, but [slowly](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8879420), and the same fears keep making more. So we use "System Reformats" (Shrooms/Iboga). Iboga raised GDNF, a growth factor that protects dopamine neurons, in rats ([He, et al, 2005](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1193648)), and one dose of psilocybin grew new connections in mouse brains that lasted a month ([Shao, et al, 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34228959/)). Whether either loosens DeltaFosB's hold hasn't been tested.
