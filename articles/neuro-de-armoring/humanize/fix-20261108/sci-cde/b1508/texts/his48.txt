Why don't these drugs work on calcified PTSD? Because even if they did work for mood by modulating serotonin transmission, PTSD is not a mood disorder. That's a bit like putting some Bengay on a broken leg. This protocol tries to get at the underpinnings of the problem. The ketone BHB blocks enzymes called HDACs (Shimazu, et al, 2013), and in mice that couldn't hold on to extinction learning (learning that a cue is now safe), an HDAC blocker made the new learning last (Whittle & Singewald, 2014). Whether ketosis does that in people hasn't been tested.
