Why Standard Meds Failed You If you have tried SSRIs (antidepressants) and failed, you are not crazy. In a small trial of male combat veterans with severe, long-term PTSD, Prozac did no better than a placebo (Hertzberg, et al, 2000). Trials in veterans have been mixed since, yet the VA still recommends sertraline and paroxetine (VA/DoD, 2023). And the side effects are well-known, including anhedonia, zombification, and sexual dysfunction.
