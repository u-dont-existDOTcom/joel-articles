The Science: Eating more potassium brings high blood pressure down ([Aburto, et al, 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23558164/)), which matters on Strategy A, since its salts bring their own sodium. If you have kidney disease or take a blood pressure drug that holds on to potassium, like an ACE inhibitor or an ARB, ask your doctor first, since potassium can build up in your blood ([NIH, 2022](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Potassium-HealthProfessional/)).
