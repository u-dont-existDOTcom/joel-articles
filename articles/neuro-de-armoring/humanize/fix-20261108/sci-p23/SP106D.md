The Science: Eating more potassium brings high blood pressure down ([Aburto, et al, 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23558164/)), which matters on Strategy A, since its salts bring their own sodium. I think more potassium for the salt you eat helps the brain too: in one trial, a diet lower in salt and higher in potassium lifted mood a little ([Torres, et al, 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18466657/)), though studies like it can't separate the potassium from the salt or the rest of the food. Stone Age diets are estimated to have had more than four times the potassium we eat now ([Sebastian, et al, 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17275582/)), so eating closer to a paleo diet probably does more than adding a couple of potatoes, but the potatoes and greens below are the easy way to start. If you have kidney disease or take a blood pressure drug that holds on to potassium, like an ACE inhibitor or an ARB, ask your doctor first, since potassium can build up in your blood ([NIH, 2022](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Potassium-HealthProfessional/)).
