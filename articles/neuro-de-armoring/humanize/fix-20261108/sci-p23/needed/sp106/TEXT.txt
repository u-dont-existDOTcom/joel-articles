The Science: Your neurons have "brakes" called KCNQ channels, which let potassium out of the cell and keep it from firing over and over. Stress does lower the potassium in your blood, since adrenaline pushes it into your cells (Brown, et al, 1983), but with less potassium outside them, nerve cells rest farther from firing, not closer (Kuwabara, et al, 2002).
