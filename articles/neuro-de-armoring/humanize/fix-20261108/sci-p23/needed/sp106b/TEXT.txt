The Science: Eating more potassium brings high blood pressure down (Aburto, et al, 2013), which matters on Strategy A, since its salts bring their own sodium. It won't calm your nerves, though, because your brain keeps the potassium around its neurons within tight limits, whatever your blood is doing (Hladky & Barrand, 2016), even when stress lowers the potassium in your blood by pushing it into your cells (Brown, et al, 1983).
