The Science: Your neurons have "brakes" called KCNQ channels, which keep a neuron from firing over and over by letting potassium out of it. Stress does lower the potassium in your blood, since adrenaline pushes it into your cells ([Brown, et al, 1983](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6314140/)), but with less potassium outside them, nerve cells rest farther from firing, not closer ([Kuwabara, et al, 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12210395/)).
