The Repair (L-Serine): Your brain makes D-Serine out of L-Serine ([Wolosker, et al, 1999](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC23961)). So taking L-Serine gives you more D-serine for that spot on the NMDA receptor, which means a session that ends with the fear down and a sense of mastery has a better chance of sticking. The conversion isn't very efficient though, so some people take D-serine itself instead (more risk, but also more benefit).
