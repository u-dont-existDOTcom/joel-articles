Uridine and 5-HTP are in this protocol for repairing neural signaling, and they can quickly change how strongly psychedelics hit you, so don't mix everything together all at once. Especially dangerous would be to take 5-HTP with anything else that raises serotonin levels, since 5-HTP is also what your body makes serotonin out of. That's how folks end up with serotonin syndrome, which can be deadly (see the 5-HTP section). The following drugs are especially dangerous to take with 5-HTP: SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs (including ayahuasca), linezolid, lithium, tramadol, methadone, fentanyl, DXM, MDMA, St. John's wort, SAM-e or L-tryptophan. Do not use it anywhere near iboga, even when microdosing, because ibogaine and noribogaine in iboga interact with serotonin transport.
