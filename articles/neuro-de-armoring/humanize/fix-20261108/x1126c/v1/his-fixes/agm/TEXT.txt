Agmatine, the "voltage regulator", acts like a brake on NMDA/glutamate signaling, which is why we use it in this protocol. But we'll keep it as an optional add-on, since it can interact with a lot more medications than the protocol's safer nutrients would (magnesium, phosphatidylcholine, etc.). People may think it's pretty safe, since it's a common health supplement, but we have to treat it with caution since it acts powerfully on imidazoline/alpha-2 adrenergic receptors, nitric-oxide pathways, and opioid reward/tolerance.
