This neural de-armoring protocol demands starving your brain of "Phasic" Dopamine, which works in the brain to produce learning and excitement when chasing novelty. The easiest way for most folks to take a break from that is to hole up alone. But the isolation trap is cutting yourself off completely, which actually spikes cortisol, the chronic stress hormone. And cortisol sensitizes your brain to glutamate, thus locking the old neural armor in place that we're trying to gently remove. Calm company, like nature walk buddies, goes the other way, anchoring you via oxytocin, the bonding hormone. Good friends are ultimately more vital than any brain repair protocol.
