I can't recommend starting lion's mane or ashwagandha, even though they're popular herbs for focus and stress, because some people report anhedonia (little pleasure or desire) and sexual dysfunction long after they stop taking them. Post-ashwa and post-lion's mane syndrome are rare, but it's the same kind of thing SSRIs and finasteride can do, so if you take either, stop it if you notice you feel less pleasure or desire than before.
