Do not use agmatine on the same day as opioids and/or kratom (will reduce tolerance), clonidine and/or guanfacine, blood pressure lowering medications (including nitrates and PDE-5 inhibitors), or other complex psychoactive stacks (anything that works on the brain, beyond what this protocol pairs it with) without having a physician competent to consider the likelihoods of problematic interactions. It's not recommended for use in pregnancy or breast-feeding. Use with extreme caution in those with a prior psychotic episode, lower end blood pressure, or kidney or liver issues.
