# The blind writer's rewrites (a fresh Fable agent, given only BRIEF.md, 2026-11-27)

P1:
I'm not advising lion's mane or ashwagandha, even though they're popular herbs for focus and stress, because some people report anhedonia (no joy or desire) and sexual dysfunction long after they stop taking them. Post-ashwa and post-lion's mane syndrome are rare, but they can last long after you stop, which is the same kind of thing SSRIs and finasteride can do, so if you're already on either herb, I'd quit it as soon as you notice any of those symptoms.

P2a:
As you de-armor, you'll tend to feel naked and exposed, and you'll tend to assume everyone can see your anxiety, which they mostly can't. Nicholas Epley, in his book Mindwise, calls this assumption the "Illusion of Transparency."

P2b:
Also, you'll tend to think people are judging you when they're mostly just thinking about themselves, because while you're armored you project the threat you feel onto neutral faces. So during this protocol, do not trust your social intuition: assume you are misreading every room, at least until your heart rate is back down to your usual resting rate, and under 90 BPM, since a racing heart means your reasoning brain is offline (see the "Metabolic Red Line" below).
