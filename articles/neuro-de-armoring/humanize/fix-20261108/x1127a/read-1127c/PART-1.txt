## The agmatine warning and his Precautions, as they stood before his answer 76

Agmatine, the "voltage regulator", acts like a brake on NMDA/glutamate signaling, which is why we do use it in this protocol. But we'll keep it as an optional add-on, since it can interact with a lot more medications than the protocol's safer nutrients would (magnesium, phosphatidylcholine, etc.). People may think it's pretty safe, since it's a common health supplement, but we have to treat it with caution since it acts powerfully on imidazoline/alpha-2 adrenergic receptors, nitric-oxide pathways, and opioid reward/tolerance.

Do not use agmatine on the same day as opioids and/or kratom (will reduce tolerance), clonidine and/or guanfacine, blood pressure lowering medications (including nitrates and PDE-5 inhibitors), or other complex psychoactive stacks (anything that works on the brain, beyond what this protocol pairs it with) without having a physician competent to consider the likelihoods of problematic interactions. It's not recommended for use in pregnancy or breast-feeding. Use with extreme caution in those with a prior psychotic episode, lower end blood pressure, or kidney or liver issues.
