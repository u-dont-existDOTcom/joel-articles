Do not use agmatine on the same day as opioids and/or kratom, clonidine and/or guanfacine, blood pressure lowering medications (including nitrates and PDE-5 inhibitors), or other complex psychoactive stacks (anything that works on the brain, beyond what this protocol pairs it with) without having a physician competent to consider the likelihoods of problematic interactions. Agmatine lowers your tolerance to opioids and kratom, so your usual dose can hit harder, which with opioids can mean an overdose, and even with kratom you can get side effects once you're more sensitive. It's not recommended for use in pregnancy or breast-feeding. Use with extreme caution in those with a prior psychotic episode, lower end blood pressure, or kidney or liver issues.
