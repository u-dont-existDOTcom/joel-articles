Uridine & 5-HTP Warning for Psychedelic Users: These two supplements are in this protocol for repair, so don't use them as "trip enhancers", and don't count on your usual psychedelic dose acting the way it used to, because in practice they can quickly change how strongly psychedelics hit you. 5-HTP is the bigger risk, since it's what your body makes serotonin out of, so taking it on top of anything else that raises serotonin is how people end up with serotonin syndrome, which can be deadly (see the 5-HTP section). That's why you should never take 5-HTP while you're on any of these: SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, linezolid, lithium, tramadol, methadone, fentanyl, DXM, MDMA, St. John's wort, SAM-e or L-tryptophan (if one of them is prescribed to you, the thing to skip is the 5-HTP, not your medication). Rule: psychedelics act on serotonin too, so do not take 5-HTP on the same day as a psychedelic trip, and do not use it anywhere near ayahuasca/harmalas or iboga, iboga microdoses included. Those two get the stricter rule because ayahuasca's harmalas inhibit MAO-A, which amplifies the serotonin load, while ibogaine and noribogaine interact with serotonin transport themselves.
