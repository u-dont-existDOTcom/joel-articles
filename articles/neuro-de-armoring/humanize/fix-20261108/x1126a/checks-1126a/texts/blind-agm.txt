Agmatine Warning (The Voltage Governor): Agmatine is optional in this protocol, where it's used as a brake on NMDA/glutamate signaling. It's sold as a supplement, but it's a neuromodulator, meaning it changes how your nerve cells signal to each other, so it can interact with a lot more medications than a basic nutrient like magnesium or a membrane nutrient like phosphatidylcholine would. That's because it doesn't only act on glutamate: it also acts on imidazoline/alpha-2 adrenergic tone, nitric-oxide pathways, and opioid reward/tolerance, which is why the precautions below name blood pressure drugs and opioids.
