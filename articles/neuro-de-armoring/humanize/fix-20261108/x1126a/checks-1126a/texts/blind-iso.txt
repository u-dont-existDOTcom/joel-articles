Behavioral Warning (The Isolation Trap): This protocol demands starving your brain of "Phasic" Dopamine, which is the burst you get from excitement, novelty and the chase, and the easiest way to do that is to hole up alone, but do not isolate completely. Total social isolation spikes cortisol, the chronic stress hormone, which sensitizes your brain to glutamate (the signal this protocol is trying to quiet), and that's how being alone locks the armor in place even if you've never had an addiction. So you still need people around you, as long as it's the calm kind of company, because their presence is what gives you the "Oxytocinergic Anchoring" that keeps you steady.
