## A Complete Guide to Bio-De-Armoring (three warnings at the top, as they would read)

Ethnobotanicals: The plant medicines in this protocol are iboga (an African root bark) and psilocybin mushrooms. Both are illegal in many jurisdictions and carry risks, and iboga's are the serious ones, since it can be dangerous for your heart. So never combine iboga with most medications (especially heart & psych meds), and if you take any medication, ask an AI whether it's one of them, since AI is pretty good at that (my full [iboga guide here](http://iboga.u-dont-exist.com/) has more, and the iboga [microdosing protocol is here](http://ibogamicroprotocol.u-dont-exist.com/)). Ask it about the mushrooms too, since lithium, for one, has been tied to seizures when taken with psychedelics.

The Rule: Seek "Boring Love", meaning parallel play (like reading next to someone) and touch that feels safe to you. The other kind, "Exciting Love", is the rush of a new romance or high-stakes drama, which spikes Phasic Dopamine and puts the armor right back up. So this may not be a good time to jump into one, though there's no need to leave the partner you have.

Uridine (the UMP in this protocol) doesn't raise serotonin, so its caution is a different one, and it comes from experience like [Scott's below](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/181671197/dopamine-receptor-burnout-daws-pattern-uridine-monophosphate), since I don't have studies on it: within a day of starting uridine, psychedelics can hit some people a lot harder, visuals included, especially "hard heads" who usually need more than an average dose. So do not take UMP on the same day as a psychedelic trip, and on your first trip once you're on it, start with a third of your old psychedelic dose. It may also make your dreams more vivid and your cravings weaker, which are the benefits you're hoping for.
