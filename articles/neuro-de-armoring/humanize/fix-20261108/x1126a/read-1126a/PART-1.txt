## A Complete Guide to Bio-De-Armoring (the warnings at the top)

⚠️ CRITICAL MEDICAL DISCLAIMER:DMSO Safety: You should use 99.9% Pharmaceutical Grade DMSO, ideally in a glass bottle. It’s sold on Amazon and other sites (I’ve never had a bad batch of DMSO). Ensure hands, lungs, and nose are clean before using. See my full DMSO reference here.

Electrolytes: Consult a doctor if you have kidney disease or heart conditions.

Ethnobotanicals:Iboga and Psilocybin are illegal in many jurisdictions and carry risks. Never combine Iboga with most medications (especially heart & psych meds). See full iboga guide here, and microdosing protocol here.

Behavioral Warning (The Isolation Trap): While this protocol demands starving the brain of “Phasic” Dopamine (excitement, novelty, the chase), do not isolate completely. Total social isolation spikes Cortisol, the chronic stress hormone, which sensitizes the brain to Glutamate and effectively locks the armor in place even without any addiction. You need the “Oxytocinergic Anchoring” of healthy social relationships, because humans are social animals.

The Rule: Seek “Boring Love” (parallel play, reading next to someone, safe touch). Avoid “Exciting Love” (romance, high-stakes drama), as this spikes Phasic Dopamine and resets the armor.

Cognitive Warning: As you de-armor, you will feel naked and exposed. You will assume everyone can see your anxiety. Dr. Nicholas Epley, *Mindwise*, calls this the “Illusion of Transparency.”

The armored brain projects its own internal threat level onto neutral faces. You will think people are judging you; in reality, they are just thinking about themselves. Rule: During this protocol, do not trust your social intuition. It is calibrated to “Threat,” not “Truth.” Assume you are misreading every room until your heart rate is below 90 BPM.

Agmatine Warning (The Voltage Governor): Agmatine is not a basic nutrient like magnesium or a membrane nutrient like phosphatidylcholine. It is a multi-target neuromodulator that can affect NMDA/glutamate signaling, imidazoline/alpha-2 adrenergic tone, nitric-oxide pathways, and opioid reward/tolerance.

Do not combine agmatine casually with opioids, kratom, clonidine/guanfacine, blood-pressure medication, nitrates, PDE-5 inhibitors, MAOIs, or complex psychiatric medication stacks without clinician review. Avoid it during pregnancy or breastfeeding, and be especially cautious with bipolar disorder, psychosis history, unstable panic, low blood pressure, kidney/liver disease, or active substance relapse risk.

Stop immediately if it causes flatness, anhedonia, emotional blunting, new depression, agitation, insomnia, dizziness, palpitations, nausea, loose stools, diarrhea, or increased craving. Do not “push through” numbness. If agmatine makes the world more gray, it is the wrong tool for that phase.

Uridine & 5-HTP Warning for Psychedelic Users: These are not “trip enhancers.” They are repair-oriented levers that can change brain sensitivity quickly. 5-HTP is serotonergic because it is a direct serotonin precursor. Do not combine it with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, linezolid, lithium, tramadol, methadone, fentanyl, DXM, MDMA, St. John’s wort, SAM-e, L-tryptophan, or other serotonin-raising stacks without clinician review. Be especially conservative around ayahuasca / harmalas because MAO-A inhibition can amplify serotonin load, and around iboga / ibogaine because ibogaine and noribogaine interact with serotonin transport biology and already carry serious cardiac and neurological risk.

Rule: do not take 5-HTP on the same day as psychedelics, and do not use it anywhere near ayahuasca/harmalas or iboga unless the whole medicine plan has been medically reviewed. Uridine is not serotonergic, but it may still change dream vividness, visual sensitivity, craving, and psychedelic tolerance. Do not take UMP on the same day as psychedelics; if sensitivity changes, assume your old psychedelic dose may now be too high.

## The 5-HTP section: its interaction paragraph

Hard interaction rule: 5-HTP should not be mixed casually with serotonin-raising drugs or supplements. Case literature and clinical reviews warn about serotonin syndrome risk when 5-HTP is combined with antidepressants or MAOIs, and serotonin syndrome is most often a multi-drug / stack problem. See the dietary supplement interaction case report and the general serotonin-syndrome overview from MedlinePlus. For this readership: avoid 5-HTP on psychedelic days, and be especially strict around ayahuasca / harmala MAOI chemistry and iboga / ibogaine.

## Scenario C, the Void: the 5-HTP add-on

5-HTP: Not a dopamine tool. Use only when the parallel problem looks like low serotonin / poor sleep / TPH2 bottleneck. Do not combine with serotonergic medications, ayahuasca/harmalas, iboga, MDMA, DXM, tramadol, or same-day psychedelics.

## A Complete Guide to Bio-De-Armoring (the warnings at the top)

⚠️ CRITICAL MEDICAL DISCLAIMER:

DMSO Safety: This protocol has you smell DMSO for anxiety and brain fog. It's a solvent that goes straight through your skin and lungs into your blood, and it can carry in whatever else is in the bottle or on your skin. So you should use 99.9% Pharmaceutical Grade DMSO, ideally in a glass bottle, since it can leach chemicals out of some plastics. It's sold on Amazon and other sites (I've never had a bad batch). Before using it, wash any lotions or other products off your hands and nose. Your lungs need to be clean too, so if you're a smoker, breathe in only a little at a time, since it can pull the tar from your lungs into your blood, and stop if you feel weak right afterwards, which is the first sign of that (there's more on all this in my full DMSO reference).

Electrolytes: This protocol has you eating a lot more potassium than most people get, so consult a doctor first if you have kidney disease or heart conditions, or take a blood pressure drug that holds on to potassium. Those are the ACE inhibitors and ARBs, and with either kind, or with weak kidneys, potassium can build up in your blood.

Ethnobotanicals: The plant medicines in this protocol are iboga (an African root bark) and psilocybin mushrooms. Both are illegal in many jurisdictions and carry risks, and iboga's are the serious ones, since it can be dangerous for your heart. So never combine Iboga with most medications (especially heart & psych meds), and if you take any medication, ask an AI first whether it interacts with iboga, since AI is pretty good at that (my full iboga guide here has more, and the iboga microdosing protocol is here).

Behavioral Warning (The Isolation Trap): This neural de-armoring protocol demands starving your brain of "Phasic" Dopamine, which works in the brain to produce learning and excitement when chasing novelty. The easiest way for most folks to take a break from that is to hole up alone. But don't isolate completely, because that actually spikes cortisol, the chronic stress hormone. And cortisol sensitizes your brain to glutamate, thus locking the old neural armor in place that we're trying to gently remove. Calm company, like nature walk buddies, goes the other way, anchoring you via oxytocin, the bonding hormone. Good friends are ultimately more vital than any brain repair protocol.

The Rule: Seek "Boring Love", meaning parallel play (like reading next to someone) and touch that feels safe. "Exciting Love" (a new romance, high-stakes drama) spikes Phasic Dopamine and puts the armor right back up, so this may not be a good time to jump into it.

Cognitive Warning: As you de-armor, you will feel naked and exposed. You will assume everyone can see your anxiety. Dr. Nicholas Epley, *Mindwise*, calls this the "Illusion of Transparency."

Your armored brain also reads the threat it feels into neutral faces, so you will think people are judging you when they're mostly just thinking about themselves. So during this protocol, do not trust your social intuition: assume you are misreading every room, at least until your heart rate is back down to your usual resting rate, and under 90 BPM, since a racing heart means your reasoning brain is offline (see the "Metabolic Red Line" below).

Lion's Mane & Ashwagandha: I'm not advising either of these, even though they're popular herbs for focus and stress, because some people report anhedonia (no desire) and sexual dysfunction long after they stop taking them. Post-ashwa and post-lion's mane syndrome are rare, but it's the same kind of thing SSRIs and finasteride can do, so it's something to keep in mind.

Agmatine Warning (The "Voltage Regulator"): Agmatine acts like a brake on NMDA/glutamate signaling, which is why we use it in this protocol. But we'll keep it as an optional add-on, since it can interact with a lot more medications than the protocol's safer nutrients would (magnesium, phosphatidylcholine, etc.). People may think it's pretty safe, since it's a common health supplement, but we have to treat it with caution since it acts powerfully on imidazoline/alpha-2 adrenergic receptors, nitric-oxide pathways, and opioid reward/tolerance.

Precautions/Side Effects: Do not use concurrently with opioids and/or kratom (will reduce tolerance), clonidine and/or guanfacine, blood pressure lowering medications (including nitrates and PDE-5 inhibitors), or other complex psychoactive stacks (anything that works on the brain, beyond what this protocol pairs it with) without having a physician competent to consider the likelihoods of problematic interactions. Not recommended for use in pregnancy or breast-feeding. Use with extreme caution in those with a prior psychotic episode, lower end blood pressure, or kidney or liver issues.

Agmatine can leave some folks flat and numb, according to reports online (although in the human studies we've only seen stomach problems), so stop it right away if you notice you're flatter or more depressed than before you started. I wouldn't push through that signal, because a few people say the numbness hung on for weeks after they quit, so it's not like one of those side effects that you'd only feel at first. Also stop if agmatine winds you up instead (more irritable, can't sleep), or you get dizziness, palpitations, nausea, loose stools or stronger cravings.

Uridine & 5-HTP Warning for Psychedelic Users: These two supplements are in this protocol for repairing neural signaling, and they can quickly change how strongly psychedelics hit you, so don't mix everything together all at once. Especially dangerous would be to take 5-HTP with anything else that raises serotonin levels, since 5-HTP is also what your body makes serotonin out of. That's how folks end up with serotonin syndrome, which can be deadly (see the 5-HTP section). The following drugs are especially dangerous to take with 5-HTP: SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs (including ayahuasca), linezolid, lithium, tramadol, methadone, fentanyl, DXM, MDMA, St. John's wort, SAM-e or L-tryptophan. Do not use it anywhere near iboga, even when microdosing, because ibogaine and noribogaine in iboga interact with serotonin transport.

Uridine (the UMP in this protocol) doesn't raise serotonin, so its caution is a different one, and it comes from experience like Scott's below, since I don't have studies on it: within a day of starting uridine, psychedelics can hit harder, visuals included. So do not take UMP on the same day as a psychedelic trip, and on your first trip once you're on it, start with a third of your old psychedelic dose, since UMP can boost the effects a lot in some people, especially "hard heads" who usually need more than an average dose. It may also make your dreams more vivid and your cravings weaker, which are the benefits you're hoping for.

## The 5-HTP section: its interaction paragraph

Serotonin syndrome usually comes from mixing 5-HTP casually with anything else that raises serotonin, like antidepressants or MAOIs. Reviews keep warning about the risk with 5-HTP, like this case report, where a young guy on sertraline (an antidepressant) took 5-HTP with a whole pile of weight-loss and energy supplements before a workout, and ended up with serotonin syndrome so bad that surgeons had to cut open both of his legs and his forearm to relieve the pressure! Luckily he recovered. If you're doing psychedelics, be strict around ayahuasca, because of its harmala MAOI chemistry, and around iboga or ibogaine. 5-HTP is fine a day before either one, and a day after ayahuasca, but after iboga, wait a few days: a day is enough after a low dose, and after a flood dose people often wait weeks before taking anything psychoactive, mainly so they don't block iboga's after-effects. While you're microdosing iboga, leave it out altogether.
