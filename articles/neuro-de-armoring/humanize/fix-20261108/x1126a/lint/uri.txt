Uridine (the UMP in this protocol) doesn't raise serotonin, so its caution is a different one: within a day of starting it, psychedelics can hit some people a lot harder, visuals included, especially "hard heads" who usually need more than an average dose (I don't have studies on this, only experience like Scott's, whose story is further down). So do not take UMP on the same day as a psychedelic trip, and on your first trip once you're on it, start with a third of your old psychedelic dose. It may also make your dreams more vivid and your cravings weaker, which are the benefits you're hoping for.
