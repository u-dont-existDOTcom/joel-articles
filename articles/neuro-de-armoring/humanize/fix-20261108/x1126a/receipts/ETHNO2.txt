Ethnobotanicals: The plant medicines in this protocol are iboga (an African root bark) and psilocybin mushrooms. Both are illegal in many jurisdictions and carry risks, and iboga can be dangerous for your heart. So never combine iboga with most medications (especially heart & psych meds). If you take any medication, ask an AI whether it's one of them, since AI is pretty good at that (my full iboga guide here lists some of them, and the iboga microdosing protocol is here). Ask the AI about the mushrooms too, since lithium, for one, has been tied to seizures when taken with psychedelics.
