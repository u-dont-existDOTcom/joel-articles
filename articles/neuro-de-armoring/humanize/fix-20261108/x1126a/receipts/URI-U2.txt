Uridine (the UMP in this protocol) doesn't raise serotonin, so its caution is a different one, and it comes from experience like Scott's below, since I don't have studies on it: within a day of starting uridine, psychedelics can hit some people a lot harder, visuals included. So do not take UMP on the same day as a psychedelic trip, and on your first trip once you're on it, start with a third of your old psychedelic dose. It may also make your dreams more vivid and your cravings weaker, which are the benefits you're hoping for.
