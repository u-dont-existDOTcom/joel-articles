## A Complete Guide to Bio-De-Armoring (the Cognitive Warning, as it would read)

Cognitive Warning: As you de-armor, you will feel naked and exposed. You will assume everyone can see your anxiety, which they mostly can't. Nicholas Epley, *Mindwise*, calls this the "Illusion of Transparency."

So during this protocol, do not trust your social intuition: assume you are misreading every room, at least until your heart rate is back down to your usual resting rate, and under 90 BPM, since a racing heart means your reasoning brain is offline (see the "Metabolic Red Line" below).
