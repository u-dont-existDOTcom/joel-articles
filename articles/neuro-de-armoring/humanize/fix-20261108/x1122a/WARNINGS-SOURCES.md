# His new warnings: what the sources say, on both sides (2026-11-23)

For his message of 2026-11-22 17:39 UTC (`../joel-20261122-1739-message.txt`) and the brief beside this file
(`WARNINGS-BRIEF.md`). Reddit stays unread in every tool, the mirrors that showed up in search results included (the
lane's rule since 2026-11-11), so what forums say is his finding, said as his. Each line below is what a source found, for
whom, at what level; my summary, not the sources' words.

## Lion's mane (Hericium erinaceus)

- **Trials: side effects rare and mild.** A 2025 systematic review (26 studies, five randomized trials; Menon et al.,
  Front Nutr, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12434001/) and a 2024 review of the clinical studies
  (https://reference-global.com/article/10.2478/bgbl-2024-0038) found mostly stomach upset; in the 52-week Alzheimer's
  trial four people left over nausea, stomach discomfort or a rash (Li et al. 2020). No trial or case report I found
  describes anhedonia or sexual problems that last after stopping.
- **Reports outside trials.** A supplement-rating site that reads community reports calls lasting sexual anhedonia and
  emotional blunting a real but unquantified community concern (https://outliyr.com/reports/lions-mane). The forum posts
  themselves are his to give.
- **So the warning can say:** rare reports, on forums and YouTube, of numbness, low libido and sexual problems that went
  on for months after stopping; no study has looked, and the trials didn't see it.

## Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera)

- **Trials: well tolerated.** No clinical human study specifically reported a blunting of all emotions (the herb
  researcher Amala Soumyanath, quoted in https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/fjollaarifi/ashwagandha-emotions-tiktok).
- **What is documented:** liver injury, rare, 2 to 12 weeks after starting, cholestatic, usually resolving in 1 to 5
  months (LiverTox, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK548536/; Björnsson et al. 2020,
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31991029/), and in India acute-on-chronic liver failure with high mortality in people
  who already had liver disease (Philips et al. 2023, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37756041/); raised thyroid
  hormone in some people. After stopping: a case of a fast heart, insomnia and anxiety after an abrupt stop (Cureus 2025,
  doi:10.7759/cureus.83577), and a case report of withdrawal anxiety and depression and, months later, psychosis
  (https://sciety.org/articles/activity/10.14293/s2199-1006.1.sor-.pp4sdac.v1).
- **Reports outside trials:** emotional blunting while taking it, widely reported since 2023 (the BuzzFeed piece above);
  lasting blunting after stopping in forum reports (his finding).
- **So the warning can say:** rare forum and YouTube reports of numbness, anhedonia and sexual problems lasting after
  stopping, which no study has looked at; documented besides, rare liver injury.

## The cases he compares them to

- **SSRIs and SNRIs (PSSD).** In June 2019 the European Medicines Agency recognized that their sexual side effects can
  last after stopping, and asked for the labels to say so
  (https://www.psychologytoday.com/ca/blog/side-effects/201906/post-ssri-sexual-dysfunction-recognized-medical-condition).
  Diagnostic criteria for post-SSRI sexual dysfunction, post-finasteride syndrome and post-isotretinoin sexual
  dysfunction were published in 2022 (Healy et al., Int J Risk Saf Med 33(1):65-76, doi:10.3233/JRS-210023); how often
  it happens is still unknown (Healy & Mangin 2024, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11450419/).
- **Finasteride.** The FDA added sexual problems that continued after stopping to the labels of Propecia and Proscar in
  2011 and 2012 (erectile dysfunction, then libido, ejaculation and orgasm disorders; reported by HealthDay,
  https://medicalxpress.com/news/2012-04-baldness-drug-sexual-side-effects.pdf); the UK's regulator added a patient alert
  card for psychiatric and lasting sexual side effects
  (https://www.gov.uk/drug-safety-update/finasteride-reminder-of-the-risk-psychiatric-side-effects-and-of-sexual-side-effects-which-may-persist-after-discontinuation-of-treatment).
- **So the comparison holds as his:** the same kind of lasting effect is recognized for SSRIs and finasteride by
  regulators, while for lion's mane and ashwagandha it rests on rare reports. The warning says which.

## Agmatine

- **Trials:** one small open study of agmatine for depression reported an antidepressant effect with only stomach upset
  and loose stools as side effects (Shopsin 2013, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25287313/).
- **Reports outside trials:** emotional dullness while taking it (nootropic summaries, e.g.
  https://nootmind.pages.dev/Supplements/Agmatine-Sulfate); one essay describes a blunting effect at usual doses that
  seems to outlast the dose (https://ea.greaterwrong.com/posts/8Sed33q54kdhZ4M9m/get-out-of-hell-free-necklace/comment/KM6PGssxAtSd3Nzdq).
  Weeks of anhedonia after stopping: his finding, rare.
- **So the warning can say:** rarely, people report anhedonia lasting weeks after stopping; the article's own line
  already says to stop at flatness or anhedonia.

## 5-HTP

- **Documented:** nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, headache, sleeplessness, palpitations; serotonin syndrome in combinations
  (Harvard Health, https://www.health.harvard.edu/mental-health/is-5-htp-safe-to-take-as-a-mood-supplement); EMS from
  contaminated products (MedlinePlus, https://medlineplus.gov/druginfo/natural/794.html).
- **Reports outside trials:** a low mood or tiredness for up to a week after stopping (a doctors' answer on HealthTap,
  https://www.healthtap.com/questions/7193972-how-long-does-it-take-for-5-htp-to-clear-from-the-system/); a user review
  of panic, depression and insomnia for four weeks after stopping (Drugs.com reviews,
  https://drugs.com/comments/5-hydroxytryptophan/sfx-depression.html). Weeks of anhedonia after stopping: his finding,
  rare.
- **So the warning can say:** rarely, anhedonia or low mood lasting weeks after stopping, shorter than what's reported
  for lion's mane and ashwagandha.
