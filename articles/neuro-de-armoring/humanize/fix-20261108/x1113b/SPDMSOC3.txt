Nebulized DMSO with heparin (a blood thinner) has been shown to cut the lung damage from toxic cotton smoke in sheep (Kimura, et al, 1988). In another study, the smoke killed all seven untreated sheep and four of the six given DMSO alone, while all seven given DMSO with heparin survived (Brown, et al, 1988). But it has shown some neurotoxic effects in rodent studies. In mice in their first month of life, even the smallest dose tested set off brain cell death, and the authors worried about the children who get it in transplants (Hanslick, et al, 2009). Translating mouse research to humans isn't straightforward.
