Agmatine can leave you flat and numb, which people do report even though the human trials only found stomach problems. So keep checking yourself against how you were before you started. Am I flatter or more depressed, so the things I enjoy don't feel good anymore, or I've stopped caring about them? Am I more irritable, and sleeping worse? Do I have dizziness, palpitations (a pounding heart), nausea or loose stools? And if you're quitting something, are the cravings stronger? If the answer to any of these is yes, stop taking agmatine, and don't push through the numbness hoping it'll settle like side effects sometimes do, since a few people say it hung on for weeks after they stopped.
