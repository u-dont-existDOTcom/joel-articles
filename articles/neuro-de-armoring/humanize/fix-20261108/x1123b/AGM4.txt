Agmatine can leave you flat and numb, which people do report even though the human trials only found stomach problems. So stop it right away if, compared with before you started, you feel flatter or more depressed, things you used to enjoy stop feeling good (anhedonia), or you stop caring about them. Stop too if it winds you up instead, making you more irritable or sleepless than before, if you get dizziness or palpitations (a pounding or racing heart), if your stomach turns (nausea or loose stools), or if you're quitting something and the cravings get stronger. It's natural to want to wait the numbness out, hoping it settles like other side effects can, but don't push through it, because in rare reports it went on for weeks after people stopped taking agmatine.
