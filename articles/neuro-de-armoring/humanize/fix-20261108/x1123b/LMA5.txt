Lion's Mane & Ashwagandha: Lots of people take these for focus and stress, but I'd leave both out of this protocol, because a few people on forums and YouTube say one of the two gave them anhedonia (when nothing feels good anymore) or sexual dysfunction, and that the problem lasted even after they stopped taking it. Lasting effects like these are rare, and they're a known risk with SSRIs and finasteride too.
