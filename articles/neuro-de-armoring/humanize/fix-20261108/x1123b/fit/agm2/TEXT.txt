Stop agmatine right away if it leaves you flatter, more numb or more depressed than before you started, or if nothing feels good anymore (anhedonia) or you've stopped caring (apathy), since that means it's the wrong tool for where you are. Stop too if you get agitated or irritable, can't sleep, or have dizziness, palpitations (a pounding or racing heart), nausea, loose stools or diarrhea, or more craving than before. Don't push through the numbness hoping it'll pass: the human trials only turned up the stomach problems, but the flatness happens in practice, and in rare reports the loss of pleasure went on for weeks after people stopped taking it.
