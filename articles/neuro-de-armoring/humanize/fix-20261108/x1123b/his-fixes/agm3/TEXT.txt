Stop agmatine right away if, compared with before you started it, you feel flatter or more numb, more depressed, unable to enjoy anything (anhedonia) or like you no longer care (apathy). Its other side effects call for stopping too, whether that's feeling more wired than before (agitated, irritable, or sleeping badly), dizziness, palpitations (a pounding or racing heart), nausea or loose stools, or stronger cravings for whatever you're trying to quit. The human trials only found the stomach problems, but people do get the flatness in practice, and in rare reports their loss of pleasure went on for weeks after they stopped taking agmatine, so don't push through the numbness hoping it will pass.
