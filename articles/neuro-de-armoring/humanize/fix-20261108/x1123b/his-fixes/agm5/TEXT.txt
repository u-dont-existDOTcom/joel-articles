Agmatine can leave you flat and numb, which people do report even though the human trials only found stomach problems. So check in with yourself against how you were before you started: Am I flatter or more depressed? Have the things I enjoy stopped feeling good, or have I stopped caring about them? Is it winding me up instead--more irritable, not sleeping? Any dizziness, palpitations (a pounding heart), nausea, loose stools, or stronger cravings for whatever I'm quitting? If it's a yes to any of these, stop it. Don't push through the numbness hoping it'll settle like side effects sometimes do, because a few people say it hung on for weeks after they stopped.
