Take 5g a day as a baseline. The point of that baseline is to counter the breakdown of the gut lining that high-intensity or high-volume training causes, like CrossFit or intense cardio. If your immune system is dealing with an acute exposure, if your whole system is under a lot of stress, or if you have active IBD (Crohn's or colitis), raise it gradually to 10-30g a day.

CAUTION: cancer cells that multiply really fast can grab glutamine too, to fuel their growth, so if you have an active colon or liver cancer, stay off the 10-30g. With a dose that big, a lot of it passes through your gut cells into your blood, and a tumor in your liver gets it at full strength, since the blood from your gut goes to your liver before anywhere else (through the portal vein).
