S1 CAUTION: cancer cells that multiply really fast can grab glutamine too, to fuel their growth, so if you have an active colon or liver cancer, stay off the 10-30g.
S2 With a dose that big, a lot of it passes through your gut cells into your blood, and a tumor in your liver gets it at full strength, since the blood from your gut goes to your liver before anywhere else (through the portal vein).
