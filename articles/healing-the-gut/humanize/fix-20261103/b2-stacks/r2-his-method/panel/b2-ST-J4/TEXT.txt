I wouldn't recommend the general "gut cleanse" stacks (the ones built around oregano oil, wormwood, black walnut, berberine or other antimicrobial herbs) as a starting point for everyone. Those belong later on (Phase 3), once there's a stronger reason to suspect overgrowth, parasites, H. pylori or some other specific problem. (My own advice is different for H. pylori, where berberine works well: see Parasites, above.)
