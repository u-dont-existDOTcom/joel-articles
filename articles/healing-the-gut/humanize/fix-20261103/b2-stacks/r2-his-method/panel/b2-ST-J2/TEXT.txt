For a general "repair and rebuild" approach, I'd look for a stack that covers three things: something for your microbiome (a probiotic or a probiotic system that you tolerate well), something that helps your gut make short-chain fatty acids, especially butyrate (that's the prebiotic or butyrate support), and something that repairs the barrier (nutrients for the gut lining like glutamine, zinc carnosine, colostrum, mucosal-support herbs or similar compounds).
