Phase 1: The Pre-Flight Safety Check
Before you throw supplements or antimicrobials at your gut, the system mechanics that are foundational to gut function have to work. This part is from T.C. Hale (Kick Your Fat in the Nuts) and Stanley Rosenberg (Accessing the Healing Power of the Vagus Nerve).
Are your pupils dilated all the time? Are you missing your gag reflex? Then according to Hale, you're stuck in fight or flight mode (which in medical lingo is called "sympathetic dominance"), and digestion is offline. Rosenberg's fix is his Basic Exercise (eye movements) or the Salamander technique (which looks like some kind of yoga, see a video demo here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OZdvU0c6cCg). Both of those help to mechanically release the vagus nerve so your body can switch into rest and digest mode.
Now let's have a look at your bile function. Do you burp extra (not just like one little burp) after fatty meals? Do your stools float (like it's hard to flush), or look like clay? Then your bile is stagnant (not flowing well), and Hale says if you take antimicrobials now, you'll get a herx reaction you can't detoxify. So thin the bile first with Beet Flow (an extract of beet greens) or phosphatidylcholine, before starting on any pathogen killing protocol.
