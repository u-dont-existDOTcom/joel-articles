Buying glutamine here, a probiotic somewhere else, then all the rest separately can be a hassle. Some people just want a kit to start with. For general repair and rebuilding I'd look for one with a probiotic or probiotic system you tolerate well, and something to help your gut make short-chain fatty acids, especially butyrate. Check what it has for the gut lining too, like glutamine, zinc carnosine, colostrum, herbs that support the lining or similar compounds.
