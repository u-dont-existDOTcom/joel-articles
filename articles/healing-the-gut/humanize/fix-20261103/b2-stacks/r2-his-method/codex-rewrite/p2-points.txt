That said, some people want a ready-made starting point rather than buying every ingredient separately. For a general "repair and rebuild" approach, I would look for a stack that covers three categories:

1. Microbiome support: a well-tolerated probiotic or probiotic system

2. Prebiotic / butyrate support: something that helps short-chain fatty acid production, especially butyrate

3. Barrier repair: nutrients that support the gut lining, such as glutamine, zinc carnosine, colostrum, mucosal-support herbs, or similar compounds
