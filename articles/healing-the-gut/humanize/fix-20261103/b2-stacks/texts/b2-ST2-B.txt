The next three kits were actually Gemini's suggestions, but so far they're the best ones I've seen, tho I haven't used them myself. You can check their reviews. For a general stack, Microbiome Labs Total Gut Restoration is the best of the bunch, because it combines probiotic and prebiotic components, plus mucosal support. For a more leaky-gut-specific stack, Dr. Ruscio's Leaky Gut Support Kit is a reasonable option. And if you want a single gut-lining repair formula, Designs for Health GI Revive is one of the more complete products, but think of it as a repair product, which is only one piece of a full gut protocol.
I wouldn't recommend general "gut cleanse" stacks built around oregano oil, wormwood, black walnut, berberine or other antimicrobial herbs as a universal starting point. Those belong later in the decision tree, when there's a stronger reason to suspect overgrowth, parasites, H. pylori, or some other specific problem. (My own advice is different for H. pylori, where berberine works well: see Parasites, above.)
Whatever you take, start low, especially with prebiotics, resistant starch, butyrate-support products and probiotics. If you have severe pain, GI bleeding, fever, dehydration or rapid weight loss, work with a qualified clinician instead of stacking supplements on your own. Same goes for active IBD flares, cancer, immune suppression, pregnancy, or complex medical conditions.
