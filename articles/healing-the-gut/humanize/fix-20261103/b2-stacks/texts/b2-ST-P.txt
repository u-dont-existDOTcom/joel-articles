Optional Ready-Made Gut Stacks
I don't think one supplement stack heals everyone's gut, and the order still matters: mechanics first, then diet and motility, and antimicrobials only if you still have symptoms. But if you want a ready-made starting point, I'd look for one that has a probiotic you tolerate, something for butyrate, and something for the gut lining (glutamine, zinc carnosine, colostrum and so on).
Gemini suggested these, and so far they're the best kits I've seen, tho I haven't used them myself, so check the reviews: Microbiome Labs Total Gut Restoration (probiotic, prebiotic and mucosal support in one), Dr. Ruscio's Leaky Gut Support Kit for leaky gut, and Designs for Health GI Revive if you only want a gut-lining formula.
I'd skip the "gut cleanse" stacks (oregano oil, wormwood, black walnut, berberine) until you have a real reason to suspect overgrowth, parasites or H. pylori, and for H. pylori my advice is different anyway: berberine works well (see Parasites, above).
Start low. And if you have severe pain, GI bleeding, fever, dehydration, rapid weight loss, an IBD flare, cancer, immune suppression, a pregnancy or another complex condition, see a qualified clinician instead.
