The best general commercial stack is Microbiome Labs Total Gut Restoration, because it combines probiotic, prebiotic and mucosal-support components. If you want one more specific to leaky gut, Dr. Ruscio's Leaky Gut Support Kit is a reasonable option. And for a single gut-lining repair formula, Designs for Health GI Revive is one of the more complete products, but it's a repair product, so don't treat it as a complete gut protocol by itself. Actually, these kits were Gemini's suggestions, but so far they're the best ones I've seen. I haven't used them myself, tho. People can check the reviews.
I wouldn't recommend the general "gut cleanse" stacks built around oregano oil, wormwood, black walnut, berberine, or other antimicrobial herbs as a starting point for everyone. Save those for later, when there's a stronger reason to suspect overgrowth, parasites, H. pylori, or some other specific problem. (My own advice is different for H. pylori, where berberine works well: see Parasites, above.)
Start low, especially with prebiotics, resistant starch, butyrate-support products and probiotics. And if you have severe pain, GI bleeding, fever, dehydration or rapid weight loss, work with a qualified clinician instead of stacking supplements on your own. Same if you're pregnant, or have active IBD flares, cancer, immune suppression or complex medical conditions.
