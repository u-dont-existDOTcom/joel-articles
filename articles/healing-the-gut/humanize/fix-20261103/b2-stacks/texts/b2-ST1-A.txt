If you want a ready-made gut stack
I don't think there's a universal supplement stack that heals everyone's gut. The right order still matters: fix the mechanics of digestion first, then reset diet and motility, and only escalate to antimicrobial or anti-parasite protocols if symptoms persist.
But some people want a ready-made starting point instead of buying every ingredient separately. For a general "repair and rebuild" approach, I'd look for a stack that covers three categories:
Microbiome support, meaning a well-tolerated probiotic or probiotic system
Prebiotic / butyrate support: something that helps with short-chain fatty acid production, butyrate especially
Barrier repair: nutrients that support the gut lining, like glutamine, zinc carnosine, colostrum, mucosal-support herbs, or anything similar
