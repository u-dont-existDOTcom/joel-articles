Optional Ready-Made Gut Stacks
I don't think there's a universal supplement stack that heals everyone's gut. And the right order still matters: you fix the digestion mechanics first, then reset diet and motility, and you only escalate into antimicrobial or anti-parasite protocols if symptoms persist after that.
But some people would rather have a ready-made starting point than buy every ingredient separately. For a general "repair and rebuild" approach, I'd look for a stack that covers three categories:
Microbiome support: a well-tolerated probiotic or probiotic system
Prebiotic / butyrate support: something that helps short-chain fatty acid production, esp. butyrate
Barrier repair: nutrients that support the gut lining, such as glutamine, zinc carnosine, colostrum, mucosal-support herbs, or similar compounds (the first three are all under Leaky gut, above)
