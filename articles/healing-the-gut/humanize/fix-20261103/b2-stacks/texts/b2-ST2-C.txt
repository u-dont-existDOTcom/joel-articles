Gemini suggested these kits, but so far they're the best ones I've seen, tho I haven't used them myself, so check the reviews. Microbiome Labs Total Gut Restoration is the best general stack, because it has probiotic, prebiotic and mucosal-support parts. If it's more of a leaky gut thing, Dr. Ruscio's Leaky Gut Support Kit is a reasonable option. And Designs for Health GI Revive is one of the more complete single formulas for the gut lining, but it's a repair product, not a whole gut protocol by itself.
I wouldn't use the general "gut cleanse" stacks, the ones built around oregano oil, wormwood, black walnut, berberine or other antimicrobial herbs, as a starting point for everyone. Those come later, when there's a stronger reason to suspect overgrowth, parasites, H. pylori or some other specific problem. (For H. pylori my own advice is different, since berberine works well for it: see Parasites, above.)
Start with low doses, especially of prebiotics, resistant starch, butyrate-support products and probiotics. And if you have severe pain, GI bleeding, fever, dehydration, rapid weight loss, an active IBD flare, cancer, immune suppression, pregnancy or a complex medical condition, work with a qualified clinician instead of stacking supplements on your own.
