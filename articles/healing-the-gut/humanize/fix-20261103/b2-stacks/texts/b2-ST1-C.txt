Optional Ready-Made Gut Stacks
I don't think there's one supplement stack that heals everyone's gut. The order still matters: fix the digestion mechanics first, then reset diet and motility, and only go to antimicrobial or anti-parasite protocols if your symptoms are still there.
But some people want a ready-made starting point instead of buying everything separately. For a general "repair and rebuild" stack, I'd want it to cover three things: something for the microbiome (a probiotic or probiotic system you tolerate well), something for prebiotics and butyrate (that helps you make short-chain fatty acids, especially butyrate), and something to repair the barrier, nutrients for the gut lining like glutamine, zinc carnosine, colostrum, mucosal-support herbs or similar.
