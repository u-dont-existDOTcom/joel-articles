Optional Ready-Made Gut Stacks

I don't think there's one supplement stack that heals everyone's gut. Whatever you take, the order matters. Get your digestion working mechanically first, then reset your diet and your gut motility, and only go after bugs and parasites with antimicrobial or anti-parasite protocols if you still have symptoms after that.

That said, some people want a ready-made stack rather than buying every ingredient for it separately. So, if I were to recommend a general "repair and rebuild" approach, it would be a stack that covers three categories:

First, support the Microbiome with a well-tolerated probiotic or probiotic system.

Then keep the probiotics well-fed with a good prebiotic, and take butyrate itself or something that helps short-chain fatty acid production, especially the main one, butyrate.

Other nutrients will help support barrier repair, such as glutamine, zinc carnosine, colostrum, mucosal-support herbs, or similar compounds.

Gemini suggested these kits to me, but so far they're the best ones I've seen, tho I haven't used them myself, so check the reviews. Designs for Health GI Revive is one of the more complete gut-lining repair formulas. You still need the rest of a gut protocol with it. There's also Dr. Ruscio's Leaky Gut Support Kit, which makes sense if leaky gut is your main problem. Microbiome Labs Total Gut Restoration is the best general stack of these, because you get mucosal support along with the probiotic and prebiotic.

I wouldn't start everyone off on the general "gut cleanse" stacks either, the ones built around oregano oil, wormwood, black walnut, berberine or other antimicrobial herbs. Those are for later (Phase 3), once you have more reason to suspect overgrowth, parasites, H. pylori or something else specific.
