Start any kit at a low dose and build up as your gut gets used to it. The prebiotic part especially can give you gas at first, since that's what your bacteria make when they ferment it (resistant starch does it too), and a new probiotic can bloat you or upset your stomach for a while.
But don't start a stack at all before seeing a doctor if you're bleeding from your gut or losing weight fast, because supplements won't fix whatever's behind that, and the same goes for bad pain, a fever or getting dehydrated. And if you're pregnant (berberine isn't safe then), your immune system is weak (probiotics are live bacteria, and a weak immune system can't always keep them in check), or you have an IBD flare, cancer or another serious illness, work out what to take with your doctor instead of putting a stack together yourself.
