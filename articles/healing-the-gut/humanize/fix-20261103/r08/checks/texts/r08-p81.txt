Whichever kit you get, start each part at a low dose and build up as your gut gets used to it. That goes most for the prebiotic (resistant starch is one too), since your gut bacteria make gas when they ferment it, and for the probiotic, which can bloat you or upset your stomach at first as well.
Bleeding from your gut or fast weight loss can mean something more serious that supplements won't fix, so see a doctor before you start. So can bad pain or a fever, and so can getting dehydrated. If you're pregnant or your immune system is weak, work out what's safe with your doctor instead of putting a stack together yourself, since berberine isn't safe in pregnancy, and probiotics are live bacteria that a weak immune system can't always keep in check. That goes for an IBD flare too, and for cancer (see the glutamine caution above) or another serious illness.
