A stack is a set of supplements you take together, and none of them replaces the phases above. I don't think there's one stack that heals everyone's gut, because guts go wrong for different reasons, and no kit can release a stuck vagus nerve or get your bile flowing. So whatever you take, keep to the order of the phases: get your digestion working mechanically first (Phase 1), then reset your diet and your meal spacing (Phase 2), and only go after bugs and parasites (Phase 3) if you still have symptoms after that. A stack is for the rebuilding in Phase 5, once you're clear of infection and it's time to feed the good bacteria again.
