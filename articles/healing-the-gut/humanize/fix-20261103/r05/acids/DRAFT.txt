Malic and citric acid (which make green apples and tart cherries sour) are intermediate products in that same cycle, also called the TCA or Krebs cycle, so in theory they're a quick energy source for those lining cells too. But there's no good evidence they add much next to glutamine. The better reason to eat them is iron: in one study, adding 1 g of malic or citric acid to a rice meal raised how much of its iron people absorbed two to three times (Gillooly et al., 1983).
