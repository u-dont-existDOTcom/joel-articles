Version 3, now in r05:

Malic and citric acid (which make green apples and tart cherries sour) are made and used partway round that same cycle, also called the TCA or Krebs cycle, so in theory they're a quick energy source for your enterocytes too. But nobody has shown that eating them gives those cells much extra fuel on top of the glutamine. They do help you absorb iron, though. In one study, people absorbed two to three times as much of the iron in a rice meal when 1 g of pure malic or citric acid was mixed in (Gillooly et al., 1983), probably because the acids help keep the iron dissolved so the gut can take it up. So something sour with an iron-rich meal is worth having anyway.
