Optional Ready-Made Gut Stacks

P1I do not think there is a universal supplement stack that heals everyone's gut. The correct order still matters: fix digestion mechanics first, reset diet and motility, then only escalate into antimicrobial or anti-parasite protocols if symptoms persist.

P2That said, some people want a ready-made starting point rather than buying every ingredient separately. For a general "repair and rebuild" approach, I would look for a stack that covers three categories:

1.Microbiome support: a well-tolerated probiotic or probiotic system
P32.Prebiotic / butyrate support: something that helps short-chain fatty acid production, especially butyrate
P43.Barrier repair: nutrients that support the gut lining, such as glutamine, zinc carnosine, colostrum, mucosal-support herbs, or similar compounds

P5The best general commercial stack I have seen so far is Microbiome Labs Total Gut Restoration, because it combines probiotic, prebiotic, and mucosal-support components. For a more leaky-gut-specific stack, Dr. Ruscio's Leaky Gut Support Kit is a reasonable option.
