Phase 1: The Pre-Flight Safety Check
Before you throw supplements or antimicrobials at your gut, the mechanics have to work. This part is from T.C. Hale (Kick Your Fat in the Nuts) and Stanley Rosenberg (Accessing the Healing Power of the Vagus Nerve).
Are your pupils dilated all the time? No gag reflex? Then according to Hale you're stuck in fight/flight ("sympathetic dominance"), and digestion is offline. Rosenberg's fix is his Basic Exercise (eye movements) or the Salamander, which mechanically release the vagus nerve so your body can switch into rest and digest.
Then bile. Do you burp after fatty meals? Do your stools float, or look like clay? Then your bile is stagnant, and Hale says if you take antimicrobials now you'll get a herx reaction you can't detoxify. So thin the bile first with Beet Flow or phosphatidylcholine, before any killing protocol.
