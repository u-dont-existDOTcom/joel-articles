Phase 4: The Investigation (The Detective)
This phase is all Benjamin Brown (Digestive Health Solution), for when even the "Nuclear Option" failed. At that point the microbiome is ruled out, and the problem is likely organ failure, environmental toxicity, or brain plasticity. (The last two are Phase 6, below.)
So here, you check the organs. Is it your pancreas? Test for Exocrine Pancreatic Insufficiency (Stool Elastase). Or is it Bile Acid Diarrhea? That's the SeHCAT test. Then treat with high-dose enzymes or bile binders, depending on what you find.
