Phase 1: The Pre-Flight Safety Check (The Mechanic & Electrician)
This first phase comes from T.C. Hale (Kick Your Fat in the Nuts) and Stanley Rosenberg (Accessing the Healing Power of the Vagus Nerve). Before you add "fuel" (supplements) or "fire" (antimicrobials), you have to make sure the engine is mechanically sound.
Hale starts with your autonomic tone. Are your pupils dilated all the time? Is your gag reflex absent? If yes, you're in "Sympathetic Dominance" (fight/flight), and your digestion is offline.
To fix that, Rosenberg has you do the "Basic Exercise" (eye movements) or "The Salamander" to mechanically release the Vagus nerve and switch your body into "Rest and Digest."
Next is bile flow, and that's Hale again. Do you burp after fatty meals? Do your stools float or look clay-colored? If yes, your bile is stagnant, and adding the "fire" now (antimicrobials) will cause a "herx" reaction you can't detoxify.
Hale's fix is to thin the bile with Beet Flow or Phosphatidylcholine before you start any killing protocol.
