Phase 6: Refractory Cases
The last phase is from Neil Nathan (Toxic) and Annie Hopper (Wired for Healing). It's for people who are "sensitive to everything," or who relapse right away even though they've stuck to the protocol perfectly.
Check your environment (Nathan). Could it be mold/mycotoxins, or Lyme? Get assessed for those, and if it comes back positive, use specific binders (Cholestyramine, Charcoal, Clay).
Then check the brain. If you react to everything, Hopper says the issue is Limbic System Impairment, and you'd use "Brain Retraining" (DNRS) to stop the brain from firing false "danger" signals to the gut.
