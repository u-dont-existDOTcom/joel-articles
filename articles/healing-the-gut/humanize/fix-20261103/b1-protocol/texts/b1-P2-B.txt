Phase 2: The Reset
Dr. Michael Ruscio (Healthy Gut, Healthy You) and Dr. Mark Pimentel (The Microbiome Connection) are behind this phase. Here you bring down the inflammation and starve out the overgrowths, without any harsh chemicals.
Go on a Paleo-Low FODMAP diet (Ruscio) or a Low Fermentation Eating plan (Pimentel). Either one is meant to stop feeding the bacteria in your small intestine.
Pimentel has you space your meals strictly 4-5 hours apart, with no snacking. That way the Migrating Motor Complex (MMC) can sweep the gut clean.
