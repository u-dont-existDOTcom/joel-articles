Phase 1: The Pre-Flight Safety Check
This phase is from T.C. Hale (Kick Your Fat in the Nuts) and Stanley Rosenberg (Accessing the Healing Power of the Vagus Nerve), and the idea is that before you add "fuel" (supplements) or "fire" (antimicrobials), the engine has to be mechanically sound.
Check your autonomic tone (Hale). Are your pupils dilated all the time? Is your gag reflex missing? If so, you're in "Sympathetic Dominance" (fight/flight), and your digestion is offline.
So how do you get out of that? Rosenberg has you do the "Basic Exercise" (eye movements) or "The Salamander" to mechanically release the Vagus nerve and switch your body into "Rest and Digest."
Then check your bile flow (Hale again). If you burp after fatty meals, or your stools float or look clay-colored, your bile is stagnant, and taking antimicrobials now (that's the "fire") will cause a "herx" reaction you can't detoxify.
His fix there is Beet Flow or Phosphatidylcholine, to thin the bile before you start any killing protocol.
