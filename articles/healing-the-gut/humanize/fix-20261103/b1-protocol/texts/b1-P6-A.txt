Phase 6: Refractory Cases (The Hazmat Team)
Are you "sensitive to everything"? Or do you relapse right away, even though you've stuck to the protocol perfectly? That's what this last phase is for, and it comes from Neil Nathan (Toxic) and Annie Hopper (Wired for Healing).
Nathan's first check is your environment. Could it be mold/mycotoxins, or Lyme? Assess for those, and if you test positive, use specific binders (Cholestyramine, Charcoal, Clay).
Then Hopper looks at the brain. If you react to everything (that first question again), the issue is Limbic System Impairment. Use her "Brain Retraining" (DNRS) to stop your brain from firing false "danger" signals to the gut.
