Phase 2: The Reset (The Architect & Scientist)
For the reset you follow Dr. Michael Ruscio (Healthy Gut, Healthy You) and Dr. Mark Pimentel (The Microbiome Connection). The point is to reduce inflammation and starve the overgrowths, without harsh chemicals.
Start with the diet. Adopt a Paleo-Low FODMAP diet (Ruscio) or a Low Fermentation Eating plan (Pimentel), since either way the goal is to stop feeding the bacteria in your small intestine.
Pimentel also wants your meals strictly spaced 4-5 hours apart, with no snacking. Why so strict? Because it allows the Migrating Motor Complex (MMC) to sweep your gut clean.
