Plant foods (Phases 2 and 5): The carnivore diet is trending, as many people (especially with leaky gut or lack of oxalate-processing bacteria) are noticing they cannot handle the fiber in plants, or the oxalates and phytates, compounds in many plants that bind minerals. It's certainly a diet worth trying, but it can lead to deficiencies over time, especially with regard to vitamins C, E, fiber, magnesium, potassium, calcium, iodine and other trace minerals, and bacteria needed to process plant foods (which already are likely missing in people who need this diet), thus making the carnivore diet sort of hard to get off.

Glutamine seems worth checking into, but plant intolerance is a symptom of a variety of underlying diseases that all have different treatments.
