Once the mechanics work, calm the inflammation down and starve the overgrowths, without any harsh chemicals. This one's from Dr. Michael Ruscio (Healthy Gut, Healthy You) and Dr. Mark Pimentel (The Microbiome Connection).

Diet-wise, go Paleo-Low FODMAP (Ruscio) or Low Fermentation Eating (Pimentel). Either way you stop feeding the bacteria in your small intestine, since both cut out the carbs they ferment most easily.

And no snacking! Pimentel wants meals 4-5 hours apart, strictly, so the Migrating Motor Complex (MMC) gets to sweep your gut clean in between.
