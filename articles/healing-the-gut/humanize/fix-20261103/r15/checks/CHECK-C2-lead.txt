Leaky gut (Phase 5): a widespread inflammatory condition often intersecting with other categories of illness such as Crohn's Disease or irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), plant food intolerance, and what many alternative health practitioners consider as a root cause of most diseases. It's associated clinically with increased levels of zonulin, the molecule released to open your intestinal tight junctions, the seals between the cells of your gut lining. Too much opening time leads to allowing stuff to leak out into your blood stream which then burdens your immune system and detoxification systems. Game-changing treatments:

Liposomal first-milking colostrum, the first milk a cow makes after giving birth, wrapped in tiny fat bubbles that help you absorb it (esp. fermented per Matt Nedin's protocol with bifidobacterium, a common probiotic, or SIBO yogurt bacteria). Matt from EndSickness.org finds that this helps especially to mop up mycotoxins, the toxins molds make, in the gut. Activated charcoal can also be helpful, altho don't take that with things you want to actually absorb.
