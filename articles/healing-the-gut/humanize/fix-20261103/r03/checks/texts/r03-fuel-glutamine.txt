L-Glutamine (watch Dr. Rhonda vid) is the main fuel for your enterocytes, and for your immune cells when they're activated. It skips the complicated digestive steps other nutrients have to go through. Inside the mitochondria it gets turned straight into alpha-ketoglutarate (α-KG), which feeds the citric acid cycle, where the mitochondria make their energy. So the cells get that energy right away, and that's what drives the repair of the gut lining and keeps LPS from slipping through.
