L-Glutamine (watch Dr. Rhonda vid) is the main fuel for your enterocytes, and for your immune cells when they're activated. It doesn't have to go through the complicated digestive steps other nutrients do. Inside the mitochondria, it gets turned straight into alpha-ketoglutarate, or α-KG. The α-KG goes into the citric acid cycle, which is how the mitochondria make their energy. The cells get that energy right away, and it drives the repair of your gut lining and keeps LPS out.
