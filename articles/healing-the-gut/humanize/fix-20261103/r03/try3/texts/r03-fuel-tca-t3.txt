The point of eating clean sour stuff like organic malic acid, tart cherries or green apples is to give the cells lining your gut another fast fuel, one that speeds up healing of the lining by a different biochemical route than glutamine. That works because malic and citric acid are TCA cycle intermediates. The TCA cycle is another name for the citric acid cycle, so these organic acids go directly into the same energy pathway in the mitochondria that glutamine feeds.
