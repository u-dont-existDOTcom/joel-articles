The TCA cycle is another name for the citric acid cycle, the one glutamine feeds. Organic acids like malic and citric acid are part of that cycle, so they go directly into the same energy pathway in the mitochondria that glutamine does. That means clean sour stuff, like organic malic acid, tart cherries or green apples, gives the cells lining your gut another quick fuel. It speeds up the healing of your gut lining as well, by a different biochemical route than glutamine.
