Take 5g a day as a baseline, to keep your enterocytes fed. Hard or long training, like CrossFit or intense cardio, breaks down the gut lining by starving your enterocytes. While you train, your body sends blood away from your gut to your working muscles, and that blood is what brings your enterocytes their oxygen and fuel. The baseline gives them the extra energy to repair that damage as well. If your immune system is dealing with an acute exposure, if your whole system is under a lot of stress, or if you have active IBD (Crohn's or colitis), raise it gradually to 10-30g a day.
