S1 Take 5g a day as a baseline, to keep your enterocytes fed.
S2 Hard or long training, like CrossFit or intense cardio, breaks down the gut lining too, by starving your enterocytes.
S3 While you train, your muscles pull blood away from your gut, and that blood is what brings your enterocytes their oxygen and fuel.
S4 The baseline is there to counter that as well.
S5 If your immune system is dealing with an acute exposure, if your whole system is under a lot of stress, or if you have active IBD (Crohn's or colitis), raise it gradually to 10-30g a day.
