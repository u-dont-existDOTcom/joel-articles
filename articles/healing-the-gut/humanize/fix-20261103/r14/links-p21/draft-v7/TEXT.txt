S1 Take 5g a day as a baseline, to keep your enterocytes fed.
S2 The 5g also counters the breakdown of the gut lining that hard or long training causes, like CrossFit or intense cardio.
S3 While you train, your body sends blood away from your gut to your working muscles, so your enterocytes get less oxygen and fuel.
S4 But the glutamine you swallow still gets to them, straight from inside your gut.
S5 If your immune system is dealing with an acute exposure, if your whole system is under a lot of stress, or if you have active IBD (Crohn's or colitis), raise it gradually to 10-30g a day.
