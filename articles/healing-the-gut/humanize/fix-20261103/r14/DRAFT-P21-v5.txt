Take 5g a day as a baseline, to keep your enterocytes fed. It also counters the breakdown of the gut lining that hard or long training causes, like CrossFit or intense cardio. That kind of training starves your enterocytes. While you train, your body sends blood away from your gut to your working muscles, and that blood is what brings your enterocytes their oxygen and fuel. If your immune system is dealing with an acute exposure, if your whole system is under a lot of stress, or if you have active IBD (Crohn's or colitis), raise it gradually to 10-30g a day.
