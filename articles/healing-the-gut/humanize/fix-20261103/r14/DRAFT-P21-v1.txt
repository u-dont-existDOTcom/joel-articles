Take 5g a day as a baseline. That's enough even if you train hard or a lot, like CrossFit or intense cardio. That kind of training breaks down the gut lining, because it pulls blood away from your gut to your muscles. If your immune system is dealing with an acute exposure, if your whole system is under a lot of stress, or if you have active IBD (Crohn's or colitis), raise it gradually to 10-30g a day.
