Take 5g a day as a baseline, to keep your enterocytes fed. The 5g also counters the breakdown of the gut lining that hard or long training causes, like CrossFit or intense cardio. While you train, your body sends blood away from your gut to your working muscles, so your enterocytes get less oxygen and fuel. But the glutamine you swallow still gets to them, straight from inside your gut. If your immune system is dealing with an acute exposure, if your whole system is under a lot of stress, or if you have active IBD (Crohn's or colitis), raise it gradually to 10-30g a day.

CAUTION: a lot of cancers feed on glutamine too, to fuel their growth. Your enterocytes take in a lot of it as soon as it reaches them, but not all of it, and the rest gets into your blood, where a cancer can get to it. They can only take in so much at a time, so the bigger the dose, the more of it gets into your blood. So if you have an active cancer, check with your oncologist before you take the 5g, and especially before you go up to the 10-30g.
