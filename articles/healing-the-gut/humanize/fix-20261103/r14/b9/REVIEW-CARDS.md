## F9-4. The glutamine caution, for any active cancer

Your enterocytes take in a lot of it as soon as it reaches them, but not all of it, and the rest gets into your blood, where a cancer can get to it. They can only take in so much at a time, so the bigger the dose, the more of it gets into your blood. So if you have an active cancer, check with your oncologist before you take the 5g, and especially before you go up to the 10-30g.
