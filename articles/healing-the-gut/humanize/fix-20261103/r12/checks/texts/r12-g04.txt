Leaky gut (Phase 5): a widespread inflammatory condition often intersecting with other categories of illness such as Crohn's Disease or IBS, plant food intolerance, and what many alternative health practitioners consider as a root cause of most diseases. It's associated with increased zonulin levels clinically. Zonulin is the molecule released to signal your intestinal tight junctions to open. Too much opening time leads to allowing stuff to leak out into your blood stream which then burdens your immune system and detoxification systems. Game-changing treatments:
Liposomal first-milking colostrum (esp. fermented per Matt Nedin's protocol with bifidobacterium, or SIBO yogurt bacteria). Matt from EndSickness.org finds that this helps especially to mop up mycotoxins in the gut. Activated charcoal can also be helpful, altho don't take that with things you want to actually absorb.
Fermented veggie juices of turmeric, red cabbage, celery, beet greens (not just beet root).
Zinc carnosine, the form of zinc which sticks to your intestinal lining.
A good BPC-157 supplement, like this one from Barrier, can also help. Note: I think Barrier's product looks smart, and their product may be really ideal, but there are some red flags in their marketing since they say it's made from plants, which is false.
Beta-glucans, in yeast extract.
