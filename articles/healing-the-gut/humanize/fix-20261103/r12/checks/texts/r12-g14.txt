Start any of these supplements or stacks at a low dose and build up slowly as your gut gets used to it. It's quite common for the prebiotic part especially to give you gas at first, since gas is what your bacteria make when they ferment the fiber or resistant starch, and a new probiotic can bloat you or upset your stomach for a while.
Some severe symptoms warrant seeing a doctor rather than considering this DIY approach at the moment. Take it seriously if you notice blood in your stool (if it's blood from upper digestive tract, it could be smelly, black & tarry, not red like from lower GI). Other serious signs to note: losing weight fast, severe pain, a fever or dehydration. And if you're pregnant, some herbs and supplements, like berberine, aren't safe. If your immune system is weak, some probiotics could be risky, since they're live bacteria that need to be kept in check to prevent overgrowth. If you have an IBD flare, cancer or another serious illness, the standard disclaimer applies: work out what to take with your doctor instead of putting a stack together yourself.
