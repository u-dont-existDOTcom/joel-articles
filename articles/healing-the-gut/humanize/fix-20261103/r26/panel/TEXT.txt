So check your organs (Brown). Do your stools look oily or greasy? Then your pancreas may not be making enough digestive enzymes, called Exocrine Pancreatic Insufficiency, and you'd test for that with Stool Elastase and treat it with high-dose enzymes. If too much of your bile is reaching your colon and giving you diarrhea (Bile Acid Diarrhea), Brown has you tested with SeHCAT and put on bile binders.
