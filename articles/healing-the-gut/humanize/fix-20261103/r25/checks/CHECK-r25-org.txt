So check your organs (Brown), starting with your pancreas: if it doesn't make enough digestive enzymes, called Exocrine Pancreatic Insufficiency, you'd test for that with Stool Elastase, and treat it with high-dose enzymes. If too much of your bile is reaching your colon and giving you diarrhea (Bile Acid Diarrhea), Brown has you tested with SeHCAT and put on bile binders.
