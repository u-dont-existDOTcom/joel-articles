Leaky gut : a widespread inflammatory condition often intersecting with other categories of illness such as Crohn’s Disease or IBS, plant food intolerance, and what many alternative health practitioners consider as a root cause of most diseases . It’s associated with increased zonulin levels clinically. Zonulin is the molecule released to signal your intestinal tight jungtions to open. Too much opening time leads to allowing stuff to leak out into your blood stream which then burdens your immune system and detoxification systems. Mitochondrial & Cellular Energy Starvation (Glutamine, Butyrate, TCA Intermediates): Your gut barrier requires massive amounts of immediate, localized energy to maintain tight junction integrity. If your enterocytes (small intestine) or colonocytes (large intestine) lack their preferred mitochondrial fuels, the barrier structurally collapses, allowing lipopolysaccharides (LPS) and endotoxins to leak into systemic circulation. L-Glutamine ( watch Dr. Rhonda vid ): The absolute premier energy substrate for enterocytes and activated immune cells. It bypasses complex digestive steps, converting directly into alpha-ketoglutarate (α-KG) inside the mitochondria to power the citric acid cycle. This immediate cellular energy hit actively drives the repair of the gut mucosal barrier and prevents LPS translocation. Dosing: Use 5g daily as a baseline to counter the gut mucosal breakdown caused by high-intensity or high-volume training (like CrossFit or intense cardio). Titrate up to 10–30g daily during acute immune exposure, high systemic stress, or if you are dealing with active IBD (Crohn’s/Colitis). The Cancer Caveat: Because hyper-proliferative cancer cells can also hijack glutamine to fuel their rapid replication, avoid high-dose protocol titrations if you have an active, existing colon or liver malignancy (the liver sees it first via portal vein delivery). Butyrate & SCFAs: While the small intestine runs on glutamine, the large intestine relies on short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) like butyrate. Getting these via raw resistant starch fermentation or your home-brewed SIBO yogurt fuels colonocytes, locks down the lower gut barrier, and systematically suppresses inflammatory signals. TCA Cycle Intermediates (Malic & Citric Acid): Organic acids like malate and citrate feed directly into the exact same mitochondrial energy pathways as glutamine. Consuming clean, sour profiles—such as organic malic acid, tart cherries, or green apples—provides rapid, alternative fuel to gut epithelial cells, accelerating mucosal healing from a different biochemical angle.
