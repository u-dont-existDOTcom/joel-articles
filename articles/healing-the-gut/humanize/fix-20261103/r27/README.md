r27's paragraph:

> So check your organs (Brown). Do your stools look oily or greasy? Then it's often your pancreas not making enough
> digestive enzymes, which doctors call Exocrine Pancreatic Insufficiency. A Stool Elastase test will tell you, and if
> that's it, you take high-dose enzymes with every meal. If it's diarrhea that keeps coming back instead, Brown says the
> issue can be Bile Acid Diarrhea, where too much of your bile reaches your colon. He has you tested for that with
> SeHCAT, and bile binders soak up the extra bile.

Sources for what it states: [Healthline, EPI signs and symptoms](https://www.healthline.com/health/exocrine-pancreatic-insufficiency/signs-and-symptoms)
(fatty, pale stools, steatorrhea, are EPI's common sign); [Healthline, EPI treatment options](https://www.healthline.com/health/exocrine-pancreatic-insufficiency/treatment-options)
(enzyme capsules at the beginning of every meal or snack); [NICE DG44](https://nice.org.uk/guidance/dg44/documents/topic-description)
(bile acids that aren't absorbed reach the colon and cause diarrhoea).
