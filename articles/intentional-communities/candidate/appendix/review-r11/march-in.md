Where do you start with the nuts and bolts? [***Creating a Life Together***](https://library.uniteddiversity.coop/Ecovillages_and_Low_Impact_Development/Creating_a_Life_Together-Practical_Tools_to_Grow_Ecovillages_and_Intentional_Communities.pdf) by Diana Leafe Christian, for membership, money, land and agreements. Want a guide to intentional community that covers the whole system? There's [***Building Belonging***](https://www.ic.org/building-belonging/) by Yana Ludwig. [***Many Voices One Song***](https://www.sociocracyforall.org/product/many-voices-one-song-ebook/) by Rau and Koch-Gonzalez is a practical manual for doing sociocracy.

[***The Continuum Concept***](https://www.arvindguptatoys.com/arvindgupta/conconcept.pdf) by Jean Liedloff (1975) is a foundational natural-child-rearing book, based on her time with the Ye’kuana. Is it settled anthropology? No, it's provocative observation, so take it with a grain of salt. [***Hunt, Gather, Parent***](https://www.amazon.com/Hunt-Gather-Parent-Ancient-Cultures/dp/1982149671) by Michaeleen Doucleff (2021) is a practical, accessible book about kids taking part in family life. It's also about autonomy, and spreading out the work of caring for them.
