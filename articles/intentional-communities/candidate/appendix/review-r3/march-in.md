[***Communities That Abide***](https://philebersole.wordpress.com/2014/06/23/dmitry-orlov-on-communities-that-abide/) by Dmitry Orlov makes an uncomfortable argument that long-lasting communities often rely on communal property, separation, boundaries and a binding ideology. From a distance, cohesion and capture can look a lot alike. Is longevity alone a meaningful measure, when a community may keep going without its members flourishing? That's the question in [“Measuring Success in Intentional Communities”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/334857266_Measuring_Success_in_Intentional_Communities_A_Critical_Evaluation_of_Commitment_and_Longevity_Theories), a paper by Rubin, Willis and Ludwig.
