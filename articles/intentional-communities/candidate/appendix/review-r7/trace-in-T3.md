CONTEXT AFTER: [***Hunt, Gather, Parent***](https://www.amazon.com/Hunt-Gather-Parent-Ancient-Cultures/dp/1982149671) by Michaeleen Doucleff (2021) is a practical, accessible book about kids taking part in family life, in a way that gives them autonomy and spreads out the work of caring for them.
