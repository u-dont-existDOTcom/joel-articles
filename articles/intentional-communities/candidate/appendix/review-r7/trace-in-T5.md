## What Helps Communities Last—and Whether Longevity Is Enough, entry 21

CONTEXT BEFORE: [***Commitment and Community***](https://www.amazon.com/Commitment-Community-Communes-Sociological-Perspective/dp/0674145763) by Rosabeth Moss Kanter is a classic. It compares a bunch of nineteenth-century communes, and the practices that built commitment in them.

PUBLISHED:

Benjamin Zablocki, [***Alienation and Charisma***](https://annas-archive.pk/md5/72f23810c1c2ab152c625338eb11c4f5) — field research on communes and the role of charisma.

REWRITE:

[***Alienation and Charisma***](https://annas-archive.pk/md5/72f23810c1c2ab152c625338eb11c4f5) by Benjamin Zablocki is field research on communes, and on the role charisma plays in them.

CONTEXT AFTER: Jo Freeman's [“The Tyranny of Structurelessness”](https://www.jofreeman.com/joreen/tyranny.htm) (1970) is the classic essay on hidden hierarchy. Can it be harder to challenge than explicit authority? She argues it can.
