## For Child-Rearing, entry 12

CONTEXT BEFORE: [***The Continuum Concept***](https://www.arvindguptatoys.com/arvindgupta/conconcept.pdf) by Jean Liedloff (1975) is a foundational natural-child-rearing book, based on her time with the Ye’kuana. Is it settled anthropology? No, it's provocative observation, so take it with a grain of salt.
