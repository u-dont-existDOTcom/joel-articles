## Contradictions in the published text

1. **Appendix, "Practical Community-Building Guides"**: Christian's *Creating a Life Together* is described as "start here for sequencing, membership, money, land, and agreements".
   - Conflicts with Joel's 2026-10-16 position: "we should not lean on Christian's book order if it contradicts my own order".
   - Also conflicts with the essay's own order ("Practice Comes Before Property"): "Practice together. Process a real conflict. Clarify values. Test membership. Decide ownership and exit. Then consider land." And with "What to Do This Month": "Start the inner pl/ork."
   - A reader would take the first step to be a book on structure, not practice.
   - Weaker second clash inside the appendix: the heading "Books and Papers, in Reading Order" lists the child-rearing books first, yet this entry says "start here". The conflict-exercises handbook (Cooperative Culture Handbook) also comes after the membership and land guide, the reverse of his order.
   - Does the rewrite still have it? Mostly fixed. "Sequencing" is dropped, and "Where do you start with the nuts and bolts?" is limited to nuts and bolts. The heading "in Reading Order" and the group order are unchanged.

2. **Body, "Resources, Land, and Exit Terms"** (outside the appendix): "For the full sequence—vision before membership, membership before pooling major resources, major resources before land—read Diana Leafe Christian's".
   - Conflicts with the same 2026-10-16 position and with "Practice Comes Before Property". Christian's sequence starts at vision and has no step of processing a real conflict before resources or land are pooled. Joel's does ("Before ownership and major resources mix, process one real conflict all the way through").
   - A reader would take Christian's order as the order to follow.
   - Does the rewrite still have it? Yes. The rewrite covers only the appendix, so this sentence stands in the published text and still points readers to her sequence.

No other contradiction found between the published appendix and the rest of the essay. Dates, the 17-year Bruderhof figure ("most of two decades" in the opening), the film credits and the two-sisters description of *American Commune* agree with the body.

**Conflicts in the rewrite: 0. Contradictions in the published text: 2.**
