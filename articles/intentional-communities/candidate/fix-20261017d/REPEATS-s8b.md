# Repeats check s8b: the new sentence in "The Grown Children Get the Final Review"

New sentence (B182): `And those who are known to lie a lot are especially targeted by abusers who know their accusations won't be believed.`

I read every block (B0 to B403) and searched the whole article for lying, honesty, being believed, abusers, predators, grooming, secrets, accusations, complaints and hearing a child. The relevant hits were B113, B177 to B184, B233, B234 and B250. The other uses of "honest" (B96, B118, B223, B241, B246, B365) are about adults or the article itself.

## 1. Repeats

None. No other passage says that children known for lying get picked out by abusers because their accusations will be dismissed. The other child-protection items (B177 to B185) each make a different point.

## 2. Contradictions

None. B183 says to hear a child even when the accused is loved, but the new sentence only reports what abusers count on. It does not say such accusations should be dismissed, and B182 goes on to say `A scared or confused kid may get mixed up telling what happened and still need protection.`

## 3. Near-misses rejected

1. B182, same paragraph: `Make sure kids understand what they say will be taken seriously if they are known to be honest.` and, a few sentences earlier, `"The Boy Who Cried Wolf" is a didactic children's story`. The first is the mirror image of the new sentence's first half (honest kids are believed, known liars are not), and the story implies the same lesson. What the new sentence adds is the safety stake: abusers choose known liars on purpose. Nothing else in the article says that, so it reads as the reason for the instruction, not a second telling of it.
2. B183: `Hear the child even when the accused is someone people love.` Also about accusations not being heard, but the cause is the accused's status, not the child's reputation for lying, and the instruction is different.
3. B250: `if complaints are too often or too broadly painted as projection, that's as much a failure, if not more so.` Also about a label used to dismiss complaints, but it concerns adult members' grievances, not child credibility or abusers.
