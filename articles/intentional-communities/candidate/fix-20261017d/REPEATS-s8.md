# Repeats and contradictions, s8

Section key. Every item involves a block there. Quotes are exact.

## 2. Contradictions

1. B191 (Door) against B290 ("Resources, Land, and Exit Terms"). B191: "If it can't, it can still avoid punishing departure." B290: "you'll need to have a way of providing people with resources when they leave", else the right to leave is "only on paper". One treats help at leaving as a nice-to-have a community may skip, the other as a requirement. B189 (Door) gives a reason for kids ("A poor or money-free community may have even less cash to offer"), and the groups differ (grown kids vs members who contributed), so this is soft; the text never ties the two standards together.

2. B184 (Protection) against B251 and B252 (both "How My Own Model Could Become a Cult"). B184: "reach without going through the community's leadership". B251: "as long as those relationships don't interfere with the community as a whole". B252: "the commune needs to step in and help people decide how to communicate with outsiders". Outside contact is a safeguard the community must not control for kids in one place, and something it may condition or mediate in the other. Kids vs adults is a possible reason, but a child's grandparent is also a parent's outside relationship, so a reader may see a pull.
