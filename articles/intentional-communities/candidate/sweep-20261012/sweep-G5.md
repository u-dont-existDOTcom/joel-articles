# Sweep

- The Grown Children | kind 2 | published "their words carry power and that adults will listen seriously" | rewrite "what they say will be taken seriously if they are known to be honest" | A reader now believes only children with a record of honesty get heard seriously, not every child; the published promise is unconditional.
- The Grown Children | kind 3 | published "may tell a story imperfectly and still need protection" (and the third thing education must never become, "a reason to dismiss a report") | rewrite "may get mixed up telling what happened, and that's to be expected" (list ends "interrogation or punishment when they're not sure") | A reader no longer learns that a muddled account must not be used to dismiss the report, or that such a child still needs protection; it reads as merely normal.
- The Grown Children | kind 3 | published "competent help and review that the home community does not control" | rewrite "competent help and review, hopefully including non-intrusive help from outside the home community" | The independence standard is gone: a reader would think review can stay inside the community and outside help is only hoped for, not that those at risk need help and review the community does not control.
- The Grown Children | kind 3 | published "If children grow up without using money, they especially need enough knowledge of the outside money world" | rewrite "Kids in or outside of intentional communities all need to learn enough about how the money world outside operates" | The condition and "especially" are lost: a reader would think every child needs this equally and that a money-free upbringing adds no particular duty (and would treat all intentional communities as money-free).
