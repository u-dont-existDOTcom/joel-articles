The secular failure

The secular communes had a solution for how to distribute property or labor, but when the deep waters of jealousy or childhood hurt or humiliation or attraction or resentment or grief came up, the group was without a common method for dealing with it. The feelings just became agenda items.

Meeting procedure isn’t going to solve every problem, no matter how sophisticated the system of labor and money and decision making may be in a community of the Federation of Egalitarian Communities or other income-sharing community. It can’t make it so that two people don’t hate each other. It can only make it so that they take turns talking about it, and that one of them has to find somebody who agrees with him or her before they get to talk about it.
