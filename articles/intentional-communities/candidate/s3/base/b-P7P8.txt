The two failures mirror each other. Secular groups distribute power but leave the inner life largely private. Spiritual groups address the inner life but concentrate power around whoever defines the path.

The deeper failure is that authority earned in one role can be spent in every other role. A founder may be a gifted teacher. A therapist may genuinely help people. A farmer or business manager may carry much of the economy. The danger begins when the same person can define the problem, control the evidence, choose the remedy, and make disagreement or exit costly.
