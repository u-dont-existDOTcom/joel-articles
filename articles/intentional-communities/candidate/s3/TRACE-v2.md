# Trace v2: R-section against the original

Differences only. P1 sits under the title, P2–P4 under "The secular failure," P5–P9 under "The spiritual failure."

## P1 "The communes I knew tended to fail inwardly…"

| Original | Rewrite | What differs |
|---|---|---|
| "tended to fail"; "two directions" | "Most… went wrong"; "two ways" | SHIFTED: hedge "tended to" becomes the quantifier "most"; "fail" becomes the weaker "went wrong"; "directions" becomes "ways," losing the opposite-poles sense. |

## P2 "This failure is caused when…"

| Original | Rewrite | What differs |
|---|---|---|
| "This failure is caused when the community distributes… but has no shared way" | "The secular ones had a solution… the group was without a common method" | SHIFTED: a conditional cause becomes a past-tense statement about "the secular ones," reading as true of secular communes generally; "caused" is gone. |
| "Those things don't disappear." | | DROPPED: the persistence claim is not stated here. |
| "They acquire agenda items." | "The feelings just became agenda items." | SHIFTED: feelings that persist and take on agenda items become feelings converted into them; "just" adds dismissiveness. |
| "distributes ownership and labor" | "had a solution for how to distribute property or labor" | SHIFTED: doing becomes having a method; ownership becomes property; "and" becomes "or." |
| | "the deep waters of"; "came up" | ADDED: calls the feelings deep; adds a trigger, when they surface. |
| | "The secular ones" | SENSE: "ones" needs inference; the nearest plural in P1 is "ways," and the sense needs "communes." |

## P3 "The Federation of Egalitarian Communities…"

| Original | Rewrite | What differs |

| "whose turn it is to speak" | "who gets to talk" | SHIFTED: speaking order becomes permission to speak. |

| Original | Rewrite | What differs |

The sitting-nearby image is gone. |

| "schedules, committees, noise, food, or policy" | "a schedule or committee… or some policy or other" | SHIFTED: "some policy or other" adds a dismissive note. |
| | "It will be an argument" | SENSE: "it" could be politics or the emotional material; the original had "People argue." |

## P5 "This is caused when the community recognizes…"

## P6 "The Farm in Tennessee…"

| Original | Rewrite | What differs |
|---|---|---|
| "grew to around 1,500 people" | "at its largest about 1,500 people" | SHIFTED: growth to a figure becomes its peak. |
| "was also deeply shaped by Stephen Gaskin" | "But in the early days… was deeply shaped" | SHIFTED: additive "also" becomes adversative "But," setting Gaskin's influence against the values. |
| "Accounts from former members describe… reaching into intimate decisions" | "From what former members have shared… ran deep into members' intimate decisions" | SHIFTED: "reaching into" becomes the stronger "ran deep into"; "accounts" becomes what members "have shared." |
| "A group can talk… while one man's opinions become unusually difficult to escape" | "It's one thing to talk… It's another to struggle to escape one man's opinions" | SHIFTED: coexistence in one group ("while") becomes a contrast between talking and struggling. DROPPED: "unusually," "become." ADDED: "struggle" shows people struggling; the original says only that the opinions are hard to escape. |

## P8 "The deeper failure is…"

| Original | Rewrite | What differs |
|---|---|---|
| | "someone has too much authority, or rather" | ADDED: a claim about excess of authority; "or rather" does not remove it. |
| "can be spent in every other role" | "carries authority earned in one domain into the others" | SHIFTED: possibility becomes actual practice; roles become domains. |
| "A founder may be a gifted teacher.
