# Section 11 pairs (D1): published paragraph and rewrite

Section 11 is "Founderism: The Four Questions". The rewrite keeps the published order.

## P18 → P18y

CONTEXT BEFORE: “Nothing suppressed” applies to myself and my relationship with my own material. It doesn't give anyone the right to pull anything out of me. Any member can choose not to share material, and they don't need to provide a therapeutic rationale for not sharing.

PUBLISHED P18:
Medicine is optional. Abstaining cannot reduce membership rights. No individual or permanent clique controls admission. Disagreement has to be answered as disagreement.

REWRITE P18y:
Nobody has to take medicine, and the six months of peer counseling before it is a rule the community holds for its ceremonies, not one of the principles above. Your decision not to take medicine can't reduce your rights as a member. No single person or permanent group of people will have the power to decide who gets into the community. And when you disagree with someone, they have to answer your disagreement as a disagreement.

CONTEXT AFTER: “Your resistance is your wound talking” is pretty much a conversation ender, which is why it's different from the other signs: once your disagreement gets diagnosed, you can't bring up any of the others either. Once you notice a community diagnosing dissent like this instead of answering it, leave. Don't wait until they schedule a group process about your leaving.

## P19 → P19s

CONTEXT BEFORE: Nobody has to take medicine, and the six months of peer counseling before it is a rule the community holds for its ceremonies, not one of the principles above. PUBLISHED P19:
“Your resistance is your wound talking” is one of the most efficient sentences ever invented for avoiding an argument. When a community begins diagnosing dissent instead of responding to it, leave before they schedule a group process about your leaving.

REWRITE P19s:
“Your resistance is your wound talking” is pretty much a conversation ender, which is why it's different from the other signs: once your disagreement gets diagnosed, you can't bring up any of the others either. Once you notice a community diagnosing dissent like this instead of answering it, leave. Don't wait until they schedule a group process about your leaving.

CONTEXT AFTER: There are some basics that can’t be voted away from you by the majority in a community. These are: safety of your body; access to adequate food and sleep; ability to see a doctor of your choice; the ability to keep your thoughts to yourself; ability to communicate with people outside the community; the ability to report harm without going through the friends of the accused person; and a usable way of leaving the community. If these things are taken away from you, even by a majority vote, it is not consensual.
