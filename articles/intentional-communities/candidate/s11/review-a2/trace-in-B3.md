# Section 11 pairs (B3): published paragraph and rewrite

Section 11 is "Founderism: The Four Questions". The rewrite keeps the published order.

## P14 → P14p

CONTEXT BEFORE: Every practice in this article can be turned against the people using it.

PUBLISHED P14:
“Nothing suppressed, everything processed” can become compulsory disclosure. I’ve seen the risk in communities using ZEGG Forum. A group begins by inviting honesty and ends by treating privacy as resistance.

REWRITE P14p:
“Nothing suppressed, everything processed” can be taken to mean that someone in the group must tell all, even if they don’t want to. I have seen that risk in groups that use the ZEGG Forum practice. A group starts out inviting honesty and ends up treating privacy as resistance.

CONTEXT AFTER: Peer counseling can become peer surveillance, even if the tone is warm. And when things like that are required as pre-reqs for people to get medicine, it can put a lot of power in the hands of the gatekeepers, right when people are at their most open. Even something couched in words like “willing to be seen” could become a demand that people disclose whatever the group wants them to.
