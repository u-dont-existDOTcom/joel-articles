Oh, and not to mention the humor. I literally laugh outside for hours at a time. Never have I been in a culture that jokes so much. Of course, it would be great to be able to really understand the more complicated jokes in Wolof (this is beyond basic conversation level; this is grad school).

In addition, being in a village setting and surrounded by people who are connected to each other in some way, most things are taken care of through their extended family or local, in addition to the more formal way of taking care of things. Whether it be land, kids, housing, food, or whatever else obligation. It’s great. Who needs a workshop on how to bring back this aspect of families, since it’s already here?
