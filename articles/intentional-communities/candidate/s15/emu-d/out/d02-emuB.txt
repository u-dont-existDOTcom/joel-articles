Not to mention the humor! I can often go outside for hours at a time and simply just listen to people laughing their heads off. It is by far the most joking culture I have ever been a part of, though I’m not sure I will ever understand some of the more complicated jokes made in Wolof.

In the villages and within families so much is taken care of by the extended family and the memory of the people there, in addition to whatever formal systems there might be. Whether it is providing land, helping with children, taking care of someone’s living arrangements, helping with food, fulfilling obligations, etc, etc. When is the last time someone gave YOU something to do?
