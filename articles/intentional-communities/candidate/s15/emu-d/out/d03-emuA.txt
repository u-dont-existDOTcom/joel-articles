But it is often soul destroying in today's world. What often happens is that the sense of obligation is still there, but the communal way of life is gone and so it is hard for the cash earning member of the family to become independent and work towards long term goals. How can he or she get ahead, if there are so many people who are dependant on him or her? But the rest can't become independent either, because all they have are the payments from their relative. The ancestral ethic is thus carried over into an individualistic cash earning system, and it eats away at people.
