All is not well in paradise

Now, I don’t in anyway think that Sénégal is some magical utopia where everyone still lives in some mythical, pre-lapsarian communal paradise, far from it.

Sénégal recently (March 2026) passed a new law (2026-08) increasing the penalty for consensual intimacy between members of the same sex to between 5-10 years in prison, and also making “apology” for homosexuality in public and certain types of financing or support that promote homosexuality a criminal act. It is not uncommon for homosexuals to be arrested in Sénégal, to be rejected by their families, and to be harassed, assaulted, and attacked by mobs. When arrested, homosexuals are said to actually be safer once in custody, as they are then protected from the mob. I cannot speak of the virtues of community while ignoring those who must hide or flee to be who they are.
