All is not well in paradise

Am I making Sénégal out to be some magical land where people live in a communal paradise, blissfully unaware of their selfish needs? No!

Sénégal passed a law in March 2026, Law # 2026-08, which increased the penalties for consensual intimate relations between persons of the same sex to 5-10 years in prison and criminalized public “apology” for homosexuality and certain types of financing and support intended to promote homosexuality. Homosexuals are arrested, shunned by their families, persecuted and sometimes attacked even by mobs. They say that once arrested they are protected from the mobs. I cannot wax poetic about the virtues of community while ignoring those who must hide who they are in order to live in this society or to leave.
