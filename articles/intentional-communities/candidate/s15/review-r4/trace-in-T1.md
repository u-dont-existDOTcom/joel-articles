Sénégal is unlike anywhere else I've lived. Five times a day the prayers pour out into the streets. I find them so soothing! And Sufism doesn't stay inside designated "spiritual" places here. It's in the music, in the pictures everywhere, in the murals, in the names, in the conversations.

CONTEXT AFTER: And the humor! People are outside laughing for hours at a time. It's the most joking culture I've ever been in, and I keep wondering if I'll ever understand the really complicated jokes in Wolof. Basic conversation is one thing. But knowing why twelve people are suddenly crying with laughter? That's graduate school.
