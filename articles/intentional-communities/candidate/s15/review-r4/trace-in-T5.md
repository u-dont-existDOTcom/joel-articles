# Section 15 pairs (T5): published paragraph and rewrite

Section 15 is "What This Looks Like From Sénégal", with two subheadings ("All is not well in paradise", "Lessons from the Black Tax"). The author lives in Sénégal and stays in a friend's family house. The rewrite keeps the published order and headings, one rewrite paragraph per published paragraph. It is deliberately loose and spoken (short reactions, questions, run-on lists, "etc."); a short reaction or question that claims nothing about the world is there on purpose as the paragraph's pause, so report it as ADDED only if it states something new. Judge what the rewrite says, not its polish.

## All is not well in paradise H2-P6

CONTEXT BEFORE: Where I live, neighbors steal our moringa pods every morning. Every morning! They could plant a moringa tree in their own yard. Apparently maintaining this relationship with ours is more entertaining. 😂

PUBLISHED:

Everybody raises sheep here, including me, and they are extremely cute. The music, animals, joking, prayer, and constant presence of other people can make life feel like another world.

REWRITE:

Everybody here has sheep (even me!), and they are super cute! It can feel like you're living in another world, with the music, the animals, the joking, the praying, and just being around people all the time.

CONTEXT AFTER: Of course, the old ways here are changing. Young people want to move to the city, get a job, get a phone, eat imported food, be part of the wider world, etc. (some young men even make the dangerous journey by sea to Europe). And can you blame them? Families that used to share almost everything are now more reliant on cash and individual opportunities.
