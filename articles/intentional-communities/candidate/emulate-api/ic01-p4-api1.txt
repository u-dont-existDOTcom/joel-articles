That is why I put “escuelita” in the title. In the Zapatista escuelita, people from outside the Zapatista communities would live with the Zapatistas in order to learn how the communities function autonomously. So, some people have to live in these communities in order to get healed and learn the methods. Then they can take these methods with them when they go on to form their own healing communities. Otherwise, the benefits will remain restricted to the original members of the communities.
