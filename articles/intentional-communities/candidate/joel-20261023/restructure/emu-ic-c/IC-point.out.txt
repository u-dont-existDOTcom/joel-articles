For the majority of readers, there will likely be many reasons you are not able to join or create a community on land at this time. You may have a job, kids, pets, debt, a house mortgage, a sick parent, etc. That is fine. The most important pl/ork doesn’t require land anyway!

The How-To Manual which follows this essay covers the whole model: inner, relational, and medicine pl/ork, container, tech, jealousy, kids, money, land, founder power, how the communities spread, leaving, etc.

It is possible to buy 40 acres of land, take whatever you were bringing into relationships/communities before you bought land, and bring it to a more beautiful location. Sure, your scenery may be better, but now you have to deal with your stuff plus goats. A group of people may practice together for two years a couple of times per week and never end up on land. So what? If the practice helped one of you through a divorce, illness, grief, better equip you for parenting, or combat loneliness…
