I would say it's more true of the big attempts at socialism rather than small communes. But it is true. Some people really don't want to take responsibility for anything. In any group of people, there tend to be only a few who are willing to take on maintenance duties. The rest enjoy "freedom" by hanging out in hammocks. This gives the leaders (even in groups that are supposed to not have leaders) a tremendous amount of power.

Any attempt at communal ownership needs to take this into account by assigning stewardship duties by name, and by having a plan to deal with leeches. The manual at the bottom of the post deals with these issues. Removing the possibility of having a boss doesn't make people any less passive, selfish, thieving, or suffering from learned helplessness.
