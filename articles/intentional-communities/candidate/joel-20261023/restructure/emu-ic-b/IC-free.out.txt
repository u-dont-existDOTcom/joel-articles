I would say this is more true for the big attempts at socialism than the small communes, but it is true. There are always going to be people who prefer to let other people take responsibility. Often, it comes down to the same three people who end up doing all the work to keep the commune running, while the rest rail against "bosses" and "work" while hanging out in hammocks. The people who end up being the de facto leaders end up with more power than anyone else.

There needs to be some sort of duty assigned to each person when it comes to maintaining the communally owned items, and some sort of way of dealing with those who prefer to leach off the rest of the group. That's what the manual I linked below covers. Removing the idea of a boss does not make people less passive, less selfish, less likely to steal, or less likely to suffer from learned helplessness.
