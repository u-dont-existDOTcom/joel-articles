In writing this I’m keenly aware that many of you have good reasons for not being able to drop what you’re doing and go join/make community, get land, etc. (job, kids, pets, debt, house, sick parent, etc…) And, I think what I’m calling pl/ork is so important that it needs to be done even if you don’t have land.

(If you’re impatient to get to the how-to of all of this, including inner, relational, medicine pl/ork, creating the container, using tech to help it, dealing with jealousy, kids, money, land, founder power, spreading, leaving, etc…there’s a How To Manual directly after this.

One of the problems I see is that people tend to buy land (forty acres) and then bring whatever crap they weren’t doing anything about to the more beautiful place they now live in. So they have all the same crap they had before, but now they also have goats. It may be that your pl/ork group never gets land. But if you’ve been showing up and doing it for two years, you’re going to be changed. When you go through a divorce, illness, grieving, parenting, loneliness, etc… you’re going to be different.
