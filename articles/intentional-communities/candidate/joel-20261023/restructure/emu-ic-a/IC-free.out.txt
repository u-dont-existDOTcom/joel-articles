That comment above is more often true of large scale socialism experiments, but even on a small commune scale, there are always those who would rather someone else be responsible. When the lawn mowing, trash removal, and general maintenance is always done by the same three people, and everyone else wants freedom to sit in their hammocks, you'll find that power is seized by those three "leaders".

Communal ownership needs to address the issue of specific stewardship duties, assigned to named parties, and a plan for dealing with leeches. (See below manual.) It is not as if, by removing the idea of a boss, people will stop being passive, selfish, thievery and otherwise exhibit learned helplessness.
