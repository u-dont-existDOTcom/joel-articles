I understand that for most people there are lots of reasons why it’s hard to jump in and participate in or start building a community on land – jobs, kids, pets, debt, mortgage, taking care of a sick parent, etc etc etc. So that’s fine! The most important pl/ork doesn’t require land!

(And if you are wondering what this crazy model is that I am talking about and want something more substantial than ‘go hang out with friends,’ there’s a How-To Manual after this essay that goes over the whole model: inner pl/ork, relational pl/ork, medicine pl/ork, container, tech, jealousy, kids, money, land, founder power, how to spread it, how to leave, etc etc etc.)

Some people buy 40 acres of land and take all of their unprocessed stuff with them there. Sure, now they’ve got a much more beautiful view, but they’ve also got all of that AND now they’ve got goats. It’s possible you could practice with a group once a week for two years and never get on land – and what difference does it make if it’s helping you with your divorce, your illness, your grief, your parenting, your loneliness?
