## 4
SECTION: Every childhood narrows the future
QUOTE: It would be great if the community could also provide resources to smooth the transition, like temporary housing, help with travel, etc. If it can’t, it can still avoid punishing departure.
KEPT: Whether or not you use money, you’ll need to have a way of providing people with resources when they leave (cash, if you use it, but also tools, transportation, temporary housing, stored up goods, the right to keep using something, or whatever). Otherwise their “right” to leave is only on paper -- it only works when the community happens to have spare money. [Leaving Voluntarily]
WHY: The same plea to give leavers practical help (housing, travel) and not punish leaving, here for grown kids; "Leaving Voluntarily" says it for everyone who leaves, and the founder-check questions already ask whether people can leave without being stigmatized.
LOSES: Only the kids-specific hedge that, with nothing to give, a community should at least not punish departure; the paragraph still reads, ending on the records sentence.
