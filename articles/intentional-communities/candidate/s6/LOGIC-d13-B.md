## P3

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Degree | "how much of the future fear is accurate" | "How accurate are the fears about the future?" | SAME. Both allow partial accuracy. |
| Disjunction | "AI may improve life enormously, destroy millions of jobs, become …, or do all three" | "Maybe AI will improve … Maybe millions of people … Maybe AI will become … Maybe all of these things will happen" | SAME. Each "maybe" is one alternative; "all of these" remains a separate, further option, as "or do all three" was. |
| Agent | "destroy millions of jobs" | "millions of people will lose their jobs to AI" | SAME. AI is still the cause. |
| Quantifier and set | "between us and nearly everything" | "between humanity and whatever it is we're trying to do" | CHANGED. "Nearly" is gone. "Whatever it is we're trying to do" reads as everything we attempt, with no exception (or, vaguely, as "our aims"), and the set moves from the things around us to our purposes. A reader now takes the forecast as AI standing between people and all their undertakings. |
| Who does what | "before Chantress Seba gets through her commune applications" | "before Chantress Seba finishes going through all the applications to her commune" | CHANGED (check). The published phrase allows two readings: applications she is sending to communes (as in "her college applications"), or applications to a commune she runs. The rewrite fixes the second, so a reader now believes she runs a commune and is screening applicants. SAME if the essay's earlier mention of her establishes that. |

## P7

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Disjunction and modality | "A tool may be accepted …, shared …, or delayed …" | "Sometimes they use it … Sometimes they share it … Sometimes they hold off …" | SAME. Three "sometimes" keep one outcome per case, and "may" → "sometimes" still says each happens with some tools. |
| Conjunction | "accepted at work and restricted at home" | "use it at work but restrict it at home" | SAME. Both conditions hold together; "but" only marks the contrast. |
| Quantifier | "shared rather than individually owned" | "share it instead of everyone owning one" | SAME on the natural reading ("individually owned" = one each). |
| Contrast and negation scope | "they at least understand that adoption is a communal decision rather than an automatic software update" | "they at least understand that adopting a technology is a decision for the group to make. The new sentence stands alone, in the indicative, about "your device". (a) Still the Amish view, or what ought to hold: same as published. (b) The author's statement of fact that technology doesn't just arrive on your device, which reverses the published implication that for most of us adoption is exactly an automatic update. |
