# Logic audit d17-B: published section vs R

Section "Which Connections to the Technosphere Do You Keep?", P1–P10 numbered as in the brief. Only relations whose wording differs are listed. Bracketed severity is my estimate of how far a reader is misled. Two AMBIGUOUS verdicts (P1; P3, Seba) sit on the published side: the published wording has two readings and R commits to one; the earlier parts of the essay that would settle them are outside this folder.

## P1

| Relation | Published | R | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| What feels urgent | "only one reason this feels urgent" | "only one reason building community feels urgent" | AMBIGUOUS [low]. "this" follows the heading, so it can mean the heading's question (which connections to keep) or the essay's community project. Reading 1: same. Reading 2: the urgency moves from deciding couplings to building community. |

## P2

Identical (not rewritten). No changed relations.

## P3

| Relation | Published | R | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Share vs. degree | "how much of the future fear is accurate" | "How accurate are the fears about the future?" | AMBIGUOUS [low]. Published is partitive: some of the fear may be right, some not. R reads that way, or as one degree of accuracy for the fears overall (all somewhat overblown). |
| Possibilities joined by "or" | "AI may [A], [B], [C], or do all three" | "Maybe [A]. Maybe [B]. Maybe [C]. Maybe all of these things will happen" | SAME. Each stays a separate possibility, with "all" as the extra case; no OR→ALSO shift. |
| Who acts | "destroy millions of jobs" | "millions of people will lose their jobs to AI" | SAME. AI is still the cause. |
| Range of "nearly everything" | "between us and nearly everything" | "between humanity and nearly everything we're trying to do" | AMBIGUOUS [low]. Broad reading: every dealing we have (same). Narrow reading: our tasks and projects only, dropping AI as a layer between us and other people, information, the world at large. |
| Who handles the applications | "before Chantress Seba gets through her commune applications" | "before Chantress Seba finishes going through all the applications to her commune" | AMBIGUOUS [low]. Published allows Seba reviewing applications to her commune or filling in her own applications to communes. R picks reviewer and asserts she has a commune; same only if the essay already says so. |

## P4

| Relation | Published | R | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kind of stance, S1 | "I'd rather build more human independence than wait for certainty" | "it makes more sense to me to focus on cultivating more human independence than to wait for certainty" | CHANGED [low]. A preference becomes a judgment of what is sensible: a reasoned claim open to dispute, where the published only declares the author's choice. |
| Scope | (none) | "on a personal level" | AMBIGUOUS [low]. "Speaking for myself" (same), or "in my own life rather than society's", which shrinks "human independence" to a private project. |
| Kind of stance, S2 | "I'd rather have community … than need an exit …" | "It's better to have community … than to need an exit …, right?" | CHANGED [low]. A first-person preference becomes an evaluative claim put to the reader for agreement; "right?" reads as either "obviously" or a request for reassurance. The published asks for neither. |

Also SAME: "Ok, so" spells out the step from P3's uncertainty that the published leaves implicit.

## P5

## P7

| Relation | Published | R | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Options under "may … or" | "A tool may be accepted at work and restricted at home, shared …, or delayed …" | "Sometimes they … Sometimes they … Sometimes they …" | SAME. "may" here already means "sometimes"; the three stay alternatives across cases. "and" → "but" (work/home) is still both at once. |
| Ownership negation | "shared rather than individually owned" | "share it instead of everyone owning one" | AMBIGUOUS [low]. Reading 1: one shared item instead of one each (same). Reading 2: only "everyone owns one" is denied, so one member may own it and lend it out, which "rather than individually owned" excludes. |

Also SAME: "evaluating technologies according to their effects on community life" → "community decisions about where and if … based on how it affects their community" ("where and if" is narrower, but the next sentences restore ownership and timing); "until its social effect becomes clearer" → "until they know more about its social effects"; "a communal decision rather than an automatic software update" → "a decision for the group to make, rather than something that gets downloaded onto your device while you sleep".

## P8

| Relation | Published | R | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who needs, how surely | "A community may prefer natural care and still need trauma surgery … or a specialist" | "A community may prefer natural care, but what happens if someone in the community needs trauma surgery …?" | CHANGED [medium]. Published: the community itself, whatever it prefers, still needs these ("still": the need outlasts the preference). R: one member might need them ("if"). A reader now sees a possible individual emergency set against the group's preference, not a standing need of the community. |
| Range of the guarantee | "what the community guarantees from common resources" | "What kind of care is going to be guaranteed … using common resources?" | CHANGED [low]. "what" (money, transport, care) narrows to kinds of care; a reader would think the common-fund decision covers only which treatments are included. |
| Who dissents | "somebody who chooses a different level of medical involvement" | "people who choose to accept (or not accept) different levels of medical care" | AMBIGUOUS [low]. Reading 1: departing from the community's level, either way (same). Reading 2: "different levels" = various levels, so the comparison with the community's norm drops out and any medical choice qualifies. |

Also SAME: "It should decide in advance [four items]" → "should hash out [item 1]" … "These are all questions that a community should consider and decide on in advance" ("These are all" covers the four).

## P10

| Relation | Published | R | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence type | "links isolation with higher risks across several outcomes" | "notes an increased risk for a range of health outcomes among people who are socially isolated" | AMBIGUOUS [low]. Reading 1: isolated people show higher risk (association, same). Reading 2: risk increased by isolation (cause). The published "links" ruled out reading 2. |

Also SAME: "social connection itself has measurable health value" → "having social connections is measurably good for a person's health" ("a person's" is generic; losing "itself" drops emphasis only); "won't cure every illness" → "not a panacea"; juxtaposed "It can change the daily conditions" → "but it can make a difference, day to day, in the conditions".
