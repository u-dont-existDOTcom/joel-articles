## P8

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concession | "may prefer natural care and still need…" | "may prefer natural care, but what happens when someone in the community needs…?" | SAME. "When" is generic (in cases where); moving the need from "a community" to "someone in the community" matches the original's sense. |

| Object of guarantee | "what the community guarantees from common resources" | "What kind of care is going to be guaranteed … using common resources?" | SAME. Narrower ("kind of care"), but paying for outside care still fits. |
| Whom it concerns | "how to treat somebody who chooses a different level of medical involvement" | "how will the community deal with people who choose to accept (or not accept) different levels of medical care?" | AMBIGUOUS. (a) Members whose level differs from the community's, in either direction (the original). (b) "Different levels" as "various levels": everyone's care choices; read literally, "choose not to accept different levels" even covers members who keep to the norm. "Deal with" can also read as handling a problem, where "treat" asks how to behave toward the person. |
