That’s where the escuelita in my title comes in. The word “escuelita,” meaning “little school,” is one I’m borrowing from the Zapatistas in Mexico. Their Escuelita had outsiders come and stay in their communities for a while, learning autonomy by living it. In the same way, people would live in the healing communities I’m proposing, to heal and to learn the relational and practical methods, so that some of them could eventually leave and set up another one somewhere else. Otherwise, we’ve built a lovely refuge for whoever got there first.
