## Dissolution and Exit

My father carried the Bruderhof with him long after he left it. You can see it in the foundation he started and in the film he made. It's in the places he took me, too, and in the questions I'm still asking.

So even if the legal entity dies, some of what people learned to give one another can go on. That gives me hope.
