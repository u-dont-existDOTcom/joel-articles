# Section 14 pairs (T2): published paragraph and rewrite

Section 14 is "Dissolution and Exit", seven paragraphs. The rewrite keeps the published order, one rewrite paragraph for each published one. P6 is about the author's own father.

## P3 → P3

CONTEXT BEFORE: If the commune dissolves, what happens to the land and any outside debts? What about its buildings, equipment, common stores or funds? What happens to each member’s agreed rights? More immediately, who is responsible for ensuring that everyone has a place to live and food to eat? Who takes responsibility for making sure people get their medications and the other care they need? Who keeps childcare

PUBLISHED:

Design this like a living will. Preassign a bridge or successor for each critical function. The institution may be unsalvageable and the people still need breakfast on Tuesday; continuity of basic life should not depend on resurrecting the commune.

REWRITE:

Think of it like a living will, and assign a bridge/successor for each critical function in advance, so basic life doesn’t depend on reviving the commune. The institution may not be salvageable, but who’s making breakfast tomorrow?

CONTEXT AFTER: Dissolution terms are also a way for a group to end without turning every friendship into evidence. Who wants that? People may leave hurt, but they keep the transition support they agreed on, and their dignity. And they can tell a coherent story of what happened.

REWRITE:

Dissolution terms are also a way for a group to end without turning every friendship into evidence. Who wants that? People may leave hurt, but they keep the transition support they agreed on, and their dignity. And they can tell a coherent story of what happened.

CONTEXT AFTER: Institutions and the relationships that make them up don’t have to die at the same time. What a relief! American Commune follows two sisters who grew up on The Farm. When one of them later became ill, former members who hadn’t seen her in years reached out with support. The communal economy they were born into had changed dramatically, but the bonds hadn’t vanished.
