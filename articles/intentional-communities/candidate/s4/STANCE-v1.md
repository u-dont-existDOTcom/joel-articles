# Stance check: section 4 rewrite ("The Four Parts That Have to Pl/ork Together")

I checked every sentence in the rewrite that states a position, wish, rule, assessment or claim about how things work against the whole published essay. Conflicts are listed most severe first.

## 1. Visions go from "can't" to "rarely"

- **Rewrite:** "It’s rare that someone will come back from an ecstatic vision with news of … what to do when a child reports something serious."
- **Essay (The Four Parts… › 4. The container):** "the vision can’t determine a departing member’s share … or decide what happens when a child reports something serious."
- **Also (The Medicine Part, Without Pretending It Isn’t There):** "insight does not wash dishes, revise agreements, or calm a frightened child."
- **Also (Children Need Freedom Inside Real Protection):** "Decide the response process before anyone’s face is attached to it."
- **Reader would wrongly believe:** that visions sometimes do give usable answers about exit shares, a founder's control of the land, or a child's serious report, when the essay says they can't.

## 2. Joel's own direction becomes a forecast for every community in the model

- **Rewrite:** "I imagine that in the long run these communities would move to a situation with less and less money."
- **Essay (The Four Parts… › 4. The container):** "My own long-term direction is toward communities that use less and less money."
- **Also (Practice Comes Before Property):** "Reducing or eliminating outside money should emerge from demonstrated capacity, not from declaring money impure"
- **Reader would wrongly believe:** that Joel expects the proposed communities to drift toward moneylessness on their own, when the essay calls it his personal direction and says a group should get there only after it has shown it can.

## 3. Jealousy taking over governance is softened to a side effect

- **Rewrite:** "In practice, one move in a jealousy situation might also affect how the community governs itself"
- **Essay (The Four Parts… › One principle runs through all of them):** "Jealousy can become governance"
- **Also (Jealousy Can Capture Monogamy or Free Love):** "The non-negotiable for me is that jealousy cannot become the hidden governor."
- **Reader would wrongly believe:** that jealousy only affects governance now and then, when the essay warns it can become the community's hidden governor and makes preventing that a non-negotiable.

## 4. Romance Guide described as a primer (minor)

- **Rewrite:** "romance is gonna happen, and my Romance Guide covers the basics."
- **Essay (Jealousy Can Capture Monogamy or Free Love):** "My Romance Guide gives the longer version."
- **Reader would wrongly believe:** that the guide is a basic introduction, when the essay presents it as the longer, detailed treatment it sends readers to.

Conflicts found: 4
