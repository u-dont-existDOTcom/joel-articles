I know that the four parts will often be interconnected. Two people get jealous, and suddenly it's a governance question. Somebody ends up controlling the shared land or tools, and now it's a psychological issue. The medicine work can make some people more important than others, and raising children brings out values the founders never talked about. So it all bleeds together, but I find it useful to compartmentalize to some extent, to make sure no area gets overlooked when I'm thinking about how a community might function.

Also, a note on agreements: a community does need some stable agreements that they're not tweaking and revising every time somebody has a revelation in breathwork. But these agreements need to stay open to lived experience.
