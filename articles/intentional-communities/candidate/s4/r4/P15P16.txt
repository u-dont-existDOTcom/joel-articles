This means that the capacity to help people is in the community as a whole, not in a single professional or guru, or a founder everybody leans on emotionally.

But the people I go to for care shouldn’t, as a result of the care relationship, hold authority over my membership, work, housing, children, medicine, or the evidence in a dispute about any of them. If the listener gets that kind of power, it becomes just another step in the hierarchy, and stops being peer care.
