3. Medicine pl/ork

Doing serious plant medicines carefully with peer guidance without exalting any one person as a special shaman.

This doesn't mean it can be done without structure and precautions. People need to be screened, and sober sitters, emergency plans, integration and clear boundaries all need to be in place, before the first ceremony. Yes, it would be pure collective learning to work out the rules while several people are altered, but I don't recommend it.
