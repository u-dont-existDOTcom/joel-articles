The four parts are just rough categories that bleed into one another. In practice, a jealousy situation can turn into a question of how the community governs itself. Who controls the shared resources can turn into a psychological issue. And the way a community uses plant medicine may create status, or the way it raises children may expose values the founders never talked about. I break them out like this to help see what a community has neglected, not because living in a community fits neatly into categories.
