These four categories leak into one another almost immediately. Governance can end up slyly incorporating personal tensions like jealousy. Those who have positions with more control of shared resources will automatically be affected by that psychologically (power corrupts, as they say). Those who know medicine tend to have another type of god complex since they may make life or death decisions for people routinely. How folks choose to raise children can expose values the founders never discussed. And myriad other categories arise, of course.
