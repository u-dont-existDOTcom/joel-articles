As part of celebrating the 10th anniversary of their autonomous governments, the Zapatistas opened up the Escuelita. CONTEXT AFTER: P15 (rewrite): One way an escuelita could fail is if it becomes a business, where residents' lives turn into a performance of community for paying outsiders, and success gets measured by how many people come instead of how many daughter communities get started. A week of feeling transformed isn't what it's for. It's for teaching enough of the social and material system that some people can build it somewhere else.

## P15 → P15f

CONTEXT BEFORE: P14 (rewrite): As part of celebrating the 10th anniversary of their autonomous governments, the Zapatistas opened up the Escuelita. Outsiders would live with families and learn autonomy by participating in the day to day life there. The first sessions had about 1,500 students, and later sessions had thousands more.

PUBLISHED P15:
An *escuelita* can fail by becoming a visitor business. Residents spend their lives performing community for paying outsiders, and guest numbers replace daughter communities as the success metric. The point is not to make people feel transformed for a week. It is to teach enough of the social and material system that some can build elsewhere.

REWRITE P15f:
One way an escuelita could fail is if it becomes a business, where residents' lives turn into a performance of community for paying outsiders, and success gets measured by how many people come instead of how many daughter communities get started. A week of feeling transformed isn't what it's for. It's for teaching enough of the social and material system that some people can build it somewhere else.

CONTEXT AFTER: P16 (rewrite): In my research, I also tried to follow what happened after the Escuelita. I found alumni networks, people bringing practices home, and organizing later on. What I didn't find was a clear chain from an Escuelita student to a newly founded communal institution, with evidence of how its members and their kids actually did over time. Zapatista influence was already traveling internationally before the Escuelita, so a resemblance later on doesn't prove by itself that it came from the Escuelita. I'm borrowing a way of teaching and the ambition to replicate, and I'm not claiming the Zapatistas have already proved my whole model.
