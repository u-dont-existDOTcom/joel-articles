REWRITE:

So it’s partly a problem of resources, not compassion -- how many people can a group take in without hurting the existing members or the people they are taking in?

CONTEXT AFTER: There is a difficult paradox to consider. The need for new members in a small community is greater, but the ability of that community to tolerate the disruption of new members is less. Whether there are eight people in the community or eighty, someone still has to grow the food, fix the equipment, balance the books, watch the kids, and maintain the water system. The water well doesn’t care how beautiful a new member’s heart is.
