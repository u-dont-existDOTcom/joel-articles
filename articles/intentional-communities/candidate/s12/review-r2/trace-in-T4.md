Section 12 is "The Math of Absorption, and Who This Isn't For". The rewrite keeps the published order and paragraphs: one rewrite paragraph for each published one. The published list after "This community may not be right for you *right now* if:" is four items set as paragraphs, and so is the rewrite's. ## P13pub → P13n5

CONTEXT BEFORE: So it’s partly a problem of resources, not compassion -- how many people can a group take in without hurting the existing members or the people they are taking in?

PUBLISHED:

The difficult paradox is that small communities need members most and can absorb disruption least. Eight people still need food grown, equipment repaired, accounts kept, children cared for, and the water system working. The well does not become more understanding because the new member has a beautiful heart.

REWRITE:

There is a difficult paradox to consider. The need for new members in a small community is greater, but the ability of that community to tolerate the disruption of new members is less. Whether there are eight people in the community or eighty, someone still has to grow the food, fix the equipment, balance the books, watch the kids, and maintain the water system. The water well doesn’t care how beautiful a new member’s heart is.

CONTEXT AFTER: Early on, members usually need to have more resource than need, and some skill to contribute, and a willingness to learn.
