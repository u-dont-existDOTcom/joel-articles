# Stance check: section 12 rewrite, "The Math of Absorption, and Who This Isn’t For"

## Conflicts in the rewrite

**1. Inner work before founding shrinks to "some"**

- Rewrite: "someone might need to stabilize and do some inner pl/ork before helping found a community"
- Conflicts with: Joel's stated position, 2026-10-02: "I DO want people to come in largely pre-healed, which is why I talk about that in the article." The published sentence in this section has no quantifier: "a person may need to stabilize and do inner pl/ork before helping found a community."
- A reader would wrongly believe: a modest amount of inner work before founding is enough, rather than arriving largely healed.

**2. Outside review required for every admission process**

- Rewrite: "Any real process would need concrete criteria and outside review."
- Conflicts with: Joel's stated position, 2026-10-11 18:35: "before critical mass, my rules apply, because it's my land ... i decide who stays, i decide who leaves, i decide everything." Also his 2026-10-07 answer: "That would violate my entire guide to force communities to accept outside intervention." The rewrite keeps this sentence unchanged from the published text (see below).
- A reader would wrongly believe: Joel holds that every "not now" admission decision, including his own on his land before critical mass, must go to outside review.

## Contradictions in the published text

**1. "Outside review" of membership decisions vs. Joel's founding-stage authority**

- Published (section 12, "The Math of Absorption, and Who This Isn’t For"): "Any real process would need concrete criteria, outside review, and a way to revisit a “not now” decision."
- Conflicts with Joel's stated position of 2026-10-11 (quoted above): before critical mass, he alone decides who stays and who leaves, as a host does. This section is explicitly about founding groups, small communities and early members, which is that stage. Where "outside" means outside the community, as it does in "When Somebody Has to Go", the sentence also runs against his 2026-10-07 position that the home community controls its own dealings with outsiders. The conflict is in the outside-review requirement. Concrete criteria and a way to revisit "not now" are consistent with his positions.
- Rewrite: still has it ("Any real process would need concrete criteria and outside review. It would also need a way to revisit a “not now” decision.").

Conflicts in the rewrite: 2. Contradictions in the published text: 1.
