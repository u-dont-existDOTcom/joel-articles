# Stance check r3: section 12, "The Math of Absorption, and Who This Isn't For"

## Conflicts in the rewrite

**1. The test of need against resource has changed.**

- Rewrite: "You have a need that far outstrips your ability to bring in a resource for it right now."
- Essay, same section (published list): "Your current need greatly exceeds your available resource." The same section defines free resource as "the attention, steadiness, skill, and practical capacity a person has available after dealing with enough of their own immediate material."
- What a reader would wrongly believe: that the bar is whether you can obtain resources to cover your own need (a self-sufficiency test that can read as a means test), not whether you have more capacity to give others than you need from them.

**2. Outside review of "not now" decisions goes against Joel's stated position.**

- Rewrite: "that's going to require actually making concrete criteria, allowing for outside review, allowing for re-visiting a "not now" decision"
- Joel, 2026-10-11 18:35 UTC: "before critical mass, my rules apply, because it's my land ... i decide who stays, i decide who leaves, i decide everything." Also Joel, 2026-10-07 (quoted in the brief): "Why should the home community not control the interaction they have with outside?"
- What a reader would wrongly believe: that Joel requires admission decisions in the founding stage, which is this section's subject, to be open to review by outsiders, when he says that before critical mass he alone decides who stays.

## Contradictions in the published text

**1. "Outside review" goes against Joel's later positions.**

- Published, this section: "Any real process would need concrete criteria, outside review, and a way to revisit a "not now" decision."
- Since publication, Joel has said that before critical mass he decides who stays and who leaves on his land (2026-10-11), and that communities should not be forced to accept outside intervention (2026-10-07). This section is about founding groups and early members, which is the stage before critical mass.
- The rewrite still has it: "allowing for outside review" is one of the things that solving the problem "is going to require".

Conflicts in the rewrite: 2. Contradictions in the published text: 1.
