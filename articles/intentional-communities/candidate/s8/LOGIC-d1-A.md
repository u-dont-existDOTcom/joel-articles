# Logic audit d1-A: "The Grown Children Get the Final Review", published vs R

Logical relations only: connectives, negation, quantifiers, modality, degree, evidence, agency and sequence. Paragraphs are numbered P1–P28 as in BRIEF.md. "(minor)" marks a real shift with low stakes.

**Most serious:** P25 "not punish …, or withhold" (the scope can flip to "it can withhold records"); P23 "if they were free to leave" (presupposes that children who stay aren't free); P17 (surprises go from a time limit to a blanket exemption); P14 (the rejected view changes, and "can … without preventing" becomes "won't stop"); P19 ("buys silence" becomes "hide behind their status"); P2 (a general need becomes the author's niche); P10 (an added concession).

### P1
No changed relations (identical).

### P2
| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Modality / reason | "I'm not trying to design a village for everyone; communities need enough agreement…" | "I'm not trying to create a village for all people, just for a community that shares…" | CHANGED: the published second clause is a general necessity ("communities need") and gives the reason he isn't designing for everyone. The rewrite turns it into the boundary of his own project ("just for"), so both the general claim and the reason disappear. |
| Cause → effect | "enough agreement on children's health that parents can actually trust one another" | "shares enough in common to trust each other with the health of their children" | CHANGED: published runs from agreement on children's health to general trust among parents. The rewrite runs from general like-mindedness to trust limited to children's health, so the reader believes broad commonality is the requirement. |

### P23
| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Condition / presupposition | "A community whose children never leave cannot know whether they would freely return." | "If all the children stay, how would anyone know whether they'd come back if they were free to leave?" | CHANGED: "freely" moves from returning to leaving, and "if they were free to leave" presupposes that the children who stay aren't free to leave. Published says only that they never leave. |
| Who knows | "A community … cannot know" | "how would anyone know" | CHANGED (minor): the community's inability to know becomes everyone's, the children included. |

Splitting the list confines it to the last one or two, so studying, travel and outside friendships lose that protection. |
