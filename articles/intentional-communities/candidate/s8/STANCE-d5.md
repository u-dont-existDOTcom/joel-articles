# Stance check: section 8 rewrite ("The Grown Children Get the Final Review")

Every sentence in the rewrite that states a position, wish, rule, assessment or claim about how things work was checked against the whole published essay.

## 1. Older children "bossing" in place of "dominating"

- **Rewrite:** "Older kids can't be allowed to quietly boss the younger ones around there, and secrecy can't be treated as independence."
- **Essay:** "A children's territory cannot become a place where adults stop paying attention, where older children quietly dominate younger ones" (The Grown Children Get the Final Review, subsection "Children Need Freedom Inside Real Protection"). The same subsection also says children should "resolve ordinary disputes, and experience some freedom from constant adult management."
- **What a reader would wrongly believe:** that adults must stop ordinary bossiness inside the children's territory, when the essay's safeguard is against older children covertly dominating younger ones.
