Amish young people decide as adults whether to join the church, and [roughly 85 percent or more of them end up joining](https://groups.etown.edu/amishstudies/social-organization/population-growth/). Pop culture makes their teenage *Rumspringa* seem wilder and more universal than it is, but it still matters that they're baptized as adults, because then belonging is a commitment instead of a fact imposed at birth.
