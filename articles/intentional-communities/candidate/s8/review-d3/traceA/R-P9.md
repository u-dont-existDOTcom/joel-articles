Experiments where kids don't sleep and live with their parents need special caution. Research on Israeli kibbutzim found that [they ended the practice of kids sleeping communally for a mix of reasons: developmental findings, parents' preferences and social change](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12395568/). Kids can benefit from high-quality group care and many loving adults in their lives, but replacing a secure attachment to their parents is a different matter.
