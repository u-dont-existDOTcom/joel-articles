I also searched [the research corpus](https://innerself.185-233-106-15.sslip.io/blog/commune-article-research/) for a well-documented case of a child in an intentional community who continued to be seriously dangerous to other people, where the record follows the whole sequence of events: the conduct, the assessment, the intervention, the review, and what happened later. I didn't find one. That may be because the records are private, or because the cases were sent elsewhere, but it means I can't claim that communities already know how to handle the hardest version on their own. The child, the family, and the people at risk all need access to competent help and review that the home community doesn't control.
