Amish young people decide for themselves, as adults, whether or not to join the church, and [roughly 85 percent or more eventually do](https://groups.etown.edu/amishstudies/social-organization/population-growth/). Popular culture has blown *Rumspringa* out of proportion, as if every teenager went wild, but adult baptism still matters, because it makes belonging a commitment instead of a fact imposed at birth.
