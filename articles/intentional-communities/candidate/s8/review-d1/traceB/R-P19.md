Hear the child even when the person accused is loved by others in the community. That's where many communities fail, because the adult may be charismatic, useful, rich, spiritually important, or just everybody's friend. You have to know what you're going to do ahead of time, before anyone's face is attached to it. First, put the child in immediate safety. Then see that a competent and fair investigation is done, and don't allow anyone to hide behind their status in the community.
