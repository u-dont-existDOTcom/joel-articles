## Contradictions in the published text

Inside section 10, no sentence takes back another. Against the rest of the essay, I found one contradiction, and it's soft.

**1. Does a spreading movement need its communities to stay in touch?**

- Section 10, "Federation Without Building Another State": "If these communities are supposed to spread, eventually somebody has to solve the boring problem of how they remain connected without building another state." The same paragraph wants communities "different enough to learn from one another."
- Closing paragraphs, under "What to Do This Month": "If you build something real, maybe we'll hear about each other. If you teach other people to build, hearing about it matters less."
- Section 10 says staying connected is required once communities spread, and it makes learning from one another a goal. The closing makes contact a "maybe" and ranks it below teaching others to build. The two fit together if the closing only means that Joel, as the originator, doesn't need to be kept informed. That reading matches the line just before it ("one institution, one founder, or one official version"). But "each other" reads as the communities themselves, so a reader gets two answers about whether the movement needs its parts connected.
- Rewrite: still has it ("does eventually need to be solved somehow"; "so communities can keep learning from one another").

Conflicts in the rewrite: 0. Contradictions in the published text: 1 (soft).
