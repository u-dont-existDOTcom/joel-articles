## P8 → P8a

CONTEXT BEFORE: Exchanges and obligations need the same honesty, even if nobody uses money internally. A gift of food, pooled medicine, lending a vehicle or a tool, trading labor, emergency housing, building something together, a visit that's mostly for training or building relationships: these are different events. Some of them create a future obligation and some don't. A federation can keep records for each purpose without putting a price on everything or forcing every transfer to come out even. If cash gets used where the federation meets the outside world, grants, loans, guarantees and shared-enterprise commitments need to be kept separate the same way. Solidarity doesn't mean unlimited joint liability, or an unlimited claim on another community's labor, stores, land or tools.

PUBLISHED P8:
And when two principles collide, don’t preserve only the final rule. Keep the hard case: what happened, which principles actually conflicted, the first judgments, final reasoning, dissent, consequences, and the exact rule version. When the rule changes, rerun the case. Otherwise each generation gets to rediscover the same constitutional fight from scratch.

REWRITE P8a:
And when two principles collide, don't just save the final rule. Keep the hard case too: what happened, which principles actually conflicted, what the first judgments were, the reasoning behind the final one, any dissent, what the consequences were, and exactly which version of the rule it was. When the rule changes, run the case again. Otherwise every generation gets to rediscover the same constitutional fight from scratch.
