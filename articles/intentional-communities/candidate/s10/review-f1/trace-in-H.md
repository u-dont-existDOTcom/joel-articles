## Federated Communities Make a Movement, Improve Resilience

PUBLISHED P3+P4:
If these communities are supposed to spread, eventually somebody has to solve the boring problem of how they remain connected without building another state. I don’t want one super-commune with a nicer logo. I want communities that can help one another, move people and knowledge around, and still remain different enough to learn from one another.

At federation scale, that outward purpose becomes concrete: spread healing practices, train people, seed new projects, and keep any one comfortable enclave from mistaking itself for the whole movement.

REWRITE P34t:
The problem of how these communities stay connected does eventually need to be solved somehow, if the idea is for them to spread without building another state. It should be solved in a way that allows help between communities and the movement of people and knowledge, and also keeps the differences alive, so communities can keep learning from one another. At the federation level, that outward purpose gets concrete: spreading healing practices, training people, seeding new projects, and keeping any one comfortable enclave from thinking it's the whole movement.

CONTEXT AFTER: The [Federation of Egalitarian Communities](https://www.egalitariancommunities.org/) (FEC) says it's rebuilding, and that's a good warning. It takes work to run a federation. Delegates. Records. Compliance. Coordinating resources. Holding meetings. Succession. Whatever dispute function it actually takes on. These things all take up labor and material capacity before any local crisis has eaten into both. If the legal or economic environment requires money, the federation also needs to have a cash budget for dealing with it. But the work of federation doesn't have to be done by a single entity. Two communities might want to be closely linked as friends, and that doesn't stop both of them from also being part of a general federation. There can also be local clusters, or single-function institutions like the FEC's [PEACH health pool](https://www.egalitariancommunities.org/initiatives), which isn't currently governed by the FEC delegates.
