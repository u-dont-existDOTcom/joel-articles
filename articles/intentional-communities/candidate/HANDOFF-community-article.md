## How a section runs (details in docs/HUMANIZATION-GATE.md)
- Joel's edits into `OWNER-EDITS.json` first. Text he gives as article text goes in as written (his characters, obvious typos fixed). An answer he gives in chat is content: fit it to its place, show my version beside his words, say what changed. Text he copies from a side-by-side page can carry struck words: check his paste against the published text and the ledger's bans.
- Gate: meaning trace (Sonnet, at most four pairs, trace brief); cold read (Sonnet; `python3 tools/humanization/reviewer/reviewer.py --article HUMANIZED-SO-FAR.md sense-section OUT --section "HEADING" [--draft FILE] --report PATH`, never hand-written); grounding review for any new heading, published ones too when the paragraphs under them change (Opus); whole-article stance check (`stance_check_prompt.py` with `--positions sN/positions.txt` and `--images images.json`, Opus), and when Joel has changed a position, against the article so far as well; linter (`tells_lint.py` with `--installed HUMANIZED-SO-FAR.md` and `--owner` for his lines; FAIL blocks). A DISPOSITIONS file for every round. `preservation_keys.py` must PASS before any trace.
- A flag two or more cold reads raise, published words included, gets a fix I propose, made and reported. A reader's finding that the published meaning was lost gets the published words back; only Joel can overrule it.
- Sweeps: `meaning_sweep.py` (`--ledger OWNER-EDITS.json`, `--owner-text` for his texts), never a hand-built prompt; a records sweep reads at most six review reports per agent run.
- Each new rule is swept over the candidate sections in the turn it's made.
- Lists: three items in one sentence read AI; "capacity" is a tell unless tied to "free resource" (O40). When the guide's own words read AI, look for the tell before asking.
- When unsure how a rule applies, give Joel examples to score on the Rule Scoring Sheet.
- When Joel shows a change and I agree: for each change, the signal I had and the check that should have acted (fix it), or a line saying only he could have known it.
- Pangram: web app only (the API key has no credits). Batches through `tools/humanization/pangram_gate.py` (`python3 tools/humanization/pangram_gate.py DRAFTS.json GATE.json gui/bNNN.js --name BNNN KEYS`; kinds draft, edit, owner, section). Each `gui/bNNN.js` is self-contained: pasted into the pane's tab (javascript tool), it fills the box, checks each text's SHA-256, clicks, and waits 10 s per text; `gui/bNNN-read.js` reads https://web.pangram.com/api/history-list and each history item and matches the results by SHA-256 (`gui/bNNN-want.json`). For big batches this turn, the items (`gui/bNNN-items.json`) were pushed to GitHub and fetched in the page from raw.githubusercontent.com (the page's CSP allows it) by a small in-page function, `window.__run(name, items)`, that runs the same loop; it's gone after a page reload, so rebuild it from the loop in any `gui/bNNN.js`. Predictions get their time from Pangram's History, not a clock read (two clock reads per turn). Under 50 words can't be checked alone. Predictions before every call (Human or AI; Mixed for a section), a score line after each batch, and each result's link (https://www.pangram.com/history/<id>) beside it (E175). Pass means 100% Human; "Mostly Human" is not a pass. Every paragraph passes alone (or with a neighbor if under 50 words), each heading with its paragraph, and the whole section; test the whole section after every change.
