Good people leave for good reasons -- dying parent, love, work, health, exhaustion, change of mind, etc. You don't want them to lose everything they've put in! Note that "outside" doesn't have to mean the nation-state. My research initially leaned too far that way. The basic need here is for a competent level of authority that is not reviewing its own conduct. That could mean another community, a federation of communities, professionals, autonomous regional institutions, courts, regulatory agencies, emergency services, etc. The key is that it's not the same people! An important correction that came from the Zapatistas: that authority can be an autonomous regional institution, without it turning into the state. But when power had crept up in their own system over the years, it took them a long review to push it back down. And no, autonomy still doesn't mean anyone gets to decide that individual people don't have certain non-waivable rights, or that outside jurisdiction just goes away.
