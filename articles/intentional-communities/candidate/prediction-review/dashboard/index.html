What today's misses taught

  • I call Human too often. In the latest batches I said Human almost every time while about a sixth read AI, so my calls barely beat always saying Human. From 18 October each call carries a chance, and I say AI when the chance is under half.
  • A whole section passed 83% of the time when none of its new paragraphs had been checked before it, about half the time with two or more new ones, and about half when one of them had read Human at low confidence or with an AI likelihood of 0.10 or more. I now call those sections AI unless I check the risky paragraph first.
  • Joel's own paragraphs, checked as he wrote them, passed 55% of the time and my calls on them were right 91%; my edits of installed text passed 92%; screened candidates (mostly Emulate's) 70%, where nearly all my misses are.
  • A model trained on the lane's 829 checked texts beat my calls on texts like the ones it had seen, but on paragraphs it had never seen anything like, it did no better than always saying Human. It's a second opinion within an article, not a substitute for reading the text.
