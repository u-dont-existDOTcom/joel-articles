## From Visitor to Member—Slowly P4 → P3

CONTEXT BEFORE: First, correspond with them. Did they read the materials? (Not everyone does!) Do they get the basic idea of why this community is here? Are they at least starting to do the relevant practices? It’s a lot cheaper to find out through a thoughtful exchange than after six months of misunderstanding, right?

PUBLISHED:

Short visit. Spend a week or two participating in ordinary life. Most people can be charming for two weeks. Congratulations: you have established that they can be charming for two weeks. 😅

REWRITE:

The short visit: If they come for a week or two and just participate in normal life, well, most people can be charming for two weeks. Not exactly a high bar! 😅 So that's about all you'll find out.

CONTEXT AFTER: The long visit: If you're not around long enough to actually experience inconvenience and conflict with the person in question, then you're not going to learn very much about them. Watch how they react when tired, corrected, attracted, disappointed, bored, etc. (Hopefully not all at once!) Even [a professional faker](http://sidney.u-dont-exist.com/) may be able to pull off an act for months!
