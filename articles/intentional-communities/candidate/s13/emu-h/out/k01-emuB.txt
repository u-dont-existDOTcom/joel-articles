Resources, Land, and Exit Terms

2. Who has the title to the land? This is very important. The person who has title has a huge amount of power over the community. Is the deed to the land in the name of the founder of the community? If so, he is really a landlord who happens to have a philosophy. If there are twelve members, but the title to the land is in the hands of only three of them, then there are really two classes of members despite what the literature may say.
