Resources, Land, and Exit Terms

WHY IT IS IMPORTANT: Who has the title to the land? This is extremely important to know. Whoever has title to the land has an enormous amount of power over the community. If the title is in the name of the founder of the community, then he is essentially nothing more than a landlord with a philosophy. If the title is owned by 3 out of 12 members, then the community is comprised of 2 different classes of members despite what the literature may say.
