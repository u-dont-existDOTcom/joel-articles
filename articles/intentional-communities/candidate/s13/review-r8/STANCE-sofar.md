# Section 13 rewrite: stance check (r8)

## Conflicts in the rewrite

**1.** Rewrite: "That's the real relational pl/ork of each of you naming what happened, allowing each of you to take the time you need…"

Essay: section 4 ("The Four Parts That Have to Pl/ork Together"), relational pl/ork: "Hearthwork distributes counseling among peers: equal time, attention without interpretation or fixing, and then you switch." (The published section 13 line is "Give each person time.")

A reader would think each person decides how long to take, but the relational pl/ork this sentence names gives everyone equal time and then switches.

**2.** Rewrite: "Do they get the basic idea of why this community is here? Are they at least starting to do the relevant practices?"

Essay: section 3 ("Why Communities Keep Dying in the Same Two Ways"): "A place where people who've done the healing first can join without pretending they're fully finished". Section 12 ("The Math of Absorption"): "So early on, members usually need to have more available resource than acute need". Joel, 2026-10-02: "I DO want people to come in largely pre-healed". The published section 13 asks without setting a floor: "Do they understand why this community exists? Have they begun the relevant practices?"

A reader would think the bar for newcomers is a basic grasp of the purpose and a practice they've only just begun, instead of arriving largely healed.

## Contradictions in the published text

**1.** Section 13's heading and first lines say: "Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community" / "The first community is the small group considering whether to build one." A few lines later, the same section says: "A group that has never done this is still a circle of promising strangers."

The rewrite still has it: "you are already a community, even if it's still an untested one", then "the group is still a circle of promising strangers". "Untested" narrows the gap, but "strangers" still takes the claim back.
