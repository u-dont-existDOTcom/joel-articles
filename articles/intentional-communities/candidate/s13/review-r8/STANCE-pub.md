## Conflicts in the rewrite

### 1. The Zapatista lesson is narrowed to regional oversight ("When Somebody Has to Go")

- **Rewrite:** "The Zapatistas have been important in opening my eyes to the possibilities of autonomous regional institutions, which can review local power…"
- **Essay, section 9 ("The Best Model I've Seen: Zapatistas"):** "the coordinating levels are explicitly described as dependent on the communities rather than superior to them." Also: "The Zapatistas reviewed theirs and pushed authority downward."
- The published version of this sentence said "autonomous institutions can review local power without becoming the state". There, a regional institution was only one option in the list before it, not the lesson.
- **What a reader would wrongly believe:** that the lesson Joel takes from the Zapatistas is regional bodies reviewing local communities, when section 9 credits them with moving authority down to the communities.
