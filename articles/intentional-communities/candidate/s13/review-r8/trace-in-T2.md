REWRITE:

How do you know? At each stage, always judge people based on their conduct. No matter how good it sounds when they talk about their spirituality or their therapy, don’t go by that. It’s too easy to go by vibes. It’s also easy to mistake people just doing what everyone else is doing for safety. Even giving it six months will give you more data, but it won’t tell you if someone is a good person.

If someone will have a role in your group that has serious consequences, involving kids, shared resources, medicine, vehicles or private records, you want to know how they handle that specific thing. I wouldn’t hand someone the van keys just because they’re great with kids! You can find out by asking if you’ve seen them do this thing before, and asking for references if appropriate. Give people access to these things in stages and ensure there’s always some way to appeal decisions about these roles to a separate authority if needed.
