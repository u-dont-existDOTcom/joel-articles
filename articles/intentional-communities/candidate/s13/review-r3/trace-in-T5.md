PUBLISHED:

Visitors, volunteers, workers, renters, and interns do not need full political membership to have rights. If a decision materially affects their compensation or access to shared resources, schedule, safety, housing, records, or discipline, they need clear terms, a way to be heard, and review. Egalitarianism that stops at the membership boundary can recreate a servant class between communes.

REWRITE:

Visitors, volunteers, workers, renters and interns don’t need full political membership to have rights. Yes, even the summer volunteer. If a decision really affects their pay or their access to shared resources, they need clear terms and a way to be heard, and the decision should be open to review. The same goes for decisions that really affect their schedule, safety or housing, and their records or discipline. Equality that stops at the membership line can bring back a servant class of non-members moving between communes.
