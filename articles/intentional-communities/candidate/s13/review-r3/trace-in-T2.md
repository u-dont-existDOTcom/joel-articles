REWRITE:

Add sexuality and gender to the list. Yes, it keeps getting longer. Could an LGBTQ+ member live openly, have relationships and stay fully protected by the community? Should children be taught to question their sexual identities at an early age? What will the community do if one of its kids grows up gay or trans? If a group hasn’t answered these yet, it hasn’t finished working out its values on freedom or on raising children, either.
