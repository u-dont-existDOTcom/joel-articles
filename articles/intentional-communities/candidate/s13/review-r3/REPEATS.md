# Repeats check: Intentional Communities (s13, review r3)

I read every block, B0 to B401. I checked each block in "The Four Parts That Have to Pl/ork Together" (B54–B88) and "Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community" (B273–B302) against every other block in its section and against the rest of the article. Then I checked the rest of the article against itself. *Mild* marks repeats a reader may only half notice. Deliberate callbacks are listed separately after the contradictions.

## 2. Contradictions

**C1. B59 and B61: one shared practice, or each person's own**
- B59: "A community needs a shared sacred inner practice that gives the difficulty of our life together a deeper meaning."
