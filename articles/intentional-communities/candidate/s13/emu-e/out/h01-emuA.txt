First, don't worry about land just yet. Second, if you have even a small handful of people who really want to try to make a community then you are a community, even if an as yet untested one. It sounds cheesy but it's true.

Third, don't even think about combining ownership or other big resources until you have gone through at least one real conflict together and really dealt with it. Not 'dealt with it' by writing a vision statement and shaking hands, or by saying "Let's all just agree to leave each other alone". But really, truly dealt with it. Do the real relational pl/ork of each of you naming what happened, allowing each of you to take the time you need, allowing whatever is there (jealousy, humiliation, fear, past material, whatever) to come up, and dealing with it until the emotional pressure is such that an agreement to do something differently actually means something. Until then, what's it worth?
