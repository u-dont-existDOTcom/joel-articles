re: land... don't worry about that yet. if you have a small handful of people who "want to be a community but aren't sure yet"... you are a community, you just don't know it yet, you haven't tried it out yet. i know that sounds all cheesy, but it's true.

re: combining... i would not combine ownership or major resources until you have gone through at least one real conflict together and dealt with it. not dealt with it by coming up with a vision statement and shaking hands, or calling a truce. i mean really dealt with it. in other words, some real relational pl/ork. somebody has to say what the problem is and each person involved has to have time to say what comes up for them - jealousy, humiliation, fear, old material, whatever it is. when the emotional pressure valve has been released enough for there to be a change in pressure, *then* you can make an agreement that will mean something. otherwise, what is it worth?
