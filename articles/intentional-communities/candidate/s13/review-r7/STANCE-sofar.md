# Stance check: section 13 rewrite ("Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community")

## Conflicts in the rewrite

### 1. Removing someone becomes a grudging permission ("When Somebody Has to Go")

- **Rewrite:** "If someone won't respect essential boundaries, and won't stop, and/or is just impossible to live with, it can be okay to eject them." The paragraph's opening sentence leads into it: "Even in a safe community it's ok to kick people out if they're a serious or general danger."
- **Essay:** section 11, "Founderism: The Four Questions" (in "How My Own Model Could Become a Cult"): "If it gets to the point of being impossible to live with, they will be kicked out." His 2026-10-11 statement says the same. The published section 13 had "A safe community cannot promise to keep everyone" and "ejection can be the right answer."
- **What a reader would wrongly believe:** that removal is something a community is allowed to do even though it is safe, when the essay says that once someone is impossible to live with they will be removed (sickness and similar exceptions aside).

### 2. The limit on autonomy is narrowed to the review bodies (the "Outside" paragraph)

- **Rewrite:** "But autonomy doesn't mean that these institutions can violate non-waivable individual rights, or that outside jurisdiction stops applying to them."
- **Essay:** section 11 (in "How My Own Model Could Become a Cult"): "There are some basic human rights that can't be voted away from you by the majority in a community". The published section 13 states the limit for autonomy in general: "But autonomy is not permission to erase non-waivable individual rights".
- **What a reader would wrongly believe:** that the limit applies only to the regional institutions doing the review, and not to the commune itself while it removes someone.

## Contradictions in the published text

None that a reasonable reader would see. I set aside clashes that hold only when a section 13 rule is read outside its section, such as its membership, terms and land rules set against Joel's own land before critical mass. That follows his 2026-10-15 ruling.

## Questions on the author's own paragraphs

None. "So work out the process before it's about anyone in particular." matches section 8 ("Decide on the response process ahead of time"). Section 11's "I can't decide any of this ahead of time" is about how a given case turns out, not about the process.

Conflicts in the rewrite: 2. Contradictions in the published text: 0.
