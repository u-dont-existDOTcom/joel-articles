# Stance check: section 13 rewrite ("Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community")

I checked every rewrite sentence that states a position, a rule, an assessment or a claim about how things work. Each was compared against the whole published essay, Joel's positions stated since publication, and his own rewritten line.

## Conflicts in the rewrite

### 1. Ejection recast as an exception to safety
- **Rewrite:** "Even in a safe community it’s ok to kick people out if they’re a serious or general danger."
- **Essay (s13, "When Somebody Has to Go"):** "A safe community cannot promise to keep everyone." On danger, broken boundaries or impossible shared life: "ejection can be the right answer."
- **What a reader would wrongly believe:** that a safe community normally keeps everyone and danger alone settles ejection. The essay treats keeping that option open as part of being safe, and says ejection only "can be" right.

### 2. "Conduct" widened to any "reason"
- **Rewrite:** "What constitutes a reason for immediate protective action, and who decides on temporary restrictions?"
- **Essay:** s13, "When Somebody Has to Go": "what conduct triggers immediate protective action". s13, "From Visitor to Member": "assess concrete conduct rather than spiritual vocabulary or therapeutic fluency". s11, "How My Own Model Could Become a Cult": "When a community begins diagnosing dissent instead of responding to it, leave".
- **What a reader would wrongly believe:** that the group may set grounds other than conduct (suspicion, a diagnosis, disagreement) for immediate protective action against someone.

### 3. The rights limit moved off the community onto the reviewers
- **Rewrite:** "But autonomy doesn't mean that these institutions can violate non-waivable individual rights, or that outside jurisdiction stops applying to them."
- **Essay:** s13, "When Somebody Has to Go": "But autonomy is not permission to erase non-waivable individual rights, and it does not make outside jurisdiction disappear." s11: "There is also a floor the majority cannot vote away".
- **What a reader would wrongly believe:** that the limit binds only the outside bodies reviewing a removal, and not the community's own autonomy when it removes someone.

## Contradictions in the published text

### 1. Is the forming group a community yet? (s13 opening; mild)
- "The first community is the small group considering whether to build one." vs. "A group that has never done this is still a circle of promising strangers."
- The heading says the forming group already is the community, then the next paragraph calls the untested group strangers. A reader can't tell whether it counts as a community yet. This is minor because many readers will take "strangers" to mean "untested" and read on.
- **Does the rewrite still have it?** Mostly fixed. "you are already a community, even if it's still an untested one" makes "promising strangers" describe the untested stage, though both statements remain.

Read within the situation its own section covers, no other passage in published s13 contradicts itself, the rest of the essay, or Joel's stated positions.

## Questions on the author's own paragraphs

None. "So work out the process before it's about anyone in particular." agrees with s8's "Decide the response process before anyone’s face is attached to it."
