Even [a professional faker](http://sidney.u-dont-exist.com/) can sometimes keep it up for months! So time gets you more evidence, not certainty.

CONTEXT AFTER: Provisional membership: decide how long it lasts and what rights and responsibilities come with it, plus how the review works and how they’d leave.
