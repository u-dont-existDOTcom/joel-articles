First thing don't worry about getting land. If you have a small handful of people who want to try this thing and you haven't tried it yet you are already a community just because you are. Sounds cheesy but it's true.

Second thing don't even think about combining any thing like ownership etc, etc until you have been through a conflict together and REALLY dealt with that conflict. Not writing a vision statement and shaking hands or calling a truce of lets leave each other alone. REALLY dealt with it. That takes some relational pl/ork of each of you naming it and spending the time to find out what it is (jealousy, humility, fear whatever) and dealing with it until the emotional pressure that brought it on has changed in some way so that whatever agreement you come to will mean something. Until then what's it worth?
