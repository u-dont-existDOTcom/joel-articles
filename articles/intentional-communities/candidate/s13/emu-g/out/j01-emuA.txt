Second, don’t consider combining ownership or other significant resources until you have experienced and dealt with at least one conflict together. “Dealt with” here doesn’t mean writing a vision statement or making a turee (temporary truce) that says “Let’s all leave each other alone.” Dealt with means that it takes all the relational pl/ork it takes for each of you to say what is going on for each of you, to take the time to figure out what it is (jealousy, humiliation, fear, old material, etc.) and to deal with it until the emotional pressure changes enough that when you make an agreement it actually means something. Until then what’s it worth?
