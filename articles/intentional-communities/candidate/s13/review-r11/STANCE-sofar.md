# Stance check: section 13 rewrite (review r11, text so far)

I checked each sentence of the rewrite that states a position, rule, assessment or claim against the whole published essay and Joel's stated positions. I read each rule within the situation its own section covers.

## Conflicts in the rewrite

### 1. The vision becomes the first step

- **Rewrite:** "First you get the vision, then you get the members, then you pool the major resources, and lastly you get the land."
- **Essay, section 16 ("Practice Comes Before Property"):** "Practice together. Process a real conflict. Clarify values. Test membership. Decide ownership and exit. Then consider land." Under "What to Do This Month" in the same section: "handle one disagreement, and keep commitments before discussing a shared future." Section 9 (Zapatistas) states the principle: "build the practice first and develop the theory afterward."
- **What a reader would wrongly believe:** a forming group should settle its vision before it practices together or gets through a disagreement.
- The published sentence only placed vision ahead of membership, pooling and land ("vision before membership, membership before pooling major resources"). The rewrite's "First", after "For the order to do all this in", puts it ahead of everything.

## Contradictions in the published text

### 1. "Already the community" against "still strangers" (inside section 13)

- **Published:** the heading "Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community", and "The first community is the small group considering whether to build one."
- **Against:** "A group that has never done this is still a circle of promising strangers."
- The heading tells a new group that it already is the community. Three paragraphs later, the same group is "still" a circle of strangers until it has processed a conflict, and that is the stage the section is talking to. "Already" and "still" describe the same group at the same moment.
- **Rewrite:** eased but not removed. It keeps both "you are already a community" and "still a circle of promising strangers", and "even if it's still an untested one" links the two.
