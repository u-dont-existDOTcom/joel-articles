REWRITE:

'Outside' doesn't have to be a nation-state. (It's true that at first [my research](https://innerself.185-233-106-15.sslip.io/blog/commune-article-research/) kinda leaned too far toward the nation-state.) What's really needed is that there be a competent layer that isn't reviewing its own conduct. That can be another community, a federation, a professional, an autonomous regional institution, a court, a regulatory agency, an emergency service, or anything else. The Zapatistas are an important correction here: autonomous institutions can review local power without becoming the state, and when power crept up in theirs, they pushed it back down. But autonomy doesn't mean that anyone can erase non-waivable individual rights, or that outside jurisdiction disappears.
