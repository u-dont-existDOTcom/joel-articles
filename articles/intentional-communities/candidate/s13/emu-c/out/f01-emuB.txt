In the meantime, it might be good to budget what kind of support the group can provide in material areas: water, housing, food stores, health care, tools, seasonal labor, transportation, schooling, transport to market, outside debts (if any), length of cash reserves (if any), and any other critical skills that the group might have. Even the best group in the world emotionally can fail if the material needs are not attended to, or if there is an over-reliance on one person for these needs.
