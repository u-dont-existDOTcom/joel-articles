Along with that, it would be a good idea to also budget for the group's physical needs. That is, the things they can offer in the way of water, housing, food stores, health care, tools, seasonal labor, transportation, schooling, market access, etc. If there are debts to pay or cash reserves, how long will they last? What critical skills will the group need that they don't currently have? It's boring stuff but in the end, a group could be great on an emotional level but be dead in the water physically due to a lack of foresight.
