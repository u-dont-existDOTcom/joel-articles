Surely those on a commune’s land who are not members of its political collective—visitors, volunteers, workers, rentors, interns, etc.—should have rights, especially regarding compensation and the use of resources if they’re in a position that makes use of them. It seems to be that anything regarding one’s schedule, safety, housing, records, and discipline should all be part of an arrangement with pre-determined, clearly defined terms that someone has the right to appeal and be heard regarding. Otherwise, isn’t it possible for an otherwise egalitarian commune to become a de facto repository for the servant class of other communes?
