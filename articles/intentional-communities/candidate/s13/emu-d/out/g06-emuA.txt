The 'outside' doesn't have to be a nation. (It's true that I've been kinda assuming that in my research.) All that's really needed is that there be a competent layer above the one under review. That can be another community, a federation, a profession, or anything else. The Zapatistas have been very important in opening my eyes to the possibilities of autonomous regional institutions, whether courts, regulatory agencies, emergency services, etc. But that doesn't mean that they can violate non-waivable individual rights, or that there can't be jurisdiction above them.
