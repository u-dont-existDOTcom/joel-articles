It looks like there's a bit of a bias towards the "outside" being a nation state in this. The real key is that there exists a competent outside which is not reviewing its own conduct. That can be a different community, a federation, a profession, an autonomous region, a court, a regulator, an emergency service, etc, etc. The Zapatistas are very important in this regard. What they don't allow is anyone within the autonomous region abrogating non-waivable individual rights (in their jurisdiction).
