It is also true that ‘outside’ doesn’t necessarily mean ‘nation state’, and in that sense, I was a little biased in my research. It can mean just a layer of competent authority that is not reviewing its own actions. This can be another community (as part of a larger federation or profession etc.), or it can be autonomous parts of that region (as the Zapatistas are demonstrating), but it will also include institutions like courts, regulators and emergency services that the community is not autonomous from. This autonomy doesn’t remove non-waivable individual rights or the jurisdiction of these ‘outside’ powers.
