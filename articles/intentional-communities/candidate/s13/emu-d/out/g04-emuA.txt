It’s not necessary that a visitor, volunteer, worker, renter, intern, or whatever have all of the rights of a political member. It is, however, very important that they be guaranteed some legal rights regarding any decision that “really affects” them. This includes anything to do with their pay, access to resources, or anything else that the members have previously agreed they would share. It also includes anything to do with their schedule, safety, housing, records, and discipline, among others. These guarantees should be clear and specific, as previously agreed, and they should include the right to a hearing and the right to appeal. It’s not that difficult, and it’s necessary if commune members want to avoid creating a servant class of non-members who flit from commune to commune performing a variety of tasks, often unpleasant, that the actual members are not interested in doing.
