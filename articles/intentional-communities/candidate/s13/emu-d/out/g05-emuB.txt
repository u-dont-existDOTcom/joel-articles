Sorry if this wasn’t clear in the original post but it is not necessarily the case that the “outside” here refers to a nation state, though perhaps some of the detail that was included leaned that way. The basic point is that it’s valuable to have some layer of competent jurisdiction that is not reviewing itself, and that can take a variety of different forms. The Zapatista example is intended as a correction of this bias. It can be another community, a federation, a professional, some other form of autonomous institution at a different scale, a court, a regulator, a fire department, etc. The point of the side constraint about non-waivable rights is to say that even if a community has a legitimate claim to autonomy it doesn’t get to eliminate all outside jurisdiction.
