REWRITE:

Good people leave for good reasons -- dying parent, love, work, health, exhaustion, change of mind, etc. You don't want to make them leave everything they've put in when they leave! That's coercive -- they HAVE to stay if they can't afford to go. Write up the exit terms when it's only theoretical -- like a pre-nup when you're planning the wedding. Not romantic, but protective! Much easier than doing it once everybody's furious.
