REWRITE:

How will you handle VISIBILITY of what you have? Even if you're a money-free community, you'll still have scarce resources and bottlenecks. It will be important for people to know what's owned or held in common, what you've set aside, what obligations you have to outsiders, who has authority to hand out the major resources, where conflicts of interest are, etc. It won't do to have the transfer of major resources (land, equipment, stores, outside cash, etc.) dependent on one person's memory or permission. It will be important to have it specified ahead of time what people's rights are to the common resources when they leave the community. Whether or not you use money, you'll need to have a way of providing people with resources when they leave (whether it's cash, if you use it, or tools, transportation, temporary housing, stored up goods, the right to keep using something, or whatever). Otherwise your "right" to leave is only on paper -- it can't be exercised unless the community has spare money!

CONTEXT AFTER: Good people leave for good reasons -- dying parent, love, work, health, exhaustion, change of mind, etc. You don't want to make them leave everything they've put in when they leave! That's coercive -- they HAVE to stay if they can't afford to go. Write up the exit terms when it's only theoretical -- like a pre-nup when you're planning the wedding. Not romantic, but protective!
