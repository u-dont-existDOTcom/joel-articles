# Stance check B: section 13 rewrite against the whole published essay

## Conflicts in the rewrite

**1. "Outside debt" turned into members' private borrowing**

- Rewrite: "what about outside debt (can an owner borrow money outside the community, and what happens if they do?)"
- Essay, section 14 (Dissolution and Exit): "If the community ends, what happens to the land, outside debts, buildings, equipment". Section 13's capacity budget also has "outside debts or cash runway if the community uses them". Everywhere else in the essay, "outside debts" means the community's own debts, and the rewrite's capacity budget uses it that way too ("outside debts to pay").
- A reader would come away believing the legal form should decide whether individual members may take private loans, when it should settle the community's own outside debt before the purchase.

**2. Specific protections for non-members turned into "equality for everybody"**

- Rewrite: "Equality is for everybody, not just members, or communes can end up recreating a servant class that moves from commune to commune."
- Essay, section 13 (From Visitor to Member—Slowly): "Visitors, volunteers, workers, renters, and interns do not need full political membership to have rights." Same part: "Full membership. Land and shared-resource rights, voice, and long-term obligations follow the founding agreements."
- A reader would come away believing visitors, workers and interns are owed the same standing as members. The essay gives them specific protections (clear terms, a hearing, review) inside a membership that comes in stages.

## Contradictions in the published text

**1. The forming group "already is the community", then it's "still a circle of promising strangers"**

- Section 13, under the heading "Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community": "The first community is the small group considering whether to build one."
- Section 13, three paragraphs later: "A group that has never done this is still a circle of promising strangers."
- Section 16 (Practice Comes Before Property, If You Can't Move) claims less for a group that has done more: "A weekly group that has practiced together for two years already contains a real piece of community."
- A reader may wonder whether a group that hasn't yet worked through a conflict is the community the heading promises or a circle of strangers.
- Rewrite: still has it. "you are already a community, even if it's still an untested one" softens it, but "the group is still a circle of promising strangers" is still there.

## Questions on the author's own paragraphs

None. "So work out the process before it's about anyone in particular" agrees with section 8's "Decide the response process before anyone's face is attached to it." I found no passage he hasn't rewritten that it conflicts with.

Conflicts in the rewrite: 2. Contradictions in the published text: 1.
