This power to remove people is also one of the easiest to abuse, though. So work out the process before it's about anyone in particular. What might call for immediate action to protect people from somebody? Who will decide whether to impose temporary restrictions on the person? Who will collect and preserve evidence? Who should step aside from these decisions? How will the person be able to reply to the charges against them? How can the decision be reviewed or appealed later, without creating danger? How will the person's interests be protected regarding housing, records, family contact, access to common stuff, outside money (if any), etc., during the separation? When will the community seek outside help? (More on that next.)
