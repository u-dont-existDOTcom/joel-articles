- Rewrite: "Equality is for everybody, not just members, or communes can end up recreating a servant class that moves from commune to commune."

- A reader would wrongly believe that non-members are owed equality with members. The essay gives them specific protections (clear terms, a hearing, review) and keeps membership rights for the people who are admitted.

**2. Outside debt**

- Rewrite: "what about outside debt (can an owner borrow money outside the community, and what happens if they do?)"

- A reader would wrongly believe that the outside-debt term to settle with the legal form is about whether individual owners may take personal loans. Everywhere else, the essay uses "outside debts" for the community's own debts to outsiders.
