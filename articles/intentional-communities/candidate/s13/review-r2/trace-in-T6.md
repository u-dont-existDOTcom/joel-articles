PUBLISHED:

Visitors, volunteers, workers, renters, and interns do not need full political membership to have rights. PUBLISHED:

The power to remove somebody is also one of the easiest powers to abuse. So work out the process before it's about anyone in particular: what conduct triggers immediate protective action, who preserves evidence, who is recused, who decides temporary restrictions, how the person replies, what review or appeal is possible without endangering others, how housing, access to common resources or outside funds if any, records, and family contact are protected during separation, and what calls for outside help.

REWRITE:

But the power to remove somebody is also one of the easiest powers to abuse. ⟦author⟧So work out the process before it's about anyone in particular.⟦/author⟧ It feels grim to plan for this. You can’t decide the outcome in advance, only the process. It should say what kind of conduct calls for protective action right away, and who preserves the evidence. Anyone who’s too close to the case steps aside, and it should be clear who decides on temporary restrictions. The person gets a way to reply, and some kind of review or appeal that doesn’t put others in danger. While they’re separated, their housing, records and contact with family need protecting, and so does their access to common resources, or to outside funds if there are any. The process should also say when to call for outside help.
