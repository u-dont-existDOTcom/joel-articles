# Stance check: section 13 rewrite ("Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community")

## Conflicts in the rewrite

**1.** Rewrite: "I won't pretend that's fun." (This is about working through a real conflict together.)
Essay, section 4 (The Four Parts That Have to Pl/ork Together): "Note, Pl/ork means play + work. Calling therapy 'inner work' forgets the inner child, who also wants to play." The same section puts dealing with jealousy and conflict under relational pl/ork.
A reader would come away believing: Joel finds working through conflict grim, though the essay counts it as pl/ork, which is play as well as work.

**2.** Rewrite: "Nobody sets out to do that, of course." (This follows "Equality that stops at the membership line can bring back a servant class between communes.")
Essay, section 1 (The New Age May Dawn Suddenly), on the Love Israel family's garden labor: "The money was opaque and seemed to travel mostly upward toward Love."
A reader would come away believing: a commune's second-class workers only ever appear by accident, so good intentions protect against it. The essay's own Love Israel story shows the opposite.

**3.** Rewrite: "You can't decide the outcome in advance, only the process."
Essay, section 11 (How My Own Model Could Become a Cult): "If someone starts worshipping Satan, say, they get booted." Also: "If it gets to the point of being impossible to live with, they will be kicked out."
A reader would come away believing: no removal can be settled ahead of time, though the essay settles some in advance. The sentence takes his section 11 line "I can't decide any of this ahead of time", said about when to make exceptions such as a sick member, and turns it into a rule for every case.

**4.** Rewrite: "It also helps to pick a mediator or ombud before there's a crisis"
Essay, section 13 as published: "Also choose a mediator or ombud before the crisis."
A reader would come away believing: choosing a mediator in advance is a nice extra, when the essay tells a group to do it as part of preparing.

**5.** Rewrite, on visitors, volunteers, workers, renters and interns: "they need clear terms and a way to be heard, and the decision should be open to review." It adds: "The same goes for decisions that really affect their schedule, safety or housing". This comes unchanged from the published text.
Essay, section 11 (Founderism: The Four Questions): "Friends who come to visit don't get written terms, though. That's not how anything works". Joel, 2026-10-11, on the time before critical mass: "i decide who stays, i decide who leaves".
A reader would come away believing: every visitor is owed clear terms and a review of decisions about their stay. Section 11 and Joel deny that for a friend visiting before a community is formalized. See the first contradiction below.

## Contradictions in the published text

**1. Visitors' terms and review (the rewrite still has it).** Section 13: "Visitors, volunteers, workers, renters, and interns … need clear terms, a way to be heard, and review." Section 11: "There are implied terms, like be respectful and be honest, and those shouldn't need to be in writing." Joel, 2026-10-11: "nobody gives clear written terms to visiting friends". Before critical mass, he also says: "i decide who stays, i decide who leaves".

Section 13 gives every visitor clear terms and a review of decisions about their housing. Section 11 and Joel's stated position give a visiting friend implied terms only, and leave the landowner to decide who stays. Both can stand if section 13 applies only to a formalized community, but neither passage says so.

Section 13's "The recruiter should not control the decision" has the same unmarked scope. Against it stands Joel's "i divest power to others when … I accept their partnership." The rewrite keeps both: the visitor sentence, and "shouldn't be up to the person who recruited them".

## Questions on the author's own paragraphs

None. "So work out the process before it's about anyone in particular" matches section 8's "Decide on the response process ahead of time, before anyone's face is attached to it." Section 11's "I can't decide any of this ahead of time" is about outcomes, not about having a process.

Conflicts in the rewrite: 5 (one comes unchanged from the published text). Contradictions in the published text: 1.
