## Resources, Land, and Exit Terms P6 → P5

CONTEXT BEFORE: Good people leave for good reasons, like a dying parent, love or work. Health and exhaustion count too. And sometimes people just change their minds. They’re allowed to. If leaving means giving up everything they put in, staying becomes coercive.

PUBLISHED:

Write the exit terms while departure is still hypothetical. This is the community equivalent of discussing a prenup while planning the wedding: unromantic, protective, and much easier before anybody is furious.

REWRITE:

That’s why exit terms are best written while nobody is leaving and it’s all still hypothetical. It’s the community version of a prenup that you work out while planning the wedding. Nobody finds that romantic, but it protects people. And it’s much easier to do before anybody is furious.
