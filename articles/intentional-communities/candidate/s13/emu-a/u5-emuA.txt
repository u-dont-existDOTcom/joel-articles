For example, imagine that someone turns out to be a generalized or serious danger to the community, or has some vital behavior which they keep returning to despite repeated effort to stop, or is somehow incompatible in a way that makes it very difficult or impossible for them to be part of the community. It may be that the best, only thing to do is to help them leave the community . Unless there's some reasonable timescale for restorative work (and it shouldn't be the commune's job to be someone's treatment program, if that's what's needed) maybe they need to leave.

The problem is that this is a power which could easily be abused, so it's important to agree on a process for dealing with this, in advance . Such a process will need to include a way for serious cases to be dealt with rapidly to prevent harm, while also ensuring things like appropriate evidence collection and recusal, and a way to review or appeal the decision without causing harm. It will need to ensure that someone who has been separated from the community still has appropriate access to housing, common resources, outside funds (if appropriate), records, family, etc. It may also need to involve outside bodies.

Initially, in researching this post, the outside bodies that came to mind were largely in the context of the nation state, such as courts, regulators and emergency services. Thanks to the example of the Zapatistas , though, it occurred to be that this isn't necessarily the case - there can be and often are autonomous bodies which can play this role. However, autonomy doesn't let anyone step outside the laws of individual rights - there are things that people just cannot consent to, and therefore things that local autonomy cannot override.

Another thing to have in place in advance is someone to mediate or act as ombud - it's important that the "neutral stranger" not be a complete stranger. However, mediation doesn't replace the need for proper safeguarding, adjudication, etc, and may not be appropriate if it seems that separation is the only option.
