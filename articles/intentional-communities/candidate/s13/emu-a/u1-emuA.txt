Don’t get land til there is a community.

Don’t get land til at least one real conflict has been processed in the community. It isn’t real til someone says, “When ** said ** it made me feel ** because ** and I need ** from **.” Don’t get land til after someone has said this and the relevant parties have agreed to something different (not just a truce, and not a vision statement, but something that is agreed to after the emotional pressure has changed) and the underlying jealousy/humiliation/etc. material has come up.

Before that, it isn’t a community, it is a circle, and it shouldn’t get a mortgage.

Meet in a rented space, borrowed space, someone’s house, etc., til the group can show that it can deal with its conflicts.

Don’t get land til the water, housing, food, healthcare, boring stuff has been budgeted for. Don’t skimp on tools, seasonal labor, transport, school, market, debt/cash if using, key skills, etc. It is possible to be really skilled at the “emotional” stuff and still die from a bad well or be undone by too much reliance on one tired person’s memory of where things are and what to do.
