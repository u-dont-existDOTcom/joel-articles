Don’t get land before community.

Don’t combine ownership and major resources without going through at least one real conflict together.

Not creating a vision statement and shaking hands on it. Not even coming to a truce about what to do different. Actually going through the conflict, emotionally, together, until a real agreement comes. Finding out what’s coming up for each person, what old material is getting touched, where the jealousy and fear and humiliation is, and dealing with that until the pressure is off enough for an agreement to be made.

Until that has happened, I wouldn’t put the circle in a mortgage together.

In the meantime there’s lots to do! Meet in rented space, borrowed space, at someone’s house, and find out if there’s a group there.

Also, in the meantime, do some capacity budgeting: water, housing, food storage, health care, tools, seasonal labor requirements, transportation, schooling, market access, outside debts or cash running out if applicable, key skills needed and more. It doesn’t matter how great everyone is at dealing with each other emotionally, if the well dries up or the one person who knows where all the stuff is and how it works collapses from exhaustion.
