# Stance check: section 13 rewrite ("Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community")

## Conflicts in the rewrite

**1.** Rewrite (section 13, budget paragraph before "Please Choose the Values"; the published version asks this with no condition): "How long will your cash reserves last, if the community uses them?"

Essay, section 16 ("Practice Comes Before Property"): "Reducing or eliminating outside money should emerge from demonstrated capacity, not from declaring money impure while somebody else quietly pays the tax bill."

Essay, section 15 ("What This Looks Like From Sénégal", under "Lessons from the Black Tax"): "Internal life can be cashless while taxes, hospitals, visas, school fees, family obligations, imported tools, and other outside relationships still arrive denominated in money."

A reader would wrongly believe a forming group may have no use for cash reserves, when the essay makes going moneyless a late stage earned by capacity and says even a cashless community still faces bills in money.

## Contradictions in the published text

**1.** Published section 13 ("Resources, Land, and Exit Terms"): "For the full sequence -- vision, then members, then pooling major resources, then land" ... "it's where I'd start for getting that order right!"

Section 16 ("Practice Comes Before Property"): "The better order is less cinematic. Practice together. Process a real conflict. Clarify values. Test membership. Decide ownership and exit. Then consider land."

Section 13 presents a vision-first order as complete and right; section 16 puts practice and a real conflict first and values third, and counts "vision in a document" as part of the wrong start.

The rewrite no longer has it: it recommends the book for "the nuts and bolts of all this (members, money, land, agreements, etc.)", with no order. The appendix still recommends the book "for sequencing".

## Questions on the author's own paragraphs

None. "So work out the process before it's about anyone in particular", "My research initially leaned too far that way" and the recruiter hint don't conflict with any part of the essay he hasn't rewritten.
