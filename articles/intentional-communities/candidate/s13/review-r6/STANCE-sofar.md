## Contradictions in the published text

**1. Already the community, or still strangers?**

- Section 13, heading and opening: "Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community" and "The first community is the small group considering whether to build one."
- Section 13, a few lines later: "A group that has never done this is still a circle of promising strangers."
- The opening says a group that is only considering a community already is one. The conflict paragraph says the same group is still strangers until it has processed a real conflict. "First community" can be read as "the seed of one", but the heading says it outright, and a reader can fairly ask which it is.
- Rewrite: still has it, and states it more sharply: "you are already a community. It sounds cheesy but it's true." Then: "Until that’s happened, the group is still a circle of promising strangers".

**2. The Zapatistas as proof that higher-level review works**

- Section 13: "The Zapatistas are an important correction here: autonomous institutions can review local power without becoming the state."
- Section 9 ("The Best Model I’ve Seen: Zapatistas"): power "had crept up and consolidated itself at higher levels over time", so in 2023 they "moved authority downward toward a Local Autonomous Government in each community". Also: "A new ecovillage can't copy the diagram everyone can see and get the trust underneath it right away."
- In section 9's own account, the Zapatistas' higher-level bodies drifted toward concentrating power until the Zapatistas dismantled them, and the fix rests on trust a new community doesn't have. Section 13 uses the same example only as evidence that such bodies can review local power safely. Section 9 does say they reversed the drift, so the word "can" still holds. But a reader who remembers section 9 will notice that section 13 leaves out the half of the example that warns against these bodies.
- Rewrite: still has it, and names "regional" outright: "autonomous regional institutions, which can review local power without becoming the state."
