PUBLISHED:

“Outside” does not always mean the nation-state. [My research](https://innerself.185-233-106-15.sslip.io/blog/commune-article-research/) initially leaned too far that way. The important thing is a competent layer that is not reviewing its own conduct: sometimes another community, a federation, a professional, an autonomous regional institution, a court, regulator, or emergency service. The Zapatistas are an important correction here: autonomous institutions can review local power without becoming the state. But autonomy is not permission to erase non-waivable individual rights, and it does not make outside jurisdiction disappear.

REWRITE:

'Outside' doesn't have to be a nation-state. (It's true that at first [my research](https://innerself.185-233-106-15.sslip.io/blog/commune-article-research/) kinda leaned too far toward the nation-state.) What's really needed is that there be a competent layer that isn't reviewing its own conduct. That can be another community, a federation, a professional, a court, a regulatory agency, an emergency service, or anything else. The Zapatistas have been very important in opening my eyes to the possibilities of autonomous regional institutions, which can review local power without becoming the state. But autonomy doesn't mean that these institutions can violate non-waivable individual rights, or that outside jurisdiction stops applying to them.
