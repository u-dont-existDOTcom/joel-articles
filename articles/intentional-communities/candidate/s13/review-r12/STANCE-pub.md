# Stance check: section 13 rewrite against the whole published essay

## Conflicts in the rewrite

**1. What mediation can't replace (When Somebody Has to Go)**

- Rewrite: "a final separation when consent to continue in a relationship or a shared life is no longer present"
- Essay ("Jealousy Can Capture Monogamy or Free Love"): "housing, children's security, membership, and access to common necessities should not depend on remaining inside one romantic agreement"
- The published sentence said only "when consent is gone", in a section about removing someone from the community. Because the rewrite adds "a relationship", a reader can take the end of a relationship, such as a breakup, as grounds for separating someone from the community.

## Contradictions in the published text

**1. Is the forming group the community or not? (Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community)**

- Published: "Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community" (heading) and "The first community is the small group considering whether to build one."
- Published, three paragraphs later: "A group that has never done this is still a circle of promising strangers."
- The heading tells a forming group it already is the community. Then any group that hasn't yet processed a real conflict, which describes most groups at the forming stage, is "still" a circle of strangers. "Already" and "still" cancel each other out for the very groups the heading speaks to.
- The rewrite still has it. "you are already a community, even if it's still an untested one" softens the clash, but "the group is still a circle of promising strangers" stays.

None. "So work out the process before it's about anyone in particular" matches "Decide the response process before anyone's face is attached to it" (The Grown Children Get the Final Review).
