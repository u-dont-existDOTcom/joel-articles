## Please Choose the Values Before Falling in Love With the View P4 → P3

CONTEXT BEFORE: Whenever you go to visit a community, have a list ready of what you really believe about children, money, property ownership, romance, medicine, food, spiritual practice, technology, privacy, how to deal with conflict, leaving the community, etc. Long list, huh? Put some question marks next to the things you’re not sure about yet.

PUBLISHED:

Add sexuality and gender to that list. Could an LGBTQ+ member live openly, form relationships, and remain fully protected by the community? Should children be taught that they should question their sexual identities at an early age? What happens if one of the children grows up gay or trans? A group that hasn’t answered those questions hasn’t finished discussing either freedom or child-rearing values.

REWRITE:

This list should also include your thoughts on sexuality and gender, and whether or not LGBTQ+ people should be able to live openly in your community, to date or be in relationships, and be fully protected by the community. You should also include whether or not children should be taught to question their sexual identities from a young age, and what should happen if one of the community’s children comes out as gay or transgender. You don’t have your community’s values on freedom and raising children complete until you answer these questions. Both of them!

CONTEXT AFTER: Are you willing to experiment? That’s a real value too! Make sure your list of values declares whether or not you are willing to experiment. The people next to you may feel that all experimenting ended in 1978, and everything that came out of it has been doctrine ever since.
