## Please Choose the Values Before Falling in Love With the View P6 → P5

CONTEXT BEFORE: Are you willing to experiment? That’s a real value too! Make sure your list of values declares whether or not you are willing to experiment. The people next to you may feel that all experimenting ended in 1978, and everything that came out of it has been doctrine ever since.

PUBLISHED:

Then compare your values with the group’s actual behavior, not only its public language. Ask how a recent conflict was handled. Ask who can block a decision. Ask what happened to the last person who left. Ask who owns the land. The view will still be there after the uncomfortable conversation.

REWRITE:

When you have a good idea of what’s important to you, you can look at how a group actually functions, not just its public face. Find out how it handled its last conflict, and who can block a decision. How did leaving go for the last person who left? Who owns the land? Then make a choice whether or not it functions in a way that is important to you. It may be an uncomfortable conversation, but the view will still be there after you’ve had it.

CONTEXT AFTER: Who will have title to the land? Very important! Whoever has it will have enormous power over the rest of the community. If the deed’s in the name of the “founder,” then he or she is really your landlord, with a philosophy. If three out of twelve members own everything, then despite what your literature says about everyone being equal, there are two classes of members.
