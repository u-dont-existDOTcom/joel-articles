CONTEXT BEFORE: Until that’s happened, you’re all still promising strangers, and you don’t want to take out a mortgage together yet. That’s okay! There’s lots you can do! You can all meet in a rented or borrowed space, or at each other’s houses, and find out how much conflict the group can handle before you need a surveyor.

PUBLISHED:

Do the boring capacity budget too: water, housing, food stores, health care, tools, seasonal labor, transport, schooling, market access, outside debts or cash runway if the community uses them, and the few skills whose absence can stop everything. A group can have extraordinary emotional skill and still die because the well fails or one exhausted person is the only one who knows where everything is and how it works.

REWRITE:

You will also want a budget of what you have in the way of resources. This is the boring stuff! But you will need to know what the group can offer in the way of water, housing, food stores, health care, tools, seasonal labor, transportation, access to markets, schooling, etc. Do you have outside debts to pay? How long will your cash reserves last? What critical skills will you need? Being great at the emotional stuff won’t stop you from being dead in the water if your well fails, or if the one person who knows where everything is and how it works gets too exhausted to keep going.

CONTEXT AFTER: Have you noticed people often choose their community in the same way they would choose a vacation rental? “Wow! Look at this beautiful land! And a pond, with the sunset in it!! Who needs a beach?! And the people seem really nice, let’s get in here!” Fast forward two years. “Wait, there’s a vaccination policy?? And you need paperwork for it?!?!”
