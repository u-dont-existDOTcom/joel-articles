## Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community P2+3 → P2

CONTEXT BEFORE: Don't worry about getting land yet. If you've got a small handful of people who want to make a community but aren't sure yet, you are already a community. It sounds cheesy but it's true.

PUBLISHED:

Before ownership and major resources mix, process one real conflict all the way through.

Don’t smooth it over with a vision statement or settle for a truce nobody believes. Name what happened. Give each person time. Find the jealousy, humiliation, fear, or old material involved. Reach an agreement only after the emotional pressure has changed enough that the agreement means something.

REWRITE:

Don't combine ownership/major resources until the group has had at least one real conflict and dealt with it all the way through. Not "dealt with it" by writing a vision statement and shaking hands, or declaring a truce nobody believes, but really dealing with it. In other words, you need to do some real relational pl/ork where someone names what happened and each person gets time. What comes up in conflicts is always jealousy, humiliation, fear, or other old material, so find out which. Deal with it until the emotional pressure has changed enough for an agreement to mean something, and then you can make one.

CONTEXT AFTER: Until that’s happened, the group is still a circle of promising strangers, and I wouldn’t ask that circle to take on a mortgage yet. In the meantime there’s plenty to do! Meet in rented or borrowed space, or at someone’s house, and find out how much conflict the group can handle before anyone hires a surveyor.
