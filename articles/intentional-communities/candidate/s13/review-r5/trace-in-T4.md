If a decision materially affects their compensation or access to shared resources, schedule, safety, housing, records, or discipline, they need clear terms, a way to be heard, and review. REWRITE:

Do visitors, volunteers, workers, renters and interns at a commune need some rights even if they're not "in" the commune politically? When decisions really affect their pay or use of resources, they need clear terms and a way to be heard, and those decisions have to be open to review. Also when decisions really affect their schedule, safety, housing, records, discipline, etc. Equality isn't just for members, or else there's a risk of creating servant classes from commune to commune.
