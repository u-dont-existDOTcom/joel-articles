# Stance check: section 13 rewrite ("Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community")

I checked every sentence of the rewrite that states a position against the whole published essay and Joel's later stated positions.

## Conflicts in the rewrite

**1.**
- Rewrite: "What comes up in conflicts is always jealousy, humiliation, fear, or other old material, so find out which."
- Essay, section 11, "How My Own Model Could Become a Cult": "When a community begins diagnosing dissent instead of responding to it, leave"
- The published line here, "Find the jealousy, humiliation, fear, or old material involved," is about the one conflict being processed. "Always" makes it true of every conflict.
- A reader would come away believing every conflict comes down to a wound, so a disagreement on its merits can be diagnosed as old material. The essay names that habit as a reason to leave.

**2.**
- Rewrite: "If someone won’t follow basic rules and boundaries, and won’t stop, … it’s okay to eject them."
- Essay, section 13, "When Somebody Has to Go": "repeatedly violates essential boundaries, or makes ordinary shared life impossible, ejection can be the right answer"
- Joel, 2026-10-11 23:12 UTC: "if they don't follow the processes … if that gets to the point of impossible to live with, they will be kicked out"
- A reader would come away believing that refusing the community's rules is enough on its own to eject someone. The essay names essential boundaries, and Joel keeps ejection for when living together has become impossible.

**3.**
- Rewrite: "If the community does not use money, it still needs to be able to provide people with resources when they leave"
- Essay, section 13, "Resources, Land, and Exit Terms": "cash if the community uses it, but also tools, transport, temporary housing" … "so exit is not a paper right that works only when the community has spare money"
- A reader would come away believing a community that uses money can settle exit in cash alone. The essay wants in-kind exit rights there too, so that leaving doesn't depend on spare cash.

**4.**
- Rewrite: "(It's true that I've been kinda assuming that in my research.)"
- Essay, section 13, "When Somebody Has to Go": "My research initially leaned too far that way."
- A reader would come away believing Joel's linked research still treats outside help as the nation-state. The essay calls that an early lean, since corrected.

## Contradictions in the published text

None found. I read each passage in order and within the situation it covers. No line in the published section takes back another of its lines, the rest of the essay or any of Joel's later positions.
