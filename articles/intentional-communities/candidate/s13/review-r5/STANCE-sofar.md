# Stance check: section 13 rewrite ("Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community"), so far

Section numbers count the essay's top-level headings, with "The New Age May Dawn Suddenly" as 1.

## Conflicts in the rewrite

**1. Every conflict turned into old material**
- Rewrite: "What comes up in conflicts is always jealousy, humiliation, fear, or other old material, so find out which."
- Essay, section 11 (Founderism), "How My Own Model Could Become a Cult": "Sometimes it really is their wounded ego projection … if complaints are too often or too broadly painted as projection, that's as much a failure". The published section 13 line has no "always" and doesn't class jealousy, humiliation and fear as old material: "Find the jealousy, humiliation, fear, or old material involved."
- A reader would wrongly believe: Joel holds that every conflict is old wounds resurfacing, the blanket reading section 11 calls a failure.

**2. Non-cash exit support narrowed to money-free communities**
- Rewrite: "If the community does not use money, it still needs to be able to provide people with resources when they leave"
- Essay, section 13 (published): exit rights in "cash if the community uses it, but also tools, transport, temporary housing, stored goods, or continued use rights". Section 11: "Each person should have a usable avenue for exit."
- A reader would wrongly believe: non-cash exit support only matters in a money-free community, so a community that uses cash can owe leavers cash alone, even when it has none to spare.

**3. Lower bar for ejection**
- Rewrite: "…won't follow basic rules and boundaries, and won't stop, and/or is just not someone people can live with, it's okay to eject them."
- Essay, section 11, "How My Own Model Could Become a Cult" (also Joel's 2026-10-11 statement), on members who don't follow the community's processes: "If it gets to the point of being impossible to live with, they will be kicked out." It adds "They should be offered compassion" and allows exceptions. Published section 13: "repeatedly violates essential boundaries, or makes ordinary shared life impossible, ejection can be the right answer".
- A reader would wrongly believe: persistently refusing the group's basic rules is enough on its own to eject someone; for people who don't follow the processes, the essay waits until living with them has become impossible.

**4. Joel's research presented as still assuming the state**
- Rewrite: "(It's true that I've been kinda assuming that in my research.)"
- Essay, section 13 (published): "My research initially leaned too far that way."
- A reader would wrongly believe: the research report Joel links still treats "outside" as the nation-state, instead of having corrected an early lean.

## Contradictions in the published text

None found: nothing in published section 13 takes back another of its sentences, or conflicts with the rest of the essay or with Joel's stated positions, when each rule is read within the situation its section is about.

## Questions on the author's own paragraphs

None. His sentence "So work out the process before it's about anyone in particular" is also the published wording, and section 8 (children) says the same: "Decide on the response process ahead of time, before anyone's face is attached to it."

Conflicts in the rewrite: 4. Contradictions in the published text: 0.
