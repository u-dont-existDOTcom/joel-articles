How often do people choose to come to a community using the same criteria as choosing a vacation rental? "Wow! Look at this beautiful land! And a pond, and look how the sun is setting in it!! Who needs a beach?! And look, the people seem really nice! Let's get in there!" *2 years later* "What do you mean you have a vaccination policy? And I have to fill out paperwork?"

Bring your values with you when you go to visit a community! What do you believe about how to raise children? Money? Owning things? Romance? Medicine? Food? Spiritual practice? Technology? Privacy? Dealing with conflict? Leaving the community? Wow, that's a long list. Put some ?s next to some of those.

And sexuality. Be sure to include how you feel about sexuality and gender. Should LGBT people be able to live openly in your community? Should they be able to date? Be in relationships? Be protected? Should children be encouraged to question their sexuality from a young age? What happens when one of the community's children comes out? You don't have your community values for freedom and raising children complete until you answer these questions. Both of you.

Are you willing to experiment? Include that in your values. You may be expecting to be able to try things out and the people next to you may feel that the experiment ended in 1978 and everything that came out of it is now doctrine.

And then look at how the group functions. Not what they say about it, but how it actually works. How did they handle a recent conflict? Who can block decisions? What happened to the last person who tried to leave? (And who owns the land?) Is that how you want to live? Sure, it will lead to some uncomfortable conversations, but the view isn't going anywhere!
