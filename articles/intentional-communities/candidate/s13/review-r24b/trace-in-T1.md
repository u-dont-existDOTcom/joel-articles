# Section 13 pairs (T1): published paragraph and rewrite

Section 13 is "Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community", with four subheadings. The rewrite keeps the published order and headings; a pair shows all the published paragraphs a rewrite paragraph carries. The rewrite is deliberately loose and spoken (short reactions, run-on lists, "etc."); judge what it says, not its polish. Where a pair has a DEDUP NOTE, the author asked for repeats to be cut: a point the note names is said where the note says, so check that claim instead of reporting the point as lost here.

## Please Choose the Values Before Falling in Love With the View P4 → P3

CONTEXT BEFORE: Bring your values with you when you go to visit a community! Make a list of what you really believe about children, money, property ownership, romance, medicine, food, spiritual practice, technology, privacy, dealing with conflict, leaving the community, etc. It’s a long list, I know! Some of them will just get a question mark, if you’re not sure yet.

PUBLISHED:

Add sexuality and gender to that list. Could an LGBTQ+ member live openly, form relationships, and remain fully protected by the community? Should children be taught that they should question their sexual identities at an early age? What happens if one of the children grows up gay or trans? A group that hasn’t answered those questions hasn’t finished discussing either freedom or child-rearing values.

REWRITE:

Sexuality and gender belong on there too. Would LGBTQ+ people be able to live openly in your community, date, be in relationships, and stay fully protected by the community? Should children be taught to question their sexual identities from a young age, and what happens if one of the community’s children comes out as gay or transgender? Until you answer these, your community’s values on freedom and on raising children aren’t complete. Yep. Both of them.

CONTEXT AFTER: Are you willing to experiment? That’s a real value too! Put it on your list, because the people you live next to might think the experiment ended in 1978, and everything that came out of it is doctrine now.

## Please Choose the Values Before Falling in Love With the View P6 → P5

CONTEXT BEFORE: Are you willing to experiment? That’s a real value too! Put it on your list, because the people you live next to might think the experiment ended in 1978, and everything that came out of it is doctrine now.

PUBLISHED:

Then compare your values with the group’s actual behavior, not only its public language. Ask how a recent conflict was handled. Ask who can block a decision. Ask what happened to the last person who left. Ask who owns the land. The view will still be there after the uncomfortable conversation.

REWRITE:

Then don’t just listen to what the group says. Look at how they really operate. A recent conflict is a good place to start: how did they handle it? There’s also who can block decisions, and what happened to the last person who left. (And who owns the land!) Is that how you want to live? It’s uncomfortable to talk about these things, I know, but the view isn’t going anywhere!

CONTEXT AFTER: Who will have title to the land? Very important! Whoever has it will have enormous power over the rest of the community. If the deed is in the name of the founder, then he or she is really a landlord with a philosophy. If three out of twelve members own everything, then whether or not your literature says everyone is equal, there are two classes of members.
