Even if the community wants to be a safe space for people, that doesn't necessarily mean that they can allow people to stay there indefinitely. If the person is obviously a serious or general danger to other people, or they won't stop violating important boundaries despite being asked, or whatever, just eject them. It's fine to try to do some restorative justice stuff if it seems like it will be quick, but if they're endangering everybody else, there's no reason to put the rest of the commune through whatever treatment they need.
