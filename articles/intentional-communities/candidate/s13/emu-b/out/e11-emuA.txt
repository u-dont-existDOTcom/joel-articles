Even in a safe space community it’s ok to kick people out if they’re serious or general danger. If someone won’t follow basic rules and boundaries, and won’t stop, and/or is just not someone people can live with, it’s okay to eject them. If the community feels like they can do restorative justice with them in a reasonable amount of time that’s cool, but if they are a danger to the community, the community doesn’t have to provide them treatment.
