Before that though, there’s lots of corresponding. It gives you a chance to see if they’ve actually read the stuff, if they “get” what the community is about, if they’ve at least started to do some of the stuff they say they should be doing, etc. It saves a lot of time, compared to letting things go on for 6 months and finding out they really don’t “get it”.
