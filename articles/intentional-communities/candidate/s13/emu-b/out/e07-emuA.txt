Have you thought about these questions: How long will a provisional membership be? What will a provisional member be responsible for and what are their rights? How will a provisional member's membership be reviewed? How might a person leave provisional membership? Who will make the decision about a person becoming a full member? (It shouldn't be the person who recruited them.) What rights will a full member have in terms of the land, other shared resources, and their voice within the group? What will a member be responsible for over the long term? (This should be addressed in the agreements you draw up when you are first starting your group.)
