1. Don't worry about getting land yet. If you've got a small handful of people who want to make a community but aren't sure yet, you are a community. It sounds cheesy but it's true.

2. Don't combine ownership/major resources until the group has had at least one real conflict and dealt with it. Not "dealt with it" by writing a vision statement and shaking hands, or declaring a truce, but really dealing with it. In other words, you need to do some real relational pl/ork where someone says "what you did was wrong" and everyone takes the time to deal with it. What comes up in conflicts is always jealousy, humiliation, fear, and other stuff from the past. Deal with it until the emotional pressure valve changes and then you can make an agreement.
