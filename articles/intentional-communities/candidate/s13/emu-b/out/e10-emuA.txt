The commons proposal is that all persons who regularly visit, work, volunteer, rent from, or intern with a political unit ought to have the right to expect that any decisions which really affect them with respect to their wages, access to resources, schedule, safety, housing, record, or discipline will be made according to clearly defined and communicated terms and with adequate voice and review. Like political members, they ought to be considered equal, for considering them unequal and incapable of political membership simply makes them a servant class to be passed from commune to commune as needed.
