That said, it’s also a lot easier to abuse, so coming up with a process ahead of time is probably a good idea. What constitutes removing somebody for others’ safety? How do document what’s going on and make sure that people who are involved in it aren’t participating in the decision? How do people appeal the decision without threatening others? How do people who are separated get housed, have their records and family connections protected, and still be able to use common resources or have access to outside funds?
