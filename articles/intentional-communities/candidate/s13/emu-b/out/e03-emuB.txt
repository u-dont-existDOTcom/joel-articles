A description is important even in a non-money community. There are always bottlenecks. It is important that people be able to know what the community has, what is set aside, what it owes to outsiders. Who has authority over distribution of major resources? Where are the conflicts of interest? Transfers of major resources (land, equipment, stored resources, outside money, etc.) need to be recorded somewhere. It is also important to specify what rights people have to resources when they leave the community. If the community does not use money, it still needs to be able to provide people with resources when they leave (tools, transportation, temporary housing, stored resources, etc). Otherwise the "right" to leave is just something on paper that can only be exercised if there is money around.
