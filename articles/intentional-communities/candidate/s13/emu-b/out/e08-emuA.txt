HOW DO YOU KNOW? At each stage, always judge people based on their conduct. No matter how good it sounds when they talk about their spirituality or their therapy, don’t go by that. It’s too easy to go by that. It’s also easy to go by people just doing what everyone else is doing. Even giving it six months will not give you enough data to know if someone is a good person.

If the person is going to be in a position where their actions have important consequences, eg, with children, with common resources, with medicine, with vehicles, with other people’s private information… then you need some evidence that they can be trusted with that specific thing. Ideally you’ve seen them do that thing. Failing that, you have some references for that thing. Failing that, make it so that you can give them increasing access, and there is some independent way to challenge it.
