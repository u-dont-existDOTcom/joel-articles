The same is true at all stages. Judge people on their actions. No matter how spiritual or therapeutic it might sound to say you’ll judge people on their “vibes”, it’s way too easy to just judge people on if they conform to what you believe is safe. Even after six months, you won’t know if someone is a good person or not.

If someone will have a role in your group that has consequences, for example with kids, shared resources, medicine, vehicles or private information, you want to know if they are good at that specific thing. You can do this by asking if you’ve seen them do this thing before, or asking for references if appropriate. Give people access to these things gradually and ensure there’s always some way for someone to appeal to a separate authority if needed.
