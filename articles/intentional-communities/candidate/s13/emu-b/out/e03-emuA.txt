Even in a money free community, there will be a finite amount of things and choke points. It is a good idea to know what is the community’s and what is held in common and what is put aside and what the community owes to non-members. It’s a good idea to know who is authorized to distribute major resources, and where there are conflicts of interest. Also, transfers of large quantities of resources (land, equipment, stores, non-community money) should not rely on memory. It is also a good idea to lay out what rights people have to cash out when they leave the community. If the community doesn’t use cash, that doesn’t mean they can’t have a way to equip people to leave (tools, transportation, temporary housing, stores, etc.). Otherwise, the right to leave is just a piece of paper. It’s nice to be able to leave a place when all the spare change comes up.
