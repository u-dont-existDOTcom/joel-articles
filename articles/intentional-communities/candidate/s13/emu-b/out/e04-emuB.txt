People leave intentional communities for many different, good reasons. Maybe a parent is dying somewhere else, maybe they fall in love, maybe they find a job they really want in another town, maybe their health or energy level won’t support the work it takes to live together, maybe they just don’t want to do this anymore. Giving up everything you have in the community to leave is coercive. Figure out the terms of exit before anyone is thinking about leaving, when it’s theoretical and you’re calm. It’s like a pre-nup, figuring it out while you’re planning the wedding isn’t very romantic, but it’s a lot easier than doing it when you’re both pissed off. Create in this order: vision, people, pool big things, land. —Diana Leafe Christian, Creating a Life Together
