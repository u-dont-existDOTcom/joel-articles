I was thinking of ‘outside’ as the nation-state, and now that I’ve written my reply I realise I wasn’t as clear on this point as I should have been. The important thing is that there is a layer above that can carry out the task competently and without reviewing its own actions. This can be another community, a larger federation, a professional, or it can be an autonomous institution within a region (like a court, a regulatory body, an emergency service, etc.) – the Zapatistas are important here. But autonomy doesn’t mean a community or region can abrogate the non-waivable rights of individuals within their jurisdiction.
