Check (5) It's a good idea to have someone already chosen to be either a mediator or an ombud (see pages 78-79). If people meet for the first time on the day the bomb explodes, it's very difficult for anyone to trust the 'neutral stranger'. And remember, mediation cannot replace the need for evidence, for safeguarding or for a ruling. It also cannot replace the final separation if one of the people no longer wants to live together.
