I think “outside” is too closely tied to the idea of nation states, and I was partly responsible for that. I think the key point is that there needs to be a competent layer that is not reviewing itself. This may be another community, a federation, a profession, etc. This is why I think the zapatistas are so important. They are still part of a state but they have created institutions that are autonomous. They review themselves in a way that the state can’t. This doesn’t mean they can disregard non-waivable individual rights or claim to be outside the jurisdiction of others.
