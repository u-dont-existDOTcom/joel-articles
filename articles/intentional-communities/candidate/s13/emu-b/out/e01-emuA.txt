Do not worry about having land yet. You may think you are not a community because you are small and you are still deciding whether or not you want to build a community. You are a community.

Do not combine ownership and major resources until you have had at least one real conflict, not one where you write a vision statement and shake hands or agree to a temporary truce, but one where you go through the conflict together in a really emotional way together. This is relational pl/ork. Someone has to name it, it has to get named and you all have to give it some time and figure out if it is jealousy or humiliation or fear or whatever it is and then you can agree because the pressure has changed.
