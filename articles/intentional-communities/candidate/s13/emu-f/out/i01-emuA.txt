On the other hand, the power to remove people is one of the easiest to abuse. So it’s important to think about how this might be done ahead of time. What might constitute cause for immediate action to protect people? What process will be used to decide whether to place temporary restrictions on someone? How will evidence be preserved? Who will recuse themselves? How can the person reply to the accusations and request a review or appeal of the decision? What will be done to provide housing, protect records, preserve access to family, allow access to common resources, and/or provide access to outside funds (if any)? When will outside help be sought?
