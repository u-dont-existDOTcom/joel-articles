Why bother? It is useful to agree on a mediator or ombud in advance as people often do not trust someone who they have not met, and who appears at the time of a crisis to act as a ‘neutral stranger’. Remember that mediation is about trying to restore good relations between people. It does not remove the need for evidence, for safeguarding, for a ruling, or for accepting the finality of separation when consent to continue in a relationship or a shared life is no longer present.
