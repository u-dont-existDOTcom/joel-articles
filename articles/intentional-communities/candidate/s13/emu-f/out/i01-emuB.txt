On the other hand, it is easy to abuse the power to remove people. So think about how to do it. When and how do people decide someone must be removed immediately for everyone's protection? Who decides what that person can and cannot do temporarily until the issue is resolved? Who makes sure all the "evidence" is kept? Who will not be involved in the decisions? How can the person who is being removed respond and/or appeal the decisions without being able to do harm? How do the people make sure the person who is removed can have a place to stay, and what will happen with records and/or family and/or access to outside resources (if any) and/or access to group resources? When will the people bring in outside help? (More on that next time.)
