T1: [1] Don't worry about getting land yet. [2] If you've got a small handful of people who want to make a community but aren't sure yet, you are already a community, even if it's still an untested one. [3] It sounds cheesy but it's true.

T2: [1] Also, don't even think about combining ownership (or any other big resource, for that matter) until you've been through at least one real conflict together and REALLY dealt with it. [2] Not dealt with it by writing out a vision statement and shaking hands, or any other fake way of "resolving" things, like a truce nobody believes ("let's just leave each other alone"). [3] But really, truly dealt with it. [4] This includes the relational pl/ork of each of you naming what really went on, giving each of you time, figuring out what came up for each of you (jealousy, humiliation, fear, old material, whatever), and dealing with it until the emotional pressure changes enough that whatever agreement you come up with means anything.
