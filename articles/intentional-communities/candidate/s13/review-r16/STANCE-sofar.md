# Stance check: section 13 rewrite ("so far")

## Conflicts in the rewrite

**1. Inheritance narrowed to the death of an owner** (Resources, Land, and Exit Terms)

- Rewrite: "you'll need to decide ... how you will account for people's contributions (if at all), what happens when an owner dies"
- Essay, section 13, same paragraph: "Decide voting rights, access and use rights for shared resources, contribution accounting if you use it, inheritance, outside debt, improvements"
- A reader would wrongly believe that inheritance only comes up when someone who owns the land dies, which leaves out a member's heirs and children. They would also believe members own the land personally, not through the legal entity the same paragraph has them choose.

## Contradictions in the published text

**1. The forming group is already the community, yet still strangers** (section 13, opening)

- Heading, then first paragraph: "Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community" / "The first community is the small group considering whether to build one."
- Three paragraphs later: "A group that has never done this is still a circle of promising strangers."
- The opening says a group that is still deciding already is the community. The later sentence says a group that hasn't yet processed a real conflict is only promising strangers, and a group that is still deciding usually hasn't. That takes back the heading's claim for the very group the heading is talking to.
- The rewrite still has it, in a softer form: "you are already a community, even if it's still an untested one", then "the group is still a circle of promising strangers". "Untested" closes part of the gap, but "strangers" still takes back "community".
