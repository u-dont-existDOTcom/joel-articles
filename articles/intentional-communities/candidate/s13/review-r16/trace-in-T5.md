REWRITE:

A community that wants to be safe can't promise to keep everyone. Yeah, that's pretty tough, but sometimes somebody's gotta go. It can be the right thing to eject someone when they're a serious danger to others (or to everyone in general), repeatedly violate essential boundaries, or are just impossible to live with. If restorative work can work with the person in question in a reasonable amount of time, all the better. But if the person is endangering everyone else, the community shouldn't have to serve as a treatment program for them.
