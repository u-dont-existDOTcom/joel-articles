In 2023 the U.S. Surgeon General’s advisory treated social connection as a public health issue instead of a sentimental extra, and it reported that roughly half of American adults experience loneliness. That’s the same longing that shaped my father’s film, and it’s suddenly everywhere again, decades after he made it.

Whether you want to call that loneliness an official “epidemic” or not, it’s obvious that a lot of people can’t tolerate modern life in isolation anymore. They may disagree about politics, food, God, sex, or whether shoes are oppressive, but they agree that something about the current arrangement is starvin’ them.
