On Google Trends, interest in “ecovillage” and “intentional community” had been drifting down for years, and then in the mid-2020s it turned sharply up.

Suddenly “maybe we should form a village” sounded less like a 1972 leftover and more like a backup plan. I can’t prove AI is what turned those lines around. The timing is hard to ignore, though. It happened around the same time AI, which had been a curiosity in the tech news, started reorganizing people’s jobs, their feeds, their relationships, and what they expected from the future.
