The same longing that pushed my father to make that film is in the air again. In 2023, the U.S. Surgeon General’s advisory reported that roughly half of American adults experience loneliness, and made social connection a public-health issue.

Whether you want to call it an epidemic or not, it’s obvious that a lot of people simply cannot tolerate modern life in isolation any more. This seems to be true regardless of politics, food, God, sex, or whether shoes are oppressive. People agree that the way things are now is starvin’ them.
