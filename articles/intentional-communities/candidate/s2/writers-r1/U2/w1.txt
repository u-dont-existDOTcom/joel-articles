Look up “ecovillage” and “intentional community” on Google Trends. Interest had been slowly sliding for years until the mid-2020s, when it turned sharply upward.

That upturn came around the time AI went from being a curiosity in the tech news to reorganizing people’s jobs, feeds, relationships, and expectations of the future. I can’t prove AI caused it, but it’s hard to look past the timing. Suddenly “maybe we should form a village” sounded less like a 1972 leftover and more like a backup plan.
