Did you notice, by the way, that the graph of Google searches for “ecovillage” and “intentional community” starts bending upwards rapidly in the mid-’20s after having drifted downward for years? (Graphs from Google Trends.)

I have no proof that the rise of AI caused this uptick, but it’s hard to ignore the coincidence in timing. AI became serious around this time, and it started changing people’s jobs and relationships, and how they see the future. Suddenly “maybe we should form a village” sounded less like a 1972 leftover and more like a backup plan.
