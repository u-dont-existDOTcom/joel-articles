In 2023, the U.S. Surgeon General put out an advisory that treated social connection as a public-health issue. My father went looking for that kind of connection when he made his movie, and now suddenly people everywhere are looking for it again. The advisory said roughly half of American adults experience loneliness.

Whether you want to call that loneliness an official “epidemic” or not, it’s obvious that a lot of people can’t tolerate modern life in isolation anymore. They may disagree about politics, food, God, sex, or whether shoes are oppressive, but they agree that something about the current arrangement is starvin’ them.
