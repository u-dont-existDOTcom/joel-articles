If you look at Google Trends for “ecovillage” and “intentional community”, you can see that interest drifted down for years, then bent upward sharply in the mid-2020s.

I have no proof that the rise of AI caused this uptick, but it’s hard to ignore the coincidence in timing. AI became serious around this time, and it started changing people’s jobs and relationships, and how they see the future. Suddenly, forming a village sounded like a backup plan.
