# Repeats check: "The Math of Absorption, and Who This Isn’t For" (B252-B273)

Every block B253-B273 was checked against the others and the rest of the article. Only pairs involving a block in that section are listed. "Math" means that section.

Note: B256 previews four points that later blocks restate (entries 4, 6, 7, 8).

## 1. Repeats, in article order

**1. B37 (Communes Are Hot Again: The Freeloader Problem) and B271 (Math)**
- B37: "some people really would rather let somebody else take responsibility"
- B271: "You want the feeling of village life but don’t want to take on governance, maintenance, conflict, care, etc."
Point: wanting community life without its work.
Later instance: told again, only recast as a self-check.
Better: B37 carries it (adds the consequence: the same three people do all the maintenance).

**2. B72 (The Four Parts That Have to Pl/ork Together: 2. Relational pl/ork) and B259 (Math)**
- B72: "the capacity to be there for people when they need help isn’t located in one professional or guru"
- B259: "some of the functions that are outsourced to therapists in modern society are distributed throughout the community"
Point: peer counseling spreads the helping role across the community.
Later instance: mostly told again; adds the contrast with modern therapists and leads into B260.
Better: B72, the natural home, which also gives the reason.

**3. B86 (The Four Parts That Have to Pl/ork Together: 4. The container) and B260 (Math)**
- B86: "when members come on board faster than the group can support them, all the peer counseling in the world"
- B260: "That does not make the community infinitely absorbent like a Heavy Flow Maxi Pad."
Point: peer counseling does not lift the limit on how much need a group can absorb.
Later instance: adds nothing by itself (the math follows in B261-B267); the claim is told again.
Better: B260 onward, the natural place; B86 is a one-line aside.

**4. B246 (Founderism: The Four Questions: How My Own Model Could Become a Cult), B256 (Math) and B272 (Math)**
- B246: "if a member is avoiding the therapy process and becoming hard to deal with, they will naturally not belong at a certain point"
- B256: "how willing they are to examine their own reactions"
- B272: "You’re not willing to look at yourself when jealousy or conflict comes up."
Point: unwillingness to examine oneself means a poor fit.
Later instances: B272 adds the jealousy or conflict trigger and the self-check form; B246 is about current members, B256 and B272 about newcomers. Partly told again.
Better: B272, the most concrete.

**5. B254 and B255 (both Math)**
- B254: "where relationships are easy to make and break, people even feel less on the same page"
- B255: "where relationships are easily formed and broken, people line up their worldviews more selectively with the people who help them reach their goals"

Later instance: adds the journal name and the wider framing; the finding itself is told twice.
Better: B254 (prose with the author's reaction); B255 illustrates the same source.

**6. B256 and B261 (both Math)**
- B256: "how much attention the newcomer currently has available for other people"
- B261: "the attention, steadiness, skill and practical capacity people have left over for others"
Point: free resource, the attention one has left over for others.
Later instance: adds the name "free resource", its counterpart "need", and steadiness, skill and practical capacity. Partly new; the idea was already in B256.
Better: B261, where the term is defined.

**7. B256 and B265 (both Math)**
- B256: "look at whether the group has enough free resource/capacity to support them without becoming a full-time caretaker operation"

**8. B256 and B270 (both Math)**

**9. B257 and B262 (both Math)**

**10. B265 (Math) and B292 (Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community: From Visitor to Member—Slowly)**

**11. B266 (Math) and B278 (Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community, opening part)**

**12. B267 and B270 (both Math)**

**13. B268 and B273 (both Math)**

## 2. Contradictions (both mild, both reconcilable but unexplained)

**A. B85 (The Four Parts That Have to Pl/ork Together: 4. The container), B253 (Math) and B278 (Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community)**
- B85: "These are less exciting topics to discuss spiritually."
- B253: "would rather sit around for six hours talking about solar panels than talk about jealousy, God, death"
- B278: "This is the boring stuff!"
Tension: B253 has people choosing practical talk over the hard topics; B85 and B278 call the practical topics (membership policy, assets, budget) the dull ones people skip. The article does not say different people avoid different things.

**B. B245 (Founderism: The Four Questions: How My Own Model Could Become a Cult) and B268 (Math)**
- B245: "Well, it is gatekeeping. Some people simply should not be let in the gate, at least not yet."
- B268: "the power to veto membership applications based on capacity remains dangerously tilted towards elitism"
Tension: gatekeeping is shrugged off in B245 and called dangerous in B268.
