# Repeats check, sample s16

Scope: every block in "Practice Comes Before Property" (B345 to B369) checked against every other block there and against the rest of the article. Only repeats that involve at least one block from that section are listed. Its two sub-sections are "If You Can’t Move" (B349 to B357) and "What to Do This Month" (B358 to B369, which also holds the closing paragraphs).

Counts: 7 repeats, 1 contradiction, 5 callbacks listed separately.

## 1. Repeats, in article order

1. Blocks B40, B365. Point: the author is not recruiting anyone or selling anything.
   - B40 (Communes Are Hot Again > The Freeloader Problem): "I am not currently recruiting anyone or founding a community"
   - B365 (Practice Comes Before Property > What to Do This Month): "I'm not trying to sell you a retreat or get you to fill out an application or anything."
   - Later instance: B365 adds that the manuals are free and the closing observation about where community gathers. The no-recruiting, no-selling reassurance itself is the same, so a reader would feel told it again, briefly.
   - Better placed: B40. It gives the reason and the stakes (he plans to buy land, and the warnings apply to him); B365 is the shorter echo at the close.

2. Blocks B88, B346, B353. Point: leaving the emotional work until after land and money are committed means the unresolved conflict arrives later, at higher stakes.
   - B88 (The Four Parts That Have to Pl/ork Together > 4. The container): "it will need to be dealt with eventually, at high stress, usually after the group has invested land, labor, or other resources"
   - B346 (Practice Comes Before Property): "the emotional side of things? That doesn't get done until"
   - B353 (Practice Comes Before Property > If You Can’t Move): "buy forty acres and then bring all their unprocessed stuff with them to a more scenic location"
   - Later instances: B346 adds the typical wrong order (land, then a vision document, then whoever pays owns it). B353 adds the individual version and the goats image ("now you have the conflict PLUS goats"). The consequence they describe is the same one B88 states, so a reader would feel told again.
   - Better placed: B346. It opens the section and names the whole wrong order in one place; B88 is the general form and B353 is the image.

3. Blocks B276, B278, B350, B354, B355. Point: the important work does not need land, and land can wait or never come.
   - B276 (Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community): "Don't worry about getting land yet."
   - B278 (Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community): "long before anyone needs a land surveyor"
   - B350 (Practice Comes Before Property > If You Can’t Move): "The most important pl/ork doesn't need land anyway."
   - B354 (Practice Comes Before Property > If You Can’t Move): "None of it has to wait until somebody has a deed in hand."
   - B355 (Practice Comes Before Property > If You Can’t Move): "A group that practices together weekly for two years may never own land."
   - Later instances: B350 to B355 add a new audience (people who can't relocate), the minimum needed for peer counseling (two people and a private place), and the permission to never own land. The message itself is the same, so a reader would feel told again with a new audience.
   - Better placed: B354. It says it most directly and sits where the can't-move reader needs it; B276 and B278 make the point in passing for forming groups.

4. Blocks B277, B347, B360. Point: get through at least one real conflict together before taking bigger steps.
   - B277 (Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community): "until you've been through at least one real conflict together and REALLY dealt with it"
   - B347 (Practice Comes Before Property): "get together to practice first, and process a real conflict, and get clear on your values"
   - B360 (Practice Comes Before Property > What to Do This Month): "Get through at least one disagreement too, and keep your commitments. One disagreement is enough!"
   - Later instances: B347 adds the full order (values, membership, ownership and exit, then land). B360 adds keeping commitments and putting off talk of a shared future. Otherwise the instruction is the same, so a reader would feel told it again twice.
   - Better placed: B277. It says what "really dealt with" means (not a vision statement, not a truce nobody believes) and why; B347 and B360 are shortened versions.

5. Blocks B314, B367. Point: the Bruderhof shaped the author's father and the film he made.
   - B314 (Dissolution and Exit): "It shaped the foundation he started and the film he made."
   - B367 (Practice Comes Before Property > What to Do This Month): "Decades after the Bruderhof shaped my father"
   - Later instance: B367 adds the present act ("publishing the film he made, and writing this"). The claim that the Bruderhof shaped him and the film is the same, so a reader would feel told it again, mildly. B367 also works as a bookend to the opening (see the callbacks below).
   - Better placed: B367, since it closes the article by echoing the opening. B314 carries more detail (the foundation, the places he took the author, the questions).

6. Blocks B354, B359. Point: start the inner and relational practice now, using the author's free guides.
   - B354 (Practice Comes Before Property > If You Can’t Move): "So your inner pl/ork can start now, right where you live."
   - B359 (Practice Comes Before Property > What to Do This Month): "Start doing some inner pl/ork, with the free reparenting guide and Hearthwork manual"
   - Later instance: B359 adds the two named guides as the way in, the option of another trusted practice, and "practice before you have an audience". B354 adds the minimum for peer counseling. The instruction to start now is the same, in the next sub-section, and both link the Hearthwork guide, so a reader would feel told again.
   - Better placed: B359. It is the call to action and carries the resources; B354 gives the reason inside the can't-move argument.

7. Blocks B356, B357, B360. Point: gather a few people or households and meet regularly.
   - B356 (Practice Comes Before Property > If You Can’t Move): "Have a weekly meal with two households. Set up a counseling exchange with one friend, in person or by video."
   - B357 (Practice Comes Before Property > If You Can’t Move): "If three households keep showing up for each other like this for six months"
   - B360 (Practice Comes Before Property > What to Do This Month): "Find two or three other people and meet regularly."
   - Later instance: B360 adds listening and switching roles, the "practice group" label, and (with B361) where to find these people. B356 and B357 add the menu of activities (meals, tools, childcare, sauna) and the six-month payoff. The core instruction is the same one told twice in adjacent sub-sections, so a reader would feel told again.
   - Better placed: B360. It is the concrete recipe and says what to practice; B356 and B357 give the looser version.

## 2. Contradictions

1. Blocks B275/B276, B360. Pull: what to call a small forming group.
   - B275 and B276 (Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community): heading "Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community", and "you are already a community".
   - B360 (Practice Comes Before Property > What to Do This Month): "Call it a practice group, not a commune."
   - Why a reader would see it: one section tells a small handful of people they already are a community, and the later one tells a group of two or three not to use the commune label. The article never says how "community" and "commune" differ here, and elsewhere it uses the two words loosely. This is a moderate pull, not an outright clash.
   - Fits its place better: B360 fits a reader about to over-claim, and B275 and B276 fit a reader worried they don't count yet. Neither says why the other doesn't apply.

Pairs I looked at and did not count:
- B277 against B360. "REALLY dealt with it" against "One disagreement is enough!" They set thresholds for different steps (combining ownership versus talking about a shared future).
- B80 against B348. Money as "antagonistic to the spirit of community" against not reducing outside money "because 'money is impure!'" One is the reason for the aim, the other is about timing.
- B40 against B346 and B347. The author plans to buy land while advising to think about land last. B236 and B237 deal with his own case.

## 3. The five repeats a reader would most notice

Entries 7, 6, 4, 3 and 2, in that order. Entries 7 and 6 sit in adjacent sub-sections and give nearly the same instruction with the same guide links. Entry 4 returns to the "one real conflict" rule twice. Entry 3 says "you don't need land" in five places, and entry 2 gives the land-first consequence three times.
