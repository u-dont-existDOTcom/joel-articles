# Repeats check, intentional communities article (b-s16)

Scope: only items involving a block in "Practice Comes Before Property" (B344-B368): its opening (B344-B347), "If You Can't Move" (B348-B356) and "What to Do This Month" (B357-B368). Quotes are exact.

## 1. Repeats, in article order

**1. Not recruiting or selling: B40, B364**
- B40 (Communes Are Hot Again > The Freeloader Problem): "I am not currently recruiting anyone or founding a community"
- B364 (Practice Comes Before Property > What to Do This Month): "trying to sell you a retreat or get you to fill out an application"
- Point: the author wants nothing from the reader.
- Later adds almost nothing. "The manuals I mentioned are free" repeats the "free" guides of B358. A reader feels told again, though it works as a closing assurance.
- B40 carries it better: it gives the reason and the plan to buy land.

**2. Money in stages: B80-B83, B347**
- B80 (The Four Parts That Have to Pl/ork Together > 4. The container): "I think the path there has three stages"
- B347 (Practice Comes Before Property): "Same with money: do it in stages"
- Point: move toward less money step by step.
- Later adds a readiness test, "based on what the group has shown it CAN do". Its aside, "kind of weird when somebody else is paying the tax bill", also echoes B83's "government requirements like taxes on land".
- B80-B83 carry the stages. B347 carries the pacing rule, so it adds something but re-announces the stages.

**3. Land first, so unprocessed material arrives later: B88, B345, B352**
- B88 (Four Parts > 4. The container): "it will need to be dealt with eventually, at high stress, usually after the group has invested land"
- B345 (Practice Comes Before Property): "later on, when everyone has more time"
- B352 (If You Can't Move): "bring all their unprocessed stuff with them to a more scenic location"
- Point: doing land and logistics before the inner and relational work makes the postponed work come back worse. B87 already says the same order: "the practical logistical foundation that gets built once the architects can work well together".
- B345 and B352 give the same picture twice within seven blocks ("now you have the conflict PLUS goats"), so a reader feels told again. B352 adds only the baggage-travels image.
- B345-B346 carry it best, since they set out the wrong order and the right one.

**4. Community helps through illness (weak): B144, B354**
- B144 (Which Connections to the Technosphere Do You Keep?): "it can make a difference, day to day, in the conditions people get sick, heal, age, and die in"
- B354 (If You Can't Move): "It can still make a difference when people are going through a divorce, an illness or grief"
- Point: community support helps through hard events.
- B354 adds that a small land-free weekly group is enough, plus more life events. A reader probably will not feel told again.
- B144 sits in the more natural place (health, Surgeon General).

**5. Practice does not need land: B275, B277, B349, B353, B354**
- B275, B277 (Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community): "worry about getting land yet"; "long before anyone needs a land surveyor"
- B349 (If You Can't Move): "The most important pl/ork doesn't need land anyway"
- B353 (If You Can't Move): "None of it has to wait until somebody has a deed in hand"
- B354 (If You Can't Move): "may never own land. So what?"
- Point: start practicing now, without land.
- B349, B353 and B354 make it three times in six blocks, so a reader feels told again. B353 adds what can start now, and B354 adds the benefit. Against B275-B277 it is the same advice for people who cannot move.
- B353 carries it best, since it names the practices. B277 is the natural first home.

**6. A real conflict comes before ownership or land: B276, B346, B359**
- B276 (Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community): "at least one real conflict together and REALLY dealt with it"
- B346 (Practice Comes Before Property): "process a real conflict, and get clear on your values"; "and then, after all that, think about land"
- B359 (What to Do This Month): "Get through at least one disagreement too, and keep your commitments"
- Point: get through a real conflict before taking on land, ownership or a shared future.
- B346 adds the full order (values, membership, ownership and exit). B359 adds "One disagreement is enough!" and moves the target to a shared future. Otherwise a reader is told the same thing again.
- B276 carries it best, since it says what "dealt with" means and why. B346 is the natural place for the thesis.

**7. Start inner pl/ork now: B353, B358**
- B353 (If You Can't Move): "So your inner pl/ork can start now, right where you live"
- B358 (What to Do This Month): "Start doing some inner pl/ork"
- Point: begin now.
- B358 adds the named free guides and "practice before you have an audience". Otherwise a reader feels told again, five blocks apart.
- B358 carries it, as the action step.

**8. Meet regularly with a few people: B353-B356, B359**
- B355 and B356 (If You Can't Move): "Have a weekly meal with two households"; "If three households keep showing up for each other like this for six months"
- B359 (What to Do This Month): "Find two or three other people and meet regularly"
- Point: a small group that keeps meeting. B353 ("takes two people and a private place to sit and talk") and B355 ("Set up a counseling exchange with one friend") also both say peer counseling needs only one other person.
- B359 adds the content (listening, switching roles, one disagreement, calling it a practice group). B355-B356 add household activities and the six-month payoff. Partly told again.
- B359 carries the plan. B355 is more concrete.

**9. Experience over theory (weak): B356, B362**
- B356 (If You Can't Move): "another year of daydreaming about the ideal village"
- B362 (What to Do This Month): "No manifesto is going to tell you any of that!"
- Point: doing or visiting teaches what thinking cannot.
- B362 applies it to visiting, a distinct step. A reader is unlikely to feel told again.
- B356 sits more naturally.

## 2. Contradictions

**C1. B275 vs B359 (mild)**
- B275 (Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community): "you are already a community"
- B359 (What to Do This Month): "Call it a practice group, not a commune."
- One treats the handful as a community, the other says not to call it that. "Community" versus "commune" could reconcile them, but the text does not say so.

**C2. B57 vs B353, B358 (label only)**
- B57 (The Four Parts): "relational pl/ork (peer counseling especially)"
- B353 (If You Can't Move): "So your inner pl/ork can start now, right where you live" is followed by peer counseling, jealousy work and conflict repair. B358 likewise puts the Hearthwork manual under "Start doing some inner pl/ork".
- Peer counseling is relational in the four-part model but called inner here.

**C3. B87 vs B354 (weakest)**
- B87 (Four Parts > 4. The container): "this container is what gives those a stable place to manifest"
- B354 (If You Can't Move): "may never own land. So what?"
- The container is what the other parts need, yet the Practice section says a group may never own land. B87's next sentence, that the container is built once the architects can work well together, partly resolves it.

Considered, not reported: B347 against B80 on why money should shrink (goal versus pace), and B359 against B276 on how much conflict is enough (different steps).
