## From "The Grown Children Get the Final Review" (changed paragraph S8P26f)

Make sure kids have relationships and a reporting route outside the community. Grandparents, cousins, local friends, teachers and other trusted adults give them perspective on their life, and someone to talk to. Make sure there's at least one trusted adult, advocate or reporting route they can reach without going through the community's leadership. In 1984, Vermont authorities [raided a religious commune in Island Pond](https://vtdigger.org/2024/06/21/40-years-later-island-pond-has-little-interest-in-revisiting-its-historic-raid/) and took 112 kids into custody over suspected abuse. A judge turned down the state's blanket request to hold them, because it couldn't name any specific case, and the kids went home that same day. State intervention can fail spectacularly, and enclosing kids completely in a community can fail too. Kids need more than one world.
