## 2. Contradictions and unexplained tensions

**C1. B247 vs B308 (and B181): decide ahead of time or not?**
- B247: "It depends on so many factors that I can't decide any of this ahead of time."
- B308: "So work out the process before it's about anyone in particular"

**C2. B245 vs B267: gatekeeping is fine, versus gatekeeping on capacity is dangerously elitist**
- B245: "Well, it is gatekeeping. Some people simply should not be let in the gate, at least not yet."
- B267: "Even then, the power to veto membership applications based on capacity remains dangerously tilted towards elitism."
- B245 accepts gatekeeping calmly ("that's fine"). B267 calls the capacity version dangerous even with safeguards. They gate on different things (taking part in the therapy processes versus capacity), but the article doesn't connect them.

**C3 (borderline). B244 vs B307: removed for a practice, versus removed for conduct**
- B244: "If someone starts worshipping Satan, say, they get booted."
- B307: "If somebody poses a serious or generalized danger, repeatedly violates essential boundaries, or makes ordinary shared life impossible, ejection can be the right answer."
- B244 removes someone for a spiritual practice "against the communal spirit". B307 and B246 set thresholds based on conduct, and B61 says "Each of us guides our own practice". B244 does give a reason, but not one that matches B307's grounds. A reader may take B244 as a joke; if not, the two standards pull apart.

**C4 (borderline). B239 vs B305: do visitors get terms?**
- B239: "Friends who come to visit don't get written terms, though."
- B305: "Visitors, volunteers, workers, renters, and interns do not need full political membership to have rights."
- B305 goes on to say visitors need "clear terms, a way to be heard, and review" when decisions materially affect them. B239 is about friends dropping in on a group that is still forming, so the two may not conflict, but the article doesn't link them.
