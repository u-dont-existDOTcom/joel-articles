## 2. Contradictions, in article order

**C1. B7 against B53 and B269**
- B7: "people would live in the healing communities I’m proposing, to heal and to learn the relational and practical methods"
- B53: "A place where people who've done the healing first can join without pretending they're fully finished"
- B269: "A founding group shouldn’t pretend it can give you those."
- The pull: B7 says people would come to the communities to heal. B53, and B263 to B269, say to do the healing and get stable before joining, and that someone who mainly needs a place to land should wait. The article never says how these fit together, for example whether it depends on the group's size or on how much need a person has. B266's "Once there are 80 members" hints at an answer but doesn't make it.
- Better: B53 and B263 to B269, because they're developed with reasons. B7 is a one-line description.

**C2. B59 against B61 (mild)**
- B59: "A community needs a shared sacred inner practice that gives the difficulty of our life together a deeper meaning."
- B61: "Each of us guides our own practice"
- The pull: B59 calls for one shared practice, and two blocks later B61 says each person guides their own. The quilt image in B61 partly reconciles them, but it doesn't say whether the practice is shared.
- Better: B61's version sits in "Founded on Freedom", the section about how individual practice and community fit together.

**C3. B80 against B353 (mild)**
- B80: "since money is antagonistic to the spirit of community."
- B353: "not from declaring money impure while somebody else quietly pays the tax bill."
- The pull: B80 rests the money-free goal on money being against the spirit of community. B353 mocks resting it on money being impure. The two can be reconciled, since B353 is about the order of the steps and what they're based on, but the article doesn't say so.
- Better: B353, because it's the later, more careful statement, while B80 states the motive.

**C4. B181 and B308 against B247 and B250**
- B181: "Decide on the response process ahead of time"
- B247: "I can't decide any of this ahead of time."
- B250: "Nobody can give that advice ahead of time, including me."
- B308: "So work out the process before it's about anyone in particular"
- The pull: B246 and B247 discuss expelling a member and say it can't be decided ahead of time. B307 and B308 discuss removing a member and say to work out the process ahead of time. The two can be reconciled (B247 is about the outcome in a particular case, B308 about the procedure), but the article doesn't say so, and both passages are about the same situation. Of the five, a reader is most likely to notice this one.
- Better: B308, because it's the developed instruction.

**C5. B239 against B305 (mild)**
- B239: "Friends who come to visit don't get written terms, though."
- B305: "they need clear terms, a way to be heard, and review."
- The pull: B239 says visitors don't get written terms. B305 says visitors (along with volunteers, workers, renters and interns) need clear terms. B305 limits this to decisions that materially affect them, and B239 is about friends visiting a group that hasn't formed yet, but the article doesn't connect the two.
- Better: each fits its own place. B305 is the general rule.
