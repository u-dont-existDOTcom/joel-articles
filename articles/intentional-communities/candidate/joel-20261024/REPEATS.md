I checked each cut against the article. My pick is A (cut) on 15 and B (keep) on 16, where both places carry something a reader needs there; the manual now repeats a few essay points on purpose, for readers who go straight to it (your 61 b). Each cut is struck through in its paragraph, with where the point is kept and the paragraph as it reads after the cut. Answer "65: your picks", or the numbers you want changed ("65.3 B").
