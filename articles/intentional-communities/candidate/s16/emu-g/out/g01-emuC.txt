Re: why you can (and should) be a pl/ork right now I know that there are tons of reasons why you can't take off and move to a piece of land to do this stuff full time. Work, kids, pets, debt, mortgage, sick parent, etc etc. That's okay, the most important pl/ork doesn't even require land!

People make the mistake of buying 40 acres and taking all of their unprocessed-ness with them. Sure it's pretty to look at while you're fighting but now there are goats involved. That's usually not an improvement.

You can let your inner pl/ork out to play anytime, anywhere. Peer counseling just needs 2 people and a place to be private. Reparenting, somatic work, jealousy work, conflict repair, etc etc can all start anytime. No land needed.

What if you work on this stuff together for 2 years and you still don't have a piece of land? Then you've wasted 2 years? How about all the times in those 2 years that you might have fallen apart during a divorce, or illness, or grief, or when your kid did something hard, or when you were just feeling lonely.

If you can't do all the things, do the things you can. Even if you're far away from everyone, or feel like you're stuck where you are, find the thing you CAN do to make your life more communally. Eat with another household once a week. Do counseling with someone, even if it's over the internet. Share some tools, or babysitting, or cooking, or car transport, or a sauna. If you're a little hippier than me, maybe acro-yoga or dance improv or a mothers circle or a cuddle puddle.

Do it for 6 months with 3 households and you'll learn more than you ever would from fantasizing about doing it.
