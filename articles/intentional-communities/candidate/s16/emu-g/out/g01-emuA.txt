A note on learning skills remotely : I know that many of you have reasons that prevent you from moving to a piece of land and getting to work building community there. Jobs, kids, pets, debt, mortgage, sick parents, etc. That's totally okay! In fact, the most important pl/ork you can do doesn't even need a piece of land.

A lot of folks make the mistake of buying a piece of land and then bringing all of their unprocessed stuff with them onto the land. Sure, now they can look out the window and see pretty things instead of an ugly neighbor's house, but now they're dealing with their unprocessed stuff in addition to goats.

The point is, you can release your inner pl/ork wherever you happen to be. Peer counseling takes two. If you and someone can find a private place to sit in, there's a lot of reparenting, somatic, jealousy, conflict, and other work that you can do to improve your life. You don't need to own the land to do it.

Two years from now, if you don't have land to move onto, will you be sad that you spent those two years practicing with someone? Probably not, because practicing these skills can be extremely helpful in getting through divorce, illness, grief, parenting, loneliness, etc.

If you're remotely located, or unable to move where you want to be, do the parts you can. Cook a weekly meal with another household. Set up a peer counseling exchange with someone (even by computer). Trade tool sharing, child care, transportation, meal prep, or sauna usage with someone nearby. If you're hippie enough, arrange acro-yoga time, dance improv, mothers circle, or a cuddle puddle.

You'll learn more in six months of actually doing three of the above with three households than you ever will by fantasizing about it.
