Start doing some inner pl/ork. Read the free reparenting guide, or get the Hearthwork manual, or whatever. Seriously work at it even before there are other people around.

Get together with 2-3 people in a practice group. Call it what you want, but don't call it a commune until you've gotten to know each other. Meet for a while. Work on your listening and switching skills. Actually disagree and work it out. Make and keep group commitments for a while. Yes, you have to work through at least one disagreement before you plan a future together.

Where will you find such people? Maybe there are some unprogrammed Quakers around? How about a group working on restorative practices? Is there a really open-hearted church or mosque or congregation? Maybe a mature enough AA or Al Anon or other group? Perhaps a meditation group? Have you met anyone who's been on a long retreat lately who seems kind and nice and not full of themselves?

Have you been to an actual community yet? If not, go visit! Twin Oaks, East Wind, the Acorn community, and many others will welcome visitors. A lot of the ecovillages, too.

You'll learn a lot that's not in any manifesto, like how much togetherness you want, how much privacy you need, what kinds of labor systems drive you bananas, whether showering with the folks is gonna work or whether you'll need to start thinking about studio apts. soon.

Do these things before you invest a lot of time and money into searching for a place. I know, I know!

Oh, and I'm not selling anything here, not even a retreat. The manuals are free, and there's no application. It's just that communities pop up where people are doing this sort of thing, trying to be more caring and playful and useful and truthful.
