For most people there will be many reasons that prevent you from going to a land-based commune immediately. Job, kids, pets, debt, mortgage, sick parent, etc. That’s ok. The most important pl/ork doesn’t require land.

Sometimes people buy 40 acres and then bring all their unprocessed baggage with them. Sure now you have a beautiful view, but now you have all that stuff… Plus goats!

So your inner pl/ork can start now, where you are. The beginning of peer counseling only requires two people and a place to sit privately and talk. Reparenting, somatic body work, working on jealousy, working on how to do conflict well, etc etc, can all be done without owning land.

If you work on these things together for two years and still don’t own land, have you wasted your time? Probably not.

If you can’t move to a commune, live remotely, or are otherwise stuck where you are, do the parts you can. Cook a weekly meal with another household. Enter into a peer counseling arrangement with someone (you can even do it via Skype or Google Hangout if you can’t be in the same room). Exchange tool sharing, child care, transport, meal prep, sauna time, etc with other households. If you’re hipster enough you can probably find people to do acro-yoga with, dance improv, mothers circles, cuddle puddles, etc.

Do any of these things with two other households for six months and you’ll learn more than you would by continuing to fantasize about what a commune might be like.
