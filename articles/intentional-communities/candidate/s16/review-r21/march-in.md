Your inner pl/ork can actually start right now, right where you live. Peer counseling just requires 2 people and a private place to sit down and talk. Reparenting work, somatic work, working on jealousy, working on conflict repair, etc etc, can all start before you own a single square inch of dirt. No deed required!

What if, after two years of practicing together every week, the group still doesn't own land? Would that be a waste of time? Nope. It can still change how people get through divorce, illness, grief, parenting and plain old loneliness.

Maybe you're remote, or stuck where you are. In that case, build the part of it you actually can. Have a weekly meal with two households. Trade counseling hours with one friend, in person or by video. Share tools or childcare. Transport or food prep work too. Or a sauna! And if you're hippie enough, add acro-yoga, dance improv, a mothers' circle or a cuddle puddle.

Have you ever been to an actual community? If you haven't, you ought to! Twin Oaks, East Wind, Acorn and other intentional communities take visitors, and so do a lot of ecovillages (just check their current visitor requirements before you go).

What will you learn? So much, and none of it is in any manifesto! How much togetherness you actually like, for one, and what kind of privacy you need. And which labor system drives you bananas! And whether showering with folks feels liberating, or makes you want to start pricing studio apartments.

Do those three things before you spend a lot of time or money looking for land!

I'm not selling anything here, by the way. I'm not even asking you to come to a retreat or fill out an application, and the manuals I mentioned are free. I've just noticed that community tends to gather wherever people find real care, play, usefulness and room to become more honest.
