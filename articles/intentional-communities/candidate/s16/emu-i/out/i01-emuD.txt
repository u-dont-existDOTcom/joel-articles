Start your own inner pl/ork. There’s a free guide to reparenting here and a free hearthwork manual here, or there are other good ways to be serious about being better inside.

Get a practice group going with two or three other people. Call it something else if “commune” is too loaded. Work on group skills.

There are a lot of places where you might find people like this: Quakers, restorative practice groups, open-hearted churches, mature recovery groups, meditation groups, etc. Just look for people who are kinder after spending time away from everyone in a retreat than they were before, rather than puffier.

Visit some real communities. Twin Oaks is good, and East Wind, and the Acorn, and many others, and also a lot of the new ecovillages. Check their requirements first.

Then you’ll know a little bit about how much you like being together with people, or how much alone time you need, or how involved you want to be in the group’s labor system, or whether you love or hate communal showers.

Do these three things before you spend serious time or money considering land.

There is no application form. There is no retreat to join. The manuals are free. I figure if it’s good people will grab it and make their own communities.

My father was formed by the Bruderhof community. I am publishing his film and writing this to try to form people, too, decades later.

Maybe someone will build a community using these ideas, and then that community will form other people, who will build more communities using these ideas, and so on.

Or maybe you’ll build something real and let me know about it. But if you teach others to build their own real things, it won’t matter.
