Find two or three other people and meet regularly. This is not quite the same thing as forming a commune. Call it a practice group. Practice the skills you need to practice, like listening and switching roles. Get through at least one disagreement too, and keep your commitments. One disagreement is enough! You can talk about a shared future later.

Go visit some real communities. Really, go! Twin Oaks, East Wind, Acorn and other intentional communities are used to having people visit them, and so are many ecovillages. Check their requirements first.

Maybe some of you will build something useful from this. Maybe that community will teach someone else, who'll build another. Passing it on doesn't need one institution or one founder. And there doesn't have to be one official version of it, either.
