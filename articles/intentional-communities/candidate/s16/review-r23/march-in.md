Start doing some inner pl/ork. Get a copy of the free reparenting guide and the Hearthwork manual, or go with another serious practice you already trust, and start practicing before you bring an audience into it!

Get two or three people together to form a practice group. Call it that, not a commune! Meet regularly to practice listening and switching roles, etc. See if you can get through at least one disagreement together (one is enough!), and keep your commitments to each other, all before you start talking about a shared future.

Where would you find such people, you ask? Well, are there any unprogrammed Quaker meetings nearby? Any restorative-practice groups, or open-hearted congregations?
