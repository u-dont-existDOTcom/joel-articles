Start doing some inner pl/ork. No, really! Get a copy of the free reparenting guide and the Hearthwork manual, or go with another serious practice you already trust, and start practicing before you bring an audience into it.

Get two or three people together to form a practice group. Call it that, not a commune! Before you start talking about a shared future, meet regularly: practice listening and switching roles, get through at least one disagreement together, and keep your commitments to each other. One disagreement is enough, I promise!
