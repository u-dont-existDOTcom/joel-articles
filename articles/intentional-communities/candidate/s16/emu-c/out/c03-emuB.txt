Start in small ways. Get together with two or three people (not a commune, just a small group) and practice being in a group – listening, switching off, working through at least one disagreement (one is enough!), keeping commitments to each other, meeting regularly, etc.

Are there any unprogrammed Quaker or restorative practice groups near you? Any open-hearted spiritual congregations? Maybe some mature recovery or meditation groups? Have you met anyone you know who recently returned from a long retreat and seems more loving (rather than self-important)?

Have you visited an actual community? Many of the larger communities – Twin Oaks, East Wind, Acorn, etc. – welcome visitors. Many of the ecovillages do, too. Find some you like the sound of, and see if you can visit (bear in mind that their requirements for visitors may have changed since this was written).

It’ll give you a sense of what you like about being together with others, what kind of privacy you might want, what kinds of labor systems bother you, whether you want communal showers or your own studio apartment, etc.
