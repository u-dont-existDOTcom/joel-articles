Practice Comes Before Property

Most people work in the reverse order: 1. Get the land! 2. Ok, now write that vision thing down! 3. And, ah, might as well just let the person who is going to own it have it. 4. All of this emotive stuff? Let's get around to that later when there's more time!

Working in this order, on the other hand, is not very cinematic: 1. Practice! 2. Process a conflict! 3. Ok, now write down some values! 4. Ok, do you want to be members of a community together? 5. How do you want to own it? How will you get out when you want to? 6. Now, might be a good idea to get some land...

Working in stages economically also makes sense: don't try to fly straight there! Once you can provide for everybody, reliably, THEN work on a way of deciding fairly who gets what of the common. THEN, IF YOU WANT TO, work on having a common purse. THEN, IF YOU WANT TO, base your reduction or elimination of outside money on what you've achieved, rather than the stance that "money is impure!" (which sounds weird when someone's paying your tax bill.)
