Practice Comes Before Property

So often, I feel like people are doing things in the wrong order. Get the land first! Put the vision in writing! Figure out who will own what! And whatever. You'll probably want to do the emotional work eventually.

A more cinematic (well, maybe not, actually) sequence would be: practice working together, work through a conflict, get clearer about your values and vision, decide who you want in the group, work out who will own what and how people can leave, then maybe think about land.

Likewise, with economics, work on it in stages. Don't try to takeoff straight for the economic goal. Only after you've proven you can reliably provide for everyone in the group, and equitably decide how to distribute the common, move to a common purse. Then, based on your new abilities, consider cutting back or eliminating outside money. It's hard to say "money is impure" while someone is paying the taxes.
