There are people who buy 40 acres before they’ve processed shit, and then just carry their drama over to a prettier backdrop… and some goats!

Pl/ork stuff can start anywhere. Peer counseling groups just require 2 people and a place to hang out. Reparenting, somatic practice, jealousy work, conflict processing, whatever else, can be done anywhere!

It’s possible that you’ll do all this stuff for two years a few days a week with a group and never find land. You will still have improved your capacity to process divorce, sickness, grieving, parenthood, loneliness, etc!

Maybe you live in a remote place or are tied to where you are… just do what you can! Cook a meal weekly with two other people. Have a counseling exchange w/ someone nearby or over the internet. Learn these tools, and you can practice them with people around you, even if you barter a little for time, space, childcare, transportation, food, sauna, etc. If you’re a little hippie, you can even trade for acro-yoga, dance improv, mothers’ circle, cuddle puddle time, etc etc.
