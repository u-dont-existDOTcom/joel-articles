Begin in small ways. Perhaps you can get together with two or three other people (please don’t call it a commune) and meet on a regular basis. Work on listening, switching off, having at least one disagreement (no more please!), and keeping your commitments.

You might find such people in unprogrammed Quaker meeting, a group interested in restorative practices, an open-hearted religious congregation, a relatively mature recovery group, a meditation group, or maybe you can find people who have recently completed a lengthy retreat and come back from it with a kind heart rather than a puffed up ego.

Have you visited an existing community? Many communities, including Twin Oaks, East Wind, and Acorn, as well as many other intentional communities and ecovillages welcome visitors (though you should check up on what’s current with each).

Visiting will give you some sense of how much togetherness you like and what kind of privacy you need, what kind of labor system you can tolerate, and whether you like the idea of communal showers.
