People still think they can take their unprocessed shit with them and just move it to a location with a better view (and goats!)

A lot of this work can start right where you are, today. Peer counseling requires 2 people and a way to not be disturbed. Reparenting, somatic work, jealousy work, conflict repair work, etc etc can all happen without the need to buy 40 acres.

Chances are the group of people you might be working with weekly for two years will never buy their land. That’s okay! This work can still be a game changer in terms of how you approach divorce, illness, grief, parenting, loneliness etc etc etc.

If you’re remote or unable to move from where you are right now, consider how you can enact some of this stuff now. Have a weekly meal with another household. Set up a peer counseling arrangement with someone (this can be done via video). Come up with some ways to share resources (tools, child care, transportation, food preparation, sauna access, etc). Find some other hippies and do acro-yoga, dance improv, mothers circle, cuddle puddle, whatever.
