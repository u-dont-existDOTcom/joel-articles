Check out unprogrammed Quaker meetings, restorative practice groups, open-hearted congegations, mature recovery groups, meditation groups, or people coming off long retreats who have emerged kinder.

Visit other communities. Twin Oaks, East Wind, Acorn, and many others are open to visitors, as are many of the new ecovillages being developed around the country. Check their requirements.

By visiting you will get a sense for how much togetherness you want and need, how you need to make privacy, what labor system would work for you, and whether or not you want communal showers.

Do these things before you start looking for land!

There is no application. I'm not selling a retreat. The manuals are free. Wherever people are taking care of each other, playing, feeling useful and honest, there is community.
