Ever been to a real, live community? If you haven't, you should! Twin Oaks, East Wind, Acorn, and a bunch of others are happy to have visitors, as are a lot of ecovillages. Some of them have requirements at this time, but that's okay...

You'll learn a ton from just visiting. Like how much togetherness you can take; what kind of privacy you might want; what kind of labor system might drive you bananas; whether showering with folks will be fun or make you want to find a studio apartment...

And you should do these things before you spend a lot of time or money trying to find a piece of land to make a community on.

Anyway, I'm not selling any retreat or asking you to apply for anything. All those manuals are free. I just really wanted you to know that you can create community wherever you go if you care, play, be useful, be honest, and more honestly.
