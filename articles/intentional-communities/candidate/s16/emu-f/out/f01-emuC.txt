There's a lot of inner pl/ork you can do wherever you happen to be. A peer counseling group needs two people and a place to talk. Reparenting, somatic work, jealousy work, working on how to do conflict well, etc etc. Doesn't need to wait until you have a deed in hand.

How many groups are meeting weekly for two years that never come to own land? What good can they do in the meantime?

If you're remote or can't move yet, how might you build out some of these features where you are? Eat a meal together weekly with two other households. Do peer counseling with a friend, in person or remote. Trade off some work together: tool library, child care, transportation, meal prep, sauna time together, etc. Get weird and hippie: acroyoga, dance improv, mothers circle, cuddle puddle. Etc etc.
