Have you ever visited an actual community? If you haven't, you really ought to! Many communities like Twin Oaks, East Wind, Acorn, etc. are quite used to having visitors. Also, a number of ecovillages are open to visitors at this point in their development.

What will you learn? So much! You'll learn how much togetherness you like. You'll learn about your need for privacy. You'll learn what kind of labor system drives you bananas. Maybe you'll love showering with people, or maybe you'll rent a studio the next day!

Do this, and a couple of the above, before you spend a lot of time and money looking for land.

Please understand that I am not trying to sell you a retreat, membership application, etc. The manuals are free! I am simply saying that, in my experience, these are the things that bring people together into community: caring, play, being useful, and being honest.
