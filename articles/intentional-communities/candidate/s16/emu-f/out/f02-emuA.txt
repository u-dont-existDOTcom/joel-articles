Have you ever been to an actual community? If you haven't, you ought to! Twin Oaks, East Wind, Acorn, and many others welcome visitors. Also, many of the ecovillages are open to visitors, although they may need to tighten up on that at certain times.

You'll learn a lot! You'll get a feel for how much togetherness you like, what kind of privacy arrangements you need, which labor systems drive you bananas, and whether communal showering makes you want to jump in with both feet or save up for a studio apartment.

Do these three things before you invest a lot of time or money in looking for land!

PS I'm not selling anything here. I'm not even asking you to come to a retreat or fill out an application. The manuals I mentioned are free. Community is like flowers, it grows where people cultivate caring, playfulness, usefulness, and honesty.
