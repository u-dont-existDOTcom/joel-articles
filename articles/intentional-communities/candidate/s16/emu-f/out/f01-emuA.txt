But a lot of your "inner pl/ork" can actually start right now where you are. The peer counseling piece, as you know, just requires 2 people and a place to sit down and talk. Reparenting work, somatic work, working on jealousy, working on conflict resolution, etc etc, can all start before you own a single square inch of dirt.

Your group can be practicing together for 2 years, and if you aren't lucky enough to be able to buy land, or if you choose not to, you can still have a tremendous impact on each other's lives in all kinds of ways.

If you are pretty stuck in a remote area, maybe start with just one piece. Commit to meeting for a meal with another household once a week. Commit to doing peer counseling (or skype counseling) with someone. Trade out some tools or child care hours, transportation, meal prep, sauna, etc. If you're a hippie like myself, maybe add in acroyoga, dance improv, mothers circle, cuddle puddle, etc.
