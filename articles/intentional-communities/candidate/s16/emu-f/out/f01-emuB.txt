Why not start the "inner pl/ork" anyway? Peer counseling takes two people and a private place to talk, that's all. Reparenting, somatic work, working on jealousy, working on conflict repair and a million other things can all be done without a deed to the land.

What if after two years of practicing together, the group isn't able to get a piece of land? What if you guys just keep practicing a few days a week forever? Would that be a waste of time? Could you still have a positive impact on how the members of the group experience divorce, illness, grieving, parenting, loneliness, etc.?

Maybe you can't move to the land, you live in a remote area or you have no way of leaving the place you're at. In that case, maybe you could "part-ork", focusing on the parts you can implement. Have a weekly meal with two other households. Trade counseling hours with a friend, either in person or via video chat. Trade other work, like tools, child care, transportation, food preparation, sauna time, etc. If you're a hippie dippy like myself, do acro-yoga, dance improv, mothers' circles, cuddle puddles, etc.
