## Practice Comes Before Property H1-P3

CONTEXT BEFORE: It would be better to get together to practice first, and process a real conflict, and get clear on your values, and test membership, and decide ownership and exit, and then, after all that, think about land. Less cinematic, I know! (It would make for a pretty bad movie.)

PUBLISHED:

The economic path should be staged the same way. A cluster of cooperating households can first take basic internal life out of purchase while outside earners contribute a defined share. It should move to a common purse only after it can provision members reliably and allocate the common fairly. Reducing or eliminating outside money should emerge from demonstrated capacity, not from declaring money impure while somebody else quietly pays the tax bill.

REWRITE:

Same with money: do it in stages. Don't try and fly right to the economic destination in one go! Wait until the group can reliably provide for everyone, and fairly decide who gets what from the common, before it goes to a common purse. And work on reducing (or eliminating) outside money based on what the group has shown it CAN do, rather than because "money is impure!" (That sounds kind of weird when somebody else is quietly paying the tax bill.)
