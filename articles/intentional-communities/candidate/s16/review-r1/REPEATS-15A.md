## 2. Contradictions

1. B337 (S, Lessons from the Black Tax) versus B81-B82 (The Four Parts That Have to Pl/ork Together, 4. The container), B338 (S) and B346 (Practice Comes Before Property). B337 says needing cash does not mean "going back to individuals working for wages and bringing them into the commune". B82: "all wages and other income earned by the members from work outside the community" (stage two). B338, the next block: "In the first stage, where some people work outside and contribute an agreed share". B346: "outside earners contribute a defined share". B337 reads as rejecting what stages one and two do. It may mean only the stage-three end state, but the text doesn't say so.

2. Low confidence. B381 and B386 (A) versus B358 (What to Do This Month). B381: "Books and Papers, in Reading Order". B386: "start here for sequencing, membership, money, land, and agreements". B358: "Start the inner pl/ork." Three different starting points, and B363 puts land last. Nothing reconciles them.
