# Repeats and contradictions involving "Practice Comes Before Property" (article 16, round 1)

Scope: only items where at least one instance sits in "# Practice Comes Before Property" (PCBP, B343-B368). Its opening is B343-B346; then "If You Can’t Move" (B347-B355) and "What to Do This Month" (B356-B368). Section tags: Intro = "The New Age May Dawn Suddenly"; Hot = "Communes Are Hot Again" (subsection "The Freeloader Problem"); Four Parts = "The Four Parts That Have to Pl/ork Together" (B78-B88 are its "The container" part); Calling = "After the Psychedelic Calling"; Zapatistas = "The Best Model I’ve Seen: Zapatistas"; Forming = "Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community" (including its subsections on land, visitors and members); Dissolution = "Dissolution and Exit"; Sénégal = "What This Looks Like From Sénégal".

## 1. Repeats

1. Communities exist to train people who build elsewhere. B7 (Intro): "some of them could eventually leave and set up another one somewhere else". B127 (Calling): "sometimes go off and start another experiment". B210 (Zapatistas): "teaching enough of the social and material system that some people can build somewhere else". B367 (PCBP, This Month): "Maybe that community will teach somebody who builds another." Later adds a little: no "one institution, one founder, or one official version" is needed. Otherwise the escuelita aim is told again, as a closing echo of B7. B7 and B210 carry it better (mechanism and failure case); B367 suits the ending.

2. Unprocessed pain travels with people. B20 (Intro): "People brought the usual human pain and suffering with them". B351 (PCBP, Can’t Move): "import every kilogram of their unprocessed material to a more scenic location". Later adds a concrete warning that moving does not fix it; the diagnosis is the same. Mild. B20 carries the claim; B351 is the sharper illustration and better placed for readers choosing to relocate.

3. The author is not recruiting but plans to buy land. B40 (Hot): "I am not currently recruiting anyone or founding a community" and "I do plan to buy land". B364 (PCBP, This Month): "I’m in Sénégal and expect to buy land, but I’m not announcing a commune." Later adds no application form, no retreat to sell, and the remark about where community gathers; "The manuals are free" also repeats the "free" of B358. The disclosure itself is told again. B40 is the better place: earlier, and it adds that the article’s warnings apply to him.

4. The staged money path. B81-B83 (Four Parts) against B346 (PCBP opening). B81: "they bring an agreed share of that income in". B82: "will be paid into the common purse of the community". B83: "Eventually, the community would also stop using money outside." B346: "outside earners contribute a defined share"; "move to a common purse only after it can provision members reliably"; "Reducing or eliminating outside money should emerge from demonstrated capacity". Later adds the readiness test for moving between stages and the warning against declaring money impure. Otherwise the three stages are told again. Its "somebody else quietly pays the tax bill" also echoes B337 (Sénégal): "it does mean someone has to handle it!" B81-B83 carry the stages (examples, Bruderhof, Twin Oaks); the readiness test is B346’s own.

5. Concentrated ownership as the core failure. B86 (Four Parts): "one person owns the land or has the final say about what will happen on it". B285 (Forming): "really a landlord with a philosophy". B344 (PCBP opening): "ownership concentrated because somebody had the money". Later adds only the cause. Mild; B86 and B285 carry the point, B344 is a passing clause in a list.

6. Inner work deferred until after land is invested. B88 (Four Parts): "usually after the group has invested land, labor, or other resources". B344 (PCBP opening): "emotional preparation deferred until everyone has more time". Later adds the whole wrong order (land, vision document, ownership, then feelings); the cost claim is the same. B344 sits in the more natural place, opening the sequencing section; B88 states the cost for any skipped part.

7. A small group is already community; land is not needed. B274 (Forming): "you are already a community". B353 (PCBP, Can’t Move): "already contains a real piece of community". Later adds that such a group may never own land yet still helps people through divorce, illness and grief. The reassurance is told again. B274 sits at the natural moment (a forming group unsure about land); B353 adds the lasting-value payoff.

8. Deal with a real conflict before committing resources. B275 (Forming): "been through at least one real conflict together and REALLY dealt with it". B298 (Forming, visitor to member; looser): "Do a trial run like that before anybody sells their house to move in." B345 (PCBP opening): "Process a real conflict." B359 (PCBP, This Month): "handle one disagreement, and keep commitments before discussing a shared future". Later adds listening and role-switching as the practice (B359) and the full order (B345); otherwise a reader is told it twice more. B275 carries it best: it says what "dealt with" means and sits where forming groups are addressed.

9. The Bruderhof shaped the author’s father; film and foundation. B312 (Dissolution): "My father carried the Bruderhof with him long after he left it." and "It shaped the foundation he started and the film he made." B366 (PCBP, This Month): "The Bruderhof shaped my father." and "He made a film, founded a research organization, and carried me through communal experiments." Later adds only "Decades later I’m publishing the film and writing this", which closes the loop to the opening. The first two sentences are the same facts again. B312 is more specific and fits its setting (relationships outlast institutions); B366’s last sentence is the only new content.

10. Land can wait; practice needs no land (all within PCBP). B345: "Then consider land." B348: "The most important pl/ork does not require land." B352: "do not wait for a deed". B357: "without the heroic land purchase". B363: "Do those three things before you spend serious time or money looking for land." B352 adds the specific practices and the two-person minimum; B363 adds the three actions; otherwise the bare claim comes four or five times in about twenty blocks. B348 with B352 carry it best, in the subsection that exists for it; B357 and B363 restate it as framing.

11. Experience teaches more than planning (within PCBP). B355: "will teach you more than another year of imagining the ideal village". B362: "Visits teach you what no manifesto can". The lessons differ (practice with households versus self-knowledge from visits), so mild, but the line has the same shape twice. B355 carries it better, tied to a concrete six-month practice.

## 2. Contradictions

1. What to call a small starting group. B273 heading and B274 (Forming): "Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community"; "you are already a community". B359 (PCBP, This Month): "Call it a practice group rather than a commune." Opposite advice with no reason given (B359 addresses people who have not yet met, but says so nowhere). B353 ("a real piece of community") sits between and softens one side without reconciling them.

2. Foundation versus first in time (possible, not certain). B57 and B87 (Four Parts): "the practical logistical foundation which is most of this article"; "this container is what gives those a stable place to manifest". B86 (Four Parts) adds that "all the peer counseling in the world" cannot make up for container failures. PCBP: B345 puts "Practice together." first and "Then consider land." last, and B352 says practice does "not wait for a deed". The article never says "foundation" means what practice rests on rather than what comes first.
