## If You Can’t Move H2-P2

CONTEXT BEFORE: I realize that for most people reading this there are a lot of reasons why they can't just head off and go build community on some land. Jobs, kids, pets, debt, a mortgage, a sick parent, or several of these arranged in a circle... That's fine! The most important pl/ork doesn't need land anyway.

PUBLISHED:

People sometimes buy forty acres and then import every kilogram of their unprocessed material to a more scenic location. The view improves. The conflict acquires goats.

REWRITE:

People sometimes buy forty acres and then bring all their unprocessed baggage with them to a more scenic location. The view's better, I'll give them that! But now the conflict has goats.

CONTEXT AFTER: But there's no reason you can't start the inner pl/ork where you live. And [peer counseling](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/p/hearthwork-an-integrated-peer-counseling)? It only needs two people and enough privacy to talk. Reparenting, somatic work, jealousy, conflict repair, etc. don't have to wait for a deed either!
