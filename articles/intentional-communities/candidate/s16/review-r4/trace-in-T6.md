## What to Do This Month H3-P3

CONTEXT BEFORE: Start working on the inner pl/ork. With what? Maybe the free [reparenting guide](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/p/inner-child-self-love-tips-and-guided) and [Hearthwork manual](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/p/hearthwork-an-integrated-peer-counseling), or another serious practice you already trust. And practice before you have an audience!

PUBLISHED:

Find two or three people. Call it a practice group rather than a commune. Meet regularly. Learn to listen, switch roles, handle one disagreement, and keep commitments before discussing a shared future.

REWRITE:

Find two or three people to form a practice group with (call it that, not a commune, at least for now). Meet regularly, and learn to listen, switch roles, handle one disagreement and keep your commitments before you start talking about a shared future. One disagreement is plenty to start with!

CONTEXT AFTER: Where do you find them? Maybe try unprogrammed Quaker meetings, restorative practice groups, open-hearted congregations, mature recovery groups, meditation groups, or people who came back from a long retreat kinder rather than more impressed with themselves.
