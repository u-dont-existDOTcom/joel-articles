## Practice Comes Before Property H1-P3

CONTEXT BEFORE: The better order is less cinematic, more like this: practice together, process a real conflict, clarify your values, test membership, decide ownership and exit, and only then think about land. I know, it wouldn't make much of a movie!

PUBLISHED:

The economic path should be staged the same way. A cluster of cooperating households can first take basic internal life out of purchase while outside earners contribute a defined share. It should move to a common purse only after it can provision members reliably and allocate the common fairly. Reducing or eliminating outside money should emerge from demonstrated capacity, not from declaring money impure while somebody else quietly pays the tax bill.

REWRITE:

Likewise, the economic side has to go in stages. You're probably not going to be able to fly straight to the economic destination! Only after the group has proven it can reliably provide for everybody, and fairly decide who gets what from the common, should it move to a common purse. And cutting back on outside money, or getting rid of it, comes from what the group has shown it can do. ("Money is impure!" is a weird thing to say while somebody else is quietly paying the tax bill.)
