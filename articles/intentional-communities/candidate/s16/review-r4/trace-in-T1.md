## Practice Comes Before Property H1-P1

PUBLISHED:

Most community projects begin in the wrong order: land first, vision in a document, ownership concentrated because somebody had the money, and emotional preparation deferred until everyone has more time.

REWRITE:

One of the main problems I see with how people go about building community is that they do the steps in the wrong order. Sound familiar? They get the land first, put the vision in a document, let ownership pile up with whoever had the money, and put off the emotional preparation until everyone has more time.

CONTEXT AFTER: The better order is less cinematic, more like this: practice together, process a real conflict, clarify your values, test membership, decide ownership and exit, and only then think about land. I know, it wouldn't make much of a movie!
