Start doing some inner pl/ork. Read the free reparenting guide and Hearthwork manual, or go with another serious practice you already trust. And seriously work at it before you have an audience!

Get together with two or three people and call it a practice group, not a commune. Meet regularly. Work on listening, and on switching roles. Keep the commitments you make to each other. And yes, get through at least one disagreement together (one is enough!). Talk about a shared future after all that.

Not sure where to find them? Try an unprogrammed Quaker meeting, or a restorative-practice group. An open-hearted congregation could work too, or a mature recovery group, or a meditation group. Or look for someone who came back from a long retreat kinder, rather than more impressed with themselves!
