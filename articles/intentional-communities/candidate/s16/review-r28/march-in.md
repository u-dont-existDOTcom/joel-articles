Start doing some inner pl/ork. Read the free reparenting guide and Hearthwork manual, or go with another serious practice you already trust. And practice it seriously before you have an audience. No rush on the audience part!

Get together with two or three people and call it a practice group, not a commune. Meet regularly. Work on listening, and on switching roles. Keep the commitments you make to each other. And get through at least one disagreement together. Just one? Yep, one is enough! Talk about a shared future after all that.
