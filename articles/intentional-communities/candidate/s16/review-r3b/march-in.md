One of the main problems I see with how people go about building community is that they do the steps in the wrong order. Sound familiar? They get the land first, put the vision in a document, let ownership pile up with whoever had the money, and put off the emotional preparation until everyone has more time.

A group that practices together weekly for two years may never own land. So what? It can still make a difference in how people get through divorce, illness, grief, parenting and plain old loneliness.
