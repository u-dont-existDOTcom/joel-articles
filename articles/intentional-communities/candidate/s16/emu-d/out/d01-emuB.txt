Practice Comes Before Property

The most obvious problem is that people are doing things in the wrong order. They buy land, and document their vision, and let the people who bring in the money own it all, and they don't spend time preparing emotionally at all.

It would be better to start practicing, and processing conflicts, and sorting out your values, and deciding who belongs, and who should own things, and what to do when someone wants to leave, and then, after that, maybe look at land. But that would make for a bad movie.

Another issue is doing things in stages on the money side. Don't try to fly right away to the place you imagine to be economically perfect. Once you are providing for everybody and have a good way of deciding how to take from the common, then maybe you can move to a common purse, and once you can provide for yourselves, then you can consider taking in less or no outside money. This is a better idea than trying to take in less money because money is impure (that sounds weird).
