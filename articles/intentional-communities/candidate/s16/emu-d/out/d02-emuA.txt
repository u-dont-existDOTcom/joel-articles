Have you ever visited an actual community? If not, you ought to! Many communities, including Twin Oaks, East Wind, Acorn, and many others welcome visitors, as do many ecovillages. Check their requirements, as they may vary from time to time.

What will you gain from such a visit? Well, for one thing, you will find out how much togetherness you like, how much privacy you really need, and which types of labor systems drive you nuts. Maybe you'll decide that you can never take communal showers again and need your own studio apartment.

Do these things before you start investing time and money looking for land.
