Have you gone to visit an actual community? If not, go. Twin Oaks, East Wind, Acorn, and many other communities, as well as many ecovillages, welcome visitors. (You may need to check with them to see what they require at this point in time.)

What you’ll learn by visiting is impossible to articulate in a manifesto. How much togetherness do you like? How much privacy do you need? Which labor systems would drive you nuts? Do you like communal showers, or does this make you want to rush out and buy a studio apartment?

Do these things before you begin to spend time and money looking for land.
