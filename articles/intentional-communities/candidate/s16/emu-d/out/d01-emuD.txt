Practice Comes Before Property

I often feel like people try and do community in the wrong order. "First let's get land!" "First let's write down a vision document!" "First let's let whoever is putting in the money own the community!" And so on. Most often what I see is that working on the emotional side of things doesn't get done until "later on when everyone has more time".

It seems to me that the order should be more like: practice, process a conflict, clarify values, decide who should/might be in the community, work out ownership, work out exit process, maybe think about land. (Unfortunately that would make for a pretty bad movie.)

Also, regarding money, it seems best to do it in a couple of steps. Don't try and fly right to "economic utopia" in one step. Wait until you can provide for everyone in the community, and figure out how to fairly distribute from the common, before you go to a common purse. And work on reducing (or eliminating) outside money based on what you CAN do rather than because "money is impure!" (that sounds kind of weird when you say it out loud.)
