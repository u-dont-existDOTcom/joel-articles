Practice Comes Before Property

Way too often, I see people do these steps out of order. They rush out and buy land, driven by a vision document. The people with the money end up owning the land. Emotional preparation comes last, because that'll have to wait until you have more time.

In my experience, the steps are better taken in this order: practice, process a conflict, clarify values, decide on membership, decide on ownership and how to get out, and then maybe get some land. I can see why this wouldn't make for a good movie, though!

On the money side, I think it's good to take things one step at a time, not trying to get directly to the economic utopia you envision. Once you can reliably provide for everybody, and decide fairly what each person should get from the common, then you might want to move toward a common purse. Once you're doing well, you can think about cutting back on your use of outside money - or eliminating it - based on what you're actually doing, rather than making it because you think money is impure or something weird like that.
