Have you actually visited a community yet? If not, you should go! Many communities (Twin Oaks, East Wind, Acorn, etc.) welcome visitors, as do many ecovillages. Check with them for up-to-date information on how to be a good visitor.

You will learn a lot about what you want and don’t want in terms of togetherness, privacy, labor systems, communal showers, and the cost of studio apartments (if you realize you need your own place after all).

Do these three things before you get too deeply into the process of looking for land.
