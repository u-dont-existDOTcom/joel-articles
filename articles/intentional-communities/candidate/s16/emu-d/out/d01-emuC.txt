Practice Comes Before Property

I think one of the problems is that you guys are doing things in the wrong order. Most people go get the land first, then write the vision down, and whoever had the money ends up owning the land.

But that's not the order it should be done! First practice it, work through the process, figure out your values, then figure out who needs to be in the group and who doesn't, figure out who should own it and how people can get out if they need to, and only then should you worry about getting the land. I admit, it doesn't make for a good movie.

Also, you need to move toward your economic goal in stages. Don't try to fly there as soon as you can! Once you're able to reliably provide for everybody, and decide fairly what parts of the common need to go to who, then you can move toward a common purse and start cutting outside money out, or eliminating it. And this should be because you can, not because you feel money is not pure or something weird like that.
