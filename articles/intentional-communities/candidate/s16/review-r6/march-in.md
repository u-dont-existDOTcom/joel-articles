But these days it can be soul destroying. People have a lot less of the communal life that held the obligation up (the shared land, work, childcare, food and daily belonging), but they still carry it. So if one of them makes it into the cash economy, it's really hard to become independent or start a stable life, because they may be expected to carry everybody. How can they get ahead, when all these people are dependent on them? And the relatives stay dependent too, since the payments don't give them a real alternative. It's the ancestral ethic playing out inside an individualistic cash economy. It can keep people alive, but it eats away at both sides too.

Maybe some of you will build something useful from this. Maybe that community will teach someone else, who'll build another. Passing it on doesn't need one institution, one founder or one official version.
