Go visit some real communities. Really, go! Twin Oaks, East Wind, Acorn and other intentional communities are used to having people visit them. Many ecovillages are too. Check their requirements first.

By visiting some communities, you'll get a better idea of how much togetherness you enjoy, what kind of privacy you need, what kind of labor system irritates you, whether communal showers feel liberating, or if you'll immediately begin looking at studio apartments for rent after the first communal shower you take. No manifesto can tell you that!

There is no application form you need to fill out. I'm not selling a retreat. The manuals are free. I've just noticed that community tends to gather wherever people find real care, play, usefulness and room to become more honest.
