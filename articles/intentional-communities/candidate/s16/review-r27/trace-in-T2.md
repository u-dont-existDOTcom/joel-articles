## If You Can’t Move H2-P4

CONTEXT BEFORE: So your inner pl/ork can start now, right where you live. [Peer counseling](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/p/hearthwork-an-integrated-peer-counseling) takes two people and a private place to sit and talk. That's it! And reparenting, somatic work, jealousy work, conflict repair, etc etc? None of it has to wait until somebody has a deed in hand.

PUBLISHED:

A weekly group that has practiced together for two years already contains a real piece of community. It may never own land. It can still change how people survive divorce, illness, grief, parenting, and ordinary loneliness.

REWRITE:

A group that practices together weekly for two years may never own land. So what? It can still make a difference when people are going through a divorce, an illness or grief. Parenting too, and plain old loneliness.

CONTEXT AFTER: Here's an idea if you're remote, or stuck where you are: build the part of it you actually can. Have a weekly meal with two households. Set up a counseling exchange with one friend, in person or by video. Share tools or childcare. Transport or food prep work too. Or a sauna! And if you're hippie enough, add acro-yoga, dance improv, a mothers' circle or a [cuddle puddle](http://cuddleparty.com/).
