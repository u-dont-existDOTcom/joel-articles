## H2-P2 → H2-P2x

1. "People sometimes buy forty acres and then" → same words. EQUIVALENT.
2. "import every kilogram of their unprocessed material to a more scenic location" → "bring all their unprocessed stuff with them to a more scenic location". EQUIVALENT. "Every kilogram" is the figure for "all", "import" for bringing it along; "unprocessed" and "more scenic location" are kept; "with them" only says who carries it.
3. "The view improves." → "It's a more beautiful view for sure,". EQUIVALENT. "It" = the more scenic location; nothing else in reach could be meant, and either reading gives the same claim. "More beautiful" is the improvement; "for sure" is the "yes, but" concession.
4. "The conflict acquires goats." → "but now you have the conflict PLUS goats." EQUIVALENT. The plain claim under the figure survives: the conflict comes along and the land adds goats. "But" states the contrast the two published sentences already carry. "You" is the generic buyer, as "People" was. "The conflict" is anchored exactly as in the published text.

ADDED: nothing that claims something new ("for sure", "PLUS", "but now" are emphasis and implied contrast).

Verdict: OK

## H2-P3 → H2-P3x

1. "Inner pl/ork can begin where you live." → "So your inner pl/ork can start now, right where you live." EQUIVALENT. "Your" is the reader's own, which "where you live" already addresses. "Now, right where" stresses not waiting for land, which the published last sentence also says. "So" marks this as the upshot of the goats paragraph, the relation the published order already carries.
2. "needs two people" → "takes two people". EQUIVALENT.
3. "enough privacy to speak" → "a private place to sit and talk". EQUIVALENT. Same minimum: somewhere private enough to talk. "Sit and talk" is the idiom for a conversation, not a posture requirement.
4. "Reparenting, somatic practice, jealousy processing, and conflict repair" → "reparenting, somatic work, jealousy work, conflict repair, etc etc?". EQUIVALENT. All four items are present; "practice" and "processing" → "work" is wording. "Etc etc" is the loose list marker, and the published first sentence already says inner pl/ork in general can begin at home.
5. "do not wait for a deed" → "None of it has to wait until somebody has a deed in hand." EQUIVALENT. "It" = the list just named (reparenting through conflict repair, etc etc), clear in place. "Has to wait" negated is "need not wait", the same independence from land that "do not wait" states. "Somebody has a deed in hand" is the same land-acquired moment as "a deed".

ADDED: nothing that claims something new. "That's it!" ("that" = two people and a private place to sit and talk) is the paragraph's pause; a reader of "needs two people and enough privacy" already takes those as the whole list.

Verdict: OK
