People sometimes buy forty acres and then import every kilogram of their unprocessed material to a more scenic location. The view improves. The conflict acquires goats.

People sometimes buy forty acres and then bring all their unprocessed stuff with them to a more scenic location. It's a more beautiful view for sure, but now you have the conflict PLUS goats.

So your inner pl/ork can start now, right where you live. And reparenting, somatic work, jealousy work, conflict repair, etc etc? None of it has to wait until somebody has a deed in hand.

CONTEXT AFTER: A group that practices together weekly for two years may never own land. So what? It can still make a difference when people are going through a divorce, an illness or grief. Parenting too, and plain old loneliness.
