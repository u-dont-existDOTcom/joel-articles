It’s possible to buy 40 acres and move all your unprocessed shit to a more beautiful location. And yes the view is better, but now you’re in conflict with goats.

It’s also possible to bring the pl/ork with you wherever you are. Peer counseling requires 2 people and a place to talk privately. Reparenting, somatic work, jealousy, conflict, etc etc can all be done without purchasing land.

A group that works on these things together every week for 2 years, may never get the land they wanted, but will have brought a level of support to their lives that changes the way they deal with divorce, illness, grief, parenting, loneliness and much more.
