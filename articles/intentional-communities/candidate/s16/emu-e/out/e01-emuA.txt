I've seen some people buy 40 acres and then try to bring all of their unprocessed stuff with them to the new, more scenary location. It is a more beautiful view for sure, but now you have this conflict PLUS goats.

Wherever you are, you can do some of the inner pl/ork from there. Peer counseling requires only two people and a place to talk privately. Reparenting, somatic work, working on jealousy, learning how to repair conflict, etc etc.

It's possible that in two years you'll still not own land even if you get a group together to practice together weekly, but you can make a huge difference in how you cope with divorce or illness or grief or parenting or loneliness.
