Where can you find some of these people? Are there any unprogrammed Quaker meetings nearby? How about groups practicing restorative practices? Maybe there's an open-hearted congregation nearby, or a mature recovery group, or a meditation group that you can check out. Or better yet, maybe you know someone who recently got back from a long retreat and seems a bit nicer, rather than just feeling good about themselves.

Have you ever been to a community? If not, you should go! Lots of communities, including Twin Oaks, East Wind, Acorn, and many ecovillages, are happy to have visitors (though you should always check current visitor requirements before going).

What do you have to gain by visiting a community? You'll find out how much you like being together with other people, how much privacy you need, what kind of labor system will drive you nuts, and whether communal showers would be great or whether you'd be better off renting a studio apartment.
