Where might you find some? Are there any unprogrammed Quaker meetings, restorative practice groups, open-hearted congregations, mature recovery groups, meditation groups, or people who have recently emerged from a long retreat without egotrip?

Have you ever visited an actual community? If not, go! Places like Twin Oaks, East Wind, Acorn, and many others welcome visitors. Many ecovillages are open to visitors, too. Check up on current requirements for each.

What will you learn from a visit? Among other things, you will learn how much togetherness you can stand; how much privacy you need; which labor systems drive you bananas; whether or not communal showers are okay with you (or whether you start calculating how quickly you can save up for a studio apartment).
