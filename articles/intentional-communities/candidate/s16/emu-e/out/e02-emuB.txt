Where can you meet some communitarians? Is there an unprogrammed Quaker meeting nearby? A group using restorative practices? An open-hearted congregation of any faith? A mature recovery group? A meditation group? Know somebody who has just returned from a long retreat and is even nicer to be around?

Ever been to a community? If the answer is no, you really ought to go! Twin Oaks, East Wind, the Acorn, and many other communities welcome visitors, as do many ecovillages. Check to see what you need to do to visit them now.

When you visit a community, you’ll get a feel for how much you enjoy being together with other people, how much privacy you need, which labor systems irritate you most, and whether or not communal showers are something you can deal with.
