People keep buying 40 acres to move all their unprocessed crap to. It is true that it looks better in the new location though! And now there’s goats in the mix!

In any case, the inner pl/ork can take place wherever you are! Peer counseling can start with just two people in a room, ideally alone with each other. Reparenting, somatic work, jealousy work, conflict and all the other blah blah can take place without any land at all!

It is likely that any given group practicing together for a couple of years a few times a week will not own a piece of land, and can still be invaluable in helping each other through divorces, illness, grief, parenting issues, loneliness, etc.
