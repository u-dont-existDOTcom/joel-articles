The vast majority of people reading this either live in a remote or constrained location, so just build as much of this as you can. Twice a week, meet for meals with two other households. Once a week, meet for counseling with one of those friends (either in person or over the internet, use video). Trade counseling services. Exchange tool libraries, child minding, transportation, food preparation, or whatever you can come up with. (Saunas, anybody? If you're hippie enough for this, how about acro-yoga, dance improv, mother's circles, cuddle puddles, whatever.)

Trust me, it's worth it. If you do this for just six months with three other households, you'll learn more than you would by daydreaming about building a village for years.
