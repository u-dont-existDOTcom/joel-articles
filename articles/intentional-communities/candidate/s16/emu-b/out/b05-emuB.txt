Where can you find them ? Maybe at unprogrammed Quaker meetings, groups involved in restorative practice, open-hearted religious congregations, mature recovery groups, meditation groups, or other people who've been on long retreats and came out sweet.

But mostly you should visit communities. Not just Twin Oaks, but East Wind or Acorn or any of a number of communities that are open to visitors. Lots of ecovillages take visitors, too. Have you visited a community? If not, find out what they need now to let visitors in.

You might get a very good idea of how much togetherness you want, how much privacy you need, what kind of labor system you could stand, and whether or not communal showers will bug you out.
