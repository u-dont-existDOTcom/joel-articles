Most people go about it backwards. They get the land first, then figure out their vision and write it down, then figure out who’s gonna own what (usually whomever has the money), and say they’ll do the emotional work stuff later when they have more time.

But a better order would be: practice, process, clarify values, figure out membership, figure out ownership/exit, figure out land. Yeah, I know it wouldn’t make for a great movie.
