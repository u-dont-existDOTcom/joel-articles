Where might you find them? Maybe among unprogrammed Quakers, people involved in restorative practices, open-hearted religious congregations, mature recovery groups, meditation groups or other people who have just returned from a long retreat and seem kind rather than self-important.

You might visit some existing communities. Communities like Twin Oaks, East Wind, Acorn and many others welcome visitors. Many of the ecovillages welcome visitors too. Have you visited a community yet? Check for current visitor requirements here .

When you visit, you will get a feel for how much togetherness you might like, how much privacy you might want, what kind of labor system might work for you, and whether communal showers might be ok.
