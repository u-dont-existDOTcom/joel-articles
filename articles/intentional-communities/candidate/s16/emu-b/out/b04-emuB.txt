Here's an idea for those of you who are in remote locations, or otherwise stuck for a while: pick a part of the village you want to create that you can actually build. Commit, with two other households, to eating together once a week. Commit to a counseling exchange with one other friend (in person or via video link). Swap out tools and child care. Arrange transportation and food prep together. Build a sauna. If you're hippie enough, try an acro-yoga class, dance improv, a mothers circle, or cuddle puddles.

Trust me, if you commit to doing this stuff with three households for six months, you'll learn a lot more than you would daydreaming.
