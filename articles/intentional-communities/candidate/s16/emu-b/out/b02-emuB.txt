I think the way most people try to go about community building is getting land, then thinking about what you want to do, then figuring out who owns it and how it's owned, and then when you have more time, working on all the emotional stuff.

The order I've been thinking about is: practice doing stuff together, learn to process conflict, get clear on values, figure out who you want in your community, decide who owns it and how people get in and out, and THEN think about land. It doesn't sound very cinematic, does it?
