But in my experience, the case for communal use is real too. Psychedelics can loosen the defenses that otherwise take over every serious conversation. And sitting through a difficult medicine night together can bond people powerfully. Then again, psychedelics can also leave people confused or grandiose. They can leave some people dependent, too. And then there’s the mess they can make between people. Is the community around them mature enough to tell the good from the bad?
