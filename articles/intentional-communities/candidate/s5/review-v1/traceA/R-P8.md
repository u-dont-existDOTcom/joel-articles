On the other hand, from my own experience, the communal aspect of using these medicines has real value. Psychedelics can be used to let go of the defenses that dominate every serious conversation, and can create powerful bonds between people who sit through difficult nights together. They can also be confusing, lead to grandiosity, create dependency, and lead to messy interactions with other people. The question is whether the community that’s supporting their use is mature enough to tell those apart.
