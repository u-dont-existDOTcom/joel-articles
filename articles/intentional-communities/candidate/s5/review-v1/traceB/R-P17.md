Yeah, I won’t idealize psychedelics. They’re not for everybody. There will be people that shouldn’t do them. There will be communities that shouldn’t be doing them. And with a lot of people hella stoked on psychedelics, there will be casualties wherever people get access before they’re competent to work with these powerful substances. If a community is going to dive into this it needs to do so in a humble way. Knowing that sometimes the answer will be no, or not now, or go work with so-and-so, or we messed that one up and someone got hurt.
