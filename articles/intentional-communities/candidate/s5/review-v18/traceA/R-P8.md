My own experience shows there are real reasons for the communal use of psychedelics, too. One being that they lower the defenses that otherwise dominate every serious conversation, and the other that they can build a strong bond between a group of people going through the harder nights together. But there are negative aspects that can come from taking psychedelics, among them confusion and grandiosity. They can also bring dependency, and they can foul up the dynamics between people. The question is, are you mature enough as a community to pick through the good and the bad?
