Legal issues differ greatly, and are sometimes not well defined, so a community should write down where it stands: what’s allowed and what’s prohibited. It should write down which activities are private (to the individual, to a group in the community, or to the community vs the outside). And it should note which activities have had a legal review. “I don’t think anybody around here has ever heard of that” is information, but it’s not the same as a legal opinion.
