# Stance check: section 5 rewrite (v18)

I checked every sentence in the rewrite that states a position, wish, rule, assessment or claim about how things work against the whole published essay. Five of them change what the essay says.

## 1. Hedge dropped on what psychedelics do
- **Rewrite** (opening, "My own experience shows…" paragraph): "One being that they lower the defenses that otherwise dominate every serious conversation, and the other that they can build a strong bond…"
- **Essay** (section 5, "The case for communal use…" paragraph): "Psychedelics can loosen defenses that otherwise dominate every serious conversation. They can create powerful bonds when people sit through difficult nights together."
- **Reader would wrongly believe:** Joel says psychedelics do lower defenses, where the essay says only that they can, and that his case for communal use rests on exactly two effects ("the other").

## 2. Joel's age changed
- **Rewrite** ("Community Is What Makes Psychedelics Pl/ork Best!"): "After my original ayahuasca ego deaths at 25, I had trouble finding anyone…"
- **Essay** (section 5, same subsection): "After my original ayahuasca ego deaths at 26, I had trouble finding anyone…"
- **Reader would wrongly believe:** Joel's first ayahuasca ego deaths happened at 25 rather than 26.

## 3. Insight and nonsense softened from identical to often similar, and only in the moment
- **Rewrite** (same subsection): "Psychedelics can create nonsense as well as genuine insight, and on the spot the two often feel very similar."
- **Essay** (section 5, same subsection): "Psychedelics produce genuine insight and convincing nonsense with identical lighting."
- **Reader would wrongly believe:** the two are only sometimes alike, and only during the experience, so a person could usually tell them apart alone afterward. That weakens the essay's reason for having friends who know your history check the downloads.

## 4. A check on the informal shaman turned into public accountability for every member
- **Rewrite** ("My Ceremony Principles"): "Things that might help would be to have rotation of roles, and to make everyone publicly accountable for what they do."
- **Essay** (section 5, "My Ceremony Principles"): "Rotating roles and public accountability may help." The target there is the person "everybody consults, trusts, and gradually stops questioning."
- **Essay** ("Founderism: The Four Questions", subsection "How My Own Model Could Become a Cult"): "Peer counseling can become surveillance delivered in a warm tone." and "A group begins by inviting honesty and ends by treating privacy as resistance."
- **Reader would wrongly believe:** Joel's remedy is to make every member publicly answerable for everything they do, as a rule for the whole group. The essay aims public accountability at whoever gathers power (the Founderism warning signs include "public accountability shrinks") and warns that group scrutiny of members slides into surveillance.

## 5. New claim that medicine's whole effect is "essentially a decree"
- **Rewrite** ("My Ceremony Principles"): "But you can't, by decree (and what the medicine does is essentially a decree), grant someone months of honest relationship they haven't had."
- **Essay** (section 5, "My Ceremony Principles"): "Medicine can deepen a practice that exists. It cannot create months of honest relationship by pharmacological decree."
- **Reader would wrongly believe:** Joel thinks everything medicine does amounts to an imposed decree. The essay uses "decree" only for what medicine cannot do, and it credits medicine with deepening a practice that already exists and with producing genuine insight.

Conflicts found: 5
