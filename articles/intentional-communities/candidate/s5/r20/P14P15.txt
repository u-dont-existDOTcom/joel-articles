Community also creates a rhythm. A lot of traditional medicine systems hold ceremonies on communal timing rather than individual appetite, and a shared calendar like that can help cut down on compulsive repetition. It can leave more time for integrating, too.

Logistics get easier in a community. Having the group arrange childcare it can trust. Not having to drive away from the ceremony. Having people that can bring water, or sit quietly with somebody as they come back to normal consciousness.
