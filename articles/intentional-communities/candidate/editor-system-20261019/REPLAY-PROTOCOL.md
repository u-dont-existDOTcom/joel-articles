## Work sequence

1. Joel reviews the draft rules and the genuinely disputed compression/classification choices. Freeze the revised protocol and eligible decision set before replay output is inspected. Do not infer approval from the interruption or the word “continue.”
2. Run a fresh writer on A and the frozen generic rules. Verify the actual provider/model identifier, reasoning setting and input access rather than relying on remembered product names. Require a complete article candidate and exact source→destination operations. Freeze the first completed output before any target-guided repair.
3. A separate source-fidelity reader receives A and the candidate, **not C or detector scores**. It checks unsupported additions, lost independent meaning, broken dependencies, actual remaining repetition, voice, links and native objects. Its findings remain diagnostic evidence, not edit authority.
4. Only after the candidate and source review are frozen may a comparison reader see C and the eligible case catalogue. Score useful editorial operations, not word overlap or imitation of headings. Record alternative solutions that meet the same reader need.
5. Joel resolves disputed choices and overall usefulness. Only an editorially eligible candidate enters any requested detector check. A score on B never transfers to the new candidate.

For a stronger causal claim that **the rules** improved performance, run fresh old- and new-method conditions with the same source, permissions, model, reasoning setting and output allowance. Label a new-method-versus-historical-B comparison as a demonstration, not a controlled causal comparison. More runs may clarify stability; no repeated-run count is imposed as a prerequisite to a useful first demonstration.

## Measuring work saved

Define non-overlapping decision units before scoring. The 22 teaching cases overlap and mix movement, wording and new material; they are not automatically a denominator of 22 equally weighted edits. Split mixed cases, remove duplicate scoring credit and explicitly exclude new owner facts, recollections, beliefs and plans.

Report the number of eligible editorial decisions actually implemented, partially implemented, suggested-only and missed. Report harmful cuts, unsupported additions, residual structural choices, connective repairs and review overhead separately. A novel but better arrangement may receive credit after owner judgment.

Gross cuts, additions, moves, unchanged prose and net length are descriptive only. Never turn a word count or normalized-block match into a percentage of labour saved. Without a timed owner review/repair task, report **remaining edit burden**, not minutes or hours. A candidate that deletes more but creates repair work may save less.

## Independence and generalization

Fresh input isolation can withhold C from the writer. It cannot make this a held-out test: the new rules were learned partly from A→C. Call it a **development-set replay**. It can show that the frozen procedure reproduces useful operations on its teaching article. It cannot prove the procedure would have been discovered before the teaching example or will generalize to unseen articles.

To test generalization, freeze the method and use a different article that did not influence rule selection, with the owner target withheld until output is frozen. Do not keep revising the rules until the same target is matched and call that independent validation.
