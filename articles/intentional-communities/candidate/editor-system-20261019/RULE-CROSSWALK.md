## Changes deliberately not made

No new phrase blacklist. No quota for cuts, headings or paragraph length. No mandatory problem→solution essay template. No instruction to preserve every source sentence. No generic permission to delete independent claims or the owner’s distinctive language. No detector score treated as proof of editorial quality. No master promotion or publication permission.

## Wiring proposal

One shared file: `docs/EDITORIAL-DEDUP-EXECUTION.md`. Link it from the structural-edit entry in `project-sources/TASK-MODES.md`, the structural-edit portion of `CANONICAL-REPO-MAP.md`, and `docs/INDEX.md`; cross-reference it from `docs/MULTISCALE-EDITORIAL-LEDGER.md`. These are pointers to one workflow, not duplicate rule copies. Retain the proposed status until owner review and behavioural evaluation.

The task-local evidence remains in `articles/intentional-communities/candidate/editor-system-20261019/`, separate from the generic workflow. A future source-only writer must not receive that case-study folder.

## Enforcement limits

Path and hash tests can prove the workflow is reachable and that case excerpts match their sources. They cannot prove that a model performed sound editorial judgment. The reader-path and remaining-repetition judgments must be checked on the actual candidate. The later replay and owner scoring are the behavioural test, not the existence of this document.
