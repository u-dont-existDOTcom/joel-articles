## The strongest lessons

### 1. Consolidate the entire recurring task, not just the two nearest sections

A presents beginning without land, practicing together, testing conflict and then considering property in three places: **Your Little Forming Group Already Is the Community**, **If You Can’t Move**, and **What to Do This Month**. B combines only the last two. C incorporates the useful conflict detail and practical options into one earlier **Practicing Community Can Start Now** section. Exact cases E07–E09 show the source and destination passages.

Collect every passage serving that reader task before drafting a merger. A paragraph may contribute one needed qualification without earning preservation of its whole wrapper. The no-land examples and serious conflict test can become a progression instead of parallel introductions of the same advice.

### 2. Place the positive answer after the problem when the source supports it

C gathers freedom, sacred-practice and living-agreement material after the failure diagnosis, under **We Want Freedom Inside & Out!** Most of that text already existed in A’s following model section. The editor did not need new autobiography to make this move. C043 was already in A’s spiritual-failure subsection (A046); C042 and C044–C047 draw on A051–A056 in the model section. The exact inner/outer reframing, “compatible” and “all the manuals I’ve seen” changes include owner development and must be scored separately. See E01–E03.

### 3. Bring the strongest whole example forward

The Zapatistas move from major section 9 to 5. This lets readers see the author’s closest whole-model example before much of the detailed design. The visit, governance account and limits were already in A. A source-only editor could have proposed the move. This is a sequencing judgment, not proof that every essay should begin with its best anecdote. See E04–E06.

### 4. A merger can select, combine and relocate; it need not preserve each old inventory

The federation’s two paragraphs of examples and obligations become one passage about bounded solidarity and tracking different offers. The resource discussion loses additional overlapping inventories. These changes reduce repeated enumeration; the claim about what the author intended is not needed to describe that effect.

There is a boundary: the removed text also contains explicit procedures. Cash reserves, critical-skill cover, grants versus loans and guarantees, resource visibility and conflicts of interest are not all literal synonyms of the surviving wording. Their disappearance is an owner editorial choice, not a new universal permission to delete independent meaning. A replay can receive credit for concise integration or a well-specified proposed omission rather than being required to imitate every deletion. See E11–E14.

### 5. Sequence within sections as carefully as between them

C explains federation before its splitting-off subsection and moves **Integration Starts Well Before Ceremony** ahead of the communal-medicine benefits. These moves were available from A alone. They change what the reader understands before the next promise or decision, rather than merely changing the table of contents. See E13 and E18.

### 6. Preserve the voice that survives selection

The goats joke, free-resource metaphor, personal communal memories and much of the spontaneous wording remain. The A→C result does not make every sentence shorter. It combines useful passages and removes explanatory wrappers. This supports structural ambition with verbal restraint—not an imposed generic lean-writing style.

Those sequencing and compression choices remain editorial judgments, not uniquely mandatory solutions. The 22 curated cases expose source/destination blocks and limits. They overlap by design; they are not 22 independent opportunities that may be added into a percentage without further scoring design.

## Do not learn the wrong lesson from owner additions

An editor could reuse the old visit narrative, reorganize the existing governance account, consolidate advice and improve headings. It could not truthfully reconstruct the new account of what hosts said about Mayan grammar, the new parenthetical claim about “all the manuals I’ve seen” on building secular communes (C036), or the change from planning to **buy land** to planning to **find land**. The illegal-entity/occupation option changes the available choices; it is not just a terser formulation. Founding Fathers and Pharaoh comparisons are new rhetoric, not recoverable edits that a benchmark should demand.

Mixed changes must be divided at the meaning boundary. The practice section combines old material with new phase, motivation and renegotiation wording. Classifying the whole section as new would undercount recoverable editing; treating all of it as recoverable would overclaim.

## What changes in the system

The shared draft adds concrete execution outputs: whole overlap clusters before cuts; a source-supported alternative route where needed; an integrated treatment of the union; repaired old and new locations; an explicit remaining-repetition check; and provenance-aware evaluation. It composes the existing rules instead of creating a separate Claude-only policy.

Code locates exact passages, computes counts and checks hashes. Editorial judgment decides whether functions overlap. The owner decides irreducible meaning, taste and value choices. Neither similarity scores nor generated checkboxes decide meaning.
