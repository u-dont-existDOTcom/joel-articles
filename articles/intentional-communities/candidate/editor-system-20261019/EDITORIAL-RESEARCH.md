# Editorial-method research and adaptation

Research recovered from the interrupted 2026-10-19 pass; the seven final source pages were reopened during recovery. These are professional standards and teaching/craft sources, not experimental evidence that the proposed AI workflow works. The article itself was not fact-checked in this pass. Only the task-relevant portions below inform the proposal.

| Source | What it supports | Adaptation here | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Editors Canada: Structural Editing, B1–B3](https://editors.ca/publications/professional-editorial-standards/structural-editing/) | Organization and content must suit the reader, purpose and medium; sequence, headings, repetition, visuals and ancillary text are structural decisions. | Diagnose at article scale, and group related information before polishing language. | Does not prescribe Joel’s order or authorize changing his position. |
| [Editors Canada: Stylistic Editing, C1–C3](https://editors.ca/publications/professional-editorial-standards/stylistic-editing/) | Improve clarity and paragraph/sentence flow while changing only what is needed and respecting the author’s intended voice. | Use the smallest wording change that completes the selected structural repair. | Concision is not a word-count target. |
| [Harvard College Writing Center: Tips for Organizing Your Essay](https://writingcenter.fas.harvard.edu/tips-organizing-your-essay) | Reverse-outline actual paragraph points; examine order, repetition, gaps and ideas near the end that belong earlier. | Make the inherited structure inspectable before preserving it by inertia. | No compulsory school-essay or one-claim-per-paragraph template is imported. |
| [UW–Madison Writing Center: Creating a Reverse Outline](https://writing.wisc.edu/handbook/processandstructure/reverseoutlines/) | Record a paragraph’s subject and function, then review logical order and overlapping points. | Gather separated treatments into overlap clusters before deciding cuts. | Outline labels remain diagnostics, not quotas for prose. |
| [UNC Writing Center: Revising Drafts](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/revising-drafts/) | Revising can change focus, organization and development; it is different from proofreading. | Separate developmental judgment from literal-preservation implementation. | The owner’s edit permission still controls claim changes. |
| [CIEP: Editorial and Publishing Glossary](https://www.ciep.uk/resource/editorial-glossary.html) | Distinguishes developmental editing from later editorial stages. | Name the actual requested service instead of equating dedup with sentence trimming. | A definition alone is not a complete method. |
| [John McPhee: Structure, The New Yorker](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2013/01/14/structure) | Nonfiction structure can develop from the available material and sustain attention. | Compare a concrete source-supported alternative to inherited order. | A practitioner’s craft account, not a validated universal formula. |

## Reuse decision

**COMPOSE/ADAPT.** The repository already covers much of this. The useful delta is an executable order of work: inspect the whole overlap cluster, integrate its unique contributions, test the reader’s route, and judge sufficiency separately from preservation. Do not put this methods research into the byline article or claim the draft is a proven AI improvement.

## Source-check notes

Editors Canada’s stylistic standard C1 explicitly limits change to what is required; its structural standard B1.1 permits a new organization where appropriate. These are compatible—not an instruction to freeze the old organization. Harvard’s reverse-outline questions explicitly consider late material that would work earlier. UW–Madison’s subject/function method distinguishes topic from what the paragraph does. Our workflow adapts those principles to a wording-preserving dedup task and adds an owner-provenance boundary that is specific to this project.
