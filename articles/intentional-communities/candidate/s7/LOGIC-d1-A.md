# Logic audit d1-A: “Jealousy Can Capture Monogamy or Free Love”

Published P1–P8 against the rewrite, paragraph by paragraph; rows list only relations whose wording differs. No "or" became "and/also": every "or" keeps its scope (jealousy or breakup; panic … or the wish to control; punishment or policy). The shifts are in agency, modality scope, cause, quantifiers and "rather than".

## P1

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agency | "will be reorganized by attraction and pair-bonding" | "will eventually reorganize itself due to attraction and pair-bonding" | AMBIGUOUS: (a) the forces reshape the community, as published; (b) the community acts, deliberately restructuring itself in response |
| Time | "will be reorganized" | "will eventually reorganize" | AMBIGUOUS: (a) sooner or later, as published; (b) only late, after a stable period; the original sets no delay, and its example happens "the next morning" |
| Cause | "becomes the community’s problem because it changes" | "becomes a problem for the entire community, because it will change" | SAME |
| Condition | "In a city … In a thirty-person commune" | "When two exes live in the same city … When they're part of a thirty-person commune" | SAME |

## P2

## P3

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agent | "Rigid monogamy can turn fear and possession into moral law" / "Free love … can distribute the same fear" | "If people are doing rigid monogamy, they can turn fear and possession into a moral imperative" / "they can just end up with a larger spreadsheet" | CHANGED: the practitioners, not the form, now do the converting; a reader takes it as something individuals do (with "they can", possibly by choice), not what the form does to fear; "moral law" (a shared rule) becomes "a moral imperative", which can be private |
| Comparison | "the same fear" | "their fear" | AMBIGUOUS: (a) the fear just named; (b) free-lovers' own, different fear; "same" tied both systems to one fear, the paragraph's claim |
| Condition | "without inner pl/ork" | "but aren't doing the inner pl/ork" | SAME |
| Negation scope | "doesn’t release …, or the wish" | "doesn't release …, or stop people from …, or wanting" | SAME (none of them) |
| Acted on | "doesn’t release … comparison, abandonment terror, or the wish to control" | "doesn't … stop people from comparing themselves, feeling terror at the prospect of abandonment, or wanting to control" | CHANGED (minor): three items become behaviours people would be stopped from, not states to be released; the implied remedy becomes restraining people rather than letting the states go, which is P7's remedy |
| Set | "the wish to control another person" | "wanting to control other people" | AMBIGUOUS: (a) each wanting to control another (a partner), as published; (b) a general wish to control people |

## P6

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contrast | "aren’t commandments for everybody, but" | "are not a set of commandments that everyone must follow. But" | SAME |
| Evidence | "they demonstrate that a group can acknowledge erotic life" | "they are an example of how a community can acknowledge the erotic aspects of life" | AMBIGUOUS: (a) proof it can be done, as published; (b) a model of how to do it, a method to copy; the original claims only possibility |
| Alternative, agent | "a group can acknowledge erotic life rather than pretending twenty adults have become neutral colleagues" | "It is not necessary for 20 adults to pretend that they've become neutral colleagues" | AMBIGUOUS: (a) pretending is pointless and acknowledgment replaces it, as published; (b) pretending is merely not required, still open to any adult who prefers it, and now the author's claim rather than what the example shows; the pretender also moves from the group to the adults |
| Negation + because | "pretending … because the bylaws say so" | "not necessary … to pretend … just because the by-laws say so" | AMBIGUOUS: (a) no need to pretend the bylaws made them neutral, as published; (b) the bylaws do require the pretense, but that alone doesn't bind the adults, i.e. they may disregard the bylaws; the original says nothing about the bylaws' authority |

## P7

| Relation | Published | Rewrite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Modality, agent | "jealousy cannot become the hidden governor" | "jealousy not be allowed to become the hidden governor" | AMBIGUOUS: (a) the same requirement; (b) someone (the group) must disallow it, an enforcer the original doesn't name |
| Added negation | (absent) | "This doesn't mean it can't come up." | SAME (spells out what "speakable" implies) |
| Modality scope | "It has to be speakable, felt in the body" | "It needs to be able to be talked about and felt in the body." | AMBIGUOUS: (a) needs to be [able to be talked about] and [felt in the body], as published; (b) needs to be able to be [talked about and felt]: bodily feeling becomes a permission, where the original requires it ("speakable" is its only capacity) |
| Alternative, agency | "gradually released rather than converted into punishment or policy" | "let go of gradually, and not used to punish, or make rules" | CHANGED: "rather than" named the other fate of unreleased jealousy; "and not used to" is a separate ban on deliberate use. A reader would think only intentional weaponizing is excluded; the original also excludes jealousy turning into punishment or policy with no one deciding to use it |
| Set | "policy" | "make rules" | AMBIGUOUS: (a) group policy, as published; (b) any rules, couples' own agreements included, which would forbid jealousy-prompted agreements P4 allows |
| Negation over "or" | "punishment or policy" | "to punish, or make rules" | SAME (neither) |
