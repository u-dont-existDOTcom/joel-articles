Inner-child reparenting means learning to identify as the best holistic inner adult, which is comprised of the three inner adult roles: Nurturer, Protector, and Guide. The newly developed adult reparents their newly recognized inner child. The child receives all that love, protection, and guidance that you give it, and as a result, your little one gains more freedom to remain alive inside you, which brings back the joy of life that many adults have forgotten about.
And this reparenting journey is built on somatic regulation first. I.e., listening to the body's feelings before making a decision with the mind. And that actually symbolizes the adult/child relationship to me in some way as well.
