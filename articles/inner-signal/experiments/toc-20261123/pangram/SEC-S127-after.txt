Put your hand over your heart and your other hand on your solar plexus (the two-hand practice in Inner Child Practices), and say to yourself in a loving way, “Little one, you are not alone. You don’t have to fix this problem. I’m here now to listen to you.” Make sure you're actually talking to your inner child, not just repeating a script. If you can't genuinely say all that and feel it, then just rest your hands there and notice what you find.
