For information about borrowed adulthood, love that doesn't require trust, practical and relational repair, spiritual struggle, differentiation and mutual peer support, see my Inner Child Self-Love Reparenting Guide. The practices in this post are designed to match with those in the guide, but include some elements of learning to get into self hypnosis, as well as other helpful language, and making sure you come back at the end, etc.

Some of the information in this post comes from Healing the Divided Self, Phillips and Frederick, 1995, pp 148-153, 171-174, about renurturing work, and how it can be combined with somatic work, and how to develop capacities for nurturing, etc. For information about attunement, and how every child is different and needs to be responded to differently, see Trauma Through a Child's Eyes, Levine and Maggie Kline, especially ch 4, 5, 10, etc, as it talks about actual kids (and this guide is applying this to how to respond to younger feeling states within an adult). The sequence for doing this reparenting therapy is not from these authors, however.
