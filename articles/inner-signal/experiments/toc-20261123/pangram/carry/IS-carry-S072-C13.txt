If you can tell there is a guard in the way, talk to it. You can say to the guard “You don’t have to let me in, just tell me what I need to do to make it safe”. If you don’t hear a little kid talking in your head, don’t invent one. Even if there isn’t a child part that you can talk to, you can work with whatever you are aware of. You can work on your preferences or something that you’re curious about or something that you’re willing to try as a beginner etc. and see how it goes. Work on developing a sense of who you are and reparent yourself as best you can.
