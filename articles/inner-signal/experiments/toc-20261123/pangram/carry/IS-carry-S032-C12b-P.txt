A. Prepare yourself to tend to them with some privacy, but still able to access support if needed. Check how much grown-up you is really in the room first. Can you take a moment before you react? When you decide something, do you follow through? If you answered "No," because right now you’re mostly still stuck identifying as the frightened little boo, hold off on the deeper conversation. Do what makes you feel adult before you try to talk to little you. If being alone was a big part of what hurt during your childhood, I’d call somebody now, mess and all, since reaching toward people is part of taking care of your little one too. Even if you don’t have someone you know who wants to listen, there are warmlines for this (not hotlines unless it’s an emergency).
