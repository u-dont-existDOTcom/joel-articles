Sometimes the mind is too activated for meditation to help, so you may need to work directly with the body to calm it down. Maybe get a massage, or massage a foot, your scalp, a temple, or the back of the neck, the top of a shoulder, etc. (Avoid massaging the front of the neck. Also avoid massaging injured or numb body parts, or bruised, inflamed, or recently surgically operated on areas, or varicose veins. Also stop if the massage makes the anxiety symptoms worse, or makes you feel bad in any way, such as painful, dizzy, tingly, etc.) Some massage therapies are super aggressive, like Rolfing, some are fairly aggressive still like Deep Tissue, some are medium-aggressive, like Thai massage, and some, like Swedish Massage, are quite gentle. Choose the level that feels right for you. You’ve got lots of other options for the body too, like heat, pressure, a weighted blanket, darkness, your favorite calming music, or a massage gun if you’re careful with it, etc. Use whatever actually helps you, and don’t go surrounding the anxiety with a heap of wellness stuff.
