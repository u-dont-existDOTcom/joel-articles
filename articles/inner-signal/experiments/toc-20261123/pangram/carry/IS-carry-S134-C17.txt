We cannot force forgiveness on ourselves. It has to come from a place of understanding, otherwise it can be bypassing. And we don’t have to forgive first to get safe, set boundaries, hold someone accountable, get justice or get better. However, if we remain in the anger forever, we are allowing the injury to dictate how we live our lives in the present. So how do we hold all of this? We just remember that it can all be true at once. They really did hurt us and they still have to answer for it. Our boundary with them is crucial to maintain, especially after forgiveness which can tend to weaken it. One day we may find that we have forgiven them for not knowing better, just like Jesus did on the Cross.

You might say to the part of you that got hurt, “I’m not going to use spiritual mumbo jumbo to shush your cries and make you forgive before you're ready. But I’m also not going to build our whole life around this wound forever.”
