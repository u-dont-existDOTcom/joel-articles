There is a simple and easy body technique to connect with your inner child. Just place one hand in the center of your chest (holding rose quartz if you have it), which physically connects you to your love, and then place your other hand just below that, on your solar plexus, where your inner child can be.

Imagine a divine Love from above, with the inner child below, in the solar plexus area. The inner adult is present. You can also use the hand on the solar plexus to massage that area.

Gently breathe in lots of love and begin to send that love from your heart down to your inner child as you breathe out, noticing what comes up for you. If some comes back up, let it, and send it on down again, so it goes around like a loop. That part's a gift from them, and they don't owe it to you, so if nothing comes back, just keep sending yours. If you feel resistance, listen to it and be patient. If you feel or see something, just meet that with love as well. If you can't find any warmth today, just leave your hands where they are and notice what's there. Don't pretend to feel something you don't. Let it go back and forth between your heart and your inner child, like a real conversation.
