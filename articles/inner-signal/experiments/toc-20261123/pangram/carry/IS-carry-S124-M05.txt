In somatic experiencing (SE), you are not digging into the trauma. You learn to track. So you might start by tracking, by discovering where the activation is, where a neutral place is, and then whether you can move your attention from one to the other, very slowly, like a snail. SE’s founder, Peter Levine, uses the concepts of titration (going from the shallow end of the pool slowly deeper), pendulation (going in and out of the deep end to learn safety there), and orientation (coming out of the pool, drying off, noticing you’re safe).
