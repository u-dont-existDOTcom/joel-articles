There are many cues you can connect to a state. Put your hand on your heart, touch two fingers together, use a breath, a word, a particular body posture, smell, song, whatever. Feel a state you have really been in, one you want to be able to get back to easily when you need it. Do your anchor thing and say to yourself that this is available to you. To get yourself back into a good hypnosis state easily, don’t go for a peak emotional state but for a usable, receptive one. See "A Shortcut Back In" for a quick way back. Do make sure this is different from both your alert and calm cue and your synchronicity wake up cue.
