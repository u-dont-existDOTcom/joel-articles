Try breathing less. (There are some good things to be said for slowing your breathing down, if you are breathing too much, or panicked.) By simply pursing your lips together on exhale, you can slow down your exhale. Slower exhales, combined with not breathing more on inhales, lead to overall reduction in breathing. That slight oxygen hunger feels a bit anxiogenic for the first 20 seconds or so, but after that, you’ll feel much calmer. Can you make your breathing very quiet? Try the continuous breathing from Systema (a Russian martial art) too: it helps not just with relaxing, but with getting into trance and flow states more deeply. You can learn it from the best video on it I've seen so far, and my somatic guide explains it more. If you feel bad, dizzy, or panicky, or have medical issues, stop.
