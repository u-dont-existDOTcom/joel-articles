# What renaming can't fix (Fable's notes on the Self-Hypnosis guide's contents)

Nothing here is done. Each item is for Joel to decide. The headings named are his; my proposed names are in `PROPOSAL-FABLE.json`.

1. **S002 "The table of contents is hiding on the left" is a note to the reader, not a section.** It is 31 words, and a reader who is looking at the contents has already found it. Suggest: make it a plain sentence at the end of S001 ("How to use this very large guide") and drop the heading.

2. **S090 "Appendices" is a note too, and sits beside the appendices instead of above them.** It is an h1 like Appendix A to K, so the sidebar shows "Appendices" and then "Appendix A" on the next line. Suggest: drop the heading and keep its 31 words as text before Appendix A (or as the last lines of "Closing"). Making the appendices h2 under it wouldn't work: the two h4s under the synchronicity cue (S110, S111) would become h5 and leave the sidebar.

3. **S018's heading promises the guards, and its text has none.** "The inner child, the guards, and the inner adult's three jobs" holds only the three jobs; the guards come ten headings later (S028). I named it for the three jobs. Suggest: accept that, or add a sentence on the guards to S018.

4. **S023 "Working with your inner child" has no text of its own and holds things that aren't the inner child.** Under it are the three jobs again (S025 to S027, which S018 introduced), the guards (S028), the inherited parent script (S029), the Protector drill (S030) and S031 "Inner child reparenting", whose name repeats the parent's. Suggest: move S025 to S027 and S030 under S018 (the three jobs), give the guards and the parent script (S028, S029) an h1 of their own, and leave S024 and S031 to S034 under S023.
