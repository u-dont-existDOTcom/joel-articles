## 2. What gets in the way

**Notes to the reader, not sections.**
- Line 01 "How to use this very large guide" and line 02 "The table of contents is hiding on the left" (31 words) are instructions about the page, not content. Line 02 is the clearest case: it describes the sidebar itself.

**Parent and first child say the same thing, or the parent shows nothing.**
- Lines 04 and 05: "Quick start: do this first" is followed by "Do you usually think in words?", a question about a single side point. A reader going for the quick list sees a question first and may not realise it is only a side branch of the quick start.
- Lines 23 and 24: "Working with your inner child" has 0 words and sits right after line 18, which is already about the inner child. Its children (Nurturer, Protector, Guide) are the "three jobs" that line 18's heading promises, so the promise and its delivery are split across two parents.
- Lines 37 and 38: "De-hypnosis: wake up inside the influence" then "If you sense you are being influenced" say nearly the same thing on consecutive lines.
- Lines 40 and 41: "Induction: gathering and softening attention" is followed by "Overly Confusing Isn't Automatically Good" as the first child. Nothing in it tells me this is about induction style, and "Before you begin" should come first.
- Lines 54 and 55: "The actual inner meeting" and "Let the next response guide you" are two top-level headings back to back, with no way to see which is the exercise and which is its follow-up.
- Lines 68 and 69: "Troubleshooting" has 0 words, and the first child "Nothing happens" is fine, but the parent shows nothing.
- Lines 91 and 92: "Appendix A..." then "Core prompts", a vague child.
- Lines 106 and 107: "Sensory, state, and strategy tools" (0 words) is a group label covering unrelated tools.
- Lines 119 and 120: "Appendix C: The extended de-hypnosis checklist" then "Hypnosis-specific audit". "Extended" suggests a short checklist exists elsewhere (lines 37 to 39?), and the child could be read as the same thing as the parent.
- Lines 134 and 135: "Forgiveness without bypass" and "Forgiving yourself" are adjacent and overlap.
