## 2. What gets in the way

### Headings that don't say what's under them, or read two ways

- 07 `# The Most Extreme Example`: example of what? It's top level, so nothing ties it to 06 above it.
- 16 `## Start From the Whole`: the whole what? For the "Higher Self" question (situation 6) I couldn't choose between this and its parent, 15.
- 21 `## When to Stop Scanning`: scanning what? The body, for danger, this contents list?
- 28 `# Working With Your Guards`: "Guards" is the guide's own word. Someone who goes numb or sleepy (situation 21) doesn't know that's a guard, and goes to 74.
- 31 and 35, `## The Two-Hand Practice` and `## Other-Hand Dialogue`: two "hand" practices in a five-item list, and neither says which one is done on paper.
- 33 `## Don't Rush the Repair`: repair of what? At 402 words it's the longest in its section, so it may be the main procedure, but the title reads as a caution.
- 45 `## Sober Induction: 3–5 Min`: "Sober" could mean not drunk or high, or plain and no-frills, or not yet in trance (since 46 asks "Already Trancey?"). Nothing says this is the script to record.
- 47 `## Mind Keeps Drifting Off?`: "drifting off" also means falling asleep, and it sits two lines above Sleep Induction.
- 53, 55 and 110: "back" points both ways. `## A Shortcut Back In` and `### Anchoring: A Way Back` mean back into the state. `# Come All the Way Back`, right after 53 and 54, means back out of it.
- 60 `### Understand the Meaning`: meaning of what?
- 62 `## Look at the Situation Too`: which situation?
- 65 `## Offer the Care They Want`: "They" is never named in the sidebar.
- 69 `# Capacity Before Content`: two abstract nouns over 446 words. I can't tell what's in it.
- 87 `## Do These With a Clinician`: "These" is the thing I need to know and it isn't named. I guessed that going back to childhood memories (situation 49) is one of them.
- 96 `## The Four Layers`: layers of what?
- 108 `## Sensory, State & Strategy`: a category label. I wouldn't open it for any situation.
- 142 `## Cosmic-Download Test`: a test of what? And a "download" doesn't sound like a hostile presence.
- 148, 151, 152 (`4. Unwinding Object`, `7. Care Workshop`, `8. Resource Embodiment`): I can't picture these three. The other five in the library I can.
- 154, 156, 159 (`Learning, Entry & Return`, `Body Awareness & Contact`, `Memory & Practitioners`): group labels for the sources. Learning what, entry to what, contact with what? For situation 10 I picked 156 only because of "Body".

### Wrong place, level or parent

- 05 to 14 are ten top-level headings in a row, 96 to 528 words each, so the top of the sidebar reads as a flat list. 07 reads as part of 06, and 10 as part of 09. 08 `## Keep Your Adult in Charge` hangs under "The Most Extreme Example", where I wouldn't expect it.
- Under 17 `# Become a Trustworthy Adult`, 18 to 20 are the three jobs, but 25 to 27 (blame, forgiving) are another subject under the same parent.
- 29 `## Your Inherited Parent Script` sits under Guards. For a parent's voice in my head I'd expect it under 36.
- 42 `# Induction: Gather and Soften` has twelve children of different kinds: a principle (43), preparation (44), the script (45), problems (46, 47), variants (48, 49), techniques (50 to 53). 44 `## Before You Begin` is the second child, 44 lines down. A first-timer expects it beside Quick Start. 46 and 47 are troubleshooting questions outside Troubleshooting.
- 56 `# The Actual Inner Meeting` has no children. Its sibling 57 `# Let the Response Guide You` has nine, and they read as the steps and snags of the meeting. In the sidebar the meeting looks like 290 words and "the response" looks like everything else.
- 69 and 89 are top-level maxims sitting between practical sections.
- 92 `## Survey and Consultation`: reaching the author sits under the app. I found it because the words are plain, but I wouldn't look under the app for it.
- 107 `### 10. The Meta Model` is item 10 of the Milton Model, though it's named as a different model.
- 111 to 113: synchronicity (726 words, the guide's only level-4 headings) is three levels down under NLP and "Sensory, State & Strategy". Someone who notices coincidences, or is frightened by them (situation 57), wouldn't open NLP.
- 141 `## Could It Be Psychosis?` comes fifth in H, after three sections on how to treat the presence. 142 might sit better in F or G.
- No line says "Appendices". The letters A to J are the only sign, and each takes two characters of a narrow line.
