## 2. What gets in the way

Most of the contents is easy to use. The question headings in Troubleshooting (71 to 84), the induction list (44 to 53) and the three practice headings (41, 67, 68) took me straight to an answer. Where I guessed, it was nearly always for one of the reasons below.

### Headings that don't say what's under them, or read two ways

- **07 "The Most Extreme Example"**: example of what? It's a top-level heading, so nothing above it tells me. I only skipped past it.
- **16 "Start From the Whole"**: the whole what? I guessed it might be the Higher Self part (situation 6) but couldn't tell.
- **21 "When to Stop Scanning"**: scanning what, the body, the room for danger, the guide? Nothing near it says.
- **19 "The Protector" and 28 "Working With Your Guards"**: "protector" is the usual word for a protective part, and here it seems to be a job of the adult. Guards and protector sound like the same thing in two places (situations 19, 21).
- **20 "The Guide"**: in a post called a Guide, this reads as the post itself.
- **31 "The Two-Hand Practice" and 35 "Other-Hand Dialogue"**: nearly the same name four lines apart. Neither says one is touch and breath and the other is writing. I got 17 by the word "hand" and guessed 18.
- **45 "Sober Induction: 3–5 Min"**: "sober" reads as not drunk or high. I guessed it means awake and not yet in a trance only because 46 "Already Trancey?" follows.
- **53 "A Shortcut Back In", 55 "Come All the Way Back", 110 "Anchoring: A Way Back"**: "back" means back into trance in two of them and out of trance in the other. For situation 32 both 53 and 110 fit.
- **54 "Turn It Off or Keep It" and 113 "When to Turn It Off"**: "it" is never named, and the same words sit under two different parents (the trance cue, the coincidence bell). For situation 57 I couldn't tell which "it" I wanted.
- **57 "Let the Response Guide You"**: response to what, from whom?
- **59 "Finding Your Own Words"**: could be naming a feeling or writing your own suggestions. **60 "Understand the Meaning"**: meaning of what?
- **61 "Invitation Feels Wrong?"**: I had to guess that "invitation" is the author's word for a suggestion. A new reader hasn't met the word yet.
- **62 "Look at the Situation Too"**: which situation?
- **69 "Capacity Before Content"**: two abstract nouns. I guessed it's about not forcing out what you can't yet bear (situation 41), but only by ruling the rest out.
- **89 "Daily Life Is the Feedback"**: a sentence, not a place. I can't tell what I'd find there that 84 "Life Not Changing?" doesn't have.
- **96 "The Four Layers"**: layers of what? **108 "Sensory, State & Strategy"**: a label from NLP with nothing under it; it tells me nothing about the eight things below.
- **107 "10. The Meta Model"**: numbered as the tenth item of the Milton Model list, so it reads as a second model inside the first.
- **133 "Take It to the Council"**: fine once I'd read situation 61; cold, I wouldn't know what council.
- **142 "Cosmic-Download Test"**: playful, but I can't tell what gets tested or why it sits under Demons.
- **148 "4. Unwinding Object", 151 "7. Care Workshop", 152 "8. Resource Embodiment"**: names I can't picture. "5. Sensory Journey" and "1. Stretch and Release" I can.
- **154 "Learning, Entry & Return", 156 "Body Awareness & Contact", 159 "Memory & Practitioners"**: source groups named in the guide's own shorthand. "Entry & Return" took me a moment to read as going in and coming out. "Contact" with what?
- **The letters "A:" to "J:"**: nothing in the sidebar says these are appendices. "I: An Induction Library" reads as the word "I", and "A: Asking the Subconscious" reads as an answer.

### Headings that say the same thing as a neighbour, or as another place

- **"Am I being influenced" lives in four places**: 06 "Protection From Hypnotists", 38 "De-Hypnosis: Wake Up", 39 "Are You Being Influenced?" and 121 "C: The De-Hypnosis Checklist". Situations 7, 8, 9 and 53 were all guesses for that reason.
- **40 "Soft Words, Less Choice" and 97 "Milton Model: Soft Words"**: the same two words for what's done to you and for what you do for yourself (situations 8, 54).
- **71 "Nothing Happens?", 73 "Subconscious Is Silent?", 74 "Inner Child Won’t Appear?"**: three ways to ask the same question, close together.
- **47 "Mind Keeps Drifting Off?", 81 "Keep Daydreaming?", 130 "E: Is Daydreaming Helping?"**: three places for a wandering mind.
- **75 "Getting Anxious?", 122 "D: Anxiety, Body First", 129 "Anxiety Script"**: fine if 75 points to D, but from the sidebar I can't tell which to open first.
- **58 "If Your Mind Objects" and 61 "Invitation Feels Wrong?"**: I couldn't tell them apart for situation 36.
- **63 "Feels Like Too Much?" and 69 "Capacity Before Content"**: probably the same subject at two levels.
- **26 "Forgiving Without Bypass" and 82 "Stuck in Spiritual Bypass?"**: "bypass" used twice, and a reader who doesn't know the term gets no help from either.
- **45 "Sober Induction: 3–5 Min" and 144 "I: An Induction Library"**: two places for a script to record (situation 28).

### Sections in the wrong place, at the wrong level, or under the wrong parent

- **07 and 08**: "The Most Extreme Example" and its child "Keep Your Adult in Charge" look like they belong under 06 "Protection From Hypnotists". As a top-level heading 07 floats.
- **05 to 14**: ten top-level headings in a row, most under 260 words. In the sidebar they read as one flat list, and the two about affirmations sit between hypnotists and evidence.
- **17 to 27**: after the three jobs (18 to 20) come seven more children that aren't jobs: scanning, borrowing an adult, steering, love and trust, blame, two on forgiving. The parent "Become a Trustworthy Adult" covers all of it only loosely, and forgiving (25 to 27) reads like a section of its own.
- **29 "Your Inherited Parent Script"**: the only child of "Working With Your Guards". Is a parent's voice a guard?
- **32 "Reparenting in Hypnosis"**: comes ten lines before induction is taught (42).
- **36 to 40**: "Is 'Your Voice' Really Yours?" and "De-Hypnosis" sit between the inner child practices and the inductions, thirty lines after "Protection From Hypnotists" on the same subject.
- **49 "Sleep Induction"**: a different purpose (47 words) dropped in the middle of the steps for staying alert and going in and out.
- **55 "Come All the Way Back" before 56 "The Actual Inner Meeting"**: in session order, coming back is last. Maybe on purpose (learn the exit first), but the sidebar doesn't say so.
- **56 and 57**: "The Actual Inner Meeting" has no children, and "Let the Response Guide You" has nine (58 to 66) that all look like the steps and snags of the meeting. I'd expect them under 56.
- **69 and 89**: both top-level, both read like a principle and not a section; 89 is 64 words.
- **92 "Survey and Consultation"**: the way to reach the author sits under the app heading, after "Closing". I found it by the word "Consultation", but I'd have looked in "Closing" first if that word weren't there.
- **111 to 113 "Synchronicity as a Bell"**: under NLP, under "Sensory, State & Strategy", with the only fourth-level headings in the guide. Someone noticing coincidences wouldn't look under NLP.
- **128 "Inner Child Love"**: under Anxiety, where it reads like a stray from the inner child part.
- **141 "Could It Be Psychosis?" and 142 "Cosmic-Download Test"**: under Demons. Someone who sees signs everywhere and can't sleep (situation 57) hasn't met a demon and won't open H. I'd expect these under G "Altered States" or next to 86 "When to Get Outside Help".

### Headings that are notes to the reader

- **80 "Want Hidden Truth Now?" (9 words), 81 "Keep Daydreaming?" (21 words), 76 "Dissociating or Unreal?" (23 words)**: these are signposts to somewhere else. Useful as signposts, but for situation 41 I clicked 80 and would have had to click again.
- **51 "Deepening, if Useful"**: "if useful" is advice, though I could still find it.
- **01 "How to Use This Huge Guide"** and **90 "Closing"**: notes, and fine where they are.
