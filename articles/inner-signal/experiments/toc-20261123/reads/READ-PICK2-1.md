## 2. What gets in the way

**Headings that don't say what's under them or read two ways**
- Line 07 "The Most Extreme Example": extreme example of what? Nothing in the sidebar says (hypnosis? influence?). Its only child, "Keep Your Adult in Charge" (08), is a section that sounds like it belongs with the inner adult material far below.
- Line 68 "Capacity Before Content": jargon, and it sits between the weekly practice and Troubleshooting. A reader can't tell it's about pacing or safety.
- Line 30 "Inner Child Practices" and line 69 "Troubleshooting" have 0 words and are only containers. Fine for Troubleshooting, but 30 hides that the practices are hands, hypnosis, rest and writing.
- Line 106 "Sensory, State & Strategy": 0 words, vague, and its children (swish, anchoring, parts integration, synchronicity) don't fit that label. "Synchronicity as a Bell" (109) with two children (110, 111) is buried four levels deep.
- Line 154 "Learning, Entry & Return" and 159 "Memory & Practitioners": source categories that don't say what the sources are about.
- Line 93 "B: NLP Without the Sales Pitch" is clear, but 105 "10. The Meta Model" is filed under "Milton Model: Soft Words", which is a different model, so it looks wrongly parented.
- Line 14 "Why Doing It Myself Worked": 528 words that could be personal story or evidence. It sits in the opening cluster after "What It's For", so I'd also try Introduction for it.
- Line 56 "Let the Response Guide You": it's level 1, same as its parent "The Actual Inner Meeting" (55), and it reads as a step of the meeting. Its children are ## (57 on), so it should probably be one level lower or the parent.
- Line 36 "Is “Your Voice” Really Yours?" and 38 "Wake Up Inside the Influence" both cover whose voice or influence you're under, as does appendix C (119).

**Same thing said twice, or a parent and its first child overlapping**
- 15 "A Useful Map of the Mind" then 16 "Start From the Whole": the pair on two lines reads as one idea.
- 17 "The Inner Adult's Three Jobs" and 18 "The Nurturer": fine, but then 21-27 (stop scanning, borrow an adult, forgiving, blame) aren't jobs, so the parent claims too much.
- 36 and 37, 38 and 39: parent and first child ask nearly the same thing.
- 41 "Induction: Gather and Soften" then 42 "Simple Beats Confusing", 43 "Before You Begin": it's unclear which is the actual induction; "Before You Begin" (setup) comes after the induction heading.
- 74 "Getting Anxious?" vs 120-127 appendix D "Anxiety, Body First" and "Anxiety Script"; 80 "Keep Daydreaming?" vs 128 "E: Is Daydreaming Helping?"; 81 and 46's case (spiritual bypass vs G altered states). I'd have to guess which one holds the answer.
- 85 "When to Get Outside Help" vs 86 "Do These With a Clinician" vs 84 "What Not to Do" vs 12 "What It's For, and Not For": four places for limits.
- 126 "Inner Child Love" sits inside the anxiety appendix, out of place; the inner child material is in the main body.

**Seemingly misplaced or wrong level**
- 08 "Keep Your Adult in Charge" under "The Most Extreme Example" (see above).
- 25-27 forgiving and blame are under the Inner Adult's three jobs rather than their own section.
- 29 "Your Inherited Parent Script" is under "Working With Your Guards" but is really the critic/parent voice; 36 section says similar.
- 90 "Survey and Consultation" under the app: reads as a product feature, not contact with the author (situation 51).
- 130-132 "Find a Hypnotist Who Fits", "An Oracle Isn't an Order", "Take It to the Council" are under F "Self-Trust and Guidance"; the hypnotist one doesn't fit there.
- 143 appendix I numbering vs B numbering: two numbered lists (1-8, 1-10, "9. No-Trance Inner Contact") in different appendices.
- 88 "Closing" comes before the app and appendices; fine, but "Daily Life Is the Feedback" (87) is a loose ending under outside help.
