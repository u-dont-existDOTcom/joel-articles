## 2. What gets in the way

**Headings that don't say what's under them or read two ways**
- "De-Hypnosis: Wake Up" (38) could mean coming out of a trance or waking from outside influence. "Come All the Way Back" (54) covers the first and the Checklist (119) the second.
- "Sober Induction: 3–5 Min" (44) does not say what "sober" means (no trance? awake?). It is also the only heading that shows an induction is for being scattered and awake.
- "Fractionation: In and Out" (49), "Deepening, if Useful" (50) and "Trance Depth Check" (51) use jargon, or are a bare name with no sign of the reader's need.
- "Capacity Before Content" (68) is abstract and sits after the weekly practice. A reader can't tell it is about pacing and readiness.
- "Take It to the Council" (132) and "Working With Your Guards" (28) give no hint of what the council or the guards are. "Guards" competes with "The Protector" (19).
- "Inner Child Love" (126) and "Anxiety Script" (127) are vague about what they are (a practice? a script for which situation?).
- "Sensory, State & Strategy" (106) is three nouns that say nothing. It has 0 words and 10 children.
- "Synchronicity as a Bell" (109) is playful, but a reader with signs-everywhere worries finds it next to "Feeling Grandiose?" and "Could It Be Psychosis?" with no link between them.
- "Skip the Entity Hobby" (142), "Cosmic-Download Test" (141) and "Warmth or Distance?" (137) are playful and hard to use without already knowing the topic.
- The appendix labels "A:" to "J:" tell nothing alone, and "F: Self-Trust and Guidance" (129) is too general.

**Same ask as the heading before it or the parent (parent and first child sit on two lines)**
- 06 Protection From Hypnotists, 07 Manipulated Under Hypnosis and 08 Keep Your Adult in Charge are one topic in three lines. 07 has a single 53-word child.
- 19 The Protector and 21 Protect, Then Stand Down, and 28 Working With Your Guards.
- 53 Turn It Off or Keep It, under 52 A Shortcut Back In, and 111 When to Turn It Off in the Synchronicity appendix. Both are about switching a cue off.
- 56 Let the Response Guide You, directly after 55 The Actual Inner Meeting, and 57 If Your Mind Objects, 60 Invitation Feels Wrong? and 62 Feels Like Too Much? Three "something is off" headings in a row are hard to tell apart.
- 69 Troubleshooting (0 words) then 70 Nothing Happens?. 74 Getting Anxious? repeats D (120), 80 Keep Daydreaming? repeats E (128), 79 Want Hidden Truth Now? repeats F. 81 Stuck in Spiritual Bypass? sits near 126.
- 11 Hypnosis Evidence, 12 What It's For, and Not For, 84 What Not to Do and 85 When to Get Outside Help cover overlapping ground. 119 C: De-Hypnosis Checklist repeats 38-40.
- 93 B: NLP Without the Sales Pitch, 117 What I Won’t Use NLP For and 118 The Whole Sequence are spread out inside one appendix.
- 14 Why Doing It Myself Worked and 02 Introduction (both personal) are far from the evidence heading 11.

**Wrong place, wrong level or wrong parent**
- 04 Do You Think in Words? is under Quick Start but is a prerequisite check, not a step.
- 16 Fragmented? Start Whole (355 words) is longer than its parent 15 and reads like a troubleshooting item.
- 17 The Inner Adult’s Three Jobs has three children (18-20), then seven more at the same level (21-27: No Adult Yet, Child Speaks, Love First, Instead of Blame, Forgiving...). Those are not jobs. Forgiveness (26, 27) feels unrelated to the jobs, and Practices (30) comes after Guards (28), a bit out of order.
- 29 Your Inherited Parent Script is under 28 Working With Your Guards but is about whose voice is speaking, which belongs with 36 Is “Your Voice” Really Yours?.
- 36 Is “Your Voice” Really Yours? (656 words) then 38 De-Hypnosis: Wake Up. Two influence topics are split by the inner-child block and the inductions.
- 43 Before You Begin applies to every session but sits under the induction heading, as does 52 A Shortcut Back In. 54 Come All the Way Back is top-level, though it is the end of the induction.
- 87 Daily Life Is the Feedback sits under 85 When to Get Outside Help, but it belongs with 83 Life Not Changing? or 88 Closing.
- 105 10. The Meta Model is the tenth "Milton Model" technique, though it is a different model.
- 109 Synchronicity as a Bell, with its two level-four children, is under "Sensory, State & Strategy" in the NLP appendix. The topic belongs with F (oracle states) or G.
- 130 Find a Hypnotist Who Fits is under F: Self-Trust and Guidance. It belongs in the "get help" part (85) or in H.
- 152 9. No-Trance Inner Contact in "An Induction Library" is by definition not an induction. 64 situations, among them "no trance at all", point there.
- 90 Survey and Consultation is a bare label under the app section, with 49 words.

**Notes to the reader rather than sections**
- 42 Simple Beats Confusing, 87 Daily Life Is the Feedback, 114 Future Pacing: Test It, 83 Life Not Changing?, and the question-style 45-48 (Already Trancey?, Mind Keeps Drifting Off?, Make Hypnosis More Alert) read as asides in the induction, not as steps. Tiny sections (75 Dissociating or Unreal? 23 words, 79 Want Hidden Truth Now? 9 words, 80 Keep Daydreaming? 21 words) look like notes.
