## 2. What gets in the way

**Same topic in several places, so I can't tell which to open.**
- Lines 06, 07, 38, 39, 40 and 119: "Protection From Hypnotists", "Manipulated Under Hypnosis", "De-Hypnosis: Wake Up", "Are You Being Influenced?", "Soft Words, Less Choice" and the appendix "C: The De-Hypnosis Checklist" all sound like the same subject. Line 36 "Is “Your Voice” Really Yours?" also overlaps. I can't tell which one is the explanation, which is the practice and which is the checklist.
- Lines 06 and 07 are two top-level lines in a row that ask nearly the same thing. Line 08, "Keep Your Adult in Charge", is a child of 07 but reads like a rule for the whole guide.
- Lines 09 and 10 ("Affirmations Done Right", "Undo a Wrong Affirmation") are two separate top-level sections. The second is really a fix for the first and would fit better as its child.
- Line 69 "Troubleshooting" and lines 70-83 repeat things asked elsewhere: 45, 46 and 47 in the inductions, 60, 62 and 63 in the meeting, line 74 against appendix D (120-127), line 80 against appendix E (128), line 76 against appendices F and G (129-134). I don't know whether to look in the main body or in the appendices.
- Line 85 "When to Get Outside Help" and its child 86 "Do These With a Clinician" say nearly the same thing on two lines in a row. Line 12 "What It's For, and Not For" also covers the same ground as 84 and 85 but sits far from them.

**Headings that don't say what's under them or can be read two ways.**
- Line 13, "The Left/Right Brain Cartoon": playful, and I can't tell if it is a claim, a debunking, or a method.
- Line 16, "Fragmented? Start Whole": could mean "if you feel fragmented", "start with the whole self", or a tool. It is a child of "A Useful Map of the Mind" and I can't tell why.
- Line 28, "Working With Your Guards": I don't know if "guards" means the protective parts that the "Protector" at line 19 describes. It is a top-level section rather than part of the inner-child material.
- Line 33, "Take the Repair Slowly": repair of what?
- Line 35, "Other-Hand Dialogue": I only guess it is writing with the other hand.
- Line 43, "Before You Begin" (506 words) reads like a note to the reader, and so does line 42, "Simple Beats Confusing". "Sober Induction" at line 44 makes me think of drugs and alcohol.
- Line 47, "Make Hypnosis More Alert", and line 48, "Sleep Induction": they are tiny (63 and 47 words) and sit in the middle of the induction steps as if they were stages of one sequence rather than alternatives.
- Line 52 and line 53: "A Shortcut Back In" and its child "Turn It Off or Keep It" — "it" is unclear until you read the text. Lines 110-111, "Installing the Cue" and "When to Turn It Off", are the same cue idea in the NLP appendix under "Synchronicity as a Bell", which is a different kind of cue.
- Line 56, "Let the Response Guide You": a top-level section right after "The Actual Inner Meeting", which reads as its first child. Its own children (57-65) are questions about what to do when it goes wrong, so the level doesn't match what is under it.
- Line 59, "Notice or Meaning?" and line 63, "Urge to Move?": too short to say what they decide.
- Line 68, "Capacity Before Content" (446 words): at the end of the practice sections, right after the weekly practice. I'd expect it before the inner-child practices or before the meeting.
- Line 87, "Daily Life Is the Feedback": under "When to Get Outside Help" but about measuring progress. It belongs with the daily or weekly practice or with "Life Not Changing?" at 83.
- Line 88, "Closing": says nothing, and I can't tell if it is a summary or a send-off.
- Line 90, "Survey and Consultation": I don't know whose survey or whose consultation.
- Line 92, "Core Prompts" on its own as the only child of A; it adds nothing the parent doesn't already say.
- Line 105, "10. The Meta Model": numbered as the tenth Milton Model pattern, but the Meta Model is a different thing from the Milton Model.
- Line 106, "Sensory, State & Strategy" (0 words): an empty parent whose name tells me nothing, and it holds items (109 "Synchronicity as a Bell", 116 "Parts Integration") that have little to do with each other.
- Line 118, "The Whole Sequence": the whole sequence of what?
- Line 130, "Find a Hypnotist Who Fits": under "F: Self-Trust and Guidance", which has nothing to do with choosing a practitioner. It looks like it belongs near 85 and 86.
- Line 131, "An Oracle Isn’t an Order", and line 132, "Take It to the Council": "council" is never explained in the heading.
- Lines 133 and 135, "G: Altered States" and "H: Demons, Jinn & Presences": they overlap with line 134 and with troubleshooting lines 75, 76 and 81.
- Lines 154-159: the J sub-headings are topics ("Inner Speech", "Memory & Practitioners"), not source types, so I can't tell what is under them.

**Sections that look like they are in the wrong place or at the wrong level.**
- Lines 17-27: "The Inner Adult’s Three Jobs" has ten children, and only three of them (18, 19, 20) are the jobs. The others (21-27, from "Protect, Then Stand Down" through "Forgiving Yourself") are not jobs. Line 21 repeats the Protector (19). The forgiveness lines (26, 27) sit under a section about the adult.
- Line 30, "Inner Child Practices" (0 words): empty parent. Only the children carry content. Lines 29 and 28 sit just above it, so "Your Inherited Parent Script" (29) reads as if it belongs under 28 rather than being its own thing.
- Lines 11, 12, 13 and 14: the evidence, scope, brain cartoon and personal origin story all sit between the affirmations and the map of the mind. Line 14 "Why Doing It Myself Worked" (528 words) is an origin story and would fit with the Introduction.
- Lines 36-37: "Is “Your Voice” Really Yours?" comes after the practices, but its subject (whose voice) is the same as lines 38-40 and the checklist in appendix C.
- Lines 41-53: line 54 "Come All the Way Back" is a top-level section and not part of the induction block, even though it closes the same sequence.
- Line 89, "Try It in the Inner Signal App": placed between "Closing" and the appendices, so it reads as an appendix without a letter.
