Past LIfe & Ancestral Trauma

One of the hottest and weirdest topics when it comes to hypnosis is past life regression. I've spoken to many people who told me that their trauma in this life came from a past life. Well, I also have some past life memories. I remember being a brontosaurus and I remember getting eaten by a T-Rex, and yes it was traumatic. But I'd still go for the inner child reparenting rather than the Brontosaurus reparenting because I highly suspect that what's mainly affecting me is something that happened to my inner child in this life (either when I was actually a child or after). According to the Buddha, our past lives are endless, so that would put us on quite the healing treadmill if we have to deal with all the past life trauma now. On the other hand, it could be an excellent therapeutic proxy, perhaps, if it feels too difficult to access the trauma in this life.

Ancestral trauma is another thing a lot of people are now talking about healing. That part is a bit harder to understand because it could mean the trauma that is passed down from generations, which is what everyone is healing essentially even if it's not named that way, or it might mean they are actually trying to heal their ancestors' trauma for their ancestors. If that's the case then I'd say the main validity there is as a loving-kindness practice and another therapeutic proxy technique. 

One doorway into either of these would be to do an age regression hypnosis, and just keep going back farther and farther until before you were in your mother's belly. 

And you don't necessarily need to have a professional do this either. They don't have some kind of magic that you lack. In fact, they might be more confused than you, or confuse you. I have one friend, T., who went to a hypnotist to get a past-life regression. He was secretly recording the session so I heard all of it. He was trying to make it work and he felt like he was maybe really remembering his past life as a pirate. 

The hypnotist was very much convinced it was real. But afterwards, T. told me he thinks he was just making it all up. My guess is most people tend to err the other way, thinking they weren't making it up. Personally, I don't think it matters if it was a real past life or not unless you're trying to prove it for some other reason, like academic research into past lives.
