# Situations

1. You've never tried anything like this and just want the short list of what to do in a session, in order, without reading the whole thing first.
2. You hardly ever talk to yourself in words in your head, and you're worried the parts of the instructions that say "say" or "ask" don't work for someone like you.
3. You half suspect hypnosis is stage-show nonsense and want to know whether any real studies support it, and whether doing it on your own counts.
4. You once paid a professional hypnotist and it did very little for you, and you're wondering if doing it by yourself could actually work better.
5. You're in treatment for something heavy from your past and want to know what this practice is fine for, what it isn't for, and whether it could replace your therapist.
6. You roll your eyes at talk of a "Higher Self" or "universal heart" and want to know whether you can still do this without buying into any of that.
7. You keep catching yourself buying, believing or clicking things you never really chose, and you want to understand how ads, apps and charismatic people shape you without you noticing.
8. Someone you follow keeps saying things like "part of you already knows I'm right" and you can't tell whether that's pressure; you want questions you can ask yourself to find out.
9. You found a hypnosis recording by a stranger and you're about to press play with your eyes closed; you want to know what to check before you let it into your head.
10. You've done body-based trauma work and read about tracking sensations and moving between a hard feeling and an easier one, and you want to know which books those ideas come from.
11. You're reading this wondering how much of it is scientifically proven and how much is just the author's own way of putting things together.
12. You've heard NLP is used to manipulate people and you want to know what this author refuses to do with it before you try anything.
13. You want to be someone your younger self can trust, but you don't know what that actually requires of you day to day, beyond saying a few nice sentences.
14. When you try to be the grown-up for yourself you go blank or turn into a harsh critic, and you want a way to get some adult help for a moment without having to produce it from nothing.
15. The younger part of you says it doesn't believe you love it, and you don't know what to say back that won't sound like a line.
16. You're furious with yourself for something you did as a teenager and want a way to look at that younger you without punishing them.
17. Talking to yourself feels silly and nothing comes, and you want something you can do with your hands and breath to make contact with your younger self.
18. Speaking out loud or imagining a conversation with your younger self feels fake, and you want to try something on paper.
19. You do all the warm, soothing things for your inner child, then go and text the person who wrecks you or skip meals, and the whole thing isn't working.
20. You can't tell if you're being kind to yourself or just indulging, and you're equally afraid of the "just toughen up" voice that raised you.
21. You sit down expecting to meet a small child inside and instead you feel numb, sleepy, suddenly hungry, or you just want to check your phone.
22. Every time you speak gently to yourself, a voice that sounds like your parents says don't be needy, don't embarrass us, and calls it all fake.

24. A younger version of you just showed up during a session and you want a step-by-step for how to be with them without making it worse.

26. You notice you do things for your family, church or friend group that you'd never choose alone, and you want questions to find where you're betraying yourself to belong.
