# Catch-up: Self-Hypnosis guide sections against the somatic guide

Compared: the Self-Hypnosis guide's sections built on the somatic guide (live post, unchanged since 2026-11-20) with the Somatic Therapy Modalities Map as it is live (post of 2026-11-21 19:14 UTC). Only differences of substance are listed. The same items are in `CATCHUP-SOMATIC.json`.

17 items: 2 high, 5 medium, 10 low. Quotes are exact copies from the two files.

Already known and left out: the hypnosis guide doesn't mention Systema breathing.

## High

**M01 · contradiction · high**

Hypnosis guide [S122]:

> Breathe in small amounts through your nose and let it out quietly, through your nose too.

Somatic guide [S008, Breathwork]:

> By simply pursing your lips together on exhale, you can slow down your exhale. Slower exhales, combined with not breathing more on inhales, lead to overall reduction in breathing.

Why it matters: The two guides give different instructions for the same named practice: here small breaths in and out through the nose, there a slowed out-breath through pursed lips with the in-breath left as it is, so a reader of both can't tell which is the method.

Suggestion: ask the author. The sentence on how to breathe in and out (nose both ways): keep it, replace it, or give both ways.

**M02 · contradiction · high**

Hypnosis guide [S122]:

> However, please don’t hold your breath until you are air hungry, which can make you more anxious in the short term.

Somatic guide [S008, Breathwork]:

> That slight oxygen hunger feels a bit anxiogenic for the first 20 seconds or so, but after that, you’ll feel much calmer.

Why it matters: This guide says to avoid air hunger (it warns against holding the breath that far) and to stop if panicky, while the source, which never mentions holds or pauses, says a slight hunger for air is expected, feels a little anxious for about 20 seconds and then calms, so a reader here stops at the point where the source says to carry on.

Suggestion: ask the author. The bracketed warning about air hunger and the last sentence of the section (when to stop): whether slight air hunger is expected, and for how long.

Suggestion: ask the author. First sentence of the section: where to tap (body or head) and for how long.

## Breathing: what conflicts with [S122] and what doesn't

- Conflicts: M01 (how to breathe out: nose here, pursed lips in the source) and M02 (air hunger: avoided here, expected and passing in the source). M15 is the lesser one (Vagal Blitz is meant to make you lightheaded; this passage says stop if dizzy).
- Breath holds and pauses: no conflict found. The somatic guide's text never mentions a hold or a pause, so "Only pause if that's comfortable" has no counterpart there.
- Circular or continuous breathing: nothing to compare. The words appear only in the heading of [S013] ("Systema: circular breathing & compassionate, relaxing martial arts"); the text under it gives no breathing instruction. Whatever the video embedded there shows isn't in the file I read.
- [S003] says some bodies need to "breathe more, less, in new ways", which fits this guide's "if you are breathing too much".
