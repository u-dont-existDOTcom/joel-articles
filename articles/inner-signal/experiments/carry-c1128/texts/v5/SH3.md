Analyzing a feeling instead of feeling it can follow you into therapy sessions too, where you start explaining a feeling as soon as it comes up. If that keeps happening, ask whether your therapist is trained in Intensive Short-Term Dynamic Psychotherapy, or look for someone who is. In that therapy, the therapist catches you each time you move away from a feeling and asks what you're feeling right then. I go into it in [Can't Stay With a Feeling?](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/201523223/cant-stay-with-a-feeling), a section of my somatic guide, along with two other therapies for staying with feelings.
