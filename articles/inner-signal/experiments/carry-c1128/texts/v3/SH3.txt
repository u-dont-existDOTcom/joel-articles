In therapy sessions, being too analytical can be a way of getting away from a feeling: you start explaining it as soon as it comes up, or you agree with the therapist just to get past it. If that keeps happening, look for someone trained in Intensive Short-Term Dynamic Psychotherapy, which I go into in my somatic guide's section [Can't Stay With a Feeling?](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/201523223/cant-stay-with-a-feeling). If instead you start to feel unreal, stop for the day, as [Dissociating or Unreal?](#§dissociating-or-unreal) says.
