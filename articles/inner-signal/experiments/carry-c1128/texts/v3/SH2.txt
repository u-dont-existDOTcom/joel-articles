Pain is one of those symptoms. If yours has lasted months and doctors have found no injury or disease to explain it, I'd look for a therapist trained in Pain Reprocessing Therapy or Emotional Awareness and Expression Therapy, the two talk therapies I go into in [my somatic guide](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/201523223/pain-that-wont-quit), alongside the self-hypnosis. They've helped people with long-lasting back, muscle and joint pain, by changing their relationship to the pain without pretending it's not there.
