One such container is a clinical therapy called [Imagery Rescripting](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/201523223/imagery-rescripting), which I go into in my somatic guide. It works a lot like the way I [re-do trauma memories](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/189761959/re-do-trauma-memories) in my inner child reparenting guide, where you imagine a painful memory going differently. Here, though, the therapist is in the scene with you, stepping in as the ideal adult who protects and nurtures your inner child. Over the sessions, you learn to be that adult yourself, so it's what I'd go to for a memory that feels too big to re-do on your own.
