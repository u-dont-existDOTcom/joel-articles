## Block 749, 5. Sensory journey, P1 (F20.1, F20.4)
BEFORE: Sensory Journey: Pay attention to the sounds in the room you’re in and how your body feels, then imagine yourself walking somewhere. Where do you want to go? ## Block 753, 7. Care workshop, P1 (F20.1)
BEFORE: Care workshop: prepare the workshop (time, place, tools, boundaries, etc). Invite the signal/feeling/young part to come only as close as is welcome. Try to avoid any language that makes you feel you're not one person. Care for it as best you can, by asking what it wants and giving that if you can, or sensing into it and responding, or just being with it. Take breaks, get back in contact if that's ok, take more breaks, etc. Keep bringing in adult functions (or borrow someone else's function). Have the adult part of you run the show. Goal is to make it "good as you" rather than "good as new", not to erase history. Tell your little one, "That's enough for today. We're going back to the life we're building." Close the workspace, reassure yourself that you are one whole person, that you're an adult now, and come back.
