# When “your voice” is everybody else’s voice

What is the difference between a human doer and a human being? If you don’t feel good enough as a human being, you will try to become good enough as a human doer by for example being beautiful, doing something well, being smart, having a certain status, being spiritual, etc. I’m not saying those can’t be real gifts, but if failing at one of them feels like losing yourself, then you’re using it to buy your sense of belonging.

# Appendix F: Self-trust, outside guidance, and oracle-like states

We can not ‘force’ forgiveness on ourselves, it has to come from a place of understanding otherwise it can be bypassing. However, if we remain in the anger forever, we are allowing the injury to dictate how we live our lives in the present. So how do we hold all of this? We just remember that it can all be true at once. They really did hurt us and they still have to answer for it, and our boundary with them still matters, but one day we may find that we have forgiven them.

“You don’t have to forgive them before you’re ready, and I’m not going to use spirituality to silence your pain, but I also won’t build our whole life around this wound forever.”

For self-forgiveness, name what you did, and take responsibility only for what was yours. Let yourself feel remorse for the harm, or for how it went against your values, but don't make yourself a permanent criminal over it.
