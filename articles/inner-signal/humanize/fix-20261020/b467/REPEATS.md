## 2. Contradictions

1. **B66–B72 (Doing it myself) vs B231 (Induction: gathering attention without disappearing), B242 (Before you begin), B263 (Already open? Contain before you deepen).** "it feels like if I needed to I could have continued for an arbitrary amount of time" / "begin with orientation and containment instead of adding depth" / "When intoxicated, acutely confused, or unable to keep yourself safe, deal with immediate support rather than exploratory hypnosis." / "Stabilize before making it stronger." Pull: the personal section uses self-hypnosis to amplify and extend loveyhuasca, chrunga and lucid-dream states. The practice sections say an already-open or intoxicated state gets containment first, not more depth. B231 and B263 name lucid dreaming and loveyhuasca among the states to contain. B263's "before making it stronger" and B71's caveat (which covers only duration) leave some room, but neither side says how the two fit. Better: B263, which gives the order the personal section skips.

2. **B187 (Your voice) vs B669–B670 (Appendix F › The council — internal first, then outward).** "Sometimes it may be wise to remove yourself, for a time, from the people or group that you normally would get answers from." / "Sometimes it is right to go it alone, but I would suggest making it the last step, after you've tried to get council." Pull: one says step away from the people you usually get answers from; the other says ask people first and go it alone last. They do different jobs (letting your own preferences surface vs. big decisions that are hard to undo). B671 warns that counsel can turn into handing over your judgment, but nothing links the two passages. Mild. Better: each fits its own job, and B187's "Not for too long" is the only bridge.

Checked, not counted: B71 vs B420/B687 (B71 gives its own reason), and B64's body "saying no" vs B207's "not always correct" (noticing a no doesn't claim it is always right).
