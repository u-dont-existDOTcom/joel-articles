We can not ‘force’ forgiveness on ourselves, it has to come from a place of understanding otherwise it can be bypassing. However, if we remain in the anger forever, we are allowing the injury to dictate how we live our lives in the present. So how do we hold all of this? We just remember that it can all be true at once. The harm happened, and the boundary still matters. The person is still accountable, and forgiveness may come later on its own.
