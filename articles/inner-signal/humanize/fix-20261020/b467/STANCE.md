# Stance check: b467 rewrite

Sections covered: "Why doing it myself worked better for me" (3 paragraphs) and "When 'your voice' is everybody else's voice" (2 paragraphs).

## Conflicts in the rewrite

**1. Why doing it myself worked better for me, paragraph 1, sentence 1**
- Rewrite: "Doing self-hypnosis on my own seems far more useful than the very little I managed to do with a professional hypnotist."
- Essay: "Self-hypnosis became much more useful for me than working with a professional hypnotist." (same section, published), under the heading "Why doing it myself worked better for me."
- A reader would wrongly believe that Joel only has an impression that self-hypnosis is more useful. The essay reports it as what happened to him. "Seems" is an added hedge.

**2. Same paragraph, sentence 2**
- Rewrite: "…but I suspect the main reason the pro didn't work very well for me was trust and precision."
- Essay: "The larger issue was trust and precision." (same section, published), followed by "When I'm giving the suggestion, I can feel the exact moment a phrase is slightly wrong."
- A reader would wrongly believe that Joel is guessing at the cause. The essay states it flatly and then shows how he knows. In the published text the doubt was on the sobriety factor ("Maybe part of that was state-dependent"); the rewrite moves it onto the main reason.

**3. Paragraph 2, sentence 1**
- Rewrite: "A hypnotist working on me can't interact with my subconscious mind quickly enough … to know what's going on in my mind"
- Essay: "An outside hypnotist can't easily conduct that rapid back-and-forth because they aren't inside the mind receiving the suggestion." (same section, published); "It cannot feel your body, share a practitioner's embodied intuition, or know an unreported response." (Try it in the Inner Signal app)
- A reader would wrongly believe that an outside hypnotist can't get what they need to know what's going on in him. The essay says this rapid back-and-forth is hard for them ("easily" has been dropped), and it credits practitioners with an embodied intuition that an app lacks.

**4. Paragraph 2, sentence 2**
- Rewrite: "there are advantages to having a skilled guide, since they may be able to see things I'm blind to"
- Essay: "A skilled guide can still notice blind spots, help regulate the process, and hold a safer container." (same section, published); "A professional can bring pacing, containment, and another set of eyes." (Appendix F)
- A reader would wrongly believe that a skilled guide only might catch blind spots. The essay says plainly that a guide can. "May" is an added hedge. Minor.

**5. When "your voice" is everybody else's voice, paragraph 2, sentence 1**
- Rewrite: "Give your inner child the opportunity to do something that has no value on its own…"
- Essay: "Give the inner child a small experiment with no résumé value" and "We're finding out whether we enjoy this, not proving our worth." (same section, published); "Love, play, confidence, creativity, and communion are worthwhile purposes" (Induction, Before you begin)
- A reader would wrongly believe that the activity should be worthless in itself. In the essay it lacks only résumé value or proof of worth, and it is worth doing for enjoyment and discovery.

**6. Same paragraph, sentence 3**
- Rewrite: "…often your inner parent tells your inner child that it is supposed to be good at something as soon as it starts doing it."
- Essay: "The inherited parent may demand immediate talent." (same section, published); "the *inherited parent*: the internalized caregiver voice formed from how the child was treated. This is different from the *inner adult*" (Working with protectors); "it doesn't establish that a perfectly capable inner parent is already available" (Sometimes start with the whole)
- A reader would wrongly believe that the voice demanding talent is the "inner parent." The essay uses that term for the caring capacity you build, so the rewrite merges the inherited critic with the reparenting adult. "May" also becomes "often."

## Contradictions in the published text

I found none that a reasonable reader would see in the published versions of these five paragraphs, either within them or against the rest of the essay.

Checked and not counted:
- "When worth isn't felt internally, it gets earned through beauty, performance…" against "Worth was never the missing skill" (The inner child, the guards, and the three inner adults). Read the way people talk, the first sentence describes people chasing a felt sense of worth, and the next sentence calls that a bargain. The rewrite says the same with "you will try to become good enough."
- "a small experiment with no résumé value: … a language, a sport, volunteering." Volunteering and languages often go on a résumé, but a reader takes a "small experiment" to mean a beginner's dabbling. The rewrite no longer uses this wording.
- "Ordinary interaction dissolved the state" against "A powerful state matters only if something survives it" (Forget the left-brain/right-brain cartoon). Appendix G ("Let the experience widen what seems possible") and the spiritual-bypass troubleshooting ("A genuine opening can show you a depth of love… Let that matter") explicitly let a passing opening count. The rewrite keeps this passage as "It wore off once I was back around people."

Conflicts in the rewrite: 6. Contradictions in the published text: 0.
