## 1. Repeats

1. **B65, B659.** B65 (Why doing it myself worked better for me): "doing it myself has one advantage nobody outside can copy: immediate dialogue with my own system." / B659 (F, opening): "there are definite advantages to both having someone work with you (pacing, containment, another set of eyes), and doing things yourself." Better: B65, concrete and first-person.

2. **B112, B675, B680.** B112 (Love doesn’t have to wait for trust): "punishing yourself now is not going to give them the capacity they needed at the time" / B675 (F, Forgiveness without bypass): "they could only make the moves they made, given their accumulated nature at the time" / B680 (F, Forgiveness): "do what you can to rectify or keep it from happening again, and then stop holding yourself hostage."
