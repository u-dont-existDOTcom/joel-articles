Troubleshooting

Nothing happens

Check if there's a way you could try this on a smaller scale.

Try out these requests: “Show me a body sensation.” “Show me just a word.” “Show me whether today is for listening or resting.”

It feels fake

Do you have a sense of what it is that feels fake? The words, an image, what you are expecting to get from this, a role you feel you have to play etc. Not having an inner voice or images doesn't mean you're resisting the work, or that you're missing a 'grown up'. If it feels like you are being asked to do something you don't want to do, then correct the invitation. It could be that there is a part that is protecting you, so talk to it.

You could say something like 'What are you protecting me from?' or 'Are you afraid I will lie to myself again?' or 'Are you afraid this will just be a performance?' If it says yes, that one can sting, because there could be something to it. Maybe you've fooled yourself before, and that part remembers. "Tell me what you remember, I'm listening." works well here. It puts you into the role of the Nurturer, which alleviates the instinct to argue back.

It could also be your inherited parent script that you are picking up, and it is trying to enforce a rule about how you should be to belong to them. Find out what rule it is enforcing. Comfort your little one before you answer any of it. Your answers can be firm or funny, as long as they aren’t mean. Then work on finding some ordinary evidence to build a more believable 'grown up' voice of your own.

If the fake feeling is telling you that you don't have the grown up ability to do this yet, then don't try and convince yourself by using an inner voice. As long as you can still observe yourself well enough to do this, try and borrow what you need from someone who you can imagine is good enough to lend it to you. This could be a family member, friend, famous person etc. What you need might be warmth, boundary, direction etc. For warmth, just make sure it's someone who liked you the way you were. Try out something which rings true to you, whether it is a sentence, an action etc, and see how you get on.
