Pattern 10: Meta Model questions — the de-hypnosis tool

To 'Milton Model' is to open things up, so the listener can fill in what fits for them. To 'Meta Model' is to stop and ask, "What do you mean by that?" In order to be ethical with people, you need to do both.

When something sounds true but vague, ask, "What do you mean, specifically?" Then find out according to who, and compared to what. If you hear "always" or "never," check whether it's really every time. If someone's afraid of doing something, ask what exactly would happen if they did. And ask how they know, and what evidence would change their mind.

So when someone is starting to tense up in their body, use the Milton Model to help them soften. And when you feel you're losing track of what's really going on, that's the time for the Meta Model.
