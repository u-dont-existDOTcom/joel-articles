Aside from focusing on things in your direct experience, you can also suggest yourself into greater alertness (it's still relaxed-alert, not paranoid-alert). Keep your eyes open. Use a small hand movement each time you make the auto-suggestion that you're gaining increasing clarity. Then stop the hand movement and see where your mind is at. That hand movement is the mental escape into the body's flow. Say something like,

“Starting with your eyes open, press your thumb to your forefinger, gently, and let go. Each time you do, you get a little more awake… Press and let go again, a bit clearer. And once more, and now you’re clearer than when you started. Now stop pressing. In the first second after you stop, see if the pressing is still echoing somewhere. Maybe your fingers still tingle, or your breath is keeping the rhythm, or your attention is waiting for the next press. Or maybe there’s no echo at all. Just notice, sit up straight and work from this level.”
