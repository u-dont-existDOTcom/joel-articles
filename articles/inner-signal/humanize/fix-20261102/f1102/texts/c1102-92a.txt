The inner child, the guards, and the inner adult’s three jobs

Your inner child won’t trust you right away. It’s not going to be enough for you to meditate, say three nice sentences to the inner child and expect to have its trust. What’s going to help your inner child to trust you is you, the person you are right now, becoming reliable.

Here I’m going to talk about what you develop in yourself as you reparent your inner child. I’m going to talk about it in terms of three jobs: your loving Nurturer, your safe Adult Protector (hereafter just the Protector) and your wise Guide. That’s one grown-up doing three jobs, not three more people moving in to help with the rent. Say a party gets to be too much for your little one. The Protector gets you both out of there, and on the way home the Nurturer tells them they did great, even though mostly they hid in the bathroom. And the Guide helps them think twice about the next weird party they might go to, avoiding another cycle of bathroom vacationing. 🙈 It’s you that needs to develop these qualities.

Also, your inner child’s worth is not dependent on these qualities… It has its own worth, and its own qualities, like playing, being tender, not taking life too seriously, being creative and wondering at the world. Those belong in your relationship with it too, not just the wounds.
