You don’t hand your adult judgment over to someone who will take care of you, and you don’t try to become a little kid again for real. In this way, you can access all of the positive aspects of what it can be like to feel younger, such as feeling wonder, silliness, approaching things through the senses more, feeling it’s ok to be vulnerable, to want someone to take care of you, to feel need, and to want to offload some decision making, while your adult part stays present. "It is okay for a younger part of me to talk. But I understand that the adult in me is always going to be here, listening, evaluating, protecting me and stopping me if needed. No younger part of me, no guide, no recording and no other person gets to make my adult decisions for me." That way you get back what was good about being young. You can be curious, spontaneous and playful, things can feel vivid again, and you can say what you need and feel things quickly. You leave behind the part of being a kid where you had to obey, couldn’t do much for yourself, got scolded, and didn’t know better yet.
