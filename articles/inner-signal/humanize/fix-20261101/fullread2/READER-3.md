## F3.1
NOW: Some people make themselves too fragmented by starting to work with parts. ‘Here’s a part! Here’s another one! I’ll get twelve of them in a room and give them all an office to hang around in!’ So it’s better to start from the Sacred.
PUBLISHED: Some people feel more fragmented when they begin by naming twelve parts and assigning everybody office space. Start with the sacred whole when that happens.
KIND: lost
SEVERITY: 1
WHY: The published advice is for the people this happens to ("when that happens"); "So it’s better to start from the Sacred" makes it a rule for everyone, and "the Sacred" with a capital is vaguer than the published "sacred whole", which ties back to the heading.

## F3.2
NOW: Start like this: ‘I’m approaching this from the universal heart. I’m approaching this from my Higher Self. I’m approaching this from the part of me that knows it belongs to life before it belongs to any wound, or role, or family, or group, or identity, or ideology, or story. So can you please tell me what’s going to be true and kind and timely and stabilizing and useful here?’ Etc.
PUBLISHED: “I begin from the universal heart. I begin from Higher Self. I begin from the part of me that belongs to life before it belongs to any wound, role, family, group, identity, ideology, or story. Please help me understand what is true, kind, timely, stabilizing, and useful.”
KIND: unclear
SEVERITY: 1
WHY: "So can you please tell me" asks a "you" the sentences before never name (they speak from the Higher Self, not to it), and "what’s going to be true" puts the truth in the future; the published asks for help understanding what is true, which also fits the guide's later point that the Higher Self is a vantage point, not a source of answers.
