# Part 11: the guide's claims and instructions (for the contradiction read, E310)

- The subconscious won't answer: there may be no (helpful) answer; ask differently, rest or finish; if trust is the issue ask "What would you need to know in order to trust me?" (answers like set boundaries, do what you said, stop leaving, don't treat knowing as doing).
- The inner child won't appear: if a guard is in the way, talk to it ("You don't have to let me in, just tell me what I need to do to make it safe"); without a child part, work with what you're aware of (preferences, curiosity, beginner experiments); develop a sense of who you are and reparent as you can.
- You get anxious: decide whether to decrease contact or let it continue; if it's getting too bad, stop searching for content and help the body first (feet, seat, body, breath, room); tell your little one and guards you're there, that the guards are trying to protect you and needn't scream; ask if they need you to do something (one step at a time), or to let the body calm down. Chest pain, fainting, suicidal thoughts, psychosis- or mania-like symptoms, severe dissociation, no sleep, urges to harm: get professional help. Appendix D's anxiety toolbox.
- Dissociate or feel unreal: eyes open, move, touch something textured, name 5 things you see, end the deeper practice for the day.
- Grandiose: eat, sleep, chores, humility, someone to disagree with you; "If it's real, it'll still be here after a good night's sleep, some food, some time..."
- Too analytical: stop improving the practice; do the two-hand practice and say "I don't have to do this perfectly. I'm here for a relationship with something deeper than my surface mind."; ask how to make it communion, not performance; silence, rest or doing something: do it. (S-636: one of the practice's four appearances.)
- Too blissful or diffuse: bring it down to something specific ("What's the smallest, most specific thing your love is asking you to do next?").
- Want the hidden truth immediately: don't pry; "Show me the capacity before the content."
- Keep daydreaming: sit up, open your eyes, ask what you're avoiding in the real world. (Check: Part 7's "Stay awake" offers guidance, working through, or avoidance; here it's avoidance only.)
- Spiritual bypass: "Where has divine love not yet descended into the wound?" A profound opening matters, but don't demand your inner child receive it on command; invite your little one in (they may go numb or call BS; be humble, grateful and patient with their rejection); prayer may have felt like abandonment or condemnation, or a teacher betrayed you: explore, maybe not more of the same practice. Practical help while life feels too small; curiosity about more happiness without believing explanations; some people have enough love and meaning to just live; spirituality needn't be another assignment.
- Endless processing: a real wound and a checking loop can coexist; ask whether processing again changes how you feel, understand, decide or act, or is reassurance-checking; covering the same ground is sometimes okay (grief, practice); with a checking loop, let the thought be without engaging and get on with what you chose; no meeting with every thought, no ritual before living. "What would the Guide choose to do now?"
- Using hypnosis without changing life: "What can I do to show my little one I'm for real?"; one small repeatable thing; a big dropped gesture proves their doubt, a small kept thing changes their mind (his words).
- What not to do: inner experiences aren't commands or proof (reality-test); no seeking downloads or a hobby of demons/jinn/Unattached Burdens (stabilize, get help if reality testing or functioning weakens); no self-hypnosis while driving, bathing, swimming, operating equipment, supervising children, or needing active attention; don't force memories, prove what happened, or skip medical attention because you can force symptoms down (his preferred wording, EM-11: "hypnosis is for pain btw"); don't ignore body needs or emotions before understanding the signals; don't extend altered states indefinitely; keep inner roles in proportion (inner child not running adult life, Protector not shutting out love, Nurturer not ignoring consequences, Guide not ignoring the inner child; analysis and tenderness); no NLP to force down guard parts, fake certainty, manipulate, or heal against the body; doubt, distraction, resistance, no response or "No" aren't signs it's working; the unconscious isn't the only safety (limits, safe place, a tested exit); normal sensations aren't proof of trauma, illness, toxins, possession, attachments, transformations or subconscious messages; imagined healing isn't physical healing; no hidden commands (a chosen cue must be understood, reviewable, removable; "Take out" may not be enough; nobody activates cues without your knowledge, or uses them for control, identity change or amnesia).
