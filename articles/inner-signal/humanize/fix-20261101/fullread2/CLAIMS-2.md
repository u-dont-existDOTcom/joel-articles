# Part 2: the guide's claims and instructions (for the contradiction read, E310)

- Accidental affirmations (his sister's Positive Magic trick): right after an unwanted thought, say "I take it out of the law of cause and effect"; it interrupts the thought stream with conscious intention. The Buddha: always come back to mindfulness.
- Hypnosis evidence: a 2024 overview of 49 meta-analyses (261 studies) found it strongest for pain, medical procedures, and children and adolescents; it varied by outcome and study quality.
- Self-hypnosis evidence: a systematic review of RCTs found benefit for pain, childbirth, children, stress, anxiety; more useful when learned as a skill and practiced several times than when listening to audio. So the guide teaches a skill, not recordings.
- Mayo Clinic: hypnosis around earlier-life stressful events may bring up strong emotions; other treatments may suit historical trauma better.
- Use self-hypnosis to: regulate yourself, talk to yourself, reparent yourself, gain clarity, change habits, make life safer now, integrate emotions, take action in ordinary life.
- Don't use it to: force trauma memories, prove trauma through memories, suppress medical symptoms, destabilize yourself, or replace appropriate clinical care. (Check against the evidence for pain, and against "What not to do", where Joel kept Emulate's medical-symptoms line: "hypnosis is for pain btw", EM-11.)
- Left/right brain: "syncing the hemispheres" is a description, not neuroscience; a study of 1011 people found no globally left- or right-brained people; some functions are lateralized.
- The modes (verbal, analytical, imaginal, emotional, somatic, intuitive, relational, spiritual) cooperate; love and analysis, intuition and rationality both present; the body says yes, no, not yet; the imagination opens the field and the adult chooses the next real action; each mode corrects the other.
- It has to show up in life: kinder behavior, humility, better judgment, real service, a more workable ordinary life.
- Self-hypnosis worked better for him than a professional hypnotist (trust: his analytical mind checked everything; precision: he can catch a wrong word or a body no); a skilled guide still helps with blind spots, regulation and safety.
- Loveyhuasca amplified it; practicing right after sky hypnosis gave stronger results; benefits can come the next morning or days later (the heart and sacral chakra session); self-hypnosis seemed to extend a chrunga trip; integrate rather than prolong an altered state.
- Without drugs, self-hypnosis or meditation may work better inside a lucid dream.
- The subconscious = all processes beneath conscious awareness; work with it as one mind or as an ecology of parts; both are maps, no priesthood.
- Parts give precision, the Higher Self gives coherence; communion is the movement between them.
- Working with the whole: invoke the wise subconscious. Working with the ecology: notice what each part wants (child parts love, guards safety, critic standards, fantasy parts possibility, body memories recognition, social part belonging, spiritual part God/source/all beings, wiser part truth); start with unity (invoke the Higher Self), differentiate (which part is afraid, protecting, avoiding, knows, too young), return to unity with the adult leading with love.
