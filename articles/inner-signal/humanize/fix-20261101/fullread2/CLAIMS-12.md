# Part 12: the guide's claims and instructions (for the contradiction read, E310)

- Get help now: imminent danger, very confused, can't keep yourself safe, concerning physical symptoms (chest pain, fainting). Prompt professional help: new or worsening suicidal thoughts, severe insomnia, new or worsening manic or psychotic symptoms, dissociation affecting function. Ongoing help: recurring panic, compulsions, destabilization from substances, major relationship instability.
- Probably enough on your own: shorter, warm practice, a small tractable subject, a full return, tracking one target for a week or two; if functioning worsens or you grow certain about unconfirmable memories, reassess instead of pushing.
- With a trained person present: trauma-focused regression, affect/somatic bridging (to find a traumatic origin you don't know), substantial flashbacks or dissociation; a trauma-informed clinician trained in that method; ask how they handle uncertainty and unexpected reactions; hypnosis can't tell you what did or didn't happen, even with a clinician.
- Trauma history, crying, wanting difficult feelings don't by themselves mean you need more support; choose your range; people differ; feeling you're handling it isn't support.
- Major trauma guidelines: trauma-focused CBT variants (CPT, PE) or EMDR; self-hypnosis and inner child work can support them; high-risk trauma work needs a bigger container.
- Ordinary-life functioning is the feedback: more loving, honest, functional, relational, grounded, precise: keep going gently; less stable: don't go deeper; sleep, food, grounding, boundaries, support.
- Closing: none of them are enemies (subconscious, body, sharp mind, the braced guard); most were trying to help without being introduced. Becoming a trustworthy adult matters more than trance depth. Listen to your little one until they come closer; keep many small promises so guards can relax; love yourself in a way your thinking accepts, think so your love doesn't become fantasy; take it back into regular life, where the adult keeps its promises.
- The Inner Signal app: voice hypnosis, information, language help, feedback on what you report; ask about a hypnotist you work with; self-hypnosis needs no app; a practitioner is optional. Enter via the page. ChatGPT version: upload a picture, drawing, poem or song; make sure the chat can see/hear it; the file, its meaning to you and the app's reading are three different things; you decide if it's right, no need to defend your medium. Even with voice the app can't feel your body, has no embodied intuition, can't know what you don't report; not a therapist. Survey; consultation.
- Appendices are optional extras (precision, safety, scripts).
- Appendix A: the subconscious isn't an LLM, but how you ask shapes the answer (prompt engineering helps you be more present, honest, loving; never more controlling). Seven elements: role, context, intention, constraint (no forcing, no false certainty, don't ask before you can handle the answer), output (image, phrase, sensation, memory, action, question, symbol, silence, rest), evaluation (true, kind, timely, stabilizing, specific, testable), iteration (reality's feedback).
- Let silence count as an answer, or the analytical mind will invent one: "If you need more time or the best and safest answer is silence, ... deeper state of comfortable rest. A peaceful silence would be a successful answer."
- Core prompts: clarity (true, kind, timely; if too intense, as a symbol, body signal, next step; capacity to be peaceful before the content of the wounding); resistance ("What are you protecting me from? What would help you trust me 1% more?"); inner child (what do you want me to know, what do you need me to do today); Protector (what would a minimally competent adult do in the external world so all of me is safe); Guide (most aligned with truth, love, life, all beings); intuition (intuition signals, analysis deciphers, body gives feedback, reality tests); programming vs. universal heart (family, religion, marketing, erotic programming, political tribe, trauma response, a childhood belonging deal; let those parts soften only as much as is safe; body feedback); after an answer: "What can I do in my ordinary life to show that I heard correctly?"; act only if it's loving, leaves you free and makes sense; nothing big or hard to undo until calm and checked against what you know.
- (His words:) the prompt tells the subconscious what you want in a language it understands; Appendix B's NLP smooths its entry; together the subconscious may feel safer answering; that doesn't make answers truer.
