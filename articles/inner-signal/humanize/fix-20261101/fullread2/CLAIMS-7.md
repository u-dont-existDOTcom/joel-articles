# Part 7: the guide's claims and instructions (for the contradiction read, E310)

- Before you begin: 1) read the induction and Come all the way back while awake; 2) choose what to work on and what you're leaving alone today, how long; it needn't be a problem (love, play, confidence, creativity, communion); leave time to come back and reflect; 3) a safe place (not driving, swimming, or responsible for anyone); if intoxicated and/or more confused than usual, or unable to keep yourself safe, get immediate support instead of exploratory hypnosis; "Already Trancey?" is for orienting when already open, not for going deeper when unstable. (Check: the guide elsewhere practices self-hypnosis on loveyhuasca and a chrunga trip, and lists loveyhuasca under "Already Trancey?": does "intoxicated" contradict that?)
- 4) Decide how to coax yourself (spacious, warm and held, firmer); preview eye closure, coming out and going back in, the end; read a recording's script beforehand; "stop" ends it, "less" asks less of you, "quiet" stops the talking; you can open your eyes and stop, then come all the way back; no part's permission needed to stop.
- 5) New or out of practice: rehearse coming in and out first, no hard stuff yet; make sure you can change state; let it get easier; a no stays a no; the narrator can't add things because you're silent.
- 6) Let your response guide the next step, not the script's expectation; utilize what's there (thought, sound, movement, objection); notice; if nothing changes, notice that too; his dad's "expect the very best, be prepared to take what you get" (also in Affirmations Done Right: repeat, F1.4); language tools in Appendix B and "Let the next response guide you"; keep safety and exit instructions plain.
- Sober induction (3–5 minutes): record, read aloud or say silently; choose an anchor (sound, contact, the two-hand practice) and an opening phrase; same anchor throughout; pause between paragraphs; don't read the directions aloud. Script: eyes rest; notice contact/pressure or a sound; time; return from thoughts to the anchor; something of interest; time like for someone finding their words; the phrase as an invitation; eyes closed or open; count 5 to 1 (anchor, gather, space, listen without preparing an answer, take your time); bring the concern or the wish for contact lightly; "What do you notice now?"; whatever comes (sensations, words, pictures, feelings, movements, nothing yet); with or without words.
- Already Trancey? (his words): for when already open (loveyhuasca, meditation, lucid dreaming, grief, music, prayer, erotic energy, exhaustion, seeing something you love); aim and stabilize, keep it kind; orient (where, date, body, grounding, feet/hands/spine, press thumbs to index fingertips); "Only what I can integrate, please. Patience pays..."; when oriented, set an intention ("Please show me the next truth/symbol/feeling/question/action that is useful for me, that I can integrate").
- Stay awake when the mind escapes: "I'm not going anywhere, and I'm coming more fully here"; sit up, eyes open, feet, natural breath; look at something; feel the spine; why am I daydreaming (guidance, working through, avoidance)? "If I'm meant to be getting guidance, please show me the action...; if I'm avoiding something, bring me back to now and show me what."
- Make the hypnosis more active (his words): if stillness makes you sleepy or escapist, get more alert (relaxed-alert); eyes open; a small hand movement with each suggestion of increasing clarity; stop and see where the mind is; script with thumb and forefinger, clearer each time, then notice any echo, sit up straight and work from this level.
- The two-hand induction: start with the two-hand practice (hand on heart for the Nurturer, on the solar plexus for the vulnerable inner child); breathe gently; imagine divine Love above, the inner child below, the inner adult present; massage the solar plexus; script: send love down, let what comes back come, keep sending if nothing does, listen. (S-636: one of the practice's four appearances, to cut or merge.)
