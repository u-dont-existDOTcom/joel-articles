# Part 8: the guide's claims and instructions (for the contradiction read, E310)

- Sleep induction differs from inner work (where you come back to write and act): the analytical mind can stand down; revelations can wait. Nimja's sleep playlist.
- Fractionation (his words): returning toward ordinary awareness, then deciding whether to go back in; a revolving door to evaluate at different levels; repetition makes transitions easier; add it with intention at some point. Script: open your eyes, come back up, feel the room, decide to stop or continue (soften or close the eyes), either is okay, you can stop at either place. (Part 6 lists unannounced fractionation as a way to take choice away: this one is announced.)
- Deepening, if useful; otherwise stay as awake as you need. Pick one place to go inside (a room in the heart, garden, temple, fire with your Protector, a clear lake, a chair where your adult waits). "Who or what needs to talk first?"
- Optional depth check: release responsibility for making anything happen; the wise subconscious may signal the inner meeting space is ready with a finger twitch, pulse, lift, heat or tingle; don't make it happen or pretend; a sign doesn't mean what comes next is true, only that something may be listening.
- Shortcut back into hypnosis: only after learning an induction and the full return; an unusual word/phrase and/or motion, paired while in a good hypnotic state (not a very emotional or child state, not trauma); his dad's OK-sign mudra to relax, even driving (don't drive in trance); keep the calm-but-alert cue separate from the inward cue; give the cue its meaning (appropriate time and place, a familiar state, change direction or stop any time); come back, use it later; shorten the induction over time or use the long one; no required repetitions, depth or sensation.
- Check the shortcut works: on and off at will, absent when unwanted, change direction or come back up; never follow a narrator, notification or overheard word through it; don't use it to rush into things you shouldn't work on yet. Never use, practice or test it while driving or needing active attention, eyes open or permissive wording notwithstanding.
- Turning it off: stop practicing, remove it from recordings and apps, use your regular induction, tell yourself while awake; it may not vanish right away; problems (unwanted responses, disorientation) need qualified help. Keeping it is fine, but it doesn't keep today's trance going: the session ends when you come back.
- Come all the way back: reverse whatever you did to enter (weight, size, younger state/parts: complete in your body, here and now, body yours, adult mind in control; come back the way you went).
- Generic bring-back: bring only what's useful now, the rest later when you can integrate it; back to this room and day; all deepening/staying instructions finished; only consciously chosen intentions and cues, neither keeps you in the state; "I will be me, in my body and with my adult judgment."
- Count up 1–5 (body and weight, breathing, the room and where you are, fingers/toes/shoulders/eyes, eyes open, session over). Then name the day/date, look left/right, near/far, move, don't stand until steady, check you're oriented (counting doesn't always do it), note only what came up and actions; do something normal.
- If a recording cuts out: "The recording has stopped and the session is over," do the return yourself, press feet or hands into a surface.
- A sleep recording may end in sleep or rest; a waking recording must not leave you altered (a role, lowered judgment or movement, blank, a non-specific reset word, indefinitely).
