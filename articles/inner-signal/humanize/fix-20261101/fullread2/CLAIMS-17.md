# Part 17: the guide's claims and instructions (for the contradiction read, E310)

- A response check (hostile presences), if stable enough to stay present: hand on heart if okay, comfortable breath, aware of the room; you don't have to know what it is; find whether warmth or arm's length helps, keep your boundary either way; you don't have to do what it says. If vulnerable (throat tightness, sadness, fear, protecting something), call your Nurturer; if not better or still hostile, stop and return to body and room. (EM-16, his words:) you needn't find out if it's a guard at once or comfort it quickly; watch whether your response makes you clearer, steadier, less hooked.
- Don't fight from fear: fighting, banishing, hating, proving power, arguing, panicking, turning into the frightened part; prayers, mantras, protection, banishing, exorcism may work for some; fear makes it the center of attention, hate gives it a hook, pride an opponent, panic your nervous system. Say: no need to hate, merge with, obey, or defeat it from fear; return attention to loving awareness.
- Compassion present, door closed: especially if hostile, cold, alien, parasitic; not sentimental love ("stay for tea"), but love with boundaries; posture (sit up, feet grounded, spine straight, shoulders back, chest open, feel your skin's boundary): "While I have compassion for you and any suffering you may be experiencing, this suffering needs to go back where it came from. The boundary of my skin is the boundary of an adult now..."
- This assumes a symbolic experience that responds to grounding and love; it may be psychosis or destabilization: under attack for days, no sleep, can't function, voices commanding you, urges to hurt yourself or others, going manic, can't communicate, can't tell symbolic from normal reality: get help immediately; not for hypnosis.
- The General Rule: more loving, grounded, relational, honest, functional: probably okay to continue gently; more paranoid, sleepless, grandiose, isolated, scared, unreal: don't go deeper; get help. (Also in Part 12, "Your functioning in ordinary life is the feedback", and Appendix G.)
- The cosmic-download humility test: presences or grandiose parts may flatter (special, cosmic info, a mission, dishes beneath you); "Thank you for the imagery, but I don't need your grand performance. Unless the info ... can help me do the dishes, ... keep my promises, take care of my body..., I don't need it." Guidance worth keeping makes ordinary life more beautiful, honest, relational, humble, reliable and useful.
- Don't become an entity hobbyist: it all comes down to keeping love present without giving up your boundaries, and making your mind harder to hook.

- Appendix J, induction library: preview; choose one in advance and what for, and whether to do it at all; any can be light, eyes open, or a no-trance inner contact; notice what happens and choose what next (move, imagine, change sensory channel, nothing, stop); always come fully back. If you can't see images, use words, rhythm, space, touch, movement or knowing.
- 1) Stretch and release: feel the support; stretch or press slightly (really or imagined), then let go; notice any difference; no more than 3 places; let release guide you; what needs it, what support; normal movement, return.
- 2) Supported floating: let the support hold 1% more of your effort (floating without losing contact) or keep the same weight; optional announced fractionation (normal awareness on the in-breath, support on the out-breath); what's easier to hear; keep the support, back to normal weight and firm contact, return.
- 3) Rhythmic breath or pulse: breath, hum, finger tap, outside sound, silent counting, or no rhythm; "here, and here again"; notice what you attend to between; stop and notice the first beat (no blank mind needed); clearer words, feelings, needs, actions? normal rhythm, return.
- 4) Unwinding object: for a concern with several strands; yarn, bubbles or knots placed beside you; look; label if needed; how many; which needs warmth, protection, action; keep one in attention, the others visible; return.
