# Part 16: the guide's claims and instructions (for the contradiction read, E310)

- The council, internal first: ask your inner child, adult, conscious and subconscious mind, intuition, analytical mind; if it's bigger, ask outside too; the bigger and less reversible, the more you reach outside (people who love you, are like you, have done it). Going it alone can be right, but as the last step after counsel. The opposite danger: giving your power away to an authority; others get neither automatic trust nor dismissal.
- Peers (Hearthwork): equal time, warmth, confidentiality, respect for "no", attention without interpretation, advice or fixing; lend each other the Nurturer and Protector, not the Guide; switch roles; a careful question can help you hear your own Guide; peers don't interpret your experience.
- Forgiveness without bypass: can't be forced (it needs understanding, or it's bypassing); staying angry forever lets the injury run your present; both are true: they hurt us and must answer for it, boundaries matter, and one day we may have forgiven or at least not be run by resentment; you don't need to forgive first to get safe, set boundaries, hold someone accountable, get justice or get better.
- They could only make the moves their accumulated nature allowed (conditioning, history, pain, capacity); harm, accountability and consequences remain; it helps you stop expecting what they can't give.
- To the hurt part: "You don't have to forgive before you're ready. I won't use spirituality to silence your pain. And I won't build our whole life around this wound forever." Questions: what happened, impact, boundaries, what shaped them, what's theirs and mine, what accountability requires, how to let go without denying it.
- Forgiving yourself: name what you did without taking on what wasn't yours; feel the remorse and the harm; don't make yourself a permanent criminal; put it right or prevent repetition; then stop holding yourself hostage (responsibility stays); come back now and then to how you became someone who could do that, until understanding changes how you feel.
- Appendix G, altered states: not a recommendation of substances, but many first did self-hypnosis, inner child contact or Higher Self dialogue while altered; learn inner child work sober first, with enough Nurturer and Protector (borrow from someone steady who won't run your trip). (Check: Part 2 and "Already Trancey?" practice self-hypnosis on loveyhuasca; Part 7 says intoxicated = get support instead of exploratory hypnosis.)
- Don't use altered states to force a revelation; use them to get more in tune with love and better your life. (His words:) a dramatic opening to love is a reference point while you build everyday access; if you can't give that love to your inner child, deal with why (a guard that has seen love with strings), don't ramp up the experience.
- Ask: help me be grounded, take only what I can integrate, remember love, come back when it's time, change one thing in my life.
- Don't use self-hypnosis to keep the state going indefinitely, force memories back, make yourself more afraid, skip sleep, put off medical problems, or become less communicative. If it isn't making you more loving, grounded, honest, rested, communicative and able to do your responsibilities, stop going deeper and integrate. (Check against Part 14: "I don't use NLP or hypnosis in general, to do more than initial memory retrieval work.")
- Stabilize before you interpret: self-hypnosis is powerful in altered states (loveyhuasca, meditation, lucid dreams, grief, sexual energy, prayer, no sleep, plant medicine); you may open faster than your adult can put things in context; a meaningful-feeling state may be the inner child, a guard, shame/sadness/anger surfacing, or an overloaded body, chaos, no sleep; scary images or amazing "downloads" don't necessarily mean what you think; first ask "What kind of problem is happening?" (his trip-sitting triage guide).
- Appendix H, demons, jinn, hostile presences, "Unattached Burdens" (he speaks from experience): in altered states you may meet something intrusive, hateful, seductive or mocking; whether it's literally a demon doesn't matter; what matters is dealing with it so you end up safer, clearer, more loving, grounded, less hooked. First the basics: medical danger, environment, body, overstimulation, opening too much; open your eyes, ground, leave bad influences, stop stimulating your mind; don't go further or get curious. "If this can be integrated in this moment, it's welcome. If not, it can wait... The adult part of me is still here..." Danger signs (harm, feared psychosis, no sleep, paranoia, chest pain, seizure, overheating, too much of something, can't function): the session is over; get real-world help.
