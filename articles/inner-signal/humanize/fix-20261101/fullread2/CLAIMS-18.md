- 8) Resource embodiment: pick a quality you understand (warmth, boundary, steadiness, curiosity, dignity); imagine 1% more of it (or observe it); what's different (body, voice); repeat; come back keeping the quality and an action, leaving the imagined you behind ("I am me").
- 9) No-trance inner contact (his words): pace (notice reality: what you see, the day, your feet; "Hopefully your name didn't suddenly change to Larry."); ask a clear question, listen gently; keep agency (an answer, or "I don't know"; rephrase; ask someone else); write whatever comes (word, sensation, impulse, objection, silence as a signal); don't treat it as validation of a theory; the adult self answers the same question, ideally by writing or doing; reality-test the signal, maybe a simple action; no elaborate exit since no trance.
