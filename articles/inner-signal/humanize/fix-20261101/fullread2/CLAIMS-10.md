# Part 10: the guide's claims and instructions (for the contradiction read, E310)

- Finish without forcing a revelation: when you've done enough, ask what would help you leave things where they are for now (thank parts; something in real life: a meal, a boundary, rest, a repair, a promise kept); tell your little one, "That's enough for today. We're going back to the life we're building." (his words).
- Use Come all the way back even if you stopped partway; then write a few lines: what was there before, what you did, what happened, what you're unsure about, phrases, body signals, what the inner child needed, what a guard wanted, boundaries, repairs, what not to force; only what you noticed, not the whole cast.
- Throughout: let the analytical part comment or object; check things and make commitments after you're back; if it says no to going deeper or to continuing, stop; you can stop any time. Images can be meaningful without being memories; seeing an image or feeling a release doesn't mean you're right about what's going on.
- The 15-minute daily practice: no second script; the same process (gather attention, notice, respond, wait, come back); a few minutes in, the middle at its own pace, a few minutes out; 15 minutes is an appointment, not a rule for your feelings; 5 minutes of silent listening is plenty to see what happens; interrupt to change direction any time.
- Other entries: 3 things seen/heard/felt, then 2, then 1; count 5 to 1 and listen; choose one entry, don't stack them.
- After a few runs with recordings, shorten them or practice without them; keep them if you like them; the shortcut cue can shorten a familiar induction.
- Afterwards ask: what needed warmth, protection, a different direction (truth, love, life); something to explore or a capacity to build; what can you now carry yourself that you used to borrow? Plan specifically ("If [cue], then I will [small action]"); check later how you sleep, relate and function, not how impressive the session felt.
- Weekly deeper practice: more time once or twice a week when useful; not necessarily intense; start from the Higher Self or neutral witnessing; follow what appears; bring in an adult function when needed; check nothing important is bypassed; leave time to come out fully and do the normal thing, ideally right away (so the subconscious sees the adult heard and acted; not another beautiful meeting with dishes still in the sink).
- Capacity before content: if medically unsafe, panicking, sleep-deprived, nauseous, deeply destabilized, manic, psychosis-like or chemically overwhelmed, stabilize first; if knowing would destabilize you, it isn't for you to know directly now; ask for capacity before content. (Check against the guide's use of altered states, and Part 7's "intoxicated ... get support instead".)
- The subconscious may know things the conscious self isn't ready for (sexuality, grief, trauma, family, betrayal, shame, vocation, a relationship, illness fears, spirituality, addiction, identity, moral issues). Don't bully it ("Tell me NOW!"): flooding, panic, false certainty, confabulation, dissociation; when flooded, adult capacities are harder to reach; regulate and get support first; ask in a tolerable way (what would make me 5% better at knowing it; symbolic form; what to do to be safe without the content; do I need to know now or get support first, and who could help).
- The safer script: please don't break it open violently; help me know it safely; prepare, ground and support me before revelation, memory and intensity; show me the next thing to do.
- Imagery isn't all literal, especially around trauma and memory (symbolizing, compressing, distorting, dramatizing, culture, fears); treat it as an image; get outside help when needed.
- Troubleshooting, nothing happens (his words): try it smaller; "Show me a body sensation." "Show me just a word." "Show me whether today is for listening or resting."
- It feels fake: what feels fake (words, image, expectations, a role)? Not having an inner voice or images isn't resistance or a missing grown-up; correct an unwanted invitation; a protecting part: ask what it protects you from, whether it fears you'll lie to yourself again or that this is a performance; a yes can sting (you may have fooled yourself before); "Tell me what you remember, I'm listening."
- The inherited parent script enforcing a belonging rule: find the rule; comfort your little one first; answer firm or funny, not mean; build a believable grown-up voice from ordinary evidence.
- If the fake feeling says you lack the grown-up ability yet: don't argue with an inner voice; if you can observe yourself, borrow warmth, boundary or direction from someone good enough (family, friend, famous person; for warmth, someone who liked you as you were); try a sentence or action that rings true.
