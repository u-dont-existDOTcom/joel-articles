## F5.1
NOW: Ask yourself why you're daydreaming. Is it to get guidance, or because you're working through something, or are you avoiding something?
PUBLISHED: “Is this daydream guidance, emotional digestion, or avoidance?”
KIND: garbled
SEVERITY: 1
WHY: The question changes grammar twice ("Is it to get guidance, or because ..., or are you ...") so the three options don't line up as one question.

## F5.2
NOW: As you move one of your hands, you're going to move deliberately, and as soon as you stop moving deliberately, you'll focus on what you've got.
PUBLISHED: Keep the eyes open and pair a small repeated hand movement with increasing clarity; stop the movement and notice what remains without trying to disappear inward.
KIND: garbled
SEVERITY: 3
WHY: "Move deliberately" and "focus on what you've got" are circular and vague, so the instruction no longer says to repeat a small movement while clarity increases, or to notice what remains once it stops.
