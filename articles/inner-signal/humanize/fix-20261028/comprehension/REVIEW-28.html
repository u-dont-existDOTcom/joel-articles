Affirmations Done Right

P2Another quote I use all the time from my dad… when someone asks me “How’s it going?” I say “Better & better!” This really confuses a lot of people. Some think it’s amazing that I’m always doing better. Some ask if I was sick or something. But it’s doing multiple jobs. On the one hand, it’s deflecting from the fake question because most people don’t actually want to hear how you are doing in detail anyway. On the other hand, more importantly, it’s reprogramming your mind to remove you from tunnel vision. You might be sad now, but if you say, “I am sad” then you’re subtly identifying with the sadness and it keeps you stuck. It’s best to say, “I’m feeling sad now, but I’m doing better and better, every day, in every way!” That’s more like a mission statement than a historical analysis. False positives, and turning the cue off

P1A downside of synchronicity cues is that they can lead to false positives. We create an association in our head on purpose when we rehearse what to do when we notice a synchronicity; however, when you are absorbed in something and/or when you are destabilized, you might think that you saw a synchronicity when in fact there wasn't any. Research has shown that people that score high on apophenia tests also score high for detecting patterns that were not really there, as well as scoring high on psychosis spectrum traits (see Blain and colleagues, and Grant and colleagues) (this is not saying that noticing patterns is bad; in fact, it's not a pathology).

P2If you feel that synchronicity cues are making these things worse (e.g. you feel like you have to look for them all the time, you can't sleep, you feel anxious, you are sure that random things happen in your life in order to tell you something, you can't tell the difference between noticing something and interpreting what it might mean, etc.), you should deactivate synchronicity cues.

P3You can do that by not rehearsing them anymore and by saying to yourself (when you are fully awake) that you don't want to use synchronicity as a cue anymore (e.g. "From now on, I am no longer going to use noticing synchronicity as a cue for waking up.
