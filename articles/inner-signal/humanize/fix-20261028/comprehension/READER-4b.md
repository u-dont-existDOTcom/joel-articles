## F4b.1
NOW: One thing you're doing, one anchor/image you're going with at first, one way of coming back, and feel free to set a tone (warm, bounded, directed, neutral witnessing, whatever) for yourself.
PUBLISHED: Choose one purpose, one starting anchor or image, and a way back. Decide whether warmth, a firm boundary, direction, or neutral witnessing would help.

SEVERITY: 1
WHY: "One thing you're doing" no longer says it is the purpose of the session, the verbless list runs on into "feel free to set a tone", and the choice of tone becomes an optional extra.
