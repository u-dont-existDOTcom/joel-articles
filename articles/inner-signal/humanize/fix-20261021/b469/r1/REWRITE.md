# Appendix C: The extended de-hypnosis checklist

Use this checklist when something is pressing on you too hard, whether it’s a person, an ad, an ideology, a spiritual teaching, a romantic pull or a voice inside you, stuff like that.

You could say something like, “Okay, I notice that I’m being influenced. I don’t have to obey it, and I don’t have to attack it either. I can slow down and ask what it’s taking for granted, and who it benefits. I can notice what my body is telling me and hold off on deciding what that proves. And I can ask what love, truth, humility and ordinary life would need from me here.

If it’s true, it can handle my questions. Real love can handle my boundaries, and real guidance will still be there after I sleep on it. And something that’s really for me won’t ask me to give up my adult self.”

Before acting on it, look for the usual signs, like urgency, secrecy, shame, a threat that you’ll be left out or a promise that you’re special, that kind of thing. Watch for a sexual charge too, or spiritual superiority. Be wary if it demands money or sex from you. Demands for your loyalty or your silence are signs too, and so is any demand for obedience.

It’s a warning if your choices keep getting narrower. Same if asking questions gets treated as betrayal, or your resistance gets treated like a pathology. Or if ordinary life starts to look small next to it.

Notice whether your child part is being seduced or scared. Being rescued can be part of it too. And check whether your adult self is still allowed to say no.

If you see several of these, slow down. Don’t go deeper into trance, and don’t make a decision you can’t undo. Come back to the room and your body, eat, sleep, give it time, get some counsel and so on.

You can tell a good guide because your adult self gets stronger with them, and they can take your no. A bad guide tries to replace your adult self. They’ll call your no resistance, ego, fear, low vibration, trauma, sin, you name it, and they’ll say it proves you need more of their guidance.

# Troubleshooting

It could also be the voice of your inherited inner parent that you are picking up, and it is trying to enforce a rule about how you should be to belong to them. Again, it isn't useful to argue with this part, but find out what rule it is enforcing and then work on finding some ordinary evidence to build a more believable 'grown up' voice of your own.

# Appendix G: Altered states

So what you think may be an altered state of consciousness that's meaningful, may or may not actually be meaningful - it could be your inner child, it could be a protector part, it could be all of the things you're ashamed of / sad about / angry about / etc, coming out to be dealt with. Or it could be that your body is overloaded, your environment is chaotic, you haven't slept enough, etc, etc, etc. Regardless of how scary an image is, regardless of the lighting that came with the "download", regardless of how "bad" you feel, it doesn't necessarily mean what you think it does!
