## Conflicts in the rewrite

### 1. Appendix C, opening self-talk
- Rewrite: "My body is telling me something, but I don’t have to decide yet what that proves."
- Essay, What not to do: "Do not treat inner voices, images, autonomous movements, or body signals as commands or proof."
- A reader would think the body's signal does prove something and only the verdict waits ("yet"). The published line says to notice it "without assuming what it proves."

### 2. Appendix C, the four tests
- Rewrite: "Would real love mind my boundaries?"
- Essay, The Leader, Guide, or Guru: "Wanting closeness is legitimate; punishing someone for needing space is a different strategy." Also The Most Extreme Example: "Legitimate influence can survive questions, sleep, boundaries, and time."
- A reader would think love that is bothered by a boundary isn't real. The essay's test is whether love survives the boundary, and it calls wanting closeness legitimate as long as nobody is punished for needing space.

### 3. Let the next response guide you, the rewrite's first sentence
- Rewrite: "bring in a “Nurturer”, “Protector”, “Leader”, etc to help you either change the “tone” of the interaction, set boundaries"
- Essay, The inner child, the guards, and the three inner adults: "They aren’t three literal beings. They’re three ways the present-day person reparents the inner child."
- This sentence also conflicts with Joel's 2026-10-21 position that the guards (protector parts) are not the Protector. Earlier in the same subsection (Offer the care that is actually wanted), unchanged text says "When a protector is recognizable, listen to its concern without demanding that it step aside." The rewrite's "a “Protector”" uses the same article.
- A reader would think the three are an open-ended set ("etc") of helper figures who assist you. They could also take "a Protector" for the protector part they just met, when the essay means the three functions of the adult you build.

### 4. Let the next response guide you, the rewrite's second sentence
- Rewrite: "You don’t have to talk to them, just use them to get whatever function you need"
- Essay, Appendix B, Running the whole NLP sequence: "Bring in the three adults. Nurturer warms, Protector makes it safe, Leader points a direction."
- A reader would take the roles as a pool of helpers to draw "whatever function you need" from. The essay gives each role one job: warmth, boundary or direction.
