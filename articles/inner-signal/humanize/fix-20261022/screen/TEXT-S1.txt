Using induction can be helpful to gather your attention and bring in information which you weren’t quite in a receptive state to hear before. It’s not about surrender, playing asleep, or doing what someone else tells you. If you are sober, distracted, analytical, tough or guarded then some actual induction may be helpful. If you’re already trancey - meditating, on loveyhuasca, lucid dreaming, in grief, responding to music or prayer, tired, or otherwise in an intense state, then jump straight to the orientation and containment below.
Make yourself more trancey than you need to be - it’s optional!
Traditionally an induction is the getting ready part of a session, and traditionally inductions are about gathering attention and going deeper. Both of these are useful, and the best inductions are utilizing - based on what is happening right now and they are creating what is needed on the fly. Going deeper can be done by counting, it can be done conversationally, there are lots of ways. It can also be done through stories, movement, sound, etc, etc, etc. The only thing you need to do is be conscious of using those methods, so it’s not a secret induction by an untrained person.
Before you do an induction it’s good to have a simple target, and see if you can get the suggestion you want before you start. Then you know what to induct for and enhance after. All you need to do is find a way to make yourself receptive to your own voice (or messages, or feelings, or whatever) that you use. It’s not a ceremony. You don’t have to go all the way to the end before you can get a response.
Keep it simple. One reason for the trance, one starting anchor or image, one way to get back. Do you want it to be warm, or firm, or directed, or neutral witnessing? Do you want to float, or go down stairs, or through gardens, or through a series of inner rooms, or to a place of colored light, or have a dialogue with your child part, etc. You can change metaphors, but do it consciously.
There’s no need for any imagery at all if you don’t want it. It can be sensation, rhythm, movement, words, spatial, simple knowing, or no response at all. Keep the words consistent with the world you’re creating. If you are counting, make that count meaningful in that world. When you are finishing, make the journey back the reverse of how you got there. It’s OK to use bits from other worlds (like making something warm or steady) but don’t use things that change your identity.
It can be helpful to choose one verb, one sensory quality, a few words to say over and over, and a transition word or phrase to use throughout your script. Read your script out loud to yourself. It will probably be full of long elegant sentences that turn into soup when you actually use them in an induction.
Read this whole induction, and especially the come all the way back bit, when you’re totally awake and clear. Make some choices about what you want to work with, what you want to avoid, and how long you want to spend. Is it to get more love, play, confidence, creativity, communion, etc? Leave time to come back, and think about what’s happening afterwards.
Make sure that you’re somewhere where you won’t put yourself in danger - not driving a car, in the water, near machinery, or responsible for someone else. If you are intoxicated or otherwise acutely confused, please deal with that first - the already open? version below may be useful but don’t try to do exploratory hypnosis while you’re not able to take care of yourself.
Decide what kind of tone you want to hear - do you need to feel allowed, or encouraged, or cajoled, or what? Read or imagine the closing here, as you may be asked to close your eyes, exit and come in again, and come out. If possible, read this script if you are playing a recording. It’s always OK to stop - just say so, or say you want less, or just say quiet, or open your eyes. You don’t have to ask any part of you if it’s OK to stop.
If you haven’t done this before, practice the entering and coming out bit before you try to deal with a hard subject. You don’t have to be watching yourself all the time, just know that you can change things, and make what you like easier and less work. If something is no, it’s still no afterwards. If you aren’t saying it, it won’t be there.
As above, this is utilizing. If what you are saying isn’t working the way you expected, let your next bit of induction be based on what’s happening, not on what you want to happen. If nothing seems to be happening, that may be all you need, but do make sure that you know how to take care of yourself and how to get out. If you find stillness makes you sleepy, or too diffuse, or that you are prone to escapism, then you can use hypnosis to make yourself more aware, rather than less. Keep your eyes open and use a repeated movement with your hand, focusing on becoming more and more clear headed with each repetition. Then stop the movement and focus on staying with the level you have achieved, rather than going inside your head.
Bring to mind “ I am going to keep my eyes open. Each time I press my thumb against my forefinger and release, I am going to become more and more clear headed. Again. Again. One more time. Now I am going to stop doing that movement deliberately and notice the first second after I stop. Is there an echo of the movement, or not. I will work at this level of awareness, keeping myself upright. “
The Two-Hand Induction This induction was something I threw together for a session and I like how it turned out. (Place one hand on your heart, and the other on your solar plexis.) If the hand on your heart were the Nurturer within, and the hand on your solar plexis were a vulnerable part of you, a child perhaps, that needed the Nurturer’s love. Now take a few gentle breaths, and remember: Love above, child below, and adult in between is present.
As you breathe, let the Nurturer send love to the child, and let the child send its signal up to the Nurturer, over and over again.
Inductions for Sleep The purpose of innerwork inductions is to get you into a state where you can then get back to your adult, write things down, and act on them. When using a sleep induction, however, you don’t want to get to that place. You don’t want to do analysis, you just want to sleep. Therefore, the instructions should be a little different. You don’t want revelations at this time. Just allow this to wait until you are ready to work. To go directly to a sleep session, check out my sleep playlist here .
Fractionation (Optional) One technique which can be used during a hypnosis session is to come back out of it, and then allow the person to go back in again, deliberately choosing to. This technique is called fractionation . It is important to always let the person know you are using this technique, as you don’t want them to think every answer must be “deeper.”
A sample script you can use is: “Open your eyes now, and return to full awareness. If you wish to continue the session, simply close or soften your eyes, and allow yourself to again become absorbed. Otherwise, you can open your eyes and come back out.
Allow yourself to become as absorbed as is useful to you, or be as awake as you need to be.
Picture yourself in a room/garden/temple/in front of a fire with your Protector nearby/in a lake/on a chair, or whatever place you chose, as an Adult, waiting to receive whomever/whatever wants to talk to you first.
Trance Depth Check (Optional) As you wait, give up the responsibility to make things happen.
If it would be useful, your wise subconscious mind may allow one of your fingers to twitch, pulse, lift, warm, tingle, or otherwise become subtlely noticeable to you to indicate that you have gone as deep as you need to go in order to meet with whomever/whatever you have come to meet with.
Do not try to make this happen, or fake it. Simply wait in love, and see what happens.
If your finger twitches, warms up, or doesn’t respond at all, it doesn’t mean it is true or false. It simply means that perhaps a part of you is listening.
Hypnosis Shortcut This is something you can work on after you have a good induction and come back that you like. My dad has a mudra (hand gesture) which helps him relax, even while he is driving. He touches two of his fingers together, similar to an “OK” sign. I don’t recommend trying to go into hypnosis while driving, however. It is important to keep the two separate.
Select a word, phrase, and/or gesture you want to use to trigger yourself into hypnosis. While in a hypnotic state, but not at the peak of emotion, nor in child state, nor in the presence of any traumatic scenes, associate the word/phrase/gesture with the idea that you are in a receptive state. Let the person/voice in the recording know that this is your definition for the word/phrase/gesture.
“I now associate this word/phrase/gesture with deliberately putting myself into a receptive state when I have taken the time to be in a place where it is appropriate to do so. I can also use it to change places or come out of a state.”
Come all the way out of the state, and at a later time, when it is appropriate, begin a session as you normally would, and use your new shortcut. It will take some time, as you will need to use your longer induction some, and your shortcut some, to train yourself. You don’t need to do it a certain number of times, you don’t need to go in really deep instantly, and you don’t need to feel any particular way. Just do it when it is appropriate, and see what happens.
As you practice, make sure to check and see if it is only going in when you want it to, and if you can change places or come out when you want to. You don’t want to accidentally create a trigger that will make you go into a state when you are being spoken to, when you see a notification pop up, or hear someone else say the word/phrase. Going in faster doesn’t mean you can deal with more, so don’t feel you need to use it all the time.
You should never try to go into hypnosis while driving or doing anything that requires you to be actively engaged. It is not safe to even try this, even if you are opening your eyes and saying “if it would be useful.”
If you ever decide you don’t want to use your shortcut anymore, stop rehearsing it, and go back to using your longer induction. If it is on a recording, you can create a new recording without it, or remove the active instructions from an app you may be using. Simply state, while awake, that you no longer wish to use the shortcut, and that the next time you go into an altered state it will be the end of the session. If the shortcut seems to still be there, or if you are having problems coming out of altered states, seek out qualified help. If you do want to use the shortcut for another session in the future, know that the next time you are in an altered state it will be the end of this one.
