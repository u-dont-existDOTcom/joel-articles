Here’s a checklist for when you find yourself in that place where you feel overly pressed upon by something. It could be a person, an ad, an ideology, spiritual teaching, romantic attraction, an inner voice, etc. “Okay. I’m being influenced. I don’t have to obey. I don’t have to attack. Let me slow it down and investigate. What assumptions are being made? Who benefits from this? What is my body telling me? (Don’t assume you know what it means.) What would love do? What would truth do? What would humility do? What would an ordinary person do?”
Remember: if it’s true, it will survive questioning. If it’s really love, it will survive boundaries. If it’s really guidance, it will still be there in the morning. If it’s really for you, it will not require you to give up being an adult.
Before you do anything, notice if any of the following (usual) tricks are being used: urgency, secrecy, shame, threat of exclusion, promise of specialness, sexual charge, spiritual superiority, demand for money, demand for sex, demand for loyalty, demand for silence, demand for obedience.
Other things to notice: is this urging you to limit your choices? Is questioning being framed as bad (a bad thing about you)? Is your resistance being treated as bad (pathologized)? Is ordinary life being framed as too small, as something you’re supposed to outgrow?
Is your inner child being seduced, terrified, rescued? Does being an adult (and saying no) remain an option?
If you notice a few of these, slow the fuck down. Don’t go deeper into the trance. Don’t make irreversible decisions. Get back in your body. Come back to the room. Then do whatever is necessary (eat, sleep, wait, seek counsel, etc.)
A good guide helps you become an adult. A good guide can handle no. A bad guide can’t handle no, because a bad guide is trying to step into the adult role for you. Therefore, no will be framed as bad (resistance, ego, fear, low vibration, trauma, sin, etc.) or as proof you need more guidance.
