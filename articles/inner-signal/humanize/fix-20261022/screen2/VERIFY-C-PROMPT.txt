## Pair 1 (C1)

PUBLISHED:
Use this checklist when a person, advertisement, ideology, spiritual teaching, romantic pull, or inner voice is pressing too hard.

“I notice influence is happening. I do not need to obey it or attack it. I can slow down. I can ask what is being presupposed. I can ask who benefits. I can notice what my body is signaling without assuming what it proves. I can ask what love, truth, humility, and ordinary life would require.

If this is true, it will survive questions. If this is love, it will survive boundaries. If this is guidance, it will survive sleep. If this is for me, it will not require me to abandon my adult self.”

Before acting, ask:

Is there urgency?

Is there secrecy?

Is there shame?

Is there a threat of exclusion?

Is there a promise of specialness?

Is there sexual charge?

Is there spiritual superiority?

Is there a demand for money, sex, loyalty, silence, or obedience?

Is there a narrowing of choices?

Is questioning treated as betrayal?

Is resistance pathologized?

Is ordinary life being downgraded?

Is the child part being seduced, scared, or rescued?

Is the adult self still allowed to say no?

Several yes answers mean slow down. Don’t deepen trance or make an irreversible decision. Return to the room, body, food, sleep, time, and counsel.

A good guide increases your adult self. A bad guide replaces it. A good guide can tolerate your no. A bad guide calls your no resistance, ego, fear, low vibration, trauma, sin, or proof that you need more of their guidance.

EDITED:
Use this checklist when something is pressing on you too hard. It could be a person, an ad, an ideology or some spiritual teaching, a romantic pull, a voice inside you, anything like that. You can tell yourself something like, “Okay, I’m being influenced here. I don’t have to obey it. I don’t have to attack it either. I can slow down for a minute and ask what it’s taking for granted, and who it benefits. My body’s telling me something, but I won’t assume I know what it proves. And what would love, truth, humility and ordinary life need from me?”

Then test it a little. Can it handle your questions? Something true can. Love can live with your boundaries, and real guidance will still be there after you sleep on it. If it asks you to give up your adult self, it’s not for you.

Before you do anything, notice if any of the following (usual) tricks are being used: urgency, secrecy, shame, threat of exclusion, promise of specialness, sexual charge, spiritual superiority, demand for money, demand for sex, demand for loyalty, demand for silence, demand for obedience.

Other things to notice: is this urging you to limit your choices? Is questioning being framed as bad (a betrayal)? Is your resistance being treated as bad (pathologized)? Is ordinary life being framed as too small, as something you’re supposed to outgrow?

Is your inner child being seduced, scared, rescued? Does being an adult (and saying no) remain an option?

If you see several of these, slow down. Don’t go deeper into trance, and don’t make a decision you can’t undo. Just come back to the room and your body for a while. Then eat, sleep, give it time, get some counsel and so on.

You can tell a good guide because your adult self gets stronger with them. And can they take your no? A bad guide can’t. They take your adult self’s place, and they’ll call your no resistance, ego, fear, low vibration, trauma, sin, you name it, or proof that you need more of their guidance.
