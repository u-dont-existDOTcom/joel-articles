# Stance check: induction section (hg17)

## Conflicts in the rewrite

**1. Already open? makes deepening the goal**

- Rewrite: "If you're already open for some reason (…) and want to aim and stabilise it so that you can deepen it."
- Essay: "The state is already open. I do not need to intensify it. I need to aim it, stabilize it, and keep it kind." (Already open?) Also: "The later Already open? practice is for orientation, not permission to intensify an unstable state." (Before you begin)
- A reader would think containing an open state is a step toward going deeper, meant only for people who want to go deeper. The essay makes it the first move for anyone already open and says the state doesn't need intensifying. (The rewrite's own opening says the reverse: "skip straight to the orientation/containment part rather than going deeper.")

**2. "If you can" added to the preview**

- Rewrite: "Preview, if you can, what you are going to do: will you close your eyes? Will you come out and go back in again?"
- Essay: "Preview the sequence, choose one purpose, and decide whether you want to begin." (Appendix J). The published step has no hedge: "Preview eye closure, any repeated exit and re-entry, and the return." (Before you begin)
- A reader would think previewing eye closure, re-entry and the return is optional. The essay makes it a standing step before every induction.

**3. Opening the eyes as a complete exit**

- Rewrite: "You can also just open your eyes and be done."
- Essay: "Use Come all the way back, even when you ended early." (Let the next response guide you, under Finish without forcing a revelation). Also: "Use the complete waking return after every induction." (Appendix J)
- A reader would think stopping early needs no return. (The published sentence has a milder form of this; see contradiction 2.)

## Contradictions in the published text

**1. "When intoxicated" rules out the loveyhuasca practice the essay teaches**

- Published: "When intoxicated, acutely confused, or unable to keep yourself safe, deal with immediate support rather than exploratory hypnosis." (Before you begin)
- Against: "Use this when loveyhuasca, meditation, lucid dreaming, … has already opened the state." (Already open?, same section, which then sets an exploratory intention). Also: "After one loveyhuasca self-hypnosis session, I woke in what felt like an answer to prayer." (Why doing it myself worked better for me). It also conflicts with Joel's 2026-10-22 answer to question 11, which chose "overwhelmed by chemicals".
- Rewrite: fixed. "If you are overwhelmed by chemicals, acutely confused, or not able to keep yourself safe, then get some immediate support…"

**2. "Simply open your eyes" against the full return**

- Published: "You can simply open your eyes and finish." (Before you begin)
- Against: "Use Come all the way back, even when you ended early." (Finish without forcing a revelation). Also: "Use the complete waking return after every induction." (Appendix J)
- "Simply" tells the reader that opening the eyes is enough. The later lines say an early stop still needs the whole return.
- Rewrite: still has it, and says it more absolutely ("just open your eyes and be done"; rewrite conflict 3).

Rewrite: 3 conflicts. Published text: 2 contradictions (the open-your-eyes exit is counted in both).
