Induction: gathering attention without disappearing

Inductions are meant to gather your attention so that you can more easily hear the stuff that is waiting for you when you are in a quiet place. They are not about giving up, going to sleep, or being "under" someone else's influence. They are very helpful if you are sober, distracted, or very analytical or guarded when you approach the work. If, however, you are already somewhat trancey from meditating, loveyhuasca, lucid dreaming, grief, listening to music, praying, or being tired, skip the induction and begin the practice at "Already open?"

Decide how deep a trance you need for what you want to do.

What is the point of an induction?

It helps you get ready for the session. It collects your attention. It may deepen your trance. You can do inductions adaptively, that is, responding to what you notice is happening as you go along. Counting is one way to begin. But you can also talk, tell a story, use movement, sounds, ask questions, or simply be curious about how the process might unfold for you. Just let the person know that you are using these techniques to gather his or her attention. There is no reason to be sneaky about what you are doing. Not being elegant does not give you the right to be covert.

If you have a simple target in mind, you might want to test that out first and then do an induction if it seems helpful. If you already are having a good response, work with that. You don't have to do a full induction if you are already in a receptive place.

Please don't use six different metaphors in one trance

Have some idea what you want to do, where you are starting, how you will get back, and what kind of atmosphere you want to create. You may want to be warm, protective, forceful, neutral, etc. You may want to float, go down stairs, walk in a garden, go inside, work with color, talk to an inner child, etc. It doesn't make sense to do all of these in one session. You can change the metaphor if you do so consciously.

You don't have to use imagery at all. Your signal from the other side can be felt, rhythmic, movement, verbal, spatial, "I know it," or nothing. If you do choose a metaphor, try to stay in that "world," even as you count, etc., and come back the same way you went in. It is okay to borrow from other places (warmth, steadiness, etc.) but don't change your identity.

If you are writing an induction, use a central verb, a sensory quality, a couple of phrases to repeat, and a transition phrase. Read the script aloud when you practice it. Long, elegant sentences turn to soup when you do an induction.

What do you do before the induction?

Read this induction and "Come all the way back" when you are awake. Set your intention: what do you want to look at, or what quality do you want to increase? What are you NOT looking at? What is your time limit? Get clear on what you want out of the session. It might be love, play, confidence, creativity, communion, etc. Be sure to set aside time at the end to come back and think about what happened.

Make sure you are in a safe place, that you won't need to drive, get in the water, work with machines, etc., and that you don't have to take care of anyone while you are doing this. If you are drunk, high, or otherwise in confusion, you need other things before you do exploratory hypnosis. (Working with someone in this condition is best done with the "Already open?" practice below.)

Decide if you want encouragement during the session. If you are doing an induction for yourself, preview the process: closing your eyes, exiting and coming back several times, and the final time you come back. If you are playing a tape, read the script first if you can. Make sure that you know that you can stop at any time if you say "stop." If you say "less" you can get less work done. If you say "quiet" the talk will stop. You can simply open your eyes when you are done. You don't even have to get permission from anyone on the inside.

If you are not used to doing inductions, you may want to practice the entering and exiting first before you do any intense work. You are in charge of this work and you don't need a supervisor. You are simply freeing up your ability to respond in a way that comes easily to you. If you say "no," it will stay "no." If you don't say anything, no one is going to say anything to you.

Respond to what is coming as you do the induction. One of the keys to hypnotherapy is utilization, that is, responding to what is actually happening. It is explained better in Appendix B, but you may want to begin to use it right now. Go to "Let the next response guide you" when you are done with the induction.

Induction for when you are sober (3 - 5 minutes)

Read this slowly to yourself on a recorder, say it aloud, or use silent words if you can. Choose one way to anchor yourself, such as a sound, a place to touch yourself, or doing the two hands practice. Say the opening phrase and use it throughout when you refer to the anchor. Pause between paragraphs, but don't read the directions.

Just relax your gaze on any object in the room

Bring your attention to the feeling of your body on whatever it’s sitting/lying on. Then just focus on where you make contact with that surface. (If something else catches your attention, like a sound or your breath, that’s fine, you can use that as an anchor instead)

Take a moment to do that…

If your mind wanders, you can just bring it back to your chosen “anchor”.

Now pick out one aspect of that to be interested in…maybe the way it feels at a certain spot, or a particular kind of sound you hear when you listen for it, or something like that.

Give it as much time as you would someone you loved who was struggling to tell you something…

Now say to yourself (using the phrase you chose before):

Now you can let your eyes close if you want, or soften your gaze, or just keep looking wherever you want. Just keep listening, and let the sound you chose be a background to what you’re really doing.

Count down from 5 to 1, if you want to….

5. Ok, just bring your attention back to your chosen anchor. 4. Focus your attention on it for a moment. 3. 2. Listen for a moment… 1.

Bring to mind whatever it is you wanted to contact. (Or just have the desire to contact it, if you can’t bring it to mind yet).

What do you notice in response?

Give it as much time as it needs to come in whatever form it comes in…

You can choose to notice it in words, or just notice it…

Already open – need to contain before deepening

Use this if the state you want to be in is already opening, but is too strong to deepen without containing first (too much). Could be after taking loveyhuasca or other substances, or emerging from deep meditation, a lucid dream, intense grieving, music, prayer, erotic energy, exhaustion, intense beauty, whatever.

Take a moment to tell yourself that the state you are in doesn’t need to be intensified, but needs to be aimed, and stabilised in a kind way.

Ground yourself firmly in the physical space, by looking around the room and noting where you are. Tell yourself the date, or at least the day. Bring your attention to your body, especially your feet, hands and spine. Apply a little pressure with your thumbs to your index fingers, and focus on the friction between them. (Getting in touch with your body firmly)

Tell yourself that you’re only going to allow into yourself what you are ready to integrate, and that anything more intense than that can wait. Reassure yourself that your body is safe, and that you are connecting with the stuff that came up as an adult, not as a child.

Once you feel stable enough, choose one of these phrases:

Ask to be shown the next piece of truth, symbol, feeling, question, action, whatever you want, but the amount you feel you can integrate, no more.

Staying awake – if your mind tries to escape

Sit up, open your eyes, feel your feet and say this:

I’m not going to go off anywhere, I’m going to come here instead.

Focus your gaze on an object while feeling your spine, and say:

Am I going off in my mind because it’s helpful – am I being guided, or working through something emotionally? – or am I running away from something?

Show me what action I need to take (if you feel you’re being guided) Or, bring me back to now, and show me what I’m running away from (if you feel you’re running away)

If your mind tends to escape when you do hypnosis, try making the hypnosis more awake instead of more relaxed. You can do it with your eyes open, and you can try doing a deliberate physical action with your hands over and over again, while you increase the state you’re aiming for. Then stop the movement, and see if there’s an echo. Something like this:

Keep your eyes open, and press your thumb to your forefinger firmly, while saying to yourself: “press…release, press…release, press…release…now stop deliberately doing that, and see if there’s an echo”. Work at that level of state, sitting upright.

Two hand induction (shamelessly stolen from the Gerson hypnosis course). You put one hand on your heart and the other on your solar plexes. The one on your heart is the nurturer and the one on your solar plexes is your vulnerable child. Then you breathe gently and affirm:

love is above. Child is below. And the adult is present to mediate the two.

Or a bit more expanded you could say:

I send love down from my heart to my child and then I receive the signal back from my child's hand up to my heart, then again and again, sending and receiving.

There is a difference between these inductions and a sleep induction. The sleep one you just want to go to sleep and not come back to your adult self to write and act on what you learned, so you can just have the inner kid and not the adult analyzing things when you go to sleep.

Next up is something you can do if you want to, it's called fractionation. You can do it at will, it's best to explain to yourself that you're doing it and not assume that it will take you deeper. It goes something like this:

Ok, open your eyes, come back to full awareness, feel yourself here and now, if you want to stop the exercise you can, but if you want to continue just soften or close your eyes again and let me know what it's like.

Also you can always add a deepener like:

If it's useful to you, you can let your attention absorb more and more deeply, but if it's not useful you can be just as awake as you need to be to take care of yourself.

Then you can start a visualization, be it going into the room in your heart or the garden or temple or sitting by a fire with your Protector or going into a clear lake or sitting in the chair with your adult... you only need one visualization to work with so choose one that you like. Then you can start the exercise by asking:

Who or what would like to speak first?

You can also check for trance if you want to, it's optional. It could go something like this:

Sit back and let go of all responsibility for anything to happen.

If it's useful to you your subconscious may let one of your fingers twich or pulse or lift or warm or tingle or have some other subtle sensation letting you know that the inner meeting space is ready.

Don't force anything or pretend it's there, just sit back and wait with love.

And then whatever it does or doesn't do is only an indication if anything at all, that something might be listening.

This is a nice way of getting back into hypnosis very quickly:

Learn an induction and the coming back down fully from hypnosis first. My dad had a mudra (finger movement, two fingers touching like an ok-sign) that put him in a relaxed state even while driving, which was nice, but I would advise keeping things separate, something for calming your self while driving and something for going into hypnosis.

Pick a word or phrase or whatever you want, you can also add a gesture if you want but it's not really necessary. While in a good state of hypnosis, not at the peak of the session, not in your child state or in the midst of an inner scene with trauma, let yourself know that this word/phrase/gesture (whatever you picked) will mean that you have set aside the time and are in a suitable place and are deliberately entering hypnosis, so you can let yourself know that you want to go back to this receptive state of mind.

Then come back up. When you want to practice this, set up a time and place that's suitable and then deliberately start the exercise and use your word/phrase/gesture to get yourself back into hypnosis quickly. There's no set number of times you need to do this, it takes some time and you should slowly shorten the induction you use to get into hypnosis, but if the shortcut doesn't work just do the longer one. You don't need to go deeply in or feel something special, just try it.

When you think you've got this down check for how useful and selective it is. Do you get into this state easily when you want to, do you not go into it when you don't want to, can you change your direction and come back up easily. It shouldn't be that you just obey somebody telling you what to do or your phone rings or somebody says the word when you overhear it and you go into hypnosis. The usefulness of this exercise is NOT how deep you go or how quickly, but how well you can take control of your own life and work on what you want to work on.

Don't practice this while driving or doing something where you need to be actively paying attention. Even opening your eyes and saying 'if it's useful to you' isn't enough to make this safe.

If you want to stop using this, just don't rehearse it anymore. If it's on a recording don't include it in active use recordings anymore, if it's an app don't have it say it when you're using it. Just go back to the old way of getting into hypnosis and let yourself know you've made the decision to stop doing this. Again, saying that you've stopped doesn't mean it's gone, but it should make it go away. If you still have problems with this or get disoriented easily get qualified help. If you decide to keep it, just use it to start a new session later, it's not meant to take you into a new state of mind while you're already in one.
