PUBLISHED section:
[B19] When the presence feels hostile, cold, alien, parasitic, or boundary-violating, don’t offer soft sentimental love as though inviting it in for tea. Use love with a boundary.

[B20] Sit upright. Press your feet into the floor. Let the spine rise. Gently draw the shoulder blades back so the chest is open but not exposed. Feel the skin boundary of the torso. Feel that this body has a perimeter.

[B21] “I recognize that suffering may be here, and I wish all suffering to return to the space where it belongs. But the boundary of this skin belongs to my adult life. You do not have permission to enter this house.”

[B22] “Compassion is present. The door is closed.”

[B24] A symbolic hostile-presence experience that settles with grounding and love is very different from a state that becomes persistent, paranoid, sleepless, dangerous, or impossible to reality-test.

[B25] If somebody believes they’re under attack for days, can’t sleep or function, hears commands, feels compelled to hurt themselves or anyone else, becomes manic, can’t communicate normally, or can’t distinguish symbolic experience from ordinary reality, stop treating the problem as hypnosis material. Get help.

[B26] If the practice makes you more loving, grounded, relational, honest, and functional, continue gently. If it makes you more paranoid, sleepless, grandiose, isolated, frightened, or unreal, stop deepening and get support.

[B28] Hostile presences, shadow forces, grandiose parts, and altered-state imagery don’t always attack through fear. Sometimes they seduce through importance: a cosmic secret, a special mission, a vast download, a feeling of being uniquely chosen, or a revelation that makes washing dishes seem beneath your new rank.

[B29] “Thank you for the imagery. I do not require a grand performance. If this information cannot become a simple, honest act of care tomorrow morning — washing the dishes, cleaning my room, keeping a promise, apologizing, resting, eating, or helping someone I love — it is not useful to my path.”

[B30] Guidance worth keeping should make ordinary life more beautiful, honest, relational, humble, reliable, and useful to other people.

[B32] Demons, jinn, hostile presences, and “Unattached Burdens” all lead to the same practical training: can love remain present without surrendering the boundary, and can the mind become harder to hook? I’m not trying to turn anyone into an entity hobbyist.

[CHANGED, replaces B19-B22]
Second, feel free to tell the presence that while you have compassion for it, the door is closed.

This is especially a good idea if the presence you’re experiencing seems even the slightest bit hostile, cold, alien, parasitic, etc. It needs to realize that you don’t welcome it and that it needs to respect your boundaries. It’s not really helpful to approach it with the kind of soft, sentimental love that you’d use if you were inviting it to stay for tea, but it can be useful to approach it with love while also making it clear that it needs to respect your boundaries.

Sit up, make sure your feet are grounded on the floor, straighten your spine, pull your shoulder blades back a little, open your chest a little, feel the boundary of your torso’s skin, feel the perimeter of your body, and tell the presence, “While I have compassion for you and any suffering you may be experiencing, this suffering needs to go back where it came from. The boundary of my skin is the boundary of an adult now, and you’re not welcome to come inside the house anymore.”

[CHANGED, replaces B24-B26]
This is assuming that you are having a symbolic experience of “hostile presences” which responds to grounding and love. It may be that what you are experiencing is actually psychotic or a form of destabilization.

If you feel like you are under attack for multiple days at a time, if you are not able to sleep, if you are not able to function normally, if you are hearing voices telling you to do things, if you feel you need to hurt yourself or somebody else, if you feel you are going manic, if you feel you can’t communicate normally, if you feel you can’t distinguish between what you are experiencing symbolically and normal reality, get help immediately. It’s not something to deal with in hypnosis.

As a general rule, if this is making you more loving, more grounded, more relational, more honest, and more functional, then it’s probably OK to keep doing it gently. If it’s making you more Paranoid, more sleepless, more grandiose, more isolated, more scared, or more unreal, don’t go deeper, get help.

[CHANGED, replaces B28-B30]
Finally, presences, shadow forces, or grandiose parts may try to respond by giving you a sense that you’re special, trying to “download cosmic info” to you, try to give you a special mission, make you feel doing the dishes is beneath you, etc. You can just say, “Thank you for the imagery, but I don’t need your grand performance. Unless the info you’re trying to download to me can help me do the dishes, clean my room, be more honest, be more helpful, be more loving, keep my promises, take care of my body by resting and eating etc. I don’t need it.

Unless the “guidance” you’re trying to give me can help me be more beautiful, honest, relational, humble, useful, and take care of my dishes and my room, I don’t need it.”

Finally, some of you may be tempted to make this an area of hobbyism – to become interested in demons or jinns or hostile presences or Unattached Burdens or whatever. I just don’t want you to do that. They all lead to the same thing, which is how to maintain a presence of love while also making your mind harder to hook. Beyond that, I don’t want you to be hobbyists in this area.
