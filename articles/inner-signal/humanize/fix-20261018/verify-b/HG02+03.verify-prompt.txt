[B2] Introduction

[B3] Hello Lovey Bunny!

[B4] You were born with a mind that can dream, protect, analyze, imitate, love, remember, hide, reveal, and heal. Nobody taught its different layers how to speak to each other.

[B5] Instead, most of us learned to push through, suppress emotion, perform confidence, obey or rebel against authority, and call that adulthood. Meanwhile, the deeper mind kept running its own programs: the child wanting safety, the guard expecting danger, the body remembering what the intellect had explained away, the intuition speaking before language, and the fantasy trying to become guidance.

[B6] A lot of hypnosis teaching treats the subconscious like a machine waiting for commands. That never did much for me. I get more from sitting down and listening until the scared kid, the guard, the body, the intuition, and the part trying to run Tuesday can all be heard without one of them seizing the microphone.

[B7] In this guide, self-hypnosis gathers attention until the conscious mind, subconscious, body, inner child, protectors, adult self, and Higher Self can participate in one honest inner meeting.

[B8] The method is simple: listen, warm, protect, clarify, reality-test, and act.

[B9] A dramatic trance is optional. The goal is a more trustworthy relationship with the whole mind, proven in ordinary life.

[B10] Communion is the closest word I have for that. The conscious mind stops shouting from the roof, and the subconscious no longer has to communicate only through symptoms, dreams, cravings, daydreams, and peculiar little body signals.

[B11] I treat the subconscious as a living ecology of parts, memories, impulses, images, protections, and wisdom. I bring in inner-child reparenting because a wounded young part won’t be repaired by a clever suggestion. I begin with Higher Self or the universal heart when the system needs coherence, and separate into parts only when that helps. I borrow from prompt engineering because the shape of a question changes the kind of answer we can hear. And I include influence literacy because hypnosis is hardly the only thing hypnotizing us.
