2) Making it so that any answer the subject may give will be taken as proof that the suggestion is working (yes, no, noticing, not noticing, resisting, not answering etc.)

3) Using phrases like "whether or not you notice", etc., which turn uncertainty into invisible success that can’t be checked.

4) Binding a concept of safety or goodness to obeying, or taking on a role, or pleasing the guide or belonging to someone else, etc.

5) Using fractionation without informing the subject that going in and out of absorption several times is part of the method.

6) Giving the subject cues (words, gestures, blinks, sounds, repeated listening etc.) that will make them do something at a later date without informing them exactly what they will be doing.

7) Suggesting amnesia, or making dream, amnesia, imagery and body experiences seem like "proof" of something that is not necessarily true.

8) Ending the recording without bringing the subject out of the altered state, or leaving them with a role to play, lessened judgment, impaired physical abilities, blankness, or otherwise altered for an indefinite period of time.

A REAL choice during a hypnosis includes at least one way to NOT carry on: turn it into a lighter hypnosis, use a different method, stay in full awareness, pause or stop the hypnosis. “No” means no, even if someone with a nice voice is telling you to do something! And silence, or simply being unsure, isn’t consent.
