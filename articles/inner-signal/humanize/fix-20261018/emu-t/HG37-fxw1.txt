Appendix H: Demons, jinn, hostile presences, and “Unattached Burdens”

I’m going to come from experience here.

Sometimes, during self-hypnosis, meditation, lucid dreaming, loveyhuasca, grief, prayer, plant-medicine-adjacent practice, or a variety of other altered states of consciousness, you may encounter something you perceive to be a demon, a jinn, a parasitic energy, some other kind of dark intelligence, or even a terrifying archetype. Sometimes it comes as a voice. Sometimes as an image. It doesn’t feel like a simple emotion or a younger part, and it feels intrusive, hateful, seductive, mocking, or simply alien.

First and foremost, it doesn’t matter for the purposes of this guide whether they are literally demons or not. Maybe they are literal beings; maybe they are symbolic mind-forms, trauma in a frightening costume, dissociated energy, or ancestral and relational burdens. All that matters is the practical question: “What response makes me safer, clearer, more loving, more grounded, and less hooked?”

Start with the room, the body, and basic safety

So what do you do when a demon, or jinn, or other bad or intrusive entity shows up? Don’t reward it with instant certainty about what it is. Run the triage first: medical danger, environment, body, overload, and how open your mind has become. Open your eyes, feel your feet, name the room, lower the stimulation, leave hostile company, and stop adding prompts. Don’t go deeper, hunt for hidden truth, or debate what it is while you’re destabilized.

“Only what can be integrated is welcome right now. Anything that’s too much can wait. The adult in me is still here, and my body can go back to safety.”

If you feel like you’re in danger of hurting yourself or others, if you feel like you’re going psychotic and afraid, if you feel like you won’t be able to sleep afterwards, if you feel extremely paranoid, if you have chest pain, if you feel like you’re going to have a seizure, if you feel like you’re too hot in a serious way, if you feel like you’ve taken too much of something, or if you feel like you just can’t function in the world anymore, then the self-hypnosis session is over. Go get some real-world help.

A response check, not a verdict about what it is

If not, and you’re stable enough to stay present, check in with yourself as to what helps. Again, it doesn’t matter whether or not it’s literally a demon; this isn’t a test of what it is. Put your hand on your heart, if that feels welcome, take a comfortable breath, and keep the room available.

2. Internalize that you do not have to know what this is. Notice whether warmth or distance helps, and keep your boundary either way. Know that you are under no obligation to do whatever it is asking of you.

3. If you feel vulnerable, tightness in your throat, sadness, fear or a sense of trying to protect something, bring in your Nurturer. If it stays hostile or gets more frightening, then stop engaging with it and come back to your body and the room.

4. Again, you do not have to know where this is coming from. Ask yourself if this way of responding helps you become clearer, steadier, and less hooked. A protective part doesn’t have to show its fear quickly, and a comforting response doesn’t prove what it is either. You can use love and boundaries without deciding what it is first.

Do not fight from fear

The first impulse may be to fight it, banish it, hate it, show it I’m more powerful, argue with it, panic, become nothing but the threatened self, etc. Some traditions use prayers, mantras, protections, banishing and exorcism forms, and some people find them useful. The problem is, if you fight from fear…

…it becomes the center of your attention. When you hate it, you give it a hook in you. Pride gives it an opponent, and panic gives it your nervous system.

So the next time it shows up, simply say, “I see that there is no reason to hate or revile what I am experiencing. I see that there is no need to merge with or become what I am experiencing. I see that there is no need to obey what I am experiencing. I see that there is no need to defeat what I am experiencing from fear. I choose instead to return my attention to my loving awareness.”

A bodily boundary: compassion present, door closed

Secondly, feel free to maintain a bodily boundary and say to the presence “while compassion is present, the door is closed.”

This is especially important if the presence feels in any way hostile, cold, alien, parasitic, etc. It doesn’t respect my boundaries. In this case, don’t approach it with soft sentimental love, as though you were inviting it in for tea. Approach it with love while maintaining a boundary.

Sit up, ground your feet, straighten your spine, pull your shoulder blades back slightly so your chest is open but not exposed, feel the boundary of the skin around your torso and that this body has a perimeter, and simply say “While I recognize that there may be suffering here and I want all suffering to go back where it came from, the boundary of this skin is the boundary of an adult now. You don’t get to come in the house anymore.”

If it might be psychosis or destabilization

Thirdly, this exercise is assuming that you are having a symbolic experience of hostile presences which responds to grounding and love. That is very different from a state that becomes persistent, paranoid, sleepless, dangerous, or impossible to reality-test, which may be psychosis or another kind of destabilization.

If you feel like you are under attack for multiple days, are unable to sleep or function normally, are hearing commands, feel like you need to hurt yourself or somebody else, are going manic, are unable to communicate normally, or feel like you can’t tell the difference between symbolic experience and normal reality, get help immediately. This is not something to deal with in hypnosis.

As a general rule, if doing this makes you more loving, grounded, relational, honest, and functional, keep going, gently. If it makes you more Paranoid, sleepless, grandiose, isolated, scared, or unreal, stop going deeper and get support.

The cosmic-download humility test

Also, some of these presences or shadow forces or grandiose parts may respond not just by trying to scare you, but by trying to give you a sense that you are special, or trying to “download cosmic info” into you, or giving you a special mission that makes the dishes seem beneath you, etc. You can simply say “Thank you for the imagery. I don’t need a grand performance. Unless the info you have to download can become a simple, honest act of care tomorrow morning, like doing the dishes, cleaning my room, keeping a promise, apologizing, resting, eating or helping someone I love, I don’t need it.”

In other words, guidance worth keeping should make ordinary life, the dishes and the room included, more beautiful, honest, relational, humble, reliable, and useful to other people.

Don’t become an entity hobbyist

Finally, I recognize that some of you may be tempted to become hobbyists in regard to demons, jinns, hostile presences, Unattached Burdens, etc. The truth is, they all lead to the same practical training: can love stay present without giving up the boundary, and can your mind become harder to hook? Beyond that, I don’t really want you becoming hobbyists in this area.
