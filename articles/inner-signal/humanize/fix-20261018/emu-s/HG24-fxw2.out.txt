It may be that you’re trying to access an adult part that isn’t there yet. If that’s the case, then stop trying to talk to an inner voice that you aren’t able to believe yet. If you have enough observing ego, borrow some from a real or imagined caring person. Try out some statements or actions etc. that you can believe, even if it’s just one sentence, and see how it goes.

Your subconscious won’t answer

There may not be an answer. There may not be an answer that will help you. You may need to try asking a different question or you may just need to rest or finish your inner work for the day. If you think it will be helpful, you can try asking your subconscious “What would you need to know in order to trust me?” Some examples of answers might be “I need to know you will set boundaries” “I need to know you will do what you said you will do” “I need to know you won’t keep leaving” “I need to know you won’t think that knowing something means that you don’t need to do anything about it” etc.

Your inner child won’t appear

If you can figure out who the guard is, talk to it. If you can’t, just work with what you are aware of. You can say to the guard “You don’t have to let me in, just tell me what I need to do to make it safe”. Even if there isn’t a child part that you can talk to, you can work with whatever you are aware of. You can work on your preferences or something that you’re curious about or something that you’re willing to try as a beginner etc. and see how it goes. Work on developing a sense of who you are and reparent yourself as best you can.

You get anxious

Find out if the anxiety is bad enough that you want to decrease contact with it or if you can manage it and let it continue. If the anxiety is getting too bad, then you need to help your body, like the following:

-Where are your feet? Where are you sitting? Focus on your body and your breathing.

-Tell your Little parts and Protector parts that you’re there for your Little parts and that you recognize that your Protector parts are trying to protect you and that you don’t need them to scream at you in order for you to know that something is wrong. Ask them if they need you to do something and if they do, ask them to show you one step at a time that you can do. If they don’t, then ask them to let your body calm down.

If you’re having chest pain, fainting, suicidal thoughts, psychosis-like symptoms, mania-like symptoms, severe dissociative symptoms, not being able to sleep, wanting to hurt yourself or somebody else, etc. then you need to get help. For more on handling anxiety, see the toolbox in Appendix D.

You dissociate or feel like nothing is real

Open your eyes, move around, feel something, name 5 things that you can see, and end your work for the day.

You get grandiose

Go eat, go to sleep, do some chores, accept some humility, find somebody to disagree with you, etc.

What do you say to yourself in that moment? “If it’s really there, it’ll still be here after a good night’s sleep, some food, some time, etc, and some subsequent humility, feedback, etc, etc.”

You’re being too blissful, diffuse, etc. Bring it down to a more specific level. “What’s the smallest, most specific thing you can do to demonstrate the love you’re feeling?”

You’re wanting to know something you’re not supposed to know yet. Refrain from doing anything, and say, “Show me more capacity first, and if what I want to know is really for me, you’ll show me later on.”

You’re daydreaming. Sit up and open your eyes, and ask yourself, “What are you avoiding doing in the real world by doing this?”

You’re doing spiritual bypass. Ask yourself, “Where has divine love not come down and hugged your hurt yet?”

Or maybe a teacher betrayed you. Whatever the problem, it’s not going to get resolved by doing more of the same.

Sometimes you just need to do something practical to take care of yourself until the horizon of your life expands enough for what you’re seeking to enter. Or you need to follow your curiosity for what will make you happy, regardless of whether it makes sense or not. Or you need to realize you already have plenty of love and meaning in your life, and this whole spirituality thing is optional.

If you determine you’re doing a check, say to yourself, “It’s okay for that thought to be there, but I don’t need to hold a meeting with it right now. I’m going to do ____ instead.”
