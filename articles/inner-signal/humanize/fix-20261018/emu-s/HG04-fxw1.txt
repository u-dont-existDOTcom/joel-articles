Quick start: do this first

Gather attention, notice, respond, wait, come all the way back. Use these to work a difficulty, or to cultivate love, curiosity, play, confidence, and other qualities. In every session, not much has to happen!

Before the first session, read this induction and full return while awake, to get familiar. Set aside a chunk of time, make yourself physically secure for the time. Prior to each session, consider just one thing you'll work on or cultivate; what you're leaving alone today. Then put the screen on the shelf: the practice is something you learn to conduct, not a checklist to keep reading.
