Stabilize before you interpret

Self hypnosis is powerful. Especially when you are in an altered state of consciousness, either through loveyhuasca, meditation, lucid dreaming, grief, sexual energy, prayer, not sleeping, or any other number of ways similar to plant medicines, often your mind opens faster then your adult can figure out what is going on.

The problem is, that you THINK that because you are in an altered state that it MEANS something. Sometimes it is your inner child talking, but sometimes it is a Protector showing you their fear, sometimes it is your shame or your grief or your anger or your longing or whatever else is buried in you that wants to be taken care of, and sometimes it is just your body’s reaction to being overloaded, or chaos in your environment, or lack of sleep, or any number of other reasons for why what you are experiencing shouldn’t make sense. Just because you had a picture that scared you doesn’t mean that it is true. Just because you had a bad feeling doesn’t mean that it is a lesson you need to learn. Just because you had a download in nice lighting doesn’t mean that you should believe it.

What should you ask yourself first?

“What kind of problem is happening?”
