Troubleshooting

Nothing happens

Start smaller:

“Show me one body sensation.” “Show me one word.” “Show me whether today is for listening or resting.”

It feels fake

What is it that feels fake? The words, an image, an expectation, a role? Be clear that not experiencing the inner voice/image is not, by itself, resistance or lack of adult capacity.
