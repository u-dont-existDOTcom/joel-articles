PUBLISHED section:
[B1] This guide doesn’t tell anyone to use substances. It does acknowledge that many people first encounter self-hypnosis, inner-child contact, or Higher Self dialogue while altered.

[B2] Do not use altered states to force revelation. Use them to deepen listening, strengthen love, and improve ordinary life.

[B3] A past opening can be a resource without becoming a state you must reproduce. Having experienced love once, being able to access it only in a particular state, and contacting it reliably in daily life are different things. Let the experience widen what seems possible while you build usable access. If the child cannot receive it, meet the difficulty rather than escalating the intensity.

[B4] During or after an altered state, these prompts are usually more useful than demanding a revelation:

[B5] “Help me stay grounded.” “Help me receive only what I can integrate.” “Help me remember love.” “Help me return when it is time.” “Help me translate this into one ordinary-life change.”

[B6] Don’t use self-hypnosis to prolong an altered state indefinitely, recover memories, intensify fear, bypass sleep, override medical needs, or make yourself less able to communicate.

[B7] If the state makes you less loving, less grounded, less honest, less able to sleep, less able to communicate, or less able to meet ordinary responsibilities, stop deepening and start integrating.

[B9] Self-hypnosis can become very powerful during loveyhuasca, meditation, lucid dreaming, grief, sexual energy, prayer, sleep loss, or other plant-medicine-adjacent states. Sometimes the mind opens faster than the adult self can organize what is arriving.
