A couple of notes in regard to this exercise. Firstly, you may want to add to the mantra: “I don’t need to hate this. I don’t need to merge with this. I don’t need to obey this. I don’t need to defeat this from fear. I just return to loving awareness.”

Secondly, feel free to maintain a bodily boundary and say to the presence “while compassion is present, the door is closed.”

This is especially important if the presence feels in any way hostile, cold, alien, parasitic, etc. It doesn’t respect my boundaries. In this case, don’t approach it with soft sentimental love. Invite it in for tea. Approach it with love while maintaining a boundary.

Sit up, ground your feet, straighten your spine, pull your shoulder blades back slightly, feel the boundary of the skin around your torso and simply say “While I recognize that there may be suffering here and I want all suffering to go back where it came from, the boundary of this skin is the boundary of an adult now. You don’t get to come in the house anymore.”

Then say “While compassion is present, the door is closed.”

Thirdly, this exercise is assuming that you are having a symbolic experience of hostile presences which responds to grounding and love. It is not meant for people who may be going psychotic or experiencing other forms of destabilization.

If you feel like you are under attack for multiple days, are unable to sleep or function normally, are hearing voices, feel like you need to hurt yourself or somebody else, are going manic, are unable to communicate normally, or feel like you can’t tell the difference between symbolic experience and normal reality, get help immediately. This is not something to deal with in hypnosis.

As a general rule, if doing this makes you more loving, grounded, relational, honest, and functional, keep doing it. If it makes you more Paranoid, sleepless, grandiose, non-relational, scared, and non-functional, don’t do it.

Finally, some of these presences or shadow forces or grandiose parts may respond not just by trying to scare you, but by trying to give you a sense that you are special, or trying to “download cosmic info” into you, etc. You can simply say “Unless the info you have to download is going to help me do the dishes or clean my room, I don’t need it.”

In other words, the guidance has to help you do the dishes or clean your room in a more beautiful, honest, relational, humble, useful way.

Finally, I recognize that some of you may be tempted to become hobbyists in regard to demons, jinns, hostile presences, Unattached Burdens, etc. The truth is, they all basically respond the same way. It’s good to learn how to relate to them with love, and it’s good to make your mind harder to hook, but beyond that, I don’t really want you becoming hobbyists in this area.
