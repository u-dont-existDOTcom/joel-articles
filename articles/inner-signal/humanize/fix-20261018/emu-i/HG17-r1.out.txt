Induction: gathering attention without disappearing

Why do you need an induction? It makes it easier to listen to some things by gathering up your attention. You don't have to surrender, play asleep, or follow anyone. It may be a good idea to do an actual induction if you're sober and likely to be distracted, too analytical, or guarding against something. If you're already fairly trancey from meditation, loveyhuasca, lucid dreams, working through a grief, music, prayer, tiredness, or whatever, just go straight to orienting yourself and making containment (below).

How much trance do you need? How deep do you want to go?

What's it doing?

It's preparation, gathering up attention. It may also be deepening. It will be more effective if you tailor it to what comes up. Counting is easy but you can do all sorts of things to gather up attention. It's OK to tell people when you're doing this rather than pretending it's all very elegant and flowing.

You may want to try your suggestion on a simple target before deepening to see if it works. It's not necessary to do the whole ceremony before responding more receptively.

Please don't use six different metaphors in one trance.

Choose what you're going to do, one way to anchor it initially, and how you'll get back. You may want it to be warm, or firm, directed, or neutral, or whatever. You don't have to combine floating, going down stairs, going through gardens, visiting inner places, seeing a colour, and talking to a child all in one go, even if some of these appeal to you. If you do change metaphors do it consciously.

It's OK to do this with no imagery. The signal may come in other ways (feelings, rhythm, movement, words, spatial, knowing, or even non-response, if that's appropriate to the context). Try to keep the language you use for counting in and elsewhere consistent with the world you've chosen. Come back out by reversing the journey in some way. It's OK to steal qualities from other inductions but don't become someone else.

Try to limit yourself to one key verb, a particular sense quality, a short bit of text to repeat, and a way of moving from one bit to another. Read it out loud before hand - long beautiful sentences tend to turn to soup in inductions.

Before you start

Read this induction and Come all the way back while awake. Consider what you want to explore, what you want to leave alone, and how long you've got. Try to use it for something good (e.g. more love, play, confidence, creativity, communion with life, whatever). Make sure you've got enough time to come back out and think about it.

Make sure you're somewhere safe - don't do this when driving, near water, playing with machinery, or with people who need looking after. Don't do this when intoxicated or in any other state where you may be acutely confused or unable to look after yourself. (However see the bit about already being open, later, as this is not about deepening when unstable).

Consider whether you want it to be encouraging, warm, firm, or whatever. You may want to warn yourself that your eyes will be asked to close and that you may come out and go in again a few times, as well as coming out at the end. If you're listening to a recording it may be a good idea to read the script first (if possible). Decide on a word to use to come out (e.g. 'stop'). You may also want words to make it less demanding (e.g. 'less'), and to stop the words (e.g. 'quiet'). You can always just open your eyes and finish - you don't have to get consensus from inner parts to do this.
