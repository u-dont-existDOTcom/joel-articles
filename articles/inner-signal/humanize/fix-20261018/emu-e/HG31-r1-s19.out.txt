When I reframe something that is dishonest with myself in the process, I’m no better off than before, so I have to be honest when I reframe. To reframe in a helpful way is to ask myself a series of questions like: “What else could be true here?” or “What is this part trying to do for me?” or “How would my Nurturer reframe this?” or “How would my Protector reframe this?” or “How would my Leader reframe this?” and so on.
