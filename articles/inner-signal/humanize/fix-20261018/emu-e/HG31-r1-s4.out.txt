What is it looks like to pace and lead?

Here is an example of pacing and leading for anxiety:

It can be as simple as: Truth, Invite, Truth, Invite etc.

“It is completely true that the sensation of _______ (or emotion of _____) is coming up for me right now. It makes complete sense that a part of me is trying to protect me by doing/thinking ________. While that is completely true, another completely true thing is that there is a part of me that is adult, and that is breathing in this room right now, and is willing to listen.
