This is a borrowed figure, so you’re still making the choice. The longer version of this exercise can be found in my Inner Child Self-Love Reparenting Guide .

You don’t have to have trustworthy people in your history. Just imagine a good adult. See if you can imagine what they would say/do in your situation, with your history, limitations, options, etc. If you can’t imagine good, imagine the least cruel thing you can. Once you’ve found something good, try it, don’t keep doing the exercise.

It can be helpful to use borrowing when you’re in the adult position and not knowing what a decent adult would do, especially when it seems like any response will be retaliatory, defensive, collapsing, leaving, etc. Borrow a decent adult’s response. You may still feel young when you do it, it’s okay, you’ll become an adult after you act like one.
