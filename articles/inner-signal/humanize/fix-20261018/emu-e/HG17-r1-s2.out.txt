In general, the most effective place to begin a session is to prepare your subject, to gather their attention, and to deepen that attention if it is helpful to do so. While the most common method of gathering attention is counting, there are a number of other ways of doing so. Other methods of gathering attention include conversation, storytelling, movement, repetition, and curiosity. Whatever method you use, describe it elegantly, and don't hide your inductions.
