Induce or contain (if client already in altered state).

Ask permission to work with this part.

Help client accurately represent this pattern. What does it look like? Where? How big? How close? What color? How does it move?

Ask what it is trying to do for the client.

Have client make adaptive changes in the pattern (move it further away, turn down the volume, change body posture, slow it down, etc.).

Introduce the three new adults (Nurturer, Protector and Leader) and have them interact with the pattern in new ways.

Have client anchor the new state (heart, fingers, breath, body posture, etc.).

Have client future pace into a situation where this pattern used to be triggered, but now he/she will perform a new behavior.

Test and retest to ensure that new pattern is useful and that you have supporting evidence for your interpretation of the situation.

Help client exit altered state and perform a normal behavior.
