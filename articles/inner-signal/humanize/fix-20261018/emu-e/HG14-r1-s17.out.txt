If speaking or visualization doesn’t work, try writing. Have your non-dominant hand write as your child/protector (even if your handwriting looks a little awkward), and have your dominant hand answer as you. For example, your non-dominant hand might write, “I don’t trust you.” Answer with your dominant hand, “I hear that you don’t trust me, but I love you, and I want to know where I’ve let you down. I won’t ask you to trust me, but I want to hear you out and make one promise to you that I will keep today.” Your non-dominant hand might respond, “What promise?” Your dominant hand might write, “To eat, or to clean my desk, or to not text someone who makes me feel unsafe.”
