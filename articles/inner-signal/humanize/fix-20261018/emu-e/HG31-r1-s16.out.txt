Sometimes when my clients notice a synchronicity they are asking straight way what it means. Sometimes it does give them a very good insight, sometimes it gives them a reason to start obsessing and look for patterns in everything. I am using noticing a synchronicity in a different way. I am using it as a signal to WAKE UP.

It is my cue to:

Whenever I notice a synchronicity, I use it to wake up in whatever state I am in at that moment, feel my body, the room I am in and go to the highest state I can where I can objectively and clearly see the situation I am in from the highest possible perspective, feeling my body and being still within the human reality. From this place I feel whatever feelings, search for the truth of the situation, and if I am in a negative state, I check if there is a message of my Inner Child, Protector, if I need to go through some grieving, if there is a message of my physical body about some problem that needs solving, or if there is a need for me to take some action in this situation. From this place of clarity and peace I am free to choose what to do next and usually I can do at least one thing that comes from a loving place. In this state I am also free and joyful and I feel grateful.

In short it is my cue to: Stop-orient-go wider-reality check-listen-do at least one thing from a loving place.

It enables me to not get into identification, but still hear the message of the state if it is a negative one.
