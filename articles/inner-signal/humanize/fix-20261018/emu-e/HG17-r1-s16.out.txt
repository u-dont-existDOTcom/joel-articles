Learn a Useful Induction and Full Return First, the reason I suggest not to use a short cut is because you should not be in trance when driving. However, I have seen my dad put two fingers in a mudra (like the ok mudra) to help him relax while driving and that is just his experience, it does not mean that you should. However, it is best to have different triggers for different things, so having a different trigger to be calmly alert while driving, and a different one to go into inward hypnosis is a good idea.

Choose a Unique Word, Phrase or Movement You may want to add a gesture, but it's not necessary. When you are in a familiar and welcome hypnotic state that you like - not at the height of an emotional experience, and not in a child state or with a traumatic scene - start to pair that state with that word, phrase or gesture. You are giving that word, phrase or gesture a meaning that you choose.

When you are in a place and time where you have set aside time and it is appropriate and you want to deliberately start hypnosis with your short cut, then you will find yourself in that familiar and welcome state, from where you can change the direction of your experience or end the hypnosis.

Return Fully When at another appropriate time you want to deliberately start practicing hypnosis, use your short cut. You can start to make your longer induction shorter as you find that the short cut works, but if it doesn't work, you can always use the familiar induction. There is no set number of times that you need to do this, or that you need to be instantaneously really deep into hypnosis or experience a particular sensation, it is completely up to you.
