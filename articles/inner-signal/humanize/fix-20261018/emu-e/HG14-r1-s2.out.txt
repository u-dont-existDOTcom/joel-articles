The problem is, that some people do not have access to their adult functions at will. Instead, they may go blank, access an inherited inner critic, or even more commonly – access a child part who may be playing at being an adult. Telling them to just access their adult is like telling them to just be able to fly.

To help these people access their adult functions, I recommend the following steps. Please note, the following is a bridge that I developed – while many of the steps have been shown to be effective, the entirety of the steps have not (to the best of my knowledge).

- Access a witnessing position, if necessary. If you already know how to distinguish your internal positions, skip to the next step and access the adult position you need.

- Borrow an adult function. You may “borrow” this from a safe adult in your life, a mentor, an older sibling, a future version of yourself, a spiritual figure, a value, or even a written plan. Borrow whatever you need – warmth, direction, etc.

- Put on the adult hat as an apprentice. Now, in this position, figure out what you would want to say or do – and then do at least a part of that.

- Return the adult hat, noticing what you borrowed and what you were able to do on your own (even if it’s only 5%).
