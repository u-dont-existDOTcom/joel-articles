Make up your mind what you are doing and stick to it. You can have a purpose, a starting point, anchor, image, way back. Is this a warm exercise, a boundary, offering direction, or neutral? If it is neutral you can use many images, floating, stairs, gardens, inner place, coloured lights, talking to inner child etc. However, if you do use these, do it consciously.

You don’t have to use images, you can use any modality, movement, words, sense of space or simply knowing. If you count up to do something, make sure it is relevant to what you are doing and count back when you finish. If you use other qualities, don’t imply that they are you.

Have a central verb, sense quality, refrain and transition phrase that you use consistently. Read your script out loud to check it. Long, elegant sentences read well on the page but can sound like soup when you’re trying to induce.
