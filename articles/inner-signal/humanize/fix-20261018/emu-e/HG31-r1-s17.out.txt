Mentally rehearse: when next synchronicity noticed (even harmless ones count),

feel 'whoa' as attention stimulated,

feel feet/body/breath/room to stimulate re-embodiment,

reiterate to yourself "when I notice synchronicity, it is a bell to wake me up, not a message to heed",

optionally ask Nurturer/Protector/Leader their thoughts,

rehearse further steps to get a more grounded and wide view,

rehearse what additional action to take, and finally rehearse waking completely,

Mind the caveats : it must be self-authored, obviously under control, reversible, and restricted to just helping orientation. It must not be used for decision-making, intuition, inhibition, or otherwise be under someone else's control.

This is a kind of implementation intention , for which there is evidence of efficacy (a good starting point is this paper by Gollwitzer ). Note that this specific application of implementation intentions probably hasn't been studied.
