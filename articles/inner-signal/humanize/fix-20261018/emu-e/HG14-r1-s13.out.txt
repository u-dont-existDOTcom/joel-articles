Does anyone here practice the following sequence to “embody” Protector?

(1) orient self (look around, feel support, etc.),

(2) assess situation (what do you know, not know, need to check out?),

(3) differentiate between actual/real dangers, uncertainty, past dangers or imagined dangers,

(4) set boundaries (just one, proportional to danger level; move, stop, delay, ask, leave, get support, etc.),

(5) verify sufficient safety (based on reality), and finally

(6) stand down and rest.

Seems to me that this would be a “mature” Protector. For example, it wouldn’t be afraid to say, “I don’t know”, or to ask another human being, or to delay interpreting what’s going on, or to use a no-trance practice. I want to be able to “do” this. I am learning discernment and to quit scanning the room after taking appropriate action.
