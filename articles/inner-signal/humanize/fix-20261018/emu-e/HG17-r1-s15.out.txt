Then I wait, giving up any sense of responsibility for what happens.

Sometimes, if it's appropriate, my wise subconscious mind will twitch, pulse, lift, warm or tingle a finger so I know it's okay to move into the inner meeting space.

"I'm not going to force it or fake it. I'll just wait in love and see what happens."

Whether something happens, like a finger moving, hand warming or change in the breath, or nothing happens at all, it doesn't really prove anything. Maybe it's listening.
