An anchor is a cue that triggers a state. It can be anything - putting your hand on your heart, touching two fingers together, taking a breath, saying a word, a particular posture, scent, song, and so on. Choose something, then find a real state to anchor it to. When you're clearly in the state, apply the anchor and say, 'This is available' . (If you're using the anchor to get back into hypnosis, find a nice useful state - you don't need to be at the peak of emotion, just in a nice receptive state. For more about that, see 'A shortcut back into hypnosis' in the main induction section. ) Make sure you also have a way to bring yourself back to a nice, calming, alert state when you want to be fully awake (see 'synchronicity' above for a way of doing that).
