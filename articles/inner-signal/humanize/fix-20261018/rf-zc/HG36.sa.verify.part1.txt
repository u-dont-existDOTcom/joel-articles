PUBLISHED section:
[B9] Self-hypnosis can become very powerful during loveyhuasca, meditation, lucid dreaming, grief, sexual energy, prayer, sleep loss, or other plant-medicine-adjacent states. Sometimes the mind opens faster than the adult self can organize what is arriving.

[B10] Intensity then gets mistaken for meaning. Sometimes the inner child is finally speaking. Sometimes a Protector is showing fear, or shame, grief, anger, longing, and other buried material are asking for care. But an intense state can also be bodily overload, environmental chaos, sleep deprivation, or plain confusion. A frightening image isn’t automatically truth, a bad feeling isn’t automatically a lesson, and a “download” doesn’t deserve belief merely because it came with excellent lighting.

[B11] Before trying to interpret the state, ask a simpler question:

[B12] “What kind of problem is happening?”

The issue comes in when you THINK because you’re in an altered state that it MEANS something. Sometimes it’s your inner child who is speaking, other times it’s a Protector showing you their fear, or it could be your shame, or your grief, or your anger, or your longing, or whatever else you have buried that wants to be taken care of. Just because you had a bad feeling doesn’t mean it’s a lesson you need to learn. Just because you had a download in a nice light doesn’t mean you should believe it.
