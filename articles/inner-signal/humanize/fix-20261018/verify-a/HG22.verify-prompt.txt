[B0] The weekly deeper practice

[B1] Once or twice a week, give the same practice more time when that is useful. More time needn’t mean greater intensity. Start from Higher Self or neutral witnessing, follow what appears, borrow an adult function when needed, and ask whether anything important is being bypassed. Leave room for the full exit and any ordinary action needed afterward.

[B2] Do the ordinary action immediately when possible. That teaches the subconscious that the adult heard and acted, instead of staging another beautiful internal meeting and then leaving the dishes exactly where they were.
