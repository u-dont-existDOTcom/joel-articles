[B0] Try it in the Inner Signal app

[B1] Use the Inner Signal app to get voice hypnosis sessions, learn the method, develop your own language, reflect on what you report, and ask questions about a hypnotist’s approach. Self-hypnosis doesn’t require an app, and finding a practitioner is an option rather than a graduation requirement.
