PUBLISHED section:
[B0] Why doing it myself worked better for me

[B1] Self-hypnosis became much more useful for me than working with a professional hypnotist. The session with the hypnotist did very little. Maybe part of that was state-dependent: I was sober and didn’t feel especially suggestible. The larger issue was trust and precision.

[B2] When I’m giving the suggestion, I can feel the exact moment a phrase is slightly wrong. I can stop, ask what the subconscious meant, and notice whether an image feels like guidance, fear, avoidance, wishful thinking, or something I don’t yet understand. I can catch the body’s no before the conscious mind writes a persuasive little essay called yes.

[B3] An outside hypnotist can’t easily conduct that rapid back-and-forth because they aren’t inside the mind receiving the suggestion. A skilled guide can still notice blind spots, help regulate the process, and hold a safer container. Self-hypnosis simply has one advantage nobody outside can duplicate: immediate dialogue with the system itself.

[B4] I found that especially amplified with loveyhuasca. My experiences included a much deeper understanding of my motivations, peaceful loving awareness operating alongside analytical clarity, the disappearance of unhelpful daydreaming, different priorities around relationships, stronger intuition, and repeated correction between intuition, subconscious response, and conscious analysis.

[B5] I also got stronger results when I practiced immediately after sky hypnosis:

[B6] Some benefits arrived the next morning or a few days later. After one loveyhuasca self-hypnosis session, I woke in what felt like an answer to prayer. I’d been dealing with a lot of lust energy and worrying it could pull me into a relationship that wasn’t right. I asked the subconscious to connect the heart chakra with the sacral chakra—to bring the love energy and sex energy into the same current. The next morning I woke in a kind of “perfected” love state. I didn’t feel that I needed to find a woman; the divine feminine felt somehow at zero distance from my heart. Ordinary interaction dissolved the state, but for a while it showed me a possibility my mind and heart may permit when the desire is sincere.

[B7] I’ve also had self-hypnosis seem to extend a short chrunga trip far beyond its normal duration. Subjectively, it felt as though the state might have continued indefinitely if ordinary life hadn’t required communication and interruption. I find that phenomenologically important. I’m not recommending that anyone prolong an altered state forever; integration is healthier than trying to live inside the extension.

[B8] Without drugs, self-hypnosis or meditation may also become much more effective inside a lucid dream. I explain that possibility in my lucid-dreaming guide.
