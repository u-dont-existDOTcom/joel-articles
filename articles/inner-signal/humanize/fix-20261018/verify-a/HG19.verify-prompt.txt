PUBLISHED section:
[B0] The actual inner meeting

[B1] Sit rather than lie down when you tend to daydream, dissociate, or fall asleep. Let the spine rise without becoming military, soften the jaw, and keep some awareness of the room. Save lying down for sleep, deep rest, or dream incubation.

[B2] “I am not leaving life. I am listening more deeply to life.”

[B3] Begin by invoking the larger field:

[B4] “Higher Self, universal heart-mind, wise subconscious, body intelligence, inner child, inner adults, protective parts, and all that serves truth and love: please help me understand what I need to know, only in the form and timing that is useful, kind, stabilizing, and real.”

[B5] You’re asking the conscious mind to listen without forcing an answer.

[B6] Thoughts do not have to stop before deeper listening begins. Let thoughts, sensations, images, doubts, and emotions pass through awareness without fighting or following them; keep the intention lightly in the background and notice what becomes salient on its own.

[B7] “Please tell me what I need to know, or ask what you need to know. I will wait silently for five minutes.”

[B8] The response may be a phrase, sensation, memory, color, child, protector, pain, warmth, silence, task, gratitude, an urge to stop, or the realization that today’s practice is over.

[B9] Listen to all of it. Believe none of it automatically.

REWRITE:
[B0, as published] The actual inner meeting

[CHANGED, replaces B1-B9]
If you tend to daydream, dissociate or fall asleep, sit up rather than lying down for this exercise. Save lying down for when you’re ready to go to sleep or receive deep rest or are trying to incubate dreams. Keep your spine straight, but not rigid, like a soldier. Relax your jaw and maintain some connection with the room you are in.

You can say to yourself, “I’m not going to leave life, I’m just going to listen more deeply to life.”

Invoking: “With the help of my Higher Self, my universal heart-mind connection, my wise subconscious mind, the intelligence in my body, my inner child, my inner adults, my protective parts and all that serves truth and love: please help me understand what I need to know, only in the form and timing that is useful, kind, stabilizing and real.”

You’re asking your conscious mind to listen without forcing an answer.

This doesn’t mean you have to silence your thoughts to listen. Notice your thoughts, sensations, images, doubts, emotions, etc., without fighting them, without going with them, but simply noticing them while you hold the intention of this exercise in the background of your mind. Notice what becomes salient to you.

You can say, “Tell me what I need to know or ask me what you need to know, and I will sit in silence for five minutes and listen.”

You might hear a phrase, feel a sensation, notice a memory, see a color, be contacted by a child or protector, feel pain, warmth, silence or know that your need to do something or be grateful for something or feel an urge to stop, or realize that this is enough listening for today.

Listen to it all and believe none of it automatically.
