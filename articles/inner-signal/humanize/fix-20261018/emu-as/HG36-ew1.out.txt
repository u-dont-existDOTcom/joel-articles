Intensity is often assumed to mean it must be significant: the inner child is talking to the conscious self; a Protector is revealing how scared s/he is underneath; shame, grief, anger or longing are ready to be healed. But sometimes intense states are just overload in the body, a chaotic environment, a lack of sleep, or confusion. Just because it looks scary doesn’t mean it’s true. Just because it feels bad doesn’t mean it’s useful. Just because the lighting was excellent doesn’t mean the download is to be believed.
