Troubleshooting

Nothing happens

Try starting a bit smaller?

Can you show me a single sensation in the body? Can you show me a single word? Can you show me whether to listen or rest today?

It feels fake

Do you know what it is that feels fake? The words? The image? What you expected? The role? Just because you don't have an inner voice/image doing this, it doesn't mean you are missing some grown-up ability or resisting doing the work. If you feel that something is asking you to do something that you do not want to do, then correct it. If you feel that a part might be protecting you from this, then talk to it.

“What are you protecting me from?” or “Are you afraid I will lie to myself again?” or “Are you afraid this will just be a performance?”

It could be an inner voice of a parent you are picking up that is enforcing a rule about how you should be to belong, if so don’t argue with it, find out what rule it is enforcing and then work on finding ordinary evidence that would make your new adult voice more believable.

If the fake feeling is telling you some grown-up ability isn’t there yet, stop trying to believe an inner voice. If you have enough observing ego, borrow whatever you need - warmth, boundary, direction etc - from someone who you can imagine is good enough, maybe a family member, a friend or a famous person. Try out something which rings true, a sentence, an action etc and see how it goes.
