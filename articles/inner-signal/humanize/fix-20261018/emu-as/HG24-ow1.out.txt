Showing yourself just one sensation in your body / one word / whether to listen today or rest?

Ok, because it feels fake...

Do you know what exactly feels fake - the words, an image, what you are expecting, the role etc? It doesn't matter that you don't have an inner voice/image to do this, it isn't about not having a 'grown up' or resisting doing the work. If it feels like you are being asked to do something you don't want to do then correct this. It could be that there is a part that is protecting you from doing this, so talk to it.

You could say something like 'What are you protecting me from?' or 'Are you afraid I will lie to myself again?' or 'Are you afraid this will just be a performance?'

It could also be an inner voice of a parent that you are picking up, and it is trying to enforce a rule about how you should be to belong to them. Again, it isn't useful to argue with this part, but find out what rule it is enforcing and then work on finding some ordinary evidence to build a more believable 'grown up' voice.

If the fake feeling is telling you that you don't have the 'grown up' ability to do this yet, then don't try and convince yourself by using an inner voice. As long as you have enough observing ego to do this, try and borrow what you need from someone who you can imagine is 'good enough' to lend it to you. This could be a family member, friend, famous person etc. Borrow what you need, e.g. warmth, boundary, direction etc. Try out something which rings true to you, whether it is a sentence, an action etc, and see how you get on.
