PUBLISHED section:
[B1] In this guide, subconscious means the deeper processes shaping perception, emotion, desire, avoidance, imagery, memory, bodily signals, habits, intuition, and automatic behavior. Sometimes it helps to address all of that as one mind; sometimes an ecology of parts gives more precision. Both are maps. Neither needs its own priesthood.

[B2] Parts give precision. Higher Self gives coherence. Communion is the movement between them.

[B3] When you need coherence, speak to the deeper mind as a whole:

[B4] “Wise subconscious, please help me understand.”

[B5] When you need precision, notice the different tendencies inside it: the child wanting love, the protector wanting safety, the critic wanting standards, the fantasy-maker wanting possibility, the body memory wanting recognition, the social self wanting belonging, the spiritual self wanting God or source or the benefit of all beings, and the wiser part wanting what is true.

[B6] Begin with unity when the system feels scattered:

[B7] “Higher Self, universal heart-mind, wise subconscious, please guide me.”

[B8] Differentiate only when the question needs it:

[B9] “Which part of me is afraid?” “Which part is protecting?” “Which part is avoiding?” “Which part knows the next step?” “Which part is too young to carry this?”

[B10] Then bring the parts back into one life:

[B11] “Let all of this belong to one life. Let the adult self lead with love.”

[B13] Some people feel more fragmented when they begin by naming twelve parts and assigning everybody office space. Start with the sacred whole when that happens.

[B14] “I begin from the universal heart. I begin from Higher Self. I begin from the part of me that belongs to life before it belongs to any wound, role, family, group, identity, ideology, or story. Please help me understand what is true, kind, timely, stabilizing, and useful.”

[B15] If Higher Self, the universal heart, or any sacred language feels unavailable or borrowed from somebody else, begin with plain witnessing instead. Notice the room, the body, and the younger state. The fact that something can notice is enough adult presence for the first step.

[B16] If you can already notice and distinguish what is happening inside, you don’t need to keep rehearsing the Witness. Work with the warmth, protection, or direction that is actually missing. Higher Self language can help you gather around a meaningful source of care; it doesn’t establish that a perfectly capable inner parent is already available. Those adult functions still grow through practice.

[B17] Differentiate only when something specific appears:

[B18] “Is this the inner child?” “Is this a protector?” “Is this the body?” “Is this fear?” “Is this intuition?” “Is this social conditioning?” “Is this divine guidance, or my interpretation of guidance?”

[B19] Start from the heart. Differentiate only as needed. Return to the heart.

REWRITE:
[CHANGED, replaces B1-B11]
Note: whenever I refer to the ‘subconscious’ I’m referring to all of the processes under the surface of your conscious awareness: perceptions, emotions, desires, avoidance tendencies, imagery, memories, body signals, habits, intuitions, automatic behaviours and so forth. You can work with all of these together as if it’s all one ‘mind’, or you can work with it as a set of parts, an ‘ecology’ if you like. There isn’t a ‘right’ or ‘wrong’ way to approach it. They’re all maps. Don’t form a priesthood around either one!

Working with parts: Precision. Working with Higher Self: Coherence. Doing both: Communion.

If you need to work with the ‘whole’, the subconscious as one:

a) ‘Invoke’ the subconscious in some way. Something simple like ‘Hello, wise subconscious…’ or whatever works for you.

If you need to work with the ‘ecology’ in the subconscious (child parts, protectors, ‘critic’, fantasy making parts, body memories, social part, spiritual part (God/source/best benefit of all beings part) ‘wiser’ part etc etc then:

a) It’s usually a good idea to start with unity. So ‘invoke’ your Higher Self: Hello my Higher Self/universal heart-mind/wise subconscious…

b) Move into differentiation. “Which part is afraid? Which part wants to protect you? Which part wants to avoid it? Which part knows what to do? Which part is too young for this?” Etc.

c) Move back into unity again. “And all of these parts are part of the one life, and as the adult in charge of this life you can lead with love, knowing that all of these parts are part of your life…” Etc.

[CHANGED, replaces B13-B19]
Sometimes, it’s better to start with the whole.

Some people make themselves too fragmented by starting to work with parts. ‘Here’s a part! Here’s another one! I’ll get twelve of them in a room and give them all an office to hang around in!’ So it’s better to start from the Sacred.

Start like this: ‘I’m approaching this from the universal heart. I’m approaching this from my Higher Self. I’m approaching this from the part of me that knows it belongs to life, not any wound, or role, or family, or group, or identity, or ideology, or story. So can you please tell me what’s going to be true and kind and timely and stabilising and useful here?’ Etc.

It can be that you can’t even access your Higher Self, or the universal heart, or whatever works for you. (Btw, if it doesn’t work for you don’t use it! It’s no good adopting somebody else’s map.) In that case, start by just witnessing whatever is coming up. Notice the room, your body, the fact that you’re in a younger state etc.

Once you have the ability to notice, and to differentiate, you don’t need to keep rehearsing it. Just work on whatever it is you need to work on (often warming up and protecting yourself and knowing what to do etc). So even though it can be useful to talk about your Higher Self, it doesn’t mean you already have all the good inner parenting functions etc that you need. You usually need to work on developing them.

Differentiate only as you need to. “Is this my inner child? Or is this a protector? Is this my body? Is this fear? Is this intuition? Is this my social part? Is this divine guidance, or is this my interpretation of divine guidance?”

Always start from the heart, differentiate when you need to, then come back to the heart again.
