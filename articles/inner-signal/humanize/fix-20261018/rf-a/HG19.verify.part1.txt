PUBLISHED section:
[B1] Sit rather than lie down when you tend to daydream, dissociate, or fall asleep. Let the spine rise without becoming military, soften the jaw, and keep some awareness of the room. Save lying down for sleep, deep rest, or dream incubation.

[B2] “I am not leaving life. I am listening more deeply to life.”

[B3] Begin by invoking the larger field:

[B4] “Higher Self, universal heart-mind, wise subconscious, body intelligence, inner child, inner adults, protective parts, and all that serves truth and love: please help me understand what I need to know, only in the form and timing that is useful, kind, stabilizing, and real.”

[B5] You’re asking the conscious mind to listen without forcing an answer.

[B6] Thoughts do not have to stop before deeper listening begins. Let thoughts, sensations, images, doubts, and emotions pass through awareness without fighting or following them; keep the intention lightly in the background and notice what becomes salient on its own.

[B7] “Please tell me what I need to know, or ask what you need to know. I will wait silently for five minutes.”

[B8] The response may be a phrase, sensation, memory, color, child, protector, pain, warmth, silence, task, gratitude, an urge to stop, or the realization that today’s practice is over.

[B9] Listen to all of it. Believe none of it automatically.

REWRITE:
[CHANGED, replaces B1-B9]
Note: If you have a tendency to day dream, dissociate or fall asleep, it is better to do this exercise sitting up until you are ready to try this lying down, either to go to sleep, get a very deep rest or incubate dreams. If you are sitting, keep your spine straight but not rigid like a soldier. Relax your jaw and stay somewhat connected to the room you are in.

“Unless I’m going to sleep or want deep rest, I am not leaving life; I am listening more deeply to life.

I invoke my Higher Self, universal heart-mind, wise subconscious mind, intelligence in my body, inner child(ren), inner adult(s), protector(s) and all that is of truth and love to help me know what I need to know in a form and at a level that will be useful, kind, stabilizing and real for me.”

Ask your conscious mind to listen.

This does not mean you have to stop thinking to listen. Just notice your thoughts, body sensations, pictures, doubts, emotions, etc. Don’t fight what comes up, but don’t go with it either. Just notice what stands out on its own as you hold the idea of what you are doing in the back of your mind.

Internally say, “Tell me what I need to know or ask me what you need to know and I’ll sit in silence for 5 minutes and listen.”

You may hear a phrase, feel a body sensation, notice a memory, see a color, be contacted by a child or protector, feel pain, warmth, complete silence, know that you need to do something, be grateful for something, feel you need to stop the listening or any number of things.

Listen to everything and believe nothing.
