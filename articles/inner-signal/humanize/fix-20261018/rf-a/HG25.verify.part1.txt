Below is one section of a long self-hypnosis guide by Joel, published on his Substack. It teaches gathering attention, listening to the subconscious as an ecology of parts (the inner child, protectors or "guards", the body, intuition), three adult functions he calls the Nurturer, the Protector and the Leader/Guru, Higher Self, "borrowed adulthood", inductions and a complete return, influence literacy ("de-hypnosis": noticing and resisting other people's influence), and safety limits (when to stop, when to get help). Its quoted lines are scripts the reader says or thinks.

PUBLISHED section:
[B1] Do not treat inner voices, images, autonomous movements, or body signals as commands or proof. Listen, then reality-test.

[B2] Do not chase grand downloads or turn demons, jinn, and “Unattached Burdens” into a hobby. Stabilize first, and seek help when reality-testing or function weakens.

[B3] Do not use self-hypnosis while driving, bathing, swimming, operating equipment, supervising children, or doing anything that requires active attention.

[B4] Do not force memory recovery, use trance to prove what happened, or suppress medical symptoms.

[B5] Do not override hunger, fatigue, pain, grief, anger, or fear before understanding the signal.

[B6] Do not prolong altered states indefinitely or treat intuition as fact without testing it.

[B7] Keep the inner roles in proportion: the child does not lead adult life; the Protector cannot exclude all love; the Nurturer cannot erase consequences; the Leader cannot bypass the child; analysis and tenderness both remain available.

[B8] Do not use NLP to override protectors, manufacture certainty, manipulate anyone, or perform healing when the body says no.

[B9] Doubt, distraction, resistance, silence, and “no” do not prove that an induction is working.

[B10] “The unconscious will keep me safe” does not replace clear limits, a safe setting, the ability to stop, and a tested exit.

[B11] Ordinary sensations do not prove hidden trauma, illness, toxicity, possession, attachment, transformation, or subconscious truth.

[B12] Imagined repair does not establish physical recovery, neurological repair, cellular change, or a cure.

[B13] Do not install hidden instructions, undisclosed third-party activation, compulsory behavior, identity switching, or amnesia. A consciously chosen reusable cue should have a clear purpose, remain reviewable, and be retired if it becomes unwanted; saying “cancel” does not guarantee instant erasure.

[B14] End every waking session with ordinary identity, movement, orientation, judgment, and a direct statement that the session has ended.

REWRITE:
[CHANGED, replaces B1]
DO NOT: View these experiences as anything but internal happenings that need to be reality tested at all times.

[CHANGED, replaces B2]
DO NOT: Engage in seeking “downloads” of information or become obsessed with demons/jinn/Unattached Burdens or the like, until you have stabilized your life, perhaps even getting help to do so, if you are not reality testing well, or not functioning well in your life.

[CHANGED, replaces B3]
DO NOT: Participate in self-hypnosis while operating a car, bathing, swimming, operating any equipment, supervising children, or engaged in any activity which requires active attention.

[CHANGED, replaces B4]
DO NOT: Attempt to “force” memories, to use trance to “prove” something happened to you, or to not get medical attention for symptoms, because you can “force” them down using hypnosis.

[CHANGED, replaces B5]
DO NOT: “Force” yourself to ignore your body’s needs, such as eating when hungry, resting when tired, responding to pain, etc., or your emotions, such as letting yourself grieve when someone dies, feeling anger when it is appropriate, acknowledging your fears, etc., until you know more about what the signals are telling you.

[CHANGED, replaces B6]
DO NOT: “Extend” the duration of altered states indefinitely or accept “hits” of intuition as “real” without reality testing them.

[CHANGED, replaces B7]
DO NOT: Allow various internal roles to get out of proportion (e.g., allow the “Inner Child” to run your adult life, the “Protector” to preclude all loving relationships, the “Nurturer” to disregard consequences to others, the “Leader” to ignore the needs of the “Child”). You are capable of analysis, as well as tenderness.

[CHANGED, replaces B8]
DO NOT: Utilize NLP techniques to “force” down “protectors,” to give yourself certainty you do not have, to manipulate others, or to “heal” yourself when your body is telling you not to.

[CHANGED, replaces B9]
Don’t consider negative indicators (doubt, distraction, resistance, lack of response, saying “No”) as good signs that the induction is working.

[CHANGED, replaces B10]
Don’t rely upon the “safety” of “your unconscious mind” as the only safety factor in any session. Be sure you have set up clear limits, are in a safe place, and have a way to terminate the session and “get out” which has been tested and proven to work.

[CHANGED, replaces B11]
Don’t believe that normal physical sensations are indications of hidden trauma, illness, toxins in your body, possession, “attachments,” “transformations,” or information from your “subconscious mind.”

[CHANGED, replaces B12]
Don’t believe that anything is “healed” in your body (neurologically, cellularly, etc.) simply because it is “healed” in your imagination.

[CHANGED, replaces B13]
Don’t put “hidden ‘commands’” in yourself, nor allow anyone to do so. Even if you are consciously placing something in yourself which you wish to utilize later, be sure that you know exactly what you are doing and why you are doing it, and be sure that you CAN “take it out” if you decide that you no longer wish to use it. It may not be as easy as simply saying, “Take out.” Nor should you allow anyone to activate these “commands” in you without your knowledge, to make you do something you don’t want to do, to make you into someone you don’t want to be, to give you amnesia, etc.

[CHANGED, replaces B14]
DO bring yourself completely back at the end of every “waking” session, declaring that the session is over.
