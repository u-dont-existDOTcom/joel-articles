Below is one section of a long self-hypnosis guide by Joel, published on his Substack. It teaches gathering attention, listening to the subconscious as an ecology of parts (the inner child, protectors or "guards", the body, intuition), three adult functions he calls the Nurturer, the Protector and the Leader/Guru, Higher Self, "borrowed adulthood", inductions and a complete return, influence literacy ("de-hypnosis": noticing and resisting other people's influence), and safety limits (when to stop, when to get help). Its quoted lines are scripts the reader says or thinks.

PUBLISHED section:
[B1] Reverse whatever you used to enter. If you floated, regain chosen weight. If you became small, return to ordinary scale. If you met a younger state or several parts, confirm present wholeness, body ownership, and adult judgment. If you traveled, come back by the same route. Keep only the qualities you deliberately chose.

[B2] “I bring back only what is useful now. The rest can remain safely held until there is more capacity. I return to the room, the body, the day, and one honest action. All instructions to deepen or remain in this session are finished. I keep only the ordinary-life intention or future-practice cue I consciously chose; neither keeps today’s altered state running. I am myself, in my body, with adult judgment fully available.”

[B3] One — feel the body’s contact and chosen weight. Two — let the breath return to its ordinary rhythm. Three — hear the room and name where you are. Four — move fingers, toes, shoulders, and eyes. Five — eyes open, present, ordinary, clear. The session has ended.

[B4] Name the day or date. Look left, right, near, and far. Move before standing, and stand only when steady. Check that you are actually oriented and alert; repeating the count is not proof of a complete return. Note only what actually appeared, and any action it calls for. Then do something normal.

[B5] If a recording stops unexpectedly: say, “The recording stopped and the session is over.” Open your eyes, name the location and day, press your feet or hands into a surface, move, look around, and use the five-step count. Don’t wait for the missing narrator to release you.

[B6] Sleep is separate. A sleep recording may end in sleep or quiet rest. A waking recording shouldn’t end by preserving a role, reduced judgment, impaired movement, blankness, an undefined reset word, or an invitation to remain altered indefinitely.

REWRITE:
[CHANGED, replaces B1]
Bring yourself out of the session in reverse order to how you got into it. If you floated up, then float back down. Make sure you’re coming back down in the weight you want to be. If you shrank yourself, make sure you’re growing back to your normal size. If you went back to a younger version of yourself, or met other parts of yourself, make sure you know that you’re whole now, in this body, using the judgement of the age you are now. If you traveled somewhere, come back the same way you left, but make sure you’re not bringing anything back with you that you didn’t mean to.

[CHANGED, replaces B2]
“Bring back with you only that which will be of use to you in your life now. Store away that which is not, until such time as you wish to use it. Come back now to this place, back into your body, back into normal life, back into the one honest action. There are no more instructions to keep you in this altered state, there is no instruction to take with you into normal life that will keep you in this altered state, there is no instruction to use in the future that will put you into this altered state again.

[CHANGED, replaces B3]
Count yourself out of the session now. 1. Coming back now into your body, back into the weight you chose to have. 2. Coming back into your normal breathing pattern. 3. Hearing the sound in the room around you now, knowing where you are. 4. Moving your fingers and your toes, your shoulders and your eyes. 5. Opening your eyes, feeling normal, feeling present, the session is now over.”

[CHANGED, replaces B4]
Bring yourself out by opening your eyes and saying to yourself where you are, and what day or date it is. Look to the left, look to the right, look at something near, look at something far away. Move around a bit, then stand up when you feel you can do so without falling over. Make sure that you are oriented and alert. Don’t just count, and think that because you were told to, that you are alert and oriented. Note to yourself what if anything came up in the session, and what if anything you need to do about it, and go and do something normal.

[CHANGED, replaces B5]
In the event that the recording stops before it should, say to yourself, “the recording has stopped, the session is over”, and use the above method to come out. Open your eyes, say where you are, say what day it is, push down with your feet or hands, move about, look around, and if need be use the 5 step method.

[CHANGED, replaces B6]
Note: When using a recording to take you to sleep, it is allright for the recording to put you to sleep, or leave you in a quiet resting state. However a recording used when awake, should not leave you in a role, with lowered judgement, impaired movement capabilities, blank, with an undefined reset word, invited to remain in an altered state, etc.
