Below is one section of a long self-hypnosis guide by Joel, published on his Substack. It teaches gathering attention, listening to the subconscious as an ecology of parts (the inner child, protectors or "guards", the body, intuition), three adult functions he calls the Nurturer, the Protector and the Leader/Guru, Higher Self, "borrowed adulthood", inductions and a complete return, influence literacy ("de-hypnosis": noticing and resisting other people's influence), and safety limits (when to stop, when to get help). Its quoted lines are scripts the reader says or thinks.

PUBLISHED section:
[B1] Reverse whatever you used to enter. If you floated, regain chosen weight. If you became small, return to ordinary scale. If you met a younger state or several parts, confirm present wholeness, body ownership, and adult judgment. If you traveled, come back by the same route. Keep only the qualities you deliberately chose.

[B2] “I bring back only what is useful now. The rest can remain safely held until there is more capacity. I return to the room, the body, the day, and one honest action. All instructions to deepen or remain in this session are finished. I keep only the ordinary-life intention or future-practice cue I consciously chose; neither keeps today’s altered state running. I am myself, in my body, with adult judgment fully available.”

[B3] One — feel the body’s contact and chosen weight. Two — let the breath return to its ordinary rhythm. Three — hear the room and name where you are. Four — move fingers, toes, shoulders, and eyes. Five — eyes open, present, ordinary, clear. The session has ended.

[B4] Name the day or date. Look left, right, near, and far. Move before standing, and stand only when steady. Check that you are actually oriented and alert; repeating the count is not proof of a complete return. Note only what actually appeared, and any action it calls for. Then do something normal.

[B5] If a recording stops unexpectedly: say, “The recording stopped and the session is over.” Open your eyes, name the location and day, press your feet or hands into a surface, move, look around, and use the five-step count. Don’t wait for the missing narrator to release you.

[B6] Sleep is separate. A sleep recording may end in sleep or quiet rest. A waking recording shouldn’t end by preserving a role, reduced judgment, impaired movement, blankness, an undefined reset word, or an invitation to remain altered indefinitely.

REWRITE:
[CHANGED, replaces B1]
Bring yourself out of the session, in reverse order of how you got into it. If you floated up, float back down again, etc., remembering that you have whatever weight you chose earlier in the session. If you shrank yourself, grow back to your normal size. If you went back to when you were younger or went inside to talk to parts of yourself, remember that you are now a whole person, this is your body, and you're using your adult judgement now. If you traveled somewhere, come back in the same way that you went, remembering to bring back only that which you chose to bring back.

[CHANGED, replaces B2]
Say to yourself, "I am bringing back only that which will be of use to me now. Everything else will remain safely stored until such time as I choose to use it. I am now coming back to this place, to my body, to normal living, to one honest action at a time. From this point on, nothing will make me go inside again unless I choose to use these practices again in the future."

[CHANGED, replaces B3]
Count yourself out of the session: "One, coming back into your body, remembering that you have whatever weight you chose at the beginning of the session. Two, coming back to normal breathing. Three, becoming aware of all the sounds around you now, and where you are. Four, moving your fingers, your toes, your shoulders, your eyes. Five, opening your eyes, feeling normal, the session is now over."

[CHANGED, replaces B4]
Whether coming out of an induction by a live person or from an audio recording, once you are told or instructed to open your eyes, you may want to enhance your coming out by saying to yourself either what day it is or what date it is, looking to your left and right, looking at something close and something far away, moving a bit before standing up. Once you feel steady, stand up. Make sure you are oriented and alert. Just because you can count does not mean you are oriented and alert. Mentally make note of any information that came up during the session and if there is something that you need or do not need to do as a result of the session. Then proceed to do whatever it is that you normally do.

[CHANGED, replaces B5]
In the event that you are using a recording and for some reason it stops, just say to yourself that the recording has stopped and the session is over. Then use the above method to come out by opening your eyes, saying where you are, saying what day it is, pushing down with your feet or hands, moving about, looking about and then using the 5 step method listed above.

[CHANGED, replaces B6]
NOTE: When using a recording to take you into a sleep state, it is fine to let the recording put you to sleep or into a quiet time of resting. But when using a recording during a waking practice, the recording should not be leaving you in a role, with lowered judgement, with lowered movement capabilities, blank, with an un-defined “reset word” or inviting you to stay in an altered state for an unlimited amount of time.
