Let yourself find a rhythm, either by focusing on natural rhythms like breathing, humming or tapping a finger, listening to some outside sounds, or even by counting in silence, or by not letting any rhythm at all. Keep it going, and say to yourself ‘here’, and ‘here’ again, and then let the rhythm drop and see if a blank space arises – if something comes to meet with more clarity, as a word or feeling, as a need or action, then welcome it, let the rhythm flow again and come back to it again.
