To cut off all contact and communication from “bad” entities, such as from hostile presences, shadow forces, grandiose parts of yourself, or anything which gives you imagery in altered states that you are special or chosen, or that you are being “downloaded” with information, etc. say:

Thank you for the imagery, but unless you can tell me something that will help me do the dishes or clean my room, or some other simple, down-to-earth, everyday task that increases love in my life or in the world, then what you are telling me is not what I need on my path.

I am only interested in guidance that will help me to do everyday tasks in a way that increases beauty, honesty, relating, humility, dependability, and usefulness to others.
