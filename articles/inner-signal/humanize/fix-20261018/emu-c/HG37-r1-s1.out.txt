I say this from experience, dear reader, as this is an appendix.

It’s possible that at some point, through self-hypnosis, meditation, lucid dreaming, loveyhuasca, deep grief experiences, prayer, or some other similar practice, or some other time of altered state, one may come into contact with an internal entity that feels different than a normal emotion, younger self, etc. Perhaps it feels like a demon, jinn, parasitic energy, dark intelligence, dark archetypal force, etc., and may manifest as a voice or image, etc. It may seem to emit hate, try to seduce, seem very scary or threatening, seem to mock, or seem to be somehow foreign or intrusive.

Whether or not these are “real” in a metaphysical sense, or are simply a symbolic representation of a mind-form, a trauma, trapped energy, dissociated energy, etc., whether they are inherited from past ancestors, or stem from some other human, etc., what is most helpful to consider at this time is, “What can I do that would be the most helpful way to respond to this?”
