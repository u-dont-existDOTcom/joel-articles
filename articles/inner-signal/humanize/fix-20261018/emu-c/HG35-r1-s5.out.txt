Some Thoughts on Forgiveness: We can’t “force” forgiveness, it has to come from a place of understanding, otherwise it becomes a bypass. And yet, sometimes it’s also not so great to stay in anger forever. How do we hold all of this? It can all be true.

A helpful understanding to come to, is that while their actions may have been harmful, in a sense, they could only make the moves they made, given their accumulated nature at the time (conditioning, history, pain, capacity, etc). This doesn’t negate that harm was caused, holding someone accountable, allowing for consequences, working on making different moves now, etc. It simply helps us come to a place where we aren’t expecting something from someone that they can’t give.

“You don’t have to forgive, but also, please don’t let this wound run your life forever, and don’t use spirituality to silence your pain.”

Some questions that may be helpful to ask when coming to a place of forgiveness, while still holding all of the above:

What actually happened? What was the impact? What boundaries need to be set? What was going on with the other person(s) that put them in a place to cause harm? What is theirs? What is mine? How do I need to hold them accountable? How can I let this go, without denying any of the above?
