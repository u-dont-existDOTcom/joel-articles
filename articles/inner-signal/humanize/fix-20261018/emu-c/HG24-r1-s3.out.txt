There may not be an answer. There may not be a helpful answer. It may be that this isn’t a helpful question to ask at all, and you need to try a different one, or rest, or finish. But if you do sense that there’s something you need to consider about trust, what if you asked:

“What would you need to know in order to trust me?”

You might be surprised by how profoundly un-mystical the answer is. “I need to know you’ll set a boundary.” “I need to know you’ll follow through.” “I need to know you’ll stop leaving.” “I need to know you’ll stop telling me that what I’m asking for isn’t actually what I need.”
