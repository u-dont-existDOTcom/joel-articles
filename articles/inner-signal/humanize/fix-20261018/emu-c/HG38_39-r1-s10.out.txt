Choose someone or something who has a characteristic you want to adopt. Close your eyes and imagine them. Now imagine yourself becoming them, standing, walking and talking like them, just 1% more, or if you are uncomfortable with this, simply observe them, but in either case be aware of how you now have more access to that quality. Notice what happens. Repeat a few times. Now think of a situation you want to use that characteristic in. Finally let go of the person/thing, but keep the characteristic, and say to yourself "I am me", and notice how you have increased access to the characteristic you wanted.
