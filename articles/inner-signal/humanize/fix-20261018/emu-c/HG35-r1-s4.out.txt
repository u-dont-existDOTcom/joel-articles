First go within; ask the child in you, the adult in you, the conscious and subconscious minds, your intuition and analysis. But if the thing is larger than any of those, go outside for more help. The bigger and more irreversible the move, the more you need to look outside.

Go to people who love you, people who are like you, or people who have traveled that path. Of course sometimes going alone is the right thing, but I would do that as a last step, after first seeking counsel.

On the other hand, it is possible to give your power away. Seeking counsel doesn’t mean abdicating to an authority figure. It doesn’t mean either trusting or not trusting someone.

Sometimes you need the support of your peers. In Hearthwork, the concept is that you give each other equal time, warmth, confidentiality, and acceptance of “no” as an answer, and you act for each other as Nurturer and Protector, but not as Guide. Then you reverse roles. Sometimes, if asked carefully and at the right time, your peers can help you hear your own Guide more clearly, but they don’t interpret what you experience.
