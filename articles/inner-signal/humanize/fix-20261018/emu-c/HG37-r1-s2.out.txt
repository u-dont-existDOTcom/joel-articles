Aborting an Experience If something comes up that appears to be a demon, a Jinn, or any other type of hostile or intrusive entity, do not automatically assume it is metaphysically real. Instead, run through the triage list: Is there a medical danger? Something wrong in the environment? Something wrong in the body? Are there overload or mind opening issues? Open the eyes. Feel the feet. Reconnect with the environment. Reduce the stimulation. Remove yourself from the company of anyone who might be hostile. Cease to prompt for any further experience. Do not attempt to further enhance the experience or delve for truth or wrestle with ontological issues until stability is regained.

Affirm, “At this point in time, only what can be integrated will be allowed to come up. Anything else can wait. The adult will be present at all times. The body will be kept in a safe place.”

If the experience becomes dangerous to yourself or others, or if it reaches the point of fear that it resembles psychosis, or if it reaches a point where it prevents sleep or is extremely paranoid or results in chest pain, seizures, serious overheating, overdose or loss of basic life functioning, consider the self-hypnosis session over and obtain assistance in the “real” world.
