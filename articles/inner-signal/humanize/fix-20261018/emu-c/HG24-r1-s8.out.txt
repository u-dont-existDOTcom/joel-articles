Three things to remember when the practice starts to turn into a performance:

1. Stop trying to make it better. Put one hand on the heart, the other on the solar plexis, and say to yourself,

2. “I am not here to do this perfectly. I am here to be in relationship with that which is deeper than the surface of my mind.”

3. “What can I do to make this more a communion than a performance?”

4. Sometimes the answer to that question is to be quiet, sometimes it is to rest, sometimes it is to do something. Do whatever the answer is that comes to yourself.
