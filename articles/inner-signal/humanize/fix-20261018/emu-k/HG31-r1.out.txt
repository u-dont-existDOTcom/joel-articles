Pattern #9: Use Nominalizations in Moderation

Nominalizations are words like healing, safety, trust, integration, communion, clarity, rest, and so on. Sometimes it’s good to use vague language, because then the subconscious fills in the blank with whatever is most appropriate and specific for that person.
