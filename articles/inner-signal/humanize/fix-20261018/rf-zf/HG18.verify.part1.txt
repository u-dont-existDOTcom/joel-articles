Below is one section of a long self-hypnosis guide by Joel, published on his Substack. It teaches gathering attention, listening to the subconscious as an ecology of parts (the inner child, protectors or "guards", the body, intuition), three adult functions he calls the Nurturer, the Protector and the Leader/Guru, Higher Self, "borrowed adulthood", inductions and a complete return, influence literacy ("de-hypnosis": noticing and resisting other people's influence), and safety limits (when to stop, when to get help). Its quoted lines are scripts the reader says or thinks.

PUBLISHED section:
[B1] Reverse whatever you used to enter. If you floated, regain chosen weight. If you became small, return to ordinary scale. If you met a younger state or several parts, confirm present wholeness, body ownership, and adult judgment. If you traveled, come back by the same route. Keep only the qualities you deliberately chose.

[B2] “I bring back only what is useful now. The rest can remain safely held until there is more capacity. I return to the room, the body, the day, and one honest action. All instructions to deepen or remain in this session are finished. I keep only the ordinary-life intention or future-practice cue I consciously chose; neither keeps today’s altered state running. I am myself, in my body, with adult judgment fully available.”

[B3] One — feel the body’s contact and chosen weight. Two — let the breath return to its ordinary rhythm. Three — hear the room and name where you are. Four — move fingers, toes, shoulders, and eyes. Five — eyes open, present, ordinary, clear. The session has ended.

[B4] Name the day or date. Look left, right, near, and far. Move before standing, and stand only when steady. Check that you are actually oriented and alert; repeating the count is not proof of a complete return. Note only what actually appeared, and any action it calls for. Then do something normal.

[B5] If a recording stops unexpectedly: say, “The recording stopped and the session is over.” Open your eyes, name the location and day, press your feet or hands into a surface, move, look around, and use the five-step count. Don’t wait for the missing narrator to release you.

[B6] Sleep is separate. A sleep recording may end in sleep or quiet rest. A waking recording shouldn’t end by preserving a role, reduced judgment, impaired movement, blankness, an undefined reset word, or an invitation to remain altered indefinitely.

REWRITE:
[CHANGED, replaces B1]
Make sure to reverse anything you've done to alter your state when you come out of it. If you've floated, make sure you regain your weight (of your choice, of course). If you've shrunk, grow to your normal size again. If you've regressed or met parts, make sure you come back to feeling complete in the here and now and acknowledge that your body belongs to you and is under control of the adult mind. If you've gone somewhere, make sure you go back the same way. Etcetera. Make sure that the only things you bring out with you are the ones you want to.

[CHANGED, replaces B2]
Here is a generic bring-back that you can use: "When I come out of this session, I will only bring with me that which is useful to me in my everyday life right now. Anything else I will bring with me at a later date, when I am better able to integrate it. When I come out of this session, I will be coming back to the room I am in now, and to the day in which I am participating in this session now. All instructions you have given me to carry me deeper in this session or to keep me in this state are now finished. The only instruction I will take with me into my everyday life, or the only cue I will use to take me into this state in the future, is the one I consciously choose to. When I come out of this session, I will be me, in my body, with my adult judgment."

[CHANGED, replaces B3]
Then count up, using the five steps listed above: 1...feel your body again, feel your weight (of your choice). 2...feel your breathing return to normal. 3...become aware of your surroundings, know where you are. 4...wiggle your fingers and toes, move your shoulders and your eyes. 5...open your eyes, back in the here and now, feeling normal and clear. This session is now over."

[CHANGED, replaces B4]
When you come out, remind yourself what day (or date) it is. Look to your left and your right. Look at something near, and something far. Move around a bit before getting up. Get up when you feel ready. Make sure you're truly oriented and alert. Don't just assume it because you've done the count. Take mental note of anything that has come up for you and any actions you need to take regarding it. Then go on and do something normal.

[CHANGED, replaces B5]
If your recording cuts out for some reason, you can say to yourself, "The recording has stopped and the session is over," and then do the above on your own.

[CHANGED, replaces B6]
Note: If you're going to sleep to a recording, it's fine to go to sleep or just to remain resting quietly at the end of it. But if you're coming out, you should not leave yourself in an altered state, including leaving yourself in a role, with judgment lowered, with movements impaired, blank, with a non-specific reset word, or indefinitely.
