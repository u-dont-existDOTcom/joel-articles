Come all the way back

Exiting a session is always done in reverse to the way you entered it. If you floated or used another method to go down, do the same to get back down and back into your body, weighting yourself as you chose to do when you entered. If you shrunk yourself, grow back to your normal size. If you went to a younger you or other parts of yourself, take a moment to acknowledge that you are now whole, this is your body and that you have the judgement of an adult. If you traveled, return in the same way. Be sure you are only bringing back what you need to bring back.
