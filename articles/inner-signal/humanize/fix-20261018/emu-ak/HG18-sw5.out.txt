A note about coming out of a recording: If the recording suddenly stops, it is best that you immediately say to yourself that the recording has stopped and that the session is over and use the above methods to come out: open your eyes, say where you are, say what day it is, push down with your feet or your hands, move about, look around and then do the 5 step method. Don’t just sit there expecting the voice to come back and release you from the session.

NOTE: If you are using a recording to take you to sleep, then it is all right to let it put you to sleep or leave you in a quiet, resting state. However, any recording that is meant to be used while you are awake should NOT leave you in a role, with lowered judgement, impaired movement capabilities, blank, with an undefined reset word, invited to stay in an altered state indefinitely, etc.
