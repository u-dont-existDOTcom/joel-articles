You get stuck in spiritual bypass

Are you asking “Where is that divine love that is not descending upon me to heal my pain?”

Maybe you had a profound opening that showed you how much more love and freedom is possible. Maybe when you prayed it seemed like you were being abandoned and condemned. Maybe you felt betrayed by a spiritual teacher. It may be worth exploring these, but you might not want to do more of the same.

Sometimes it’s best to get some practical help, and let yourself be curious about finding more happiness, without necessarily believing the explanation of how it’s going to work. Sometimes people have enough love and meaning to just live their lives, and trying to be spiritual is not in their cards.

Are you stuck in endless processing?

You may have a real wound that needs to be dealt with, but it’s possible you’re caught in a checking loop. Consider asking yourself “Will processing this again actually help me feel, understand, decide, or do something differently? Or is this a form of checking to see if I can get the reassurance that something is finally okay?” Sometimes, whether you’re grieving or doing spiritual practice, it’s okay to cover the same ground again and again.

But if you think you’re in a checking loop, let the thought be there without arguing with it or trying to make it go away, and then go back to whatever you’ve chosen to do. It’s not necessary to hold a meeting with every thought that comes up, or to do a certain ritual before your life can begin.
