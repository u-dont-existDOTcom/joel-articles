- Borrow an adult function. You may “borrow” this from a safe adult in your life, a mentor, an older sibling, a future version of yourself, a spiritual figure, a value, or even a written plan. Borrow only what you need right now – warmth, a boundary or direction.

- Put on the adult hat as an apprentice. Now, in this position, figure out what you would want to say or do – and then do at least a part of that.
