Induction: gathering attention without disappearing

Why would you want to be induced into a trance? Mainly because it makes it easier to hear what you have to hear, especially if it’s quiet. No, you will not fall asleep, give up your free will, or obey anybody if you’re induced. Generally, if you’re sober and feeling scattered, anal, distracted, or guarded, an actual induction may be needed. On the other hand, if you’ve already meditated, taken loveyhuasca, entered a lucid dream, are out of your head from grief, music, prayer, exhaustion, etc. go straight to orienting yourself, and containing the work.

How deep? As deep as you need to be able to hold focus, warmth, receptivity, embodiment, and safety to stop at any time. Deeper than that is optional.

What is the induction actually doing?

1. It’s preparing you for what you’re doing, gathering your attention, and if necessary, deepening your state to assist the work. You can modify what you’re saying as you go along. Counting is an excellent way of guiding someone into trance. Other ways may be more suitable to your purposes. Use them. If you use something other than counting, state what you’re doing. It can be done covertly, but why be inelegant?

2. If you have a simple target, try it before you induce. If it’s working, deepen to assist it. If not, induce to assist receptivity. You don’t have to complete a whole ceremony before allowing yourself to respond to what you’re doing.

Please do not use 6 different metaphors in the same trance!!!

Choose what you want to do, start with one thing (anchor or image), and have a way of getting back. Do you want warmth? firmness? direction? neutrality? Do not use floating, staircases, gardens, inner spaces, colored lights, and dialoguing with your inner child all in the same induction, unless you change from one to the other very consciously.

You don’t even have to use imagery. You can use sensation, rhythm, movement, words, spatial sense, simple knowing, or no response at all. Keep the language you use for counting in keeping with the world you’ve chosen. When you’re done with the induction, reverse your journey home. You can borrow useful qualities, but don’t borrow somebody else’s identity.

Use one central verb, one central sensory quality, one short refrain, and one way of transitioning. Read your induction aloud before you use it. Sentences tend to be long and elegant in writing, but they turn to soup in induction.

Things to do before you start:

Read this induction, and come all the way back before you begin. Decide what you want to explore, what you want to leave alone, how much time you have, and go after what you want. Love, play, confidence, creativity, communion, etc. are all good things to work on. Be sure to leave enough time to come all the way back, and think about what you’ve done.
