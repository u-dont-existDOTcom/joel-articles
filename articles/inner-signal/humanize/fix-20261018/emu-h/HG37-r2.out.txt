IF YOU NEED A BODILY BOUNDARY WITH COMPASSION, CLOSE THE DOOR:

If the presence feels hostile, cold, alien, parasitic, violating, etc., you may not want to bring any kind of soft, sentimental love to this. You may want to bring a love that has a healthy boundary.

Sit up tall, press your feet into the floor, elongate your spine, pull your shoulder blades back gently. Feel your body’s skin-boundary – feel your torso’s boundary.

SAY (aloud or internally): “Even if there is suffering here, I wish for all suffering to go where it needs to go, but my skin-boundary is my adult body. You are not permitted in my house.”

Then say: “Even though I am feeling compassion, I am closing the door.”

IF YOU THINK YOU ARE GOING PSYCHOTIC OR GETTING DE-STABILIZED:

There is a big difference between experiencing some symbolic “hostile presences” in yourself that settle with grounding and loving awareness, and going into a persistent state of paranoia, inability to sleep, danger to self or others, inability to communicate with others, inability to reality-test, mania, etc., etc.

If this work is making you more loving, grounded, relational, honest, functional, etc., then proceed. If it’s making you more paranoid, unable to sleep, grandiose, unable to communicate or function, afraid, unable to reality-test, etc., then don’t proceed and get help!

THE “WASHING THE DISHES” TRICK FOR HALLOWED/COSMIC DOWNLOADS:

Sometimes these presences/shadows/parts try to convince you of their importance in other ways, such as by pretending to give you some “cosmic download.” To these, say:

“Unless the information you are giving me helps me to wash the dishes or clean my room or do something simple and loving for somebody, it’s not going to help me on my path.”
