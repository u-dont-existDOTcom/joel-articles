APPENDIX J: Inductions to build a library of

What are you going to do next in the sequence? Do you want to do it at all? If you’re using a family of inductions, have you tried the lighter ones, or doing it with your eyes open, or even just doing no-trance contact in place of the trance induction? What happened when you tried to do it (even if it seemed that nothing happened)? What’s the next thing to try – something new, moving your body, using imagery, using a different sensory channel, just sitting still for a while, giving up on it…? Do you need to bring yourself fully awake out of the induction?

NOTE: If you’re not able to generate imagery with any vividness, you can translate the metaphors into words, rhythms, spatial, touch, movement, or even just knowing concepts.
