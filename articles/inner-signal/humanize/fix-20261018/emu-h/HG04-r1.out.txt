Quick start: do this first

Conjure, notice, respond, wait, return. Use these to work a difficulty, or to cultivate love, curiosity, play, confidence, and other qualities. In every session, not much has to happen!

Before the first session, read this induction and full return while awake, to get familiar. Set aside a chunk of time, make yourself physically secure for the time. Prior to each session, consider just one thing you'll work on or cultivate; what you're leaving alone today. After putting this on the shelf,

you'll follow whatever you notice - sensation, image, phrase, feeling, rebellion, silence - as you're invited. Vary the invites as needed to be responsive & warm, to create a boundary, to proceed, etc. Complete the return. After, note what happened & any real-world actions.

If at any point you feel panicky, disoriented, more unreal, or less functional, this is not about bravely sticking with difficult feelings (that's another choice, made freely & while staying present). Return to the room & be appropriately supported.

Do you usually think in words?

When you're by yourself, do you talk to yourself in your head -- silent words & phrases -- a lot, some, little, never? Does it vary depending on what you're doing or thinking about? If it doesn't come naturally, can you make yourself think in words, silently? (Thinking in words doesn't mean you hear them.)

This doesn't mean that in any given moment you may not notice feelings before words. You may not know. This isn't about being right, just finding what's useful. "Not sure" is an answer.

If you don't normally think in words, you may want to say things aloud, in writing, on a recording, etc., when this guide says "say." Or, just do what it invites, without preparing what you're going to say. When it says "ask" the child or protector, or "listen" for a response, it doesn't mean they'll be saying full sentences to you. It also doesn't mean you'll get pictures in your head instead. Just do whatever you can, and try different ways as needed.
