Appendix H: Working with Demons, Jinn, Other Hostile Presences, and Unattached Burdens

I’m going to come from a bit more experience here.

Sometimes, during self-hypnosis, meditation, lucid dreaming, loveyhuasca, extreme grief, prayer, or a variety of other altered states of consciousness, you may encounter something you perceive to be a demon, a jinn, a parasitic energy, some other kind of dark intelligence, or even a terrifying archetype. Sometimes it comes as a voice. Sometimes as an image. But it is not a simple emotion, and not merely a younger part.

First and foremost, it doesn’t matter for the purposes of this guide whether they are literally demons or not. All that matters is what the best way of dealing with them is, in terms of what leaves you feeling the safest, clearest, most loving, most grounded, etc.

So what do you do when a demon, or jinn, or other bad or intrusive entity shows up? The same thing you do first for anything else— you check in with the room, your body, and safety in general.

“If there is anything that can be integrated right now, you may come forward. But know that you can wait, and that you will not be able to come forward until you are something that can be integrated. Know also that I will always be here as an adult, and that my body will bring you to a place of safety.”

If you feel like you’re in danger of hurting yourself or others, if you feel like you’re going psychotic and afraid, if you feel like you won’t be able to sleep afterwards, if you feel extremely paranoid, if you have chest pain, if you feel like you’re going to have a seizure, if you feel like you’re too hot in a serious way, if you feel like you’ve taken too much of something, or if you feel like you just can’t function in the world anymore, then the self-hypnosis session is over. Go get some real-world help.
