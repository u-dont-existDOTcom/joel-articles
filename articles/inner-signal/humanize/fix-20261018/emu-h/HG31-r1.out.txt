Creating an anchor or doorway back in.

There are lots of ways to anchor something. Put your hand on your heart, touch two fingers together, use a breath, a word, body posture, smell, song, anything. Feel something that you have got to in reality that you want to get back to easily. As you feel it do the anchor thing and say to yourself that this is what you are anchoring and it is available to you. Make sure you do it when you have clearly got it. To get back into a hypnotic state easily don't go for a peak emotional state but find a good receptive state that you can use. See the main induction section on a shortcut back into hypnosis. Make sure that this is separate from the alert, calming, synchronicity wake up thing.
