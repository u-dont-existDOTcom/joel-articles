Sleep induction

This is not like other inductions on this website. The goal of other inductions is to get all the way back to your Adult so you can write and do things. The goal of this induction is to go to sleep, so it's okay to not do any analyzing or allow yourself any revelations.

For a hypnosis to help you sleep, try out Nimja's sleep playlist here .

If you want to do fractionation (optional)

Fractionation is when you bring the person back up and send them back down into absorption. As long as you're doing it openly (telling them you're doing it), it's okay, because it teaches the person that they can always get out of trance. Not all questions should have the answer "deeper."

At some point you can say, "When you're ready, open your eyes and bring yourself all the way back up. You can decide if you want to end the hypnosis here, or you can close/soften your eyes and send yourself back down into absorption again."

If you want to deepen (optional)

"When it's useful for me to deepen my absorption, I can. When it's not useful, I'm just as awake as I need to be."

Then you can do a room in your heart, or a garden, or a temple, or sitting next to a fire with your Protector nearby, or floating on a clear lake, or sitting in a chair, etc. Don't do more than one of these.

See who or what wants to talk to you first.

To check how deep in trance you are (optional)

Give up all responsibility for making it happen.

"When my wise subconscious knows that I am at the meeting space inside myself that it wants to talk to me at, it can make one of my fingers twitch, or pulse, or lift up, or feel warm, or tingly, or somehow different, if it wants to."

You're not going to make it happen or fake it. You're just going to wait with love for it to happen.

It doesn't mean that anything will happen, but if something happens with one of your fingers, or you feel some other bodily change, it doesn't necessarily mean that it's real, but it does mean that something is listening to you.

shortcut to getting back under hypnosis quickly

Once you've learned some of the inductions on this website and also the bringing yourself all the way back down induction, you can make up your own shortcut for getting back under hypnosis. My dad used to do a mudra (hand sign) by bringing two fingers together in an "okay" sign, and it would help him relax. He even did it while driving, so don't use the same thing for calming yourself while driving as you do for going into inward hypnosis!
