Appendix J: A practical induction library

Practise: Preview, choose a purpose, decide to start. All ‘families’ can be light, eyes open or with no trance contact replacement. Monitor what happens (even if nothing seems to change) and decide on next action: move on, imagine, change sensory channel, remain still, stop the sequence etc. Always do a complete waking return from induction.

NB If you cannot ‘see’ any images vividly, translate the metaphor into words, rhythm, spatial relations, touch, movement, knowing etc.

Stretch and release (to use)

Focus on a feeling of support (and perhaps the position of one hand or foot). Either actually or in imagination stretch slightly and/or press into the support and then release. Notice any difference in feeling (even if nothing seems to change). Repeat this with no more than 3 areas of the body. Use the notion of ‘release’ to guide you through the sequence. Ask yourself where in your body (and/or voice) you need to stretch or release and what you need by way of support to do this. Then bring back normal movement and return.
