Most clinical practice guidelines for the treatment of major trauma recommend one or more of trauma-specific variants of cognitive behavioral therapy (often referred to by specific names, such as cognitive processing therapy, prolonged exposure, etc.) or eye movement desensitization and reprocessing , and in some cases one or the other or both in addition to each other. These approaches in turn can be further supported by techniques such as self-hypnosis and inner child work. You need that bigger container when working with high-risk trauma.
