Sober induction: 3–5 minutes

Practise reading this slowly into a recorder, or reading it aloud, or if you can learn it and use silent words. Choose to use one of the three anchors – sound, bodily contact or two hands - throughout and choose an opening phrase. Whenever the words below refer to ‘the anchor’ use the one you’ve chosen. Pause for a few moments at the end of each paragraph. Don’t read aloud the instructions for how to do the reading.

Let your eyes settle somewhere in the room, somewhere they can rest for a little while, and notice the difference between looking for something and letting what is already there come into view.

As you notice the surface beneath you, notice the feel of the contact, the pressure, whatever you can actually feel. And while you notice that, a sound may reach you, or a breath may draw your attention, without needing you to arrange either one.

There’s time to do this.

It might be that a thought about something that’s happened during the day is still with you, or perhaps there’s a thought about doing this properly. Whatever is there, can you find it in yourself to turn your attention back to the anchor, even if there is a thought there?

and when you do come back, you may find one detail that holds your interest, something about the sound, or the contact, or that particular way of listening, and let attention stay with it long enough to become curious

Perhaps you can give yourself the time that you might give to someone dear to you who has begun to speak and hasn’t yet found the words. You can remain nearby, listening, without finishing their sentence for them

Let the phrase you chose come to mind, say it as an invitation, and notice how it is received

You can close your eyes, soften them or keep them open as you proceed. Let that be a choice you make. Listening may deepen while a sound from the room remains available in the background

Count slowly from 5 to 1, giving each number its own moment

5, perhaps notice again what you have chosen to be your anchor for today, 4, let attention gather around what you find, 3, leave a little space after the words, 2, listen into that space, without preparing an answer, 1, stay here for a moment

Bring to mind, lightly, your chosen concern. Or simply, stay with the wish to contact. Let it be present without rehearsing its whole story.

What do you notice now?

Allow yourself some time to notice what is coming. It might be sensations, words, images, feelings, the urge to move in some way or nothing clear yet.

Allow yourself some time to notice this, with or without words.

Already open? Contain before you deepen

This is most useful after you have already opened yourself to a state, through loveyhuasca, meditation, lucid dreaming, dealing with grief, listening to music, prayer, engagement with erotic energy, exhaustion, or being overwhelmed by beauty. This is to stabilize your state before making it stronger.

“The state is already open. I do not need to intensify it. I need to aim it, stabilize it, and keep it kind.”

Look around the room. Name where you are. Name the date, or at least the day. Feel your feet, hands, and spine. Focus on the physical sensation of pressing your thumbs gently but firmly against your index fingers, feel the friction.

“I will only allow myself to go as far as I can integrate. Anything too much can wait. My body may stay safe, my adult self is here, my inner child doesn't have to do this alone.”

Once you’re oriented, choose one intention: “Please show me the next useful truth, symbol, feeling, question, or action — only in the amount I can live well with.”

Stay awake when your mind likes to escape

You can do this practice sitting up, with your eyes open, feeling your feet and letting your breath be natural.

Begin by saying: “I am not going away. I am coming more fully here.”

Then choose an object to look at, and feel your spine.

Ask yourself if your daydreaming is helping you to receive guidance or digest emotional experiences, or if you are escaping from something.

Then you can ask for help: if it is guidance, to show you the action inside it; if it is avoidance, to bring you gently into the present and show you what you are avoiding.

If you find that being still leads you to escape into sleepiness and diffuse states, you can work with increasing alertness rather than relaxation, keep your eyes open and work with doing a movement with your hands, which you repeat for a while and then stop deliberately in order to notice what remains without trying to disappear inward.

You can say something like: ‘While you keep your eyes open, press your thumb and forefinger together gently and then let go; do this a few times, becoming more and more clear and alert each time. Now deliberately stop doing that movement and notice the first second after the rhythm ends, and whether there is an echo in the fingers, the breath, the attention, or nowhere at all.’ Stay upright and work from exactly that level of alertness.

The two-hand induction

Where are YOUR hands? Put one on YOUR heart and the other on YOUR solar plexis.

The hand on YOUR heart is the Nurturer, the one on the solar plexis is the vulnerable child in YOU.

Then breathe gently and affirm

“ The love is above and the child is below. And the adult is present.”

“I send love to the child and I receive the child’s signal. Then I send love again and I listen again.”

Sleep induction

This is not like other inductions. The goal of other inductions is to get all the way back to your Adult so you can write and do things. The goal of this induction is to go to sleep, so analysis can stay off duty and revelation can wait.

Optional fractionation: practice coming back

Fractionation means returning toward ordinary awareness and then deciding whether to enter absorption again. As long as you're doing it openly (naming the technique before you use it), it's okay, because it teaches that trance has a door in both directions. Every answer cannot secretly mean "deeper."

At some point you can say, "Open your eyes and return toward ordinary awareness. Feel the room. You can decide if you want to end the hypnosis here. If you choose to continue, close or soften your eyes and notice what changes as attention gathers again. You may stop at either point."
