Sober induction: 3–5 minutes

Practise reading the following slowly into the recorder or read it aloud or learn it and say it silently. Decide whether to use sound, bodily contact or the two hands as your anchor throughout. Choose one of the opening phrases to use in the following. Whenever you see ‘the anchor’ in the following say the anchor you’ve chosen. Pause at the end of each paragraph for a few moments. Don’t read aloud the instructions for how to do the reading.

Begin by allowing your eyes to settle somewhere in the room

Notice the contact between your body and the surface that you are on. Notice the feel of that contact. As you notice the contact notice too the sound / your breath

There’s time to do this

Perhaps there are some thoughts, about things that have happened today, or perhaps about doing this thing properly, whatever it is, can you turn your attention back to the anchor even if there is a thought there

Perhaps there is something in the way that you are listening to the sound, or touching your body, or holding your hands that catches your attention.

Give yourself the time you would give to someone you were really interested in hearing, who has started to talk to you, but is not yet saying what he or she wanted to.

Notice what comes to you when you say the phrase: <your chosen phrase here>

You can close your eyes, soften them or keep them open as you proceed, it’s up to you. As you listen your listening may deepen but you may always return to the sound in the room.

Count slowly to yourself from 5 to 1

As you say 5 notice again your chosen anchor. 4 3 2 1 Take a moment here

Bring to mind gently the thing you’ve chosen to work with today / your wish to make contact

What do you notice now?

Take some time to notice whatever comes, it might be sensations, it might be words, it might be images, it might be feelings, it might be urges to move your body in some way, it might be nothing.

Take some time to notice this, with or without words

Already open, contain before you deepen

This is good to use when you have opened a state through some other means, e.g. loyeuhasca, meditation, lucid dreaming, dealing with a grief, listening to music, prayer, working with erotic energy, being exhausted, being moved by something beautiful. It is to stabilise the state before you try to make it stronger.

I don’t need to intensify this state, it’s already open, I need to aim it, stabilise it and keep it kind

Look around you, notice where you are, say to yourself where you are, say to yourself the date or the day, notice your body, your feet, hands, spine. Pick one place, for instance, where your thumb meets your index finger, press them firmly together and concentrate only on the physical sensation of the friction

"I'm going to allow myself to go only as far as I am able to integrate. Too much can wait. My body is safe. I'm present as an adult. My inner child doesn't have to do this alone".

Once you're oriented, pick one of these phrases to be your intention: "Please show me the next thing I need to know, as a symbol, feeling, question or action, in a way that I can live my life."

Be awake when you might otherwise escape

Sit up, open your eyes, feel your feet and breath naturally.

Acknowledge: "I'm not going to go away, I'm going to come more into myself".

Pick an object to look at and feel your spine.

Ask yourself, are you daydreaming in order to hear guidance or in order to integrate emotional experience or are you escaping from something?

If you're trying to hear guidance, ask to have the guidance to be about what to do. If you're escaping from something, ask to be brought gently into the present and shown what you're avoiding.

If you find that doing still work you escape into sleepiness or diffuse states of mind, change the work so that it is increasing levels of alertness and not relaxation. A good way to work in a state of alertness, with your eyes open, is to have the client do something with their hands for a little while and then deliberately stop doing it. For example:

"Open your eyes and press your thumb and forefinger together. Let it go. Do that a few times. Each time you let go, become more clear, more alert. Now deliberately stop doing that. Pay attention to the first second after you stop doing that. Do you feel an echo in your fingers? In your breath? In your attention? Or does it go nowhere?" Stay upright and work with that level of alertness.

Induction using two hands

Where are YOUR hands? Put one of YOUR hands on YOUR heart. Put the other of YOUR hands on YOUR solar plexis.

The hand on YOUR heart is the Nurturer. The hand on YOUR solar plexis is the vulnerable child in YOU.

Breathe gently and affirm

The love is above and the child is below. And the adult is present.

I send love down to the child. And I receive signal from the child. Then I send more love down. And I listen to the signal again.

Induction to go to sleep

This induction is different from the others, because in the others you need to get all the way back to your Adult to be able to write, do things, etc. In this case, the point is to go to sleep, so it is good for analysis to NOT be on duty and for revelation to wait.
