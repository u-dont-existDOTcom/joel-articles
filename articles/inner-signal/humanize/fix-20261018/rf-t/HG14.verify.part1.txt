PUBLISHED section:
[B6] For that starting point, I use a provisional, author-developed bridge I call borrowed adulthood. It combines familiar practices—witnessing, modeling, supported action, and gradual transfer of responsibility—without claiming that the complete sequence has been validated as one treatment.

[B7] Witness, when needed. Notice what is present without demanding warmth, wisdom, or certainty. If you already distinguish the internal positions, move to the adult function you need.

[B8] Borrow one function. Choose a safe adult, mentor, older sibling, future self, spiritual figure, value, or written plan. Borrow only the warmth, boundary, or direction needed now.

[B9] Become the adult apprentice. Choose the exact words or action yourself and carry at least one small part.

[B43] People assume inner-child work begins with the child. Very often, you meet the guard at the door first.

[B44] You sit down to connect and get numbness, cynicism, anger, sudden hunger, a phone urge, a sexual urge, planning, sleepiness, dissociation, or the thought, “This is stupid.” I wouldn’t call those failures. They’re often the protector making an appearance in its usual charming way.

[B77] Rest also doesn’t require helplessness. Reduce decisions for a defined period, put unfinished tasks somewhere visible, and let the internal adult keep watch. You remain able to move, stop, and return.

[CHANGED, replaces B6]
To jumpstart a development of a new part, I have developed a kind of provisional bridge that I call “borrowed adulthood.” It is important to note here that I’m not suggesting that all of the steps in this process have been formally tested and proven.

[CHANGED, replaces B7]
Access your witnessing position if necessary. If you already know how to access different internal positions, move immediately to accessing the adult position you want to borrow and go to step 3.

[CHANGED, replaces B8]
If you have a safe adult in your life you can borrow from them, if not borrow from a mentor, older brother/sister, imagine your future self, your god/goddess or higher power, a value you have, a plan you have written, etc. It’s ok to just borrow what you need, just like you would borrow a cup of sugar from a neighbor.

[CHANGED, replaces B9]
Next, put on your adult hat as if you are an apprentice adult and do or say what you think you should do or say. You should do at least part of what you think you should do or say.

[CHANGED, replaces B43]
If you're not careful, you might find yourself expecting that when you go within to meet your inner child, you'll find your inner child.

[CHANGED, replaces B44]
Instead, you might feel numb, cynical, angry, hungry, like you need to look at your phone, sexually aroused, like you need to make plans, sleepy, dissociated, or you might even think that what you're doing is stupid. Those are not failures, they are you experiencing what is often a very charming protector or guard.
