Stabilize before you interpret

Self hypnosis is extremely powerful, and when you are in an altered state of consciousness, whether it be from loveyhuasca, or meditation, or when lucid dreaming, or from grief, or sexual energy, or prayer, or not sleeping, or any other way similar to using plant medicines, often you can get your mind to open faster then your adult self can figure out what is going on.

The problem is, you THINK because you are in an altered state, it MEANS something. Sometimes it is your inner child talking, but just as often it is a Protector showing you their fear, or it is your shame or your grief or your anger or your longing or whatever else is in you that wants to be taken care of. Sometimes it is your body’s reaction to being overloaded, or chaos in your environment, or lack of sleep or just your confusion. Just because you had a picture that scared you, doesn’t mean it is true. Just because you had a bad feeling, doesn’t mean it is a lesson you need to learn. Just because you had a download and the lighting was nice, doesn’t mean you should believe it.

What is a simpler question you can ask yourself first?

“What kind of problem is happening?”
