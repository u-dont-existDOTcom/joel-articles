You can rebel, but you can’t choose

This happens because when you were a child, your attention went into avoiding pain, reading danger, meeting expectations and keeping the bond, so you didn’t have the opportunity to explore what you like, what you want to try out and who you are. This is not a claim about one specific brain system but a practical way to look at it.

Sometimes you need to distance yourself for a certain time from the people or group that you normally get your answers from. But not for too long, as then you defeat the purpose of this exercise! The distance helps quieter preferences surface, so you can tell your own signal from the social echo.
