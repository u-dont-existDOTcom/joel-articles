Reframing — changing the meaning without lying

Dishonest reframing declares that the problem was secretly good all along, so I prefer to ask myself questions that widen the meaning without lying, like: “What else could be true here?” or “What is this part trying to do for me?” or “How would my Nurturer reframe this?” or “How would my Protector reframe this?” or “How would my Leader reframe this?” and so on.
