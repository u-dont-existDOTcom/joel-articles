When this needs more support than a guide can provide

Remember, this is self-hypnosis, a relationship with yourself. If the situation calls for support, this guide isn't the right thing.

If you need help now: Get help now if you feel in imminent danger, very confused, or unable to keep yourself safe. Get help now if you have concerning physical symptoms (chest pain, fainting, etc.) You should seek professional help promptly if you have new or worsening suicidal thoughts, severe insomnia, new or worsening symptoms of mania or psychosis, or if dissociative symptoms are affecting your functioning. These are not problems that this guide will resolve in 4-12 weeks.
