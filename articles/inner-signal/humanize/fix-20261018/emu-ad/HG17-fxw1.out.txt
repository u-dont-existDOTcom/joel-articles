Sober induction: 3–5 minutes

Note: It may be useful to practice how you are going to read this into the recorder. You could either read it, or you could learn it. If you learn it, you might like to say some of the words in your head, silently. You will need to choose which of the three anchors you will use throughout – sound, bodily contact or two hands. You will also need to choose a phrase to use for the opening. Whenever the script says ‘the anchor’ use the one you have chosen. At the end of each paragraph pause for a few moments. Do not read out the instructions about how to read.

Allow your eyes to settle somewhere in the room.

As you notice where you are, notice the feel of it, the contact, and as you notice the contact a sound, a breath.

There’s time to do this.

Perhaps there’s a thought there about something that has happened today, or a thought about doing this, but even if there is a thought there, is there a way of allowing your attention to be with the anchor?

And in allowing your attention to be with the anchor, is there something about it that you can be interested in, a particular way of listening,

Perhaps giving yourself as much time as you would if someone dear to you was trying to say something to you,

Notice the receiving of the words of the phrase you have chosen.

You can close your eyes now if you like, or soften them, or keep them open, as you proceed. You choose. Perhaps as you listen your listening deepens, and yet in the background of your listening, a sound.

Counting slowly from 5 down to 1.

5, perhaps noticing again your chosen anchor for today, 4, 3, 2, listening into the space, 1.

Bring to mind lightly the concern you have chosen to work with today. Or if you prefer, simply bring to mind the wish to come into contact.

Perhaps you can notice what it is that you notice.

Allow yourself some time to notice whatever it is that is coming to you, whether it is sensations, or words, or images, or feelings, or movements, or even if it is nothing.

Allow yourself some time to notice this, with or without words.
