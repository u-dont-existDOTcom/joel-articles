[B42] “Where has divine love not yet descended into the wound?”

[B43] A genuine opening can show you a depth of love or freedom you hadn’t known was possible. Let that matter without demanding the child receive it on command. Prayer can also evoke abandonment or condemnation, or a teacher’s betrayal can damage something sacred. Ask what was lost and what needs questioning; more of the same practice isn’t automatically the answer.

[B44] Sometimes practical care helps while the available horizon of life still feels too small. Curiosity about deeper happiness is worth following without pretending to believe an explanation. Other people already have enough love and meaning for the life they want. Spirituality doesn’t need to become another assignment.

[B46] A real wound and a checking loop can coexist. Ask whether this round helps you feel, understand, decide, or respond differently—or whether you’re checking the same question for reassurance. Grief and practice can revisit familiar ground; novelty isn’t required every time.

[B47] When it is a checking loop, let the thought remain without arguing with it or chasing it away, and return to a chosen activity or relationship. You don’t have to hold an inner meeting every time a thought appears or complete a ritual before you’re allowed to live.

[B48] “What direction would the Leader choose now?”

[B50] “What would prove to the inner child that the adult is real?”

[B51] Then do the thing.

[CHANGED, replaces B42-B44]
You’re doing spiritual bypass. Ask yourself, “Where has divine love not come down and hugged your hurt yet?”

You’ve had some amazing opening that showed you how much more love and freedom is possible, but your kid isn’t receiving it. Or maybe prayer has led you to feel abandoned or condemned by God. Or maybe a teacher betrayed you. Whatever the problem, it’s not going to get resolved by doing more of the same.

Sometimes you just need to do something practical to take care of yourself until the horizon of your life expands enough for what you’re seeking to enter. Or you need to follow your curiosity for what will make you happy, regardless of whether it makes sense or not. Or you need to realize you already have plenty of love and meaning in your life, and this whole spirituality thing is optional.

[CHANGED, replaces B46-B48]
You’re processing the same stuff over and over again. First ask yourself, “Do I really have something I need to deal with, or am I just doing a check?” It’s okay to go over the same stuff over and over again (especially when you’re grieving or doing a practice), but sometimes you’re just doing a check.

If you determine you’re doing a check, say to yourself, “It’s okay for that thought to be there, but I don’t need to hold a meeting with it right now. I’m going to do ____ instead.”

Ask yourself, “What would your Leader do in this situation?”
