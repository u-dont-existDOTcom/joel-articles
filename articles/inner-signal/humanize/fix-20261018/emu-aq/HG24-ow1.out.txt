Would you be able to start a bit smaller?

Show yourself one sensation in your body, one word, whether to listen or rest today etc?

Ok… It still feels kind of “fake” though…

Do you know what is fake about it? The words/image/what you are expecting/ the role? It might be that you just don’t have that ‘thing’ in your head (voice/image etc) yet. It isn’t because you don’t have a ‘thing’ in your head doing this that you don’t have this ‘grown up’ thing yet or that you are resisting doing this. If it feels like you are being asked to do something you don’t want to do, then correct this. It could be that you have a part that is trying to protect you from doing this, so talk to this part.

Maybe try asking it “what are you protecting me from?” Or “are you afraid that I am going to lie to myself again?” Or “are you afraid that this is going to be a performance?”

It could be that you have picked up an inner voice of a parent that is enforcing a rule that if you do this then you will belong. In this case, don’t argue with this part. Try and find out what rule it is enforcing and then try and find some ‘every day life’ evidence to support your new ‘grown up’ voice.

If it feels like you are picking up that you don’t have this ‘thing’ in your head yet, that tells you that you shouldn’t try and believe what this ‘thing’ (inner voice) is saying yet. As long as you have some ‘observing ego’ to do this work, you can borrow what you need from someone that you know has got this ‘thing’ (warmth, boundaries, direction etc). It could be a family member, friend, famous person etc. Try something out that rings true for you, it could be a sentence, action etc and see how it goes.
