Say to yourself what day it is or what the date is, look to your left and to your right, look at something near and something far away, move about a bit before you stand up, stand up only when you’re steady, and make sure that you are actually oriented and alert (repeating the count isn’t proof that you’re all the way back). Take note of only what actually came up during your session and what you may or may not need to do as a result, and then just go about doing something normal.

In the event that a recording that you are listening to stops unexpectedly, simply say to yourself “the recording has stopped and the session is over” and then use the above method to come out by opening your eyes, saying where you are, saying what day it is, pushing down with your feet or your hands, moving about, looking around and then using the 5 step method. Don’t sit there waiting for the voice to come back and release you.

Note: If you are using a recording to go to sleep, then it is all right to let the recording put you to sleep or have you in a quiet resting state. However, if it’s a recording for waking practice, then it should not leave you in a role, with lowered judgement, impaired movement capabilities, blank, with an undefined reset word, or invited to stay altered indefinitely.
