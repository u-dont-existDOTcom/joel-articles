Come all the way back

Come out of the session by reversing the entry method; if you floated, come back down by the same method; land with your chosen weight. If you shrunk, come back to your normal size. If you meet your younger self or parts, make sure that you acknowledge that you are now whole, this is your body, and you are using your adult judgement. If you traveled, then come back the same way you left. Make sure that you are only bringing back what you had intended to bring back.

Say, “I am bringing back only what is useful now. All that is not needed at this time will remain safely stored away until such time as I am ready. I am now coming back to this place and to my body and to normal day to day living, and to one honest action. All instructions to go deeper or stay in this session are now over. I keep only the ordinary-life intention or future-practice cue I consciously chose, and neither one keeps today’s altered state running. I am myself, in my body, with my adult judgment fully available.”

Count yourself out: 1. Coming back into your body and your chosen weight. 2. Coming back to your normal breathing pattern. 3. Hearing the room, and naming where you are. 4. Moving your fingers and your toes and your shoulders and your eyes. 5. Opening your eyes, feeling present, normal and clear. Your session is now over.
