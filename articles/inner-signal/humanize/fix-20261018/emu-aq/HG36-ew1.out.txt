It can be useful to notice that a certain state of intensity may have meaning: perhaps it is a way of connecting with inner child parts, or a Protector who is afraid, or other parts who feel shame/grief/anger/longing etc. It can also be that the body is overloaded, the environment is chaotic, there’s a sleep deficit or there’s confusion about something. But just because something appears in a vivid image, or feels bad, or happens to be in the right light for a ‘download’, doesn’t mean it’s true.
