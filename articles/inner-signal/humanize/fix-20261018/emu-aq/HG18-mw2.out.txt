Whether coming out of an induction by a live person or from an audio recording, once you are told or instructed to open your eyes, you may want to enhance your coming out by saying to yourself either what day it is or what date it is, looking to your left and right, looking at something close and something far away, moving a bit before standing up. Once you feel steady, stand up. Make sure you are oriented and alert. Just because you can count does not mean you are oriented and alert. Mentally make note of any information that came up during the session and if there is something that you need or do not need to do as a result of the session. Then proceed to do whatever it is that you normally do.

In the event that you are using a recording and for some reason it stops, just say to yourself that the recording has stopped and the session is over. Then use the above method to come out by opening your eyes, saying where you are, saying what day it is, pushing down with your feet or hands, moving about, looking about and then using the 5 step method listed above.

NOTE: When using a recording to take you into a sleep state, it is fine to let the recording put you to sleep or into a quiet time of resting. But when using a recording during a waking practice, the recording should not be leaving you in a role, with lowered judgement, with lowered movement capabilities, blank, with an un-defined “reset word” or inviting you to stay in an altered state for an unlimited amount of time.
