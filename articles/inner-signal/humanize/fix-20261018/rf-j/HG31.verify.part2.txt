PUBLISHED section:
[B46] The Milton Model opens possibility. The Meta Model asks exactly what somebody means. Ethical practice needs both.

[B47] “How specifically?” “According to whom?” “Compared to what?” “Always?” “Never?” “What exactly would happen?” “How do I know?” “What evidence would change my mind?”

[B48] Use Milton Model to soften into the body. Use Meta Model to return to truth.

[B51] Fear, craving, fantasy, belief, and memory may have sensory qualities: near or far, bright or dim, loud or quiet, moving or still, heavy or light, inside or outside the body. Work with qualities you actually notice. If none are apparent, skip this tool rather than inventing an image or voice.

[B52] Ask how the experience is represented. What changes when the image moves two feet back, the voice gets quieter, or the edge becomes less sharp?

[B53] “I am not deleting the signal. I am adjusting the volume so I can listen without being overwhelmed.”

[B54] Before moving one imaginary pixel, ask the guard whether that change is acceptable.

[B55] “Would it be okay if we moved this image back just a little, or lowered the volume slightly, purely so my adult self can stay present enough to hear what you need to tell me?”

[B57] An anchor is a cue connected to a state: a hand on the heart, two fingers touching, a breath, a word, a posture, scent, or song. Find a real state you want easier access to, feel it, and pair the cue while it is clearly present with the phrase, “This is available.” For hypnotic re-entry, choose a usable receptive state rather than peak emotional intensity; follow “A shortcut back into hypnosis” in the main induction section. Keep alert calming and the synchronicity wake-up cue separate.

[B59] People notice a synchronicity and immediately ask what it means. Sometimes that opens insight; sometimes it starts compulsive pattern-hunting. I prefer using the moment of noticing as a wake-up bell. The coincidence doesn’t have to contain a message. It has already captured attention, which is enough.

[B60] The cue I give myself is:

[B61] “Whenever I notice something that feels like a synchronicity, I wake up inside the state I am in. I feel my body and the room. I remember that the coincidence is not proof of an interpretation. I let attention widen beyond the story my current mood is telling. I ask what is fact, what is interpretation, what needs warmth, what needs protection, and what needs action. I return to the highest perspective I can access without leaving the body, the facts, or ordinary human reality.”

[B62] I make room for whatever is genuinely available: objectivity, clarity, ease, peace, freedom, joy, and gratitude. I do not have to manufacture any of them.

[B63] Synchronicity noticed → pause → orient → widen → reality-test → listen → choose one loving action.

[B64] The cue interrupts identification without erasing the state’s message. A negative state may still contain an inner-child need, a Protector warning, grief, a bodily problem, or a real demand for action.

[CHANGED, replaces B46-B48]
To 'Milton Model' is to open things up. To 'Meta Model' is to say "What do yo mean by that?" In order to be ethical with people, you need to do both.

"What do you mean specifically? According to who? Compared to what? Always? Never? What specifically would happen if you did? How do you know that? What evidence could you have that would change your mind?"

When people are starting to get stiff in their bodies, 'Milton' it. When you feel like you're losing touch with the truth of what's going on, 'Meta' it.

[CHANGED, replaces B51-B55]
If someone's fear, craving, fantasy, belief, memory, whatever, has sensory properties in your experience (it has a place in your visual field, or sounds a certain way, or is heavy, or moving, or inside / outside your body, or something like that), you can work with those properties. If there are no sensory properties that you can notice, don't try to create any -- just don't use this tool!

How is this represented for you? Can you allow yourself to move that image back a couple of feet? Can you soften the edges of that image? Can you turn the volume down on that voice in your head? Etc.

"And I can allow myself to know that I am not deleting the message, just turning down the volume so that I am able to be present enough to hear what it is."

And, always ask the guard if it's ok to do this.

"Is it ok to move that image back a couple of feet and turn the volume down a bit, so that I can be present enough to hear what you need me to hear?"

[CHANGED, replaces B59-B64]
Using synchronicity (The synchronicity wake up cue)

People often ask what the synchronicity means. Sometimes they get a great insight from it, but other times they get obsessed trying to work out patterns etc. I am using the noticing of the synchronicity to WAKE UP.

It is my cue to:

Whenever I notice it I use it as a cue for waking up from whatever state I am in, feeling my body, feeling the room I'm in, remembering that what I'm noticing is not proof for what I'm interpreting from the coincidences, widening my state so that I can see beyond what my current state is showing me. I then look for the truth of what is going on (What is really going on, what is just my interpretation? What needs to be warmed? What needs to be protected? What action needs to be taken?) and I go into the highest state I can get into where I can objectively and clearly see what is going on from that situation from the highest perspective I can get to, whilst remaining in my body, not leaving the facts of the situation and not leaving my regular human state. From that state I feel my feelings. I look for the truth of the situation. If I am in a negative state, I check if it is a message from my Inner Child, my Protector or if I just need to grieve. I check if it is a message from my physical body (if there is a problem with my body that needs to be taken care of) or if there is something that needs to be done about the situation. From that place I choose what to do. I choose at least one thing to do that is from a loving place. I allow myself to be in whatever state is real for me, which now includes objectivity, clarity, ease, peace, freedom, joy, gratitude, etc.

In other words: Stop-orient-go wider-reality check-listen-do at least one thing from a loving place.

This way I don't go into identification but I still hear the message of the state if it's negative.
