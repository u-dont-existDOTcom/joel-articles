PUBLISHED section:
[B33] Use this when loveyhuasca, meditation, lucid dreaming, grief, music, prayer, erotic energy, exhaustion, or intense beauty has already opened the state. Stabilize before making it stronger.

[B34] “The state is already open. I do not need to intensify it. I need to aim it, stabilize it, and keep it kind.”

[B35] Look around the room. Name where you are. Name the date, or at least the day. Feel your feet, hands, and spine. Press your thumbs gently but firmly against your index fingers. Focus on the physical friction of skin meeting skin.

[B36] “Only what can be integrated is welcome now. Anything too much can wait. The body may stay safe. The adult self remains present. The inner child does not have to carry this alone.”

[B37] Once you’re oriented, choose one intention:

[B38] “Please show me the next useful truth, symbol, feeling, question, or action — only in the amount I can live well with.”

[B40] Sit upright. Open the eyes. Feel the feet. Let the breath be natural.

[B41] Say: “I am not going away. I am coming more fully here.”

[B42] Look at one object. Feel the spine.

[B43] “Is this daydream guidance, emotional digestion, or avoidance?”

[B44] “If it is guidance, show me the action inside it. If it is avoidance, bring me gently into the present and show me what I am avoiding.”

[B45] If stillness makes you sleepy, diffuse, or escape-prone, hypnosis can become more alert rather than more relaxed. Keep the eyes open and pair a small repeated hand movement with increasing clarity; stop the movement and notice what remains without trying to disappear inward.

[B46] “Eyes open. Press thumb and forefinger together gently. Release. Again — clearer, not sleepier. One more time. Now stop the deliberate movement. Notice the first second after the rhythm ends. Is there an echo in the fingers, the breath, the attention, or nowhere at all? Stay upright and work from exactly that level of alertness.”

[B48] Place one hand on the heart. Place the other hand on the solar plexus.

[B49] Let the heart hand represent the Nurturer. Let the solar plexus hand represent the vulnerable child.

[B50] Breathe gently.

[B51] “Love above. Child below. Adult present.”

[B52] “I send love to the child. I receive the child’s signal. I send love again. I listen again.”

[B54] Sleep induction has a different job. In inner work, the adult returns, writes, and acts. At bedtime, analysis can remain off duty. Revelation can wait; the task is rest.

[B55] For sleep hypnosis, see Nimja’s sleep playlist.

[B57] Fractionation means returning toward ordinary awareness and then deciding whether to enter absorption again. Used openly, it teaches that trance has a door in both directions. Name the technique before using it; every answer cannot secretly mean “deeper.”

[B58] “Open the eyes and return toward ordinary awareness. Feel the room and decide whether to stop here. If you choose to continue, close or soften the eyes and notice what changes as attention gathers again. You may stop at either point.”

[B60] “If it is useful, attention may become more gathered. If it is not useful, I remain exactly as awake as I need to be.”

[B61] Choose one simple scene: a room in the heart, a garden, a temple, a fire with the Protector nearby, a clear lake, or a chair where the adult waits. One scene is plenty.

[B62] “Who or what needs to speak first?”

[B64] “As I wait, I give up the responsibility of making anything happen.

[B65] If it is useful, the wise subconscious may let one finger twitch, pulse, lift, warm, tingle, or become subtly noticeable when the inner meeting space is ready.

[B66] I will not force it. I will not fake it. I will simply wait with love and notice.”

[B75] To retire the cue, stop rehearsing it, remove it from recordings or active app instructions, and return to the familiar induction. State your decision while awake; a cancellation sentence does not prove the association vanished instantly. Persistent unwanted responses or disorientation need qualified help. Any retained cue is for a future, separately chosen session; today’s altered state ends with the return.

[CHANGED, replaces B33-B38]
This is useful when you've already opened a state, through means other than doing a deliberate process - like taking loveyhuasca, doing a different style of meditation, being in a lucid dream, dealing with grief, listening to music, praying, engaging with erotic energy, exhaustion or even being overwhelmed by the beauty of something. When you've done that, then you want to stabilize the state before making it stronger.

Say to yourself 'The state is already open, now I just need to aim it, stabilize it and be kind to myself as I do.'

Look around you in the room, notice where you are and what date (or at least what day) it is. Feel your feet, hands and spine to ground yourself. Focus on the physical sensation of pressing your thumbs against your index fingers, feel the friction.

Say to yourself 'I will only allow myself to go as far as I can integrate. If there is more, it can wait. My body will be safe, my adult self will be present as my inner child deals with this.

Once you are oriented, then pick one of the following: 'Please show me the next thing I need to know, in a form (symbol, feeling, question, action) that I can integrate.'

[CHANGED, replaces B40-B46]
Staying awake when your mind wants to escape You can do this sitting up, with your eyes open, feeling your feet, and allowing your breathing to be natural.

Start by saying 'Today I am not going to escape from here into my mind, instead I will be more present here.'

Pick an object to look at, feel your spine as you do.

Now ask yourself 'Am I daydreaming in a way that is receiving guidance or helping me integrate emotional experiences, or am I escaping?'

Then say 'Please help me, if I am receiving guidance, to see what action I need to take, if I am escaping, please help me be present and show me what I am escaping.'

If whenever you stop, you escape into sleepiness and diffuse states, work on increasing your alertness instead of relaxing, keep your eyes open, work on doing something with your hands, doing it for a while, and then deliberately stopping it and noticing what comes afterwards without escaping into your mind.

For example say to yourself 'While you keep your eyes open, press your thumb and forefinger together and let go again a few times. Each time do it more clearly and in a more alert state. Now deliberately stop doing it and notice what happens in the first second after you do, do you feel an echo, or can you feel nothing?' Stay upright while you work at this level.
