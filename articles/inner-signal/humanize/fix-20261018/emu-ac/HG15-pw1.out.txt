You can rebel, but you can’t choose

This comes from not having explored yourself as a child, because you were too busy focusing on not being in pain. So you don't know what you like, what you want to try out, because you never gave yourself the opportunity to find out who you are. Here is not meant to refer specifically to a brain system.

It may be that for a certain time you have to distance yourself a bit from the people or group in order to find out what you want. Just not too long, else you are defeating the very purpose of this exercise!
