Stabilize before you interpret

Self hypnosis is a very powerful thing. There are many times you will find yourself in an altered state of consciousness. Whether it’s from loveyhuasca, or meditation, or lucid dreaming, or being in a state of grief or sexual energy, or prayer, or not sleeping, or any number of other ways you may find yourself altered similar to plant medicines. In these times your mind may open faster then your adult self can keep up with.

The issue comes in when you THINK because you’re in an altered state that it MEANS something. Sometimes it’s your inner child who is speaking, other times it’s a Protector showing you their fear, or it could be your shame, or your grief, or your anger, or your longing, or whatever else you have buried that wants to be taken care of. Sometimes it’s your body reacting to being overloaded, or chaos in your environment, or a lack of sleep, or plain old confusion. Just because you had a scary picture doesn’t mean it’s true. Just because you had a bad feeling doesn’t mean it’s a lesson you need to learn. Just because you had a download in a nice light doesn’t mean you should believe it.

What may be a simpler question to ask yourself first?

“What kind of problem is happening?”
