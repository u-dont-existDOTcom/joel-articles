Appendix G: Altered states

NOTE: I am not saying to use these, but many people try to do this for the first time when they are in a mind altered state.

If you do decide to use these, remember you do not want to force a revelation out of this state, you want to be able to use this in your regular life.

If you have had an opening before, you have to understand that there is a difference between having the experience, having it while in this state and having it in your day to day life. So even though it has opened, if the child inside of you cannot take it in, you need to meet them where they are instead of making it more intense.

Use phrases such as “Help me to ground” or “Help me to only take that which I can take in” or “Help me to remember love” or “Help me to come back when the time is right” or “Help me to take one change with me that I can use in my regular life”.

You should not be using self hypnosis to:

If you are in an altered state of consciousness and this is making it so that you do not love, do not know how to ground, do not know how to be honest, do not sleep, do not communicate, do not do regular life things then you need to STOP trying to go deeper and work on integrating what you have.

St Stabilize before you interpret.

Self hypnosis can be a very powerful tool. Especially when you are in an altered state of consciousness. Whether it be loveyhuasca or meditation, whether it be lucid dreaming or grief, sexual energy or prayer, not sleeping or something similar to plant medicines, your mind will often open faster then your adult can figure out what is going on.

The problem is that you THINK that because you are in an altered state that it MEANS something. Sometimes it is your child talking, but sometimes it is a Protector showing you what they are afraid of. Sometimes it is shame, or grief, or anger, or longing, or whatever else you have buried inside of you that wants to be taken care of. Sometimes it is your body’s reaction to being overloaded, or things being chaotic in your environment, or not getting enough sleep, or just being confused. Just because you had a picture that scared you doesn’t mean that it is true. Just because you had a bad feeling doesn’t mean that it is a lesson you need to learn. Just because you had a download in nice lighting doesn’t mean that you should believe it.

Before you try to interpret it, what is a simpler question you can ask yourself first?

“What kind of problem is happening?”
