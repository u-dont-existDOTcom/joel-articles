Most people assume when approaching their inner child that the first thing they’ll encounter is…their inner child.

But more often than not, what you actually experience is numbness, cynicism, anger, hunger, the sudden urge to check your phone, a sexual urge, mental planning, sleepiness, dissociation, or the simple thought that “this is stupid”. I wouldn’t call those failures. They’re often the protector, the guard at the door, showing up in its usual charming way.
