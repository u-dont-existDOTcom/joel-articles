What you don’t want to do is bully your subconscious with statements like,

“Why don’t you just tell me?” “What are you hiding?” “Tell me right now!” or “I demand that you reveal the truth!”

This can lead to flooding, panic, false certainty, confabulation, dissociation, and just generally making your day suck more. When you’re flooded, it can be hard to access your adult capacities to pause, choose, reality test, plan and make choices for yourself. Until your adult has enough of these capacities, especially regulation and support, it is not good for your subconscious to communicate with you. Silence is safer. But when your adult does have enough regulation, support, humility, and other capacities, you can talk with your subconscious.

“What do you need for my conscious mind to be able to know what you’re saying 5% better?” “Can you tell me through a symbol?” “Can you tell me what you need me to do to be safe without telling me what it is?” “Do you need me to get support to be able to know what it is? Who?”
