Now pick one that is important to do something about, put the others somewhere you can see them, and come back to this page.

A quick exercise for when one concern has several strands. Imagine yarn unwinding, bubbles settling, or knots placed where you can see them beside you. When you've placed them all, take a minute to look at them all (without changing them, though you could label them if needed, by saying a word or two). Count only the strands you actually set down; nothing is erased. Which one needs warmth, protection, or action? Now keep one useful strand, put the others somewhere you can see them, and return.

Listen to the sounds of the room you are currently in and feel your body. Now imagine yourself walking somewhere, where do you want to go? As you walk, listen to the sounds of your footsteps and take in your surroundings. What texture are the objects you pass? What landmarks do you see? Now that you've collected enough attention, imagine the scenery changing until you are in a place where you can directly speak to whoever/whatever it is you want to speak to. Or you can just cut straight to the chase if you want. Observe what you want to observe, just don't make it into a "what are they going to do to you?" kind of thing. When you're ready to finish, imagine walking back the way you came and close the space.

First get ready as if you were going to work with a patient. Time, place, tools and boundaries. When you invite them in, the signal, the feeling or the younger state to come as close as you want them to be. Be careful to not use language which makes you fragmented. Ask them what they need, wait and see if you want to reconnect with them. If not, rest and see again. If you have an adult part which can do this or borrow a function from someone else, speak to them. You decide the words, the pace and what you want to do. The goal is to be as good as you can and not erase your history. Finally, close as you would with a patient. Say that you are whole, an adult and come back.

Choose someone or something who has a characteristic you want to adopt. Close your eyes and imagine them. Now imagine yourself becoming them, standing, walking and talking like them, just 1% more, or if you are uncomfortable with this, simply observe them, but in either case be aware of how you now have more access to that quality. Notice what happens. Repeat a few times. Now think of a situation you want to use that characteristic in. Finally let go of the person/thing, but keep the characteristic, and say to yourself "I am me", and notice how you have increased access to the characteristic you wanted.

Choose a quality you understand, like warmth, a boundary, steadiness, curiosity or dignity. Things to do in between: keep the eyes open, notice where the practice is taking place and on what day, notice the feet, read something, write something, notice that one is still relatively normally aware.

Invitation: choose a question and keep it in mind, do not specifically focus or narrow the awareness further.

Choice: do whatever comes to mind, answer the question or write it down, write that one does not know, rest, change the question, ask someone else, stop the practice, etc.

Autonomous signal: write anything that comes to mind, no interpretation.

Deepener: none, it is not necessary to reach a state that is less alert.

Other autonomous: have the adult part of yourself answer the question in any way, preferably in writing or by taking an action.

Integration/exit: reality test the autonomous signal and take an action based on that, end the practice (no special exit is needed since no state was achieved that one needs to exit from).

Invitation: choose a question and keep it in mind, do not specifically focus or narrow the awareness further.

Autonomous signal: write down any word, sensation, impulse, objection, or silence that comes, without taking it as proof.

Deepener: none, full alertness isn’t a lesser version of the practice.

Handoff: have the adult part of yourself answer the question in any way, preferably in writing or by taking an action.

Integration/exit: reality test the autonomous signal and do one small action based on that, end the practice (no special exit is needed since no state was achieved that one needs to exit from).
