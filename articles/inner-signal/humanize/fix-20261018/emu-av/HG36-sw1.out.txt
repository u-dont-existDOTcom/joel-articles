Stabilize before you interpret

Self-hypnosis can be quite powerful when doing loveyhuasca, meditation, lucid dreaming, grieving, working with sexual energy, praying, not sleeping well, or doing a lot of the things people do around plant medicine work. Sometimes, people open up too fast for their adult self to make sense of what’s going on.

Sometimes it’s intensity, and that’s all it is. Sometimes it’s an inner child emerging. Sometimes it’s a Protector letting someone know they’re scared. Sometimes it’s shame, grief, anger, longing, or a host of other things coming up. Sometimes it’s the body experiencing too much going on inside. Sometimes it’s too much going on outside. Sometimes it’s lack of sleep, or a lack of ability to make sense of what’s going on. Sometimes it’s none of the above. Regardless, unless it’s a clear as day message, just because something came in a certain light, that doesn’t mean it’s true. Just because something felt bad, that doesn’t mean it’s true or helpful. Just because it felt like a “download”, that doesn’t mean it’s true.

Ask yourself some questions to help discern what’s actually true:
