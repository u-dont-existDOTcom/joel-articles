Mentally rehearse the following: Imagine that you have noticed a synchronicity that is obviously harmless.

Feel the "whoa" of stimulation that you just got from noticing it.

Use that stimulation to feel your feet, your body, your breathing, your room.

Reiterate to yourself that whenever you notice a synchronicity, it is just a bell that rings to wake you up, it is not proof for what you are interpreting from it.

Ask yourself: "What does my Nurturer think about this?", "What does my Protector think about this?", "What does my Leader think about this?"

Rehearse going back to a more grounded state and to a more wide view of the situation.

Rehearse yourself taking at least one action. Rehearse yourself waking up completely.

Mind the caveats! You must be the one authoring these. You must see them. You can always undo them. You must use them only to help you orient. They do not authorize you to make a decision. They are not proof for what your intuition is telling you. They do not stop you from hearing the message of your intuition if it is warning you about something. You can't use them to control other people.

This is very similar to implementation intention . I'm not aware of research into implementation intention for this specific thing, but the general mechanisms appear to work; a paper explaining how and why they work can be found here .

A downside of synchronicity cues is that they can lead to false positives. We do this on purpose when we rehearse what to do when we notice a synchronicity (we create an association in our head); however, when you are absorbed in something and/or when you are destabilized, you might think that you saw a synchronicity when in fact there wasn't any. Research has shown that people that score high on apophenia tests also score high for detecting patterns that were not really there, as well as scoring high on psychosis spectrum traits [1, 2] (this is not saying that noticing patterns is bad; in fact, it's not a pathology).

If you feel that synchronicity cues are making these things worse (e.g. you feel like you have to look for them all the time, you can't sleep, you feel anxious, you are sure that random things happen in your life in order to tell you something, you can't tell the difference between noticing something and interpreting what it might mean, etc.), you should deactivate synchronicity cues.

You can do that by not rehearsing them anymore and by saying to yourself (when you are fully awake) that you don't want to use synchronicity as a cue anymore (e.g. "From now on, I am no longer going to use noticing synchronicity as a cue for waking up."). Remove it from your list of cues, so that you won't rehearse it anymore.
