# Stance check: induction section rewrite (HG17)

I checked every rewrite sentence that states a position, rule or claim against the whole published essay and against Joel's 2026-10-21 positions.

## Conflicts in the rewrite

**1. Choosing what to leave alone becomes optional**
- Rewrite (Before you begin, step 2): "You might also want to pick what you are NOT going to do."
- Essay (Quick start: do this first): "Choose one purpose and what you are leaving alone today."
- A reader would believe that deciding what stays off-limits is an optional extra, when the essay makes it part of setting up each session.

**2. "Communion" becomes communion with things**
- Rewrite (Before you begin, step 2): "you can use it for good stuff too, like love, play, confidence, creativity, or communion with things."
- Essay (Introduction): "The goal is a more trustworthy relationship with the whole mind… Communion is the closest word I have for that."
- A reader would believe the communion purpose means communing with things in general, when the essay uses the word for the inner meeting of conscious mind, subconscious, body and parts.

**3. Entry-and-exit drills before any other use**
- Rewrite (Before you begin, step 5): "If you have not done hypnosis before, … practice the coming in and out part first before trying to do it for anything else."
- Essay (When this needs more support than a guide can provide): "For ordinary learning, start with shorter sessions, warmth, a manageable subject, and a full return."
- A reader would believe a beginner must rehearse going in and out with no purpose before using the practice at all, when the essay only keeps difficult subjects out of early sessions.

**4. The adult self tied to age**
- Rewrite (Already open?): "I am here, in my adult body, with my adult experience." (It replaces the published "The adult self remains present.")
- Essay (Love doesn't have to wait for trust): "Don't decide from the person's chronological age." And (Sometimes start with the whole): "Those adult functions still grow through practice."
- A reader would believe that being grown up, with an adult body and life experience, is what keeps the adult present. The essay separates adult capacity from age, and the line blurs the consciously built adult with chronological adulthood: the kind of inner-adult conflation Joel asked on 2026-10-21 to check for.

**5. Any leftover response or disorientation means getting a professional**
- Rewrite (A shortcut back into hypnosis): "If you still have the response when you don't want it, or you feel disoriented after the state, please get qualified help."
- Essay (Let the next response guide you, Change the amount of contact): "Persistent disorientation needs human help rather than a stronger induction." And (Troubleshooting, You dissociate or feel unreal): "Open your eyes. Move. Touch something textured. Name five objects. End the deeper practice for the day."
- A reader would believe any lingering cue response, or any disorientation after a session, needs qualified help. The essay says a retired association may not vanish instantly, handles brief unreality with grounding, and keeps qualified help for persistent cases (the published sentence reads "Persistent unwanted responses or disorientation").

## Contradictions in the published text

**1. "No exploratory hypnosis when intoxicated" against the loveyhuasca practice**
- Published (Before you begin): "When intoxicated, acutely confused, or unable to keep yourself safe, deal with immediate support rather than exploratory hypnosis." The next sentence: "The later Already open? practice is for orientation, not permission to intensify an unstable state."
- Against it, in the same section (Already open? Contain before you deepen): "Use this when loveyhuasca, meditation, lucid dreaming, grief, music, prayer, erotic energy, exhaustion, or intense beauty has already opened the state." The practice ends with an intention: "Please show me the next useful truth, symbol, feeling, question, or action — only in the amount I can live well with."
- Against it in the rest of the essay: Why doing it myself worked better for me says "I found that especially amplified with loveyhuasca", and it treats loveyhuasca and chrunga as drugs ("Without drugs, self-hypnosis or meditation may also become much more effective inside a lucid dream"). Appendix G says of altered states: "Use them to deepen listening, strengthen love, and improve ordinary life." Capacity before content draws its line at "chemically overwhelmed", not at intoxication.
- What a reader sees: one sentence rules out exploratory hypnosis while intoxicated and limits Already open? to orientation. Yet the same section gives a loveyhuasca practice that asks for the next truth, and Joel reports that loveyhuasca amplified his self-hypnosis. The reader can't tell whether practicing on loveyhuasca is ruled out.
- Rewrite: still has it. Its "intoxicated, acutely confused, or otherwise not capable of protecting yourself" goes further and treats anyone intoxicated as unable to protect themselves.

Conflicts in the rewrite: 5. Contradictions in the published text: 1.
