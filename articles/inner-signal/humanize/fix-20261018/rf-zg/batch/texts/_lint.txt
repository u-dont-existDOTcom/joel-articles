Induction: gathering attention without disappearing

Inductions serve to gather up attention in order to access quiet stuff. They are not about surrendering, playing asleep, or obeying someone. You may want to do an actual induction if you are sober, distracted, analytical, or otherwise not very trancey. You can skip the induction and go straight to orientation and containment if you are already trancey, due to meditation, loveyhuasca, lucid dreaming, grief, music, prayer, or tiredness, or if for any other reason you feel in touch with deeper stuff.

You can control and decide how trancey you want to get. Deeper trance is optional.

What an induction is actually doing

It gets you ready to do whatever you are doing. It does this by gathering up your attention, deepening the state, and then utilizing that state to say more things that make that state more likely. There are all sorts of ways to do this. The method here involves counting, but there are many others. You might just talk with someone, tell a story, move, play with sound, or make use of curiosity. However you do it, say so. Don’t pretend it’s some other thing in the name of elegance.

You can always test a simple target suggestion before deepening, to see if you are already getting your target response, and then deepen from that, if needed. The induction is just there to get you into a place from which to listen to what you want to hear. It doesn’t have to be some sort of ceremony.

Please don’t put six metaphors in one trance

You need to keep it simple. One purpose, one anchor or image to start, one way back, and a decided on feeling (warm, bounded, directed, or neutral). You don’t need floating, stairs, gardens, inner rooms, color, or a dialogue with a child. If you do metaphors at all, use one, and only switch to another consciously.

Metaphors and imagery are optional. You might signal and receive signal through sensation, rhythm, movement, words, spatial sense, simple knowing, or the lack of a signal. If you are using words, keep them related to the world you have created. If you are counting, do it in a way that makes sense in that world. When you’re done with your induction, come back the other way. You can borrow qualities from your induction (warmth or steadiness) but don’t change who you are.

When writing your induction, pick a central verb, a sensory quality, a short refrain, and a way to transition between ideas. Read it out loud when you are writing it. Long elegant sentences become soup when you’re being induced.

Before you begin

1. Read this page, and the Come all the way back page, when you are fully awake.

2. Decide what you want to do during the induction. You might be asking a question, building a quality, or exploring a state. You might also want to pick what you are NOT going to do. Consider your time. Remember that you can use this for good stuff too, like love, play, confidence, creativity, or communion with things. Make sure to leave time at the end for coming back and for reflection.

3. Make sure you are in a safe place. Don’t do this if you are driving, in water, operating machinery, or otherwise in a place where you need to attend to yourself or others. If you are intoxicated, acutely confused, or otherwise not capable of protecting yourself, then do not do exploratory hypnosis. You can, however, use the later part of this (the already open? section) to orient yourself, but don’t make it deeper than it already is.

4. Decide how you want to be coaxed into it. Do you want to feel like you have a lot of room, or do you want to feel held and warm? Do you need more firm direction, or are you fine with a gentle suggestion? Preview to yourself what you are going to do. This might involve closing your eyes, or even coming out of it and going back in. It will include coming back at the end. If you are recording your induction, read the script to yourself first (if you can). Know that you can end the induction any time you want by saying “stop” or by opening your eyes. You can also say “less” to make it ask less of you, or “quiet” to make it stop talking. You do not need permission from any inner part to stop, even if you feel like a part is telling you that you need permission.

5. If you have not done hypnosis before, or if you haven’t done it in a while, practice the coming in and out part first before trying to do it for anything else. You don’t have to watch yourself the whole time, but it is good to make sure you can do the state change at first. You are making something easier, so it will require less work. The answer no will stay no. Silence won’t create a narrator who says things you didn’t.

6. Let what happens next be guided by your response. Utilization is the act of letting your current response guide where you go next. Learn to notice what does happen when you do these things. Also learn that if nothing happens, that doesn’t mean there was a positive, hidden change. If you need more tools on utilization, see Appendix B or read on to Let the next response guide you .

Induction for when you are sober (3-5 min)

Sober induction: 3–5 minutes

Read this to yourself on a recorder, or read it aloud, or say it silently if you are good at that. Pick a sound, a contact on your body, or the two hands thing (see link above) to be your anchor for this. Pick a way to start this induction and use it for all of them. Note the anchor in the induction below. Don’t read the instructions, just pause between paragraphs.

Allow your eyes to rest on something in the room.

As you rest, allow yourself to notice something you can feel, perhaps the sensation of something beneath you. You may allow your attention to be drawn to a sound, or a breath, or something else.

Allow yourself some time to do this.

You may notice a thought arising, about what you're doing or about something that's on your mind. Whenever you notice a thought, simply allow yourself to re-engage with whatever you've chosen to anchor yourself.

You may focus your attention on one aspect of what you're doing, perhaps a particular detail of a sound, or a sensation, or the act of listening itself.

Allow yourself some time to do this, as if you were listening to someone speaking to you, someone you care about.

Now, in your mind's eye, say to yourself the phrase you have chosen. Allow yourself to notice how it resonates with you.

You may now choose to close your eyes, or soften your gaze, or keep your eyes open as you see fit. You may continue to deepen your listening as you wish, perhaps keeping in mind in the background something you're hearing.

When you feel ready, count from 5 down to 1, allowing yourself some time for each count.

As you focus your attention once more on your chosen anchor, 4... allow yourself to focus more of your attention on what you're listening for, 3... allowing yourself some time... 2... simply listening, 1... allowing yourself some time to settle in.

Bring to mind, if you wish, something you'd like to come into contact with.

Allow yourself to notice what comes to you in response to your question, if anything. Allow yourself to notice how it comes to you, if it does - sensation, words, pictures, feelings, movements, etc.

Allow yourself some time to notice, with or without words.

Already open? Contain before you deepen

Use this when you're already open (from loveyhuasca, meditation, lucid dreaming, grief, beautiful music, prayer, erotic energy, exhaustion, seeing something incredibly beautiful, etc), and you just need to aim and stabilise what's already there, rather than risking deepening an experience you won't be able to integrate.

Recognise to yourself that your state is already open. All you need to do is aim it and stabilise it.

Orient yourself: look around and say out loud where you are, what date (or day) it is, feel your body, ground yourself, feel your feet, hands, spine, etc. Make some physical contact with yourself (press your thumbs to the tip of your index fingers, fairly firmly, so you can feel the friction). Focus on the physical sensation.

Say to yourself, 'I recognise that anything that comes to me that I cannot integrate, I will not allow in. My body is safe. I am here, in my adult body, with my adult experience. My inner child does not need to face this on her/his/their own.'

Once you're oriented, choose one thing you want to do:

Say to yourself, 'I ask that you show me the next step of truth/symbol/feeling/question/action I need to take, and that you show me only what I am able to integrate.'

Stay awake when your mind likes to escape

Sit up, open your eyes, feel your body, notice your breath, and say to yourself, 'I recognise that I am not going anywhere. I am only coming closer to what is here.'

Look around you at something, feel your spine and ask yourself, 'Am I daydreaming to receive guidance, to resolve an emotional state, or to avoid what is actually here?'

Then say to yourself, 'I ask that if I am receiving guidance, you show me the action I need to take; and if I am avoiding what is here, please bring me back into the present, and show me what I am avoiding.'

If your state of being still is one where you tend to become sleepy, diffuse or mentally escaping, try making your hypnosis more active. For example, keeping your eyes open, and making a deliberate movement with one hand as you bring yourself into a clearer state, and then ceasing the deliberate movement and noticing what state you fall into. For example, you could say to yourself:

'As I keep my eyes open, I press my thumb and forefinger together firmly, then release. As I do this, I bring myself into a clear state. Again, I press my fingers together, and again I release them, as I bring myself into an even clearer state. One more time... and now I stop pressing my fingers deliberately, and I notice in the first second after I cease my deliberate movement, whether there is an echo effect, or whether I simply fall into a different state. I notice, I keep myself upright, and I work at this level.'

The two-hand induction

Two hand induction -- Put one hand on your heart, and the other on your solar plexes.

Label the hand on the heart as your nurturer and the hand on the solar plexes as your vulnerable child ( or feel free to make different associations if you like ).

Breathe...

Your Nurturer is on top and your child is below, with the Adult witnessing all.

Nurturer is sending love down to the child, child is signaling up to the Nurturer. Nurturer is sending love down to the child, child is signaling up to the Nurturer.

Sleep induction

For sleep you can cut the last part out since the goal is different than inner work where the Adult ultimately needs to come back in to write things down and take actions. When you are going to sleep you want your analytical mind to stand down until the next day when you have time for revelations.

Optional fractionation: practice coming back

There is an optional fractionation you can add to this at some point. I always announce when I am doing it and note that I am breaking the pattern of always going deeper at each answer.

Would you like to open your eyes and come all the way back up? Or you can decide to soften your eyes and go deeper right now? Either way is ok, you can stop the induction at any time.

Deepening, if useful

If you feel it will be useful to go deeper, you can. But if you don't, you can stay just as awake as you need to be.

Pick an inner scene to go into when you get there. It can be a room in your heart, a garden, a temple, sitting by a fire with the Protector nearby, at a lake with clear water where you can see the bottom, or sitting in a chair across from the Adult, whatever feels right to you. Just pick one.

Who wants to talk first?

Optional trance depth check

There is also an optional way you can check how deep you are in trance...

I will now let go of responsibility and just wait...

If it would be useful, my subconscious mind which knows what is best for me in the long term might choose to activate a finger in one of my hands so that it twitches, pulses, comes up by itself, heats up, tingles, or in some way becomes noticeable to me that it is ok to go into this meeting space now.

I will not try to make it happen, I will not pretend it is happening, I will just wait with love for what is true...

Sometimes a finger will move, sometimes the whole hand will heat up, sometimes you will notice your breathing has changed, and sometimes nothing will seem to change at all. Even if something does change, that doesn't mean it is "True" in any way, it just might be listening.

A shortcut back into hypnosis

There is a shortcut way to get into hypnosis...

Learn the induction above and the coming back down part at the end in full. When you feel comfortable with it, you can do the following....

My dad uses a mudra ( putting two fingers together like the "Ok" sign ) to help him relax when he drives. That is great for calming down and being more alert, not great for going into a trance while driving. Don't do that.

Pick a word, phrase or motion ( optional ) that you will use to instantly go into hypnosis. Practice saying the word or phrase while doing the motion ( if you picked one ) when you are in a good hypnotic state, but not at the peak of emotion since you don't want to go into a child state or re-traumatize yourself. You are picking the state here, it means whatever you say it means.

From now on, when I deliberately pick a time and place to use this word or phrase to go into hypnosis, I will be able to do so, and when I am done I can change my state and come back up just as easily.

Now come back up and at some point when it is appropriate for you to begin a practice, start using this. You can slowly go faster with the longer induction as you find this useful, or just do the longer induction when this doesn't seem to be enough for what you are working on. You don't have to do it any particular number of times, you don't have to go a certain depth instantly, and you don't need to have any particular sensation when you do this.

Check to make sure it is useful and that you can turn it on and off at will, and that you aren't going into it when you shouldn't. This should not be used to respond to a narrator, a notification, or anything you might overhear, it is a tool for you to use when you pick to use it. Going into hypnosis faster doesn't mean you should be working on things you aren't ready for.

Don't try this when driving or doing anything where you need to be actively paying attention. It isn't safe to go into a trance where you are listening inwardly when you need to be listening outwardly, even if you have your eyes open and even if you use the " if it would be useful " type language.

To turn it off, just stop rehearsing it, remove it from any recordings you may have, stop having it as an active part of any apps you may use, and go back to the regular induction. You can say something like the below when you are awake to indicate that you are done using it, but keep in mind that saying something doesn't make it true. If you feel you still have the response when you don't want it, or you feel disoriented after the state, please get qualified help. Just saying something like the below doesn't necessarily make it true, but it is a good start. If you want to keep it for future use, indicate that you will only use it to begin a session at a future time, and that the state you are in now is ending.
