Intensity is often interpreted to mean that something important is happening. Sometimes it does mean that the inner child is expressing him or herself, or a Protector is afraid, or that some shame/grief/anger/etc. needs to be worked with. But it could also simply mean that the body is over-loaded, or the environment is chaotic, or there is a lack of sleep, or that the person is confused. Just because something is intense, scary, feels bad, or came in the middle of the day with great lighting, doesn’t mean it should be believed.
