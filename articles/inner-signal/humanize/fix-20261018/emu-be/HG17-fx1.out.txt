Induction: gathering attention without disappearing

Inductions are a way of gathering up your attention so that you can access quiet stuff. They are not about surrendering or playing asleep or obeying someone. You may want to do an actual induction if you're sober and distracted or analytical or guarded or just not trancey. If you're already pretty trancey, whether due to meditation or loveyhuasca or lucid dreams or grief or music or prayer or tiredness or whatever, skip to the next part about orientation and containment. Don't go deeper when you're already in touch with deeper stuff.

Just get as trancey as you need to, focused and warm and receptive and embodied and able to stop, if that serves what you're doing. It's optional to go deeper.

What are you actually doing here?

Getting yourself ready to do whatever you're doing. That's it. A simple way to do this is to gather up your attention in some way, deepen if that seems useful, and to let what you notice as you do this inform how you talk and what you do. There are all sorts of ways to gather attention and there are all sorts of ways to deepen. Here's one that uses counting, but do whatever you want. If you want to talk to someone, or tell a story, or move, or play with sound, or get curious, or whatever, just say that's what you're doing. Don't pretend it's something else in the name of elegance.

Try out a simple target suggestion to see if you're getting the target response you want. You can always deepen later, to however deep you need to be in order to hear what you want to hear.

Please don't throw six metaphors at someone in a trance

Keep it simple. Know what you are doing. One thing you're doing, one way to anchor or image or whatever, one way to get back, one way you want it to feel (or neutral witnessing). Warm and bounded and directed is good. No need to float and go down stairs and go into gardens and go into inner places and work with color and talk to your kid self all at once. If you work with metaphors at all (you don't have to), just do one. You can always switch, but do it consciously.

It's not necessary to work with metaphors or imagery, but if you're using signal words or the like, make them coherent with whatever way of signaling you're using (sound, sensation, rhythm, movement, words, spatial, simple knowing, lack of signal, etc.). If you are using words, use words that are related to whatever world you're creating. If you're counting, count in a way that is coherent with that world. Come back out again in a way that is related to whatever you've been doing. You can borrow aspects of the induction, but you don't need to change your basic self.

Choose a verb to use as a centering word, a sensory thing, a short phrase to repeat that refrains, and a way to transition. Read it out loud as you write. Long, elegant sentences sound like soup in an induction.

Before you begin:

1) Read this page and the Come all the way back page when you're awake.

2) Decide what you want to do in the induction. What are you not going to do today? How long do you want to take? It doesn't have to be about problems. You can do good stuff with this, like love and play and confidence and creativity and communion and whatever. Take a few minutes at the end to come back and reflect.

3) Make sure you're in a place where it is safe to do this. Don't do it while driving or in water or with machinery or wherever you need to be attending to yourself or others. If you are overwhelmed by chemicals or in acute confusion or otherwise unable to attend to your safety, get support for that instead of doing exploratory hypnosis. (The later part of this is a good way to orient, but don't use it to go deeper if you're not stable.)

4. Deciding how you will let yourself be coaxed into hypnosis. Do you want to make it feel spacious, held, warm, or do you need to be coaxed more firmly? Preview what you’re going to do. That includes closing your eyes if you’re going to do that, or coming out of it and going back in, or whatever you are going to do. Preview coming out of it at the end. If you’re going to record it for yourself and you can manage this, read it through first. Know that you can end the induction by saying “stop” or opening your eyes at any time. If there’s something you don’t want to hear, or the induction is asking more of you than you want to give, you can say “less” and the induction will ask less of you, or “quiet” and it will stop talking. Even if a part of you says you have to get permission to do this, you don’t.

5. If you’ve never done this before, or if you’ve not done it for a while, practice the coming in and out part, and leave the doing something difficult for another time. You don’t have to watch yourself all the time, but at first make sure you can change your state. Then, as your response to this becomes useful, you can make it more effortless. Remember, no is always no. And not saying yes means that the narrator can’t add things.

6. Whatever response you do get, let that guide you. It may not be what the script is expecting, but that’s OK, because you can use whatever response you do get to guide you to the next one. This is called utilization. It also means that if you notice something, that you can let that guide you. And if you don’t notice anything, that’s not positive. For more about how you can do this, see Appendix B or carry on reading Let the next response guide you after the induction. For more language patterns you can use, see Appendix B. The induction simply tells you what to do to keep you safe and what to do to get out, because if you use all the patterns suggested in Appendix B, there will be lots of things for you to respond to!

A sober induction (3-5 minutes)

To use this, read it slowly to yourself onto a recorder, or read it out loud if you like, or read it silently in your head if you’re good at that. Choose an anchor – either a sound, a contact or the two hands thing (see below) and a phrase to use at the beginning and use it whenever you see it in the text. Don’t read out the instructions in , but do pause between each paragraph.

Allow yourself some time to let your eyes rest on something in the room.

Allow yourself some time to let yourself notice something you can feel … the sensation of something beneath you … the sensation of contact … the sensation of pressure … or notice something you can hear … or something else you can notice …

Allow yourself some time to do that.

Notice whether any thoughts come into your head … and whenever you notice you’re having a thought, allow yourself to re-engage with … and leave the thought unfinished.

Notice whether there’s something about that you can focus on … perhaps a particular aspect of the sound … or a particular aspect of the sensation … or something about listening to it …

Allow yourself some time to do that, the amount of time you might give someone you cared about who had started to speak but hadn’t finished.

Begin by saying to yourself, '

You may close your eyes, soften your gaze, or keep your eyes open. You may choose to deepen your listening if you wish. You may keep in the back of your mind the sound of

Count to yourself from 5 to 1 when you're ready. Take your time.

5, focus on your anchor again. 4, focus more on what you find. 3, 2, listen to the space, don't prepare an answer. 1, stay there for a moment, take your time.

Bring to mind your chosen concern, or simply your wish to contact yourself inside, if you wish. Let it be there for now. It doesn't need to tell its story yet.

What do you notice now?

Allow yourself to notice what you can if you wish, whether it's sensation, words, pictures, feelings, movements or whatever, if it's not clear yet.

Allow yourself some time to notice, with or without words.

Already open If you're already in an open state of mind - maybe from a ayahuasca ceremony, meditation practice, lucid dreaming, grief, listening to beautiful music, prayer, erotic energy, exhaustion, seeing something incredibly beautiful, or whatever, and all you need is to aim and stabilise your openness without deepening it any further, then you can just do this.

Recognise to yourself that you're already open, and all you need to do is aim, stabilise and be kind to your opening.

First, orient yourself. Look around you and recognise where you are, what day or date it is, feel your body, ground yourself, feel your feet, feel your hands, feel your spine or whatever. Make some physical contact, for example, put the tips of your thumbs on the tips of your index fingers, press firmly enough to feel the friction, and focus on the physical sensation.

Then say to yourself, 'Only what I can integrate is welcome right now. Anything that is too much for me can wait. My body is safe. I am here now, and I am an adult. My inner child doesn't need to face this alone.'

When you're oriented, set your intention.

Say to yourself, 'I ask you to show me the next thing I need to know, whether it's truth, symbol, feeling, question, action or whatever. But please show me only what I can integrate.'

Stay awake If you find your mind escaping when you're still, try these.

Sit up, open your eyes, feel your feet, let your breath be natural and say to yourself, 'I recognise that I'm not going anywhere. I'm only coming closer to what's here.'

Look around you at something, feel your spine, ask yourself, 'Am I daydreaming to receive guidance, to resolve an emotional state, or to run away from what's here?'

Then say to yourself, 'If I'm receiving guidance, please show me what I need to do to act on it. If I'm running away, please bring me back to the present and show me what I'm avoiding.'

If you find your state of stillness makes you too sleepy, diffuse or mentally escapist, try some more active hypnosis. You don't have to relax more to go deeper into hypnosis, you can become more alert. Try keeping your eyes open and deliberately moving one hand, deliberately moving yourself into a clearer state. Then stop the deliberate movement and notice what's there without trying to disappear. For example, say to yourself,

While keeping your eyes open and in full view, press your thumb and forefinger together. As you bring them together gently press and release as you bring yourself into a clearer state of mind. Repeat a second time, “As you press your fingers together and let them go, bring yourself into an even clearer state of mind.” Repeat one more time then stop. In the first second after you stop deliberately pressing your fingers together, notice if there is an echo of it in your fingers, in your breathing, in your attention or not even notice if it isn’t there. Just notice. Keep yourself upright and work at this level.

Induction using two hands

Place one hand over your heart and one over your solar plexis.

Mentally associate the hand over your heart as your Nurturer and the one over your solar plexis as your vulnerable child.

Breathe gently…

In your mind say, “Love above. Child below. Adult present.”

“As I breathe, I am sending love down to my child and I am open to receiving a signal back from my child. As I continue to breathe, I am sending more love down to my child and I am receiving even more of a signal from my child.”

Induction for going to sleep

In order to go to sleep, you need to alter the script from above a little bit. The script above is for doing inner work with yourself and eventually you want to bring your Adult back in to write down what you have learned and take action items from your inner work session. For going to sleep, you just want to turn over the job of analyzing what is going on in your life to your Subconscious and have it work while you are sleeping and then bring it to your attention the next day when you have time to actually do something about the revelations you receive while you are sleeping.

Check out Nimja’s playlist for going to sleep using hypnosis.

Fractionation (coming back and deciding if you want to go back in) at some point in the above inductions if you want (note always tell them you are doing this before you do it)

“Whenever you are ready, open your eyes and come back up. It is entirely up to you whether or not you want to continue on with this process. You can close or soften your eyes again and allow your attention to gather again. Either way is ok. You can stop at either time.”

Deepener (if you feel it will be useful)

Before you go in, decide what scene you are going to go into when you get there (i.e. a room in your heart, a garden, a temple, a place where you can sit with your Protector by a fire, a lake where the water is so clear you can see the bottom, a place where your adult is waiting for you in a chair, etc.) Just pick one.

Decide who or what you need to talk to first.

Checking trance depth (if you feel it will be useful)

“As I wait in anticipation of connecting with my inner scene, I am letting go of the responsibility for it to actually happen.”

“If it is useful for me, my wise Subconscious will make one of my fingers in one of my hands twitch, pulse, come up on its own, heat up, tingle or do something that will alert me to the fact that my inner meeting space is ready for me.”

“I will not try to make it happen and I will not pretend it is happening. I will just wait with love for it to happen.”

Sometimes it is a finger. Sometimes it is a whole hand heating up. Sometimes it is a change in your breathing pattern. Sometimes it is nothing. Even if it does happen, it doesn’t mean it is “True”, per se. It just means that maybe it will be useful for you.

Shortcut way to get into hypnosis

Learn the above induction and the coming back down part at the end. When you feel comfortable, do the following….

Extra Resource #4 (optional) : Install a shortcut to hypnosis . Note: My Dad told me he used to do this while driving (using a mudra -- putting two fingers together in the 'Ok' sign), but I do NOT recommend putting yourself into a trance while driving! It's good to relax while driving (also using a mudra) but please keep the cue for that distinct from any cue you use to go inward in hypnosis.

You may wish to pick a word, phrase or motion (optional) to use to go back into hypnosis. In order to make this work, you will need to say that word/phrase (and do the motion, if you're using one) while you're in a really good state of hypnosis. Don't do this at a highly emotional time, when you're in a child state, when you're watching a trauma scene, etc. You're PICKING a good state and saying that this will take you back to THIS way of listening, whenever you use this word/phrase (and motion, if you're using one) at a time/place of your choosing.

" And whenever you say this word/phrase at a time/place of your choosing to begin, you'll be able to go into this wonderful way of listening, and you'll be able to change that, or you'll be able to finish . "

Now come all the way back up. At some point, when it's appropriate for you to begin again, you can use this. As you find it useful, you may wish to shorten the longer induction you're using, or you may choose to always use the longer induction if this doesn't feel like enough. You don't have to use it a certain number of times, go down a certain level, have a certain sensation, etc. when you do this.

Check to make sure that it's useful, and that you can turn it off at will (and that it stays off when you want it to), and that you can change it while you're in it, and that it's NOT going to make you listen to any narrator, notification, etc. that you overhear. It is to be useful to you when you want to use it, and it is NOT to make you go into hypnosis to work on things that you're not ready to work on.

Do NOT do this while driving or anything where you need to be actively paying attention! It's just not safe to go into a trance where you're listening inwardly when you need to be listening outwardly, even if you have your eyes open and even if you use 'if' type language.

To turn this off, you can stop rehearsing it, take it out of any recordings you've made, take it out of any apps you're using actively, and just do the longer induction. You can say that you're done with using it while you're up, but it may not be gone. If you find that you're still going into it when you don't want to, or you're still getting disoriented, please get some help with this from someone who is qualified. If you want to keep this to use in the future, that's fine, but only to begin a session at a later date of your choosing. For now, you're done with this state.
