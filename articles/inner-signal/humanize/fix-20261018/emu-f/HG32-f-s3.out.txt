Are some people being put forward as spiritually superior?

Is there a demand that something be given up – money, sex, loyalty, silence, obedience?

Is there a narrowing of choices for yourself?

Is any questioning considered a betrayal?

Is there a way of making resistance seem pathological?

Is there a down-grading of ordinary life?

Is there a seduction, or scaring or rescuing of the child in?

Can the adult in still say “no” ?
