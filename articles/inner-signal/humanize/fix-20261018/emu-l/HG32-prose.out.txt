Appendix C: The extended de-hypnosis checklist

MANY TIMES THERE COMES TIMES IN LIFE WHEN YOU FEEL YOU NEED TO DISCRIMINATE IF SOMETHING IS GOOD OR BAD FOR YOU, SOMEONE IS GOOD OR BAD FOR YOU, AN ADVERTISEMENT, NEW IDEOLOGY, SPIRITUAL TEACHING, ROMANTIC ATTRACTION, VOICE IN YOUR HEAD ETC..

USE THE FOLLOWING LIST TO HELP YOU DISCRIMINATE, KNOWING SOMETHING IS TRYING TO INFLUENCE YOU GIVES YOU THE POWER TO NOT FALL FOR IT, ASK YOUR SELF THE FOLLOWING QUESTIONS

IS THIS TRUE, IS THIS LOVE, SHOULD I SLEEP ON IT, IS IT REALLY FOR ME.

IS THERE A SENSE OF URGENCY, IS IT TO BE DONE IN SECRET, IS THERE SHAME BEING USED, AM I BEING TOLD I WILL NOT FIT IN IF I DONT DO WHAT IS ASKED, AM I BEING TOLD I AM SPECIAL, IS THERE A SEXUAL CHARGE, DOES IT MAKE ME FEEL SPIRITUALLY BETTER THEN OTHERS, IS IT TRYING TO GET MY MONEY, SEX, LOYALTY, SILENCE, OBEDIENCE, IS IT SHOWING ME A WAY THAT TAKES AWAY MY CHOICES, DOES IT TELL ME THERE IS ONLY ONE WAY, THAT IF I DONT DO IT, THINK IT ETC. THAT THERE IS SOMETHING WRONG WITH ME, IS IT TRYING TO DOWN GRADE MY REGULAR EVERY DAY LIFE, IS IT TRYING TO SEDUCE MY INNER CHILD, SCARE MY INNER CHILD, RESCUE MY INNER CHILD, AM I AS AN ADULT STILL ABLE TO SAY NO.

IF YOU HAVE SEVERAL YES ANSWERS, SLOW DOWN, DONT GO DEEPER IN TO THE TRANCE, TAKE THE TIME TO GET BACK IN TO YOUR BODY, ROOM, FOOD, SLEEP ETC. AND GET SOME TIME AND ADVICE FROM OTHERS.

A GOOD GUIDE WILL HELP INCREASE YOUR ADULT, A BAD ONE WILL WANT TO REPLACE YOUR ADULT. A GOOD GUIDE WILL LET YOU SAY NO, A BAD ONE WILL TELL YOU YOUR NO IS COMING FROM RESISTANCE, EGO, FEAR, LOW VIBRATION, TRAUMA, SIN ETC. AND THAT YOU NEED MORE GUIDANCE.

FOR HYPOSNOSIS USE THE ABOVE, AND THE FOLLOWING

ARE YOU BEING TOLD WHAT WILL BE DONE, MECHANICS USED ETC. WHEN YOU ANSWER THE FOLLOWING, IF YOU CAN, AT LEAST ONE OF THE ANSWERS SHOULD BE TO STOP AND THINK ABOUT IT, MODIFY THE PROCESS, COME FULLY AWAKE, OR STOP THE PROCESS AL TOGETHER. ARE YOU BEING TOLD THAT NOT SEEING WHAT IS BEING DONE MAKES IT MORE POWERFUL, ARE ROLES, OWNERSHIP, OBEDIENCE, AGE, IDENTITY, BELONGING ETC. BEING USED TO TAKE YOU OUT OF YOUR ADULT, ARE YOU BEING TOLD WHAT WILL BE DONE, MECHANICS USED ETC. FRACTIONATION, WHAT WILL EFFECT YOU WHEN YOU HEAR CUES ETC. AFTER THE PROCESS, WHAT WILL BE EFFECTS ON YOUR MEMORY ETC. WHAT WILL BE HAPPENING IN YOUR BODY, ARE YOU BEING TOLD WHAT WILL BE DONE TO BRING YOU OUT, RESTORING YOU TO YOUR IDENTITY ETC. THAT THE PROCESS IS OVER ETC.

WHEN IN DOUBT COME FULLY ALERT, ASK FOR THE SCRIPT TO READ, CHANGE THE PROCESS ETC. OR LEAVE.
