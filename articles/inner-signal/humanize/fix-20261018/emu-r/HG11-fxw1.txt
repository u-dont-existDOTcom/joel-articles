Forget the left-brain/right-brain cartoon

“Syncing the hemispheres” can be a very useful description of how the state feels, but I don’t want to over-interpret what is going on neuroscientifically. A large resting state fMRI study of 1011 people did not support the common pop- neuropsychological notion that people in general can be left or right brained, although there is certainly evidence for the lateralization of some functions or networks.

The invitation is to elicit a high level of cooperation between the verbal, analytical, imaginal, emotional, somatic, intuitive, relational, and spiritual modes. It’s not an electoral process; nobody needs to win!

The mind of love retains its warmth, the mind of analysis its brightness. The mind of intuition offers its signals without becoming dogma. The mind of rationality tests the signals without killing them. The body has a voice- it can say yes, no or not yet. The imagination has opened the field and the adult chooses the next real action. There is peace but there is also precision.

Love without analysis can become fantasy, analysis without love can become sterile. Intuition without testing can become delusion; testing without intuition can become dead. These modes can each serve to correct the others.

And of course it’s got to lead somewhere! A powerful state only matters if something survives it: kinder behavior, more humility, better judgment, real service, or a more workable ordinary life.
