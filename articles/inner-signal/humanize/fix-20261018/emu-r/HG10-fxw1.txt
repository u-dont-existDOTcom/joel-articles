Mayo Clinic cautions that using hypnosis around stressful earlier-life events can trigger strong emotional reactions, and that other treatments may be better for historical trauma.

Use Self-Hypnosis for: regulation, dialogue, reparenting, clarity, habit change, present life safety, emotional integration, ordinary-life action

Don't use Self-Hypnosis for: forcing trauma memories, proof of trauma memories, suppressing medical symptoms, destabilizing yourself, instead of correct treatment
