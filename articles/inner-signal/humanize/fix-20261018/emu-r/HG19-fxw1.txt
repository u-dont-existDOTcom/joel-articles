The actual inner meeting

If you tend to daydream, dissociate or fall asleep, sit up rather than lying down for this exercise. Save lying down for when you’re ready to go to sleep or receive deep rest or are trying to incubate dreams. Keep your spine straight, but not rigid, like a soldier. Relax your jaw and maintain some connection with the room you are in.

You can say to yourself, “I am not leaving life. I am listening more deeply to life.”

Invoking: “Higher Self, universal heart-mind, wise subconscious mind, the intelligence in my body, my inner child, my inner adults, my protective parts, and all that serves truth and love: please help me understand what I need to know, only in the form and timing that is useful, kind, stabilizing and real.”

You’re asking your conscious mind to listen without forcing an answer.

This doesn’t mean you have to silence your thoughts to listen. Notice your thoughts, sensations, images, doubts, emotions, etc., without fighting them, without going with them, while you hold the intention of this exercise lightly in the background of your mind. Notice what stands out on its own.

You can say, “Tell me what I need to know or ask me what you need to know, and I will sit in silence for five minutes and listen.”

You might hear a phrase, feel a sensation, notice a memory, see a color, be contacted by a child or protector, feel pain, warmth, silence or know that you need to do something or be grateful for something or feel an urge to stop, or realize that this is enough listening for today.

Listen to it all and believe none of it automatically.
