The weekly deeper practice

Practise this once or twice a week and spend more time with it if that would be useful but more time doesn’t have to mean more intensity. Start from Higher Self or neutral witnessing and let it unfold, borrowing an adult function when needed, asking whether anything important is being bypassed and always allowing for a full exit and any necessary normal action.

Wherever possible take any normal action immediately afterwards to impress on the subconscious that the adult heard and acted, otherwise it’s just one more beautiful internal meeting, and the dishes stay exactly where they were.
