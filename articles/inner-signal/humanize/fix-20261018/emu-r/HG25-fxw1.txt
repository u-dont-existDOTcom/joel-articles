What not to do

DO NOT: Treat inner voices, images, autonomous movements or body signals as commands or proof. Listen, then always reality test them.

DO NOT chase grand “downloads” or make a hobby of demons, jinn, “Unattached Burdens”, etc. Stabilize yourself first. Get help if necessary, especially if not reality testing well or if not functioning well in life.

DO NOT do self-hypnosis while driving a car, bathing, swimming, operating equipment, supervising children, or otherwise doing something that requires active attention.

DO NOT try to “force” memories, use trance to “prove” what happened, or ignore medical symptoms by “forcing” them down through hypnosis.

DO NOT “force” yourself to ignore basic body signals, like hunger, tiredness, pain, etc., or basic emotions, like grief, anger, fear, etc., before you understand what the signal is saying.

DO NOT “extend” altered states indefinitely or take intuitive hits as “real” without reality testing them.

DO NOT allow any inner role, like the “Inner Child” or “Protector” or “Nurturer” or “Leader” to get out of proportion. The child doesn’t run adult life, the Protector can’t shut out all love, the Nurturer can’t erase consequences, the Leader can’t skip over the child, and both analysis and tenderness stay available.

DO NOT use NLP techniques to “force” down “protectors” or to manufacture certainty, manipulate people, or “heal” something when the body is saying no.

Don’t convince yourself that negative indicators like doubt, distraction, resistance, or lack of response, or saying “No” are good indicators that the induction is working.

Don’t rely solely upon the “safety” of “the unconscious mind”. Always make certain you have clear limits, a safe setting, the ability to stop, and a way out you’ve tested.

Don’t believe that normal physical sensations prove hidden trauma, illness, body toxins, possession, “attachments”, “transformations” or some hidden “subconscious” truth.

Don’t believe that anything is “healed” physically or neurologically/cellularly, simply because it is “healed” in the imagination.

Don’t put in, or let anyone put in, hidden “commands”. A cue you choose consciously to reuse should have a clear purpose, stay reviewable, and be removed if you no longer want it. Just saying “cancel” doesn’t guarantee it’s instantly erased. Don’t allow anyone to put in undisclosed third-party activation, or to make YOU do anything or change to a different person or have amnesia, etc.

DO bring yourself all the way back at the end of every waking session: yourself again, moving, oriented, with your judgment back, and say straight out that the session has ended.
