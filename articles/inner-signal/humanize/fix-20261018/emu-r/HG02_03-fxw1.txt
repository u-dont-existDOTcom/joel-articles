Have you ever considered the fact that you were born with a brain and mind that is capable of many different layers of consciousness: dreaming, protecting, analyzing, imitating, loving, remembering, hiding, revealing and healing, etc. etc.?

Instead of learning how to integrate all of these different layers, you were taught (and/or you learned on your own) how to push through, repress or supress your emotions, to pretend to be confident, to follow or defy authority, and so on and so forth, and to call that adulthood. Meanwhile, your deeper mind kept running its own "programs": the child mind wanting safety, the guard expecting danger, the body mind remembering what you explained away, the intuitive mind speaking before words, the fantasy mind trying to become guidance, etc.

A lot of hypnosis teaching acts as if the subconscious mind worked like a machine, and all you had to do was feed in a command and the machine would do what you put in. That never did much for me. For me, a much more useful model was to sit down and listen to the different parts of myself (the scared kid, the guard, the body, the intuition, the part trying to run Tuesday) until they all could be heard without one of them seizing the microphone.

This guide teaches a method of self-hypnosis that gathers your attention and brings the conscious mind, subconscious mind, body, inner child, protector parts, the adult self, and the Higher Self all into the same room to be able to listen and talk together honestly.

Where do I start? Easy. Just listen, warm up to, protect, clarify, reality test and act on it!

It doesn’t have to be a dramatic trance experience but it does have to lead to a more trustworthy relationship between all parts of the mind, proven in ordinary life.

Let’s call it communion. The conscious mind doesn’t have to shout it’s message from the roof top and the subconscious doesn’t have to rely on symptoms, dreams, cravings, day dreams, and other weird body signals to communicate.

I work with the premise that the subconscious is a living ecology of parts, memories, impulses, images, protections and wisdom. When the system needs coherence, I begin with Higher Self or the universal heart, and I separate into those parts only when that helps. I bring in inner-child reparenting because a wounded young part won’t be repaired by a clever suggestion. I also pull in elements from prompt engineering, because the shape of a question changes the kind of answer we can hear, and teach literacy in the domain of influence, since hypnosis is far from the only thing that’s hypnotizing us.
