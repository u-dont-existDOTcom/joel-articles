“I notice you. You’re trying to protect something, and I want to understand. Can I ask you what you’re afraid would happen if you stopped?”

Now be patient, and listen. It may be that they’re afraid that you’ll lie to yourself again, or that this will turn into another performance, or that feeling the pain will destroy you, or that you’ll use spirituality to dodge reality, forgive too early, call a fantasy guidance, or leave them hanging the moment the session is over.

Now, tell the protective part that makes sense, that it doesn’t have to move yet, and ask what would help it trust you 1% more.

Usually the protector needs evidence more than another spiritual insight: things like maintaining a boundary, doing a certain task, eating properly, holding back from sending a text, keeping the promise, or sleeping, or reaching out to a therapist, or acknowledging that you do, in fact, owe someone money, or writing down the truth.

Protection that knows when to stop scanning

The Protector is more than an inner voice saying no. Want to “embody” it? Practice the following sequence:

(1) orient self (look around, feel support, etc.),

(2) assess situation (what do you know, not know, need to check out?),

(3) differentiate between actual/real dangers, uncertainty, past dangers or imagined dangers,

(4) set boundaries (just one, proportional to danger level; move, stop, delay, ask, leave, get support, etc.),

(5) verify enough safety (not perfect certainty, just enough real evidence for the next step), and finally

(6) stand down and rest: protecting includes ending the scan once more vigilance isn’t helping.

Seems to me that this would be a “mature” Protector. For example, it wouldn’t be afraid to say, “I don’t know”, or to ask another human being, or to delay interpreting what’s going on, or to use a no-trance practice. I want to be able to “do” this. I am learning discernment that acts when needed, and to quit scanning the room after the danger has passed.

Inner child reparenting

I connect my reparenting therapy to self hypnosis. I think they’re a perfect fit. So, while you are in hypnosis, the child may arrive (this can be any age, image, posture, voice, memory, emotion, vulnerable self, or no child at all. If there is no child, stay with a protector you recognize, or with whatever sensation, feeling or preference is actually there).

If there is a child that appears, don’t feel like you have to do anything with them. Just be with them.

See if you can tell the child: “Little one, I am here. You don’t have to explain everything, you don’t even have to be healed today. You don’t have to impress me. Just tell me if there’s something you need to help you feel less alone.”

It can be anything. Need warmth? need to play? need protection? need to grieve? need to be angry? need food? rest? a boundary? a safer relationship? need to stop fantasizing? need to stop working so hard? need me to keep a promise to you for one day?

Don’t rush the repair

Find a younger you /protector/feeling/signal in your body that needs tending to and then:

Prepare yourself to do the tending (Get yourself into a place where you have time, privacy, support etc. Be as adult as you can)

Look at what’s there before you decide what it means

Ask for permission to move closer or what is needed (warmth, space, words, silence, no contact etc)

Do something to meet one of the needs you find when you move closer. Don’t try to fix it all.

Wait a while for the effect of what you have done to sink in. Some changes need quiet, sleep or just ordinary time.

Reconnect with that part of you ( don’t expect it to become one with you, go away, forgive, or be happy, just connect if it wants to)

Rest, and stop before the care turns into another performance. Then come back later and ask what actually helped.

Your aim is not to make it all good as new, as if your history should disappear, but good as you: more supported and connected, and still recognizably yourself. Feel free to change the language if the part talk is not useful, think signal, feeling, younger you or anything that helps you connect with the issue

Play and rest still need an adult present

Reparenting doesn't have to be solemn. It's really okay to let your younger states enjoy playing with things that interest them. Touch textures, draw, move your body, talk to your plushie in a different voice, as long as you can keep reality testing intact and remember that you are not actually a child/pet/doll/whatever and are not owned, it's okay to recover your play ability.

Resting without being helpless: sometimes you just need to rest, and that doesn't have to mean being helpless. Cut down on decisions for a set period, put unfinished tasks somewhere you can see them, and let the adult in you keep watch. You can still move, stop and come back.

Non-dominant hand dialogue

If speaking or visualization feels fake, try writing. Have your non-dominant hand write as your child/protector (even if your handwriting looks a little awkward), and have your dominant hand answer as you. For example, your non-dominant hand might write, “I don’t trust you.” Answer with your dominant hand, “I hear that you don’t trust me, but I love you, and I want to know where I’ve let you down. I won’t ask you to trust me, but I want to hear you out and make one promise to you that I will keep today.” Your non-dominant hand might respond, “What promise?” Your dominant hand might write, “I will eat, clean the desk, and not text the person who makes us feel unsafe.”
