A useful map of the mind

In this guide, when I refer to your ‘subconscious’ I am referring to the deeper processes shaping perceptions, emotions, desires, avoidance, imagery, memories, body signals, habits, intuitions, automatic behaviours etc. Sometimes it is useful to work with all of these as one ‘mind’, other times it can be more effective to work with the ‘ecology’ of parts. Neither way is ‘right’ or ‘wrong’ they’re both maps, and neither one needs its own priesthood.

Working with parts is about precision, working with the Higher Self is about coherence – and communion is the movement between them.

When you need coherence, work with the ‘whole’ – the subconscious as one:

“Invoke” the subconscious – e.g. ‘Hello wise subconscious can you help me understand…..’

When you need precision, recognise the ‘ecology’ in the subconscious – there may be a child part that wants love, a protector part that wants to keep you safe, a ‘critic’ that wants you to live up to certain standards, a fantasy making part that wants possibility, body memory that wants recognition, a social part that wants belonging, a spiritual part that wants to connect to God/source/do what is in the best benefit of all beings, a ‘wiser’ part that wants what is true, etc etc

When things feel scattered, it is a good idea to start with unity:

“Invoke” your Higher Self – e.g. ‘Hello my Higher Self/universal heart-mind/wise subconscious please guide me.’

Then move into differentiation if needed:

Ask “Which part is afraid? Which part is protecting? Which part is avoiding? Which part knows what to do? Which part is too young for this?”

Then move back into unity: “Let all of this belong to one life. Let the adult self lead with love.”

Sometimes start with the whole

Some people feel more fragmented when they start by naming 12 parts and giving everybody an office. For these people, it is better to start from the Sacred whole.

You can start like this: ‘I begin from the universal heart. I begin from Higher Self. I begin from the part of me that belongs to life before it belongs to any wound, role, family, group, identity, ideology or story. Please help me understand what is true, kind, timely, stabilizing and useful.’

Sometimes you won’t have access to your Higher Self, or to the universal heart, or whatever language feels sacred for you, or it feels borrowed from somebody else. In that case, just begin by witnessing: notice the room, your body, and that you’re in a younger state. The fact that something in you can notice is enough of an adult function to begin with.

Once you can notice, and distinguish, you don’t need to keep rehearsing the Witness. Begin working on whatever is actually missing: warmth, protection, direction etc. So while the language of the Higher Self can help you gather around a meaningful source of care, it doesn’t mean that a perfectly capable inner parent is already there. The adult functions still grow through practice.

Differentiate only when something specific comes up. “Is this coming from my inner child? Is it a protector? Is this my body speaking? Is this fear speaking? Is this intuition? Is this social conditioning speaking? Is this divine guidance or my interpretation of guidance?”

But always start from the heart, differentiate as needed, and then come back to the heart.
