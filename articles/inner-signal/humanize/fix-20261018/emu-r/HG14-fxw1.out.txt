How to let your ‘child’ talk without giving up control

It’s possible to let the ‘younger’ parts of you express themselves without surrendering your adult judgement to them, and without literally reverting to a younger age. Instead, the aim is to access the positive aspects of ‘younger’ feeling states, such as a sense of wonder or playfulness, more direct sensory experiences, vulnerability, need, and a desire to let someone else make decisions for you.
