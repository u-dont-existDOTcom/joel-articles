Appendix F: Self-trust, outside guidance, and oracle-like states

(See how to use synchronicity as a cue above.) It turns noticing into orientation and reality-testing, not an oracle.

People’s suggestibility varies hugely, both between individuals and according to state, whether you’re talking about external or internal suggestion, and the type of suggestion (commands, imagery, music, metaphors, spiritual etc). There are definitely advantages to both working with someone and doing it yourself, and it depends on what you’re trying to do. A professional can bring pacing, containment and another set of eyes, and doing it yourself gives you instant follow-up, real-time correction and a private line to your own protectors.
