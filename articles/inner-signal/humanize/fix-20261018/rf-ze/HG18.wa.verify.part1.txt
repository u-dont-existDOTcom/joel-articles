Below is one section of a long self-hypnosis guide by Joel, published on his Substack. It teaches gathering attention, listening to the subconscious as an ecology of parts (the inner child, protectors or "guards", the body, intuition), three adult functions he calls the Nurturer, the Protector and the Leader/Guru, Higher Self, "borrowed adulthood", inductions and a complete return, influence literacy ("de-hypnosis": noticing and resisting other people's influence), and safety limits (when to stop, when to get help). Its quoted lines are scripts the reader says or thinks.

PUBLISHED section makes it once, or "REPEATS: <n>" followed by one line per repeat ("R: <the point>, in B<a> and B<b>").

PUBLISHED section:
[B1] Reverse whatever you used to enter. If you floated, regain chosen weight. If you became small, return to ordinary scale. If you met a younger state or several parts, confirm present wholeness, body ownership, and adult judgment. If you traveled, come back by the same route. Keep only the qualities you deliberately chose.

[B2] “I bring back only what is useful now. The rest can remain safely held until there is more capacity. I return to the room, the body, the day, and one honest action. All instructions to deepen or remain in this session are finished. I keep only the ordinary-life intention or future-practice cue I consciously chose; neither keeps today’s altered state running. I am myself, in my body, with adult judgment fully available.”

[B3] One — feel the body’s contact and chosen weight. Two — let the breath return to its ordinary rhythm. Three — hear the room and name where you are. Four — move fingers, toes, shoulders, and eyes. Five — eyes open, present, ordinary, clear. The session has ended.

[B4] Name the day or date. Look left, right, near, and far. Move before standing, and stand only when steady. Check that you are actually oriented and alert; repeating the count is not proof of a complete return. Note only what actually appeared, and any action it calls for. Then do something normal.

[B5] If a recording stops unexpectedly: say, “The recording stopped and the session is over.” Open your eyes, name the location and day, press your feet or hands into a surface, move, look around, and use the five-step count. Don’t wait for the missing narrator to release you.

[B6] Sleep is separate. A sleep recording may end in sleep or quiet rest. A waking recording shouldn’t end by preserving a role, reduced judgment, impaired movement, blankness, an undefined reset word, or an invitation to remain altered indefinitely.

REWRITE:
[CHANGED, replaces B1]
In order to exit from the session you will need to reverse how you entered, for example if you floated then feel yourself gain weight again, if you shrank then go back to your normal size. If you went back to a younger state or came out as different parts of yourself then make sure you are in the here and now, whole, in your body, and recognise yourself to be an adult in control of your own body. If you travelled somewhere to enter trance then come back the same way. Make sure that you only keep what you want to keep.

[CHANGED, replaces B2]
Script: Come back bringing with you only what you want to bring back into your conscious life. Nothing else needs to come back at this time. You are now back in the room, back in your body, back in your day. My instructions to you now are complete. You may choose to adopt this intention in your normal life or this cue at a later date. Both of these things will not continue this session. You are back to your normal self, in your body, with your adult judgement.

[CHANGED, replaces B3]
1, feeling the weight of your body again 2, returning to your normal breathing pattern 3, aware of your surroundings and where you are 4, moving your fingers, toes, shoulders and eyes 5, opening your eyes back to normal life, completely present and totally normal. The session is now over.

[CHANGED, replaces B4]
When you open your eyes think to yourself what day or date it is, look to your left and to your right, look at something far away and something close. Move your body about a bit before getting up. Make sure you are back to normal. Don’t just assume that because you counted back it worked. It’s all good and well thinking you are out when you really want to be out but check you actually are out before you get up. Note down what happened, if anything, and what you are going to do about it. Then go and do something normal to fully re-integrate back into normal life.

[CHANGED, replaces B5]
If your recording cuts out before the exit script plays say to yourself that the recording has cut out and the session is over. Then open your eyes, say where you are, what day it is, push down with your feet or hands, move about, look around and count yourself back using the above 5 step process.

[CHANGED, replaces B6]
Note: If you are using a recording to help you sleep then it doesn’t matter if it leaves you asleep or in a quiet state. This advice is for waking recordings only. Waking recordings should never leave you in a state, with a role, with lowered judgement, with lowered ability to move, blank, with a non-specific reset word or in any other way that will keep you in an altered state for an indefinite period.
