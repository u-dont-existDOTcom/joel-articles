Below is one section of a long self-hypnosis guide by Joel, published on his Substack. It teaches gathering attention, listening to the subconscious as an ecology of parts (the inner child, protectors or "guards", the body, intuition), three adult functions he calls the Nurturer, the Protector and the Leader/Guru, Higher Self, "borrowed adulthood", inductions and a complete return, influence literacy ("de-hypnosis": noticing and resisting other people's influence), and safety limits (when to stop, when to get help). Its quoted lines are scripts the reader says or thinks.

PUBLISHED section:
[B1] Reverse whatever you used to enter. If you floated, regain chosen weight. If you became small, return to ordinary scale. If you met a younger state or several parts, confirm present wholeness, body ownership, and adult judgment. If you traveled, come back by the same route. Keep only the qualities you deliberately chose.

[B2] “I bring back only what is useful now. The rest can remain safely held until there is more capacity. I return to the room, the body, the day, and one honest action. All instructions to deepen or remain in this session are finished. I keep only the ordinary-life intention or future-practice cue I consciously chose; neither keeps today’s altered state running. I am myself, in my body, with adult judgment fully available.”

[B3] One — feel the body’s contact and chosen weight. Two — let the breath return to its ordinary rhythm. Three — hear the room and name where you are. Four — move fingers, toes, shoulders, and eyes. Five — eyes open, present, ordinary, clear. The session has ended.

[B4] Name the day or date. Look left, right, near, and far. Move before standing, and stand only when steady. Check that you are actually oriented and alert; repeating the count is not proof of a complete return. Note only what actually appeared, and any action it calls for. Then do something normal.

[B5] If a recording stops unexpectedly: say, “The recording stopped and the session is over.” Open your eyes, name the location and day, press your feet or hands into a surface, move, look around, and use the five-step count. Don’t wait for the missing narrator to release you.

[B6] Sleep is separate. A sleep recording may end in sleep or quiet rest. A waking recording shouldn’t end by preserving a role, reduced judgment, impaired movement, blankness, an undefined reset word, or an invitation to remain altered indefinitely.

REWRITE:
[CHANGED, replaces B1]
In order to come out of a session, you should do the opposite of what you did to enter. If you floated or went to a place, come back down using the same method. If you decided to have a certain weight as you came down, use that weight when you land. If you made yourself smaller, go back to your normal size. If you went to a place where you met a younger self or parts of yourself, make sure to tell them that you are whole now, this is your body, and you will be using adult judgement, etc. If you traveled in some way, then come back the same way you went. Be sure that you are bringing back only what you intended to bring back from the session.

[CHANGED, replaces B2]
You may want to say, “I am bringing back with me only what I need at this time. All other things will be safely stored until I need them. I am coming back now to this place, to my body, and to normal living, one honest action at a time. Except for (whatever you may be keeping for ordinary life or cues for practicing in the future, if you are keeping any), nothing I have heard will cause me to go deeper or stay in the session.”

[CHANGED, replaces B3]
Begin to count yourself out: “1. Coming back now into your body, with whatever weight you choose to have. 2. Coming back to your normal breathing pattern. 3. Coming back to hearing all of the sounds around you right now in (wherever you are). Start moving your fingers and toes, your shoulders, your eyes. 4. Still coming back. 5. Open your eyes feeling normal, and knowing that the session is over.”

[CHANGED, replaces B4]
Say what day it is or the date, if you need to, and look to the left and the right. Look at something close and something far away. Feel free to move around a bit before getting up. Get up when you feel like it. Make sure that you are alert and oriented to the here and now. Just because you counted doesn’t mean that you are out. Take note of things that you may or may not need to do, if anything came up in the session that you need to act on. Then go about your normal activities.

[CHANGED, replaces B5]
If you are listening to a recording and it stops before it is supposed to, then just say to yourself that it has stopped and the session is over. Use the above method to bring yourself out by opening your eyes, saying where you are, saying what day it is, pushing down with your feet or hands, moving around, looking around, and/or using the 5 step method above. Don’t just sit there waiting for the recording to start up again to “let you out.”

[CHANGED, replaces B6]
NOTE: If you are going to sleep to a recording, it is fine to let the recording put you to sleep or leave you in a quiet place. However, a recording for use while awake should not leave you in a role, with lowered judgement, impaired movement capabilities, blank, with an undefined reset word, etc.
