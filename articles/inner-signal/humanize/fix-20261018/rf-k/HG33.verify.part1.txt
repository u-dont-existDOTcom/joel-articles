PUBLISHED section:
[B1] Anxiety is often a Protector trying to prevent danger, shame, abandonment, failure, exposure, illness, rejection, loss of control, or a feeling it believes would be unbearable. Don’t begin by cross-examining it. Help the body first.

[B2] Body first. Inner child second. Inquiry third. Action fourth.

[B4] Start with the hand-on-heart, hand-on-solar-plexus practice.

[B5] “Little one, you are not alone. You do not have to solve this. I am here now. The adult is listening.”

[B6] Don’t recite the words like an incantation you’re hoping the subconscious will grade. Say them the way you’d speak to a frightened child you actually love.

[B8] Use this gently. Buteyko-style reduced breathing can help some people who overbreathe or panic, but don’t force breath holds. Air hunger can briefly increase anxiety before it settles, so go slowly.

[B9] Breathe through the nose. Let the inhale be small. Let the exhale be quiet. Pause only if comfortable. Let the breath become less dramatic. Stop if dizzy, strained, panicky, or medically concerned.

[B26] First, body. Feet, chair, breath, room.

[B27] Little one, I am here. Protector, thank you for warning me. You do not have to scream for me to listen.

[B28] If there is real action needed, show me one step. If there is no action needed, help the body stand down gently.

[B29] I will not abandon the signal. I will not worship the signal. I will listen, test, and act.

[CHANGED, replaces B1-B2]
Remember that often the anxiety comes from a protector part of you, who is trying to help you, but who believes you are in danger, or may be shamed, or abandoned, or fail, or be exposed, or be sick, or be rejected, or lose control, or whatever. Therefore, instead of the interrogation above, start with yourself:

Always work with yourself in a series – first your body, then your inner child, then some inner work, then some action:

[CHANGED, replaces B4-B6]
Here is some inner child love. Put your hand over your heart and your other hand on your solar plexis, and say to yourself in a loving way, “Little one, you are not alone. You don’t have to fix this. I’m here now. The adult is listening.” Make sure you are actually talking to your inner child, who is scared to death.

[CHANGED, replaces B8-B9]
Then work on your reduced breathing / Buteyko’ breathing. (NOTE: there are some good things to be said for slowing your breathing down, if you are breathing too much, or panicked. However, please don’t hold your breath until you are air hungry, which can make you more anxious in the short term. Go slow on this.) Inhale small amounts through your nose, exhale quietly, and pause if you can. Can you make your breathing very quiet? If you feel bad, dizzy, or panicky, or have medical issues, STOP!

[CHANGED, replaces B26-B29]
Where are my body parts? What are my feet doing? How is my body positioned in the chair? How is my body breathing? How is my body situated in the room?

If you’re there little one, let me know I’m here. Thank you protector for letting me know you’re here, you didn’t have to scream I got your message.

If there is something we need to do let me know what the first thing is that we need to do at this moment, if there is nothing to do help my body stand down. I’m not going to abandon you but I’m not going to worship you, I will listen to what you have to say and reality test it and then take some action.
