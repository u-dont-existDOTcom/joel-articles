What not to do

DO NOT: See the voices, images, movements or body signals as real, but always reality test them.

DO NOT obsess about “downloading” grandiose information or become obsessed with demons, jinn, “Unattached Burdens”, etc. Stabilize yourself. Get help if necessary, especially if not reality testing well or if not functioning well in life.

DO NOT do self-hypnosis while driving a car, bathing, swimming, or otherwise doing something that requires active participation.

DO NOT try to “force” memories to “prove” something to yourself or ignore medical symptoms by “forcing” them down through hypnosis.

DO NOT “force” yourself to ignore basic body signals, like hunger, tiredness, pain, etc., or basic emotions, like grief, anger, fear, etc.

DO NOT “extend” altered states indefinitely or take intuitive hits as “real” without reality testing them.

DO NOT allow any inner state, like the “Inner Child” or “Protector” or “Nurturer” or “Leader” to take over completely. They each need the others.

DO NOT use NLP techniques to “force” down “protectors” or to do anything else that does not make logical sense, like to manipulate people or to “heal” something that the body is saying cannot be healed.

Don’t convince yourself that negative indicators like doubt, distraction, resistance, or lack of response, or saying “No” are good indicators that the induction is working.

Don’t rely solely upon the “safety” of “the unconscious mind”. Always make certain that the circumstances are as safe as possible and that there is a safe way out.

Don’t believe that normal physical sensations are indicators of trauma, illness, body toxins, demonic possession, “attachments”, “transformations” or anything else, just because the “subconscious” says they are.

Don’t believe that anything is “healed” physically or neurologically/cellularly, simply because it is “healed” in the imagination.

Don’t ever allow anyone to put “commands” in without giving them to yourself consciously and knowing why and wanting them, so that they can be used repeatedly and reviewed and removed if they are no longer wanted. There is no way to “put something in” that cannot be removed, but saying “I remove them” may not be enough. Don’t allow anyone to give commands to someone else to give to “YOU” or to make YOU do anything or change to a different person or have amnesia, etc.

DO bring yourself back to normal consciousness as yourself at the end of each session while awake.
