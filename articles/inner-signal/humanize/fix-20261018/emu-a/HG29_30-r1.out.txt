Appendix A: Prompt engineering for your subconscious

Your subconscious is not an AI but some things are transferable. You need to make sure that prompt engineering serves you to be more present, more honest, more loving and hear the messages of your subconscious better.

The following elements can be relevant for prompt engineering when you need to be more precise:

Role: Who is it that you talk to

Context: Which situation is it

Intention: What do you want to achieve

Constraint: What constraints do you want to set to make it more safe or to be more precise (like do not force things, be truthful, be loving etc.)

Output: Which kind of output is most helpful for you? Images, words, signals in your body, memories, next actions, questions, symbols etc. Or sometimes it is best to simply rest.

Evaluation: How will you know if you got a good answer? Criteria like is it true, kind, timely, brings stability, specific, testable etc.

Iteration: Change the prompt depending on the feedback you get from life

In some prompts it is good to include that it is safe for the system to not give an answer. Otherwise your analytical brain will feel like it needs to come up with something and distort things.

And if the system realizes that it needs more time to process or it is most helpful to not give an answer, then the system can deepen the comfortable rest in the body and give a nice silence as an answer. It is completely fine and a successful run if the answer is silence.

Sometimes there is simply nothing going on or the answer is that it is not the right time and you should rest and build up capacity or do the obvious things you know you need to do.

Here are some core prompts you can use:

Prompt for more clarity:

Dear wise system of my subconscious, I would be grateful if you could show me what is true, kind and timely. If it is too intense to bring the truth to my conscious mind, please only bring a symbol, a signal in my body or show me the next step. Please show me the capacity first and then show me the content.

Prompt to understand resistance:

What are you protecting me from and what can I do so that you can trust me 1% more?

Prompt to connect with your inner child:

What do you need me to know and what can I do for you today so that you don’t feel alone with it?

Prompt to ask a Protector what to do in the real world to feel safe:

What can I do in the real world to make this system feel safe?

Prompt to ask a Leader/Guru what to do:

What would be the thing to do that would be true, in love, in life and beneficial for all beings?

Prompt to combine intuition and analysis:

Give me the intuitive signal. Then help me analyze what that might mean. Then give me feedback in my body if my interpretation is correct. Then let me test it in reality.

Prompt to decondition and get good intuition:

Dear wise system of my subconscious, it would be great if you could help me decondition and give me true intuition that comes from the universal heart and helps me lead a better life in the ordinary world. If there is some family, religious, marketing, erotic, political or other trauma programming, or a part that wants to belong and made bargains as a child, please show me how this is conditioning and soften it or move it back. But only do it in a way that is safe. Leave only the true guidance that helps me. I will be grateful if you could bring the signal as a feeling in my body so I can feel the difference.

Prompt for daydreaming:

If this daydream has useful information, please show me what I can learn from it. If it is only to avoid dealing with something, please help me get back to the present and deal with what needs to be dealt with.

Prompt to understand anxiety:

What are you trying to protect and what can I do so that you can trust my adult self?

Prompt to see the truth but not get overwhelmed:

Please only show me the truth if it does not overwhelm me. Please show me the capacity first and then show me the content.

Prompt to integrate in ordinary life:

What can I do in ordinary life to make sure that I did integrate the message correctly?

It is great to combine prompt engineering with the power of language from NLP to get better answers.
