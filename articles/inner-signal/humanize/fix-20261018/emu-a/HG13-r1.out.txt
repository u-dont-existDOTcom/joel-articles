Working With the Subconscious Mind: How Many Pieces to Work With?

Working With the Subconscious Mind: How Many Pieces to Work With? In my posts, the words “subconscious”, “unconscious”, “feeling body”, “subpersonalities” etc. are meant to point to the part of your mind that is responsible for perception, emotion, desire, avoidance, imagery, memory, bodily signals, habit patterns, intuition, and automatic behaviour. Sometimes it’s helpful to work with this part of your mind as a whole, and sometimes it’s more helpful to work with it in a precise and differentiated way.

Working With Subpersonalities vs. Working with Higher Self It’s helpful to move back and forth between working with subpersonalities for precision and working with Higher Self for coherence. Here are some examples:

Working with Higher Self for Coherence “Oh, wise subconscious mind, what would you show me?”

Working with Subpersonalities for Precision “There are parts of my subconscious that want love like a child, parts that want to keep me safe, parts that want to criticize, parts that want to create fantasies, parts that hold the body’s memories, parts that want social acceptance, parts that want connection to God or source or to the benefit of all, and wiser parts that want what is really best. How do I work with all of these parts?”

Starting with Higher Self When You’re Fragmented “Oh, Higher Self/ universal heart-mind/ wise subconscious mind, what would you show me?”

Differentiating Subpersonalities When Needed “Which part is afraid? Which part wants to protect me? Which part wants to avoid this? Which part has the answer I’m looking for? Which part is too young for this?”

Integrating Again into One “My whole mind wants to live one life together, led by the love of the adult self.”

Sometimes, Start With the Whole If you’re too fragmented to even start working with parts, it’s better to start by seeing your whole being as sacred:

“I’m going to approach this from the perspective of universal heart or Higher Self, or from the part of me that existed before I took on all these roles and families and groups and stories and ideas and ideologies and identities and situations in my life.”

If you can’t approach it from the point of view of Higher Self or universal heart yet, then you can start by being the Witness of your experience, noticing your room and your body and that you were younger at some point in your life.

If you can do that, you don’t really need to work on being the Witness any more, and you can go straight to developing whatever inner function you need, such as inner warmth, or inner protection, or inner direction. Working with Higher Self is a good way to do this, because it helps you gather your resources around something meaningful. Just believing that you have inner parents is not going to make them appear – you have to develop them!
