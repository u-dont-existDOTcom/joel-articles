Troubleshooting

Nothing happens

If you're having problems, start by starting small .

What to do if something feels "fake"

First notice if it is the words, the picture, or what you are asked to do that feels fake. Note that if you don't hear a voice or see a picture, it does not mean that there is resistance, or that you cannot do adult things in the inside. But if it is something asked of you that you don't want to do, stop and correct it. If you sense that there is a part that doesn't want you to do it, you may want to find out what it is trying to protect you from. You might say something like, "I notice that you don't want me to do this. What are you trying to protect me from? Are you afraid that I'm going to fake it? Are you afraid that I am going to play pretend and that this will be like what I went through before?" etc.

It may be your parents' voice in your head trying to enforce the rules you used to need to belong to them. Don't argue with it; find out what rules it is enforcing, and then try to come up with evidence that will convince it that the new voice is more believable.

If you sense that you just don't have what you need yet to do a step, stop pushing yourself to have a voice that "sounds good." If you can at least observe yourself, you can ask for help to borrow what you need from someone else (real or imagined). Try the action or words and see how they are received.

What to do if the subconscious doesn't answer

It may be that there is no answer, or that the questions you are asking are not the right ones. Try different ones. If you prefer to stop, do that. If there is a part that is concerned about trusting your subconscious, find out what it needs. You might ask, for example, "What do you need to know before you will trust me?" You may find that the answer is extremely un-mystical, e.g., "You have to set a boundary." Or, "You have to promise to do ____." Or, "You can't keep leaving us." Or, "You have to stop doing intellectual substitutions for actually doing something."

What to do if your inner child won't show up

If you can tell that there is a guard, talk to it. If not, work with whatever you can sense. Even if it is just a feeling or a preference, you can say, "OK, you don't want to come out, that's fine. But can you at least tell me what you want?"

If you can't sense a child-state, then treat the session as an identity building session or do some reparenting .

What to do if you get anxious

First decide whether the anxiety is something you can and want to deal with while you are experiencing it, or whether you need to discontinue contact until you have less anxiety. If the anxiety is too strong and you need to deal with it right away, work on calming your body . If you can deal with it, talk to the little one or to the protector. "OK, you're anxious, I'm here. I'm not going anywhere. If you're a protector, I hear your message. Now, are there things you can do to help that don't involve yelling at the top of your lungs? Is there something you need me to do? If not, is there anything you can do to calm down?" See also the section on anxiety in Appendix D .

If you experience the kind of anxiety that causes physical symptoms such as chest pains or fainting, thoughts of suicide, symptoms similar to psychosis or mania, severe dissociation, or an inability to sleep, or if you feel as if you might hurt someone, please seek professional help.

What to do if you dissociate or feel unreal

Open your eyes, move your body, touch something, name five things that you can see, etc. Discontinue the practice for that day.

What to do if you become grandiose

Eat some food, get some sleep, do some household chores, or do whatever else it takes to bring you back to earth. You might want to remind yourself that:

"I don't need to do anything but eat, sleep, be humble, and get feedback."

What to do if you get too analytical

If you're acting like this is some kind of technical thing that you have to get right, remind yourself of what it's really about. Put your hands on your heart and solar plexus and say,

"This isn't about doing this perfectly; it's about connecting with my subconscious mind. I want to be in relationship with my subconscious mind, not just do exercises." Then ask, "What can I do to make this more about communion and less about performance?" Then take whatever answer comes. It may be to do nothing, or to get some rest, or to do something.

What to do if you get too blissful or diffuse

Ask the bliss to become more specific. Ask it what it wants you to do.

What to do if you feel that there must be something more to know

Remind yourself not to try to pry, and to remember that the subconscious will give you the content when you have the capacity.

What to do if you daydream

Sit up, open your eyes, and ask yourself what you need to do in real life that you are fantasizing about doing.

What to do if you get stuck in spiritual bypass

Remind yourself that there's somewhere that divine love hasn't penetrated yet.

What to do if you feel that spiritual practice is not working

If you've had some genuine spiritual openings, it may be that it isn't working because your inner child is not ready to accept the love and freedom that you have glimpsed. You may feel abandoned and/or condemned by God after prayer, or hurt by the betrayal of a spiritual teacher or by religious leaders in general. It may or may not be helpful to talk to this part; but remember that you may not solve the problem by doing more spiritual practice.

It may be that you need some basic reparenting . It may be that you don't yet have any place in your life that feels meaningful enough to give you enough happiness to explore more deeply. It may be that you just need to be curious about it, even if you can't yet believe that it could be true. It may be that other people already have enough meaning and love, and that you don't need any more. It may be that you don't need any more spirituality.

What to do if you get stuck in processing

It may be that there is something real to process, but that there is also a part that keeps checking to see if it's all gone yet. You may want to find out if it's going to be helpful for you to process it again or if you are just checking. Note that in grief and in practice you may repeatedly cover the same ground without it being "checking."

If you conclude that you are checking, remind yourself that there's nothing to do with the thought but leave it alone, and go back to what you chose to be doing. You don't have to hold a family meeting every time a thought comes along; and you don't have to do any ritual to banish it before you live your life.

What to do if you feel like you're not doing anything

Remind yourself to ask the Leader what you need to do.

What to do if you do hypnosis but don't change your life

Ask yourself what you need to do to convince your inner child that you are real. Then do it.
