I’ve also had self-hypnosis seem to extend a short chrunga trip far beyond its normal duration. Subjectively, it felt as though the state might have continued indefinitely if ordinary life hadn’t required communication and interruption. I find that phenomenologically important. I’m not recommending that anyone prolong an altered state forever; integration is healthier than trying to live inside the extension.

Without drugs, self-hypnosis or meditation may also become much more effective inside a lucid dream. I explain that possibility in my lucid-dreaming guide.
