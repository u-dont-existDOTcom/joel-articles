Appendix C: The extended de-hypnosis checklist

Use this list for any person, ad, ideology, spiritual teaching, romantic attraction, or voice in your head…

If you can’t stand the test… there’s something you don’t want you to notice.

Questions to ask yourself before acting on anything…

Is there any sense of urgency in the delivery?

Is there any sense of secrecy in the delivery?

Is there any shame or fear in the delivery?

Does it tell you that you’re wrong or bad or not enough and only they/it can help you fix it?

Does it tell you that you’re special or chosen and only they/it understands you?

Is there any sense of sexual charge in the delivery?

Is there a sense of spiritual superiority in the delivery?

Does it want anything from you (money, sex, loyalty, silence, etc)?

Does it pressure you for anything and make you feel like you don’t have other choices?

Is your questioning or the questioning of others described as resistance?

Is your resistance to something described as a problem that needs to be overcome?

Is the delivery down-grading ordinary life or your ability to succeed in it on your own?

Is the delivery targeting your child self (enticing, scaring, rescuing, etc)?

Does it let your adult self make the final decision?

If you answer yes to several of these questions, it’s time to slow down, not go deeper into trance, and re-gain contact with your body, your food, your rest, your time, and others who can help you figure out what’s going on.

A good guide will increase your adult self. A bad guide will replace your adult self with something else. A good guide will respect your “no.” A bad guide will describe your “no” as resistance, ego, fear, low vibrational energy, past life or childhood trauma, sin, etc, and say that what you need is more of their guidance to help you get over your “no.”

For hypnosis specifically, you might want to add:

Are the induction mechanics openly communicated to you ahead of time?

Is there at least one way to answer each question that will bring you to full alertness, cause the hypnosis to be modified, or cause the hypnosis to be stopped entirely?

Does the delivery indicate that not noticing is a good thing and something you shouldn’t tell anyone else?

Does the induction use concepts like role, ownership, obedience, age, identity, belonging, etc, to get you to step out of your adult self?

Are the effects of fractionation, cues, etc, on your later behavior and memory openly communicated to you ahead of time?

Does the induction try to make you doubt or discount your body’s experience by imposing concepts on it?

Does the exit restore you to full use of your ordinary identity and critical faculties and let you know that the session is over?

If you can’t answer yes to all of these, bring yourself to full alertness and ask to see the script or for the hypnosis to be modified.
