Introduction

Hello Lovey Bunny!

You are born with a full mind, a dreaming mind, protector, analyst, imitator, lover, remember, hider, revealor, healer etc…

You have never been taught how to live with all of those “layers” of yourself, and you were socialized with how to push, supress your emotions, pretend to be confident, have a good relationship with authority (or not), and so on and so forth. A lot of deep programs that all levels of your mind are running to help you navigate in the world, from your inner child to your inner guard, from your body to your intuition, from your fantasy to your “how to go throught my day manager”.

While many hypnosis teaching is about how to order your “subordinate” subconscious mind, I find much more interesting and rewarding to learn how to communicate well with all of them.

What we will do in that self hypnosis is prepare the ground and get the attention of all of them: your conscious mind, your subconscious mind, your body, your inner child, your inner protectors, your “adult”, your Higher Self, to have a meeting in your mind.

The method is pretty simple: we will learn to listen, warm them up, make them feel safe, ask for what we want clearly, test if it works in reality and act accordingly.

You don’t even need to go in some dramatic trance state, the only way to know if your new relationship with your whole mind is better is to test it in your life!

Let’s stop your conscious mind to talk like a crazy on the rooftop of your head and your subconscious mind to talk to you with “mysterious” signs like symptoms, dreams, crave, daydream, and other weird signals your body is throwing at you. Let’s make them talk like two adult human being! That’s communion!

I will make a lot of reference to your subconscious mind as if it was a rich ecology of “things” inside you, talk about Inner Child Reparenting, start from your Higher Self or the universal Love/Heart to get coherence in your mind, but also separate parts of you when needed, use prompt engineering and teach you how to be literate about influence as lots of things are hypnotizing you.
