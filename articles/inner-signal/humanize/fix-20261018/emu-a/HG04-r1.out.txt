Quick start: do this first

How does this work? In short: you gather your attention, you notice something, you do one thing in response, you wait, and you come all the way back out again. It’s a lot like playing or falling in love, or becoming curious, or gaining confidence. Nothing dramatic needs to happen.

Before you do your first session, read through the induction and full return while you’re awake. Find a time and a place where you will be physically secure for this time. Decide on one purpose for this session. Notice what you’re leaving alone for today. Put the screen aside and do the work.

As you do the induction, something will happen that you can notice: a sensation, an image, a phrase, a feeling, a “no way!” or a silence. Follow this guide for the next thing to do. This will vary based on what you need: warmth, a boundary, direction, time, and more. At the end of the session, come all the way back out, as described in this guide. Then write down what happened, and notice what you need to do in the “real world” to make this work.

If at any time you feel panicky, disoriented, unreal, or unable to function, the thing to do is come back out. This is not the same as noticing a bad feeling that you’ve chosen to experience, and being able to be with that feeling.

Do you think in words?

Before you pick a script, notice whether you have an inner voice. Do you notice words or phrases in your head, not spoken out loud, when you are thinking to yourself? All the time? Sometimes? Rarely? Almost never? (Note that the words do not have to be spoken aloud in your head. Just notice whether you use words when you think.) Does this vary depending on what kind of thinking you’re doing? Can you choose to think in words silently to yourself, even if you don’t usually do this?

This is different from noticing that in a particular situation, you notice feelings first and words later. It’s ok to say that you’re not sure; the point of these questions is to help you pick a way of doing this that will work for you, not to diagnose you.

If you don’t think in words, please read “say” and “ask” and “listen” in this guide as suggestions, not commands. You may want to say things out loud, or write them down, or use a recording, or just do the suggestion without wording it first. Also, do not assume that the child or protector needs to say things back to you in complete sentences. Nor does it mean that you will necessarily get an image instead; try things out and see what works.
