APPENDIX H: DEMONS, JINNS, OR OTHER HOSTILE PRESENCES (AKA “UNATTACHED BURDENS”)

I’m going to talk from experience here.

Sometimes people, whether they’re self-hypnotizing, meditating, lucid dreaming, loveyhuasching, grieving, praying, doing other plant medicines-adjacent stuff, or engaging in some other altered state, will come across something that seems to be some kind of demon, jinn, parasitic energy, dark intelligence, or other terrifying archetype.

I’m not going to get into the metaphysics of whether this is literally a demon, a mind formed symbol, a result of trauma, a burden you’ve picked up from another person or your ancestors, or some other kind of energy. It doesn’t really matter. What matters is what you do about it that’s going to help you feel better.

What do I do if this happens?

First thing to do when you experience a demon, jinn, or other kind of hostile or intrusive presence is to do all of the normal things you would do when you encounter any part: check yourself for medical issues, check your environment, check your body, make sure you’re not overstimulated, and make sure your mind isn’t too open. That means opening your eyes, feeling your feet, being aware of your surroundings, reducing stimulation, getting away from any people who may be contributing to the hostile energy, and stopping what you’re doing to open your mind more. Don’t do anything to increase the depth of the experience, don’t look for more to the experience, and don’t worry about whether or not it’s real or not real. Tell yourself:

“Right now, the only thing I want to deal with is something I can integrate. I’m bringing my adult self into this experience and I’m going to make sure my body is as safe as it can be.”

If you feel you’re in danger of hurting yourself or others, if you feel you’re going psychotic and afraid, if you feel you won’t be able to sleep after this, if you feel extremely paranoid, if you have chest pain, if you feel you’re overheating too much, if you feel you’re seizing, if you think you’ve taken too much of something, or if you can’t function, end the self-hypnosis session and get help in the real world.

Otherwise, check in with yourself and see what’s going to help you without worrying about the metaphysics of the situation. You may decide to put your hand on your heart and say:

“I don’t have to worry about what this is right now. Right now I’m going to do what’s going to help me feel better.”

If that leaves you feeling vulnerable, you can go ahead and bring in your Nurturer. If not, or if doing that makes things more hostile or scary, stop trying to work with the experience.

Either way, remember that you don’t have to know where it’s from to work with it, and you don’t have to know what your protective parts are afraid of right away, and you don’t have to know if your thought about what’s going on is right or wrong to work with it in a way that’s going to help you. Either way, you’re going to work with it in the way that’s going to help you feel better.

Second thing to do is don’t fight it out of fear. This is usually the first impulse people have when they experience these entities. They want to fight them, banish them, hate them, prove they’re better or stronger than them, argue with them, freak out, or become them. Some people may want to do traditional forms of dealing with these entities, such as prayers, mantras, protective rituals, banishing rituals, or exorcism.

Fear, hatred, pride, panic etc are all feeding it attention and giving it a platform.

Silently say to it “I do not need to hate you or merge with you or do what you say or fight you out of fear, I will return to loving awareness.”

If you need to set a body boundary, do so with compassion, like closing a door.

If it is acting very hostile or cold or alien or parasitic or boundary violating don’t try a soft sentimental approach, but rather one with a boundary.

Sit up straight, feel your feet, straighten your spine, pull your shoulder blades back a little, feel the boundary of your torso skin etc.

Then say something like “While I recognise that there is suffering here, and I want suffering to go home, the boundary of my skin is an adult thing and you are not allowed to come inside my house.”

Then say “Compassion, door closed.”

If you think you might be going psychotic or destabilised…

Even if you’re just getting some stuff symbolically about hostile presences etc which settles down with some grounding and loving awareness, it’s very different to someone who’s going persistently and paranoid, not sleeping, not functioning, a danger to themselves or others, hearing voices, going manic, not communicating well, not able to reality test etc. If that’s the case get some help, it’s not something to try and deal with via hypnosis.

The simple rule as to whether or not you’re destabilising or not, is that if what you’re doing is making you more loving, grounded, relational, honest, functional etc then it’s probably ok to carry on with, if on the other hand it’s making you more paranoid, sleepless, grandiose, not relational, fearful, not real etc then don’t carry on with it and get some help.

If you feel like you’re getting downloads from space or whatever…

Sometimes when people are dealing with hostile presences or shadowy forces or grandiose parts etc and odd imagery in different states of consciousness, rather than attack them or frighten them, they will try to give them some grandiose ‘download from the cosmos’ or something to make them feel important or give them a mission or whatever to get them off the path. To deal with this one can say something like…

“Unless whatever you have to say to me can help me to be more loving when I wash up or clean my room or whatever next morning, then it’s of no use to me on my path.”

In other words, is the guidance making you more beautiful, honest, relational, humble, useful etc in your everyday life? If not, then it’s probably of no use to you.

Dealing with ‘demons’ or ‘jinn’ or ‘hostile presences’ or ‘Unattached Burdens’ etc all come down to pretty much the same thing, learning to deal with it via love and making yourself less hooky. I’m not encouraging people to become entity hobbyists…
