Come all the way back

Come out the way you came in – so if you floated up – float back down – and land in a weight you are comfortable with. If you made yourself smaller – make yourself bigger again – if you called up a younger version of yourself or parts of yourself – let them know you are now whole and in your body and using your adult judgement etc. If you traveled somewhere – travel back – make sure you don’t bring anything back that you didn’t mean to.
