Note: If you are using a recording and it stops for any reason, simply say to yourself that the recording has stopped and the session is over and use the above method of coming out: open your eyes, say where you are, say what day it is, push down with your feet or your hands, move about, look around, etc. and then use the 5 step method. Don’t just sit there waiting for the voice to come back to release you.

It is all right to let the recording put you to sleep or have you in a quiet resting state if you are using it to go to sleep. However, if it’s a recording for using when you are awake, it should not leave you in a role, with lowered judgement, impaired movement capabilities, blank, with an undefined reset word, invited to stay altered indefinitely, etc.
