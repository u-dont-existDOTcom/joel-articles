Closure: When you notice that something is happening for you (a sensation, image, phrase, feeling, objection, or even a deepening silence), gently respond to the next thing that is offered to you (this may be a need to offer yourself warmth, to establish a boundary, to follow a direction, to simply listen for a while longer, etc) until you feel the experience move to completion and you can return fully to yourself and this room together. If you feel moved to note something or to take action in the real world in response to your experience, please do so now.

*NOTE: If at any point you feel yourself becoming panicky, disoriented, unreal, or unable to function, move to closure. This is distinct from choosing to engage deeply with a difficult feeling while remaining present.
