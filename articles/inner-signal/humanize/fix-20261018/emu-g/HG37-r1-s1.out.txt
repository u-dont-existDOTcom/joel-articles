I’m writing this appendix from a place of experience.

Sometimes, in self-hypnosis, meditation, lucid dreaming, loveyhuasca, periods of grief, prayer, or other altered states of consciousness, people encounter something that isn’t just an emotion, or a young part. It may seem like a demon, jinn, parasitic energy, dark intelligence, terrifying archetypal being, or come as a voice or image of some sort. Whatever it is, it’s unwanted, intrusive, and seemingly full of hate, or seduction, or mockery, or just feels different.

Do I need to provide a guide for what these beings might be? Maybe they are literal beings. Maybe they are a symbolic form the mind creates to hold difficult feelings, past trauma, dissociated energy, or things picked up from parents, partners, or ancestors. It doesn’t matter. The question is:

What is the best way to respond to them, in order to feel safe, clear, loving, grounded, and un-hooked?
