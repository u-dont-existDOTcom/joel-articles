Client is king so you need to pick one purpose for the induction, one starting place, anchor or image, and one way to get back. Then you can bring in any of the other ingredients you want, but in a conscious way. If you feel the need to switch metaphors, do so consciously. You may want to bring in some feelings of warmth, or of setting boundaries, or of direction, or of being a neutral witness. You may want to use floating, stairs, gardens, going to an inner room, colored lights, talking to your inner child. It doesn't all have to be used.

You don't even need to use imagery. You can use any sense, rhythm, body movement, words, spatial sense, intuitive knowledge, or even no response at all to create the signal. Just be sure to use your chosen world when counting and when coming out of the induction. You can borrow qualities from other worlds, just be sure you don't borrow them into your own identity.

Start simple. Choose one verb, one sensory quality, one short refrain, and one way of transitioning. Read your script out loud - it's easy to get carried away writing long, elegant sentences that turn into soup when you start the induction.
