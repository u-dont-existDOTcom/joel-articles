Try for a moment to let yourself feel that you are not weighting down upon your support at all, then try feeling as though you were weighting just a tiny bit- only one percent- of the effort onto the support, so that you are almost floating above it. Then try feeling your normal amount of weighting down and pressure upon it. Notice if there are any differences that you feel. (You can try to fractionate this by switching your attention back and forth between the normal weighting and the supported weighting, perhaps on in breaths and out breaths. If you are going to do that, be aware that you are doing it before you begin.) Then you can return back to normal weighting, firmly touching your support, as you choose.
