What’s being claimed here? What’s the inevitable choice being framed for me here? What’s being presupposed? Who benefits from framing it this way? What’s the emotionally charged bit? What’s not being said? What do I need to say in order to opt out of the frame, and is it safe for me to do so (will I suffer negative consequences – physical, social, financial, reputational, spiritual etc – if I say no)?

The above is a rough guide to help bring more of the ‘adult’ into the conversation. Use more of the above when the state is more powerful.
