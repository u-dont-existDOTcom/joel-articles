A quick exercise for when you have several concerns going on. Visualise each one as a strand of yarn, or even better, have some by you to help. When you've placed them all, take a minute to look at them all (without changing them, though you could label them if needed, by saying a word or two). How many did you end up with? Now pick one that is important to do something about, put the others somewhere you can see them, and come back to this page.
