What is Pacing and Leading?

“Even though the anxiety is here, and the activation in the body is here, and even though that protector is doing its’ best, one breath at a time the adult in me is here to listen.”

Pacing and Leading is as simple as:

“It is completely true that (insert current sensation or emotion), and it is completely true that (insert current behavior or thought as a protective response), and at the same time the adult in me is here, breathing in this room, and ready to listen.”
