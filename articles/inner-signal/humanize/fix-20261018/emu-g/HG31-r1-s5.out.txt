What’s the purpose of Awareness Verbs? To help with awareness, obviously! But how? The easiest way to explain is probably through an example. So, let’s say that after a question, instead of saying ‘Take a moment to think about it’, instead, using one of the Awareness Verbs from the list at the bottom, perhaps it could be ‘Take a moment to notice the answer’. Or even, ‘notice what form the answer takes’. What difference does it make?

Here is a list of Awareness verbs: Notice Realize Recognise Sense Feel Wonder Imagine Picture Listen Allow Discover Remember Consider
