Starting Point: Lie on the floor and/or place yourself in a position where you can feel support under one hand or foot. Gently press and/or stretch and then release the area. Note any sensations, or just imagine the press/stretch/release. Repeat with a maximum of three areas. Use the word ‘release’ to guide you. Now move or not – whichever feels most alive. See if you can sense what’s emerging; an area, a voice within. Try to find a way of supporting what wants your attention. When ready, restore movement and return.
