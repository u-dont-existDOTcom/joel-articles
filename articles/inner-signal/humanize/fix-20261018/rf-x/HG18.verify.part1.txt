Below is one section of a long self-hypnosis guide by Joel, published on his Substack. It teaches gathering attention, listening to the subconscious as an ecology of parts (the inner child, protectors or "guards", the body, intuition), three adult functions he calls the Nurturer, the Protector and the Leader/Guru, Higher Self, "borrowed adulthood", inductions and a complete return, influence literacy ("de-hypnosis": noticing and resisting other people's influence), and safety limits (when to stop, when to get help). Its quoted lines are scripts the reader says or thinks.

PUBLISHED section:
[B1] Reverse whatever you used to enter. If you floated, regain chosen weight. If you became small, return to ordinary scale. If you met a younger state or several parts, confirm present wholeness, body ownership, and adult judgment. If you traveled, come back by the same route. Keep only the qualities you deliberately chose.

[B2] “I bring back only what is useful now. The rest can remain safely held until there is more capacity. I return to the room, the body, the day, and one honest action. All instructions to deepen or remain in this session are finished. I keep only the ordinary-life intention or future-practice cue I consciously chose; neither keeps today’s altered state running. I am myself, in my body, with adult judgment fully available.”

[B3] One — feel the body’s contact and chosen weight. Two — let the breath return to its ordinary rhythm. Three — hear the room and name where you are. Four — move fingers, toes, shoulders, and eyes. Five — eyes open, present, ordinary, clear. The session has ended.

[B4] Name the day or date. Look left, right, near, and far. Move before standing, and stand only when steady. Check that you are actually oriented and alert; repeating the count is not proof of a complete return. Note only what actually appeared, and any action it calls for. Then do something normal.

[B5] If a recording stops unexpectedly: say, “The recording stopped and the session is over.” Open your eyes, name the location and day, press your feet or hands into a surface, move, look around, and use the five-step count. Don’t wait for the missing narrator to release you.

[B6] Sleep is separate. A sleep recording may end in sleep or quiet rest. A waking recording shouldn’t end by preserving a role, reduced judgment, impaired movement, blankness, an undefined reset word, or an invitation to remain altered indefinitely.

REWRITE:
[CHANGED, replaces B1]
Exiting a session is always done in reverse to the way you entered it. If you floated or used another method to go down, do the same to get back down and back into your body, weighting yourself as you chose to do when you entered. If you shrunk yourself, grow back to your normal size. If you went to a younger you or other parts of yourself, take a moment to acknowledge that you are now whole, this is your body and that you have the judgement of an adult. If you traveled, return in the same way. Be sure you are only bringing back what you need to bring back.

[CHANGED, replaces B2]
It is my intent to bring back with me only those things that I want at the present time. All other things will be put back in their proper place until I am ready to use them. I am now returning to my normal day to day existence with the intention of taking one honest action. All instructions to go deeper and all instructions to continue this session are now over. My only instructions are the ones needed for my ordinary life and/or my next practice.

[CHANGED, replaces B3]
When you are ready, you can count yourself awake, 1, as you come back into your body and you take on the weight that you choose to have, 2, as you come back to your normal breathing pattern, 3, as you become aware of the sounds in the room around you and you know where you are, 4, as you start to move your fingers and toes, your shoulders and eyes and 5, as you open your eyes, feeling totally present in your body, totally normal and clear headed. The session is over.

[CHANGED, replaces B4]
Reminders It may be helpful for you to say something to yourself like, 'It's (day of the week)' or 'Today's date is...' Once your vision is clear, look to your left, then to your right. Look at something close and something far away. Move around a bit before standing up. Don't stand up until you feel that you can do so safely. Make sure you are oriented and alert. Just because you were able to count doesn't mean you are totally alert. Mentally note what, if anything, came up for you in this session and if there is anything you need to do, if not, go about whatever it is you normally do at this time.

[CHANGED, replaces B5]
A note about coming out of a recording: If the recording suddenly stops, it is best that you immediately say to yourself that the recording has stopped and that the session is over and use the above methods to come out: open your eyes, say where you are, say what day it is, push down with your feet or your hands, move about, look around and then do the 5 step method. Don’t just sit there expecting the voice to come back and release you from the session.

[CHANGED, replaces B6]
NOTE: If you are using a recording to take you to sleep, then it is all right to let it put you to sleep or leave you in a quiet, resting state. However, any recording that is meant to be used while you are awake should NOT leave you in a role, with lowered judgement, impaired movement capabilities, blank, with an undefined reset word, invited to stay in an altered state indefinitely, etc.
