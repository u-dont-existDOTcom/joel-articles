[B41] “The body already knows something about resting, doesn’t it?”

[B42] Pattern 9: Nominalizations

[B43] Nominalizations turn processes into nouns: healing, safety, trust, integration, communion, clarity, rest. Their vagueness can sometimes help because the subconscious supplies its own concrete meaning.

[B44] “A little more safety can arrive in whatever way the body understands.”

[B45] Pattern 10: Meta Model questions — the de-hypnosis tool

[B46] The Milton Model opens possibility. The Meta Model asks exactly what somebody means. Ethical practice needs both.

[B47] “How specifically?” “According to whom?” “Compared to what?” “Always?” “Never?” “What exactly would happen?” “How do I know?” “What evidence would change my mind?”

[B48] Use Milton Model to soften into the body. Use Meta Model to return to truth.

[B49] Sensory, state, and strategy tools

[B50] Submodalities — editing the form, not denying the truth

[B51] Fear, craving, fantasy, belief, and memory may have sensory qualities: near or far, bright or dim, loud or quiet, moving or still, heavy or light, inside or outside the body. Work with qualities you actually notice. If none are apparent, skip this tool rather than inventing an image or voice.

[B52] Ask how the experience is represented. What changes when the image moves two feet back, the voice gets quieter, or the edge becomes less sharp?

[B53] “I am not deleting the signal. I am adjusting the volume so I can listen without being overwhelmed.”

[B54] Before moving one imaginary pixel, ask the guard whether that change is acceptable.

[B55] “Would it be okay if we moved this image back just a little, or lowered the volume slightly, purely so my adult self can stay present enough to hear what you need to tell me?”

[B56] Anchoring — giving the body a doorway back

[B57] An anchor is a cue connected to a state: a hand on the heart, two fingers touching, a breath, a word, a posture, scent, or song. Find a real state you want easier access to, feel it, and pair the cue while it is clearly present with the phrase, “This is available.” For hypnotic re-entry, choose a usable receptive state rather than peak emotional intensity; follow “A shortcut back into hypnosis” in the main induction section. Keep alert calming and the synchronicity wake-up cue separate.

[B58] Using synchronicity as your own wake-up bell

[B59] People notice a synchronicity and immediately ask what it means. Sometimes that opens insight; sometimes it starts compulsive pattern-hunting. I prefer using the moment of noticing as a wake-up bell. The coincidence doesn’t have to contain a message. It has already captured attention, which is enough.

[B60] The cue I give myself is:

[B61] “Whenever I notice something that feels like a synchronicity, I wake up inside the state I am in. I feel my body and the room. I remember that the coincidence is not proof of an interpretation. I let attention widen beyond the story my current mood is telling. I ask what is fact, what is interpretation, what needs warmth, what needs protection, and what needs action. I return to the highest perspective I can access without leaving the body, the facts, or ordinary human reality.”

[B62] I make room for whatever is genuinely available: objectivity, clarity, ease, peace, freedom, joy, and gratitude. I do not have to manufacture any of them.

[B63] Synchronicity noticed → pause → orient → widen → reality-test → listen → choose one loving action.

[B64] The cue interrupts identification without erasing the state’s message. A negative state may still contain an inner-child need, a Protector warning, grief, a bodily problem, or a real demand for action.

[B65] Installing the synchronicity cue

[B66] Imagine noticing an ordinary, harmless synchronicity.

[CHANGED, replaces B59-B64]

From this place of clarity and peace I am free to choose what to do next and usually I can do at least one thing that comes from a loving place. I make room for whatever is genuinely there, like clarity, peace, freedom, joy and gratitude, without having to manufacture any of it.

In short it is my cue to: Stop-orient-go wider-reality check-listen-do at least one thing from a loving place.
