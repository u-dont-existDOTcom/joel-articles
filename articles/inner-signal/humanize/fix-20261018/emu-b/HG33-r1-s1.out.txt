Remember that the anxiety is coming from a part of yourself that is a Protector who believes they are trying to save yourself from something bad happening. Maybe they are trying to protect yourself from danger, shame, abandonment, failure, exposure, illness, rejection, losing control, or something that the protector believes will be unbearable for yourself. Rather than getting into a dialogue where it feels as though yourself is interrogating the protector, start with yourself by taking care of the body.

Always work with yourself by taking care of the body first, then the inner child, then doing some inner inquiry work, and then taking some kind of action.
