I will re-inhabit my body: first my feet, and my chair, and my breath, and the room around me.

When this is done, if there is a little person, I will reassure him: "It's okay, you're safe now, I'm here. Thank you, protector, for alerting me; you don't have to scream, I can hear you."

Then I will see if there is anything I need to do. If there is, I'll just pick the first little thing to do, and do that. If there's nothing I need to do, I'll see if I can help my body stand down.

Either way, I don't abandon the signal, or worship it -- I'll do something.
