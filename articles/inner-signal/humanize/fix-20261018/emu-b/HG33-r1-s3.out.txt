NOTE: There are some good things to be said for slowing down the breathing, especially for overbreathers and those subject to panic. Some of this is based on the Buteyko approach. However, it should not be forced, and creating air hunger can increase anxiety for a short period. Take things slowly.

Try breathing through the nose. Try small inhales. Try quiet exhales. Try pausing if it is comfortable. See if it is possible to quiet the breathing. STOP if this creates dizziness, strain, a sense of panic or any other medical concerns.
