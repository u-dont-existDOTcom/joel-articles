The companion Inner Child Self-Love Reparenting Guide gives the fuller treatment of borrowed adulthood, love without compulsory trust, practical and relational repair, spiritual struggle, differentiation, and mutual peer support. The corresponding practices here follow that account while retaining the self-hypnosis entry, responsive language, and complete return.

Healing the Divided Self, pp.148–153 and 171–174, discusses renurturing, somatic work, and developing nurturing capacities. Levine and Maggie Kline’s Trauma Through a Child’s Eyes, especially chapters 4–5 and 10, discusses attunement and individual responses in actual children and caregiving. Applying those ideas to an adult’s younger feeling-states is an adaptation. My three-adult arrangement and borrowed-adulthood sequence are not presented as those authors’ tested protocol.
