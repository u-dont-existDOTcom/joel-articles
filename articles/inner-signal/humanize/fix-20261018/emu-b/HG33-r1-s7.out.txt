Sometimes the mind is too activated to let meditation to take care of it. It may be useful to start from the body instead. Consider massage, either self or given by someone else, as the first resort. Maybe massage the top of one foot with the other, or the scalp, or a temple, or the back of the neck, or the top of a shoulder, or whatever feels good to touch. (Avoid the front of the neck. Avoid injured, numb, bruised, inflamed, recently surgically-operated-on areas, and varicose veins. Stop if it makes the anxiety symptoms or anything else feel bad, whether immediately or later, such as painful, dizzy, tingly, etc.) Other body approaches that may help include heat, pressure, weighted blanket, darkness, favorite calming music, massage gun, etc.
