In my guides when I refer to your ‘subconscious’ I am referring to everything going on beneath the surface of your conscious mind – perceptions, emotions, desires, aversions, imagery, memories, body signals, habits, intuitions, automatic behaviours etc. Sometimes it is useful to work with all of these as one ‘mind’, other times it can be more effective to work with the ‘ecology’ of parts. Neither way is ‘right’ or ‘wrong’ it is just a question of using the right map for the job in hand.

Working with parts is about precision, working with the Higher Self is about coherence – you need to move in and out of communion with both.

To work with the ‘whole’ – the subconscious as one:

“Invoke” the subconscious – e.g. ‘Hello wise subconscious can you help me understand…..’

To work with ‘parts’ recognise the ‘ecology’ in the subconscious – there may be a child part that wants love, a protector part that wants to keep you safe, a ‘critic’ that wants you to live up to certain standards, a fantasy making part, body memory parts, a social part, a spiritual part that wants to connect to God/source/do what is in the best benefit of all beings, a ‘wiser’ part etc etc

Generally it is a good idea to start with unity:

“Invoke” your Higher Self – e.g. ‘Hello my Higher Self/universal heart-mind/wise subconscious please can you help me…..’

Then move into differentiation if needed:

Ask “Which part is afraid? Which part is protecting? Which part is avoiding? Which part knows what to do? Which part is too young for this?”

Then move back into unity – recognising that it is all part of the same life and ideally all parts should be led by the ‘adult’ self operating from a place of love.
