For some people, it is better not to start by asking for the 12 parts, or by giving everybody an office. For these people, it is better to start from the Sacred whole.

In order to do this, I start with a prayer: ‘Let’s begin by connecting to the universal heart (or to my Higher Self). The universal heart (or the Higher Self) is the part of me that existed before I was born and that will exist after I am gone. It is the part of me that is not wounded, the part of me that is not caught in any roles, or families or groups. The part of me that has no identities, no ideologies, and no stories. From this place, let’s ask for what is true, kind, timely, stabilizing and useful.’

Sometimes, people don’t have access to their Higher Self, or to the universal heart, or whatever language you might use that feels sacred for you. In that case, I’ll just begin by asking them to start witnessing, to notice things around them, or in their body, or to notice that they are in a younger state. The fact that they can notice is usually enough of an adult function to begin with.

Once people can notice, and distinguish, there’s no point in working more on their Witness. Begin working on whatever else they need: warmth, protection, direction etc. So while the language of the Higher Self is useful in order to bring people together, it doesn’t mean that all the adult functions are already there. You’ll need to work on growing them.
