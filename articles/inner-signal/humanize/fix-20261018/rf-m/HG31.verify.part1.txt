[B79] Dishonest reframing declares that the problem was secretly good all along. I prefer questions that widen meaning without lying:

[B80] “What else might be true?” “What is this part trying to do for me?” “What would the Nurturer, the Protector, and the Leader each say about this?”

These reframe statements are dishonest because they make the problem into something that is good. Instead ask yourself: What else might be true? or What else could this be about? or How might this part reframe this? or How might my Nurturer reframe this? or How might my Protector reframe this? or How might my Leader reframe this? etc., etc.
