Appendix C: The extended de-hypnosis checklist

Here’s a short checklist to remind yourself, when something or somebody is pressing too hard on you. It could be anything; a person, an ad, an ideology, a spiritual teaching, a romantic pull, a voice, you name it. “Ok. So it seems like I’m being influenced. I don’t have to obey, but I also don’t have to attack. Let me just slow down and inquire into this: What is being taken for granted here? Who benefits from this? What is my body telling me here? (but not believing everything it says) What would love, truth, humility, ordinary life, etc. do here?

If it’s true, it will stand inquiry. If it’s really love, it will stand boundaries. If it’s really guidance, it will still be there after you slept on it. If it’s really for you, it will not ask you to give up your adult self.

Before giving in to whatever it is, check, if the usual methods are used. Urgency. Secrecy. Shame. ‘You will be left out!’ ‘You are special!’ or similar. Sexual charge. Spiritual superiority. They want money or sex from you. They demand loyalty, silence or anything else from you. They demand you to obey.

They are trying to narrow down your choices. Inquiry is considered a form of betrayal. Your ‘resistance’ is a pathology. Ordinary life is made to seem small and unimportant.

Your child part is seduced, or scared, or otherwise rescued. You’re not allowed to say ‘no’ with your adult self.

If many of the above apply, then slow down. Don’t go deeper into trance. Don’t make irreversible decisions. Just being in your body in the room for a while, will help a lot. Then eat, sleep, give it time, get counsel, etc.

A good guide will help you to become more of your adult self. And a good guide can take your ‘no’. A bad guide cannot take your ‘no’, because a bad guide puts him- or herself in the place of your adult self. So your ‘no’ is seen as resistance, ego, fear, low vibration, trauma, sin, or whatever is the poison of the day, or proof that you need more guidance.
