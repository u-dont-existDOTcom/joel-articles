## Sober induction: 3–5 minutes
You can read this slowly to yourself on your recorder, you can read it out loud or say it to yourself if you are good at that. Choose a sound, or a contact, or the two hands thing (below) or something to anchor yourself and the thing you will say to open it up. Use the same anchor for everywhere that anchor is mentioned. Don't read the directions, just pause between paragraphs.
You can allow your eyes to rest on something in the room for a bit, and let what's already there come into view.
You can allow yourself to notice something you can feel, like the sensation of something under you, and notice the sensation of the contact, the pressure. Or maybe there is a sound, or something else to notice.
Allow yourself some time to do this.
You might notice yourself thinking, and whenever you notice yourself thinking, you can allow yourself to re-engage with your anchor and leave the thought unfinished.
You might notice something that you are doing that holds your interest, something about the sound, or sensation, or listening or something else.
Allow yourself some time to do this, as much time as you would for someone you care about who has started to talk and hasn't gotten to what she wants to say yet, so you are going to stick around till she gets it all out.
Repeat the phrase that you chose, if you want to. Say it as an invitation and notice how it's received.
You can close your eyes now if you want to. If not, it's fine to keep them open, or soften your gaze. The listening might go deeper while there's still a background sound you can hear.
Count 5 to 1, taking your time if you like.
5. Let your attention come back to your chosen anchor. 4. Let more of your attention come to what you've found. 3. Leave a little space. 2. See if you can listen to a space after the words without preparing an answer. 1. Take your time.
Bring to mind, if you want, the thing that you want to talk about, or just the wish to have inner contact. Just let it be there for now. You don't need to tell the whole story yet.
What do you notice now?
Let yourself notice what comes to you, if anything. It may not be clear yet what it is. It may be sensations, words, pictures, feelings, movements, or anything else.
Let yourself take your time to notice, with or without words.
## Already open? Contain before you deepen
This is for when you're already open for some reason (loveyhuasca, meditation, lucid dreaming, going through grief, listening to beautiful music, prayer, erotic energy, exhaustion, seeing something you love, etc) and want to aim and stabilize it.
Know that you're already open, you just need to aim and stabilize it, and keep it kind.
Orient yourself by looking around. Where are you. What's the date or day. How does your body feel? Where are you grounded? Can you feel your feet/hands/spine/etc? Make some contact with yourself, eg press your thumbs against the tips of your index fingers gently but firmly so that you can feel friction. Focus on that.
Say something like, "Only what I can integrate, please. If there's too much it can wait. My body may stay safe. I'll be here as an adult, this won't just be my inner child."
When you feel oriented, make an intention.
Something like, "Please show me the next truth/symbol/feeling/question/action that is useful for me, that I can integrate."
## Stay awake when your mind likes to escape
Say to yourself, "I'm not going anywhere, and I'm coming more fully here." Sit up, open your eyes, feel your feet, let your breathing be natural.
Look around you and choose something to look at. Feel your spine. Ask yourself why you're daydreaming. Is it to get guidance, or because you're working through something, or are you avoiding something?
Say something like, "If I'm meant to be getting guidance, please show me the action I need to take. If I'm avoiding something, please bring me back to now and show me what I'm avoiding."
If being still tends to make you sleepy, or diffuse your attention, or make you want to mentally escape, you can make the hypnosis more active. Instead of getting more relaxed with the hypnosis, try getting more alert. Keep your eyes open. As you move one of your hands, you're going to move deliberately, and as soon as you stop moving deliberately, you'll focus on what you've got. Don't escape inside yourself. Say something like,
“Starting with your eyes open press your thumb to your fore finger. And as you press your finger together gently and release, you begin to bring yourself into a place of clearer awareness… again you press your fingers together and release, bringing yourself into an even clearer state of awareness. And again as you press your fingers together and release you bring yourself into a clear state of awareness, and now you stop pressing your fingers together and in the first second after you stop pressing your fingers together deliberately, you can notice if there is any echo of the pressing in your fingers, in your breathing, in your attention or nowhere at all. Just notice, keep yourself up straight and work at this level.

## A shortcut back into hypnosis
Once you have learned the above induction and how to come back down at the end, you can do the following….
Ex: My dad used to use a mudra (like putting two fingers together like an "Ok" sign) when he needed to relax. He even did it when he was driving. Not that I recommend driving in a trance, don't do that! Make sure that your relaxation/ stay alert cue is not the same as your go inward to hypnosis one.
Have an unusual word, phrase, and/ or motion (optional) that you will use to go into hypnosis again. Say the word and/ or do the phrase while doing the motion (if applicable). Be sure to do this when you are in hypnosis and in a good place. Don't do this when you are in a very emotional state, child state, or thinking about something that could be considered trauma. The idea is for you to be able to pick a good state to go into, and you can decide what that is.
When you pick the time and place (it needs to be an appropriate time and place), say the word and/ or do the phrase to begin going into this state that you picked, and are familiar with. It's fine to change the direction you are going in, or stop this at any point.
Now come all the way back up again, and at a later time, when it is appropriate, start using this. Over time, you can shorten the length of the longer induction, or use the longer induction if this doesn't seem to be enough for your needs. There is no need to have to do this a certain number of times, go to a certain depth, or even have a certain sensation when you do this.
Also, be sure that this is working for you. Be able to turn it on and off at will. Make sure it isn't there when you don't want it to be. Be able to change directions or come back up when you are in it. Don't use it in a way that you are going to follow instructions that you are hearing from a narrator, notifications, or something that you overhear. Don't use this to go into hypnosis faster for things that you shouldn't be working on yet.
There is something you should never do while using this induction. And that's use it, practice it or test it while you're driving or doing something where you need to be actively paying attention. It's just not safe to be going into a trance where you are actively paying attention to what's going on inside of you when you need to be paying attention to what's going on outside of you. Even though your eyes can be open, even though you're using "if it would be useful for you to do so" type language it's just not safe.
If you want to turn this one off, simply don't practice it anymore. Take it out of your recordings, take it out of your apps if you're using it in an app, and start using your regular induction. Tell yourself, when you're awake, that you won't be using it anymore. Just because you say that it'll be gone doesn't mean that it'll be gone right away. If it's causing you a problem, if you're still responding to it when you don't want to respond to it, if you're having issues with disorientation, etc, get some help from someone who's qualified to help you with this. If, on the other hand, you want to keep it so that you can use it to go back into trance at a later date when you do want to go into a trance, that's fine too. For now, though, once you come out of this trance, it'll be over for today.
## Come all the way back
If you have done anything to alter your state when coming out of a trance, make sure to reverse those things. If you have floated, make sure to gain your weight again (whatever weight you want of course). If you have shrunk, make sure to grow back to your regular size. If you have met a younger state or are working with parts, make sure to come back to feeling complete in your body in the here and now, that your body belongs to you, and that it is under control of your adult mind. If you have gone somewhere, make sure to go back the same way you went there. Etcetera. Make sure to only take with you when you come out of a session the things that you want to!
Here is a generic bring-back that you can use: "When I come out of this session, I will only bring with me that which is useful to me in my everyday life right now. Anything else I will bring with me at a later date, when I am better able to integrate it. When I come out of this session, I will be coming back to the room I am in now, and to the day in which I am participating in this session now. All the instructions you have given me to carry me deeper into this session or keep me in this state are now finished, the only intention I will take with me into my everyday life or the only cue I will use to take me into this state at a later date will be the one I consciously choose to and neither of them will keep me in this state. When I come out of this session I will be me, in my body and with my adult judgment."
Then do a count up: On 1 start to feel your body again and what it's resting on and feel your weight (of your choice). On 2 start to feel your breathing return to normal. On 3 start to hear the room around you and name where you are. On 4 start to wiggle your fingers and toes, move your shoulders and your eyes. On 5 open your eyes, back in the here and now, feeling normal and clear and the session is now over.
When you come out make sure you can name what day (or date) it is, look to your left and right, look at something near to you and something further away. Move around a bit. Don't get up until you feel like you can do so without falling over. Make sure that you are oriented and alert (counting does this, but not always). Take mental note of only what actually came up for you and any actions you need to take regarding it. Then go on and do something normal.
If your recording cuts out for some reason, you can say to yourself, "The recording has stopped and the session is over," and then do the above on your own. Once your eyes are open, press your feet or hands into a surface as well.
Note: If you are going to sleep to a recording then it is fine to go to sleep or just remain resting quietly at the end of it, however if you are coming out of a recording then you should not leave yourself in an altered state, this includes leaving yourself in a role, with lowered judgment, with lowered ability to move, blank or with a non-specific reset word or for an indefinite length of time.
## The actual inner meeting
Note: If you have a tendency to day dream, dissociate or fall asleep, it is better to do this exercise sitting up and save lying down for when you want to go to sleep, get a very deep rest or incubate dreams. If you are sitting, keep your spine straight but not rigid like a soldier. Relax your jaw and stay somewhat connected to the room you are in.
“I am not leaving life; I am listening more deeply to life.
I invoke my Higher Self, universal heart-mind, wise subconscious mind, intelligence in my body, inner child(ren), inner adult(s), protector(s) and all that is of truth and love to help me know what I need to know in a form and at a level that will be useful, kind, stabilizing and real for me.”
Ask your conscious mind to listen.
This does not mean you have to stop thinking to listen. Just notice your thoughts, body sensations, pictures, doubts, emotions, etc. Don’t fight what comes up, but don’t go with it either. Just notice what stands out on its own as you hold the idea of what you are doing in the back of your mind.
Internally say, “Tell me what I need to know or ask me what you need to know and I’ll sit in silence for 5 minutes and listen.”
You may hear a phrase, feel a body sensation, notice a memory, see a color, be contacted by a child or protector, feel pain, warmth, complete silence, know that you need to do something, be grateful for something, feel you need to stop the listening or any number of things.
Listen to everything and believe nothing automatically.
## Let the next response guide you
Once you've read the induction and done the inner meeting, this will make more sense. Read through these, and then try doing it without looking at the screen.
Respond to each thing that comes up with an accurate notice and a small reasonable invitation (or act or taking more time etc). The 'thing' might be a sensation, image, gesture, feeling, words, or anything. The notice and invitation don't need to take all those forms, just notice and invite what happens.
## Finding your own words
An example notice might be, "I notice my chest tightening when I imagine asking for help". Don't add interpretation like "this must be the abandoned child part of me, or a protective mechanism" - just notice what you notice. An example invitation might be, "And as I notice that, I can take some time before deciding what it means, and notice if I want to say anything, or change it, or just stay like that".
Then just listen. You don't need fancy language - often it will be better to just ask what happens next, or whatever. If you notice a feeling developing, it might be best not to say anything while it's developing (if you can help it) but some days you need to talk to understand, so just do that.
You can be pretty straightforward when that's the help you chose - "Let the explanation wait a moment. What do I feel as I say that?" can be a perfectly valid thing to say to yourself, and help you focus. It's ok to just be with an unpleasant feeling, you don't have to wait till it's a nice one before you know you're ok. As long as you're still present, and able to change if you want to, and happy to carry on, a sad feeling or tension or whatever doesn't mean it's not ok.
It's worth trying to separate the notice, the meaning, and your thoughts on the meaning, but in practice it will often be pretty fuzzy. When you can, it can be helpful to separate it out like that though - "I noticed... this seemed to mean... here's why that might be true / false" or something like that.
Is this a notice or meaning? "My stomach went tight" is a notice, "He's a complete bastard" is a meaning, even if you didn't think it in words. You can then go on to find out what he did, notice what it was that gave you the idea, and consider if there might be other explanations. It's also ok to use phrases that you didn't think in your head - if something comes up that you're analyzing, it's still good to analyze, even if it's not in your head already. Nonverbal thoughts and early thoughts aren't necessarily any better or worse than other thoughts. Feelings are good to pay attention to, but it's still good to consider what they mean.
## Let the response change the invitation
Your shoulders tightening, or 'hmm, that feels kind of wrong', is a signal to investigate further, but 'No! That is Not Right!' is a signal to stop what you're doing and change it. If you don't feel like it fits, don't keep doing it. Just change it. You don't need any other reason.
"I notice the tightness, and I can just leave it like that. I wonder if that fits, or if I'd rather do something different?"
Sometimes it just isn't right - sometimes it's something you hadn't thought of. Neither is automatically a protector, and just being silent doesn't mean there's a process.
## Look at the situation too
If there is another person involved in the situation, it may be a good idea to look at what they are doing as well. Are they able to communicate in a mature fashion? Try and be specific in your description, and be willing to alter it if you find out that it was incorrect. Just because someone is disagreeing with you or trying to set boundaries around you does not mean they are in their emotionally immature parts. You don’t need to subject yourself to something that may be dangerous to determine if this is the case.
On the other hand, you may find that you have acted inappropriately and you feel guilty. Having feelings of guilt does not mean that you have done something wrong or that you have been manipulated. Have you done something that was wrong or that caused harm? If so, what can you do to fix it? On the other hand, did you give in to someone else just to keep them from being disappointed in you? What do you want to do or achieve the next time you interact with this person? Do you need them to understand what you are feeling/thinking? Do you just need to communicate information to them? Do you need to set boundaries with them? Do you need to end the interaction prematurely? You don’t have to make them understand before you can act.
## Change the amount of contact
When you start to contact it, if it feels too much, immediately start to widen your awareness to something else in the room, in your body, etc. Keep moving back and forth between the difficult thing and the easier thing. This is called pendulation and titration. Do as much or as little as you feel you can. You set the pace, you don’t have to do a certain number.
Ok, I think that is enough for now.
Feel free to go back and explore more if you wish to do so. If, at any point, you start to lose contact with the here and now, feel that things are becoming more and more unreal, or you feel that you are not able to switch to something else or stop, stop trying to explore inside of yourself and just do the entire “coming back” part. If you have trouble orienting yourself and it lasts for a prolonged period of time, please get some human help rather than trying a stronger “induction” back to the here and now.
If you feel an urge to move, turn, reach, push, or change your body’s position, feel free to either actually do something that will not cause your body pain or discomfort, or just imagine doing what you feel you need to do, and see what happens next. You do not need to “act out” the urge, feel that you need to move, shake, release, etc. If you do move and you feel that you are causing yourself pain or other symptoms that you are concerned about, stop moving.
## Offer the care that is actually wanted
If any “younger” parts come up, offer them the opportunity to spend time with you, or some space if they would rather. If one of them says “don’t come closer”, stay at the distance they choose. You don’t have to consider them literally “younger”, nor do you have to ask them why they are there or what happened to them. If you can identify any “protector” parts, talk to them and find out what they are concerned about. All of them are welcome to the “meeting”. You don’t have to get rid of any of them.
If you feel that it would be useful to bring in your “Nurturer”, “Protector” or “Guide” to help you either change the “tone” of the interaction, set boundaries, or find your way toward the life you want, feel free to do so. You don’t have to talk to them, just use the one whose job you need, and if you can’t do it yet, borrow it. You only need to use the “Witness” if you need help getting started with a step. If you are already able to do a certain step, you don’t have to do it again.
Try some different ideas as to what might be helpful for this ‘inner part’ to feel better (being welcomed by you, visualizing someone else, giving them the sort of words they would like to hear, you placing yourself in a different body position, you moving further away etc). Only try one idea at a time to see if it works. Just because an idea ‘should’ be a resource, that doesn’t mean that it will be.
It is possible that when you try and give the part what it wants, you might feel very upset. It may be that it never got what it needed at the time and now that you are giving it what it wants, it is processing the upset of not getting what it wanted before. If this happens, allow yourself to respond for as long as you want to do so, and for as long as you can ‘stay there’ (ie remain in the process). If you feel that the voice, distance, touch, image etc is too much, then change it to something that is less. Don’t think that you can’t ‘take’ the love.
Allow yourself to see where the metaphor takes you. Ask questions like ‘if the problem had an image or story, what would it be?’ or ‘what would need to happen to make things right?’ If it feels like you need to ‘do something’ to give yourself the resource that you feel you need (or to put a boundary in place, or to move away or to take some other action) then try ‘doing that’ for as far as it takes to be of use to you. Remember that whatever you find out, is likely to be metaphorical. It may be a good hypothesis for what you need to do but it is not a proven fact.
Alternatively, you may want to use the session to develop a capacity that you want to develop (ie love, play, courage, curiosity etc). You can also use it to rehearse something that you have decided to do.
## Finish without forcing a revelation
Ending the session . Don’t feel that you have to end the session with a ‘great revelation’! When you have done as much as you want to do, it is a good idea to find out if there is anything that you need to do to make sure that you can leave things where they are for the time being. This might include thanking parts for their help, or making sure that you do something ‘in real life’ (eat a good meal, put a boundary in place, get some rest, repair a relationship, keep a promise etc) or it may be that there is nothing to do!
Note : throughout the exercise, allow your ‘analytical’ part to make comments and/or tell you that it doesn’t like what you are doing and/or tell you to stop doing the exercise. Just tell it that you will check things out when you come back and make any commitments etc when you are back. You can stop whenever you want to. Remember that the images that you see can be meaningful without being actual memories of things that happened and just because you see an image, or feel a body release, that doesn’t mean that you are right about what is going on!
When you have finished, even if you stopped part way through, use the ‘ Come all the way back ‘ procedure to bring yourself back. When you are back, it is a good idea to write a few lines in a notebook about what you have done. Just jot down the essentials (what was there before you started, what you have done to change things, what you are unsure about, any phrases that were said, any body signals, any information that the ‘inner child’ said that it needed, anything that a ‘protector’ wanted you to know, any boundaries that you put in place, any ‘repairs’ that you did, anything that you learnt about what it is a mistake to try and force etc). You don’t need to write down the whole cast of characters, just what you actually noticed.
## The 15-minute daily practice
Notice there is no need to learn a second script! The process is the same. You are simply practicing for fifteen minutes or so (a convenient time, not a dose or a timetable). That is long enough to induce yourself (it only takes a few minutes), practice and let it unfold at its own pace (five minutes is plenty to see what happens when you simply sit in silence, listening, and you can interrupt yourself at any time to change direction if that feels better) and come out again (that takes a few minutes too).
You can also try other ways of inducing yourself such as becoming aware of 3 things you can see, 3 things you can hear, 3 things you can feel in your body; then 2 of each, 1 of each, counting from 5 to 1 and beginning to listen. Choose one way to induce yourself, you don't need to try all of them! (In fact, it might be counter-productive!)
Once you have practiced listening a few times with the recordings, you may want to shorten them to speed up your induction, or even practice without playing them back at all. On the other hand, if you like using the recordings, there is no reason to stop! Being able to guide yourself does not mean you have to! (NB. You may wish to use the re-entry cue to help you re-enter your process, this will also shorten your induction if you already know what to do.)
As you come out of the process ask yourself what you learned needed warmth, what needed to be protected, what you need to do differently to be more in accord with truth and love and life. Is there something you want to explore more fully or a capacity you want to build? And most importantly, what can you carry with you as you move forward from this? If you identified something you needed to do differently, make a plan with yourself to do that thing. The more specific you can be, the more likely you are to change: “If _________ (fill in the cue for the old pattern), then I will do ________ (fill in one small action that will begin to change the pattern).” Then check back later and see how you are doing – sleeping, relating, functioning, etc., not only how impressive the session felt
