## 1
QUOTE: Know that you can say stop at any time and the induction will end. If you want to change something, say less if you want it to be asking for less from you, or quiet and the talking will stop.
HARD: "say stop", "say less" and "or quiet" are not in quote marks, so "say less" first reads as "speak less" and "or quiet and the talking will stop" has no verb. It also never says who hears the word (a narrator? a recording? yourself?).
SCORE: 3
PLAIN: Know that you can say "stop" at any time and the induction will end. If you want to change something, say "less" if you want it to be asking for less from you, or "quiet" and the talking will stop.

## 2
QUOTE: When you're writing this, think about what the verb you're using is, what the sensory quality is, what the short refrains are, and how you're going to transition between ideas.
HARD: "this" has no referent (the reader has not been told they will write a script), and "the verb you're using", "sensory quality" and "short refrains" are craft terms with no example, so the instruction can't be followed.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: When you're writing this (your own induction script), think about what the verb you're using is (the main action word), what the sensory quality is (what it feels, sounds or looks like), what the short refrains are (the short lines you repeat), and how you're going to transition between ideas.

## 3
QUOTE: No will stay no. The narrator can't add stuff just because you don't say anything.
HARD: "No will stay no" reads like a typo or fragment. "The narrator" has not appeared before, so the reader doesn't know who or what that is (a recording, a script, you?), and "add stuff" is vague.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: No will stay no (if you say no, it stays no). The narrator (the voice leading the induction) can't add stuff (new suggestions) just because you don't say anything.

## 4
QUOTE: A large part of your subconscious mind is made out of other people. That is why you are reading a self hypnosis guide!
HARD: "made out of other people" is stated with no gloss, and the second sentence skips the step that connects it to hypnosis, so the "That is why" doesn't follow. Joel should confirm the second gloss.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: A large part of your subconscious mind is made out of other people (the rules and voices you took in from them). That is why you are reading a self hypnosis guide (hypnosis is how you reach that part of your mind)!

## 5
QUOTE: It's what protects you when your consciously installed parental Protector isn't functioning, the same as when you were a child.
HARD: A capital-P Protector (the adult role you build) is set against the "childhood protector part" in the sentence before, and "installed" and "parental" are unexplained. The three adult roles (Nurturer, Protector, Guide) are only named in a parenthetical in the next paragraph, so the reader has no way to tell the two protectors apart yet.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: It's what protects you when your consciously installed parental Protector (the adult Protector you build up on purpose) isn't functioning, the same as when you were a child.

## 6

HARD: "that you can do that" has no clear referent: do what (protect yourself? stop protecting?). The request can't be read on the first pass.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: but that you want to know what could make them trust you 1% more that you can do that (protect yourself).

## 7
QUOTE: Learn how to notice if there is no change, and that that is not a positive change.
HARD: The double "that" and "not a positive change" leave the point open: does no change mean it is not working, or just not progress? The reader can't state what to watch for.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: Learn how to notice if there is no change, and that no change is not a positive change (don't take it as a sign it's working).

## 8

SCORE: 2
PLAIN: skip straight to the orientation/containment part (the later step where you get your bearings and keep the experience contained) rather than going deeper.

## 9
QUOTE: Those can all be real gifts, although they still become a kind of bargain for belonging when failing in these areas feels like losing what it means to have your own inherent value.
HARD: A chain of abstractions ("bargain for belonging", "losing what it means to have your own inherent value") in one long sentence. The reader has to unpack who pays what to get what.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: Those can all be real gifts, although they still become a kind of bargain for belonging (you pay with them to be accepted) when failing in these areas feels like losing what it means to have your own inherent value (as if your worth depended on them).
