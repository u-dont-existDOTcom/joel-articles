## 1
QUOTE: Sometimes it just isn't right - sometimes it's something you hadn't thought of. Neither is automatically a protector, and just being silent doesn't mean there's a process.
HARD: "it", "Neither", "a protector" and "a process" have no referents, so a reader cannot tell what is not right, what the two cases are, or what the silence is being compared to.
SCORE: 3
PLAIN: Sometimes the invitation just isn't right - sometimes the reaction is about something you hadn't thought of. Neither case is automatically a protector (a part guarding you), and just being silent doesn't mean there's a process (that something is happening inside).

## 2
QUOTE: You don’t have to talk to them, just use the one whose job you need, and if you can’t do it yet, borrow it. You only need to use the “Witness” if you need help getting started with a step.
HARD: "Witness" (and "Guide") are never defined in this part, and "the one whose job you need", "borrow it" and "a step" point at nothing the reader has been shown.
SCORE: 3
PLAIN: You don’t have to talk to them, just use the one whose job you need (the tone, the boundary or the way forward), and if you can’t do that job yet, borrow it from them. You only need to use the “Witness” [one-line gloss of the Witness, taken from where the guide first introduces it] if you need help getting started with a step of this process.

## 3
QUOTE: Say the word and/ or do the phrase while doing the motion (if applicable). Be sure to do this when you are in hypnosis and in a good place.
HARD: The cue is meant "to go into hypnosis", yet this says to use it "when you are in hypnosis", and the next paragraph's "this state that you picked" has no clear antecedent; the reason for doing it in a good state is missing.
SCORE: 3
PLAIN: Say the word and/ or phrase while doing the motion (if applicable). Be sure to do this while you are already in hypnosis and in a good place (that is how the word or motion gets attached to that state).

## 4
QUOTE: As you move one of your hands, you're going to move deliberately, and as soon as you stop moving deliberately, you'll focus on what you've got.
HARD: "what you've got" has no referent and the sentence loops on "move deliberately"; the point (notice what is left right after the movement stops) only appears in the script below.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: As you move one of your hands, you're going to move deliberately, and as soon as you stop moving deliberately, you'll focus on what you've got (any echo of the movement, as in the script below).

## 5
QUOTE: Respond to each thing that comes up with an accurate notice and a small reasonable invitation (or act or taking more time etc). The 'thing' might be a sensation, image, gesture, feeling, words, or anything. The notice and invitation don't need to take all those forms, just notice and invite what happens.
HARD: "notice" and "invitation" are new abstract nouns with no example until the next section, "(or act or taking more time etc)" is hard to parse, and "take all those forms" is unclear.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: Respond to each thing that comes up with an accurate notice (saying what you notice) and a small reasonable invitation (a small offer to yourself, or an act, or taking more time etc; examples are just below). The 'thing' might be a sensation, image, gesture, feeling, words, or anything. The notice and invitation don't need to cover all those forms (you don't need one for each kind of thing), just notice and invite what happens.

## 6
QUOTE: however if you are coming out of a recording then you should not leave yourself in an altered state, this includes leaving yourself in a role, with lowered judgment, with lowered ability to move, blank or with a non-specific reset word or for an indefinite length of time.
HARD: One sentence holds the contrast with going to sleep and six ways of leaving yourself altered that run together ("blank or with a ... or for ..."), and "non-specific reset word" is not explained.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: however if you are coming out of a recording (as opposed to going to sleep to it) then you should not leave yourself in an altered state. This includes leaving yourself in a role, with lowered judgment, with lowered ability to move, blank, with a non-specific reset word (a vague “reset” instead of the steps above), or for an indefinite length of time.

## 7
QUOTE: It's also ok to use phrases that you didn't think in your head - if something comes up that you're analyzing, it's still good to analyze, even if it's not in your head already.
HARD: "phrases that you didn't think in your head" and "not in your head already" are circular, and after two reads the point (put words to what came up wordlessly) is still not clear.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: It's also ok to use phrases that you didn't think in your head (to put words to something that came up without words) - if something comes up that you're analyzing, it's still good to analyze, even if it's not in your head already as words.

## 8
QUOTE: Choose a sound, or a contact, or the two hands thing (below) or something to anchor yourself and the thing you will say to open it up. Use the same anchor for everywhere that anchor is mentioned.
HARD: "open it up" has no referent, "anchor yourself" is not glossed, and "the same anchor for everywhere that anchor is mentioned" is hard to follow.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: Choose a sound, or a contact, or the two hands thing (below) or something to anchor yourself (something to come back to when your mind wanders), and the thing you will say to open it up (to open up the inner contact). Use the same anchor for everywhere that anchor is mentioned (each time the script says “your anchor”).

## 9
QUOTE: At some point in time you can add this, but you must say you are doing it before doing it.
HARD: "fractionation" (in the heading) is never explained, "add this" does not say to what, and "say you are doing it" does not say to whom or where.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: At some point in time you can add this to your induction (fractionation: coming back up partway, then choosing whether to go back in), but you must say (to yourself, or in your recording) that you are doing it before doing it.

## 10
QUOTE: Make sure that your relaxation/ stay alert cue is not the same as your go inward to hypnosis one.
HARD: "cue" is used before the next paragraph introduces it, and one clause packs a relaxation cue, a stay-alert cue and a go-inward cue.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: Make sure that your relaxation/ stay alert cue (a word or motion that sets off that state, like the mudra) is not the same as your go inward to hypnosis one (the cue described next).

## 11
QUOTE: are now finished, the only intention I will take with me into my everyday life or the only cue I will use to take me into this state at a later date will be the one I consciously choose to and neither of them will keep me in this state.
HARD: Two "the only ... or the only ..." subjects share one "the one" and a trailing "neither of them", all in a comma-spliced run-on, so what is chosen and what must not keep you in the state tangles.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: are now finished. The only intention I will take with me into my everyday life, or the only cue I will use to take me into this state at a later date, will be the ones I consciously choose, and neither of them will keep me in this state.

## 12
