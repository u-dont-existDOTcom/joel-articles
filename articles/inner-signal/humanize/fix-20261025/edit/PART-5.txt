## Sleep induction
Sleep induction is different than inner work, where you need to come back up to write things down and do actions.
You can tell your analytical mind to stand down, any revelations can wait.

## Optional fractionation: practice coming back
At some point in time you can add this, but you must say you are doing it before doing it.
“Ok, open your eyes and come back up, feel the room around you, you can decide if you want to stop the work at this point or if you want to continue, if you want to continue soften your eyes or close them and notice the change that comes over you when you do, if you want to stop, that is ok too, either way is ok and you can stop at either place.”
## Deepening, if useful
Allow your attention to gather, if it will be of use to you. If it won’t be of use to you at this time, you can remain as awake as you need to be.
Pick a place to go to when you go inside. It might be a room in your heart, a garden, a temple, sitting in front of a fire with your Protector, down by a clear lake where you can see the bottom, in a comfortable chair where your adult is going to be, whatever you want. Just pick a place.

## Optional trance depth check
“As you wait and release the responsibility of making it happen
It might be useful for your wise subconscious to twitch a finger in one of your hands, or make a finger pulse, or come up by itself, or to heat up a finger and make it noticeable to you, or tingle in a noticeable way to let you know that your inner meeting space is ready for you to go in to.
You don’t have to make it happen, or pretend that it is happening, just wait with love and notice what happens.
Sometimes a finger will move, sometimes an entire hand will heat up, sometimes you will notice a change in your breathing, sometimes you won’t notice anything at all. Even if something happens, it doesn’t mean it is “True”, it just might mean that something is listening.
## A shortcut back into hypnosis
Once you have learned the above induction and how to come back down at the end, you can do the following….
Ex: My dad used to use a mudra (like putting two fingers together like an "Ok" sign) when he needed to relax. He even did it when he was driving. Not that I recommend driving in a trance, don't do that! Make sure that your relaxation/ stay alert cue is not the same as your go inward to hypnosis one.
Have an unusual word, phrase, and/ or motion (optional) that you will use to go into hypnosis again. Say the word and/ or do the phrase while doing the motion (if applicable). Be sure to do this when you are in hypnosis and in a good place. Don't do this when you are in a very emotional state, child state, or thinking about something that could be considered trauma. The idea is for you to be able to pick a good state to go into, and you can decide what that is.
When you pick the time and place (it needs to be an appropriate time and place), say the word and/ or do the phrase to begin going into this state that you picked, and are familiar with. It's fine to change the direction you are going in, or stop this at any point.
Now come all the way back up again, and at a later time, when it is appropriate, start using this. Over time, you can shorten the length of the longer induction, or use the longer induction if this doesn't seem to be enough for your needs. There is no need to have to do this a certain number of times, go to a certain depth, or even have a certain sensation when you do this.
Also, be sure that this is working for you. Be able to turn it on and off at will. Make sure it isn't there when you don't want it to be. Be able to change directions or come back up when you are in it. Don't use it in a way that you are going to follow instructions that you are hearing from a narrator, notifications, or something that you overhear. Don't use this to go into hypnosis faster for things that you shouldn't be working on yet.
There is something you should never do while using this induction. And that's use it, practice it or test it while you're driving or doing something where you need to be actively paying attention. It's just not safe to be going into a trance where you are actively paying attention to what's going on inside of you when you need to be paying attention to what's going on outside of you. Even though your eyes can be open, even though you're using "if it would be useful for you to do so" type language it's just not safe.
If you want to turn this one off, simply don't practice it anymore. Take it out of your recordings, take it out of your apps if you're using it in an app, and start using your regular induction. Tell yourself, when you're awake, that you won't be using it anymore. Just because you say that it'll be gone doesn't mean that it'll be gone right away. If it's causing you a problem, if you're still responding to it when you don't want to respond to it, if you're having issues with disorientation, etc, get some help from someone who's qualified to help you with this. If, on the other hand, you want to keep it so that you can use it to go back into trance at a later date when you do want to go into a trance, that's fine too. For now, though, once you come out of this trance, it'll be over for today.
## Come all the way back
If you have done anything to alter your state when coming out of a trance, make sure to reverse those things. If you have floated, make sure to gain your weight again (whatever weight you want of course). If you have shrunk, make sure to grow back to your regular size. If you have met a younger state or are working with parts, make sure to come back to feeling complete in your body in the here and now, that your body belongs to you, and that it is under control of your adult mind. If you have gone somewhere, make sure to go back the same way you went there. Etcetera. Make sure to only take with you when you come out of a session the things that you want to!
Here is a generic bring-back that you can use: "When I come out of this session, I will only bring with me that which is useful to me in my everyday life right now. Anything else I will bring with me at a later date, when I am better able to integrate it. When I come out of this session, I will be coming back to the room I am in now, and to the day in which I am participating in this session now. All the instructions you have given me to carry me deeper into this session or keep me in this state are now finished, the only intention I will take with me into my everyday life or the only cue I will use to take me into this state at a later date will be the one I consciously choose to and neither of them will keep me in this state. When I come out of this session I will be me, in my body and with my adult judgment."
Then do a count up: On 1 start to feel your body again and what it's resting on and feel your weight (of your choice). On 2 start to feel your breathing return to normal. On 3 start to hear the room around you and name where you are. On 4 start to wiggle your fingers and toes, move your shoulders and your eyes. On 5 open your eyes, back in the here and now, feeling normal and clear and the session is now over.
When you come out make sure you can name what day (or date) it is, look to your left and right, look at something near to you and something further away. Move around a bit. Don't get up until you feel like you can do so without falling over. Make sure that you are oriented and alert (counting does this, but not always). Take mental note of only what actually came up for you and any actions you need to take regarding it. Then go on and do something normal.
If your recording cuts out for some reason, you can say to yourself, "The recording has stopped and the session is over," and then do the above on your own. Once your eyes are open, press your feet or hands into a surface as well.
Note: If you are going to sleep to a recording then it is fine to go to sleep or just remain resting quietly at the end of it, however if you are coming out of a recording then you should not leave yourself in an altered state, this includes leaving yourself in a role, with lowered judgment, with lowered ability to move, blank or with a non-specific reset word or for an indefinite length of time.
## The actual inner meeting
Note: If you have a tendency to day dream, dissociate or fall asleep, it is better to do this exercise sitting up and save lying down for when you want to go to sleep, get a very deep rest or incubate dreams. If you are sitting, keep your spine straight but not rigid like a soldier. Relax your jaw and stay somewhat connected to the room you are in.
“I am not leaving life; I am listening more deeply to life.
I invoke my Higher Self, universal heart-mind, wise subconscious mind, intelligence in my body, inner child(ren), inner adult(s), protector(s) and all that is of truth and love to help me know what I need to know in a form and at a level that will be useful, kind, stabilizing and real for me.”
Ask your conscious mind to listen.
This does not mean you have to stop thinking to listen. Just notice your thoughts, body sensations, pictures, doubts, emotions, etc. Don’t fight what comes up, but don’t go with it either. Just notice what stands out on its own as you hold the idea of what you are doing in the back of your mind.
Internally say, “Tell me what I need to know or ask me what you need to know and I’ll sit in silence for 5 minutes and listen.”
You may hear a phrase, feel a body sensation, notice a memory, see a color, be contacted by a child or protector, feel pain, warmth, complete silence, know that you need to do something, be grateful for something, feel you need to stop the listening or any number of things.
Listen to everything and believe nothing automatically.
## Let the next response guide you
Once you've read the induction and done the inner meeting, this will make more sense. Read through these, and then try doing it without looking at the screen.
Respond to each thing that comes up with an accurate notice and a small reasonable invitation (or act or taking more time etc). The 'thing' might be a sensation, image, gesture, feeling, words, or anything. The notice and invitation don't need to take all those forms, just notice and invite what happens.
## Finding your own words
An example notice might be, "I notice my chest tightening when I imagine asking for help". Don't add interpretation like "this must be the abandoned child part of me, or a protective mechanism" - just notice what you notice. An example invitation might be, "And as I notice that, I can take some time before deciding what it means, and notice if I want to say anything, or change it, or just stay like that".
Then just listen. You don't need fancy language - often it will be better to just ask what happens next, or whatever. If you notice a feeling developing, it might be best not to say anything while it's developing (if you can help it) but some days you need to talk to understand, so just do that.
You can be pretty straightforward when that's the help you chose - "Let the explanation wait a moment. What do I feel as I say that?" can be a perfectly valid thing to say to yourself, and help you focus. It's ok to just be with an unpleasant feeling, you don't have to wait till it's a nice one before you know you're ok. As long as you're still present, and able to change if you want to, and happy to carry on, a sad feeling or tension or whatever doesn't mean it's not ok.
It's worth trying to separate the notice, the meaning, and your thoughts on the meaning, but in practice it will often be pretty fuzzy. When you can, it can be helpful to separate it out like that though - "I noticed... this seemed to mean... here's why that might be true / false" or something like that.
Is this a notice or meaning? "My stomach went tight" is a notice, "He's a complete bastard" is a meaning, even if you didn't think it in words. You can then go on to find out what he did, notice what it was that gave you the idea, and consider if there might be other explanations. It's also ok to use phrases that you didn't think in your head - if something comes up that you're analyzing, it's still good to analyze, even if it's not in your head already. Nonverbal thoughts and early thoughts aren't necessarily any better or worse than other thoughts. Feelings are good to pay attention to, but it's still good to consider what they mean.
## Let the response change the invitation
Your shoulders tightening, or 'hmm, that feels kind of wrong', is a signal to investigate further, but 'No! That is Not Right!' is a signal to stop what you're doing and change it. If you don't feel like it fits, don't keep doing it. Just change it. You don't need any other reason.
"I notice the tightness, and I can just leave it like that. I wonder if that fits, or if I'd rather do something different?"
Sometimes it just isn't right - sometimes it's something you hadn't thought of. Neither is automatically a protector, and just being silent doesn't mean there's a process.
## Look at the situation too
If there is another person involved in the situation, it may be a good idea to look at what they are doing as well. Are they able to communicate in a mature fashion? Try and be specific in your description, and be willing to alter it if you find out that it was incorrect. Just because someone is disagreeing with you or trying to set boundaries around you does not mean they are in their emotionally immature parts. You don’t need to subject yourself to something that may be dangerous to determine if this is the case.
On the other hand, you may find that you have acted inappropriately and you feel guilty. Having feelings of guilt does not mean that you have done something wrong or that you have been manipulated. Have you done something that was wrong or that caused harm? If so, what can you do to fix it? On the other hand, did you give in to someone else just to keep them from being disappointed in you? What do you want to do or achieve the next time you interact with this person? Do you need them to understand what you are feeling/thinking? Do you just need to communicate information to them? Do you need to set boundaries with them? Do you need to end the interaction prematurely? You don’t have to make them understand before you can act.
