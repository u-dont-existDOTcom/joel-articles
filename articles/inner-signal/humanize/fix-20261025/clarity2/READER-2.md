## 1
QUOTE: Or are you blending them? It might not be chronological age.
HARD: "It" has no referent (the age of what?) and the sentence does not follow from the blending question; "positions" is also used as a term without ever being explained.
SCORE: 3
PLAIN: Or are you blending them? How old each position feels might not match your chronological age.

## 2
QUOTE: You can choose to let the parts of you that feel younger express themselves without handing over the adult part of you to the caretaker in the other person or without actually going back in age.
HARD: "the caretaker in the other person": no other person has been mentioned, so the reader cannot tell who or what the adult part is not being handed to.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: You can choose to let the parts of you that feel younger express themselves without handing over the adult part of you to whoever is acting as the caretaker or without actually going back in age.

## 3
QUOTE: When you're placing your younger self on trial, how valuable is it to consider how valuable it might have been for them to do what they did at the time, given what they knew at the time?
HARD: "how valuable is it to consider how valuable it might have been" stacks the same word twice; after two reads the reader still cannot state the question.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: When you're placing your younger self on trial, how valuable is it to consider what those actions did for them at the time, given what they knew at the time?

## 4
QUOTE: Working with parts: Precision. Working with Higher Self: Coherence. Doing both: Communion.
HARD: Three bare labels (Precision, Coherence, Communion), and "Higher Self" itself, arrive before anything has explained them, so the reader cannot tell what each approach gives.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: Working with parts gives you precision. Working with your Higher Self gives you coherence. Doing both gives you communion.

## 5
QUOTE: Just work on whatever it is you need to work on (often warming up and protecting yourself and knowing what to do etc). So even though it can be useful to talk about your Higher Self, it doesn't mean you already have all the good inner parenting functions etc that you need.
HARD: "warming up" (warming up what?), and a "So" that does not follow from the sentence before it (from noticing and differentiating to talking about the Higher Self).
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: Just work on whatever it is you need to work on (often being warm to yourself, protecting yourself and knowing what to do etc). Even though it can be useful to talk about your Higher Self, it doesn't mean you already have all the good inner parenting functions etc that you need.

## 6
QUOTE: It is possible for trust to question your promises, remember when you failed to protect him/her, and take it's time.
HARD: "trust" is made the one that questions and remembers, so the reader has to work out that it means the child.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: It is possible for the child to question your promises, remember when you failed to protect him/her, and take it's time to trust you.

## 7
QUOTE: Don't make it so that doing it successfully is having to feel good about it, or being able to check it off a list.
HARD: "doing it successfully is having to feel good about it" is tangled; the reader has to rebuild that success should not depend on feeling good or on finishing a list.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: Don't make your success depend on feeling good about it, or on being able to check it off a list.
