## 1
QUOTE: You probably don't need any more support than this: If you keep the practice shorter, make it warm, pick a small and tractable subject, make sure you come back out, and then see how it affects a specific target (anxiety, daydreaming, helping a relationship, facing a task you've been avoiding, helping you sleep, being less reactive emotionally, improving your intuitive accuracy, etc) for a week or two, and if you notice that your functioning is getting worse, or that you're getting more certain about unconfirmable memories, then reassess instead of pushing harder.

PLAIN: You probably don't need any more support than this guide gives you, as long as you keep the practice shorter, make it warm, pick a small and tractable subject, make sure you come back out, and then see how it affects a specific target (anxiety, daydreaming, helping a relationship, facing a task you've been avoiding, helping you sleep, being less reactive emotionally, improving your intuitive accuracy, etc) for a week or two. ## 2
QUOTE: Tell your Little parts and childhood protector parts that you’re there for your Little parts and that you recognize that your protector parts are trying to protect you and that you don’t need them to scream at you in order for you to know that something is wrong.
HARD: Three "that" clauses chained with "and" in one sentence, and "them" could mean the Little parts or the protectors, so the reader can't tell who is being told what.
SCORE: 2
PLAIN: Tell your Little parts and your childhood protector parts three things: that you’re there for your Little parts, that you recognize your protector parts are trying to protect you, and that you don’t need the protectors to scream at you in order for you to know that something is wrong.
