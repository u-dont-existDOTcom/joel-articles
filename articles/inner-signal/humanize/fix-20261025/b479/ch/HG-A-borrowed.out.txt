The problem is that some people cannot access their adult at will. They go blank, or access an inherited inner critic, or worse still a child part who may be playing at being an adult (what I call forced adulthood). For such people, saying ‘just access or use or be in or be from (whatever) their adult’ is the equivalent of saying to a baby bird – ‘just fly’.

So, I came up with the idea of constructing a bridge – a temporary fix – a borrowed adulthood, if I can put it that way. It’s made up of a number of things that I know work (such as modelling and supported action), even though I don’t know if the whole sequence has been tried before.
