Take some time to give your inner child some love. Put your hand on your heart and one on your solar plexis and just in a loving way, say, “You are not alone. You don’t have to fix this. I am here. The adult in me is here now to help you through this.” Be sure you are really speaking to your inner child that is scared to death. If you can’t do this in a loving way today, just put your hands there and see what you feel. You don’t have to fake it!
