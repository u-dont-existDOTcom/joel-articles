Thoughts for pondering When you receive an answer, you might also want to ask, "What can I do to prove I heard correctly?" However, if you feel you're being instructed to do something, I would do it only if it's loving and not against your freedom. In addition, it should make sense to you. And if it's something major and hard to un-do, wait until you're calm and see if it matches up with what you know to be true.
