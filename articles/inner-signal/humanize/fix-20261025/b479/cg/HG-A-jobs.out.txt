The thing to remember is that your inner child is not going to trust you straight away. It is not enough for you to sit in meditation and say three nice sentences to your inner child and expect it to trust you. It is when you become reliable to it in the person that you are now that you will be able to help your inner child to start to trust you.

In this article I will be talking about developing yourself as you reparent your inner child. I will talk about this in terms of three jobs – your loving Nurturer, your safe Adult Protector (hereafter called Protector) and your wise Guide. This may seem confusing as it is all done by one grown-up – you – but it helps to think about it in terms of three jobs as each one helps to keep the others in balance, and it is all about you developing yourself.
