Here is some inner child love. Put your hand over your heart and your other hand on your solar plexus, and say to yourself in a loving way, “Little one, you are not alone. You don’t have to fix it. I’m here now, and the adult is here now to listen to you.” Make sure you are actually talking to your inner child, who is scared to death. If you can’t do this in a loving way today, just put your hands there and see what you feel. You don’t have to fake it!
