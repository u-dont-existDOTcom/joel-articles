My challenge was that these people don’t have access to their adult. When they try, they either go blank or they go into an inherited inner critic, or they go into a child part that may be playing adult (which I’ve called forced adulthood). To expect someone in this state to access their adult is like expecting a baby bird to fly.

So what I’ve developed (which I’m not sure has been tried before) is a way of creating a kind of bridge to their own emerging adult that I call a borrowed adulthood, using well-known processes like modeling and acting with support.
