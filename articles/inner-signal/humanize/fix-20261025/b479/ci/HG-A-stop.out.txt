Make sure that you are in a safe place to do this. Do not do this if you are driving a car, in the water, or otherwise need to attend to yourself or others, if you are intoxicated, or if you are in a state of acute confusion. If you are already partally or fully opened, you can use the 'Already open? Contain yourself before deepening' section to help orient yourself, but do not attempt to deepen yourself if you are not in a safe state.

Decide to do it. How are you going to trick yourself into doing it? How are you going to make it feel spacious or warm or whatever? Think about what you are going to do - are you going to close your eyes? Are you going to come out and go in again? What are you going to do at the end? Read the script for the recording you are going to use, if possible. Remind yourself that you can say 'stop' at any time and the induction will end. Remind yourself that if it gets to be too much, you can say 'less' and the induction will ask less of you. Remind yourself that if you want the talking to stop, you can say 'quiet' and the talking will stop. Remind yourself that you can open your eyes at any time and that doing so will stop the induction and bring you all the way back. Remind yourself that you do not need to get permission from any parts to stop the induction, even if a part tells you that you do need to.
