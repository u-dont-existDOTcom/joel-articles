What to do when the Nurturer doesn't work If you feel vulnerable, like your throat is closing, or you feel sad or afraid, or protective, then try talking to your Nurturer. If that doesn't work - or if it seems hostile - then give up and go back to coming back to your body, and the room you're in.

There's no point in trying to find out if it's a guard at this stage, as it's probably going to be difficult to comfort it. Does it work? Does it help you clear up more quickly?
