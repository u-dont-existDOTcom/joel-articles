## When “your voice” is everybody else’s voice
It can be difficult to connect with your inner child if your inner child didn’t have the opportunity to develop. This happens often when you grow up in a situation where you don’t have a private self, for example when your family, religion, or community ‘takes over’ very early in your life. Or you are pushed to achieve very early in life. The result is that you don’t know what you feel, what you want, what you believe, what you like and dislike until you have heard someone else’s opinion about it. If you are in that situation, it can be difficult to follow the advice to ‘love yourself’. You don’t even know who ‘yourself’ is! So you can do reparenting and finding yourself in parallel. For others, it is very easy to work with the inner child, they can feel it easily.
You are in this camp if:
You don’t know what you want, until you have heard what someone else wants.
Your opinion is very different when you are with different people
You agree with people at the moment but when you are by yourself you realize that you don’t agree with them
You take responsibility for the emotions of others, at the expense of yourself
When you try to love yourself, it feels more like a show
When you try to find out how your inner child feels, you feel that you are guessing instead of feeling
You can rebel, but you can’t choose
One way of explaining this is that in your childhood you didn’t spend enough attention in finding out what you like and what you want to try out and who you are when nobody is grading you. You were too occupied in not being in pain, reading out what was dangerous and what you had to do to stay in a good bond, etc. This is not about a specific place in your brain, but a way of looking at it.
Temporary Separation. Sometimes it may be wise to remove yourself, for a time, from the people or group that you normally would get answers from. Not for too long, however, or you’ll defeat the purpose of the exercise. You want to let your quieter preferences bubble up to the surface so you can be sure you’re not just echoing your social group.
What is the difference between a human being and a human doing? If you don’t feel good enough as a human being, you will try to become good enough as a "human doing" by, for example, being beautiful, doing something well, being smart, having a certain status, being spiritual, etc. Those can all be real gifts, although they still become a kind of bargain for belonging when failing in these areas feels like losing what it means to have your own inherent value.
So in order to find yourself, you can ask yourself:
What do I like when nobody is looking?
What do I believe when I don’t check with other people?
What do I want that is neither in rebellion nor in compliance with others?
Where am I buying my sense of belonging at the expense of betraying myself?
In what arena am I trying to buy my sense of belonging?
What can I try on that I don’t need to be good at?
With whom can I be myself?
What do I feel in my body and what do I make out of it?
Give your inner child the opportunity to do something that has no value for its resume and that it doesn’t need to be good at. For example painting, dancing, gardening, learning a language, playing a sport, doing volunteer work, playing a musical instrument, etc. Because often your inherited parent script tells your inner child that it is supposed to be good at something as soon as it starts doing it, which is humiliating. The more consciously sculpted adult in you can answer, “We’re finding out whether we enjoy this, not proving our worth.” So try things out long enough until the humiliation is gone.
When you are a child you feel that you need to belong to the family in order to survive. This is why people do things for their family, religion, romantic partner, political tribe, spiritual group, institution, online community etc., that they wouldn’t otherwise do. Part of adulting is working out where you are selling yourself in order to belong and choosing your own ground, and finding out you can disappoint someone and not disappear. A large part of your subconscious mind is made out of other people. That is why you are reading a self hypnosis guide!
Where am I betraying myself in order to belong?
What does it feel like to belong without betraying myself?
Where do I think I am following my own guidance, but I am in fact following my belonging program?
Where do I think I am doing things out of love, but I am actually doing it out of fear of not belonging?
Where do I think I am listening to my conscience, but I am actually listening to my conditioning?
Where do I think I am doing something out of devotion, but I am actually doing it out of compliance?
Where do I think I am free, but I am actually rebelling?
And then look what your body tells you. It is not always correct, but it is a good place to start.
## De-hypnosis: wake up inside the influence
One of the best side-effects of learning self-hypnosis is that sudden "waking up" to all the ways your attention is being shaped in your day-to-day life. There are actually multiple patents on using screens to send subliminal messages, but even without that kind of potentially illegal tech, the screen is one connection to the omnipotent Algorithm. Which shows us ads and guides our preferences. Then we wonder why we vote for this or that politician. But there are also the older, more mundane influences: erotic imprinting (as if breast implants are actually sexy), religious fear, which tags along with shame, charismatic authority and pressure to belong. And all of that drilled repeatedly into us, because one exposure won't do the trick. Sometimes it’s benign – like lullabies or prayers or being taught by a good teacher or listening to a beautiful song or being in love. But you may want to ask yourself what effect it’s having on your ability to see clearly and make choices.
Manipulation often comes with any of the following; urgency, you are special, FOMO (fear of missing out), not be included, sexual charge, shame, confused, someone will rescue you, destiny, you should obey, secret knowledge, and an excessive sense of certainty about something way too quickly and you have a sense that one good question will break the spell.
First of all, if you sense you are being hypnotically influenced, stop. Don’t obey immediately, but don’t rebel automatically, just realize that you are being influenced.
Secondly, some of the ways that hypnotic language is used to manipulate are where you have consented to something you didn’t realize, or you being given only one choice, or something not yet agreed upon is embedded in a sentence. For example;
You will soon realize that I am right …. Or you may, in time, decide to admit that …. Or the only reason you do not agree is …. Or part of you already knows …. Or your higher self wants …. Or everyone who is awake knows …. Or the reason you do not believe that is true is because your ego does not want you to …
If you are unsure, you may like to ask yourself the following questions;
What am I being asked to agree to? What choice am I being given? What is being assumed that I may not agree with? Who benefits if I comply? What is the emotionally charged part of what is being said? What is not being said? If I say no, what will the consequences be? (Will I be punished, shamed, laughed at, not be included, be told I am not ‘awake’ etc?)
This is a good guide to help engage your ‘adult’ in the conversation. The more powerful the ‘state’ the more you will need to make use of these.
## Soft language can still take away choice
Be aware that even when using ‘permissive’ language, you can still take away choice. Be aware of;
Indirect suggestions can by-pass the subject’s ‘skeptical guard’. They do this by not directly telling the subject what to do. This does not mean that indirect suggestions are not specific. It simply means that they are worded in a vague, conversational, suggestive manner.
1) Utilizing induction techniques while seemingly explaining something (even something like "anti hypnosis" can use the same mechanics of induction).
2) In any way making it so that whatever the subject says, shows that a suggestion has worked, when this may not be the case (this includes answering yes, no, noticing something, not noticing something, trying not to do something, not answering, etc etc etc)
3) Including phrases such as whether or not you notice etc etc
4) Including any concepts that may make a subject think they must obey, or take on a role, or that it is good/safe to please the guide or belong to someone etc etc.
5) Fractionating a subject without telling them that you are going to take them in and out of absorption a few times.
6) Giving a subject cues (words, gestures, blinks, sounds, listening a certain number of times etc) that you intend to use to make them do something at a later date, without informing them exactly what it is that they will be doing.
7) Including suggestions for amnesia, or making a subject think that something (dream, amnesia, imagery, body experiences etc) has happened when this may not necessarily be the case.
8) Not taking a subject out at the end of a recording, or leaving them with a role to play, with their judgement down, with their physical abilities down, with their minds blank, or in any other way different for an indefinite length of time.
In order for a subject to be making a REAL choice whilst in a hypnosis, there MUST be at least one way for a subject NOT to carry on with the hypnosis if they wish. This could be by making the hypnosis lighter, using a different method of hypnosis, being in full awareness whilst in a hypnosis, or being able to pause or stop the hypnosis. Just so you know, if you say no, you are quite entitled to mean NO, even if someone with a nice voice is asking you to do something! Also, just because you don't say anything, or don't know what to say, DOES NOT mean yes!

## Induction: gathering attention without disappearing
Inductions serve to gather you up so you can access quiet stuff. They're not about surrendering or playing asleep or obeying someone. Sometimes it's nice to do an actual induction if you're sober but distracted or analytical or otherwise not very trancey. If on the other hand, you're already pretty trancey for whatever reason (meditation, loveyhuasca, lucid dreaming, grief, music, prayer, tired, whatever...), skip straight to the orientation/containment part rather than going deeper.
Get trancey enough to do what you want to do (focused, warm, receptive, embodied, able to stop, and optionally deeper)
## What an induction is actually doing
Getting yourself ready to do something. Part of that is gathering your attention up. Part of that might be deepening. Part of that is letting what you notice inform the words you're hearing and what you do next. There are lots of different ways to do this. For this method, I'm going to be counting, but you could do all sorts of things. If you want to just talk, or tell a story, or move, or make sounds, or be curious, or whatever, by all means, just do that. The only thing is, say that you're doing it. Don't pretend you're doing something else, in the name of elegance or whatever.
Test: Are you getting the response you want out of this already? If so, you might not need to deepen.
## Please don’t put six metaphors in one trance
Keep it simple. One thing you're doing, one anchor/image you're going with at first, one way of coming back, and feel free to set a tone (warm, bounded, directed, neutral witnessing, whatever) for yourself. If you're going to use metaphors (you don't have to) just do one, and if you switch metaphors, do it in a way that's conscious.
Using metaphors and imagery is entirely optional. You can use whatever form of signal makes sense to you (feelings, rhythms, movements, words, spatial, simple knowing, lack of signal, whatever) but if you're using words, try to keep them related to whatever world you're creating. If you're going to count, do it in a way that makes sense in your world. And when you're done, come back the other way. (You can borrow qualities, but don't change who you are.)
When you're writing this, think about what the verb you're using is, what the sensory quality is, what the short refrains are, and how you're going to transition between ideas. Read it out loud as you're writing it. Long, elegant sentences don't read well when you're being induced. It's all just soup.
## Before you begin
1. Read this page, and the Come all the way back page, when you're awake.
2. Get clear on what it is you want to do during the induction. What do you want not to do today. How long do you want to spend on this? (You don't have to do this on a problem. You can do it on something you want to experience more of: love, play, confidence, creativity, communion, whatever.) Make sure you're leaving some time at the end to come back, and reflect on what you experienced.
3. Put yourself in a place where you can safely do this. Don't do this if you're driving, in the water, operating machinery, or otherwise need to be attending to yourself or other people. If you are overwhelmed by chemicals, acutely confused, or not able to keep yourself safe, then get some immediate support instead of doing exploratory hypnosis. (The later part of this can be used to help orient you if you're already open, but don't use it to make yourself deeper if you're not stable.)
4. Deciding to do it . How will you coax yourself to do this? Make it spacious? Make it warm and held? Something else? Preview what you are going to do: will you close your eyes? Will you come out and go back in again? What will you do at the end? If you are going to use a recording, read the script if you can. Know that you can say stop at any time and the induction will end. If you want to change something, say less if you want it to be asking for less from you, or quiet and the talking will stop. You can also just open your eyes and stop, and then come all the way back anyway. You don't need to ask any parts for permission to stop, even if a part tells you that you do.
5. If you are new to this or haven't done it for a while, work on the coming in and coming out part first, don't bring in the hard stuff till later. You don't have to watch yourself all the time, but for the first time, make sure you can change state. Let yourself make it easier and easier to do this and notice that it takes less and less of you to do it. No will stay no. The narrator can't add stuff just because you don't say anything.
6. Let your response guide you as to what to do next regardless of what the script is expecting. Utilize. Use what you have to go to the next place, whatever it is. Learn how to notice what is there. Learn how to notice if there is no change, and that that is not a positive change. For more on language you can use, see Appendix B, or keep reading below for information on using the tools after induction in Let the next response guide you . Either way, keep the instructions about how to stay safe and how to get out plain.
## Sober induction: 3–5 minutes
You can read this slowly to yourself on your recorder, you can read it out loud or say it to yourself if you are good at that. Choose a sound, or a contact, or the two hands thing (below) or something to anchor yourself and the thing you will say to open it up. Use the same anchor for everywhere that anchor is mentioned. Don't read the directions, just pause between paragraphs.
You can allow your eyes to rest on something in the room for a bit, and let what's already there come into view.
You can allow yourself to notice something you can feel, like the sensation of something under you, and notice the sensation of the contact, the pressure. Or maybe there is a sound, or something else to notice.
Allow yourself some time to do this.
You might notice yourself thinking, and whenever you notice yourself thinking, you can allow yourself to re-engage with your anchor and leave the thought unfinished.
You might notice something that you are doing that holds your interest, something about the sound, or sensation, or listening or something else.
Allow yourself some time to do this, as much time as you would for someone you care about who has started to talk and hasn't gotten to what she wants to say yet, so you are going to stick around till she gets it all out.
Repeat the phrase that you chose, if you want to. Say it as an invitation and notice how it's received.
You can close your eyes now if you want to. If not, it's fine to keep them open, or soften your gaze. The listening might go deeper while there's still a background sound you can hear.
Count 5 to 1, taking your time if you like.
5. Let your attention come back to your chosen anchor. 4. Let more of your attention come to what you've found. 3. Leave a little space. 2. See if you can listen to a space after the words without preparing an answer. 1. Take your time.
Bring to mind, if you want, the thing that you want to talk about, or just the wish to have inner contact. Just let it be there for now. You don't need to tell the whole story yet.
What do you notice now?
Let yourself notice what comes to you, if anything. It may not be clear yet what it is. It may be sensations, words, pictures, feelings, movements, or anything else.
Let yourself take your time to notice, with or without words.
## Already open? Contain before you deepen
This is for when you're already open for some reason (loveyhuasca, meditation, lucid dreaming, going through grief, listening to beautiful music, prayer, erotic energy, exhaustion, seeing something you love, etc) and want to aim and stabilize it.
Know that you're already open, you just need to aim and stabilize it, and keep it kind.
Orient yourself by looking around. Where are you. What's the date or day. How does your body feel? Where are you grounded? Can you feel your feet/hands/spine/etc? Make some contact with yourself, eg press your thumbs against the tips of your index fingers gently but firmly so that you can feel friction. Focus on that.
Say something like, "Only what I can integrate, please. If there's too much it can wait. My body may stay safe. I'll be here as an adult, this won't just be my inner child."
When you feel oriented, make an intention.
Something like, "Please show me the next truth/symbol/feeling/question/action that is useful for me, that I can integrate."
## Stay awake when your mind likes to escape
Say to yourself, "I'm not going anywhere, and I'm coming more fully here." Sit up, open your eyes, feel your feet, let your breathing be natural.
Look around you and choose something to look at. Feel your spine. Ask yourself why you're daydreaming. Is it to get guidance, or because you're working through something, or are you avoiding something?
Say something like, "If I'm meant to be getting guidance, please show me the action I need to take. If I'm avoiding something, please bring me back to now and show me what I'm avoiding."
If being still tends to make you sleepy, or diffuse your attention, or make you want to mentally escape, you can make the hypnosis more active. Instead of getting more relaxed with the hypnosis, try getting more alert. Keep your eyes open. As you move one of your hands, you're going to move deliberately, and as soon as you stop moving deliberately, you'll focus on what you've got. Don't escape inside yourself. Say something like,
“Starting with your eyes open press your thumb to your fore finger. And as you press your finger together gently and release, you begin to bring yourself into a place of clearer awareness… again you press your fingers together and release, bringing yourself into an even clearer state of awareness. And again as you press your fingers together and release you bring yourself into a clear state of awareness, and now you stop pressing your fingers together and in the first second after you stop pressing your fingers together deliberately, you can notice if there is any echo of the pressing in your fingers, in your breathing, in your attention or nowhere at all. Just notice, keep yourself up straight and work at this level.

## Optional fractionation: practice coming back
At some point in time you can add this, but you must say you are doing it before doing it.
“Ok, open your eyes and come back up, feel the room around you, you can decide if you want to stop the work at this point or if you want to continue, if you want to continue soften your eyes or close them and notice the change that comes over you when you do, if you want to stop, that is ok too, either way is ok and you can stop at either place.”
## Deepening, if useful
Allow your attention to gather, if it will be of use to you. If it won’t be of use to you at this time, you can remain as awake as you need to be.
Pick a place to go to when you go inside. It might be a room in your heart, a garden, a temple, sitting in front of a fire with your Protector, down by a clear lake where you can see the bottom, in a comfortable chair where your adult is going to be, whatever you want. Just pick a place.
