# The hypnosis guide: what it says more than once (2026-10-25)

Part 4 of the guide's clarity check. Ten fresh readers each read the whole guide and owned one part of it: for each point made in their part, every other place that makes it again, which place keeps it, and what to cut. They overlapped a lot: 40 of their 104 items were the same cut twice, or a cut that is on the caveats page, so those are listed once at the end. I checked each cut against the guide with your rule from 65 (a preview of a later telling goes; a definition or a thesis stays where a reader needs it). This guide is a reference that people jump into (the troubleshooting, the prompt list, the appendices, the induction library), so much of what reads as repetition is the instruction a reader needs where they are, and I kept that. **64 cuts: my pick is cut on 18 and keep on 46.**
