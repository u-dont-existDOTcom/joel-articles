## 5
SECTION: De-hypnosis: wake up inside the influence
QUOTE: Manipulation often comes with any of the following; urgency, you are special, FOMO (fear of missing out), not be included, sexual charge, shame, confused, someone will rescue you, destiny, you should obey, secret knowledge, and an excessive sense of certainty about something way too quickly and you have a sense that one good question will break the spell.
KEPT: Before you do what it wants you to do, see if it does any of the following: create a sense of urgency, secrecy, shame, threat of exclusion if you don’t do what it says, specialness, sexual charge, spiritual superiority, ask you for money, ask you for sex, ask you for loyalty, ask you to be quiet, ask you to obey it, try to limit your choices, make it seem like it’s bad to question it, act like not obeying it is a problem that needs to be solved, make normal life seem boring/stupid/irrelevant in its presence, try to seduce/scare/rescue your child, not let your adult self say no. [Appendix C: The extended de-hypnosis checklist]
WHY: Lists the same warning signs of manipulation (urgency, specialness, shame, exclusion, sexual charge, rescue, obey, secrecy) that the section itself sends the reader to Appendix C for.
LOSES: FOMO, destiny, confusion and too-quick certainty as signs (Appendix C's list lacks them) and the line that one good question breaks the spell; readers who skip the appendices lose the list. The paragraphs before and after still read.
