## 5

QUOTE: It's worth trying to separate the notice, the meaning, and your thoughts on the meaning, but in practice it will often be pretty fuzzy. ## 7
SECTION: Finish without forcing a revelation
QUOTE: Remember that the images that you see can be meaningful without being actual memories of things that happened and just because you see an image, or feel a body release, that doesn't mean that you are right about what is going on!
KEPT: Remember that whatever you find out, is likely to be metaphorical. It may be a good hypothesis for what you need to do but it is not a proven fact. [Offer the care that is actually wanted]
WHY: says again that an image or body sensation in the session is not proof of what happened
LOSES: that an image can be meaningful without being a memory, and the "body release" example
