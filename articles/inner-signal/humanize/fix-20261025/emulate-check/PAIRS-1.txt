=== Sometimes start with the whole, P2 [block 86]
NEW: Start like this: ‘I’m approaching this from the universal heart. I’m approaching this from my Higher Self. I’m approaching this from the part of me that knows it belongs to life, not any wound, or role, or family, or group, or identity, or ideology, or story. So can you please tell me what’s going to be true and kind and timely and stabilizing and useful here?’ Etc.
HIS (published-20260928.md, Sometimes start with the whole, P2): “I begin from the universal heart. I begin from Higher Self. I begin from the part of me that belongs to life before it belongs to any wound, role, family, group, identity, ideology, or story. Please help me understand what is true, kind, timely, stabilizing, and useful.”

=== Sometimes start with the whole, P3 [block 87]
NEW: It can be that you can’t even access your Higher Self, or the universal heart, or whatever works for you. (Btw, if it doesn’t work for you don’t use it! It’s no good adopting somebody else’s map.) In that case, start by just witnessing whatever is coming up. Notice the room, your body, the fact that you’re in a younger state etc. Being able to notice is enough adult presence for the first step.
HIS (published-20260928.md, Sometimes start with the whole, P3): If Higher Self, the universal heart, or any sacred language feels unavailable or borrowed from somebody else, begin with plain witnessing instead. Notice the room, the body, and the younger state. The fact that something can notice is enough adult presence for the first step.

=== Sometimes start with the whole, P4 [block 88]
NEW: Once you have the ability to notice, and to differentiate, you don’t need to keep rehearsing it. Just work on whatever it is you need to work on (often being warm to yourself, protecting yourself and knowing what to do etc). So even though it can be useful to talk about your Higher Self, it doesn’t mean you already have all the good inner parenting functions etc that you need. You usually need to work on developing them. ⟦And I don’t think the Higher Self has any specific wisdom.⟧ ⟦It’s a vantage point, where more wisdom and compassion can come up, but problems can also look like they were never problems at all from up there, and that can ironically lead instead to more foolish indifference.⟧
HIS (published-20260928.md, Sometimes start with the whole, P4): If you can already notice and distinguish what is happening inside, you don’t need to keep rehearsing the Witness. Work with the warmth, protection, or direction that is actually missing. Higher Self language can help you gather around a meaningful source of care; it doesn’t establish that a perfectly capable inner parent is already available. Those adult functions still grow through practice.

=== The inner child, the guards, and the inner adult’s three jobs, P1 [block 91]
NEW: Your inner child won’t trust you right away. It’s not going to be enough for you to meditate, say three nice sentences to the inner child and expect to have its trust. What’s going to help your inner child to trust you is you, the person you are right now, becoming reliable.
HIS (published-20260928.md, The inner child, the guards, and the three inner adults, P1): The inner child won’t trust you because you said three kind sentences during one meditation. Trust grows when the present-day adult becomes reliable.

=== The inner child, the guards, and the inner adult’s three jobs, P2 [block 92]
NEW: Here I’m going to talk about what you develop in yourself as you reparent your inner child. ⟦I’m going to talk about it in terms of three jobs: your loving Nurturer, your safe Adult Protector (hereafter just the Protector) and your wise Guide.⟧ ⟦That’s one grown-up doing three jobs, and each job keeps the others in balance.⟧ It’s you that needs to develop these qualities.
HIS (published-20260928.md, The inner child, the guards, and the three inner adults, P2): I organize that adult capacity into three functions: Nurturer, Protector, and Leader/Guru. They aren’t three literal beings. They’re three ways the present-day person reparents the inner child.

=== The inner child, the guards, and the inner adult’s three jobs, P3 [block 93]
NEW: Also, your inner child’s worth is not dependent on these qualities… It has its own worth, and its own qualities, like playing, being tender, not taking life too seriously, being creative and wondering at the world. Those belong in your relationship with it too, not just the wounds.
HIS (published-20260928.md, The inner child, the guards, and the three inner adults, P3): The child’s worth doesn’t depend on developing those capacities. Worth was never the missing skill. The child also brings play, tenderness, irreverence, creativity, and wonder. Those qualities belong inside the adult relationship, not just the wounds.
