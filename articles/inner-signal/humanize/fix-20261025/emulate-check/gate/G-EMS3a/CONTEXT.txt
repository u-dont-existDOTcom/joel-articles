Memory, support, and choosing a practitioner

Chapter 7 of Trauma and Memory, by Peter Levine, 2015, is useful on the difference between a compelling experience and what really happened. This US Veterans Administration website contains useful general information about hypnosis, such as the fact that it can be very strong and should be carefully used, and that it may result in false memories. This ASCH website contains information about the usefulness of getting certified in hypnosis, and why it doesn't guarantee competence. Links are available for these and other useful groups in the evidence and specialist sections, but each one backs up only its own claim.
