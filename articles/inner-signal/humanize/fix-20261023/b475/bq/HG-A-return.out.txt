Bring Back: To be safe, you may want to use some version of the following generic bring back: When you come out of this session, you will only bring with you that which you feel is useful for you to use in your everyday life at this time. Anything else, you will bring with you at a later date when you are able to integrate it into your life. When you come out of this session, you will come back to the room you are in now and you will come back to the day you are participating in this session on. All of the instructions that I have given you to carry you deeper into this session or to keep you in this state for the duration of the session are now over. The only instruction that you are taking with you into your everyday life or cue that you will be using to carry you into this state again at a later date is the one you have consciously chosen to use and neither of them will keep you in this state. When you come out of this session, you will be you, in your body, with your adult judgment.

Counting Out: 1...begin to feel your body again and what it is resting on...feel your weight (insert your weight here or let the listener choose his/her own weight to feel) 2...your breathing beginning to return to normal again 3...begin to hear the room around you...knowing that you are (insert where listener will be when listening to recording) 4...wiggle your fingers and toes...move your shoulders...move your eyes 5...open your eyes when you are ready...back in the here and now...feeling normal and clear The session is now over.

When you come out, remind yourself what day (or date) it is. Look to your left and to your right. Look at something near and something far. Feel free to move around, but don't get up until you feel that you can do so without falling over. (Make sure that you are oriented and alert. Sometimes, counting out does this, but not always.) Take mental note of only that which you felt actually came up for you and what, if anything, you feel you need to do about it. Then go on and do something normal.

(In case the recording cuts out for some reason, tell the listener that he/she can say to him/her self, "The recording has stopped and the session is over." Then have him/her do the above on his/her own. Also, have the listener press his/her feet or hands into a surface when he/she opens his/her eyes.)

NOTE: If the listener is going to sleep to the recording, it is alright to let him/her go to sleep or rest quietly at the end of the session. However, if the listener is coming out, you should not let him/her out in an altered state, in a role, with lowered judgment, with impaired movement, blank, with a non-specific reset word or for an indefinite amount of time.
