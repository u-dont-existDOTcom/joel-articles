This is a generic bring back, feel free to use it. "When I come out of this session I will only bring with me that which is useful to me in my everyday life right now, anything else I will bring with me at a later date when I am better able to integrate it. When I come out of this session I will be coming back to the room I am in now, on the day I am participating in this session on, now. All the instructions you have given me to carry me deeper in to this session or keep me in this state are now finished, the only instruction I will take with me in to my every day life or the only cue I will use to take me in to this state at a later date will be the one I consciously choose to and neither of them will keep me in this state. When I come out of this session I will be me, in my body and with my adult judgement."

Now I will count up and on 5 you will be back in the here and now. 1... You begin to feel your body again, you feel it resting where ever it is resting, you feel your body weight, whatever you choose it to be. 2... Your breathing returns to normal again. 3... You begin to hear your surroundings again, you are in where ever you are. 4... Wiggle your fingers and toes, move your shoulders, move your eyes. 5... Open your eyes, you are back in the here and now feeling normal and clear. The session is now over.

When you come out make sure you remember what day (or date) it is, look to your left and right, look at something near to you and something further away. Move around a bit, don't get up till you feel like you can without falling over. Make sure that you are oriented and alert, counting somebody up will normally do this but not always. Just take mental note of only what you did bring up and whether there is anything you need to do about it then just go on and do something normal.

If the recording cuts out for some reason just say "The recording has stopped and the session is over" and do the above yourself. Once your eyes are open also press your feet or hands in to a surface.

Note: If you are going to sleep to a recording then it is fine to go to sleep or just remain resting quietly, however if you are coming out of a recording then you should not leave yourself in an altered state, this includes leaving yourself in a role, with lowered judgement, with lowered ability to move, blank or with a non specific reset word or for an indefinite length of time.
