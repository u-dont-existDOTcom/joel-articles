As you come out again, remind yourself what day (or what date) it is, look to your left and to your right. Look at something close to you, and something far away. Move around a bit. Don’t get up until you feel like you can do so without falling over (even though the counting should have made sure you were oriented and alert, sometimes it doesn’t). Remind yourself of anything that you need to do about whatever came up for you in this meditation, and go and do something normal.
