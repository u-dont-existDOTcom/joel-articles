Show yourself one sensation, one word, ask yourself if you want to listen today or if you need to rest.

Do you know what is fake? The words, image, is it something you are expecting of yourself, is it a role you are playing etc. It may not be that you don’t want to do the work, you don’t want a ‘grown up’ etc. It could be that you feel as if you have to do something that you don’t want to. If this is the case correct that, see if there is a part that is protecting you and talk to them.
