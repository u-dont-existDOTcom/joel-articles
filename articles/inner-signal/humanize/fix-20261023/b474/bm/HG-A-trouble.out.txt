Is there a way you could try this on a smaller scale?

Just looking at one sensation in the body / one word / deciding whether to do it or listen to something else today and give yourself a rest.

Do you have a sense of what it is that feels fake? The words, an image, what you are expecting to get from this, a role you feel you have to play etc. It may be that the fact you don't have an inner voice or image to do this with is what feels fake. It may not be because you are not grown up enough or that you are resisting doing the work. If it feels like you are doing something you don't want to do then correct that. It may be that there is a part of you that is protecting you in some way, so talk to that part.

"What are you protecting me from?" "Are you afraid I will be lying to myself again?" "Are you afraid this will be a performance?"
