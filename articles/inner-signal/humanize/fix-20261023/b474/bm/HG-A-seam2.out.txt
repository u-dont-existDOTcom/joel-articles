There is one thing that you should never do with this process. And that is to do it when you are driving or doing something where you need to be actively paying attention. It is not safe to go into a trance where you are actively paying attention to what is inside of you when you should be paying attention to what is outside of you. Even though your eyes will be open and you are using “if it would be useful for you to do so” kind of language, it is just not safe.

On the other hand, if for some reason you want to “turn this one off” …. all you have to do is not practice it anymore. Take it out of your recordings, take it out of your apps if you are doing that, and just start using your regular induction again. It would be a good idea to say (when you are awake) that you are no longer going to use it …. but just saying that it is gone doesn’t mean that it will be gone right away. If for some reason this is a problem for you (you are still responding to it when you don’t want to, or you are having problems with disorientation or whatever), then get some help from someone who is qualified to help you with that. If, on the other hand, you want to keep it around …. so that you can use it at a later date to go back into trance when you want to …. that’s fine too. But for now …. when you come out of this trance …. it will be over for today.
