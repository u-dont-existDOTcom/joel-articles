The Coming Out Part 2- Coming out of the Experience When you do come out, tell yourself what day it is (or what date it is), and look to your left and your right. Look at something nearby and something further away. Move around a bit, but don’t get up until you feel fairly steady. It is a good idea to make sure that you are oriented and alert (although the counting should have taken care of this). Take mental note of anything that you need to do in regard to what came up, and then proceed to do something normal.
