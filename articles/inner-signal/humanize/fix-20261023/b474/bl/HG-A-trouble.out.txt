Could you try to start small?

Showing yourself one sensation in your body/ one word/ listening or not listening today (rest)?

Do you know what is fake? The words, the image, what you are expecting, the role etc. It doesn’t matter that you don’t have the inner voice/image that does this. It may not be that you don’t have a ‘grown up’ or that you are resisting doing this kind of work. If it feels that you are doing something you don’t want to do, then correct this. It could be that a part of you is protecting you, so you could talk to this part of you.

“What are you protecting me from?” or “Are you afraid that I’m going to lie to myself again” or “Are you afraid that I’m going to do something that is a performance” etc.
