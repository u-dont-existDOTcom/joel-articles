## Item 1

1. MEANING: CHANGED.
   - Kept: stop practicing, take it out of recordings and apps, back to the regular induction. The get-help advice is still an imperative and still says "qualified". The trance ends when you come out.
   - Changed: "State your decision while awake" became "You may want to say that you won't use this one anymore in your waking life". An instruction is now an option, and "while awake" (when you say it) became "in your waking life" (where you won't use it).
   - Lost: "association" (now just "it"). "does not prove ... vanished instantly" became "doesn't mean it's going to be gone right away", which also implies it will go eventually.
   - Lost: "Persistent". The help trigger is now "a problem ... whatever": wider (the safe direction) but not the same.
   - Weaker and added: "a future, separately chosen session" became "at a later date when you want to" ("separately chosen" is gone), plus a new approval, "that's fine". "all you have to do is" makes the steps sound complete and easy (minor; the next two sentences soften it).
   - Smallest fixes: (a) "Say, while you're awake, that you won't use this one anymore, but saying that it's gone doesn't mean the association is gone right away." (b) "If it keeps causing you a problem". (c) "...go back into a trance in a later session that you choose separately, that's fine."
2. VOICE: OK. Casual words only ("whatever", "that's fine", "all you have to do"); no feeling or life fact added. ("that's fine" is a mild approval, which PUBLISHED already implies.)
3. SENSE: OK. "this one" reads as the shortcut straight after "Never do this", assuming the section already named it ("cue" never appears in NOW). The last "it" is the trance, and "over for today" leads into "Come all the way back".

VERDICT: CHANGED

## Item 2

1. MEANING: CHANGED.
   - Kept: reverse what you did, floated to weight back, small to regular size, present wholeness and body ownership, same route back.
   - Changed: "met a younger state or several parts" became "have regressed or are working with parts". Regressing (going back into a younger age) is not the same as meeting a younger state. The inner-child case is no longer named, so a reader who only met their younger self may think this step is not for them.
   - Changed: "adult judgment" became "under control of your adult mind" (judging became controlling). The script right after says "adult judgment", so the term no longer lines up.
   - Weaker: "Keep only the qualities you deliberately chose" became "only take ... the things that you want to". Wanting is weaker than a deliberate choice (the script right after says "consciously choose"). "qualities" to "things" is wider but harmless.
   - Added: nothing a reader would take as a new claim ("Etcetera.", "of course" and the "!" are emphasis).
   - Smallest fixes: "have regressed" to "have met a younger state"; "that it is under control of your adult mind" to "that you have your adult judgment"; "the things that you want to" to "the things that you deliberately choose to".
2. VOICE: OK. "Etcetera.", "of course" and the "!" are casual emphasis; no feeling, opinion or life fact added.
3. SENSE: OK. Directly under "Come all the way back" it does what the heading promises, and the examples settle that "when coming out of a trance" is when to reverse (not when the state was altered). Its last line leads into the "bring-back" script ("only bring with me...").

VERDICT: CHANGED
