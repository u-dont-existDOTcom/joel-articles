Yes I definitely do have this sense of unification. Though when I use the word unification I think of it more as what you are describing and using on a minute to minute basis, from talking, crossing the road, recognising people, texting, etc. It does include a lot of my day to day life and makes for a very interesting day! A good day if you are happy I guess, but very similar to what other people may experience as a day.

I have benefitted immensely from Soh, but I do not understand his claim that you don't need this sense of unification of the senses to function. That just doesn't make sense to me. I've always had a feeling that atR practitioners were making claims that they shouldn't be (John Tan often scolded Soh for this), and managed to prove one of them wrong when I met up with him in Singapore. At one point in the conversation Soh said that he wasn't in bliss 24/7, even though he claimed to be in bliss 24/7. He also wasn't really smiling at the time, so I asked him 'aren't you in bliss 24/7?' to which he said 'yes'. I said 'so you're in bliss now?' to which he said 'well no not right now' 😜
