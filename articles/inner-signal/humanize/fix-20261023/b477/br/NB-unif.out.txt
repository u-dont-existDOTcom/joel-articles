When I use the term unification, I mean that all of this happens. Without this, you could not carry on a conversation, cross the street, recognize friends or respond to text messages, etc. To be clear, the vast majority of people spend the vast majority of their time in this state (assuming they are generally happy with their life).

I am eternally grateful to Soh for his insights and guidance; however, he has at times said that unification of the senses is not necessary to function in the world. This does not make any sense. I have long suspected that AtR practitioners sometimes make claims that cannot be substantiated. In fact, this was a frequent complaint from John Tan to Soh. I was able to prove this on a very basic level during my first meeting with Soh in Singapore. He claimed to be in a state of 24/7 bliss, yet he was not smiling. I said to him, “I thought you were in bliss 24/7?” and he responded, “Yes.” I continued, “So you are in bliss right now?” and he responded, “Well, actually no.” 😜
