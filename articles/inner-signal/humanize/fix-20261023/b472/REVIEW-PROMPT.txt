## Item 2 (b472_s3b)

SECTION: Come all the way back

THE PUBLISHED PARAGRAPH (Joel's guide as published):
Name the day or date. Look left, right, near, and far. Move before standing, and stand only when steady. Check that you are actually oriented and alert; repeating the count is not proof of a complete return. Note only what actually appeared, and any action it calls for. Then do something normal.

BEFORE:
Then count up, using these five steps: 1...feel your body again and what it's resting on, feel your weight (of your choice). 2...feel your breathing return to normal. 3...hear the room, name where you are. 4...wiggle your fingers and toes, move your shoulders and your eyes. 5...open your eyes, back in the here and now, feeling normal and clear. This session is now over.

WAS:
When you come out, remind yourself what day (or date) it is. Look to your left and your right. Look at something near, and something far. Move around a bit before getting up. Get up only when you feel steady. Make sure you're truly oriented and alert. Don't just assume it because you've done the count. Take mental note of anything that has come up for you and any actions you need to take regarding it. Then go on and do something normal.

NOW:
When you come out, remind yourself what day (or date) it is. Look to your left and your right. Look at something near, and something far. Move around a bit, and don't get up until you feel steady. Make sure you're actually oriented and alert (the count is supposed to bring you back, but it doesn't always do the whole job). Take mental note of anything that has come up for you and any actions you need to take regarding it. Then go on and do something normal.

AFTER:
If your recording cuts out for some reason, you can say to yourself, "The recording has stopped and the session is over," and then do the above on your own. Once your eyes are open, press your feet or hands into a surface as well.

THE CHANGE:
"Move around a bit before getting up. Get up only when you feel steady. Make sure you're truly oriented and alert. Don't just assume it because you've done the count." -> "Move around a bit, and don't get up until you feel steady. Make sure you're actually oriented and alert (the count is supposed to bring you back, but it doesn't always do the whole job)."
