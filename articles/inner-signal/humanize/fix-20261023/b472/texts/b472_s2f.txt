If you ever want to retire the shortcut, stop practicing it, take it out of your recordings and any apps that play it, and go back to your usual induction. Decide that while you’re wide awake, and tell yourself so. Don’t be surprised if it still works on you for a while, though, since saying it’s gone doesn’t erase it overnight. You can also keep it, but only to start a session you’ve chosen ahead of time. If it keeps pulling you in when you don’t want it to, or you come out of it disoriented, find someone qualified to help you with it, like a therapist who knows hypnosis.
On the way out, undo whatever you did to get in, and only bring back the things you actually want. If you floated up like a balloon, come back down and feel your weight again (whatever weight you choose, of course). If you shrank, grow back to your normal size. If you were little again for a while, or spent time with your parts, come back to being all of you, here and now, in a body that’s yours, with your adult self in the driver’s seat. If you went somewhere, come back the way you came. Etcetera.
