Then count up, using these five steps: 1...feel your body again and what it’s resting on, feel your weight (of your choice). 2...feel your breathing return to normal. 3...hear the room, name where you are. 4...wiggle your fingers and toes, move your shoulders and your eyes. 5...open your eyes, back in the here and now, feeling normal and clear. This session is now over.
When you come out, remind yourself what day (or date) it is. Look to your left and your right. Look at something near, and something far. Move around a bit before getting up, and only get up once you feel steady. Then check that you’re really oriented and alert, because doing the count doesn’t always bring you all the way back. Take mental note of anything that has come up for you and any actions you need to take regarding it. Then go on and do something normal.
