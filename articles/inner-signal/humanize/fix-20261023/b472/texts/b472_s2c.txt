If you want to turn the shortcut off, stop practicing it, take it out of your recordings and out of any apps you’ve set up with it, and go back to your usual induction. Tell yourself while you’re awake that you won’t use it anymore, but don’t expect that just saying it’s gone will make it go away right away. If you keep responding to it when you don’t want to, or you feel disoriented after using it, get help from someone who’s qualified. You can keep it if you want, but only for starting a session later on, when you choose to. Either way, today’s trance ends when you come back.
Make sure to reverse anything you’ve done to alter your state when you come out of it. If you’ve floated, make sure you regain your weight (of your choice, of course). If you’ve shrunk, grow to your normal size again. If you’ve regressed or met parts, make sure you come back to feeling complete in the here and now and acknowledge that your body belongs to you and is under control of the adult mind. If you’ve gone somewhere, make sure you go back the same way. Etcetera. Make sure that the only things you bring out with you are the ones you want to.
