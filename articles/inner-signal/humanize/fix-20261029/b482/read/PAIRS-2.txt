P561 (Reframing — changing the meaning without lying, P1)

PUBLISHED (the meaning reference; it may cover more than this paragraph):
“What else might be true?” “What is this part trying to do for me?” “What would the Nurturer, the Protector, and the Leader each say about this?”

If vulnerability appears—a knot in the throat, sadness, fear, or a sense of trying to protect something—you can bring in the Nurturer. If the experience remains hostile or becomes more frightening, stop engaging with it and return to the body and room.

BEFORE:

PUBLISHED (the meaning reference; it may cover more than this paragraph):
A past opening can be a resource without becoming a state you must reproduce. Having experienced love once, being able to access it only in a particular state, and contacting it reliably in daily life are different things. Let the experience widen what seems possible while you build usable access. If the child cannot receive it, meet the difficulty rather than escalating the intensity.

Return to the difficult material only when you choose. If you’re losing track of the present, becoming increasingly unreal, or unable to shift or stop, end inward exploration and use the full return. Persistent disorientation needs human help rather than a stronger induction.

BEFORE:
A lot of people have OPENED themselves up to love at one point in their life. If you've had a similar experience, know that there is a difference between having experienced it at one point, or only being able to access it in a certain state, and being able to get to it at any point in your life. Let that experience show you what's possible, while you build a way to get to it in everyday life. (If you can't give that love to the child, try to deal with why you can't, instead of trying to ramp up the experience. Often it’s a protective part that’s seen love come with strings before. Hear it out first.)

P755 (9. No-trance inner contact, P1)

PUBLISHED (the meaning reference; it may cover more than this paragraph):
The induction and inner meeting bring you to a place where you can listen. From here, learn to answer what happens instead of reciting a speech that has already decided what every part will say. Read these examples before practicing, then put the screen aside.

Observable pacing: Keep the eyes open, name the room and day, feel the feet, and read or write in ordinary awareness. Invitation: Ask one clear question and wait without narrowing attention further. Genuine choice: Answer, write “unsure,” rest, change the question, seek another person’s view, or stop. Autonomous signal: Record a word, sensation, impulse, objection, or silence without interpreting it as proof. Deepener: None. Full alertness is not an inferior version of the practice. Handoff: Let the internal adult respond in writing or through one practical choice. Integration and exit: Reality-test the signal and do one small action. Because no altered state was cultivated, simply close the exercise and continue the day.

BEFORE:
For the no trance inner contact, I would do: Pacing: notice you can see, it's (day of the week), you have feet, you can read, you can write, etc. Suggestion: in your mind, ask one clear question and wait, but don't focus too hard. Choice: you can answer the question, write an answer, write that you don't know, take a break, rephrase the question, ask someone else, stop doing this, etc. Autonomous signal: write whatever you get (a word, sensation, impulse, objection, or silence) without interpreting it as proof. Deepener: none (full alertness isn't a lesser version). Handoff: have your adult self answer the question in whatever way is appropriate, preferably by writing or doing. Integration/exit: reality test the signal and do one small thing based on it. Since you didn't go into trance, just close the exercise and carry on with your day.

AFTER:
For the no trance inner contact, I would do: Pacing: notice you can see, it's (day of the week), you have feet, you can read, you can write, etc. Suggestion: in your mind, ask one clear question and wait, but don't focus too hard. Choice: you can answer the question, write an answer, write that you don't know, take a break, rephrase the question, ask someone else, stop doing this, etc. Autonomous signal: write down what you get, whether that's a word, a sensation, an impulse, an objection, or silence, and don't take it as proof. Deepener: none. Doing the inner contact fully alert doesn't make it a lesser version. Handoff: have your adult self answer the question however makes sense, ideally by writing something or doing something. Integration/exit: reality test the signal, then act on it in one small way.
