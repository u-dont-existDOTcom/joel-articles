Your inherited parent script

One important thing to become aware of when approaching your inner child is your inherited parent script. Your parent script is made up of a collection of rules and ways of treating yourself that you adopted as a child (mostly based on how you were treated by your parent(s)). It doesn’t always take the form of a voice. This is not the inner adult (Nurturer, Protector, Guide) that you consciously build. Often this script acts as a critic or warden, telling your inner child not to cry, not to be “needy”, not to embarrass them, not to be too independent, etc. So when you begin talking to your child in a new, loving way, the script may jump in to say that what you’re doing is “fake”.
