Appendix G: Altered states

This is NOT to say that you SHOULD use substances, but many people have used them when they first did self-hypnosis, contacted their inner child, or talked to their Higher Self. I’d learn the inner child work sober first, with enough Nurturer and Protector in the room before you go deep (see the inner child reparenting section). Borrow some from someone else if you need to, but make sure they're stable and not someone who will try to direct your trip.

Do not do it to try to FORCE yourself to get to a certain revelation about yourself, but rather to help yourself become more in tune with love and to better your life.

A lot of people have OPENED themselves up to something at one point in their life. If you've had a similar experience, know that there is a difference between having experienced it at one point, or only being able to access it in a certain state, and being able to get to it at any point in your life. Let that experience inform what you OPEN yourself up to. (If you can't give it to the child, try to deal with why you can't, instead of trying to ramp up the experience. Often it’s a protective part that’s seen love come with strings before. Hear it out first.)

Use your altered state of consciousness, either while you're in it or after, to help yourself by asking things like:

Help me to become more grounded. Help me to only take what I can integrate. Help me to remember love. Help me to come back when it's time. Help me to figure out how to change one thing in my life to make it better.

DO NOT use self-hypnosis to try to keep the altered state of consciousness going indefinitely, to try to recover memories, to make yourself MORE afraid, to help you not need to sleep, to help you not need to take care of medical problems, to help you become LESS communicative, etc.

In other words, if using self-hypnosis while you're in an altered state of consciousness is NOT helping you become MORE loving, MORE grounded, MORE honest with yourself, helping you SLEEP, become MORE communicative, MORE able to do your everyday responsibilities, etc, then stop trying to go DEEPER into your altered state of consciousness, and start working on integrating what you've already gotten from it.
