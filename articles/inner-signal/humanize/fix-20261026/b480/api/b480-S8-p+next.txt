Instead, you might feel numb, cynical, angry, hungry, like you need to look at your phone, sexually aroused, like you need to make plans, sleepy, dissociated, or you might even think that what you're doing is stupid. Those are not failures. Often they are you experiencing a very charming guard. It's what protects you when your consciously installed parental Protector isn't functioning, the same as when you were a child. Before figuring out what/where it is, make sure to ask what it is trying to stop or what it wants. It may, of course, be your inner child, mad that it’s taken you so long to come back, or it may be your inherited parent script, or something from earlier in the day, or a combination.

(To the guard, in the same Nurturer voice you meant for your inner child: “I see you. I'm not going to force my way past you. What's going on?” Then wait and listen patiently. If nothing comes, that's fine for now. At some point you can ask, “What are you afraid would happen if you stopped protecting me by…..?”)
