Here I’m going to talk about what you develop in yourself as you reparent your inner child. I’m going to talk about it in terms of three jobs: your loving Nurturer, your safe Adult Protector (hereafter just the Protector) and your wise Guide. It’s one person doing three jobs rather than getting three more people to move in with you to help with the rent! Each job keeps the others in balance. It’s you that needs to develop these qualities.
