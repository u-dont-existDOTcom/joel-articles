Another thing to be aware of as you work with approaching your inner child is to be aware of your inherited parent script. This is a set of rules (ways of treating yourself) that you inherited mostly from your parent(s) when you were a child. It doesn’t always take the form of a voice (it’s not the same as your inner adult, a/k/a Nurturer, Protector, Guide, etc., which you will build up intentionally later). Often it is critical, or warden-like (“Don’t cry! Don’t be so ‘needy’. Don’t embarrass people. Don’t be so independent. Etc.”). So, when you approach your inner child and try a new way of talking to it, your inherited parent script may come in and say something like, “What you’re doing is fake.”
