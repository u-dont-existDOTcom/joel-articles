This is a tricky one, as we can not force it and it needs to come from a place of understanding so as not to be bypassing. Yet if we are stuck in the anger forever, we are allowing that injury to determine how we live our life now. So how do we hold all of this? We remember that it can all be true at once. Yes, they hurt us. Yes, they need to answer for it. Yes, we still need a boundary with this person but one day, we may find that we have forgiven them or at the very least, we are no longer being run by the resentment and we didn’t even have to do it first to be safe and have boundaries and hold them accountable and get justice and to get better.

One helpful way of understanding here can be that while the person’s moves were harmful, in a sense they could only make the moves they made based on their accumulated nature at the time. This does not get them out of the fact that they have harmed you or that you can not hold them accountable or have them face consequences or work to make different moves etc. It can help us to come to a place in which we are no longer expecting something from them that they can not give, while also not making it ok that they can not make those moves.

We don’t have to do it before we are ready and we don’t have to be spiritual about it to not hurt, but we also don’t have to build our life around this forever.
