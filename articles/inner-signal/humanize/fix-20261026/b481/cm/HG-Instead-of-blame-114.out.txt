The next time you find yourself in the courtroom of your mind trying a version of yourself from a younger age, ask yourself some more productive questions: What was that age of you trying to get or get away from? What did he/she know at the time? What choices did he/she think he/she had? What did he/she think would happen if he/she took those choices? What did he/she not have that he/she needed, whether it was something you needed to be able to do yourself, or some help or protection you needed from others? What can you learn from this now, or what can you do to make things right now, and what will you do differently in the future when you're in a similar situation? No matter how much what that younger you did may have harmed you and others, punishing yourself for it now won't give that younger you what he/she needed at the time.
