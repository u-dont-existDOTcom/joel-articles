Just a quick note. I feel that I need to make clear that reframing is not meant to be a way of being dishonest with yourself. It isn't meant to convince yourself that what you're saying is the problem is actually a good thing. The prompt for a reframe is: Is there anything else that might be true? or What might this part be trying to do? or How might your Nurturer reframe that? or How might your Protector reframe that? or How might your Guide reframe that? etc, etc.
