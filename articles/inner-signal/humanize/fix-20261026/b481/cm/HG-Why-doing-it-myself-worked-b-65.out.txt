Self hypnosis is something that I find I can do usefully on my own that I couldn't do, or at least didn't do, very much with a 'pro' hypnotist. One reason was that I was sober when I did that, and wasn't that susceptible to hypnosis. But I think a bigger reason is that

when I do it on my own I know when I'm not saying something to my subconscious mind in just quite the right way. And I can talk to my subconscious mind and ask it what it thinks is not quite right about what I just said. Similarly, when I'm working with an image, I can tell the difference between an image that's telling me something useful, and one that's either telling me what I'm afraid of, what I want to avoid, what I want to be true, or something that I just don't know how to understand yet. Similarly, I can tell when my body is telling me no before my conscious mind convinces my body to say yes.
