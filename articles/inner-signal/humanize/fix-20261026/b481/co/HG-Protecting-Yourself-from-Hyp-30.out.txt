There are a lot of ways to influence people without engaging their conscious mind. The methods marketers and interface designers use are well-known. The Federal Trade Commission has a report on their “dark patterns” - designs used to trick and manipulate consumers. The Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development notes patterns used commercially to “steer, deceive, coerce, or manipulate people into taking actions that may otherwise be against their interests.”

Conscious agreement, unconscious receptivity, emotional compliance, legal and ethical consent - can people have their minds influenced without the conscious agreement of their adult self?
