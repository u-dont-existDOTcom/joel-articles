When you feel like you’re being pushed too hard at something (whether its a person, advertisement, ideology, spiritual teaching, woman you’re attracted to, voice inside your head, etc.) say to yourself, “Okay, I can sense that something is trying to influence me. I don’t have to obey it. I don’t even have to attack it. But I do need to slow myself down and ask myself some questions. What would this “thing” influence me to believe that isn’t true in order to get what it wants? What is my body telling me to do here (how is my body feeling)? What would love do here? What would truth do here? How would I approach this humbly? What would life have me do here?”
