5. Temporary Separation: It may be useful to remove yourself from certain people or a group that you normally would go to for answers. Sometimes you will come back to those people on different terms, but sometimes you will need to separate for a long time because you have such an ingrained pattern of losing yourself with those people. As you differentiate, you will know which is which. You need to do whatever it takes to allow your own preferences to bubble up within you, and you need to know that they aren’t the same as the people that you are with socially.

What’s the difference between a “human being” and a “human doing”? If you don’t feel good enough as a human being, then you will need to feel good enough as a “human doing.” This means that you need to be beautiful, or good at something, or smart, or have a certain status, or be spiritual, etc. While you may indeed be beautiful, or good at something, or smart, etc., if you feel that if you aren’t then you won’t be good enough as a human being, then you have made a deal with yourself that is preventing you from knowing your own inherent value.
