Try speaking to the guard in your Nurturer voice (the same voice you might use to comfort your inner child): “I see that you’re there. I don’t want to go past you, I just want to find out what’s going on.” Then wait and listen patiently. If you don’t get any information, that’s okay, you can always come back to it later and ask, “What are you afraid will happen if you stop protecting me by…..?”
