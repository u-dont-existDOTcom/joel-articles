7. Ending the session It isn't necessary to end the session with any great revelation! When you feel you have done enough in the session, it's useful to find out if there is anything you need to do to enable you to leave things where they are for the time being. This might be thanking some parts for helping you, or making sure you do something in real life (eat a good meal, put a boundary in place, get some rest, repair a relationship, keep a promise etc). Whatever it is, tell your little one, 'That's enough for today. We're going back to the life we're building.'
