Please don’t put six metaphors in one trance

Keep it simple. One thing you're doing, one anchor/image you're going with at first, one way of coming back, and feel free to set a tone (warm, bounded, directed, neutral witnessing, whatever) for yourself. If you're going to use metaphors just do one, and if you switch metaphors, do it in a way that's conscious.

Using metaphors and imagery is entirely optional. You can use whatever form of signal makes sense to you (feelings, rhythms, movements, words, spatial, simple knowing, lack of signal, whatever) but if you're using words, try to keep them related to whatever world you're creating. If you're going to count, do it in a way that makes sense in your world. When you're done, make sure to come back the other way. (You can borrow qualities, but don't change who you are.)

When you're writing this, think about what the verb you're using is, what the sensory quality is, what the short refrain is, and how you're going to transition between ideas. And then read it out loud as you write it. Long, elegant sentences are NOT going to help you when you’re trying to induce. It’s all soup to you at that point.
