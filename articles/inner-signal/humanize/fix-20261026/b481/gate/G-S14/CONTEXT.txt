Protecting Yourself from Hypnotists

Hypnotism can happen in a therapist’s office, but it can also happen through music, marketing, religious rituals, sexual chemistry, charismatic authority, social media, grief, fear, political messaging, sense of belonging, fatigue, love, trauma, beauty, etc.

There are a lot of ways to influence people without engaging their conscious mind. Marketers and interface designers know this perfectly well. The Federal Trade Commission has a report on “dark patterns” - designs that trick or manipulate consumers. The Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) describes commercial patterns that steer, deceive, coerce, or manipulate people toward choices that may work against their interests.

Conscious agreement, unconscious receptivity, emotional compliance, legal and ethical consent - can people have their minds influenced without the conscious agreement of their adult self?

As associated with the Silva Mind Control method:

“If you don’t learn to control your mind, someone else will!”

With self-hypnosis you can do much of the same thing from the inside, by befriending and listening to the deeper part of your mind. This opens up a whole new area of choice for you as an adult in the world.
