When not engaging the conscious mind, there are still numerous ways to influence a person. This is known by marketers and interface designers. The Federal Trade Commission recently published a report on “dark patterns,” or designs that trick or manipulate people. The OECD calls these “patterns that have been designed by commercial entities to induce users to make choices that may not be in their best interest by steering, deceiving, coercing, and/or manipulating them.”
