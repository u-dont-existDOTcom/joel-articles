In the same way, we can not force ourselves to forgive someone. It has to come from a place of understanding or it can end up being a bypass. But, if we continue to live in a place of being angry forever, we are allowing that injury to run our life in the present. How can we hold all of this? We just have to remember that it can all be true. Yes, they hurt us and they have to answer for it & we still need to have a boundary with that person, but one day we may get to a place of forgiveness, or at the very least we may get to a place where we are not allowing the resentment to run our lives. And we don’t even have to get to the place of forgiveness in order to make ourselves safe, set boundaries, hold the person accountable, get justice, get better, etc.

It can be helpful to understand that the person could only really make the moves that they made based on where they were at with their accumulated nature. This doesn’t mean that we can’t hold them accountable for the harm they caused or allow them to face the consequences of their actions, or work on making different moves, etc., but it can help us get to a place where we are no longer expecting something from them that they are not able to give us due to the limitations of their nature. It also doesn’t mean that their limitations are not important.
