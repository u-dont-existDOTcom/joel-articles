You may want to try speaking to your guard in the same voice you use to speak to your inner child, the voice of your Nurturer. You may say something like: "I see you and I'm not going to try to force my way past you. I'd just like to understand what's going on here. So tell me, what is it you're protecting me from?" Then wait and listen patiently for whatever may come. If nothing comes, that's OK for now, but at some point you may want to ask, "What are you afraid will happen if you stop protecting me by…..?"
