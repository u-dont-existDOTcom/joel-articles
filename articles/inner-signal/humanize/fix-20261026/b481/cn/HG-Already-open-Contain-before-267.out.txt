Ground yourself: take a look around, where are you? When is it? How does your body feel? Where are you grounded? Can you feel your feet? Your hands? Your spine? ... Take some time to establish some contact with yourself, for instance by pressing the tip of one of your thumbs against the tip of the corresponding index finger, not too hard, but firmly enough to feel some friction. Concentrate on this feeling.

Protect yourself: tell yourself something along the lines of "I will only take in what I can integrate. If there is too much, it can wait. My body will remain safe. I will experience this as an adult, not as my inner child" or similar.
