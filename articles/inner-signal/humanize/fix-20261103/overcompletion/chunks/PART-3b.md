# Part 3b of the guide: blocks 306 to 357

## Context before this part (read it, don't report on it)

[304] ### Turning the shortcut off, or keeping it

[305] If you want to turn the shortcut off, simply don't practice it anymore. Take it out of your recordings, take it out of your apps if you're using it in an app, and start using your regular induction. Tell yourself, when you're awake, that you won't be using it anymore. Just because you say that it'll be gone doesn't mean that it'll be gone right away. If it's causing you a problem, if you're still responding to it when you don't want to respond to it, if you're having issues with disorientation, etc, get some help from someone who's qualified to help you with this. If, on the other hand, you want to keep it so that you can use it to go back into trance at a later date when you do want to go into a trance, that's fine too. Keeping it doesn't keep today's trance going, though: once you come back, today's session is over.

## The part to read

[306] # Come all the way back

[307] If you have done anything to alter your state when entering the trance, make sure to reverse those things. If you have floated, make sure to gain your weight again (whatever weight you want of course). If you have shrunk, make sure to grow back to your regular size. If you have met a younger state or are working with parts, make sure to come back to feeling complete in your body in the here and now, that your body belongs to you, and that it is under the control of your adult mind. If you have gone somewhere, make sure to come back the same way you went. Etcetera.

[308] Here is a generic bring-back that you can use: "When I come out of this session, I will only bring with me that which is useful to me in my everyday life right now. Anything else I will bring with me at a later date, when I am better able to integrate it. When I come out of this session, I will be coming back to the room I am in now, and to the day in which I am participating in this session now. All the instructions to carry me deeper into this session or keep me in this state are now finished. The only intention I will take with me into my everyday life, and the only cue I will use to take me into this state at a later date, will be the ones I consciously choose, and neither of them will keep me in this state. When I come out of this session I will be me, in my body and with my adult judgment."

[309] Then do a count up: On 1 start to feel your body again and what it's resting on and feel your weight (of your choice). On 2 start to feel your breathing return to normal. On 3 start to hear the room around you and name where you are. On 4 start to wiggle your fingers and toes, move your shoulders and your eyes. On 5 open your eyes, back in the here and now, feeling normal and clear and the session is now over.

[310] When you come out, make sure you can name what day (or date) it is, look to your left and right, look at something near to you and something further away. Move around a bit. Don't get up until you feel like you can do so without falling over. Make sure that you are oriented and alert (counting does this, but not always). Make a mental note of just what actually came up and anything you need to do about it, then go and do something normal, like eating or washing up.

[311] If your recording happens to cut out partway through, woops... you could end up lying there dazed & confused, waiting on a voice that isn't coming back. Don't worry, you don't have to be a vegetable forever. Just tell yourself, "The recording has stopped and we're all done now. Welcome back to me!" (that exclamation actually helps, because trance is kinda monotone). Then do the exit-trance count and the present-time checks above on your own. Once your eyes are open, "touch grass" as they say, for grounding. Or if no grass, just push your adorable feet or hands into something solid. The physical feeling connects your brain to your body.

[312] Sleep recordings are another story, since you're assuming you'll drift off midway with those. But if the sleep recording is designed to also wake you back up, make sure it's doing so intelligently, not just using a vague exit word that might half wake you up and still leave you a bit blank or "under the spell" so to speak.

[313] # The actual inner meeting

[314] Note: If you have a tendency to daydream, dissociate or fall asleep, it is better to do this exercise sitting up and save lying down for when you want to go to sleep, get a very deep rest or incubate dreams. If you are sitting, keep your spine straight but not rigid like a soldier. Relax your jaw and stay somewhat connected to the room you are in.

[315] “I am not leaving life; I am listening more deeply to life.

[316] I invoke my Higher Self, universal heart-mind, wise subconscious mind, intelligence in my body, inner child(ren), inner adult(s), guard(s) and all that is of truth and love to help me know what I need to know, only in a form and at a time that will be useful, kind, stabilizing and real for me.”

[317] Ask your conscious mind to listen, and don't force an answer.

[318] This does not mean you have to stop thinking to listen. Just notice your thoughts, body sensations, pictures, doubts, emotions, etc. Don’t fight what comes up, but don’t go with it either. Just notice what stands out on its own as you hold the idea of what you are doing in the back of your mind.

[319] Internally say, “Tell me what I need to know or ask me what you need to know and I’ll sit in silence for 5 minutes and listen.”

[320] You may hear a phrase, feel a body sensation, notice a memory, see a color, be contacted by your inner child or a guard, feel pain, warmth, complete silence, know that you need to do something, be grateful for something, feel you need to stop the listening or any number of things.

[321] Listen to everything and believe nothing automatically.

[322] # Let the next response guide you

[323] The induction and the inner meeting get you to where you can listen. After that, it's about answering what happens, instead of reading out a script that has already decided what every part of you is going to say. Read through these sections, and then try doing the inner meeting without looking at the screen.

[324] Respond to each thing that comes up with an accurate notice and a small reasonable invitation (or action or taking more time etc). The thing might be a sensation, image, gesture, feeling, words, or anything. The notice and invitation don't need to cover all those forms, just notice and invite what happens.

[325] ## Finding your own words

[326] An example notice might be, "I notice my chest tightening when I imagine asking for help". Don't add interpretation like "this must be the abandoned child part of me, or a protective mechanism" - just notice what you notice. An example invitation might be, "And as I notice that, I can take some time before deciding what it means, and notice if I want to say anything, or change it, or just stay like that".

[327] Then just listen. You don't need fancy language - often it will be better to just ask what happens next, or whatever. If you notice a feeling developing, it might be best not to say anything while it's developing (if you can help it), but some days you need to talk to understand, so just do that.

[328] You can be pretty straightforward when that's the help you chose - "Let the explanation wait a moment. What do I feel as I say that?" can be a perfectly valid thing to say to yourself, and help you focus. As long as you're still present, and able to change if you want to, and happy to carry on, a sad feeling or tension or whatever doesn't require an exit.

[329] ### Notice, meaning, and your thoughts on the meaning

[330] It's worth trying to separate the notice, the meaning, and your thoughts on the meaning, but in practice it will often be pretty fuzzy. When you can, it can be helpful to separate it out like that though - "I noticed... this seemed to mean... here's why that might be true / false" or something like that.

[331] Is this a notice or meaning? "My stomach went tight" is a notice, "He's a complete bastard" is a meaning, even if you didn't think it in words. You can then go on to find out what he did, notice what it was that gave you the idea, and consider if there might be other explanations. It's also ok to put a meaning into words now, to look at it, even if it came to you without words. Nonverbal thoughts and early thoughts aren't necessarily any deeper, truer, or more reliable than other thoughts. Feelings are good to pay attention to, but it's still good to consider what they mean.

[332] ## Let the response change the invitation

[333] Your shoulders tightening, or 'hmm, that feels kind of wrong', is a signal to investigate further, but 'No! That is Not Right!' is a signal to stop what you're doing and change it. If you don't feel like it fits, don't keep doing it, and don't try saying it again more persuasively. Just change it. You don't need any other reason.

[334] "I notice the tightness, and I can just leave it like that. Then again, is that what I really want?"

[335] Sometimes the invitation just isn't right or helpful, even if it sounds like something a hypnotist might say. Each time we play around with this, we explore new possibilities which bring up new inner signals for us to feel out.

[336] Doubting is sometimes a guard to listen to, and sometimes simply part of normal consideration. Lack of doubt doesn't necessarily mean the process is working in the best way, either.

[337] ## Look at the situation too

[338] If there is another person involved in the situation, it may be a good idea to look at what they are doing as well. Can they hear you disagree, take responsibility, and make room for what you're going through? Try and be specific in what you conclude about them, and be willing to change it if you find out that it was wrong. Just because someone is disagreeing with you or trying to set boundaries with you does not mean they are in their emotionally immature parts. You don’t need to subject yourself to something that may be dangerous before protecting yourself.

[339] Look at your own side too. You may find that you feel guilty. Having feelings of guilt does not mean that you have done something wrong or that you have been manipulated. Have you done something that was wrong or that caused harm? If so, what can you do to fix it? On the other hand, did you give in to someone else just to keep them from being disappointed in you? What do you want to do or achieve the next time you interact with this person? Do you need them to understand what you are feeling/thinking? Do you just need to communicate information to them? Do you need to set boundaries with them? Do you need to end the interaction? You don’t have to make them understand before you can act.

[340] ## Change the amount of contact

[341] When you start to contact the difficult thing, if it feels too much, immediately start to widen your awareness to something else in the room, in your body, etc. Keep moving back and forth between the difficult thing and the easier thing. This is called pendulation, and approaching in smaller amounts is titration. Do as much or as little as you feel you can.

[342] You can say, "I can let this much be here, and notice what's supporting me right now." And when you've had enough, "Ok, I think that is enough for now."

[343] Feel free to go back and explore more if you wish to do so. If, at any point, you start to lose contact with the here and now, feel that things are becoming more and more unreal, or you feel that you are not able to switch to something else or stop, stop trying to explore inside of yourself and just do the entire "coming back" part. If you have trouble orienting yourself and it lasts for a prolonged period of time, please get some human help rather than trying a stronger induction.

[344] ### If you feel an urge to move

[345] If you feel an urge to move, turn, reach, push, or change your body’s position, feel free to either actually do something that will not cause your body pain or discomfort, or just imagine doing what you feel you need to do, and see what happens next. You do not need to act out the urge, shake, release, etc. If you do move and you feel that you are causing yourself pain or other symptoms that you are concerned about, stop moving.

[346] ## Offer the care that is actually wanted

[347] If any younger parts come up, offer them the opportunity to spend time with you, or some space if they would rather. If one of them says “don’t come closer”, stay at the distance they choose. You don’t have to consider them literally younger, nor do you have to ask them why they are there or what happened to them. If you can identify any guards (childhood protector parts), talk to them in the same Nurturer voice you meant for your little one, and find out what they are concerned about. All of them are welcome to the meeting. You don’t have to get rid of any of them.

[348] If you feel that it would be useful to bring in your Nurturer, Protector or Guide to help you either change the tone of the interaction, set boundaries, or find your way toward the life you want, feel free to do so. You don’t have to talk to them, just use the one whose job you need, and if you can’t do it yet, borrow it. You only need to use the Witness if you need help getting started with a step. If you are already able to do a certain step, you don’t have to do it again.

[349] Try some different ideas as to what might be helpful for this inner part to feel better (being welcomed by you, visualizing someone else, giving them the sort of words they would like to hear, you placing yourself in a different body position, you moving further away etc). Only try one idea at a time to see if it works.

[350] It is possible that when you try and give the part what it wants, you might feel very upset. It may be that it never got what it needed at the time and now that you are giving it what it wants, it is processing the upset of not getting what it wanted before. That doesn’t mean your love isn’t working. If this happens, allow yourself to respond for as long as you want to do so, and for as long as you can stay there (ie remain in the process), but don’t push for more tears, since crying more doesn’t mean you’re healing more. If you feel that the voice, distance, touch, image etc is too much, then change it to something that is less. Don’t think that you can’t take the love.

[351] Allow yourself to see where a metaphor takes you. Ask questions like ‘if the problem had an image or story, what would it be?’ or ‘what would need to happen to make things right?’ If it feels like you need to do something to give yourself the resource that you feel you need (or to put a boundary in place, or to move away or to take some other action) then try doing that, as far as it helps you. Remember that whatever you find out is likely to be metaphorical. It may be a good hypothesis for what you need to do, but it isn’t a record of what actually happened.

[352] Alternatively, you may want to use the session to build up something you want more of (eg love, play, courage, curiosity etc). You can also use it to rehearse something that you have decided to do.

[353] ## Finish without forcing a revelation

[354] Don’t feel that you have to end the session with a great revelation (those are often flaky, not standing the test of time). When you have done as much as you want to do, ask yourself what would help you leave things where they are for now. This might include thanking parts for their help, or making sure that you do something in real life (put a boundary in place, get some rest, repair a relationship, keep a promise etc). You can tell your little one, "That’s enough for today, choochoo. Aren't you excited to get back to the life we're building?"

[355] When you have finished, even if you stopped part way through, use the ‘Come all the way back’ procedure to bring yourself back. When you are back, it is a good idea to write a few lines in a notebook about what you have done. Just jot down the essentials (what was there before you started, what you have done to change things, what happened, what you are unsure about, any phrases that were said, any body signals, any information that the inner child said that it needed, anything that a guard wanted you to know, any boundaries that you put in place, any repairs that you did, anything you learned about what not to force etc). You don’t have to list every part of you that might have been involved, only what you actually noticed.

[356] ### Throughout the exercise

[357] Allow your analytical part to make comments and/or tell you that it doesn’t like what you are doing. Just tell it that you will check things out, and make any commitments etc, when you are back. If it says no to going deeper, or tells you to stop doing the exercise, I’d stop there. You can stop whenever you want to. Remember that the images you see can be meaningful without being memories of things that actually happened. And just because you see an image, or feel a body release, doesn’t mean you are right about what is going on.

## Context after this part (read it, don't report on it)

[358] # The 15-minute daily practice

[359] You don't need to learn a second script for this. It's the same process (gather your attention, notice what's here, respond, wait, and come all the way back), just for fifteen minutes or so. That is long enough to induce yourself (it only takes a few minutes), practice and let it unfold at its own pace, and come out again (that takes a few minutes too). Fifteen minutes is just a handy appointment with yourself, though, and your feelings don't have to keep to it. Five minutes is plenty to see what happens when you simply sit in silence, listening, and you can interrupt yourself at any time to change direction if that feels better.
