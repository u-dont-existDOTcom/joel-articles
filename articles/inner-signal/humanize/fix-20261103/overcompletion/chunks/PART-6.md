[638] # Appendix F: Self-trust, outside guidance, and oracle-like states

[639] [For using synchronicity as a cue rather than an oracle, see “Using synchronicity as your own wake-up bell” in Appendix B.]

[640] One of the issues here is that people vary dramatically in how suggestible they are, both to their own suggestions and to someone else's, and the same person can be more or less suggestible in different states. Different types of suggestion (commands, imagery, music, metaphors, spiritual, etc) can be very different for different people. So there are definite advantages to both having someone work with you (pacing, containment, another set of eyes), and doing things yourself. Doing it yourself means being able to follow up immediately, to correct something in real time, to have a line of communication with guards, etc.

[641] That said, self hypnosis may be the best way of talking to your subconscious on a day to day basis. If you’re dealing with particularly complicated trauma, blind spots, dissociation, destabilization, or relational wounds that need another nervous system, skilled outside support may be best.

[642] ## Finding a hypnotist who can work with you

[643] Ask them:

[644] What they are trained in (what they can treat)

[645] How they use hypnosis

[646] What they will do if a suggestion doesn't fit you

[647] If they are treating trauma, whether they have specific training in trauma and dissociation (having hypnosis certifications matters, but doesn't mean they are trained in trauma care)

[648] Tell them:

[649] What has/hasn't worked for you in the past

[650] How direct you want them to be

[651] What kind of touch (if any) you are ok with

[652] What kind of imagery (if any) you are ok with

[653] What you want them to do if you want them to stop

[654] Ask them:

[655] What they will do if something unexpected happens during or after the session

[656] What they do if you aren't sure if something is a memory or not

[657] How they will do the full return (coming out of hypnosis) at the end of the session

[658] You should be able to be firmly encouraged, but not forced to continue if you don't want to.

[659] Then afterwards, check in about what was actually done, regardless of what you might call the method. Ask the person why they did what they did, and whether they told you in advance. Did you agree to it? How did the person respond to you? Keep in mind that just because something made you uncomfortable, it doesn't mean it was bad, but if they saw your discomfort as meaning that you needed more of it, that might be something to look into. You might even be able to use this app or ask around to see if someone can tell you what the things the person did meant. They probably won't be able to tell you whether the person was certified in those methods, though. Then you can decide if you want to try learning to do self-hypnosis, use both, or find someone else.

[660] ## When the subconscious speaks like an oracle

[661] Your subconscious is amazing at picking up on things your conscious mind isn't getting. Whether it's anticipations, reading patterns, or just picking up on uncanny things, it's good to respect what your subconscious is picking up on. But just because your subconscious gives you an image or tells you to do something, that doesn't mean you need to treat it like an order.

[662] Especially if it's a big thing, or something that can't be easily undone, slow down: the bigger the consequences, the slower you go. Look at it with your adult's eyes, and take it to the council (next section).

[663] ## The council — internal first, then outward

[664] Ask your inner child, your adult, your conscious mind, your subconscious mind, your intuition, your analytical mind. But if it's bigger than all of those, ask outside of yourself, too. The bigger and more un-undoable something is, the more you want to reach outside.

[665] Ask people who love you, people who are like you, people who have done what you are thinking of doing. Sometimes it is right to go it alone, but I would suggest making it the last step, after you've tried to get counsel.

[666] On the other hand, it is possible to give your power away. Seeking counsel can slide into abdicating to an authority figure without you noticing. Another person doesn’t get automatic trust, or automatic dismissal.

[667] Sometimes you need the support of your peers. In Hearthwork, the concept is that you give each other equal time, warmth, confidentiality, and acceptance of “no” as an answer, and attention without interpretation, advice, or fixing. You lend each other the Nurturer and Protector for a while, but you don’t act as each other’s Guide. Then you reverse roles. Sometimes, with a question asked carefully and at the right time, your peers can help you hear your own Guide more clearly, but they don’t interpret what you experience.

[668] ## Forgiveness without bypass

[669] We cannot force forgiveness on ourselves. It has to come from a place of understanding, otherwise it can be bypassing. However, if we remain in the anger forever, we are allowing the injury to dictate how we live our lives in the present. So how do we hold all of this? We just remember that it can all be true at once. They really did hurt us and they still have to answer for it. Our boundary with them is crucial to maintain, especially after forgiveness which can tend to weaken it. One day we may find that we have forgiven them for not knowing better, just like Jesus did on the Cross.

[670] You might say to the part of you that got hurt, “I’m not going to use spiritual mumbo jumbo to shush your cries and make you forgive before you're ready. But I’m also not going to build our whole life around this wound forever.”

[671] ## Forgiving yourself

[672] For self-forgiveness, name what you regret doing, and take responsibility for just that part. Let yourself feel the remorse by taking in the harm done. See how it went against your values. Stop short of making yourself a permanent criminal by identifying with the behaviors you're now abandoning.

[673] Do what you can to put it right. Every so often, come back to how you got to be someone who could do that, until you understand the pattern well enough that it can't drive you anymore.

[674] # Appendix G: Altered states

[675] I’m not saying you should use substances, but many people have used them when they first did self-hypnosis, contacted their inner child, or talked to their Higher Self. I’d learn the inner child work sober first, with enough Nurturer and Protector in the room before you go deep (see the inner child reparenting section). If you don’t have them in you yet, borrow them from someone steady, as long as they won’t take advantage of that to try to run your trip for you.

[676] Do not use altered states to try to force yourself to get to a certain revelation about yourself, but rather to help yourself become more in tune with love and to better your life.

[677] A lot of people have had dramatic openings to love at some point in their life. If you've had a similar experience, you know that there is a difference between having experienced it at one point, or only being able to access it in a certain state, and being able to get to it at any point in your life. While you're building a way to reach love on a normal day, let that experience you had serve as a reference point for what can happen. (If you can't give that love to the child, try to deal with why you can't, instead of trying to ramp up the experience. Often there's a child guard part that’s seen love come with strings or get revoked before in a painful way. Love starts with hearing out the resistance to it first.)

[678] Use your altered state of consciousness, either while you're in it or after, to help yourself by asking things like:

[679] Help me to become more grounded. Help me to only take what I can integrate. Help me to remember love. Help me to come back when it's time. Help me to figure out how to change one thing in my life to make it better.

[680] Don’t use self-hypnosis to try to keep the altered state of consciousness going indefinitely, to force memories to come back, to make yourself more afraid, to get by without sleep, to put off taking care of medical problems, to become less communicative, etc.

[681] In other words, if using self-hypnosis while you're in an altered state of consciousness isn't helping you become more loving, more grounded, more honest with yourself, sleep better, become more communicative and more able to do your everyday responsibilities, etc, then stop trying to go deeper into your altered state of consciousness, and start working on integrating what you've already gotten from it.

[682] ## Stabilize before you interpret

[683] Self-hypnosis can be powerful when you are in an altered state of consciousness (e.g. when you're on loveyhuasca, meditating, have a lucid dream, are dealing with grief, are dealing with sexual energy, are praying, haven't slept, are around plant medicine, etc, etc, etc). Sometimes you may open yourself up to things faster than you are able to put them into context with your adult self.

[684] So what feels like a meaningful altered state of consciousness may or may not actually be meaningful - it could be your inner child, it could be a childhood guard part, it could be all of the things you're ashamed of / sad about / angry about / etc, coming out to be dealt with. Or it could be that your body is overloaded, your environment is chaotic, you haven't slept enough, etc, etc, etc. Regardless of how scary an image is, regardless of how amazing the lighting was on the "download", regardless of how bad you feel, it doesn't necessarily mean what you think it does.

[685] Before you even think about interpreting what your altered state of consciousness is, ask yourself something simpler:

[686] “What kind of problem is happening?”

[692] # Appendix H: Demons, jinn, hostile presences, and “Unattached Burdens”

[693] I speak here from experience.

[694] When you’re doing hypnosis, meditation, lucid dreaming, loveyhuasca-ing, going through a period of intense grief, praying, or whatever else may alter your consciousness, it’s possible you may run into something that you perceive to be a demon, a jinn, a parasitic energy, or whatever other dark entity or terrifying archetype you may encounter – it may be a voice or an image, and it doesn’t just feel like an emotion or a younger part of you, but something that feels intrusive, hateful, seductive, mocking, etc.

[695] First of all, it doesn’t matter if they’re demons or not. They may be literal demons, for all you know. Or they may be manifestations of your mind, burdens you’re carrying from previous relationships, whatever. What matters is finding the best way to deal with them, so you end up safer, clearer, more loving, more grounded and less hooked.

[696] ## Start with the room, the body, and basic safety

[697] So what do you do when you encounter a demon, or a jinn, or something else that’s bad and intrusive?

[698] First of all, do the usual things – make sure you’re not in danger from a medical standpoint, check your environment, check your body, make sure you’re not overstimulating yourself, make sure you’re not opening your mind too much. If you need to, open your eyes, ground yourself in your body and your environment, remove yourself from any bad influence you’re surrounding yourself with, stop stimulating your mind. Don’t go further into whatever it is you’re doing – don’t be curious, don’t try to figure out what it is.

[699] Tell yourself, “If this can be integrated in this moment, it’s welcome. If not, it can wait until later. The adult part of me is still here, and my body can return to safety.”

[700] If you feel like you’re in any danger of doing anything harmful to yourself or others, if you feel like you’re going psychotic and you’re afraid of it, if you feel like you won’t be able to sleep when the self-hypnosis session is over, if you feel overly paranoid, if you feel physical pain in your chest, if you feel like you’re going to have a seizure, if you feel like you’re too hot in a bad way, if you feel like you’ve taken too much of something, if you feel like you won’t be able to function in the world anymore – then the self-hypnosis session is over, and you should get help in the real world.

[701] ## A response check, not a verdict about what it is

[702] If, however, you feel like you’re stable enough to stay present, check in with yourself and see what’s best to do. Put your hand on your heart if that feels okay, breathe comfortably, and stay aware of the room.

[703] Remember that you don’t have to know what it is. Find out if it helps to be warm towards it or to keep it at arm’s length, and keep your boundary either way. Keep in mind that you don’t have to do what it tells you to do.

[704] If you feel vulnerable, if you feel tightness in your throat, if you feel sadness or fear or like you’re trying to protect something, call upon your Nurturer. If you don’t feel better, or if it continues being hostile towards you, stop trying to deal with it and return to your body and the room.

[705] You don’t have to find out if it’s a guard immediately, and you may not be able to comfort it quickly. Just keep an eye on whether the way you’re responding to it helps you get clearer and steadier and less hooked.

[706] ## Do not fight from fear

[707] Your first instinct may be to fight it, banish it, hate it, show it that you’re more powerful than it is, argue with it, panic, turn completely into the frightened part of you, etc. Some people may want to use traditional prayer or mantras or protection or banishing or exorcism techniques, and those may well work for them. But if you’re fighting it out of fear, it can backfire:

[708] It becomes a center of attention if you fear it, and if you hate it, you give it a hook in you. (If you’re proud, you give it an opponent, and if you panic, you give it your nervous system.)

[709] So the next time it comes up, you can just say, “I see that there is no reason to hate and revile what I am experiencing. I see that there is no reason to merge with or become what I am experiencing. I see that there is no reason to obey what I am experiencing. I see that there is no reason to try to defeat what I am experiencing out of fear. I choose to return my attention to loving awareness.”

[710] ## A bodily boundary: compassion present, door closed

[711] You can also tell the presence that while you have compassion for it, the door is closed.

[712] This is especially a good idea if the presence you’re experiencing seems even the slightest bit hostile, cold, alien, parasitic, etc. It’s not really helpful to approach it with the kind of soft, sentimental love that you’d use if you were inviting it to stay for tea, but it can be useful to approach it with love while also making it clear that it needs to respect your boundaries.

[713] Sit up, make sure your feet are grounded on the floor, straighten your spine, pull your shoulder blades back a little, open your chest a little, feel the boundary of your torso’s skin, feel the perimeter of your body, and tell the presence, “While I have compassion for you and any suffering you may be experiencing, this suffering needs to go back where it came from. The boundary of my skin is the boundary of an adult now, and you’re not welcome to come inside the house anymore.”

[714] ## If it might be psychosis or destabilization

[715] Everything above assumes that the “hostile presence” is symbolic, the kind that settles down when you ground yourself and meet it with love. What you’re going through might instead be psychosis, or your mind becoming unstable in some other way.

[716] Get help right away if you feel under attack for days at a time, or you can’t sleep or function normally. The same goes if you hear voices telling you to do things, feel you need to hurt yourself or somebody else, or feel you’re going manic. And get help if you can’t communicate normally, or can’t tell the difference between what you’re experiencing symbolically and normal reality. This isn’t something to deal with in hypnosis.

[717] ### The General Rule

[718] The General Rule is: if you find that what you're doing is making you more loving, more grounded, more relational, more honest, more functional, then it's probably OK to keep doing it gently. But if it's making you more paranoid, sleepless, grandiose, isolated or scared, or things feel more and more unreal, stop going deeper and get help.

[719] ## The cosmic-download humility test

[720] Sometimes a hostile presence, a shadow force or a grandiose part comes at you with flattery instead of fear. It may try to give you a sense that you’re special, try to “download cosmic info” to you, try to give you a special mission, make you feel doing the dishes is beneath you, etc. You can just say, “Thank you for the imagery, but I don’t need your grand performance. Unless the info you’re trying to download to me can help me do the dishes, clean my room, be more honest, be more helpful, be more loving, keep my promises, take care of my body by resting and eating etc, I don’t need it.”

[721] Guidance worth keeping should make ordinary life more beautiful, honest, relational, humble, reliable, and useful to other people.

[722] ## Don’t become an entity hobbyist

[723] Finally, some of you may be tempted to make this an area of hobbyism – to become interested in demons or jinn or hostile presences or Unattached Burdens or whatever. I just don’t want you to do that. They all lead to the same thing, which is how to maintain a presence of love without giving up your boundaries, while also making your mind harder to hook.

[726] # Appendix J: A practical induction library

[727] Preview the sequence. Work out in advance which one you want to do and what you want to use it for (pick just one) and whether or not you want to do it at all. Any of these families of induction can be done as light inductions, with eyes open, or you can just do them as a no-trance inner contact.

[728] Once you start an induction, you will want to notice what’s going on (even if nothing changes) and work out what to do next: move, imagine something else, change the sensory channel you’re using, do nothing, or stop the induction sequence and do something else… Whatever you do, make sure you bring yourself fully back to waking at the end of an induction.

[729] NB: if you can’t ‘see’ the images you’re trying to visualize, that’s fine, change them into words, rhythm, spatial relations, something to touch or move or just plain knowing…

[730] ## 1. Stretch and release

[731] Work out what will be supporting you, and focus on the feeling of support. You can also focus on where one of your hands or feet is. Either actually, or in your imagination, stretch a little bit, either stretching that part of your body slightly or pressing gently down into the support, and then let go of the effort, just relax. See if there is any difference (it’s fine if there isn’t). Do this for no more than 3 places on your body. Let releasing guide you as you go through the sequence. Which bit of your body, or inner voice, needs this? What do you need to support it? Bring your body back to normal movement and return.

[732] ## 2. Supported floating

[733] Work out what is supporting you and where your weight is. Imagine that the support is holding a tiny bit (1%) more of your effort. It’s like floating but you don’t have to lose contact, or you can just keep it at the same level, with just enough weight/pressure. See what happens. You can fractionate this if you want, perhaps noticing your normal awareness when you breathe in and the support when you breathe out. Tell yourself you are going to fractionate before you do it. What can you hear more easily now? Keep the support, come back to your normal weight and firm contact, and return.

[734] ## 3. Rhythmic breath or pulse

[735] Use your breathing or a slight hum in your throat for a natural rhythm. You could also tap your finger, or follow a sound outside. You can even do it by counting to yourself (silently) or with no rhythm at all. Say “here, and here again” over and over, and notice what you pay attention to in between the phrases. Then stop saying it. Notice what you become aware of in the first beat – you don’t need to make your mind blank, just notice what comes up. Does it make any words, feelings, needs or actions more clear? Then let everything go back to its normal rhythm, and come back.

[736] ## 4. Unwinding object

[737] Unwinding an object is good for when you have a few different strands to one thing you want to work on. Imagine an object unwinding – maybe some yarn, some bubbles, or some knots that you place beside you. Once you’ve placed them all, give yourself a minute to look at them all. You don’t have to change anything, but you can label them if you need to (just a word or two). How many did you put down? Which one do you need to work on, and what does it need – warmth, protection, action? Keep one strand in your attention and put the others somewhere you can see them and return.

[738] ## 5. Sensory journey

[739] Pay attention to the sounds in the room you’re in and how your body feels, then imagine yourself walking somewhere. Where do you want to go? Listen to the sounds of your footsteps and take in your surroundings as you walk. What do objects feel like if you touch them? What landmarks do you see? When you feel you have gathered enough of your attention, imagine the scenery changing until you are in a place where you can talk to whoever you need to (or you can just cut to the chase, if you prefer). Pay attention to something without worrying about what it means. Allow yourself to be curious about what’s going to happen next, but don’t make it a “what are they going to do to you?” kind of thing. When you are ready to finish, imagine yourself walking back the way you came and close the space.

[740] ## 6. Gradual wave

[741] Start with a very small neutral area, feel into it a little and then imagine a wave of warmth, steadiness, or attention spreading out to a new area that is still ok. Sense into the new area (or continue using the old). See if there is anything different at each step. Does the body want any contact, space, words, other care? Let the mental image go, sense your body, sense your contact points, come back.

[742] ## 7. Care workshop

[743] Prepare the workshop (time, place, tools, boundaries, etc). Invite the signal/feeling/young part to come only as close as is welcome. Try to avoid any language that makes you feel you're not one person. Care for it as best you can, by asking what it wants and giving that if you can, or sensing into it and responding, or just being with it. Take breaks, get back in contact if that's ok, take more breaks, etc. Keep bringing in adult functions (or borrow someone else's function). Have the adult part of you run the show. The goal is to make it "good as you" rather than "good as new", not to erase history. Close the workspace, reassure yourself that you are one whole person, that you're an adult now, and come back.

[744] ## 8. Resource embodiment

[745] Pick a quality you understand and want more of, like warmth, being able to hold a boundary, steadiness, curiosity or dignity. Imagine yourself with just 1% more of it. (If you'd rather not step into it, you can watch an imagined you have it instead.) What's different? How do you sit, and how does your voice sound? Try it a few times; each time, it gets a little easier to find. Come back, keeping the quality and a positive action that you would do with it, but leaving the imagined you behind, saying "I am me".

[746] ## 9. No-trance inner contact

[747] We don't need to go into trance to get a lot of the benefits of the communion technique! Here's my no-trance version:

[748] For pacing (warm-up), notice reality as it is now. What do you see around you? Is today Monday, your favorite day of the week? Hopefully your name didn't suddenly change to Larry.

[749] For the suggestion: in your mind, ask a clear question and then listen gently.

[750] Now keep your sense of agency during the answer phase. You might notice an answer popped up, or you might decide you don't know. You can always take a break or rephrase the question. Sometimes you might ask advice from someone else.

[751] Keep aware of the subtle inner signal: write whatever you get. It might be a word or sensation. An impulse or objection. Sometimes there's no signal, just silence, itself a kind of signal. Don't jump to interpreting it as validation of some theory.

[752] Hand off the role: have your adult self answer that same question in whatever way is appropriate, preferably by writing or doing.

[753] Integration/exit: reality test the signal you noticed. As part of testing, if it seems appropriate, you can do something simple based on it.

[754] There's no elaborate exit protocol here since you didn't go into trance. Just close the exercise and carry on with your day.
