## The part to read

[358] # The 15-minute daily practice

[359] You don't need to learn a second script for this. It's the same process (gather your attention, notice what's here, respond, wait, and come all the way back), just for fifteen minutes or so. That is long enough to induce yourself (it only takes a few minutes), practice and let it unfold at its own pace, and come out again (that takes a few minutes too). Fifteen minutes is just a handy appointment with yourself, though, and your feelings don't have to keep to it. Five minutes is plenty to see what happens when you simply sit in silence, listening, and you can interrupt yourself at any time to change direction if that feels better.

[360] You can also try other ways of inducing yourself, such as becoming aware of 3 things you can see, 3 things you can hear, 3 things you can feel in your body; then 2 of each, 1 of each. Then count from 5 to 1 and begin to listen. Choose one way to induce yourself. You don't need to try all of them (in fact, stacking them might be counter-productive).

[361] Once you have practiced listening a few times with the recordings, you may want to shorten them to speed up your induction, or even practice without playing them back at all. On the other hand, if you like using the recordings, there is no reason to stop. (Once you know the induction well, the cue from "A shortcut back into hypnosis" can shorten it too.)

[362] As you come out of the process, ask yourself what needed warmth, what needed to be protected, what you need to do differently to be more in accord with truth and love and life. Is there something you want to explore more fully or a capacity you want to build? And most importantly, what can you now carry yourself that you used to have to borrow? If you identified something you needed to do differently, make a plan with yourself to do that thing. The more specific you can be, the more likely you are to change: “If _________ (fill in the cue for the old pattern), then I will do ________ (fill in a small action that will begin to change the pattern).” Then check back later and see how you are doing – sleeping, relating, functioning, etc., not only how impressive the session felt.

[363] # The weekly deeper practice

[364] Once or twice a week, when it’s useful, give this practice more space and time so even the unplanned, unknown and maybe hidden needs can show up. Just because it's unplanned doesn't mean it will be more shocking. Start from your Higher Self, from the field of acceptance and understanding, or however you can describe it that makes it feel accessible. If you need the Protector or another of your adult jobs, bring it in or borrow it from someone safe. Mr. or Ms. Protector can check to make sure you’re not skipping past anything important. Leave enough time at the end to come all the way back up & out.

[365] Then, if there's something from the session that asked you to do something in real life, and your Protector agrees, then go ahead and do it when you can. That's how you show your subconscious you mean business.

[366] # Capacity before content

[367] Note: if it is medically unsafe, or you are in a place of panic, sleep deprivation, nausea, deep destabilization, mania, psychosis-like symptoms, feeling overwhelmed by chemicals etc., focus on stabilizing first, with help if you need it (see “When this needs more support than a guide can provide”), and only then look for what your subconscious needs you to know. If knowing what your subconscious knows would destabilize you, it is not meant for you to know directly at this time. Ask for capacity before content.

[368] Sometimes, it feels like your subconscious knows something that your conscious self is not prepared to know. It may be something related to your sexuality, grief, trauma, family issues, betrayal, shame, vocation, a relationship, illness fears, spirituality, addiction, identity, moral issues etc.

[369] What you don’t want to do is bully your subconscious into telling you what it knows by saying things to yourself like:

[370] “What is it you won’t tell me? What are you hiding? Tell me NOW! I am telling you to tell me!”

[371] This can lead to flooding, panic, false certainty, confabulation, dissociation etc., and can make your day suck. When you are flooded, it is much harder to access your adult capacities, so focus on being able to regulate and on getting support first. Then ask for what you need to know in a way that you can tolerate:

[372] “What can you tell me, that will make my conscious self 5% better at knowing the thing you want to tell me?” “Can you tell me in symbolic form so I am not completely overwhelmed by the revelation?” “Can you tell me what I need to do to be safe, without actually telling me what it is?” “Do I need to know what it is now, or do I need to get some support first, so I have the capacity to know it, and who would be a good person to help me?” etc.

[373] Or, instead of demanding answers, say to yourself something like this:

[374] “Dear Wise Subconscious Mind, if there is something you know that my conscious self is not ready to know, please don’t break it open violently. Do what you can to help me know it in a way that is safe. Prepare me before you break it open. Ground me before you show me the memory. Support me before you make it intense. And show me the next thing to do.” etc.

[375] Remember: not everything in hypnotic imagery is literal, especially if it relates to trauma and memory. The mind makes it symbolic, squashes things together, distorts, dramatizes, pulls from cultural influences, fears etc. So, treat it like an image, and get some help outside of yourself when you need it.

[376] # Troubleshooting

[377] ## Nothing happens

[378] Don't fret, even for the greatest masters, many times nothing happens. After all, only like 15% of people are very suggestible. But sometimes it's just confusing when you throw everything at yourself at once. Check if there's a way you could try this on a smaller scale... like is it even the right time for this? You can directly ask that.

[379] ## "It feels fake"

[380] Let's drill into that "it"... do you have a sense of what exactly feels feigned, imposterous, or preposterous? The words, an image, what you are expecting to get from this, a role you feel you have to play... am I getting warm? Not having an inner voice or images doesn't mean you're resisting the work, nor that you're missing a 'grown-up'. If it feels like you are being asked to do something you don't want to do, then correct the invitation. It could be that there is a part that is protecting you, so talk to it.

[381] You could say something like 'What are you protecting me from, helicopter mom?' or 'Are you sus because you think this will just be a silly performance?' If it says yes, that one can sting, because there could be something to it. Maybe you've fooled yourself before, and that part remembers. But as ex-US-President George W. Bush wisely explained, "Fool me once, shame on — shame on you. Fool me — you can't get fooled again." !!!

[382] But seriously... you can then say, "Tell me what you remember, I'm listening." It puts you into the role of the Nurturer, which alleviates the instinct to argue back.

[383] It could also be your inherited parent script that you are picking up, and it is trying to enforce a rule about how you should be to belong to them. Find out what rule it is enforcing. Comfort your little one before you answer any of it. Your answers can be firm or funny, as long as they aren’t mean. Then work on finding some ordinary evidence to build a more believable 'grown up' voice of your own.

[384] If the fake feeling is telling you that you don't have the grown up ability to do this yet, then don't try and convince yourself by using an inner voice. As long as you can still observe yourself well enough to do this, try and borrow what you need from someone who you can imagine is good enough to lend it to you. This could be a family member, friend, famous person etc. What you need might be warmth, boundary, direction etc. For warmth, just make sure it's someone who liked you the way you were. Try out something which rings true to you, whether it is a sentence, an action etc, and see how you get on.

[385] ## The subconscious will not answer

[386] There may not be an answer. There may not be an answer that will help you. You may need to try asking a different question or you may just need to rest or finish your inner work for the day. If trust is actually a concern, you can try asking your subconscious “What would you need to know in order to trust me?” Some examples of answers might be “I need to know you will set boundaries,” “I need to know you will do what you said you will do,” “I need to know you won’t keep leaving,” or “I need to know you won’t think that knowing something means that you don’t need to do anything about it.”

[387] ## The inner child will not appear

[388] If you can tell there is a guard in the way, talk to it. You can say to the guard “You don’t have to let me in, just tell me what I need to do to make it safe”. Even if there isn’t a child part that you can talk to, you can work with whatever you are aware of. You can work on your preferences or something that you’re curious about or something that you’re willing to try as a beginner etc. and see how it goes. Work on developing a sense of who you are and reparent yourself as best you can.

[389] ## You get anxious

[390] Find out if the anxiety is bad enough that you want to decrease contact with it or if you can manage it and let it continue. If the anxiety is getting too bad, then you need to stop searching for content and help your body first, like the following:

[391] -Where are your feet? Where are you sitting? Focus on your body, your breathing, and the room around you.

[392] -Tell your little one and your guards (childhood protector parts) that you’re there for your little one, that you recognize that your guards are trying to protect you, and that you don’t need them to scream at you in order for you to know that something is wrong. Ask them if they need you to do something, and if they do, ask them to show you one step at a time that you can do. If they don’t, then ask them to let your body calm down.

[393] If you’re having chest pain, fainting, suicidal thoughts, psychosis-like symptoms, mania-like symptoms, severe dissociative symptoms, not being able to sleep, wanting to hurt yourself or somebody else, etc., then you need to get professional help. For more on handling anxiety, see the toolbox in Appendix D.

[394] ## You dissociate or feel unreal

[395] Open your eyes, move around, feel something textured, name 5 things that you can see, and end the deeper practice for the day.

[396] ## You become grandiose

[397] Go eat, go to sleep, do some chores, accept some humility, find somebody to disagree with you, etc.

[398] What do you say to yourself in that moment? “If it’s real, it’ll still be here after a good night’s sleep, some food, some time, and some subsequent humility, feedback, etc.”

[399] ## You become too analytical

[400] “What if you didn’t try to improve the practice?” Put one hand on your heart and the other on your solar plexus, as in the two-hand practice, and say, “I don’t have to do this perfectly. I’m here for a relationship with something deeper than my surface mind.” “What can you do to make this a communion, not a performance?” Maybe it’s silence, resting, or doing something. Whatever it is, just do it.

[401] ## You become too blissful or diffuse

[402] If the bliss spreads out into a big warm haze, it can’t do much for you or anyone else yet. Bring it down to Earth by asking, “What’s this lofty love asking me to do? Is there a task I'm ignoring? Or a boundary I need to set? Am I getting enough rest and nourishment? Am I hiding from a needed conversation or relationship repair?”

[403] ## You want the hidden truth immediately

[404] Don’t pry.

[405] “Show me the capacity before the content.”

[406] ## You keep daydreaming

[407] Sit up and open your eyes, and ask yourself, “What are you avoiding doing in the real world by doing this?”

[408] ## You get stuck in spiritual bypass

[409] “Where has divine love not yet descended into the wound?”

[410] Maybe you had a profound opening that showed you how much more love and freedom is possible. Let that matter, but don’t demand that your inner child receive it on command. Invite your little one in with you. They might go numb, or call BS on that. If so, they might just need to see if you’re actually humble enough, and grateful enough for them, to listen to their rejection and be patient. Maybe when you prayed it seemed like you were being abandoned and condemned. Maybe you felt betrayed by a spiritual teacher. It may be worth exploring these, but you might not want to do more of the same practice.

[411] Sometimes it’s best to get some practical help while life still feels too small, and let yourself be curious about finding more happiness, without necessarily believing the explanation of how it’s going to work. Sometimes people have enough love and meaning to just live their lives, and trying to be spiritual doesn’t have to be another assignment.

[412] ## You get stuck in endless processing

[413] You may have a real wound that you need to deal with and be caught in a checking loop at the same time. Consider asking yourself “Will processing this again actually help me feel, understand, decide, or do something differently? Or is this a form of checking to see if I can get the reassurance that something is finally okay?” Sometimes, whether you’re grieving or doing your practice, it’s okay to cover the same ground again and again.

[414] The best thing to do, though, if you notice you’re in a checking loop, is allow the thought to be there without engaging with it and get on with what you have chosen to do. You don’t have to have a meeting with every thought that pops into your head before you start your life. You don’t have to complete a ritual before you start your life.

[415] What would the Guide choose to do now?

[416] ## You keep using hypnosis but not changing life

[417] Ask yourself, “What can I do to show my little one I’m for real?” Pick one thing to do that’s small enough to repeat, then go do it. A big gesture you later drop would only prove them right to doubt you, but a small thing you keep doing can change their mind.

[418] # What not to do

[419] Don’t view your inner experiences as commands or proof. They’re internal happenings that need to be reality tested at all times.

[420] Don’t go seeking “downloads” of information, or make a hobby of demons/jinn/Unattached Burdens or the like. Stabilize your life first, and get help to do so if you are not reality testing well, or not functioning well in your life.

[421] Don’t do self-hypnosis while driving, bathing, swimming, operating any equipment, supervising children, or doing anything else that requires active attention.

[422] Don’t try to “force” memories, to use trance to “prove” something happened to you, or to skip medical attention for symptoms just because you can “force” them down using hypnosis.

[423] Don’t force yourself to ignore your body’s needs, such as eating when hungry, resting when tired, responding to pain, etc., or your emotions, such as letting yourself grieve when someone dies, feeling anger when it is appropriate, acknowledging your fears, etc., until you know more about what the signals are telling you.

[424] Don’t “extend” the duration of altered states indefinitely.

[425] Don’t allow your inner roles to get out of proportion (e.g., letting your inner child run your adult life, the Protector shut out all love, the Nurturer disregard consequences, or the Guide ignore the needs of your inner child). You are capable of analysis, as well as tenderness.

[426] Don’t use NLP techniques to force down guard parts, to give yourself certainty you do not have, to manipulate others, or to heal yourself when your body is telling you not to.

[427] Don’t consider negative indicators (doubt, distraction, resistance, lack of response, saying “No”) as good signs that the induction is working.

[428] Don’t rely upon the safety of your unconscious mind as the only safety factor in any session. Be sure you have set up clear limits, are in a safe place, and have a tested way to end the session and get out.

[429] Don’t believe that normal physical sensations are indications of hidden trauma, illness, toxins in your body, possession, “attachments,” “transformations,” or information from your “subconscious mind.”

[430] Don’t believe that anything is “healed” in your body (neurologically, cellularly, etc.) simply because it is “healed” in your imagination.

[431] Don’t put hidden commands in yourself, nor allow anyone to do so. Even if you are consciously placing something in yourself which you wish to utilize later, be sure that you know exactly what you are doing and why you are doing it, and be sure that you can review it and can “take it out” if you decide that you no longer wish to use it. It may not be as easy as simply saying, “Take out.” Nor should you allow anyone to activate these commands in you without your knowledge, or use them to make you do something you don’t want to do, to make you into someone you don’t want to be, to give you amnesia, etc.

[432] # When this needs more support than a guide can provide

[433] You should get help now if you are in imminent danger, if you are very confused, if you cannot ensure your own safety, and/or if you are experiencing concerning physical symptoms (e.g., chest pain, fainting, etc). You should also seek professional help promptly if you are experiencing new or worsening suicidal thoughts, severe insomnia, new or worsening manic or psychotic symptoms, or if dissociative symptoms are impacting your ability to function.

[434] You should seek suitable ongoing help if you are experiencing recurring panic attacks, compulsions, destabilization from substances, major relationship instability, etc.

[435] You probably don't need any more support than this if you keep the practice shorter, make it warm, pick a small and tractable subject, make sure you come back out, and then see how it affects a specific target (anxiety, daydreaming, helping a relationship, facing a task you've been avoiding, helping you sleep, being less reactive emotionally, improving your intuitive accuracy, etc) for a week or two. If you notice that your functioning is getting worse, or that you're getting more certain about unconfirmable memories, then reassess instead of pushing harder.

[436] ## Methods to explore with a trained person present

[437] If you want to deliberately do trauma-focused regression (going backwards in your memory or imagination to times when you were younger), or affect and somatic bridging (following associations from an emotional or somatic experience to find the origin of that experience, particularly if it was a traumatic experience you don't know about), or if you find that you're having substantial flashbacks or dissociative reactions, then you should do this with a trauma-informed clinician present. Make sure they're trained in the specific method you want to do. Ask any potential clinician what they would do and how they deal with uncertainty and unexpected reactions. Note that even if you work with a person, hypnosis is not a way of knowing what did or did not happen to you.

[438] Note that having experienced trauma, crying, and even wanting to feel difficult feelings are not necessarily signs that you need more support than working on your own. You get to pick how wide or narrow a range you want to work in, but be aware that different people need different ranges. Pay attention to how you respond and what support you have. Feeling like you are handling it isn't a substitute for having support.

[439] The main clinical practice guidelines for the treatment of major trauma recommend one or more of trauma-specific variants of cognitive behavioral therapy (often referred to by specific names, such as cognitive processing therapy, prolonged exposure, etc.) or eye movement desensitization and reprocessing, depending on the person and the guideline. These approaches in turn can be further supported by techniques such as self-hypnosis and inner child work. But high-risk trauma work needs a bigger container than that.

[440] ## Your functioning in ordinary life is the feedback

[441] If you find yourself more loving, more honest, more functional, more relational, more grounded, more precise, etc. than when you started this process, keep on keeping on gently. If you notice that your ordinary life is becoming less stable, don't try to go deeper. Go for more sleep, more eating, more grounding, more healthy boundaries with other people, more support from other people, etc.

[456] # Appendix A: How to ask the subconscious a useful question

[457] Your subconscious mind is not an AI large language model. However, there is a bit of overlap in the way you can ask questions to both: how you ask shapes the answer. So, if you know how to do prompt engineering for AI, this can be very useful to help you be more present, honest, loving etc. But you should never use it to be more controlling.

[458] To get as much precision as possible, you need to include the following in your prompt:

[459] Role: Who or what are you talking to?

[460] Context: Give some context so it knows what you’re talking about.

[461] Intention: What do you want to change or accomplish?

[462] Constraint: What do you need to do to make sure your question is safe, true, kind etc.? No forcing, no false certainty, and no asking for an answer before you can handle it.

[463] Output: How do you want to receive the answer? As an image, phrase, sensation in your body, memory, action, question, symbol, silence, rest etc.

[464] Evaluation: How are you going to evaluate the answer? How will you know if it’s true, kind, timely, stabilizing, specific, testable etc?

[465] Iteration: Iterate by changing your prompt depending on what you get from reality as feedback.

[466] Most importantly, always let silence count as an answer, otherwise the analytical part of your mind will make up an answer just so it fits with your prompt. I usually add the following to my prompt:

[467] “If you need more time or the best and safest answer is silence, then I ask you to make my body go into a deeper state of comfortable rest. A peaceful silence would be a successful answer to my question.”

[468] Once you do that, here are the core prompts you can use:

[469] ## Core prompts

[470] For getting clarity: “I ask you, my wise subconscious mind, to show me what is true, kind and timely. If what you want to show me is too intense for me to integrate, please transfer it to my conscious mind in a way I can integrate it, for instance as a symbol, a signal in my body, a next step I can take. Most importantly, show me first my capacity to be peaceful, and then, the content of my wounding.”

[471] When you experience resistance: “What are you protecting me from? What would help you trust me 1% more?”

[472] When you want to ask your inner child something: “What do you want me to know? What do you need me to do today so that you are not alone in dealing with it?”

[473] When you want to bring in your Protector: “What would a minimally competent adult do here, in the external world, so that all of me is safe?”

[474] When you want to ask your Guide: “What direction is the most in alignment with truth, love, life and all beings?”

[475] When you want to listen to your intuition: “I ask my intuition to give me a signal, my analytical mind to decipher it, my body to give me feedback on whether my deciphering is correct, and reality to test it.”

[476] When you want to know if a particular intuition you have is coming from your universal heart or from your programming: “I ask my wise subconscious mind to help me see if the intuition or impulse I have about …is coming from my universal heart or from my family, my religion, marketing, erotic programming, my political tribe, my trauma response or an old deal I did as a child to belong. If it’s coming from my programming, I ask the parts responsible for it to step back and soften only as much as is safe, so that I can have true guidance from my universal heart that will help me make my life better in the most simple ways possible. Let my body give me feedback as to the difference.”

[477] Once you get an answer, you can ask: “What can I do in my ordinary life to show that I heard correctly?” If it tells you to do something, I'd only do it if it's loving and leaves you free. It would have to make sense too. And even with all that, I wouldn't do anything big or hard to undo until I'm calm and I've checked it against what I actually know.

[478] You use the prompt to tell your subconscious what you want in a language it can easily understand. The NLP in Appendix B below can help smooth the prompt's entry and reduce resistance. Used together properly, the subconscious can feel safer in giving its response, though that doesn't make the answers any more true.
