With EFT (Emotional Freedom Techniques, or tapping), you tap gently on your body while you say something like, “Even though I have this anxiety, I am willing to be kind to the part of me that is afraid,” and you stay with what you feel. There isn’t a ton of evidence for EFT, but there are some studies on anxiety. A 2025 systematic review found 7 randomized controlled trials for anxiety disorders, and noted that EFT was more effective than no intervention in several of them, though it isn't one of the first-line therapies for anxiety.
