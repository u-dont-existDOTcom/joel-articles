[686] # Appendix H: Demons, jinn, hostile presences, and “Unattached Burdens”

[687] I speak here from experience.

[688] When you’re doing hypnosis, meditation, lucid dreaming, loveyhuasca-ing, going through a period of intense grief, praying, or whatever else may alter your consciousness, it’s possible you may run into something that you perceive to be a demon, a jinn, a parasitic energy, or whatever other dark entity or terrifying archetype you may encounter – it may be a voice or an image, and it doesn’t just feel like an emotion or a younger part of you, but something that feels intrusive, hateful, seductive, mocking, etc.

[689] First of all, it doesn’t matter if they’re demons or not. They may be literal demons, for all you know. Or they may be manifestations of your mind, burdens you’re carrying from previous relationships, whatever. What matters is finding the best way to deal with them, so you end up safer, clearer, more loving, more grounded and less hooked.

[690] ## Start with the room, the body, and basic safety

[691] So what do you do when you encounter a demon, or a jinn, or something else that’s bad and intrusive?

[692] First of all, do the usual things – make sure you’re not in danger from a medical standpoint, check your environment, check your body, make sure you’re not overstimulating yourself, make sure you’re not opening your mind too much. If you need to, open your eyes, ground yourself in your body and your environment, remove yourself from any bad influence you’re surrounding yourself with, stop stimulating your mind. Don’t go further into whatever it is you’re doing – don’t be curious, don’t try to figure out what it is.

[693] Tell yourself, “If this can be integrated in this moment, it’s welcome. If not, it can wait until later. The adult part of me is still here, and my body can return to safety.”

[694] If you feel like you’re in any danger of doing anything harmful to yourself or others, if you feel like you’re going psychotic and you’re afraid of it, if you feel like you won’t be able to sleep when the self-hypnosis session is over, if you feel overly paranoid, if you feel physical pain in your chest, if you feel like you’re going to have a seizure, if you feel like you’re too hot in a bad way, if you feel like you’ve taken too much of something, if you feel like you won’t be able to function in the world anymore – then the self-hypnosis session is over, and you should get help in the real world.

[695] ## A response check, not a verdict about what it is

[696] If, however, you feel like you’re stable enough to stay present, check in with yourself and see what’s best to do. Put your hand on your heart if that feels okay, breathe comfortably, and stay aware of the room.

[697] Find out if it helps to be warm towards it or to keep it at arm’s length, and keep your boundary either way. Keep in mind that you don’t have to do what it tells you to do.

[698] If you feel vulnerable, if you feel tightness in your throat, if you feel sadness or fear or like you’re trying to protect something, call upon your Nurturer. If you don’t feel better, or if it continues being hostile towards you, stop trying to deal with it and return to your body and the room.

[699] ## Do not fight from fear

[700] Your first instinct may be to fight it, banish it, hate it, show it that you’re more powerful than it is, argue with it, panic, turn completely into the frightened part of you, etc. Some people may want to use traditional prayer or mantras or protection or banishing or exorcism techniques, and those may well work for them. But if you’re fighting it out of fear, it can backfire:

[701] It becomes a center of attention if you fear it, and if you hate it, you give it a hook in you.

[702] So the next time it comes up, you can just say, “I see that there is no reason to hate and revile what I am experiencing. I see that there is no reason to merge with or become what I am experiencing. I see that there is no reason to obey what I am experiencing. I see that there is no reason to try to defeat what I am experiencing out of fear. I choose to return my attention to loving awareness.”

[703] ## A bodily boundary: compassion present, door closed

[704] You can also tell the presence that while you have compassion for it, the door is closed.

[705] This is especially a good idea if the presence you’re experiencing seems even the slightest bit hostile, cold, alien, parasitic, etc. It’s not really helpful to approach it with the kind of soft, sentimental love that you’d use if you were inviting it to stay for tea.

[706] Sit up, make sure your feet are grounded on the floor, straighten your spine, pull your shoulder blades back a little, open your chest a little, feel the boundary of your torso’s skin, feel the perimeter of your body, and tell the presence, “While I have compassion for you and any suffering you may be experiencing, this suffering needs to go back where it came from. The boundary of my skin is the boundary of an adult now, and you’re not welcome to come inside the house anymore.”

[707] ## If it might be psychosis or destabilization

[708] Everything above assumes that the “hostile presence” is symbolic, the kind that settles down when you ground yourself and meet it with love. What you’re going through might instead be psychosis, or your mind becoming unstable in some other way.

[709] Get help right away if you feel under attack for days at a time, or you can’t sleep or function normally. The same goes if you hear voices telling you to do things, feel you need to hurt yourself or somebody else, or feel you’re going manic. And get help if you can’t communicate normally, or can’t tell the difference between what you’re experiencing symbolically and normal reality. This isn’t something to deal with in hypnosis.

[710] ### The General Rule

[711] The General Rule is: if you find that what you're doing is making you more loving, more grounded, more relational, more honest, more functional, then it's probably OK to keep doing it gently. But if it's making you more paranoid, sleepless, grandiose, isolated or scared, or things feel more and more unreal, stop going deeper and get help.

[712] ## The cosmic-download humility test

[713] Sometimes a hostile presence, a shadow force or a grandiose part comes at you with flattery instead of fear. It may try to give you a sense that you’re special, try to “download cosmic info” to you, try to give you a special mission, make you feel doing the dishes is beneath you, etc. You can just say, “Thank you for the imagery, but I don’t need your grand performance. Unless the info you’re trying to download to me can help me do the dishes, clean my room, be more honest, be more helpful, be more loving, keep my promises, take care of my body by resting and eating etc, I don’t need it.”

[714] ## Don’t become an entity hobbyist

[715] Finally, some of you may be tempted to make this an area of hobbyism – to become interested in demons or jinn or hostile presences or Unattached Burdens or whatever. I just don’t want you to do that. They all lead to the same thing, which is how to maintain a presence of love without giving up your boundaries, while also making your mind harder to hook.

[718] # Appendix J: A practical induction library

[719] Preview the sequence. Work out in advance which one you want to do and what you want to use it for (pick just one) and whether or not you want to do it at all. Any of these families of induction can be done as light inductions, with eyes open, or you can just do them as a no-trance inner contact.

[720] Once you start an induction, you will want to notice what’s going on (even if nothing changes) and work out what to do next: move, imagine something else, change the sensory channel you’re using, do nothing, or stop the induction sequence and do something else… Whatever you do, make sure you bring yourself fully back to waking at the end of an induction.

[721] NB: if you can’t ‘see’ the images you’re trying to visualize, that’s fine, change them into words, rhythm, spatial relations, something to touch or move or just plain knowing…

[722] ## 1. Stretch and release

[723] Work out what will be supporting you, and focus on the feeling of support. You can also focus on where one of your hands or feet is. Either actually, or in your imagination, stretch a little bit, either stretching that part of your body slightly or pressing gently down into the support, and then let go of the effort, just relax. See if there is any difference (it’s fine if there isn’t). Do this for no more than 3 places on your body. Let releasing guide you as you go through the sequence. Which bit of your body, or inner voice, needs this? What do you need to support it? Bring your body back to normal movement and return.

[724] ## 2. Supported floating

[725] Work out what is supporting you and where your weight is. Imagine that the support is holding a tiny bit (1%) more of your effort. It’s like floating but you don’t have to lose contact, or you can just keep it at the same level, with just enough weight/pressure. See what happens. You can fractionate this if you want, perhaps noticing your normal awareness when you breathe in and the support when you breathe out. Tell yourself you are going to fractionate before you do it. What can you hear more easily now? Keep the support, come back to your normal weight and firm contact, and return.

[726] ## 3. Rhythmic breath or pulse

[727] Use your breathing or a slight hum in your throat for a natural rhythm. You could also tap your finger, or follow a sound outside. You can even do it by counting to yourself (silently) or with no rhythm at all. Say “here, and here again” over and over, and notice what you pay attention to in between the phrases. Then stop saying it. Notice what you become aware of in the first beat – you don’t need to make your mind blank, just notice what comes up. Does it make any words, feelings, needs or actions more clear? Then let everything go back to its normal rhythm, and come back.

[728] ## 4. Unwinding object

[729] Unwinding an object is good for when you have a few different strands to one thing you want to work on. Imagine an object unwinding – maybe some yarn, some bubbles, or some knots that you place beside you. Once you’ve placed them all, give yourself a minute to look at them all. You don’t have to change anything, but you can label them if you need to (just a word or two). How many did you put down? Which one do you need to work on, and what does it need – warmth, protection, action? Keep one strand in your attention and put the others somewhere you can see them and return.

[730] ## 5. Sensory journey

[731] Pay attention to the sounds in the room you’re in and how your body feels, then imagine yourself walking somewhere. Where do you want to go? Listen to the sounds of your footsteps and take in your surroundings as you walk. What do objects feel like if you touch them? What landmarks do you see? When you feel you have gathered enough of your attention, imagine the scenery changing until you are in a place where you can talk to whoever you need to (or you can just cut to the chase, if you prefer). Pay attention to something without worrying about what it means. Allow yourself to be curious about what’s going to happen next, but don’t make it a “what are they going to do to you?” kind of thing. When you are ready to finish, imagine yourself walking back the way you came and close the space.

[732] ## 6. Gradual wave

[733] Start with a very small neutral area, feel into it a little and then imagine a wave of warmth, steadiness, or attention spreading out to a new area that is still ok. Sense into the new area (or continue using the old). See if there is anything different at each step. Does the body want any contact, space, words, other care? Let the mental image go, sense your body, sense your contact points, come back.

[734] ## 7. Care workshop

[735] Prepare the workshop (time, place, tools, boundaries, etc). Invite the signal/feeling/young part to come only as close as is welcome. Try to avoid any language that makes you feel you're not one person. Care for it as best you can, by asking what it wants and giving that if you can, or sensing into it and responding, or just being with it. Take breaks, get back in contact if that's ok, take more breaks, etc. Keep bringing in adult functions (or borrow someone else's function). Have the adult part of you run the show. The goal is to make it "good as you" rather than "good as new", not to erase history. Close the workspace, reassure yourself that you are one whole person, that you're an adult now, and come back.

[736] ## 8. Resource embodiment

[737] Pick a quality you understand and want more of, like warmth, being able to hold a boundary, steadiness, curiosity or dignity. Imagine yourself with just 1% more of it. (If you'd rather not step into it, you can watch an imagined you have it instead.) What's different? How do you sit, and how does your voice sound? Try it a few times; each time, it gets a little easier to find. Come back, keeping the quality and a positive action that you would do with it, but leaving the imagined you behind, saying "I am me".

[738] ## 9. No-trance inner contact

[739] We don't need to go into trance to get a lot of the benefits of the communion technique! Here's my no-trance version:

[740] For pacing (warm-up), notice reality as it is now. What do you see around you? Is today Monday, your favorite day of the week? Hopefully your name didn't suddenly change to Larry.

[741] For the suggestion: in your mind, ask a clear question and then listen gently.

[742] Now keep your sense of agency during the answer phase. You might notice an answer popped up, or you might decide you don't know. You can always take a break or rephrase the question. Sometimes you might ask advice from someone else.

[743] Keep aware of the subtle inner signal: write whatever you get. It might be a word or sensation. An impulse or objection. Sometimes there's no signal, just silence, itself a kind of signal. Don't jump to interpreting it as validation of some theory.

[744] Hand off the role: have your adult self answer that same question in whatever way is appropriate, preferably by writing or doing.

[745] Integration/exit: reality test the signal you noticed. As part of testing, if it seems appropriate, you can do something simple based on it.

[746] There's no elaborate exit protocol here since you didn't go into trance. Just close the exercise and carry on with your day.
