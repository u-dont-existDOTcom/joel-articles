# Part B of the guide: blocks 247 to 484

## Context before this part (read it, don't report on it)

[245] If you are new to this or haven't done it for a while, work on the coming in and coming out part first. Don't bring in the hard stuff till later. You don't have to watch yourself all the time, but for the first few times, make sure you can change state. Let yourself make it easier and easier to do this and notice that it takes less and less of you to do it.

[246] Let your response guide you as to what to do next regardless of what the script is expecting. Utilize: use what you have (a thought, sound, movement, or objection) to go to the next place, whatever it is. Learn how to notice what is there. If nothing changes, notice that too. As my dad always said, "Be prepared to take what you get. The best might come afterwards!" For more on language you can use, see Appendix B, or keep reading below for information on using the tools after induction in Let the next response guide you. Either way, keep the instructions about how to stay safe and how to get out plain.

## The part to read

[247] ## Sober induction: 3–5 minutes

[248] You can read this slowly into your recorder, or you can read it out loud or say it to yourself if you are good at that. Choose a sound, or a contact, or the two-hand practice (above) or something to anchor yourself, and choose a phrase you will say to open it up. Use the same anchor wherever the script mentions it. Don't read the directions out loud, just pause between paragraphs.

[249] Script:

[250] Let your eyes rest on something in the room for a bit, maybe a doorknob or a spot on the wall. Notice the difference between looking for something and letting what's already there come into view.

[251] You can allow yourself to notice something you can feel, like the sensation of something under you, and notice the sensation of the contact, the pressure. Or maybe there is a sound, or something else to notice.

[252] Allow yourself some time to do this.

[253] You might notice yourself thinking, and whenever you notice yourself thinking, you can allow yourself to re-engage with your anchor and leave the thought unfinished.

[254] You might notice something that holds your interest, something about the sound, or sensation, or listening or something else.

[255] Allow yourself some time to do this, as much time as you would for someone you care about who has started to talk and hasn't gotten to what she wants to say yet, so you are going to stick around till she gets it all out.

[256] Repeat the phrase that you chose, if you want to. Say it as an invitation and notice how it's received.

[257] You can close your eyes now if you want to. If not, it's fine to keep them open, or soften your gaze. The listening might go deeper while there's still a background sound you can hear.

[258] Count 5 to 1, taking your time if you like.

[259] Let your attention come back to your chosen anchor. 4. Let more of your attention come to what you've found. 3. Leave a little space. 2. See if you can listen to a space after the words without preparing an answer. 1. Take your time.

[260] Bring to mind, if you want, the thing that you want to talk about, or just the wish to have inner contact, and let it sit there without telling the whole story yet.

[261] What do you notice now?

[262] Let yourself notice what comes to you, if anything. It may not be clear yet what it is. It may be sensations, words, pictures, feelings, movements, or anything else.

[263] Let yourself take your time to notice, with or without words.

[264] ## When you’re already trancey

[265] This is for when something has already opened you up before you start: loveyhuasca, meditation, lucid dreaming, going through grief, listening to beautiful music, prayer, erotic energy, exhaustion, seeing something you love, etc.

[266] Know that you're already open, you just need to aim and stabilize it, and keep it kind.

[267] Orient yourself by looking around. Where are you? What's the date or day? How does your body feel? Where are you grounded? Can you feel your feet/hands/spine/etc? Make some contact with yourself, eg press your thumbs against the tips of your index fingers gently but firmly so that you can feel friction. Focus on that.

[268] Say something like, "I ask to experience only the therapy that I can integrate. Patience pays, so if there's too much, let it simmer. May my body stay safe, and may my little one know there's an adult here to carry the heavy stuff."

[269] Once you’ve got your bearings, make your first wish.

[270] "Please show me the next truth. The next symbol or feeling. The question or action that's useful for me at this time, and no more than I can handle."

[271] ## Stay awake when your mind likes to escape

[272] If your mind keeps drifting off, sit up and open your eyes. Then feel your feet on the floor, and just let your breathing go at its own pace. Tell yourself, "I'm not going anywhere. I'm happily arriving more fully here each moment."

[273] Pick something in the room to look at, and feel your spine at the same time (focusing on two things makes it harder for the mind to use multitasking mode to wander). Then ask yourself what the daydreaming is doing for you. Is it bringing you guidance? Does it help you work through a feeling, or are you using it to avoid something?

[274] Ask for any diffuse benefits to become more practically focused: "If this is guidance, show me what I need to do about it. If I'm avoiding something, bring me gently back to the present and show me what it is."

[275] ### Make the hypnosis more alert

[276] Aside from focusing on things in your direct experience, you can also suggest yourself into greater alertness (it's still relaxed-alert, not paranoid-alert). Keep your eyes open. Use a small hand movement each time you make the auto-suggestion that you're gaining increasing clarity. Then stop the hand movement and see where your mind is at. That hand movement is the mental escape into the body's flow.

[277] ## Sleep induction

[278] Sleep induction is different than inner work, where you need to come back up to write things down and act on them.

[279] You can tell your analytical mind to stand down, any revelations can wait.

[280] Check out Nimja’s sleep playlist to go to sleep listening to hypnosis.

[281] ## Optional fractionation: practice coming back

[282] Fractionation means coming partway back toward normal awareness, then deciding whether to go back into the trance. It gives the trance a revolving door, so you can check how the session is going from a few different depths instead of "flying blind." Going in and out a few times also makes going in and coming out easier and quicker, until it feels natural. At some point in a session, you can do it on purpose, like this:

[283] “Okay, open your eyes and come back up. Feel the room around you. You can decide if you want to stop the work at this point or continue. If you want to continue, soften your eyes or close them, and notice the change that comes over you when you do. If you want to stop, that’s okay too. Either way is okay, and you can stop at either place.”

[284] ## Deepening, if useful

[285] Allow your attention to gather, if it will be of use to you. If it won’t be of use to you at this time, you can remain as awake as you need to be.

[286] Pick a place to go to when you go inside. It might be a room in your heart, a garden, a temple, sitting in front of a fire with your Protector, down by a clear lake where you can see the bottom, in a comfortable chair where your adult is going to be, whatever you want.

[287] Who or what needs to talk first?

[288] ## Optional trance depth check

[289] “As you wait, you release the responsibility of making anything happen.

[290] It might be useful for your wise subconscious to let you know that your inner meeting space is ready for you to go into by making a finger twitch, pulse, lift up by itself, heat up or tingle in a way you notice.

[291] You don’t have to make it happen, or pretend that it is happening, just wait with love and notice what happens.”

[292] Sometimes a finger will move, sometimes an entire hand will heat up, sometimes you will notice a change in your breathing, sometimes you won’t notice anything at all. Even if something happens, it doesn’t mean that what comes next will be true, only that some part of you might be listening.

[293] ## A shortcut back into hypnosis

[294] Once you have learned a useful induction and how to come all the way back up at the end, you can do the following:

[295] Have an unusual word, phrase, and/or motion (optional) that you will use to go into hypnosis again. Say the word and/or phrase while doing the motion (if applicable). Be sure to do this when you are in hypnosis and in a good place. Don't do this when you are in a very emotional state, child state, or thinking about something that could be considered trauma.

[296] For example, my dad used to use a mudra (like putting two fingers together like an "OK" sign) when he needed to relax. He even did it when he was driving. Not that I recommend driving in a trance, don't do that! Make sure the cue you use to calm down while staying alert is not the same as your cue to go inward into hypnosis.

[297] Give your cue the meaning you choose: “When I pick the time and place (an appropriate time and place) and say the word and/or phrase to begin, I can go into this state that I picked and am familiar with. I can change direction, or stop, at any point.”

[298] Now come all the way back up again, and at a later time, when it is appropriate, start using this. Over time, you can shorten the length of the longer induction, or use the longer induction if this doesn't seem to be enough for your needs. You don't have to do this a certain number of times, go to a certain depth, or even have a certain sensation when you do this.

[299] Also, be sure that this is working for you: you can turn it on and off at will, it isn't there when you don't want it to be, and you can change direction or come back up when you are in it. Don't use it in a way that makes you follow instructions that you are hearing from a narrator, notifications, or something that you overhear. Don't use this to go into hypnosis faster for things that you shouldn't be working on yet.

[300] There is something you should never do with this shortcut: use it, practice it or test it while you're driving or doing something where you need to be actively paying attention. It's just not safe to be going into a trance where you are actively paying attention to what's going on inside of you when you need to be paying attention to what's going on outside of you. Even though your eyes can be open, even though you're using "if it would be useful for you to do so" type language, it's just not safe.

[301] ### Turning the shortcut off, or keeping it

[302] If you want to turn the shortcut off, simply don't practice it anymore. Take it out of your recordings, take it out of your apps if you're using it in an app, and start using your regular induction. Tell yourself, when you're awake, that you won't be using it anymore. Just because you say that it'll be gone doesn't mean that it'll be gone right away. If it's causing you a problem, if you're still responding to it when you don't want to respond to it, if you're having issues with disorientation, etc, get some help from someone who's qualified to help you with this. If, on the other hand, you want to keep it so that you can use it to go back into trance at a later date when you do want to go into a trance, that's fine too. Keeping it doesn't keep today's trance going, though.

[303] # Come all the way back

[304] If you have done anything to alter your state when entering the trance, make sure to reverse those things. If you have floated, make sure to gain your weight again (whatever weight you want of course). If you have shrunk, make sure to grow back to your regular size. If you have met a younger state or are working with parts, make sure to come back to feeling complete in your body in the here and now, that your body belongs to you, and that it is under the control of your adult mind. If you have gone somewhere, make sure to come back the same way you went. Etcetera.

[305] Here is a generic bring-back that you can use: "When I come out of this session, I will only bring with me that which is useful to me in my everyday life right now. Anything else I will bring with me at a later date, when I am better able to integrate it. When I come out of this session, I will be coming back to the room I am in now, and to the day in which I am participating in this session now. All the instructions to carry me deeper into this session or keep me in this state are now finished. The only intention I will take with me into my everyday life, and the only cue I will use to take me into this state at a later date, will be the ones I consciously choose, and neither of them will keep me in this state. When I come out of this session I will be me, in my body and with my adult judgment."

[306] Then do a count up: On 1 start to feel your body again and what it's resting on and feel your weight (of your choice). On 2 start to feel your breathing return to normal. On 3 start to hear the room around you and name where you are. On 4 start to wiggle your fingers and toes, move your shoulders and your eyes. On 5 open your eyes, back in the here and now, feeling normal and clear and the session is now over.

[307] When you come out, make sure you can name what day (or date) it is, look to your left and right, look at something near to you and something further away. Move around a bit. Don't get up until you feel like you can do so without falling over. Make sure that you are oriented and alert (counting does this, but not always). Make a mental note of just what actually came up and anything you need to do about it, then go and do something normal, like eating or washing up.

[308] If your recording happens to cut out partway through, woops... you could end up lying there dazed & confused, waiting on a voice that isn't coming back. Don't worry, you don't have to be a vegetable forever. Just tell yourself, "The recording has stopped and we're all done now. Welcome back to me!" (that exclamation actually helps, because trance is kinda monotone). Then do the exit-trance count and the present-time checks above on your own. Once your eyes are open, "touch grass" as they say, for grounding. Or if no grass, just push your adorable feet or hands into something solid. The physical feeling connects your brain to your body.

[309] Sleep recordings are another story, since you're assuming you'll drift off midway with those. But if the sleep recording is designed to also wake you back up, make sure it's doing so intelligently, not just using a vague exit word that might half wake you up and still leave you a bit blank or "under the spell" so to speak.

[310] # The actual inner meeting

[311] Note: If you have a tendency to daydream, dissociate or fall asleep, it is better to do this exercise sitting up and save lying down for when you want to go to sleep, get a very deep rest or incubate dreams. If you are sitting, keep your spine straight but not rigid like a soldier. Relax your jaw and stay somewhat connected to the room you are in.

[312] “I am not leaving life; I am listening more deeply to life.

[313] I invoke my Higher Self, universal heart-mind, wise subconscious mind, intelligence in my body, inner child(ren), inner adult(s), guard(s) and all that is of truth and love to help me know what I need to know, only in a form and at a time that will be useful, kind, stabilizing and real for me.”

[314] Ask your conscious mind to listen, and don't force an answer.

[315] This does not mean you have to stop thinking to listen. Just notice your thoughts, body sensations, pictures, doubts, emotions, etc. Don’t fight what comes up, but don’t go with it either. Just notice what stands out on its own as you hold the idea of what you are doing in the back of your mind.

[316] Internally say, “Tell me what I need to know or ask me what you need to know and I’ll sit in silence for 5 minutes and listen.”

[317] You may hear a phrase, feel a body sensation, notice a memory, see a color, be contacted by your inner child or a guard, feel pain, warmth, complete silence, know that you need to do something, be grateful for something, feel you need to stop the listening or any number of things.

[318] Listen to everything and believe nothing automatically.

[319] # Let the next response guide you

[320] The induction and the inner meeting get you to where you can glisten. From there, you answer whatever comes up, and you can't know ahead of time what every part of you is going to say. Read through these sections first, and then put the screen away and try the inner meeting.

[321] Respond to each thing that comes up with an accurate notice and a small reasonable invitation (or action or taking more time etc). The thing might be a sensation, image, gesture, feeling, words, or anything. The notice and invitation don't need to cover all those forms, just notice and invite what happens.

[322] ## Finding your own words

[323] An example notice might be, "I notice my chest tightening when I imagine asking for help". Don't add interpretation like "this must be the abandoned child part of me, or a protective mechanism" - just notice what you notice. An example invitation might be, "And as I notice that, I can take some time before deciding what it means, and notice if I want to say anything, or change it, or just stay like that".

[324] Then just listen. You don't need fancy language - often it will be better to just ask what happens next, or whatever. If you notice a feeling developing, it might be best not to say anything while it's developing (if you can help it), but some days you need to talk to understand, so just do that.

[325] You can be pretty straightforward when that's the help you chose - "Let the explanation wait a moment. What do I feel as I say that?" can be a perfectly valid thing to say to yourself, and help you focus. As long as you're still present, and able to change if you want to, and happy to carry on, a sad feeling or tension or whatever doesn't require an exit.

[326] ### Notice, meaning, and your thoughts on the meaning

[327] It's worth trying to separate the notice, the meaning, and your thoughts on the meaning, but in practice it will often be pretty fuzzy. For example, "I noticed... this seemed to mean... here's why that might be true / false" or something like that.

[328] Is this a notice or meaning? "My stomach went tight" is a notice, "He's a complete bastard" is a meaning, even if you didn't think it in words. You can then go on to find out what he did, notice what it was that gave you the idea, and consider if there might be other explanations. It's also ok to put a meaning into words now, to look at it, even if it came to you without words. Nonverbal thoughts and early thoughts aren't necessarily any deeper, truer, or more reliable than other thoughts.

[329] ## Let the response change the invitation

[330] Your shoulders tightening, or 'hmm, that feels kind of wrong', is a signal to investigate further, but 'No! That is Not Right!' is a signal to stop what you're doing and change it. If you don't feel like it fits, don't keep doing it, and don't try saying it again more persuasively. You don't need any other reason.

[331] "I notice the tightness, and I can just leave it like that. Then again, is that what I really want?"

[332] Sometimes the invitation just isn't right or helpful, even if it sounds like something a hypnotist might say. Each time we play around with this, we explore new possibilities which bring up new inner signals for us to feel out.

[333] Doubting is sometimes a guard to listen to, and sometimes simply part of normal consideration. Lack of doubt doesn't necessarily mean the process is working in the best way, either.

[334] ## Look at the situation too

[335] If there is another person involved in the situation, it may be a good idea to look at what they are doing as well. Can they hear you disagree, take responsibility, and make room for what you're going through? Try and be specific in what you conclude about them, and be willing to change it if you find out that it was wrong. Just because someone is disagreeing with you or trying to set boundaries with you does not mean they are in their emotionally immature parts. You don’t need to subject yourself to something that may be dangerous before protecting yourself.

[336] Look at your own side too. You may find that you feel guilty. Having feelings of guilt does not mean that you have done something wrong or that you have been manipulated. Have you done something that was wrong or that caused harm? If so, what can you do to fix it? On the other hand, did you give in to someone else just to keep them from being disappointed in you? What do you want to do or achieve the next time you interact with this person? Do you need them to understand what you are feeling/thinking? Do you just need to communicate information to them? Do you need to set boundaries with them? Do you need to end the interaction? You don’t have to make them understand before you can act.

[337] ## Change the amount of contact

[338] When you start to contact the difficult thing, if it feels too much, immediately start to widen your awareness to something else in the room, in your body, etc. Keep moving back and forth between the difficult thing and the easier thing. This is called pendulation, and approaching in smaller amounts is titration.

[339] You can say, "I can let this much be here, and notice what's supporting me right now." And when you've had enough, "Ok, I think that is enough for now."

[340] Feel free to go back and explore more if you wish to do so. If, at any point, you start to lose contact with the here and now, feel that things are becoming more and more unreal, or you feel that you are not able to switch to something else or stop, stop trying to explore inside of yourself and just do the entire "coming back" part. If you have trouble orienting yourself and it lasts for a prolonged period of time, please get some human help rather than trying a stronger induction.

[341] ### If you feel an urge to move

[342] If you feel an urge to move, turn, reach, push, or change your body’s position, feel free to either actually do something that will not cause your body pain or discomfort, or just imagine doing what you feel you need to do, and see what happens next. If you do move and you feel that you are causing yourself pain or other symptoms that you are concerned about, stop moving.

[343] ## Offer the care that is actually wanted

[344] If any younger parts come up, offer them the opportunity to spend time with you, or some space if they would rather. If one of them says “don’t come closer”, stay at the distance they choose. You don’t have to consider them literally younger, nor do you have to ask them why they are there or what happened to them. If you can identify any guards (childhood protector parts), talk to them in the same Nurturer voice you meant for your little one, and find out what they are concerned about. You don’t have to get rid of any of them.

[345] If you feel that it would be useful to bring in your Nurturer, Protector or Guide to help you either change the tone of the interaction, set boundaries, or find your way toward the life you want, feel free to do so. You don’t have to talk to them, just use the one whose job you need, and if you can’t do it yet, borrow it. You only need to use the Witness if you need help getting started with a step.

[346] Try some different ideas as to what might be helpful for this inner part to feel better (being welcomed by you, visualizing someone else, giving them the sort of words they would like to hear, you placing yourself in a different body position, you moving further away etc). Only try one idea at a time to see if it works.

[347] It is possible that when you try and give the part what it wants, you might feel very upset. It may be that it never got what it needed at the time and now that you are giving it what it wants, it is processing the upset of not getting what it wanted before. That doesn’t mean your love isn’t working. If this happens, allow yourself to respond for as long as you want to do so, and for as long as you can stay there (ie remain in the process), but don’t push for more tears, since crying more doesn’t mean you’re healing more. If you feel that the voice, distance, touch, image etc is too much, then change it to something that is less.

[348] Allow yourself to see where a metaphor takes you. Ask questions like ‘if the problem had an image or story, what would it be?’ or ‘what would need to happen to make things right?’ If it feels like you need to do something to give yourself the resource that you feel you need (or to put a boundary in place, or to move away or to take some other action) then try doing that, as far as it helps you. Remember that whatever you find out is likely to be metaphorical. It may be a good hypothesis for what you need to do, but it isn’t a record of what actually happened.

[349] Alternatively, you may want to use the session to build up something you want more of (eg love, play, courage, curiosity etc). You can also use it to rehearse something that you have decided to do.

[350] ## Finish without forcing a revelation

[351] Don’t feel that you have to end the session with a great revelation (those are often flaky, not standing the test of time). When you have done as much as you want to do, ask yourself what would help you leave things where they are for now. This might include thanking parts for their help, or making sure that you do something in real life (put a boundary in place, get some rest, repair a relationship, keep a promise etc). You can tell your little one, "That’s enough for today, choochoo. Aren't you excited to get back to the life we're building?"

[352] When you have finished, even if you stopped part way through, use the ‘Come all the way back’ procedure to bring yourself back. When you are back, it is a good idea to write a few lines in a notebook about what you have done. Just jot down the essentials (what was there before you started, what you have done to change things, what happened, what you are unsure about, any phrases that were said, any body signals, any information that the inner child said that it needed, anything that a guard wanted you to know, any boundaries that you put in place, any repairs that you did, anything you learned about what not to force etc). You don’t have to list every part of you that might have been involved, only what you actually noticed.

[353] ### Throughout the exercise

[354] Allow your analytical part to make comments and/or tell you that it doesn’t like what you are doing. Just tell it that you will check things out, and make any commitments etc, when you are back. If it says no to going deeper, or tells you to stop doing the exercise, I’d stop there. You can stop whenever you want to. Just because you see an image, or feel a body release, doesn’t mean you are right about what is going on.

[355] # The 15-minute daily practice

[356] You don't need to learn a second script for this. Just do the same practice, from the induction to coming all the way back, for fifteen minutes or so. That is long enough to induce yourself (it only takes a few minutes), practice and let it unfold at its own pace, and come out again (that takes a few minutes too). Fifteen minutes is just a handy ointment with yourself, though, and your feelings don't have to keep to it. Five minutes is plenty to see what happens when you simply sit in silence, listening, and you can interrupt yourself at any time to change direction if that feels better.

[357] You can also try other ways of inducing yourself, such as becoming aware of 3 things you can see, 3 things you can hear, 3 things you can feel in your body; then 2 of each, 1 of each. Then count from 5 to 1 and begin to listen. You don't need to try all of them (in fact, stacking them might be counter-productive).

[358] Once you have practiced listening a few times with the recordings, you may want to shorten them to speed up your induction, or even practice without playing them back at all. On the other hand, if you like using the recordings, there is no reason to stop. (Once you know the induction well, the cue from "A shortcut back into hypnosis" can shorten it too.)

[359] As you come out of the process, ask yourself what needed warmth, what needed to be protected, what you need to do differently to be more in accord with truth and love and life. Is there something you want to explore more fully or a capacity you want to build? And most importantly, what can you now carry yourself that you used to have to borrow? If you identified something you needed to do differently, make a plan with yourself to do that thing. The more specific you can be, the more likely you are to change: “If _________ (fill in the cue for the old pattern), then I will do ________ (fill in a small action that will begin to change the pattern).” Then check back later and see how you are doing – sleeping, relating, functioning, etc., not only how impressive the session felt.

[360] # The weekly deeper practice

[361] Once or twice a week, when it’s useful, give this practice more space and time so even the unplanned, unknown and maybe hidden needs can show up. Just because it's unplanned doesn't mean it will be more shocking. Start from your Higher Self, from the field of acceptance and understanding, or however you can describe it that makes it feel accessible. If you need the Protector or another of your adult jobs, bring it in or borrow it from someone safe. Mr. or Ms. Protector can check to make sure you’re not skipping past anything important. Leave enough time at the end to come all the way back up & out.

[362] Then, if there's something from the session that asked you to do something in real life, and your Protector agrees, then go ahead and do it when you can. That's how you show your subconscious you mean business.

[363] # Capacity before content

[364] Note: if it is medically unsafe, or you are in a place of panic, sleep deprivation, nausea, deep destabilization, mania, psychosis-like symptoms, feeling overwhelmed by chemicals etc., focus on stabilizing first, with help if you need it (see “When this needs more support than a guide can provide”), and only then look for what your subconscious needs you to know. If knowing what your subconscious knows would destabilize you, it is not meant for you to know directly at this time. Ask for capacity before content.

[365] Sometimes, it feels like your subconscious knows something that your conscious self is not prepared to know. It may be something related to your sexuality, grief, trauma, family issues, betrayal, shame, vocation, a relationship, illness fears, spirituality, addiction, identity, moral issues etc.

[366] What you don’t want to do is bully your subconscious into telling you what it knows by saying things to yourself like:

[367] “What is it you won’t tell me? What are you hiding? Tell me NOW! I am telling you to tell me!”

[368] This can lead to flooding, panic, false certainty, confabulation, dissociation etc., and can make your day suck. When you are flooded, it is much harder to access your adult capacities, so focus on being able to regulate and on getting support first. Then ask for what you need to know in a way that you can tolerate:

[369] “What can you tell me, that will make my conscious self 5% better at knowing the thing you want to tell me?” “Can you tell me in symbolic form so I am not completely overwhelmed by the revelation?” “Can you tell me what I need to do to be safe, without actually telling me what it is?” “Do I need to know what it is now, or do I need to get some support first, so I have the capacity to know it, and who would be a good person to help me?” etc.

[370] Or, instead of demanding answers, say to yourself something like this:

[371] “Dear Wise Subconscious Mind, if there is something you know that my conscious self is not ready to know, please don’t break it open violently. Do what you can to help me know it in a way that is safe. Prepare me before you break it open. Ground me before you show me the memory. Support me before you make it intense. And show me the next thing to do.” etc.

[372] Remember: not everything in hypnotic imagery is literal, especially if it relates to trauma and memory. The mind makes it symbolic, squashes things together, distorts, dramatizes, pulls from cultural influences, fears etc. Get some help outside of yourself when you need it.

[373] # Troubleshooting

[374] ## Nothing happens

[375] Don't fret, even for the greatest masters, many times nothing happens. After all, only like 15% of people are very suggestible. But sometimes it's just confusing when you throw everything at yourself at once. Check if there's a way you could try this on a smaller scale... like is it even the right time for this? You can directly ask that.

[376] ## "It feels fake"

[377] Let's drill into that "it"... do you have a sense of what exactly feels feigned, imposterous, or preposterous? The words, an image, what you are expecting to get from this, a role you feel you have to play... am I getting warm? Not having an inner voice or images doesn't mean you're resisting the work, nor that you're missing a 'grown-up'. If it feels like you are being asked to do something you don't want to do, then correct the invitation. It could be that there is a part that is protecting you, so talk to it.

[378] You could say something like 'What are you protecting me from, helicopter mom?' or 'Are you sus because you think this will just be a silly performance?' If it says yes, that one can sting, because there could be something to it. Maybe you've fooled yourself before, and that part remembers. But as ex-US-President George W. Bush wisely explained, "Fool me once, shame on — shame on you. Fool me — you can't get fooled again." !!!

[379] But seriously... you can then say, "Tell me what you remember, I'm listening." It puts you into the role of the Nurturer, which alleviates the instinct to argue back.

[380] It could also be your inherited parent script that you are picking up, and it is trying to enforce a rule about how you should be to belong to them. Find out what rule it is enforcing. Comfort your little one before you answer any of it. Your answers can be firm or funny, as long as they aren’t mean. Then work on finding some ordinary evidence to build a more believable 'grown up' voice of your own.

[381] If the fake feeling is telling you that you don't have the grown up ability to do this yet, then don't try and convince yourself by using an inner voice. As long as you can still observe yourself well enough to do this, try and borrow what you need from someone who you can imagine is good enough to lend it to you. This could be a family member, friend, famous person etc. What you need might be warmth, boundary, direction etc. For warmth, just make sure it's someone who liked you the way you were. Try out something which rings true to you, whether it is a sentence, an action etc, and see how you get on.

[382] ## The subconscious will not answer

[383] There may not be an answer. There may not be an answer that will help you. You may need to try asking a different question or you may just need to rest or finish your inner work for the day. If trust is actually a concern, you can try asking your subconscious “What would you need to know in order to trust me?” Some examples of answers might be “I need to know you will set boundaries,” “I need to know you will do what you said you will do,” “I need to know you won’t keep leaving,” or “I need to know you won’t think that knowing something means that you don’t need to do anything about it.”

[384] ## The inner child will not appear

[385] If you can tell there is a guard in the way, talk to it. You can say to the guard “You don’t have to let me in, just tell me what I need to do to make it safe”. Even if there isn’t a child part that you can talk to, you can work with whatever you are aware of. You can work on your preferences or something that you’re curious about or something that you’re willing to try as a beginner etc. and see how it goes. Work on developing a sense of who you are and reparent yourself as best you can.

[386] ## You get anxious

[387] Find out if the anxiety is bad enough that you want to decrease contact with it or if you can manage it and let it continue. If the anxiety is getting too bad, then you need to stop searching for content and help your body first, like the following:

[388] -Where are your feet? Where are you sitting? Focus on your body, your breathing, and the room around you.

[389] -Tell your little one and your guards (childhood protector parts) that you’re there for your little one, that you recognize that your guards are trying to protect you, and that you don’t need them to scream at you in order for you to know that something is wrong. Ask them if they need you to do something, and if they do, ask them to show you one step at a time that you can do. If they don’t, then ask them to let your body calm down.

[390] If you’re having chest pain, fainting, suicidal thoughts, psychosis-like symptoms, mania-like symptoms, severe dissociative symptoms, not being able to sleep, wanting to hurt yourself or somebody else, etc., then you need to get professional help. For more on handling anxiety, see the toolbox in Appendix D.

[391] ## You dissociate or feel unreal

[392] Open your eyes, move around, feel something textured, name 5 things that you can see, and end the deeper practice for the day.

[393] ## You become grandiose

[394] Go eat, go to sleep, do some chores, accept some humility, find somebody to disagree with you, etc.

[395] What do you say to yourself in that moment? “If it’s real, it’ll still be here after a good night’s sleep, some food, some time, and some subsequent humility, feedback, etc.”

[396] ## You become too analytical

[397] “What if you didn’t try to improve the practice?” Put one hand on your heart and the other on your solar plexus, as in the two-hand practice, and say, “I don’t have to do this perfectly. I’m here for a relationship with something deeper than my surface mind.” “What can you do to make this a communion, not a performance?” Maybe it’s silence, resting, or doing something. Whatever it is, just do it.

[398] ## You become too blissful or diffuse

[399] If the bliss spreads out into a big warm haze, it can’t do much for you or anyone else yet. Bring it down to Earth by asking, “What’s this lofty love asking me to do? Is there a task I'm ignoring? Or a boundary I need to set? Am I getting enough rest and nourishment? Am I hiding from a needed conversation or relationship repair?”

[400] ## You want the hidden truth immediately

[401] Don’t pry.

[402] “Show me the capacity before the content.”

[403] ## You keep daydreaming

[404] Sit up and open your eyes, and ask yourself, “What are you avoiding doing in the real world by doing this?”

[405] ## You get stuck in spiritual bypass

[406] “Where has divine love not yet descended into the wound?”

[407] Maybe you had a profound opening that showed you how much more love and freedom is possible. Let that matter, but don’t demand that your inner child receive it on command. Invite your little one in with you. They might go numb, or call BS on that. If so, they might just need to see if you’re actually humble enough, and grateful enough for them, to listen to their rejection and be patient. Maybe when you prayed it seemed like you were being abandoned and condemned. Maybe you felt betrayed by a spiritual teacher. It may be worth exploring these, but you might not want to do more of the same practice.

[408] Sometimes it’s best to get some practical help while life still feels too small, and let yourself be curious about finding more happiness, without necessarily believing the explanation of how it’s going to work. Sometimes people have enough love and meaning to just live their lives, and trying to be spiritual doesn’t have to be another assignment.

[409] ## You get stuck in endless processing

[410] You may have a real wound that you need to deal with and be caught in a checking loop at the same time. Consider asking yourself “Will processing this again actually help me feel, understand, decide, or do something differently? Or is this a form of checking to see if I can get the reassurance that something is finally okay?” Sometimes, whether you’re grieving or doing your practice, it’s okay to cover the same ground again and again.

[411] The best thing to do, though, if you notice you’re in a checking loop, is allow the thought to be there without engaging with it and get on with what you have chosen to do.

[412] What would the Guide choose to do now?

[413] ## You keep using hypnosis but not changing life

[414] Ask yourself, “What can I do to show my little one I’m for real?” Pick one thing to do that’s small enough to repeat, then go do it. A big gesture you later drop would only prove them right to doubt you, but a small thing you keep doing can change their mind.

[415] # What not to do

[416] Don’t view your inner experiences as commands or proof. They’re internal happenings that need to be reality tested at all times.

[417] Don’t go seeking “downloads” of information, or make a hobby of demons/jinn/Unattached Burdens or the like. Stabilize your life first, and get help to do so if you are not reality testing well, or not functioning well in your life.

[418] Don’t do self-hypnosis while driving, bathing, swimming, operating any equipment, supervising children, or doing anything else that requires active attention.

[419] Don’t try to “force” memories, to use trance to “prove” something happened to you, or to skip medical attention for symptoms just because you can “force” them down using hypnosis.

[420] Don’t force yourself to ignore your body’s needs, such as eating when hungry, resting when tired, responding to pain, etc., or your emotions, such as letting yourself grieve when someone dies, feeling anger when it is appropriate, acknowledging your fears, etc., until you know more about what the signals are telling you.

[421] Don’t “extend” the duration of altered states indefinitely.

[422] Don’t allow your inner roles to get out of proportion (e.g., letting your inner child run your adult life, the Protector shut out all love, the Nurturer disregard consequences, or the Guide ignore the needs of your inner child). You are capable of analysis, as well as tenderness.

[423] Don’t use NLP techniques to force down guard parts, to give yourself certainty you do not have, to manipulate others, or to heal yourself when your body is telling you not to.

[424] Don’t consider negative indicators (doubt, distraction, resistance, lack of response, saying “No”) as good signs that the induction is working.

[425] Don’t rely upon the safety of your unconscious mind as the only safety factor in any session. Be sure you have set up clear limits, are in a safe place, and have a tested way to end the session and get out.

[426] Don’t believe that normal physical sensations are indications of hidden trauma, illness, toxins in your body, possession, “attachments,” “transformations,” or information from your “subconscious mind.”

[427] Don’t believe that anything is “healed” in your body (neurologically, cellularly, etc.) simply because it is “healed” in your imagination.

[428] Don’t put hidden commands in yourself, nor allow anyone to do so. Even if you are consciously placing something in yourself which you wish to utilize later, be sure that you know exactly what you are doing and why you are doing it, and be sure that you can review it and can “take it out” if you decide that you no longer wish to use it. It may not be as easy as simply saying, “Take out.” Nor should you allow anyone to activate these commands in you without your knowledge, or use them to make you do something you don’t want to do, to make you into someone you don’t want to be, to give you amnesia, etc.

[429] # When this needs more support than a guide can provide

[430] You should get help now if you are in imminent danger, if you are very confused, if you cannot ensure your own safety, and/or if you are experiencing concerning physical symptoms (e.g., chest pain, fainting, etc). You should also seek professional help promptly if you are experiencing new or worsening suicidal thoughts, severe insomnia, new or worsening manic or psychotic symptoms, or if dissociative symptoms are impacting your ability to function.

[431] You should seek suitable ongoing help if you are experiencing recurring panic attacks, compulsions, destabilization from substances, major relationship instability, etc.

[432] You probably don't need any more support than this if you keep the practice shorter, make it warm, pick a small and tractable subject, make sure you come back out, and then see how it affects a specific target (anxiety, daydreaming, helping a relationship, facing a task you've been avoiding, helping you sleep, being less reactive emotionally, improving your intuitive accuracy, etc) for a week or two. If you notice that your functioning is getting worse, or that you're getting more certain about unconfirmable memories, then reassess instead of pushing harder.

[433] ## Methods to explore with a trained person present

[434] If you want to deliberately do trauma-focused regression (going backwards in your memory or imagination to times when you were younger), or affect and somatic bridging (following associations from an emotional or somatic experience to find the origin of that experience, particularly if it was a traumatic experience you don't know about), or if you find that you're having substantial flashbacks or dissociative reactions, then you should do this with a trauma-informed clinician present. Make sure they're trained in the specific method you want to do. Ask any potential clinician what they would do and how they deal with uncertainty and unexpected reactions. Note that even if you work with a person, hypnosis is not a way of knowing what did or did not happen to you.

[435] Note that having experienced trauma, crying, and even wanting to feel difficult feelings are not necessarily signs that you need more support than working on your own. You get to pick how wide or narrow a range you want to work in, but be aware that different people need different ranges. Pay attention to how you respond and what support you have. Feeling like you are handling it isn't a substitute for having support.

[436] The main clinical practice guidelines for the treatment of major trauma recommend one or more of trauma-specific variants of cognitive behavioral therapy (often referred to by specific names, such as cognitive processing therapy, prolonged exposure, etc.) or eye movement desensitization and reprocessing, depending on the person and the guideline. These approaches in turn can be further supported by techniques such as self-hypnosis and inner child work. But high-risk trauma work needs a bigger container than that.

[453] # Appendix A: How to ask the subconscious a useful question

[454] Your subconscious mind is not an AI large language model. However, there is a bit of overlap in the way you can ask questions to both: how you ask shapes the answer. So, if you know how to do prompt engineering for AI, this can be very useful to help you be more present, honest, loving etc. But you should never use it to be more controlling.

[455] To get as much precision as possible, you need to include the following in your prompt:

[456] Role: Who or what are you talking to?

[457] Context: Give some context so it knows what you’re talking about.

[458] Intention: What do you want to change or accomplish?

[459] Constraint: What do you need to do to make sure your question is safe, true, kind etc.? No forcing, no false certainty, and no asking for an answer before you can handle it.

[460] Output: How do you want to receive the answer? As an image, phrase, sensation in your body, memory, action, question, symbol, silence, rest etc.

[461] Evaluation: How are you going to evaluate the answer? How will you know if it’s true, kind, timely, stabilizing, specific, testable etc?

[462] Iteration: Iterate by changing your prompt depending on what you get from reality as feedback.

[463] Most importantly, always let silence count as an answer, otherwise the analytical part of your mind will make up an answer just so it fits with your prompt. I usually add the following to my prompt:

[464] “If you need more time or the best and safest answer is silence, then I ask you to make my body go into a deeper state of comfortable rest. A peaceful silence would be a successful answer to my question.”

[465] Once you do that, here are the core prompts you can use:

[466] ## Core prompts

[467] For getting clarity: “I ask you, my wise subconscious mind, to show me what is true, kind and timely. If what you want to show me is too intense for me to integrate, please transfer it to my conscious mind in a way I can integrate it, for instance as a symbol, a signal in my body, a next step I can take. Most importantly, show me first my capacity to be peaceful, and then, the content of my wounding.”

[468] When you experience resistance: “What are you protecting me from? What would help you trust me 1% more?”

[469] When you want to ask your inner child something: “What do you want me to know? What do you need me to do today so that you are not alone in dealing with it?”

[470] When you want to bring in your Protector: “What would a minimally competent adult do here, in the external world, so that all of me is safe?”

[471] When you want to ask your Guide: “What direction is the most in alignment with truth, love, life and all beings?”

[472] When you want to listen to your intuition: “I ask my intuition to give me a signal, my analytical mind to decipher it, my body to give me feedback on whether my deciphering is correct, and reality to test it.”

[473] When you want to know if a particular intuition you have is coming from your universal heart or from your programming: “I ask my wise subconscious mind to help me see if the intuition or impulse I have about …is coming from my universal heart or from my family, my religion, marketing, erotic programming, my political tribe, my trauma response or an old deal I did as a child to belong. If it’s coming from my programming, I ask the parts responsible for it to step back and soften only as much as is safe, so that I can have true guidance from my universal heart that will help me make my life better in the most simple ways possible. Let my body give me feedback as to the difference.”

[474] Once you get an answer, you can ask: “What can I do in my ordinary life to show that I heard correctly?” If it tells you to do something, I'd only do it if it's loving and leaves you free. It would have to make sense too. And even with all that, I wouldn't do anything big or hard to undo until I'm calm and I've checked it against what I actually know.

[475] You use the prompt to tell your subconscious what you want in a language it can easily understand. The NLP in Appendix B below can help smooth the prompt's entry and reduce resistance. Used together properly, the subconscious can feel safer in giving its response, though that doesn't make the answers any more true.

[476] # Appendix B: NLP without turning yourself into a sales seminar

[477] NLP stands for Neuro-Linguistic Programming.

[478] You do not need to know this stuff to use the main path, but if you want to freshen up on your skills in the art of working with imagery, body states, behavioral rehearsal, talking to yourself in effective sentences etc., then it might be useful, as long as it helps you listen. Using the techniques can be an easy way to fall into the trap of thinking that NLP is a bag of tricks rather than something that needs to be done with love and truth and contact and consent.

[479] ## The four NLP layers

[480] The four levels of NLP are:

[481] Sensory Level of NLP: working with the form of an inner experience (where do you see or hear it, how far away, how big, how bright, how loud, how do you hold your body, where is the pressure etc.)

[482] State Level of NLP: making useful states (warmth, adult presence, courage, love etc.) easier to get to.

[483] Strategy Level of NLP: practicing a better response.

[484] Language Level of NLP: pacing what is true; opening up people’s awareness; presuming only that you can do something safe; linking cause and effect carefully; embedding invitations; asking de-hypnotic questions to break the effects of vague, absolute, manipulative or grandiose language.
