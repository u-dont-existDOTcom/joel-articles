## Main conclusions

EAET is included within the 2025 experiential dynamic therapy review, while integrating several techniques and a specific focus on persistent pain/physical symptoms. No randomized direct comparison of EAET against AEDP, ISTDP, or Affect Phobia Therapy was identified in this targeted search.

The method comparison must separate therapist challenge from experiential facilitation. ISTDP can be highly direct or graded/supportive; AEDP can actively address defenses; EAET can involve demanding emotional work. Therapist style cannot be reliably reduced to a fixed intensity ranking.

EAET's 2024 older-veteran trial favored it over CBT for pain. Its 2017 fibromyalgia trial did not show superiority on the six-month primary mean pain outcome, despite some secondary advantages. The 2026 internet study tested weekly written guidance within self-help, not self-help versus face-to-face therapy. Its primary between-condition difference was nonsignificant.

Do not use the pooled EDT meta-analysis to infer AEDP-specific efficacy or count included EAET studies again as independent confirmation. Do not treat a whole-treatment outcome as proof that suppressed anger caused the condition or that a prescribed emotion sequence is universally necessary.

Priority additions for editorial consideration: the missing emotional-psychotherapy family; EAET alongside Pain Reprocessing Therapy; imagery rescripting adjacent to protective/nurturing memory work. Cognitive functional therapy is a bounded pain-rehabilitation candidate. Emotion-Focused Therapy must be distinguished from tapping, which also uses EFT. WET and DBT-PTSD are useful comparators; Hakomi and NARM need explicit evidence limits.
