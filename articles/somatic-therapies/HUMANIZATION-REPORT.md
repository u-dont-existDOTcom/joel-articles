## What was actually humanized

The source's main AI signature was its repeated card architecture: `Goal → Best use → Not ideal → warning/caveat`, repeated across modality after modality. That made the article read like a series of generated mini-essays even where the underlying material was good.

The candidate keeps the five-job map but reconstructs the prose around the actual reader question: **what does my nervous system need help doing right now?** The modalities now enter because they answer that question rather than because every modality receives the same template.

The pass also:
- removed repeated explanatory aftercare and conclusion-after-conclusion sentences;
- varied thought duration instead of giving every modality the same rhetorical space;
- kept practical lists where lists genuinely help navigation;
- moved from generic professional phrasing toward direct first-/second-person guide prose;
- preserved the article's real oddness and personality instead of manufacturing new quirks: Professor Baby Sheep, the head-shaving/loveyhuasca line, Louka, the heart-chakra loop, and the blunt yoga/shaking warnings remain;
- removed several initially generated metaphors and personalizations during the cold audit because they were not source-derived.
