The owner-directed conceptual direction includes:

- CBT belongs in the article precisely because it is **not** another somatic modality; it is the major comparison case that can make the somatic project intelligible.
- Give CBT genuine credit for reality testing, behavioral experiments, changing avoidance, testing beliefs/predictions, and cognitive/narrative updating.
- Reject both `somatic fundamentalism` and `CBT fundamentalism`: bodily signals are neither automatically true commands nor automatically irrational noise.
- Strong current conceptual synthesis: **listen before correcting; then test what needs testing.** This is conceptual authority/source-pool language, not yet owner-final publication wording.
- Premature cognitive restructuring can create a gaslighting-like failure mode when bodily information is dismissed before it is investigated; do not make the stronger claim that CBT itself is gaslighting.
- The social/institutional critique should be defensible: standardized/manualizable cognitive therapies fit systems organized around diagnosable symptoms, measurable functioning, standardized interventions, and randomized trials; this does not justify claiming DSM mainly defines health by employability or that CBT dominance is conspiratorial.
- Candidate larger thesis: the body is not infallible; neither is the thinking mind. Healing becomes more interesting when neither is appointed dictator.
- The supplied comparison invites thinking about therapies as different ways of interrogating experience: CBT, SE, TRE, Focusing, Sensorimotor Psychotherapy/Ogden, 5Rhythms, and Alexander Technique.

## Bounded existing-work scan completed for the rethink

The scan supports **composition/adaptation**, not invention of a new universal therapy taxonomy.

- CBT/PTSD cognitive-therapy literature supports behavioral experiments, belief/prediction testing, cognitive restructuring, and work with avoidance/safety behaviors as genuine mechanisms/tools.
- Payne, Levine & Crane-Godreau (2015) describes SE as developed by Levine from 1977 onward, explicitly bottom-up rather than primarily cognitive, and gradual/indirect rather than direct/intense exposure; it calls SE a supplement to cognitive and exposure therapies.
- This supports deliberate methodological contrast but does **not by itself prove** the stronger historical-causal claim that Levine invented SE because CBT/exposure was inadequate.
- Berceli origin materials support war-related tremor observations, Lowen/Bioenergetic influence, and eventual separation of tremor from psychodynamics; no equally direct anti-CBT origin claim has been verified.
- Gendlin/Focusing provides established adjacent work on bodily `felt sense` / implicit meaning.
- Sensorimotor Psychotherapy explicitly works with posture, movement, sensation, impulse, protective organization, and new action possibilities.
- Alexander Technique and 5Rhythms can illustrate adjacent embodied experiments, but should not be silently presented as evidence-equivalent PTSD therapies.
- A systematic review found no convincing evidence that manualized psychotherapy is intrinsically superior to nonmanualized psychotherapy, though manualization has obvious research/standardization advantages.
- A 2026 American Journal of Psychiatry article argues that functioning and quality of life need a clearer/core role in psychiatric diagnosis, supporting a nuanced outcome critique rather than an employability caricature.
