## Existing-work scan conclusion

**Compose/adapt rather than invent a replacement taxonomy.**

- PTSD cognitive-therapy literature supports behavioral experiments, belief/prediction testing, cognitive restructuring, and work with avoidance/safety behaviors.
- Payne, Levine & Crane-Godreau (2015) describes SE as developed by Levine from 1977 onward, explicitly bottom-up rather than primarily cognitive, gradual/indirect rather than direct/intense exposure, and a supplement to cognitive/exposure therapies. This supports deliberate methodological contrast but does not by itself prove the stronger causal-origin claim that Levine invented SE because CBT/exposure was inadequate.
- Berceli origin materials support war-related tremor observations, Lowen/Bioenergetic influence, and eventual separation of tremor from psychodynamics; no equally direct anti-CBT origin claim has been verified.
- Gendlin/Focusing provides established adjacent work on bodily felt sense/implicit meaning.
- Sensorimotor Psychotherapy works with posture, movement, sensation, impulse, protective organization, and new action possibilities.
- Alexander Technique and 5Rhythms can illustrate adjacent embodied experiments but should not be presented as evidence-equivalent PTSD therapies.
- A systematic review found no convincing evidence that manualized psychotherapy is intrinsically superior to nonmanualized psychotherapy, despite obvious research/standardization advantages.
- A 2026 *American Journal of Psychiatry* article argues that functioning and quality of life need a clearer/core role in psychiatric diagnosis, supporting a nuanced outcome critique rather than an employability caricature.
