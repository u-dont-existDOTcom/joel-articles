## Owner correction: accuracy must operate at the scale of the argument, not become defensive sentence-by-sentence perfectionism — 2026-09-04

After the owner rewrote the section successfully, the Chat repeatedly reopened already-functional sentences because a specialist could name protocol-level exceptions or narrower formulations. Joel identified the result as a core writing failure: excessive precision turns natural argument into surgically qualified lists, makes prose difficult to understand, prevents the writer from taking a position, and can create new contradictions through over-specified logic.

The correction is **not** `facts do not matter`. It is a scope rule for editorial judgment:

> **Be accurate enough that the sentence does not materially mislead the intelligent target reader, but do not burden a targeted argument with every specialist distinction that does not change the point being made. Accuracy is evaluated at the scale of the argument and reader need, not by maximizing defensibility of every clause against every conceivable expert objection.**

For this article specifically:

- A broad commonality may be the right level of description even when protocols differ in technical mechanism. Do not replace a useful shared description with a multi-item protocol taxonomy merely because each therapy has nuances.
- If Joel is making an argument — for example that mainstream trauma-memory therapies often alter, inhibit, reprocess, or cognitively reorganize trauma responses rather than privileging biological completion — do not dissolve that argument into neutral mechanism lists merely because the claim is contestable.
- Distinguish **material correction** from **specialist nuance**. A correction is material when the existing wording would cause the intended reader to understand the central contrast incorrectly. A nuance that leaves the contrast intact belongs in a citation, footnote, later subsection, or nowhere.
- Do not repeatedly tell Joel a sentence is wrong after he has already revised it to incorporate earlier advice unless new evidence reveals a genuinely material error. Iterative micro-correction that moves the goalposts is itself an editorial failure.
- Natural prose is allowed to generalize. A sentence can be simplified, asymmetric, and argumentative while still being honest.
- Precision should clarify the thought. If the precision makes the thought harder to see, inspect whether the detail belongs at all.
