## Decision

V3 correctly separated different editing operations, but its first prospective execution exposed a missing layer.

The supervisor can correctly say:

- this is owner/source material;
- this is technical material;
- this is safety material;
- this is a narrative relation;

and still generate model-shaped prose if those labels become the paragraph plan.

V4 therefore separates four questions that V3 allowed to collapse:

1. **What has authority or semantic custody?**
2. **Where must that material survive?**
3. **What is actually alive/eligible for composition now?**
4. **How should the literal prose move?**

A preservation/source bank answers question 1 and partly question 2. It does **not** answer questions 3 or 4.

## Core correction

### Semantic custody is not composition eligibility

A fact/function may be required to survive the article while being **ineligible to drive the current paragraph**.

Examples:
- a source-derived duration may need to survive but may not belong in the current narrative movement;
- a safety warning may need adjacency to a practice but should not automatically close every explanatory paragraph;
- a use-context list may be legitimate content but still lack any owner/source thought route that makes it the next thing to say;
- a heading-level architecture distinction may be necessary for the article while being too schematic to serve as Joel's next sentence.

This is the missing control exposed by Shaking prospective-001.

## V4 execution order

### Gate A — authority/source-role classification

Run this **before V3 route classification**.

For every item in the smallest useful source span, classify its highest supported role:

1. `OWNER_EXACT` — owner-final/accepted/locked exact realization.
2. `OWNER_TOPOLOGY` — direct owner cognition with recognizable order/weighting but wording may be source-pool rather than final.
3. `SOURCE_SEMANTIC` — source-derived proposition/function whose meaning must survive, but no natural owner thought route is established.
4. `ARCHITECTURE_OBLIGATION` — article-map/placement/function requirement established by owner/current state, not necessarily a reader-facing sentence.
5. `TECHNICAL_OBJECT` — evidence, safety, finite external model, citation, navigation, literal parameter/range, or operational fact whose genre may properly remain technical.
6. `MODEL_REALIZATION` — assistant/model wrapper, bridge, taxonomy, first-person skin, recap, paragraph grouping, or other realization with no independent authority.
7. `PROVENANCE_UNRESOLVED` — current working material whose exact source role has not been recovered sufficiently.

A single sentence may contain more than one role. Split semantically when necessary; do not make one source-role record per sentence by default.

**Blocking rule:** `MODEL_REALIZATION` is never promoted to a preservation unit merely because it is present in the working copy.

`PROVENANCE_UNRESOLVED` may not be upgraded to owner thought or exact source realization by inference.

### Gate B — custody/destination ledger

For every non-scaffold semantic item, record where its survival is actually required:

- `EXACT_HERE`
- `SEMANTIC_HERE`
- `SAME_SUBSECTION`
- `SAME_STAGE`
- `ARTICLE_ANYWHERE_WITH_NAMED_DESTINATION`
- `PLACEMENT_UNRESOLVED`
- an already-authorized supersession/deletion/consolidation disposition.

This answers **where it must survive**, not how it must be written.

Do not silently convert `must survive` into `must remain in the current writer movement`.

If exact placement authority is absent, use `PLACEMENT_UNRESOLVED` rather than freezing the current model placement by inertia.

### Gate C — composition frontier

Only after Gates A and B, determine which material is permitted to enter the writer's current cognitive field.

Classify each surviving item:

- `LIVE_NOW` — directly generated by the preceding literal thought/source route and necessary to continue it;
- `SUPPORT_NOW` — needed locally for attribution, intelligibility, safety, or evidence of the live thought, but not a new paragraph job;
- `CUSTODY_ONLY` — must survive somewhere but the current thought has not reached it;
- `TECHNICAL_SURFACE` — legitimate finite/reference material whose literal treatment is an editing/placement problem rather than a narrative-generation problem;
- `NEEDS_AUTHOR_COGNITION` — function is required but no natural thought route exists;
- `SCAFFOLD_DROP` — realization-only material with no unique function.

The writer normally sees only `LIVE_NOW` plus the minimum `SUPPORT_NOW` needed to make it coherent.

`CUSTODY_ONLY`, `TECHNICAL_SURFACE`, and the full preservation inventory remain with the supervisor/verifier.

### Gate D — sufficiency test before prose

Before any writer call or model realization, ask:

**After removing model scaffold and custody-only obligations, is there enough authorial/source thought movement left to justify prose?**

If **yes**, use the relevant V3 Route 1/2 realization.

If **no**, do not manufacture connective tissue. Use one of:

- source restoration if a higher-authority natural realization exists;
- Route 3 owner-cognition recovery;
- Route 4 technical/reference treatment;
- Route 5 deletion/consolidation;
- explicit unresolved placement/custody state.

Thin cognition is a valid stop state.

## V3 route reinterpretation under V4

V3 Routes 0–5 remain useful, but they are assigned **after** Gates A–D.

### Route 0
Unchanged: preserve exact owner/accepted/known-green boundaries absent a real defect.

### Route 1
Requires `OWNER_TOPOLOGY` or equivalent direct authorial movement, not merely several owner-supplied facts.

### Route 2
Tightened.

A `SOURCE_SEMANTIC` relation is not enough by itself. Route 2 generation requires a locally live source relation with enough sequence/pressure to support a reader-facing realization **without inventing a complete conceptual allocation**.

If all that exists is:
`fact A + fact B + placement requirement`,
that may be semantic custody or article architecture, not a prose thought route.

### Route 3
Expanded as the default when a required narrative function remains after scaffold removal but no authorial/source movement can be recovered.

### Route 4
Includes finite source-derived material that must survive but does not currently have a narrative route. Technical/reference material may remain technical or await placement rather than receiving a first-person wrapper.

### Route 5
Unchanged: do not humanize empty scaffold.

## Diagnostic maps are forbidden as composition plans

Movement maps, source-role maps, preservation ledgers, trajectory labels, and V3 route assignments are **supervisor controls**.

They may determine:
- what may change;
- what must survive;
- what needs author input;
- what may be technical;
- where an audit should focus.

They may **not** determine:
- paragraph count;
- paragraph order by source type;
- one paragraph per movement;
- one sentence per preservation unit;
- whether a subsection needs a conclusion;
- whether every custody item must appear in the current candidate.

If the candidate's paragraph architecture visibly mirrors the supervisor ledger, presume leakage and audit it as a likely failure.

## Structural recurrence gate

After generation, compare the candidate's **hidden conceptual allocation** with the failure schema that triggered the repair.

Do this before polishing wording.

Examples:

Old failure:
`benefit -> mechanism -> evidence -> safety -> recommendation`

New candidate:
`benefit paragraph -> mechanism paragraph -> evidence paragraph -> safety paragraph`

Result: **FAIL — same architecture under new formatting**.

Old failure:
`Stage role -> Stage contrast -> safety -> duration -> use-case inventory`

New candidate:
`Stage contrast paragraph -> duration/use-case paragraph`

Result: **FAIL — same allocation redistributed**.

The recurrence gate is relational, not lexical. It does not ban lists, contrast, technical paragraphs, or finite reference material.

## Strategy-switch trigger hardened

A targeted repair counts as the **same method** when its main transformation is one or more of:

- paragraph splitting/merging while preserving the same conceptual allocation;
- relabeling the same categories;
- changing sentence cadence around the same complete mini-plan;
- moving source-bank items into separate cleaner cards;
- replacing one first-person wrapper with another while keeping the same taxonomy;
- adding another prohibition without changing the writer's information architecture.

When the same structural failure survives one such targeted repair, another same-method attempt is blocked.

The next action must change one of:

- source authority recovered;
- composition frontier;
- placement/distribution;
- owner cognition available;
- writer information exposure;
- generation architecture/tool/model/context.

## Retrieval under V4

Trajectory retrieval remains supervisor-side.

New requirement:

**A retrieved lesson must change a Gate-A/B/C/D decision or it does not earn influence over composition.**

Retrieval is not allowed to produce a paragraph plan such as:
`separate the narrative bit from the reference bit`.

Instead it may produce decisions such as:
- mark three units `CUSTODY_ONLY` rather than `LIVE_NOW`;
- recover owner topology before generation;
- classify the current list as `TECHNICAL_SURFACE`;
- delete a model wrapper while retaining its source proposition;
- stop generation because the frontier is too thin.

This reduces the chance that a useful historical diagnosis becomes a new editorial-card architecture.

## Preservation under V4

Run the canonical preservation gate normally, but source-role and destination status must be established first.

A preservation receipt answers:
**did the required content survive the authorized transformation?**

It does not answer:
- is this the right paragraph?
- is this the right wrapper?
- is this natural Joel prose?
- should this unit have been composition-active now?
- did the candidate reproduce the same model architecture?

Therefore a V4 candidate must pass both:

1. semantic preservation / zero unexplained substantive deltas;
2. structural recurrence / composition-frontier audit.

Neither substitutes for the other.
