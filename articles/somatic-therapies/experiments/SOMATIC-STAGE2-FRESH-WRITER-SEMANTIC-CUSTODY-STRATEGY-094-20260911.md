## Automatic rejection before preservation

The architecture critic rejects a candidate before preservation audit if materially true:

- a reader can reconstruct the custody checklist one-for-one from consecutive sentences/paragraphs;
- required safety conditions become a closed negative catalogue merely because four functions exist;
- several alternatives become a neat modality menu;
- a sentence carries multiple independent obligations because they all need to survive;
- a paragraph behaves like `fit -> uses -> mechanism -> warning -> takeaway` or another mini-profile;
- technical reference fields appear as a finite completeness taxonomy without an independent reader-facing reason for that structure;
- paragraph endings gather several remaining obligations into a tidy close;
- bullets were removed but list-thinking survives in comma/semicolon/clause chains;
- the prose looks like preservation proof rendered for the reader.

`The functions are mandatory`, `this is safety`, or `this is technical` are **not** sufficient reasons to waive these defects. Those facts protect meaning, not the literal realization.
