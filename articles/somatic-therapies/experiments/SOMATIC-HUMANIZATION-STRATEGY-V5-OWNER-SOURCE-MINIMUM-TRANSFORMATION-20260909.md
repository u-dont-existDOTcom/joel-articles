## Decision

V4 improved source authority, custody, and composition-frontier control. It still allowed one failure mode after Gate D passed:

> The model received valid owner cognition plus research and converted it into a polished, fully reconciled conceptual system.

In the failed Shaking candidate this appeared as:

`factual distinction -> tidy synthesis -> matched branch A / branch B -> stage-role block -> safety -> duration -> use-case inventory`.

The problem is no longer missing cognition. It is **over-synthesis during realization**.

V5 therefore changes the production architecture when direct owner source is available.

## Core rule

**For production Joel-byline prose, direct owner thought should normally be minimally transformed rather than re-generated.**

The model is not asked to `write a paragraph from the owner's ideas`.

It is asked to:

1. fact-correct the source outside the prose;
2. preserve the owner's thought order, weighting, asymmetry, uncertainty, and stopping point;
3. remove only private-chat/editorial wrappers and reader-incoherent deixis;
4. add only the minimum connective or antecedent needed for public-facing coherence;
5. stop before unresolved source-bank obligations become a new model outline.

This is production editing, not a generator-capability experiment.

## Two-lane separation

### Lane P — production article repair

Use V5 minimum transformation when the owner has directly supplied the needed cognition.

Priority order:

1. owner exact/publication-ready wording when available;
2. owner source wording with minimal public-facing cleanup;
3. owner topology with only unavoidable connective realization;
4. model-generated prose only for a relation the owner/source has not already realized enough to edit.

### Lane R — generator R&D

If the goal is to test whether a model can independently realize the thought:

- use a genuinely fresh context when independence matters;
- do not copy owner syntax into the generator prompt;
- keep the result outside article authority unless it independently earns inclusion;
- do not let a generator failure block the production article when owner source already solves the writing problem.

Production success and generator success remain separate ledgers.

## V5 execution order

### Step 1 — freeze the direct owner source

Preserve the literal source answer before research/model interpretation.

Record:

- order in which the owner reached the thoughts;
- which points were assertions versus tentative possibilities;
- uncertainty/friction that materially shaped the thought;
- where the owner naturally stopped;
- private-chat material that is not publication content.

Do not rewrite yet.

### Step 2 — fact-correct outside prose

Research only the factual uncertainty that could change the thought.

Return a compact correction ledger:

- owner/source claim;
- verified correction;
- confidence/source quality;
- whether the correction changes topology, only wording, or nothing.

Do not let the research answer become an automatic paragraph.

For the current SE/TRE case:

- owner uncertainty: is shaking already part of SE / does Levine's model conflict with deliberate shaking?
- correction: SE explicitly includes shaking/trembling discharge; it also gives special importance to completing some specific interrupted motor responses; the SE paper does not reject TRE-like tremoring;
- direct Levine evaluation of TRE: not established from a primary source in the bounded search;
- topology consequence: remove the false `SE senses -> separate shaking completes what SE cannot` sequence, but keep Joel's practical possibility that deliberate shaking may be useful when more active discharge is wanted or subtle SE is not producing much felt movement.

### Step 3 — classify owner-source bytes

For each owner-source movement, classify:

- `PUBLIC_READY` — can survive substantially as spoken/written;
- `PUBLIC_CLEANUP` — same thought/order; grammar, antecedent, or chat wrapper needs cleanup;
- `FACT_CORRECT` — source thought survives but one factual premise must be corrected;
- `EDITOR_ONLY` — instruction to model/editor, not reader prose;
- `BANK` — valid thought, but current publication movement has not reached it.

This is not a paragraph outline.

### Step 4 — preserve epistemic shape

When the owner is tentative, do not automatically convert that into a decision tree.

Preserve differences such as:

- `maybe` versus `therefore`;
- one main thought plus a second possibility versus balanced branch A / branch B;
- `I'm not wedded to this order` as an architectural stance versus a polished taxonomy claim like `the boundary is fuzzy`;
- curiosity versus conclusion.

A research correction may remove a mistaken question while leaving the surrounding uncertainty/non-rigidity intact.

### Step 5 — no paragraph target

Do **not** tell the production writer to create:

- one paragraph;
- two paragraphs;
- a complete explanation;
- a balanced comparison;
- a transition + caveat + takeaway.

Instead, minimally clean the owner source until the current public-facing thought reaches its natural stop.

Paragraphing is decided afterward from literal thought duration, not before composition.

### Step 6 — stage labels remain navigation

The five stages are owner map/navigation, not an ontology that every paragraph must explain.

Do not make prose about `the Stage-1/Stage-2 boundary` unless the reader genuinely needs that meta-explanation.

Prefer the practical relation the stage map is trying to capture:

- what the person can feel/do now;
- what another practice may add;
- whether something is optional;
- what happens next if anything.

A modality may serve more than one stage function without requiring a reconciliation paragraph.

### Step 7 — quarantine finite source-bank material

Duration ranges, use-context inventories, mechanism lists, caveats, and safety functions remain under supervisor custody unless the owner's current thought naturally reaches them.

For the Shaking case, the `10–45 minute` range and source-derived use-context list are **not** part of the primary owner-source realization by default.

They may later be:

- retained as concise technical/reference material;
- moved to a named practical note;
- integrated only when a live thought actually reaches them;
- proposed for deletion/consolidation only with normal owner authority.

Do not append them merely because they are preserved.

### Step 8 — do not complete every implication

After minimum transformation, ask:

- Did the owner actually say this?
- Is this sentence necessary for the next live reader question?
- Did the model add a synthesis that only explains the preceding material?
- Did two tentative ideas become matched alternatives?
- Did a factual correction become a theory-comparison mini-essay?
- Did the preservation bank become the tail of the paragraph?

If yes, remove the synthesis and return to the owner-source path.

## V5 admission gate

Before owner delivery of any new production candidate, identify the three strongest model-shape risks.

Automatic **FAIL / strategy switch**, not another paraphrase, when any of the following is substantively true:

1. the candidate globally reconciles uncertainty that the owner left live;
2. owner possibilities become a symmetric decision tree;
3. article navigation/stage taxonomy becomes the subject instead of the practical thought;
4. a research distinction becomes an invented opposition or tidy synthesis;
5. source-bank items reappear as a complete tail because they still need semantic custody;
6. the candidate would require a paragraph explaining why the paragraph's own structure is non-rigid.

These are relational failure tests, not phrase bans.

## If V5 still fails

Do **not** escalate to V6 by adding more prohibitions.

Use the even more conservative production fallback:

1. retain literal owner sentences/clauses wherever publicly coherent;
2. make only factual corrections and grammatical/deictic repairs;
3. leave roughness/asymmetry that is genuinely owner-derived;
4. ask Joel only for a missing public-facing sentence when no minimal transformation can bridge the gap without invention.

If independent generator research is still desired, run it separately in a fresh context.
