## Blocking diagnosis — contrast completion

The failure is not the literal words `can`, `doesn't`, `talking about`, or `not`.

The model repeatedly completes conceptual space by supplying an opposite/complement:

`positive proposition -> negated alternative`

`category selection -> excluded opposite`

`X -> not Y`

This can make otherwise plain prose feel preassembled because the model closes around the claim instead of following the next genuinely necessary thought.

It is a broader form of the earlier conditional-aftercare failure. It does not require an `if` clause. A sentence can be individually reasonable and still participate in an AI-shaped run when neighboring sentences repeatedly state a point and then close its conceptual opposite.

## Admission gate

Before delivering new model-written Joel prose, cold-read for repeated contrast completion.

Reopen or reject when several nearby sentences mainly do any of the following:

- say what something can be and then what it does not have to be;
- select a category and immediately exclude its opposite;
- state a recommendation and then complete it with a negative complement;
- use `rather than`, `not`, `doesn't`, `instead of`, `but not`, or equivalent relations mainly to fill the other side of a conceptual pair;
- repeatedly turn a live observation into a polished binary distinction.

Do **not** ban contrast or negation. Real thought often requires them. The question is whether the contrast changes the reader's position or merely makes the conceptual unit feel complete.
