### Lane B — independent generator research

Treat this as a separate research problem. The bare-topic night-walk probe was owner-reported high-confidence AI, so independent generation is currently **failed/not demonstrated**. Do not keep adding prose anti-pattern rules inside the same saturated generator context.

The next architecture is **dynamic thought origination**: generate small candidate thoughts first, select one, then realize it locally in fresh contexts without exposing a complete outline or preservation inventory.

## Causal hypothesis

The principal remaining failure is **premature global conceptual commitment**.

When asked for a complete passage from a topic or ledger, the model tends to decide a latent article plan early: what subpoints exist, which deserve paragraphs, where the caveat goes, how the piece balances, and what the ending means. Surface humanization rules then edit prose produced by that already model-shaped plan.

The aquatic case reduced that degree of freedom because Joel supplied the substantive cognition and its irregular topology. The model mostly had to realize rather than decompose the topic.

This hypothesis predicts:

- more anti-pattern rules will improve criticism faster than generation;
- static outline-first generation can improve coherence while preserving the model's over-complete conceptual allocation;
- dynamic local planning should reduce premature closure if the next conceptual move is chosen only after the literal current text exists;
- owner-supplied cognition should continue outperforming bare-topic model origination for Joel-byline production;
- style/profile prompting alone should not solve the cognition problem.

## Existing-work scan and disposition

This strategy was independently snapshotted before literature review in `SOMATIC-HUMANIZATION-STRATEGY-INDEPENDENT-SNAPSHOT-20260909.md`.

Relevant established work found afterward:

1. **Human composing-process research** treats planning, translating/drafting, and revising as recursive and strategically interleaved rather than a simple linear sequence. This supports adaptation of dynamic local planning rather than a no-planning doctrine.
2. **Plan-and-Write** and related hierarchical story-generation work show that explicit planning can improve coherence, diversity, and topic adherence. Plan-and-Write explicitly compared static planning with a dynamic schema that interweaves planning and realization. Therefore the strategy should not equate `planning` with the problem; the target is premature complete planning.
3. **LLM self-correction research** is mixed. A 2024 TACL critical survey concluded that prompted LLM self-feedback has not shown reliable general self-correction under fair settings, while reliable external feedback and large-scale fine-tuning are more successful. This supports retiring same-model recursive critique as the main generator-improvement engine.
4. **Human-AI creative-writing experiments** report better quality/interestingness and less homogenization when humans contribute creative input rather than merely accepting AI output. This independently supports the production lane's owner-cognition priority.
5. **Creativity/homogenization studies** show that LLM assistance can improve individual creative output while reducing aggregate diversity; recent work suggests diversified AI inputs/personas can mitigate some of this. This supports diversity at the *thought-seed stage* as an experimental variable, not surface persona imitation in final prose.
6. **Personalized generation / LaMP-style retrieval** shows that retrieving user-specific profile/history can improve personalized outputs. Use this only as a style/context aid with source-integrity safeguards; it does not establish faithful informal author imitation or independent human-like cognition.

Decision against established baselines:

- **Reuse:** preservation/source integrity; external/fresh evaluation; profile retrieval for calibration where relevant.
- **Adapt:** recursive human composing model + dynamic Plan-and-Write into local thought planning.
- **Compose:** combine owner-thought topology, dynamic planning, independent seed diversity, and post-generation preservation reconciliation.
- **Do not reuse as primary strategy:** same-model Self-Refine loops, static complete outlines, persona/style mimicry as a substitute for cognition.
- **Experiment remains warranted:** no established method directly solves Joel's observed `owner-fed Human / independent-origin AI` split.

## Proposed experimental architecture

### Stage 0 — freeze the hypothesis and evaluation boundary

Use a natural boundary long enough for recurring thought architecture to become visible. Do not use a two-sentence micro-probe to claim success or failure of cadence/topology.

For independent-generation tests, the model must originate the substantive thought in every compared condition. Owner ideas may not enter the test candidate.

### Stage 1 — independent seed generation

Use **genuinely fresh contexts**. Each seed generator gets only the bare topic and one narrow instruction: produce one thought seed, not publication prose and not an outline.

A seed can be:

- one concrete observation;
- one causal relation;
- one unresolved tension;
- one practical curiosity;
- one social consequence;
- one counterintuitive detail.

Generate several seeds independently. Do not ask one model instance to enumerate `five different angles`; that itself encourages a complete category set.

### Stage 2 — seed diversity / selection

A separate fresh selector sees the candidate seeds but not prior detector results or rejected prose.

Select for:

- reality contact;
- a specific relation rather than a topic summary;
- unresolved pressure that can actually continue;
- non-exhaustiveness;
- asymmetry where the thought warrants it;
- a consequence or curiosity that changes what the reader would notice.

Reject seeds that already contain a complete mini-essay, balanced taxonomy, thesis/caveat/takeaway package, or generic significance statement.

A later ablation can compare ordinary independent seeds with deliberately diverse cognitive stances/personas. Persona diversity is allowed only at the **seed** stage; final prose must not imitate demographic or stylistic stereotypes.

### Stage 3 — local realization

A fresh writer receives:

- the selected seed;
- only the minimum literal local context needed;
- factual/provenance locks needed to prevent invention.

It does **not** receive:

- the full preservation inventory;
- a complete desired outline;
- the Pangram result;
- the anti-pattern library;
- explanations of previous failures;
- a demand to cover the entire topic.

Write one bounded local movement. Stop when that thought stops.

### Stage 4 — dynamic continuation

If the natural boundary needs another movement, do not reveal the hidden destination to the same writer. A fresh continuation context receives the literal text now written and asks what the most locally live next thought is.

This is **dynamic planning**: the next plan is made after the current prose exists.

### Stage 5 — reconciliation

Only after a natural local realization exists:

- run source/provenance checks;
- reconcile required semantic functions;
- place unconsumed obligations elsewhere rather than forcing them into the paragraph;
- verify no owner/source material was silently lost or invented.

For independent bare-topic probes there is no article preservation ledger, but factual invention and synthetic autobiography remain disallowed.

### Stage 6 — evaluation

Use a genuinely fresh reader for AI-shape/quality diagnosis. Same-context critique is diagnostic only and cannot certify independence.

Evaluate separately:

1. thought quality/topology;
2. prose realization;
3. fidelity/provenance where applicable;
4. detector status only if Joel explicitly authorizes a Pangram test.

If the seed itself is generic or over-complete, fail at Stage 2/6 rather than humanizing its sentences.

## Strong baseline experiment

Use the **same bare topic** across conditions so topic does not confound the result.

### Condition A — direct one-shot baseline

Bare topic -> one complete 250–400 word passage.

This reproduces the general night-walk architecture class.

### Condition B — static outline baseline

Bare topic -> short outline -> fresh writer receives complete outline -> 250–400 word passage.

This tests whether ordinary planning helps quality but preserves the suspected global-completion failure.

### Condition C — dynamic seed architecture

Bare topic -> several fresh independent thought seeds -> fresh selector -> fresh local writer -> fresh dynamic continuation if needed -> 250–400 word natural boundary.

### Optional Condition D — diversified-seed ablation

Same as C, but seed generators receive materially different cognitive stances. This tests whether diversity at origination helps beyond merely splitting the pipeline.

Hold approximately constant:

- topic;
- target length/natural boundary;
- factual constraints;
- model family where possible;
- no owner cognition;
- no detector feedback during generation.

Primary discriminating observation:

Does Condition C reduce recurring `topic decomposition -> equalized paragraph jobs -> explanatory closure` relative to A and B while remaining coherent?

Detector testing, if later authorized, should compare exact whole natural boundaries rather than cherry-picked sentences.

## Strategy-switch criteria

Do **not** add another generation prohibition merely because one candidate fails.

Switch again if any of these occur:

1. Condition C repeatedly produces the same model-shaped paragraph allocation as A/B.
2. Seed generators independently converge on generic complete mini-theses despite fresh contexts.
3. Fresh selectors consistently choose the most polished/model-shaped seed.
4. Local writers reconstitute a full hidden outline from one seed and still over-complete the topic.
5. Production owner-fed realizations begin failing despite preserved topology, suggesting the current positive effect was narrower than believed.

Cheapest discriminating tests before the next switch:

- inspect seeds before prose;
- compare direct vs dynamic generation on the same topic;
- compare selector choices against a blind human/owner choice when available;
- compare one local writer versus a fresh continuation architecture.

If dynamic origination fails, the next materially different route is **adaptation/training rather than more prompting**: build a dataset of source thought + model candidate + owner correction/accepted realization and evaluate retrieval-augmented or parameter-level personalization on held-out Joel-byline boundaries. This remains a later strategy, not the current one.
