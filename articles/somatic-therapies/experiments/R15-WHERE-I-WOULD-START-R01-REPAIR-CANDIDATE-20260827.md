# Where I Would Start

I wouldn't make everybody start in the same place. If one event is clearly the problem and you can stay present while touching it, I may go straight to EMDR. If touching trauma makes you disappear, flood, or stay wrecked for the rest of the day, I would rather use something gentler first.

The gentler work can become avoidance too. I do not want someone spending forever “building capacity” because nobody is willing to treat the thing that is actually hurting them. I care about what happens in ordinary life: are you sleeping better, functioning better, relating better, getting freer? If sleep itself is the bottleneck, I have a separate [sleep guide](http://sleep.u-dont-exist.com/).

I also wouldn't call a whole method gentle or intense. Present-focused self-hypnosis is not immersive memory work. A little shaking is not forced catharsis. With Brainspotting I can use it to resource and orient, or I can go into much hotter material.
