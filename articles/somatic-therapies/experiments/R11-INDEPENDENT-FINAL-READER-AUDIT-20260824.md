# Somatic Therapies r11 — independent final-reader audit

Date: 2026-08-24  
Target: `R11-WHOLE-ARTICLE-READER-FACING-CORRECTION-CANDIDATE-20260824.md`  
Status: **diagnostic evidence only; no rewrite authority by itself; no Pangram/detector used**

## Independence boundary

The audit was performed in a fresh Chat context after reading only repository-level operating guidance and the literal r11 candidate. Before the audit, the reader did **not** read Somatic PR discussion, detector results, humanization history, preservation/cold-audit receipts, prior critiques, or drafting rationale. The r11 file's own non-reader metadata was necessarily visible when opening the target and was excluded from the reader-facing judgment.

The audit treated the piece as a research-conversational practical guide for a general audience. Technical density was not treated as AI by itself; personal anecdote was not treated as a generic humanization cure; polish was not treated as a defect.

## Overall diagnosis

The article did not read as uniformly AI-written. Several passages felt distinctly authored: Professor Baby Sheep; the head-shaving/loveyhuasca material; Louka and the $10 class; `less chance of being called an expert`; `democratic like EFT`; the housemate/dress incident; gaze phenomenology; and the blunt yoga language.

The residual AI signature was mainly **above the sentence level**: the article repeatedly behaved as if an invisible editor were demanding classification, qualification, modality-boundary policing, evidence taxonomy, safety aftercare, and protection against every foreseeable over-inference.

## Severity-ranked findings

### HIGH — repeated responsible-explainer layer

The evidence-plane distinction was re-established several times: early map framing, Shaking mechanisms, Brainspotting qualification, the final evaluation manifesto, and Sky/Vagal. Individual distinctions were often valid; the recurrence made the prose feel guarded and systematized.

Repair type: establish the evidence rule once, then keep only passage-specific qualifications that prevent a real misunderstanding.

### HIGH — five-Job taxonomy too tidy for the material

The five Jobs appeared formally comparable, but the prose repeatedly had to explain cross-membership and exceptions: modalities did not belong permanently to one Job; yoga could appear elsewhere; mild shaking and regular shaking occupied different Jobs; Sky/Vagal could occur before their apparent placement. Job 5 was also much thinner than Jobs 1–4.

Repair type: loosen or remove the taxonomy rather than adding more exception language.

### HIGH — backstage filing commentary

Examples included `support for the whole map, not as an EMDR add-on`, `For Job 1 I mean... Here I mean...`, `Yoga is not really confined to this point on the map anyway`, and `I put them here because...`.

Repair type: express distinctions as reader decisions—what a practice does, how intense it is, what kind of material it fits—rather than explanations of where the author filed it.

### HIGH — stay/stop/return test repeated past its useful recurrence

The governing safety idea was strong, but it was repeatedly re-derived in the Introduction, map framing, inner-child section, shaking, Brainspotting, and Sky/Vagal.

Repair type: state the governing rule clearly once and repeat only where a modality-specific safety boundary requires it.

### MEDIUM-HIGH — `How I Judge Whether Any of This Is Helping` became a second conclusion

The housemate example served the section. The long final evidence manifesto then sounded like the article's conclusion, after which Sky/Vagal restarted the article.

Repair type: keep the concrete durability/outcome test and reduce the broad recap so the article has one real stopping point.

### MEDIUM-HIGH — Sky/Vagal concentrated the over-completion problem

Its final paragraph repeated release-is-not-healing, restimulation, avoidance, readiness, orientation/sleep/recovery, and evidence/mechanism separation.

Repair type: retain practice-specific physical and mental-health cautions; remove whole-article lessons already established elsewhere.

### MEDIUM-HIGH — undefined inner-child framework terms

`Nurturer`, `Protector`, `borrowed adulthood`, `neutral witness`, and `one ordinary Protector action` arrived as if defined elsewhere.

Repair type: minimal inline definition/self-containment.

### MEDIUM — `subcortical` label created its own disclaimer

The body-before-story distinction was already clear. The article then introduced `subcortical` and immediately qualified it to prevent a literal brain-region reading.

Repair type: either make the term load-bearing or define it economically once.

### MEDIUM — Brainspotting skeptical aftercare

`It feels more effective to me` was already explicitly subjective, but the next sentence defended against interpreting it as proven universal superiority. The peripheral-vision anecdote similarly stopped to rebut an over-simple physiological claim.

Repair type: remove caveats aimed at inferences the prose did not invite; retain factual corrections that materially change interpretation.

### MEDIUM — Brainspotting aftercare menu

The section reopened shaking, walking, several yogas, EFT, rest, stretching, toning, and reconnection.

Repair type: one governing aftercare principle plus the most relevant examples.

### MEDIUM — neurological de-armoring interrupts the opening

The material may belong early, but the article moved from the promised somatic guide into sleep, medication, pain, nutrition, glutamate/GABA, neuroinflammation, LDN, DMSO, gut toxins, zeolite, charcoal, EMF, and auric fields before the guide's main sequence had settled.

Repair type: preserve the substantive function while making its reader-facing reason and relation to therapy tolerance more direct.

### MEDIUM — Job 5 looked like taxonomy completion

The section was much shorter and more generic than the preceding material, suggesting an `integration` bucket added to complete the schema.

Repair type: retain the integration content without pretending it has equal taxonomic weight.

### MEDIUM — unresolved notebook prose in housemate experiment

`If one hour later... then perhaps... but I should try again` read like a contemporaneous research note rather than a resolved publication sentence.

Repair type: convert it into the durability criterion without inventing a later outcome, unless a real follow-up result exists.

### LOW-MEDIUM — dangling Brainspotting score

`I give Brainspotting so many more points` referred to no visible scoring system.

Repair type: preserve the `democratic like EFT` judgment while removing or anchoring the undefined score.

### LOW-MEDIUM — playful material is human but early

The Professor Baby Sheep/head-shaving passage was among the least AI-shaped material. The issue was placement/curiosity, not tone or oddness.

Repair type: do not professionalize it; assess only whether its placement delays the reader's main question too much.

### LOW — working-document monospace

Ordinary phrases such as `blockage`, `shaking = mental health`, and `titration`/`pendulation` appeared in backticks.

Repair type: normal publication typography unless the code-like treatment has a purpose.

## Curiosity drops

1. Introduction → neurological de-armoring → playful interlude → map disclaimer: the reader was promised the somatic therapies but waited through two detours and a framework disclaimer before receiving the main organization.
2. Latter half of Shaking Qigong: source-specific material remained interesting, but mechanism taxonomy, evidence-plane distinction, caution, stack, yin qualification, and placement recap prolonged the section after its main question was answered.
3. Final third: the housemate example restored attention, but the broad evidence manifesto felt conclusive before Sky/Vagal started another major section.

## Three strongest remaining weaknesses

1. The article repeatedly explained and safeguarded its reasoning after the reader already understood it.
2. The five-Job map over-governed material that was naturally more fluid and overlapping.
3. Too much backstage editorial reasoning remained reader-visible.

## Blind AI-assistance judgment

If received with no context, the reader **would suspect substantial AI editorial/writing assistance**, although not that the underlying observations, opinions, anecdotes, or entire prose were generated by AI.

The strongest suspicion came from:
- `This Is a Map, But You Are the Explorer` through the evidence-types paragraph;
- the latter half of Shaking Qigong;
- repeated Brainspotting qualification/aftercare;
- the long final paragraph of `How I Judge Whether Any of This Is Helping`;
- especially the final Sky Hypnosis/Vagal Blitz paragraph.

The characteristic signal was a real authorial source voice repeatedly processed by a layer optimizing for structure, completeness, epistemic hygiene, safety, and resistance to foreseeable objections.

## Owner correction after audit

Immediately after receiving this audit, Joel stated that he did not like the `Job` framing and **never wrote it that way**, then requested a rewrite. That direct owner correction establishes that the five-Job taxonomy was not merely an audit concern; it was an imposed editorial architecture and is superseded. Implementation is recorded separately in the r12 candidate/receipt and the updated article architecture/owner locks.
