## Operation 4 — add Sensorimotor Psychotherapy for a distinct Stage-3 function

Add an H2 `Sensorimotor Psychotherapy` after the complete Brainspotting subsection.

Conceptual function, to be owner-reviewed before exact prose is locked:

- bodily experience is a primary entry point rather than starting from the trauma story;
- work can track posture, movement, somatic activation, defensive impulses, and action patterns;
- this is not merely more interoceptive monitoring: the distinct experiment is what happens when bodily action/posture patterns are noticed or changed;
- this provides a natural concrete connection to the accepted SIBAM `Behavior` layer without claiming that Sensorimotor is simply an implementation of SIBAM.

## Operation 5 — add Focusing for the `before the story` end of Stage 3

Add an H2 `Focusing` after Sensorimotor Psychotherapy.

Conceptual function, to be owner-reviewed before exact prose is locked:

- Gendlin's `felt sense` is a bodily sense of a whole situation that is initially vague / not yet clear;
- Focusing waits with that bodily sense rather than first deciding what the problem means;
- words or clearer meaning may emerge from the felt sense later;
- this makes Focusing a particularly literal fit for Joel's Stage-3 formulation `trauma feelings before the story comes in`.

Bounded sources:
- International Focusing Institute / Gendlin, `Implicit entry and Focusing`: https://focusing.org/gendlin/docs/gol_2032.html
- Gendlin, `Focusing`: https://focusing.org/gendlin/docs/gol_2048.html
- International Focusing Institute, `On Focusing and Trauma`: https://focusing.org/trauma

Do not turn this into a general claim that every bodily feeling is trustworthy or meaningful. The article's existing methodological section and Jules/MCT counterweight already prevent that inference; do not repeat those caveats here unless the literal prose creates a new ambiguity.
