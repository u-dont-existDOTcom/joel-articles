## Sensorimotor Psychotherapy

Suppose you feel an impulse to push away, but your hands barely move. In Pat Ogden’s Sensorimotor Psychotherapy, the therapist might help you explore that movement slowly, following what happens as you begin to push. You’re working directly with the Behavior part of SIBAM here, and posture can be explored too: you can try changing how you’re sitting and see what happens to the feeling before you’ve figured out why you hold yourself that way.

## Focusing

With Focusing, you can know exactly what happened and still start from “there’s something about it…” Eugene Gendlin called that unclear bodily sense of the whole situation a felt sense. You give it a little room without needing an explanation yet. If a word or image comes, you try it against the feeling; a reasonable-sounding description can still feel wrong, so you let it go and see what fits better. The feeling can change while you’re doing this, sometimes with a small release.
