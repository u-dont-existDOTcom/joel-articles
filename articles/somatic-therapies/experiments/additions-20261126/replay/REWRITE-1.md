## Imagery Rescripting

Imagery rescripting is a lot like the way I [re-do trauma memories](https://ibogaqueen.substack.com/i/189761959/re-do-trauma-memories) in my inner child reparenting guide. Like EMDR, it takes you back into the memory.

In a session you picture the scene again, and the therapist steps into it to protect you or to give you what you needed back then. Later you learn to step in yourself, as the adult you are now. Since you are back inside the memory, stop when it gets to be too much, and ground yourself afterward like you would after EMDR.

In [a trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32892758/) of 155 adults whose PTSD started in childhood, it helped about as much as EMDR, which can't tell you which of the two will suit you, or how it does with trauma from later in life.
