## What the lesson missed (the next lesson, somatic-2026-11-27c)

1. **Say the therapist's part in the words the reader already has from his guides**, not in a general paraphrase of the therapy: his "the ideal adult, protecting and nurturing your inner child", not "someone to protect you or give you what you needed". The brief gave the facts in my draft's words, and the writer kept them.
2. **No either-or pairs** ("to protect you or to give you what you needed", "which of the two will suit you, or how it does with ..."): his version says one thing each time.
3. **The evidence as his own reading of it, hedged the way he hedges** ("it looks like it works on average about as well as"), with the limit as what it can't tell "you or anyone else", not a relative clause on the finding.

The rewrite also kept the lesson's own wording of a step ("Later you learn to step in yourself"), so the next brief gives the way and not sentences to copy. This test's paragraphs stay out of any article: it was a test of the lesson, and his version is what goes in.
