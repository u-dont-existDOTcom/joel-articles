And if what you can't let in is kindness, when someone cares about you and it just bounces off, there's Accelerated Experiential Dynamic Psychotherapy. The therapist works to keep you from feeling alone with whatever comes up, and when some of their care does get in, they ask what that's like and where you feel it in your body.

As with water therapy, I'd ask before booking: "What do you do when I start slipping away from a feeling, or get overwhelmed? And what if I think you've read me wrong?" If the answer leaves no room for you to slow down or to disagree, keep looking.
