I'd ask for a chronic pain specialist and a written plan for the flare-ups you can see coming, including the new or severe signs that mean you go to the ER right away, but don't let amelioration of symptoms be the end of the investigation.

While the investigation goes on, if the tests keep coming back normal, I'd also look into two talk therapies that treat the pain as real: Pain Reprocessing Therapy and Emotional Awareness and Expression Therapy. (More on both in my somatic guide.)
