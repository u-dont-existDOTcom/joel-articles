If, as soon as a feeling starts in a session, you're already explaining where it comes from, or agreeing with whatever the therapist says so the moment will pass, Intensive Short-Term Dynamic Psychotherapy would say it's anxiety about the feeling that makes you slip away like that. In that therapy, the therapist points it out as you do it and keeps pushing for the feeling, while watching for signs of that anxiety, like tense muscles, sighing, or your thinking going foggy, to tell when it's getting to be too much for you. If you can only take a little of it, like most people who try this therapy, you'd get a slower version, where the therapist stops to help you settle whenever the anxiety climbs.
