If your pain has lasted months, and a doctor has checked and found no injury or disease to explain it, two therapies are worth knowing about. In the first, Pain Reprocessing Therapy, you learn to treat the pain as a false alarm. It's real, but it's your brain signaling danger where the doctors haven't found any injury. Part of how you learn that is by paying close attention to how the pain feels in your body without fearing it. Then, a little at a time, you go back to things you've been avoiding because of it, like going for walks.
