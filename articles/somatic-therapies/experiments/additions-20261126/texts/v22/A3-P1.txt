If your pain has lasted months, and a doctor has checked and found no injury or disease to explain it, there are two therapies I'd look into. In the first, Pain Reprocessing Therapy, you learn to treat the pain as a false alarm: your brain is making real pain, signaling danger where the doctors haven't found any injury. Part of how you learn it's a false alarm is by paying close attention to how the pain feels in your body, without being scared of it. You also slowly go back to things you've been avoiding because of the pain, like going for walks.
