# Reads of draft 14 (the changed sentences): against his fixes; needed, fit, contradictions

## Against his fixes
MARKED:
- A1-S1: 10 (minor) — "because you start explaining it" gives the explaining as the cause of slipping away, and S3 then gives another cause ("It's usually anxiety ... that makes you slip away"). The explaining and agreeing are how you slip away. Rewrite: "Even once you can feel your body just fine, you can still slip away from a feeling the moment it comes up in a session, by explaining it, or by agreeing with whatever the therapist says so you don't have to stay in it."
- A1-S4: 10 — two "it"s that point at different things: "a slower version of it" (the therapy) and "whenever it climbs" (the anxiety). Rewrite: "If you can only handle a little of that anxiety, like most people who try this therapy, you'd get a slower version, where the therapist stops to help you settle whenever the anxiety climbs."
- A1-S8: 13, 15 — it opens on what to ask before anything says why these three need asking about, and "whether you can tell them they've read you wrong" is a yes/no question every therapist answers "of course". "Any of these therapists" also points at therapists when only therapies were named. Rewrite: "In all three the therapist is the one saying what you feel and when you've slipped away from it, so before booking I'd ask what they do when you get overwhelmed, and what they do when you tell them they've read you wrong." (This also drops "slip away from a feeling", which S9 never judges; see finding 1.)
- A3-S12 (with S13): 10 — after "the two trials' numbers", S13's "comparing the two" reads first as the two trials. Rewrite S13: "I haven't found a trial comparing the two therapies." Or join them: "A 30% drop is a lower bar than getting your pain down to 0 or 1 out of 10, so these numbers can't tell you which therapy works better, and I haven't found a trial that compared them."
- IC1-S1: 10, 16 — "for this" (redoing memories) and "when I wrote this" (the section) are two different "this", and "It turns out" already says he didn't know, so "though I didn't know it" says it twice. Rewrite: "After I wrote this section, I found out there's an actual clinical therapy that works this way, called Imagery Rescripting."
- IC2-S2: 4 — "and they helped" is hung on the end with nobody and no amount in it, and the reader of this section (attacks that come and go) isn't told what the limit means for them. Rewrite: "So far they've mostly been tested on chronic back pain and other muscle and joint pain, where they helped most of the people who got them, so I can't say how they'd do for attacks like these (more on both in my somatic guide)."

## Needed, fit, contradictions
1. A1-S8, needed: "when you slip away from a feeling" is not needed in the question. S2 already says what the therapist does then, and S9 only judges the answer on slowing down and disagreeing. Covered by the rewrite above.
2. IC1-S2, fit (MISREAD / CAN'T TELL): "a therapist can step into it" reads for a moment as if a person enters your memory, and "a therapist" could be any therapist. Rewrite: "If a memory feels too big to redo on your own, an Imagery Rescripting therapist has you picture them stepping into it as the adult you needed, until you learn to be that adult yourself."
3. IC1, order (optional): S2 follows straight from the J. paragraph ("it might take years to imagine that correction"), and S1 doesn't. If the two are swapped or joined, the paragraph opens on the reader's case: "If a memory feels too big to redo on your own, there's an actual clinical therapy for that, called Imagery Rescripting, which I only found out about after I wrote this section. The therapist has you picture them stepping into the memory as the adult you needed, until you learn to be that adult yourself."
4. Contradiction against the guide (A1-S5, not a changed sentence): the guide's "The Dominant Trauma-Memory Therapies Are Missing Something" says exposure desensitization "can itself be re-traumatizing", and A1-S5 recommends a therapy that gets you used to a feared feeling "bit by bit" with nothing to say why this is different. "At a pace your anxiety can handle" half answers it. If it needs more, a clause would do: "... getting you used to the feeling itself (not the memory) bit by bit, at a pace your anxiety can handle."
5. IC2-S1 against its section: no contradiction. "While the investigation goes on" and "treat the pain as real" square it with "don't let amelioration of symptoms be the end of the investigation" and "normal test results don't prove the pain is imaginary".
