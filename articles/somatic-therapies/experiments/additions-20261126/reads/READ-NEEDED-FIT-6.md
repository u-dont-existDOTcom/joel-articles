## 1. Needed read

**A1 (Can't Stay With a Feeling?).** Reader: someone partway through Layer 3 who can feel their body but slides off feelings in talk therapy. They decide whether to look for one of these therapies, which one fits, and what to ask first.

- A1-S3 "(Some people could give a whole lecture on their feelings without feeling one of them.)" is not needed. S2 already lets the reader recognize themselves. Fix: cut it, or fold it into S2.
- A1-S5 clause "while watching your body for anxiety (tense muscles, sighing, a foggy head)" is not needed. The therapist does the watching, and the reader can't act on it. Fix: cut the parenthetical.
- Missing act: the reader isn't told how to find one. Fix: add "search the therapy's name plus 'therapist' and your area or 'online'".

**A2 (Imagery Rescripting).** Reader: someone in Layer 4 with one stuck childhood scene, choosing between this and EMDR.

- A2-S5 "Does it work?" is not needed. It is scaffolding. Fix: start S6 with "Studies so far:".
- Missing act: the text never says who does this or how to start. S3 says a therapist first and later you, but not whether to look for a trained therapist or try it alone. Fix: say to look for a therapist trained in imagery rescripting and to ask them the grounding question.

**A3 (Pain That Won't Quit?).** Reader: someone with chronic pain deciding whether to try one of two therapies and which.

- A3-S12 "I haven't found a trial comparing the two." is not needed. S11 already says not to compare the numbers. Fix: merge into S11.
- Missing act: if no doctor has checked the pain, the reader's first step is missing. S2 implies it. Fix: add "If no doctor has checked it yet, start there."

## 2. Fit read

- A1-S5: "calls out each slide... keeps turning you back" clashes with Layer 3's promise of a "nuanced, loving and gentle way". The therapy sounds confrontational before S6 softens it. Fix: say up front that this is the firmer option and gentler versions exist.
- A1-S4: "talk therapies" sits in a layer titled "before the story comes in", and the guide files talk therapy under story and cognitive work in Layer 5. Fix: add "these work on the feeling, not the story".
- A2-S4: "my inner child guide" is not named anywhere in the guide, which says "inner-child reparenting". Fix: use the guide's own term and link.
- A2-S7: "Since you know the new version is imagined, it changes how the memory feels" doesn't follow. Knowing it's imagined explains why the memory facts stay, not why the feeling changes. Fix: split into two plain claims.
- A3-S1: "pain is what brought you to this guide" doesn't fit a trauma guide. Fix: "If chronic pain is part of what you're dealing with".
- A3-S3: "the pain is a false alarm" can read as "your pain isn't real". Fix: "real pain, but the danger it signals is false".
- A3-S13: "the second" and "the first" are ambiguous after the trial numbers. Fix: name EAET and Pain Reprocessing Therapy.
- A3 heading and spot: an h1 straight after the inner-child sentence ending Layer 5, with no bridge, reads as a seventh Layer. Fix: make it h2 or add a one-line bridge.

## 3. Contradictions

- A3-S3 "you learn that the pain is a false alarm" (a cognitive reframe) against the guide's "they are working against the body's understanding of things by creating new cognitive-behavioral patterns that suppress the old conditioned fear responses". The guide also says bottom-up work comes first. A3 recommends a reframing therapy without saying how it differs. Fix: say it pairs the reframe with body attention (S3 half-says this), or say the guide's caution applies less here.
