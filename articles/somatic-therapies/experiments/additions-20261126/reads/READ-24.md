## Against his fixes
MARKED:
- IC2-S2: 10 — "Neither one treats the pain". Read quickly, the first five words say that neither therapy treats the pain, which is the opposite of what the sentence is there to say, and the reader only finds out at "as imaginary". Drafts 22 and 23 had the same double sense of "treat" ("They both treat the pain as real"), but there the wrong reading was harmless (they treat the pain); with "Neither" in front it turns into "they don't work". One word takes it away, and it is the verb READ-21 passed: "Neither one sees the pain as imaginary, and they've helped people with long-lasting back, muscle and joint pain."

What it was checked against:
- IC2-S2 was also checked against 2, 3, 6, 8, 12, 16, 19 and 21. Nothing else marked.
  - 3: "Neither one ... as imaginary" is a negative where draft 23 said the thing itself ("as real"). It is not marked, because it answers a worry this reader really has and that his own section has just raised in the same word ("normal test results don't prove the pain is imaginary"), and IC2-S1 has just sent them to a talk therapy for pain, which is exactly when a reader asks "so it's in my head?".
  - 12: "Neither one" points at the two therapies IC2-S1 ends on, and "imaginary" ties the sentence to his paragraph two above. It follows.
  - 16: it uses his word "imaginary" again, but his line is about what the tests show and this one is about how the therapies regard the pain, so it isn't the same point said twice.
  - 10, "they've": after "Neither one" (each of the two), "they" is the pair, so "they've helped people with ... back, muscle and joint pain" is true of the two taken together. This is draft 21's wording ("and they've helped"), which READ-21 looked at and didn't mark. It is weaker than draft 23's "both ... have helped", which said each one had, and which READ-23 marked. A reader with joint pain can still take it as either therapy, so it isn't as exact as draft 22's "between them they've"; see Contradictions. Not marked.
  - 2 and 8: the two halves are built differently and are joined with ", and", the way he joins.
  - 6: it ends the paragraph on whom the therapies have helped, which is a reason to follow the pointer and not a summing-up.
  - 19: "back, muscle and joint pain" is a list of concrete things and stays whole.
  - 21: nothing.
