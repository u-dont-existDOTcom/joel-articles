## Needed, fit, contradictions
1. Needed: yes.
   - A1-S7 tells a reader choosing among the three what this therapist is there for: they won't be left by themselves with a feeling. It is also what makes "their care" in A1-S8 the therapist's. Without it A1-S8 would open on care nobody has shown yet.
2. Fit: none.
   - A1-S7: ANYWAY - / CAN'T TELL - / FROM - / MISREAD -.
   - ANYWAY was looked at: every therapist is in the room with you for the whole session, so "stays with you" alone would tell the reader nothing. The second half settles it (see 16 above).
   - MISREAD was looked at twice. Draft 26's "keep you from feeling", which read alone said the opposite of the section, is gone. And the heading asks whether you "can't stay with a feeling" while here the therapist "stays with you": the two uses sit together without confusion, since one is you with a feeling and the other is somebody with you.
3. Contradictions: none, with three things looked at closely.
   - Against his A1-S9 ("All three keep steering you back toward your feelings"): staying with you isn't steering, but A1-S8's "they ask what that's like and where you feel it" is, so the third therapy is still covered by his sentence, and "All three" counts right (ISTDP, Affect Phobia Therapy, AEDP).
   - Against his A1-S9 ("which can get to be too intense sometimes") and A1-S11 ("be gentler"): a therapist who stays with you can still take you somewhere too intense, so the two agree and his question in A1-S10 still applies to this therapy.
   - Against A1-S2 to A1-S4 (the ISTDP therapist catches you and watches your anxiety): nothing in A1-S7 says the other two therapists leave you alone, so it doesn't run them down by contrast.
   - What the sentence says AEDP does (the therapist staying alongside the client so they aren't alone with what they feel) matches what I know of AEDP. I didn't check a source here.
