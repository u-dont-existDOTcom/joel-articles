## Does the cut lose anything a reader needs?
No.
- What went is the stated purpose of the staying ("so you're not alone with it"). "Stays with you" already says you aren't alone, so the clause said the first half again as its result (16). Its "it" also had only "whatever comes up" to point at.
- What a reader could miss is the warmth: the clause was the part that made the staying plainly a kindness, and A1-S8 leans on that with "their kindness". "Stays with you through whatever comes up" still reads as a kindness by itself, so "their kindness" still has something to point back to.
- The one new risk is the literal reading (every therapist stays in the room). See 4 and ANYWAY below; a reader doesn't take it that way here.

1. Needed: yes.
   - A1-S7 is what the AEDP therapist does all through, and it is the kindness that "their kindness" in A1-S8 points back to. Without it A1-S8 would be the first the reader hears of the therapist being kind.
2. Fit: none.
   - A1-S7: ANYWAY - / CAN'T TELL - / FROM - / MISREAD -.
