# Read of draft 9 (A1, A3, IC1, IC2): needed, fit, contradictions

Figures in A3-S6 and A3-S10 were not checked in this read.

## 1. Needed read

**A1, "Can't Stay With a Feeling?"** Reader: has finished Layer 3, can feel their body, but slips away from feelings in sessions. They decide whether to look for a talk therapy, which of three, and how to vet a therapist.

1. A1-S5, "Most people who go get a slower version of it, with more help settling down when the anxiety climbs." The share ("most people") has no source, and "go" has no place to go to. The reader only needs to know a gentler version exists and to ask for it. Fix: "A slower version exists for people who get overwhelmed easily; ask for it."
2. Missing act in A1: nothing says how to find a therapist who does these, or to ask where they trained. A3-S14 says it. Fix: add "ask where they trained in it" to A1-S10.
3. Missing in A1: the reader is spending money on three therapies and is told nothing about the evidence. The guide says "These don't all have the same level or type of evidence." Fix: one plain clause on the evidence, or that you haven't checked it.

**A3, "Pain That Won't Quit?"** Reader: has chronic pain, is partway through or finished with the guide, and decides whether to try one of two therapies, which one, and how to start.

4. Missing act in A3-S2: it implies "get checked first" but never says it. A reader who hasn't been checked may skip that. Also nothing says new or changed pain goes back to the doctor. Fix: "If nobody has checked yet, start there, and get new or different pain checked again."
5. Missing act in A3: nothing says how to find a practitioner, or whether you can try it alone. Fix: one line on where to look, once verified.

**IC1, Imagery Rescripting.** Reader: reading how to redo a painful memory themselves, deciding whether to go it alone or get help.

6. Missing act: the sentence names a therapy but doesn't say when to use it. Fix: "If the memory feels too big to do alone, look for a therapist who does Imagery Rescripting."

**IC2, the pain therapies.** Reader: in pain, perhaps in crisis, deciding whether to look into these two therapies.

7. Both names are given with no description, and "I go into both in my somatic guide" does not say where. Fix: link "somatic guide" to the section "Pain That Won't Quit?" and add a few words saying what each therapy is.

## 2. Fit read

8. A1-S4 and A1-S5, "the anxiety is getting to be too much", "when the anxiety climbs". Nothing before this mentions anxiety. A1-S2 is about slipping away from a feeling. Fix: "the anxiety that makes you slip away", said once in S4.
9. A1-S11, "If the answer leaves no room for you to slow down, or to disagree with them about what you're feeling, keep looking." A1-S4 describes a therapist who points out each time you slip away, which is a form of disagreeing with what you say you feel. A reader could rule out the very therapy just described. Fix: "room to say 'that's too much' and have them slow down."
10. A3-S3, "you learn that the pain is a false alarm". Read quickly, this sounds like "the pain isn't real". Fix: "the pain is real, but it's a false alarm, your brain signaling danger where there isn't any."
11. A3-S13, "So if your pain seems tied to stress or to people in your life, I'd start with the second, and otherwise with the first." The "So" follows A3-S12 (no trial compares them), but the choice really follows from A3-S7. "Otherwise the first" also ignores that A3-S5 says its trial covered only mild to moderate back pain. Fix: "Since I haven't found a trial comparing them, I'd go by fit: ... and if it isn't back pain, know the evidence is thinner."
12. A3 placement: it lands right after Layer 5's closing paragraph. If it has a smaller heading it reads as part of Layer 5. Fix: give it the same heading level as "Optional High-Intensity State-Shift and Release Practices".
13. IC1-S1 sits before "For example, I'll take one instance from my own childhood." That example now reads as an example of Imagery Rescripting, but it is Joel's own do-it-yourself version. Fix: change the next sentence to "Here's one I redid myself" or move IC1 after the example.
14. IC1-S1, "where a therapist walks you through it at first". "At first" implies you later do it alone, which the clinical therapy doesn't claim. Fix: drop "at first".
15. IC2-S1, "two therapies have done well in trials for pain with no injury or disease found behind it". A3-S5 and A3-S10 limit the trials to mild to moderate back pain and to veterans aged 60 and up with muscle and bone pain. "Done well" is vague and the scope is wider than the evidence. Fix: "have helped in trials, mostly for back and muscle pain."

## 3. Contradictions

16. IC2-S1, "pain with no injury or disease found behind it", against the guide's "Sometimes it really is so simple that the best doctors and scanners can't figure it out, like your house is moldy." Finding nothing so far does not mean nothing is there, and the paragraph just told the reader not to stop investigating. Fix: "no injury or disease found so far".
17. A3-S3, "you learn that the pain is a false alarm", which IC2-S2 sends inner child readers to, against the inner child guide's "a treatment that helps doesn't explain what's causing it." A3-S3 states the cause as fact. Fix: "the therapy's premise is that the pain is a false alarm", or "the therapy teaches that...".
