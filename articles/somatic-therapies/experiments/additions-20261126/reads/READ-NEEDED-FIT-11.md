## 2. Fit read

- A1-S5: ANYWAY - / CAN'T TELL - / FROM - / MISREAD yes. "Most people who try this therapy can't take much of that anxiety" reads at speed as "most people can't take this therapy", which scares off the reader the sentence is meant to reassure. Fix: "Most people who come to this therapy can only take a little of that anxiety at first. That's expected, and they get a slower version, where the therapist stops to help them settle whenever it climbs."
- A3-S3: ANYWAY - / CAN'T TELL - / FROM - / MISREAD yes. "partly by paying close attention to how it feels in your body" sits right after "your brain signaling danger where there isn't any", so it reads as how the brain makes the false alarm (paying attention to pain causes it), not as how you learn. Fix: "In Pain Reprocessing Therapy, you learn that the pain, though real, is a false alarm: your brain signaling danger where there isn't any. Part of how you learn it is by paying close attention to how the pain feels in your body."
- IC1-S1: ANYWAY - / CAN'T TELL - / FROM - / MISREAD yes (small). "steps into the memory with you as the adult you needed" can be read as you being the adult, and then "do that yourself" has nothing to point to. Fix: "... called Imagery Rescripting, where a therapist first steps into the memory as the adult you needed, and later you step in as that adult yourself."
- IC2-S2: ANYWAY - / CAN'T TELL - / FROM yes / MISREAD -. This section's reader is curled up on the bathroom floor with attacks; "helped people with chronic back pain" drops the "mild to moderate" that A3-S5 keeps, so the finding sounds like it covers more than it does. Fix: "Both treat the pain as real, and in trials they've helped people with mild to moderate chronic back pain and with other long-lasting muscle and joint pain."

Checked and fine: A1-S9's "As with water therapy, I'd ask before booking" matches the live line ("I would ask a water therapist about that before I booked"); IC2-S1's "while the investigation goes on" fits his "don't let amelioration of symptoms be the end of the investigation"; IC2-S3 matches his "(For more on that, check my detox guide.)".
