## What the lessons missed (the next lessons, somatic-2026-11-27m to o)

1. **Fix in place.** His fixes keep the near miss's sentences and change the few words a reader could trip on; a writer
   told to rewrite from the same lessons rebuilt the paragraphs and read lower in four of six. The brief should say
   "change only these words" the way lesson bad-trips-2026-11-19b does for my own tries.
2. **How it helps, in one "by" clause on the claim it explains**, not a sentence per therapy.
3. **The evidence as his own reading, joined to the paragraph before** ("And in 2024 we got evidence ..., at least in
   ..."), with no description of the trial after it.
4. **A closing wish as a short list of plain acts** ("change the approach, listen to me and be gentler"), not clauses
   with conditions. This one was lesson somatic-2026-11-27l, in the brief: the writer had it and
   didn't follow it, so it gets no new lesson.
