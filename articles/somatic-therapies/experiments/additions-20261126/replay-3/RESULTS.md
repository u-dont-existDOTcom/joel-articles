## What the lessons missed

1. **Say it from what the text and its sources already hold.** Told to say something specific about how the therapy
   works, the writer reached past the guide for a fact; his manner words came from the section itself. Lesson a, as
   corrected by the rules check ("which may be its manner"), says so; the general rule that a fix adds no claim the
   sources don't hold was in the brief and wasn't enough. No new lesson: the corrected a, and the brief's "no new
   claims", carry it.
2. **A referent named still leaves the opening to lead in.** The booking rewrite named the three but kept them in an
   aside inside the opener; lesson c as corrected says both halves in any sentence. No new lesson.
