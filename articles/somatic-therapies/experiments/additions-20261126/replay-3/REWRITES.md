## A1-P4

And if what you can't let in is kindness, when someone cares about you and it just bounces off, there's [Accelerated Experiential Dynamic Psychotherapy](https://www.apa.org/pubs/videos/4310759). The AEDP therapist tells you out loud how they feel about you and about what you just told them, instead of sitting there neutral. When some of their kindness does get in, they ask what it's like to have them care about you right then, and where you feel it in your body.

## A1-P5

A therapist who keeps steering you back toward your feelings, which all three of these therapies have them do, can make those feelings get too intense sometimes. So before booking an ISTDP, Affect Phobia or AEDP therapist, I'd ask the kind of thing I'd ask a water therapist: "What happens if I get overwhelmed, or if you say I'm dodging a feeling and I don't think I am?" I'd want to hear that they would change the approach, listen to me and be gentler.
