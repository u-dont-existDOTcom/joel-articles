Water therapy and Brainspotting leave it up to you to stay with whatever comes up, but in a talking session a feeling starts and you're already explaining where it comes from. Some people agree with everything the therapist says so the moment will pass. [Intensive Short-Term Dynamic Psychotherapy](https://psykologtidsskriftet.no/debatt/2021/09/intensive-short-term-dynamic-psychotherapy-methods-evidence-indications-and) was built around that habit. The therapist tells you you're doing it, as you do it, and keeps pushing for the feeling itself however many times you change the subject.
