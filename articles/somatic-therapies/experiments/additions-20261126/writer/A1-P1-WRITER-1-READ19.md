Water therapy and Brainspotting leave it up to you to stay with whatever comes up, which doesn't help much if every time a feeling starts, you're already explaining where it comes from. Or you agree with everything the therapist says so the moment will pass. [Intensive Short-Term Dynamic Psychotherapy](https://psykologtidsskriftet.no/debatt/2021/09/intensive-short-term-dynamic-psychotherapy-methods-evidence-indications-and) was built around both of those ways of slipping away. The therapist tells you you're doing it, as you do it, and keeps pushing for the feeling itself however many times you change the subject.
