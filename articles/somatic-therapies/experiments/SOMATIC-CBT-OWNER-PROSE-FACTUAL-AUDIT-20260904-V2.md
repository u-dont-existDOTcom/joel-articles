## Findings

1. **Exposure classification needs one explicit scope distinction.** EMDR clearly includes deliberate trauma-memory activation / imaginal exposure; the VA National Center for PTSD explicitly describes EMDR patients as engaging in imaginal exposure while doing eye movements. PE is formally exposure-based. However, current standard CPT is cognitive-only and does not require its former written trauma-account exposure component. Therefore `all of these are exposure therapies` is too broad if `trauma-focused CBT` includes current CPT. The defensible broader claim is that the dominant trauma-focused treatments deliberately bring traumatic memories, meanings, cues, or feared predictions into active therapeutic attention, while differing in formal mechanism.

2. **`Top-down` still does not cleanly describe EMDR.** EMDR's AIP model is memory-network / information-processing centered, explicitly includes physical sensations, and posits an intrinsic neurophysiological psychological self-healing system. It is therefore not straightforwardly a cortical/cognitive top-down therapy even though its treatment target is centrally organized around traumatic memory networks rather than the SE emphasis on current interoceptive/proprioceptive/autonomic/motor completion. A more exact contrast is memory-/appraisal-centered versus sensorimotor/autonomic-process-centered.

3. **The single shared protocol sentence remains overgeneralized.** `slowly increase your exposure to painful memories until you can fully re-live the experience without overwhelming your nervous system` fits neither current CPT nor standard EMDR exactly and oversimplifies PE. PE uses repeated imaginal and in-vivo exposure; EMDR activates selected aspects of a target memory during bilateral stimulation; current CPT focuses on identifying/challenging trauma-related stuck points and can omit a trauma narrative entirely.

4. **`CBT trauma therapies then go further after that into regular CBT` is not generally correct as sequence.** Cognitive processing/restructuring is integral to CPT rather than a later generic-CBT phase after exposure. Different trauma-focused CBT protocols combine exposure, cognitive restructuring, emotion processing, and avoidance work differently.

5. **Dropout/adverse-event sentence is substantially repaired.** It is defensible to say reasons for dropout and adverse events have often been poorly/inconsistently reported. However, the claim that exposure can be re-traumatizing should remain attributed to Levine/SE as a theoretical/clinical risk, not presented as a settled general empirical result across exposure therapy. Payne/Levine/Crane-Godreau explicitly describe flooding and potential re-traumatization and contrast SE's titrated approach with exposure.

6. **`suppressing, ignoring, or arguing away feelings` remains too broad for the therapies named.** PE deliberately evokes and processes emotion; EMDR tracks affect and physical sensation; CPT explicitly works with emotions as well as beliefs. Levine's critique more accurately targets cortical executive suppression and conditioning/appraisal models that can fail to engage incomplete subcortical/autonomic/motor processes. The owner argument survives if framed as these therapies treating bodily activation primarily as memory/fear/appraisal material to process rather than as an incomplete defensive process with its own trajectory.

7. **SIBAM paragraph is materially improved.** The owner revision correctly avoids calling dissociation/hyperarousal root causes and instead uses them diagnostically as patterns of nervous-system dysregulation. `Dissociation is when these layers are not well-connected` and `Hyper-arousal is when channels are so tightly linked that one rapidly activates the others` are useful SIBAM-style simplifications, but should be understood as model descriptions rather than exhaustive clinical definitions. SIBAM terminology itself is not contained in the 2015 Frontiers paper and needs its own direct source if retained as a formal named model.

8. **EMDR-versus-SE final contrast is directionally strong and much more nuanced than v1.** `barometer and metabolizer of distress` acknowledges EMDR's body role better than the earlier `barometer` alone. The strongest exact distinction is that EMDR/AIP is organized around reprocessing an inadequately processed trauma memory network, including body sensations, whereas SE gives special causal/therapeutic primacy to interoceptive/proprioceptive tracking, autonomic regulation, subtle motor impulses, procedural memory, biological completion, and discharge of incomplete defensive responses. Avoid implying that EMDR ignores bodily self-healing; AIP explicitly posits intrinsic neurophysiological self-healing.

9. **Meaning-handles-itself / cognitive-restructuring remainder is strongly supported.** Payne/Levine/Crane-Godreau state that cognitive re-evaluations often emerge spontaneously during or shortly after autonomic/kinesthetic discharge and that cognitive restructuring can become easier as the CRN returns toward normal functioning, while still preserving a role for explicit cognitive restructuring.
