## Cold audit — pass 1: semantic sanity / thought movement

### Sentence 1

`Sometimes the body is already reacting before you've decided what you want to do.`

This is substantially source-grounded. It establishes the Stage-1 temporal problem that activation can outrun deliberate choice.

### Sentence 2

`You can be in the middle of a reaction and still not know whether you actually want to keep going into the trauma.`

This is not merely a deeper version of sentence 1. It introduces an additional premise: that being in the middle of an already-running reaction means one is already `going into the trauma` and deciding whether to continue.

That equivalence is not established by the current working source. A reaction can already be happening before deliberate trauma processing has begun at all. The source protects both `activation outruns choice` and the standing choice whether to continue processing, but those are distinct functions. The candidate collapses them into one chronology.

Therefore `keep going into the trauma` is an unexplained substantive delta / semantic conflation unless separately authorized.

### Sentence 3

`Somatic Experiencing can stay with the reaction that's already there before you make that choice.`

This sentence has two problems:

1. `Somatic Experiencing can stay with the reaction` is vague about agency. A modality does not literally stay with something; the reader is left to infer whether the person, therapist, attention, or method is doing the staying.
2. `before you make that choice` resolves the preceding manufactured chronology rather than following a clearly established source relation.

The sentence therefore reads more like an abstract payoff to the mini-argument than a concrete next move.

## Cold audit — pass 2: cognitive cadence / model shape

The passage forms an unusually complete three-sentence micro-essay:

`problem -> sharpened problem -> modality payoff`

Even though all three sentences stay near the same topic, they occupy clean rhetorical jobs and close the unit exactly when the method name arrives.

There is also repeated temporal machinery:

- `before you've decided`;
- `still not know whether`;
- `before you make that choice`.

And `reaction` appears in all three sentences. The effect is not merely repetition of a necessary referent; it makes the underlying architecture visible.

This is consistent with the active `RHYTHM-AND-THOUGHT-SHAPE.md` warning about nested mini-essay closure and equalized conceptual jobs. A thought can remain topically coherent while still being packaged into a model-shaped rhetorical unit.

## Heading fit

The third sentence finally names Somatic Experiencing, so heading fit is stronger than in the earlier generic-reaction probes. However, the relation it supplies is too abstract (`can stay with the reaction`) to distinguish the modality meaningfully from generic present-focused attention.

Heading fit: **partial / insufficient as final prose**.

## Fidelity / preservation

Forward traceability is not clean enough for promotion:

- activation outruns choice -> sentence 1: supported;
- standing choice whether to continue processing -> sentence 2: intended source function, but merged with an unsupported `already going into the trauma` chronology;
- SE as early modality when activation outruns choice -> sentence 3: intended source function, but realized through vague agency and the chronology introduced by sentence 2.

Unexplained substantive deltas: **at least 1** (`already reacting` -> `already going into the trauma / keep going`).

Preservation status for promotion: **FAIL**.

Later Stage-1 functions remain deferred, not deleted.

## Stopping point

The stop is formally neat rather than demonstrated as natural. Sentence 3 supplies a modality payoff and closes the three-beat unit, but it creates an obvious unresolved reader question: what does `stay with the reaction` actually mean here?

That question could lead into a distinct movement, but the current sentence is too vague to earn the stop cleanly. The problem is not simply that more explanation is needed; the relation itself is under-specified.

## Method diagnosis: hidden curriculum migrated into the state oracle

The sealed-affordance experiment correctly stopped giving the explorer a named list of source affordances. However, the world-model/state-transition layer still had access to the full preservation architecture and selected the next unresolved pressure after each sentence.

After sentence 1 it returned a pressure equivalent to `what remains undecided?`; after sentence 2 it returned `can the method do anything useful while that decision is unresolved?`.

That sequence steered the prose toward two protected functions:

`activation outruns choice -> standing choice whether to continue processing -> SE modality relation`.

So the checklist did not disappear. It migrated from the writer prompt into the state oracle.

### Provisional correction

For the next experiment, separate **generation-state inference** from **preservation validation** more sharply:

1. The next-pressure selector must not inspect unsatisfied future preservation obligations when deciding what the literal prose now makes live.
2. It may use the visible preceding article context, literal prose-so-far, heading promise, and ordinary curious-reader continuity.
3. Source/preservation knowledge should operate primarily as a **veto/validation layer**: reject invented or distorted next sentences, but do not suggest which protected function should be visited next.
4. The validator should return the smallest failure reason needed (`unsupported chronology`, `new mechanism`, `restatement`, etc.) without naming the content item the writer should insert.
5. Only after a natural movement genuinely stops should the full preservation inventory reopen for reconciliation and routing.

This is closer to an interactive environment with constraints than to a hidden lesson plan.
