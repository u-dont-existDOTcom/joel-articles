## Reader-facing boundary

When the Child Doesn't Believe Me

In inner-child therapy, you can know exactly what the younger part needs and still get pulled so far into the child-state that there isn't enough adult perspective left to give it.

For me, it isn't always that I can't access the love. Sometimes the love itself seems unsafe. My inner child looks at the grown-up version of me and basically says, “Yeah, that doesn't look like things went well for us.” Then I can get resentful at the child and end up at war with the younger version of myself. The older part starts making vows about protection and nurturing and the child is sitting there thinking, “Big fuckity whoopty doo. What are you gonna do for me?”

I call the adult roles the Nurturer and Protector. The Protector says no. The Protector keeps small promises. I will not just comfort you internally while abandoning you externally.

If these adult voices don't feel available yet, I begin as the witness who notices. I may borrow one sentence, boundary, or direction from somebody safe. I call that borrowed adulthood.

Relaxation by itself hasn't fixed this for me. If I'm still blocked, I may use a little meditation or self-hypnosis to go deeper into it. I mean a little. I'm not talking about forcing trauma memories or doing an intensive retreat.
