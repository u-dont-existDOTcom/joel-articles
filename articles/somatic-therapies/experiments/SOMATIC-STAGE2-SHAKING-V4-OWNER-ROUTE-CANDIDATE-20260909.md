### Candidate replacement

Shaking can also happen inside Somatic Experiencing. Levine treats trembling as one form of autonomic discharge, although he also argues that the important movement may be the specific defensive action that got interrupted rather than movement for its own sake. So the boundary between Stage 1 and Stage 2 is fuzzy. Sometimes SE helps you feel where the activation is and stay with it, and then a more active shaking practice gives you another way to work with it. Other times the subtler SE work does not get much happening for you, and you try shaking to see what changes.

The Stage 2 version is more deliberate than the small exploratory dose in Stage 1, while still being something you can stop, orient from, and settle afterward. A regular practice might run 10–45 minutes. Possible use cases include chronic freeze, non-verbal emotional processing, stress that accumulates between therapy sessions, and activation left over after deeper work.
