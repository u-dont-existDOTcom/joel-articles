## Literal candidate

Somatic Experiencing doesn't require you to start with a detailed trauma story. Sometimes all you have at first is a sensation, and you haven't decided what, if anything, you want to do next. You can leave that undecided. The body is already doing something, but noticing it does not commit you to following it into a memory. You can pay attention to the sensation and still look around the room. If the reaction starts carrying you farther than you meant to go, stop there. Whether you go back to the sensation after that is still a choice.

## Cold audit — pass 1: semantic movement

The paragraph remains on one live relation: bodily experience can already be present before the person has committed to a narrative or to continuing deeper processing, so the relevant practical question is whether attention can remain movable while the reaction is present.

It does not advance into the clear-target exception, child-state guidance, Nurturer/Protector, self-hypnosis, yoga, TRE, or later Stage-1 material.

Sentence 4 (`noticing it does not commit you to following it into a memory`) is a direct continuation of the narrative/choice pressure rather than a new conceptual job.

Sentences 5–7 form one causal movement: attention remains capable of shifting to the room; if the reaction begins carrying the person farther than intended, stop; the option to return remains undecided. This is close to an approach/return sequence, but it arose from the reader question created by the preceding sentences rather than from an exposed preservation checklist.
