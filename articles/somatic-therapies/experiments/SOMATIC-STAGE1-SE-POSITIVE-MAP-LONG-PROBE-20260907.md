## Model-generated probe — exact scored boundary

How much of a difficult reaction can you stay with before it starts making the decisions for you? You can be willing to face a subject and still find that once the body gets going, willingness isn't doing much of the steering anymore.

Levine calls working with a smaller amount at once titration. The amount isn't something you can decide from the name of the trauma. You find it while you're in the work. Something that sounds manageable in conversation can feel very different once attention lands on the part that is actually charged, and the change may happen before you have a neat explanation for it. For a while you are still there with the reaction. Then there is less room around it.

Sometimes attention moves toward a place that is easier to stay with and, after a while, returns to the difficult part. SE calls that pendulation. When you come back, is the body actually coming with you, or is the first reaction still running even though your attention has moved?
