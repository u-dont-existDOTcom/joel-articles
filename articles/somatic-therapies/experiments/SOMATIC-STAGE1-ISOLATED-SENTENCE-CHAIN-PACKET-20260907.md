## Why sentence chaining

Paragraph-level isolated generation still encouraged one fresh model to complete the sparse local fact into:

`state -> negated prerequisite -> balanced clarification -> abstract landing`.

Do not solve this by adding more anti-pattern instructions.

Instead, compose one literal sentence at a time through genuinely fresh writer contexts. Each writer sees only the current literal prose-so-far and minimal fidelity restrictions. The canonical supervisor audits each sentence after it returns.

This is **not** the earlier sentence-state architecture:

- no sentence-by-sentence conceptual state update;
- no `what changed?` question;
- no next-pressure selector;
- no preservation inventory;
- no future Stage-1 obligation selection;
- no requirement that each sentence introduce novelty;
- no cadence target;
- no requirement to conclude a paragraph.

The literal prose-so-far is the only generative trajectory.

## Isolation requirement

Every sentence generation must occur in a **new genuinely fresh context** that has not seen:

- Stage-1 preservation inventory;
- prior Stage-1 candidates/audits other than the literal prose supplied in that sentence packet;
- detector history;
- cadence/humanization maps;
- supervisor questions or diagnoses;
- future sentence targets;
- the rationale in this file.

Do not continue in the same fresh writer chat after supervisor feedback. A materially accepted next sentence becomes literal input to a *new* blank writer context.
