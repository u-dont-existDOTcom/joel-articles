## A12 — conceptual rethinking is allowed; silent argument substitution is not

**Trigger:** the Chat believes the inherited argument, source packet, causal model, sequence, certainty, or section architecture needs substantive rethinking.

**Required behavior:** the Chat may perform the rethink. If it materially changes the argument, surface it to Joel as an explicit proposal before using it as article prose. State:

1. the current claim/architecture being questioned;
2. the proposed rethink;
3. why the rethink seems necessary;
4. the source, reality check, curiosity chain, or reasoning that produced it;
5. what downstream claims/functions would change.

**FAIL if:** a model-authored `humanization` candidate silently changes a material claim, causal relation, recovery sequence, certainty, recommendation, source basis, or section function and presents the result as merely better wording.

**Repair:** withdraw the silent candidate change, present the conceptual rethink explicitly, and wait for owner disposition before making it article authority.

## A13 — follow live curiosity before polishing a claim that feels invented or under-motivated

**Trigger:** a central claim reads like unsupported generic abstraction, invented fluff, or a conclusion without a visible reason to care.

**Required behavior:** before paraphrasing, test the obvious curiosity chain: where did the idea come from; what observation/source/experience supports it; what happened in the contrasting case; what consequence makes the claim matter here? Search/source-recover when that inquiry can materially improve the thought.

**FAIL if:** the Chat repeatedly rewrites an under-motivated claim without asking the obvious origin/consequence question that could reopen the thought.

**Boundary:** external prose is evidence/content only, not a style donor. If an exact source cannot be recovered, report that rather than fabricating provenance.

## A14 — cadence/rhythm must clear the existing thought-shape gate

**Trigger:** every model-authored candidate.

**Required behavior:** apply `project-sources/RHYTHM-AND-THOUGHT-SHAPE.md` literally at sentence and paragraph scale.

**FAIL if:** the candidate has a credible metronomic AI cadence even when its vocabulary and architecture are superficially improved. Local Somatic warning signs include:

- neighboring sentences with similar rhetorical weight repeatedly doing `state -> explain/judge -> stop`;
- repeated `You can...`, `You may...`, `Sometimes...`, conditional verdict, reset patterns or functional equivalents;
- comparable sentence end-weight and clean full-stop beats across developing thoughts;
- paragraphs with equalized conceptual bar-length that each land a tidy verdict;
- naturally connected spoken thoughts split into polished isolated units.

**Repair:** change the governing thought relation or sentence joining, not merely length. Do not randomize sentence length, add fragments, roughness, slang, or punctuation noise.

## A15 — confirmed typo is not idiolect; confirmed unusual wording is

**Trigger:** unusual or apparently erroneous owner wording.

**Required behavior:** if Joel confirms it is a typo, correct it normally and do not protect the error. If status is unclear, do not silently normalize identity-bearing language. Once Joel confirms wording is intentional, preserve it unless he later changes it.

`one-one` is confirmed intentional in this Somatic lane. Ordinary confirmed typos are not.
