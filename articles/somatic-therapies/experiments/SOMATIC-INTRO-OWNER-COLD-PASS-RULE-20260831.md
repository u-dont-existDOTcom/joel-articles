## Two-consecutive-cold-clear gate

A candidate may not be presented as having cleared the requested cold-pass process until the **literal same candidate** receives two consecutive cold-pass clears.

- Each cold pass must attack the prose for known model-shaped thought movement, semantic-card architecture, conditional menus/taxonomies, explanatory aftercare, polished cross-domain transfer, significance staging, equalized paragraph closure, preservation-as-outline, and recurrence of the active Somatic failure package.
- If a cold pass causes any substantive rewrite, the consecutive-clear count resets to zero for the new literal candidate.
- Continue cold passing/revising until the same unchanged candidate has two consecutive clears or a genuine unresolved uncertainty requires owner input.
- Same-context cold passes are not independent verification and must not be represented as such.
