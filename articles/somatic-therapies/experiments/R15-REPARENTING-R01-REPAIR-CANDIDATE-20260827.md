# Somatic Work and Inner-Child Reparenting

Sometimes I can't do much useful reparenting until I do something with the body first. If I am so flooded that I am basically the child-state, there may not be much adult perspective left online to comfort or protect that child.

In my inner-child work I call those adult functions the Nurturer and Protector. Sometimes I can only find a neutral witness at first, or what I call borrowed adulthood: taking the stance of a steady adult when I can't yet find that steadiness inside myself. Even one ordinary protective action can matter. Gentle [self-hypnosis](http://hypnosis.u-dont-exist.com/) can help too if it stays present-focused instead of pulling me deeply into memories or child dialogue.

In my experience, EFT, Somatic Experiencing, trauma-sensitive yoga, gentle shaking or qigong, non-forced longer exhales, and gentle Buteyko-style breathing can relax the body and soften tension around the solar plexus or abdomen enough that heart chakra love is easier to access. I may use them before reparenting or keep using them while I am doing it.

Brainspotting can be part of the reparenting itself. Using it to resource or carefully titrate what comes up is very different from going into highly charged developmental material. With diffuse developmental or relational trauma, I am more inclined to wait on EMDR until there is at least some access to a Nurturer and Protector who can help hold what the memory work opens. A stable person with one discrete event may be ready much earlier. Deep memory work, immersive child dialogue, deep hypnosis, and suggestive exploration are things I save for when I can stay present, stop voluntarily, and come back afterward.

Once the body softens even a little, I do not have to use the opening to go looking for pain. I can bring to mind a pet, a baby, a friend, a partner, or anyone I naturally love. Feel the real love that is already there, and see whether a little of it can move from the heart toward the younger part around the solar plexus and then back up in a loop.
