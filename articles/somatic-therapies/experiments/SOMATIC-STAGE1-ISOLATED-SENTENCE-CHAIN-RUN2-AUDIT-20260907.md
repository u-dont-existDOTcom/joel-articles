## Literal prose audit

### 1. Reader-state movement is too small

Seed:

`body already responding before there is a clear sense of what to do next`

Continuation:

`notice what is happening without deciding anything yet`

The only new action is `notice`. The second half restates the seed's existing undecidedness. The reader begins and ends in essentially the same conceptual position.

### 2. Generic placement

`notice what is happening without deciding anything yet` could appear under mindfulness, MCT, generic body-awareness, Focusing, yoga, or many other headings with little change.

It does not yet develop a specifically Somatic Experiencing / Stage-1 relation. This is not a demand for jargon; it is a heading-fit problem.

### 3. Undefined sufficiency

`it can be enough` creates a soft sufficiency claim without specifying enough **for what**:

- enough to stay present?
- enough to retain meaningful choice?
- enough for the current moment?
- enough as a therapeutic intervention?

The source does not authorize noticing alone as a general sufficiency criterion. `can be` softens the claim but does not resolve the undefined role.

### 4. Cross-boundary generic attention repetition

The accepted SIBAM discussion immediately upstream has already established directing attention toward layers of experience. The continuation's `notice what is happening` is therefore not strong new reader value by itself.
