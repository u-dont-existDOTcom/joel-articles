## Exact model-generated scored boundary

With yoga, body awareness is attached to something you're actually doing. You're moving, and the pose gives you immediate feedback about whether it still feels workable. That can be as practical as noticing that a position helps you relax or makes sitting afterward more comfortable. It doesn't have to turn into another project of monitoring the body.

The teacher can undo that pretty quickly. “You might try…” leaves room for an answer from your own body; “stay there” doesn't. Keeping your eyes open or leaving the pose can be part of the practice.

So for this stage I'm talking about restorative or trauma-sensitive yoga, not a class built around intensity.
