## What failed

- each modality became a self-contained mini-card with setup -> examples -> warning/closure;
- multiple sentences packed examples into tidy enumerations (`sensation, orientation, an impulse...`; `look around the room, get outside help...`; yoga forms and prohibitions; shaking examples);

- cadence repeatedly used compact declarative beats, conditional `if` clauses, and end-stopped verdicts;
- section headings plus equally complete modality units preserved the hidden checklist architecture even without literal bullet formatting.

The failure is therefore **topological and rhythmic**, not a formatting bug. Removing bullets while preserving list-thinking does not humanize the prose.

## Process change — isolated local continuation for Stage 1

Do not generate the next Stage-1 candidate as a complete section from the semantic/function inventory.

Instead:

1. Work in physical/local isolation from the preservation checklist during composition.
2. Start from the literal current sentence/fragment at the live location.
3. Generate only the next locally necessary prose sentence (or, when the thought genuinely requires it, the smallest naturally continuous unit) from the live pressure of what immediately precedes it.
4. Continue sentence by sentence, carrying only the literal prose already written plus the immediate local thought; do not expose the writer to the full modality/function inventory while composing.
5. Do not aim for equal subsection completeness. A modality may take one sentence, several uneven sentences, or stop without a summary if the thought stops there.
6. Do not convert examples, cautions, options, or modality features into tidy closed enumerations merely because several items must ultimately survive.
7. After a natural Stage-1 realization exists, run preservation traceability against the hidden inventory. If a required function is missing, reconstruct locally; do not append a catch-up list or explanatory card.
8. Run the existing cadence/thought-shape adversarial gate across the complete natural Stage-1 boundary before owner delivery.

This is a process change, not another instruction-only reminder: the writer is deliberately deprived of the full checklist during generation so the checklist cannot directly dictate prose topology.
