## Brainspotting

I used my finger to direct my gaze slowly around my field of vision to see how different memories felt, or what memories came up, at different angles. That was basically how I figured out very quickly that I could do a simple version myself.

One thing I notice in myself is that looking up-left often feels more like remembering, while up-right feels more like constructing or thinking something through. NLP famously claimed something similar as a general rule. That general rule hasn't held up in controlled research, so I don't use it to read other people's eyes that way. I just pay attention to what my own system seems to do reliably. Gaze and eye movement do interact with memory retrieval more broadly.

I like Brainspotting because it makes it easy to go in and out of the uncomfortable spot—to feather-touch the boundary instead of jumping straight into the deep end. In somatic language that's close to titration and pendulation. It feels more effective to me than just hammering the distress.

And I give it a lot of points for being democratic like EFT. You can experiment with a simple version yourself; you don't need an expensive expert just to try it, although you can work with one too. A formal Brainspotting session adds therapist attunement, focused attention, and bodily awareness around the gaze position.

I wouldn't take that as an invitation to jump into the deepest trauma alone. If you can't tell when you're getting overwhelmed, stay present, or stop when you need to, that's the limit.

Brainspotting makes particular sense to me when I don't have a clean memory to target—diffuse developmental trauma, pre-verbal or body-held material, or trauma that hasn't responded well to purely cognitive approaches. Those are the knots where the body seems to know more than the story. I can feel my way into it without knowing exactly what I'm looking for. That connects naturally with inner-child reparenting too: something younger can show up through the body before I have a neat memory to narrate, and the Nurturer or Protector can join the work when it does. For further reading, see [D’Antoni et al., 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8835026/), a comparative study involving EMDR, Brainspotting, and related methods.

Afterward, if my body still wants gentle discharge, [**Shaking Qigong**](http://shakingclass.innersignalselfhypnosis.com/) can fit. If I feel floaty or far away, I would rather walk and get oriented to ordinary life.
