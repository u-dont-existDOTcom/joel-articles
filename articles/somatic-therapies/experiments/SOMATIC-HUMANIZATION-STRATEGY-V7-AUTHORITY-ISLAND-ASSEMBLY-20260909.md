## Core rule

**Production prose may be assembled only from authority-backed content islands and authority-backed relations.**

Reader curiosity can test whether a transition makes sense. It cannot by itself decide which concept comes next when the surrounding order is model-derived.

The model may not choose:
- which modality comes first;
- which preserved fact should become the next paragraph;
- which two islands should be causally connected;
- a transition merely because it would make the Stage read smoothly.

Those choices require direct owner topology, a natural source realization carrying the same function, or an explicit owner-approved architecture decision.

## Authority islands

Classify each potential Stage-2 contribution as one of:

### A. OWNER_EXACT / OWNER_ACCEPTED
Use intact unless a real publication defect requires minimum cleanup.

### B. OWNER_TOPOLOGY
Direct Joel reasoning establishes the relation/order but not final wording. Use V5 minimum transformation.

### C. NATURAL_OWNER_SAME_FUNCTION
Recovered natural owner prose from prior versions/chats/publication source that independently carries the same Stage-2 function. It may be restored or minimally adapted, subject to provenance and placement checks.

### D. PROTECTED_OBJECT
Exact anecdote, quotation, link/native object, joke, or owner-protected material. Preserve exact/functional authority as registered.

### E. TECHNICAL_EVIDENCE_OBJECT
Dose, mechanism, evidence distinction, safety condition, or source-derived practical parameter. Preserve semantic custody; placement is independent of narrative generation.

### F. MODEL_ONLY_REALIZATION
Current r01/merged prose wrapper or ordering with no independent owner/source topology. It supplies semantic clues only. It cannot determine publication order, transition, or wording.

### G. GAP
A needed relation/placement for which no authority-backed realization exists. Keep explicitly unresolved as `NEEDS_AUTHOR_COGNITION` or `PLACEMENT_UNRESOLVED`.

A gap is preferable to invented connective tissue.

## Stage-2 assembly procedure

### Step 1 — recover authority islands before drafting

Before generating any new Stage-2 prose, search exact repository/history for natural owner same-function realizations, especially material created after model-only Job-2 attempts failed.

For EFT/tapping, the immediate recovery target is the fresh-owner Job-2 reconstruction history that later produced contextual Human regions around tapping/head-massage. Do not assume the current r01 EFT wording is the best available source.

### Step 2 — freeze islands independently of order

Record:
- exact text/source identity;
- source role;
- semantic function;
- certainty/provenance;
- placement authority, if any;
- whether a relation to another island is actually owner/source-backed.

Do not convert the inventory into an outline.

### Step 3 — assemble only supported relations

A pair of islands may be placed consecutively when at least one is true:
- Joel directly supplied the relation/order;
- a natural owner same-function source already realizes the relation;
- an accepted architecture explicitly establishes the sequence;
- the second is mandatory adjacent safety for the first.

Mere topical fit, reader curiosity, current r01 order, or model preference is insufficient.

### Step 4 — minimum transformation only where needed

Use exact natural owner wording when publication-ready.
Use V5 minimum transformation for direct owner topology.
Use technical prose for technical objects without converting them into narrative voice.

Do not regenerate an entire island simply to make neighboring prose more uniform.

### Step 5 — keep unsupported gaps visible

If two valid islands have no supported bridge, do not manufacture one.

Supervisor state may read:
`OWNER ISLAND A -> GAP: placement/relation unresolved -> OWNER ISLAND B`.

Resolve by:
1. further source recovery;
2. explicit owner architecture decision;
3. smallest possible owner cognition request if recovery is exhausted.

### Step 6 — whole-Stage audit after assembly

The complete reader-visible Stage must still pass:
- preservation with zero unexplained substantive deltas;
- section-scale recurrence audit;
- no repeated modality mini-profiles;
- no technical-object sequence masquerading as thought topology;
- no hidden model-selected order justified after the fact as reader flow.
