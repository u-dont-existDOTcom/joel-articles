Trauma-Sensitive / Restorative Yoga

Whether yoga settles you or makes things worse can depend a lot on what kind of yoga you are doing. Keep in mind that power yoga, hot yoga, and so forth are not what I mean here; I mean trauma-sensitive yoga, restorative yoga, gentle somatic yoga, or sometimes very gentle yin. If a teacher tells you to open your hips and release your trauma, that does not necessarily mean the class is trauma-informed. Sometimes the slowest, easiest-to-leave version is the useful one.

With yin I would use props and keep the holds short. One obvious question is whether you still have choices while you are in the pose. Can you keep your eyes open, come out whenever you want, and say that a sensation is useful, neutral, or too much? Can the posture have more than one correct version, depending on your body?

I would avoid intense stretching, forced breathwork or breath retention, aggressive hip openers, long passive holds that you have to endure, and hands-on adjustments without clear consent. Pushing through is not the point of this kind of yoga. You have to retain the choice while you are actually doing it.
