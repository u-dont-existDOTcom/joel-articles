## Owner correction

The live owner position is:

- at the level of what the patient actually does, EMDR does involve deliberate reactivation of traumatic memory and therefore has a defensible exposure component;
- however, EMDR should not be collapsed into CBT/Prolonged Exposure, because its Adaptive Information Processing (AIP) model gives bodily sensations and other memory channels an active role in reprocessing;
- the stronger contrast with Somatic Experiencing is not `EMDR ignores the body / SE uses the body`, but what each model gives primacy to and what kind of change it thinks is occurring;
- both prior Chat formulations need more nuance before changing the owner draft.

## Bounded source check

### Exposure classification

VA PTSD materials support two different levels of description:

- PE is explicitly an exposure therapy.
- VA describes EMDR patients as engaging in imaginal exposure to a trauma memory while bilateral stimulation is used.
- VA/DoD guidelines classify PE, CPT, EMDR and other interventions more broadly as trauma-focused psychotherapies whose primary components may be exposure **and/or** cognitive restructuring.
- CPT does not require a formal exposure component in all current forms; VA notes cognitive therapy alone was effective in a dismantling study and CPT may include a written exposure component.

Therefore:

> `EMDR contains deliberate trauma-memory exposure/activation` is defensible. `PE, CPT and EMDR are all the same kind of exposure therapy` is not.

A technically cleaner article formulation can distinguish `exposure as a therapeutic ingredient or act` from `exposure therapy as a formal treatment family/mechanism`.

### EMDR model

VA and EMDR/AIP sources support that:

- EMDR targets inadequately processed trauma memories;
- assessment includes image, negative cognition, distress, emotional and physical sensations;
- desensitization asks the patient to focus on aspects of the memory during sets of bilateral stimulation;
- new associations, affects, insights and physical sensations are incorporated during processing;
- Phase 6 body scanning checks for residual unpleasant bodily sensations and residual sensations are processed;
- AIP theorizes maladaptively stored memory networks and adaptive integration/reconsolidation.

Important nuance:

- `working-memory taxation` is a leading empirical mechanism hypothesis, not identical to the AIP theory itself; mechanism-of-action research remains unsettled.
- EMDR is therefore not accurately described as merely using the body as a passive distress thermometer.

### Somatic Experiencing contrast

Payne, Levine & Crane-Godreau (2015) explicitly give SE a different center of gravity:

- interoceptive, proprioceptive and kinesthetic awareness;
- subcortical/autonomic/emotional-motor regulation;
- `biological completion` of thwarted defensive responses;
- incomplete survival actions represented as procedural memories;
- completion through imagery and/or subtle movement rather than necessarily full physical reenactment;
- autonomic discharge may accompany completion.

So the useful contrast is approximately:

> **EMDR is organized around reprocessing a target trauma-memory network, with bodily sensation as one integral channel of that network. SE is organized around the present sensorimotor/autonomic organization itself, especially incomplete defensive responses, and treats bodily/process change as the primary route through which the trauma state can reorganize.**

This is a bounded conceptual distinction, not a claim that EMDR is purely top-down or that SE contains no memory/information-processing account.

## Correction to external analysis supplied by Joel

Useful:

- body scan and somatic residuals are integral to standard EMDR;
- the major SE/EMDR difference concerns the theorized process and intervention, not whether the body is noticed;
- SE gives motor/autonomic completion much more direct therapeutic importance.

Overstated or inaccurate if copied literally:

- `SE says the body is not storing information` — inaccurate; the Frontiers paper explicitly discusses implicit/procedural memories of incomplete survival actions.
- `the primary therapeutic agent in EMDR is working-memory taxation` — too settled; working-memory taxation has empirical support but remains one mechanism hypothesis, while AIP is EMDR's guiding clinical theory.
- `SE physically executes the thwarted survival behavior` — too literal/universal; Payne/Levine/Crane-Godreau describe imagery and subtle movement, and say imagined movement can sometimes bring relief.
- `EMDR is just sitting still while BLS happens` — reductive; bodily sensations, spontaneous changes, associations and the body scan are part of the protocol, though EMDR does not normally organize treatment around deliberate motor completion in the SE sense.
