# Introduction

Long after a traumatic event, I can understand perfectly well that I'm safe and still react physically as if I'm not.

With complex or developmental trauma, I usually don't start with the deepest memory. If touching it means I disappear into the experience and can't easily stop or feel like myself again afterward, I work on that before I call the overwhelm “deep processing.”

That may mean regulation, or letting some of the survival activation move through the body instead of trying to think my way out of it. If I'm stable enough already, I may not need any of that and can go straight to EMDR or another trauma-focused method.

The same problem comes up in [inner-child therapy](http://innerchild.u-dont-exist.com/). I can know exactly what a younger part needs and still be too caught in the trauma to give it. If I'm basically in the child-state myself, I need the body to settle enough that I can relate to that younger part instead of just being it.
