## 0. Reasoning selection and phase

### Exploration

Use **interpretive / whole-argument reasoning** first to recover:

- what the reader has already been told anywhere relevant in the article;
- what the current heading promises;
- what source relation is authorized for the local movement;
- what is still genuinely unresolved for the reader.

Use **analytic/formal reasoning** to test contribution, entailment strength, chronology, agency, conditionals, placement, fidelity, and stopping point.

Use **abductive / causal reasoning** only after a literal failure is established, to explain which architecture feature caused it and choose a discriminating repair.

Do not invoke empirical/statistical, systems, dialectical, analogical, or decision frameworks unless a later question actually requires them.

### Design decision

**ADAPT**, do not reinvent.

Keep from the prior map:

- no sentence-by-sentence state transition;
- no preservation inventory as composition outline;
- no next-pressure oracle after every period;
- no target cadence or sentence-length pattern;
- retrospective anti-paraphrase / earned-presence checks;
- cross-boundary subtraction;
- counterfactual placement;
- heading-removal;
- strongest-objection;
- source/fidelity veto;
- acoustic/thought-shape audit.

Repair:

1. hidden reader-question leakage;
2. article-wide repetition missed by immediate-only subtraction;
3. example/marker -> criterion/sufficient-condition escalation;
4. activation -> deliberate-processing chronology drift;
5. matched positive/negative completion;
6. length pressure stretching one natural movement beyond its true stop.

The supervisor may synthesize a reader question for reasoning. The writer must **not** see its wording.

## 2. Reader-state ledger — multi-scale, supervisor only

Do not model only the immediately preceding paragraph.

Before each natural movement, freeze three levels of reader knowledge.

### A. Earlier article-wide load-bearing knowledge

For the current Stage-1 lane this includes, among other things:

- the Introduction has already shown Joel's owner Stage-1 map sentence:
  `Stay present so you can stay with what happens and choose what comes next.`

That sentence is **already-read content**. It is not a sentence template for the Stage-1 body copy.

### B. Immediate upstream knowledge

The accepted trauma-memory/SIBAM section has already established:

- Sensation and Meaning definitions;
- examples such as tension and heat;
- attention can be directed among experience layers;
- meaning/narrative work can coexist with bottom-up work.

These are reader-state facts, not paragraph-filling prompts.

### C. Current section contract

Literal headings:

`# Stage 1: Stay Present and Keep Choice`

`## Somatic Experiencing`

Authorized local source relation for the first movement:

- Somatic Experiencing is an early Stage-1 example when activation outruns deliberate choice.

This is semantic custody, not a required sentence realization.

## 3. Hidden reader deficit — supervisor only

The supervisor may ask internally what the reader still lacks after A + B + C.

For example, the unresolved issue may concern the practical difference between activation merely being present and the person still having voluntary choice about what happens next.

**Do not pass this synthesized question to the writer.**

It is an evaluation target, not prose.

The writer should not be rewarded for repeating the supervisor's wording as a rhetorical question.

## 4. Writer-plane input

The local writer may receive only:

- the literal final accepted paragraph immediately before Stage 1;
- the literal Stage-1 and Somatic Experiencing headings;
- the bounded source relation selected for the current movement, clearly marked as semantic constraint rather than publication wording;
- its own literal prose-so-far.

The writer must not receive:

- the synthesized supervisor reader question;
- Joel's Stage-1 map sentence as a phrase to restate;
- the full Stage-1 preservation inventory;
- named future techniques or obligations;
- a sentence count / word count;
- a target cadence contour;
- a required conclusion or balanced opposite case.

## 5. Generation rule

Write naturally until a genuine movement pause.

Do not ask after each sentence:

- what changed?
- what concept comes next?
- what opposite case should be covered?
- which preservation item remains?
- how can this sentence sound different?

A period is punctuation, not a world-state transition.

A movement may contain several sentences or only one. Its length is an outcome.

## 6. Supervisor gate A — internal earned presence

After a natural movement exists, reduce each sentence to its smallest semantic/pragmatic contribution.

If adjacent sentences collapse to substantially the same contribution, delete/merge unless removing one causes real loss.

Do not replace deleted material merely to restore length.

## 7. Supervisor gate B — multi-scale subtraction

For each surviving contribution ask two questions:

1. What does this add beyond the **immediate preceding section**?
2. What does this add beyond **earlier load-bearing article knowledge**, especially the already-visible owner stage map?

A sentence fails if it merely:

- repeats a SIBAM definition/example;
- repeats that bottom-up and meaning work can coexist;
- paraphrases the Stage-1 map sentence without developing a new relation;
- re-announces the heading promise;
- supplies reassurance against an implication already prevented upstream.

A repeated premise may survive only when it is necessary to establish a genuinely new local relation.

## 8. Supervisor gate C — supervisor-question echo

Compare the candidate with the hidden reader deficit/question.

Flag any sentence whose main function is simply to say the supervisor's internal question aloud.

A direct reader question can survive when it independently performs a real social/pragmatic act and advances the reader. It does not survive merely because the map was phrased as a question.

This gate specifically prevents the architecture from generating its own prompt as prose.

## 9. Supervisor gate D — counterfactual placement

Ask:

**Could this movement be moved into the preceding SIBAM/trauma-memory section with little or no change?**

If yes, presume heading drift.

Also ask:

**Could this movement appear under any generic somatic-therapy heading with little change?**

If yes, the SE / Stage-1 relation may still be too weak.

## 10. Supervisor gate E — heading-removal / editorial-wrapper test

Hide the headings and read the prose.

The prose should still carry its substantive relation.

Then separately test editorial scaffolding such as:

- `I put X here because...`;
- `This belongs in Stage 1 because...`;
- `This is where X fits...`.

If removing the classification wrapper and stating the underlying relation directly loses nothing, treat the wrapper as scaffolding rather than publication prose.

Do not make section-placement explanation the default first-sentence job.

## 11. Supervisor gate F — role / entailment-strength audit

For every substantive relation, label the role actually supported by source/owner authority.

Possible roles include:

- example;
- marker;
- observation;
- support/action;
- option;
- necessary condition;
- sufficient condition;
- criterion/definition;
- causal mechanism;
- expected outcome.

Then inspect the candidate for **role promotion**.

Block transformations such as:

`example -> criterion`

`marker -> sufficient condition`

`option -> requirement`

`association -> mechanism`

`possible consequence -> expected outcome`

unless the stronger relation is explicitly supported.

Current benchmark:

`look around the room` is an authorized Stage-1 orientation action/example. It does **not** by itself authorize:

`if you know you are in the room, you therefore retain enough decision capacity to continue.`

Source authorization of a noun/action is not authorization of every logical role assigned to it.

## 12. Supervisor gate G — whole-movement chronology and agency invariants

Run chronology and agency over the **entire movement**, not only the opening sentence.

For the current lane preserve this invariant:

`activation already happening` ≠ `deliberate trauma processing already underway`.

Therefore block unsupported transitions such as:

- `already reacting` -> `already going into the trauma`;
- `activation is happening` -> `getting farther into the trauma`;
- `reaction is present` -> `continuing memory processing`;

unless the literal context has independently established intentional processing.

Also check actor/action/object and whether modality, person, therapist, attention, or body has silently changed agency.

## 13. Supervisor gate H — conditional antecedent + binary-completion audit

For every `if`, `unless`, `otherwise`, `if not`, positive/negative pair, or exception:

### Antecedent clarity

Identify exactly what condition the clause refers to.

Reject ambiguous constructions where `If you can't` could mean several materially different things.

### Binary-completion test

When prose creates:

`if X -> A / if not X -> B`

or

`positive case / negative case`

ask whether **both branches are independently live for the reader at this boundary**.

Logical availability is not enough.

Do not complete the opposite case merely because it makes the explanation feel comprehensive, balanced, safe, or paragraph-sized.

Matched opposites are especially suspect when the second branch appears only after a request for more length.

## 14. Supervisor gate I — strongest relevant objection / counterexample

For any load-bearing claim, test the strongest relevant counterexample.

Example:

Claim: room awareness is sufficient evidence of retained choice.

Counterexample: someone can know exactly where they are while still lacking meaningful voluntary choice about whether to continue engaging the material.

If a simple counterexample breaks the claim, revise the model, not merely the wording.

Do not invent confidence percentages.

## 15. Supervisor gate J — source / preservation veto

After reader-movement and logic gates:

- verify source/provenance;
- verify actor/agency;
- verify chronology;
- verify certainty;
- prohibit invented symptoms, mechanisms, outcomes, memories, timeframes, and factual micro-details;
- require zero unexplained substantive deltas for promotion eligibility.

A source-grounded term does not rescue an unsupported relation among terms.

## 16. Supervisor gate K — acoustic / thought-shape audit

After semantic compression, inspect:

- equal-weight sentence staircase;
- matched antithesis;
- `problem -> question -> if yes -> if no` completeness;
- `rule -> example -> reassurance` cards;
- neat semantic return/sandwich closure;
- mini-essay landing;
- artificial fragments or long/short alternation added to sound human.

Repair the thought relation, not meter.

## 17. Natural movement boundary versus requested sample length

This is a blocking correction.

**Never equate `the user wants more text` with `the current thought must continue`.**

If the current movement reaches a genuine pause before the requested sample is large enough:

1. freeze the completed movement;
2. do not add the opposite case, caveat, example, terminology, or takeaway merely for length;
3. return to the supervisor plane;
4. recompute reader state using:
   - article-wide knowledge;
   - immediate upstream article;
   - the newly completed movement;
5. identify the next genuinely unresolved reader deficit **without consulting the unsatisfied preservation inventory as an outline**;
6. start a **new natural movement**.

The writer again does not see the synthesized supervisor question.

A paragraph or section sample may contain more than one natural movement. Human cognitive continuity does not require one movement to become exhaustive.

## 18. Multi-movement assembly audit

When two or more natural movements are assembled into one paragraph/section sample, inspect the boundary between them.

Ask:

- Did movement 2 actually arise from the reader state created by movement 1?
- Is there an unnecessary summary/bridge between them?
- Did the second movement begin only because a preservation obligation remained?
- Does the combined span become `setup -> explanation -> exception -> takeaway` even though each movement passed alone?
- Would a paragraph break better reflect the genuine thought transition?

Do not hide movement changes merely to make prose look continuous.

## 19. Preservation bank

The full Stage-1 inventory remains sealed during local generation and reader-state selection.

Only after the natural movement(s) and gates A–K pass:

- map what was genuinely carried;
- reopen the preservation inventory;
- mark remaining functions deferred, not deleted;
- route them into later work;
- do not reopen a completed movement to consume obligations.
