## Owner-directed conceptual direction

- CBT belongs in the article precisely because it is **not** another somatic modality; it is the major comparison case that makes the somatic project intelligible.
- Give CBT genuine credit for reality testing, challenging interpretations, behavioral experiments, changing avoidance, and testing feared predictions.
- Reject both extremes: bodily signals are not automatically infallible truths that should be obeyed, and they are not automatically irrational noise merely because explicit cognition says the environment is safe.
- Strong synthesis: **listen before correcting; then test what needs testing.** This is conceptual source language until separately accepted as exact publication wording.
- The bodily-intelligence thesis is epistemic: do not discard information merely because it arrives somatically rather than propositionally.
- Premature cognitive restructuring can create a gaslighting-like failure mode when the meaning of a bodily reaction is decided before the experience is investigated. Do not make the stronger claim that CBT itself is gaslighting.
- A body reaction may reflect a conditioned false alarm, subtle real-world information not yet conceptualized, or both.
- CBT's institutional fit can be discussed through diagnosable symptoms, measurable functioning, standardization, manualization, and randomized trials without reducing psychiatry to employability or inventing a conspiracy-shaped motive.
- `CBT Dad` remains a candidate joke/device, not owner-final prose.
- Larger thesis candidate: **the body is not infallible; neither is the thinking mind.**

## Therapy-question comparison supplied for consideration

- CBT: Is this thought/belief/fear accurate, and what happens if I behave differently?
- Somatic Experiencing: What is the organism doing underneath the story, and what happens when the interrupted physiological process gets enough space to change?
- TRE: What happens if voluntary control gets out of the way enough for tremoring to organize itself?
- Focusing: Is there implicit bodily knowing here that has not differentiated into words yet?
- Sensorimotor Psychotherapy / Ogden: What action, movement, posture, or protective organization is trying to happen, and what changes when it is experimentally altered?
- 5Rhythms: Which qualities of movement are accessible or habitually avoided?
- Alexander Technique: What habitual/unnecessary effort is being added that might be stopped rather than corrected by doing more?

These are conceptual source-pool formulations, not automatically publication-ready claims or evidence-equivalent recommendations.

## 2026-09-03 owner correction on inadequacy / genealogy

Joel directly rejected the prior Chat's overly narrow evidentiary distinction:

> yes, CBT was inadequate, that's why other therapies were invented otherwise we'd just have CBT

Owner meaning to preserve:

- The article **should say that existing cognitive/verbal/exposure approaches were inadequate as a complete way of understanding and treating trauma, and that this inadequacy is why alternative/somatic approaches were developed.**
- `Inadequate` does **not** mean useless or ineffective. CBT/exposure retain real capabilities and a strong evidence base; the claim is that they did not exhaust the problem.
- For Somatic Experiencing specifically, the 2015 Payne/Levine/Crane-Godreau account is stronger than the Chat previously allowed. It explicitly contrasts SE with primarily cognitive therapy and direct/intense exposure, calls the simple stimulus/response conditioning model inadequate for complex behavior, identifies a drawback in talk therapy for trauma, and describes the relevant regulatory system as relatively unaffected by rational thought. Combined with the account that Levine developed SE from 1977 onward, this supports the article's causal/historical interpretation that SE was developed to address inadequacies in the prevailing cognitive/verbal/exposure paradigm.
- Do **not** require a source to contain the literal sentence `I invented this because CBT failed` before allowing a reasonable causal/historical inference from an explicit problem critique plus a method designed to solve that problem.
- Do not silently broaden this into the claim that every embodied practice in the article was invented solely as an anti-CBT reaction. Keep each modality's actual genealogy distinct where relevant.
- For TRE, Joel supplied a stronger source-pool account that Berceli found conventional individualized cognitive/verbal psychotherapy structurally inadequate for mass trauma/war-zone use and developed a non-verbal, scalable tremor method. Verify exact publication/source support before making detailed historical claims in final publication prose.
