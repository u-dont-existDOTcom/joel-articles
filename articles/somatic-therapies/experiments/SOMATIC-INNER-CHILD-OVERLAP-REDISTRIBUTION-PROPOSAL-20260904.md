## Why this is being proposed

After Joel moved the actual five-stage map into the Introduction and retired `The Five Stages at a Glance`, the r01 section `Somatic Work and Inner-Child Reparenting Can Overlap` was re-read in its new article context.

Its standalone bridge function is now substantially duplicated:

- the current Introduction already says Joel uses somatic therapies partly to make the body safer to inhabit before deeper inner-child work;
- the protected `Don’t be so serious, you silly willy!` / Professor Baby Sheep material already demonstrates a direct somatic ↔ inner-child bridge;
- the five-stage map is now established before either of those sections, so the old first sentence `Inner-child work does not have to wait until Job 5` is also stale because `job` is rejected and Stage 5 is no longer the first legitimate home for inner-child work.

The section nevertheless contains unique protected/practical material that should not be discarded.
