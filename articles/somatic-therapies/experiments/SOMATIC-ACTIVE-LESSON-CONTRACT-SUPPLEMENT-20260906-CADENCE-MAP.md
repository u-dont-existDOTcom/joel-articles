## Two cadence layers

### 1. Acoustic / syntactic cadence

Track, without turning them into a recipe:

- sentence duration;
- clause accumulation;
- short interruptions;
- where periods fall;
- whether a sentence remains open, partially closes, or actually lands;
- rising, suspended, or releasing pressure;
- simple, embedded, accumulating, trailing, or interrupted syntax.

Do not mechanically alternate long and short sentences. A cadence contour is a pressure score, not a metrical template.

### 2. Semantic persistence / cognitive cadence

This is at least as important as sentence rhythm.

A model-shaped passage may vary sentence lengths while still moving through its preservation obligations in a neat sequence:

`modality fit -> mechanism -> terminology -> exception -> safety/caveat -> takeaway`

That is still list-thinking, even when no bullets are visible and one item has been disguised as a sprawling sentence.

For generation, choose the locally live unresolved pressure first. Allow one thought to stay active for an irregular amount of prose. Do **not** advance to the next preservation obligation merely because the current one has now been covered.

A later protected function may be pulled naturally into the live thought. If it is not, stop rather than forcing it into the same paragraph. Its omission from a bounded experiment is not deletion authority; it remains required somewhere in the eventual preserved section.

## Preservation inventory is not a composition outline

The preservation inventory remains blocking for fidelity, but it must be hidden from the actual generative trajectory as much as practicable.

Required order for a bounded reconstruction:

1. Freeze the preservation units and authorized-change boundary.
2. Identify one live pressure/question from the literal current prose and article function.
3. Compose from that pressure and the literal preceding thought rather than walking through the inventory.
4. Stop where the thought genuinely stops, even when later preservation units remain.
5. Only afterward run forward/reverse preservation traceability and route any still-required units into later natural movements.

Do not solve fidelity by turning the inventory into prose architecture.

## Cold-audit failures to block

Reject or repair a candidate when a cold read exposes any of the following, even if sentence lengths look varied:

- repeated miniature `setup -> explanation -> qualification -> landing` units;
- each modality receiving a similarly complete mini-essay;
- tidy `if X / but if Y` balancing created mainly to distribute obligations;
- a checklist hidden inside one long sentence;
- terminology packaged into a balanced explanatory sentence merely because the terms must be covered;
- a final sentence that conveniently carries the remaining modality, caveat, off-ramp, and paragraph landing at once;
- a sentence that explains why the previous sentence mattered rather than making the next necessary move;
- a paragraph that changes conceptual jobs because the outline says the prior job is complete rather than because the thought actually changed.

## Cadence-map representation

A useful private representation can include:

- duration: tiny / short / medium / long / sprawling;
- closure: open / partial / landed;
- relation to previous: continue / deepen / interrupt / exemplify / reverse / detour / return;
- pressure: rise / suspend / release;
- syntax: simple / accumulating / embedded / trailing / interrupted;
- paragraph role: opening pressure / development / detour / consequence / stop.

Example only, not a reusable template:

`M-open -> S-open -> XL-accumulate -> M-detour -> L-deepen -> S-interrupt -> L-return -> stop`

The point is to delay closure where the thought is genuinely still alive. Do not impose this exact contour on later prose.

## Owner correction — conditional aftercare, 2026-09-07

Joel rejected the Stage-1 paragraph:

`If you are stable and have one clear target, you may already be ready for focused trauma work. Capacity-building should not become an endless waiting room where the actual trauma is never treated.`

His diagnosis was more specific than the prior generic `if X / but if Y` rule:

- `If X, Y` itself can sound model-shaped when it turns a nuanced readiness judgment into a clean decision rule;
- the following sentence can worsen the shape when it **explains the prior sentence** rather than moving the thought;
- negative explanatory completion (`and here is what should not happen`) is especially suspect when it merely closes the conceptual space around the prior claim.

Joel replaced the paragraph with:

`To know how ready you are for focused trauma work, ask yourself how stable you are, and whether you need to work on something specific, or a more broad feeling or pattern.`

Durable lesson: do not protect an `If X, Y -> explanation of Y -> statement of what not to do` realization merely because the underlying exception or warning is semantically valid. Preserve the function separately and let the live thought choose its surface and destination.

This does **not** ban conditionals or negative sentences. A conditional can be necessary; a negative can carry real new content. The failure is the interaction when they form a tidy rule-plus-aftercare package.

### Nested enumeration

The SE sentence contains a technical triad and a second procedural triad in the same sentence. The problem is not the literal number three. The sentence serializes several protected terms into one list and then translates them into another list. This is a high-risk shape whenever one coordinated enumeration exists mainly to compact or operationalize another.

Blocking check: if a substantive sentence can be diagrammed as `A / B / C -> X / Y / Z`, deletion-test the packaging aggressively. Do not repair by changing one side to two or four items.

### Compact program shape

The `Borrowing an Adult Perspective` movement can be diagrammed as:

`condition/state -> problem -> remedy -> implementation`.

Plain words and short sentences do not make this human-shaped when every sentence occupies one clean algorithmic slot. Reject the same failure when it appears as `state -> instruction -> operational example` or another compact procedural chain whose completeness is doing more work than the live thought.

### Preservation-unit serialization

The broader causal diagnosis is **preservation-unit serialization**. When the model knows several functions must survive, it tends to emit them as data fields:

- terminology triads;
- action triads;
- recommendation catalogues;
- short rule sequences;
- one paragraph per inventory class.

This can persist after bullet removal, sentence-length variation, and local word changes. Semantic legitimacy does not rescue the topology.

### Owner text is not generator evidence

Joel's direct replacement sentence may improve production prose, but inserting it does not demonstrate that the model's generation method improved.

Future evaluation must separate:

- direct owner text;
- restored source text;
- structural deletion/movement;
- new model-generated surface.

Only the last category directly tests the current generator. Do not use an improved mixed boundary to claim generation success when the improvement came from owner substitution or source restoration.
