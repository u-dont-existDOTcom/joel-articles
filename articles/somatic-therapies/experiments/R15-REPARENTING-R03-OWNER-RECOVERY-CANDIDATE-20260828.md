# Somatic Work and Inner-Child Reparenting

In inner-child therapy, you can know exactly what the younger part needs and still get pulled so far into the child-state that there isn't enough adult perspective left to give it.

In my inner-child work, I call the adult roles the Nurturer and Protector. Sometimes neither one feels available yet. That's when I use what I call borrowed adulthood: I take the position of a steady adult for a while, even if I don't actually feel steady.

A neutral witness counts when no parent image feels safe. The Protector turns warmth into something the child can believe. Hold the boundary. Handle the task. Eat the meal. Keep the promise.

The nice thing about reparenting yourself is that you have time. If the younger part is hurting, I don't have to force my way into it. I can take a few minutes and come back. If it still feels like too much, Pema Chodron's “feather touch” is enough. Touch it lightly, then come back when you're ready.

Gentle [self-hypnosis](http://hypnosis.u-dont-exist.com/) can help too. Gather attention. Listen inwardly. Ask what needs warmth, what needs safety, and what needs action. Then return to ordinary life and do one small real thing. Don't use it here to force trauma memories.

When the body feels tight or there’s too much emotional tension to access the heart chakra, use an unforced slower exhale. With Buteyko-style breathing, the slow exhale should be passive, relaxed, and unforced.

If I can’t feel the love directly, I think of a person or pet I actually love, feel that first, and redirect it.

There’s one very simple technique you can do at any time to feel a deeper connection to your inner child. Place one hand on the center of your chest (heart chakra), to feel a connection to love. Place the other hand on the center of your solar plexus just under your chest (solar plexus chakra) to feel a connection to your inner child. The solar plexus is the most vulnerable part of the torso. One slap there hurts quite a lot, and so it’s natural to feel the vulnerable inner child living in this region. Now imagine sending love from the heart to the inner child, then receiving love from inner child and sending back to heart, in an infinite beautiful loop!
