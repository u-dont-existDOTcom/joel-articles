## Blocking distinction

A failed or exhausted **generation method** does not automatically make the underlying article material a **content blocker**.

When method escalation closes a route, identify exactly what is closed:

- the repeated operation;
- the failure family it keeps reproducing;
- the evidence claim that can no longer be made from another same-method attempt.

Do not infer additional restrictions such as:

- freezing the section;
- forbidding ordinary editing or assembly;
- preventing adjacent material from being added;
- preventing structurally different operations;
- preventing the article from advancing while that prose is still working rather than final.
