## Architectural consequence accepted by continuation

The Jules/MCT paragraph does **not** replace the five-stage therapeutic map and does not create a sixth stage or `Stage 0`.

Instead it adds a standing **off-ramp from therapeutic processing at every stage**:

- the five stages describe kinds/timings of therapeutic work **when therapeutic work is actually helping**;
- at any point, the right move can be to stop processing for now and return attention to ordinary life;
- Stage 1 `Stay Present and Keep Choice` therefore includes choice about whether to continue processing at all;
- Stages 2–4 remain available only when that kind of work is useful;
- Stage 5 `Integrate, Differentiate, and Build a Life` does not imply ordinary living must wait until Stage 5. Ordinary life is available throughout; Stage 5 names more deliberate integration of identity, relationships, values, boundaries, agency, and direction;
- `How I Judge Whether Any of This Is Helping` should explicitly ask whether processing is increasing freedom and life participation or becoming another organizing obsession/rumination loop.
