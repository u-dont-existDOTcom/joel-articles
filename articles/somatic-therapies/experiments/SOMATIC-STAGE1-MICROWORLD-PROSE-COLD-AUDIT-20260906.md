## Literal candidate audited

> Your body can already be well into a reaction before you've decided what you want to do with it. You can pay attention to what is happening in the body without immediately making yourself go deeper, and when you start losing track of where you are, look around the room.

## Verdict

**Reject as current Stage-1 prose.**

The candidate is less overtly architectural than prior attempts, but a fresh cold audit finds credible model-shaped and fidelity defects that the generation-time self-audit missed.

## Cold audit — pass 1: thought movement

The two sentences form a compact `problem -> response` unit.

Sentence 1 is mostly clean: it establishes that bodily activation can precede deliberate choice.

Sentence 2 is not one continuous local move. It compresses at least three separate source functions into one accumulating instruction:

1. attend to bodily experience;
2. do not force deeper processing;
3. orient to the room.

That is a hidden checklist inside one sentence. The coordinating structure (`without ... and when ..., ...`) makes the packing look natural syntactically while still advancing through several obligations semantically.

The candidate therefore repeats the exact failure class the active cadence lesson warns about: multiple preservation functions can remain list-thinking even when they appear inside one long sentence.

## Fidelity / reverse-traceability defect

`when you start losing track of where you are` is stronger and more specific than the current source.

The working Stage-1 source routes room orientation to states such as flooding / child-state identification and uses `look around the room` as a small present-oriented move. It does not require or establish literal loss of awareness of one's location as the condition for orientation.

The candidate therefore introduces an unnecessary implication of disorientation. Treat this as an unexplained substantive delta.

Preservation status for promotion: **FAIL**.

## Heading fit

The passage is under `## Somatic Experiencing`, but nothing in the literal prose distinguishes the method from generic trauma/mindfulness advice. A heading can carry some naming burden, so this is not independently fatal, but the passage has not yet earned a clear modality-specific relation.

Heading fit: **weak / incomplete**.

## Stopping-point failure

The prior generation record treated the second sentence as a natural stop. That was incorrect.

After `look around the room`, the live thought is still active: the reader has been given an orienting action, but the relation between that action and preserving choice has not yet unfolded. The passage does not need a summary sentence, but it also should not stop merely because the locally exposed affordance list has been exhausted.

This is the opposite of overcompletion: **anti-overcompletion caused premature truncation**.

The current Pangram-lab lesson already states that a Pangram-green or shorter truncation can still be intellectually incomplete when a real live question remains. The same principle applies before detector work.

## Microworld-method diagnosis

The microworld mechanism improved one thing: it removed a predetermined multi-step paragraph route.

But it still exposed four named local affordances to the explorer at once:

- activation outruns choice;
- bodily attention;
- non-forcing of deeper processing;
- room orientation.

That is enough to recreate an implicit checklist. The writer packed three of those affordances into sentence 2.

So the environment was still too informative. A real interactive environment does not hand the agent a list of the next valid conceptual actions.

## Provisional method correction

For the next experiment:

1. The world model may retain the full source-grounded affordance set privately.
2. The explorer should **not receive an affordance list**.
3. After each sentence, the world model should return only the changed local state and the strongest unresolved pressure, not named content items to cover.
4. If a source-grounded action becomes locally available, allow it to emerge from the state; do not enumerate alternative actions in advance.
5. After every sentence, reject any sentence that carries more than one conceptual job unless those jobs are inseparable in the underlying causal event.
6. The stop gate must ask not only `is there another allowed move?` but `does an ordinary curious reader still have a live question created by the literal sentence?` If yes, stopping is not yet justified.

This correction remains provisional until owner judgment, but it is more faithful to the intended interactive-world analogy than the prior exposed-affordance design.
