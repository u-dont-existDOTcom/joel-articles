## New failure family — format-level preservation serialization

Making the preservation inventory explicit does not automatically solve model-shapedness.

A candidate can stop disguising an inventory as prose and still serialize it at the **document-format level** through:

- uniform bullet slots;
- clean category decomposition;
- exhaustive coverage;
- neatly curated item order;
- tables or callouts whose visible structure mirrors the preservation bank.

This is **format-level preservation serialization**.

The core question remains the same as with prose serialization:

> Does this document shape exist because the reader genuinely needs that navigation, or because the model has several preservation units to emit?

## Blocking consequences

- Do not treat bullets, tables, callouts, or labeled reference cards as automatic escapes from AI-shaped prose.
- Do not repair a rejected list by changing item count, adding subheads, converting it to a table, or adding decorative prose.
- When the source inventory really must remain visible, preserve its source order and irregularity rather than upgrading it into a cleaner taxonomy than the source supplies.
- Prefer the smallest natural prose nucleus plus rough/minimal source notes when that better matches the material.
- A true safety/reference list may still be legitimate, but its reader-navigation function must independently justify the format.
