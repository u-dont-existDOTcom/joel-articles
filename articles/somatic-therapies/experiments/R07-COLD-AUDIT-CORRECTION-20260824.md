## Cold audit 2 — literal AI-shape diagnosis

The fresh cold read still finds substantial model shape in r07 Job2→end. The main problems are structural/expository, not absence of anecdotes:

1. **Responsible-explainer symmetry.** Several modalities receive the same pattern: define it, explain where it fits, list use cases, add a limit/caveat, then close the unit. The original Goal→Best use→Not ideal cards are gone, but a softer version of the same guide rhythm survives.

2. **Balanced evidence mini-essays.** The Shaking/Qigong passage moves from ordinary mechanisms → energetic explanation → explicit statement that neither proves the other → warning against overuse. Each thought is sensible, but the sequence reads like a model making the treatment maximally defensible rather than one continuous live thought.

3. **Generic aftercare symmetry.** `After Brainspotting` and `After EMDR` are both useful functions, but their parallel presence creates a recursive modality-card rhythm. Practice-specific safety must remain adjacent; generic integration advice may be consolidatable if preservation permits.

4. **Checklist/taxonomy language.** The Job 5 sentence listing beliefs, life story, past-vs-present reality, values, boundaries, agency, inner-child reparenting, identity, differentiation, forgiveness, and life direction is a classic comprehensive model list. The thought is plausible but over-completed.

5. **Repeated first-person wrappers around generic advice.** Phrases such as `I would resist`, `I would not`, `I think of the use cases`, and similar constructions sometimes express real Joel judgment, but elsewhere they function as a first-person skin over generic guide language. First person itself is not humanization.

6. **Recursive caution/closure in the optional high-intensity section.** Specific Sky/Vagal physical warnings are necessary and protected. But physical caution → psychological trap → readiness check → evidence disclaimer creates a neat responsible-guide completion sequence. The section may be continuing past the live thought even while every individual sentence is defensible.

7. **Register switches around owner material.** Strong owner passages are often followed by polished model synthesis. The Brainspotting sequence is a clear example: direct finger/gaze/edging material suddenly becomes formal definition, readiness capacities, use-case taxonomy, citation, and structured aftercare. The contrast itself makes the model scaffold more visible.

8. **Generic technical prose is not automatically wrong.** Some material must remain technical/research-heavy because this is a practical research guide. The repair target is unnecessary packaging, symmetry, recap and overcompletion—not removal of evidence or conversion into autobiography.
