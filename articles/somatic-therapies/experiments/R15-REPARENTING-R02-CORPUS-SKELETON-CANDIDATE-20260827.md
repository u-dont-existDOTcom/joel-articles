# Somatic Work and Inner-Child Reparenting

The child-state is me in a sense, but it isn't the part of me that can do the reparenting. If I'm so flooded that I'm basically the child-state, there may not be much adult perspective left to comfort or protect that child.

In my inner-child work I call those adult functions the Nurturer and Protector. Sometimes I can only find a neutral witness at first. Borrowed adulthood is what I call it when I take the stance of a steady adult even when I can't find that steadiness inside myself yet. Even one ordinary protective action can matter. Gentle [self-hypnosis](http://hypnosis.u-dont-exist.com/) can help too if it stays present-focused instead of pulling me deeply into memories or child dialogue.

Sometimes I need to do something with the body before I can do much useful reparenting. EFT, Somatic Experiencing, trauma-sensitive yoga, gentle shaking or qigong, non-forced longer exhales, and gentle Buteyko-style breathing can relax the body and soften tension around the solar plexus or abdomen. That can make heart chakra love easier to access. I may use them before reparenting or keep using them while I am doing it.

Brainspotting can be part of the reparenting itself. Using it to resource or carefully titrate what comes up is different from opening highly charged developmental material. With diffuse developmental or relational trauma, I'm more inclined to wait on EMDR until I have at least some access to the Nurturer and Protector, so those adult functions can help hold what the memory work opens. A stable person with one discrete event may be ready much earlier. I save deep memory work, immersive child dialogue, deep hypnosis, and suggestive exploration for when I can stay present, stop voluntarily, and come back afterward.

Once the body softens even a little, I don't have to use that opening to go looking for pain. I can bring to mind a pet, a baby, a friend, a partner, or anyone I naturally love. Feel the real love that's already there. See whether a little of it can move from the heart toward the younger part around the solar plexus and then back up in a loop.
