Exact owner text supplied in Chat:

[Jules Evans’ post on How to Stop Thinking](https://www.ecstaticintegration.org/p/how-to-think-less) is particularly provoking, as he details the stories of folks who tried all kinds of therapies to stop ruminating, and eventually found that the best therapy was to stop imagining they needed therapy. Go to the gym, do regular real life stuff, just chill. Ironically, as Jules showcases, this advice has a formally studied therapy behind it, called Metacognitive Threapy (MCT), where you simply notice the thoughts without worrying about what they’re saying (based on Buddhist beginner’s meditation). On the other hand, many people actually do need to analyze their issues, but they’re avoiding doing that because it’s painful.
So the answer is basically the same regardless of what your problem is in life: pay attention to what works and what doesn’t, without making that process itself into an endless spiral.
