## Causal correction — owner input is not itself the discriminating variable

The aquatic Human/medium result and bare-topic night-walk AI/high result differ on many variables at once. The aquatic passage had direct owner cognition, Hale as a named source, unusual concrete details, an externally supplied order of attention, asymmetric weighting, and a model realization constrained by that route. The night-walk passage had none of those constraints.

Therefore `owner input -> Human` is too coarse to treat as causal.

Important negative controls already exist:

1. `u-dont-existDOTcom/pangram-humanization-lab/cases/somatic-intro-owner-human-vs-model-ai-low-20260827.md` records three model attempts made after inspecting Human owner exemplars. Those attempts contained concrete trauma content, Pema Chödrön, Peter Levine, pendulation, inner-child material, colloquial phrasing, and selective explanation, yet Joel reported all three AI / low confidence. Named humans, specificity, colloquiality, and imitation of visible Human features were therefore not sufficient.
2. `SOMATIC-STAGE1-TRIAD-PROGRAM-SHAPE-OWNER-CORRECTION-AUDIT-20260907.md` shows owner-supplied semantic functions can still be serialized into nested lists, compact programs, and modality cards when the model controls the prose architecture. Owner content alone was not sufficient.
3. The rejected massage/aquatic candidate had unusually specific owner material but the model reorganized that one irregular thought into modality/evidence/safety/recommendation cards and produced AI-shaped prose by owner judgment.
4. The later aquatic candidate preserved the owner's original connected order and weighting and was owner-reported Human / medium for the full exact boundary.

Current best-supported production hypothesis:

> The useful discriminant is not simply `human ideas versus model ideas`; it is the degree to which an externally grounded authorial thought route constrains the model's ability to replace that route with a complete model-generated conceptual allocation.

This remains a hypothesis, not a Pangram mechanism.

## Production humanization router

Before generating, assign the natural span to the first matching route below. Do not apply a later, more generative route merely because it is more elaborate.

### Route 0 — owner-final / owner-accepted / known-green exact boundary

Examples include the accepted trauma-memory section, exact owner five-stage map, exact aquatic Human/medium candidate, and the compact Somatic Experiencing Human/low boundary within its tested exact context.

Operation:
- preserve for detector reasons;
- edit only for a named real editorial/factual/authority defect;
- detector/style intuition alone is not reopening authority;
- any changed exact boundary loses automatic transfer of the prior detector result.

### Route 1 — direct owner thought topology exists

The source contains a recognizable sequence of attention, causality, surprise, weighting, judgment, or practical consequence.

Operation:
1. recover the owner movement before editorial categories;
2. identify what is central versus incidental;
3. identify where the thought actually stops;
4. realize the minimum reader-facing surface while preserving actor/provenance;
5. reconcile preservation/evidence afterward;
6. bank unconsumed functions instead of forcing subsection completeness.

Aquatic bodywork is the current positive example.

### Route 2 — no direct owner topology, but a source-grounded live relation exists

There may be research, practitioner evidence, or current article context that establishes one real relation, but not enough owner cognition for a full subsection.

Operation:
- realize only the bounded relation earned by the source and article context;
- keep attribution explicit;
- use evidence to support the relation locally;
- do not invent Joel's personal reaction or judgment;
- do not make the remaining source bank into paragraph jobs.

The bounded Stage-2 Hale/massage sentence is a current example of this route and remains non-authoritative pending Joel judgment.

### Route 3 — required function exists, but authorial cognition is genuinely missing

The article must preserve or address a function, but the current source pool supplies only semantic obligations or generic model prose and no adequate authorial route.

**Do not free-write a complete replacement.** The critic has repeatedly outperformed the generator under this condition.

Operation:
1. search the current article/source pool first so Joel is not asked to repeat material already available;
2. identify the single highest-information missing authorial variable;
3. ask one irreducible question aimed at cognition, not wording;
4. preserve the answer as source-pool material;
5. realize it using Route 1.

High-information owner questions target one of:
- what actually bothers or interests Joel here;
- which fact/function matters more and why;
- what he thinks the common framing gets wrong;
- what causal relation he believes;
- what he is uncertain about;
- what he would actually tell a reader to notice or do;
- what can be omitted without losing his point;
- where he thinks the thought stops.

Do **not** ask for an anecdote merely to add human texture. Fresh owner cognition is not synonymous with fresh autobiography, consistent with `HUMANIZATION-COLD-AUDIT-GATE.md`.

### Route 4 — intrinsically technical / safety / evidence / navigation material

Some prose is properly technical, list-like, or procedural because the content itself is finite and operational: contraindications, safety requirements, an external model's actual finite components, navigation, or literal steps.

Operation:
- preserve the appropriate genre;
- improve only unnecessary packaging around it;
- do not convert a legitimate warning/list into faux personal prose to make it look Human;
- separate necessary finite structure from model-created completeness.

A real externally defined list such as SIBAM is not equivalent to a model inventing a three-part list to consume preservation units.

### Route 5 — inherited model scaffold with no unique function

The span is a bridge, recap, significance announcement, caveat wrapper, heading-created filler, or other model realization that adds no unique authorized proposition/function.

Operation:
- do not humanize empty scaffold;
- propose deletion/consolidation under the preservation/change-control rules;
- prove every genuine function survives elsewhere;
- do not call something redundant merely because it is inferable.

## Owner-cognition escalation protocol

Use only after Route 3 is established.

### Step A — neutral source recovery

Search from literal current anchors. Do not presume the existing model paragraph correctly describes Joel's intended thought.

### Step B — identify the missing variable

Write one private sentence:

`The article already establishes <X>; what is missing is Joel's <judgment / causal relation / priority / uncertainty / distinction / desired reader action / stopping point>.`

If that sentence cannot be made precise, more source inspection is needed before asking Joel.

### Step C — ask one high-information question

Prefer questions whose answer discriminates among materially different possible passages.

Bad:
`Can you tell me more about massage?`

Better, when source inspection supports the ambiguity:
`Apart from Hale using massage after water, what is the actual reason you want massage in Stage 2—does it do something important for you there, or do you mainly want readers to know it is another settling/bodywork option?`

The exact question must be generated from the live source; the example is not a template.

### Step D — do not over-interview

One answer should normally produce one bounded thought movement. Ask another question only if a genuinely independent unresolved authorial decision remains.

### Step E — realization

Use the answer's thought topology as composition evidence, not necessarily its conversational wording. Preserve direct phrases when they are owner-final/locked, identity-bearing, semantically superior, or otherwise justified.

## Production algorithm

For each unresolved natural boundary:

1. recover authority and exact neighboring context;
2. classify Route 0–5;
3. freeze preservation units and authorized delta at the smallest useful scope;
4. if Route 3, recover one owner cognition before prose;
5. generate only the bounded movement actually earned;
6. run provenance/actor/certainty/source integrity;
7. run thought-topology and cadence audit;
8. run same-context cold audit and mark it provisional when saturated;
9. obtain genuinely fresh final-reader diagnosis when required by the repository gate;
10. keep Pangram uncalled unless Joel explicitly requests it;
11. update section provenance/omission/carry-forward state.

## Strategy-switch rule

Do not add another anti-pattern to the writer merely because a Route-1/2 realization fails.

If a failure recurs, diagnose which stage failed:

- **wrong/missing thought route** -> Route 3 owner-cognition recovery;
- **good thought route, bad model realization** -> minimum-dose re-realization or learned editor fallback;
- **good candidate but selector/routing chose wrong source function** -> routing/selection repair;
- **technical content looks list-like only because it is genuinely finite** -> preserve genre rather than humanizing it away;
- **empty scaffold** -> delete/consolidate instead of rewriting;
- **known-green exact boundary** -> leave it alone absent a real editorial defect.

If the same realization failure persists after the thought route is externally supplied, the prompting architecture appears exhausted for that failure class. Switch to the revision-trajectory learning fallback rather than accumulating more prose prohibitions.
