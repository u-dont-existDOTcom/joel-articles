## Research correction — SE already includes shaking/discharge

Primary source checked fresh:

Payne P, Levine PA, Crane-Godreau MA. *Somatic experiencing: using interoception and proprioception as core elements of trauma therapy.* Front Psychol. 2015;6:93. doi:10.3389/fpsyg.2015.00093.

Source-verified distinction:

- SE explicitly includes biological completion and autonomic discharge;
- spontaneous trembling/shaking can arise during SE and may be supported therapeutically;
- SE follows visceral sensations and subtle motor impulses associated with incomplete defensive responses;
- the authors explicitly state that “not just any muscular activity will do” in their clinical model and emphasize the specific interrupted defensive movement;
- deliberate shaking methods such as TRE/Shaking Medicine therefore overlap with one SE mechanism but should not be described as identical to SE.

A 2022 characterization of spontaneous movement practices similarly distinguishes TRE, where exercises intentionally elicit tremors, from SE, where spontaneous shaking/trembling may arise during therapy.

## Animal-evidence boundary

Levine’s animal-discharge account is an attributed theoretical/naturalistic observation, not settled comparative physiology.

The 2015 SE paper itself says the wild-animal observations of movement, breathing changes, yawning, shaking, and trembling were based on Levine’s naturalistic experience and anecdotal reports from animal professionals and that the authors could not find significant peer-reviewed treatment of the phenomena.

Do not upgrade that claim in article prose.

## Architecture correction

The five stages are **functional jobs rather than mutually exclusive modality silos**.

SE may perform:

- Stage 1: interoception, orientation, titration, pendulation, present-time capacity/choice;
- Stage 2: biological completion/autonomic discharge, sometimes including spontaneous trembling or specific motor completion.

Therefore do not write:

`SE finds the energy -> shaking releases what SE cannot`.

Current owner route is closer to:

`SE may help someone feel/track activation and stay with it -> SE may itself discharge/complete -> deliberate shaking is a Stage-2-biased option when a more active/sustained discharge practice seems useful or when subtler SE work is not getting much moving.`
