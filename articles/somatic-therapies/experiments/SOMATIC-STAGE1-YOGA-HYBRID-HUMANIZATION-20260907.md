## Candidate

## Trauma-Sensitive / Restorative Yoga

I like yoga here partly because the interoception has somewhere to go. How does this feel when I move, and can it help me relax better, sit straighter, meditate better, get in shape at the same time?

For this kind of work I want the slow, choice-based versions. Trauma-sensitive or restorative yoga make the most sense to me. Gentle somatic yoga can work too, and even yin if it is propped, short, and easy to leave. No power yoga. No hot yoga. Definitely nobody announcing, “Open your hips and release your trauma.”

The teacher's language matters. “You might try…” is different from “stay there.” You can keep your eyes open. Come out whenever you want. Notice whether the pose feels useful, neutral, or too much. A posture can have several valid versions; choose one that actually fits your body.

I would not use this as an excuse for intense stretching or long passive holds. I would skip forced breathwork and breath retention here too, and hands-on adjustments need clear consent. Same with aggressive hip opening or any instruction to push through sensation.
