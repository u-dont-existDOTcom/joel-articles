Trauma-Sensitive / Restorative Yoga

When I say yoga here, I do not mean power yoga, hot yoga, or somebody announcing, “Open your hips and release your trauma.” I mean trauma-sensitive yoga, restorative yoga, gentle somatic yoga, or very gentle yin.

With yin I would use props and keep the holds short. Can I keep my eyes open? Can I come out whenever I want? Is the sensation useful, neutral, or too much? A posture can have more than one correct version; choose one that fits your body.

I would avoid intense stretching, forced breathwork, breath retention, aggressive hip openers, long passive holds that you have to endure, and hands-on adjustments without clear consent. Pushing through is not the point.
