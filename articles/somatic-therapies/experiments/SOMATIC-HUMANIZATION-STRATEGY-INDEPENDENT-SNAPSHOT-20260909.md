# Somatic humanization strategy — independent conception snapshot — 2026-09-09

Status: **PRE-LITERATURE STRATEGY SNAPSHOT / NOT ARTICLE PROSE / NOT PROMOTED LESSON**

Purpose: preserve the independent conception before checking established work, so later convergence with literature can be distinguished from ideas derived from it.

## Current evidence

1. Repeated anti-pattern accumulation and same-context self-critique have materially improved diagnosis but have not demonstrated general independent human-shaped generation.
2. Owner-thought topology/source weighting materially improved realization at the exact aquatic boundary; Joel reported the full candidate Human / medium confidence.
3. The separate bare-topic night-walk probe required the model to originate both thought and prose; Joel reported it high-confidence AI.
4. Therefore production success and independent-generator success must remain separate ledgers.

## Working causal hypothesis

The main remaining failure is upstream of wording. When the model is asked to originate a complete passage from a topic or preservation inventory, it tends to decide too much of the conceptual route before writing: it allocates coverage, balances subpoints, assigns paragraph jobs, and then realizes that latent plan. Local rules alter the realization but leave the globally pre-completed cognitive architecture intact.

The aquatic success reduced this freedom because the owner had already supplied an irregular causal route and unequal weighting. The model's job was mostly realization rather than topic decomposition.

## Proposed strategy switch

Stop treating the next advance as another rule-augmented rewrite method. Separate **thought origination**, **route selection**, **local realization**, and **fidelity reconciliation**.

### Production lane — owner-thought realization

Use the richest relevant owner cognition/source material first. Recover one live thought route with causal order and weighting. Give the writer only the local route needed for the current natural boundary, not the complete preservation inventory. Realize that thought; afterward reconcile against the preservation/source ledger. Do not force unused obligations into the same paragraph.

### Research lane — independent generation

Test a materially different architecture:

1. In genuinely fresh contexts, generate several **small thought seeds**, each containing one observation, tension, causal relation, or practical curiosity rather than a section outline.
2. Do not ask those generators to write publication prose, cover the topic, be balanced, or satisfy a checklist.
3. Use a separate selector to choose one seed based on reality contact, specificity of causal relation, unresolved pressure, and whether it changes what a reader would actually notice—not based on Pangram or Joel-surface mimicry.
4. Give a fresh writer only that seed plus the minimal literal local context. Let it realize one bounded thought and stop before topic completion.
5. If another thought is needed, recompute from the literal new endpoint in another fresh context rather than revealing the whole destination in advance.
6. Run preservation/research reconciliation only after the local realization exists.
7. Run a genuinely separate cold reader on the natural boundary. If the same structural failure recurs, change the thought-generation/selection architecture rather than adding another prose prohibition.

## Why this differs from earlier attempts

- Not `outline -> prose`.
- Not `preservation inventory -> prose`.
- Not same-context writer/critic role-play.
- Not owner-style feature imitation.
- Not a positive thought-shape template applied to a full section.
- Not local paraphrase after a detector or critic failure.
- Not a human-text spine or source-prose transplant.

The intended intervention is to reduce **premature global conceptual commitment** and preserve local discovery/continuation.

## Cheapest discriminating tests

A. **Bare-topic independent test:** same class as the night-walk probe. Compare direct one-shot generation against fresh-context seed -> selector -> local realization. The content must be independently originated in both conditions.

B. **Owner-fed production test:** take an unresolved Somatic span with real owner thought topology and compare direct checklist-aware realization against topology-local realization. This tests production utility but is not evidence about independent origination.

C. **Failure localization:** judge thought architecture before prose surface. If the seed itself is generic/comprehensive/model-shaped, do not spend effort humanizing its sentences.

## Current recommendation before literature check

For production, continue owner-thought realization and minimum-dose assembly. For generator research, stop adding anti-pattern rules and test the fresh-context incremental thought-seed architecture. Keep the two result ledgers separate.
