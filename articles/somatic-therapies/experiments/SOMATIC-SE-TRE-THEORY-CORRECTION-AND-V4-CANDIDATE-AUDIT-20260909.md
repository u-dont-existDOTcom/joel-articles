## 1. Research correction

### SE does not reject shaking/tremoring

The 2015 Payne / Levine / Crane-Godreau SE paper explicitly treats spontaneous gentle shaking and trembling as possible forms of autonomic discharge. It also describes SE as facilitating discharge/regulation of excess autonomic arousal and completion of thwarted defensive responses.

Levine/Ergos and Somatic Experiencing International likewise describe spontaneous shaking/trembling as part of the release of survival activation.

### `Not just any muscular activity will do` was overinterpreted

The same 2015 paper says, from SE clinical experience, that profound shifts may occur when activity corresponds to the specific defensive movement interrupted in the precipitating event, and contrasts that with ordinary voluntary vigorous exercise.

That statement does **not** establish any of the following:

- generalized tremoring is inappropriate;
- spontaneous or induced tremoring has no discharge value;
- TRE is incompatible with SE;
- TRE is equivalent to ordinary voluntary vigorous exercise;
- every therapeutic discharge must reproduce one trauma-specific motor action.

The correct distinction is additive rather than oppositional:

1. SE recognizes shaking/trembling as one possible discharge process.
2. SE also proposes that, for some trauma material, completion of a specific interrupted defensive motor action may be especially important.

Those claims can coexist.

### TRE relation

TRE intentionally uses exercises to evoke a largely involuntary/neurogenic tremoring response. David Berceli's own TRE literature explicitly cites Levine as a major precursor in the theory of therapeutic tremoring.

Therefore TRE and SE have substantial conceptual overlap around tremor/discharge, while remaining different methods:

- SE is a broader clinical framework using interoception, proprioception, titration, orienting, pendulation, spontaneous discharge, and completion of defensive responses;
- TRE deliberately elicits tremoring through a standardized exercise process.

This supports **overlap / possible complementarity**, not incompatibility and not identity.

### What does Levine himself think of TRE?

Current bounded web research did **not** recover a primary-source interview, article, book passage, or other direct statement in which Levine explicitly evaluates David Berceli's TRE method.

TRE Global's official homepage places a Peter Levine quotation among its expert/testimonial material. The quotation says that, with appropriate guidance, humans can shake off overwhelming events in ways comparable to animals. But that wording is independently traceable to Levine's own *Healing Trauma* discussion and does not itself name TRE.

A 2025 French publisher markets Berceli's TRE book as recognized by Peter Levine, but this is publisher/marketing evidence rather than a primary Levine statement.

Disposition for article claims:

- **Allowed:** TRE's tremoring premise is strongly aligned with part of Levine's SE discharge model.
- **Allowed:** TRE/Berceli literature draws explicitly on Levine's shaking/discharge theory.
- **Not currently justified:** `Levine endorses TRE`, `Levine recommends TRE`, or any stronger direct-opinion claim.
