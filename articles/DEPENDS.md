Each row is one pair: a passage where a point has its home, and a passage in another place that takes from it. When the words on either side change, `depends.py check` names the other side to re-read. In each table "Here" is the side in the post named above the table and "There" is the other side, with its post's short name (the one in brackets in the headings).

- **Relation.** *same words*: the passage repeats the home's words. *own words*: it says the same in other words. *pointer*: it sends the reader to the home. *differs*: it says something else. *link*: a link to that section.
- **State.** *in line*: nothing to do. *behind*: the home says something this passage has not taken in yet. *his to settle*: the two differ and only Joel can say which stands. *left as it is*: the passage stays, with at most a link to the home. For a link, the note on where the link stands.
