Yes, I think part of the problem is that the Buddha (or the people writing his words down) stored the pieces of the puzzle in different drawers. For instance, he talks about the importance of direct sensations in Ud 1.10, but explains how contact creates “the world” in a chain in SN 12.44. He talks about how consciousness and mind/body are like two bundles of reeds that lean against each other (Jenga!) in SN 12.67. He describes the chain in more detail in DN 15. He talks about going through the formless unity states and beyond unity in MN 140. If one starts with consciousness, as in Advaita, or matter, as in materialism, it won’t work. If one sees how they mutually sustain each other, it makes more sense. I try to put the pieces back in the puzzle box in my research report. 😛
