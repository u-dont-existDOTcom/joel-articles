This article is version 2.0 of the original one-pager that was called Already Perfect. Yes, it was already perfect but I had to write it this way anyway. Not because of “free will” but because my boss, Oliver, told me to! 🤪 In other words, already perfect describes the condition in which everything is already perfect without me. If a tree falls in the woods and no one is around to hear it does it make a sound? If no one is around to color in the coloring book does the color still color the coloring book? Well, I don’t know but read on and perhaps you will get a sense of what my experience is.
