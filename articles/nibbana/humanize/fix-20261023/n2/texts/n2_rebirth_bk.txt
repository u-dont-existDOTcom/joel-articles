Stream entry’s greatest prize is a “release date”: AN 3.88 allows at most seven more lives before final awakening. There is actually quite a lot of modern academic evidence for rebirth. But who’s going around getting reborn if no permanent self exists? Buddhists don’t imagine any permanent self-essence/soul moving between bodies. Instead, rebirth is the causal continuation of one life into the next, sometimes referred to as the mind-stream. In brief, craving and clinging lead to becoming, which leads to birth. More like a driverless car. The later Buddhist philosopher Milindapañha compared rebirth to one lamp being used to lighting another.
