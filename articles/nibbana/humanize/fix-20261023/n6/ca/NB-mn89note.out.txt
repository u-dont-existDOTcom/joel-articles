But it's not like Soh says, 24/7 bliss non stop, because that is obviously false. From what the suttas say, it seems that ease is something that the noble disciple can reach through recollection (AN 11.12) not something that he has to remain in all the time. Also remember this quote from the Buddha in MN 75 where he says that what normal people perceive as sensual pleasure, he feels as pain.
