I’m using awareness and consciousness to mean two different things. Awareness would be when something appears without reflection. Seeing blue is just that, blue. But consciousness would be when something appears and then reflects on itself. Seeing blue and thinking, “I’m blue!” In the pali there are not two different nouns to describe these two different things. But in DN 11, MN49 the pali speaks of a viññånaü anidassanaü anantaü sabbato pabhåü (“Awareness that is non-manifesting, without end, and luminous all around”). It is in this awareness that the elements and namrupa (“mind and body”, not “name and form”) do not find footing. It is in contrast to the usual type of viññånaü (“sense consciousness”) that is established when one thing perceives another.
