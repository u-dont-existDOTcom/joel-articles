I think there used to be a quote from the Buddha along the lines of life is like a dream that I used to love, but I just found out that it is actually from the Diamond Sutra, which is much later Mahayana scripture putting words in the Buddha's mouth. However, the following passages from the early Pali text show similar symbolism: SN 22.95 Form is empty, ever transforming, just like a foam... Feelings are fleeting, just like a bubble... Perception is just a mirage... and consciousness is the best magic trick Dhammapada 170 See this world like a bubble, like a mirage. So I think it is safe to assume that he intended this.
