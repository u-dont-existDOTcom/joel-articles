Just to clarify, when I speak of Awareness and consciousness I’m speaking of Awareness as non-reflective appearances appearing as they are, i.e. Blue appears blue. And consciousness as the next step, i.e. reflecting upon that appearance, “I’m blue!” Pāli doesn’t have the nouns to distinguish the two, hence it speaks in DN 11 & MN 49 of viññāṇaṃ anidassanaṃ anantaṃ sabbato pabhaṃ (non-manifesting awareness, without end, and luminous all around). It is in this awareness where the elements and namaṃ & rūpaṃ (mind & body, mis-translated as name & form) will find no footing, which is different from the viññāṇaṃ one is established with when perceiving objects with the senses.
