I thought this quote was directly from "Buddha" himself but it appears that this comes from a much later Mahayana text that puts words in Buddha's mouth, namely The Diamond Sutra . However, there are some similar symbolic sayings that come from the early Pali texts: All form...is empty, constantly transforming, just like a foam, All feeling...fleeting, just like a bubble, All perception...only a mirage SN 22.95 All consciousness...is basically the best magic trick See the world as a bubble. See it as a mirage. Dhammapada 170 So I think it was intended.
