For my purposes, there is a difference between awareness and consciousness. Awareness is when blue appears, simply appearing, without reflection. Consciousness is when blue appears, and then it appears that it appeared, or, “I’m blue!” The Pali doesn’t seem to have words for each of these, but it does speak in several places (DN 11 and MN 49 to name two) about a non-manifesting (anidassanaṃ), limitless (anantaṃ), all-luminous (sabbato pabhaṃ) awareness (viññāṇaṃ) where the elements and name & form (nāmarūpa, mistranslated) do not find footing. This is distinguished from ordinary sense-consciousness (also viññāṇaṃ) which is established based on perception of something.
