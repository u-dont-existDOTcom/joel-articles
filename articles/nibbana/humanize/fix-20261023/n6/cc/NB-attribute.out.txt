I used to think he said it, but it seems that it's from the Diamond Sutra, which is quite a bit later and puts words in the Buddha's mouth. However, there is some similar symbolism in the earlier Pali texts. In SN 22.95 , he talks about form being empty and ever-changing (like foam), feeling being fleeting (like a bubble), perception being merely a mirage, and consciousness being basically a magic trick. In Dhp 170 , he says to see the world as a bubble and a mirage. I think it's safe to imagine that he intended something similar to "life is like a dream".
