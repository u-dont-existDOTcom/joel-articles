The closest thing I can compare it to is the out-of-body part of some near-death experiences. People sometimes report seeing things from impossible angles, and some of those reports (controversially) were verified by others in the room. My room event had nothing like that to check, though, since I wasn’t floating around noticing any details. So those cases can’t back mine up. They just help me show what I mean by an experience that wasn’t stuck at the body’s vantage point anymore. If you want to go down that rabbit hole, there’s this systematic analysis of near-death reports, the AWARE-II cardiac-arrest study, and a recent veridical-NDE evidence scale.
