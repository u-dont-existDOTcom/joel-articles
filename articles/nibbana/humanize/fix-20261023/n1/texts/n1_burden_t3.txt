In SN 22.22 the five aggregates are the burden and craving is what picks it up, so the Buddha splits the accounting cleanly. The tangle’s heavy by nature (even an arahant feels the first dart), and craving’s what keeps us carrying it. And binding is literally heavy in physics too (quarks supply less than 1% of a proton’s mass, roughly 99% comes from gluon-binding energy). Matter weighs what its binding weighs. Light weighs nothing.
