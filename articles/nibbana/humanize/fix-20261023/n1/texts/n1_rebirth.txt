Stream entry’s greatest prize is a “release date”: AN 3.88  allows at most seven more lives before final awakening. There is actually quite a lot of modern academic evidence for rebirth. But who’s going around getting reborn if no permanent self exists? Buddhists don’t imagine any permanent self-essence/soul moving between bodies. What continues is a chain of causes with no traveler riding along, often called a mind-stream: craving and clinging condition becoming, and becoming becomes birth. More like a driverless car. The later Buddhist philosopher Milindapañha compared rebirth to one lamp being used to lighting another.
