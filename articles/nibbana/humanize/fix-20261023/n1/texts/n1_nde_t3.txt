The closest comparison that’s worth mentioning is the out-of-body perception in some near-death experiences. People sometimes report seeing things from impossible angles, and a controversial subset of those reports were verified by others in the room (see this systematic analysis of near-death reports, the AWARE-II cardiac-arrest study, and a recent veridical-NDE evidence scale). Nobody could check anything like that in my room event, since I wasn’t floating around noticing specific details, so those reports don’t prove anything about mine. They might still help explain what I mean by an experience that wasn’t limited by the body’s vantage point anymore.
