This distinction isn’t laid out explicitly in the suttas. AN 11.12 says a noble disciple can reach ease and joy through recollecting the Buddha, Dhamma, & Saṅgha – the community of realized disciples – or recollecting ethical conduct, generosity, or qualities shared with heavenly beings. In my experience there can also be physical loosening of unification. My percentages are just a playful way of saying how much binding has dissolved. Around 1%, a person can still do everything fine. Around 5%, they can hardly do anything. (And partial untying goes beyond that range too.)
