Science only gets to circle the edges of consciousness, though it finds some wild stuff out there: people functioning with no felt controller, several things cognized at once, split-brain patients who can seem like two knowers sharing a skull, and one recent theory that models minds as nested parallel observers. The full nerdy tour is in the research report. Science gives my argument a third wheel, making it safer to ride, but it will never show nibbāna itself – self and sense organs unweaving into self-presenting light flows of bliss. That requires meditation.
