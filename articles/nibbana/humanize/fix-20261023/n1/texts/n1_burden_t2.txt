In SN 22.22 the Buddha puts the weight in one place and the carrying in another. The five aggregates are the burden, and craving is what picks it up. The tangle is heavy all by itself (even an arahant still feels the first dart), and craving is why we keep lugging it around. Physics backs this up, and I mean literally: quarks supply less than 1% of a proton’s mass, and roughly 99% comes from gluon-binding energy. Matter weighs what its binding weighs. Light weighs nothing.
