The closest comparison that’s worth mentioning is the out-of-body perception in some near-death experiences. People sometimes report seeing things from impossible angles, and a controversial subset of those reports were verified by others in the room. Those cases had a target that other people could check, and my room experience didn’t, since I wasn’t floating around noticing specific details. So they can’t vouch for my event, but they help show what I mean by an experience that wasn’t stuck at the body’s vantage point anymore. If you want to dig in, there’s this systematic analysis of near-death reports, the AWARE-II cardiac-arrest study, and a recent veridical-NDE evidence scale.
