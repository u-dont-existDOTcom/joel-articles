This distinction isn’t laid out explicitly in the suttas. AN 11.12 says a noble disciple can reach ease and joy through recollecting the Buddha, Dhamma, & Saṅgha – the community of realized disciples – or recollecting ethical conduct, generosity, or qualities shared with heavenly beings. In my experience there can also be physical loosening of unification. My percentages are just playful shorthand for how much binding has dissolved, and partial untying isn’t limited to the range I give: at around 1% a person can still do everything fine, and by roughly 5% they can barely function.
