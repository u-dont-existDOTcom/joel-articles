I used to attribute “life is like a dream” directly to the historical Buddha. The famous stanza I loved is actually from the later Mahāyāna Diamond Sutra, which puts some words in his mouth. The early Pāli texts were already reaching for the same kind of pictures, though. In SN 22.95 form is like foam, feeling is like a bubble, perception is a mirage and consciousness is a magic trick, and Dhammapada 170 tells us to look at the world like a bubble, or a mirage.
