I used to attribute “life is like a dream” directly to the historical Buddha, until I found out the famous stanza I loved comes from the later Mahāyāna Diamond Sutra, which puts some words in his mouth. The early Pāli texts do have closely related images (SN 22.95 compares form to foam, feeling to a bubble, perception to a mirage and consciousness to a magic trick, and Dhammapada 170 asks us to see the world as a bubble and mirage).
