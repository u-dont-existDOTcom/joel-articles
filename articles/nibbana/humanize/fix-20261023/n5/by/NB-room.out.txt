There was a sense of the awareness as being gone, and in a sense, me being gone, and the flows of colors in the room were still experiencing themselves in an indescribable perfect way. They were disjoint, but not isolated, as they flowed into and through each other, connected in all places, without being the same experience, or even in the same ‘scene’, as if there were no one even to ‘see’ the scene. There wasn’t even a ‘cosmic knower’ that was ‘knowing’ all of this, and rejoicing over this new found thing. It wasn’t even a black void either, it was more like these flows of light, infinitely more intimate and beautiful and alive than the ‘flows of light’ that I normally perceive, but with no one to own them.
