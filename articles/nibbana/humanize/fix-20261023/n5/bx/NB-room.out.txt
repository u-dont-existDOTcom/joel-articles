It went completely. It's kind of hard to explain but all awareness of anything as something just went. Me went. All that was left was a room full of flows of different coloured lights that where each experiencing themselves in a way that is indescribably perfect. They where all completely disjoint and yet not isolated as they flowed in and out of each other. All of it connected and perfectly experienced at every point but not as one experience, not as a scene that is being witnessed. There was nothing witnessing it. No big cosmic being realizing how amazing it was that it existed. In fact it was way more intimate, way more beautiful and way more alive then anything I have ever experienced in normal perception but yet with no one to experience it. It was not like a black void or anything, it was amazing.
