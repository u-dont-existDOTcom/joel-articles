First, there was the experience of how awareness, this thing that was me, was gone. Now, the only thing that existed were the flows of colored light that made up the room I was in. Each flow was experiencing itself in an indescribably perfect way. Though disjoint, they were not isolated; the flows ran into and through each other, and everywhere they touched, they were connected. This was not one experience, nor was it one scene being seen by a witness. There was no one to see it at all. It wasn’t even like there was a black void there, witnessing it. It was infinitely more intimate, infinitely more beautiful, and infinitely more alive than anything I had ever experienced in my life, and there was no one there.
