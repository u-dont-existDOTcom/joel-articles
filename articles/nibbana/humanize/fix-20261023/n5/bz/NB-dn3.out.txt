In fact the words are probably transmitted pretty well, after all they were communally transmitted for quite a while and we have several collections that all agree to a fairly large degree (though there are variants, rearrangements, and likely even additions, but those came pretty early on too). That leads to a practical problem.

In several sutta’s, most notably DN 3 and MN 56 the Buddha does not teach the dhamma that leads to opening the dhamma eye until a student’s mind is “ready, pliable, rid of hindrances, elated, and confident.” What are we to do with maps that allow folks to get to the stages without preparing in this way?
