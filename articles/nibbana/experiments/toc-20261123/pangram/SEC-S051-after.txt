Who Gets Reborn?

Stream entry’s greatest prize is a “release date”: AN 3.88 allows at most seven more lives before final awakening. There is actually quite a lot of modern academic evidence for rebirth. But who’s going around getting reborn if no permanent self exists? Buddhists don’t imagine any permanent self-essence/soul moving between bodies. Rather, Buddhists see rebirth as a causal process, with continuity (often called a “mind-stream”) but no “thing” moving from one life to the next. Craving and clinging lead to becoming, and becoming leads to birth. More like a driverless car. The later Buddhist text Milindapañha compared rebirth to one lamp being used to light another.
