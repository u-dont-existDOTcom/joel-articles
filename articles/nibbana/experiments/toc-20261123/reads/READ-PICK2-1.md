## Part 2. What gets in the way

Headings that don't say what is under them or can be read two ways:
- Line 22 "## Spiritual Bypass Warning" has 0 words, so it is an empty parent. Its first child "Realization Isn't Enough" is not obviously about spotting fake teachers. The term "spiritual bypass" is jargon, and it is unclear whether it covers teachers or practitioners.
- Line 16 "## The Room Event" gives no hint that it is the author's deepest experience. It reads as a private label.
- Line 47 "## AtR’s Authentication Culture": "AtR" is not explained in the sidebar, and "authentication" could mean anything. It is also hard to tell that it is about community verdicts of awakening.
- Line 58 "## Come and See for Yourself!" could be the practice section, a closing invitation, or a quote.
- Line 60 "## Vanishing "I": Dangerous?" sits under an Appendix. I would expect a safety answer in the main flow or the warnings.
- Line 20 "## Stream Entered" and line 48 "## What Is Stream Entry?" sound alike. The first is the author's story and the second is the explainer, but the contents does not show that.
- Line 7 "## Enlightened or Deluded?" is ambiguous. It could be the author's doubts, a general test, or "Am I a Stream-Enterer?" (line 50). Its only child, "Loving-Kindness (Mettā)", looks unrelated.
- Line 6 "## The Afterglow Wore Off" and line 9 "## Useless Peak States?" cover close to the same worry. A reader with a faded peak experience can't tell which one to open.
- Line 30 "### My Nibbāna Taxonomy" under "Nibbāna's Volume Knobs": two names for what looks like one idea.
- Line 53 "## Science Can't Settle This" has 92 words. It looks like the science section, but its three children (lines 54 to 56) are about trying a practice.
- Line 33 "### What Awareness Means" does not say whose awareness. It could be read against the "one universal awareness" view, but it is not clear that it does that.

Headings that repeat or echo the one before or their parent:
- Lines 21 and 22: "The Enlightenment Manual" (22 words) then "Spiritual Bypass Warning" (0 words). The parent says nothing, and the first child is empty.
- Lines 13 and 15: "The Bāhiya Sutta" and "Desperate Like Bāhiya" both lead with Bāhiya. Line 57 "A Cow Killed Bāhiya" is a third.
- Lines 28 and 29: "What "Nibbāna" Means" then "Nibbāna's Volume Knobs". Many headings in lines 17 to 19 and 28 to 31 begin with "Nibbāna", so the sidebar becomes a block that is hard to scan.
- Lines 5 and 6: "My First Psychedelics" then "The Afterglow Wore Off" are fine in order, but line 9 "Useless Peak States?" re-asks the question.
- Lines 1 and 2: "Introduction & Navigation" and "Why Did I Write This?" are both introductions.

Sections that seem misplaced, at the wrong level or under the wrong parent:
- "A Cow Killed Bāhiya" (line 57) is far from "The Bāhiya Sutta" (line 13). It is also inside the Manual, near the end.
- "Dōgen's Myriad Things" (line 14, 64 words) sits as a child of "The Bāhiya Sutta". It is a Zen teacher's take, so it could easily be missed.
- "Loving-Kindness (Mettā)" (line 8) is a child of "Enlightened or Deluded?" and sits in the autobiography.
- "The Enlightenment Manual" holds both theory (what nibbāna means, light, rebirth) and practice. "Try a Tiny Bit of Nibbāna" is far down, under "Science Can't Settle This".
- "Friends Are Everything" (line 24) and "Realization Isn't Enough" (line 23) are children of the bypass warning, but "Friends" reads like a general point.
- The Appendix has one child and a 0-word parent.
- Line 8 "Loving-Kindness (Mettā)" is the only place for the "teaching loving-kindness to people who hate themselves" situation, and it sits under the story.
- "Why Monks Stay Vague" (line 10) and "What the Books Never Say" (line 44) sit far apart in the sidebar, though they look related.
