## 2. What gets in the way

**Headings that don't say what's under them or read two ways**
- Line 22 "Spiritual Bypass Warning" has 0 words, so it's an empty folder. Its two children (23, 24) are about needing more than realization and needing friends, which isn't obviously "bypass". I can't tell whether it's about fake teachers, skipping groundwork, or isolation.
- Line 21 "The Enlightenment Manual" has 22 words and is followed straight by a warning. I can't tell whether the manual starts at 25 or at 22.
- Line 59 "Appendix" has 0 words and one child about flashbacks. "Appendix" doesn't say it is about safety, and the flashback heading is playful ("dangerous or cool?") and reads like a side question.
- Line 33 "Awarenesses" is a bare noun. It could be anything, and I can't tell it's about the senses.
- Line 14 "The Myriad Things Experience Themselves" is only 64 words under a Bāhiya heading. I can't tell what it is.
- Line 08 "May All Beings Be Loving, Peaceful & Free" is a blessing, not a topic. It sits under "Was I enlightened or deluded?" and I can't tell what it covers (a loving-kindness practice? a prayer?).
- Line 39 "Light Language Like Chantress Seba Never Seen Before!" is a name and an exclamation. It doesn't say what the section teaches.
- Line 40 "Go Into The Light" could be about death, a meditation, or the theory.
- Line 20 "Stream entered" is short and could be read as a section about the author or about the stage. It is easy to confuse with line 48 "Entering The Stream To End Rebirth".
- Line 28 "What “Nibbāna” Is Actually Saying" could be etymology or a definition. Line 52 "Why nibbāna is “awakening”" also looks like word meaning, so two places claim the same job.
- Line 29 "Nibbāna Has Volume & Tuning Knobs" doesn't tell me it is about degrees or grading.
