## Part 2. What gets in the way

**Headings that don't say what's under them or read two ways**
- `# The Enlightenment Manual` (22 words) then `## Spiritual Bypass Warning` (0 words) then `### Realization Isn't Enough`: the sidebar shows a 0-word heading as a warning, but the content (how to tell a real teacher, whether realization is enough) sits one level down. "Spiritual Bypass" is jargon, and a reader with a teacher who calls everything illusion may not connect to it.
- `AtR’s Authentication Culture`: "AtR" is not explained in the sidebar. A reader can't tell it is an online community, so situations about being "declared awakened" don't point here.
- `Practice Outlasts Death`: it could be about rebirth, about dying unprepared, or about the afterlife. It sits under "What Is Stream Entry?", but a reader asking what stream entry guarantees has to guess it is the answer.
- `Capacity for Suffering`: reads like a general topic. It is under "Contact Builds a World", with other self-and-consciousness headings, so it is unclear whether it answers "does feeling no self end suffering?".
- `Awareness Is Plural`: reads as a claim and not a topic. A reader with "everything is one universal awareness" in mind might not link it to a response to that.
- `The Room Event` and `Stream Entered`: neither says it is the author's deepest experience. "The Room Event" gives no hint of what happened. Stream Entered could be read as a definition.
- `Binding Makes the Mind` and `Why the Tangle Is Heavy`: "binding" and "tangle" are unexplained images, and the two seem to say much the same. A reader can't tell which comes first.
- `Enlightened or Deluded?` (under "The Afterglow Wore Off") and `Am I a Stream-Enterer?` ask almost the same question, so a reader isn't sure which gives the checklist.
- `Go Into the Light` could be about meditation or about dying.
- `Appendix` (0 words) holds only `Vanishing "I": Dangerous?`, which is a safety topic for many readers and is hidden in an appendix.

**Headings that repeat their parent or neighbour**
- `Nibbāna Is Unimaginable`, `Two Scopes of Nibbāna`, `What "Nibbāna" Means`, `Nibbāna's Volume Knobs`, `My Nibbāna Taxonomy`, `Is Nibbāna an Experience?`, `Nibbāna as Light Flows`: seven headings share the word and don't say what each does. The first definition (`What "Nibbāna" Means`) comes far after `Two Scopes of Nibbāna`.
- `Nibbāna's Volume Knobs` and its child `My Nibbāna Taxonomy` look like the same thing twice.
- `The Bāhiya Sutta` and `Desperate Like Bāhiya` and `A Cow Killed Bāhiya`: three Bāhiya headings far apart.
- `What Is Stream Entry?` and the earlier `Stream Entered` sit far apart and look duplicated.
- `Why Monks Stay Vague` and `What the Books Never Say`: the same question twice in two places.
- `Sutta Light Language` and `Light Has No Time` follow `Nibbāna as Light Flows` and repeat its topic.
- `Realization Isn't Enough` is a line below `Spiritual Bypass Warning` and the two read as the same point.

**Wrong place, level or parent**
- `Spiritual Bypass Warning`, `Friends Are Everything`, and `The Risk of Skipping Ahead` (warnings) sit in different places in the Manual, so no single place collects the warnings.
- `Loving-Kindness (Mettā)` is a child of `Enlightened or Deluded?`, which hides it. A reader looking for mettā won't look there.
- `Dōgen's Myriad Things` (64 words) is a child of `The Bāhiya Sutta` and a reader looking for Zen won't find it.
- `Nibbāna Is Unimaginable`, `Enlightened Fingers`, and `Two Scopes of Nibbāna` sit under `The Room Event` and are really teaching points, not part of the story.
- `Science Can't Settle This` (92 words) is a short parent of the practice steps (`Try a Tiny Bit of Nibbāna`, `Did It Work or Go Wrong?`, `What Remains Afterward`), which look like children of science.
- `A Cow Killed Bāhiya` is in the Manual, far from `The Bāhiya Sutta`.
- `# Introduction & Navigation`, `# Why Did I Write This?` and `# My Crazy Wonderful Life` are all top-level, and the story has many children, while the manual is one parent. This makes the sidebar lopsided.
