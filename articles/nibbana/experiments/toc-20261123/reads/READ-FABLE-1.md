## 2. What gets in the way

**Empty or near-empty parents.** "Spiritual Bypass Warning" (0 words) and "Appendix" (0 words) are headings with nothing under them. In the sidebar "Spiritual Bypass Warning" sits directly above "Realization Isn't Enough", so a reader can't tell whether the warning is about that child, the whole Manual, or the guide's warnings generally. Its name suggests "teachers who dismiss problems as illusion" and "don't skip the groundwork" equally. "The Enlightenment Manual" (22 words) is also nearly empty, and it opens with a warning, not a manual, which is a surprising order.

**Hidden or unexplained terms.** "AtR’s Authentication Culture": "AtR" is not explained in the contents, so I'd only click it if I already knew the community. "Stream Entered" (line 24) and "What Is Stream Entry?" (line 48) look like the same topic. One is Joel's story and one is the explainer, but nothing in the titles says so. "The Room Event" is cryptic: it could be the author's deepest experience or a side incident. "Desperation Did the Trick" (line 15) is also a story beat that sounds like a how-to.

**Headings that say the same thing as a neighbour.**
- "Is It Safe When "I" Vanish?" (Appendix) and "Did It Work? Is It Safe?" (line 55) both ask about safety, so I can't tell which one is the crisis section.
- "What Is Stream Entry?" and "Am I a Stream-Enterer?" and "Stream Entered" overlap.
- "Nibbāna Is Unimaginable", "What "Nibbāna" Means", "Is Nibbāna an Experience?", "Two Scopes of Nibbāna", "Nibbāna's Volume Knobs" and "My Nibbāna Taxonomy" are six "what is nibbāna" headings in three different parts. "What the Books Never Say" overlaps with "Why Monks Stay Vague".
- "The Bāhiya Sutta" (line 13) and "Then a Cow Killed Bāhiya" (line 57) are far apart, and the second is the end of the first story. A reader looking for the cow story may go to the first.
- "Nibbāna as Light Flows" and its children (Sutta Light Language, Is Light Unfabricated?, Light Has No Time) are hard to tell apart.

**Wrong place, level or parent.**
- "Loving-Kindness (Mettā)" sits under "Enlightened or Deluded?" in the story half, with 264 words; a reader looking for the practice would expect it in the Manual.
- "Dōgen's Myriad Things" (64 words) sits under "The Bāhiya Sutta", and a reader wanting a Zen teacher's view would not guess that. It is also too small to be its own section.
- "Try a Tiny Bit of Nibbāna" is a level-3 under "Science Can't Settle This", although it is the how-to practice, which makes it hard to find. "Did It Work? Is It Safe?" and "What Remains Afterward" sit in the same place.
- "Go Into the Light" and "What's Next After Death?" are far apart, and the death material is split.
- "Practice Outlasts Death" and "Who Gets Reborn?" sit under "What Is Stream Entry?", which is fine, but "Waking From the Dream" there is vague.
- "Is It Safe When "I" Vanish?" is the one urgent safety section and it is under Appendix at the end, while a frightened reader will look near the top or in the practice part.
- "Capacity for Suffering" sits under "Contact Builds a World" along with "Awareness Is Plural" and others, so it is hard to see it also answers the questions about split brains and no self.

**Vague or two-way headings.** "Waking From the Dream", "Maps Make Counterfeits", "Binding Makes the Mind" and "Why the Tangle Is Heavy" are metaphors with no plain topic. "Free Will vs Conditions" and "Contact Builds a World" are okay but opaque. "Science Can't Settle This" could be about the whole guide or its nearby sections.
