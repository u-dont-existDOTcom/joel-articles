## Part 2. What gets in the way

**Headings that don't say what's under them or read two ways**
- Line 08 "Loving-Kindness (Mettā)" sits under "Enlightened or Deluded?" (07). Nothing in either heading links them, so a reader looking for mettā doesn't expect it there, and one looking for how to judge awakening doesn't see why mettā is in it.
- Line 07 "Enlightened or Deluded?" could be a story beat or a test for the reader's own state.
- Line 22 "Spiritual Bypass Warning" has 0 words and its text doesn't show. The term is jargon, and it doesn't say whether it is about teachers, about the reader, or about safety.
- Line 25 "What's Next After Death?" could be rebirth, near-death experience, or what the author thinks. It sits beside "Who Gets Reborn?" (51), far away.
- Line 40 "Go Into the Light" could be a practice instruction or a death or near-death topic.
- Line 47 "AtR’s Authentication Culture" uses an abbreviation that is never explained, and "authentication" is odd. A reader can't tell this is about an online community that declares people awakened.
- Line 52 "Waking From the Dream" is poetic, and it could be about stream entry, rebirth, or the dream metaphor.
- Line 58 "Come and See for Yourself!" could be the conclusion or an invitation to practise. It is unclear whether it is a closing or a practice section.
- Line 17 "Nibbāna Is Unimaginable" and line 19 "Two Scopes of Nibbāna" are general doctrine headings under a personal-story section (16), so they don't say they are about the Room Event.
- Line 34 "Ignorance Is the Root" and line 37 "Capacity for Suffering" don't say how they differ from 35 and 36.

**Headings that repeat their neighbour or parent**
- Line 10 "Why Monks Stay Vague" and line 44 "What the Books Never Say" ask nearly the same thing, in two distant places.
- Lines 28, 29, 30, 31 ("What "Nibbāna" Means", "Nibbāna's Volume Knobs", "My Nibbāna Taxonomy", "Is Nibbāna an Experience?") overlap, and the sidebar doesn't show which is which.
- Lines 38 and 39 ("Nibbāna as Light Flows", "Sutta Light Language") say much the same. Line 42 "Why Light Isn't Fabricated" and line 41 "Isn't It "Unconditioned"?" also overlap.
- Lines 06 and 09 ("The Afterglow Wore Off", "Useless Peak States?") cover similar ground.
- Lines 48 and 50 ("What Is Stream Entry?", "Am I a Stream-Enterer?") are near twins at parent and child level.
- Bāhiya appears at 13, 15 and 57, and only the first says "Sutta".

**Wrong place, level or parent**
- Lines 54-56 ("Try a Tiny Bit of Nibbāna", "Did It Work or Go Wrong?", "What Remains Afterward") are practice sections under "Science Can't Settle This" (53). The practice should be its own section, since it is the thing readers most want.
- Line 24 "Friends Are Everything" is under "Spiritual Bypass Warning" (22), a parent that is empty and doesn't say it is about community.
- Line 60 "Vanishing "I": Dangerous?" is the safety answer, but it is in the Appendix at the end, far from the practice sections, and the Appendix (59) has 0 words.
- Lines 33-37 sit under "Contact Builds a World" (32), but "Capacity for Suffering" and "Ignorance Is the Root" read as separate topics from how a world is built.
- Line 21 "The Enlightenment Manual" has only 22 words and is a very wide container for lines 22-58.
- Line 57 "A Cow Killed Bāhiya" is a story section placed late, in the manual, away from the Bāhiya sections (13, 15).
- Lines 13, 15 and 16 mix story, sutta and personal experience, so a reader can't tell where the memoir stops and the explanation begins.
