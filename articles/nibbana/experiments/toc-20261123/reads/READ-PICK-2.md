## 2. What gets in the way

- **"Introduction & Navigation" then "Why Did I Write This?" then "My Crazy Wonderful Life" (lines 1-3), all top level.** The second is a motive section, and the third is the memoir, yet "My Crazy Wonderful Life" is a parent whose first child ("The Weight of the World") is a new topic. The parent's text doesn't show, so it is unclear whether it is an overview or a story.
- **"The Enlightenment Manual" (21) holds nearly everything after the story, a huge grab-bag.** Its children mix practical items ("How to Test It Yourself"), theory (light, ignorance, free will), community ("AtR's Authentication Culture") and a story ("A Cow Killed Bāhiya"). The name promises a how-to, but most of it is explanation. Readers hunting for the manual won't know where it begins.
- **"Spiritual Bypass Warning" (22) has 0 words and is the first child of the Manual.** Its two children ("Realization Isn't Enough", "Friends Are Everything") are what carry the warning. The heading reads like a banner, and "Friends Are Everything" doesn't read as a bypass issue. Unclear for the isolation, fake-teacher and "is it wise to publish" questions, which a reader may assume live here.
- **"Appendix" (59), 0 words, with one child, "When the "I" Vanishes".** The child doesn't say it is an appendix, and an appendix with one section is odd. The child sounds like core experience material (also fits situation 8 or 7), but it sits at the bottom.
- **"Enlightened or Deluded?" (07) with the child "Loving-Kindness (Mettā)" (08).** The child has nothing visibly to do with the parent. Mettā sits in the story section, so someone looking for loving-kindness teaching would not expect it here.
- **"The Bāhiya Sutta" (13) then "Dōgen's Myriad Things" (14).** A Zen teacher under a sutta, unexplained. Later "Desperate Like Bāhiya" and "A Cow Killed Bāhiya" repeat the name, so "Bāhiya" appears in three places and it isn't clear which holds the short-instruction teaching versus the story versus the death.
- **"The Map Past Oneness" (11) and "The Suttas on Unification" (12) sit close and sound alike.** "Unification" and "Oneness" may be read as the same thing, and "unification" could mean jhāna (absorption) or the oneness experience. Ambiguous.
- **"Nibbāna Is Unimaginable" (17), "Enlightened Fingers" (18), "Two Scopes of Nibbāna" (19) under "The Room Event".** "Two Scopes of Nibbāna" is a concept that sounds like it belongs with "What "Nibbāna" Means" (28) and "Nibbāna's Volume Knobs" (29), which seems a duplicate of it. Also "Nibbāna Is Unimaginable" nearly repeats "Why Monks Stay Vague" (10) and "What the Books Never Say" (44).
- **"What "Nibbāna" Means" (28) then "Nibbāna's Volume Knobs" (29) then "My Nibbāna Taxonomy" (30) then "Is Nibbāna an Experience?" (31).** Four "Nibbāna ..." headings in a row. Meaning, Volume Knobs and Taxonomy overlap (grading versions of the goal). "Volume Knobs" is metaphorical, and the child "Taxonomy" is what says "grading".
- **"Contact Builds a World" (32) with children "What Awareness Means", "Ignorance Is the Root", "Why the Tangle Is Heavy", "Binding Makes the Mind", "Capacity for Suffering".** The parent says one thing and its first child is a definition of another. "Ignorance Is the Root" is the biggest (535 words) yet is a child, and "Why the Tangle Is Heavy" speaks of a tangle that was never introduced. "Capacity for Suffering" could be about split brains and no-self, or about suffering in general, so it is two-way.
- **"Nibbāna as Light Flows" (38) and children, with "Sutta Light Language", "Go Into the Light", "Isn't It "Unconditioned"?", "Is Light Unfabricated?", "Light Has No Time".** "Isn't It "Unconditioned"?" and "Is Light Unfabricated?" ask nearly the same question. "Go Into the Light" could be the near-death light, a meditation instruction, or the physics. Physics (photons, relativity) isn't named in any heading, so "Light Has No Time" is the only clue.
- **"What's Next After Death?" (25) versus "Practice Outlasts Death" (49) and "Who Gets Reborn?" (51).** Death and rebirth are split between three places, one under a different parent.
- **"What Is Stream Entry?" (48) vs "Stream Entered" (20).** Same topic at two levels and in two places (story and manual). "Am I a Stream-Enterer?" (50) is a third.
- **"Waking From the Dream" (52)** under Stream Entry doesn't say what it is; it could be metaphor for any awakening.
- **"How to Test It Yourself" (53) followed by "Try a Tiny Bit of Nibbāna" (54)** say nearly the same thing, and the parent's text is only 92 words, so the parent and first child collapse to one idea. "What Remains Afterward" (56) could be the aftermath of the practice or of death.
- **"A Cow Killed Bāhiya" (57) and "Come and See for Yourself!" (58) at the end of the Manual.** The cow is a story, not a manual item, and is far from "The Bāhiya Sutta" (13).
- **"AtR’s Authentication Culture" (47):** "AtR" is not explained, and the community can't be recognised by a newcomer.
- **"What the Books Never Say" (44), "How the Teaching Faded" (45), "Maps: Shortcut or Fake?" (46)** could be read as one sequence (history), but "Maps" is ambiguous (meditation maps vs. geography), and it looks like "The Map Past Oneness" (11).
- **"What Is "Already Perfect"?" (26)** is also the article's title idea, but sits mid-manual and is easy to miss.
- **Cut-off headings:** none show "…" here, so no problem there.
