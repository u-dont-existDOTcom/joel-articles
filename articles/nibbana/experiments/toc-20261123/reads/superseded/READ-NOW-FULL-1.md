## 2. What gets in the way

**Ambiguous or non-descriptive headings**
- "Awareness es" (line 33) looks like a typo or fragment; I can't tell what it covers.
- "Introduction & Navigation" and "Why Did I Write This?" are two top-level lines in a row, and both could hold the "how to read this" material. A reader with no orientation may open the wrong one.
- "Spiritual Bypass Warning" has 0 words and is a parent of two children. "Spiritual realization isn’t enough these days" and "Spiritual friends are everything" are not obviously about bypassing, so the parent's meaning has to be guessed. It also sits first in the Manual, before "What’s Next?", so it reads as the manual's first step.
- "What’s Next?" (line 25) is vague. Next after what: the story, or the warning?
- "Stream entered" (line 20) could be a claim, a stage description, or the author's own event. It sits after "The Room Event", and "Entering The Stream To End Rebirth" later repeats the topic.
- "Then I Found The Map Past Oneness" and "What the Suttas Say About Unification" are close in meaning and sit next to each other. "Map" also recurs in "A Map Can Save Years", which is far away and about something else (modern maps and teachers).
- "My Nibbāna Taxonomy" vs "Nibbāna Has Volume & Tuning Knobs": the child restates the parent. In the sidebar they sit on two lines in a row.
- "What “Nibbāna” Is Actually Saying" and "What the Books Never Made Clear" both promise a plain statement of what nibbāna is. "Nibbāna is a meditative state and attainment" is a third that does too. A reader can't tell the three apart.
- "How the Experiential Key Can Fade" uses "Experiential Key" without introducing it. It also overlaps with "When the Afterglow Wore Off" and "A Peak State Can Be Useless Or Worse" in the story part.
- "Stream-entry requires more than a 2-factor authentication key" is a metaphor that hides its topic. "AtR’s Authentication Culture" shares the metaphor.
- "AtR" appears undefined, in lines 13 and 47. A new reader doesn't know it is a community.

**Repeats of the parent or the line before**
- "# The Enlightenment Manual" (22 words) is followed straight away by a 0-word warning and then "What’s Next?". Nothing says what the manual contains.
- "Entering The Stream To End Rebirth" (parent) and its children "Practice can carry into a future life", "Ending the dream of saṃsāra", "Why nibbāna is “awakening”". The last one reads as a different topic. The parent heading is also close to "Stream entered".
- "The Theory of Light Flows As Nibbāna" and its child "Light is unestablished…like nibbāna" say nearly the same thing. "Go Into The Light" is ambiguous: it could be death, near-death, or a meditation instruction.
- "But isn’t nibbāna asaṅkhata, or “unconditioned”?" and "If nibbāna is “unfabricated,” is light also?" are back to back and ask the same kind of question.

**Wrong place, level or parent**
- "Was I enlightened or deluded?" has a child that is a loving-kindness blessing with emoji. The child doesn't say what it contains, and it looks like a sign-off, not a section.
- "The Myriad Things Experience Themselves" (64 words, under the Bāhiya heading) looks like a quote or slogan, not a section.
- Line 17, "Nibbāna is describable but unimaginable", sits under "The Room Event", which is a personal story. It looks like a teaching point filed under a personal experience. Lines 18 and 19 are the same.
- "Free Will Vs Conditions" (line 27) seems out of place among the nibbāna-definition sections. The same goes for "What “Already Perfect” Means", which sits before it and could belong earlier, near the title question.
- "Science Can’t Settle This, but You Can Test It" has the practice children below it, but its title is about science. A reader who wants the practice has to guess it is there. A science-minded reader might also expect science content and not find it.
- "Bāhiya Got Enlightened and Then a Cow Killed Him" (line 57) is in the Manual at the end, but "AtR pointed me to the Bāhiya Sutta" and "Desperation Did The Trick For Me & Bāhiya" are in the story. The Bāhiya material is split across three places.
- "Are disappearing flashbacks dangerous or cool?" in the Appendix is a safety question. It is far from "What Counts as Success, Confusion, or Danger".
- "Come and See for Yourself!" is the last item of the manual and could be a conclusion or a practice.
