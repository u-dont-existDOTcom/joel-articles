## 2. What gets in the way

**Truncation.** Most ## and ### headings are cut off at about 25 characters. Many of them are cut right where the point would be: "What the Suttas Say About…", "What “Nibbāna” Is Actually…", "What Counts as Success…", "Was I enlightened or delud…". Cut like that they all read as generic. The sentence-style headings under 48 and 49 ("Practice can carry into a …", "Stream-entry requires m…") are cut before the claim, so I can't tell what they say.

**Headings that don't say what's under them.**
- "Spiritual Bypass Warning" (0 words) is a level-2 heading, and the next line is a ### that holds the real content. Is it a warning for me, about other teachers, or about this method? I could read it as the home for situations 5, 21 and 27, and I can't tell which it covers.
- "Appendix" has 0 words and its one child is a single question about flashbacks. It looks like a warning that fell into the wrong place. Anyone worried about a frightening experience will not look under "Appendix".
- "Awarenesses" is a bare plural. It could be about consciousness, about the Buddhist view, or about "universal awareness". It is the only place I'd look for a one-awareness-versus-Buddha comparison, and I'm not sure it is that.
- "Go Into The Light" could be a practice or a near-death topic. "Light Language Like Cha…" sits right beside it, so the two are easy to mix up.
