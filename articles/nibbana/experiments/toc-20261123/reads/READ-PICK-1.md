## 2. What gets in the way

**Unclear or two-way headings**
- "Spiritual Bypass Warning" (22) is a 0-word parent with two children; it doesn't say what the warning is about. "Bypass" is jargon, and the parent shows as an empty line. Its children ("Realization Isn't Enough", "Friends Are Everything") don't obviously belong to it, and "Friends Are Everything" is what a lonely reader needs, but nothing signals that.
- "Appendix" (59) is 0 words and its one child, "When the "I" Vanishes", is the only place that sounds like it handles the self dropping out and science findings. "Appendix" hides that. It also reads as skippable.
- "AtR’s Authentication Culture" (47): "AtR" is never explained, and "authentication" could mean anything. I wouldn't know it is about communities that declare people awakened.
- "The Suttas on Unification" (12): "unification" is jargon. It could be about oneness or about meditative absorption.
- "The Map Past Oneness" (11) and "Maps: Shortcut or Fake?" (46): both "map", and I can't tell them apart.
- "Enlightened or Deluded?" (07) and its child "Loving-Kindness (Mettā)" (08): the child seems unrelated to the parent. Loving-kindness for self-haters (situation 34) is hard to find under a heading about enlightened vs deluded.
- "What Awareness Means" (33) sits under "Contact Builds a World" (32); it is the most direct heading for the "one universal awareness" question but is buried as a child of a different topic.
- "Nibbāna's Volume Knobs" (29) is playful and doesn't say it is about partial vs complete release or grading. Its child "My Nibbāna Taxonomy" repeats the idea.
- "Nibbāna as Light Flows" (38) and its children "Sutta Light Language", "Go Into the Light", "Light Has No Time": I can't tell which holds physics and which holds death/near-death. "Go Into the Light" could be a death topic or a meditation instruction.
- "Is Light Unfabricated?" (42) and "Isn't It "Unconditioned"?" (41) are adjacent and ask almost the same thing.
- "Is Nibbāna an Experience?" (31) vs "What "Nibbāna" Means" (28) vs "Nibbāna Is Unimaginable" (17): overlapping in the sidebar.
- "What Is Stream Entry?" (48) and "Stream Entered" (20) look the same; the first is a manual part and the second a story part. The parent "What Is Stream Entry?" and first child "Practice Outlasts Death" are a poor pair, because the child doesn't say it answers the parent.
- "Did It Work or Go Wrong?" (55) covers both success and danger, so I wouldn't know whether it handles a scary meditation or a brief self-dropout while driving.
- "Why the Tangle Is Heavy" (35) and "Ignorance Is the Root" (34): "Tangle" is never introduced, so the situation about consciousness getting tangled again and again is only findable by guessing.
- "Useless Peak States?" (09) and "The Afterglow Wore Off" (06): overlapping questions about peak states that fade.
- "Why Did I Write This?" (02) sits as its own level 1 with 109 words, then "Introduction & Navigation" (01): both sound like orientation.

**Placement and level**
- "Dōgen's Myriad Things" (14) is a level-3 child of "The Bāhiya Sutta" with 64 words and doesn't say what it is; the "famous Zen teacher" link is easy to miss.
- "The Enlightenment Manual" (21) has only 22 words and works as a divider. Story sections ("A Cow Killed Bāhiya", 57) end up inside the manual, even though that is the Bāhiya story. It should sit with the Bāhiya sections in the story half.
- "Nibbāna Is Unimaginable", "Enlightened Fingers", "Two Scopes of Nibbāna" (17-19) sit under "The Room Event" as children, but "Two Scopes" reads like theory and sounds like it belongs in the manual.
- "What's Next After Death?" (25) sits between the warnings and the nibbāna definitions, so it looks out of order.
- Several manual topics are at the same level as big topics: "Free Will vs Conditions" (27) and "What Is "Already Perfect"?" (26) read as a separate cluster from the nibbāna sections, and nothing signals the groups.
