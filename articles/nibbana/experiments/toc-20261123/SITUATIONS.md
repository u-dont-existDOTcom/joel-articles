# Situations

1. You keep seeing the word nirvana and want to know where it comes from and what it literally meant before it became a cliché.
2. Months after a huge experience on mushrooms, the feeling of oneness is gone and you wonder whether it was ever real or just the drug.
3. You have landed on a very long article and don't know how it is organised, what to read first, or whether you need to do something else before starting.
4. You've read plenty of Buddhist books and still can't find a plain description of what the goal feels like, and you wonder why teachers are so vague.
5. A charismatic spiritual teacher seems to dismiss everyone's problems as illusion, and you want to know how to tell a real teacher from a fake one.
6. You want a simple practice you can try tonight that might loosen your sense of being one solid self.
7. During a meditation your sense of body started to come apart and it turned frightening, so you want to know what to do right now.
8. Your sense of self sometimes drops out for a few seconds while you are cycling or driving, and you don't know if that is dangerous.
9. A Buddhist friend insists that nibbana is not an experience at all, only the end of certain mental chains, and you want to know if the old texts say otherwise.
10. You had a life-changing opening and want a checklist of questions to judge honestly whether you are really a first-stage awakened person.
11. A friend was declared awakened by an online community and you are unsure whether to trust that verdict or the community.
12. You keep hearing 'stream entry' and want to know what the first stage of awakening is and what it guarantees.
13. You know the teachings well but have had no big experience, and you ask whether understanding or faith alone can still get you to the first stage, even if you die unprepared.
14. If there is no permanent self, you can't see who or what is supposed to be reborn.
15. You want the actual story of the author's deepest experience: what it was like, what was gone and what was left.
16. You hear that someone's ten fingers each felt like separate perfect streams of light, and you want to know what that was.
17. You have heard that you must master powerful deep meditative absorptions before any insight can happen, and you wonder if that is true.
18. You are looking for the one old teaching that gives short, direct instructions for being freed through the senses, with the story around it.
19. A man is told he has reached full liberation and is killed by a cow soon after, and you wonder whether that means the path failed him.
20. You like physics and want to know how light, photons and relativity are supposed to connect to the end of suffering.
21. You worry that a technique that exposes the goal so plainly lets people skip the groundwork, and you ask if it is wise to publish it.
22. You wonder why consciousness gets tangled up again and again, and what the root mistake is that keeps suffering going.
23. You have read that people with split brains or on autopilot function without a felt controller, and you ask whether feeling no self means you no longer suffer.
24. Since one experience a while ago you feel less trapped, less suicidal and less run by your story, and you wonder if that counts as a stage of awakening.
25. You are curious how someone raised in a rough Philadelphia neighbourhood by unusual parents became interested in all this.
26. You are at the end of your rope with life and wonder whether desperation helps or blocks spiritual progress, and what kept the author from giving up.
27. You are isolated and ask whether you can do spiritual work alone or need other people.
28. A teacher says everything is one universal awareness, and you want to know how the Buddha's view differs from that.
29. You had a peak experience that should have changed everything, yet your life went back to normal and you feel puzzled.
30. A friend says everything is already perfect, so there is nothing to do, and you wonder if that is a trap or a truth.
31. You want to know whether science supports or contradicts any of this, such as no felt controller or split-brain findings.
32. Someone you know is dying and you wonder what the old traditions and near-death stories say about the light at death.
33. You can do a little loosening in meditation and want to know what partial versus complete release looks like and how to grade it.
34. You teach loving-kindness to people who hate themselves and it doesn't work for them, and you wonder why.
35. You want to understand how separate sensory flows are supposed to become one world and one self, step by step.
36. You are wondering which modern maps and teachers of awakening are worth using, and what a famous Zen teacher said about the same event.
