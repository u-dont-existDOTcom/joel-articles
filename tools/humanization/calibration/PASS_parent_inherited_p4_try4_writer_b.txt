One place to look is the parent you inherited, meaning the way you learned to treat yourself from how people treated you growing up, long before you started building the Nurturer, Protector and Guide. A lot of the time it's more like a critic, or a warden with rules like "Go to your room until you can be nice." On the other hand, it can be warm. Where it wasn't, your little one still wanted to belong, so bit by bit they learned to keep whatever those rules didn't allow out of sight.
