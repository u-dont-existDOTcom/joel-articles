Turning the depth down like that isn't giving up on the relationship with your little one. Unless it's the inner child idea itself that you don't want, and maybe you've half known it for a while. Then it's not a pacing problem, and none of my pacing advice overrides that choice. Drop the language, and don't bring it back in through some "gentler" exercise or by calling everyday tasks reparenting. Care and protection still go on, in whatever words fit you. So do guidance and other people's support.
