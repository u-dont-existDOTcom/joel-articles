Your little one might not be the only one getting accused. When life has gone badly, part of you may try to get some control back by finding someone to blame: some younger you must be guilty for not growing into a grown-up who could have kept everyone from getting hurt. If some younger you is guilty, then what happened could have gone differently, and that feels like control, even though blaming doesn't build anything. But figuring out which age of you is to blame doesn't give you what you were missing back then. That part may start picking on a different age.
