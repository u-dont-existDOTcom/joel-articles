Before you started building the Nurturer, Protector and Guide, you already had a way of treating yourself, learned from how people treated you growing up: the parent you inherited. It can be warm, but for a lot of people it starts out as a critic or a warden with rules like don't cry, don't be needy, don't get angry, don't embarrass us, don't get too independent; where it was a critic or a warden, bit by bit your little one learned to put whatever those rules forbade out of sight to keep belonging: the crying and the anger, but also the wanting, and whatever embarrassed the family. The critic version is the inherited critic voice, but it doesn't always talk: for a lot of people it comes as feelings, expectations or habits instead, so "voice" isn't quite the word.
