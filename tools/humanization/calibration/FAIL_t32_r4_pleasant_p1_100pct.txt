Whatever you pick from that map, don't judge whether a session was safe by how pleasant it felt. A session that's doing you good can still bring up grief and fear. There can be hard dreams too, or stuff you'd really rather not meet. A session can be hard without harming you, and an intense one might not have gotten you anywhere. Even "I stayed present" isn't enough by itself. What tells you more is whether you could stop and come back to the room. Then there's whether you actually want to go back toward what came up.
