Checking doesn't have to happen in your head, either. If you're going on a date mainly to prove you're normal, or that you're really attracted to someone, it's doing the same job as another hour of worrying. It might calm you down for a day, and then the next wobble feels even bigger. If you'd want their company anyway, I'd still go, and just drop the proving part. Bailing just to make the doubt go away can be the same checking from the other side. (Feeling unsafe is different, and so is touch or sex you want to stop. You're always allowed out of those.) Trying things out to find who you're attracted to isn't checking, either, as long as you're not grading every moment for a verdict.
