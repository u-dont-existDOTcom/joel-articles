If it's your orientation or your gender in question, I wouldn't go the other way and write it off as "just an intrusive thought" only because it's unwanted, or because your family wouldn't like it. There's no rush to decide either way, because you'll find it out over time from what you keep noticing with real people and in how you actually live, and that counts for more than any one feeling, however strong it is. Even if somebody tells you otherwise, feeling the opposite tomorrow wouldn't show you the "real you" either.
