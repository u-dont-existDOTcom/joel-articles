You can give the promise a place in the room. Maybe a childhood toy, or a stuffed animal you've had for years, or a note to yourself where you'll see it, whatever feels steady and kind. It isn't your little one talking, even if it was theirs. And it doesn't know anything you don't, so you still decide what to do instead of asking it. Decide what it stands for while you're sober and calm, and let it remind you of the Nurturer or Protector you're practicing becoming.
