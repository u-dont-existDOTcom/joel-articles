Also notice what else in you answers your little one, besides what you choose to say. If their "I don't believe you" gets answered with an accusation, they can learn your love is only there as long as they take it the right way. The part of you that resents them for not believing you and the part that wants to promise them things both talk in your grown-up voice, but that doesn't make those two the same part. You might find one part doing both, two separate parts, or two blended together. Knowing which tells you whether the catch is in your love itself, or in a part that only sounds like you.
