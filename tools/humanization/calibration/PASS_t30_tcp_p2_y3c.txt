The other one shows up when you get close to something painful, as a sudden pull to do anything but feel it. It might believe there's no grown-up around who could hold what's coming up. I'd want out too. It can be as small as a yawn that makes you feel tired of the whole thing, though Re-evaluation Counseling counts yawning as emotional discharge, sort of the opposite. Both kinds started out trying to prevent something that really hurt, and they can keep at it even when what they do now hurts you. If you treat either one like an enemy, or like proof that something's wrong with you, it'll probably just think "See? I knew it," and stay in charge. I wonder what it would do if it didn't have to.
