If the day came and went and you didn't do it, maybe you were scared, or not sure what you were going for, or didn't know how, or were too tired, or something practical got in the way. Look at what actually happened before you decide which one it was. Fear can feel a lot like being tired. And if you did do it and didn't feel better right away, you haven't failed, but checking it off doesn't prove it helped either. Keep or adjust it by what it actually added: are you any safer, closer to your little one, more capable, or enjoying things more?
