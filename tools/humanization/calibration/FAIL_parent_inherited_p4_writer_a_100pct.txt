Before you started building the Nurturer, Protector and Guide, you already had a way of treating yourself, learned from how people treated you growing up: the parent you inherited. It can be warm, but for a lot of people it starts out as a critic or a warden with rules like don't cry, don't be needy, don't get angry, don't embarrass us, don't get too independent. That's the inherited critic voice, though it doesn't always talk (sometimes it's feelings, expectations or habits). To keep belonging, your little one learned which feelings and needs to put out of sight.
