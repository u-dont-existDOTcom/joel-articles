How many tries is enough? I really don't know, and I'd be suspicious of anyone who gives you a number, including you, since some things shift in a day and some take about as long as learning guitar. If it's making things worse, or you just can't do it right now, I'd change course sooner. Not wanting to do it anymore can be a reason too. But if it sounds like "I can't do this again," that might be a part of you that's trying to protect you, and I'd hear it out first. And if you keep doing it right and what was supposed to happen still doesn't, I think you've learned something real about it. Something with clearly better research behind it is worth switching for too.
