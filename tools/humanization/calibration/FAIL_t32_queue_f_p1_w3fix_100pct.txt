Anxiety deserves the same kind of listening. It might really be warning you about overload or humiliation. Or danger, or a boundary. But a part can be trying to protect you from something that won't happen, and you can listen to it without obeying it. With most decisions there's a way to find out. What did it say would happen, and what actually happened? Sometimes it was right, too. If it might be danger right now, though, or something in your body a doctor should see, I'd handle that first and check later whether the anxiety was right.
