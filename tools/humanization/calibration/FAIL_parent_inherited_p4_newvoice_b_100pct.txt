When it feels fake, I'd look at the parent you inherited. I mean the way you learned to treat yourself from how people treated you growing up, long before the Nurturer, Protector and Guide came along. For some people it's warm. For a lot of people it starts out as a critic or a warden, with rules: don't cry, don't be needy, don't get angry, don't embarrass us, don't get too independent. If that's you, your little one learned bit by bit to hide what those rules didn't allow, so they could keep belonging. The crying and the anger, but also the wanting, and whatever embarrassed the family.
