If you're going on a date mainly to prove you're normal, or that you're really attracted to someone, you're doing the same checking you'd otherwise do in your head, just out in real life. Trying things out to find who you're attracted to is fine, though, as long as you're not testing every single moment for a final answer. A date you go on to prove something might calm you down for a day or so. Then you start going over the date itself, and the next doubt comes even bigger. If you'd want their company anyway, I'd still go, and just drop the proving part. Cancelling or leaving early just to make the doubt go away is still checking, but you can always get out if you feel unsafe, or if there's touch or sex you want to stop, and that's a different thing.
