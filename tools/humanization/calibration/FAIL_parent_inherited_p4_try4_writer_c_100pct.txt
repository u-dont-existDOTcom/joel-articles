I'd start with the parent you inherited, meaning the way you learned to treat yourself from how people treated you growing up. It was there long before you started building the Nurturer, Protector and Guide. It can be warm, but often it's more like a critic, or a warden with rules. Where it was like that, bit by bit your little one learned to keep whatever wasn't allowed out of sight, so they could still belong. I think a lot of those rules were never even said out loud.
