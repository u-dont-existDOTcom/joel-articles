Weeks in and nothing much is changing? I'd want to quit too, or else keep doing the exact same thing forever so I never had to find out. Before either one, I'd look for what exactly is in the way. Sometimes those weeks were mostly reading about it and getting ready to, which can feel a lot like doing it (this article included). Or you did sit down with your little one, and fear got there first, or a part of you that's trying to protect you. It can be as plain as never having the place to yourself. Whatever it turns out to be, I'd work on that one thing instead of giving up on everything.
