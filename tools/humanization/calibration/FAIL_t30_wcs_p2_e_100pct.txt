Maybe you've been comforting your little one for weeks, and the words are all there, but the loving grown-up behind them isn't, not yet. Your little one would notice. I'd probably want to fix it by saying sweeter and sweeter things, which won't work, and I wouldn't decide reparenting is useless over it, either. I'd borrow that grown-up first. Then try again, and see if your little one can tell the difference. If it helps for an evening and then it's gone, that still counts as a good evening. It just isn't proof the whole thing's fixed, or even that this was what helped.
