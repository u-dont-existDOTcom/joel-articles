Set one boundary. Say no to something that's costing you too much (money, time, whatever it is). Handle that task you've been putting off, or the money mess, even if all you do today is open the envelope. Keep one small promise to your little one. Clean your room! And if a relationship keeps making you less safe, leave it, which is not a small thing at all, I know. Your little one has had enough grown-ups who said sweet things and then did nothing, so this is how they start to believe you.
