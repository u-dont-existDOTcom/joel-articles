Give the Vow a Physical Reminder

You can give the vow a home somewhere in your room. Maybe a stuffed animal on your bed, or a toy you've had since you were little, or a note on the mirror. Whatever feels steady and kind enough to see every day.

Decide what it means while you're calm and sober, though, so it reminds you of the grown-up who takes care of your little one and keeps them safe. Then don't go asking it for advice. It only knows what you knew when you put it there.
