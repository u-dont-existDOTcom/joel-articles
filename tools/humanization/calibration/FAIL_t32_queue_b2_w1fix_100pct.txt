Still, I wouldn't write off genuine feelings about who you're attracted to, or your gender, as "just intrusive thoughts" only because they're confusing or unwanted. Or because the people around you would disapprove (I'm not pushing anyone toward or away from any orientation or gender). Nobody has to settle it now, and what you do in the meantime can go by what you care about. What keeps coming back in your own experience over time tells you more, even if you've kept it out of your life so far. Whichever passing feeling is loudest isn't automatically the "real you", and neither is what a therapist or anyone else says it means.
