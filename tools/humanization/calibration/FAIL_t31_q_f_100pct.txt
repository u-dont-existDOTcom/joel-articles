Anxiety deserves the same kind of listening, but it isn't a fortune teller. It might really be warning you about something, like being overloaded, or a boundary. Most of the time, I'd ask it what it's trying to protect, and then check what it predicted against what actually happens. Listening to it doesn't mean letting it drive. If you're actually in danger right now, though, get safe first, and get a possible medical warning sign checked instead of testing it. And some of what it says isn't a prediction at all. If a part of you is saying no to going deeper, or to an altered state, that's reason enough to stop, not something to test. With touch or sex, even a hesitation is enough.
