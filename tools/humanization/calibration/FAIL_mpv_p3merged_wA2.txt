Plenty of people are still at the putting-up-with part. Even with little or no warmth for your little one yet, the relationship can start with the Protector's plain acts and the warmth can come after, which feels backwards, because you'd think you have to feel it first before you could do anything for them. The first care you give them might be a real meal, or cancelling one obligation that was going to wear them out this week. Take one act from all this and make it specific: when, what's the smallest first step, and do you need help, or money, or a form? If you can see a hard moment coming, like asking your roommate for their half of the rent, practice what you'll say out loud a few times beforehand. It's strange saying it to nobody, but your little one is hearing all of it, the shaky tries too.
