### When to Change the Strategy

Weeks in and nothing much is changing? I'd want to quit too, or else keep doing the exact same thing forever so I never had to find out. Sometimes those weeks were mostly reading about it and getting ready to, which can feel a lot like doing it (this article included). And if it was comforting your little one, was the loving grown-up really there? Your little one would notice if it wasn't, the way you notice a hug from someone who's checking their phone. I'd borrow that grown-up first. Then try again, and see if your little one can tell the difference.

How many tries is enough? I really don't know, and I'd be suspicious of anyone who gives you a number, including you, since some things shift in a day and some take about as long as learning guitar. If it's making things worse, or you just can't do it right now, I'd change course sooner. Not wanting to do it anymore can count too, but I'd hear that no out first, sudden or not, since it might be a part of you that's trying to protect you. And if you keep doing it right and what was supposed to happen still doesn't, I think you've learned something real about it. Something with clearly better research behind it is worth switching for too.

The trap I'd watch for is explaining away every miss. It didn't work, so you must have done it wrong, so maybe you needed the right candle first. Next time it's something else, and none of it is anything you actually noticed while you were doing it. At some point nothing could prove the method wrong.
