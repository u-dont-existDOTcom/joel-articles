Sometimes a voice, hunch or presence does answer, and you can listen without letting it take over. Follow its advice if it's loving, wise and helpful and leaves you free, but recheck anything big or hard to undo, once you're calm, against what's really happened (if you're in danger, get safe first). Those checks are only for advice, though. If what comes is a hard feeling, you'd hear it out whether it passes them or not. When nothing answers, watch what sets the reaction off, what it does in your body, and what it makes you do or avoid. You can guess what the reaction is protecting you from, but it might actually prevent something else.
