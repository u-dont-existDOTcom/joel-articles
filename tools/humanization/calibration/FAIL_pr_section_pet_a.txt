Give the Vow a Physical Reminder

The vow can live somewhere you'll see it, too. I mean an actual thing, like a stuffed animal that's been on your bed forever, or a toy from when you were little, or a sticky note on the bathroom mirror. Just make sure it feels kind to you now.

Pick what it stands for on a calm, sober day, so it reminds you of the Nurturer and Protector you've been practicing. And then don't start asking it what to do! It only knows what you decided that day.

If you have a dog or cat who's usually calm, they can lie against your leg while you slow your exhales. They're alive, which none of the other things you're using are, and it isn't their job to make this go well. If they get up halfway through and go stretch out on the kitchen tiles, let them.
