A new roommate doesn't win you over with a speech on move-in day (hopefully, right?). The rent just has to keep showing up. Your Protector, the grown-up you, earns that protective part's trust about the same way, mostly out in ordinary life. The things you do can be small, like keeping a promise to yourself nobody else knew about. Or saying no to someone, and not texting an hour later to take it back. That second one can be really hard.

That part doesn't have to move out. It's been living there way longer than the grown-up you. It can keep watch while you take one small step toward your little one, and I think that's what it wants to see anyway, whether there's a grown-up here now who could stop if things got to be too much. If it says no, I'd listen. When it lets you, keep an eye on that part too, for any change at all. If it's been calling all this stupid, it might turn out to be scared of getting its hopes up. I wouldn't want to get mine up either. I'd tell it something like, "You don't have to hope yet, I'll keep coming back even when I mess up." Sometimes it doesn't say anything back for a while.
