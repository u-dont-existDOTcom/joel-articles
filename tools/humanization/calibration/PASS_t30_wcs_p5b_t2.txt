What would that grown-up do on a Wednesday? I'd start with maybe five percent of what they did in there. If it was comfort, that might be texting a friend "rough day" instead of "I'm fine," which can honestly be harder than the meditation was. Still, one small thing like that can teach your little one more than ten more good meditations. It doesn't have to happen after every session, and once isn't proof you've got it all down.
