Still, I wouldn't write off genuine feelings about who you're attracted to, or your gender, as "just intrusive thoughts" only because they're confusing or unwanted. Or decide it's "not really me" because the people around you would disapprove (I'm not pushing anyone toward or away from any orientation or gender). Maybe it's been there a long time, and you keep pushing it away. Nobody has to settle that now, and what you do meanwhile can follow what you care about. Over time, what keeps showing up in your actual life tells you more than how often a thought comes back. No single feeling settles it for you, and neither does anyone else, though a good therapist can help you see the pattern.
