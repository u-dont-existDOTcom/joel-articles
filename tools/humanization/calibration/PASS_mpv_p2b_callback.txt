You can protect your little one from yourself too, like by repairing after an internal attack. "What's an 'internal attack'?" Calling yourself an idiot for losing your keys again, for one. Your little one was listening, so you go back and apologize to them. If they're sarcastic about it ("Oh, so NOW you care?"), you let it go by instead of snapping back. They don't trust you yet, and you don't ask them to. They complain about something, and if they're right, you say so. All of that can show as clearly to them as the clean room did. It's evidence you're safe to be around and that you accept them, which love needs, but without warmth they can still feel like they're only being put up with.
