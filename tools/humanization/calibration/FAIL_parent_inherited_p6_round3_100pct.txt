The new grown-up voice doesn't have to win your little one over first, or win the argument inside you where the parent you inherited calls it fake, before it can start helping. The new grown-up can start outside you: a person who likes you as you are, a plan you wrote down on a calmer day, or one small thing you do to protect your little one. That plan can sound stiff or a bit embarrassing when you read it in a bad moment, but it doesn't have to sound right then to be worth following, and the wording can wait for a calm day.
