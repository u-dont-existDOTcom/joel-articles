Weeks in and nothing much is changing? I'd want to quit too, or else keep doing the exact same thing forever so I never had to find out. Sometimes those weeks were mostly reading about it and getting ready to, which can feel a lot like doing it (this article counts). And if it was comforting your little one, was the loving grown-up really there? Your little one would notice if it wasn't, the way you notice a hug from someone who's checking their phone. I'd borrow that grown-up first. Then try again, and see if your little one can tell the difference.
