Set one boundary. "What's a 'boundary'?", I might be asking. That looks like saying no to something that's costing you too much (money, time, whatever it is). Handle that task you've been putting off, or the tax man, or your DUIs in Maryland, even if all you do today is open the envelope. Keep one small promise to your little one. Clean your room! Your little one has had enough grown-ups who said sweet things and then did nothing, so this is how they start to believe you.
