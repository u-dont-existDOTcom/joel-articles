Give the Vow a Physical Reminder

The vow can live somewhere you'll see it, too. I mean an actual thing, like a stuffed animal that's been on your bed forever, or a toy from when you were little, or a sticky note on the bathroom mirror.

Pick what it stands for on a calm, sober day, so it reminds you of the Nurturer and Protector you've been practicing. And then don't start asking it what to do! It only knows what you knew when you put it there.
