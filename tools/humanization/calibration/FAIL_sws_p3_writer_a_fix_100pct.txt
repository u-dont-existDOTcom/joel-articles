Once you have a rough idea of who's in there, naming them isn't the end of it. They react to each other too, so notice what each reaction teaches the others (I think that matters more than the names). If a part of you that's trying to protect you warns you and actually gets heard, it doesn't have to get louder. But if the inherited critic voice answers every warning with "don't be such a baby," that part learns it has to keep shouting, and your little one is right there hearing both.
