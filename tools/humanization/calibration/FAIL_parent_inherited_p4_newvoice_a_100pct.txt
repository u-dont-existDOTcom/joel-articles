One place to look is the parent you inherited. I mean the way you learned to treat yourself from how people treated you growing up, long before you started building the Nurturer, Protector and Guide. For some people it's warm. For a lot of people it starts out as a critic. Or a warden, with rules like don't cry, don't be needy, don't get angry, don't embarrass us, don't get too independent. If yours was like that, your little one learned bit by bit to hide the crying and the anger so they could keep belonging. And the wanting too, and whatever embarrassed the family.
