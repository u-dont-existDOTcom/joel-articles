How many tries is enough? I really don't know, and I'd be suspicious of anyone who gives you a number, since some things shift in a day and some take about as long as learning guitar. If it's making things worse, or you just can't do it right now, I'd change course sooner. Not wanting to do it anymore can count too, but I'd hear that no out first, sudden or not, since it might be a part of you that's trying to protect you. And if what it said would happen keeps not happening when you're doing it right, I think you've learned something real about it. A clearly better-supported way is worth switching for too.
