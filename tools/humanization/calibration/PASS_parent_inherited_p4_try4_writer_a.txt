I'd look at the parent you inherited: how you learned to treat yourself from how people treated you growing up, long before you started building your Nurturer, Protector and Guide. It can be warm, but it's often more like a critic, or a warden with rules. The rules can even sound reasonable, like "You can come back to the table when you're done crying." If yours was like that, your little one learned bit by bit to hide what wasn't allowed, so they could keep belonging. They might still be doing it.
