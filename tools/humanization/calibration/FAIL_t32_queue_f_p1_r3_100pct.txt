Anxiety deserves the same kind of listening. It might really be warning you about something, overload maybe, or a boundary someone's about to cross. Being scared of something doesn't make it true, though, even when it feels like it does. I'd ask it what it's worried about and then look at what actually happens, without promising to do what it says. If you always steer around what it fears, you never find out whether it was right. You might find it's been right more than you gave it credit for, or that it cries wolf about the same thing every time. If it might be danger right now, or something in your body a doctor should see, I'd handle that first and check later whether the anxiety was right.
