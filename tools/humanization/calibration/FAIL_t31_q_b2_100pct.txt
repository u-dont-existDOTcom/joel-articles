And there's the opposite mistake. If it's your orientation or your gender in question, I wouldn't write it off as "just an intrusive thought" only because it's unwanted, or because your family wouldn't like it. You don't have to decide either way today. And if tomorrow's feeling points the other way, it's no more the "real you" than today's was, whatever anybody else tells you. What you keep finding with real people, and in how you actually live, will tell you more over time than the loudest feeling of the hour.
