When you look into why it feels fake, I'd start with the parent you inherited. I mean the way you learned to treat yourself from how people treated you growing up, long before your Nurturer, Protector and Guide. For some people it's warm. For a lot of people, though, it starts out as a critic or a warden, with rules like don't cry, don't be needy, don't get angry, don't embarrass us, don't get too independent. Where it was like that, your little one learned bit by bit to keep belonging by hiding the crying and the anger, but also the wanting, and whatever embarrassed the family.
