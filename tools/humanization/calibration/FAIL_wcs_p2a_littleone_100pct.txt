Some things should shift pretty fast, and some are more like learning guitar, so I wouldn't pick a magic number of tries. If it's making things worse, though, I'd change course sooner, since your little one is the one who has to sit through it. Same if it just isn't doable right now. Not wanting to do it anymore can count too, but I'd hear that no out first, sudden or not, since it might be a part of you that's trying to protect you. And if what the method said would happen keeps not happening, even when you're doing it right, or something clearly better supported comes along, that's a reason to switch.
