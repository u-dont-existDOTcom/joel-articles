### Two Common Protective Patterns

Two kinds of reaction really are trying to protect you, more often than not. The first one is on duty all the time, and it can sound like a skeptic: "You're just making this up." It may have spent years keeping you from being humiliated again, or left again. That's a long time to stay on guard. It might be tired by now, but it's experienced and good at what it does. It has probably watched you get excited about other healing things, then drop them.

The other kind usually doesn't use words. When you get close to something painful, there's a sudden pull to do anything but feel it, from numbing out to keeping busy. Maybe you get so sleepy you can hardly keep your eyes open. Or you're standing at the open fridge, not even hungry. In the moment it feels completely reasonable. That part of you may be stepping in because, as far as it can tell, there's no grown-up around who could hold what's coming up.

You might want to treat either one like an enemy, or take it as proof that you're bad at this. I get why. But that usually just gives it one more reason to stay in charge. Both of these parts probably started out trying to prevent something, like falling apart in front of people. They often keep at it even after it starts to hurt you.
