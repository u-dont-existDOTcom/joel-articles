Instead of asking which age of you deserves the blame, ask these: What was that age of you trying to get, or get away from? What did it understand back then? Which options could it actually see? And could it stand to take any of them? What happened because of the choice it made? What was missing? Maybe you didn't have the skills yet, or nobody was there to help or protect you. And what can you learn, or repair, or do differently now?
