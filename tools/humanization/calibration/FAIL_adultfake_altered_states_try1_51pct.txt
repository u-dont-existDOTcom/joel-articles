Sometimes the adult voice only stops feeling fake in an altered state, say on a psychedelic or deep in meditation. Your little one is suddenly right there, and the love or grief that sounded like therapy talk is obvious. Old memories can come up too, but how vivid they are doesn't prove they happened. Still, hang on to what you felt, even after you're sober and can't feel any of it. Then see if you can bring some of it back into ordinary life.
