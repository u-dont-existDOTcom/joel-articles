A full pause is the last resort, though a safety problem can force one on you. It still doesn't mean the relationship failed. Make it specific. What made it necessary, and what was the last level that was really workable? What visible change, plus your own say-so, would make a gentle return reasonable? Wait for that. You might wonder if you're ready yet, but don't keep testing lighter versions with your little one in the meantime, not even just picturing them. Come back at or below that last workable level. And if an earlier session was hard and you haven't checked how it landed yet, do that before stepping up again.
