If the day's over and you didn't do it, look at what actually happened before deciding whether it was fear, or being unsure what you were going for, or not knowing how, or exhaustion, or something practical. If you did it but didn't feel better right away, that isn't a failure, though checking it off doesn't prove it helped either. That's the "how would I know if it's helping?" question again. Keep or adjust each act by what it adds: are you any safer, closer to your little one, more capable, or did you enjoy it?
