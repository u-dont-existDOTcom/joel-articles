Bring the challenge back gradually, and only when you want to and you're recovering well. If you're not sure yet whether you want to, you can wait. Some people try to toughen themselves up in a cauldron, but what you're looking for is the edge where you can actually learn. Dreams and imagery that come up can show what feels important or frightening right now. Sometimes they show what feels missing, or what feels needed. They aren't evidence that what you saw in them happened, though, and shouldn't be used to confirm a memory or rebuild one. Or to suggest one.
