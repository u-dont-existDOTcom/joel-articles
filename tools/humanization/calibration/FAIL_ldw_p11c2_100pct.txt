Whichever one it picks on (you at five, as a teenager, as a younger adult, or just last year), punishing them now can't go back and give them what they didn't have, either. At each of those ages, you acted from what you could actually see, what you'd been taught, what scared you, whatever help you had, and the options you could reach then. That doesn't make every choice harmless, though. What you actually did can hurt people and damage a life, and the harm needs repair. Keeping everyone from getting hurt was never your job at five or fifteen.
