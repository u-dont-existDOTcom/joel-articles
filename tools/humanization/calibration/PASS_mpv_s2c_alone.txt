Some of it happens where nobody else can see. When your little one gets sarcastic with you, don't snap back. When they don't trust you, don't make them say they do. Not snapping back is a kind of love, and they'll notice it as much as a clean room, but it's not the whole thing. "So what else is there?" Well, when they complain and they're right, admit it. And when you catch yourself calling yourself an idiot (again), go back and say sorry to them, because they were the one listening.
