If you do want the relationship but the depth you're at is too much, change the dose. During an exercise you might think, "I'm not sure I could stop this and come back if I had to." Or you've lost track of where you are, or going inward is itself making things worse. Then stop the exercise and turn outward first. Don't try another inward technique to prove you can take it. When you're steadier, the next gentlest step might be something protective out in your life, or real support from a person. Or a short eyes-open hello to your little one, before any imagery or deeper talk.
