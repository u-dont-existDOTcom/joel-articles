Set one boundary. "What's a 'boundary'?", I might be asking. That looks like saying no to something that's costing you too much (money, time, whatever it is). Handle that task you've been putting off, or the tax man, or your DUIs in Maryland, even if all you do today is open the envelope. Keep one small promise to your little one. Clean your room! And if a relationship keeps making you less safe, meditate on why it makes you feel unsafe, and what keeps you in it, and whether either may be due to a lack of the healed inner child/inner adult relationship. If you're in danger, get safe first, which is not a small thing at all, I know.
