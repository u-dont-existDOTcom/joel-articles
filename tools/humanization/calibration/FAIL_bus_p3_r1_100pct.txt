Your Protector (I know, almost the same name, but this one's the grown-up you) earns that protective part's trust mostly out in ordinary life. Over time, that part sees what you actually do and starts to believe a trustworthy adult has shown up. One kind talk in your head usually won't outweigh years of evidence the other way, and I don't think it should. The things you do can be small, like keeping a promise to yourself nobody else knew about. Or saying no to someone, and not texting an hour later to take it back. That second one can be really hard.
