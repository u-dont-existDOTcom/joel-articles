Sometimes it's just a thought, though, and you don't have to make a new inner person out of every sentence your mind comes up with. Go with the simplest thing that helps, like noticing "Oh, that's thinking," letting it go by, and coming back to the room you're in. Some thoughts won't go by. If one keeps coming back with a fear or a plan or a demand, maybe sounding like you at a certain age, and it all hangs together, you could try talking with it as a part of you.
