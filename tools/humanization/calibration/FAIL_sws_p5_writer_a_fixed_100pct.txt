Sometimes that part does answer, even if all you get is a hunch. Hear it out, but only take advice that's loving and leaves you free. If it's pushing for something hard to undo, wait until you're calm, then see if that still fits what's actually happened (get somewhere safe first if you're in danger). Those checks are for advice, though, so if what comes is a feeling, even anger, you just listen. And if nothing comes, you can still watch what sets the reaction off and what it makes you do. Whatever you guess the reaction is trying to stop, it might be stopping other things too.
