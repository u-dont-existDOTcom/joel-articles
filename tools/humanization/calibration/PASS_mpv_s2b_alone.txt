Then there's the protecting nobody else sees. Your little one gets sarcastic with you, and you let it go by instead of snapping back. They don't trust you yet, and you don't ask them to. They complain about something, they're right, and you say so. You call yourself an idiot for losing your keys again, and then you apologize to them, since they're the one who was listening. All of that shows as clearly to them as the clean room did. It's proof you love them, but they can still tell the difference between being put up with and being loved.
