When you do get to your happy place, try replaying what the other person actually did. Say you told them a comment they made at dinner stung. Maybe they got defensive for a minute, and then later they came back and said, "Okay, I see it." Or maybe it turned into how hard their week has been, and somehow you ended the night reassuring them. If it's the second one, and it's the second one pretty much every time, the psychologist Lindsay Gibson has a whole shelf of books about people like that. Her word for them is emotionally immature, and one thing she suggests is just noticing what they're doing and what it does to you.
