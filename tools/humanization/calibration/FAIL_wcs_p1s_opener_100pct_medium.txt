If you've been at something for weeks and not much is changing, I think part of you might be hoping someone will just tell you to quit, and part of you might be scared to. Before either, I'd check whether you actually did the part you meant to practice, or mostly read about it and got ready to (reading this counts as getting ready). And if it was comforting your little one, was the loving grown-up really there? Your little one would notice if it wasn't, the way you notice a hug from someone who's checking their phone. I'd borrow that grown-up first, then come back to your little one.
