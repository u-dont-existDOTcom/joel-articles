Sometimes the exercise is the part that works. You tell your little one exactly what they needed to hear, and by Wednesday you're skipping lunch and saying yes to everything again. My first guess would be that I didn't go deep enough. I don't think that's it, though, and I don't think this one failed either. Maybe the grown-up just hasn't come out of the exercise with you yet. Next time you're about to text a friend "I'm fine," I'd think about what that grown-up would say instead, and try about five percent of it. "Rough day" might be enough. One small thing like that can teach your little one more than ten more good meditations. It doesn't have to happen after every session, and once isn't proof you've got it all down.
