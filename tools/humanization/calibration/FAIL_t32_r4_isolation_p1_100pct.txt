If being alone is part of the wound, reaching toward people can be one way the grown-up you takes care of your little one. You can call that reparenting if the inner-child language fits. If it doesn't, you don't need to. A decent parent wouldn't tell a lonely kid to become totally self-sufficient before they can see their friends. Sometimes the grown-up move is calling someone. Maybe you already know who. Or it's setting up a visit, or asking a social worker what's out there. Maybe it's getting yourself into a room where people share something real.
