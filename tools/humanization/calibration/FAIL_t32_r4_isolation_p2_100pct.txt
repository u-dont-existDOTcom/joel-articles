Look for more than one door. Who do you already trust? Which friendships go both ways? You could take a class, or volunteer. A spiritual community counts too, if it really fits you. Branching out is no reason to drop somebody good. Not every door has to open, either. Several imperfect supports usually hold up better than one partner or therapist being the only place you belong. Bring the Protector along too. Belonging doesn't make a group good, even when you finally feel wanted there. A high-control group isn't "support" to lean into. Neither is someone in authority who exploits people, or a relationship where you've actually been hurt or threatened.
