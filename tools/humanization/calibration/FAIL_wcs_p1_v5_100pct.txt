Say you've been at something for weeks and not much is changing. It's tempting to call the whole method a dud, or else to keep doing the exact same thing forever so you never have to admit it isn't working. Did you actually get to the part you meant to practice, or did you mostly read about it and get ready to? Reading this article counts as getting ready, sorry. 😅 And was the grown-up you needed really there? If you're trying to comfort your little one and the loving grown-up just isn't there yet, more loving sentences won't fix that. It's like getting hugged by someone who's checking their phone. I'd borrow that grown-up first, then come back to your little one.
