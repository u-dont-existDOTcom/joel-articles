# Section pairs (HV2): published paragraph and rewrite

From an essay about founding intentional communities. Each rewrite keeps the published paragraph's place in the essay.

## B1pub → B1r

CONTEXT BEFORE: A safe community cannot promise to keep everyone. If somebody poses a serious or generalized danger, repeatedly violates essential boundaries, or makes ordinary shared life impossible, ejection can be the right answer. Restorative work is useful when it can work on a reasonable timescale; nobody has a duty to turn the commune into a treatment program for a person who is endangering everyone else.

PUBLISHED:

The power to remove somebody is also one of the easiest powers to abuse. Decide the process before the name: what conduct triggers immediate protective action, who preserves evidence, who is recused, and how the person replies.

REWRITE:

It’s also one of the easiest powers to abuse. So work out the process before it’s about anyone in particular: what conduct triggers immediate protective action, who preserves evidence, who steps back from the decision, and how the person gets to reply.

CONTEXT AFTER: “Outside” does not always mean the nation-state.

## B2pub → B2r

CONTEXT BEFORE: Long visit. Stay long enough for inconvenience and conflict to appear. [...] Provisional membership. Define the period, rights, responsibilities, review process, and exit. Full membership. Land and shared-resource rights, voice, and long-term obligations follow the founding agreements.

PUBLISHED:

At each stage, assess concrete conduct rather than spiritual vocabulary or therapeutic fluency. Vibes are easy, but so is mistaking conformity for safety.

REWRITE:

At each stage, look at what the person actually does, not at whether they say “namaste” a lot. Vibes are easy, but so is mistaking conformity for safety.

CONTEXT AFTER: Before provisional membership, do more than hang out.

## B3pub → B3r

CONTEXT BEFORE: Correspond first. Has the person read the materials? [...] A thoughtful exchange costs far less than a six-month misunderstanding.

PUBLISHED:

Short visit. Spend a week or two participating in ordinary life. Most people can be charming for two weeks. Congratulations: you have established that they can be charming for two weeks. 😅

REWRITE:

Short visit. Spend a week or two taking part in everyday life. Most people can be charming for a couple of weeks. So congratulations, you’ve found out that they can be charming for a couple of weeks. 😅

CONTEXT AFTER: Long visit. Stay long enough for inconvenience and conflict to appear.

## B4pub → B4r

CONTEXT BEFORE: There is consolation in the fact that institutions and relationships do not die at the same time. American Commune follows two sisters raised at The Farm. When one later became ill, former members she had not seen in years reached out with support.

PUBLISHED:

My father carried the Bruderhof long after leaving it. It shaped the foundation he started, the film he made, the places he took me, and the questions I’m still asking.

REWRITE:

My father carried the Bruderhof with him after he left it. It shaped the foundation he started, the film he made, the places he took me to, and the questions I’m still asking.

CONTEXT AFTER: Even after the legal entity ends, some of what people learned to give one another may continue.
