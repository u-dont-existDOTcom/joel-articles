# Section pairs (HV1): published paragraph and rewrite

From an essay about founding intentional communities. Each rewrite keeps the published paragraph's place in the essay.

## A1pub → A1r

CONTEXT BEFORE: The section is "Resources, Land, and Exit Terms". Its heading comes just before.

PUBLISHED:

Whoever owns the land holds enormous power over the community.

REWRITE:

Whoever owns the land has enormous power over it.

CONTEXT AFTER: If the founder’s name is on the deed, you have a landlord with a philosophy. If three of twelve members own everything, you have two membership classes whether or not the welcome packet uses the word “equal.”

## A2pub → A2r

CONTEXT BEFORE: Then make the material system legible. A money-free community still has scarce things and bottlenecks. [...] Define exit rights in whatever unit makes sense—cash if the community uses it, but also tools, transport, temporary housing, stored goods, or continued use rights—so exit is not a paper right that works only when the community has spare money.

PUBLISHED:

Good people leave for good reasons: a dying parent, love, work, health, exhaustion, or a changed mind. If leaving means forfeiting everything contributed, staying becomes coercive.

REWRITE:

Good people leave for good reasons: a dying parent, love, work, health, exhaustion, or just a change of mind. If leaving means giving up the money you put in, staying becomes coercive.

CONTEXT AFTER: Write the exit terms while departure is still hypothetical.

## A3pub → A3r

CONTEXT BEFORE: The heading "Dissolution and Exit", then an illustration.

PUBLISHED:

Founders rarely want to discuss dissolution while choosing a name and imagining the first harvest. That is when the discussion needs to happen.

REWRITE:

Founders hardly ever want to talk about dissolution while they’re picking a name and dreaming about the first harvest. But that’s when the talk needs to happen.

CONTEXT AFTER: If the community ends, what happens to the land, outside debts, buildings, equipment, common stores or funds, and each member’s agreed rights?

## A4pub → A4r

CONTEXT BEFORE: A membership process protects existing members and newcomers. Nobody benefits from joining a group that cannot support what it has promised.

PUBLISHED:

Correspond first. Has the person read the materials? Do they understand why this community exists? Have they begun the relevant practices? A thoughtful exchange costs far less than a six-month misunderstanding.

REWRITE:

Correspond first. Has the person read the materials? Do they get why this community exists? Have they started the practices that matter here? A thoughtful back-and-forth costs a lot less.

CONTEXT AFTER: Short visit. Spend a week or two participating in ordinary life.
