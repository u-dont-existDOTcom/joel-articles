# Pairs: published paragraph and rewrite

## P1 → P1x

CONTEXT BEFORE: But no amount of gorgeous land will make up for the mess that is created when one person owns the land or has the final say about what will happen on it.

PUBLISHED P1:
The first three parts shape the community's inner life. The container gives that work a stable place to happen.

REWRITE P1x:
The first three parts are the inside of the container, and this part is the container itself.

CONTEXT AFTER: Skip one, and the group eventually has to deal with it under pressure.

## P2 → P2x

CONTEXT BEFORE: Everyone on the farm works one shift a day in the kitchen or the fields.

PUBLISHED P2:
The kitchen crew plans the week's meals. The garden team gives the kitchen a steady supply of greens through the summer.

REWRITE P2x:
The kitchen crew plans the week's meals, and the garden team is part of the food side of the community.

CONTEXT AFTER: In winter the greens come from the cold frames by the south wall.

## P3 → P3x

CONTEXT BEFORE: Money questions come up at every meeting.

PUBLISHED P3:
The treasurer keeps the accounts. The audit committee gives members a way to check the treasurer's work.

REWRITE P3x:
The treasurer keeps the books, and the audit committee is how members can check what the treasurer did.

CONTEXT AFTER: Nobody has found a mistake yet, but people sleep better.

## P4 → P4x

CONTEXT BEFORE: The houses sit in a ring around the old orchard.

PUBLISHED P4:
The well supplies water to all twelve houses.

REWRITE P4x:
All twelve houses get their water from the well.

CONTEXT AFTER: In a dry August it can run low by evening.
