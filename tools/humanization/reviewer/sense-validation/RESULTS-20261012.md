## What changed

- Every prompt that judges contradictions (`sense_section.txt`, `stance_check_prompt.py`, `grounding.txt`, `repeats.txt`) says to read the way people talk: "can't" often means "isn't allowed" or "I won't accept it", and a contradiction gets reported only where a reasonable reader would see one.
- `sense_section.txt` and `sense.txt`: before saying a paragraph never says why, or calling a claim one from nowhere, look for the reason in the paragraph's other sentences; a reason given in other words counts.
- `sense_section.txt`: a phrase whose best reading is clearly what the writer means is left out entirely, and not reported as garbled or awkward instead. Run E1 below, made before this line went in, called his clause "garbled" while giving the right reading.
