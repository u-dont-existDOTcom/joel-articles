## The rule
- An emoji goes in only if it's on the allowlist below, and only where a spot begs for one (`project-sources/MASTER-INSTRUCTIONS.md`, Emojis). Anything not on the allowlist counts as banned until Joel says yes to it.
- Each one is a change like any other: its paragraph alone and its section, with the headings, go through Pangram (E96). One that flips comes out.
- No gratuitous emojis. One that only labels a thing or an activity stays out: 🐶 after "dog", 🎶 after a song, 💆 after a massage, 🔥 after "burning" all came out on 2026-10-01. Joel: "dog emoji seemed gratuitous to me, we don't need an emoji just because it could be there."
- A feeling's emoji can stay even when the words name the feeling, if it gives the line its beat. Joel, 2026-10-01 15:59, on the 😌 after "Breathing easier.": "the breathing easier emoji was good though, otherwise, \"breathing easier\" sounds flat and it's too abrupt to jump to the next sentence for me". Test: read the line without it; if it goes flat, or the next sentence comes too fast, it's earning its place.
- ASCII can beat an emoji where it fits. Joel: "ai slop generally doesn't use ascii art like that so that is probably better to use where it fits but emojis are obviously more versatile."
- "(hehe)" is fine; "lol" isn't. Joel: "Note that lol is different from hehe, it's more wry." Wry humor is banned.
