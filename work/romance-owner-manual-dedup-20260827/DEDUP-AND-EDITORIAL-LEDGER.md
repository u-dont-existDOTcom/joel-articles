## Editorial-process lesson

The correct repair is adaptation/composition, not a new parallel framework. The repository already had section-level Mermaid architecture, paragraph-job/live-question checks, a purposeful-recurrence ledger, dependency audit, orphan audit, preservation proof, and cold audit. The failure was that a worker could certify these abstractly without materializing a multi-scale ledger and reading the assembled article literally from top to bottom. A second failure in R4 showed that even a correct dedup diagnosis can produce a non sequitur when a heading carrying transition logic is removed. A third correction showed that a prose-only export can make a native-video anchor look like unfinished prose. The durable gate must therefore track section/paragraph jobs, heading transition function, and native-object context together.
