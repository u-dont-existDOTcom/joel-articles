## Exact owner prose

### Long distance

I don’t think anyone wants to have a long-distance relationship, unless they’re not ready for a real in-person one, or it just so happens that their dream partner is currently far away. There is something super romantic about preferring one person no matter the distance. But it’s also not something we evolved to feel fulfilled by. And the less you’re physically together, the less you actually know each other. And actually, I would find it more romantic for my partner to say, “Let’s be together at any cost, physically” rather than “You should go take that better job and I’ll stay here.” 
If you’re just starting one of these, I’d suggest you ask your partner about how long they are comfortable maintaining distance, to see if it really matches what you want. If you’re already in a relationship of any type, it’s worth discussing how much distance you’d tolerate and for how long.
