## Candidate

Suppose you pick someone up at a bar and you both agree it's just for fun. You can both mean it. Your oxytocin and vasopressin aren't making the same promise.

Then the next morning, you’re wondering why they didn’t call. Well, not getting pulled in any further is the best outcome still available, actually, since you just played Russian Roulette with a potential life bond to a stranger.

“I don’t know whether I want something serious with you. I’m not pretending I do. I’d still like to have sex.”

That may be candid about what you expect now. It doesn’t settle what either of you will owe if the act creates something neither of you expected. Sex can make a child. If this girl gets pregnant, she’s gonna call you. Then what? You block her? Agree to child support? Try to convince her to abort when she thinks abortion is killing her child? This is what penetrative sex can do. No contraception is perfect.

With STIs I can at least tell you what I know and what I don’t. I can’t tell you in advance whether I’ll get attached. Neither can you. We can both mean “this is only sex” and still find out afterward that one of us got pulled in. If sex is pretty numb for both of us, maybe neither of us does.
