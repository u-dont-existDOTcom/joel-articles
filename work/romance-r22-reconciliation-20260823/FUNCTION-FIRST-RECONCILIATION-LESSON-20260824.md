# Function-first reconciliation lesson — 2026-08-24

Status: durable editorial-process correction derived from Romance r22 owner review. This file records the incident-specific lesson; the reusable rule is promoted into the repository review/humanization protocols.

## Failure

An older-vs-current feature audit identified useful sentences in the older Romance version and proposed transplanting them into r22 without first checking whether their underlying functions had already been re-realized elsewhere in the complete current section.

In `Two Pillars Don't Hold The Roof Up`, an older three-sentence block carried three functions:

1. mutual friends can notice patterns neither partner sees;
2. someone else can help/comfort a partner when the other partner has become the wrong person to do it;
3. people who know both partners can reality-check a one-sided story against the person they know.

The exact r22 section already carried functions 2 and 3 more concretely through the B. and H. material. Only function 1 was genuinely missing. Proposing the whole old block therefore duplicated two functions and reintroduced generic explanatory prose.

## Correct method

Historical prose is a **feature source, not an insertion source**.

For each old passage:

1. decompose it into separate rhetorical/semantic functions;
2. inspect the complete current natural section for each function, including different wording and concrete examples;
3. classify each old function as absent, partial, equivalent, or stronger/more concrete in current prose;
4. recover only the genuinely missing remainder;
5. integrate the smallest necessary realization;
6. cold-read the literal resulting paragraph and section for duplication, sequencing, and live-reader necessity;
7. check durable detector history before recommending verbatim old wording inside a known-green detector boundary.

A diff is evidence of changed wording, **not evidence of lost function**. Phrase mismatch must never be treated as semantic absence.

## Owner correction

For the one missing `Two Pillars` function, Joel preferred:

`But sometimes a friend who actually knows us both sees the pattern before either of us does.`

The complete old three-sentence block remains rejected.
