# Highest-value owner decisions

Most r22 changes are equal or better. The older version contributes a smaller number of features that are genuinely worth deciding rather than discarding:

1. **Talk:** explicit plan for future libido mismatch before resentment.
2. **Affection:** `don't turn simmer into homework`; perhaps `how wanted each person feels` as a barometer variable.
3. **Slow / brakes:** make explicit that the purpose is time to `figure out what this actually is`.
4. **Spiritual practice:** restore clearer testimonial attribution and reconsider `jade egg = basically the solo version` overstatement.
5. **Muses:** analytical `turn it into prose` mechanism; possibly the gentle/hypnotic-leadership idea.
6. **Two Pillars:** `hat model` / social-role overload and/or one concise statement of what mutual friends can actually do.
7. **Choosing together:** `I can hear a whole future in those two words` phrase.
8. **Attraction/exclusivity:** history→modern-vow bridge.
9. **Psychedelics:** concrete sober stress-test list (`irritated, jealous, broke, bored...`).
10. **Already in it:** explicit `say the pinkest elephant and see whether they stay in the conversation` diagnostic.

Lower-value/mostly stylistic choices:
- Talk `That conversation is already part of making love` compact line;
- Window `Ten profound conversations...` phrasing;
- slow/steady `first night isn't the final ceiling` explicit recap;
- patient `refusing to help would have been cold` nuance;
- Not A Performance `Do your female/male thing now` joke;
- After leaving `look at yourself as much as them` emphasis.

No meaningful older-version advantage found in the r22 Crucible safety compression or Queen of Orgasms compression.
