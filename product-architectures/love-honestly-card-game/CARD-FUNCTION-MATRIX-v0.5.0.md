- Dating-app anecdotes, Gandarussa, marriage-history exposition, and cervical-orgasm evidence are not independently turned into cards merely because they are memorable.
- Cervical sex enters only as a **shared aspiration/interest question** inside the making-love card, because the owner explicitly identified that as relationship-relevant.

## Constraints

- Add exactly six substantive cards and extend `sex-03`; total substantive deck becomes 87.
- All six new cards and revised `sex-03` apply to `discover`, `build`, `repair`, and `full` so both new and established couples can encounter them.
- Existing card IDs and storage schema v2 remain stable.
- Intentional-community cards must distinguish community **as a way of life** from community merely as friends/support around a private couple.
- Long-distance cards must preserve the owner's preference for physical togetherness while asking rather than prescribing each couple's tolerance.
- The Twin Flame card must permit `one destined person`, `repeatable sacred/archetypal connection`, `something else`, or `I don't use the concept` without making one answer correct.
- `sex-03` must ask about making love versus sex and eventual cervical-sex interest while preserving the right to prefer either, both at different times, or neither specific goal.
- No compatibility score, secret test, automatic external request, due-date lock, or spoken-answer storage is introduced.
