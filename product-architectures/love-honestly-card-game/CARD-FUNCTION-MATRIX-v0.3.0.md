## Whole-deck constraints

- No existing card ID is removed or renamed; v0.2.1 stored sessions continue to hydrate.
- New or revised functions receive unique `articleUpdateFunction` metadata.
- Every new high-stakes card has an explicit card-level reality experiment.
- Source tags support provenance and About-panel credits; citations do not clutter every play card.
- Joel's “Women are poetry; men are prose” formulation remains in the source ledger. The playable listen-or-solve prompt works across sexes without erasing the stated masculine/feminine observation.
- No card treats female success as pathology, lower male income as feminizing, flirtation as consent, bodily response as stay evidence, role-play as prescription, or spiritual intensity as durable awakening.
