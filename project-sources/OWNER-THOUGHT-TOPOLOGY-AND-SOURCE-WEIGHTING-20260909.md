# Owner Thought Topology and Source Weighting — Joel-byline Supplement

Date: 2026-09-09
Status: **ACTIVE REUSABLE EDITORIAL / HUMANIZATION SUPPLEMENT**

This supplements `project-sources/RHYTHM-AND-THOUGHT-SHAPE.md`, the preservation gate, source-integrity rules, and article-local owner-correction ledgers. It is not a phrase blacklist and does not authorize detector calls.

## Why this supplement exists

A recurring model failure is to preserve all the correct facts/functions while changing the **shape of the author's thought**. The model sees several usable source units and reorganizes them into editorial categories such as:

`modality -> benefit -> mechanism -> evidence -> caveat -> recommendation -> safety`

or into separate cards for each source type.

This can be semantically complete and still be wrong for Joel's byline because the source thought may have been one irregular causal movement with unequal emphasis.

## Blocking rule — preserve owner thought topology before editorial taxonomy

When a direct owner source supplies a recognizable order of attention, causality, surprise, weighting, or practical consequence, treat that topology as protected composition evidence.

Before drafting, recover:

- what thought actually came first;
- what caused the next thought;
- what was an aside versus the center;
- where the owner explicitly increased or decreased emphasis (`more importantly`, `the small problem`, `what really bothered me`, etc.);
- what practical question the thought ended on;
- which source/fact entered because the live thought needed it rather than because an article template expected it.

Do not replace that movement with a cleaner benefits/mechanism/evidence/safety architecture unless there is a concrete article-level reason to do so.

## Source bank is not thought architecture

Preservation units, citations, safety facts, modality names, evidence, caveats, and owner observations are verification inputs. They are not automatically paragraph jobs.

Do not create a new paragraph, heading, bullet, or mini-essay merely because another source type or preservation unit remains unused.

A heading can itself create false content pressure: once the model creates a standalone heading, it tends to fill it with a complete modality card even when the source only supplied one live relation. **Modality inclusion does not require subsection completeness.** A one-sentence mention or cross-reference may be the correct current realization while fuller treatment remains banked.

## Preserve asymmetry and weighting

If the owner gives one issue one sentence and then spends most of the thought on a second issue, preserve that asymmetry unless the article has a stronger reason not to.

Do not give a minor safety note, evidentiary caveat, or secondary benefit equal rhetorical weight merely because it is a protected function.

Do not move a minor complication to the end just because safety/caveat material conventionally closes a section. That can reverse the author's actual emphasis and expose preservation-ledger cleanup.

## Keep provenance with the actor

Do not turn a practitioner observation, third-party report, hypothetical, or general mechanism into Joel's first-person experience merely to make prose feel lived.

Examples of prohibited provenance drift:

- practitioner says a modality grounds people -> `I use it to ground myself`;
- general post-session need -> `after I spend an hour processing...`;
- plausible reaction -> invented autobiographical symptom or memory.

First-person prose is allowed only when the source establishes Joel's actual experience, judgment, intention, or reader-facing act.

## Evidence should annotate the live thought, not reset the prose

Research does not automatically deserve its own `There is actual research...` paragraph.

When evidence supports a specific claim already alive in the passage, prefer local citation or a short source note. Use a separate evidence paragraph only when the evidence itself creates a new live question, changes the argument, or needs enough explanation to matter independently.

Likewise, do not convert limitations into responsible-explainer aftercare when ordinary attribution already establishes the correct evidence plane.

## Real sequences are not automatically procedural prose

A source may genuinely contain an ordered practice sequence. Preserve the sequence's substantive relations, but do not automatically render every step as a clean algorithmic slot.

Ask why each move follows the prior one. If several functions can live more naturally at different article destinations, distribute them while preserving the cross-reference and source relation. A preservation ledger may remain complete even when one paragraph does not consume every step.

## Partial-boundary preservation

When owner/detector evidence shows that an earlier contiguous portion of a candidate is working and a later portion fails, do not reopen the working portion merely to create a new holistic rewrite.

Freeze the proven useful boundary provisionally, diagnose the first structural change at the failure point, and repair only the failing movement unless a genuine upstream defect is found.

Detector labels remain exact-boundary evidence, not authorship proof or permanent style authority.

## Required pre-delivery questions

For substantial Joel-byline source realization, answer privately:

1. Can I draw the owner's original thought movement without sorting it into article categories?
2. Did I preserve the owner's actual weighting and asymmetry?
3. Did any heading force me to invent enough material to make a complete subsection?
4. Did I move any observation to a different actor or invent first-person experience?
5. Did evidence, safety, or advice become a source-type reset rather than entering where the thought needed it?
6. Am I consuming preservation units because they remain on the ledger, or because the live thought has reached them?
7. If a sequence is real, did I preserve its causal relations without turning it into a staircase merely for clarity?
8. If part of the boundary already works, am I leaving it untouched?

A failure on any substantive question blocks delivery until repaired or explicitly justified.
