# Controversial-Topic Evidence Audit

Use this only as a specialized extension of `ARGUMENT-AND-EVIDENCE-ARCHITECTURE.md` for allegations, contested public figures, documentary disputes, reputational claims, or politically charged evidence packets. Do not substitute it for the universal protocol.

## Dated comparison matrix

| Date | Audited source claim | Subject's public account | Primary documentary record | Provenance/document ID | Knowledge status | Contradiction/update | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Track separately:

- relationship depth;
- actual knowledge;
- benefits, gifts, or financial reliance;
- physical or institutional access;
- reputation assistance;
- judgment and due diligence;
- later minimization or contradiction;
- knowing participation or criminal complicity.

Do not collapse these into one moral verdict.

## Documentary distinctions

- Public availability is not actual knowledge.
- An original email is not a forwarded email; preserve sender, recipient, date, thread context, and attachment provenance.
- A calendar entry or schedule is not a verified meeting.
- An aircraft or travel photograph proves only what the image, metadata, and corroborating records establish.
- Auto-transcripts may merge narrator commentary, archival quotation, documentary audio, and jokes; verify quotation boundaries against audio/video.
- Later admissions must update earlier defenses and every dependent article section.

## Source-access ledger

| Source | Requested/accessed | Full or partial | Original/secondary | Missing pages/attachments | Reliability issue | What cannot be concluded |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Do not hide inaccessible documents. Reflect access limits in confidence and wording.

## Output discipline

Apply the conclusion ladder. Distinguish serious judgment failure from knowing complicity. Do not treat failure to prove criminality as proof that no relationship, benefit, awareness, due-diligence failure, assistance, minimization, or contradiction occurred.

## Deterministic ledger use

For a substantial documentary allegation, use `argument_ledger.py` in `standard` or `full` mode when available. Create separate evidence IDs for original documents, forwards, schedules, verified meetings, photographs, travel records, public statements, and later admissions. Use independence groups so copies and retellings of one source are not counted as corroboration.

The script may identify which article and derivative destinations explicitly depend on a disputed premise. It may not decide motive, criminality, complicity, or fairness, and it may not edit the article.
