# Production Humanization Preflight — Joel-byline Protocol

Status: **BLOCKING before paid Pangram calls in production humanization/detector repair.** Direct owner workflow correction, 2026-08-23.

Use this with the preservation gate, humanization/coherence protocol, rhythm/thought-shape protocol, and detector-localization protocol. It separates **production article repair** from **detector research** so scarce Pangram calls are not spent discovering prose that editorial judgment should have repaired first.

## 1. Production Pangram calls validate; they do not draft

For a real Joel article, do not use Pangram as the default iterative writing partner.

Before a paid production call, produce the strongest faithful candidate you can by editorial reasoning alone. Pangram should normally answer:

> Did this fully considered candidate pass?

It should not normally answer:

> Which sentence should I fiddle with next?

A detector window remains localization evidence, not causal edit authority.

## 2. Diagnose the whole natural boundary before editing

After locating the relevant natural section/transition, make one **AI-shape diagnosis sweep** across the entire boundary.

Identify every model-shaped feature you actually believe is present, including where relevant:

- balanced therapist/explainer stance replacing Joel's committed judgment;
- false symmetry or obligatory `both sides` treatment;
- comprehensive qualification that sounds responsible but is not part of the live thought;
- recap, moral, or diagnosis after the example already made the point;
- generic caveats whose main job is to pre-empt objections;
- checklist/taxonomy structure that equalizes unequal ideas;
- overly neat thesis → mechanism → synthesis packaging;
- source summary that never turns into Joel's thought;
- generic transitions or miniature conclusions;
- equalized paragraph duration/cadence;
- abstract labels replacing concrete consequence or lived pressure;
- missing disagreement, uncertainty, asymmetry, opinion, humor, or stakes that genuinely exist in the source;
- a section continuing past its real stopping point into explanatory aftercare.

This is not a phrase blacklist. Do not manufacture roughness or delete a sound sentence merely because it resembles a known pattern.

## 3. Fix the interacting editorial problem as a whole

In production mode, a good candidate may change several realization-level variables at once.

If the passage is model-shaped because five features reinforce one another, repair all five coherently rather than spending five calls isolating them one by one. Scientific identifiability is not the production objective.

All edits remain bounded by:

1. current owner authority;
2. the preservation-unit ledger and frozen change whitelist;
3. provenance/claim/certainty/actor/chronology constraints;
4. article architecture and paragraph jobs;
5. the actual source pool and Joel's thought.

`Fix the whole thing` means fix the whole **authorized realization/architecture problem**. It never means sacrifice unique meaning for Pangram.

## 4. Repeat unpaid AI-shape cold audits before Pangram

After drafting, cold-read the complete natural boundary without looking at the detector score.

Ask:

- What, if anything, still sounds model-written here?
- Am I still giving a balanced therapy/explainer synthesis where Joel actually has a position?
- Is anything over-completed, checklist-shaped, generic, defensive, or too neatly closed?
- Does every sentence still have a live job?
- Does the passage stop when the thought stops?
- Does this sound like an author thinking/speaking from the actual stakes rather than a model producing the responsible account of the topic?

If you can still name a substantive AI-shape problem you genuinely believe, revise again **without spending Pangram**. Re-run preservation/architecture checks as required after substantive edits.

Do not let preservation success clear a style/shape objection. A required claim/function may force the cognition to remain while leaving its current realization fully editable. Likewise, do not clear several `mixed` findings one-by-one without checking whether they aggregate into one definite paragraph/section pattern.

Continue until the answer is effectively: **no, not at all**—not because the prose has been made quirky, but because there is no remaining model-shape diagnosis you actually endorse **after cumulative-pattern, reader-model, why-now, and antecedent checks**.

## 5. Paid-call surprise gate

When fresh-model audit evidence is part of the unpaid pre-Pangram review, `docs/HUMANIZATION-FRESH-CRITIC-GATE.md` is blocking. Freshness alone is not competence, and a global Human/AI verdict is not admission evidence.

Immediately before a production Pangram call ask:

> **If Pangram returned AI on this exact candidate, would I be genuinely surprised?**

If the answer is no, do not spend the call yet.

The candidate is detector-ready only after:

- preservation proof passes with zero unexplained substantive deltas;
- architecture/coherence gates pass;
- the AI-shape sweep has been exhausted editorially;
- fresh-model evidence, when used, may include a **full tell-ledger sweep** plus narrow observable defect audits, but never a global Human/AI authorship verdict;
- the full tell-ledger sweep, when used, returns every tell exactly once as PRESENT / ABSENT / UNCERTAIN; all PRESENT/UNCERTAIN rows are resolved before admission;
- for high-rigor fresh global sweeps, prefer an authenticated provider-native Claude CLI when it can provide the required Opus model/effort/output/isolation; otherwise use the authorized OpenRouter fallback. Do not transfer reasoning-effort calibration across surfaces: current OpenRouter-only evidence supports xhigh as the API fallback default and max for decision-changing unresolved/disputed tells; Claude CLI effort requires separate CLI-route calibration;
- every narrow model axis whose result affects admission has a current positive/negative calibration for that exact defect; malformed excerpt controls and hidden-authorship labels do not count;
- all applicable calibrated axes have no unresolved FAIL, and uncalibrated-axis model output is advisory only;
- scaffold/content-neutralization analysis, when used, is treated as a diagnostic probe rather than an authorship veto;
- no unresolved or cumulatively blocking `mixed` AI-shape finding remains after the direct editorial read;
- reader model, why-now, antecedent/referent, and any implicated cadence/overclosure checks pass independently of preservation;
- the exact boundary/hash/call-ledger safety checks pass.

A failed Pangram call can still reveal new context/boundary behavior. When that happens, diagnose the whole resulting boundary again rather than automatically toggling the highlighted sentence.

### 5a. Separate editorial context from detector certification boundary

The unpaid review boundary and the paid detector boundary serve different jobs.

- **Editorial/fresh-reader audit:** use enough surrounding natural context to judge why-now, audience, transitions, antecedents, and section fit.
- **First Pangram certification of a changed model-written unit:** submit the smallest complete changed reader-visible unit that can stand on its own (for example, the revised paragraph, H2, or section).
- **Natural-boundary Pangram:** run later only when context interaction or integrated publication behavior is itself decision-relevant.

Do not use a context-rich natural boundary as a substitute for the changed-unit measurement. In particular, if any neighboring prose is already known-bad, unresolved, or detector-red, exclude it from the first certification call for the changed unit. Otherwise a whole-boundary failure cannot tell whether the changed unit failed or the neighbor contaminated the score.

Execution order for a technically meaningful changed section is therefore:

**changed unit alone → interpret exact result → natural boundary only if still useful for integration/context**.

If a larger boundary was mistakenly tested first and contains known-bad neighboring prose, preserve that result as boundary-level evidence but do not treat it as evidence that the changed unit itself failed. The corrective next call is the changed unit alone, not another rewrite.

## 6. Controlled experiments belong to detector-research mode

Minimal pairs, one-factor edits, factorial designs, exact repeats, and mechanistic causal localization are valuable when **learning how Pangram behaves** is itself the task.

Use them in production only when one narrow uncertainty will genuinely change the next editorial decision and cannot be resolved well by editorial reasoning.

Do not spend a hard-capped production section budget on a ladder of controlled experiments merely because it is scientifically cleaner. Article production optimizes for the best faithful passage with few detector calls; detector research optimizes for causal knowledge. Keep those objectives separate.

If detector research is useful after a production candidate already works, run it as a separate evidence lane and do not let synthetic/minimal-pair prose become article authority.

## 7. Paid-call discipline

For production humanization:

- baseline measurement is useful when current status is unknown;
- after baseline, prefer one holistic, fully considered repair to several knowingly incomplete probes;
- every paid call must test a preflight-passing candidate or answer a narrow decision-changing diagnostic question;
- never pay for exact duplicate text unless a repeat is explicitly preregistered as detector research;
- keep the stable six-paid-call section cap;
- cache/resume exact prior work rather than resubmitting;
- preserve model/version/input SHA/result identity and lesson closeout;
- short snippets remain lower-confidence evidence and should not be endlessly optimized for confidence alone.

## 8. Romance evidence behind this correction

The 2026-08-23 Romance Affection audit showed the production failure mode. Six local calls included broad rewrites that retained substantially the same balanced relationship-advice architecture, one exact duplicate paid text, an over-compressed diagnostic, and finally a 100%-Human transition boundary that omitted much of Affection and therefore localized context rather than solving the full section.

Reviewing the prose holistically instead exposed the interacting model shape: balanced `opposite failure` framing, explicit `you need both` synthesis, checklist-like barometer explanation, generalized shared-responsibility counseling, and aftercare. Joel accepted a holistic repair direction addressing the pattern together and reported that the resulting passage tested **100% Human, medium confidence**.

The adjacent Casual experiments also showed that changing one highlighted region could flip unchanged neighboring prose. This reinforces the production rule: detector windows and sentences are not independent knobs.

Exact detector/research evidence lives in `u-dont-existDOTcom/pangram-humanization-lab`; this file governs how that evidence is used in Joel article production.
