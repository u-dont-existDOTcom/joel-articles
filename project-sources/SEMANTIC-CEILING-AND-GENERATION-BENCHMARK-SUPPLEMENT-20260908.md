# Semantic Ceiling and Generation-Benchmark Separation — 2026-09-08

Status: **ACTIVE EDITORIAL / GENERATION-METHOD SUPPLEMENT**.

This supplements, rather than replaces, the current humanization, preservation, cadence, reasoning-selection, and article-local authority rules.

## Core rule

**Do not lengthen a semantically complete article movement merely to obtain enough model-generated words to evaluate recurring cadence or topology.**

A production boundary and a generation benchmark have different adequacy requirements:

- **production adequacy:** the prose should stop when the source-backed reader-facing thought actually stops;
- **benchmark adequacy:** a test of recurring model cadence/topology needs enough contiguous model-written text for recurrence to become visible.

When those requirements conflict, do not sacrifice the article to the benchmark.

## Semantic ceiling

A **semantic ceiling** is reached when the authorized/source-backed local thought has made its necessary move and additional words would mainly:

- explain why the prior point mattered;
- invent cases, phenomenology, audience expectations, or clinical rules;
- generalize a local relation into a broad rule;
- add implications or caveats only to keep the passage going;
- restate the same relation in new vocabulary;
- manufacture a takeaway or landing.

At that point, stop the production prose even if the generated span is too short to diagnose model cadence reliably.

Short production prose is not a failed benchmark; it simply means that boundary is unsuitable for the benchmark question.

## Benchmark routing

If the current production slot is too narrow to expose recurring generator behavior:

1. keep the strongest short production realization separate;
2. do not claim that its brevity proves generator improvement;
3. move the cadence/topology test to a **richer natural boundary** whose source functions genuinely support a longer passage;
4. keep generator-evidence, owner contribution, source restoration, and production-quality ledgers separate.

Never pad with unrelated owner prose, unrelated Human prose, adjacent modalities, or source facts that do not belong to the live thought merely to reach a target length.

## Relation to owner-structure maps

Positive owner-derived thought maps remain useful, but they do not create extra semantic content. A map can guide how an authorized thought moves; it cannot justify extending a thought after its source-backed pressure is exhausted.

Likewise, withholding terminology may remove glossary-driven cadence without eliminating the model's tendency to overcomplete one narrow thought into a mini-essay. Audit for both.
