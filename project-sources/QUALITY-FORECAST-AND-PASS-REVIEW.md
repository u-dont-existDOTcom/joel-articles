# Quality Forecast, Calibration Sample, and Revision-Pass Review

## Before a major draft

For a long P3/P4 article or a voice-critical reconstruction, give a compact forecast before writing unless Joel explicitly requests a one-shot draft.

State:

- **Audience confidence:** low / medium / high — clarity about reader role, knowledge, relationship, intended action, stakes, register, and venue
- **Voice confidence:** low / medium / high
- **Material sufficiency:** thin / adequate / rich
- **Research readiness:** unscoped / partial / publication-ready
- **Visual readiness:** none / provisional / well supplied
- **Expected ceiling for this pass:** rough / competent / strong / publishable / exceptional
- **Main limiting factor:** one sentence

Be honest. Do not manufacture confidence because the protocol is elaborate.

## Approximate improvement bands

Percentages are heuristic relative uplift estimates, not measured guarantees. Explain the input that would produce the gain.

Typical examples:

- **About 10% better:** Joel confirms the target reader, desired informality/lightness, one missing personal detail, or one disputed claim; or supplies the current raw HTML instead of stripped text.
- **About 25% better:** a same-register exemplar, full source packet, approved outline, permission matrix, and one representative calibration section with line-level feedback.
- **About 50% better:** rich article-specific notes plus approved voice profile, full labeled corpus, canon facts, sitemap/article index, exact HTML, image/PDF corpus, primary sources, and Joel's corrections on a representative section.

The richer the existing inputs, the smaller the remaining marginal gain. Do not promise that more files automatically improve quality; badly labeled or AI-contaminated samples can make it worse.

## Informality and humor calibration

Infer the audience contract and register from the topic, current article, publication context, article-specific notes, and voice profile. State the best guess briefly before tone calibration:

- reader role/identity and knowledge level;
- relationship to the writer/subject;
- intended action, decision, or understanding;
- stakes and publication venue;
- deliberate section-level audience shifts.

Then state:

- informality: restrained / conversational / very informal;
- lightheartedness: sparse / intermittent / sustained baseline;
- humor: none / dry / playful / irreverent / mixed;
- serious zones that should remain comparatively straight.

Ask Joel only when a different answer would materially change the article.

## Coherence architecture gate before a representative sample

For P2S/P3/P4 reconstruction from interviews, notes, or an AI-shaped source, complete the private architecture card from `HUMANIZATION-AND-COHERENCE.md` before prose. Record:

- real pressure and reader stake;
- controlling claim and certainty;
- intellectual/lived route;
- actor, chronology, and causality;
- evidence roles and strongest complication;
- governing movement and paragraph jobs;
- stopping point;
- exact raw language retained and the reason for each substantial span.

A sample built before this gate may be fluent but remains structurally uncalibrated. Do not use detector feedback as a substitute for the card.

## Representative sample before the full article

For a new article over roughly 1,200 words, a major reconstruction, or uncertain mixed-register work:

1. choose one representative weak or central section;
2. write roughly 250–600 words in the inferred register;
3. state the tone/humor assumptions in one sentence;
4. obtain calibration before drafting the full piece.

Skip this checkpoint when Joel says to draft the whole article now or has already approved a representative section in the current conversation.

The approved section calibrates decisions, not sentence patterns. Do not clone its rhythm across every section.

## After each revision pass

For working drafts, provide a compact external assessment:

- **Current quality:** rough / competent / strong / publishable / exceptional
- **What improved most:** one sentence
- **Largest remaining weakness:** one sentence
- **Best next pass:** one specific action
- **Expected marginal benefit:** small / moderate / large
- **Over-editing risk:** low / medium / high

When Joel requests final article only, keep this outside the HTML or omit it. Once the piece is strong, recommend surgical rather than broad rewriting.

## Corpus is not a ceiling

A profile describes the writer; it does not freeze every weakness. When the corpus is thin or the writer wants growth:

- distinguish identity-bearing traits from craft limitations;
- offer a better structure, sharper evidence, more varied pacing, or stronger scene construction;
- show the proposed improvement in a sample;
- preserve the writer's worldview and actual material;
- do not silently turn “improvement” into generic prestige prose.

## Coherence-first detector-repair status

After every author-intent or detector-repair pass, report:

- **Coherence:** pass / fail — whether the passage reads as finished prose without the interview and in surrounding article context
- **Fidelity:** pass / fail — whether claim, actor, cause, certainty, attribution, scope, and unusual judgment remain correct
- **Semantic reset:** not needed / confirmed inherited thought / corrected wrong thought
- **Source-pool use:** selected coherently / transcript accumulation / question-order contamination
- **Architecture card:** complete / incomplete / inherited from source by mistake
- **Governing movement:** named / fragmented into modules
- **Raw-language handling:** fresh synthesis / justified exact reuse / clause mosaic
- **Verbatim dependencies:** zero / fully justified / unresolved
- **Paragraph chain:** pass / fail
- **Stopping point:** deliberate / recap / unresolved
- **Carry-forward bank:** not needed / current / unresolved items
- **Unused valuable input:** fully dispositioned / possible loss
- **Destination audit:** not yet due / completed
- **Final bank resolution:** complete / unresolved count
- **Opening-thesis architecture:** grounded / abstraction stack / repaired
- **Consolidation disclosure:** none / complete / missing explanation
- **Process provenance:** absent / intentionally relevant / leaked
- **Candidate baseline:** exact strongest coherent version currently controlling
- **Detector evidence:** document / sentence / boundary results kept distinct
- **Lock status:** exact coherent blocks and boundary context locked / pending retest / not lockable because architecture still fails
- **Owner correction status:** none / pure P1 recorded without retest / substantive correction pending retest / substantive corrected version retested
- **Learning ledger:** current / missing rows / one-off lesson improperly promoted
- **Reconstruction ceiling:** within limit / exceeded / author wording requested
- **Substantive corrections discovered:** exact claim, cause, actor, heading, attribution, link referent, omission, or `none`
- **Regression risk:** low / medium / high risk that the next paraphrase will reduce coherence or fidelity
- **Source span reviewed:** exact first/last block and neighboring headings; complete / excerpt-only / scan-PDF-only
- **Research access:** full text / abstract / snippet / secondhand / not applicable
- **Section provenance:** owner-authored untouched / owner-edited final / assistant-produced owner-accepted / owner-final scan-PDF / superseded assistant
- **Assistant recheck queue:** current / resolved / unresolved count
- **Cumulative omission audit:** current / stale / unresolved assistant-created items
- **Owner deletions:** resolved / accidentally banked or resurrected
- **Architecture attempts:** local surgical only / first architecture / second architecture / Joel rewrite required
- **Next-section advancement:** next source located and analyzed / exact questions ready / retrieval genuinely failed
- **Pass-scope disclosure:** global / current section / supplied block only

A detector-repair pass cannot be rated strong or publishable merely because the score improved. Coherence and fidelity must both pass; Joel's correction outranks the classifier.

## Evidentiary stability

Add these dimensions to every substantial research/polemic forecast and pass review:

- **Factual stability:** weak / provisional / stable
- **Premise stability:** whether any load-bearing premise remains vulnerable
- **Fairness to contrary evidence:** incomplete / adequate / strong
- **Conclusion calibration:** overstated / matched / overly timid
- **Rhetorical dependence:** whether weak evidence, jokes, insults, character claims, or legacy appeals carry the argument
- **Collapse risk:** low / medium / high risk that one correction would invalidate the article architecture
- **Evidence-stream independence:** established / partly dependent / unclear
- **Source-role map:** complete / source treated too simply
- **First-person research provenance:** supported / overclaimed / not applicable
- **Component attribution:** primary source / established theory / experience / synthesis / mechanism / analogy / countermodel separated or conflated
- **Philological claim status:** formula/grammar audit complete / provisional / not applicable
- **Mechanism-step discipline:** access / binding / unity / reflective knowing / self-ascription / appropriation kept distinct or collapsed

A draft cannot be rated publishable or exceptional merely because prose, structure, and voice are strong. A beautifully written argument with an unstable load-bearing premise remains structurally weak.

## Ledger burden and retirement point

When the deterministic ledger is used, report:

- **Ledger scope:** minimal / standard / full
- **Mapped claims:** total and load-bearing count
- **Dependency depth:** shallow / moderate / excessive
- **Mapping inflation risk:** low / medium / high
- **Retirement status:** still materially useful / decisive case stable / should stop expanding

The ledger has become counterproductive when new entries are mostly contextual, texture, duplicate, or remote dependencies; when every paragraph is being mapped; or when the decisive conclusion no longer changes. At that point, freeze the ledger and continue with surgical editorial work.
## Derivative stability for artifact-heavy work

Add:

- **Derivative stability:** untested / archival-source-valid / transfer-conversion-valid / regression-checked / destination-confirmed

For a final Substack artifact family, report separately:

- archival source fidelity and editor-body extraction;
- transfer-conversion validity, including wrapper, editor-lock removal, comment substitution, rich native-object preservation, and source-triggered paywall handling;
- compatibility regression status against the last confirmed helper/profile;
- per-object actual destination status for images, digest previews, video, Share, Subscribe, comments, and paywalls.

Do not describe a helper as complete when it is only archival-source-valid or transfer-conversion-valid. `Copied` is not a destination result.

## Review-family portability

For substantial annotated-review work, add:

- **Audience contract:** reader role, knowledge, relationship, intended action, stakes, register, and venue
- **Audience stability:** preserved / deliberate section shift / drift detected / repaired
- **Audience drift type:** none / role / expertise / goal / relationship / scope / action / register / assumed knowledge / stakes / venue
- **Project-state integrity:** current / stale / missing
- **Revision status:** `rNN-candidate` / `rNN-approved`; last approved baseline named and hashed
- **Raw comments:** immutable source file named, hashed, and counted
- **Comment reconciliation:** complete / unresolved count + owner-accepted exceptions / superseded decisions unresolved
- **Review baselines:** original-vs-current present; previous-delivery-vs-corrected present when required
- **Review row scope:** changed-passages-only / all-passages
- **Authoritative artifact:** exact revision ID, filename, date, hash, and superseded predecessor
- **Family packaging:** authoritative ZIP complete / omissions recorded
- **Package integrity:** untested / clean-unzip verified / checksums verified
- **Continuation handover:** present / not required
- **Handoff provenance ledger:** present / not required / missing
- **Handoff omission audit:** present / not required / missing
- **Handoff assistant-recheck queue:** present / not required / missing

Do not rate a review pass complete when comments were applied but movement, dependencies, audience, or derivative destinations remain unresolved.


## Review-interface stability for substantial revisions

Report: **untested / static-only regression / generator-selftested / interaction-tested / local-file confirmed / offline confirmed / destination-confirmed**. A static quick diff is not a completed review interface. State whether selected-text and whole-cell comments, decisions, four sliders, Copy JSON/Markdown, file exports, persistence, reasoning, filtering, search, exact hashes, and `joel-commentable-diff-review-v4` compatibility survived regeneration. Name the exact file tested; do not transfer a passing status from an earlier working copy.

## Developmental, epistemic, and propagated-master status

For substantial whole-article or detector-sensitive work, also report:

- **Article purpose:** mystery/problem, destination, route, and payoff recorded / incomplete
- **Opening promise:** stakes established without premature answer / missing / overexplained
- **Distinctiveness timing:** strongest Joel-specific material early enough / delayed behind generic material
- **Credibility budget:** sequenced / overspent early / high-cost claims unevaluable
- **Heading architecture:** argument accurately represented / hierarchy or ToC misrepresents importance
- **Section autonomy:** pass / sections require missing prior proof / excessive duplicated proof
- **Ending function:** named and performed / mismatched / hidden in appendix / recap
- **Epistemic planes:** world claim, author commitment, inferential status, and detector evidence separated / conflated
- **Citation boundary:** source support and author inference distinct / citation absorption detected
- **Falsifiability:** what would count against originality/lost-key claims recorded / self-sealing
- **Abductive discipline:** independent prior prediction and competing explanations checked / proof inflation
- **Evidence timing:** locally placed / point-and-move completed / fake hedging
- **Experience layers:** experience / mechanism / ontology / universality / reader prediction separated or collapsed
- **Quality floor:** fidelity pass; coherence no regression; editorial/article-function no regression; owner preference no regression when available
- **Current master:** propagated / pending reconstruction / stale
- **Correction closure:** closed count / open count and current state
- **Body access:** reference known / partial / full / installed / verified
- **Master completeness:** no assembly gaps / blocked by named gap

## Correction-dataset and evaluation status

When developing the skill rather than one article, report:

- **Example schema:** exact source/candidate/owner text, neighbors, hashes, provenance, conceptual correction, detector boundary, and quality judgment complete / incomplete
- **Development set:** frozen version and count
- **Holdout:** frozen, never used for rule creation / contaminated / absent
- **Controls:** human false positives, AI false negatives, detector-green bad prose, P1, heading, boundary, movement, expansion, and compression cases represented / gaps
- **Blind quality review:** fidelity/coherence/editorial/article-function ranking completed before detector results / contaminated by detector visibility
- **Detector logging:** exact hash, boundaries, model/version/date, repeated-call stability, sentence/document/boundary levels separated
- **Rule promotion:** recurring cross-case support / one-off / retired after regression
- **Short-excerpt anti-halo:** prose shape judged before topic/truth/provenance/detector / contaminated
- **Content-neutralization:** portable architecture tested / not tested
- **Minimal-pair boundary:** complete passage + neighbors retained / sentence-only or token-only record
- **Lexical precision effect:** exact delta and editorial operation recorded / magic-word rule
- **Micro-verdict calibration:** qualitative / percentages explicitly requested / pseudo-precise

Use a multi-objective gate. A detector-only win is a failed candidate. Stop collecting random prose when new examples merely confirm existing rules; target roughly 10–20 high-information pairs or triples across openings, conclusions, research, tender material, practical safety, register transitions, movement-only edits, substantial expansions, and cases where Joel preferred the assistant wording or changed very little.
