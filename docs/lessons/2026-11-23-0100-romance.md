got to Human High to a fresh writer with rule 3, "ADD NOTHING". It added nothing, and the three paragraphs where round

both rounds 8 of the 14 paragraphs he fixed read Human High by themselves from the lessons alone, and 437 Human with a
Medium window; his own versions all read Human High. What the four others show, each a way his fix went that the brief
didn't yet say:
- a list of concrete things from life (his "nature, music, poetry, art, flowers, food") is not a list to cut: the
  rewrite that cut it to two read Human (Low) where round 1, which kept it, read High; and cutting "coerced" from
  "abused, coerced, or seriously deceived" lost a kind of harm. He cut an item only where it overlapped another or was
  part of it, and said so ("to whom and how publicly overlapped", "a subset of the others");
- a contrast or a pivot said in general words reads AI even when everything around it is fixed: "is different from
  becoming the permanent support system for a man who won't take responsibility" after the story already told it, and
  "But none of that by itself explains why", pointing back at a list of values without naming them (AI, High, where
  round 1's "it takes something more to explain why" had read Human). The writer kept both on purpose ("its contrast
  stays"); his fixes keep the concrete half and name what the pivot points back to;
- a question in two halves whose second half nobody could answer as asked ("what counts as betrayal, and what's just a
