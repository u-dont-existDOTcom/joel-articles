## From his fixes

- **somatic-2026-11-27g. "And" between two clauses is a fake connection when the second isn't simply added to the first;
  say how they relate.** His note on IC2: "\"and \" was a fake connection between clauses, don't do that." Mine: "They
  both treat the pain as real, and they've helped people with long-lasting back, muscle and joint pain." His: "They've
  helped people with long-lasting back, muscle and joint pain, by changing their relationship to the pain without
  pretending it's not there." ([Human High](https://www.pangram.com/history/81b522f7-4574-4678-87e9-9ea7f544d323), after
  six tries of mine that read Medium at best.) Applied to A1's AEDP paragraph ("works to keep you from feeling alone ...,
  and when some of their care does get in, they ask ...", two acts in one sentence): the section went from
  [24% AI](https://www.pangram.com/history/79b83fb1-6ec3-4eb8-9a64-0e2e66992e8e) to
  [17%](https://www.pangram.com/history/6abb3f4b-54ab-41e5-8b4f-4e9c660a244b).

- **somatic-2026-11-27h. Say how a thing helps, not the stance it takes.** His note: "\"they treat the pain as real\" was
  not explaining how they help"; his "by changing their relationship to the pain" says how. Applied to the AEDP paragraph's
  "works to keep you from feeling alone with whatever comes up" (the therapist's aim): "stays with you through whatever
  comes up, so you're not alone with it" (what they do); the section read
  [Human Written, no AI window](https://www.pangram.com/history/bd7b5cbd-12b7-4a4b-99f3-693d4c9e2eba).

- **somatic-2026-11-27i. A paragraph that opens on a study ("In a 2024 trial of veterans aged 60 and up, this therapy beat
  CBT: ...") reads AI when nothing ties it to what came before.** His note: "you're always starting like this \"in a 2024
  trial of..\" that's an ai tell, especially because it does not connect, not introduced, nothing.." His: "And in 2024 we
  got evidence EAET works better than CBT, at least in veterans over age 60. 63% of them had their pain drop by at least
  30% after EAET, compared to 17% with CBT." The joiner, what we learned, his hedge ("at least in"), then the numbers.

- **somatic-2026-11-27j. A degree or kind word a reader can take two ways is said exactly.** His: "too much" became "too
  intense sometimes" ("you meant too intense, not too many feelings, and sometimes, not all the time"); "stress and
  feelings about the people in your life" became "stressful feelings about the people in your life" ("good or bad
  feelings?"); "You also slowly go back to things ..." became "As you're able, you then slowly go back to things ..." (the
  pace and the order).

- **somatic-2026-11-27k. Whose, and when.** "The therapist catches you at it each time" became "The ISTDP therapist catches
  you each time they notice you slipping": which therapist, with three in the section, and what "at it" is. Applied:
  "The AEDP therapist" for the AEDP paragraph's "The therapist". With two therapies to tell apart he gave each its short
  form in brackets where it is named ("(PRT)", "(EAET)") and used them where the reader has to choose ("I haven't found a
  trial comparing EAET with PRT").

- **somatic-2026-11-27l. A closing wish is a short list of plain acts.** "I'd want them to say they'd slow down, and that
  they'd listen." (Human Low at best in six tries) became "I'd want to hear that they would change the approach, listen to
  me and be gentler." ([Human High](https://www.pangram.com/history/598494c2-0559-4fb2-8ce8-2794d1373d9c)).
