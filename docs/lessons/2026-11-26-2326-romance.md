# Lessons from the turn of his message of 2026-11-26 23:26 UTC (romance lane, the card game)

His message, after playing the game on his own: "ok i tried playing by myself. one thing i would change is the top text
should be the biggest heading, because people read the biggest one first and that's the one that explains what this is
about." The record: [r46](../../articles/romance/humanize/fix-20261107/r46/README.md).

- **romance-2026-11-26d. The biggest text on a screen is read first, so it says what the screen is about.** The
  one-person label screen of v0.9.0 copied the couples' pattern: a small line naming the step ("Where this card leaves
  you") over a big instruction ("Pick the one that fits best."). The reader's eye went to the instruction and had to
  work back up to learn what it was for. How this could have been right without his asking: the design skill's first
  check of a screen is its order of reading (what is seen first, second, third), and a new screen is built by asking
  what it is about before copying the layout of a screen beside it. When a screen is built from another, its heading
  is read again for whether it names the screen's question or only tells the player what to do.
