## romance-2026-11-22a: a marking page shows the whole article; a cut is rare, long and shown

Joel: "it would have helped if you just had the whole page so i could see the whole context, make that a rule from now on
to show me all the context so the part i am looking at makes sense pls" and "make it more clear when you cut parts out
(which should not happen unless it would take up a lot of space, like don't cut out one paragraph to save space, that's
just annoying now i have to look at the orig article). make that a repo rule".

What happened: the page's Markdown (`PAGE-T4.md`) held only the 62 card paragraphs under their headings. The rule that a
marking page is "the whole article" (gate E265) was there; the builder couldn't tell a whole article from an excerpt, so
nothing stopped it. Now `tools/humanization/suggestion_review.py` (1.12) refuses a page built from 2026-11-22 unless its
REVIEW.json names the whole article it shows (`"whole"`) and the page holds every block of it in order. A stretch may be
left out only when it is at least 8 paragraphs and 800 words with no card in it, between `<!-- CUT -->` and
`<!-- /CUT -->` lines; the page shows it as a dashed box that says how much is left out, from which words to which, and
opens to show it, so he never needs the original. The article's pictures and videos show where they sit, as boxes with
their links (a YouTube video links to its youtube.com page; no players on the page). Tests: `tests/test_suggestion_review.py`,
`WholePageTests`.

## romance-2026-11-22d: what his 59 versions changed (ways 18 to 21 of the read against his fixes)

Read against the paragraphs they replaced, with his comments. Four kinds of fix recur that `his_fixes_brief.md` didn't
name yet; they are its ways 18 to 21.

- **18. Short stand-alone lines.** A two-to-six-word sentence that sums up, labels or turns: "It's philosophical.", "That
  was my mistake.", "I like that idea.", "Based on what?", "It doesn't prove anything.", "That wasn't the whole story.",
  "Everything.", "Anyway.", "See people together. Go places." His comments: "the ai tell is the short plain statements.
  feels robotic. I am robot. I like food. I drink water. I speak English."; "pangram hates those short lines, they are
  becoming Ai territory sadly"; "short sentences like that trip up pangram unfrotunately as ai has colonized them". His
  fixes join the line to the sentence it belongs to or give it what only he would say ("It's philosophical, and it bites.";
  "But could I explain that to an objective friend?"; "But that wasn't even really 1% of the whole story."). A short line
  of speech or a question in a story stays ("Then what? You block her?").
- **19. A list of three, or a run of questions where one is part of another.** "We're twin flames. She knows my motives.
  I'm Lord Krishna." (two quoted lines left); "How dependable are we?" ("a subset of the others and we don't need 3");
  "how much you say, and to whom, and how publicly" ("to whom and how publicly overlapped. plus list of 3"); "share a
  home, raise children, support each other financially, take care of each other, maybe have sex" ("too much of a
  listicle"). He keeps the items that carry the point, gives one a case from life ("why you spend hours playing with your
  cat"), and turns a question into its two real options ("Is that a pattern, or just life getting in the way normally?").
- **20. A sentence about the article or a section that isn't true where it stands.** "Everything I've been putting
  forward so far ..." after sections that weren't all that. His: "Much of this article so far has leaned toward ...".
- **21. A label for the thing instead of the thing.** "She was lying, but 'she lied' only named the momentary behavior";
  "What counts as betrayal, and what's just a sign we need a serious talk?"; "her jealousy was not simply tracking what
  I was doing in the present"; "safety gives the space for desire" ("that other ai tell of an abstract concept doing
  something"); "announcing" ("another ai tell"). His fixes say what it is: "Non-strategic lying is literally diagnosed as
  'pathological lying'", "Is that, in and of itself, a betrayal, since it's not under our control?", "her jealousy wasn't
  about what I was doing then and there", "feeling safe gives the space for desire".

Already in the rules, and missed on the page: "doesn't prove" (his preference), "X does not Y" (his preference), the point
first (73, 219), a reason spelled out (107's "so that we don't just abandon our children", 215's "rather than as a reaction
after you see something is unsustainable").
