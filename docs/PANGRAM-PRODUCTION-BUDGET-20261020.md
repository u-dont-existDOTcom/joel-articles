# Production Pangram budget, prediction and owner-escalation rule

**ACTIVE — direct Joel correction, 2026-10-20; applies to every Joel-byline article and every production humanization worker, including ChatGPT, Claude, Emulate-assisted, GUI, API and Codex execution.**

This is the normative `joel-articles` production testing policy. The Pangram lab inherits it for Joel article production and keeps the detector transport/cache/accounting implementation. It supersedes the 2026-09-26 "no limit on checks per turn" permission and tighter production ceilings take precedence over older six-call *section* safety caps and aggregate-call accounting exemptions. It does **not** impose production caps on independently authorized, budgeted detector-method research experiments. Explicit fresh owner authorization is required for extra production submissions.

## Latest owner correction — six hypothesis-gated tries per paragraph (2026-10-20)

**ACTIVE; supersedes the earlier two-try paragraph ceiling.** A paragraph may require a sequence of **up to six new Pangram submissions total** when resolving one failing sentence reveals a *different* failing sentence in Pangram 4.1's shading. **Six is a safety ceiling, never a target or prepaid allowance.** Stop at the first point the worker lacks a clear, specific, faithful reason to test again.

**New submission admission after any failed result:**

- First inspect the exact recorded GUI sentence shading, actual verdict and *real contiguous* context. Name the precise target sentence(s) still red or suspiciously light green; distinguish a **newly exposed sentence** from the **same unchanged failure**. The latter must not be rebranded as a new issue or a new paragraph.
- Before editing, name the concrete AI tell or context/meaning mechanism and the Human tells that should survive. Specify exactly what will change, why that change should address the observed problem, what would count as improvement, and the Human/AI/context-stability prediction **before** another submission.
- Make one substantive, meaning-preserving repair, pass the preservation, cold-read and architecture checks without detector expenditure, then evaluate whether a new Pangram call has information value. A fresh red sentence exposed by a successful repair is a legitimate *candidate* for the next iteration; a punctuation roulette, cosmetic synonym swap or repeated unchanged hypothesis is not.
- **Stop and ask Joel immediately** whenever no credible next diagnosis exists, even at attempt one. After two successive **unexplained prediction misses on the same unresolved target**, or repetition of an ineffective mechanism without a distinct reason, stop and ask Joel to identify the Human/AI tells. Two incorrect predictions about *different, well-diagnosed, newly exposed sentences* do not automatically end the paragraph's six-attempt allowance. Record every miss and its explanation in the scorecard; never manufacture a post-hoc prediction.
- For any one paragraph lineage, count **all** potentially paid new attempts, including the initial check, across every variant/chat/transport/worker. Recover cached results and ambiguous submissions before another action; an ambiguous submission occupies one slot unless proven unsubmitted. **Never exceed six without a new explicit owner decision.** The former two-attempt limit and unconditional two-prediction-miss stop are superseded only as stated here.
- Keep work to one section or approximately five paragraphs at once. Section certification stays one initial plus at most one justified retest **after** paragraph work; whole-article certification remains final. The historical six-call *section* safeguard is not a second aggregate limit on independent paragraph repairs in this updated production lane, but does not grant a free-standing right to test six times on every paragraph. Preserve total spend visibility and don't conceal repeated submissions by relabeling them research.
- If the GUI reports zero credits or the result is ambiguous/unavailable, **stop new paid work** regardless of remaining paragraph attempts. Do not spend just to test these instructions.

## Production scope and submission ceilings

1. **One small active work unit.** Draft/review at most **one natural section or approximately five consecutive paragraphs** at a time. For a long section, work in batches of up to five paragraphs, but do **not** detector-test its entire section during each batch. This rule includes workers running automatic batches and prompts to Emulate or other helpers.
2. **Paragraph first.** Preserve the already-accepted Human text. Record owner-supplied valid Pangram outcomes without resubmission. For changed/untested prose, freeze and test each detector-eligible paragraph *individually* after the editorial preflight. A Human *section* never hides a red individual paragraph. Paragraphs below Pangram's supported minimum are `UNMEASURABLE_ALONE`; do not pad with unrelated Human prose to manufacture an individual pass. Ask Joel about a genuinely unresolved short-paragraph exception.
3. **At most six new paid/credit-consuming submissions for one paragraph repair lineage**, including the first considered test. Every continuation requires a written *specific new causal diagnosis and expected improvement*, especially when fixing one sentence reveals another red/light-green sentence in context. Stop immediately without such a hypothesis; six is a hard maximum, not a routine budget. No sentence/heading/punctuation/whitespace roulette; the same lineage retains its identity across wording variants, sections, batches, model sessions, GUI/API routes, branches and chats. An ambiguity that may have submitted occupies one slot pending History/recovery; cache/readback uses none.
4. **Prediction-error brake.** Before *every* new call, predict `Human` or `AI` and explain actual words/structure supporting the prediction; record the individual GUI sentence-shading expectation separately. Do not pay to confirm obviously AI-shaped prose. After any wrong prediction, record the miss, diagnose it, and compare the exact currently flagged sentence to the previous result. **Stop on two successive unexplained misses for the same unresolved underlying target**, or immediately on no credible diagnosis. When a justified repair exposes a demonstrably *different* sentence, a fresh specific hypothesis may justify another call under the six-attempt paragraph ceiling; it does not reset the lineage or hide previous misses.
5. **Section certification is downstream, not an editing loop.** Only after the section's detector-eligible paragraphs have individually passed Human (or have explicit recorded Joel exceptions) and semantic/fidelity/architecture gates pass may a worker check the complete natural section once, with its real headings and reader-visible surface. Allow **at most one more section-level check** after a justified, substantive and localized repair; otherwise stop and ask Joel. Prefer reviewing a *specific* two-paragraph interaction offline before the second paid section submission; diagnostic pair measurements are exceptional, not a route to unlimited calls. Do not check the same whole section repeatedly after each paragraph, even if its text changed.
6. **Whole-article certification is final.** Do not run whole-article or large multi-section Pangram checks during ordinary paragraph/section iteration. After all sections and individual paragraphs are editorially accepted and locally measured, make **one final exact reader-visible whole-article** certification call if the task requires it. If it fails, localize the affected boundary using the existing result, repair the local issue, and allow **at most one additional whole-article certification call** for the newly assembled final draft. Further article-wide tests need explicit fresh Joel authorization. An edit invalidates the old *status*, but does not automatically justify a new costly test before the article is again at final readiness.
7. **No batch loopholes.** The six-attempt ceiling counts submissions for the same *paragraph/repair lineage*, not number of texts in a single API request. A whole section/full article is not recategorized as a paragraph, and large article checks do not become exempt merely because historical section caps did not apply to aggregates. New strategy, new task ID, "scientific test", and GUI/API substitution are not budget resets. If genuine detector research is separately authorized, preserve a distinct frozen experiment design, call budget and evidence plane. Never promote its synthetic probes as article prose.
8. **Transport and spend.** Follow the owner's current GUI preference and cost-routing policy; GUI is not assumed free or immune from measured-count limits. Before submission, check cache, History, pending task IDs, call ledger and exact text identity. Record the precise tested scope, word count, detector route/model/version, prior prediction, reason this call can change a decision, budget slots remaining, and cost estimate only when supported by actual rate data. Do not infer remaining credits from a provider error. Never run a paid detector request just to validate this policy.

These ceilings bound *new production Pangram submissions*. Non-billable editorial reviews, source-preservation and coherence checks still occur as needed; they are not license for endless autonomous paraphrase rounds. If a paragraph has no credible next repair hypothesis—or reaches six attempts without passing—request the missing owner insight immediately. Six attempts are not required before asking, and changing a different sentence does not reset the lineage.

## Mandatory escalation when blocked

Stop before another Pangram submission; present the **literal paragraph with enough adjacent context**, not a path or paragraph number alone. Include the frozen Human/AI predictions and reasons, all available measured classifications/shadings (up to six), exact contiguous boundary, any apparent AI tells, **the positive Human tells**, the currently blocked sentence, what was learned from earlier misses, and the substantive claims/functions a rewrite cannot sacrifice.

When no credible AI tell remains, use Joel's requested form, without falsely claiming a pass:

> I can't find AI tells here, and here are the human tells I see, but it doesn't pass. Can you explain?

Then show what the worker sees in that exact prose and ask for Joel's explanation or first-person realization. Await his input on that span; continue independent work elsewhere without spending on the blocked span. If Joel judges a paragraph good enough despite Pangram, record an explicit **owner exception** rather than claiming Human certification.

## Prediction scorecard and reporting

- Before any Pangram call, append an immutable prediction with the exact text/hash, *natural scope*, Human/AI classification, cited tell evidence, intended next decision and try number. Do not fabricate retrospective guesses or call known AI prose a prediction success.
- After a measured call, append the actual result/model/version, billable-status/route, accuracy bit, mistake type and owner teaching. Rebuild the existing article's scorecard and missed-predictions ledger after each check-bearing session; verify the dashboard actually points at its latest data when the dashboard is used. Other articles follow the same schema or explicitly link their own equivalent.
- Report **cumulative and most recent rolling-window accuracy with sample sizes** and comparison to an always-AI baseline; keep 85% a measured rolling reference/goal rather than a boast. Do not pool short-snippet, paragraph and aggregate accuracy without showing the boundary mix. Distinguish Pangram failure from prediction failure and faithfully repaired text from detector camouflage.
- Include the number and scope of *new* Pangram submissions and the per-paragraph/section/article budget remaining in worker receipts. At the stop point, show the concrete owner question without automatically queuing more paid checks.
- **The tool for all of this, every lane (2026-10-20, Joel at 17:38 UTC: "individual article work scorecards which should also feed into universal scorecard and optimization work"):** `tools/humanization/pangram_ledger.py`. Each lane keeps one append-only `pangram-ledger.jsonl`, listed in `tools/humanization/pangram_lanes.json`. `predict` writes the prediction before the call (scope, Human/AI, chance, AI tells, human tells, diagnosis, what the call decides, the try number) and refuses a call this rule forbids: no evidence, a lineage past its tries (six for a paragraph, two for a section or a whole article), a submission with no result yet, a retry with no diagnosis, two unexplained misses in a row, or the same text twice. `result` (or `results` for a reader's file) scores it, `explain` records each miss's cause, `status` shows each lineage's tries, `check` refuses a batch with an unpredicted text, and `build` writes each lane's `SCORECARD.md` and the universal `state/PANGRAM-SCORECARD.md` (detector versions apart, the boundary mix, calibration, misses by cause). It reads each lane's older records too, with one scoring rule for every lane. A caller that uses it has a mechanical counter for this rule; its limits are the floor of the rule, and the diagnosis and stop judgments are still the worker's.
