# Semantic-Card Succession Attack — 2026-08-31

Status: ACTIVE OWNER-DIRECTED GENERATION CORRECTION for the Somatic Introduction experiment.

## Failure identified

The clause-level refractory rule reduced local overcompletion but left a larger AI pattern intact: each paragraph or sentence still arrived as one clean semantic card (`deep/stopping`, `inner child`, `body/safety`, `calm`) and then handed off to the next card.

## Attack

Do not admit a new source topic merely because it is waiting in the semantic inventory. A new thought may enter only if it actually disturbs, mutates, complicates, or redirects the thought already alive on the page.

Operationally:

- no topic-card queue;
- no `now the inner-child point`, `then the body point`, or equivalent hidden routing;
- prefer unresolved cross-contamination between thoughts over completing one topic and opening another;
- if a thought enters and forms a self-contained conceptual unit, reject or fracture it before output;
- uncertainty about whether two thoughts are the same problem may remain unresolved in the prose;
- source coverage remains deferred during this exploratory pass and will be reconciled later under the normal preservation gate.
