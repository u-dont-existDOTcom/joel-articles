# Owner correction — generate freely, reconcile meaning afterward

Updated: 2026-08-31
Status: **CURRENT OWNER-DIRECTED GENERATION CORRECTION**

Joel explicitly corrected the current Somatic Introduction experiment: the exploratory first pass does **not** need to exactly preserve or match every original thought. Semantic matching can be optimized afterward.

## Consequence

For the exploratory generation phase, do not carry the full source meaning as a hidden composition leash. That constraint was causing the model to reproduce the source's conceptual inventory even when told to play, write online, or avoid familiar architecture.

The revised sequence is:

1. **Free exploratory composition:** write incrementally from curiosity, local sound, remembered/available thought, and what the literal page makes interesting next. Do not force coverage of the original semantic inventory. Do not fabricate biography, facts, quotations, or source claims.
2. **Cold read for life/naturalness:** judge whether the exploratory prose actually escaped the familiar model topology before investing in reconciliation.
3. **Semantic comparison after generation:** compare the exploratory passage against the owner-supplied Introduction and identify retained, missing, changed, and newly introduced substantive thoughts.
4. **Selective reconciliation:** restore only meaning that Joel wants preserved, preferably by recomposing from the live exploratory text rather than appending catch-up sentences or reinstating the original checklist architecture.
5. **Final preservation gate:** before article promotion or detector certification, all required owner-approved substantive meanings/functions must be reconciled and unexplained substantive deltas reduced to zero or explicitly owner-authorized.

This correction changes generation order, not final fidelity standards. It authorizes temporary semantic divergence during exploration so that prose architecture is not precommitted by the preservation inventory.

## Important boundary

This is not permission to invent personal experience, chronology, mechanisms, evidence, quotations, or factual specifics. Exploration may discover different emphasis, sequence, omissions, questions, analogies, or conceptual routes using only authorized factual/experiential material.
