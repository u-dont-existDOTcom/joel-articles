# Attractor Recovery — 12-Step Prose Generation Protocol

Date: 2026-08-31
Scope: Somatic Introduction exploratory generation; reusable only after owner review.
Status: current owner-directed generation experiment; not article authority; no detector authorization.

The problem is not lack of awareness of model-shaped prose. The model repeatedly recognizes the attractor after generation and then falls back into it while repairing. Therefore this protocol treats the completion attractor as a recurrent process failure that cannot be solved by willpower, reminders, or another anti-pattern list.

1. **Admit the pull is stronger than instruction.** Do not assume that noticing the default completion trajectory means it can simply be avoided.
2. **Give up the goal of finishing.** During exploratory generation there is no obligation to complete the paragraph, section, semantic packet, or argument.
3. **Hand sequence control to the live page.** The next sentence may come only from what is interesting, unresolved, remembered, questioned, or newly noticed after rereading what actually exists.
4. **Keep several thoughts alive at once.** Do not serialize cognition into one thread. Let later material interrupt earlier material; allow threads to disappear and return.
5. **Use mutation rather than synthesis.** Recombine partial thoughts to produce new candidate thoughts, but reject babies that merely restate an already-understood point or create a tidy conceptual summary.
6. **Do not exhaust the thought-space.** Generate only the few scratch fragments that genuinely arise. Silence, uncertainty, deletion, memory search, and external research are valid next states.
7. **Treat elegance as a relapse warning.** A neat contrast, symmetry, three-beat distinction, complementary branch, paragraph moral, rhetorical reversal, or satisfying close is presumptively suspect when it appears automatically.
8. **Do not repair the last criticism on command.** A diagnosis must not force its opposite into the next draft. If a defect is found, remove the defective move and return to open cognition rather than manufacturing the corresponding correction.
9. **Reread after every sentence.** Ask only whether the sentence says something new and alive. If it merely explains, restates, packages, or completes what already landed, delete it. Do not replace it unless another thought actually appears.
10. **Permit paragraph boundaries without completion.** A paragraph may stop because attention moved, not because its conceptual job is finished. Do not make each paragraph carry exactly one finished semantic unit.
11. **Keep fidelity out of the first generative pass.** The owner source remains semantic escrow, not a composition checklist. Missing meanings are reconciled afterward; reconciliation must not restore the rejected architecture automatically.
12. **When relapse occurs, reset the process rather than polish the product.** Name the attractor, discard the affected continuation, return to the last still-live sentence or fragment, and resume mutation/curiosity from there. Repeated relapse is evidence that the generation mechanism failed, not evidence that more line editing is needed.

## Relapse signatures observed in this task

- premise -> explanation -> qualification -> synthesis;
- lost choice -> regulation -> recovered choice -> EMDR;
- claim -> complementary opposite case;
- one finished conceptual unit per paragraph;
- casual wording over a preplanned semantic architecture;
- three-beat `not X -> X may still happen -> Y matters` reasoning;
- diagnosis -> immediate targeted correction;
- inserting a required source concept because it is waiting offstage rather than because the current prose led there;
- manufactured spontaneity such as announcing curiosity before delivering a prepared insight.

## Output rule

Exploratory prose is not shown as successful merely because it sounds looser. Before owner-facing evaluation, reread the literal candidate and explicitly ask whether the old attractor is visible at the level of thought progression, not merely wording. If yes, block it and reset.
