# Somatic Introduction — detached mutational generation correction

Date: 2026-08-31
Scope: current owner-directed Introduction humanization experiment only
Authority: current direct Joel correction; article master unchanged

## Owner correction

The writer must not merely diagnose an AI-shaped pull and then immediately manufacture the corresponding fix. That creates a new serial optimization pull: diagnosis -> targeted repair -> evaluate -> targeted repair.

For the exploratory generation pass:

- detach from both the original completion pull and the corrective/fix pull;
- allow several partial thoughts to remain active in parallel;
- use mutation/recombination to create genuinely new candidate thoughts, but reject mutations that merely restate already-understood material;
- write locally and reread sentence by sentence;
- delete a sentence if it overexplains, restates, summarizes, or closes a thought merely because the paragraph feels unfinished;
- a paragraph may stop while a thought is still open;
- another paragraph may begin from a different live thread rather than completing the previous one;
- do not force required source concepts such as EMDR, regulation, discharge, or inner-child work into the exploratory prose merely to satisfy coverage; semantic reconciliation happens afterward;
- do not drop a source concept in randomly either: it re-enters only when a live thought actually leads to it;
- do not treat a minimum word count as a paragraph-completion quota; if more length is needed, move to another live paragraph/thread rather than overcomplete the current one;
- do not manufacture audience-facing explanation during the exploratory pass;
- silence, uncertainty, deletion, and stopping are valid generation states.

## Persona boundary

A "Joel" stance may be used only as a taste/judgment constraint based on authorized voice material and current owner corrections. It may not invent autobiography, memories, symptoms, mechanisms, chronology, or factual claims.
