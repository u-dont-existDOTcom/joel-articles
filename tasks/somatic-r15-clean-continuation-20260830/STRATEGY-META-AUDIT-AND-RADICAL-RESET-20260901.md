## Required audit decision

Return exactly one strategy disposition:

### `CONTINUE_CURRENT_ARCHITECTURE`
Allowed only when there is affirmative evidence of convergence, not merely novelty. Examples: the same mechanism survives multiple fresh trials, genuine cold PASSes are becoming repeatable, a cold PASS also receives Pangram Human, or failure severity is decreasing without mechanism churn.

### `SIMPLIFY_OR_ROLL_BACK`
Use when the mechanism appears over-constrained and a smaller earlier architecture is better supported than continued gate accretion.

### `RADICAL_RESET`
Use when local repair is cycling, evaluation is contaminated, or the mechanism keeps changing without external success. A radical reset must change at least one of: physical context isolation, model/runtime, role architecture, representation architecture, or experiment objective. **Adding another prose prohibition is not a radical reset.**

### `ABANDON_LANE`
Use when the strategy has accumulated enough negative evidence that continued testing has low information value relative to another already-available lane.
