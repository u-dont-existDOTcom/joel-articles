## Problem

The current bounded target is the first AI-written part of the Somatic Therapies Introduction. The required outcome is not merely a detector change. The replacement must cease to exhibit the discourse movement Joel immediately recognizes as model-shaped while preserving every protected meaning, safety function, evidence distinction, link, native object, and provenance boundary. Owner-written Human material cannot be used as evidence that AI-target prose improved.

Repeated attempts changed wording, surface register, source exposure, and prompt size but reproduced a common macroform: an abstract proposition followed by qualification, explicit relation, a closed set of options, and explanatory closure. A fresh-context writer given an exhaustive semantic packet reproduced this form without seeing the source prose. The current compressed brief still presents an ordered inventory of concepts and therefore may still function as a latent paragraph plan.

## Preliminary causal model

The likely shared cause is **coverage-induced discourse planning**, not a lexical defect. An instruction-following model asked to realize several abstract meanings in a short coherent introduction has a strong incentive to:

1. convert the meanings into a coverage plan;
2. service them in prompt order;
3. state relations that a reader could infer;
4. balance distinctions to avoid apparent contradiction;
5. close each paragraph and then close the set.

Removing the source prose breaks sentence-level anchoring but does not break this planning pressure. Compressing the semantic ledger makes the plan less verbose, not less plan-like. Long anti-pattern lists may also preserve the same latent plan while merely changing its surface realization.

The proposed semantic-custodian / fresh-writer / blind-validator separation improves contamination control, but its generator remains inside the failed family so long as the writer receives the full semantic inventory before composing a complete section.

## Candidate mechanisms worth distinguishing

### 1. Low-structure regeneration

Give a fresh writer the compressed concept brief and reject AI-shaped candidates. Preliminary status: probably still a descendant of `EXHAUSTIVE-CONSTRAINT-TO-EXPOSITORY-PROSE`; fresh context removes history, not the semantic macroplan.

### 2. Coverage-blind stochastic generation with downstream filtering

Give independent writers only one governing pressure or reader problem, generate several complete candidates, then let a separate custodian reject candidates that fail semantic coverage. This changes optimization order: generation is not asked to service a checklist. Risk: the probability of complete preservation may be too low, and same-model samples may be highly correlated.

### 3. Fragment generation followed by assembly

Generate local moves independently and assemble them later. Preliminary concern: assembly is likely to create conceptual cards, seams, and model-written connective tissue—the exact problem under repair.

### 4. Progressive disclosure / serial constraint repair

Reveal one missing function at a time. Preliminary concern: this may merely turn the exhaustive checklist into a multi-turn checklist, while same-context patching preserves the original macroplan.

### 5. Subtractive topology surgery on the existing target

Do not regenerate a complete section. Begin from the clean R15 target and permit only deletion, splitting, merging, and relocation of existing clauses or sentences, with no synonym substitution, new bridge, new claim, style polishing, or imported language. The purpose is to test whether the obvious AI signal is carried by the adjacency and accumulation of explanatory closures rather than by every proposition individually.

This is provisionally causally different from paraphrase/regeneration because it cannot synthesize a new comprehensive essay plan. It can only remove redundant relation-announcing language or alter the order in which existing thoughts arrive. It is compatible with a provenance ledger and makes semantic losses mechanically visible.

Risks:

- the source clauses themselves may already encode the model-shaped architecture;
- preserving all protected functions may leave too little deletable material;
- reordering alone may harm coherence;
- a reasoning model could still use deletion as covert detector optimization;
- the target may contain owner-final or unknown-frozen material that cannot be treated as disposable;
- success on the bounded introduction may not transfer to the whole-document boundary.

### 6. Fresh owner-language acquisition

This would be maximally causally different, but Joel has explicitly corrected the workflow: the ideas are already complete and chats must perform the humanization rather than shifting authorship labor back to him. It is therefore not an available default strategy for this task.

## Preliminary evaluator design

A validator should not answer “human or AI?” from a feature checklist. That invites mimicry and turns evaluation into another detector. It should read only the candidate and report the actual movement of thought in ordinary language, then identify the earliest point at which the continuation becomes predictable because the prose starts servicing an apparent plan. It should distinguish local awkwardness from macro-level preconstruction and should not prescribe replacement prose.

A separate semantic custodian may use the complete ledger only after a candidate survives that reading. The custodian should return exact lost, weakened, or altered functions, with provenance class (`OWNER_LOCK`, `AI_TARGET`, `UNKNOWN_FROZEN`), but should not patch the candidate. A semantic failure should either kill the candidate or, at most, justify one new independent attempt with one newly indispensable boundary condition—not a cumulative checklist.

## Preliminary kill logic

A strategy family should be killed when materially independent attempts reproduce the same macroform, not merely when Pangram stays red. Three fresh samples from the same model and same semantic brief are not strong independence. One strict subtractive-topology attempt may be enough to falsify that family if the custodian shows that every potentially shape-breaking deletion removes an indispensable function. At most two independently conceived topology variants appear defensible before stopping.

## Question for the bounded existing-work scan

The scan should test, rather than overwrite, this conception:

- whether content-planning research predicts that semantic inventories become discourse plans;
- whether fresh-context or best-of-N sampling supplies meaningful independence;
- known biases and limitations of LLM-as-judge for discourse-shape evaluation;
- whether edit-based or deletion-first generation offers a mature analogue to subtractive topology surgery;
- whether a stronger established method can separate semantic custody from prose realization without reintroducing checklist servicing.
