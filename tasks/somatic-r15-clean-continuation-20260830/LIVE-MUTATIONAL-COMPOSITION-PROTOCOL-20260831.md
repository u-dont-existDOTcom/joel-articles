# Live Mutational Composition Protocol — 2026-08-31

Status: ACTIVE OWNER-DIRECTED GENERATION EXPERIMENT for the Somatic Introduction. This is a prose-generation protocol, not article authority, detector evidence, or permission to invent facts/autobiography.

## Owner correction

Joel's current direction is to stop treating prose generation as completion of a semantic packet. During the exploratory generation pass, exact source matching may be deferred; reconciliation can happen afterward. The first pass should behave more like live human composition: partial, curious, interruptible, non-exhaustive, and allowed to stop.

## Generation mechanism

1. **No audience during discovery.** Do not write to satisfy a reader, teacher, word count, paragraph quota, semantic checklist, or detector. Do not make a thought publishable merely because it exists.
2. **Tiny scratch, not exhaustive scratch.** A real writer may jot two or three fragments, pause, delete one, remember something, search something, or stop. Do not enumerate the whole thought-space.
3. **Parallel rather than serialized cognition.** Several unfinished thoughts may coexist. A later thought may interrupt, mutate, or redirect an earlier one. Do not force A -> B -> C completion or require each semantic unit to finish before another enters.
4. **Genetic recombination.** Maintain a small pool of live thoughts/fragments. Recombine pairs or clusters to produce 'baby' thoughts. Mutations are valuable only when they create genuinely new cognition or a fresh relation; a restatement of an already-known claim is a failed mutation.
5. **Local sentence generation.** Write one sentence from the currently live mutation, then reread the literal sentence before deciding what comes next. The future paragraph is not preallocated.
6. **Overexplanation kill check after every sentence.** Ask whether the sentence changes the thought, opens a real question, changes case/time/premise/consequence, or adds a genuinely new relation. If it merely restates, interprets, summarizes, diagnoses, justifies, recaps, or explains what the prior sentence already made available, delete it. Either replace it with a less completed realization or move to another live thought.
7. **No completion pressure.** Paragraphs do not need to 'earn' closure. If nothing interesting appears, stop. Silence is preferable to aftercare.
8. **Curiosity/play over optimization.** Treat the material as something to poke at rather than an assignment. Interesting contradictions, odd word choices, tensions, or questions may outrank planned coverage.
9. **Joel stance may guide taste, not fabricate biography.** A provisional 'Joel' persona may help with judgment, skepticism, humor, or what feels worth pursuing, but it may not invent experiences, memories, symptoms, chronology, evidence, or claims not authorized by source/owner material.
10. **Reconciliation comes later.** After a live exploratory passage exists, compare it against the authoritative source/preservation units. Recover missing functions selectively without reinstalling the original model-shaped architecture. Any production candidate still requires normal preservation, provenance, architecture, cold-audit, owner, and detector gates.

## Known failure modes this protocol is meant to prevent

- semantic obligation -> rhetorical card sequence;
- problem -> intervention -> exception flowcharts;
- exhaustive scratch disguised as creativity;
- paragraph-length seeking / freshman essay padding;
- explanation after the reader already has the inference;
- polished bridges inserted only to make separate thoughts look planned;
- serial processing that prevents later thoughts from changing earlier ones;
- 'mutations' that only paraphrase existing content;
- treating a successful mutation as something that must immediately be explained.

## Current experiment rule

For the Somatic Introduction, exploratory prose may temporarily omit or mutate source content because Joel explicitly authorized semantic matching to be optimized afterward. No exploratory passage is article authority until later reconciliation and normal gates pass.
