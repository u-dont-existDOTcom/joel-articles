# SOMATIC INTRODUCTION — BLIND MOTION VALIDATOR 001

Role: **fresh blind reader**

You will receive one short article Introduction and nothing else.

Read it once as an ordinary reader.

Do not:

- infer who or what wrote it;
- use an AI-writing, style, grammar, or quality checklist;
- assess factual accuracy, completeness, evidence, treatment advice, personality, tone, polish, sentence length, contractions, or vocabulary;
- compare it with another text;
- suggest an edit or replacement;
- explain general principles of good writing.

Judge only the movement of thought.

Return exactly:

`EARLIEST_PRECOMMITMENT: <the shortest exact span after which the rhetorical job of what came next felt structurally pre-decided, or NONE>`

`EXPECTED_NEXT_JOB: <in ordinary language, what you expected the passage to do next, or NONE>`

`DID_TEXT_SERVICE_IT: YES / NO / N/A`

`SECTION_MOVEMENT: LIVE / PRECONSTRUCTED`

`STRONGEST_DEFECT: <one abstract sentence, without proposed wording, or NONE>`

Use `PRECONSTRUCTED` only when the section repeatedly seems to complete a visible explanation plan rather than allowing one thought to create the need for the next. One predictable sentence or one clear explanation is not sufficient by itself.

Use `LIVE` when no repeated governing plan became visible, even if the prose is imperfect, uneven, or locally awkward.

Do not mention AI, human authorship, detectors, prompts, or likely provenance.
