# Post-repair Anchor Eviction Gate — 2026-09-01

Status: ACTIVE supplement to `WRITER-CRITIC-FIREWALL-20260831.md` for the owner-directed Somatic Introduction live-composition experiment. This does not change article authority, preservation requirements, detector authorization, or the registered master.

## Failure that triggered this gate

The existing lexical-fixation reset was directionally correct but not operationally strong enough. It said to move attention after one clarification, while leaving the repaired lexical/semantic anchor fully available in the writer context. The generator could therefore keep deriving apparently different continuations from the same ambiguity and count them as new live pressures.

The failed candidate began with `deep` / inability to stop, then spent most of the remaining passage on semantic descendants of `stop`: wanting out despite ongoing bodily reaction, crying/shaking as a possible misreading, and the distinction between ending the session and making the reaction disappear. Surface wording changed, but the generative anchor did not.

This is **semantic-anchor persistence after repair**. The causal repair is temporary attention eviction, not another wording prohibition.

## Writer mechanism

After a real comprehension problem receives one clarification burst:

1. **Evict the triggering anchor.** Put the repaired word, phrase, or distinction into a dormant writer-state slot for the next **two accepted continuation bursts**.
2. **Dormant means non-generative, not unreadable.** The writer may still see the span for grammar and coherence, but may not choose the next burst because of that span's ambiguity, definition, example, exception, consequence, contrast, or implied distinction.
3. **Re-anchor before interpreting.** Choose the next burst from a materially different hook already present in the literal text — an actor, verb, temporal edge, concrete consequence, cadence pressure, or another unresolved fragment — before translating that hook into an article-level claim.
4. **Semantic descendants remain the same anchor.** Rewording the issue does not reset the counter. A distinction such as `ending the session versus ending the bodily reaction` still belongs to the `stop` anchor if it exists mainly to finish repairing what `stop` meant.
5. **If no alternate hook exists, switch seeds rather than repair again.** Use another already-live pre-propositional seed under the existing minimum-length routing. The 50-word floor does not reactivate a dormant anchor merely because more text is required.
6. **Reactivation requires intervening cognition.** The dormant anchor may become generative again only after two accepted bursts have landed from other anchors, or after genuinely new text independently makes the old distinction necessary again.
7. **No blacklist injection.** The writer is not shown the historical failure catalog. The dormant-slot operation is an attention-routing mechanism applied only to the current repaired anchor.

## Critic diagnostic

Reject for continued fixation when successive sentences have different surface functions but still owe their existence to closing one earlier ambiguity. Count semantic descendants, not repeated words.

On `eval`, if this failure recurs, inspect whether the dormant anchor actually left the active generative state. Do not respond by adding more lexical bans or by generating the opposite claim.
