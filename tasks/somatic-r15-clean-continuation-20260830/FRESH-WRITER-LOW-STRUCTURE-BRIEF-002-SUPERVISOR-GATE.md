# Fresh-writer low-structure generation gate

**Status:** controlling companion for `FRESH-WRITER-LOW-STRUCTURE-BRIEF-002.md`  
**Date:** 2026-08-31

## Audited strategy decision

The prior semantic-packet strategy is rejected as a descendant of `EXHAUSTIVE-CONSTRAINT-TO-EXPOSITORY-PROSE`. Removing the old sentences did not remove the old planning pressure: an exhaustive decomposed ledger made the writer responsible for servicing every conceptual unit and became an implicit essay outline.

This replacement changes the generation information state. The writer receives only the low-structure brief. The semantic custodian retains the complete provenance, meaning, attribution, safety, link, and preservation ledger downstream.

## Writer information firewall

The writer must not receive:

- the source Introduction;
- any earlier rewrite;
- owner Human exemplars or unrelated donor prose;
- Pangram results or highlighted spans;
- failed-strategy history;
- an anti-AI pattern catalogue;
- the exhaustive semantic or preservation ledger;
- a paragraph plan, required ordering, or per-claim sentence allocation.

One candidate is produced per genuinely fresh context. Do not ask that context to critique, polish, repair, rank, or regenerate its own candidate.

## Gate order

1. **Blind discourse-shape gate.** A fresh evaluator sees only the candidate and the fact that it is an article Introduction. It does not see the semantic ledger, producer rationale, prior candidates, or detector result. It reports only whether the prose has an obvious model-composed explanatory architecture and identifies the strongest structural defect. The producing context may not adjudicate this gate.
2. **Disposition.** Any candidate with clear model shape is discarded intact. Do not patch it, conversationalize it, or request a second pass from the same context.
3. **Semantic/provenance gate.** Only a blind-shape survivor is checked against the complete custodian ledger for meaning, attribution, certainty, links, scope, and owner-language provenance.
4. **Missing-content response.** A material omission does not authorize patching the survivor. Start a new fresh context with a minimally revised low-structure brief that incorporates only the missing constraint needed to make the next attempt valid. Do not expose the full ledger.
5. **Owner read, then detector.** Pangram is last. Owner-authored text is accounted for separately and cannot be counted as the writer's humanization gain.

## Pool rule

A candidate pool is permitted only across genuinely separate contexts. Multiple versions, self-selection, or repeated cold starts inside one context are not independent and do not constitute a pool.

## Failure/kill condition

Stop this strategy family after three independently generated candidates if all three fail the blind discourse-shape gate in materially similar ways. Return `NO_VALID_STRATEGY` or authorize a materially different family; do not lengthen the prompt, add a larger anti-pattern list, or expose the full semantic ledger.

No paid detector call is authorized merely because a candidate passes semantic preservation. It must first survive the blind discourse-shape gate and owner inspection.
