# Clause Refractory Anti-Attractor Protocol — 2026-08-31

Status: ACTIVE owner-directed exploratory generation method for the Somatic Introduction. Not article authority and not detector authorization.

## Owner correction

After repeated failures, Joel identified that even live-mutational composition still allowed each thought to complete its own local arc. The next change is to intervene before local completion rather than critique it afterward.

## Mechanism

1. Generate a tiny private pool of thought mutations before prose.
2. Select one live mutation only; do not expose the prose generator to the full semantic map or the full rejected-pattern history.
3. Write at clause scale, not paragraph scale.
4. Once a clause lands, its most obvious explanatory, balancing, summarizing, or completing continuation enters a temporary refractory period and is ineligible.
5. Look sideways rather than forward: allow another live thought to interrupt, contaminate, or redirect the line.
6. Do not mechanically alternate topics. Refractory means the obvious continuation cannot win immediately; it does not require random switching.
7. Treat sudden elegance, symmetry, contrast, distilled insight, tidy qualification, and local arc completion as danger cues before acceptance.
8. If a continuation survives only because it repairs the previous criticism or produces the opposite of a rejected move, reject it as same-attractor behavior.
9. A thought may return later only after another thought has changed the local state enough that its continuation is no longer the original obvious completion.
10. No semantic reconciliation during this exploratory pass unless a source obligation arises naturally. Reconcile later under normal preservation gates.

## Goal

Prevent each interesting thought from becoming a self-contained model mini-essay before another thought can interfere with it.
