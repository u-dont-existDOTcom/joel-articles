# Owner correction — online composition / write-read-think loop

Updated: 2026-08-31
Status: **CURRENT OWNER-DIRECTED GENERATION CORRECTION**

## Owner description

Joel describes his actual writing process as online composition rather than batch realization:

- he writes word by word or in very small increments;
- he reads the literal text while writing it and notices whether it sounds natural;
- he often does not yet know what to write next;
- when the next move is not available, he stops and thinks rather than completing a pre-existing outline;
- later cognition may come from remembering something, reconsidering what has just been written, or searching/checking something he remembers hearing about;
- what gets written next is therefore conditioned by the actual text and cognition that now exist, not by a complete future coverage plan decided before the paragraph begins.

## Generative correction

For the Somatic Introduction, do not treat the task as:

`semantic inventory -> complete architecture -> paragraph realization -> cold edit`

Instead approximate the owner process as:

`minimal start -> literal reread -> next-live-thought decision -> minimal continuation -> literal reread -> ...`

Important consequences:

1. **No future obligation is allowed to pull the current sentence merely because it still needs coverage later.** Semantic obligations remain in escrow and are audited after a natural realization exists.
2. **Not knowing what comes next is an allowed and useful state.** Do not fill uncertainty with the model's most probable explanatory bridge.
3. **Rereading is generative input.** The literal existing sentence/paragraph can change what the next thought should be.
4. **Thinking/remembering/checking may intervene between increments.** New factual material may enter only with normal source/provenance authority; this is not permission to fabricate memory or specificity.
5. **Do not write the whole Introduction mentally and then emit it word by word.** That would preserve batch architecture beneath a simulated slow surface.
6. **Do not expose private chain-of-thought or hidden intermediate drafts.** The operational requirement is genuine incremental recomputation, not publication of internal reasoning.
7. A rewrite counts as successful only if the resulting thought movement differs from the known model topology; slower emission of the same topology is a failure.

## Relation to prior failed experiments

The `no observer / no writer` experiment did not alter the generative mechanism. The model translated the metaphor into another global instruction and immediately rebuilt the familiar semantic sequence. This owner correction is more concrete: change the **temporal granularity and feedback structure of composition**, not the metaphysical framing of the writer.

## Current experiment

For the next Somatic Introduction attempt:

- use the owner-supplied Introduction only as semantic authority, not sentence-order authority;
- begin with the smallest natural opening that genuinely presents itself;
- after each small increment, reread only the literal accumulated prose and ask what, if anything, naturally needs to happen next;
- when no next move is genuinely available, remain unresolved rather than forcing coverage;
- consult semantic escrow only after a locally natural stopping point to identify missing obligations; if something is missing, reconstruct from the live prose rather than appending a checklist catch-up;
- preserve the exact inner-child link and all substantive meanings;
- only after the whole section exists run preservation and cold audits.
