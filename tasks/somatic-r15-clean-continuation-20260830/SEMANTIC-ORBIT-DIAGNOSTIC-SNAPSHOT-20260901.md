# Semantic Orbit Diagnostic Snapshot — 2026-09-01

Status: pre-existing-work independent conception snapshot for the owner-directed Somatic Introduction live-composition experiment. This is diagnostic process material only; it is not article authority, detector evidence, or a candidate.

## Literal failure observed

The 62-word exploratory passage remained in one semantic orbit. Its sentences repeatedly realized the same underlying proposition in different surface forms: cognitive understanding can be complete while bodily reaction continues; more explanation may therefore be irrelevant. The passage did not merely contain low-load connective material; nearly every sentence remained a paraphrastic descendant of the initial thought.

## Independent mechanism hypothesis

The previous repair deferred sentence-level conceptual review by introducing a provisional burst buffer. That removed the insight-density ratchet, but the actual active writer instruction still supplied only **one small pre-propositional snag**, despite the higher-level strategy describing a small parallel pool. With no sentence-level review and no second live pressure entering production, autoregressive continuation let the first seed monopolize attention until the hidden length controller stopped the buffer.

Working name: **single-seed attentional monopoly / semantic orbit**.

## Constraints

- Keep source obligations occluded during exploratory generation.
- Do not invent autobiography, symptoms, chronology, evidence, mechanisms, or factual specificity.
- Keep the numerical minimum hidden from the writer.
- Keep the critic outside production.
- Do not restore bullet-ready multi-seed semantic cards; that earlier mechanism already failed.
- Do not force random topic switching merely to manufacture diversity.
- No Pangram/detector action and no article-authority change.

## Candidate insight before literature/tool scan

Restore genuinely parallel cognition **upstream of propositions**, but prevent one seed from owning the whole provisional buffer. Possible mechanism class: maintain two or three pre-propositional pressures that are individually incomplete; allow a separate scheduler/attention process to expose another already-live pressure during production without telling the writer why, without using uncovered source obligations, and without requiring a rhetorical bridge. The next design should distinguish useful interruption from arbitrary divergence and should benchmark against established anti-degeneration/diversity mechanisms before bespoke implementation.
