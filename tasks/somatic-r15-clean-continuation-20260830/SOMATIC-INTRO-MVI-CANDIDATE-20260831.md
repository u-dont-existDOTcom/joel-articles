# Introduction

An event can be over and a person can know perfectly well that it is over while the body is still reacting as if danger is present. Sometimes the story is already understood and more thinking isn't the thing that changes what the body is doing. Some accumulated survival activation may need a bodily way to discharge or resolve.

With complex or developmental trauma, I care much less about “depth” once the person is no longer present enough to choose whether to stop. Going straight to the deepest memory can take someone there. I want them present enough to stop if they want to, and I want them basically themselves again afterward. If that isn't happening, “deep processing” may just be a nicer name for overwhelm. Regulation is part of the work when it gets that room back. In [inner-child work](http://innerchild.u-dont-exist.com/), someone may already know exactly what the younger part needs and still be too caught in the trauma/child-state for the adult perspective to be available.

I wouldn't make someone do somatic preparation just to make the treatment sequence cleaner. When that room is already there, going straight to EMDR or another trauma-focused method may make more sense.
