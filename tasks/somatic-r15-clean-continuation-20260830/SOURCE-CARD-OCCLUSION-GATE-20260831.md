# Source-Card Occlusion Gate — 2026-08-31

Status: ACTIVE supplement to `LIVE-MUTATIONAL-COMPOSITION-PROTOCOL-20260831.md` for the owner-directed Somatic Introduction generation experiment.

## Failure that triggered this gate

An `eval` found that even after semantic amnesia, opaque collision, matched-pair quarantine, and dialectic quarantine, the generator still knew which source topics remained uncovered. That latent coverage map caused a new paragraph to begin with a source-domain label (`In inner-child work...`) after the prior thought had locally ended. The prose therefore remained a sequence of semantic cards even though the cards were less polished.

## Rule

During exploratory generation, source obligations are **reconciliation-only state**, not topic-selection state.

1. **Occlude uncovered obligations.** After the initial live seed is chosen, do not consult the remaining source-unit list, treatment menu, paragraph jobs, or coverage map to decide what comes next.
2. **No coverage-driven topic admission.** A topic may not enter because it is important, missing, next in the source, or eventually required for preservation.
3. **Literal-pressure admission only.** A new topic may enter only when something in the literal words already on the page creates a live curiosity, irritation, ambiguity, memory, consequence, or association that makes that topic independently salient.
4. **No source-domain reset.** Starting a new paragraph with a source-domain label is presumptively a card reset unless the preceding literal prose made that domain unavoidable.
5. **One seed can occupy the whole exploratory pass.** It is valid for an exploratory pass to stay with one thought and omit every other source obligation. Missing content is repaired later in reconciliation, not by sneaking cards into discovery.
6. **No artificial wandering.** Occlusion does not mean random topic switching. If the current literal text produces no live next thought, stop rather than import another source card.
7. **Reconciliation remains separate.** Only after a genuinely live passage exists may the authoritative source/preservation units be reopened to recover missing functions. Reconciliation must not retroactively justify coverage-driven prose generated during discovery.

## `eval` consequence

When an `eval` finds card succession, diagnose whether the cause was latent source coverage rather than merely a bad transition. If so, restart from the last live seed with all unreconciled source topics occluded from topic selection.
